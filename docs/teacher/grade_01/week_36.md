# Teacher's Guide — 1st Grade, Week 36

## ELA 1: Year-end review and celebration of learning

**Prepare:** End-of-year phonics screener (all patterns), a cold grade 1 passage, the week 1 writing sample, the year's writing portfolio, and a summer reading list.

**Watch for:**
- Rushing the screener: assessments are untimed except fluency.
- Forgetting capitals in names in the letter: check the greeting and signature.
- Using only 'good' and 'nice' to describe: try cozy, bright, noisy, gentle.

### Day 1 (Monday): Phonics screener
*Student:* Complete an end-of-year phonics and sight-word screener covering all patterns.

*How to teach:* Give the screener one-on-one, marking errors by pattern (short vowels, digraphs, blends, silent e, vowel teams, r-controlled, diphthongs).

- **Q:** Read: ship, frog, cake, rain, star, cow.
  **A:** ship, frog, cake, rain, star, cow.
- **Q:** Read: boy, light.
  **A:** boy, light.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Fluency & comprehension
*Student:* Read a cold grade 1 passage; retell and answer questions.

*How to teach:* Your child reads a cold passage; retells and answers questions.

- **Q:** What should a retell include?
  **A:** Characters, setting, problem, events in order, ending.
- **Q:** What is a cold passage?
  **A:** One you haven't read before.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Writing on demand
*Student:* Write a letter to a future first grader with capitalized names and the date.

*How to teach:* Your child writes a letter to a future first grader with date, greeting, body, closing, signature.

- **Q:** How do you write the date?
  **A:** Month day, year: e.g., June 5, 2027.
- **Q:** What goes at the end of a letter?
  **A:** Closing (Your friend,) and your name.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Real-life words
*Student:* Find words around the house that are cozy, bright, or noisy and use them in sentences.

*How to teach:* Find words around the house that are cozy, bright, noisy and use them in sentences.

- **Q:** Name something cozy.
  **A:** Example: a blanket.
- **Q:** Use noisy in a sentence.
  **A:** Example: The blender is noisy.

### Day 5 (Friday): Celebration
*Student:* Share a portfolio of favorite writing from the year with family; choose summer reading books.

*How to teach:* Share the portfolio; compare week 1 and now; choose summer books.

- **Q:** What did you get better at in writing?
  **A:** Answers vary; compare: more sentences, capitals, spelling.
- **Q:** Name a summer book you want to read.
  **A:** Answers vary.

**Assessment:** End-of-year screener, fluency check, and on-demand writing sample compared with week 1.

**Answer key:** Screener: proficient = 90%+ on each pattern; note weak patterns for summer practice. Fluency: end-of-grade-1 benchmark is roughly 50–60 WCPM on a grade-level passage. Letter rubric: 4 = date with comma, greeting, 3+ sentences, closing, signature, capitals on names; 3 = minor errors; 2 = missing parts; 1 = incomplete. Compare to week 1 sentence to show growth.

## Math 1: Year-end celebration and assessment

**Prepare:** Printed end-of-year assessment part 2 (see key), a math journal or folded paper booklet, a clipboard for the scavenger hunt, and favorite game materials.

**Watch for:**
- Rushing through a long test: break it into short sessions and read aloud.
- Forgetting half-hour clock reading: review the hour hand 'between two numbers' before the test.
- Confusing nickel and dime values: review the coin chart.

### Day 1 (Monday): Cumulative test
*Student:* End-of-year assessment part 2: adding within 100, measurement, time, coins, data and shapes.

*How to teach:* Give the end-of-year assessment part 2 over 1–2 sittings, reading every item aloud.

- **Q:** 45 + 30 = ?
  **A:** 75.
- **Q:** Short hand between 3 and 4, long hand on 6. Time?
  **A:** 3:30.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Math journal
*Student:* Reflect on favorite learning and make a page.

*How to teach:* Your child makes a journal page about a favorite thing they learned, with a drawing and an example problem they solved.

- **Q:** What was your favorite math topic? Show one example.
  **A:** Answers vary; the example must be correct (e.g., 9 + 4 = 13 by making ten).
- **Q:** What strategy helps you add 9 + 6?
  **A:** Make ten: 9 + 1 = 10, plus 5 = 15.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Math scavenger hunt
*Student:* Hunt for shapes, numbers, and measurements outdoors.

*How to teach:* Take a walk outside to find shapes (circles, rectangles), numbers (house numbers, signs), and things to compare by length. Record findings on a clipboard.

- **Q:** Find a rectangle outside. How do you know?
  **A:** Example: a door — 4 sides, 4 square corners.
- **Q:** Read a house number. How many tens and ones?
  **A:** Answers vary; e.g., 47 = 4 tens 7 ones.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Teach someone
*Student:* Teach a family member your favorite math game (e.g., Make Ten Go Fish) and explain the math in it.

*How to teach:* Your child teaches a family member a favorite game and explains the math in it ('In Go Fish for 10, you need partners that make 10').

- **Q:** What math do you practice in Make Ten Go Fish?
  **A:** Pairs that make 10 (1+9, 2+8, 3+7, 4+6, 5+5).
- **Q:** What does War practice?
  **A:** Comparing numbers and how many more.

### Day 5 (Friday): Celebration
*Student:* Math fair: show projects and journals to family.

*How to teach:* Hold a math fair: your child shows projects (survey graph, clock, journals) and explains one thing they learned.

- **Q:** Tell how your survey graph works.
  **A:** Each square/bar shows how many people chose that answer; the tallest bar is the most popular.
- **Q:** What's one thing you got better at?
  **A:** Answers vary — encourage specific examples.

**Assessment:** End-of-year assessment.

**Answer key:** End-of-year part 2 (suggested) with answers: 1) 30+40=70 2) 52+7=59 3) 38+5=43 4) 61+20=81 5) 90−40=50 6) Order 3 pictured objects by length (check) 7) A shoe measured with cubes drawn → count shown 8) Clocks: 7:00, 10:30, 2:30 9) 3 dimes 4 pennies = 34¢ 10) Name & value: quarter 25¢, nickel 5¢ 11) Graph (apples 5, pears 2, grapes 4): most = apples, total 11, apples − pears = 3 12) Circle the shape with 3 sides & 3 corners (triangle) 13) Shade one fourth of a square. Proficient: 90% or better; review any weak strand over the summer.

## Science 1 (NGSS): Science fair and year review

**Prepare:** Science notebook, the showcase project, poster board, markers, and a year-end review sheet (see key).

**Watch for:**
- Presenting without data: always show test results.
- Forgetting key vocabulary: review vibrate, opaque, transparent, reflect, phase, offspring.
- Mixing up unit ideas: sort ideas by unit (sound, light, sky, living things).

### Day 1 (Monday): Notebook review
*Student:* Page through your science notebook and flag your 3 favorite investigations.

*How to teach:* Page through the notebook; flag 3 favorite investigations.

- **Q:** What was your favorite investigation and why?
  **A:** Answers vary.
- **Q:** What did you learn from it?
  **A:** Answers vary — must be accurate.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Poster
*Student:* Make a poster for your showcase project: problem, design sketch, test data and improvement.

*How to teach:* Make a poster: problem, design sketch, test data, improvement.

- **Q:** What goes first?
  **A:** The problem.
- **Q:** Why include data?
  **A:** To show whether it worked.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Science fair
*Student:* Present the project and one favorite investigation to family, answering their questions.

*How to teach:* Present the project and an investigation to family.

- **Q:** How should you present?
  **A:** Clearly, using data.
- **Q:** Answer a question.
  **A:** In a complete sentence.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Reflect
*Student:* Write or dictate 3 sentences about what you learned about sound, light, sky or living things.

*How to teach:* Your child writes 3 sentences about what they learned.

- **Q:** Write one sentence about sound.
  **A:** Sound is made by vibrations.
- **Q:** One about the sky.
  **A:** The sun rises in the east and sets in the west.

### Day 5 (Friday): Year-end check
*Student:* Complete a year-end science review of key ideas from each unit.

*How to teach:* Give the year-end science review.

- **Q:** What makes sound?
  **A:** Vibrations.
- **Q:** What causes a shadow?
  **A:** An opaque object blocking light.

**Assessment:** Science fair presentation and year-end review.

**Answer key:** Year-end review (suggested): 1) Sound is made by vibrations 2) Thin/short/tight = higher pitch 3) We need light to see 4) Glass transparent, wax paper translucent, cardboard opaque 5) Shadow = opaque object blocks light 6) Sun rises east, sets west 7) Moon phases repeat about monthly 8) Stars are present by day but hidden by sunlight 9) Summer has more daylight than winter 10) Roots take in water; leaves make food 11) Velcro copies burrs 12) Young animals are like but not exactly like parents. Proficient 11–12. Presentation: clear problem, data, improvement.

## A Child's Place in Time and Space: Market day and year review

**Prepare:** Craft supplies for 6 bookmarks or crafts, price tags, coins, and the year's maps, symbol cards, then-and-now booklet, and culture projects. Prepare the year-end review sheet.

**Watch for:**
- Pricing too high: goods must be priced so buyers can afford them.
- Forgetting that a vote decides the plan: majority wins.
- Mixing up units at review: sort review items by unit.

### Day 1 (Monday): Plan a market
*Student:* Vote as a family (direct democracy) on what goods to make and sell at a pretend market.

*How to teach:* Hold a family vote (direct democracy) on what goods to make and sell.

- **Q:** What kind of democracy is a family vote?
  **A:** Direct democracy.
- **Q:** What decides the result?
  **A:** The choice with the most votes.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Make goods
*Student:* Make 6 bookmarks or crafts, decide on fair prices under $1, and make price tags.

*How to teach:* Make 6 goods; set fair prices under $1; make tags.

- **Q:** What is a fair price?
  **A:** One that pays for materials and that buyers think is worth it.
- **Q:** If bookmarks cost 25¢ each, how much for 2?
  **A:** 50¢.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Market day
*Student:* Hold a family market using coins; practice buying and selling.

*How to teach:* Hold a family market with coins.

- **Q:** You sell a 35¢ item and get 50¢. How much change?
  **A:** 15¢.
- **Q:** Are you a producer or a consumer when you sell?
  **A:** A producer (seller).

### Day 4 (Thursday): Year review
*Student:* Review maps, symbols, then-and-now booklet and culture projects.

*How to teach:* Review maps, symbols, then-and-now, culture projects.

- **Q:** What do the flag's stripes stand for?
  **A:** The 13 original colonies.
- **Q:** Name a continent.
  **A:** Any of the 7.

### Day 5 (Friday): Year-end check
*Student:* Complete a year-end review: label continents and oceans, match symbols, sort then-and-now pictures, and sort goods and services.

*How to teach:* Give the year-end review.

- **Q:** Is a haircut a good or service?
  **A:** Service.
- **Q:** Which ocean is along California?
  **A:** Pacific.

**Assessment:** Year-end review and market participation.

**Answer key:** Year-end review (suggested): label 7 continents and 4 oceans; match symbols (flag—50 states/13 colonies; bald eagle—national bird; Statue of Liberty—freedom, gift from France; Liberty Bell—liberty); sort then/now (slate, stagecoach, washboard = then; tablet, car, washing machine = now); sort goods (bread, toy) and services (haircut, doctor). Proficient 90%+. Market: priced goods, made change correctly.

## PE 1: Field day celebration

**Prepare:** All field-day equipment, water, sunscreen, shade; prizes/paper for awards.

**Watch for:**
- Thinking only winners matter: field day celebrates effort and encouragement.
- Skipping water breaks on hot days: drink often.

### Day 1 (Monday): Field day 1
*Student:* Run the pillowcase sack race and the locomotor relay, cheering for others.

*How to teach:* Run the sack race and locomotor relay; cheer for others.

- **Q:** What do you say to cheer someone on?
  **A:** 'Go, go, go!' 'You can do it!'

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Field day 2
*Student:* Compete in the beanbag toss and a family kickball game.

*How to teach:* Beanbag toss and family kickball.

- **Q:** Which toss cues help in beanbag toss?
  **A:** Swing back, step opposite, follow through.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Field day 3
*Student:* Obstacle course and tug-of-war.

*How to teach:* Obstacle course and tug-of-war (pull steadily; no sudden letting go).

- **Q:** How do you stay safe in tug-of-war?
  **A:** Hold with both hands, never wrap the rope around you, and don't let go suddenly.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Awards
*Student:* Make awards for effort and encouragement.

*How to teach:* Make awards for effort and encouragement.

- **Q:** What award would you give and why?
  **A:** Example: 'Best Cheerleader' for encouraging everyone.

### Day 5 (Friday): Reflect
*Student:* Share favorite activity and feelings.

*How to teach:* Share favorite activities and feelings from the year.

- **Q:** What was your favorite PE activity this year?
  **A:** Answers vary.

**Assessment:** Field day participation.

**Answer key:** Participation: tries every event, cheers for others, follows safety rules, shows good sportsmanship. Proficient = all four.

## Health 1: Health review and celebration

**Prepare:** Jeopardy-style question cards (feelings, hygiene, food, activity, safety), paper for a 'My Healthy Me' book, and healthy party foods.

**Watch for:**
- Rushing through review: talk through any wrong answers.
- Forgetting earlier units (feelings): review all five areas.
- Making a party with only sweets: include healthy foods from MyPlate.

### Day 1 (Monday): Review game
*Student:* Play a health Jeopardy game with family.

*How to teach:* Play a health Jeopardy game.

- **Q:** How long do you scrub hands?
  **A:** 20 seconds.
- **Q:** Name the 5 food groups.
  **A:** Fruits, vegetables, grains, protein, dairy.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Health journal
*Student:* Make a 'My Healthy Me' book with one page each on feelings, hygiene, food, activity and safety.

*How to teach:* Make a 'My Healthy Me' book (feelings, hygiene, food, activity, safety).

- **Q:** Feelings page example.
  **A:** When I feel angry I take 3 belly breaths.
- **Q:** Safety page example.
  **A:** I wear a helmet when I ride my bike.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Teach someone
*Student:* Teach a family member a health skill.

*How to teach:* Teach a family member a health skill.

- **Q:** Which skill?
  **A:** Handwashing, belly breathing, 2-V-1 helmet check.
- **Q:** How do you know they learned it?
  **A:** They can do it alone.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Healthy party
*Student:* Plan and prepare a healthy snack party.

*How to teach:* Plan and prepare a healthy snack party.

- **Q:** Plan a healthy menu.
  **A:** Fruit kabobs, veggie sticks with dip, cheese cubes, water.
- **Q:** Which groups are included?
  **A:** Fruit, vegetables, dairy.

### Day 5 (Friday): Celebrate
*Student:* Present your book at the party.

*How to teach:* Present the book at the party.

- **Q:** What is your favorite page?
  **A:** Answers vary.
- **Q:** What will you keep doing this summer?
  **A:** Answers vary.

**Assessment:** Year-end review.

**Answer key:** Year-end review (suggested): 1) Name 4 feelings 2) Calm-down strategy 3) No–Go–Tell 4) Handwashing 20 seconds 5) Brush 2 minutes twice daily 6) 9–12 hours sleep 7) 5 food groups 8) 60 minutes activity 9) 5 senses and organs 10) Look left-right-left 11) Two ways out in a fire 12) Only trusted adults give medicine; Poison Control 1-800-222-1222. Proficient 11–12. Book: 5 accurate pages.
