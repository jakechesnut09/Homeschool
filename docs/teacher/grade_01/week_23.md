# Teacher's Guide — 1st Grade, Week 23

## ELA 1: Planning an opinion piece; commas in a series

**Prepare:** OREO organizer (Opinion, Reason, Example, Opinion restated), two library books (e.g., 'Mercy Watson to the Rescue' by Kate DiCamillo and an 'Elephant and Piggie' book by Mo Willems), and lined paper.

**Watch for:**
- Putting commas between only two items: commas go between items in a list of three or more ('red, blue, and green').
- Forgetting the comma between day and year in dates: 'July 4, 2026.'
- Choosing a favorite book without a reason from the book: point to a part of the book that supports your opinion.

### Day 1 (Monday): Commas in a series
*Student:* Write lists of three things ('I like red, blue, and green.') using commas.

*How to teach:* Model a list sentence: 'I like red, blue, and green.' Your child writes 3 list sentences.

- **Q:** Add commas: 'I ate eggs toast and jam.'
  **A:** I ate eggs, toast, and jam.
- **Q:** How many commas in a list of three things (with 'and')?
  **A:** Two.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Commas in dates
*Student:* Write today's date, your birthday, and a holiday date with commas.

*How to teach:* Write today's date, your child's birthday, and a holiday with a comma between the day and the year.

- **Q:** Write today's date with a comma.
  **A:** November 24, 2026 (or the day you teach it).
- **Q:** Where is the comma in 'January 1 2027'?
  **A:** After 1: January 1, 2027.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Read two books
*Student:* Read two library books (e.g., 'Mercy Watson' and 'Elephant and Piggie'); choose your favorite.

*How to teach:* Read two books. Your child chooses a favorite.

- **Q:** Which book was your favorite and why?
  **A:** Answer with a reason from the book.
- **Q:** Who are the main characters in Elephant and Piggie?
  **A:** Gerald (an elephant) and Piggie (a pig).

### Day 4 (Thursday): Plan
*Student:* Fill in an OREO organizer: your opinion, two reasons, an example, and a closing.

*How to teach:* Fill the OREO organizer: opinion, two reasons, an example, a closing.

- **Q:** What does the O at the end stand for?
  **A:** Opinion restated (closing).
- **Q:** Give a reason for liking Mercy Watson.
  **A:** Example: Mercy is funny because she loves hot buttered toast.

### Day 5 (Friday): Review & check
*Student:* Comma check (5 sentences); read your organizer to a listener.

*How to teach:* Your child adds commas in 5 sentences and reads the organizer to a listener.

- **Q:** Fix: 'We saw cows pigs and goats.'
  **A:** We saw cows, pigs, and goats.
- **Q:** Fix: 'May 5 2026'
  **A:** May 5, 2026.

**Assessment:** Comma practice sentences and completed opinion organizer.

**Answer key:** Comma sentences (suggested): 1) I like red, blue, and green. 2) We saw cows, pigs, and goats. 3) I ate eggs, toast, and jam. 4) My birthday is May 5, 2026. 5) School starts on September 2, 2026. Organizer: proficient = clear opinion, 2 reasons from the book, 1 example, closing that restates the opinion.

## Math 1: Adding tens and adding a one-digit number

**Prepare:** Base-ten blocks (at least 10 rods and 20 units), a hundred or 120 chart, and a quiz of 10 addition problems (see key).

**Watch for:**
- Adding the ones digit of 30 + 20 as '5' or writing 32: 3 tens + 2 tens = 5 tens = 50.
- Adding tens to ones (34 + 20 = 36): line up tens with tens and ones with ones.
- In 38 + 6, writing 314 (3 tens and 14 ones written side by side): trade 10 ones for a ten: 4 tens 4 ones = 44.

### Day 1 (Monday): Add tens
*Student:* Solve 30 + 20, 40 + 50 with ten rods.

*How to teach:* Show 30 + 20 with 3 rods and 2 rods: 5 rods = 50. Say 'three tens plus two tens is five tens.' Practice 40 + 50, 10 + 60.

- **Q:** 40 + 50 = ?
  **A:** 90.
- **Q:** 20 + 70 = ?
  **A:** 90.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Two-digit + tens
*Student:* Solve 34 + 20 with blocks and on the chart.

*How to teach:* Build 34 and add 2 rods: 54. Then show on the chart: start at 34 and move down 2 rows.

- **Q:** 34 + 20 = ?
  **A:** 54.
- **Q:** 61 + 30 = ?
  **A:** 91.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Two-digit + ones
*Student:* Solve 42 + 5 without making a new ten.

*How to teach:* Build 42, add 5 units: 4 tens 7 ones = 47. No new ten needed because 2 + 5 is less than 10.

- **Q:** 42 + 5 = ?
  **A:** 47.
- **Q:** 73 + 6 = ?
  **A:** 79.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Make a new ten
*Student:* Solve 38 + 6 by making 40 + 4.

*How to teach:* Build 38 + 6. 8 ones + 6 ones = 14 ones, so trade 10 ones for a rod. Or think: 38 + 2 = 40, then 40 + 4 = 44.

- **Q:** 38 + 6 = ?
  **A:** 44.
- **Q:** 57 + 5 = ?
  **A:** 62.
- **Q:** 29 + 3 = ?
  **A:** 32.

### Day 5 (Friday): Review & check
*Student:* Mixed addition problems with drawings of tens and ones.

*How to teach:* Your child solves mixed problems by drawing tens as sticks and ones as dots.

- **Q:** 26 + 30 = ?
  **A:** 56.
- **Q:** 45 + 7 = ?
  **A:** 52.

**Assessment:** Addition within 100 quiz.

**Answer key:** Quiz (suggested): 1) 20+30=50 2) 60+30=90 3) 25+40=65 4) 53+20=73 5) 41+7=48 6) 82+5=87 7) 36+8=44 8) 49+3=52 9) 67+6=73 10) 18+50=68. Proficient 9–10 with drawings showing tens and ones.

## Science 1 (NGSS): Animal external parts and their jobs

**Prepare:** Animal photos (Smithsonian Learning Lab or books), tweezers, a clothespin, a spoon, chopsticks, rice, gummy worms, a cup of water, marbles, and paper plates.

**Watch for:**
- Thinking animal parts are just for looks: each external part has a job that helps the animal survive.
- Thinking one beak works best for all food: different shapes suit different foods.
- Thinking a turtle can leave its shell: the shell is part of its body.

### Day 1 (Monday): Parts and jobs
*Student:* Match photos of claws, beaks, shells, fins and ears to the job each does (grab, eat, protect, swim, hear).

*How to teach:* Match photos of claws, beaks, shells, fins, ears to their jobs.

- **Q:** What job do claws do?
  **A:** Grab, dig, or climb.
- **Q:** What job do fins do?
  **A:** Help fish swim and steer.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Bird beak lab
*Student:* Use tweezers, a clothespin, a spoon and chopsticks as 'beaks' to pick up rice, gummy worms and marbles; record which beak works best for each food.

*How to teach:* Use tools as 'beaks' to pick up foods; record which works best.

- **Q:** Which tool worked best for rice?
  **A:** Tweezers (a thin, pointy beak).
- **Q:** Which for water?
  **A:** The spoon — like a pelican's pouch or a scooping bill.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Protection parts
*Student:* Compare photos of a turtle shell, porcupine quills and an armadillo; tell how each part protects the animal.

*How to teach:* Compare turtle shell, porcupine quills, armadillo plates; tell how each protects.

- **Q:** How do porcupine quills protect?
  **A:** They're sharp and stick into predators.
- **Q:** How does an armadillo protect itself?
  **A:** Hard bony plates; some can roll into a ball.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Moving parts
*Student:* Compare fins, wings, flippers and legs; act out how each animal moves and say what part helps.

*How to teach:* Compare fins, wings, flippers, legs; act out movement.

- **Q:** What helps a bird fly?
  **A:** Wings.
- **Q:** What helps a sea turtle swim?
  **A:** Flippers.

### Day 5 (Friday): Review & check
*Student:* Draw an animal, label 3 external parts and write what each does.

*How to teach:* Your child draws an animal with 3 labeled parts and jobs.

- **Q:** Label a duck.
  **A:** Webbed feet — swim; bill — eat; feathers/wings — fly and stay warm/dry.
- **Q:** Label a cat.
  **A:** Claws — climb/catch; ears — hear; whiskers — feel.

**Assessment:** Labeled animal drawing with functions.

**Answer key:** Rubric: 4 = animal drawn with 3 accurately labeled external parts and a correct job for each (e.g., eagle: talons grab prey, beak tears food, wings fly); 3 = 2 correct; 2 = labels without jobs; 1 = incomplete.

## A Child's Place in Time and Space: Oral history interview

**Prepare:** An interview question sheet (6 questions), a phone/recording device, a grandparent or older neighbor (in person or video), Venn diagram paper, stapled booklet, and then/now picture cards.

**Watch for:**
- Asking only yes/no questions: ask 'What... How... Tell me about...'
- Forgetting to listen: let the person finish before asking the next question.
- Thinking 'then' means 1,000 years ago: a grandparent's childhood counts as 'then' too.

### Day 1 (Monday): Prepare questions
*Student:* Write 6 questions about school, games, jobs and transportation when the interviewee was young.

*How to teach:* Write 6 questions about school, games, jobs, transportation.

- **Q:** Write a good interview question.
  **A:** Example: What games did you play when you were my age?
- **Q:** Why not yes/no questions?
  **A:** They give short answers; open questions give stories.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Interview
*Student:* Interview a grandparent or older neighbor (in person or by video call) and record answers.

*How to teach:* Interview a grandparent or older neighbor; record answers.

- **Q:** How do you show good manners in an interview?
  **A:** Say thank you, listen, don't interrupt.
- **Q:** What should you do with answers?
  **A:** Record or write them down.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Compare
*Student:* Compare their answers with your life today in a Venn diagram.

*How to teach:* Compare their answers with your life in a Venn diagram.

- **Q:** What's the same?
  **A:** Answers vary — e.g., both liked playing outside.
- **Q:** What's different?
  **A:** Answers vary — e.g., they had no internet.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Share
*Student:* Make a 'Then and Now' booklet with one fact per page.

*How to teach:* Make a 'Then and Now' booklet with one fact per page.

- **Q:** What's on a page?
  **A:** One fact from the interview with a then/now drawing.
- **Q:** Who is the source?
  **A:** The person interviewed.

### Day 5 (Friday): Unit 4 check
*Student:* Assessment: sort pictures of then and now and explain changes in schools, transportation and family life.

*How to teach:* Give the Unit 4 assessment.

- **Q:** Slate or tablet: then or now?
  **A:** Slate = then; tablet = now.
- **Q:** Stagecoach or car?
  **A:** Stagecoach = then; car = now.

**Assessment:** Unit 4 assessment and oral-history booklet.

**Answer key:** Unit 4 test (suggested): sort pictures — then: slate, one-room school, stagecoach, washboard, butter churn, long dress; now: tablet, school bus, car, washing machine, refrigerator, jeans. Explain one change in school, transportation, and family life each. Proficient = 11–12 sorted + 3 explanations. Booklet: 4+ pages with accurate facts.

## Art & Music 1: Art of Mexico; music of Mexico

**Prepare:** Tissue paper, scissors; photos of papel picado; mariachi recording; lyrics to 'De Colores' or 'La Cucaracha.'

**Watch for:**
- Cutting along the open edge: cut shapes on folded edges to make symmetric holes.
- Thinking mariachi is only trumpets: mariachi uses violins, trumpets, guitars, vihuela and guitarrón.

### Day 1 (Monday): Papel picado
*Student:* Look at photos of papel picado and learn when it is used.

*How to teach:* Look at papel picado; explain it is a Mexican folk art used for celebrations.

- **Q:** When do people hang papel picado?
  **A:** At celebrations: Día de los Muertos, holidays, weddings, birthdays, fiestas.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Make it
*Student:* Fold and cut tissue paper banners.

*How to teach:* Fold tissue paper several times; cut small shapes on the folds; unfold.

- **Q:** What does 'papel picado' mean?
  **A:** Perforated (cut) paper.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Mariachi
*Student:* Listen to mariachi music; name the instruments (violin, trumpet, guitarrón).

*How to teach:* Listen to mariachi music; name the instruments.

- **Q:** Name two mariachi instruments.
  **A:** Violin, trumpet, guitar, vihuela, guitarrón.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Sing
*Student:* Learn 'De Colores' or 'La Cucaracha'.

*How to teach:* Learn 'De Colores' or 'La Cucaracha.'

- **Q:** What language is the song in?
  **A:** Spanish.

### Day 5 (Friday): Share
*Student:* Explain why people make papel picado.

*How to teach:* Explain why people make papel picado.

- **Q:** Why make papel picado?
  **A:** To decorate and celebrate special days in Mexican culture.

**Assessment:** Papel picado and song.

**Answer key:** Papel picado: folded tissue banner with cut shapes; child explains use (celebrations such as Día de los Muertos, holidays, weddings). Song: sings 'De Colores' or 'La Cucaracha' chorus. Proficient = banner + explanation + song.

## PE 1: Striking with hands and paddles

**Prepare:** Balloons, paper plates, paint stirrers or rulers, tape, markers. Supervise balloons (choking hazard if popped).

**Watch for:**
- Swinging wildly: use a flat, firm surface and gentle upward taps.
- Watching the paddle instead of the balloon: eyes on the balloon.

### Day 1 (Monday): Balloon tap
*Student:* Keep a balloon in the air using open hands, then only one hand, then elbows; count taps.

*How to teach:* Keep a balloon in the air with open hands, then one hand, then elbows; count taps.

- **Q:** What is striking?
  **A:** Hitting an object with a body part or tool.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Make a paddle
*Student:* Tape a paper plate to a paint stirrer or ruler to make a paddle and decorate it.

*How to teach:* Tape a paper plate to a paint stirrer or ruler to make a paddle; decorate it.

- **Q:** Why should the paddle face be flat?
  **A:** A flat surface sends the balloon straight.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Paddle tap
*Student:* Tap the balloon up with the paddle; count taps.

*How to teach:* Tap the balloon up with the paddle; count taps.

- **Q:** How hard should you tap?
  **A:** Gently, just enough to send it up.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Partner volley
*Student:* Hit the balloon back and forth.

*How to teach:* Hit the balloon back and forth with a partner.

- **Q:** Where do your eyes go?
  **A:** On the balloon.

### Day 5 (Friday): Review & check
*Student:* Count consecutive balloon taps with the paddle and record your best of 3 tries.

*How to teach:* Count consecutive paddle taps; record best of 3 tries.

- **Q:** What was your best number of taps?
  **A:** Record child's count.

**Assessment:** Consecutive taps count.

**Answer key:** Record the best of 3 tries of consecutive paddle taps. 10+ = proficient; 5-9 = developing; under 5 = beginning. Form: flat paddle, gentle upward taps, eyes on balloon.

## Health 1: Taking care of my body

**Prepare:** The body outline from week 21, sunglasses, a list of healthy habits, and a Unit 5 assessment sheet.

**Watch for:**
- Thinking loud music can't hurt ears: very loud sounds can damage hearing.
- Looking at the sun with sunglasses on: never look directly at the sun.
- Thinking growth foods are only meat: protein and dairy come from many foods (beans, eggs, milk, yogurt).

### Day 1 (Monday): Body care
*Student:* Add healthy habits around your body outline.

*How to teach:* Add healthy habits around the body outline.

- **Q:** Habit for teeth?
  **A:** Brush twice a day.
- **Q:** Habit for heart?
  **A:** Move 60 minutes a day.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Protecting senses
*Student:* Discuss protecting eyes (sunglasses) and ears (not too loud).

*How to teach:* Discuss protecting eyes and ears.

- **Q:** How do you protect your eyes?
  **A:** Sunglasses, don't look at the sun, safety goggles.
- **Q:** How do you protect your ears?
  **A:** Keep volume low; cover ears near very loud noises.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Growth foods
*Student:* Connect protein and dairy to growing.

*How to teach:* Connect protein and dairy to growing.

- **Q:** What does dairy help grow?
  **A:** Strong bones and teeth (calcium).
- **Q:** What does protein help?
  **A:** Build muscles.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Share
*Student:* Present your body outline to family, naming senses, organs and the habits that keep them healthy.

*How to teach:* Present the body outline to family.

- **Q:** Name a sense and a habit.
  **A:** Eyes — sunglasses.
- **Q:** Name an organ and a habit.
  **A:** Heart — exercise.

### Day 5 (Friday): Unit 5 check
*Student:* Assessment: label 5 sense organs and 4 inner organs and describe one way bodies grow.

*How to teach:* Give the Unit 5 assessment.

- **Q:** Label the 5 sense organs.
  **A:** Eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin.
- **Q:** Name one way bodies grow.
  **A:** Get taller.

**Assessment:** Unit 5 assessment.

**Answer key:** Unit 5 test: label 5 sense organs (eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin) and 4 inner organs (brain, heart, lungs, stomach) and describe one way bodies grow (taller, bigger hands/feet, lose baby teeth). Proficient 9–10 of 10.
