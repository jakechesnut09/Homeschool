# Teacher's Guide — 7th Grade, Week 36

## English Language Arts 7: Portfolio and reflection

**Prepare:** Writing folder; reading log.

**Watch for:**
- Choosing pieces only by grade instead of growth.
- Reflection letters without specifics.

### Day 1 (Monday): Select work
*Student:* Choose the best narrative, analysis, argument, research and poem pieces for the portfolio.

*How to teach:* Your child chooses the best narrative, analysis, argument, research and poem pieces for the portfolio. Check before moving on: ask "Why include a piece that shows growth?" Look for: Shows learning over time.

- **Q:** Why include a piece that shows growth?
  **A:** Shows learning over time.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Revise one piece
*Student:* Revise one earlier piece using everything learned this year.

*How to teach:* Your child revises one earlier piece using everything learned this year. Check before moving on: ask "What to revise?" Look for: Apply a skill learned later, e.g., embedding quotes.

- **Q:** What to revise?
  **A:** Apply a skill learned later, e.g., embedding quotes.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Reflection letter
*Student:* Write a reflection letter describing growth, challenges and goals for grade 8.

*How to teach:* Your child writes a reflection letter describing growth, challenges and goals for grade 8. Check before moving on: ask "What makes it strong?" Look for: Specific examples of growth and goals.

- **Q:** What makes it strong?
  **A:** Specific examples of growth and goals.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Reading celebration
*Student:* Total the year's reading log and give a 3-minute book talk on the favorite book of the year.

*How to teach:* Total the reading log. Your child gives a 3-minute book talk on the favorite book of the year. Check before moving on: ask "Book talk elements?" Look for: Title, author, hook, no spoilers, recommendation.

- **Q:** Book talk elements?
  **A:** Title, author, hook, no spoilers, recommendation.

### Day 5 (Friday): Portfolio presentation
*Student:* Present the portfolio to family, explaining one piece in depth, and create a summer reading list.

*How to teach:* Your child presents the portfolio to family, explaining one piece in depth, and creates a summer reading list. Check before moving on: ask "Summer reading list should include?" Look for: Variety of genres, some challenge.

- **Q:** Summer reading list should include?
  **A:** Variety of genres, some challenge.

**Assessment:** Portfolio and presentation.

**Answer key:** Portfolio rubric: five pieces present, one revised with visible changes, reflection letter with specific examples and goals. Presentation: explains one piece in depth.

## Mathematics 7: Statistics project & final exam

**Prepare:** Survey form, ruler (for drop test), graph paper, calculator; cumulative review set; final exam (IM end-of-year or Khan course challenge).

**Watch for:**
- Comparing groups by single values instead of measures of center and spread.
- Using a sample of convenience for 'random'.

### Day 1 (Monday): Plan an investigation
*Student:* Write a statistical question comparing two groups (e.g., reaction times of kids vs adults) and plan a random sample.

*How to teach:* Your child writes a statistical question and sampling plan. Check before moving on: ask "Is 'How fast is my reaction?' statistical?" Look for: No — it has one answer; compare groups for variability.

- **Q:** Is 'How fast is my reaction?' statistical?
  **A:** No — it has one answer; compare groups for variability.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Collect and display
*Student:* Collect data from at least 10 people per group with a ruler-drop test and create dot plots or box plots.

*How to teach:* Your child collects 10+ per group with the ruler-drop test and creates dot plots. Check before moving on: ask "Why the same procedure for everyone?" Look for: Fair comparison.

- **Q:** Why the same procedure for everyone?
  **A:** Fair comparison.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Analyze and report
*Student:* Compute means and MADs, write conclusions, and present findings to the family.

*How to teach:* Your child computes mean and MAD, writes conclusions, presents. Check before moving on: ask "Mean 18 cm vs 14 cm, MAD 3. Meaningful?" Look for: Difference 4 is about 1.3 MADs — not strongly meaningful.

- **Q:** Mean 18 cm vs 14 cm, MAD 3. Meaningful?
  **A:** Difference 4 is about 1.3 MADs — not strongly meaningful.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Final review
*Student:* Complete a cumulative review covering all 8 units.

*How to teach:* Cumulative review; target weak units from earlier checks. Check before moving on: ask "Area of circle with r = 3?" Look for: 9π ≈ 28.26.

- **Q:** Area of circle with r = 3?
  **A:** 9π ≈ 28.26.

### Day 5 (Friday): Final exam
*Student:* Take the year-end grade 7 math exam and set goals for Math 8.

*How to teach:* Give the final exam; set goals for Math 8. Check before moving on: ask "Solve −2x + 7 = 15." Look for: x = −4.

- **Q:** Solve −2x + 7 = 15.
  **A:** x = −4.

**Assessment:** Final exam and statistics investigation report.

**Answer key:** Final exam sample answers: 1) Scale factor 6→15: 2.5. 2) y = 4x through (3,12). 3) C for d = 10: 31.4. 4) $80 with 15% off: $68. 5) −7 − (−3) = −4. 6) 3x − 5 = 16 → 7. 7) Complement of 38: 52. 8) Prism 3 × 4 × 5: 60. 9) P(sum 7) = 1/6. Investigation rubric: 4 = statistical question, random sample, correct mean/MAD, valid conclusion; 3 = minor errors; 2 = missing parts; 1 = incomplete.

## Spanish 1A: Final integrated performance assessment

**Prepare:** Final exam packet: an authentic short text (pen-pal profile or town website page) and a short audio (e.g., a Spanish Proficiency Exercises clip) with comprehension questions you write in advance. Can-do checklist.

**Watch for:**
- Translating every word in a reading. Use cognates, context and key words for the gist.
- Memorizing a speech for the interpersonal part. Listen and respond to the actual questions.

### Day 1 (Monday): Interpretive
*Student:* Read an authentic text (a pen-pal profile or town website) and listen to a short audio, answering comprehension questions.

*How to teach:* Read the authentic text and listen to the audio; answer questions.

- **Q:** What strategy helps with unknown words?
  **A:** Look for cognates (e.g., 'familia,' 'música'), use context and pictures, and focus on main ideas.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Interpersonal
*Student:* Hold a 4-minute conversation with a parent about yourself, family, school, food, and plans.

*How to teach:* Hold a 4-minute conversation on all topics.

- **Q:** Answer: ¿Qué te gusta comer y qué no te gusta?
  **A:** Me gusta(n) ___ pero no me gusta(n) ___ (with correct gusta/gustan).

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Presentational
*Student:* Write and present 'Mi vida' (12–15 sentences) covering all 1A topics.

*How to teach:* Write and present 'Mi vida' (12–15 sentences).

- **Q:** Write an opening sentence.
  **A:** Example: Hola, me llamo ___, tengo ___ años y soy de ___.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Cultures
*Student:* Present one cultural comparison from the year (food, greetings, plazas, or celebrations).

*How to teach:* Present one cultural comparison from the year.

- **Q:** Give an example comparison sentence.
  **A:** Example: En España la gente se saluda con dos besos, pero en mi familia damos abrazos.

### Day 5 (Friday): Reflection
*Student:* Complete a can-do self-assessment and set goals for Spanish 1B.

*How to teach:* Complete the can-do self-assessment; set goals for Spanish 1B.

- **Q:** Write one can-do statement you've mastered.
  **A:** Example: 'I can introduce myself and describe my family.'

**Assessment:** Integrated performance assessment rubric (interpretive, interpersonal, presentational).

**Answer key:** Integrated performance assessment rubric (1–4 each mode): Interpretive — identifies main idea and 4+ key details from text and audio; Interpersonal — 4-minute conversation, answers and asks questions on self, family, school, food, plans, uses sentences (Novice Mid), comprehensible; Presentational — 'Mi vida' 12–15 sentences covering greetings/identity, description (ser), likes (gustar), school, food, family (tener), town (estar/ir) and plans (ir a), with accurate agreement and verb forms; Cultures — accurate comparison with specific details. Proficient = 3s across.

## Visual & Performing Arts 7: Year reflection

**Prepare:** Sketchbook, portfolio, camera/phone, slideshow app. Ideas for a summer sketchbook challenge (e.g., one drawing a day, 30-day prompts).

**Watch for:**
- Measuring growth only by realism. Growth includes ideas, risk-taking, process and confidence.
- Goals that are too vague. Make them specific and measurable.

### Day 1 (Monday): Before & after
*Student:* Compare first and last drawings of the year and list three skills that improved.

*How to teach:* Compare first and last drawings; list three improved skills.

- **Q:** Which skill improved most? Show evidence.
  **A:** Open answer — e.g., value range from 3 to 9 tones.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Digital portfolio
*Student:* Photograph all work and create a digital portfolio slideshow.

*How to teach:* Photograph all work; build a digital portfolio slideshow.

- **Q:** Why photograph art in even, indirect light?
  **A:** To avoid glare and shadows and show true colors.

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Free choice
*Student:* Create one free-choice artwork in your favorite medium.

*How to teach:* Create one free-choice artwork.

- **Q:** Which elements and principles did you use?
  **A:** Open answer — e.g., complementary colors and emphasis.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Reflection letter
*Student:* Write a letter to your future self about your artistic identity.

*How to teach:* Write a letter to your future self about your artistic identity.

- **Q:** What is artistic identity?
  **A:** The interests, styles, subjects and values that make your art your own.

### Day 5 (Friday): Goals
*Student:* Set goals for VAPA 8 and choose a summer sketchbook challenge.

*How to teach:* Set goals for VAPA 8 and choose a summer challenge.

- **Q:** Write a SMART art goal.
  **A:** Example: 'Fill 30 sketchbook pages with observational drawings by August 31.'

**Assessment:** Digital portfolio and reflection letter.

**Answer key:** Digital portfolio rubric: photos of all major works, organized by unit, with titles and short reflections. Reflection letter proficient: names favorite medium and why, describes 3 skills that improved with evidence, describes artistic identity (style, interests) and goals for next year.

## Health Education 7: Year review and wellness plan

**Prepare:** Pull out the week 1 wellness wheel and the journal. Prepare a review game (Jeopardy categories: Nutrition, Mental Health, Safety, Drugs, Growth, Community Health) and a year-end exam.

**Watch for:**
- Treating the wellness plan as a one-time document. Schedule check-ins to adjust.
- Writing a plan without measurable goals. Each section needs a SMART goal.

### Day 1 (Monday): Wellness wheel
*Student:* Redo the wellness wheel and compare with week 1.

*How to teach:* Your child redoes the wellness wheel and compares with week 1.

- **Q:** Which area improved most and why?
  **A:** Open answer — look for a specific behavior change linked to a course skill.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Review
*Student:* Review all six units with a game.

*How to teach:* Play the review game across all six units.

- **Q:** Name one skill from each of two units you'll keep using.
  **A:** Examples: reading labels (nutrition), box breathing (stress), refusal steps (drugs).

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Final exam
*Student:* Take the year-end health exam.

*How to teach:* Give the year-end exam (see key).

- **Q:** What do clean, separate, cook and chill prevent?
  **A:** Foodborne illness.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Wellness plan
*Student:* Write a summer wellness plan covering nutrition, activity, sleep and stress.

*How to teach:* Your child writes a summer wellness plan with a SMART goal for each area.

- **Q:** Write a SMART activity goal.
  **A:** Example: 'I will bike or swim 45 minutes at least 5 days a week through August.'

### Day 5 (Friday): Reflect
*Student:* Share the plan with family and set check-in dates.

*How to teach:* Share the plan with family and put check-in dates on the calendar.

- **Q:** Why set check-in dates?
  **A:** They help you track progress, stay motivated and adjust the plan.

**Assessment:** Final exam and wellness plan.

**Answer key:** Final exam (suggested 20 items, 2 from each area): six nutrients; 4 g sugar = 1 tsp; 60 min activity; clean-separate-cook-chill; stress signs and two coping strategies; 988; I-message formula; four bullying types; Drop-Cover-Hold On; 911 info; choking sign/abdominal thrusts; dopamine/prefrontal cortex; nicotine addiction; fentanyl and naloxone; refusal steps; puberty hormones; 8–10 hours sleep; consent; bacteria vs virus; vaccines/herd immunity; AQI. 18–20 advanced, 14–17 proficient. Wellness plan: SMART goals for nutrition, activity, sleep and stress, plus check-in dates.
