## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Biography basics* (90 min): Compare biography and autobiography; start a timeline of Helen Keller's life.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Building fractions* (60 min): Build 3/4, 5/6 and 2/3 by joining unit fraction strips.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Backyard habitat* (40 min): Survey a backyard or park and list organisms and their needs.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Rules at home* (30 min): List family rules and explain the reason behind each one.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Families* (30 min): Learn the 4 orchestra families and how each makes sound.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Forehand cues* (30 min): Learn the forehand cues (side to target, swing low to high, follow through) and shadow 20 swings.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Emergency or not* (20 min): Sort 12 situations into emergencies (call 911) and non-emergencies (tell an adult).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Primary account* (90 min): Read the water-pump excerpt from 'The Story of My Life' and discuss how she felt.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Pattern blocks* (60 min): Use pattern blocks with the hexagon as one whole to show halves, thirds and sixths.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Habitat cards* (40 min): Match organisms to habitats (desert, ocean, forest, wetland).
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Rules vs. laws* (30 min): Compare rules and laws and sort examples (bedtime, speed limits, library fines).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Britten* (30 min): Listen to Britten's 'The Young Person's Guide to the Orchestra' and raise a card for each family.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Drop hit* (30 min): Drop a ball, let it bounce and hit a forehand to a wall 20 times.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Calling 911* (20 min): Practice a pretend 911 call stating your name, full address and what happened.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Conjunctions* (90 min): Combine sentences about Helen Keller using and, but, or, so; spell over-, under-, sub- words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fractions of a set* (60 min): Explore fractions of a group of 8 counters, e.g., 3/8 are red.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Survive well, less well* (40 min): Sort organisms by how well they would survive in a desert.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Consequences* (30 min): Discuss what consequences for breaking laws are meant to do (protect, teach, repair).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Timbre game* (30 min): Identify 10 instruments by sound alone in a listening game.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Partner rally* (30 min): Rally with a partner over a rope or line, counting hits.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *First aid* (20 min): Learn basic first aid for small cuts, scrapes and nosebleeds and tour the home first aid kit.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 104, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Main idea* (90 min): Identify the main idea of a biography chapter and its key details.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Pizza problems* (60 min): Solve fraction stories like 'Ana ate 3 of 8 equal slices; what fraction did she eat?'
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Pill bug choice* (40 min): Test whether pill bugs prefer damp or dry soil in a shoebox choice chamber.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *No-rules game* (30 min): Play a ball game with no rules, then add rules and discuss how the game changed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Make an instrument* (30 min): Build a shoebox rubber-band guitar and explore how band thickness changes pitch.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Target* (30 min): Hit forehands to hula hoop targets near and far, adjusting force.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Earthquake* (20 min): Practice drop, cover and hold on in 3 rooms and identify safe spots.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 105, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Complete the timeline and write a summary of Helen Keller's achievements.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Write fractions for 12 shaded models and draw models for 4 fractions.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Write an argument for which habitat suits a cactus best.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write a paragraph about why communities need rules and laws.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete an instrument family quiz.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a forehand check scored on the 3 cues.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make an emergency contact card with address, phone numbers and a trusted neighbor.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Biography: Helen Keller
- Objective: Describe a sequence of events in a person's life
- Objective: Use coordinating conjunctions
- Objective: Identify main idea
- Vocabulary: biography, autobiography, sequence, conjunction
- Materials: The Story of My Life by Helen Keller (Project Gutenberg, chapter 4 excerpt), Helen Keller biography (library)
- Assessment: Timeline and summary

### Math 3: Non-unit fractions
- Objective: Understand a/b as a parts of size 1/b
- Objective: Represent fractions with models
- Vocabulary: non-unit fraction, whole, part
- Materials: fraction strips, pattern blocks
- Assessment: Fraction models check

### Science 3: Habitats meet needs
- Objective: Describe how habitats meet organisms' needs
- Objective: Compare survival in different habitats
- Vocabulary: habitat, needs, survive, ecosystem
- Materials: habitat cards, outdoor space
- Assessment: Habitat argument; pill bug data

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Why we have rules and laws
- Objective: Determine reasons for rules and laws
- Objective: Explain consequences
- Vocabulary: rule, law, consequence, fair, safety
- Materials: list of family rules, town ordinance examples
- Assessment: Rules and laws paragraph

### Art & Music 3: Instrument families and timbre
- Objective: Identify instrument families by timbre
- Vocabulary: timbre, strings, woodwinds, brass, percussion
- Materials: Classics for Kids, recording of Britten's 'Young Person's Guide to the Orchestra', shoebox, rubber bands
- Assessment: Instrument family quiz

### PE 3: Forehand striking
- Objective: Use the forehand stroke
- Vocabulary: forehand, follow through, side to target
- Materials: paddle, ball
- Assessment: Forehand check

### Health 3: Emergencies and 911
- Objective: Describe emergencies and practice getting help
- Vocabulary: emergency, 911, first aid, address
- Materials: toy phone, first aid kit
- Assessment: Emergency contact card
