## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Reading launch* (90 min): Choose a library book for a 20-minute independent reading routine and start a reading log; read 'The Lion and the Mouse' and discuss its moral.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Build numbers* (60 min): Build 3-digit numbers rolled on dice with base-ten blocks and record each in standard, expanded and word form.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Forces around us* (40 min): List 20 pushes and pulls seen at home and sort them in a science notebook T-chart.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Map parts* (30 min): Identify title, compass rose, map key and scale on a printed local map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Line hunt* (30 min): Find 10 kinds of lines in your home and draw each in a sketchbook line chart.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Safety rules* (30 min): Learn PE safety rules and personal space with a 5-minute bubble-space tag game.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Five food groups* (20 min): Explore the MyPlate Kids page and name the 5 food groups with 3 examples each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Recount a fable* (90 min): Read 'The Tortoise and the Hare,' retell it in 5 sentences using first/next/then/finally, and spell closed vs. open syllable words (rab-bit, ti-ger).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Number line placement* (60 min): Place 10 three-digit numbers on an open number line from 0 to 1,000 and explain which benchmarks helped.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Tug of war* (40 min): Play a gentle towel tug-of-war with a family member and draw force arrows for balanced and unbalanced pulls.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Where in the world* (30 min): Locate your continent, country, state and city on a globe and make a 'nested circles' address.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Line in art* (30 min): Look at a Van Gogh painting ('The Starry Night') and describe how the lines show movement.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Dynamic warm-up* (30 min): Learn a 6-move dynamic warm-up (jog, high knees, arm circles, skips, lunges, butt kicks).
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Food group sort* (20 min): Cut pictures from grocery flyers and sort them onto a paper-plate MyPlate.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Sentence types* (90 min): Learn the four sentence types and end punctuation; find each type in 'The Fox and the Crow' and write one of each about the fable.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Compare and order* (60 min): Compare pairs of 3-digit numbers with <, >, = and order a set of 6 numbers from least to greatest.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Book stack* (40 min): Push a book with equal force from both sides, then unequal force, and record what happens to its motion.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Grid maps* (30 min): Use a grid to locate places on a town map and give directions using cardinal directions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Feelings in lines* (30 min): Draw lines to show calm, angry, excited and sleepy to music.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Cool-down* (30 min): Practice a static stretch cool-down and explain why muscles need it.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Nutrients* (20 min): Learn one job of each nutrient (protein builds muscles, calcium builds bones) and match foods to jobs.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Writing a memory* (90 min): Brainstorm small-moment memories and draft a half-page narrative about a time you learned a lesson.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Place value game* (60 min): Play 'Greatest Number' with digit cards, then write 5 numbers that are 10 more, 10 less, 100 more and 100 less.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Video and model* (40 min): Watch a Mystery Science forces lesson and draw arrow diagrams for a stationary and a moving object.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Draw a map* (30 min): Draw a map of your neighborhood with a key and compass rose.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Line design* (30 min): Create a full-page line design using at least 8 line types.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Active game* (30 min): Play 15 minutes of freeze tag using the warm-up and cool-down routine.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Food log* (20 min): Record everything you eat for one day and tally servings from each group.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a spelling check on syllable types, answer 4 text-based questions on a new fable, and discuss its lesson aloud using complete sentences.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a 12-item place value quiz (forms, comparing, 10/100 more or less) and correct errors with blocks.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Explain in writing with diagrams why an object starts moving only when forces are unbalanced.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Complete a map skills quiz and explain your neighborhood map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review & sing* (30 min): Share the line design and sing a warm-up echo song keeping a steady beat.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Lead the family through the warm-up and name 3 safety rules.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Identify which food group you ate least yesterday and name 2 foods to add.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Reading routines and Aesop's fables
- Objective: Establish a daily independent reading routine
- Objective: Recount fables and state the moral
- Objective: Write a reading-life narrative
- Vocabulary: fable, moral, character, recount
- Materials: Aesop's Fables (Project Gutenberg), reading log, spelling notebook
- Assessment: Fable comprehension questions, spelling check, narrative draft

### Math 3: Place value to 1,000
- Objective: Read and write numbers to 1,000 in standard, word and expanded form
- Objective: Compare 3-digit numbers
- Vocabulary: digit, place value, expanded form, hundreds
- Materials: base-ten blocks, place value chart, dice
- Assessment: 12-item place value quiz; observation of block models

### Science 3: Pushes, pulls and balanced forces
- Objective: Identify forces as pushes and pulls
- Objective: Compare balanced and unbalanced forces
- Vocabulary: force, push, pull, balanced, unbalanced
- Materials: rope or towel, toy car, books, science notebook
- Assessment: Force diagram explanation

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Maps and globes
- Objective: Use map elements (title, compass rose, key, scale)
- Objective: Locate our community on maps
- Vocabulary: map, globe, compass rose, map key, scale
- Materials: world map, California map, local street map
- Assessment: Map skills quiz; neighborhood map

### Art & Music 3: Kinds of line
- Objective: Use a variety of lines expressively
- Objective: Describe lines in artworks
- Vocabulary: line, contour, zigzag, expressive
- Materials: sketchbook, pencils, markers
- Assessment: Line design and description

### PE 3: Warm-up, cool-down and safety
- Objective: Perform a safe warm-up and cool-down
- Objective: Follow safety rules
- Vocabulary: warm-up, cool-down, personal space
- Materials: open space, cones
- Assessment: Leads warm-up correctly

### Health 3: MyPlate food groups
- Objective: Identify the five food groups
- Objective: Explain why variety matters
- Vocabulary: food group, MyPlate, variety, nutrient
- Materials: MyPlate poster, grocery flyers, paper plate
- Assessment: Food log analysis
