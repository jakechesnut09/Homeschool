## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *What is a myth?* (90 min): Learn about Ancient Greece and the Olympian gods, then make a gods chart with symbols.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Tens rods* (60 min): Model 4 × 30 with tens rods and record as 4 × 3 tens = 12 tens = 120.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Trait sort* (40 min): Sort animal parent and offspring pictures and identify inherited traits.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Ranchos* (30 min): Read about Mexican land grants, ranchos and the work of vaqueros.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Sol-mi* (30 min): Learn sol and mi hand signs and sing 'Rain, Rain, Go Away' with them.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Target game* (30 min): Set up 10 plastic bottles and knock them down with overhand throws from a set line.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Friend qualities* (20 min): List 8 qualities of a good friend and rank your top 3.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 67, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Pandora* (90 min): Read the Pandora myth ('The Paradise of Children') and recount it in sequence.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Pattern practice* (60 min): Solve sets like 6 × 7, 6 × 70 and explain the place-value pattern.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Family traits* (40 min): Survey family members for traits (earlobes, tongue rolling, hair color) and record data.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Diseños* (30 min): Examine a hand-drawn rancho map (diseño) on Calisphere and identify landmarks used as boundaries.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Adding la* (30 min): Add la and sing 'Ring Around the Rosie' with hand signs.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Keep-away* (30 min): Play monkey in the middle, using fakes and quick passes.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Empathy* (20 min): Read 'The Invisible Boy' (or similar) and discuss how Brian felt and what changed.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 68, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Agreement* (90 min): Practice subject-verb agreement with sentences about the gods; study Greek roots (bio, graph, photo).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Word problems* (60 min): Solve 6 problems such as '8 boxes of 40 crayons' and check with skip counting by tens.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Plants inherit* (40 min): Compare seeds from one plant to offspring plants.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Hide and tallow* (30 min): Learn how cattle hides and tallow were traded with ships and what was bought in return.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Do-re-mi* (30 min): Add do, re and mi and sing the pentatonic scale up and down.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Practice log* (30 min): Re-test the wall target and graph your accuracy improvement since week 11.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Differences* (20 min): Interview a friend or relative about something different about them and something you share.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 69, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Lesson* (90 min): Write what lesson the Pandora myth teaches about curiosity.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Division fluency* (60 min): Practice division facts with fact family cards and a Math Playground fact game.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Graph* (40 min): Graph survey data on traits.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Place names* (30 min): Find rancho or Spanish place names on a local map and research one name's origin.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Folk song* (30 min): Sing a pentatonic folk song ('Bow Wow Wow' or 'Hot Cross Buns') in solfege.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Hit the pin* (30 min): Play a family game of hit-the-pin with turn-taking and fair-play rules.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Inclusion* (20 min): Plan 3 ways to include someone left out at a park, co-op or team practice.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 70, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a subject-verb agreement quiz and answer questions on the Pandora myth.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit 3 assessment* (60 min): Complete the Unit 3 test on strategies, properties and multiples of 10, plus a fluency check.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Explain what inherited traits are using examples.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain in writing how the rancho economy worked and what names or traditions remain.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Echo-sing 8 solfege patterns with correct hand signs.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a throw and catch skill check scored on the cue checklist.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a 'recipe for friendship' with ingredients and steps.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Introduction to Greek myths
- Objective: Recount a myth and determine its lesson
- Objective: Use subject-verb agreement
- Objective: Learn Greek roots
- Vocabulary: myth, god, goddess, Mount Olympus
- Materials: A Wonder-Book for Girls and Boys by Nathaniel Hawthorne (Project Gutenberg), Greek myth retelling collection (library)
- Assessment: Agreement quiz; myth recount

### Math 3: Multiplying by multiples of 10 and fluency
- Objective: Multiply one-digit numbers by multiples of 10
- Objective: Fluently multiply and divide within 100
- Vocabulary: multiple of 10, place value, fluency
- Materials: base-ten rods, fact cards
- Assessment: Unit 3 test and fluency check

### Science 3: Inherited traits
- Objective: Identify traits inherited from parents
- Objective: Analyze data on traits
- Vocabulary: trait, inherit, offspring, parent
- Materials: family photos, pet or animal pictures
- Assessment: Trait data graph and explanation

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Ranchos and the Mexican era
- Objective: Describe the rancho economy and its legacy
- Vocabulary: rancho, vaquero, diseño, hide and tallow trade
- Materials: Calisphere diseño images, local street map
- Assessment: Rancho explanation

### Art & Music 3: Solfege and hand signs
- Objective: Sing with solfege syllables and Curwen hand signs
- Vocabulary: solfege, do, re, mi, sol, la
- Materials: solfege hand-sign chart
- Assessment: Solfege echo check

### PE 3: Throwing games
- Objective: Apply throwing and catching in games
- Objective: Show fair play
- Vocabulary: accuracy, fair play
- Materials: balls, plastic bottles
- Assessment: Throw/catch skill check

### Health 3: Healthy friendships
- Objective: Describe qualities of good friends
- Objective: Respect differences and show empathy
- Vocabulary: friendship, respect, empathy, inclusion
- Materials: friendship picture book (e.g., 'The Invisible Boy' by Trudy Ludwig, library)
- Assessment: Friendship recipe
