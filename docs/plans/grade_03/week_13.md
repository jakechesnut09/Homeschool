## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise the report for definitions of key words and a strong conclusion; spell -ment and -er/-or words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Nines patterns* (60 min): Find the tens-ones pattern in 9s and practice with the finger trick and 10s minus a group.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Claim* (40 min): Write a claim: living in groups helps [animal] survive.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Three institutions* (30 min): Learn the purposes of missions, presidios and pueblos and sort facts about each.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Pitch sort* (30 min): Listen to 10 sounds and sort them as high or low.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Lead pass* (30 min): Throw ahead of a partner running a straight cone path so they catch without stopping.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *What is stress* (20 min): Discuss what stress feels like and list 5 things that cause you stress.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Add text features* (90 min): Add a heading, labeled diagram, caption and glossary to the published report.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Eights* (60 min): Double 4s facts to find 8s; solve 2 × 4 × 3 two ways to show the associative property.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Evidence* (40 min): Choose 3 pieces of evidence from the evidence chart.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Nearest mission* (30 min): Locate the mission nearest your home on a map and research when and why it was founded.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Direction* (30 min): Draw the up-down contour of 'Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star' as a line.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Flying disc* (30 min): Learn the backhand disc throw and play catch for 15 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Breathing* (20 min): Practice belly breathing and 5-finger breathing (trace your hand while breathing in and out) for 3 minutes.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 63, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Video listening* (90 min): Watch a short nature video on your animal and note main ideas and details.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Sevens* (60 min): Use 5s + 2s to find 7s facts and practice with a fact board game.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Reasoning* (40 min): Explain how each piece of evidence supports the claim.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Native experience* (30 min): Read about how mission life changed the work, diet, health and culture of Native peoples.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Body levels* (30 min): Sing 'Row, Row, Row Your Boat' showing pitch with hand levels.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Football* (30 min): Throw a small football with a spiral grip to a stationary then moving partner.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Calm-down kit* (20 min): Build a calm-down kit in a shoebox with a stress ball, drawing paper, a favorite book and breathing cards.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 64, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Present* (90 min): Present your report to the family and answer 3 questions.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Table patterns* (60 min): Shade patterns in a multiplication table (diagonal squares, even products) and explain them.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Present* (40 min): Present the argument and answer questions.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Photo analysis* (30 min): Analyze a historic Calisphere photograph of a mission with a see-think-wonder chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Melody maps* (30 min): Draw melody maps for 2 short songs and play them on a keyboard app.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Feedback* (30 min): Give a partner two specific compliments and one cue to improve after their throws.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Move it out* (20 min): Try 3 movement stress relievers (stretching, a brisk walk, a dance break) and rate how each felt.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 65, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Unit 3 assessment* (90 min): Complete the Unit 3 test on main idea, text features and cause/effect with a new passage.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a 40-fact mixed check and set a fluency goal.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Unit 4 assessment* (40 min): Complete the unit quiz on animal groups.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write a paragraph explaining how the missions changed the region and its first peoples.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a listening quiz identifying melodies that go up, down or stay the same.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a 10-pass moving drill and record completions.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a calm-down plan poster listing your 4 best strategies in order.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Publishing the animal report
- Objective: Revise and publish an informational report with a text feature
- Objective: Present information
- Objective: Use roots to understand words
- Vocabulary: revise, root word, presentation
- Materials: report draft, poster board
- Assessment: Unit 3 test; informational report rubric

### Math 3: Facts of 7, 8, 9 and the associative property
- Objective: Use known facts and the associative property to solve 7s, 8s, 9s
- Objective: Find patterns in the multiplication table
- Vocabulary: associative property, multiplication table, pattern
- Materials: multiplication chart, fact cards
- Assessment: 40-fact mixed fluency check

### Science 3: Argument: groups help survival
- Objective: Construct an argument with evidence
- Objective: Present an argument orally
- Vocabulary: claim, evidence, reasoning, argument
- Materials: evidence chart, writing paper
- Assessment: Unit 4 quiz; claim-evidence-reasoning argument

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Missions, presidios and pueblos
- Objective: Describe the mission system and its effects on Native peoples
- Vocabulary: mission, presidio, pueblo, Franciscan
- Materials: Calisphere mission photographs, California missions map
- Assessment: Mission paragraph

### Art & Music 3: High and low pitch
- Objective: Identify high and low pitch and melodic direction
- Vocabulary: pitch, high, low, melody, contour
- Materials: xylophone, keyboard or piano app
- Assessment: Pitch direction listening quiz

### PE 3: Throwing and catching on the move
- Objective: Lead a moving receiver
- Objective: Throw different objects
- Vocabulary: lead pass, target, disc
- Materials: balls, flying disc, small football, cones
- Assessment: Moving passing drill

### Health 3: Managing stress and strong emotions
- Objective: Practice healthy stress management strategies
- Objective: Build a calm-down plan
- Vocabulary: stress, calm down, deep breathing, coping
- Materials: shoebox, stress ball, paper, crayons
- Assessment: Calm-down plan poster
