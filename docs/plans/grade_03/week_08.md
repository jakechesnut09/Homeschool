## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 16–17* (90 min): Read chapters 16–17 (the fair) and describe Templeton's motivation.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fair sharing* (60 min): Share 24 counters equally among 3, 4, 6 and 8 cups and write division equations for each.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Germination bag* (40 min): Plant beans in wet paper towels in a zip bag and tape to a window; predict what will happen.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Oral tradition* (30 min): Read or listen to a California Indian story (e.g., a Coyote story) and discuss its lesson.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Improvise* (30 min): Answer a parent's 4-beat clapped 'question' with your own improvised 4-beat 'answer' 10 times.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Sizing and turning* (30 min): Size the rope to armpit height and practice turning it from the wrists without jumping.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Digestion path* (20 min): Trace food from mouth to intestines on a diagram.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Dialogue punctuation* (90 min): Copy dialogue from the novel and learn quotation mark and comma rules; spell words with -ed and -ing using the doubling rule.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *How many groups* (60 min): Make groups of a given size (e.g., 20 grapes in bags of 5) and record the number of groups.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Seed dissection* (40 min): Soak a bean and open it to find the seed coat, embryo and food supply; draw and label.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Ceremonies* (30 min): Learn about a traditional ceremony and its meaning.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Ostinato* (30 min): Create a repeating ostinato on a pot or bucket to accompany 'Row, Row, Row Your Boat.'
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Basic bounce* (30 min): Practice the two-foot basic bounce in 5 sets of 20-second tries, resting between sets.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Stomach model* (20 min): Mash crackers with water in a zip bag to model the stomach.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 38, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 18–19* (90 min): Read chapters 18–19 and chart how Wilbur has changed since chapter 1.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Division on a number line* (60 min): Show division as repeated subtraction by jumping backward on a number line.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Flower parts* (40 min): Dissect a flower from the garden or store and identify parts used for reproduction.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Basketry* (30 min): Study California Indian basketry and its significance.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Compose* (30 min): Compose a 4-measure rhythm piece in 4/4 time using stick notation and Chrome Music Lab Rhythm.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Count* (30 min): Count consecutive jumps in 3 trials and record the best in a jump log.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Intestine length* (20 min): Measure a string as long as the small intestine (about 20 feet) and lay it out.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 39, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise your story's dialogue for correct punctuation and temporal words (later, meanwhile).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Story problems* (60 min): Solve 6 sharing and grouping word problems and label which kind each is.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Measure growth* (40 min): Measure and record bean sprout growth and draw daily observations.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Compare* (30 min): Compare a California Indian story to an Aesop fable.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Assign household percussion to each line and rehearse your piece 3 times.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Rhythm practice* (30 min): Jump to a slow-tempo song, then a faster one, keeping time with the beat.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Fiber and water* (20 min): Discuss how fiber and water help digestion.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 40, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Complete a dialogue punctuation check and discuss Charlotte's sacrifice in a family book talk.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a division meaning quiz and write one sharing and one grouping problem.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Draw a circular model of a flowering plant's life cycle with labels.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write about a tradition still practiced today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Unit 2 check* (30 min): Perform the composition for family and explain how you organized the rhythms.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Record a new personal best and explain how practice changed your count.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write the digestion steps in order.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Charlotte's Web, chapters 16–19
- Objective: Track character change
- Objective: Punctuate dialogue with quotation marks
- Objective: Revise a narrative
- Vocabulary: quotation marks, character change, revise
- Materials: Charlotte's Web, revision checklist
- Assessment: Dialogue punctuation check; book talk participation

### Math 3: Meaning of division
- Objective: Understand division as sharing and as grouping
- Objective: Write division equations
- Vocabulary: divide, quotient, dividend, divisor, share
- Materials: counters, cups
- Assessment: Division meaning quiz

### Science 3: Plant life cycles
- Objective: Model the life cycle of a flowering plant
- Objective: Observe seed germination
- Vocabulary: life cycle, seed, germinate, seedling, pollination
- Materials: bean seeds, paper towels, zip bags, ruler
- Assessment: Plant life cycle model

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Customs, beliefs and traditions
- Objective: Describe customs, beliefs and stories of local nations
- Vocabulary: custom, tradition, ceremony, oral tradition
- Materials: California Indian stories (library), museum website
- Assessment: Tradition writing

### Art & Music 3: Rhythm composition
- Objective: Improvise and compose rhythms
- Objective: Perform an original rhythm piece
- Vocabulary: improvise, compose, ostinato
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Rhythm, pots, pans, containers
- Assessment: Rhythm composition performance

### PE 3: Single rope basics
- Objective: Jump a self-turned rope with rhythm
- Objective: Track personal bests
- Vocabulary: rhythm, turn, basic bounce
- Materials: jump rope
- Assessment: Personal best jumps in log

### Health 3: Digestive system
- Objective: Describe how the digestive system works
- Vocabulary: digestion, esophagus, stomach, intestines, fiber
- Materials: zip bag, cracker, water, string
- Assessment: Digestion sequence
