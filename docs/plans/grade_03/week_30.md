## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Choose a topic* (90 min): Choose a California natural wonder (Yosemite, redwoods, Death Valley, Monterey Bay) and write 5 research questions.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Ruler fractions* (60 min): Practice measuring lines to the nearest half and quarter inch.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Log analysis* (40 min): Graph 4 weeks of your own temperature data and find the warmest and coolest days.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Benjamin Franklin* (30 min): Learn about Franklin's role in independence and read 3 sayings from Poor Richard's Almanack.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Feedback* (30 min): Play your song for a family member and collect 2 'glows' and 1 'grow.'
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Balloon volleyball* (30 min): Rally a balloon over a rope net using two-hand volleys; count team hits.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Sleep* (20 min): Learn that 8–12-year-olds need 9–12 hours of sleep and why the brain needs it.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 147, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Search tools* (90 min): Use keyword searches and an index to find sources; record titles in a source list.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Collect data* (60 min): Measure 15 leaves or pencils to the nearest quarter inch and record.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Compare* (40 min): Compare your local data to the same weeks in a city with a different climate.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Thomas Jefferson* (30 min): Learn about Jefferson writing the Declaration of Independence.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Revise* (30 min): Revise rhythm or melody based on the feedback and document changes.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Four square* (30 min): Learn four square rules and play 15 minutes, using an open-palm underhand hit.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Sleep log* (20 min): Track bedtime, wake time and hours slept for 5 nights.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 148, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Dictionary* (90 min): Use a dictionary and glossary to define 6 domain words; spell inter- and trans- words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Line plot* (60 min): Create a line plot with the data, labeling the scale in fourths.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Vocabulary game* (40 min): Play a weather vocabulary matching game with flash cards (precipitation, climate, cirrus, etc.).
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Frederick Douglass* (30 min): Read an adapted excerpt from Douglass's Narrative about learning to read and discuss the risk he took.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Practice* (30 min): Practice performing the revised song with steady tempo.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Wall ball* (30 min): Rally a playground ball against a wall with a partner, alternating hits.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Sun protection* (20 min): Learn sun safety (SPF 30+, hats, shade) and test sunscreen on UV beads or dark paper in the sun.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 149, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Read sources* (90 min): Read 2 sources and highlight answers to your questions.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Analyze* (60 min): Answer questions about the line plot: most common length, longest, shortest.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Weather report* (40 min): Present a 2-minute TV-style weather report for your city using your data and a map.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Harriet Tubman* (30 min): Learn about Harriet Tubman's escape and Underground Railroad journeys.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Accompaniment* (30 min): Add a percussion ostinato accompaniment.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Rally game* (30 min): Play a 2v2 balloon or beach ball game over a net, aiming for open space.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Hygiene routine* (20 min): Create a morning and evening hygiene checklist (bathing, hair, nails, clean clothes).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 150, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Turn in question list and source list for review.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit 8 assessment* (60 min): Complete the Unit 8 test on graphs and line plots.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Unit 8 assessment* (40 min): Complete the unit test on weather data, seasons and climates.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write a paragraph about the risk one hero took and why it mattered.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Compare the original and revised versions and explain which is better and why.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a volley skill check: 10 controlled hits to a partner.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Analyze your sleep log and set a bedtime goal.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Research questions and sources
- Objective: Generate research questions
- Objective: Use search tools and text features
- Objective: Use reference materials
- Vocabulary: research, source, keyword, glossary
- Materials: library books, kid-safe search, dictionary
- Assessment: Research question and source list

### Math 3: Measurement data and line plots
- Objective: Measure to the nearest half and quarter inch
- Objective: Show data on line plots
- Vocabulary: line plot, half inch, quarter inch
- Materials: ruler, pencils, leaves
- Assessment: Unit 8 test

### Science 3: Weather and climate review
- Objective: Analyze collected weather data
- Objective: Review weather and climate concepts
- Vocabulary: pattern, average, climate
- Materials: weather log, vocabulary cards
- Assessment: Unit 8 test; weather report

### Continuity and Change (Local History): American heroes: founders and freedom seekers
- Objective: Describe the lives of American heroes who took risks to secure freedoms
- Vocabulary: hero, risk, freedom, abolition
- Materials: biographies, Frederick Douglass Narrative excerpt
- Assessment: Hero paragraph

### Art & Music 3: Revising music
- Objective: Revise a composition with feedback
- Vocabulary: revise, feedback, accompaniment
- Materials: song draft, percussion
- Assessment: Revised composition with change notes

### PE 3: Net and wall games
- Objective: Play net games
- Vocabulary: net, volley, rally
- Materials: balloon, rope
- Assessment: Skill check

### Health 3: Sleep, sun and hygiene
- Objective: Explain the importance of sleep, sun protection and personal hygiene
- Vocabulary: sleep, sunscreen, SPF, hygiene, routine
- Materials: sleep log, sunscreen, UV beads (optional)
- Assessment: Sleep log analysis and hygiene checklist
