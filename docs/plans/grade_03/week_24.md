## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise reasons and conclusion using the opinion rubric.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Same denominator* (60 min): Compare fractions like 3/8 and 5/8 with strips and record with <, >, =.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Solutions* (40 min): Read about wildlife crossings, fish ladders and habitat restoration.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Community issues* (30 min): Identify a local issue (crosswalk safety, park repairs, litter) by observing your neighborhood.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Choose* (30 min): Choose a folk song from another culture (e.g., 'De Colores,' 'Sakura,' 'Funga Alafia') and explain your choice.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Beat* (30 min): March, clap and step to the beat of 4 songs with different tempos.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Medicine vs. drug* (20 min): Define medicine and drug and sort examples.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 117, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Type it* (90 min): Type the final essay, practicing keyboarding; study Greek roots tele, phon, scope.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Same numerator* (60 min): Compare 2/3 and 2/6 and explain why more pieces means smaller pieces.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Evaluate* (40 min): Compare 2 solutions for the salmon problem using criteria.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Letter to an official* (30 min): Write a polite letter to a city council member about the issue with a suggested solution.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Learn* (30 min): Learn the words and melody, including their meaning.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Levels* (30 min): Dance using high, middle and low levels on a caller's signal.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Labels* (20 min): Read an over-the-counter medicine box for dosage, age and warnings.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 118, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Speech* (90 min): Turn the essay into a 2-minute speech with note cards.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Same whole* (60 min): Explain why comparisons only work when the wholes are the same size, using two different pizzas.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Claim* (40 min): Make a claim about the best solution with evidence.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Family vote* (30 min): Hold a family vote on a real decision using secret ballots and count the majority.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Accompany* (30 min): Add a simple percussion accompaniment that fits the song's style.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Mirror* (30 min): Mirror a partner's slow-motion dance moves, then switch leaders.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Safe use* (20 min): Learn the rule: take medicine only from a parent or trusted adult, never alone.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 119, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Deliver* (90 min): Deliver the speech to family, speaking clearly and making eye contact.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fraction war* (60 min): Play fraction war with cards, justifying each comparison with a drawing.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Poster* (40 min): Create a poster about the chosen solution.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Change makers* (30 min): Read about a kid who made a difference in their community and discuss how.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Perform* (30 min): Perform the folk song for family, introducing where it comes from.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *GoNoodle* (30 min): Learn and perform a GoNoodle dance video.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Look-alikes* (20 min): Compare pictures of candy and medicine look-alikes and explain the danger.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 120, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Unit 6 assessment* (90 min): Complete the Unit 6 test on biographies, conjunctions and sentence types.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Compare 12 pairs of fractions and justify 3 in writing.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Unit 7 assessment* (40 min): Complete the unit quiz on habitats and environmental change.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Unit 5 assessment* (30 min): Complete the unit test on rules, laws and citizenship.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Unit 6 check* (30 min): Reflect in writing on what went well and what you'd practice next time.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Create and perform an 8-count movement phrase to music.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write 5 medicine safety rules for a younger child.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Opinion essay: revising and publishing
- Objective: Revise and type an opinion essay
- Objective: Give an opinion speech
- Objective: Use Greek roots
- Vocabulary: revise, conclusion, keyboarding
- Materials: computer, rubric
- Assessment: Unit 6 test; published opinion essay rubric

### Math 3: Comparing fractions
- Objective: Compare fractions with the same numerator or denominator
- Objective: Justify with models
- Vocabulary: compare, greater than, less than, same denominator, same numerator
- Materials: fraction strips, fraction circles
- Assessment: Comparing fractions check

### Science 3: Solutions to environmental problems
- Objective: Evaluate the merit of solutions to environmental change
- Vocabulary: solution, merit, wildlife crossing, restoration
- Materials: articles, poster supplies
- Assessment: Unit 7 quiz; solution poster

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Making a difference in civic life
- Objective: Participate in civic life by voicing ideas and voting
- Vocabulary: petition, vote, majority, community issue
- Materials: letter template, city official contact
- Assessment: Unit 5 test; letter to official

### Art & Music 3: Folk song performance
- Objective: Choose and perform a folk song from another culture
- Vocabulary: folk song, perform, culture
- Materials: song recording and lyrics
- Assessment: Folk song performance and reflection

### PE 3: Rhythm and movement
- Objective: Move in time to a beat
- Objective: Use levels and directions
- Vocabulary: rhythm, beat, tempo, level
- Materials: music
- Assessment: 8-count phrase

### Health 3: Medicine safety
- Objective: Distinguish medicines from other drugs
- Objective: Describe safe medicine use
- Vocabulary: medicine, prescription, over-the-counter, dosage, label
- Materials: empty medicine boxes
- Assessment: Medicine safety rules
