## Day 1 — Monday (school day 111, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Opinion choice* (90 min): Choose which person showed the greatest courage and list reasons with evidence.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Strip equivalents* (60 min): Use fraction strips to find fractions equal to 1/2, 1/3 and 3/4.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Changes* (40 min): List natural and human-caused environmental changes (drought, fire, building).
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Citizens* (30 min): Define citizenship and list rights and responsibilities of citizens.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Taiko* (30 min): Watch a Japanese taiko performance and copy a simple taiko rhythm with large arm motions.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Whiffle ball* (30 min): Play a family whiffle ball game with tee or soft pitch.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Personal information* (20 min): Sort information into okay to share online (favorite color) and private (address, school, photos).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 112, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Organizer* (90 min): Plan an essay with introduction, 3 reasons with evidence and conclusion; study roots struct and tract.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Number line equivalents* (60 min): Stack number lines for halves, fourths and eighths and mark equivalent points.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Drought test* (40 min): Water one plant and withhold water from another; observe effects.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Civic virtues* (30 min): Discuss honesty, respect, responsibility and courage with real examples.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Gamelan* (30 min): Listen to Indonesian gamelan and describe its layered, repeating patterns.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Paddle ball* (30 min): Play paddle ball over a line, scoring when the ball lands in open space.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Passwords* (20 min): Create a strong passphrase and learn why passwords are shared only with parents.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 113, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Sentence variety* (90 min): Practice simple, compound and complex sentences about your person.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Whole numbers* (60 min): Show that 3 = 3/1 and 1 = 4/4 = 6/6 with models.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Case study* (40 min): Read about how the California drought affected salmon.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Plan a service project* (30 min): Plan a small service project (park cleanup, food drive, cards for seniors).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Djembe* (30 min): Learn bass, tone and slap strokes and a West African call-and-response rhythm on a bucket.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Strategy* (30 min): Discuss and test placing hits away from fielders.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Online strangers* (20 min): Discuss what to do if someone online asks personal questions or to meet.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 114, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Draft* (90 min): Draft the essay with linking words (because, therefore, for example).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Equivalence game* (60 min): Play a matching game with equivalent fraction cards and explain matches.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Predict* (40 min): Predict what happens to organisms when a wetland is drained.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Serve* (30 min): Carry out the service project with family.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Music map* (30 min): Mark each music tradition on a world map with a drawing of its main instrument.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Game* (30 min): Play a striking game of choice and keep score fairly.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Family agreement* (20 min): Write a family technology agreement with screen time, apps and rules.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 115, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Read the draft to a partner and get 2 compliments and 1 suggestion.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete an equivalent fractions quiz with models.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Explain cause-and-effect of environmental change in writing.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write a reflection on how the project helped the community.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Discuss how each style reflects the culture and purpose that created it.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a striking unit check combining paddle and batting skills.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Unit 4 check* (20 min): Complete a quiz on fire, traffic, water, emergency and online safety.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Opinion essay: drafting
- Objective: Write an opinion essay with reasons from texts
- Objective: Write simple, compound and complex sentences
- Vocabulary: opinion, reason, evidence, linking words
- Materials: biographies, opinion organizer
- Assessment: Opinion essay draft

### Math 3: Equivalent fractions
- Objective: Recognize and generate equivalent fractions with models
- Objective: Express whole numbers as fractions
- Vocabulary: equivalent, whole number, same size
- Materials: fraction strips, number lines
- Assessment: Equivalent fractions quiz

### Science 3: Environmental change
- Objective: Describe how environmental change affects organisms
- Vocabulary: environmental change, drought, pollution, habitat loss
- Materials: articles, plants, water
- Assessment: Cause-effect explanation

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Citizenship and public virtue
- Objective: Discuss citizens' responsibilities
- Objective: Carry out community service
- Vocabulary: citizen, responsibility, volunteer, public virtue
- Materials: service project supplies
- Assessment: Service project reflection

### Art & Music 3: Music around the world
- Objective: Explore world music traditions
- Objective: Perform a drumming pattern
- Vocabulary: taiko, gamelan, djembe, culture
- Materials: recordings, bucket or drum
- Assessment: World music map

### PE 3: Striking games
- Objective: Apply striking skills and placement strategy in games
- Vocabulary: placement, strategy, open space
- Materials: bat, paddles, balls
- Assessment: Striking unit check

### Health 3: Online safety
- Objective: Identify ways to stay safe online
- Vocabulary: personal information, password, cyberbullying, screen time
- Materials: computer or tablet
- Assessment: Unit 4 quiz; tech agreement
