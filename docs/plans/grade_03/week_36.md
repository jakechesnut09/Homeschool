## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Reading review* (90 min): Review the reading log and choose top 3 books; write a recommendation.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fraction review* (60 min): Review equivalence and comparison with strips and number lines.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Forces review* (40 min): Review forces and magnets with a quick ramp and magnet demo.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Plan* (30 min): Plan a 'Museum of Our Community' with sections for geography, first peoples, settlement, government and economy.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Set up* (30 min): Hang the art exhibit and set up a performance area.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Plan* (30 min): Create a summer activity calendar with 60 minutes of activity per day across at least 4 activity types.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Present* (20 min): Present the campaign to your chosen audience.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Writing portfolio* (90 min): Select the best narrative, informative and opinion pieces for a portfolio.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Measurement review* (60 min): Review time, volume, mass and area problems.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Life science review* (40 min): Review life cycles, traits and habitats with a card sort.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Build* (30 min): Build exhibits from the year's maps, timeline, oral history and reports with labels.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Gallery walk* (30 min): Lead family on a gallery walk, explaining each artwork.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Try something new* (30 min): Try a new activity (swimming, skateboarding, yoga) for 30 minutes and rate it.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Feedback* (20 min): Ask the audience what they learned and one change they will make.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Final assessment* (90 min): Complete the end-of-year reading, spelling and grammar assessment.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Geometry review* (60 min): Review quadrilaterals and perimeter with a scavenger hunt.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Earth science review* (40 min): Review fossils, weather and hazards with a vocabulary game.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse a docent tour script explaining each exhibit.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Concert* (30 min): Perform the concert program.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Family outing* (30 min): Go on a family hike or bike ride and measure the distance.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Final quiz* (20 min): Take the end-of-year health quiz covering all units.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Reflection* (90 min): Write a letter to your future 4th-grade self about your growth.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Final assessment* (60 min): Complete the end-of-year math assessment.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Final assessment* (40 min): Complete the end-of-year science assessment.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Showcase* (30 min): Host the museum for family and friends and lead tours.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Critique* (30 min): Evaluate one artwork and one song using criteria from the year.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Log* (30 min): Fill in a week of the summer activity log and set a summer skill goal (e.g., 50 consecutive rope jumps).
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Summer goals* (20 min): Set 2 summer health goals (activity, nutrition, sleep or safety).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Celebrate* (90 min): Share your portfolio at a family reading celebration and set a summer reading goal.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Reflection* (60 min): Build a math portfolio of best work and write a reflection on growth.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Showcase* (40 min): Share the science notebook and favorite investigation with family.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Reflection* (30 min): Write a reflection on how your community has changed and what has stayed the same.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Reflection* (30 min): Write about your growth as an artist and musician this year.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Share the summer plan and lead the family in your favorite warm-up.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Reflection* (20 min): Write a reflection on the healthiest change you made this year.
