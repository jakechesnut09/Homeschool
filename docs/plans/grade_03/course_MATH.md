# Math 3 (Grade 3)

Third-grade mathematics focused on the four critical areas of the CA framework: multiplication and division within 100, fractions as numbers (especially unit fractions), area, and two-dimensional shapes. Students also round, add and subtract within 1,000, solve two-step word problems, measure time, mass and liquid volume, and represent data with scaled graphs and line plots.

*Framework:* California Common Core State Standards for Mathematics; 2023 CA Mathematics Framework  
*Time:* 60 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Place Value, Rounding, Add & Subtract within 1000 (weeks 1–5)
Place value lets us round, estimate, and add or subtract large numbers efficiently.

- Week 1: Place value to 1,000
- Week 2: Rounding to the nearest 10 and 100
- Week 3: Addition within 1,000
- Week 4: Subtraction within 1,000
- Week 5: Add/subtract problem solving

### Unit 2: Meaning of Multiplication and Division (weeks 6–10)
Multiplication joins equal groups; division splits a total into equal groups or finds the number of groups.

- Week 6: Equal groups and multiplication
- Week 7: Arrays and the commutative property
- Week 8: Meaning of division
- Week 9: Multiplication and division as related operations
- Week 10: Multiplication and division word problems

### Unit 3: Multiplication Strategies and Fact Fluency (weeks 11–14)
Properties of operations let us use facts we know to find facts we don't.

- Week 11: Facts of 2, 5, 10, 0 and 1
- Week 12: Facts of 3, 4, 6 and the distributive property
- Week 13: Facts of 7, 8, 9 and the associative property
- Week 14: Multiplying by multiples of 10 and fluency

### Unit 4: Area (weeks 15–17)
Area measures how much flat space a shape covers and connects to multiplication.

- Week 15: Understanding area
- Week 16: Area and multiplication
- Week 17: Area of composite figures

### Unit 5: Two-Step Problems and Patterns (weeks 18–19)
Good problem solvers represent, solve, and check for reasonableness.

- Week 18: Two-step word problems with four operations
- Week 19: Arithmetic patterns

### Unit 6: Fractions as Numbers (weeks 20–25)
Fractions are numbers made of equal-size unit parts and live on the number line.

- Week 20: Unit fractions and partitioning
- Week 21: Non-unit fractions
- Week 22: Fractions on the number line
- Week 23: Equivalent fractions
- Week 24: Comparing fractions
- Week 25: Fractions review and application

### Unit 7: Time, Liquid Volume and Mass (weeks 26–28)
We measure with standard units and use operations to solve measurement problems.

- Week 26: Time to the minute and elapsed time
- Week 27: Liquid volume
- Week 28: Mass

### Unit 8: Data: Scaled Graphs and Line Plots (weeks 29–30)
Graphs organize data so we can compare and answer questions.

- Week 29: Scaled picture graphs and bar graphs
- Week 30: Measurement data and line plots

### Unit 9: Geometry and Perimeter (weeks 31–34)
Shapes are classified by attributes; perimeter is the distance around a shape.

- Week 31: Quadrilaterals and attributes
- Week 32: Perimeter
- Week 33: Perimeter and area relationships
- Week 34: Geometry project

### Unit 10: Year Review and Fluency Celebration (weeks 35–36)
Connecting the year's big ideas shows what we know and can do.

- Week 35: Cumulative review: operations and fluency
- Week 36: Cumulative review: fractions, measurement, geometry

## Standards

- **3.G.A.1** Understand that shapes in different categories may share attributes; recognize rhombuses, rectangles, and squares as quadrilaterals.
- **3.G.A.2** Partition shapes into parts with equal areas and express the area of each part as a unit fraction of the whole.
- **3.MD.A.1** Tell and write time to the nearest minute and measure time intervals in minutes; solve elapsed-time word problems.
- **3.MD.A.2** Measure and estimate liquid volumes and masses using grams, kilograms, and liters; solve one-step word problems with these units.
- **3.MD.B.3** Draw scaled picture graphs and scaled bar graphs to represent data with several categories; solve one- and two-step 'how many more/less' problems.
- **3.MD.B.4** Generate measurement data with rulers marked in halves and fourths of an inch; show the data on a line plot.
- **3.MD.C.5** Recognize area as an attribute of plane figures and understand concepts of area measurement using unit squares.
- **3.MD.C.6** Measure areas by counting unit squares (square cm, square m, square in, square ft, and improvised units).
- **3.MD.C.7** Relate area to multiplication and addition; use tiling, side-length multiplication, the distributive property, and decomposition of rectilinear figures.
- **3.MD.D.8** Solve real-world and mathematical problems involving perimeters of polygons, including unknown side lengths and rectangles with the same perimeter and different areas (or vice versa).
- **3.NBT.A.1** Use place value understanding to round whole numbers to the nearest 10 or 100.
- **3.NBT.A.2** Fluently add and subtract within 1000 using strategies and algorithms based on place value and properties of operations.
- **3.NBT.A.3** Multiply one-digit whole numbers by multiples of 10 in the range 10–90.
- **3.NF.A.1** Understand a fraction 1/b as one part when a whole is partitioned into b equal parts; understand a/b as a parts of size 1/b.
- **3.NF.A.2** Understand a fraction as a number on the number line; represent fractions on a number line diagram.
- **3.NF.A.3** Explain equivalence of fractions and compare fractions by reasoning about their size (same numerator or same denominator); express whole numbers as fractions.
- **3.OA.A.1** Interpret products of whole numbers as the total number of objects in equal groups (5 x 7 as 5 groups of 7).
- **3.OA.A.2** Interpret whole-number quotients as the number in each share or the number of shares (56 ÷ 8).
- **3.OA.A.3** Use multiplication and division within 100 to solve word problems with equal groups, arrays, and measurement quantities.
- **3.OA.A.4** Determine the unknown whole number in a multiplication or division equation relating three whole numbers.
- **3.OA.B.5** Apply properties of operations (commutative, associative, distributive) as strategies to multiply and divide.
- **3.OA.B.6** Understand division as an unknown-factor problem.
- **3.OA.C.7** Fluently multiply and divide within 100; know from memory all products of two one-digit numbers by the end of grade 3.
- **3.OA.D.8** Solve two-step word problems using the four operations; represent with equations using a letter for the unknown; assess reasonableness with estimation.
- **3.OA.D.9** Identify arithmetic patterns (including in the addition and multiplication tables) and explain them using properties of operations.
