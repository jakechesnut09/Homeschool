## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 13–15* (90 min): Read chapters 13–15 and discuss why the Wizard is a 'humbug.'
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Liter benchmark* (60 min): Fill a 1-liter bottle and estimate which household containers hold more or less than 1 liter.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Local data* (40 min): Look up average monthly temperature and rainfall for your city and copy them into a table.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Federal system* (30 min): Learn what the federal government does and what states do.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Choose* (30 min): Choose a familiar place (bedroom, park, kitchen) and list its important features.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Plan* (30 min): Choose a song and a theme (ocean, storm, animals) and list 4 movement ideas using levels and pathways.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Peer pressure* (20 min): Define peer pressure and list positive and negative examples.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 132, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 16–18* (90 min): Read chapters 16–18 and explain how the Wizard 'gives' each friend a gift.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Measure containers* (60 min): Measure the capacity of 5 containers with a marked liter bottle and record in a table.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Rainfall bar graph* (40 min): Draw a bar graph of average monthly rainfall with a labeled scale.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *California's role* (30 min): Find California's senators and representatives and explain how they represent us.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Map* (30 min): Draw a bird's-eye-view map of the place with a key.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Create* (30 min): Choreograph a 32-count dance with a beginning shape, 3 movement phrases and an ending shape.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Refusal strategies* (20 min): Learn 5 ways to say no (say no firmly, give a reason, change the subject, walk away, suggest something else).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 133, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Word choice* (90 min): Replace weak words with vivid ones in a passage and explain the effect; spell schwa words (-al, -el).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Read scales* (60 min): Read liquid levels on beaker diagrams with different scales.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Temperature line graph* (40 min): Draw a line graph of average monthly high temperatures.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Tribal nations* (30 min): Learn how sovereign tribes govern themselves and work with state and federal governments.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Build* (30 min): Build a 3D cardboard model of the place based on your map.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Practice* (30 min): Rehearse the dance 5 times, adding one change of tempo and one change of level.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Role-play* (20 min): Role-play refusing a vape or alcohol offer in 3 scenarios.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 134, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Theme* (90 min): Write a paragraph about the theme 'you already have what you need' with evidence.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Volume problems* (60 min): Solve add/subtract/multiply word problems with liters (e.g., 4 jugs of 3 L).
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Interpret* (40 min): Use both graphs to describe the typical weather in winter, spring, summer and fall.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Map* (30 min): Label California, neighboring states and California tribal lands on a map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Details* (30 min): Add small details (furniture, trees, signs) from paper and clay.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Perform* (30 min): Perform the dance for family and teach them one phrase.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Poster* (20 min): Design a drug-free poster with a positive message for kids.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 135, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Hold a book discussion and answer comprehension questions on chapters 13–18.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a liquid volume quiz and estimation challenge.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Predict next month's typical weather from the graphs and compare it to your log later.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Unit 6 assessment* (30 min): Complete the unit test on government.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Unit 7 check* (30 min): Evaluate the model using criteria (accurate, detailed, sturdy) and give a tour.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Describe how you used force, levels and pathways and what you would change.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Unit 5 check* (20 min): Complete a quiz on medicines, tobacco, alcohol and refusal skills.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, chapters 13–18
- Objective: Determine theme from key details
- Objective: Use dialogue in narrative
- Objective: Choose words for effect
- Vocabulary: theme, humbug, word choice
- Materials: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz
- Assessment: Theme paragraph; discussion

### Math 3: Liquid volume
- Objective: Estimate and measure liquid volume in liters and milliliters
- Objective: Solve volume problems
- Vocabulary: liquid volume, liter, milliliter, capacity
- Materials: 1-liter bottle, measuring cups, containers, water
- Assessment: Liquid volume quiz

### Science 3: Seasonal weather patterns
- Objective: Represent weather data in graphs
- Objective: Describe typical weather for each season
- Vocabulary: season, average, bar graph, line graph
- Materials: graph paper, local monthly climate averages
- Assessment: Seasonal graphs and written interpretation

### Continuity and Change (Local History): States and tribes in the federal system
- Objective: Describe how California, other states and tribes participate in the federal system
- Vocabulary: federal, state, sovereign nation, representative
- Materials: U.S. map, map of tribal lands
- Assessment: Unit 6 test

### Art & Music 3: Model of an everyday place
- Objective: Construct a map and model of a familiar place
- Objective: Evaluate with criteria
- Vocabulary: model, map, bird's-eye view, scale
- Materials: cardboard, paper, glue, markers
- Assessment: Place model with criteria evaluation

### PE 3: Creative dance
- Objective: Create a dance
- Vocabulary: choreography, sequence
- Materials: music
- Assessment: Original dance

### Health 3: Refusal skills and drug-free choices
- Objective: Demonstrate refusal skills
- Objective: Encourage drug-free choices
- Vocabulary: refusal, peer pressure, drug-free
- Materials: scenario cards, poster supplies
- Assessment: Unit 5 quiz; drug-free poster
