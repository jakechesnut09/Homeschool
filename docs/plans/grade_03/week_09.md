## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 20–22* (90 min): Finish the novel and discuss the theme of friendship and the cycle of life.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fact families* (60 min): Use triangle cards to write 4 related equations for 8 fact families.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Butterfly* (40 min): Watch a butterfly metamorphosis video and sequence egg, larva, pupa, adult cards.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Trade networks* (30 min): Read about California Indian trade networks and trace a route on a map from the coast to the mountains.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Primary colors* (30 min): Mix red, yellow and blue on a palette to make orange, green and violet; record recipes.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Skier* (30 min): Learn the skier jump (side to side) and complete 3 sets of 10.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Heart* (20 min): Learn the heart is a muscle that pumps blood; listen to a heartbeat through a paper towel tube.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Theme* (90 min): Write a paragraph stating the novel's theme supported by 2 quotations; spell drop-e words (hope/hoping).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unknown factor* (60 min): Solve 15 missing-factor equations such as 6 × ? = 42 and ? ÷ 4 = 7.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Grasshopper* (40 min): Compare grasshopper incomplete metamorphosis to the butterfly in a Venn diagram.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Shell bead money* (30 min): Learn how the Chumash made and used shell bead money and why it was valuable inland.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Color wheel* (30 min): Paint a 6-color wheel on a paper plate.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Bell* (30 min): Learn the bell jump (front to back) and the side swing.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Pulse* (20 min): Measure pulse before and after exercise and graph results.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Publish* (90 min): Edit and publish your animal story with illustrations.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Arrays to division* (60 min): Use an array to show that 35 ÷ 5 means 5 × ? = 35.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Clay models* (40 min): Build clay models of each stage of a ladybug life cycle.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Barter game* (30 min): Play a barter simulation trading household 'resources' (acorns, obsidian, shells, baskets) with family members.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Intermediate* (30 min): Mix and add the 6 intermediate colors (red-orange, yellow-green, etc.) to make a 12-color wheel.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Alternate foot* (30 min): Learn the alternate-foot jog step and try 20 in a row.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Lungs model* (20 min): Build a balloon-lung model in a plastic bottle and demonstrate breathing.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 44, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Book talk* (90 min): Give a 2-minute book talk recommending Charlotte's Web to a friend.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fact family puzzles* (60 min): Complete multiplication-division puzzle grids and explain how one fact helps solve another.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Bean check* (40 min): Measure and graph bean plant growth so far.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Then and now* (30 min): Compare traditional trade with a present-day tribal business or enterprise from a tribe's website.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Brush care* (30 min): Practice wash, dry-brush and stipple strokes, then clean and store brushes correctly.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Heart rate* (30 min): Jump for 1 minute, take your pulse, and compare it to your resting heart rate.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Protect* (20 min): List ways to protect the heart and lungs (exercise, no smoke).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 45, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Unit 2 assessment* (90 min): Complete the Unit 2 test (comprehension, vocabulary, verbs, dialogue) and share your published story.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a quiz of 20 unknown-number equations within 100.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Explain in writing what all insect life cycles have in common.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write a paragraph explaining how trade helped communities get what their land did not provide.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Label all 12 colors on the wheel and explain how to mix any one of them.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Perform 3 tricks in a row for a family member who checks them off.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain how the heart and lungs work together.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Charlotte's Web, chapters 20–22 and story publishing
- Objective: Determine the theme of the novel
- Objective: Publish a narrative
- Objective: Present a book talk
- Vocabulary: theme, friendship, book talk
- Materials: Charlotte's Web, publishing paper
- Assessment: Unit 2 test; published narrative rubric

### Math 3: Multiplication and division as related operations
- Objective: Relate multiplication and division with fact families
- Objective: Find unknown numbers in equations
- Vocabulary: fact family, inverse, unknown factor
- Materials: triangle fact cards
- Assessment: Unknown-number equations quiz

### Science 3: Animal life cycles: insects
- Objective: Compare complete and incomplete metamorphosis
- Objective: Model insect life cycles
- Vocabulary: metamorphosis, larva, pupa, nymph
- Materials: pictures or video of butterflies and grasshoppers, clay
- Assessment: Venn diagram and written explanation

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Trade and economy of first peoples
- Objective: Describe how local nations traded and used resources
- Objective: Compare traditional and modern tribal economies
- Vocabulary: trade, barter, shell bead money, economy
- Materials: California Indian trade route map, household items for barter
- Assessment: Trade paragraph

### Art & Music 3: The color wheel
- Objective: Mix primary into secondary and intermediate colors
- Objective: Use paint tools safely
- Vocabulary: primary, secondary, intermediate, color wheel
- Materials: tempera or watercolor paints, brushes, paper plate palette
- Assessment: 12-color wheel

### PE 3: Jump rope tricks
- Objective: Perform footwork variations
- Objective: Observe heart rate response
- Vocabulary: skier, bell, side swing, criss-cross
- Materials: jump rope, timer
- Assessment: Trick checklist

### Health 3: Heart and lungs
- Objective: Describe the circulatory and respiratory systems
- Vocabulary: heart, blood, lungs, oxygen, pulse
- Materials: paper towel tube, balloon, plastic bottle
- Assessment: Heart-lung explanation
