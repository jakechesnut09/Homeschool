## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 1–2* (90 min): Read chapters 1–2 and contrast Kansas and Oz with descriptive details.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Recipe fractions* (60 min): Read a simple recipe and model 1/2, 3/4 and 1/3 cup amounts with measuring cups.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Weather words* (40 min): Define temperature, precipitation, wind and cloud cover and set up a daily weather log page in your science notebook.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Branches* (30 min): Use Ben's Guide to learn what each branch does and who works in it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Shape vs. form* (30 min): Compare a drawn circle to a ball and roll basic forms (sphere, cylinder, cube) in clay.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Virginia Reel* (30 min): Learn the Virginia Reel figures (forward and back, do-si-do, reel) with family.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Effects* (20 min): Compare images of healthy and smoker's lungs and list effects on the heart and lungs.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 3–4* (90 min): Read chapters 3–4 and describe the Scarecrow; spell silent-letter words (kn, wr, gh).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Garden plot* (60 min): Partition a garden drawing into equal areas and write each as a unit fraction.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Rain gauge* (40 min): Build a rain gauge by taping a ruler to a straight-sided jar and place it in an open spot outdoors.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Checks and balances* (30 min): Play a role game where one branch makes, one carries out and one judges a family 'law.'
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Pinch pot* (30 min): Make a pinch pot with even walls by pressing and turning.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Jarabe Tapatío* (30 min): Learn the basic heel-toe steps of the Mexican Hat Dance.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Vaping* (20 min): Read a kid-level article on vaping and learn that vapes contain addictive nicotine.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Adverbs* (90 min): Use comparative and superlative adverbs (faster, most carefully) in sentences about the journey.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fraction stations* (60 min): Rotate through number line, comparison and equivalence stations.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Wind vane* (40 min): Build a wind vane from a straw, index-card arrow and pencil eraser, and mark N/S/E/W with a compass app.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *National leaders* (30 min): Identify the current President, your members of Congress and the Supreme Court.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Coil pot* (30 min): Build a coil pot, joining coils with score-and-slip.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Practice* (30 min): Practice both dances to music, counting steps aloud.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Secondhand smoke* (20 min): Discuss secondhand smoke and how to avoid it.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 5–6* (90 min): Read chapters 5–6 and write whether you agree with the Tin Woodman's view that a heart matters most.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Error analysis* (60 min): Find and fix errors in 5 worked fraction problems.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Thermometer* (40 min): Read an outdoor thermometer at 8 a.m., noon and 4 p.m. and explain why temperatures change through the day.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Three-branch chart* (30 min): Make a tree chart of the three branches with jobs and leaders.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Texture* (30 min): Add carved or pressed textures to both pots with household tools.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Culture* (30 min): Learn where each dance comes from and why it is danced.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Influences* (20 min): Identify how flavors, packaging and peers can make vaping seem appealing.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 125, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Complete a chapters 1–6 quiz and a point-of-view journal entry.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit 6 assessment* (60 min): Complete the Unit 6 test on fractions.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Explain what each homemade instrument measures and record a full day's weather using all of them.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Answer questions on the branches and explain why power is divided.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Evaluate the pots for even walls and secure joins.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Perform both dances for family.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write 5 facts about tobacco and vaping.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, chapters 1–6
- Objective: Describe setting and characters
- Objective: Distinguish own point of view from characters'
- Objective: Use comparative adverbs
- Vocabulary: setting, cyclone, point of view, adverb
- Materials: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz by L. Frank Baum (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Chapters 1–6 quiz; point-of-view journal

### Math 3: Fractions review and application
- Objective: Apply fraction understanding to problems
- Objective: Partition shapes into equal areas
- Vocabulary: fraction, equivalent, area
- Materials: fraction tools, recipe card
- Assessment: Unit 6 test

### Science 3: Weather tools
- Objective: Identify weather conditions and the tools that measure them
- Objective: Build simple weather instruments
- Vocabulary: temperature, precipitation, wind, thermometer, rain gauge, wind vane
- Materials: straight-sided jar, ruler, tape, thermometer, straw, index card, pencil with eraser, pin
- Assessment: Instrument explanation and first log entries

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Three branches of government
- Objective: Explain the legislative, executive and judicial branches and checks and balances
- Vocabulary: legislative, executive, judicial, checks and balances
- Materials: Ben's Guide, chart paper
- Assessment: Three-branch chart

### Art & Music 3: Form and clay
- Objective: Create 3D forms in clay using pinch and coil methods
- Vocabulary: form, sculpture, pinch pot, coil, score and slip
- Materials: air-dry clay, fork, water cup
- Assessment: Pinch and coil pots

### PE 3: Folk dances
- Objective: Perform folk dances from different cultures
- Vocabulary: folk dance, promenade, do-si-do
- Materials: music
- Assessment: Folk dance performance

### Health 3: Tobacco and vaping
- Objective: Describe the harmful effects of tobacco, vaping and secondhand smoke
- Vocabulary: tobacco, nicotine, vaping, secondhand smoke, addiction
- Materials: healthy vs. smoker's lung images, KidsHealth article
- Assessment: Tobacco facts
