## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *King Midas* (90 min): Read 'The Golden Touch' and chart Midas's wishes, actions and consequences.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Cover it* (60 min): Cover book covers and placemats with sticky notes and count to find area in improvised units.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Peanut variation* (40 min): Measure 20 peanut shells and record lengths.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Gold Rush* (30 min): Learn how the 1848 gold discovery brought people from around the world to California.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Staff* (30 min): Learn the 5 lines and 4 spaces and draw a treble clef 5 times.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Stationary* (30 min): Dribble 30 times with each hand using fingertips at waist height.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Active listening* (20 min): Practice active listening: partner tells a story, you repeat back the main points and a feeling.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Arachne* (90 min): Read the myth of Arachne and Athena and describe Arachne's pride with evidence.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Square units* (60 min): Measure shapes on grid paper by counting square centimeters and square inches.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Line plot* (40 min): Make a line plot of the lengths and describe the variation.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Immigrant communities* (30 min): Read about Chinese, Mexican, Chilean and African American Gold Rush communities and their contributions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Note names* (30 min): Learn E-G-B-D-F (lines) and F-A-C-E (spaces) with a mnemonic.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Walking* (30 min): Dribble while walking a straight line and back, switching hands at the turn.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *I-messages* (20 min): Write I-messages ('I feel ___ when ___ because ___') for 5 common conflicts.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 73, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Pronouns* (90 min): Practice pronoun-antecedent agreement in sentences about both myths; spell diphthongs oi/oy, ou/ow.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Compare areas* (60 min): Build three different shapes with 12 tiles each and explain why they have the same area.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Leaf variation* (40 min): Collect 15 leaves from one tree and compare size, shape and color.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Primary source* (30 min): Analyze a miner's letter or daguerreotype and write 3 observations and 2 questions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Write* (30 min): Write 10 notes on the staff from letter names.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Running* (30 min): Dribble while jogging the length of a driveway or court.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Conflict steps* (20 min): Learn 5 steps (cool down, talk, listen, brainstorm, agree) and make a pocket card.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 74, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Opinion plan* (90 min): Plan an opinion paragraph: Which character learned the hardest lesson? Give 2 reasons.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Square feet* (60 min): Make a square-foot paper template and estimate then measure the area of a rug.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Dogs* (40 min): Compare different dog breeds and discuss variation within a species.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Timeline* (30 min): Build a timeline of newcomers to your region from first peoples to today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Read and play* (30 min): Read and play 'Hot Cross Buns' (E-D-C) on a keyboard app from notation.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Eyes up* (30 min): Dribble while calling out the number of fingers a family member holds up.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Role-play* (20 min): Role-play solving a sibling conflict over a shared toy using the steps.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 75, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write the opinion paragraph with linking words and discuss it in a book talk.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Find the areas of 10 shapes on grid paper and explain why units must not overlap.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Write a conclusion about variation with evidence from your data.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Unit 3 assessment* (30 min): Complete the unit test on explorers, missions, ranchos and settlers.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a 15-note naming quiz.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Dribble through a 6-cone course without losing control.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain the conflict-resolution steps and when you would use them.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Myths: King Midas and Arachne
- Objective: Describe characters' motivations
- Objective: Use pronoun-antecedent agreement
- Objective: Begin opinion writing about a text
- Vocabulary: greed, pride, pronoun, antecedent
- Materials: Hawthorne's 'The Golden Touch', Arachne retelling
- Assessment: Opinion paragraph about a myth

### Math 3: Understanding area
- Objective: Define area as covering a flat surface
- Objective: Measure area with unit squares
- Vocabulary: area, square unit, tile, gap, overlap
- Materials: square tiles, sticky notes, grid paper
- Assessment: Area by counting square units check

### Science 3: Variation within a group
- Objective: Observe and measure variation within a species
- Objective: Analyze data
- Vocabulary: variation, species, data
- Materials: peanuts in shells or leaves from one tree, ruler
- Assessment: Variation conclusion with data

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Gold Rush and new settlers
- Objective: Research newcomers who settled the region and their contributions
- Vocabulary: Gold Rush, immigrant, forty-niner, statehood
- Materials: Gold Rush photographs and letters (Calisphere, Library of Congress)
- Assessment: Unit 3 test; newcomer timeline

### Art & Music 3: The treble staff
- Objective: Read pitches on the treble staff
- Vocabulary: staff, line note, space note, treble clef
- Materials: staff paper, keyboard app
- Assessment: Note-name quiz

### PE 3: Hand dribbling
- Objective: Dribble with fingertips while stationary and moving
- Vocabulary: dribble, fingertips, control, waist height
- Materials: playground ball or basketball
- Assessment: Dribble course

### Health 3: Communication and conflict resolution
- Objective: Demonstrate active listening, I-messages and conflict-resolution steps
- Vocabulary: active listening, I-message, compromise, conflict
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Role-play and pocket card
