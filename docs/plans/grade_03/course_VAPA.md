# Art & Music 3 (Grade 3)

Third-grade visual art and music alternating in units. In art, students explore line, shape, color, value, texture, pattern, printmaking and sculpture through observation and imagination, study artists and cultures, and curate an exhibition. In music, students read and write rhythms and melodies, sing with solfege, explore instruments and music of California and the world, and compose and perform original music.

*Framework:* 2019 California Arts Standards for Public Schools (Visual Arts and Music); 2020 CA Arts Education Framework  
*Time:* 30 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Line, Shape and Observational Drawing (weeks 1–4)
Artists use line and shape to record what they observe and imagine.

- Week 1: Kinds of line
- Week 2: Contour and observational drawing
- Week 3: Geometric and organic shapes
- Week 4: Imaginative drawing

### Unit 2: Rhythm and Meter (weeks 5–8)
Music is organized in steady beats and rhythmic patterns we can read, write and create.

- Week 5: Steady beat and rhythm
- Week 6: Reading rhythms
- Week 7: Half notes and meter
- Week 8: Rhythm composition

### Unit 3: Color and Painting (weeks 9–12)
Artists mix and choose colors to create mood and meaning.

- Week 9: The color wheel
- Week 10: Warm and cool colors
- Week 11: Tints, shades and value
- Week 12: Color and musical mood

### Unit 4: Melody, Pitch and Singing (weeks 13–17)
Melodies move up, down and stay the same, and we can read and sing them from notation.

- Week 13: High and low pitch
- Week 14: Solfege and hand signs
- Week 15: The treble staff
- Week 16: Singing expressively
- Week 17: Melody performance

### Unit 5: Printmaking, Texture and Pattern (weeks 18–20)
Printmaking repeats images to create pattern and texture.

- Week 18: Texture
- Week 19: Pattern and printmaking
- Week 20: Cultural textiles and prints

### Unit 6: Music of California and the World (weeks 21–24)
Music reflects the people, places and purposes that create it.

- Week 21: Instrument families and timbre
- Week 22: Music of California
- Week 23: Music around the world
- Week 24: Folk song performance

### Unit 7: Sculpture and Form (weeks 25–27)
Sculptors build three-dimensional forms that we can see from all sides.

- Week 25: Form and clay
- Week 26: Assemblage sculpture
- Week 27: Model of an everyday place

### Unit 8: Composing and Performing (weeks 28–31)
Composers organize and revise musical ideas to share with an audience.

- Week 28: Improvising melodies
- Week 29: Composing a song
- Week 30: Revising music
- Week 31: Performing original music

### Unit 9: Art and Community: Exhibition and Concert (weeks 32–36)
Art tells stories of people and places, and sharing art builds community.

- Week 32: Art tells stories
- Week 33: Community mural
- Week 34: Preparing an exhibition
- Week 35: Concert preparation
- Week 36: Art show and concert

## Standards

- **MU:Cn10.1.3** Demonstrate how interests, knowledge and skills relate to personal choices and intent when creating, performing and responding to music.
- **MU:Cn11.1.3** Demonstrate understanding of relationships between music and the other arts, other disciplines, varied contexts and daily life.
- **MU:Cr1.1.3** Improvise rhythmic and melodic ideas and describe connection to specific purpose and context.
- **MU:Cr2.1.3** Demonstrate selected and organized musical ideas for an improvisation, arrangement or composition; use standard and/or iconic notation to document them.
- **MU:Cr3.1.3** Evaluate, refine and document revisions to personal musical ideas, applying teacher-provided and collaboratively developed criteria and feedback.
- **MU:Cr3.2.3** Present the final version of personal created music to others and describe connection to expressive intent.
- **MU:Pr4.1.3** Demonstrate and explain how personal interest in, knowledge about, and purpose of varied musical selections inform choices.
- **MU:Pr4.2.3** Demonstrate understanding of the structure and elements of music (rhythm, pitch, form) in music selected for performance; read and perform rhythmic and melodic patterns using iconic or standard notation.
- **MU:Pr4.3.3** Demonstrate and describe how intent is conveyed through expressive qualities (dynamics, tempo).
- **MU:Pr5.1.3** Apply teacher-provided and collaboratively developed criteria and feedback to evaluate and refine performances.
- **MU:Pr6.1.3** Perform music with expression and technical accuracy, demonstrating performance decorum and audience etiquette appropriate for the context and venue.
- **MU:Re7.1.3** Demonstrate and describe how selected music connects to and is influenced by specific interests, experiences or purposes.
- **MU:Re7.2.3** Demonstrate and describe how a response to music can be informed by the structure, the use of the elements of music and context (personal and social).
- **MU:Re8.1.3** Demonstrate and describe how the expressive qualities (dynamics, tempo, timbre) are used in performers' and personal interpretations to reflect expressive intent.
- **MU:Re9.1.3** Evaluate musical works and performances, applying established criteria.
- **VA:Cn10.1.3** Develop a work of art based on observations of surroundings.
- **VA:Cn11.1.3** Recognize that responses to art change depending on knowledge of the time and place in which it was made.
- **VA:Cr1.1.3** Elaborate on an imaginative idea.
- **VA:Cr1.2.3** Apply knowledge of available resources, tools and technologies to investigate personal ideas through the art-making process.
- **VA:Cr2.1.3** Create personally satisfying artwork using a variety of artistic processes and materials.
- **VA:Cr2.2.3** Demonstrate an understanding of the safe and proficient use of materials, tools and equipment for a variety of artistic processes.
- **VA:Cr2.3.3** Individually or collaboratively construct representations, diagrams or maps of places that are part of everyday life.
- **VA:Cr3.1.3** Elaborate visual information by adding details in an artwork to enhance emerging meaning.
- **VA:Pr4.1.3** Investigate and discuss possibilities and limitations of spaces, including electronic, for exhibiting artwork.
- **VA:Pr5.1.3** Identify exhibit space and prepare works of art, including artists' statements, for presentation.
- **VA:Pr6.1.3** Identify and explain how and where different cultures record and illustrate stories and history of life through art.
- **VA:Re7.1.3** Speculate about processes an artist uses to create a work of art.
- **VA:Re7.2.3** Determine messages communicated by an image.
- **VA:Re8.1.3** Interpret art by analyzing use of media to create subject matter, characteristics of form and mood.
- **VA:Re9.1.3** Evaluate an artwork based on given criteria.
