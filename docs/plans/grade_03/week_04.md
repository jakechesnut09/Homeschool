## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Abstract nouns* (90 min): Learn abstract nouns (kindness, courage, friendship) and identify the abstract noun each fable teaches about.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Counting up* (60 min): Find differences like 603 − 278 by counting up on an open number line.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Marble run plan* (40 min): Plan a cardboard marble run that makes a marble travel 1 meter and stop in a cup.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Then and now* (30 min): Compare historic and current photos of your region from Calisphere.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Brainstorm* (30 min): Combine two animals into an imaginary creature and list where it lives, what it eats and its special power.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Locomotor review* (30 min): Practice walk, run, hop, skip, gallop, slide and leap across a yard.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Ad tricks* (20 min): Analyze 2 cereal boxes for tricks (cartoon characters, prizes, bright colors).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Edit* (90 min): Edit your narrative for capitals, end punctuation and plurals; practice prefixes un- and re- in spelling.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Compensation* (60 min): Use friendly-number strategies (e.g., 502 − 199 = 503 − 200) for 10 problems.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Build* (40 min): Build the marble run using tape and cardboard tubes.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Dams and aqueducts* (30 min): Learn about a California dam or aqueduct and its effects.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Sketch* (30 min): Sketch the creature using basic shapes first, then contour lines.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Pathways* (30 min): Move in straight, curved and zigzag pathways around cones.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Commercials* (20 min): Watch 2 food commercials and identify who the target audience is and what they promise.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Ending* (90 min): Write a satisfying ending that reflects on the lesson learned, then publish a final copy.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Standard algorithm* (60 min): Model decomposing a hundred with blocks, then subtract with the algorithm, including across zeros.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Test* (40 min): Test and record where the marble fails; identify the force causing each failure.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Farming* (30 min): Explore how irrigation changed the Central Valley.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Elaborate* (30 min): Add a detailed habitat background and at least 3 patterns or textures.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Transitions* (30 min): Combine run-to-leap and skip-to-slide sequences.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Healthy ad* (20 min): Design an ad that makes a vegetable appealing.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Fluency* (90 min): Practice reading a fable aloud 3 times for expression and record yourself.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Check with addition* (60 min): Solve 8 subtraction problems and check each with the inverse addition; find patterns in an addition table.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Improve* (40 min): Modify the design and retest, recording changes.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Cause and effect* (30 min): Make a chart of human changes to the land and their effects.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Final* (30 min): Finish the drawing with colored pencil, layering light to dark.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Obstacle course* (30 min): Complete an obstacle course with transitions.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Share* (20 min): Present the ad and explain the techniques used.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Unit 1 assessment* (90 min): Read a new fable and answer questions on moral and character, take the un-/re- spelling test, and read your narrative aloud.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a subtraction quiz with word problems and choose the most efficient strategy for each.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Unit 1 assessment* (40 min): Complete the unit quiz on balanced/unbalanced forces and motion patterns.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write about one positive and one negative environmental change.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Unit 1 check* (30 min): Self-assess with a line/shape/detail checklist and clap the syllable rhythm of your creature's name.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Show a 4-skill locomotor sequence.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write how ads might change what a kid wants to eat and how to think it through.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Publishing a personal narrative
- Objective: Edit and publish a narrative
- Objective: Use abstract nouns
- Objective: Present a story orally
- Vocabulary: abstract noun, edit, publish, closure
- Materials: editing checklist, dictionary
- Assessment: Unit 1 reading assessment; published narrative scored with rubric

### Math 3: Subtraction within 1,000
- Objective: Subtract 3-digit numbers with and without decomposing
- Objective: Relate addition and subtraction
- Vocabulary: difference, decompose, regroup, inverse
- Materials: base-ten blocks, open number line
- Assessment: Subtraction quiz including across-zero problems

### Science 3: Forces and motion design challenge
- Objective: Apply force concepts to a design
- Objective: Test and improve a prototype
- Vocabulary: prototype, criteria, improve
- Materials: cardboard, straws, tape, marbles
- Assessment: Unit 1 quiz; marble run testing log

### Continuity and Change (Local History): People change the land
- Objective: Trace how people have modified the local environment
- Vocabulary: modify, dam, aqueduct, agriculture, urban
- Materials: Calisphere photographs, then-and-now photos
- Assessment: Cause/effect chart and paragraph

### Art & Music 3: Imaginative drawing
- Objective: Elaborate on an imaginative idea
- Objective: Connect rhythm to words
- Vocabulary: imagination, elaborate, background
- Materials: sketchbook, colored pencils
- Assessment: Imaginative drawing with self-assessment checklist

### PE 3: Locomotor transitions
- Objective: Combine locomotor skills smoothly
- Vocabulary: gallop, slide, skip, leap, transition
- Materials: cones, music
- Assessment: Locomotor sequence

### Health 3: Food advertising
- Objective: Explain how advertising influences food choices
- Vocabulary: advertising, persuade, slogan, influence
- Materials: cereal boxes, recorded commercials
- Assessment: Healthy food ad and explanation
