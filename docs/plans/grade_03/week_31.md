## Day 1 — Monday (school day 151, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Note cards* (90 min): Take notes in your own words on note cards from source 1.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Sort shapes* (60 min): Sort shape cards by sides, angles and parallel sides and name each sort.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Hazards* (40 min): Read SciJinks pages and list 6 weather-related hazards with a sentence describing each.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Abraham Lincoln* (30 min): Read the Gettysburg Address and discuss its message of 'a new birth of freedom.'
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse your song 3 times with accompaniment.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Kickball rules* (30 min): Learn kickball rules (fair/foul, outs, innings) and practice rolling pitches.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Helpers* (20 min): List health helpers (doctor, nurse, dentist, pharmacist, school nurse, optometrist) and what each does.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 152, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Sort notes* (90 min): Sort note cards into categories (location, plants and animals, history, visiting).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Quadrilateral family* (60 min): Make a chart showing how squares, rectangles and rhombuses are all quadrilaterals.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *California hazards* (40 min): Research flooding, drought and wildfire in California, including a recent event.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Martin Luther King Jr.* (30 min): Read an excerpt of 'I Have a Dream' and list the dreams he describes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Program notes* (30 min): Write program notes explaining your song's meaning and how you made it.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Kicking for placement* (30 min): Practice kicking a rolled ball to open spaces in the field.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Interview* (20 min): Interview a health worker (in person or by phone) with 5 prepared questions.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 153, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Compare sources* (90 min): Compare key points from two sources on your topic in a chart.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Geoboard builds* (60 min): Build quadrilaterals on a geoboard or dot paper that are not rectangles, rhombuses or squares.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Impacts* (40 min): Make a chart of each hazard's impacts on people, homes, animals and land.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Anne Hutchinson* (30 min): Learn how Anne Hutchinson stood up for religious freedom.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Perform* (30 min): Perform the premiere for family, reading your program notes first.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Base running* (30 min): Practice running bases, rounding first base and tagging up, with timed runs.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Pharmacy* (20 min): Visit a pharmacy counter and learn how pharmacists keep medicine safe.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 154, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Context clues* (90 min): Use context clues to figure out unknown words in your sources; review affixes.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Always, sometimes, never* (60 min): Discuss statements like 'A square is a rhombus' and justify.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Preparation* (40 min): Create a family emergency kit checklist and evacuation plan for the hazard most likely where you live.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Hero report* (30 min): Write a one-page report on a chosen hero with a portrait.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Evaluate* (30 min): Watch a recording and score it on criteria (steady beat, accurate pitch, expression).
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Game day* (30 min): Play a 3-inning kickball game, cheering for both teams with specific encouragement.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Public health* (20 min): Learn how public health workers keep water, food and restaurants safe.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 155, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a paragraph on what two sources agree and disagree on.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a shape attributes quiz.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Explain in a paragraph how one hazard affects a community and how people prepare.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Unit 7 assessment* (30 min): Complete the unit test on symbols, documents and heroes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Unit 8 check* (30 min): Write a reflection on the whole composing process.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a game play check on kicking, fielding and sportsmanship.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a 'health helpers' booklet with one page per helper.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Taking notes and comparing sources
- Objective: Take notes and sort evidence into categories
- Objective: Compare two texts on the same topic
- Objective: Use context clues
- Vocabulary: notes, category, paraphrase, context clue
- Materials: note cards, sources
- Assessment: Notes and source comparison paragraph

### Math 3: Quadrilaterals and attributes
- Objective: Classify quadrilaterals by attributes
- Objective: Recognize shared attributes across categories
- Vocabulary: quadrilateral, rhombus, rectangle, square, parallel, right angle
- Materials: geoboard or dot paper, shape cards
- Assessment: Quadrilateral classification quiz

### Science 3: Weather-related hazards
- Objective: Identify weather-related hazards and their impacts on people
- Vocabulary: hazard, flood, drought, wildfire, hurricane, tornado
- Materials: NOAA SciJinks, news articles, chart paper
- Assessment: Hazard impact chart and paragraph

### Continuity and Change (Local History): American heroes: leaders for equality
- Objective: Describe heroes who advanced freedom and equality
- Objective: Analyze speeches
- Vocabulary: civil rights, equality, speech, religious freedom
- Materials: Gettysburg Address text, 'I Have a Dream' excerpt
- Assessment: Unit 7 test; hero report

### Art & Music 3: Performing original music
- Objective: Present original music
- Objective: Evaluate performance with criteria
- Vocabulary: premiere, program notes, criteria
- Materials: song, recording device
- Assessment: Performance and reflection

### PE 3: Kickball and base games
- Objective: Play base games
- Vocabulary: base, out, inning
- Materials: ball, bases
- Assessment: Game play check

### Health 3: Health helpers
- Objective: Identify health care professionals and community health helpers
- Vocabulary: doctor, nurse, dentist, pharmacist, public health
- Materials: interview questions
- Assessment: Health helpers booklet
