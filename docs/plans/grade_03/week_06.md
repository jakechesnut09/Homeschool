## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 6–7* (90 min): Read chapters 6–7 and explain how Wilbur learns his fate and how it changes the plot.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Equal groups* (60 min): Make equal groups with counters on plates and write repeated addition and multiplication sentences.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Balloon charge* (40 min): Rub a balloon on hair and test whether it attracts tissue paper bits.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Tribal map* (30 min): Locate your region's original peoples on a California Indian territories map (e.g., Ohlone, Chumash, Miwok, Tongva, Kumeyaay, Yokuts).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Notes* (30 min): Learn quarter notes ('ta') and paired eighth notes ('ti-ti') and read 8 flash cards aloud.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Static balance* (30 min): Hold balances on 1, 2, 3 and 4 body parts for 5 seconds.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Meal plan* (20 min): Plan a balanced dinner for your family using all 5 food groups.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Verb tenses* (90 min): Write sentences about the novel in past, present and future tense; spell -ful and -less words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Groups in pictures* (60 min): Find equal groups in photos (egg cartons, window panes) and write a multiplication equation for each.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Rolling can* (40 min): Use a charged balloon to roll an empty aluminum can without touching it.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Land acknowledgment* (30 min): Learn whose land you live on and write a land acknowledgment.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Rests* (30 min): Add the quarter rest and clap rhythms where the rest is a silent open-hand gesture.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Line walk* (30 min): Walk forward, backward and sideways on a tape line or curb.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Shopping list* (20 min): Write a shopping list and estimate cost.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 8–9* (90 min): Read chapters 8–9 and explain how each chapter builds on the last in a plot chart.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Skip counting* (60 min): Skip count by 2s, 5s and 10s on a hundreds chart and connect skip counts to products.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Two balloons* (40 min): Charge two balloons and observe repulsion; record observations.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Living cultures* (30 min): Visit a local tribe's official website to learn about its present-day community.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Read* (30 min): Read and clap 8 four-beat rhythm cards, then play them on a bucket drum.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Partner balance* (30 min): Create partner counterbalances.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Cook* (20 min): Help prepare the planned meal, practicing safe kitchen habits.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Story plan* (90 min): Plan an original animal story with a story map (characters, setting, problem, events, solution).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Story problems* (60 min): Draw pictures to solve 6 equal-group story problems like '4 bags with 6 apples each.'
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Simulation* (40 min): Explore the PhET 'Balloons and Static Electricity' simulation and draw charges.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Language* (30 min): Learn a few words from a local tribal language if available.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Dictation* (30 min): Listen to 5 clapped rhythms and write each one with stick notation.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Balance sequence* (30 min): Create a 3-balance sequence.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Family message* (20 min): Make a poster encouraging your family to eat a rainbow of fruits and vegetables.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 30, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a paragraph on how Charlotte's plan begins and take a verb tense quiz.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Match 10 pictures to equations and write a multiplication story for 3 × 7.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Write 3 testable cause-and-effect questions about static electricity and answer one.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write facts about the local nation then and now.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Clap a 6-card rhythm quiz with no mistakes.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Perform the sequence.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Unit 1 check* (20 min): Review your goal tracker and take a quiz on food groups, labels and activity.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Charlotte's Web, chapters 6–10
- Objective: Explain how characters' actions move the plot
- Objective: Use simple verb tenses
- Objective: Plan a narrative
- Vocabulary: plot, chapter, past tense, future tense
- Materials: Charlotte's Web, story map
- Assessment: Verb tense quiz; plot paragraph

### Math 3: Equal groups and multiplication
- Objective: Represent multiplication as equal groups
- Objective: Write multiplication equations
- Vocabulary: factor, product, equal groups, multiply
- Materials: counters, cups, paper plates
- Assessment: Picture-equation matching check

### Science 3: Static electricity
- Objective: Investigate static electric forces
- Objective: Ask cause-and-effect questions
- Vocabulary: static electricity, charge, attract, repel
- Materials: balloons, tissue paper, comb, aluminum can
- Assessment: Questions and observation log

### Continuity and Change (Local History): First peoples of our region
- Objective: Identify the American Indian nations of the local region
- Objective: Locate homelands on a map
- Vocabulary: tribe, nation, homeland, sovereign
- Materials: California tribal territories map, local tribe website
- Assessment: Facts summary

### Art & Music 3: Reading rhythms
- Objective: Read and write quarter notes, eighth notes and quarter rests
- Vocabulary: quarter note, eighth notes, quarter rest, ta, ti-ti
- Materials: rhythm flash cards, whiteboard
- Assessment: Rhythm reading and dictation quiz

### PE 3: Balance
- Objective: Hold balances and move along lines
- Vocabulary: balance, base of support, center of gravity
- Materials: tape line or low beam, cushions
- Assessment: Balance sequence

### Health 3: Planning balanced meals
- Objective: Plan and prepare a balanced meal
- Objective: Encourage others to eat healthily
- Vocabulary: balanced meal, portion, recipe
- Materials: recipe, kitchen tools
- Assessment: Unit 1 quiz; meal plan; goal tracker review
