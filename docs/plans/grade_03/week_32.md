## Day 1 — Monday (school day 156, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Outline* (90 min): Organize notes into a report outline.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Measure around* (60 min): Use string and a ruler to find the perimeter of books, a table and a picture frame.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Solutions* (40 min): Read about levees, lightning rods, storm shelters and fire breaks and how each reduces damage.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Resources* (30 min): Sort picture cards into natural, human and capital resources.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Rivera murals* (30 min): Look at a Diego Rivera mural and identify the story it tells about workers.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Strength* (30 min): Retest curl-ups and 90-degree push-ups and record in the fitness log.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Pollution* (20 min): Learn how air and water pollution can affect breathing and health.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 157, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Draft* (90 min): Draft introduction and two body paragraphs.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Polygons* (60 min): Find perimeters of polygons on grid paper by adding side lengths.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Compare* (40 min): Compare 3 flood solutions in a table by cost, effectiveness and effects on nature.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Goods and services* (30 min): Sort 20 local jobs into producers of goods and providers of services.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Chumash rock art* (30 min): View images of Chumash rock paintings and discuss what they may mean and why they must be protected.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Flexibility* (30 min): Retest sit-and-reach and shoulder stretch after a proper warm-up.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Air quality* (20 min): Check the daily Air Quality Index on AirNow for a week and decide which days are good for outdoor play.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 158, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Draft* (90 min): Finish the body and conclusion; add a map with caption.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unknown side* (60 min): Find a missing side length when the perimeter is known.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Flood model* (40 min): Build a sand-and-pan river model, pour water to flood a 'town,' then test a clay levee.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Local producer* (30 min): Visit or research a local farm, bakery or factory and list the resources it uses.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Context* (30 min): Discuss how knowing when and where an artwork was made changes your response to it.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Endurance* (30 min): Retest the timed half-mile walk/run or PACER.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Trash audit* (20 min): With gloves, sort one day of household trash into landfill, recycling and compost and weigh each.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 159, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Affixes* (90 min): Determine meanings of words formed with affixes in the report (unprotected, rebuild); review homographs.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fence problems* (60 min): Solve fencing word problems for a dog pen and garden.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Claim* (40 min): Write a claim about which flood solution has the most merit for a riverside town, with evidence from the model.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Production chart* (30 min): Make a flow chart showing how a local product is made from resources to store.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Plan mural* (30 min): Sketch a mural plan showing important people and places in your community.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Compare* (30 min): Graph baseline vs. current scores for each test.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Reduce plan* (20 min): Write a family plan to reduce, reuse and recycle 3 items.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 160, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Self-check the draft against the research rubric.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a perimeter quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Drafting the research report
- Objective: Write an informative report from notes
- Objective: Use affixes to determine meaning
- Objective: Use maps and photos
- Vocabulary: introduction, conclusion, caption
- Materials: notes, maps
- Assessment: Report draft with rubric self-check

### Math 3: Perimeter
- Objective: Find perimeter of polygons
- Objective: Find unknown side lengths
- Vocabulary: perimeter, polygon, side length
- Materials: ruler, string, grid paper
- Assessment: Perimeter quiz

### Science 3: Design solutions for hazards
- Objective: Evaluate the merit of design solutions that reduce hazard impacts
- Vocabulary: levee, lightning rod, storm shelter, fire break, merit
- Materials: baking pan, sand or soil, clay, water, articles
- Assessment: Claim about hazard solution with model evidence

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Resources and production
- Objective: Describe natural, human and capital resources used by local producers
- Vocabulary: natural resource, human resource, capital resource, goods, services
- Materials: local producer research, resource cards
- Assessment: Production flow chart

### Art & Music 3: Art tells stories
- Objective: Explain how cultures record history through art
- Objective: Consider time and place
- Vocabulary: mural, narrative art, rock art, context
- Materials: Diego Rivera mural images, Chumash rock art images (Calisphere)
- Assessment: Mural sketch

### PE 3: Fitness reassessment
- Objective: Retest fitness
- Vocabulary: progress, goal
- Materials: fitness log
- Assessment: Fitness progress comparison

### Health 3: Environment and health
- Objective: Describe how the environment affects health and how to protect it
- Vocabulary: pollution, air quality index, recycle, compost
- Materials: AirNow website, household trash
- Assessment: AQI log and environment paragraph
