## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Persephone* (90 min): Read the Persephone myth and explain how it explained the seasons.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Tile and count* (60 min): Tile rectangles and show that rows × columns gives the same total as counting.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Plant experiment setup* (40 min): Place one plant in sunlight and one in a dark closet; predict differences.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Founding* (30 min): Research when and why your town was founded (railroad, farming, port, mining) using the historical society website.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Dynamics* (30 min): Sing 'This Land Is Your Land' with forte, piano, crescendo and decrescendo.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Crossover* (30 min): Learn a low crossover dribble from hand to hand and practice 3 sets of 20.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *What is bullying* (20 min): Read the KidsHealth article on bullying and distinguish bullying (repeated, power imbalance) from ordinary conflict.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 77, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Illustrations* (90 min): Compare two illustrations of Icarus and explain how each shows mood.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Multiply sides* (60 min): Measure side lengths of rectangles drawn on grid paper and multiply to find area.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Examples* (40 min): Read about flamingos' color from diet and hydrangea color from soil.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Founders* (30 min): Read about 2 founding individuals or families and their contributions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Tempo* (30 min): Sing a lullaby at a slow tempo and a march at a fast one and discuss why.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Zigzag* (30 min): Dribble a zigzag cone pattern, switching hands at each cone.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Types* (20 min): Sort scenario cards into physical, verbal, social and cyberbullying.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 78, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Icarus* (90 min): Read the Icarus myth and discuss nonliteral phrases like 'flying too close to the sun.'
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unknown side* (60 min): Solve problems like 'A rug has area 24 sq ft and width 4 ft; how long is it?'
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Measure plants* (40 min): Measure and observe plants and record height and color.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Old maps* (30 min): Compare a historic map of your town (Sanborn fire insurance map or USGS topo) with a current map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Compare performances* (30 min): Compare two recorded versions of the same song and describe the differences in expression.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Speed* (30 min): Change dribbling speed when a family member calls 'red light' or 'green light.'
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Upstander* (20 min): Practice 4 upstander responses (speak up, stay with the target, walk away together, get help).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 79, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Capitalize titles* (90 min): Write the titles of all myths and books read so far with correct capitalization; spell homophones (their/there/they're).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Distributive area* (60 min): Split a 7 × 8 rectangle into 7 × 5 and 7 × 3 and add the areas.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Human examples* (40 min): Discuss how exercise and diet can influence traits.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Timeline* (30 min): Begin a community timeline with founding events and dates.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Expression plan* (30 min): Mark dynamics on a song sheet to match the meaning of its words.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Dribble tag* (30 min): Play dribble tag, keeping your dribble while tagging and dodging.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Reporting* (20 min): Practice telling a trusted adult the who, what, where and when of a bullying incident.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 80, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Answer questions about both myths and listen to a family member's retelling, asking 3 questions.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete 8 area problems using multiplication and the distributive property.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Write evidence that environment influences traits from the plant experiment.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write a paragraph explaining why your community began where it did.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Perform the song with your marked expression for family.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a dribble skills check: crossover, zigzag and speed change.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write and sign an upstander pledge.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Myths: Persephone and Icarus
- Objective: Explain how myths explain nature
- Objective: Explain how illustrations show mood
- Objective: Capitalize titles
- Vocabulary: season, mood, nonliteral
- Materials: Persephone and Icarus retellings (library), art prints
- Assessment: Title capitalization check; myth questions

### Math 3: Area and multiplication
- Objective: Relate tiling to multiplying side lengths
- Objective: Find area of rectangles with formulas and arrays
- Vocabulary: length, width, side length, rows
- Materials: tiles, ruler, grid paper
- Assessment: Area problems worksheet

### Science 3: Environment and traits
- Objective: Gather evidence that environment influences traits
- Vocabulary: environment, influence, nutrients
- Materials: two plants, sunny and dark spots, ruler
- Assessment: Plant experiment report

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Why our community was founded
- Objective: Trace why the community was established and who founded it
- Vocabulary: founder, establish, railroad, incorporate
- Materials: local historical society website, historic town map
- Assessment: Founding paragraph

### Art & Music 3: Singing expressively
- Objective: Use dynamics and tempo to convey intent
- Objective: Compare performances
- Vocabulary: dynamics, tempo, crescendo, decrescendo
- Materials: song sheets, two recordings of one song
- Assessment: Expressive performance

### PE 3: Dribbling with changes
- Objective: Change direction and speed while dribbling
- Vocabulary: crossover, speed, direction
- Materials: ball, cones
- Assessment: Dribble skills check

### Health 3: Bullying and upstanders
- Objective: Describe bullying and ways to respond to and report it
- Vocabulary: bullying, bystander, upstander, report
- Materials: scenario cards, KidsHealth bullying article
- Assessment: Upstander pledge
