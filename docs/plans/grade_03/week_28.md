## Day 1 — Monday (school day 136, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 19–21* (90 min): Read chapters 19–21 and track the journey south on a map of Oz.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Gram and kilogram* (60 min): Hold a paper clip (about 1 g) and a 1 kg bag of rice as benchmarks; estimate 8 objects.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Weather vs. climate* (40 min): Sort 10 statements as weather (today's rain) or climate (rainy winters every year) and explain the difference.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Flag* (30 min): Learn the history and meaning of the stars and stripes and the Pledge of Allegiance.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Question-answer* (30 min): Play a 4-beat melodic 'answer' to a parent's melodic 'question' on a keyboard app.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Hula hoop pass* (30 min): Pass a hula hoop around a hand-holding family circle without letting go, timing each round.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Germs* (20 min): Learn the difference between bacteria and viruses and how they spread.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 137, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 22–24* (90 min): Finish the novel and recount the main events in a summary.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Weigh objects* (60 min): Use a kitchen scale to measure the mass of 8 objects and compare to estimates.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Climate zones* (40 min): Color a world map by climate zone (polar, temperate, tropical, desert) using a NASA Climate Kids reference.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Bald eagle* (30 min): Learn why the bald eagle was chosen as a national symbol and how it was saved from extinction.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Pentatonic* (30 min): Improvise freely on black keys (pentatonic) over a steady drum beat.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Blanket flip* (30 min): Work as a group to flip a blanket over while everyone stays standing on it.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Glitter germs* (20 min): Put lotion and glitter 'germs' on hands, shake hands with family, and see how far they spread.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 138, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Spoken vs. written* (90 min): Rewrite informal spoken lines into formal written English and vice versa; spell contractions.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Balance scale* (60 min): Build a hanger balance and compare masses of two objects.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Compare cities* (40 min): Compare yearly temperature and rainfall for Anchorage, Singapore and Cairo in a table.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Statue of Liberty* (30 min): Learn the history of the Statue of Liberty and read lines from Emma Lazarus's 'The New Colossus.'
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Story music* (30 min): Improvise music for a story scene (storm, sneaking, celebration).
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Balloon keep-up* (30 min): Keep 3 balloons in the air as a team for 60 seconds using only assigned body parts.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Handwashing* (20 min): Practice the CDC 5-step handwashing method for 20 seconds, then test with glitter.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 139, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Plan* (90 min): Plan a new Oz adventure for Dorothy with a new character and setting.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Mass problems* (60 min): Solve one-step word problems about mass of fruit and packages.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *People adapt* (40 min): Research how people in one climate region dress, build homes and farm.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Liberty Bell* (30 min): Learn the story of the Liberty Bell and the words inscribed on it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Record* (30 min): Record your 3 best improvisations.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Teamwork talk* (30 min): Play 'minefield' (guiding a blindfolded partner through obstacles with words) and discuss trust.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Cough and sneeze* (20 min): Spray a water bottle 'sneeze' at paper to see droplet spread, then practice covering with an elbow.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 140, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Complete an end-of-novel quiz.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit 7 assessment* (60 min): Complete the Unit 7 test on time, liquid volume and mass.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Write a description of one climate region with at least 3 data facts.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Make a symbols booklet with a picture and the ideal each symbol represents.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Listen back and choose a favorite idea to develop into a song.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a timed team challenge and name the encouraging words that helped.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a handwashing sign for the bathroom with all 5 steps.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, chapters 19–24
- Objective: Recount the novel
- Objective: Recognize spoken vs. written English
- Objective: Plan a new adventure
- Vocabulary: formal, informal, sequel
- Materials: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz
- Assessment: End-of-novel quiz

### Math 3: Mass
- Objective: Estimate and measure mass in grams and kilograms
- Objective: Solve mass problems
- Vocabulary: mass, gram, kilogram, balance, scale
- Materials: kitchen scale, paper clips, household objects
- Assessment: Unit 7 test

### Science 3: Climates of the world
- Objective: Distinguish weather from climate
- Objective: Describe climates in different world regions
- Vocabulary: climate, weather, region, polar, tropical, temperate
- Materials: world map, colored pencils, NASA Climate Kids
- Assessment: Climate region description

### Continuity and Change (Local History): National symbols
- Objective: Know the histories of national symbols and what ideals they represent
- Vocabulary: symbol, flag, bald eagle, Liberty Bell, Pledge of Allegiance
- Materials: symbol images from Library of Congress
- Assessment: Symbols booklet

### Art & Music 3: Improvising melodies
- Objective: Improvise melodic ideas for a purpose
- Vocabulary: improvise, question, answer, pentatonic
- Materials: xylophone or keyboard app
- Assessment: Improvisation recordings

### PE 3: Cooperative challenges
- Objective: Work cooperatively
- Vocabulary: cooperate, teamwork
- Materials: hula hoop, blanket
- Assessment: Team challenge

### Health 3: Germs and handwashing
- Objective: Explain how germs spread
- Objective: Practice handwashing and cough etiquette
- Vocabulary: germ, bacteria, virus, communicable disease
- Materials: glitter, lotion, soap, spray bottle
- Assessment: Handwashing sign
