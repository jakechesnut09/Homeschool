# PE 3 (Grade 3)

Third-grade physical education building locomotor, balance, jump rope, throwing, catching, dribbling, striking and dance skills through daily active practice. Students learn the components of health-related fitness, measure heart rate, complete baseline and end-of-year fitness checks, set goals, and practice cooperation, strategy and fair play in small-sided games.

*Framework:* California Physical Education Model Content Standards; 2009 CA Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 30 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Fitness Foundations (weeks 1–3)
Knowing our fitness helps us set goals to grow stronger and healthier.

- Week 1: Warm-up, cool-down and safety
- Week 2: Fitness components and heart rate
- Week 3: Baseline fitness assessment

### Unit 2: Locomotor Skills, Balance and Tumbling (weeks 4–7)
Controlled movement depends on balance, force and smooth transitions.

- Week 4: Locomotor transitions
- Week 5: Jumping and landing
- Week 6: Balance
- Week 7: Rolling and tumbling

### Unit 3: Jump Rope (weeks 8–10)
Rhythm and practice make jumping rope an energizing lifetime activity.

- Week 8: Single rope basics
- Week 9: Jump rope tricks
- Week 10: Long rope and routine

### Unit 4: Throwing and Catching (weeks 11–14)
Using the right cues improves accuracy and control.

- Week 11: Overhand throw cues
- Week 12: Catching
- Week 13: Throwing and catching on the move
- Week 14: Throwing games

### Unit 5: Dribbling with Hands and Feet (weeks 15–19)
Keeping control while moving lets us play invasion games well.

- Week 15: Hand dribbling
- Week 16: Dribbling with changes
- Week 17: Foot dribbling
- Week 18: Kicking and passing
- Week 19: Small-sided games

### Unit 6: Striking with Paddles and Bats (weeks 20–23)
Striking needs a ready position, eye on the object and follow-through.

- Week 20: Striking with paddles
- Week 21: Forehand striking
- Week 22: Batting
- Week 23: Striking games

### Unit 7: Rhythms and Dance (weeks 24–27)
Dance combines movement, rhythm and cultural tradition.

- Week 24: Rhythm and movement
- Week 25: Folk dances
- Week 26: Line dances
- Week 27: Creative dance

### Unit 8: Cooperative and Small-Sided Games (weeks 28–31)
Teamwork, strategy and fair play make games fun for everyone.

- Week 28: Cooperative challenges
- Week 29: Tag games and strategy
- Week 30: Net and wall games
- Week 31: Kickball and base games

### Unit 9: Fitness Check and Field Day (weeks 32–36)
Tracking progress shows how effort leads to improvement.

- Week 32: Fitness reassessment
- Week 33: Fuel and hydration
- Week 34: Field day prep
- Week 35: Field day
- Week 36: Summer fitness plan

## Standards

- **PE-3.1.1** Perform smooth transitions between sequential locomotor skills (e.g., running into a jump).
- **PE-3.1.10** Dribble a ball with the feet and kick a ball to a stationary or moving target.
- **PE-3.1.11** Strike an object with a paddle or racket using forehand motion, and strike a stationary ball with a bat.
- **PE-3.1.12** Perform simple folk, line and creative dances in time to music.
- **PE-3.1.2** Jump for distance and height, landing on both feet with hips, knees and ankles bent to absorb force.
- **PE-3.1.3** Balance on, move along and dismount from a line or low beam with control; hold balances on different bases of support.
- **PE-3.1.4** Perform a forward roll and other rolling actions safely with control.
- **PE-3.1.5** Jump a self-turned rope repeatedly using a variety of footwork patterns.
- **PE-3.1.6** Jump a long rope turned by others, entering and exiting.
- **PE-3.1.7** Throw an object overhand with accuracy and appropriate force toward a target.
- **PE-3.1.8** Catch thrown objects at different levels (above head, at waist, below waist) and while moving.
- **PE-3.1.9** Dribble a ball with the hand while walking and running, changing direction and speed.
- **PE-3.2.1** Explain the key elements of overhand throwing, catching, kicking and dribbling.
- **PE-3.2.2** Explain how force, levels, directions and pathways affect movement.
- **PE-3.2.3** Explain and apply simple offensive and defensive strategies (open space, guarding) in small-sided games.
- **PE-3.2.4** Explain how practice and feedback improve skill.
- **PE-3.3.1** Participate in moderate to vigorous physical activity for increasing periods of time.
- **PE-3.3.2** Demonstrate muscular strength and endurance (curl-ups, push-ups, plank).
- **PE-3.3.3** Demonstrate flexibility of major muscle groups (sit-and-reach, shoulder stretch).
- **PE-3.3.4** Demonstrate aerobic endurance (PACER, timed run/walk).
- **PE-3.3.5** Measure and record heart rate before, during and after activity.
- **PE-3.4.1** Identify the components of health-related fitness (aerobic capacity, muscular strength, endurance, flexibility, body composition).
- **PE-3.4.2** Explain how the heart, lungs and muscles respond to physical activity.
- **PE-3.4.3** Explain the purpose of warm-up and cool-down activities.
- **PE-3.4.4** Set a personal fitness goal and track progress.
- **PE-3.4.5** Explain how healthy food and water fuel physical activity.
- **PE-3.5.1** Work cooperatively with a partner or small group to complete tasks.
- **PE-3.5.2** Accept responsibility for one's actions and demonstrate fair play.
- **PE-3.5.3** Encourage others and give positive, specific feedback.
- **PE-3.5.4** Follow safety rules and use equipment properly.
