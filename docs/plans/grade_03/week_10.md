## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Text feature hunt* (90 min): Find headings, captions, bold words, glossary and index in an animal nonfiction book and explain each feature's job.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Tape diagrams* (60 min): Draw tape diagrams for 6 multiplication and division word problems.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Frogs* (40 min): Sequence the frog life cycle and describe changes from tadpole to adult.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Tribal council* (30 min): Read how a local tribe's council is elected and what decisions it makes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Sort* (30 min): Sort paint chips or magazine scraps into warm and cool color groups.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Long rope* (30 min): Enter, jump 5 times and exit a long rope turned by two family members (or rope tied to a fence).
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Brain* (20 min): Learn the brain's role as the control center and why helmets matter.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 47, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Main idea* (90 min): Read a ReadWorks passage on camouflage and identify the main idea and 3 supporting details.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Measurement problems* (60 min): Solve problems like 'A rope 32 feet long is cut into 4 equal pieces' using drawings.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Birds* (40 min): Examine an egg (shell, membrane, yolk) and diagram a chicken's life cycle.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Constitution* (30 min): Read short excerpts from a published tribal constitution and identify who can vote and what leaders do.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Picasso* (30 min): Look at Picasso's 'The Old Guitarist' and describe the mood the blue colors create.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Rhymes* (30 min): Jump to traditional rhymes like 'Teddy Bear, Teddy Bear' and 'Cinderella.'
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Reaction time* (20 min): Do the ruler-drop reaction test 5 times and record results.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 48, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Adjectives* (90 min): Identify adjectives in animal descriptions and write 5 descriptive sentences; spell change-y-to-i words (cry/cries).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Which operation?* (60 min): Sort 10 word problems by operation and justify each choice to a parent.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Mammals* (40 min): Compare a human and a dog life cycle using a timeline.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Sovereignty* (30 min): Discuss what it means that tribes are sovereign nations with their own governments.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Warm landscape* (30 min): Paint a simple landscape using only warm colors.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Routine plan* (30 min): Plan a 30-second single-rope routine to music using 4 tricks.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Senses test* (20 min): Do a blindfolded taste and smell test.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 49, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Choose an animal* (90 min): Select an animal for an informational report and write 5 questions you want to answer.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Problem writing* (60 min): Write a multiplication and a division story about a bake sale and solve them.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Compare* (40 min): Make a chart comparing life span and stages of 5 organisms.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Relationships* (30 min): Make a 3-column chart of tribal, state and federal government responsibilities.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Cool landscape* (30 min): Paint the same landscape again using only cool colors.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Practice* (30 min): Rehearse the routine 5 times, smoothing transitions between tricks.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Protect senses* (20 min): List ways to protect eyes and ears (sunglasses, volume limits).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 50, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Answer text-feature questions on a new passage and take the spelling check.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit 2 assessment* (60 min): Complete the Unit 2 test on the meaning of multiplication and division.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Answer questions about which stages all life cycles share.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Answer 6 questions about tribal government and explain one in a complete paragraph.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Display both paintings and write 3 sentences comparing their moods.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Perform the routine and note one trick to keep improving.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a senses chart with organ, job and protection.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Text features in informational texts
- Objective: Use text features to locate information
- Objective: Identify main idea
- Objective: Learn adjectives
- Vocabulary: adaptation, heading, caption, glossary, main idea
- Materials: ReadWorks animal passages, National Geographic Kids book (library)
- Assessment: Text feature check; main idea organizer

### Math 3: Multiplication and division word problems
- Objective: Solve equal groups, array and measurement word problems
- Objective: Represent with drawings and equations
- Vocabulary: measurement, equation, tape diagram
- Materials: tape diagram templates, ruler
- Assessment: Unit 2 test

### Science 3: Animal life cycles: frogs, birds, mammals
- Objective: Compare diverse animal life cycles
- Objective: Identify birth, growth, reproduction, death in all
- Vocabulary: amphibian, tadpole, hatch, mammal
- Materials: life cycle picture cards, egg
- Assessment: Life cycle comparison chart

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Tribal government today
- Objective: Describe tribal governments and constitutions and their relationship to state and federal governments
- Vocabulary: tribal council, constitution, sovereignty, federally recognized
- Materials: local tribe's government web page, chart paper
- Assessment: Government chart and questions

### Art & Music 3: Warm and cool colors
- Objective: Use warm and cool colors to create mood
- Objective: Interpret mood in artworks
- Vocabulary: warm, cool, mood
- Materials: paints, Picasso 'The Old Guitarist' image
- Assessment: Warm/cool landscape pair with comparison

### PE 3: Long rope and routine
- Objective: Jump a long rope
- Objective: Create a jump rope routine
- Vocabulary: long rope, enter, exit, routine
- Materials: long rope, music
- Assessment: Jump rope routine performance

### Health 3: Brain, nerves and senses
- Objective: Describe the nervous system and five senses
- Objective: Protect senses
- Vocabulary: brain, nerves, senses, reflex
- Materials: blindfold, food samples, ruler
- Assessment: Senses chart
