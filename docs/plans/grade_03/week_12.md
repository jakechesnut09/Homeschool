## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Report plan* (90 min): Organize notes into an outline with an introduction, 3 topic paragraphs and conclusion.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Threes* (60 min): Use 2s facts plus one more group to solve 3s facts with arrays.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Group examples* (40 min): Watch videos of wolf packs, fish schools and bee colonies and list benefits.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Cabrillo* (30 min): Read about Juan Rodríguez Cabrillo's 1542 voyage and trace his route along the California coast.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Listen and paint* (30 min): Paint freely while listening to Smetana's 'The Moldau,' letting colors follow the river music.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Self-toss* (30 min): Self-toss and catch a beanbag 20 times, then a ball, calling 'reach and give.'
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Feelings words* (20 min): Build a feelings word bank of 20 emotions.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Diagrams* (90 min): Study a labeled diagram of an animal and explain what it adds to the words; spell -tion and -sion words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fours as doubles* (60 min): Double 2s facts to find 4s facts and record in a table.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Ant observation* (40 min): Observe ants outdoors (or a video) and record how they work together.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Portolá and Crespí* (30 min): Read about the 1769 Portolá expedition and an excerpt from Father Crespí's diary describing the land.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Dynamics* (30 min): Learn forte (loud) and piano (soft) and sing a song using both.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Levels* (30 min): Catch tosses above the head (thumbs together) and below the waist (pinkies together).
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Body signals* (20 min): Identify where you feel anger, worry and excitement in your body.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Draft* (90 min): Draft introduction and first two paragraphs using linking words (also, another, and, but).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Break apart arrays* (60 min): Split arrays such as 6 × 7 into 5 × 7 + 1 × 7 and color each part.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Evidence chart* (40 min): Read articles and fill an evidence chart: hunting, defense, raising young, staying warm.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Early map* (30 min): Examine an early map of California from the Library of Congress and note what mapmakers got right and wrong (e.g., California as an island).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Contrast* (30 min): Paint two small pieces to a calm piece and an energetic piece of music.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Partner* (30 min): Play partner catch, taking a step back after each 3 successful catches.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Expressing* (20 min): Sort healthy and unhealthy ways to express anger.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Commas* (90 min): Practice commas in a series and in addresses by writing a letter to a zoo asking a question.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Sixes* (60 min): Use 5s plus one group and 3s doubled to learn 6s facts; play a fact war card game.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Fish school model* (40 min): Model a fish school vs. a lone fish in a predator tag game and record results.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Explorer card* (30 min): Make an explorer trading card with dates, route, goals and effects on local people.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Compare* (30 min): Explain how colors in each painting match the music's dynamics and tempo.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Moving* (30 min): Jog and catch tosses from a partner on the move.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Journal* (20 min): Write a feelings journal entry about your day.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 60, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Finish the report draft and read it to a family member for feedback on clarity.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Solve a mixed 3s/4s/6s quiz and explain one fact using the distributive property.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Summarize evidence for one animal group.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Present the explorer card and answer questions about the explorer's impact.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Unit 3 check* (30 min): Choose your best painting and score it on a color-use rubric.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Record your best streak of consecutive partner catches.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Role-play expressing a feeling using an I-message.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Writing an informational report
- Objective: Organize facts into paragraphs
- Objective: Use linking words
- Objective: Use images to understand text
- Vocabulary: topic sentence, linking words, diagram
- Materials: notes, report template
- Assessment: Report draft; letter with correct comma use

### Math 3: Facts of 3, 4, 6 and the distributive property
- Objective: Use doubling and the distributive property to find 3s, 4s, 6s
- Objective: Break apart arrays
- Vocabulary: distributive property, break apart, double
- Materials: grid paper, colored pencils
- Assessment: Mixed facts quiz with distributive explanation

### Science 3: Why animals live in groups
- Objective: Identify ways group living helps animals survive
- Objective: Gather evidence from texts and video
- Vocabulary: herd, pack, colony, school, survival
- Materials: animal videos, articles, evidence chart
- Assessment: Evidence chart

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Explorers along the California coast
- Objective: Research explorers who visited the region
- Objective: Analyze an early map
- Vocabulary: explorer, expedition, voyage, route
- Materials: Library of Congress early California maps, world map
- Assessment: Explorer trading card

### Art & Music 3: Color and musical mood
- Objective: Connect color and musical expression
- Objective: Evaluate artwork with criteria
- Vocabulary: mood, dynamics, expression, forte, piano
- Materials: paints, recording of Smetana's 'The Moldau'
- Assessment: Paintings with rubric self-evaluation

### PE 3: Catching
- Objective: Catch at different levels with soft hands
- Vocabulary: hands ready, thumbs together, pinkies together, give
- Materials: balls, beanbags
- Assessment: Catching streak record

### Health 3: Identifying feelings
- Objective: Identify feelings and healthy ways to express them
- Vocabulary: emotion, feeling, express, body signals
- Materials: feelings chart, journal
- Assessment: Feelings journal and role-play
