## Day 1 — Monday (school day 126, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 7–9* (90 min): Read chapters 7–9 and describe the Cowardly Lion's brave actions.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Nearest minute* (60 min): Read and set an analog clock to the nearest minute for 15 times.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Clouds* (40 min): Learn cumulus, stratus and cirrus clouds using SciJinks, then identify today's clouds and sketch them in the log.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *City government* (30 min): Look up your mayor or city manager and council members on the city website and list their jobs.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Nevelson* (30 min): Look at Louise Nevelson's assemblage sculptures and discuss how painting them one color unifies them.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Grapevine* (30 min): Learn the grapevine step right and left in 8 counts.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Effects* (20 min): Learn how alcohol slows the brain, affecting judgment, balance and reaction time.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 127, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Illustrations* (90 min): Study 3 Denslow illustrations and explain what they show about mood and character.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Elapsed on a number line* (60 min): Use an open number line to find elapsed time between start and end times.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Daily data* (40 min): Record temperature, rainfall, wind direction and cloud type at the same time each day.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Local services* (30 min): List services local government provides (police, fire, parks, libraries) and who pays for them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Plan* (30 min): Collect recycled materials and sketch a plan for a free-standing sculpture.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Cha-Cha Slide* (30 min): Learn and dance the Cha-Cha Slide.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Coordination* (20 min): Try walking a line while spinning in place (with an adult) to model impaired balance, then discuss.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 128, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Possessives* (90 min): Write singular and plural possessives (Dorothy's shoes, the witches' castle); spell soft c and g words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Start and end unknown* (60 min): Solve problems where the start time or end time is unknown.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Forecasts* (40 min): Compare your observations to the NOAA/National Weather Service forecast for your town and note matches.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *State government* (30 min): Learn about the governor, the legislature and the State Capitol in Sacramento.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Build* (30 min): Build the sculpture, testing balance as you add pieces.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Electric Slide* (30 min): Learn and dance the Electric Slide.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Growing bodies* (20 min): Discuss why alcohol is especially harmful to growing brains and why the legal age is 21.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 129, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 10–12* (90 min): Read chapters 10–12 and chart each character's wish and the Wizard's demand.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Daily schedule* (60 min): Record your day's schedule and calculate the length of 5 activities.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Data table* (40 min): Organize the week's measurements into a data table with units and calculate the week's high and low.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Council meeting* (30 min): Watch 15 minutes of a recorded city council meeting and note one decision made.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Paint* (30 min): Paint the whole sculpture one color to unify it.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Heart rate* (30 min): Dance 3 songs in a row, then measure heart rate and compare to rest.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Influences* (20 min): Identify alcohol messages in ads and media.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 130, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a possessives quiz and write a character trait paragraph.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a time and elapsed-time quiz.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Describe the week's weather pattern in 3 sentences using numbers from your table.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Make a comparison chart of local, state and national government.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Present the sculpture and view it from all sides with family.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Perform a full line dance without cues.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Answer 6 questions about alcohol's effects.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, chapters 7–12
- Objective: Explain how illustrations contribute to the story
- Objective: Form possessives
- Objective: Track character traits
- Vocabulary: illustration, possessive, apostrophe
- Materials: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz with W.W. Denslow illustrations
- Assessment: Possessives quiz; character trait paragraph

### Math 3: Time to the minute and elapsed time
- Objective: Tell time to the nearest minute
- Objective: Solve elapsed-time problems
- Vocabulary: elapsed time, minute, a.m., p.m., interval
- Materials: analog clock, number line
- Assessment: Time quiz

### Science 3: Collecting weather data
- Objective: Collect and record daily weather data
- Objective: Identify cloud types
- Vocabulary: data, table, cumulus, stratus, cirrus, forecast
- Materials: weather instruments, weather log, NOAA forecast page
- Assessment: Weather log and pattern description

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Local and state government
- Objective: Describe local and state government roles
- Vocabulary: mayor, city council, county, governor, legislature
- Materials: city website, California state government website
- Assessment: Government comparison chart

### Art & Music 3: Assemblage sculpture
- Objective: Create a sculpture from found and recycled materials
- Vocabulary: assemblage, recycled, balance, unity
- Materials: boxes, tubes, lids, tape, glue, one color of paint
- Assessment: Assemblage sculpture

### PE 3: Line dances
- Objective: Perform line dances with rhythm
- Objective: Monitor heart rate
- Vocabulary: line dance, grapevine, pivot
- Materials: music
- Assessment: Line dance performance

### Health 3: Alcohol
- Objective: Describe the effects of alcohol on the body and behavior
- Vocabulary: alcohol, brain, judgment, coordination, legal age
- Materials: brain diagram
- Assessment: Alcohol questions
