## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Script features* (90 min): Read a readers' theater script and identify cast list, scenes and stage directions.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fact fluency* (60 min): Complete a full multiplication and division fact assessment.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Final test* (40 min): Run 3 final wind and rain trials on the improved model and record results.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Human capital* (30 min): Define human capital as the skills and knowledge people bring to work.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Program* (30 min): Choose 4 songs from the year (including your original song) for a concert.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Field day 1* (30 min): Compete in the 50-yard dash, standing long jump and softball throw with family or co-op friends.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Poster* (20 min): Create a campaign poster with a catchy slogan and picture.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Write a script* (90 min): Convert a fable into a short script, turning narrative into dialogue.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Add/subtract review* (60 min): Solve mixed 3-digit add/subtract problems with estimation.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Data* (40 min): Make a bar graph comparing performance before and after improvements.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Skills* (30 min): List skills you have built in school this year and how each could be used in a job.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse the songs learned in units 4 and 6.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Field day 2* (30 min): Complete sack race, three-legged race and water-balloon toss stations.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Brochure* (20 min): Make a tri-fold brochure with facts and tips.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 173, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Rehearse* (90 min): Rehearse the script with expression and timing.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Problem solving* (60 min): Solve 5 multi-step word problems and explain thinking.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Poster* (40 min): Create a poster showing the problem, design, test data and improvements.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Career interview* (30 min): Interview a family member about the skills their job requires and how they learned them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Rehearse original* (30 min): Rehearse your original song with accompaniment.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Team relay* (30 min): Run a team obstacle relay that combines dribbling, jumping and throwing skills.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *PSA script* (20 min): Write a 1-minute PSA script.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 174, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Perform* (90 min): Perform for family and record it.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Math games day* (60 min): Play a fact board game and a target-number game to practice operations.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Present* (40 min): Present the solution and argue how well it meets the criteria and constraints.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Goals* (30 min): Set 2 learning goals for next year that build your human capital.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Dress rehearsal* (30 min): Run the full program in order with introductions.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Sportsmanship* (30 min): Make and present sportsmanship awards to teammates, naming specific kind actions.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Record* (20 min): Record the PSA video or audio.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 175, ~300 min)

- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a cumulative operations test.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Readers' theater
- Objective: Refer to parts of a drama using scene terms
- Objective: Perform with fluency and expression
- Vocabulary: script, scene, stage directions, cast
- Materials: readers' theater script adapted from a fable or Potter tale
- Assessment: Script and performance

### Math 3: Cumulative review: operations and fluency
- Objective: Demonstrate fluency with multiplication/division within 100 and add/subtract within 1,000
- Objective: Solve multi-step problems
- Vocabulary: fluency, strategy, reasonableness
- Materials: fact cards, review packet
- Assessment: Cumulative operations test

### Science 3: Engineering: improve and present
- Objective: Improve a design and argue for its merit
- Vocabulary: iterate, improve, merit, evidence
- Materials: model house, poster board
- Assessment: Unit 9 quiz; design presentation

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Human capital
- Objective: Relate learning in school to personal human capital
- Vocabulary: human capital, skill, career, education
- Materials: interview sheet
- Assessment: Unit 8 test

### Art & Music 3: Concert preparation
- Objective: Prepare a concert program
- Vocabulary: program, concert, dress rehearsal
- Materials: songs from the year
- Assessment: Concert program

### PE 3: Field day
- Objective: Participate in field day
- Vocabulary: sportsmanship
- Materials: field day equipment
- Assessment: Field day participation

### Health 3: Health promotion project: create
- Objective: Create accurate health promotion materials
- Vocabulary: poster, brochure, public service announcement
- Materials: poster board, paper, recording device
- Assessment: Campaign materials
