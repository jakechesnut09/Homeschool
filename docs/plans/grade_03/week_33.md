## Day 1 — Monday (school day 161, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Revise and edit* (90 min): Revise and edit the report with an adult.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Same area* (60 min): Build all rectangles with area 24 and record their perimeters.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Problem* (40 min): Define the challenge: protect a small model house from strong wind and heavy rain.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Label hunt* (30 min): Check labels on 20 household items and record where each was made.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Grid transfer* (30 min): Draw a grid on your sketch and the butcher paper to scale up the design.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Hydration* (30 min): Track water cups drunk before, during and after a 20-minute active play session.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Plan* (20 min): Choose a park, beach or street to clean and plan supplies and safety rules.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 162, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Publish* (90 min): Type and publish the report with a photo and map.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Same perimeter* (60 min): Draw all rectangles with perimeter 20 and record their areas.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Criteria and constraints* (40 min): List criteria (stays standing at fan high speed, inside stays dry) and constraints (cardboard, tape, 1 plastic bag, 3 days).
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Map* (30 min): Place dots on a world map for each item's origin and count local, national and foreign goods.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Draw* (30 min): Draw the design lightly onto the large paper.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Fuel* (30 min): Sort snack cards into good pre-activity fuel vs. sometimes foods, then do a 15-minute jog-and-skip workout.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Cleanup* (20 min): Carry out a 1-hour cleanup with family and tally types of trash collected.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 163, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Visual display* (90 min): Create a slideshow or poster for the presentation.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Best pen* (60 min): Find the rectangular pen with the most area using 24 feet of fence.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Research* (40 min): Look at how real hurricane-resistant houses use roof straps, shutters and raised foundations.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Local goods* (30 min): Visit a farmers market and list goods made or grown locally.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Background* (30 min): Paint the sky, land and large background shapes.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Circuit* (30 min): Complete a 6-station fitness circuit (1 minute each) and record heart rate after each round.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Data* (20 min): Graph the trash collected and identify the most common item.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 164, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Present* (90 min): Present research to the family and answer questions.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Area or perimeter?* (60 min): Decide whether 10 real-life situations need area or perimeter.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Sketch* (40 min): Sketch 3 different house designs with labels.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Why trade* (30 min): Discuss why we buy goods from other places (cost, climate, specialization).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Details* (30 min): Paint people, buildings and details, using tints and shades.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Heart and lungs* (30 min): Explain how heart and breathing rates changed during the circuit using your data.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Share* (20 min): Write a short letter or post for a community board sharing results and a request to help.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 165, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Unit 8 assessment* (90 min): Complete the Unit 8 test on informational reading and research skills.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Solve a mixed area/perimeter problem set.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Score each design against the criteria and choose one to build.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write about one item's journey from where it was made to your home.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Evaluate the mural with family and add finishing touches.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a short quiz on fueling, hydration and the body's response to exercise.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Unit 6 check* (20 min): Complete a quiz on germs, dental care, sleep, health helpers and the environment.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Publishing and presenting research
- Objective: Publish with technology
- Objective: Present with visual displays
- Objective: Answer audience questions
- Vocabulary: slideshow, visual display, presentation
- Materials: computer, poster or slides
- Assessment: Unit 8 test; research report and presentation rubric

### Math 3: Perimeter and area relationships
- Objective: Compare rectangles with same perimeter and different area
- Objective: Compare same area and different perimeter
- Vocabulary: perimeter, area, dimensions
- Materials: square tiles, grid paper
- Assessment: Area vs. perimeter problem set

### Science 3: Engineering: define the problem
- Objective: Define a design problem with criteria and constraints
- Objective: Generate multiple solutions
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraints, engineer, brainstorm
- Materials: design notebook, photos of storm-resistant buildings
- Assessment: Design notebook with scored sketches

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Where goods come from
- Objective: Identify goods made locally, elsewhere in the U.S. and abroad
- Vocabulary: import, export, trade, global
- Materials: household product labels, world map, sticky dots
- Assessment: Origins map and journey writing

### Art & Music 3: Community mural
- Objective: Create a mural representing your community
- Vocabulary: mural, grid, collaborate
- Materials: butcher paper, tempera paint, pencils
- Assessment: Community mural

### PE 3: Fuel and hydration
- Objective: Explain how food and water fuel activity
- Vocabulary: hydration, energy, fuel
- Materials: water bottle
- Assessment: Fuel quiz

### Health 3: Healthy community
- Objective: Promote a healthy community environment through action
- Vocabulary: community, cleanup, advocate
- Materials: gloves, trash bags, clipboard
- Assessment: Unit 6 quiz; cleanup data graph
