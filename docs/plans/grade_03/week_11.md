## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Just So Story* (90 min): Read Kipling's 'How the Leopard Got His Spots' and discuss how a pourquoi tale explains nature.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Twos and tens* (60 min): Find doubles and tens patterns on a hundreds chart and practice 2s and 10s facts.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Choose an organism* (40 min): Research a chosen organism's life cycle (sea turtle, salmon, oak tree).
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Arrival* (30 min): Read about the arrival of Spanish, Mexican and American newcomers and list changes each brought.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Value scale* (30 min): Paint a 7-step value scale by adding white (tints) and black (shades) to one color.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Cues* (30 min): Learn the cues (side to target, arm back, step with opposite foot, follow through) and shadow-throw 20 times.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Growth* (20 min): Compare baby photos to now and measure your height.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Real reasons* (90 min): Read a nonfiction passage on leopard camouflage and complete a cause/effect chart.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fives* (60 min): Use clock faces and nickels to practice 5s facts and notice the 0/5 ending pattern.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Build model* (40 min): Build a spinning paper-plate life cycle model.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Perspectives* (30 min): Compare a settler's account and a Native account of the same period and note differences.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Monochromatic* (30 min): Paint a small monochromatic picture of a tree using only one color plus white and black.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Wall target* (30 min): Throw 20 tennis balls at a taped wall target from 15 feet and tally hits.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Different rates* (20 min): Discuss why kids grow at different rates and that it is normal.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 53, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Comparatives* (90 min): Use comparative and superlative adjectives (faster, fastest) to compare animal features; spell -able and -ible words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Zero and one* (60 min): Explore 0 and 1 rules with counters and write why 8 × 0 = 0 in a sentence.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Present* (40 min): Present the model and explain each stage.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Changes* (30 min): Chart changes to land, food, language and daily life for local nations.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *O'Keeffe* (30 min): Look at Georgia O'Keeffe's close-up flower paintings and discuss why she painted them so large.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Distance* (30 min): Throw for distance in an open field using a big step and full follow-through; mark best throw.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Trusted adults* (20 min): List trusted adults and professionals to ask about body questions.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 54, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Note taking* (90 min): Take notes on your report animal from 2 sources, sorting facts into body, habitat, food and adaptations.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Pattern explain* (60 min): Explain why 5s products are half of 10s products using arrays.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Bean wrap-up* (40 min): Transplant bean sprouts to soil and describe the next stages expected.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Resilience* (30 min): Learn how local nations have kept languages, ceremonies and basketry alive today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Flower painting* (30 min): Paint a large close-up flower that fills the page, using at least 4 tints and shades.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Partner check* (30 min): Watch a partner's throws and check off each cue on a checklist.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Doctor visit* (20 min): Learn what happens at a check-up.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 55, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a paragraph comparing the fiction and nonfiction explanations of the leopard's spots.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a timed-free fact check on 2, 5, 10, 0 and 1 and record facts to practice.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Unit 3 assessment* (40 min): Complete the unit quiz on life cycles.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Unit 2 assessment* (30 min): Complete the unit test on local American Indian nations and present one fact you will remember.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Explain where you used light and dark values and how they create form.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Explain the cues and throw 10 at the target to compare accuracy with Monday.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Unit 2 check* (20 min): Complete a quiz on body systems and growth.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: How and why: animal adaptations
- Objective: Describe cause/effect relationships in scientific texts
- Objective: Compare a pourquoi tale to fact
- Objective: Take notes
- Vocabulary: cause, effect, pourquoi tale, comparative
- Materials: Just So Stories by Rudyard Kipling (Project Gutenberg), note cards
- Assessment: Fiction vs. nonfiction paragraph; notes check

### Math 3: Facts of 2, 5, 10, 0 and 1
- Objective: Use patterns to multiply by 2, 5, 10, 0 and 1
- Objective: Explain zero and identity properties
- Vocabulary: identity property, zero property, even, odd
- Materials: hundreds chart, fact cards
- Assessment: Fact check of 2s, 5s, 10s, 0s, 1s

### Science 3: Life cycle models
- Objective: Develop and present a life cycle model
- Objective: Explain patterns among life cycles
- Vocabulary: model, reproduce, offspring, life span
- Materials: paper plates, markers, brads
- Assessment: Unit 3 quiz; life cycle model

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Newcomers meet first peoples
- Objective: Discuss how newcomers and local Indian nations interacted
- Objective: Recognize resilience
- Vocabulary: settler, interaction, conflict, resilience
- Materials: primary source excerpts, timeline strip
- Assessment: Unit 2 test

### Art & Music 3: Tints, shades and value
- Objective: Create tints and shades
- Objective: Make a monochromatic painting
- Vocabulary: tint, shade, value, monochromatic
- Materials: paints, white and black paint, Georgia O'Keeffe flower images
- Assessment: Value scale and O'Keeffe-inspired flower painting

### PE 3: Overhand throw cues
- Objective: Use the 4 overhand throw cues
- Objective: Throw with accuracy and force
- Vocabulary: side to target, step with opposite foot, follow through
- Materials: tennis balls, tape wall target
- Assessment: Throw cue checklist and target tally

### Health 3: Growing and changing
- Objective: Recognize people grow at different rates
- Objective: Identify trusted adults
- Vocabulary: growth, development, trusted adult, pediatrician
- Materials: baby photos, height chart
- Assessment: Unit 2 quiz
