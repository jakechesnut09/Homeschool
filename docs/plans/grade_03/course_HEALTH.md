# Health 3 (Grade 3)

Third-grade health education covering nutrition and physical activity, body systems and growth, feelings, friendship and conflict resolution, injury prevention and safety (fire, traffic, water, emergencies, online), safe medicine use and the harms of tobacco, vaping and alcohol, and personal and community health. Students practice skills such as reading food labels, goal setting, refusal skills and handwashing, and finish with a health promotion project.

*Framework:* 2008 California Health Education Content Standards; 2019 CA Health Education Framework  
*Time:* 20 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Nutrition and Physical Activity (weeks 1–6)
A variety of foods and daily activity give our bodies the energy to learn, play and grow.

- Week 1: MyPlate food groups
- Week 2: Breakfast, snacks and water
- Week 3: Reading food labels
- Week 4: Food advertising
- Week 5: Physical activity and health
- Week 6: Planning balanced meals

### Unit 2: Growth and Development: Body Systems (weeks 7–11)
Body systems work together, and healthy habits keep them strong.

- Week 7: Skeletal and muscular systems
- Week 8: Digestive system
- Week 9: Heart and lungs
- Week 10: Brain, nerves and senses
- Week 11: Growing and changing

### Unit 3: Mental, Emotional and Social Health (weeks 12–17)
Understanding feelings and communicating well help us build healthy relationships.

- Week 12: Identifying feelings
- Week 13: Managing stress and strong emotions
- Week 14: Healthy friendships
- Week 15: Communication and conflict resolution
- Week 16: Bullying and upstanders
- Week 17: Personal goals and growth mindset

### Unit 4: Injury Prevention and Safety (weeks 18–23)
Knowing safety rules and how to get help prevents injuries.

- Week 18: Fire safety
- Week 19: Pedestrian, bike and passenger safety
- Week 20: Water and outdoor safety
- Week 21: Emergencies and 911
- Week 22: Safe and unsafe situations
- Week 23: Online safety

### Unit 5: Medicines, Alcohol, Tobacco and Other Drugs (weeks 24–27)
Medicines can help when used safely; tobacco, vaping and alcohol harm growing bodies.

- Week 24: Medicine safety
- Week 25: Tobacco and vaping
- Week 26: Alcohol
- Week 27: Refusal skills and drug-free choices

### Unit 6: Personal and Community Health (weeks 28–33)
Daily habits and a clean environment protect our health and our community's.

- Week 28: Germs and handwashing
- Week 29: Dental health
- Week 30: Sleep, sun and hygiene
- Week 31: Health helpers
- Week 32: Environment and health
- Week 33: Healthy community

### Unit 7: Health Promotion Project (weeks 34–36)
We can use what we know to help others be healthy.

- Week 34: Health promotion project: plan
- Week 35: Health promotion project: create
- Week 36: Health promotion project: present and reflect

## Standards

- **H-3.1.1.A** Distinguish between medicines and other drugs and describe safe medicine use.
- **H-3.1.1.G** Describe the major body systems (skeletal, muscular, digestive, circulatory, respiratory, nervous) and their functions.
- **H-3.1.1.M** Identify feelings and healthy ways to express them.
- **H-3.1.1.N** Identify the food groups and the importance of eating a variety of foods from each group.
- **H-3.1.1.P** Explain how germs spread and how to prevent communicable disease.
- **H-3.1.1.S** Identify safety rules at home, at school and in the community (fire, water, bicycle, pedestrian, passenger).
- **H-3.1.2.A** Describe the harmful effects of tobacco, vaping and secondhand smoke.
- **H-3.1.2.G** Explain how the five senses work and how to protect them.
- **H-3.1.2.M** Describe characteristics of healthy friendships and respect for differences.
- **H-3.1.2.N** Describe the benefits of eating breakfast, healthy snacks and drinking water.
- **H-3.1.2.P** Describe proper dental care and its importance.
- **H-3.1.2.S** Describe emergency situations and how to get help, including calling 911.
- **H-3.1.3.A** Describe the effects of alcohol on the body and behavior.
- **H-3.1.3.G** Recognize that people grow and develop at different rates.
- **H-3.1.3.M** Describe bullying and ways to respond to and report it.
- **H-3.1.3.N** Explain how physical activity contributes to good health.
- **H-3.1.3.P** Explain the importance of sleep, sun protection and personal hygiene.
- **H-3.1.3.S** Distinguish between safe and unsafe situations and identify trusted adults to tell.
- **H-3.1.4.P** Describe how the environment affects health and ways to protect it.
- **H-3.1.4.S** Identify ways to stay safe online and with technology.
- **H-3.2.1.A** Identify influences such as media and peers on alcohol, tobacco and drug use.
- **H-3.2.1.N** Explain how media and advertising influence food and activity choices.
- **H-3.3.1.G** Identify trusted adults and professionals who can answer questions about growth and the body.
- **H-3.3.1.N** Use food labels and reliable sources to find nutrition information.
- **H-3.3.1.P** Identify health care professionals and community health helpers.
- **H-3.4.1.A** Demonstrate refusal skills to avoid alcohol, tobacco and other drugs.
- **H-3.4.1.M** Demonstrate effective communication, listening and conflict-resolution skills.
- **H-3.4.1.S** Demonstrate refusal skills and how to ask for help in unsafe situations.
- **H-3.5.1.M** Use a decision-making process for social situations.
- **H-3.5.1.N** Use a decision-making process to choose healthy foods and snacks.
- **H-3.5.1.S** Use a decision-making process to avoid injury.
- **H-3.6.1.M** Set a personal goal and identify steps and supports to reach it.
- **H-3.6.1.N** Set a short-term goal to improve eating or physical activity and track progress.
- **H-3.7.1.G** Practice behaviors that keep body systems healthy.
- **H-3.7.1.M** Practice healthy ways to manage stress and strong emotions.
- **H-3.7.1.N** Practice planning and preparing balanced meals and snacks.
- **H-3.7.1.P** Practice personal hygiene and health habits.
- **H-3.7.1.S** Practice safety behaviors (helmets, seat belts, fire escape plans).
- **H-3.8.1.A** Encourage others to be tobacco-, alcohol- and drug-free.
- **H-3.8.1.N** Encourage family and peers to make healthy food and activity choices.
- **H-3.8.1.P** Promote a healthy school, home and community environment.
