## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 11–12* (90 min): Read chapters 11–12 and discuss why the web words ('Some Pig') work; list strong words Charlotte chooses.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Build arrays* (60 min): Build arrays with tiles for 10 facts and label rows × columns.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Define a problem* (40 min): Define a problem (keeping a cabinet door closed or finding lost pins) with criteria and constraints.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Food* (30 min): Learn how acorns were gathered, leached and ground; look at photos of grinding stones.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Half note* (30 min): Learn half notes and half rests and clap rhythms that combine them with quarters.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Log roll* (30 min): Practice log rolls down a mat with arms overhead and body straight, 5 each direction.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Bones* (20 min): Learn major bones (skull, ribs, spine, femur) and why bones need calcium.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Irregular verbs* (90 min): Practice irregular past tense verbs (spin/spun, write/wrote, catch/caught) using sentences about the book; spell -ly and -ness words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Turn it around* (60 min): Rotate arrays to show 3 × 6 = 6 × 3 and record turnaround facts.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Brainstorm* (40 min): Sketch 3 possible magnet solutions and compare them against the criteria.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Shelter* (30 min): Compare tule, redwood bark and plank houses by region.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Conducting* (30 min): Conduct 2-beat, 3-beat and 4-beat patterns to a march, a waltz and a pop song.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Egg roll* (30 min): Practice tucked side 'egg rolls' and rocking chair rocks to learn a rounded back.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Vinegar bone* (20 min): Soak a cleaned chicken bone in vinegar to see what happens when calcium is removed; observe after 3 days.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 13–14* (90 min): Read chapters 13–14 and figure out the meaning of 5 tricky words from context.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Arrays around us* (60 min): Hunt for arrays at home (muffin tin, egg carton) and write two equations for each.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Build* (40 min): Build the best solution prototype.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Clothing and tools* (30 min): Study Calisphere photographs of tools and basketry.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Measures* (30 min): Draw bar lines into 6 written rhythms in 4/4 and 3/4 time.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Forward roll* (30 min): Practice forward rolls down a gentle incline with an adult spotting the neck and hips.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Joints* (20 min): Find hinge and ball-and-socket joints in your body and test their movements.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 34, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Draft story* (90 min): Draft the beginning and middle of your animal story with at least 2 lines of dialogue.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Array problems* (60 min): Solve array word problems (chairs in rows, garden beds) using grid paper drawings.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Test* (40 min): Test and evaluate the prototype; record what worked.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Model* (30 min): Build a model shelter from natural materials.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Waltz* (30 min): Listen to Strauss's 'The Blue Danube' and sway on beat 1 of each measure.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Routine* (30 min): Combine a balance, a roll and a jump with soft landing into a 3-part routine.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Muscles* (20 min): Feel biceps and triceps work in pairs while lifting a book.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 35, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take an irregular verbs quiz and answer comprehension questions on chapters 11–15.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete an arrays quiz and explain the commutative property using a drawing.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Unit 2 assessment* (40 min): Present the design and complete the unit quiz on magnets and static electricity.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain how geography shaped daily life.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Identify the meter of 4 recordings and complete a bar-line worksheet.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Perform the routine and explain the safety cue 'chin to chest.'
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Label a skeleton and muscle diagram and list 2 ways to keep bones and muscles strong.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Charlotte's Web, chapters 11–15
- Objective: Determine meanings of words and phrases
- Objective: Form irregular verbs
- Objective: Draft a narrative with dialogue
- Vocabulary: irregular verb, dialogue, literal
- Materials: Charlotte's Web, dictionary
- Assessment: Irregular verbs quiz; comprehension questions

### Math 3: Arrays and the commutative property
- Objective: Model multiplication with arrays
- Objective: Show the commutative property
- Vocabulary: array, row, column, commutative property
- Materials: grid paper, tiles, muffin tin
- Assessment: Arrays quiz with written explanation

### Science 3: Magnet design problem
- Objective: Define a design problem solvable with magnets
- Objective: Generate and compare solutions
- Vocabulary: design problem, criteria, constraints, solution
- Materials: magnets, paper clips, cardboard, string
- Assessment: Unit 2 quiz; design presentation

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Adapting to the environment
- Objective: Describe how geography influenced food, clothing, shelter and tools
- Vocabulary: acorn, shelter, tule, basket, adapt
- Materials: Calisphere photographs, acorns
- Assessment: Shelter model and explanation

### Art & Music 3: Half notes and meter
- Objective: Read half notes
- Objective: Feel meter in 2, 3 and 4
- Vocabulary: half note, meter, measure, bar line, time signature
- Materials: rhythm cards, recordings of a march and a waltz
- Assessment: Meter identification worksheet

### PE 3: Rolling and tumbling
- Objective: Perform rolls safely with control
- Objective: Link balances and rolls in a sequence
- Vocabulary: log roll, forward roll, tuck, spotting
- Materials: mat, old mattress or soft grass
- Assessment: Tumbling routine with safe technique

### Health 3: Skeletal and muscular systems
- Objective: Describe the skeletal and muscular systems
- Vocabulary: skeleton, bone, joint, muscle, tendon
- Materials: chicken bone (cleaned), vinegar, jar
- Assessment: Labeled diagram
