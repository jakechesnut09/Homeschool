## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Stanzas* (90 min): Read Stevenson's 'My Shadow' and label lines and stanzas; describe what each stanza adds.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Plan the steps* (60 min): Underline the question and plan both steps for 5 two-step problems before solving.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Fossil formation* (40 min): Watch a fossil video and sequence the steps of fossil formation.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Questions* (30 min): Write 10 open-ended interview questions for an older relative or neighbor about how the community has changed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Rubbings* (30 min): Make crayon rubbings of 10 textured surfaces (leaves, coins, screens, bark).
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Pass* (30 min): Make 30 inside-foot passes to a partner with the plant foot beside the ball.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Fire rules* (20 min): Learn rules about matches, lighters, stoves and space heaters, and find hazards in a home picture.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Rhythm* (90 min): Clap the rhythm of 'The Swing' and practice reading it aloud with expression; spell VCCV multisyllable words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Equations with letters* (60 min): Write a single equation with a letter for the unknown for each two-step problem.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Make fossils* (40 min): Make imprint fossils by pressing shells into clay; fill with plaster to make casts.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Interview* (30 min): Conduct and record the interview, listening and asking follow-up questions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Visual texture* (30 min): Draw 6 textures (fur, bark, scales, feathers, brick, water) in a sketchbook grid.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Target* (30 min): Kick to a target between two cones from 15 feet; tally successes.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Smoke alarms* (20 min): Test home smoke alarms with an adult and learn what the sound means.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 88, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Literal vs. nonliteral* (90 min): Find nonliteral phrases in poems and draw literal vs. real meanings.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Estimate first* (60 min): Estimate answers with rounding and mental math before solving; check reasonableness.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Fossil types* (40 min): Compare body fossils, trace fossils and imprints using Smithsonian images.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Notes* (30 min): Listen to the recording and take notes on the 5 most important details.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Dürer* (30 min): Look closely at Albrecht Dürer's 'Young Hare' and speculate how he painted each hair.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Shoot* (30 min): Shoot on goal using laces and follow-through.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Escape plan* (20 min): Draw a home floor plan with two exits from every room and an outside meeting place.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 89, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Write a poem* (90 min): Write a rhyming poem in 2 stanzas about a shadow or swing.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Party planning* (60 min): Solve a multi-step party budget problem (packs of plates, cost, change).
- [ ] **Science 3** — *La Brea* (40 min): Learn about the La Brea Tar Pits and the animals found there.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Share* (30 min): Share 3 interesting stories from the interview with family.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Texture collage* (30 min): Cut your rubbings into shapes to build a textured animal collage.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Moving pass* (30 min): Pass to a partner jogging across the field.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Fire drill* (20 min): Practice a home fire drill, crawling low under 'smoke' and timing how long it takes.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 90, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Record a fluent reading of a Stevenson poem and complete a poetry terms check.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a two-step problem quiz and explain one solution with a bar model.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Explain how fossils form in a comic strip.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write a one-page summary of the oral history and add key events to the timeline.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Present the collage and name the texture used for each part.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a kicking check (pass accuracy and shot) scored on cues.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Demonstrate stop, drop and roll and explain your escape plan.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Poetry elements: stanza, rhythm, rhyme
- Objective: Use poetry terms to describe parts of poems
- Objective: Read poems fluently
- Objective: Distinguish literal and nonliteral language
- Vocabulary: stanza, line, rhyme, rhythm, nonliteral
- Materials: A Child's Garden of Verses by Robert Louis Stevenson (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Poetry terms check; fluency recording

### Math 3: Two-step word problems with four operations
- Objective: Solve two-step problems with all four operations
- Objective: Represent with equations using letters
- Vocabulary: two-step, equation, variable, estimate
- Materials: bar model templates
- Assessment: Two-step problem quiz

### Science 3: What fossils tell us
- Objective: Describe how fossils form
- Objective: Identify types of fossils
- Vocabulary: fossil, sediment, imprint, extinct
- Materials: clay, shells, plaster of Paris or salt dough
- Assessment: Fossil formation comic strip

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Oral history
- Objective: Plan and conduct an oral history interview
- Vocabulary: oral history, interview, memory, primary source
- Materials: recording device, interview question sheet
- Assessment: Oral history summary

### Art & Music 3: Texture
- Objective: Create visual and tactile texture
- Objective: Speculate about an artist's process
- Vocabulary: texture, rubbing, tactile, visual texture
- Materials: crayons, copy paper, textured household objects, Dürer 'Young Hare' image
- Assessment: Texture animal collage

### PE 3: Kicking and passing
- Objective: Pass and kick to a target
- Vocabulary: pass, shoot, plant foot
- Materials: soccer ball, goal or two cones
- Assessment: Kicking check

### Health 3: Fire safety
- Objective: Identify fire safety rules
- Objective: Make and practice a home escape plan
- Vocabulary: smoke alarm, escape plan, meeting place, stop, drop and roll
- Materials: graph paper, smoke alarm test button
- Assessment: Escape plan and drill
