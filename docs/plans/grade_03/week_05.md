## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapters 1–2* (90 min): Read chapters 1–2 and describe Fern's character with evidence; discuss whether Fern's father was fair.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Bar models* (60 min): Draw bar models for 6 add/subtract word problems (result, change and start unknown).
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Magnet hunt* (40 min): Test 15 household objects with a magnet and sort into attracted / not attracted.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Field trip* (30 min): Visit a local park, river or hill and sketch landforms and plants.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Beat vs. rhythm* (30 min): Pat the steady beat on your knees while singing 'Yankee Doodle,' then clap the rhythm of the words.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Landing* (30 min): Practice soft landings with bent knees.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Benefits* (20 min): List ways activity helps the heart, muscles, bones, mood and sleep.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapter 3–4* (90 min): Read chapters 3–4 and write 3 questions and answers about Wilbur's loneliness; spell dis- and pre- words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Letters for unknowns* (60 min): Write equations with a letter for the unknown (e.g., 425 + n = 700) and solve.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Poles* (40 min): Explore north and south poles and record which combinations attract or repel.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Human features* (30 min): Identify roads, buildings and bridges in the area.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Body percussion* (30 min): Perform a 4-beat body percussion pattern (stomp, pat, clap, snap) to a steady beat.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Standing long jump* (30 min): Measure 3 standing long jumps.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Activity log* (20 min): Log all physical activity minutes for a day and compare to the 60-minute goal.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Verbs* (90 min): Identify action and linking verbs in a passage from chapter 4 and write 6 sentences about the barn.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Two-step problems* (60 min): Solve 5 two-step add/subtract problems about a library book drive, estimating first.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Through materials* (40 min): Test whether magnetic force works through paper, cardboard, water and plastic.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Field map* (30 min): Make a map of the field study site.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Tempo* (30 min): Walk, jog and tiptoe to recorded music at slow, medium and fast tempos.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Vertical jump* (30 min): Measure vertical jump with chalk on a wall.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Screen time* (20 min): Track screen time and brainstorm active swaps.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Chapter 5* (90 min): Read chapter 5 and describe first impressions of Charlotte using a character web.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Write your own* (60 min): Write and solve two original word problems using data from a family trip mileage chart.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Distance* (40 min): Measure how far a paper clip can be from a magnet before it is pulled, using different numbers of paper sheets.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Present* (30 min): Present findings with photos.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Echo* (30 min): Echo-clap 10 four-beat rhythm patterns played by a parent or recording.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Hopscotch* (30 min): Play hopscotch with variations.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Goal setting* (20 min): Set a one-week SMART goal to add activity or a fruit/vegetable serving.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 25, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Complete a chapters 1–5 quiz and a prefix spelling check (dis-, pre-, mis-).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit 1 assessment* (60 min): Complete the Unit 1 test on rounding and adding/subtracting within 1,000.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Write cause-and-effect sentences explaining magnetic interactions.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Unit 1 assessment* (30 min): Complete the unit test on geography.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Perform the beat on a bucket drum while a family member claps the rhythm, then switch.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Explain how arms help create force.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Check day 1 of the goal tracker and plan supports.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Charlotte's Web, chapters 1–5
- Objective: Describe characters and setting
- Objective: Ask and answer questions with text evidence
- Objective: Learn action and linking verbs
- Vocabulary: setting, runt, verb, linking verb
- Materials: Charlotte's Web by E.B. White (library), reader's notebook
- Assessment: Chapters 1–5 quiz; prefix spelling check

### Math 3: Add/subtract problem solving
- Objective: Solve one- and two-step add/subtract word problems
- Objective: Use a letter for the unknown
- Vocabulary: unknown, equation, bar model, reasonable
- Materials: bar model templates
- Assessment: Unit 1 test

### Science 3: Magnetic forces
- Objective: Investigate magnetic attraction and repulsion
- Objective: Test what magnets attract
- Vocabulary: magnet, attract, repel, pole, magnetic field
- Materials: refrigerator magnets, bar magnets, household objects, paper clips
- Assessment: Cause/effect explanation

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Geography field study
- Objective: Observe local geography firsthand
- Objective: Present geographic findings
- Vocabulary: field study, observe, map
- Materials: notebook, camera
- Assessment: Unit 1 test; field map

### Art & Music 3: Steady beat and rhythm
- Objective: Distinguish steady beat from rhythm
- Objective: Move to changing tempos
- Vocabulary: beat, rhythm, tempo
- Materials: drum or upside-down bucket, recorded songs
- Assessment: Beat and rhythm performance

### PE 3: Jumping and landing
- Objective: Jump for distance and height with safe landings
- Vocabulary: takeoff, landing, absorb, force
- Materials: tape, measuring tape
- Assessment: Jump measurements

### Health 3: Physical activity and health
- Objective: Explain how physical activity benefits the body
- Objective: Set an activity goal
- Vocabulary: physical activity, 60 minutes, screen time, goal
- Materials: activity log, timer
- Assessment: SMART goal and tracker
