## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Draft* (90 min): Draft the new Oz adventure with dialogue and temporal words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Read scaled graphs* (60 min): Read picture graphs where one symbol equals 5 and answer questions.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *CA regions* (40 min): Mark the coast, Central Valley, Sierra Nevada and desert regions on a California map.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Declaration* (30 min): Examine the Declaration of Independence and discuss 'all men are created equal' and 'unalienable rights.'
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Lyrics* (30 min): Write 4 lines of lyrics about a topic you care about.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Flag tag* (30 min): Play flag tag with belt flags or tucked scarves, using open space to avoid taggers.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Teeth* (20 min): Learn tooth parts and types (incisors, canines, molars) using a mirror.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 142, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise for word choice and correct dialogue punctuation; spell -ous and -ive words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Survey* (60 min): Survey family and friends about favorite fruits and tally the results.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *City data* (40 min): Collect monthly temperature and rainfall for San Diego, Sacramento, South Lake Tahoe and Death Valley.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *U.S. Capitol* (30 min): Learn the history of the U.S. Capitol and what happens there.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Rhythm* (30 min): Set the lyrics to rhythms using quarter, eighth and half notes.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Dodging* (30 min): Practice dodging skills (fake, pivot, change speed) in a cone-lane challenge.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Egg experiment* (20 min): Soak one hard-boiled egg in soda and one in water overnight and compare stains on the 'enamel.'

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 143, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Compare* (90 min): Compare a scene from the book to a film version and discuss differences.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Make a bar graph* (60 min): Draw a scaled bar graph (scale of 2) of the survey data.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Graph and compare* (40 min): Graph the 4 cities' July highs and yearly rainfall on bar graphs and compare.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *California symbols* (30 min): Learn the California flag, grizzly bear, poppy and state motto 'Eureka.'
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Melody* (30 min): Create a melody for the rhythm in Song Maker, ending on do.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Capture the flag* (30 min): Play capture the flag with friends, planning offense and defense roles.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Brushing* (20 min): Watch a Mouth Healthy Kids video and practice brushing for 2 minutes with a timer.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 144, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Publish* (90 min): Publish the narrative with an illustration.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Compare data* (60 min): Answer one- and two-step 'how many more/fewer' questions from your graph.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Travel guide* (40 min): Write a one-page travel guide describing one region's climate and what to pack in each season.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Local landmark* (30 min): Research a local landmark (historic building, statue or park) and its history.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Notate* (30 min): Write the song in standard or iconic notation.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Fair play* (30 min): Role-play honest calls ('I was tagged') and discuss why honesty keeps games fun.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Flossing* (20 min): Practice flossing technique on all teeth.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 145, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Unit 7 assessment* (90 min): Complete the Unit 7 test and share your Oz story aloud.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Interpret a new scaled graph and create a picture graph with a key.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Share the travel guide and explain how elevation and the ocean affect California climates.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Present the local landmark with a drawing and 5 facts.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Share the draft and explain how the music fits the words.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Write a short reflection on which strategy helped your team most.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Start a 2-week brushing and flossing tracker.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Oz narrative and book vs. film
- Objective: Write a narrative with dialogue and descriptive details
- Objective: Compare book and film versions
- Vocabulary: narrative, compare, adaptation
- Materials: narrative plan, film clip (optional)
- Assessment: Unit 7 test; narrative rubric

### Math 3: Scaled picture graphs and bar graphs
- Objective: Create scaled picture and bar graphs
- Objective: Solve how-many-more problems
- Vocabulary: scale, key, bar graph, picture graph, data
- Materials: grid paper, survey sheet
- Assessment: Graphing check

### Science 3: California climates
- Objective: Obtain and combine information to describe climates in California's regions
- Vocabulary: Mediterranean climate, desert, alpine, coastal fog
- Materials: California map, climate data
- Assessment: California climate travel guide

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Landmarks and essential documents
- Objective: Know histories of landmarks and essential documents
- Vocabulary: landmark, U.S. Capitol, Declaration of Independence, California state symbols
- Materials: Declaration of Independence image (National Archives), California symbols list
- Assessment: Landmark presentation

### Art & Music 3: Composing a song
- Objective: Compose and notate a short song
- Vocabulary: compose, notation, lyrics, phrase
- Materials: staff paper, Chrome Music Lab Song Maker
- Assessment: Song draft

### PE 3: Tag games and strategy
- Objective: Use strategy in tag games
- Vocabulary: dodge, feint, strategy
- Materials: cones, flags
- Assessment: Strategy reflection

### Health 3: Dental health
- Objective: Describe proper dental care
- Objective: Practice brushing and flossing
- Vocabulary: plaque, cavity, floss, enamel, fluoride
- Materials: toothbrush, floss, 2 hard-boiled eggs, soda, water
- Assessment: Egg experiment notes; brushing tracker
