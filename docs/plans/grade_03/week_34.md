## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Peter Rabbit* (90 min): Read 'The Tale of Peter Rabbit' and map its plot.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Park design* (60 min): Begin designing a neighborhood park on grid paper with at least 4 rectangular areas.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Build* (40 min): Build the model house from cardboard and tape following your chosen sketch.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Scarcity* (30 min): Discuss why we can't have everything we want (limited money, time).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Select* (30 min): Review the year's portfolio and select 6 best works.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Relays* (30 min): Practice baton-pass and shuttle relays with a family member.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Topic* (20 min): Choose a health topic from the year (eat a rainbow, wash hands, helmet use, drug-free) to promote.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 167, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Benjamin Bunny* (90 min): Read 'The Tale of Benjamin Bunny' and map its plot.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Measure the park* (60 min): Calculate area and perimeter for each section of the park.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Wind test* (40 min): Test the house in front of a box fan at low, medium and high, keeping distance the same; record failure points.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Trade-offs* (30 min): Choose items from a store flyer with a $20 play budget and name what you gave up.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Mat* (30 min): Mount each artwork on a construction paper mat.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Sack race* (30 min): Practice pillowcase sack hops and three-legged race starts.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Audience* (20 min): Choose an audience (younger cousins, co-op class, family) and what they need to know.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 168, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Compare* (90 min): Compare settings, plots and themes of the two tales in a chart; review dictionary syllable guides.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fair share* (60 min): Partition the picnic area into equal parts for 4 families and name each fraction.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Rain test* (40 min): Place a dry paper towel inside and spray 30 squirts of water from above; record leaks.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Opportunity cost* (30 min): Identify the opportunity cost in 5 scenario cards (e.g., playing outside vs. watching a show).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Artist statements* (30 min): Write a 3-sentence artist statement for each selected work.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Throw* (30 min): Practice the softball throw for distance using overhand cues.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Message* (20 min): Write a clear, positive message with 3 supporting facts.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 169, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Response* (90 min): Write a comparison paragraph about the two tales.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Present* (60 min): Present the park design and explain calculations to the family.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Improve* (40 min): Modify the weakest part of the design based on test results.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Party budget* (30 min): Plan a birthday party with a $50 budget, comparing costs and benefits of choices.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Plan the space* (30 min): Compare a hallway wall and a digital slideshow gallery as exhibit spaces and choose one or both.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Jump* (30 min): Practice standing long jump and record best of 3.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Plan materials* (20 min): Plan materials: a poster, a brochure and a 1-minute public service announcement.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 170, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Answer comparison questions and read a Potter passage aloud fluently.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit 9 assessment* (60 min): Complete the Unit 9 test on geometry, area and perimeter.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Retest the improved house under the same conditions and compare results in a table.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write about a real trade-off you made and its opportunity cost.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Arrange the exhibit layout and make labels.

- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Share the plan with a parent and revise it.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Author study: Beatrix Potter
- Objective: Compare themes, settings and plots of stories by the same author with the same characters
- Vocabulary: author study, theme, setting, series
- Materials: The Tale of Peter Rabbit and The Tale of Benjamin Bunny by Beatrix Potter (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Comparison paragraph; fluency check

### Math 3: Geometry project
- Objective: Apply geometry, area and perimeter in a design
- Objective: Partition shapes into equal areas
- Vocabulary: design, scale, floor plan
- Materials: grid paper, colored pencils
- Assessment: Unit 9 test and park design project

### Science 3: Engineering: build and test
- Objective: Build and fair-test a prototype
- Objective: Identify failure points
- Vocabulary: prototype, fair test, failure point, variable
- Materials: cardboard, tape, plastic bag, box fan, spray bottle, paper towel
- Assessment: Testing log with before/after table

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Choices and trade-offs
- Objective: Understand trade-offs and opportunity cost in economic choices
- Vocabulary: scarcity, trade-off, opportunity cost, budget
- Materials: play money, restaurant menu or store flyer
- Assessment: Party budget and trade-off writing

### Art & Music 3: Preparing an exhibition
- Objective: Select and prepare artwork for exhibition
- Objective: Write artist statements
- Vocabulary: exhibit, artist statement, curate, mat
- Materials: portfolio, construction paper for mats, labels
- Assessment: Exhibit preparation

### PE 3: Field day prep
- Objective: Practice field day events
- Vocabulary: relay, event
- Materials: cones, sacks
- Assessment: Mini field day

### Health 3: Health promotion project: plan
- Objective: Plan a health promotion campaign for a chosen audience
- Vocabulary: campaign, message, audience, advocate
- Materials: planning sheet
- Assessment: Campaign plan
