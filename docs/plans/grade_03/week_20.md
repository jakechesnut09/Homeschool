## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Word connections* (90 min): Describe people and places in your life with words from the poems (cozy, gentle, noisy).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Equal parts* (60 min): Fold paper rectangles and circles into halves, thirds, fourths, sixths and eighths.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Mammoth and elephant* (40 min): Compare a mammoth and an elephant and infer the mammoth's environment.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Organize* (30 min): Organize events from the unit into eras on a poster timeline.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Adinkra* (30 min): Learn about Ghanaian adinkra cloth and the meanings of 6 symbols.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Balloon* (30 min): Keep a balloon up with a paddle 20 times in a row.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Water rules* (20 min): Learn pool, lake and ocean safety rules, including what a rip current is and how to escape one.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 97, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Poem types* (90 min): Read and write an acrostic and a cinquain; spell consonant-le words (table, puzzle).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit fractions* (60 min): Label each part as a unit fraction and explain why parts must be equal.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Dinosaurs and birds* (40 min): Compare dinosaur and bird fossils.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Images* (30 min): Add copies of photos, maps and newspaper clippings with captions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Personal symbols* (30 min): Design 3 personal symbols that represent your family values.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Up bounces* (30 min): Bounce a ball upward on the paddle and count consecutive hits.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Life jackets* (20 min): Try on a life jacket and check it is the right size and fastened correctly.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 98, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Anthology* (90 min): Compile 6 favorite poems (3 published, 3 original) into an illustrated anthology.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Same area, different shape* (60 min): Partition squares into fourths in 3 different ways and prove each part has equal area.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Research* (40 min): Research one extinct organism and its environment.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Land changes* (30 min): Add notes on how the land changed in each era (farms, roads, dams, buildings).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Symbol cloth* (30 min): Print your symbols in a grid on fabric or paper using stamps.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Down dribble* (30 min): Bounce a ball downward with the paddle like a dribble while walking.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Buddy system* (20 min): Practice the buddy system and 'reach or throw, don't go' rescue rule.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 99, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Record* (90 min): Record a poetry podcast reading 3 poems fluently.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Fraction strips* (60 min): Make a set of fraction strips (1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/6, 1/8) to use throughout the unit.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Present* (40 min): Present a museum card for the organism.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Present* (30 min): Present the timeline to family, explaining 3 turning points.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Hokusai* (30 min): Look at Hokusai's woodblock print 'The Great Wave' and discuss how prints spread stories.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Wall hits* (30 min): Hit a ball against a wall, letting it bounce once between hits.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Decisions* (20 min): Use decide-act-check steps for 4 outdoor scenarios (thunderstorm at the beach, getting lost on a hike).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 100, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Unit 5 assessment* (90 min): Complete a poetry test on terms, nonliteral language and imagery.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Identify and draw unit fractions for 10 shapes.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Unit 6 assessment* (40 min): Complete the unit quiz on fossils.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Unit 4 assessment* (30 min): Complete the unit test on community history and primary sources.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Unit 5 check* (30 min): Present your symbol cloth and explain what each symbol means.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Record your best counts for up bounces and wall hits.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a water safety poster with 5 rules.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Poetry anthology and recording
- Objective: Make real-life word connections
- Objective: Create an audio recording of poems
- Objective: Compile a poetry book
- Vocabulary: anthology, recording, performance
- Materials: poems, recording device
- Assessment: Unit 5 test; poetry anthology and recording

### Math 3: Unit fractions and partitioning
- Objective: Partition shapes into equal parts
- Objective: Name unit fractions
- Vocabulary: fraction, unit fraction, numerator, denominator, equal parts
- Materials: paper strips, paper shapes, scissors
- Assessment: Unit fraction identification check

### Science 3: Extinct organisms and modern relatives
- Objective: Compare extinct organisms to living ones
- Objective: Present findings
- Vocabulary: extinct, dinosaur, mammoth, relative
- Materials: Smithsonian Learning Lab images
- Assessment: Unit 6 quiz; museum card

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Community history timeline
- Objective: Create an illustrated community history timeline
- Vocabulary: timeline, era, change over time
- Materials: poster board, primary source copies
- Assessment: Unit 4 test; illustrated timeline

### Art & Music 3: Cultural textiles and prints
- Objective: Explain how cultures tell stories through printed art
- Vocabulary: textile, adinkra, symbol, woodblock
- Materials: Met collection images, fabric scrap or paper, stamps
- Assessment: Symbol cloth presentation

### PE 3: Striking with paddles
- Objective: Strike with a paddle with control
- Vocabulary: paddle, ready position, flat face
- Materials: paddle or racket, balloon, ball
- Assessment: Paddle control counts

### Health 3: Water and outdoor safety
- Objective: Identify water, heat and sun safety rules
- Objective: Make safe decisions outdoors
- Vocabulary: life jacket, buddy system, rip current, heat illness
- Materials: life jacket
- Assessment: Water safety poster
