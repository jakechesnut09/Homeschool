## Day 1 — Monday (school day 106, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Ruby Bridges* (90 min): Read 'The Story of Ruby Bridges' and discuss the courage she showed.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Partition 0 to 1* (60 min): Divide number lines from 0 to 1 into equal intervals and label unit fractions.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Tide pools* (40 min): Learn how tide pool animals survive waves and drying out.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Preamble* (30 min): Read the Preamble from the National Archives website and paraphrase each phrase in kid-friendly words.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Mariachi* (30 min): Listen to mariachi music and identify the violin, trumpet, vihuela and guitarrón.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Grip and stance* (30 min): Learn the stacked-hands grip and side stance; practice 20 dry swings.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Safe vs. unsafe* (20 min): Sort 10 scenario cards into safe, unsafe or 'check with an adult.'

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 107, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Jackie Robinson* (90 min): Read a Jackie Robinson biography and list challenges he faced.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Locate a/b* (60 min): Mark 3/4, 5/8 and 2/3 on number lines by counting unit lengths.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Chaparral* (40 min): Study chaparral plants' adaptations to dry summers and fire.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Why it was written* (30 min): Learn why the founders wrote the Constitution in 1787.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *California Indian song* (30 min): Listen to a California Indian song recording from a tribe or museum website and discuss its purpose.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Tee hitting* (30 min): Hit 20 balls off a tee, focusing on a level swing.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Trusted adults* (20 min): Draw a 'helping hand' tracing with 5 trusted adults, one on each finger.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 108, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Subordinating conjunctions* (90 min): Write complex sentences with because, when, although, after; study Latin roots port and rupt.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Beyond one* (60 min): Extend number lines to 2 and label fractions like 5/4 and 6/3.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Redwood forest* (40 min): Study redwood forest organisms and fog.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Bill of Rights* (30 min): Read a kid-friendly summary of the Bill of Rights and pick the 3 rights you think matter most.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Gold Rush songs* (30 min): Learn and sing 'Oh My Darling, Clementine' and discuss what it tells about miners' lives.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Direction* (30 min): Hit balls to left, center and right by adjusting your stance.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Refusal skills* (20 min): Practice 'say no, get away, tell someone' with firm voice and body language.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 109, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Compare* (90 min): Compare the two people's challenges and actions in a chart; write how your view differs from the author's.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Ruler fractions* (60 min): Find halves and fourths of an inch on a ruler and connect to the number line.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Compare* (40 min): Compare which organisms could survive in each habitat.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Family constitution* (30 min): Write a family constitution with a preamble and 5 articles.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Compare* (30 min): Compare instruments, tempo and purpose of the three styles in a chart.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Safety* (30 min): Practice the bat safety zone and placing (not throwing) the bat after hitting.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Body safety* (20 min): Learn body safety rules: your body belongs to you and secrets about touching are never okay to keep.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 110, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Hold a discussion on 'What is courage?' using evidence from both texts.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Place 10 fractions on number lines and explain how you located each.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Write an argument with evidence about one organism's survival in a California habitat.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Answer questions on the Constitution and explain your family constitution.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Write a paragraph about the style you connected to most and why.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a batting check of 10 hits scored for contact and form.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Role-play 3 refusal scenarios with a parent.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Biography: Ruby Bridges and Jackie Robinson
- Objective: Compare two biographies
- Objective: Use subordinating conjunctions
- Objective: Distinguish own point of view from the author's
- Vocabulary: courage, integration, point of view, subordinating conjunction
- Materials: The Story of Ruby Bridges by Robert Coles (library), Jackie Robinson biography (library)
- Assessment: Comparison chart; discussion participation

### Math 3: Fractions on the number line
- Objective: Place fractions on a number line
- Objective: Represent fractions greater than 1
- Vocabulary: number line, interval, endpoint
- Materials: number line strips, ruler
- Assessment: Number line placement check

### Science 3: California habitats
- Objective: Analyze survival in California habitats
- Objective: Construct an argument with evidence
- Vocabulary: chaparral, tide pool, redwood forest, adaptation
- Materials: California habitat articles, maps
- Assessment: Habitat argument

### Continuity and Change (Local History): The U.S. Constitution
- Objective: Explain the purposes of the Constitution and the Bill of Rights
- Vocabulary: Constitution, Preamble, Bill of Rights, rights
- Materials: Preamble text (National Archives), Bill of Rights summary
- Assessment: Family constitution

### Art & Music 3: Music of California
- Objective: Explore music from California's cultures and history
- Vocabulary: mariachi, folk song, tradition, purpose
- Materials: recordings, song sheets
- Assessment: Music comparison chart and paragraph

### PE 3: Batting
- Objective: Strike a ball off a tee with a level swing
- Vocabulary: bat, tee, swing, grip
- Materials: bat, tee or tall cone, whiffle ball
- Assessment: Batting check

### Health 3: Safe and unsafe situations
- Objective: Distinguish safe and unsafe situations
- Objective: Practice refusal and help-seeking
- Vocabulary: safe, unsafe, trusted adult, refusal, body safety
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Refusal role-play
