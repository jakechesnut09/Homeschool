## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Anansi tales* (90 min): Read an Anansi the Spider folktale and identify the trickster's problem, choices and lesson.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Open number line addition* (60 min): Solve 8 sums such as 367 + 248 by jumping hundreds, tens and ones on an open number line.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Pendulum build* (40 min): Build a pendulum from string and washers and count swings in 15 seconds.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Climate* (30 min): Describe the climate of your region using temperature and rainfall data.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Shape sort* (30 min): Cut 20 shapes from scrap paper and sort them into geometric and organic groups.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Strength* (30 min): Record curl-ups and 90-degree push-ups.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Label parts* (20 min): Find serving size, calories, sugar, sodium and fiber on a Nutrition Facts label.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Stone Soup* (90 min): Read a version of 'Stone Soup' and chart how the villagers change from start to end; spell r-controlled words (ar, or, er, ir, ur).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Partial sums* (60 min): Add 3-digit numbers by adding hundreds, tens and ones separately, then combining partial sums.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Change length* (40 min): Change the string length and record the pattern in swing count; predict the next result.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Resources* (30 min): List local natural resources (water, soil, timber, minerals, fish).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Matisse* (30 min): Look at Henri Matisse's paper cut-outs (e.g., 'The Snail') and speculate how he made them.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Flexibility* (30 min): Measure sit-and-reach and shoulder stretch.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Compare cereals* (20 min): Compare sugar and fiber in 3 cereals and rank them from healthiest to least healthy.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Plural nouns* (90 min): Practice regular (-s, -es) and irregular plurals (mouse/mice, child/children) with a sorting activity and sentences.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Standard algorithm* (60 min): Model regrouping with base-ten blocks, then record the standard algorithm for 10 problems.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Bouncing ball* (40 min): Drop a ball and measure bounce heights; describe the pattern of decreasing bounces.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Water source* (30 min): Research where your town's drinking water comes from.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Cut-paper collage* (30 min): Create a Matisse-inspired collage of organic shapes cut freehand from painted paper.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Endurance* (30 min): Complete a timed half-mile walk/run or PACER and record.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Ingredients* (20 min): Read ingredient lists and learn that ingredients are listed from most to least.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Revise for detail* (90 min): Add sensory details and feelings to the middle of your personal narrative, using a revision checklist.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Estimate and check* (60 min): Estimate each sum by rounding, solve, and judge if the answer is reasonable for 8 word problems.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Playground patterns* (40 min): Observe a swing or seesaw and describe repeating motion patterns.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Resource map* (30 min): Make a resource map with symbols.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Kandinsky* (30 min): Listen to a lively orchestral piece and make a geometric composition of circles, lines and triangles inspired by Kandinsky.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Goal* (30 min): Set a SMART fitness goal for the semester.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Store hunt* (20 min): At the grocery store, compare 2 brands of the same food and choose the healthier one with reasons.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 15, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Compare the lessons of Anansi and Stone Soup in a Venn diagram and discuss with a family member.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a mixed addition quiz using any strategy and explain one solution aloud.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Use your pendulum data to predict swings for a new length, then test the prediction.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write how one resource is important to your community.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Give a 1-minute artist talk explaining the shapes you chose and why.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Begin a weekly fitness log to track progress.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Answer 5 questions about a new food label.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Folktales from around the world
- Objective: Compare lessons in folktales from different cultures
- Objective: Form regular and irregular plurals
- Objective: Add details to narratives
- Vocabulary: folktale, culture, trickster, plural
- Materials: Anansi folktale (library or Storyline Online), Stone Soup (library)
- Assessment: Venn diagram comparison; plural nouns check

### Math 3: Addition within 1,000
- Objective: Add 3-digit numbers using place value strategies and the standard algorithm
- Objective: Estimate to check sums
- Vocabulary: sum, regroup, compose, estimate
- Materials: base-ten blocks, place value mat
- Assessment: Addition quiz and oral explanation of one strategy

### Science 3: Patterns of motion
- Objective: Observe patterns in motion
- Objective: Use patterns to predict future motion
- Vocabulary: pattern, predict, pendulum, motion
- Materials: string, washers, tape, timer, ball
- Assessment: Prediction test and explanation

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Climate and natural resources
- Objective: Describe local climate and natural resources
- Vocabulary: natural resource, climate, soil, water source
- Materials: local climate data, resource cards
- Assessment: Resource map and paragraph

### Art & Music 3: Geometric and organic shapes
- Objective: Use geometric and organic shapes in compositions
- Objective: Discuss artists' processes
- Vocabulary: geometric, organic, shape, composition
- Materials: colored paper, scissors, glue, Matisse and Kandinsky images
- Assessment: Collage and artist talk

### PE 3: Baseline fitness assessment
- Objective: Complete baseline fitness tests
- Objective: Set a fitness goal
- Vocabulary: goal, curl-up, push-up, sit-and-reach, PACER
- Materials: tape measure, timer, mat
- Assessment: Baseline fitness scores and written goal

### Health 3: Reading food labels
- Objective: Use food labels to compare foods
- Vocabulary: nutrition facts, serving size, ingredients, sugar, sodium
- Materials: 3 cereal boxes, 2 yogurt containers
- Assessment: Food label questions
