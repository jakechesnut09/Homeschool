## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Character traits* (90 min): Read 'The Ant and the Grasshopper' and fill a trait chart for each character with text evidence.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Nearest ten* (60 min): Locate 2-digit and 3-digit numbers between two tens on a vertical number line and round 15 numbers to the nearest 10.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Plan a test* (40 min): Plan a fair test: does ramp height change how far a toy car travels? Identify what stays the same.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Landform vocabulary* (30 min): Define and sketch 10 landforms and water bodies.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Blind contour* (30 min): Draw your hand three times without looking at the paper, moving the pencil slowly with your eyes.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Components* (30 min): Learn the 5 components of fitness and act out an exercise for each.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Breakfast power* (20 min): Discuss how breakfast fuels the brain and plan 3 balanced breakfasts with at least 3 food groups each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Vowel teams* (90 min): Sort and spell ai/ay and ee/ea words, then read 'The Boy Who Cried Wolf' aloud for fluency.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Nearest hundred* (60 min): Use a 0–1,000 number line to round 15 numbers to the nearest hundred, marking the halfway point each time.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Ramp investigation* (40 min): Run 3 trials at each of 3 ramp heights and record distances in a data table.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *California regions* (30 min): Locate the coast, Central Valley, Sierra Nevada and desert regions on a physical map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Still life* (30 min): Arrange 3 pieces of fruit and draw their outer and inner contour lines from observation.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Pulse* (30 min): Find your pulse and count heart rate at rest for 15 seconds × 4.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Snack decisions* (20 min): Use the decide-act-check steps to choose between snack pairs (chips vs. apple with peanut butter).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Nouns* (90 min): Identify common and proper nouns in 'The Boy Who Cried Wolf' and write 5 sentences using both.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Tricky cases* (60 min): Round numbers like 450, 995 and 305 and explain in writing why 450 rounds to 500.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Friction test* (40 min): Roll the car down the same ramp onto carpet, tile and towel and measure distances.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Local features* (30 min): Identify the landforms and water near your town using a local physical map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Add detail* (30 min): Return to the still life and add texture lines, shadows and a table edge to make it more realistic.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Heart rate change* (30 min): Measure heart rate before and after 2 minutes of jumping jacks; record in a log.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Water* (20 min): Measure how many cups of water you drink in a day and compare to the recommended amount.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 9, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Narrative leads* (90 min): Revise the first paragraph of your personal narrative with a strong lead (action, dialogue or sound).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Rounding in the world* (60 min): Round prices from a grocery flyer to the nearest 10 cents and nearest dollar and estimate a shopping total.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Analyze* (40 min): Graph results and write a claim about force, friction and motion supported by data.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Salt dough map* (30 min): Build a salt dough relief map of your region showing main landforms.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Shoe drawing* (30 min): Draw a sneaker from observation, including laces, stitching and tread patterns.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Circuit* (30 min): Complete a 6-station fitness circuit (one per component).
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Sugary drinks* (20 min): Measure the teaspoons of sugar in a can of soda using sugar and a teaspoon and compare to water.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 10, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a paragraph explaining how one character's actions taught the moral, then partner-discuss which fable lesson matters most.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a 10-problem rounding check and write a rule for rounding in your own words.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Explain how you kept the test fair and what you would improve.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Label a blank map of your region and describe its features.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Lay out week 1 and week 2 drawings side by side and write 2 ways your observation improved.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Explain why heart rate rises during exercise.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a 'healthy snack menu' card for the kitchen with 6 choices.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Fables: characters and morals
- Objective: Describe characters' traits and how actions lead to a lesson
- Objective: Identify common and proper nouns
- Objective: Read vowel team words
- Vocabulary: trait, motivation, noun, proper noun
- Materials: Aesop's Fables, character trait chart
- Assessment: Character paragraph with evidence; noun practice sheet

### Math 3: Rounding to the nearest 10 and 100
- Objective: Round numbers to the nearest 10 and 100 using a number line
- Objective: Use rounding to estimate
- Vocabulary: round, estimate, benchmark, halfway
- Materials: number line strips, place value chart
- Assessment: 10-problem rounding check plus written rounding rule

### Science 3: Investigating force and motion
- Objective: Plan a fair test of force on motion
- Objective: Measure distance traveled
- Vocabulary: variable, fair test, friction, distance
- Materials: toy car, ramp (board and books), tape measure, carpet, tile and towel surfaces
- Assessment: Lab report with data table and claim

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Landforms and bodies of water
- Objective: Identify landforms and water features in the local region
- Vocabulary: valley, mountain, coast, desert, river, lake
- Materials: physical map of California, clay or salt dough
- Assessment: Labeled regional map

### Art & Music 3: Contour and observational drawing
- Objective: Draw from observation using contour line
- Objective: Add detail to enhance meaning
- Vocabulary: contour, observe, blind contour, detail
- Materials: sketchbook, pencils, fruit, shoe, mirror
- Assessment: Observational drawings with written comparison

### PE 3: Fitness components and heart rate
- Objective: Name fitness components
- Objective: Measure heart rate
- Vocabulary: aerobic, strength, flexibility, heart rate, pulse
- Materials: timer, fitness log
- Assessment: Heart rate log

### Health 3: Breakfast, snacks and water
- Objective: Explain the benefits of breakfast, healthy snacks and water
- Vocabulary: breakfast, snack, hydration, energy
- Materials: measuring cup, water bottle, snack options
- Assessment: Healthy snack menu
