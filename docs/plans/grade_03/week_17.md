## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Myth plan* (90 min): Plan an original myth explaining a natural event (why the moon changes shape) with a story map.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Decompose shapes* (60 min): Split L-shaped figures into rectangles and add their areas.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Camouflage game* (40 min): Scatter colored paper 'insects' on grass and time how many a family member finds; record which colors survive.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Primary vs. secondary* (30 min): Sort 10 sources (diary, textbook, photo, encyclopedia, letter) into primary and secondary.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse a chosen song, recording yourself once.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Touches* (30 min): Practice 50 inside-foot 'toe taps' and inside-foot touches back and forth.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Growth mindset* (20 min): Turn 6 fixed-mindset statements ('I can't do this') into growth-mindset statements ('I can't do this yet').

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Draft* (90 min): Draft the myth using a god or hero character and dialogue; spell irregular words (said, laugh, though).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Two ways* (60 min): Find the area of the same figure two different ways and compare.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Analyze* (40 min): Graph results and explain the advantage of camouflage.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Photo analysis* (30 min): Analyze 3 historic photos of your town using a see-think-wonder chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Feedback* (30 min): Listen to the recording with a parent and choose 2 things to fix (pitch, words, dynamics).
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Cones* (30 min): Dribble through a cone line using both feet and small touches.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Choose a goal* (20 min): Set a personal goal (learn to ride without training wheels, read a chapter book, make a new friend).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 83, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise the myth for a clear lesson and strong verbs.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Room design* (60 min): Design a dream bedroom floor plan on grid paper and calculate each area.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Other advantages* (40 min): Research how longer beaks or thicker fur help individuals survive.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Newspapers* (30 min): Read a short historic newspaper article about your area on Chronicling America.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Compose* (30 min): Compose an 8-beat melody using do-re-mi-sol-la in Chrome Music Lab Song Maker.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Stop* (30 min): Dribble and stop the ball with the sole of the foot on a signal.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Steps and supports* (20 min): List 4 steps toward the goal and the people who can support you.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 84, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Present* (90 min): Read the myth aloud with expression to an audience.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Garden problems* (60 min): Solve area word problems about garden plots and patios.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Write* (40 min): Explain how a variation could help an animal find a mate or food.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Then and now* (30 min): Take a photo of the same spot as a historic picture and compare them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Etiquette* (30 min): Discuss performer and audience etiquette (bow, listen quietly, applaud).
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Soccer tag* (30 min): Play soccer dribble tag, protecting the ball from a defender.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Track* (20 min): Track progress on a 2-week calendar and note obstacles.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 85, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Unit 4 assessment* (90 min): Complete the Unit 4 test on myths, agreement and capitalization.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit 4 assessment* (60 min): Complete the Unit 4 test on area concepts and composite figures.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Unit 5 assessment* (40 min): Complete the unit quiz on traits and variation.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write what changed and what stayed the same, citing both photos.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Unit 4 check* (30 min): Perform the song and your melody for family using proper concert etiquette.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a timed foot-dribble course and record your time.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Unit 3 check* (20 min): Complete a quiz on feelings, stress, friendship, conflict and bullying.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Writing a modern myth
- Objective: Write an original pourquoi myth
- Objective: Use domain words
- Objective: Present a myth
- Vocabulary: pourquoi, origin, narrator
- Materials: story map, myth word bank
- Assessment: Unit 4 test; original myth rubric

### Math 3: Area of composite figures
- Objective: Find areas of rectilinear figures by decomposing
- Objective: Solve real-world area problems
- Vocabulary: rectilinear, decompose, composite figure
- Materials: grid paper, colored pencils
- Assessment: Unit 4 test

### Science 3: Variation and survival advantage
- Objective: Explain how variation may provide advantages for survival
- Vocabulary: advantage, camouflage, adaptation
- Materials: colored paper bits, green and brown backgrounds
- Assessment: Unit 5 quiz; camouflage data analysis

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Primary sources: photos and newspapers
- Objective: Distinguish primary and secondary sources
- Objective: Use them to trace change
- Vocabulary: primary source, secondary source, newspaper, archive
- Materials: Chronicling America, Calisphere, camera
- Assessment: Then-and-now comparison

### Art & Music 3: Melody performance
- Objective: Refine a song through feedback
- Objective: Compose a short melody
- Vocabulary: rehearse, perform, audience etiquette
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Song Maker, song sheet
- Assessment: Song and melody performance

### PE 3: Foot dribbling
- Objective: Dribble with the feet using soft touches
- Vocabulary: inside of foot, touch, control
- Materials: soccer ball, cones
- Assessment: Timed foot-dribble course

### Health 3: Personal goals and growth mindset
- Objective: Set a personal goal and identify steps and supports
- Vocabulary: goal, steps, support, growth mindset, 'yet'
- Materials: goal worksheet
- Assessment: Unit 3 quiz; goal worksheet
