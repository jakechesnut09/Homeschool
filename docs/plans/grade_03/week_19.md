## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Rossetti* (90 min): Read 'Who Has Seen the Wind?' and sketch the images the poet creates.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Even and odd* (60 min): Investigate whether sums and products of evens and odds are even or odd.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Fossil clues* (40 min): Examine fossil cards (fish, fern, shark tooth) and infer past environments.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Early businesses* (30 min): Research 3 early businesses in your town (general store, mill, farm, cannery).
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Patterns* (30 min): Photograph or sketch 5 patterns found in fabrics, tiles and nature.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Open space* (30 min): Practice moving to open space to receive a pass in a 2-person keep-away.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Pedestrian* (20 min): Walk a neighborhood route with an adult practicing stop-look-listen and crosswalk rules.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 92, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Hughes* (90 min): Read 'April Rain Song' and list sensory words; spell VCV words (cab-in, ti-ger).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Table patterns* (60 min): Find and color patterns in the addition table and write rules.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Rock layers* (40 min): Build a layered sandwich model of rock layers and discuss older vs. newer layers.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Entrepreneur* (30 min): Read about a local entrepreneur and the risks they took to start a business.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Stamp prints* (30 min): Carve potato or sponge stamps and print an alternating pattern border.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Guarding* (30 min): Practice defensive stance and staying between the attacker and the goal.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Helmet fit* (20 min): Fit your helmet using the eyes-ears-mouth test from Safe Kids.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 93, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Shades of meaning* (90 min): Order related words on a line (whisper, say, shout; think, believe, know).
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Input/output* (60 min): Complete input-output tables and name the rule.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Desert ocean* (40 min): Investigate why marine fossils are found in deserts and mountains.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Private property* (30 min): Discuss why owning land and businesses encouraged settlers to build and invest.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Foam plate* (30 min): Draw a design firmly into a foam tray with a pencil to make a print plate.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *2v1* (30 min): Play 2v1 games where attackers use passes to beat one defender.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Bike rules* (20 min): Learn bike hand signals and ride a safe route, signaling turns.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 94, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Free verse* (90 min): Write a free-verse poem about weather using sensory words.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Explain it* (60 min): Write an explanation of why 4 times any number is always even.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Data table* (40 min): Complete a data table matching fossils to environments.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Then and now* (30 min): Compare early industries with today's major employers in a T-chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Print edition* (30 min): Roll paint onto the plate and print an edition of 3 prints, signing and numbering each.
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Mini game* (30 min): Play a mini soccer or basketball game with family or friends.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Car safety* (20 min): Learn California booster seat rules and the 5-step seat belt fit test.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 95, ~300 min)

- [ ] **ELA 3** — *Review & check* (90 min): Explain in writing how one poet's word choice creates a feeling.
- [ ] **Math 3** — *Unit 5 assessment* (60 min): Complete a short test on two-step problems and patterns plus a mixed fact check.
- [ ] **Science 3** — *Review & check* (40 min): Write a claim about a past environment using fossil evidence.
- [ ] **Continuity and Change (Local History)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain in writing how one early business shaped today's local economy.
- [ ] **Art & Music 3** — *Review* (30 min): Choose your best print and explain why using criteria (clear lines, even ink).
- [ ] **PE 3** — *Review* (30 min): Write about one strategy you used and one fair-play moment.
- [ ] **Health 3** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a pedestrian, bike and passenger safety checklist.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 3: Imagery and word choice
- Objective: Explain how word choice creates images
- Objective: Distinguish shades of meaning
- Objective: Write free verse
- Vocabulary: imagery, sensory words, free verse, shades of meaning
- Materials: 'Who Has Seen the Wind?' by Christina Rossetti, 'April Rain Song' by Langston Hughes (library anthology)
- Assessment: Word choice response; shades of meaning check

### Math 3: Arithmetic patterns
- Objective: Identify and explain patterns in tables
- Objective: Use properties to justify patterns
- Vocabulary: pattern, even, odd, rule
- Materials: addition and multiplication tables
- Assessment: Unit 5 test

### Science 3: Fossils as evidence of past environments
- Objective: Analyze fossil data to infer past environments
- Vocabulary: environment, evidence, marine, infer
- Materials: fossil picture cards, rock layer diagram
- Assessment: Fossil evidence claim

### Continuity and Change (Local History): Early economy and entrepreneurs
- Objective: Describe economies established by settlers and their influence today
- Vocabulary: entrepreneur, private property, business, industry
- Materials: local business histories, historic photos
- Assessment: Economy explanation

### Art & Music 3: Pattern and printmaking
- Objective: Create repeating patterns through relief printmaking
- Vocabulary: print, relief, pattern, repeat, edition
- Materials: foam meat trays (washed), pencils, tempera paint, roller or sponge
- Assessment: Print edition

### PE 3: Small-sided games
- Objective: Use simple offense and defense strategies
- Vocabulary: offense, defense, open space, guard
- Materials: ball, cones
- Assessment: Game play observation and reflection

### Health 3: Pedestrian, bike and passenger safety
- Objective: Practice safety rules for walking, biking and riding in vehicles
- Vocabulary: pedestrian, crosswalk, helmet, seat belt, booster seat
- Materials: bike helmet, bicycle
- Assessment: Safety checklist
