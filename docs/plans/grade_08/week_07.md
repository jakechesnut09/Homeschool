## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 4* (75 min): Read chapter 4 and analyze the turning point in the park; list the consequences Ponyboy and Johnny face.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Slope triangles* (60 min): Draw three different slope triangles on the same line and show they are similar, so rise/run is equal for each.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Gravitational PE* (50 min): Drop a ball from 3 heights onto a tray of flour or sand and measure crater size; model energy stored by height.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Structure of the Constitution* (50 min): Scavenger hunt through Articles I–VII to locate powers of each branch.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *The boot* (45 min): Learn the stem-change 'boot' pattern and conjugate poder and dormir, noting nosotros/vosotros don't change.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Color wheel* (40 min): Mix and paint a 12-part color wheel of primary, secondary and tertiary colors.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Draw plays* (40 min): Draw three plays (one run, two pass) on a play card with routes for each player.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Energy balance* (25 min): Learn how calories in and out relate to growth; calculate a sample MyPlate Plan for your age and activity.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 5 & Frost* (75 min): Read chapter 5 and the poem 'Nothing Gold Can Stay'; annotate the poem's imagery and connect 'gold' to Ponyboy and Johnny.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Finding slope* (60 min): Calculate the slope of 10 lines on grids using any two points; check with Desmos.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Elastic PE* (50 min): Launch rubber bands stretched 5, 10 and 15 cm and measure distance traveled; relate stretch to stored energy.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Checks & balances* (50 min): Create a diagram of checks between branches with real examples (veto, judicial review).
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *More verbs* (45 min): Add costar, volver, encontrar, almorzar, and jugar (u→ue) and complete a Conjuguemos stem-changer drill.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Tints, shades, tones* (40 min): Paint a 7-step tint-to-shade strip for one hue and a tone strip adding gray.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Run plays* (40 min): Walk through, then run the plays at full speed in 3-v-3 or 2-v-2.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Key nutrients* (25 min): Research calcium, iron and vitamin D needs during adolescence and list three food sources each.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 6* (75 min): Read chapter 6 and analyze the church fire as a moment that changes how characters see themselves.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Writing equations from slope* (60 min): Use a point (x, y) on a line through (0, 0) or (0, b) and similar triangles to derive y = mx and y = mx + b.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Magnetic PE* (50 min): Measure the distance at which a paper clip jumps to a magnet; model how arrangement changes stored energy with diagrams.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Federalism* (50 min): Sort powers into federal, state and shared; discuss enumerated vs implied powers.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Sleep survey* (45 min): Ask family ¿Cuántas horas duermes? and ¿A qué hora vuelves a casa? and graph the results.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Color schemes* (40 min): Paint the same simple landscape thumbnail 4 times in complementary, analogous, monochromatic and warm/cool schemes.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Defense* (40 min): Practice person-to-person and zone coverage and compare which stops short passes better.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Food log* (25 min): Log one day of meals and compare with your MyPlate Plan.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 34, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Allusion study* (75 min): Explain the allusions to 'Gone with the Wind' and Frost and how each deepens the theme.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Unit 2 review* (60 min): Mixed review of dilations, similarity and slope with a partner-check answer key.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Modeling systems* (50 min): Draw energy models (before/after diagrams) for a roller coaster, bow and arrow and two magnets.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *How a bill becomes law & the press* (50 min): Trace a current bill on congress.gov; compare two news articles on it to evaluate the role of a free press.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Puedo...* (45 min): Write 10 sentences about what you can and cannot do in your house using poder + infinitive.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Color and mood in art* (40 min): Compare Picasso's 'The Old Guitarist' (Blue Period) and Matisse's 'The Dessert: Harmony in Red' and explain how color shapes mood.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Game* (40 min): Play a 5-on-5 or small-sided flag football game with rotating quarterbacks.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Fix it* (25 min): Identify one nutrient gap and plan three snacks to fill it.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: The Outsiders, chapters 4–6: turning point and allusion
- Objective: Analyze how the church scene and Frost's poem develop theme
- Objective: Explain the impact of allusions
- Vocabulary: allusion, turning point, symbol, gallant, Robert Frost
- Materials: The Outsiders, Robert Frost, 'Nothing Gold Can Stay' (1923)
- Assessment: Theme paragraph.

### Mathematics 8: Slope from similar triangles
- Objective: Use similar slope triangles to explain why slope is constant on a line
- Objective: Calculate slope from a graph
- Vocabulary: slope, slope triangle, rise, run, constant
- Materials: graph paper, colored pencils, Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Potential energy and fields
- Objective: Model gravitational, elastic and magnetic potential energy
- Objective: Explain how changing distance between interacting objects changes stored energy
- Vocabulary: potential energy, gravitational potential energy, elastic potential energy, magnetic field, system
- Materials: rubber bands, magnets, paper clips, books, ruler
- Assessment: Potential energy model diagrams and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: How the Constitution works
- Objective: Explain federalism, separation of powers and checks and balances
- Objective: Describe how a bill becomes law and how citizens participate
- Vocabulary: federalism, separation of powers, checks and balances, enumerated powers, implied powers, necessary and proper clause, free press
- Materials: Constitution transcript, current newspaper or news site
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Spanish 1B: Stem-changing verbs o→ue and u→ue
- Objective: Conjugate poder, dormir, costar, and jugar
- Objective: Explain the boot pattern
- Vocabulary: poder, dormir, costar, volver, encontrar, jugar, puedo, duermo
- Materials: boot-chart template, Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: Stem-changer quiz (80%+).

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Color theory and mood
- Objective: Mix a 12-part color wheel and value scales
- Objective: Use color schemes intentionally to express mood
- Vocabulary: hue, tint, shade, tone, complementary, analogous, monochromatic, warm, cool
- Materials: acrylic or tempera paints in red, yellow, blue, white, black, brushes, palette, heavy paper
- Assessment: Color wheel, scheme studies and quiz.

### Physical Education 8: Flag football: plays and game play
- Objective: Design and run offensive plays
- Objective: Use zone and person-to-person pass defense
- Vocabulary: play, huddle, zone defense, person-to-person defense, interception
- Materials: football, flag belts, cones, whiteboard or paper
- Assessment: Play cards and rules quiz.

### Health Education 8: Energy balance and teen nutrient needs
- Objective: Explain energy balance
- Objective: Identify nutrients critical during growth spurts
- Vocabulary: calorie, energy balance, calcium, iron, metabolism
- Materials: MyPlate Plan tool, nutrition labels
- Assessment: Food log analysis and quiz.
