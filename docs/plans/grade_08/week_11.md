## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Rhetorical appeals* (75 min): Learn ethos, pathos and logos and label the dominant appeal in 6 modern advertisements with a sentence of explanation each.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Points on a line* (60 min): Test whether 8 ordered pairs are solutions of a given equation and explain the connection to points on its graph.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Moon journal* (50 min): Begin a 14-day moon observation log (sketch shape and time nightly); research tonight's phase.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Causes* (50 min): Read about impressment and Tecumseh's confederacy; chart causes of the war.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *e→ie* (45 min): Learn pensar, querer, preferir, empezar, entender, cerrar in the boot pattern and complete a Conjuguemos drill.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Treble clef* (40 min): Learn line and space names in treble clef and reach 20 correct in a row on the musictheory.net note trainer.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Grip and ready* (40 min): Learn the eastern forehand grip and ready position; rally against a wall for 10 minutes.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Family preview* (25 min): Parent and student preview the unit, discuss family values and decide on any opt-out. (Sexual health lesson: parents may opt out under CA Ed. Code 51938; parent previews materials.)

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Read the speech* (75 min): Read Henry's speech aloud and annotate rhetorical questions, repetition and each appeal in the margins.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Coordinates and constraints* (60 min): Model a budget like $2 apples and $3 pears with $12 by writing 2x + 3y = 12; graph using intercepts.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Modeling phases* (50 min): In a dark room, hold a ball on a pencil at arm's length around a lamp to model all 8 phases; sketch each.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Major battles* (50 min): Map major battles (Washington burned, Fort McHenry, New Orleans).
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Preferences* (45 min): Write 10 sentences comparing clothing choices: Prefiero la camisa roja, pero mi hermano quiere la azul.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Bass clef* (40 min): Learn bass clef names and repeat the note-trainer goal.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Forehand* (40 min): Practice drop-and-hit forehands, focusing on low-to-high swing for topspin.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Grade 7 recap* (25 min): Review puberty, hormones and reproductive anatomy from grade 7 with a labeling warm-up.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Rhetoric: Patrick Henry's 'Give Me Liberty'
- Objective: Identify ethos, pathos and logos in a speech
- Objective: Evaluate a speaker's claims and reasoning
- Objective: Recognize indicative and imperative mood
- Vocabulary: rhetoric, ethos, pathos, logos, rhetorical question, indicative mood, imperative mood
- Materials: Patrick Henry, 'Speech to the Second Virginia Convention' (1775), 6 printed or online ads
- Assessment: Appeals and moods quiz; oral delivery.

### Mathematics 8: Solutions to linear equations in two variables
- Objective: Understand that points on a line are solutions to its equation
- Objective: Use linear equations such as 2x + 3y = 12 to model situations
- Vocabulary: solution, standard form, coordinates, intercepts
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Moon phases and eclipses
- Objective: Model the cause of lunar phases
- Objective: Explain solar and lunar eclipses
- Vocabulary: lunar phase, waxing, waning, gibbous, crescent, solar eclipse, lunar eclipse, umbra
- Materials: lamp without shade, foam or Styrofoam ball on a pencil, moon observation chart
- Assessment: Phase model sketches and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The War of 1812 and the Monroe Doctrine
- Objective: Explain the causes and outcomes of the War of 1812
- Objective: Analyze the Monroe Doctrine
- Vocabulary: impressment, War Hawks, Tecumseh, Treaty of Ghent, Monroe Doctrine, nationalism
- Materials: 'The Star-Spangled Banner' text, map of War of 1812
- Assessment: Foreign policy quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Stem-changing e→ie verbs: pensar, querer, preferir
- Objective: Conjugate e→ie verbs
- Objective: Express preferences and intentions about clothes
- Vocabulary: pensar, querer, preferir, empezar, entender, cerrar, pienso, prefiero
- Materials: Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: Stem-changer quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Pitch reading on the staff
- Objective: Identify notes in treble and bass clefs
- Objective: Read simple melodies
- Vocabulary: staff, treble clef, bass clef, ledger line, sharp, flat, natural, interval
- Materials: musictheory.net note-identification exercise, keyboard app or piano
- Assessment: Note-naming and interval quiz.

### Physical Education 8: Tennis: forehand, backhand and serve
- Objective: Perform the forehand and two-hand backhand
- Objective: Serve overhand into a target box
- Vocabulary: forehand, backhand, ready position, topspin
- Materials: tennis racket, tennis balls, wall or court, chalk
- Assessment: Forehand checklist from video.

### Health Education 8: Introduction: sexual health, values and trusted adults
- Objective: Explain the purpose of CHYA instruction and family opt-out
- Objective: Identify trusted adults and valid sources
- Vocabulary: California Healthy Youth Act, values, valid source, confidential
- Materials: parent letter/preview of unit materials, KidsHealth for Teens
- Assessment: Trusted-adult/resource list.
