## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Socratic seminar* (75 min): Discuss 'Why was reading so dangerous to slaveholders?' citing at least three passages.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Linear function models* (60 min): Build functions for situations such as a phone battery draining 8% per hour from 100%; interpret rate and initial value.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Encoding messages* (50 min): Send a message to a family member using flashlight blinks in binary code; discuss errors.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Causes of war* (50 min): Compare President Polk's 1846 war message with Lincoln's 'spot resolutions' and decide whose claim is better supported.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Vacation words* (45 min): Learn 20 vacation places and activities and caption your own travel photos.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Vocal warm-ups* (40 min): Practice breathing, projection and 5 tongue twisters ('red leather, yellow leather'); record before and after.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Mid-year re-test* (40 min): Retest the two fitness items tied to your SMART goals.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Grade 7 recap* (25 min): Review alcohol's effects on the body from grade 7 with a quick quiz.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 92, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Plan* (75 min): Outline an informative essay with a controlling idea, three subtopics and evidence for each.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Qualitative graphs* (60 min): Match 6 stories (a bike ride with a stop, a filling bathtub) to graphs and describe where they increase, decrease or stay constant.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Analog vs digital* (50 min): Read about records, cassettes and digital audio; list advantages of each in a T-chart.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo* (50 min): Read Articles 8 and 9 and evaluate whether promises of property and citizenship to Mexican residents were kept.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Preterite -ar review* (45 min): Write 10 sentences about a past trip using -ar preterite verbs (visité, nadé, tomé).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Physical warm-ups* (40 min): Do a mirror exercise and a walk exploring how different characters lead with head, chest or hips.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Analyze* (40 min): Compare results with week 1 and identify whether you progressed, plateaued or declined.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *BAC* (25 min): Learn factors affecting BAC (size, sex, food, time) and why the teen brain is more vulnerable.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 93, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Draft* (75 min): Draft the essay, integrating quotations and paraphrases with parenthetical page citations.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Sketching graphs* (60 min): Sketch graphs from verbal descriptions and write a story for 3 given graphs.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Telephone game* (50 min): Play 'telephone' with a spoken message vs a written numeric code to model noise in analog vs digital transmission.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *The Gold Rush* (50 min): Read a forty-niner's letter home and examine Calisphere photos; chart impacts on Native Californians, Californios and Chinese miners.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Travel brochure* (45 min): Read a Spanish tourism brochure for Puerto Rico and list five attractions with descriptions.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Tableau* (40 min): Create 3 frozen tableaux showing a scene from a fairy tale and photograph them.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Overtraining* (40 min): Learn signs of overtraining and injury (persistent soreness, fatigue) and how rest prevents them.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Impairment* (25 min): Try a coordination task while 'impaired' with drunk-buster-style challenges (walking a line while counting backward).

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Informative essay on Douglass
- Objective: Write an informative essay explaining how Douglass presents literacy as a path to freedom
- Objective: Participate in a text-based seminar
- Vocabulary: informative essay, synthesis, paraphrase, citation
- Materials: Narrative, informative essay template
- Assessment: Informative essay (rubric) and Unit 4 test.

### Mathematics 8: Linear functions, rates of change, and graphs of situations
- Objective: Model situations with linear functions and interpret rate of change
- Objective: Describe and sketch qualitative and piecewise graphs
- Vocabulary: piecewise function, increasing, decreasing, constant, qualitative graph
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Quiz on function models and graph interpretation.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Analog vs digital signals
- Objective: Compare analog and digital signals
- Objective: Explain why digital signals are more reliable for transmitting information
- Vocabulary: analog signal, digital signal, binary, encode, noise, bandwidth
- Materials: flashlight, paper and pencil, phone
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The Mexican-American War and the Gold Rush
- Objective: Explain causes and outcomes of the Mexican-American War
- Objective: Describe the Gold Rush and its effects on Californians
- Vocabulary: Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo, Mexican Cession, Gadsden Purchase, Gold Rush, forty-niners, Foreign Miners' Tax
- Materials: Polk's war message excerpt, Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo Articles 8–9, Calisphere Gold Rush photos
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Spanish 1B: Las vacaciones: places and activities
- Objective: Name vacation places and activities
- Objective: Talk about past trips
- Vocabulary: el museo, el parque de diversiones, el zoológico, el lago, las montañas, tomar el sol, bucear, visitar
- Materials: travel photos, world map
- Assessment: Vacation vocabulary quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Theatre warm-ups and the actor's instrument
- Objective: Use vocal and physical warm-ups
- Objective: Explore how voice and body create character
- Vocabulary: projection, articulation, diction, posture, gesture, tableau
- Materials: open space, tongue-twister list, mirror
- Assessment: Character introduction performance.

### Physical Education 8: Semester fitness check and plan revision
- Objective: Re-check progress on SMART goals
- Objective: Revise activity plan for semester 2
- Vocabulary: plateau, overtraining, progress
- Materials: fitness journal, stopwatch, mat
- Assessment: Revised goals and mid-year progress report.

### Health Education 8: Alcohol, BAC and impaired driving
- Objective: Explain BAC and impaired judgment
- Objective: Describe CA laws for minors and DUI
- Vocabulary: blood alcohol concentration, impairment, zero tolerance, DUI
- Materials: BAC factor chart, CA DMV handbook excerpt
- Assessment: Safe ride plan and quiz.
