## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Background & preface* (75 min): Read a short biography of Douglass and William Lloyd Garrison's preface; note why the book needed a white abolitionist's endorsement in 1845.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Substitution with y =* (60 min): Solve systems where one equation is solved for y (y = 2x + 1 and y = -x + 7) by setting expressions equal.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Vibrations* (50 min): Stretch rubber bands over a tissue box and pluck; change tension and length to change pitch and record observations.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Jackson's rise* (50 min): Compare voter-turnout data from 1824 and 1828 and explain how the end of property requirements expanded white male suffrage.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Spelling rules* (45 min): Learn why buscar→busqué, pagar→pagué, empezar→empecé and practice 15 yo forms.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Ragtime* (40 min): Listen to Scott Joplin's 'Maple Leaf Rag' and identify syncopation.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Muscle map* (40 min): Label a muscle chart with 10 major muscles and one exercise for each.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Spectrum* (25 min): Sort relationship behaviors onto a healthy-unhealthy-abusive spectrum.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 77, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapters 1–2* (75 min): Read chapters 1–2 and annotate how Douglass describes not knowing his birthday and being separated from his mother.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Substitution in general* (60 min): Solve 6 systems by substitution where you must rearrange one equation first; check with Desmos.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Sound through materials* (50 min): Make a cup-and-string telephone and compare how well sound travels through string, air and a table.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *The Bank War* (50 min): Read an excerpt from Jackson's 1832 veto message and summarize his argument against the Second Bank.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *StudySpanish practice* (45 min): Complete the StudySpanish.com preterite regular-verb exercises and correct errors.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Louis Armstrong* (40 min): Listen to 'West End Blues' and analyze the trumpet improvisation.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Lower body* (40 min): Learn proper squat, lunge and glute bridge form; do 3 sets of 10-12 reps.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Red flags* (25 min): Identify digital red flags such as checking phones, demanding passwords and constant texting.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Douglass's Narrative: preface and chapters 1–4
- Objective: Determine Douglass's purpose and audience
- Objective: Analyze how he connects individuals and events to expose slavery's cruelty
- Vocabulary: autobiography, abolitionist, dehumanize, preface, overseer
- Materials: Frederick Douglass, 'Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass, an American Slave' (1845, Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Reading quiz and purpose paragraph.

### Mathematics 8: Solving systems algebraically
- Objective: Solve systems by substitution
- Objective: Solve systems by inspection when lines are parallel or identical
- Vocabulary: substitution, inspection, elimination
- Materials: notebook, Desmos for checking
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Sound waves
- Objective: Explain that sound is a mechanical wave that needs a medium
- Objective: Relate pitch to frequency and loudness to amplitude
- Vocabulary: sound wave, medium, pitch, loudness, decibel, vibration
- Materials: paper cups, string, rubber bands, tissue box, glasses of water, spoon
- Assessment: Sound absorption investigation report.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Jacksonian democracy and Indian Removal
- Objective: Analyze Jackson's presidency and the expansion of suffrage
- Objective: Explain the Indian Removal Act and the Trail of Tears
- Vocabulary: Jacksonian democracy, spoils system, Indian Removal Act, Trail of Tears, Worcester v. Georgia, Bank veto
- Materials: Cherokee Memorial (1830) excerpt, Jackson's Second Annual Message, map of removal routes
- Assessment: Document-based paragraph.

### Spanish 1B: Spelling changes: -car, -gar, -zar in the preterite
- Objective: Apply yo-form spelling changes
- Objective: Narrate a shopping trip
- Vocabulary: busqué, pagué, empecé, toqué, llegué, almorcé, practiqué
- Materials: StudySpanish.com
- Assessment: Preterite spelling-change quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Jazz and ragtime
- Objective: Identify ragtime, swing and improvisation
- Objective: Explain how jazz musicians apply the elements of music
- Vocabulary: ragtime, swing, improvisation, big band, scat, bebop
- Materials: recordings (Scott Joplin, Louis Armstrong, Duke Ellington, Ella Fitzgerald), PBS LearningMedia jazz videos
- Assessment: Listening quiz.

### Physical Education 8: Major muscles and safe resistance training
- Objective: Identify 10 major muscle groups
- Objective: Perform exercises with safe technique
- Vocabulary: agonist, antagonist, repetition, set, resistance band
- Materials: resistance bands, light dumbbells or water jugs, mat, muscle chart
- Assessment: Technique checklist for six exercises.

### Health Education 8: Healthy and unhealthy dating relationships
- Objective: Compare healthy, unhealthy and abusive relationships
- Objective: Define affirmative consent
- Vocabulary: affirmative consent, dating violence, jealousy, controlling behavior, red flag
- Materials: relationship spectrum chart, loveisrespect.org info
- Assessment: Relationship analysis.
