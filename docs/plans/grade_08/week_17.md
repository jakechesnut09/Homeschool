## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapters 5–6* (75 min): Read chapters 5–6 and analyze Mr. Auld's warning that learning would 'spoil' a slave and Douglass's response: 'the pathway from slavery to freedom.'
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Input-output machines* (60 min): Play 'guess my rule' with a family member using input/output cards, then decide which rules are functions.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Light behavior sort* (50 min): Shine a flashlight through clear plastic, wax paper, foil and cardboard; classify as transparent, translucent or opaque.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Manifest Destiny* (50 min): Analyze John Gast's 'American Progress' painting and John O'Sullivan's 1845 essay; list who is shown and who is missing.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *DOPs* (45 min): Learn lo/la/los/las and replace the object in 15 sentences: Compré la camisa → La compré.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Motifs* (40 min): Create 4 two-measure motifs in Song Maker and choose the strongest.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Endurance vs. strength* (40 min): Compare rep ranges and rest periods for endurance and strength; do an endurance circuit (15-20 reps).
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *What is trafficking* (25 min): Learn what human trafficking is and that it happens in California, often by someone the victim knows.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 7* (75 min): Read chapter 7 and list every strategy Douglass used to learn to read and write, including trading bread for lessons.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Is it a function?* (60 min): Classify 10 relations (tables, mapping diagrams, graphs, statements like 'a person's age -> height') as functions or not, using the vertical line test.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Reflection* (50 min): Use a mirror and flashlight to test the law of reflection; measure angles with a protractor.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Trails west* (50 min): Map the Oregon, California, Mormon and Santa Fe trails and calculate travel times at 15 miles per day.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Placement* (45 min): Practice pronoun placement before conjugated verbs and attached to infinitives and progressives.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Phrases* (40 min): Develop the motif into an 8-measure phrase with a question-answer structure.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Balance* (40 min): Plan a full-body workout that pairs opposing muscles (chest/back, quads/hamstrings).
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Grooming* (25 min): Identify grooming and recruitment tactics online and in person (gifts, secrecy, isolation).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 83, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 8* (75 min): Read chapter 8 and analyze the word choice and tone of the passage about his grandmother.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Function notation & language* (60 min): Write statements like 'distance is a function of time' and evaluate functions from equations and graphs.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Refraction* (50 min): Observe a pencil in water and a coin that 'appears' in a cup when water is added; draw ray diagrams.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Pioneer women* (50 min): Read diary excerpts from women on the Oregon Trail and a short biography of Annie Bidwell; explain why Wyoming granted women suffrage in 1869.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *¿Lo compraste?* (45 min): Answer a parent's questions about purchases using pronouns: ¿Compraste los zapatos? Sí, los compré.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Form* (40 min): Organize phrases into ABA form.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Try it* (40 min): Complete your designed workout and record sets, reps and perceived exertion.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Sextortion* (25 min): Learn what sextortion is, that it is never the victim's fault, and steps: stop, don't pay, save evidence, tell an adult, use NCMEC Take It Down.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Douglass's Narrative: chapters 5–8, literacy and freedom
- Objective: Trace the central idea that literacy is the pathway to freedom
- Objective: Use commas, ellipses and dashes to indicate pauses, breaks and omissions
- Vocabulary: literacy, pathway, emancipation, ellipsis, dash, The Columbian Orator
- Materials: Narrative, punctuation practice sheet
- Assessment: Summary and punctuation quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Introduction to functions
- Objective: Define a function as a rule with exactly one output for each input
- Objective: Identify functions from tables, graphs, mappings and descriptions
- Vocabulary: function, input, output, independent variable, dependent variable, domain
- Materials: index cards, graph paper
- Assessment: Quiz on identifying functions.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Light: reflection, refraction, absorption, transmission
- Objective: Model how light interacts with materials
- Objective: Explain reflection and refraction
- Vocabulary: reflection, refraction, absorption, transmission, transparent, translucent, opaque
- Materials: flashlight, mirror, glass of water, pencil, wax paper, aluminum foil, cardboard
- Assessment: Ray diagram quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Manifest Destiny and the trails west
- Objective: Explain the idea of Manifest Destiny and who it affected
- Objective: Describe the experiences of pioneers including women
- Vocabulary: Manifest Destiny, Oregon Trail, Mormon Trail, pioneer, Great Plains, water rights
- Materials: John Gast 'American Progress' (1872) image, pioneer diary excerpts, U.S. river map
- Assessment: Quiz and painting analysis.

### Spanish 1B: Direct object pronouns lo, la, los, las
- Objective: Replace direct objects with pronouns
- Objective: Place pronouns correctly
- Vocabulary: lo, la, los, las, lo compré, ¿Dónde la compraste?, voy a comprarlo
- Materials: shopping bag with items
- Assessment: Direct object pronoun quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Composing a melody
- Objective: Generate melodic phrases with expressive intent
- Objective: Organize phrases into a form
- Vocabulary: motif, phrase, antecedent, consequent, cadence, form
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Song Maker, staff paper
- Assessment: Composition draft.

### Physical Education 8: Designing a strength workout
- Objective: Balance agonist and antagonist exercises
- Objective: Apply sets, reps and rest for muscular endurance vs. strength
- Vocabulary: muscular endurance, muscular strength, rest interval, progression
- Materials: resistance bands, workout log
- Assessment: Written strength workout plan.

### Health Education 8: Human trafficking, sextortion and online exploitation
- Objective: Recognize recruitment tactics and warning signs
- Objective: Know how to get help
- Vocabulary: human trafficking, grooming, sextortion, exploitation, NCMEC
- Materials: CA DOJ or NCMEC Take It Down information
- Assessment: Safety tip card.
