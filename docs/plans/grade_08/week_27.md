## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Wiesel's Nobel speech* (75 min): Read Elie Wiesel's Nobel speech and analyze his claim about neutrality.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Powers of 10 on a number line* (60 min): Place numbers like 3 x 10^5 and 7 x 10^(-3) on number lines labeled with powers of 10.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *DNA structure* (50 min): Build a DNA ladder model using candy or beads for bases (A-T, C-G).
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Sherman's March* (50 min): Map Sherman's march from Atlanta to Savannah and debate whether total war was justified.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Plan* (45 min): Plan a small service activity (food drive, letters to seniors, park cleanup) and write the plan in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Casting & planning* (40 min): Cast family or friends and plan simple props and set.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Sun salutation* (40 min): Learn and perform a sun salutation sequence five times.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Data* (25 min): Analyze teen pedestrian and cyclist injury data.

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Conflicting accounts* (75 min): Compare two accounts of the Annex's discovery and identify discrepancies.

- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Distraction test* (25 min): Try texting while walking a cone path and record errors.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Remembering: Holocaust nonfiction and research
- Objective: Analyze conflicting accounts and interpretations
- Objective: Conduct a short research project
- Vocabulary: testimony, memoir, conflicting accounts, research question
- Materials: Elie Wiesel, Nobel acceptance speech (1986), USHMM survivor testimonies
- Assessment: Research profile and Unit 6 test.

### Mathematics 8: Powers of 10 and scientific notation
- Objective: Write large and small numbers in scientific notation
- Objective: Compare quantities using powers of 10
- Vocabulary: scientific notation, power of 10, order of magnitude
- Materials: NASA planetary fact sheet printout, calculator
- Assessment: Quiz on scientific notation.

### Science 8 (Integrated): DNA, genes and proteins
- Objective: Describe DNA structure and how genes code for proteins
- Objective: Extract DNA from strawberries
- Vocabulary: DNA, nucleotide, base pair, protein, amino acid, genetic code
- Materials: strawberries, zip bag, dish soap, salt, rubbing alcohol (cold), coffee filter, candy or beads for model
- Assessment: Lab report and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The end of the war
- Objective: Explain Sherman's March and Lee's surrender
- Objective: Analyze Lincoln's Second Inaugural Address
- Vocabulary: Sherman's March, total war, Appomattox Court House, Second Inaugural Address, assassination
- Materials: Lincoln's Second Inaugural Address (1865), casualty statistics
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Spanish 1B: Unit 6 project: A service report
- Objective: Plan and report on a service project
- Objective: Present to an audience
- Vocabulary: el proyecto, el informe, la donación, juntar
- Materials: materials for a small service project
- Assessment: Service report rubric and unit 6 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Staging a reading
- Objective: Rehearse and perform a staged reading of the original play
- Objective: Reflect on the process
- Vocabulary: staged reading, director, rehearsal, props
- Materials: scripts, simple props and costumes
- Assessment: Staged reading and reflection.

### Physical Education 8: Yoga and flexibility
- Objective: Perform a sun salutation sequence
- Objective: Explain static vs. PNF stretching
- Vocabulary: static stretch, PNF, range of motion, sun salutation
- Materials: yoga mat or towel, strap or towel
- Assessment: Yoga flow sequence and flexibility measurement.

### Health Education 8: Distracted walking, biking and safe rides
- Objective: Describe pedestrian and cycling risks
- Objective: Choose safe rides
- Vocabulary: distracted driving, blind spot, defensive walking
- Materials: traffic safety statistics, bike
- Assessment: Safety checklist.
