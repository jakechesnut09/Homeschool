## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Draft introduction & body* (75 min): Draft an introduction with a hook and claim and two body paragraphs, each with cited evidence and reasoning.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *On or off the line?* (60 min): Use 'two runners' or 'two water tanks' scenarios to find when two linear quantities are equal using tables and graphs.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Making waves* (50 min): Use a Slinky or rope to make transverse and longitudinal waves; sketch and label each.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Narratives of slavery* (50 min): Read two WPA Slave Narrative interviews and a chapter excerpt from Harriet Jacobs's 'Incidents in the Life of a Slave Girl'; note family, labor and punishment details.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Preterite -ar* (45 min): Learn the preterite -ar endings (-é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -asteis, -aron) and conjugate comprar, hablar, and mirar.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Spirituals* (40 min): Listen to the Fisk Jubilee Singers and analyze 'Swing Low, Sweet Chariot' (connects to HSS slavery unit).
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Short-term responses* (40 min): Record heart rate, breathing rate and skin temperature at rest, after 5 minutes of jogging, and after 5 minutes of rest.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Vocabulary* (25 min): Define gender identity, gender expression and sexual orientation using a medically accurate glossary; discuss with a parent.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Counterclaim & conclusion* (75 min): Draft a counterclaim-and-rebuttal paragraph and a conclusion with a call to action.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Graphing systems* (60 min): Graph 6 systems by hand and identify the intersection point; verify it in both equations.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Wave on a String* (50 min): Use PhET to change amplitude and frequency; measure wavelength and record how frequency affects wavelength.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Resistance and rebellion* (50 min): Read excerpts from 'The Confessions of Nat Turner' (1831) and accounts of Denmark Vesey's plan; list Southern legal responses.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Time expressions* (45 min): Learn ayer, anoche, la semana pasada, hace dos días and write 10 sentences about last weekend.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Work songs* (40 min): Listen to work songs and identify call-and-response patterns.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Cardiorespiratory* (40 min): Learn how stroke volume and cardiac output increase with training; do a 25-minute interval run.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Stereotypes* (25 min): Identify gender stereotypes in ads, toys and media and how they limit people.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 73, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Revise* (75 min): Revise with a parent or peer using a rubric, focusing on whether every piece of evidence clearly supports the claim.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Special systems* (60 min): Graph systems with parallel lines and identical lines and connect them to no solution and infinitely many solutions.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Amplitude and energy* (50 min): Drop a pebble from different heights into a pan of water and observe wave height; relate amplitude to energy.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Culture and spirituals* (50 min): Listen to 'Wade in the Water' and 'Follow the Drinking Gourd' and analyze possible double meanings in the lyrics.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Present vs. preterite* (45 min): Contrast nosotros forms (hablamos) and identify present or past from context in 15 sentences.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *The blues* (40 min): Listen to Bessie Smith and B.B. King; identify 12-bar form and AAB lyric pattern.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Muscles and bones* (40 min): Explain how resistance exercise builds muscle and bone density; do a plyometric circuit (jumps, skips).
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Respect* (25 min): Discuss how bullying based on identity harms mental health and practice upstander responses.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Argument essay workshop
- Objective: Write an argument essay with a clear claim, evidence, counterclaim and rebuttal
- Objective: Revise for logic and clarity
- Vocabulary: thesis, counterclaim, rebuttal, evidence, call to action
- Materials: source notes, argument essay template
- Assessment: Argument essay (rubric) and Unit 3 test.

### Mathematics 8: Systems of linear equations by graphing
- Objective: Interpret the intersection of two lines as the solution of a system
- Objective: Estimate solutions by graphing
- Vocabulary: system of equations, point of intersection, solution of a system
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Quiz on graphing systems.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Wave properties
- Objective: Identify wavelength, amplitude and frequency
- Objective: Relate amplitude to energy
- Vocabulary: wave, crest, trough, wavelength, amplitude, frequency, transverse, longitudinal
- Materials: Slinky or long rope, ruler, pan of water, PhET Wave on a String
- Assessment: Wave diagram labels and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Life under slavery and resistance
- Objective: Describe life under slavery using first-person sources
- Objective: Analyze forms of resistance from everyday acts to rebellion
- Vocabulary: enslaved, resistance, Nat Turner's Rebellion, Denmark Vesey, spirituals, manumission
- Materials: WPA Slave Narratives excerpts (Library of Congress), Harriet Jacobs excerpt, spiritual lyrics
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Spanish 1B: ¿Qué compraste? Preterite of regular -ar verbs
- Objective: Narrate completed past actions with -ar verbs
- Objective: Use past time expressions
- Vocabulary: compré, compraste, compró, ayer, anoche, la semana pasada, el año pasado, hace + tiempo
- Materials: Conjuguemos account, receipts
- Assessment: Preterite -ar quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Spirituals, work songs and the blues
- Objective: Trace the roots of American music in African American traditions
- Objective: Identify blues form and call and response
- Vocabulary: spiritual, work song, call and response, blues scale, blue note, 12-bar blues
- Materials: recordings via Library of Congress or Smithsonian Folkways, keyboard
- Assessment: Blues lyric and performance.

### Physical Education 8: Body systems and exercise
- Objective: Explain short-term responses to exercise
- Objective: Explain long-term training adaptations
- Vocabulary: cardiac output, stroke volume, adaptation, oxygen debt
- Materials: heart-rate monitor or watch, fitness journal
- Assessment: Body systems diagram.

### Health Education 8: Gender identity, sexual orientation and respect
- Objective: Define gender identity, gender expression and sexual orientation
- Objective: Promote respect and reduce harassment
- Vocabulary: gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, stereotype, inclusion
- Materials: CHYA-aligned glossary, scenario cards
- Assessment: Respect paragraph.
