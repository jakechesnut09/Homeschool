## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Dunbar* (75 min): Read 'Sympathy' and 'We Wear the Mask'; analyze the caged-bird and mask metaphors and the effect of the refrains.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Sphere volume* (60 min): Compare a sphere to a cylinder of the same radius and height 2r to justify V = (4/3) pi r^3; find volumes of 4 balls.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Timeline to scale* (50 min): Make a 4.6 m timeline (1 mm = 1 million years) and mark major events like first life and dinosaurs.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Missouri Compromise* (50 min): Color a map showing the 36°30' line and explain Henry Clay's role in balancing free and slave states.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Ir/ser preterite* (45 min): Learn that ir and ser share preterite forms and determine meaning from context in 15 sentences.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Selecting* (40 min): Read 4 age-appropriate monologues and choose one.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Bocce/bean bag* (40 min): Play bocce-style target toss, using underhand toss and controlled force.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Prescription stimulants* (25 min): Learn why 'study drugs' are risky when misused and the law on sharing prescriptions.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *'The New Colossus'* (75 min): Mark the rhyme scheme of the Petrarchan sonnet and explain how the turn changes the meaning.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Composite volumes* (60 min): Find volumes of composite solids like an ice-cream cone with a hemisphere on top and a grain silo.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Eras and extinctions* (50 min): Research the 5 mass extinctions and add them to the timeline.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Wilmot Proviso* (50 min): Read the one-sentence Wilmot Proviso (1846) and explain why it divided Congress along sectional rather than party lines.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *¿Adónde fuiste?* (45 min): Write where each family member went last summer and how it was: Fuimos a la playa; fue divertido.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Research* (40 min): Research the play's story, playwright and period.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Bowling* (40 min): Bowl a playground ball at 10 plastic-bottle pins; learn approach and release.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Inhalants* (25 min): Learn how household inhalants damage the brain and can cause sudden death.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Form and sound: Dunbar, Lazarus and Frost
- Objective: Compare how structure (sonnet, rhymed stanzas, refrain) contributes to meaning
- Objective: Analyze sound devices
- Vocabulary: sonnet, rhyme scheme, meter, refrain, alliteration, personification, connotation
- Materials: Paul Laurence Dunbar, 'Sympathy' and 'We Wear the Mask', Emma Lazarus, 'The New Colossus', Robert Frost, 'The Road Not Taken'
- Assessment: Structure comparison and quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Spheres and dimensions as functions
- Objective: Find the volume of spheres and hemispheres
- Objective: Analyze how volume changes as a function of radius or height
- Vocabulary: sphere, hemisphere, cubic units
- Materials: ball, orange, rice, Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Science 8 (Integrated): The geologic time scale
- Objective: Construct a model of geologic time to scale
- Objective: Explain major events used to divide Earth's history
- Vocabulary: eon, era, period, epoch, mass extinction, absolute dating, half-life
- Materials: adding machine tape or toilet paper (5 m), meter stick, markers, coins or M&Ms for half-life model
- Assessment: Timeline model and written explanation.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Compromises over slavery's expansion
- Objective: Analyze the Missouri Compromise, Wilmot Proviso and Compromise of 1850
- Objective: Explain why California's admission as a free state mattered
- Vocabulary: Missouri Compromise, Wilmot Proviso, Compromise of 1850, Fugitive Slave Act, popular sovereignty, sectionalism
- Materials: maps of free and slave states in 1820 and 1850, Henry Clay speech excerpt
- Assessment: Compromise quiz and map.

### Spanish 1B: Preterite of ir and ser
- Objective: Use fui, fuiste, fue in context
- Objective: Describe where you went and how it was
- Vocabulary: fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fueron, ¿Adónde fuiste?, ¿Qué tal fue?, fue fantástico
- Materials: photo album
- Assessment: Ir/ser preterite quiz and conversation rubric.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Monologue work
- Objective: Select, analyze and memorize a monologue
- Objective: Research the play's context
- Vocabulary: monologue, soliloquy, blocking, memorization
- Materials: monologue from a public-domain play (e.g., Puck's epilogue, a scene from 'Our Town'-style texts in the library)
- Assessment: Monologue off-book check.

### Physical Education 8: Archery-style and bowling target skills
- Objective: Use a consistent pre-shot routine
- Objective: Control force for accuracy
- Vocabulary: pre-shot routine, accuracy, follow-through
- Materials: bean bags, plastic bottles as pins, playground ball, target poster
- Assessment: Target triathlon score sheet.

### Health Education 8: Stimulants, inhalants, caffeine and cannabis potency
- Objective: Describe risks of misused prescription stimulants and inhalants
- Objective: Compare cannabis potency today and past
- Vocabulary: stimulant, inhalant, THC, edible, potency
- Materials: NIDA for Teens articles
- Assessment: Drug-facts chart.
