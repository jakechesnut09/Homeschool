## Day 1 — Monday (school day 161, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Note-taking* (75 min): Take notes on cards organized by sub-question, recording source and page for every fact.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Zooming in on sqrt 2* (60 min): Use successive number lines to trap sqrt 2 between tenths, then hundredths, without a calculator.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Adaptation vs acclimation* (50 min): Sort 10 examples (tanning vs dark skin, polar bear fur) into inherited adaptations or individual changes.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *New immigrants* (50 min): Graph immigration by region of origin 1870–1910 and read 'The New Colossus.'
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Infographic* (45 min): Read a Spanish infographic on teen internet use in Spain or Mexico and answer questions about the data.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Devices* (40 min): Learn repetition, canon, unison and retrograde by applying each to your phrase.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Aerobic test* (40 min): Complete the PACER or mile and record.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Agencies* (25 min): Learn the roles of the CDC, CDPH and your county health department.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 162, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Paraphrase practice* (75 min): Practice paraphrasing 5 passages and compare them with the originals to avoid plagiarism.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Ordering real numbers* (60 min): Order sets like {pi, sqrt 10, 3.1, 22/7, 3.15} on a number line with justification.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Local adaptations* (50 min): Research a California species (e.g., island fox) and its adaptations; make an infographic.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Angel Island and exclusion* (50 min): Read translated Angel Island wall poems and the Chinese Exclusion Act (1882); compare with Ellis Island experiences.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Comparison* (45 min): Compare those statistics with U.S. teen data in six Spanish sentences.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Structure* (40 min): Organize phrases into ABA form.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Strength tests* (40 min): Complete curl-ups and push-ups to cadence.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Epidemics* (25 min): Study how public health tracks outbreaks using a simple case-map activity.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Research: notes, paraphrase and citation
- Objective: Take organized notes
- Objective: Quote and paraphrase without plagiarizing
- Objective: Cite sources in MLA format
- Vocabulary: paraphrase, plagiarism, quotation, MLA, works cited, in-text citation
- Materials: sources, note cards or digital notes, Purdue OWL MLA guide
- Assessment: Notes and works-cited page.

### Mathematics 8: Approximating irrational numbers
- Objective: Approximate irrational numbers to the hundredth
- Objective: Compare and order irrational and rational numbers on a number line
- Vocabulary: approximation, real number line, truncate
- Materials: number line strips, calculator
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Adaptation and unit review
- Objective: Distinguish adaptations from individual changes
- Objective: Synthesize evidence for evolution
- Vocabulary: adaptation, acclimation, common descent
- Materials: notebook
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Immigration, cities and nativism
- Objective: Describe new immigration and urban growth
- Objective: Explain nativism including the Chinese Exclusion Act
- Vocabulary: new immigrants, Ellis Island, Angel Island, tenement, assimilation, nativism, Chinese Exclusion Act
- Materials: Jacob Riis photographs, Angel Island poems, 'The New Colossus' by Emma Lazarus
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Unit 8 assessment: Digital life debate
- Objective: Compare technology use across cultures
- Objective: Argue a simple opinion
- Vocabulary: la ventaja, la desventaja, creo que, en mi opinión, estoy de acuerdo
- Materials: infographic in Spanish
- Assessment: Opinion piece rubric and unit 8 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Choreographic devices
- Objective: Apply choreographic devices
- Objective: Structure an original dance study
- Vocabulary: repetition, canon, unison, retrograde, ABA, motif
- Materials: open space, music
- Assessment: Recorded draft.

### Physical Education 8: Final fitness assessment
- Objective: Complete the end-of-year fitness assessment
- Objective: Evaluate progress across the year
- Vocabulary: post-test, Healthy Fitness Zone, progress
- Materials: PACER audio, stopwatch, mat, tape measure
- Assessment: Year-long fitness progress graph and summary.

### Health Education 8: Public health and health careers
- Objective: Describe public health agencies
- Objective: Explore health careers
- Vocabulary: public health, epidemiologist, CDPH, county health department
- Materials: CDPH website, career profiles
- Assessment: Career profile card.
