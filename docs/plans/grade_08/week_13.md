## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *'Ain't I a Woman?'* (75 min): Read the speech and trace how each repetition of the refrain answers a specific claim made by male opponents.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Variables on both sides* (60 min): Solve 10 equations such as 7x - 4 = 3x + 12 and explain the strategy of collecting variable terms.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Orbit model* (50 min): Swing a ball on a string in a circle (outdoors) and release it to see inertia; relate the string to gravity.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Free Black communities* (50 min): Read about Richard Allen and the AME Church and free Black schools in the North.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Store phrases* (45 min): Learn ¿En qué puedo servirle?, ¿Qué talla usa?, Me queda grande/bien, and practice a fitting-room dialogue.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Triads* (40 min): Build C, F and G major triads and compare major and minor triads on Chrome Music Lab.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Review* (40 min): Review grade 7 forearm pass and set with 50 partner contacts each.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Abstinence* (25 min): Discuss why abstinence is the only certain way to avoid pregnancy and STIs and reasons teens choose it. (Sexual health lesson: parents may opt out under CA Ed. Code 51938; parent previews materials.)

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Two versions* (75 min): Compare the 1851 Robinson report with the 1863 Gage version and discuss why the texts differ and which is more reliable.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Fractions in equations* (60 min): Clear denominators by multiplying by the LCD to solve 6 equations such as (2/3)x + 5 = (1/2)x + 7.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Gravity and Orbits sim* (50 min): Use PhET 'Gravity and Orbits' to turn gravity off and change masses; record effects on orbits.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Education reform* (50 min): Read Horace Mann's arguments for public schools.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Markets* (45 min): Read about markets such as Chichicastenango (Guatemala) and El Rastro (Madrid) and when bargaining is appropriate.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *I-IV-V* (40 min): Play the I-IV-V-I progression in C with the left hand.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Approach* (40 min): Learn the 3-step spike approach (left-right-left) and arm swing using a tossed ball.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Methods overview* (25 min): Review a chart comparing the effectiveness of FDA-approved methods (no demonstrations).

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Declaration of Sentiments* (75 min): Read the Declaration of Sentiments and chart how Stanton borrows the structure of the Declaration of Independence.

- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Textiles* (45 min): Watch a short video on Guatemalan Maya weaving (huipiles) and write five sentences about the product and its meaning.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Twelve-bar blues* (40 min): Play the 12-bar blues progression in C.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Block* (40 min): Practice the two-hand block footwork and timing at the net.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Decision model* (25 min): Apply a decision-making model to a scenario about waiting to have sex.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Women's rights voices: Sojourner Truth and Elizabeth Cady Stanton
- Objective: Analyze how speakers acknowledge and respond to opposing viewpoints
- Objective: Correct inappropriate shifts in verb voice and mood
- Vocabulary: counterargument, rebuttal, refrain, concession, shift in mood
- Materials: Sojourner Truth, 'Ain't I a Woman?' (1851, Gage version and Robinson version), Declaration of Sentiments (1848)
- Assessment: Argument analysis quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Equations with variables on both sides and rational coefficients
- Objective: Solve equations with variables on both sides including fractions and decimals
- Objective: Write and solve equations from contexts
- Vocabulary: coefficient, rational number, least common denominator
- Materials: notebook, calculator for checking
- Assessment: Quiz on equations with rational coefficients.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Gravity, orbits and galaxies
- Objective: Explain how gravity keeps planets and moons in orbit
- Objective: Describe the role of gravity in forming the solar system and galaxies
- Vocabulary: orbit, inertia, solar nebula, galaxy, Milky Way
- Materials: string, small ball or eraser, PhET Gravity and Orbits sim
- Assessment: Formation model and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Reform movements
- Objective: Describe reform movements in education and women's rights
- Objective: Identify American literary themes of the period
- Vocabulary: reform, temperance, Seneca Falls Convention, Declaration of Sentiments, transcendentalism, common school
- Materials: Declaration of Sentiments, Emerson or Thoreau excerpt
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Spanish 1B: De compras: shopping role-plays and culture
- Objective: Shop for clothing in a store or market
- Objective: Bargain politely in a market
- Vocabulary: la tienda, el mercado, la talla, me queda bien, regatear, barato/a, caro/a, el descuento
- Materials: play money, clothing items
- Assessment: Shopping role-play rubric and listening quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Chords and accompaniment
- Objective: Build major and minor triads
- Objective: Accompany a melody with I-IV-V chords
- Vocabulary: triad, root, third, fifth, major chord, minor chord, chord progression, I-IV-V
- Materials: keyboard, Chrome Music Lab Chords tool
- Assessment: Chord performance check.

### Physical Education 8: Advanced volleyball: spike, block and rotation
- Objective: Perform an approach and spike
- Objective: Rotate and use a 3-hit offense
- Vocabulary: approach, spike, block, rotation, free ball
- Materials: volleyball, net or rope, cones
- Assessment: Spike approach checklist.

### Health Education 8: Abstinence and preventing pregnancy
- Objective: Explain abstinence as the only 100% effective method
- Objective: Describe effectiveness of FDA-approved methods in medically accurate, age-appropriate terms
- Vocabulary: abstinence, contraception, effectiveness, FDA-approved
- Materials: CDC or KidsHealth effectiveness chart, decision-making model
- Assessment: Short-response check.
