## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Act 5: the play within a play and comparison essay
- Objective: Analyze how Shakespeare transforms Ovid's tragedy into comedy
- Objective: Write a comparison essay
- Objective: Perform a scene with expression
- Vocabulary: play within a play, parody, burlesque, transformation, performance
- Materials: Folger edition, Ovid text, simple props
- Assessment: Comparison essay (rubric), performance and Unit 7 test.

### Mathematics 8: Applying the Pythagorean theorem
- Objective: Apply the theorem in 2-D and 3-D real-world problems
- Objective: Find distance between two points on the coordinate plane
- Vocabulary: distance, diagonal, coordinate plane, three-dimensional
- Materials: rectangular box, tape measure, graph paper
- Assessment: Quiz on Pythagorean applications.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Natural selection in numbers
- Objective: Use graphs and percentages to show changes in trait frequency
- Objective: Interpret data from the PhET Natural Selection sim
- Vocabulary: allele frequency, generation, selective pressure, population
- Materials: PhET Natural Selection sim, graph paper, calculator
- Assessment: Graph analysis quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Railroads and the West
- Objective: Explain the effects of the transcontinental railroad and the Homestead Act
- Objective: Analyze federal Indian policy and wars on the Plains
- Vocabulary: transcontinental railroad, land grant, Homestead Act, reservation, Dawes Act, Wounded Knee, boarding school
- Materials: railroad and land-grant maps, Chief Joseph's 1877 surrender speech
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: La tecnología y la computadora
- Objective: Name technology and online activities
- Objective: Use pedir and servir (e→i)
- Vocabulary: la computadora portátil, el teléfono celular, navegar en la red, el correo electrónico, bajar, la contraseña, pedir, servir
- Materials: computer, Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: Technology and e→i quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Stop-motion animation: production
- Objective: Produce, edit and share a stop-motion animation
- Objective: Analyze audience response
- Vocabulary: editing, sound effects, title card, credits, distribution
- Materials: Stop Motion Studio app, figures, set
- Assessment: Completed animation.

### Physical Education 8: Track meet and pacing strategy
- Objective: Compete in a family/co-op track meet
- Objective: Use even pacing in the 800 m
- Vocabulary: negative split, pace, personal record
- Materials: stopwatch, cones, measuring wheel or track
- Assessment: Track meet results and reflection.

### Health Education 8: Chronic disease risk factors
- Objective: Identify controllable and uncontrollable risk factors
- Objective: Explain prevention
- Vocabulary: chronic disease, risk factor, type 2 diabetes, asthma, heredity
- Materials: family health history form, CDC fact sheets
- Assessment: Chronic disease research presentation.
