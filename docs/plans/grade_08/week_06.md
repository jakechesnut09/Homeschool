## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Pre-reading* (75 min): Read a short article about Tulsa in the 1960s and S. E. Hinton writing the book at 16; predict conflicts from the cover and title.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Similarity sequences* (60 min): Describe a sequence of a dilation plus rigid motions that maps one polygon to a similar one; do 4 grid problems.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Energy of motion* (50 min): Brainstorm examples of moving objects and rank them by kinetic energy; read an introduction to KE = 1/2 m v^2.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Federalists vs Anti-Federalists* (50 min): Read excerpts of Federalist No. 10 and an Anti-Federalist essay (Brutus No. 1); chart main arguments.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Comparatives* (45 min): Learn más/menos...que and tan...como and compare 10 pairs of objects in your house.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Proportion guide* (40 min): Draw an oval head with guidelines: eyes halfway down, nose at one-third, mouth at one-third of the remaining distance; label each.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Spiral grip* (40 min): Practice the laces grip, step and follow-through; complete 50 partner throws at 10 yards.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Plan* (25 min): Plan a short PSA promoting help-seeking or reducing stigma for teens.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapters 1–2* (75 min): Read chapters 1–2 and start a character chart (Ponyboy, Johnny, Dally, Darry, Sodapop, Cherry) with direct and indirect characterization quotes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Similar polygons* (60 min): Test pairs of rectangles and triangles for similarity by comparing side ratios and angles; explain why all squares are similar but not all rectangles.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Mass and kinetic energy* (50 min): Roll balls of different mass from the same ramp height into a cup; measure how far the cup slides as evidence of energy.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Federalist No. 51* (50 min): Analyze 'If men were angels' and explain checks and balances.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Irregulars* (45 min): Learn mejor, peor, mayor, menor and compare family members' ages and skills.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Eyes and noses* (40 min): Fill a sketchbook page with 4 eyes and 4 noses drawn from a mirror, shading the eyelid and nostril shadows.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Catching* (40 min): Practice the diamond (above chest) and basket (below chest) catch positions on 40 partner throws.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Create* (25 min): Create the PSA as a poster, 60-second video or slide.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 3* (75 min): Read chapter 3 and write a journal entry analyzing Cherry's line 'Things are rough all over' and what it reveals about class.

- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Religious freedom* (50 min): Read Jefferson's Virginia Statute for Religious Freedom and connect it to the First Amendment.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Superlatives* (45 min): Learn el/la más... de and write superlatives about your family, house, and town.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Mouths and ears* (40 min): Draw 4 mouths (neutral, smiling, open, profile) and 2 ears, noting where ears line up with eyes and nose.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Routes* (40 min): Run slant, out, post and curl routes against air; partner throws on time.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Share* (25 min): Share the PSA with family and gather feedback.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~385 min)

- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Flag pulling* (40 min): Practice flag-pulling defense and evasive moves (juke, spin) in a 1-v-1 grid.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Goal check* (25 min): Review progress on your tech-balance goal and adjust.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: The Outsiders, chapters 1–3: characters and class
- Objective: Analyze how the narrator Ponyboy introduces characters and social conflict
- Objective: Distinguish active and passive voice
- Vocabulary: Greasers, Socs, characterization, active voice, passive voice, nonconformist
- Materials: S. E. Hinton, 'The Outsiders' (library copy), character chart
- Assessment: Chapter quiz and discussion participation.

### Mathematics 8: Similarity
- Objective: Define similarity as a sequence of rigid motions and dilations
- Objective: Use the Angle-Angle criterion to show triangles are similar
- Vocabulary: similar, Angle-Angle (AA) criterion, proportional, corresponding sides
- Materials: ruler, protractor, graph paper, mirror, tape measure
- Assessment: Mirror-height lab write-up plus similarity problem set.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Kinetic energy
- Objective: Describe kinetic energy as energy of motion
- Objective: Graph how kinetic energy depends on mass and speed
- Vocabulary: energy, kinetic energy, joule, proportional, nonlinear
- Materials: ramp, marbles or balls of different masses, paper cup with a door cut in it, ruler
- Assessment: Kinetic energy graphs and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Ratification and the Bill of Rights
- Objective: Compare Federalist and Anti-Federalist arguments
- Objective: Explain the freedoms protected in the Bill of Rights
- Vocabulary: Federalist, Anti-Federalist, Federalist Papers, Bill of Rights, amendment, establishment clause
- Materials: Federalist No. 10 and No. 51 excerpts, Bill of Rights transcript
- Assessment: Scenario quiz on the Bill of Rights.

### Spanish 1B: Comparisons and superlatives
- Objective: Compare people and things
- Objective: Use superlatives and irregular comparatives
- Vocabulary: más...que, menos...que, tan...como, mejor, peor, mayor, menor, el/la más
- Materials: photos of rooms or real-estate ads
- Assessment: Comparisons quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Facial proportions
- Objective: Draw a face using classical proportion guidelines
- Objective: Observe how real faces vary from the 'ideal'
- Vocabulary: proportion, guidelines, symmetry, features, three-quarter view
- Materials: mirror, pencils, drawing paper, ruler
- Assessment: Self-portrait sketch checked for proportion.

### Physical Education 8: Flag football: throwing, catching and routes
- Objective: Throw a spiral with correct grip and release
- Objective: Run basic pass routes
- Vocabulary: spiral, route, line of scrimmage, quarterback
- Materials: football, flag belts or bandanas, cones
- Assessment: Throw analysis with one correction.

### Health Education 8: Mental health advocacy and unit assessment
- Objective: Advocate for help-seeking
- Objective: Show mastery of unit concepts
- Vocabulary: advocacy, help-seeking
- Materials: poster paper or slide software
- Assessment: PSA and unit test.
