## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapters 9–10* (75 min): Read the Covey chapters and analyze the chiasmus 'You have seen how a man was made a slave; you shall see how a slave was made a man.'
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Comparing functions* (60 min): Compare a function given by a table with one given by an equation (e.g., two car rental companies) and decide which grows faster.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Making a spectrum* (50 min): Use a glass of water in sunlight or a prism to make a rainbow; explain how white light separates.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Mexican California* (50 min): Use Calisphere photographs and diseños (land-grant maps) to describe rancho life, the economy of hides and tallow and Mexico's 1829 abolition of slavery.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Review: units 1–2* (45 min): Review restaurant phrases, venir, ser/estar, house, comparisons, stem-changers, commands, and the progressive with a practice test.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Revise* (40 min): Revise for unity and variety based on feedback.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Pre-exercise fuel* (40 min): Plan three pre-workout snacks and explain why carbohydrates are the main fuel; do a 30-minute activity.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Scenarios* (25 min): Apply the decision-making model to three relationship scenarios.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 11 & Appendix* (75 min): Read the escape chapter and appendix; discuss why Douglass withholds the details of how he escaped.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Linear vs nonlinear* (60 min): Graph y = 2x + 1, y = x^2 and A = pi r^2 from tables and explain why only the first is linear.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Colored filters* (50 min): Look at colored objects through red and blue cellophane and explain the results with absorption and reflection.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Texas Revolution* (50 min): Compare a Texan account and a Mexican account (José Enrique de la Peña) of the Alamo and identify each perspective.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Review: units 3–4* (45 min): Review clothing, e→ie verbs, demonstratives, numbers, preterite -ar, and direct object pronouns with Conjuguemos drills.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Notate* (40 min): Notate the final version in MuseScore or by hand.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Hydration* (40 min): Weigh yourself before and after a 30-minute workout to estimate sweat loss and calculate fluid needs.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Future goals* (25 min): Write how decisions now affect future goals (school, sports, career).

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Douglass's Narrative: chapters 9–11; semester review
- Objective: Analyze the turning point in Douglass's fight with Covey
- Objective: Review semester reading, writing and grammar skills
- Vocabulary: turning point, resistance, chiasmus, appendix
- Materials: Narrative, semester review packet
- Assessment: Semester 1 exam.

### Mathematics 8: Representing and comparing functions; semester review
- Objective: Compare two functions represented in different ways
- Objective: Distinguish linear from nonlinear functions
- Vocabulary: linear function, nonlinear function, rate of change, initial value
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Semester 1 cumulative exam.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Light and color; semester review
- Objective: Explain color as reflected and absorbed wavelengths
- Objective: Review semester 1 concepts
- Vocabulary: visible spectrum, white light, prism, pigment
- Materials: glass of water or prism, white paper, colored cellophane or plastic, flashlight
- Assessment: Semester 1 exam.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Mexican California and Texas independence; semester review
- Objective: Describe Mexican settlements, ranchos and the land-grant system
- Objective: Explain the Texas War for Independence
- Objective: Review semester 1
- Vocabulary: rancho, Californio, land grant, mission secularization, Alamo, Lone Star Republic
- Materials: Calisphere images of ranchos and Californios, accounts of the Alamo
- Assessment: Semester 1 exam.

### Spanish 1B: Semester 1 review and exam
- Objective: Review units 1–4
- Objective: Demonstrate Novice Mid–High proficiency
- Vocabulary: repaso
- Materials: semester flashcards, unit tests
- Assessment: Semester 1 exam (written and oral).

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Final composition and presentation
- Objective: Revise and present a documented composition
- Objective: Explain choices
- Vocabulary: revision, unity, variety, notation
- Materials: Song Maker or notation app (MuseScore free)
- Assessment: Composition and program notes rubric.

### Physical Education 8: Fueling performance: nutrition, hydration and sleep
- Objective: Plan pre- and post-exercise snacks
- Objective: Explain hydration and sleep's role in recovery
- Vocabulary: carbohydrate loading, electrolyte, dehydration, recovery
- Materials: food labels, water bottle, sleep log
- Assessment: Athlete's fuel plan poster.

### Health Education 8: Sexual health review and decision making
- Objective: Apply a decision-making model
- Objective: Demonstrate help-seeking
- Vocabulary: decision-making model, consequence, goal
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Unit test or alternative assignment.
