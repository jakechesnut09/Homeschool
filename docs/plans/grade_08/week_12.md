## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Declaration structure* (75 min): Outline the Declaration's four parts and explain how the second paragraph's sentences build from self-evident truths to the right to revolution.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Hanger diagrams* (60 min): Use hanger diagrams to reason about equations like 3x + 1 = x + 7 and record each balanced move.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Misconceptions* (50 min): Write what causes seasons; examine Earth-Sun distance data showing Earth is closest to the Sun in January.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Factories* (50 min): Read letters from Lowell mill workers (Harriet Robinson) and describe working conditions.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Demonstratives* (45 min): Learn este, ese, aquel and their forms and point out clothing at three distances in 12 sentences.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Keyboard geography* (40 min): Locate all Cs and play C-D-E-F-G five-finger patterns in each hand.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Tennis rallies* (40 min): Rally cooperatively with a partner aiming for 10 consecutive hits.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Fertilization* (25 min): Study a diagram of fertilization and implantation with parent guidance. (Sexual health lesson: parents may opt out under CA Ed. Code 51938; parent previews materials.)

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Loaded words* (75 min): List 10 words with strong connotations ('tyranny', 'usurpations', 'merciless') and explain how they shape tone toward the King.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Balanced moves* (60 min): Decide which moves keep equations equivalent; solve 8 two-step and multi-step equations with integers.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Light angle lab* (50 min): Shine a flashlight straight down and at an angle on graph paper; count squares lit to compare energy concentration.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Transportation revolution* (50 min): Map the Erie Canal and National Road; explain Clay's American System.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Big numbers* (45 min): Learn 100–1,000,000 including doscientos/quinientos/setecientos and read 20 prices aloud.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *C major scale* (40 min): Practice the C major scale hands separately with thumb-under fingering at 60 bpm.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Tennis scoring* (40 min): Learn love-15-30-40-deuce scoring and play a tiebreaker game.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Trimesters* (25 min): Create a timeline of fetal development across three trimesters.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Gettysburg Address* (75 min): Close-read the Gettysburg Address, marking parallelism ('we can not dedicate—we can not consecrate') and the past-present-future structure.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Distributive property equations* (60 min): Solve equations like 4(x - 3) = 2x + 6 and -2(5 - x) = 3x + 1, showing every step.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Day-length data* (50 min): Graph monthly day length for your city and a city in the Southern Hemisphere; describe patterns.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Inventions* (50 min): Make a chart of inventions (cotton gin, steamboat, telegraph, steel plow) and effects.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Currencies* (45 min): Learn the currencies of eight Spanish-speaking countries and convert three clothing prices to dollars.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Building scales* (40 min): Use the W-W-H-W-W-W-H formula to build G and F major scales and play them.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Table tennis* (40 min): Learn the shakehand grip, push and forehand drive on a table or improvised surface.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Prenatal care* (25 min): Explain how nutrition, alcohol, tobacco and prenatal checkups affect a developing baby.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: The Declaration of Independence and the Gettysburg Address
- Objective: Analyze how the structure of a foundational document builds an argument
- Objective: Analyze word choice and tone in a speech
- Objective: Use conditional and subjunctive mood for effect
- Vocabulary: preamble, grievance, structure, parallelism, connotation, subjunctive mood
- Materials: Declaration of Independence and Gettysburg Address transcripts (National Archives)
- Assessment: Structure comparison response.

### Mathematics 8: Balanced moves and solving equations
- Objective: Solve equations using balanced moves
- Objective: Use the distributive property and combine like terms to solve equations
- Vocabulary: equation, solution, inverse operation, like terms, distributive property
- Materials: hanger diagram worksheet, algebra tiles or paper cutouts, balance scale (optional)
- Assessment: Quiz on multi-step equations.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Seasons
- Objective: Explain that seasons result from Earth's tilt, not distance from the Sun
- Objective: Analyze day-length and sunlight-angle data
- Vocabulary: axis, tilt, solstice, equinox, direct sunlight, indirect sunlight
- Materials: flashlight, graph paper, globe or ball, sunrise/sunset data for your city
- Assessment: Seasons explanation and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Industrial Revolution in the Northeast
- Objective: Explain how factories, canals and railroads transformed the Northeast
- Objective: Describe immigration and city growth
- Vocabulary: Industrial Revolution, Lowell mills, Erie Canal, American System, interchangeable parts, Irish Famine
- Materials: Lowell mill girl letter excerpts, canal map
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Demonstrative adjectives and numbers 100–1,000,000
- Objective: Point out items with este/ese/aquel
- Objective: Use large numbers for prices
- Vocabulary: este/esta, ese/esa, aquel/aquella, estos, cien, doscientos, mil, un millón
- Materials: price tags, currency converter
- Assessment: Demonstratives and large-number quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Keyboard basics and scales
- Objective: Play a C major scale with correct fingering
- Objective: Build major scales using whole and half steps
- Vocabulary: whole step, half step, major scale, fingering, key signature, tonic
- Materials: keyboard, piano or free online keyboard app
- Assessment: Scale and melody playing check.

### Physical Education 8: Tennis and table tennis: rallies and scoring
- Objective: Rally cooperatively and competitively
- Objective: Keep score in tennis and table tennis
- Vocabulary: deuce, love, rally, spin
- Materials: tennis gear, table tennis paddles and balls, table or board
- Assessment: Scoring quiz and match play observation.

### Health Education 8: Reproduction, pregnancy and fetal development
- Objective: Describe fertilization and stages of pregnancy
- Objective: Explain the importance of prenatal care
- Vocabulary: fertilization, embryo, fetus, prenatal care, placenta
- Materials: diagrams of fetal development, parent-selected resources
- Assessment: Timeline quiz.
