# Visual and Performing Arts 8 (Grade 8)

A year-long arts survey aligned to the 2019 California Arts Standards for grade 8. Students rotate through visual arts (perspective drawing, portraiture, painting), music (notation, keyboard, composing, American music history), theatre (acting, scene study, playwriting), media arts (photography and stop-motion animation) and dance (choreography), building a portfolio and ending with a home exhibition and performance.

*Framework:* 2019 California Arts Standards for Public Schools, Prekindergarten Through Grade Twelve; 2020 California Arts Education Framework  
*Time:* 40 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Drawing Foundations and Perspective (weeks 1–5)
Artists use line, value and perspective to create the illusion of space on a flat surface.

- Week 1: Line, contour and observation
- Week 2: Value and shading
- Week 3: One-point perspective
- Week 4: Two-point perspective and cityscapes
- Week 5: Drawing unit project and critique

### Unit 2: Portraiture, Color and Identity (weeks 6–9)
Portraits and color choices express identity and reflect culture.

- Week 6: Facial proportions
- Week 7: Color theory and mood
- Week 8: Portraits and identity
- Week 9: Painting the identity portrait

### Unit 3: Music Literacy and Keyboard (weeks 10–14)
Reading rhythm, pitch and chords lets musicians perform and understand music.

- Week 10: Rhythm reading
- Week 11: Pitch reading on the staff
- Week 12: Keyboard basics and scales
- Week 13: Chords and accompaniment
- Week 14: Performance and listening analysis

### Unit 4: American Music and Composition (weeks 15–18)
American music grew from spirituals, blues and jazz; composers organize musical ideas into forms.

- Week 15: Spirituals, work songs and the blues
- Week 16: Jazz and ragtime
- Week 17: Composing a melody
- Week 18: Final composition and presentation

### Unit 5: Acting and Scene Study (weeks 19–23)
Actors build believable characters through voice, body and objectives.

- Week 19: Theatre warm-ups and the actor's instrument
- Week 20: Objectives, obstacles and tactics
- Week 21: Monologue work
- Week 22: Scene study
- Week 23: Watching and responding to theatre

### Unit 6: Playwriting and Production (weeks 24–27)
Original drama develops through research, writing, rehearsal and revision.

- Week 24: Playwriting: ideas and structure
- Week 25: Writing the script
- Week 26: Table read and revision
- Week 27: Staging a reading

### Unit 7: Media Arts: Photography and Stop-Motion (weeks 28–31)
Media artists plan, capture and edit images to tell stories and influence viewers.

- Week 28: Photography: composition
- Week 29: Photo essay and media influence
- Week 30: Stop-motion animation: planning
- Week 31: Stop-motion animation: production

### Unit 8: Dance and Choreography (weeks 32–34)
Choreographers shape movement with elements of dance and structures to express ideas.

- Week 32: Elements of dance
- Week 33: Choreographic devices
- Week 34: Dance performance

### Unit 9: Portfolio and Exhibition (weeks 35–36)
Curating and presenting work shapes how audiences experience it.

- Week 35: Curating the portfolio
- Week 36: Exhibition and showcase

## Standards

- **DA:Cr1.1.8** Implement the elements of dance to develop choreographic content using a variety of stimuli.
- **DA:Cr2.1.8** Collaborate to select and apply a variety of choreographic devices and dance structures to choreograph an original dance study.
- **DA:Pr4.1.8** Use body-designs and spatial relationships with intent and clarity, adapting to performance space.
- **DA:Pr6.1.8** Perform dance using artistic expression and a variety of production elements for a live or recorded audience.
- **DA:Re8.1.8** Select a dance and explain how the artistic expression is achieved through relationships among the elements of dance, use of body, technique and context.
- **MA:Cr1.1.8** Generate ideas, goals and solutions for original media artworks through application of focused creative processes.
- **MA:Cr2.1.8** Design, propose and evaluate artistic ideas, plans, prototypes and production processes for media arts productions.
- **MA:Cr3.1.8** Implement production processes to integrate content and stylistic conventions for determined meaning in media arts productions.
- **MA:Pr6.1.8** Design the presentation and distribution of media artworks through multiple formats.
- **MA:Re7.1.8** Analyze the intent, meanings and influence of a variety of media artworks.
- **MU:Cn11.0.8** Demonstrate understanding of relationships between music and the other arts, other disciplines, varied contexts and daily life.
- **MU:Cr1.1.8** Generate rhythmic, melodic and harmonic phrases and harmonic accompaniments within expanded forms that convey expressive intent.
- **MU:Cr2.1.8** Select, organize and document personal musical ideas for arrangements, songs and compositions within expanded forms.
- **MU:Cr3.2.8** Present the final version of a documented personal composition, song or arrangement demonstrating unity, variety and development.
- **MU:Pr4.2.8** Compare the structure of contrasting pieces of music selected for performance, explaining how the elements of music are used in each.
- **MU:Pr5.1.8** Identify and apply personally developed criteria to rehearse, refine and determine when the music is ready to perform.
- **MU:Pr6.1.8** Perform music with technical accuracy, stylistic expression and culturally authentic practices to convey the creator's intent.
- **MU:Re7.2.8** Compare how the elements of music and expressive qualities relate to the structure within programs of music.
- **MU:Re8.1.8** Support personal interpretation of contrasting programs of music and explain how creators or performers apply the elements of music to convey intent.
- **TH:Cn11.2.8** Research the story elements of a staged drama/theatre work and discuss how a playwright might have intended it to be produced.
- **TH:Cr1.1.8** Imagine and explore multiple perspectives and solutions to staging problems in a drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Cr2.1.8** Articulate and apply critical analysis, background knowledge, research and historical and cultural context to the development of original ideas for a drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Cr3.1.8** Use repetition and analysis to revise devised or scripted drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Pr4.1.8** Develop a physical and vocal characterization appropriate to a character's objectives in a drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Pr5.1.8** Use a variety of acting techniques to increase skills in a rehearsal or drama/theatre performance.
- **TH:Pr6.1.8** Perform a rehearsed drama/theatre work for an audience.
- **TH:Re8.1.8** Recognize and share artistic choices when participating in or observing a drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Re9.1.8** Respond to a drama/theatre work using supporting evidence, personal aesthetics and artistic criteria.
- **VA:Cn11.1.8** Distinguish different ways art is used to represent, establish, reinforce and reflect group identity.
- **VA:Cr1.1.8** Document early stages of the creative process visually and/or verbally in traditional or new media.
- **VA:Cr1.2.8** Collaboratively shape an artistic investigation of an aspect of present-day life using a contemporary practice of art and design.
- **VA:Cr2.1.8** Demonstrate willingness to experiment, innovate and take risks to pursue ideas, forms and meanings that emerge in the process of art-making.
- **VA:Cr2.2.8** Demonstrate awareness of ethical responsibility to oneself and others when posting and sharing content online.
- **VA:Cr3.1.8** Apply relevant criteria to examine, reflect on and plan revisions for a work of art in progress.
- **VA:Pr5.1.8** Collaboratively prepare and present selected theme-based artwork for display, and formulate exhibition narratives for the viewer.
- **VA:Pr6.1.8** Analyze why and how an exhibition or collection may influence ideas, beliefs and experiences.
- **VA:Re7.1.8** Explain how a person's aesthetic choices are influenced by culture and environment and impact the visual image one conveys to others.
- **VA:Re7.2.8** Compare and contrast contexts and media in which viewers encounter images that influence ideas, emotions and actions.
- **VA:Re8.1.8** Interpret art by analyzing how the interaction of subject matter, characteristics of form and structure, use of media, art-making approaches and relevant contextual information contributes to understanding.
- **VA:Re9.1.8** Create a convincing and logical argument to support an evaluation of art.
