## Day 1 — Monday (school day 136, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Reading Ovid* (75 min): Read 'Pyramus and Thisbe' and summarize the plot in 8 key events.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Multiplying & dividing* (60 min): Compute products and quotients like (3 x 10^8)(2 x 10^3) and adjust answers to proper scientific notation.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Mutation model* (50 min): Change one letter in a sentence ('THE CAT ATE THE RAT') to model substitution, insertion and deletion.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Plans compared* (50 min): Compare the Ten Percent Plan, Johnson's plan and the Radical Republicans' plan in a three-column chart.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Genres* (45 min): Learn 12 genre words and classify 15 well-known movies and shows.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Analyzing photos* (40 min): Analyze Dorothea Lange's 'Migrant Mother' and Ansel Adams's Yosemite images for composition and intent.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Stress and exercise* (40 min): Learn how endorphins and reduced cortisol help mood; rate your mood before and after a 20-minute run.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Harassment* (25 min): Define sexual harassment and identify examples in person and online.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 137, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Archetypes* (75 min): Identify the star-crossed lovers, forbidden love and tragic misunderstanding patterns.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Ovid's 'Pyramus and Thisbe' and patterns from myth
- Objective: Identify archetypal patterns in classical myths
- Objective: Analyze how later works draw on them
- Vocabulary: myth, archetype, star-crossed lovers, metamorphosis, allusion
- Materials: Ovid, 'Metamorphoses' Book IV, 'Pyramus and Thisbe' (Project Gutenberg translation)
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Operations with scientific notation
- Objective: Multiply and divide numbers in scientific notation
- Objective: Add and subtract numbers in scientific notation and choose sensible units
- Vocabulary: scientific notation, coefficient, unit conversion
- Materials: calculator, NASA planetary data
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Mutations
- Objective: Model how mutations change proteins
- Objective: Classify mutations as harmful, beneficial or neutral
- Vocabulary: mutation, substitution, insertion, deletion, sickle cell
- Materials: codon chart, paper strips
- Assessment: Mutation model and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Plans for Reconstruction
- Objective: Compare Lincoln's, Johnson's and Congress's Reconstruction plans
- Objective: Explain the 13th Amendment
- Vocabulary: Reconstruction, Ten Percent Plan, Radical Republicans, Thirteenth Amendment, Black codes, impeachment
- Materials: 13th Amendment text, Mississippi Black Codes (1865) excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: El cine y la televisión
- Objective: Name film and TV genres
- Objective: Give opinions about shows and movies
- Vocabulary: la película de horror, la comedia, el drama, los dibujos animados, la telenovela, el programa, emocionante, infantil
- Materials: TV listings
- Assessment: Genre and opinions quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Photography: composition
- Objective: Apply rule of thirds, leading lines and framing
- Objective: Analyze intent in famous photographs
- Vocabulary: rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, depth of field, point of view
- Materials: phone or digital camera
- Assessment: Photo portfolio of 5 images with captions.

### Physical Education 8: Exercise and stress management
- Objective: Explain how exercise reduces stress
- Objective: Use breathing and mindful movement
- Vocabulary: endorphins, cortisol, mindfulness, box breathing
- Materials: journal, timer
- Assessment: Stress-relief activity plan.

### Health Education 8: Dating violence, harassment and assault prevention
- Objective: Describe prevention and reporting
- Objective: Practice bystander intervention
- Vocabulary: sexual harassment, bystander, Title IX, reporting
- Materials: scenario cards, loveisrespect.org
- Assessment: Bystander role-play.
