## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~385 min)

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Drafting the research paper
- Objective: Organize research into a clear informative paper
- Objective: Revise for coherence and precise language
- Vocabulary: thesis, topic sentence, synthesis, transition, formal style
- Materials: notes, outline template, word processor
- Assessment: Research paper (rubric).

### Mathematics 8: Modeling: Fermi problems and estimation
- Objective: Break large estimation problems into smaller computable parts
- Objective: Use scientific notation and volume to estimate quantities
- Vocabulary: Fermi problem, estimate, assumption, order of magnitude
- Materials: measuring tape, calculator, ping-pong ball or marble
- Assessment: Fermi-problem poster rubric.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Capstone: define and design
- Objective: Define a real design problem with criteria and constraints
- Objective: Generate and evaluate solutions
- Vocabulary: design brief, criteria, constraints, decision matrix
- Materials: recyclable materials, tape, cardboard, rubber bands, scale
- Assessment: Design brief and decision matrix.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Labor and farmer movements
- Objective: Describe child labor and working conditions
- Objective: Explain labor unions, the Grange and Populism
- Vocabulary: labor union, strike, collective bargaining, American Federation of Labor, laissez-faire, Grange, Populist Party
- Materials: Lewis Hine child labor photographs (Library of Congress), Omaha Platform excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Spanish I review: present tense system
- Objective: Review all Spanish I present-tense structures
- Vocabulary: repaso
- Materials: flashcards, Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: Practice test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Dance performance
- Objective: Perform an original dance with expression
- Objective: Reflect on artistic choices
- Vocabulary: performance, expression, production elements
- Materials: music, costume pieces, recording device
- Assessment: Performance and reflection.

### Physical Education 8: Two-week personal fitness plan: design
- Objective: Write a two-week plan using FITT and training principles
- Objective: Plan for barriers
- Vocabulary: FITT, overload, specificity, progression
- Materials: planning template, fitness journal
- Assessment: Two-week fitness plan.

### Health Education 8: Health advocacy campaign: plan
- Objective: Choose an issue and audience
- Objective: Research valid data
- Vocabulary: campaign, target audience, call to action
- Materials: research sources, planning template
- Assessment: Campaign plan.
