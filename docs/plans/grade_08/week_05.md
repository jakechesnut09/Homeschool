## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Finish drafting* (75 min): Complete the story draft with dialogue and a resolution of the conflict.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Projecting and scaling* (60 min): Use a ruler to dilate a triangle from a point P with scale factor 2 and 1/2 by measuring along rays; compare side lengths and angles.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Define the problem* (50 min): Write criteria and constraints for a device that protects an egg in a 2-meter drop or a cart crash, using limited materials.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Delegates* (50 min): Create profile cards for Madison, Washington, Franklin, Sherman, Morris and Wilson.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Rooms* (45 min): Learn 10 rooms and draw and label a floor plan of your home.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Plan* (40 min): Thumbnail 3 compositions for 'A place that matters to me.'
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Leadership styles* (40 min): Discuss what makes a good coach or team captain; list five leadership behaviors.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Kinds of loss* (25 min): List types of loss (death, moving, divorce, friendship ending, pet) and how each may feel.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Revise for pacing* (75 min): Revise using a checklist: vary sentence length, add transitions that signal time shifts, and cut slow passages.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Circular grid* (60 min): Dilate shapes on a circular (polar) grid, then explain why dilations preserve angles but not lengths.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Design & decision matrix* (50 min): Sketch 3 designs and choose one with a decision matrix scoring cost, mass and expected protection.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Representation debate* (50 min): Role-play the Virginia vs New Jersey Plan debate and write the Great Compromise in your own words.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Furniture* (45 min): Learn 15 furniture items and label your bedroom with sticky notes.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Draft* (40 min): Lightly sketch the chosen composition.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Plan* (40 min): Write a 20-minute plan: warm-up, one tag or relay game with rules, and cool-down.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Grief reactions* (25 min): Learn that grief is not linear and identify common physical and emotional reactions.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Edit & publish* (75 min): Edit for verbals, dialogue punctuation and commas; type and publish the final draft.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Dilations on a square grid* (60 min): Dilate polygons on a coordinate grid with center at the origin and write the rule (x, y) -> (kx, ky) for k = 3, 1/2 and 1.5.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Build & test* (50 min): Build the prototype and test it; record results and observe how the device increases collision time.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Slavery and the Constitution* (50 min): Analyze the Three-Fifths Clause, slave trade clause and fugitive slave clause; discuss how they contradicted the Declaration.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Colors* (45 min): Learn color words with agreement (rojo/roja, azul, marrón) and describe the colors of 10 items in your room.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Render* (40 min): Add value and detail.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Modify* (40 min): Write two modifications so a younger sibling or someone with limited mobility can play your game fully.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Supporting others* (25 min): Write what to say and not say to a grieving friend.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Narrative writing workshop and unit assessment
- Objective: Draft, revise and edit a complete suspense narrative
- Objective: Demonstrate understanding of unit concepts on a cold-read assessment
- Vocabulary: transition, pacing, dialogue tag, revision, ellipsis
- Materials: draft, revision checklist, editing checklist
- Assessment: Published narrative (rubric) and Unit 1 cold-read test.

### Mathematics 8: Dilations
- Objective: Dilate figures from a center with a given scale factor
- Objective: Describe dilations with coordinates centered at the origin
- Vocabulary: dilation, scale factor, center of dilation, enlargement, reduction
- Materials: ruler, graph paper, compass, GeoGebra
- Assessment: Quiz on dilations by hand and with coordinates.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Engineering challenge: protect the egg
- Objective: Define criteria and constraints for a collision-protection device
- Objective: Test, compare and improve designs using Newton's third law
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraints, prototype, iterate, impact force
- Materials: raw eggs or water balloons, straws, tape, cardboard, cotton balls, rubber bands, plastic bags
- Assessment: Engineering notebook rubric and Unit 1 test.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The Constitutional Convention
- Objective: Evaluate the major debates and compromises at the Convention
- Objective: Describe the roles of key delegates
- Vocabulary: Virginia Plan, New Jersey Plan, Great Compromise, Three-Fifths Compromise, electoral college, commerce clause
- Materials: Constitution transcript, delegate profile cards
- Assessment: Compromise quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Mi casa y mi dormitorio
- Objective: Name rooms and furniture
- Objective: Describe your bedroom
- Vocabulary: la cocina, la sala, el dormitorio, el baño, la cama, la cómoda, el armario, la alfombra
- Materials: floor plan paper, sticky notes
- Assessment: House vocabulary quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Drawing unit project and critique
- Objective: Complete a finished drawing using line, value and perspective
- Objective: Critique artwork with criteria
- Vocabulary: composition, focal point, critique, criteria
- Materials: drawing materials
- Assessment: Final drawing and written critique.

### Physical Education 8: Leading a group activity
- Objective: Plan and lead a warm-up and game
- Objective: Adapt activities so everyone can participate
- Vocabulary: leadership, inclusion, modification, etiquette
- Materials: cones, balls, lesson-plan template
- Assessment: Leadership rubric for the led session.

### Health Education 8: Grief, loss and change
- Objective: Describe common grief reactions
- Objective: Identify healthy coping
- Vocabulary: grief, loss, coping, support system
- Materials: health journal, picture book or article on grief
- Assessment: Support letter.
