# Physical Education 8 (Grade 8)

Eighth-grade physical education that builds on grade 7 by emphasizing team strategy, leadership and the science of exercise. Students compare fitness results to last year, measure skill-related fitness, play new invasion, net, target and striking/fielding games (flag football, team handball, tennis, table tennis, golf, cricket), learn safe resistance training and how body systems adapt, explore social dance and mind-body fitness, compete in multi-part track events, and finish by designing, following and evaluating a two-week personal fitness plan. Sessions work in a yard, park or community center with family or a homeschool group.

*Framework:* California Physical Education Model Content Standards (Grade 8); 2009 CA Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 40 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Fitness Check-In and Skill-Related Fitness (weeks 1–3)
Health-related fitness keeps you well; skill-related fitness helps you perform—both can be measured and improved.

- Week 1: Health-related fitness check-in
- Week 2: Skill-related fitness components
- Week 3: Goals, motivation and activity tracking

### Unit 2: Adventure Challenges and Team Leadership (weeks 4–5)
Leadership means organizing others, solving problems together and including everyone.

- Week 4: Adventure challenges and problem solving
- Week 5: Leading a group activity

### Unit 3: Invasion Games: Flag Football and Team Handball (weeks 6–10)
Set plays, transitions and defensive systems turn individual skills into team success.

- Week 6: Flag football: throwing, catching and routes
- Week 7: Flag football: plays and game play
- Week 8: Team handball: skills
- Week 9: Team handball: transition and game play
- Week 10: Invasion games tournament and officiating

### Unit 4: Net and Wall Games: Tennis, Table Tennis and Advanced Volleyball (weeks 11–14)
Spin, placement and team rotations add tactical depth to net games.

- Week 11: Tennis: forehand, backhand and serve
- Week 12: Tennis and table tennis: rallies and scoring
- Week 13: Advanced volleyball: spike, block and rotation
- Week 14: Net games practice and feedback

### Unit 5: Exercise Science and Resistance Training (weeks 15–19)
Body systems adapt to training; safe technique, nutrition and sleep make training effective.

- Week 15: Body systems and exercise
- Week 16: Major muscles and safe resistance training
- Week 17: Designing a strength workout
- Week 18: Fueling performance: nutrition, hydration and sleep
- Week 19: Semester fitness check and plan revision

### Unit 6: Target and Striking/Fielding Games (weeks 20–23)
Accuracy depends on consistent mechanics and controlling force and angle.

- Week 20: Golf and disc golf
- Week 21: Archery-style and bowling target skills
- Week 22: Cricket and kickball strategy
- Week 23: Student-designed game

### Unit 7: Social, Square and Contemporary Dance (weeks 24–26)
Partner and group dance build rhythm, cooperation and cardiovascular fitness.

- Week 24: Square and folk dance
- Week 25: Social and partner dance
- Week 26: Contemporary dance fitness choreography

### Unit 8: Mind-Body Fitness and Stress Management (weeks 27–28)
Yoga, breathing and mindful movement build flexibility and reduce stress.

- Week 27: Yoga and flexibility
- Week 28: Exercise and stress management

### Unit 9: Track and Field: Multi-Part Events (weeks 29–31)
Complex events are mastered by breaking them into phases and practicing each.

- Week 29: Relays and hurdles
- Week 30: Jumps and throws: triple jump and shot technique
- Week 31: Track meet and pacing strategy

### Unit 10: Lifetime Activities and Two-Week Fitness Plan (weeks 32–36)
Choosing activities you enjoy and planning around barriers keeps you active for life.

- Week 32: Lifetime activities: cycling, hiking and swimming safety
- Week 33: Final fitness assessment
- Week 34: Two-week personal fitness plan: design
- Week 35: Two-week personal fitness plan: implement
- Week 36: Plan evaluation and transition to high school PE

## Standards

- **PE-8.1.1** Demonstrate mature, efficient form in team physical activities by combining and applying movement skills in complex game situations.
- **PE-8.1.2** Demonstrate offensive, defensive and transition skills (cutting, screening, zone and person-to-person defense) in team invasion games.
- **PE-8.1.3** Demonstrate advanced striking, serving and volleying skills with implements in net/wall and target games.
- **PE-8.1.4** Perform social, square and contemporary dances with correct timing, partner etiquette and transitions.
- **PE-8.1.5** Demonstrate technique in multi-part individual events (hurdles, relays, triple jump) and outdoor/lifetime activities.
- **PE-8.2.1** Analyze and select offensive and defensive tactics (give-and-go, zone defense, set plays) appropriate to a game situation.
- **PE-8.2.2** Analyze a movement or skill using biomechanical concepts (levers, angle of release, force absorption, momentum) and give corrective feedback.
- **PE-8.2.3** Explain how practice schedules, feedback and mental rehearsal improve skill learning.
- **PE-8.3.1** Participate in moderate-to-vigorous physical activity at least four days each week, including activities outside of class.
- **PE-8.3.2** Assess health- and skill-related fitness components (agility, balance, coordination, power, reaction time, speed) and interpret results.
- **PE-8.3.3** Meet Healthy Fitness Zone standards for aerobic capacity, muscular strength and endurance, flexibility and body composition.
- **PE-8.3.4** Develop, implement and evaluate a two-week personal fitness plan that applies the principles of training.
- **PE-8.4.1** Explain how the muscular, skeletal, cardiorespiratory and nervous systems respond and adapt to exercise.
- **PE-8.4.2** Identify major muscle groups and select exercises with safe technique (resistance bands, body weight, light free weights) to strengthen each.
- **PE-8.4.3** Explain the role of nutrition, hydration and sleep in physical performance and recovery.
- **PE-8.4.4** Describe how physical activity reduces stress and improves mental and emotional health.
- **PE-8.5.1** Organize and lead group activities, demonstrating responsible leadership, rules knowledge and etiquette.
- **PE-8.5.2** Accept responsibility for team success, resolve conflicts respectfully and include others of differing abilities.
- **PE-8.5.3** Evaluate personal progress toward goals and identify the motivation and barriers that affect lifelong activity.
