## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Portfolio selection* (75 min): Select your best narrative, argument, analysis and research pieces and write a cover note for each.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Review: geometry* (60 min): Review transformations, similarity, angles, Pythagorean theorem and volume with 15 problems.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Presentation prep* (50 min): Create slides or a poster showing problem, designs, data and final solution.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Timeline review* (50 min): Complete the 1750–1900 timeline and mark 10 turning points with one-sentence explanations.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Interpretive* (45 min): Read an authentic text and listen to a native-speaker clip, answering comprehension and inference questions.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Setup* (40 min): Set up the exhibit.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Plan day* (40 min): Complete the scheduled workout and log it.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Wellness wheel* (25 min): Re-rate your wellness wheel from grade 7 and compare.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Final exam: reading* (75 min): Take the final reading assessment on unfamiliar literary and informational passages.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Review: algebra* (60 min): Review linear equations, systems, functions, exponents and scientific notation with 15 problems.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Presentation* (50 min): Present the capstone to family and answer questions.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Essential question essay* (50 min): Write an essay: 'How did Americans struggle to fulfill the promise of the Declaration from 1776 to 1900?'
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Interpersonal* (45 min): Hold a 5-minute unscripted conversation about home, shopping, a past trip, and technology.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Rehearse* (40 min): Rehearse performance pieces.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Plan day* (40 min): Complete the final scheduled workout of the two weeks.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Plan* (25 min): Write a high school wellness plan with goals for physical, mental, social and safety health.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Final exam: writing & language* (75 min): Write a timed argument essay and complete grammar and vocabulary items.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Final exam part 1* (60 min): Take part 1 of the cumulative final (geometry, number system, data).
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Final exam part 1* (50 min): Physical science (forces, energy, waves) and Earth science.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Final exam part 1* (50 min): Take part 1 of the final covering the Revolution through the antebellum era.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Presentational writing* (45 min): Write a 15–20 sentence narrative about a memorable day using present and preterite.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Showcase* (40 min): Host the showcase.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Evaluate* (40 min): Write an evaluation of your plan: adherence, enjoyment and results, plus one change.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Supports* (25 min): List supports and resources you will use in high school.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Reading reflection* (75 min): Write a reflection on your independent reading this year and set summer reading goals.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Final exam part 2* (60 min): Take part 2 of the cumulative final (expressions, equations, functions).
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Final exam part 2* (50 min): Life science (heredity, evolution).
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Final exam part 2* (50 min): Take part 2 covering the Civil War through Industrial America.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Presentational speaking* (45 min): Present a 2-minute cultural comparison from the year.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Feedback* (40 min): Collect guest feedback.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Looking ahead* (40 min): Research high school PE course requirements and write two goals for grade 9.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Review* (25 min): Review key concepts from all six units.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Celebration* (75 min): Share a favorite piece aloud and discuss your growth with family.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Reflection & portfolio* (60 min): Correct final exam errors and write a one-page reflection on your three most improved skills; organize the math portfolio for Algebra I.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Reflection* (50 min): Write a reflection on growth as a scientist and set goals for high school Biology.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Reflection* (50 min): Write a reflection on the most important person and event studied this year and why.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Reflection* (45 min): Complete the Spanish I can-do self-assessment and set goals for Spanish II.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Reflection* (40 min): Write a reflection.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Field day* (40 min): Organize and lead a family or co-op field day with stations from this year's units.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Final* (25 min): Take a cumulative final assessment and share your plan with a parent.
