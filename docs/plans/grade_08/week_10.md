## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Draft body paragraphs* (75 min): Draft three body paragraphs using the claim-quotation-commentary pattern with embedded, cited quotations.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Negative slope* (60 min): Model a draining tank or spending a gift card balance; graph and interpret the negative rate of change.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *What is gravity?* (50 min): Drop objects of different mass and observe they land together; discuss mass vs weight.

- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Clothing* (45 min): Learn 20 clothing words and label clothes from your closet.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Literary analysis essay and film comparison
- Objective: Write a multi-paragraph literary analysis essay
- Objective: Analyze how the 1983 film adaptation stays faithful to or departs from the novel
- Vocabulary: adaptation, fidelity, cinematography, counterclaim, commentary
- Materials: The Outsiders (1983 film, library DVD or streaming), essay outline
- Assessment: Literary analysis essay (rubric) and Unit 2 test.

### Mathematics 8: Slope signs, special lines, writing equations
- Objective: Interpret negative, zero and undefined slopes
- Objective: Write equations of lines from two points or a graph
- Vocabulary: negative slope, horizontal line, vertical line, slope-intercept form
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Quiz on writing linear equations.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Gravity is attractive and depends on mass
- Objective: Use evidence to argue that gravity depends on mass and distance
- Objective: Compare weight on different planets
- Vocabulary: gravity, gravitational force, mass, weight, distance
- Materials: bathroom scale, NASA planet data, balls, stretchy fabric or bedsheet
- Assessment: Gravity argument and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Life, economy and culture in the early Republic
- Objective: Describe daily life, art and literature of early America
- Objective: Outline early treaties with American Indian nations
- Vocabulary: capitalism, contract, McCulloch v. Maryland, Treaty of Greenville, folklore
- Materials: 'Rip Van Winkle' by Washington Irving (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Spanish 1B: La ropa
- Objective: Name clothing items
- Objective: Describe what people wear for different weather
- Vocabulary: la camisa, los pantalones, la falda, el vestido, los zapatos, la chaqueta, llevar, la gorra
- Materials: closet items, catalog photos
- Assessment: Clothing vocabulary quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Rhythm reading
- Objective: Read and perform rhythms with whole through sixteenth notes and rests
- Objective: Count in 4/4, 3/4 and 6/8
- Vocabulary: time signature, measure, beat, whole note, quarter note, eighth note, sixteenth note, rest, syncopation
- Materials: musictheory.net, metronome app, pencil and staff paper, pots or a drum pad
- Assessment: Rhythm dictation and performance check.

### Physical Education 8: Invasion games tournament and officiating
- Objective: Officiate a game fairly
- Objective: Apply team strategy in tournament play
- Vocabulary: official, foul, tournament bracket
- Materials: balls, flag belts, whistle, bracket sheet
- Assessment: Officiating observation and team reflection.

### Health Education 8: Budget meals and food security
- Objective: Plan and cook a healthy budget meal
- Objective: Describe food insecurity and community resources
- Vocabulary: food insecurity, unit price, CalFresh, food bank
- Materials: grocery ads, kitchen, recipe
- Assessment: Meal plan, cooking and unit test.
