## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Launch & reading inventory* (75 min): Complete a reading interest survey, choose an independent reading book, and write a one-page 'reading life' reflection; read Poe's biography.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Diagnostic & moves warm-up* (60 min): Take a 15-problem Grade 7 readiness check (proportions, integers, expressions); then use tracing paper to slide, turn and flip a paper cutout and describe each move in words.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Science notebook & frames of reference* (50 min): Set up a science notebook; observe a toy car rolling past from two viewpoints and write how each observer would describe its motion.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Course launch & historical thinking* (50 min): Set up an interactive notebook and timeline (1750–1900); practice sourcing a document by asking who, when, why and how reliable.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Diagnostic* (45 min): Hold a 3-minute conversation with a parent about your summer, family, and likes, then take a short 1A diagnostic quiz to find gaps to target.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Sketchbook setup* (40 min): Decorate a sketchbook cover and do 5 one-minute gesture drawings of a family member or pet.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Lead the warm-up* (40 min): Lead a family member or sibling through the 10-move dynamic warm-up learned in grade 7, then run 10 minutes of tag-style games.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Grade 7 recap* (25 min): Review the stress-coping strategies from grade 7 and rate how often you use each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Read 'The Tell-Tale Heart'* (75 min): Read the story aloud (or with the LibriVox recording) and annotate every place the narrator insists he is not mad.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Translations* (60 min): Translate triangles on a grid using directed line segments; draw 4 images and label corresponding points A to A'.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Measuring speed* (50 min): Mark 0.5 m intervals with tape, time a toy car over 3 m, and calculate average speed in m/s for 3 trials.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Magna Carta & English Bill of Rights* (50 min): Read excerpts of the Magna Carta (1215) and English Bill of Rights (1689); list rights each limited the king's power over.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Table setting* (45 min): Learn 12 table-setting words and set the table while naming each item aloud: Pongo el tenedor a la izquierda.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Blind contour* (40 min): Do three blind contour drawings of your hand without looking at the paper; write what you noticed.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *PACER re-test* (40 min): Complete the 20-meter PACER (or timed mile) and record your score next to last year's.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Resilience* (25 min): Read a KidsHealth article on resilience and list the 'bounce back' skills it describes.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Narrator reliability* (75 min): Write a paragraph citing three pieces of evidence that the narrator is unreliable and explain how that builds suspense.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Rotations* (60 min): Rotate figures 90, 180 and 270 degrees clockwise and counterclockwise about a given center using tracing paper; record the center and angle for each.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Distance-time graphs* (50 min): Graph your toy-car data and walking data; interpret steepness as speed and flat segments as stopping.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Mayflower Compact* (50 min): Read the full Mayflower Compact (1620) and annotate examples of self-government and consent.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Restaurant foods* (45 min): Learn main dishes, desserts, and drinks (el bistec, el arroz, el flan) and build a three-course menu.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Contour drawing* (40 min): Draw a shoe in modified contour line, looking 80% at the object.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Strength re-test* (40 min): Complete curl-ups and 90-degree push-ups to cadence and record repetitions.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Protective factors* (25 min): Map your protective factors (people, activities, strengths) on a support web.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Grammar: gerunds* (75 min): Learn that gerunds are -ing verbals used as nouns; find 5 in the story and write 8 original sentences using gerunds as subject, object and object of preposition.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Reflections* (60 min): Reflect figures across horizontal, vertical and diagonal lines; complete IM Lesson 'Grid Moves' style practice of 8 problems mixing all three moves.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Velocity and direction* (50 min): Use the PhET 'Forces and Motion: Basics' motion screen to compare speed and velocity; record 3 observations.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Enlightenment thinkers* (50 min): Read short excerpts from Locke's Second Treatise and Montesquieu's Spirit of the Laws; make a chart linking each idea to American government.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Polite requests* (45 min): Learn me trae..., ¿me puede traer...?, ¿algo más?, la cuenta por favor and practice with a parent as camarero.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Cross-contour* (40 min): Add cross-contour lines to a drawing of a banana or mug to show form.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Flexibility re-test* (40 min): Complete the back-saver sit-and-reach and trunk lift; record both sides.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Growth mindset* (25 min): Rewrite five fixed-mindset statements as growth-mindset statements.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Review & check* (75 min): Take a reading quiz on the story and roots (acu-, dis-, -ous); share a 2-minute oral summary using precise vocabulary.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: identify and perform 6 single transformations and write a precise description of each; correct errors in a math notebook.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on speed calculations and interpreting 4 distance-time graphs; write a one-paragraph summary of the lab.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a paragraph answering 'Where did American ideas about rights come from?' citing two documents.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a restaurant vocabulary quiz and review the diagnostic results to set two personal goals.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Reflect* (40 min): Choose your best drawing and write a reflection on progress.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Compare* (40 min): Graph grade 7 vs. grade 8 scores for each item and write which components improved or declined and why.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Check* (25 min): Write a short reflection on a setback you overcame and which protective factors helped.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: 'The Tell-Tale Heart': unreliable narrators
- Objective: Analyze how a first-person narrator's point of view creates suspense
- Objective: Identify gerunds and their functions in sentences
- Vocabulary: unreliable narrator, point of view, suspense, gerund, dissimulation, acute (Latin acu- 'sharp')
- Materials: Edgar Allan Poe, 'The Tell-Tale Heart' (Project Gutenberg), reader's notebook, independent reading book
- Assessment: Reading quiz plus evidence paragraph scored on claim, evidence and explanation.

### Mathematics 8: Moving figures: translations, rotations, reflections
- Objective: Describe translations, rotations and reflections in precise language
- Objective: Perform rigid motions on a grid and with tracing paper
- Vocabulary: transformation, translation, rotation, reflection, image, pre-image, center of rotation, line of reflection
- Materials: tracing paper, graph paper, protractor, ruler
- Assessment: Friday quiz on performing and describing single rigid motions (80% target).

### Science 8 (Integrated): Describing motion
- Objective: Describe motion using position, distance, speed and a frame of reference
- Objective: Make and interpret distance-time graphs
- Vocabulary: motion, reference frame, position, speed, velocity, distance-time graph
- Materials: toy car, masking tape, meter stick, stopwatch or phone timer, graph paper
- Assessment: Speed lab data table and graph plus Friday quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Foundations: English rights and Enlightenment ideas
- Objective: Explain how the Magna Carta, English Bill of Rights and Mayflower Compact shaped colonial ideas
- Objective: Identify Enlightenment ideas of Locke and Montesquieu
- Vocabulary: Magna Carta, English Bill of Rights, Mayflower Compact, natural rights, social contract, Enlightenment
- Materials: notebook, timeline strip, National Archives document excerpts
- Assessment: Document-based paragraph scored for claim and evidence.

### Spanish 1B: ¿Qué tal el verano? Diagnostic and restaurant vocabulary
- Objective: Reactivate Spanish 1A skills in a new context
- Objective: Name table items and restaurant foods
- Vocabulary: el camarero, la camarera, el plato principal, el postre, la cuchara, el tenedor, el cuchillo, la servilleta
- Materials: 1A can-do checklist, place setting
- Assessment: Restaurant vocabulary quiz and diagnostic conversation.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Line, contour and observation
- Objective: Use contour and blind contour drawing to improve observation
- Objective: Start a sketchbook documenting the creative process
- Vocabulary: contour line, blind contour, gesture, sketchbook, observation
- Materials: sketchbook, pencils (HB, 2B, 4B), eraser, household objects
- Assessment: Sketchbook check: 8 drawings with reflection.

### Physical Education 8: Health-related fitness check-in
- Objective: Re-test grade 7 FITNESSGRAM items
- Objective: Compare results to last year and the Healthy Fitness Zone
- Vocabulary: Healthy Fitness Zone, baseline, muscular endurance
- Materials: PACER audio (free online), cones, stopwatch, mat, fitness journal
- Assessment: Completed fitness comparison graph with written analysis.

### Health Education 8: Resilience and protective factors
- Objective: Define resilience and protective factors
- Objective: Identify personal strengths
- Vocabulary: resilience, protective factor, risk factor, self-esteem
- Materials: health journal, KidsHealth articles
- Assessment: Support web and reflection.
