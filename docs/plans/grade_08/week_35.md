## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Planning* (75 min): Plan a 5-minute presentation of the research with key points and evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Business idea and costs* (60 min): Choose a small business (lemonade stand, dog walking, baking) and write a cost function with fixed and per-item costs.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Build* (50 min): Build prototype version 1.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Multimedia presentation
- Objective: Present research findings clearly with evidence
- Objective: Integrate multimedia to clarify and strengthen claims
- Vocabulary: multimedia, visual aid, eye contact, pacing, audience
- Materials: slides software, images with credits, timer
- Assessment: Presentation rubric and reflection.

### Mathematics 8: Modeling project: planning a small business
- Objective: Use linear functions to model cost and revenue
- Objective: Use a system to find a break-even point
- Vocabulary: cost, revenue, profit, break-even point, fixed cost, variable cost
- Materials: Desmos, spreadsheet or graph paper
- Assessment: Business modeling project rubric.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Capstone: build, test, iterate
- Objective: Build and test a prototype
- Objective: Use test data to improve the design
- Vocabulary: prototype, iteration, test data, optimization
- Materials: project materials, measuring tools
- Assessment: Testing data and iteration log.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Industrial America museum project
- Objective: Research a topic from industrial America using primary sources
- Objective: Present findings in an exhibit
- Vocabulary: primary source, secondary source, citation, thesis, exhibit
- Materials: library books, Library of Congress and Calisphere sources, poster board or slides
- Assessment: Exhibit rubric and Unit 10 test.

### Spanish 1B: Spanish I review: past tense and culture
- Objective: Review the preterite and cultural topics
- Vocabulary: repaso
- Materials: unit tests, travel blog
- Assessment: Self-scored practice tasks.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Curating the portfolio
- Objective: Select and prepare work for exhibition
- Objective: Write exhibition narratives
- Vocabulary: curate, exhibition, label, artist statement
- Materials: year's artwork, mats or cardstock
- Assessment: Portfolio selection and statements.

### Physical Education 8: Two-week personal fitness plan: implement
- Objective: Follow and log the plan
- Objective: Monitor intensity
- Vocabulary: perceived exertion, heart-rate zone, adherence
- Materials: fitness journal, watch or heart-rate monitor
- Assessment: Activity log for plan week 1.

### Health Education 8: Health advocacy campaign: carry out
- Objective: Create and share campaign materials
- Objective: Measure impact
- Vocabulary: advocacy, impact, survey
- Materials: art supplies or video tools
- Assessment: Campaign and impact reflection.
