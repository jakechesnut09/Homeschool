# Health Education 8 (Grade 8)

A skills-based grade 8 health course that builds on Health 7 with deeper study of resilience, suicide prevention and the effects of social media on mental health; energy balance, body image and evaluating supplements; comprehensive sexual health aligned with the California Healthy Youth Act (reproduction, abstinence and medically accurate prevention, HIV/STIs, gender identity and respect, healthy relationships and consent, human trafficking and sextortion awareness); opioids, naloxone, addiction and recovery; CPR and violence prevention; and consumer and community health. Sexual health lessons (weeks 11-18) are designed for parent preview, and parents may opt their student out under CA Education Code 51938; an alternative assignment is provided.

*Framework:* 2008 California Health Education Content Standards (Grades 7–8); 2019 CA Health Education Framework; California Healthy Youth Act (Ed. Code 51930–51939)  
*Time:* 25 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Mental, Emotional and Social Health: Resilience and Help-Seeking (weeks 1–6)
Resilience, healthy tech habits and knowing how to get help protect mental health; mental illness is treatable.

- Week 1: Resilience and protective factors
- Week 2: Technology, social media and mental health
- Week 3: Mental illness and reducing stigma
- Week 4: Suicide prevention and self-harm awareness
- Week 5: Grief, loss and change
- Week 6: Mental health advocacy and unit assessment

### Unit 2: Nutrition, Body Image and Performance (weeks 7–10)
A growing body needs energy balance; media can distort body image, and valid information protects against harmful products.

- Week 7: Energy balance and teen nutrient needs
- Week 8: Body image, media and eating disorders
- Week 9: Supplements, energy drinks and diet claims
- Week 10: Budget meals and food security

### Unit 3: Growth, Development and Sexual Health (CA Healthy Youth Act) (weeks 11–18)
Accurate information, respect for all people, clear boundaries and trusted adults support healthy relationships and decisions. Parents may opt their student out of sexual health lessons (Ed. Code 51938).

- Week 11: Introduction: sexual health, values and trusted adults
- Week 12: Reproduction, pregnancy and fetal development
- Week 13: Abstinence and preventing pregnancy
- Week 14: HIV and STI prevention
- Week 15: Gender identity, sexual orientation and respect
- Week 16: Healthy and unhealthy dating relationships
- Week 17: Human trafficking, sextortion and online exploitation
- Week 18: Sexual health review and decision making

### Unit 4: Alcohol, Tobacco and Other Drugs: Beyond the Basics (weeks 19–24)
Addiction is a treatable disease; recognizing marketing, overdose signs and the law empowers safe choices and helping others.

- Week 19: Alcohol, BAC and impaired driving
- Week 20: Opioids, fentanyl and naloxone
- Week 21: Stimulants, inhalants, caffeine and cannabis potency
- Week 22: Youth-targeted marketing
- Week 23: Addiction, treatment and recovery
- Week 24: Helping a friend and unit assessment

### Unit 5: Injury Prevention, Violence Prevention and Emergency Response (weeks 25–30)
Knowing how to respond in an emergency and how to prevent violence can save lives.

- Week 25: Hands-only CPR, AED and choking
- Week 26: Heat, water and wildfire smoke emergencies
- Week 27: Distracted walking, biking and safe rides
- Week 28: Dating violence, harassment and assault prevention
- Week 29: Weapons safety and threat reporting
- Week 30: Safety unit review and assessment

### Unit 6: Personal, Consumer and Community Health (weeks 31–36)
Smart health consumers, public health systems and personal plans prevent disease across a lifetime.

- Week 31: Chronic disease risk factors
- Week 32: Health consumer skills
- Week 33: Public health and health careers
- Week 34: Health advocacy campaign: plan
- Week 35: Health advocacy campaign: carry out
- Week 36: High school wellness plan and year review

## Standards

- **H-8.1.2.A** Explain the effects of alcohol on the developing brain, blood alcohol concentration and impaired judgment, including driving under the influence.
- **H-8.1.2.G** Describe the human reproductive process, including fertilization, pregnancy and fetal development (CHYA; parent opt-out).
- **H-8.1.2.M** Describe resilience, self-esteem and protective factors and how they support mental health.
- **H-8.1.2.N** Explain energy balance and the nutritional needs of active, growing adolescents.
- **H-8.1.2.P** Explain risk factors for chronic diseases (heart disease, diabetes, cancer, asthma) and how behavior affects risk.
- **H-8.1.3.G** Explain that abstinence is the only certain way to prevent pregnancy and STIs, and describe the effectiveness of FDA-approved methods of preventing pregnancy and STIs, medically accurate and age-appropriate (CHYA; parent opt-out).
- **H-8.1.3.S** Explain hands-only CPR, AED use and choking response for teens and adults.
- **H-8.1.4.A** Describe opioids, fentanyl, overdose signs and how naloxone is used to reverse an opioid overdose.
- **H-8.1.4.M** Describe grief and loss and healthy ways to cope.
- **H-8.1.4.S** Describe risk factors for and prevention of dating violence, sexual harassment and assault.
- **H-8.1.5.A** Describe the misuse of prescription stimulants, inhalants, caffeine and energy drinks and high-potency cannabis products.
- **H-8.1.5.G** Describe how HIV and other sexually transmitted infections are transmitted, prevented, tested for and treated (CHYA; parent opt-out).
- **H-8.1.5.M** Identify warning signs of suicide and self-harm, myths and facts, and immediate steps for getting help (including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
- **H-8.1.5.N** Describe the signs, health risks and treatment of eating disorders and disordered eating.
- **H-8.1.6.G** Describe gender identity, gender expression and sexual orientation and the importance of treating all people with dignity and respect (CHYA).
- **H-8.1.6.S** Describe safety risks of distracted walking, cycling and riding with impaired or distracted drivers.
- **H-8.1.7.A** Describe addiction as a treatable disease and the stages of treatment and recovery.
- **H-8.1.7.P** Describe the role of public health agencies and health careers in protecting community health.
- **H-8.1.8.G** Describe characteristics of healthy, unhealthy and abusive dating relationships and the meaning of affirmative consent.
- **H-8.1.8.S** Describe safe storage of firearms and steps to take if a weapon is seen or a threat is heard.
- **H-8.1.9.G** Recognize warning signs of human trafficking and sexual exploitation (including sextortion) and how to seek help.
- **H-8.2.1.G** Analyze influences of media, peers and culture on beliefs about relationships and sexuality.
- **H-8.2.1.M** Analyze how social media and technology use influence mood, self-image, sleep and relationships.
- **H-8.2.1.P** Analyze how advertising and health claims influence the purchase of health products and services.
- **H-8.2.2.A** Analyze tobacco, vaping, alcohol and cannabis industry marketing tactics aimed at youth.
- **H-8.2.2.N** Analyze how media, culture and peers influence body image and food choices.
- **H-8.3.1.M** Access valid mental health resources in the community, at school and online.
- **H-8.3.2.G** Identify trusted adults and valid local resources for reproductive health care and relationship support, including minors' rights to confidential services in California.
- **H-8.3.2.N** Evaluate sports supplements, energy drinks and diet products using valid information.
- **H-8.3.2.P** Evaluate health products, services and online health information for validity as a responsible consumer.
- **H-8.4.1.A** Demonstrate how to help a friend who may be using substances and how to get adult help.
- **H-8.4.1.S** Demonstrate strategies to de-escalate conflict and report threats or harassment.
- **H-8.4.2.G** Demonstrate refusal, negotiation and help-seeking communication in relationship situations.
- **H-8.5.1.G** Use a decision-making model to evaluate the consequences of sexual activity and choose healthy behaviors consistent with family values.
- **H-8.5.2.A** Explain California laws and consequences related to alcohol, tobacco and drug possession and use by minors.
- **H-8.6.1.M** Set a goal to strengthen a mental or emotional health skill and monitor progress.
- **H-8.6.1.P** Develop a personal health plan for the transition to high school that addresses multiple areas of health.
- **H-8.7.2.N** Plan and prepare healthy, budget-friendly meals and snacks; describe food insecurity and community food resources.
- **H-8.7.2.S** Practice responding to emergencies, including heat illness, water emergencies and wildfire smoke.
- **H-8.8.1.M** Advocate for reducing stigma around mental illness and for help-seeking.
- **H-8.8.2.P** Plan and carry out a health advocacy campaign that influences others to make healthy choices.
