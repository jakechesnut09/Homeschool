## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Bottom transformed* (75 min): Read Act 3, Scene 1 and analyze how Bottom's oblivious dialogue with Titania creates humor through dramatic irony.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Finding a pattern* (60 min): Draw right triangles on a grid, build squares on each side and record areas to discover a^2 + b^2 = c^2.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Bird beak lab* (50 min): Use tweezers, spoons and clothespins as 'beaks' to collect different 'foods'; record which beaks survive.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Violence and the KKK* (50 min): Read 1871 congressional testimony from survivors of Klan violence and explain the purpose of the Enforcement Acts.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Watch* (45 min): Watch a family film with Spanish audio (e.g., Coco) with Spanish subtitles and take notes on characters.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Animation basics* (40 min): Watch a short stop-motion example and calculate frames needed for 30 seconds at 12 fps.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Triple jump phases* (40 min): Practice hop-step-jump from a standing start, then with a short approach.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Kit* (25 min): Inventory and restock a home first-aid kit.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: A Midsummer Night's Dream, Acts 3–4
- Objective: Analyze how mistaken identity and dramatic irony drive the comedy
- Objective: Compare how the interwoven plots are structured
- Vocabulary: comedy, mistaken identity, subplot, dramatic irony, resolution, ass's head
- Materials: Folger edition, three-column plot chart
- Assessment: Plot structure chart and quiz.

### Mathematics 8: The Pythagorean theorem and its converse
- Objective: Explain a visual proof of the Pythagorean theorem
- Objective: Use the converse to determine whether a triangle is a right triangle
- Vocabulary: hypotenuse, legs, Pythagorean theorem, converse, right triangle
- Materials: graph paper, scissors, ruler, string
- Assessment: Written proof explanation and quiz.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Variation and survival
- Objective: Explain how variation affects survival and reproduction
- Objective: Simulate predation on a population
- Vocabulary: natural selection, adaptation, variation, predator, prey, fitness
- Materials: colored beads, dried beans or paper dots, patterned fabric or wrapping paper, tweezers, spoons, clothespins
- Assessment: Simulation data and CER.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The end of Reconstruction and Jim Crow
- Objective: Explain the rise of the KKK and the end of Reconstruction
- Objective: Describe Jim Crow laws and voting restrictions
- Vocabulary: Ku Klux Klan, Enforcement Acts, Compromise of 1877, Jim Crow, poll tax, literacy test, segregation
- Materials: Thomas Nast cartoons, congressional testimony excerpts on Klan violence
- Assessment: Unit 9 test and DBQ essay.

### Spanish 1B: Unit 7 assessment: Crítica de cine
- Objective: Write and present a film review
- Objective: Summarize a plot in the preterite
- Vocabulary: la crítica, el argumento, el/la protagonista, recomendar, las estrellas
- Materials: a family film with Spanish audio or subtitles
- Assessment: Film review rubric and unit 7 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Stop-motion animation: planning
- Objective: Plan a stop-motion animation with storyboard
- Objective: Understand frame rate
- Vocabulary: stop-motion, frame, frame rate, storyboard, animatic
- Materials: phone with free Stop Motion Studio app, clay or LEGO figures, tripod or stack of books
- Assessment: Storyboard and test animation.

### Physical Education 8: Jumps and throws: triple jump and shot technique
- Objective: Perform hop-step-jump sequence
- Objective: Use the glide or standing put with a soft ball
- Vocabulary: hop-step-jump, momentum, put, release angle
- Materials: sand area or soft grass, tape measure, softball or 4-lb medicine ball
- Assessment: Triple jump and put distance log with explanation.

### Health Education 8: Safety unit review and assessment
- Objective: Demonstrate emergency skills
- Objective: Show mastery of unit concepts
- Vocabulary: emergency action plan
- Materials: first aid kit, CPR pillow
- Assessment: Skills demo and unit test.
