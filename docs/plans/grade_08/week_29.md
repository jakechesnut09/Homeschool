## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Introducing Shakespeare* (75 min): Watch a short Folger video on the Globe Theatre, then read Act 1, Scene 1 and paraphrase Egeus's complaint and Hermia's choice.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Tilted squares* (60 min): Draw squares on a grid with tilted sides; find their areas by decomposing or surrounding with a larger square.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Artificial selection* (50 min): Compare wild teosinte to modern corn and dog breeds to wolves; list traits humans selected for.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Freedmen's Bureau* (50 min): Read letters from freedpeople to the Freedmen's Bureau seeking family members, schools and fair wages.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Acabar de* (45 min): Learn acabar de + infinitive and write 8 sentences about things you just did.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Media influence* (40 min): Compare how the same event is shown in news photos, ads and social media and how each influences viewers.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Baton exchange* (40 min): Practice the upsweep and downsweep handoff while jogging, then at speed.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Safe storage* (25 min): Learn why safe storage matters and what to do if you find a gun: don't touch, leave, tell an adult.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 142, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *The mechanicals* (75 min): Read Act 1, Scene 2 and list each mechanical's assigned role in 'Pyramus and Thisbe' and what Bottom's eagerness reveals.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Side lengths as square roots* (60 min): Write side lengths of those squares as square roots (e.g., sqrt 10) and estimate each between two whole numbers.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Genetic engineering* (50 min): Read about insulin-producing bacteria and Golden Rice; summarize the steps.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *14th and 15th Amendments* (50 min): Paraphrase the citizenship, equal protection and voting clauses and explain whom they protected and whom they left out.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Gustar-like verbs* (45 min): Learn me aburre(n), me interesa(n), me encanta(n), me importa(n), me falta(n) and write opinions about movies.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Planning a photo essay* (40 min): Plan a 10-photo essay on 'A day in my community.'
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Relay* (40 min): Run a 4 x 100 m relay (or shuttle relay) with a team, staying in the exchange zone.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Warning signs* (25 min): Identify warning signs that someone may hurt themselves or others.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 143, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Into the woods* (75 min): Read Act 2, Scenes 1–2 and draw a character map showing who loves whom before and after Puck's love potion.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Perfect squares* (60 min): Memorize perfect squares 1–225; solve x^2 = p for 10 values of p including non-perfect squares.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *CRISPR & ethics* (50 min): Read about CRISPR and write a balanced pro/con chart about editing crop and human genes.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Black political leaders* (50 min): Research Hiram Revels and Robert Smalls and write a short biography card for each.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Señor Wooly* (45 min): Watch a free Señor Wooly video and describe what the characters like, bore, or interest them.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Shooting* (40 min): Shoot photos following the plan.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Hurdle drills* (40 min): Practice lead-leg and trail-leg drills walking beside low pool-noodle hurdles.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Reporting* (25 min): Practice reporting a threat seen online to a trusted adult.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: A Midsummer Night's Dream, Acts 1–2
- Objective: Read and paraphrase Shakespearean verse and prose
- Objective: Identify the play's three plot worlds
- Objective: Explain puns and verbal irony
- Vocabulary: iambic pentameter, blank verse, pun, verbal irony, Athenians, mechanicals, fairies
- Materials: William Shakespeare, 'A Midsummer Night's Dream' (Folger Shakespeare free online edition), character map
- Assessment: Paraphrase task and quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Square roots and side lengths of squares
- Objective: Find areas of tilted squares on a grid and relate side length to square root
- Objective: Estimate square roots between consecutive integers
- Vocabulary: square root, perfect square, radical, irrational number
- Materials: graph paper, ruler
- Assessment: Quiz on square roots.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Technology and inheritance
- Objective: Describe selective breeding and genetic engineering
- Objective: Evaluate benefits and risks of genetic technologies
- Vocabulary: artificial selection, selective breeding, genetic engineering, GMO, CRISPR
- Materials: images of dog breeds, corn and wild teosinte
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Freedom and new rights
- Objective: Explain the 14th and 15th Amendments
- Objective: Describe the Freedmen's Bureau and the migration of freedpeople
- Vocabulary: Freedmen's Bureau, Fourteenth Amendment, Fifteenth Amendment, sharecropping, Exodusters, Buffalo Soldiers
- Materials: Freedmen's Bureau letters (National Archives), 14th and 15th Amendment texts
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Acabar de + infinitive and verbs like gustar
- Objective: Say what just happened
- Objective: Use aburrir, interesar, encantar, importar, quedar, faltar
- Vocabulary: acabar de, aburrir, interesar, encantar, importar, faltar, doler, me aburre
- Materials: Señor Wooly video
- Assessment: Gustar-like verbs quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Photo essay and media influence
- Objective: Create a photo essay telling a story
- Objective: Analyze how images in media influence viewers
- Objective: Practice ethical sharing
- Vocabulary: photo essay, sequence, caption, digital citizenship, consent
- Materials: phone or camera, free photo editor
- Assessment: Photo essay.

### Physical Education 8: Relays and hurdles
- Objective: Perform a blind relay baton exchange
- Objective: Clear low hurdles with correct lead leg
- Vocabulary: baton exchange, exchange zone, lead leg, trail leg
- Materials: baton or paper towel tube, low hurdles (pool noodles on cones), stopwatch
- Assessment: Hurdle technique analysis.

### Health Education 8: Weapons safety and threat reporting
- Objective: Explain safe firearm storage
- Objective: Respond to weapons and threats
- Vocabulary: safe storage, threat, Say Something, de-escalation
- Materials: Sandy Hook Promise Say Something resources
- Assessment: Scenario worksheet.
