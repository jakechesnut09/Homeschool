# Spanish 1B (Grade 8)

Spanish 1B completes Spanish I, continuing directly from Spanish 1A and moving students from Novice Mid toward Novice High. Students order in restaurants, describe their homes and chores, shop for clothing, and begin narrating in the past with the preterite, then discuss vacations, community service, movies, and technology. New structures include venir, ser vs. estar, comparisons, stem-changing verbs, tú commands, the present progressive, demonstratives, direct and indirect object pronouns, gustar-like verbs, and saber vs. conocer. Together with Spanish 1A it prepares students for Spanish II in grade 9.

*Framework:* 2019 California World Languages Standards; 2020 CA World Languages Framework (Novice Mid to Novice High)  
*Time:* 45 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: En el restaurante (weeks 1–4)
Dining out connects food, courtesy, and culture; ser vs. estar sharpens description.

- Week 1: ¿Qué tal el verano? Diagnostic and restaurant vocabulary
- Week 2: The verb venir and describing a meal
- Week 3: Ser vs. estar
- Week 4: Unit 1 assessment: En el restaurante

### Unit 2: Mi casa y los quehaceres (weeks 5–9)
Describing and comparing homes and chores introduces stem-changers, commands, and actions in progress.

- Week 5: Mi casa y mi dormitorio
- Week 6: Comparisons and superlatives
- Week 7: Stem-changing verbs o→ue and u→ue
- Week 8: Los quehaceres and affirmative tú commands
- Week 9: Present progressive and unit 2 assessment

### Unit 3: La ropa y las compras (weeks 10–14)
Shopping requires preferences, prices, and pointing things out, in stores and markets.

- Week 10: La ropa
- Week 11: Stem-changing e→ie verbs: pensar, querer, preferir
- Week 12: Demonstrative adjectives and numbers 100–1,000,000
- Week 13: De compras: shopping role-plays and culture
- Week 14: Unit 3 assessment: Catálogo de moda

### Unit 4: ¿Qué compraste? The Preterite Begins (weeks 15–18)
The preterite lets us tell what we did; object pronouns make speech efficient.

- Week 15: ¿Qué compraste? Preterite of regular -ar verbs
- Week 16: Spelling changes: -car, -gar, -zar in the preterite
- Week 17: Direct object pronouns lo, la, los, las
- Week 18: Semester 1 review and exam

### Unit 5: De vacaciones (weeks 19–23)
Narrating trips uses the full regular preterite plus ir and ser, while geography shapes culture.

- Week 19: Las vacaciones: places and activities
- Week 20: Preterite of regular -er and -ir verbs
- Week 21: Preterite of ir and ser
- Week 22: Destinos del mundo hispano
- Week 23: Unit 5 assessment: Mi viaje (travel blog)

### Unit 6: Ayudamos en la comunidad (weeks 24–27)
Communities thrive when people help one another; we tell who did what for whom.

- Week 24: Ayudamos en la comunidad
- Week 25: Indirect object pronouns and the verb dar
- Week 26: Irregular preterite: hacer and decir
- Week 27: Unit 6 project: A service report

### Unit 7: El cine y la televisión (weeks 28–30)
Media reflects culture, and gustar-like verbs express reactions.

- Week 28: El cine y la televisión
- Week 29: Acabar de + infinitive and verbs like gustar
- Week 30: Unit 7 assessment: Crítica de cine

### Unit 8: La tecnología (weeks 31–33)
Technology shapes how teens communicate everywhere; saber and conocer express knowledge.

- Week 31: La tecnología y la computadora
- Week 32: Saber vs. conocer
- Week 33: Unit 8 assessment: Digital life debate

### Unit 9: Repaso de Español I y evaluación final (weeks 34–36)
We show Novice High proficiency and get ready for Spanish II.

- Week 34: Spanish I review: present tense system
- Week 35: Spanish I review: past tense and culture
- Week 36: Spanish I final integrated performance assessment

## Standards

- **WL-COMM1-NH** Interpretive communication (Novice High): understand and infer meaning from simple authentic oral, written, and signed texts on familiar topics.
- **WL-COMM1-NM** Interpretive communication (Novice Mid): understand words, phrases, and some simple sentences in texts on familiar topics.
- **WL-COMM2-NH** Interpersonal communication (Novice High): exchange information in conversations on familiar topics using phrases and simple sentences, asking and answering questions.
- **WL-COMM2-NM** Interpersonal communication (Novice Mid): exchange information on familiar topics using practiced phrases and simple questions.
- **WL-COMM3-NH** Presentational communication (Novice High): present information on familiar topics using simple sentences in speaking and writing.
- **WL-COMM3-NM** Presentational communication (Novice Mid): present information on familiar topics using practiced phrases and some simple sentences.
- **WL-CONN1-NH** Connections (Novice High): use Spanish to build knowledge in geography, history, and media literacy.
- **WL-CONN1-NM** Connections (Novice Mid): use Spanish to reinforce knowledge of other disciplines such as math and geography.
- **WL-CULT1-NH** Cultures (Novice High): identify and describe products and practices of Spanish-speaking cultures and relate them to perspectives.
- **WL-CULT1-NM** Cultures (Novice Mid): identify products and practices of Spanish-speaking cultures and compare them with one's own.
- **WL-CULT2-NH** Intercultural interaction (Novice High): use culturally appropriate language and behavior in familiar everyday situations.
- **WL-STRUC1-NH** Structures (Novice High): use the preterite, object pronouns, and gustar-like verbs to communicate at the Novice High level.
- **WL-STRUC1-NM** Structures (Novice Mid): use agreement, comparisons, stem-changing verbs, commands, and the present progressive to communicate.
