## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Choose a debate* (75 min): Read two articles on opposite sides of a teen-relevant debate and annotate each article's claim and strongest evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Sorting equations* (60 min): Solve and sort 12 equations into one solution, no solution and infinitely many solutions; describe what the final line looks like for each.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Size data* (50 min): Compare planet diameters from NASA data; calculate how many Earths fit across Jupiter and the Sun.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Geography of the South* (50 min): Label cotton-producing states, major rivers and port cities on a blank map; explain how climate and soil favored cotton.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Design* (45 min): Design a catalog page with eight items, colors, sizes, and prices in euros or pesos.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Form listening* (40 min): Listen to 'Für Elise' and diagram its rondo form (A-B-A-C-A).
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Practice types* (40 min): Learn blocked vs. random practice; do 10 minutes of each with tennis forehands and note the difference.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Transmission* (25 min): Learn how HIV and common STIs are and are not transmitted using a fact/myth sort. (Sexual health lesson: parents may opt out under CA Ed. Code 51938; parent previews materials.)

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 67, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Fact vs interpretation* (75 min): Mark each place the articles disagree and label whether they disagree on facts or on interpretation of the same facts.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Graphical meaning* (60 min): Graph each side of an equation as a line in Desmos to see one intersection, parallel lines or the same line.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Toilet paper solar system* (50 min): Make a distance scale model on a toilet-paper roll (1 square = about 10 million km) in a hallway or outside.

- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Contrasting structures* (40 min): Compare the verse-chorus form of a pop song with 'Für Elise' using a chart of form, dynamics and tempo.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Mental rehearsal* (40 min): Visualize a serve for 2 minutes before 20 serves and compare accuracy to 20 serves without it.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Symptoms* (25 min): Learn that many STIs have no symptoms and why testing matters.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Contemporary argument: conflicting sources
- Objective: Analyze two texts that provide conflicting information
- Objective: Evaluate the credibility of sources
- Objective: Evaluate a speaker's argument
- Vocabulary: credibility, bias, conflicting evidence, fact, interpretation, primary research
- Materials: paired argument articles from CommonLit or ReadWorks (e.g., cell phones in schools), credibility checklist
- Assessment: Source evaluation chart and claim statement.

### Mathematics 8: How many solutions?
- Objective: Classify equations as having one, no, or infinitely many solutions
- Objective: Create examples of each type
- Vocabulary: no solution, infinitely many solutions, identity, equivalent expressions
- Materials: index cards, Desmos
- Assessment: Quiz on the number of solutions.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Scale of the solar system
- Objective: Analyze data on sizes and distances of solar system objects
- Objective: Build scale models and explain their limitations
- Vocabulary: astronomical unit, scale model, diameter, light-year
- Materials: toilet paper roll, calculator, NASA planetary fact sheet, marker
- Assessment: Scale model and Unit 3 test.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Cotton and the Southern economy
- Objective: Explain how the cotton gin expanded slavery
- Objective: Describe the geography and social structure of the South
- Vocabulary: cotton gin, plantation, cash crop, Cotton Kingdom, yeoman farmer, Black Belt
- Materials: maps of cotton production and enslaved population 1820 vs 1860, graph paper
- Assessment: Quiz and North-South comparison response.

### Spanish 1B: Unit 3 assessment: Catálogo de moda
- Objective: Create a clothing catalog page
- Objective: Integrate clothing, prices, and preferences
- Vocabulary: el catálogo, la moda, de moda, el estilo
- Materials: paper or slide software
- Assessment: Catalog project rubric and unit 3 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Performance and listening analysis
- Objective: Prepare and perform a short piece
- Objective: Compare structure in contrasting pieces
- Vocabulary: form, AB, ABA, rondo, dynamics, tempo, articulation
- Materials: keyboard, recordings of Beethoven 'Für Elise' and a pop song
- Assessment: Recorded performance and listening comparison.

### Physical Education 8: Net games practice and feedback
- Objective: Explain blocked vs. random practice
- Objective: Give peer feedback using biomechanics
- Vocabulary: blocked practice, random practice, feedback, mental rehearsal
- Materials: rackets, balls, volleyball, practice log
- Assessment: Net games written test.

### Health Education 8: HIV and STI prevention
- Objective: Describe transmission and prevention of HIV and other STIs
- Objective: Explain testing and treatment
- Vocabulary: STI, HIV, transmission, asymptomatic, testing
- Materials: CDC STI fact sheets, HIV.gov basics
- Assessment: STI/HIV quiz.
