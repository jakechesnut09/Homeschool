## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Read Part I* (75 min): Read Part I and list every warning given about the paw; predict what the first wish will bring.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Rigid motions preserve measures* (60 min): Measure side lengths and angles of a polygon and its image after three different moves; write a conclusion about what stays the same.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Pushes and pulls* (50 min): Draw force diagrams (arrows) for 6 everyday situations such as a book on a table and a tug-of-war.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Great Awakening* (50 min): Read a short excerpt from a George Whitefield or Jonathan Edwards sermon and discuss how revival ideas encouraged questioning authority.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Venir* (45 min): Learn the forms of venir (vengo, vienes, viene, venimos, venís, vienen) and complete a Conjuguemos drill to 90%.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Value scale* (40 min): Make a 9-step value scale and a smooth gradient bar.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Six components* (40 min): Learn the six skill-related components and match each to a sport skill (e.g., power = volleyball spike); do a 20-minute circuit of one drill per component.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Screen audit* (25 min): Record your screen time by app for two days using device settings or a log.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Read Parts II–III* (75 min): Finish the story and complete a plot diagram marking where suspense peaks.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Parallel lines stay parallel* (60 min): Rotate a pair of parallel lines 180 degrees about a point using GeoGebra and explain why the images are parallel to the originals.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Net force* (50 min): Use the PhET Forces and Motion tug-of-war screen to predict and test motion for 8 combinations of forces; record net force calculations.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Taxes and protest* (50 min): Create a cause-and-effect chain from the French and Indian War debt to the Stamp Act, Townshend Acts and boycotts.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *¿Quién viene?* (45 min): Write invitations for a family dinner and sentences stating who comes, when, and from where.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Shading techniques* (40 min): Practice hatching, cross-hatching, stippling and blending in 4 squares.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Agility and speed* (40 min): Run the Illinois agility course set up with cones and a 40-yard dash; record best of two trials.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Algorithms* (25 min): Learn how feeds are designed to keep users scrolling; analyze one platform's features.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Dialogue that propels* (75 min): Choose 3 lines of dialogue (e.g., Sergeant-Major Morris's 'Better let it burn') and write how each propels action or reveals character.

- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Power* (40 min): Measure standing long jump and vertical jump (chalk-on-wall method); record best of three.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Comparison* (25 min): Discuss how edited images and highlight reels affect self-image; find two examples of filtered vs. real photos.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: 'The Monkey's Paw': foreshadowing and dialogue
- Objective: Analyze how dialogue and incidents propel the plot and reveal character
- Objective: Identify participles and participial phrases
- Vocabulary: foreshadowing, mood, participle, participial phrase, credulity (Latin cred- 'believe'), fate
- Materials: W. W. Jacobs, 'The Monkey's Paw' (Project Gutenberg), plot diagram template
- Assessment: Timed constructed response and vocabulary quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Properties of rigid motions and coordinates
- Objective: Verify that rigid motions preserve lengths and angle measures
- Objective: Use coordinates to describe the effect of translations, rotations and reflections
- Vocabulary: rigid transformation, corresponding sides, corresponding angles, coordinate plane, ordered pair
- Materials: graph paper, tracing paper, ruler, protractor, GeoGebra
- Assessment: Exit ticket: write the coordinate rule for 4 transformations and justify which properties are preserved.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Forces, net force and friction
- Objective: Identify contact and non-contact forces
- Objective: Calculate net force and predict motion from balanced or unbalanced forces
- Vocabulary: force, newton, net force, balanced forces, unbalanced forces, friction
- Materials: rubber bands, small spring scale or rubber band 'force meter', shoe, different floor surfaces, string
- Assessment: Friction CER and force diagram quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The Great Awakening and the road to revolution
- Objective: Connect the Great Awakening to questioning authority
- Objective: Sequence events from the French and Indian War to Lexington and Concord
- Vocabulary: Great Awakening, French and Indian War, Stamp Act, boycott, Boston Massacre, Intolerable Acts
- Materials: map of the 13 colonies, Paul Revere engraving image
- Assessment: Sourcing response on the Boston Massacre and timeline quiz.

### Spanish 1B: The verb venir and describing a meal
- Objective: Conjugate venir
- Objective: Describe dishes and who comes to dinner
- Vocabulary: venir, vengo, vienes, delicioso/a, picante, rico/a, salado/a, dulce
- Materials: Conjuguemos account, a family meal
- Assessment: Venir quiz (80%+).

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Value and shading
- Objective: Create a 9-step value scale
- Objective: Render 3-D forms using light and shadow
- Vocabulary: value, gradation, highlight, core shadow, cast shadow, hatching, cross-hatching, stippling
- Materials: pencils, blending stump or tissue, desk lamp, white egg or ball
- Assessment: Still-life drawing graded with a value rubric.

### Physical Education 8: Skill-related fitness components
- Objective: Define the six skill-related fitness components
- Objective: Measure agility, balance, power, reaction time, speed and coordination
- Vocabulary: agility, power, reaction time, coordination, balance, speed
- Materials: cones, tape measure, ruler, tennis ball, stopwatch
- Assessment: Skill-related fitness profile chart.

### Health Education 8: Technology, social media and mental health
- Objective: Analyze how social media affects mood and sleep
- Objective: Set a healthy tech goal
- Vocabulary: algorithm, comparison, FOMO, screen time
- Materials: phone or device screen-time report, Common Sense Education lesson
- Assessment: Screen-time analysis and SMART goal.
