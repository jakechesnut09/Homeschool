## Day 1 — Monday (school day 156, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Choosing a topic* (75 min): Brainstorm topics on 'An innovation that changed the world' (e.g., the printing press, vaccines, the transcontinental railroad) and choose one.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Cube roots* (60 min): Build cubes of side 1–5 with sugar or snap cubes; solve x^3 = p for perfect and non-perfect cubes and estimate.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Darwin's finches* (50 min): Analyze Grant beak-depth data before and after the 1977 drought.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Carnegie and Rockefeller* (50 min): Diagram vertical integration (Carnegie Steel) and horizontal integration (Standard Oil).
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Rules* (45 min): Learn saber (facts, how to) vs. conocer (people, places) and the personal a, then sort 20 sentences.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Elements* (40 min): Learn the elements of dance through movement exploration.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Bike safety* (40 min): Do a helmet fit and ABC bike check and practice hand signals on a quiet street.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Claims* (25 min): Analyze three health product ads for testimonials and unsupported claims.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 157, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Research questions* (75 min): Write a focused research question and 5 related sub-questions; get feedback and narrow the scope.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Decimal expansions* (60 min): Use long division to write fractions such as 5/11 and 7/12 as decimals; identify repeating patterns.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Peppered moths* (50 min): Graph moth color frequencies before and after the Industrial Revolution; explain the change.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Robber barons or captains of industry?* (50 min): Read the 'Gospel of Wealth' and two political cartoons; write an argument paragraph taking a side.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Sé + infinitive* (45 min): Write 10 sentences about tech skills you know how to do: Sé crear una página web.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Dance styles* (40 min): Watch Alvin Ailey's 'Revelations' excerpt and a folk dance; compare.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Ride* (40 min): Ride a planned 30- to 60-minute route with a parent.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Evaluate sources* (25 min): Use a source checklist on three health websites.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 158, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Finding sources* (75 min): Use the public library's online databases and search engines with advanced operators to find 6 possible sources.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Repeating decimals to fractions* (60 min): Use the 'multiply by 10^n and subtract' method to convert 0.7777..., 0.272727... and 1.16666... to fractions.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Antibiotic resistance* (50 min): Model resistance with beans or candies; explain why finishing prescribed antibiotics matters.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Inventors* (50 min): Research Edison, Bell, Lewis Latimer and the Wright brothers and make a chart of how each invention changed daily life.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Conozco* (45 min): Write about people and places you know, including the personal a.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Improvising* (40 min): Improvise movement to 3 contrasting pieces of music.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Water safety* (40 min): Learn water safety rules and, if available, swim 10 minutes at a local pool.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Quackery* (25 min): Learn signs of health fraud and the FTC's role.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 159, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Evaluating sources* (75 min): Apply the CRAAP test (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose) and keep 4 credible sources.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Rational or irrational?* (60 min): Sort 16 numbers (sqrt 16, pi, 0.121212..., sqrt 2, 22/7) and justify each placement.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Writing a case study* (50 min): Choose one case and write a 4-paragraph explanation of the selection process.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Government and business* (50 min): Explain how tariffs, railroad land grants and subsidies helped business expand.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Interview* (45 min): Interview a parent about their tech skills and places they know, using both verbs.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Movement phrases* (40 min): Create a 16-count phrase.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Hike* (40 min): Plan and complete a hike with a map, water and Leave No Trace principles.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Compare* (25 min): Compare a brand-name and generic medicine or product label.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Research: questions and sources
- Objective: Develop a focused research question
- Objective: Locate credible print and digital sources
- Vocabulary: research question, primary source, secondary source, database, credibility, CRAAP test
- Materials: public library card and databases, Purdue OWL, research notebook
- Assessment: Research proposal and source evaluation.

### Mathematics 8: Cube roots and rational vs irrational numbers
- Objective: Solve x^3 = p using cube roots
- Objective: Convert repeating decimals to fractions and classify numbers as rational or irrational
- Vocabulary: cube root, perfect cube, repeating decimal, terminating decimal, rational number, irrational number
- Materials: sugar cubes or snap cubes, calculator
- Assessment: Quiz on cube roots and the real number system.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Case studies: finches, moths and bacteria
- Objective: Explain natural selection in real populations
- Objective: Analyze data on antibiotic resistance
- Vocabulary: antibiotic resistance, peppered moth, Galapagos finches, speciation
- Materials: finch beak data table, peppered moth data
- Assessment: Case study essay.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Big business and inventors
- Objective: Describe industrialists and their business methods
- Objective: Identify major inventions and their effects on daily life
- Vocabulary: monopoly, trust, vertical integration, horizontal integration, philanthropy, robber baron, captain of industry
- Materials: Carnegie 'The Gospel of Wealth' excerpt, political cartoons of Rockefeller
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Saber vs. conocer
- Objective: Distinguish saber and conocer
- Objective: Talk about skills and people/places you know
- Vocabulary: saber, sé, conocer, conozco, la a personal, saber + infinitivo
- Materials: sorting cards
- Assessment: Saber/conocer quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Elements of dance
- Objective: Explore body, energy, space and time
- Objective: Analyze dance works
- Vocabulary: body, energy, space, time, levels, pathways, tempo
- Materials: open space, music
- Assessment: Phrase performance.

### Physical Education 8: Lifetime activities: cycling, hiking and swimming safety
- Objective: Plan a safe outdoor outing
- Objective: Identify community recreation options
- Vocabulary: route plan, hand signals, water safety, Leave No Trace
- Materials: bicycle and helmet, daypack, map app, local parks guide
- Assessment: Outing plan and community recreation list.

### Health Education 8: Health consumer skills
- Objective: Evaluate health product claims
- Objective: Identify quackery
- Vocabulary: quackery, consumer, testimonial, peer-reviewed
- Materials: health product ads, CRAAP checklist
- Assessment: Consumer guide.
