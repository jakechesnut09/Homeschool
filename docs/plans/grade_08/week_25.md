## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Act 2, Scene 1* (75 min): Read Scene 1 and discuss Anne's growth and her relationship with Peter.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Paper folding* (60 min): Fold a sheet of paper in half repeatedly, record layers as powers of 2, and predict layers after 10 folds.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Plant cuttings* (50 min): Start a pothos cutting in water and a sprouting potato eye; predict traits of offspring.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Emancipation Proclamation* (50 min): Read the full Proclamation and determine exactly which enslaved people it freed and why Lincoln framed it as a war measure.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *IOPs* (45 min): Learn indirect object pronouns and identify the indirect object in 15 sentences.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Script format* (40 min): Study script format and write the opening page.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Rhythm* (40 min): Count beats in different songs and clap the tempo; march to 8-counts.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Check-call-care* (25 min): Review emergency steps and practice a 911 call script.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Scene 2* (75 min): Read Scene 2 and analyze dramatic irony—what the audience knows about the Annex's fate that characters do not.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Multiplying powers* (60 min): Expand expressions like 10^3 x 10^4 to discover a^m x a^n = a^(m+n); practice 12 problems.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Comparing reproduction* (50 min): Compare budding, binary fission and fragmentation with sexual reproduction using a Venn diagram.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Black soldiers* (50 min): Read Corporal James Henry Gooding's letter to Lincoln on equal pay and Frederick Douglass's 'Men of Color, To Arms!'
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Dar* (45 min): Learn present dar (doy, das...) and preterite dar (di, diste, dio) and complete a drill.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Draft scene 1* (40 min): Draft the first half with dialogue revealing character.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Swing* (40 min): Learn the East Coast swing basic and an underarm turn.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Hands-only CPR* (25 min): Practice compressions at 100-120 per minute on a pillow to a song beat.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Scene 3* (75 min): Read Scene 3 (the stolen bread) and analyze how scarcity tests the characters.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Powers of powers* (60 min): Expand (5^2)^3 to discover (a^m)^n = a^(mn); mix with products.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Variation modeling* (50 min): Model gametes by randomly drawing one of two cards per trait from each 'parent'; compare 5 offspring.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Leaders* (50 min): Compare short biographies of Grant, Lee and Jefferson Davis on a character chart.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Le/les clarification* (45 min): Practice clarifying le/les with a + person: Le di la ropa a mi abuela.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Draft scene 2* (40 min): Draft the climax and resolution.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Salsa* (40 min): Learn the salsa basic and cross-body lead.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *AED* (25 min): Watch an AED demonstration and sequence the steps.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Irony paragraph* (75 min): Write a paragraph explaining how dramatic irony makes the audience feel suspense or dread, with two examples.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Dividing powers* (60 min): Expand 7^6 / 7^2 to discover a^m / a^n = a^(m-n); solve 12 mixed problems.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Pros and cons* (50 min): Write a short argument on the advantages of each reproduction type in changing environments.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Soldier life* (50 min): Read letters and diaries from a Union and a Confederate soldier and compare their reasons for fighting.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Gifts and help* (45 min): Write 10 sentences about what you gave or did for others using IOPs.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Staging problems* (40 min): Identify two staging challenges (e.g., a storm, a crowd) and write creative solutions in stage directions.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Intensity* (40 min): Dance 15 minutes continuously and measure heart rate to check if it is MVPA.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Choking* (25 min): Practice the universal choking sign and abdominal thrust positioning (no real thrusts).

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: The Diary of Anne Frank, Act 2, Scenes 1–3: dramatic irony
- Objective: Analyze how dramatic irony creates suspense
- Objective: Trace character change over time
- Vocabulary: dramatic irony, suspense, foil, hope, despair
- Materials: play
- Assessment: Irony paragraph and quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Exponent rules for products, quotients and powers
- Objective: Generate exponent rules by expanding repeated multiplication
- Objective: Apply product, quotient and power-of-a-power rules
- Vocabulary: exponent, base, power, product of powers, quotient of powers, power of a power
- Materials: paper for folding, notebook
- Assessment: Quiz on exponent rules.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Asexual and sexual reproduction
- Objective: Compare asexual and sexual reproduction
- Objective: Explain why sexual reproduction produces variation
- Vocabulary: asexual reproduction, sexual reproduction, clone, gamete, offspring, variation
- Materials: potato or onion, cutting from a houseplant (pothos), glass of water, bread for mold (optional)
- Assessment: Variation model and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Emancipation and Black soldiers
- Objective: Analyze the Emancipation Proclamation
- Objective: Describe the experiences of Black soldiers and leaders on both sides
- Vocabulary: Emancipation Proclamation, contraband, 54th Massachusetts, United States Colored Troops, war aims
- Materials: Emancipation Proclamation transcript, USCT photographs, Grant and Lee biographies
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Indirect object pronouns and the verb dar
- Objective: Use me, te, le, nos, les
- Objective: Say who gives what to whom
- Vocabulary: me, te, le, nos, les, dar, doy, di
- Materials: Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: IOP and dar quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Writing the script
- Objective: Write dialogue and stage directions in proper script format
- Objective: Solve staging problems
- Vocabulary: script format, dialogue, stage directions, parenthetical, setting
- Materials: script template, word processor
- Assessment: Complete first draft.

### Physical Education 8: Social and partner dance
- Objective: Perform swing and salsa basics
- Objective: Keep rhythm and lead/follow
- Vocabulary: lead, follow, basic step, tempo
- Materials: music, heart-rate monitor or watch
- Assessment: Partner routine performance.

### Health Education 8: Hands-only CPR, AED and choking
- Objective: Perform hands-only CPR
- Objective: Use an AED and respond to choking
- Vocabulary: CPR, AED, compression, abdominal thrusts
- Materials: American Heart Association hands-only CPR video, pillow or CPR manikin
- Assessment: CPR skills checklist.
