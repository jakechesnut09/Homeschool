## Day 1 — Monday (school day 106, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *'Paul Revere's Ride'* (75 min): Read the poem and compare Longfellow's version with the historical record of April 18, 1775.

- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Kansas-Nebraska Act* (50 min): Map the act and explain how popular sovereignty undid the Missouri Compromise and led to violence in Kansas.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Geography* (45 min): Locate eight famous sites (Machu Picchu, Chichén Itzá, Salar de Uyuni, El Yunque, la Alhambra) and identify their landforms.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Narrative poetry, recitation and original poems
- Objective: Analyze narrative poetry
- Objective: Recite a poem with expression
- Objective: Write original poems using studied techniques
- Vocabulary: narrative poem, meter, recitation, enjambment, imagery
- Materials: Henry Wadsworth Longfellow, 'Paul Revere's Ride', Poetry Out Loud recitation tips
- Assessment: Recitation rubric, original poems and Unit 5 test.

### Mathematics 8: Scatter plots and patterns
- Objective: Construct scatter plots from bivariate data
- Objective: Describe association, clusters and outliers
- Vocabulary: bivariate data, scatter plot, positive association, negative association, outlier, cluster
- Materials: tape measure, data from family members or an online data set, graph paper
- Assessment: Scatter plot project and quiz.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Patterns in the fossil record
- Objective: Analyze fossil data for patterns of diversity, extinction and change
- Objective: Interpret whale evolution fossil evidence
- Vocabulary: fossil record, extinction, diversity, transitional fossil
- Materials: fossil images (online museum sites), graph paper
- Assessment: Fossil data analysis quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Bleeding Kansas, Dred Scott and the rise of Lincoln
- Objective: Explain the Kansas-Nebraska Act and the Dred Scott decision
- Objective: Analyze the Lincoln-Douglas debates
- Vocabulary: Kansas-Nebraska Act, Bleeding Kansas, Dred Scott v. Sandford, Republican Party, Lincoln-Douglas debates
- Materials: Dred Scott decision excerpt, Lincoln's 'House Divided' speech (1858)
- Assessment: DBQ paragraph.

### Spanish 1B: Destinos del mundo hispano
- Objective: Research a destination
- Objective: Connect geography and culture
- Vocabulary: Machu Picchu, las ruinas, el volcán, la selva, el desierto, la isla, el patrimonio
- Materials: world map, library books
- Assessment: Research notes check and oral summary.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Scene study
- Objective: Rehearse a two-person scene with blocking
- Objective: Use feedback to improve
- Vocabulary: blocking, upstage, downstage, stage left, stage right, cue
- Materials: scene script, family scene partner
- Assessment: Scene performance video.

### Physical Education 8: Cricket and kickball strategy
- Objective: Bat and bowl in cricket
- Objective: Apply fielding strategy
- Vocabulary: wicket, bowler, batter, fielding position
- Materials: flat bat or paddle, tennis ball, cones or bucket as wicket, kickball
- Assessment: Striking/fielding comparison chart.

### Health Education 8: Youth-targeted marketing
- Objective: Analyze marketing tactics
- Objective: Create counter-advertising
- Vocabulary: target marketing, influencer, flavoring, counter-ad
- Materials: vape, alcohol or cannabis ads (online examples), Truth Initiative materials
- Assessment: Counter-ad.
