## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Act 1, Scene 3* (75 min): Read Scene 3 and analyze the conflicts between Anne and Mrs. Van Daan and Anne and her mother.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Two-way tables* (60 min): Survey 20 people (or use given data) on two yes/no questions such as 'plays an instrument' and 'likes math'; organize in a two-way table.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Embryo comparison* (50 min): Compare images of fish, chicken, pig and human embryos at early and late stages; note similarities.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *First Inaugural* (50 min): Read the closing paragraphs of Lincoln's First Inaugural ('better angels of our nature') and explain his message to the South.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Vocabulary* (45 min): Learn 18 community-service words and match them to photos of volunteer work.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Story sources* (40 min): Brainstorm 10 premises from news stories, history (e.g., a Gold Rush camp) and personal experience.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Basic calls* (40 min): Learn honor your partner, do-si-do, swing and promenade.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Signs* (25 min): List signs a friend may be using substances.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 117, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Scenes 4–5* (75 min): Read Scenes 4–5 and analyze how the Hanukkah celebration and the break-in noise build tension.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Relative frequency* (60 min): Compute row and column relative frequencies as percents and decide whether an association exists.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Explaining patterns* (50 min): Write a paragraph explaining what embryological similarities suggest about common ancestry.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Resources compared* (50 min): Graph 1860 population, railroad miles and factory data for North and South and predict which side had the advantage.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Local needs* (45 min): List community organizations in your town and describe in Spanish what each one does.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Choosing a premise* (40 min): Choose one and write a one-paragraph premise naming characters, setting and central conflict.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Squares* (40 min): Practice a simple square dance with family or a group of four to eight.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Conversation* (25 min): Role-play expressing concern to a friend using I-messages.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 118, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Diary vs play* (75 min): Compare one diary entry with its dramatized scene and note how the playwrights changed it.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Analyzing associations* (60 min): Analyze 3 data sets (e.g., curfew vs chores) and write a claim with evidence from relative frequencies.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Evidence synthesis* (50 min): Make a graphic organizer with three lines of evidence for evolution.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Strategies* (50 min): Compare the Union's Anaconda Plan with the Confederacy's defensive strategy on a map.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Reading* (45 min): Read a short article about a youth volunteer project in Latin America and summarize it in five sentences.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Plot structure* (40 min): Outline exposition, rising action, climax and resolution for a 5–8 minute play.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Calling* (40 min): Take a turn as caller, giving clear calls on the beat.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Getting help* (25 min): Identify adults and hotlines (e.g., SAMHSA helpline) to involve.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 119, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Character analysis* (75 min): Write a character analysis of Anne supported by four quotations.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Unit 6 review* (60 min): Review scatter plots, lines of fit and two-way tables with a mixed practice set.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Unit review* (50 min): Review rock strata, geologic time and fossil evidence with a practice test.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Early battles* (50 min): Map Fort Sumter, First Bull Run and Antietam using American Battlefield Trust maps and summarize outcomes.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Survey* (45 min): Ask family members what volunteer work they have done and record answers in the preterite.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Character sketches* (40 min): Write character sketches with objectives and obstacles for 2–3 characters.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Folk dance* (40 min): Learn a Mexican folk dance step (e.g., zapateado basic) or the Virginia reel.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Review* (25 min): Review unit concepts with a jeopardy-style game.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: The Diary of Anne Frank, Act 1, Scenes 3–5
- Objective: Analyze how incidents and dialogue reveal character and propel action
- Objective: Compare the play with diary excerpts
- Vocabulary: conflict, tension, characterization, diary, Hanukkah
- Materials: play, excerpts from 'Anne Frank: The Diary of a Young Girl' (library)
- Assessment: Character analysis and quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Two-way tables and categorical association
- Objective: Construct two-way tables
- Objective: Use relative frequencies to look for association in categorical data
- Vocabulary: categorical data, two-way table, relative frequency, association
- Materials: survey sheet, calculator
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Embryological evidence
- Objective: Compare embryos across species
- Objective: Synthesize fossil, anatomical and embryological evidence
- Vocabulary: embryo, embryology, pharyngeal arches, ancestry
- Materials: embryo image cards (printed)
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The war begins: North vs South
- Objective: Compare Northern and Southern advantages
- Objective: Analyze Lincoln's First Inaugural Address
- Vocabulary: Fort Sumter, border states, Anaconda Plan, Union, Confederacy, blockade
- Materials: Union and Confederate resource statistics, Lincoln's First Inaugural Address (1861)
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Ayudamos en la comunidad
- Objective: Name volunteer activities
- Objective: Describe community needs
- Vocabulary: el trabajo voluntario, el centro de la comunidad, ayudar, los ancianos, la gente sin hogar, recoger, donar, reciclar
- Materials: local volunteer listings
- Assessment: Community vocabulary quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Playwriting: ideas and structure
- Objective: Generate ideas for an original short play
- Objective: Structure a plot with conflict and resolution
- Vocabulary: playwright, premise, conflict, exposition, climax, resolution, stage directions
- Materials: notebook, script format template
- Assessment: Play outline and character sketches.

### Physical Education 8: Square and folk dance
- Objective: Perform basic square dance calls
- Objective: Use partner etiquette
- Vocabulary: promenade, do-si-do, allemande, caller
- Materials: music player, square-dance music (free online)
- Assessment: Performance and self-assessment.

### Health Education 8: Helping a friend and unit assessment
- Objective: Help a friend who is using substances
- Objective: Demonstrate mastery
- Vocabulary: intervention, trusted adult, confidential
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Role-play and unit test.
