## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Read Part 1* (75 min): Read to Rainsford's arrival at the chateau; annotate imagery that creates an ominous mood.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Vertical & supplementary angles* (60 min): Use a 180-degree rotation to explain why vertical angles are equal; solve 8 missing-angle problems.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Balloon rocket* (50 min): Build a balloon rocket on a string line; measure distance traveled for small, medium and large balloon inflations and identify the force pair.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *State constitutions* (50 min): Compare excerpts of the 1776 Virginia Declaration of Rights and 1780 Massachusetts Constitution; list common principles.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Culture: comer fuera* (45 min): Read about tapas in Spain, comida corrida in Mexico, and sobremesa, and compare meal times with yours.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Two-point boxes* (40 min): Draw 6 boxes in two-point perspective above, at and below eye level.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Tarp flip* (40 min): With a group (siblings, co-op), stand on a tarp and flip it over without stepping off; debrief what worked.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Facts and myths* (25 min): Sort myth/fact cards about suicide (e.g., asking about suicide does not plant the idea).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Read Part 2* (75 min): Finish the story; chart Rainsford's internal and external conflicts and how Zaroff's dialogue reveals his values.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Parallel lines and transversals* (60 min): Draw two parallel lines cut by a transversal, measure all 8 angles, and use translations to explain why corresponding angles match.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Force pairs* (50 min): Label action-reaction pairs in 8 scenarios (swimmer, rocket, rowing, hammer and nail) and explain why the pairs do not cancel.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Articles of Confederation* (50 min): Read selected articles and sort features into strengths and weaknesses.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Authentic menu* (45 min): Read a printed authentic Spanish-language menu and answer 10 questions about dishes and prices.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Building a house* (40 min): Draw a house with roof, doors and windows in two-point perspective.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Blind maze* (40 min): Guide a blindfolded partner through a cone maze using only verbal directions, then switch roles.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Warning signs* (25 min): Identify verbal, behavioral and situational warning signs.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Mentor techniques* (75 min): Collect 5 suspense techniques from the unit (short sentences, foreshadowing, cliffhangers, sensory details, unreliable narrator) with examples.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Triangle angle sum* (60 min): Tear the three corners off a paper triangle and line them up on a straight line; write an argument using parallel lines for the 180-degree sum.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Marble collisions* (50 min): Roll marbles of the same and different sizes along a ruler groove into each other; record and explain what happens after collisions.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Land ordinances* (50 min): Draw a township grid using the Land Ordinance of 1785 and read the Northwest Ordinance's provisions on education and slavery.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Role-play prep* (45 min): Write cue cards for a restaurant role-play using usted, polite requests, and venir.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Art history* (40 min): Analyze perspective in Raphael's 'School of Athens' and a modern cityscape on Google Arts & Culture.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Helium hoop* (40 min): Lower a hula hoop to the ground as a group using only fingertips; try three strategies.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Ask-Listen-Tell* (25 min): Practice with a parent how to ask a friend directly, listen and tell a trusted adult.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Plan a narrative* (75 min): Plan an original 2–3 page suspense story with a narrator, conflict, rising action and a twist, using a story map.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Exterior angles* (60 min): Explain the exterior angle theorem and solve 6 algebraic angle problems such as (2x + 10) + (3x - 5) = 120.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Collision analysis* (50 min): Watch slow-motion crash test footage and explain the forces on the car and the dummy; connect to seatbelts and airbags.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Shays' Rebellion* (50 min): Read letters reacting to Shays' Rebellion (Washington, Jefferson) and explain why leaders called for a new constitution.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Role-play* (45 min): Perform the restaurant role-play with a parent from arrival to paying la cuenta.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Cityscape* (40 min): Begin an original cityscape with at least 5 buildings.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Raft crossing* (40 min): Cross a 10-meter 'river' using only four carpet squares or pool noodles as a group.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Resources* (25 min): Save 988 and the Crisis Text Line in your phone and list three trusted adults.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Draft the opening* (75 min): Write the opening scene (at least 400 words) using a hook, sensory details and one deliberate foreshadowing clue.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Unit 1 test* (60 min): Unit test on rigid transformations, coordinate rules, congruence and angle relationships; reflect on two missed items.

- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Debrief* (40 min): Write a reflection on which communication behaviors helped or hurt the group and one thing you will do differently.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Check* (25 min): Complete a wallet card with warning signs and help steps; discuss feelings with a parent.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: 'The Most Dangerous Game' and narrative techniques
- Objective: Analyze conflict and characterization through dialogue and action
- Objective: Plan an original suspense narrative
- Vocabulary: internal conflict, external conflict, pacing, characterization, imagery, palpable
- Materials: Richard Connell, 'The Most Dangerous Game' (1924, public domain), narrative planning sheet
- Assessment: Narrative plan and opening scene checked against a narrative rubric.

### Mathematics 8: Angles: parallel lines, transversals and triangles
- Objective: Identify alternate interior, corresponding and vertical angles
- Objective: Explain why triangle angles sum to 180 degrees
- Vocabulary: transversal, alternate interior angles, corresponding angles, vertical angles, exterior angle, supplementary
- Materials: protractor, paper triangles, scissors, ruler
- Assessment: Unit 1 test (transformations, congruence, angle relationships).

### Science 8 (Integrated): Newton's third law and collisions
- Objective: Identify action-reaction force pairs
- Objective: Explain collisions using Newton's third law
- Vocabulary: Newton's third law, action-reaction pair, collision, momentum
- Materials: balloons, string, straw, tape, marbles, ruler with a groove
- Assessment: Collision lab notes and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The Articles of Confederation
- Objective: Evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of the Articles
- Objective: Explain the importance of the Land Ordinance and Northwest Ordinance
- Vocabulary: Articles of Confederation, ratify, Northwest Ordinance, Land Ordinance of 1785, Shays' Rebellion
- Materials: Articles of Confederation excerpts, township grid handout
- Assessment: Articles quiz and argument paragraph.

### Spanish 1B: Unit 1 assessment: En el restaurante
- Objective: Order a full meal in a role-play
- Objective: Read an authentic menu
- Vocabulary: la propina, las tapas, la sobremesa, ¿Qué me recomienda?
- Materials: printed authentic menu from a Spanish or Mexican restaurant
- Assessment: Restaurant role-play rubric and unit 1 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Two-point perspective and cityscapes
- Objective: Draw forms in two-point perspective
- Objective: Analyze perspective in Renaissance and modern art
- Vocabulary: two-point perspective, vertical line, cityscape, linear perspective
- Materials: ruler, pencils, fine-tip markers
- Assessment: Two-point cityscape.

### Physical Education 8: Adventure challenges and problem solving
- Objective: Solve group physical challenges
- Objective: Reflect on communication and trust
- Vocabulary: trust, debrief, problem solving
- Materials: rope, hula hoops, blindfold, pool noodles, tarp
- Assessment: Written debrief reflection.

### Health Education 8: Suicide prevention and self-harm awareness
- Objective: Recognize warning signs
- Objective: Use the Ask-Listen-Tell steps and 988
- Vocabulary: warning sign, 988 Lifeline, self-harm, crisis
- Materials: 988lifeline.org information, list of trusted adults
- Assessment: Help-seeking wallet card and role-play.
