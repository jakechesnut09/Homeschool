## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Historical context and The Diary of Anne Frank, Act 1, Scenes 1–2
- Objective: Build background knowledge of the Holocaust
- Objective: Analyze how dialogue introduces characters in a drama
- Vocabulary: Holocaust, Nazi, persecution, Secret Annex, stage directions, dialogue
- Materials: Frances Goodrich and Albert Hackett, 'The Diary of Anne Frank' (play, library), USHMM Holocaust Encyclopedia articles
- Assessment: Background timeline and quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Lines of fit and linear models
- Objective: Informally fit a line to data and judge its fit
- Objective: Use the equation of a model to make predictions and interpret slope and intercept
- Vocabulary: line of fit, trend line, residual, prediction, linear model
- Materials: spaghetti strand or ruler, graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Quiz on lines of fit and interpretation.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Anatomical similarities
- Objective: Compare homologous and analogous structures
- Objective: Infer evolutionary relationships from anatomy
- Vocabulary: homologous structure, analogous structure, vestigial structure, common ancestor
- Materials: forelimb diagram handout, chicken wing (optional, cooked), colored pencils
- Assessment: Cladogram and quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Nullification and secession
- Objective: Compare Webster and Calhoun on state vs federal authority
- Objective: Explain why Southern states seceded after 1860
- Vocabulary: nullification, secession, states' rights, Confederacy, John Brown's raid, Harpers Ferry
- Materials: Webster's Second Reply to Hayne excerpt, South Carolina Declaration of the Causes of Secession (1860)
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Spanish 1B: Unit 5 assessment: Mi viaje (travel blog)
- Objective: Narrate a trip in the past
- Objective: Integrate all preterite forms
- Vocabulary: el blog, el recuerdo, primero, luego, después, por fin
- Materials: slides or blog template
- Assessment: Travel blog rubric and unit 5 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Watching and responding to theatre
- Objective: Analyze artistic choices in a recorded production
- Objective: Write a theatre review
- Vocabulary: review, design elements, set, lighting, costume
- Materials: recorded production (PBS Great Performances or a free filmed school production)
- Assessment: Theatre review.

### Physical Education 8: Student-designed game
- Objective: Design an original game with rules and safety
- Objective: Teach and refine it
- Vocabulary: rules, safety, boundary, scoring system
- Materials: any equipment available, game design template
- Assessment: Written game rules and teaching session.

### Health Education 8: Addiction, treatment and recovery
- Objective: Explain addiction as a treatable disease
- Objective: Describe treatment and recovery
- Vocabulary: addiction, tolerance, withdrawal, treatment, recovery
- Materials: NIDA for Teens articles, health journal
- Assessment: Explanatory paragraph.
