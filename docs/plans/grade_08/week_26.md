## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: The Diary of Anne Frank, Act 2, Scenes 4–5 and film comparison
- Objective: Analyze the play's ending and theme
- Objective: Evaluate a filmed production's faithfulness to the script
- Vocabulary: theme, epilogue, adaptation, director's choices
- Materials: play, 1959 film 'The Diary of Anne Frank' (library)
- Assessment: Film comparison chart and quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Zero, negative exponents and different bases
- Objective: Explain why a^0 = 1 and a^(-n) = 1/a^n
- Objective: Simplify expressions with negative exponents and products of different bases
- Vocabulary: zero exponent, negative exponent, reciprocal
- Materials: notebook
- Assessment: Quiz on integer exponents.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Genes, chromosomes and Punnett squares
- Objective: Explain that genes on chromosomes come in pairs from two parents
- Objective: Predict offspring probabilities with Punnett squares
- Vocabulary: gene, allele, chromosome, dominant, recessive, genotype, phenotype, Punnett square
- Materials: coins, paper, colored pencils
- Assessment: Punnett square quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Turning points and home fronts
- Objective: Explain why Gettysburg and Vicksburg were turning points
- Objective: Describe civilian life and wartime technology
- Vocabulary: Gettysburg, Vicksburg, total war, ironclad, draft riots, field hospital
- Materials: Gettysburg Address text, Mathew Brady and Alexander Gardner photographs
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Irregular preterite: hacer and decir
- Objective: Conjugate hacer and decir in the preterite
- Objective: Report what people did and said
- Vocabulary: hice, hiciste, hizo, dije, dijiste, dijo, dijeron, ¿Qué hiciste?
- Materials: StudySpanish.com
- Assessment: Hacer/decir preterite quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Table read and revision
- Objective: Revise a script using a table read
- Objective: Give and receive specific feedback
- Vocabulary: table read, revision, feedback, pacing
- Materials: script copies, family readers
- Assessment: Second draft and revision notes.

### Physical Education 8: Contemporary dance fitness choreography
- Objective: Choreograph a dance-fitness routine
- Objective: Lead a group through it
- Vocabulary: choreography, transition, formation, cardio dance
- Materials: music, video device (optional)
- Assessment: Dance-fitness routine performance.

### Health Education 8: Heat, water and wildfire smoke emergencies
- Objective: Recognize heat illness
- Objective: Respond to water emergencies and poor air quality
- Vocabulary: heat exhaustion, heat stroke, reach-throw-don't go, AQI
- Materials: AirNow.gov, first aid guide
- Assessment: Scenario quiz.
