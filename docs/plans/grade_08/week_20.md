## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *'O Captain! My Captain!'* (75 min): Read the elegy for Lincoln and map the extended metaphor (ship = nation, captain = Lincoln, voyage = war).
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Volume of prisms review* (60 min): Review V = Bh with rectangular prisms; measure a cereal box and compute its volume.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Layering jar* (50 min): Pour sand, soil and gravel into a jar of water in stages; let it settle and observe layering.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Early abolition* (50 min): Read Pennsylvania's 1780 Gradual Abolition Act and the Northwest Ordinance's Article 6; map Northern states' abolition dates.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Endings* (45 min): Learn the shared -er/-ir preterite endings (-í, -iste, -ió, -imos, -isteis, -ieron) and conjugate comer and salir.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Objectives* (40 min): Learn objective-obstacle-tactic and analyze a scene from a familiar film.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Putting* (40 min): Practice the putting grip and pendulum stroke to a cup or target from 3, 6 and 9 feet.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Opioids* (25 min): Learn how opioids affect breathing and why fentanyl in counterfeit pills is deadly.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 97, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *'I Hear America Singing'* (75 min): Read the poem and analyze Whitman's catalog of workers and free-verse rhythm.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Cylinder volume* (60 min): Measure 3 cans, compute V = pi r^2 h, and check by filling with water and measuring in a measuring cup (1 mL = 1 cubic cm).
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Superposition* (50 min): Build play-dough layers with 'fossils' and a 'fault'; core-sample with a straw and interpret the sequence.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Garrison and Douglass* (50 min): Read Garrison's 'To the Public' (1831) and Douglass's 'What to the Slave Is the Fourth of July?' (1852); compare tone and strategy.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Drill* (45 min): Complete a Conjuguemos preterite -er/-ir drill to 90%.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Improvisation* (40 min): Improvise 3 two-person scenes where each character wants something different.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Chipping* (40 min): Practice chipping whiffle balls into a hula hoop from 10 and 20 feet.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Overdose signs* (25 min): Identify overdose signs (pinpoint pupils, slow breathing, unresponsive).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 98, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *'I, Too'* (75 min): Read Hughes's response to Whitman and explain how 'the darker brother' reshapes the idea of who sings America.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Cone vs cylinder lab* (60 min): Build a paper cone with the same base and height as a can; count how many cones of rice fill the can to discover V = (1/3) pi r^2 h.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Cross-cutting relationships* (50 min): Order events in 4 rock-layer diagrams with intrusions and faults.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Underground Railroad* (50 min): Map major routes and read about Harriet Tubman's missions and William Still's records.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Leer and oír* (45 min): Learn the y-forms (leyó, leyeron, oyó) and write five sentences with them.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Subtext* (40 min): Say the line 'I'm fine' 5 ways with different subtext.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Disc drive* (40 min): Practice the backhand drive with reach-back and follow-through; measure distances.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Naloxone* (25 min): Watch a naloxone nasal-spray training video and practice the steps on a model.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 99, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Comparing themes* (75 min): Write a paragraph comparing the theme of 'I Hear America Singing' and 'I, Too' using evidence from both.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Missing dimensions* (60 min): Solve 6 problems finding the radius or height of cylinders and cones given volume.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Index fossils* (50 min): Correlate layers from three 'sites' using index fossils.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: Poetry of American identity: Whitman and Hughes
- Objective: Analyze extended metaphor and figurative language
- Objective: Determine theme in poems responding to one another
- Vocabulary: extended metaphor, free verse, elegy, anaphora, catalog, speaker
- Materials: Walt Whitman, 'O Captain! My Captain!' and 'I Hear America Singing', Langston Hughes, 'I, Too' (1926) (Poetry Foundation)
- Assessment: Comparison paragraph and figurative language quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Volume of cylinders and cones
- Objective: Find volumes of cylinders
- Objective: Use the relationship between cones and cylinders to find cone volumes
- Vocabulary: cylinder, cone, radius, height, volume, pi
- Materials: cans of various sizes, paper to build a cone, rice or dry beans, ruler, measuring cup
- Assessment: Cone-cylinder lab write-up and quiz.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Rock layers and relative dating
- Objective: Apply the law of superposition to interpret rock strata
- Objective: Use index fossils to correlate layers
- Vocabulary: strata, sedimentary rock, superposition, relative age, index fossil, unconformity
- Materials: clear jar, sand, soil and gravel, colored clay or play-dough, plastic straws
- Assessment: Cross-section sequencing quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The abolition movement
- Objective: Describe abolitionist leaders and strategies
- Objective: Explain abolition in early state constitutions and the Northwest Ordinance
- Vocabulary: abolition, gradual emancipation, The Liberator, Underground Railroad, Black codes
- Materials: Pennsylvania 1780 Gradual Abolition Act excerpt, 'The Liberator' first issue, Douglass speech excerpt
- Assessment: Abolition quiz.

### Spanish 1B: Preterite of regular -er and -ir verbs
- Objective: Conjugate -er/-ir verbs in the preterite
- Objective: Narrate with mixed regular verbs
- Vocabulary: comí, bebiste, salió, vivimos, escribieron, aprender, volver, salir
- Materials: Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: Regular preterite quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Objectives, obstacles and tactics
- Objective: Identify a character's objective, obstacle and tactics
- Objective: Apply them in improvisation
- Vocabulary: objective, obstacle, tactic, beat, subtext, improvisation
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Improv performance and beat analysis.

### Physical Education 8: Golf and disc golf
- Objective: Perform a golf putt, chip and short swing
- Objective: Throw backhand and forehand disc golf drives
- Vocabulary: putt, chip, backhand drive, angle of release
- Materials: plastic golf balls or whiffle balls, club or broom handle, frisbee, hula hoops
- Assessment: Scorecard and written analysis.

### Health Education 8: Opioids, fentanyl and naloxone
- Objective: Identify signs of an opioid overdose
- Objective: Explain how naloxone works
- Vocabulary: opioid, fentanyl, overdose, naloxone (Narcan), Good Samaritan law
- Materials: CDPH or SAMHSA fact sheets, naloxone training video
- Assessment: Overdose response sequence.
