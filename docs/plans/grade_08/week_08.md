## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 7* (75 min): Read chapter 7 and analyze Randy's conversation with Ponyboy as evidence that Socs face problems too.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Rates review* (60 min): Find unit rates in tables, graphs and stories (e.g., 3 miles in 45 minutes) and write y = kx equations.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Energy Skate Park* (50 min): Use the PhET Energy Skate Park sim to observe energy bar graphs as the skater moves; record 3 patterns.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Washington's precedents* (50 min): List precedents Washington set (cabinet, two terms, 'Mr. President') and their lasting effects.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Chores* (45 min): Learn 14 chores and make a family chore chart in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Presidential portraits* (40 min): Compare Gilbert Stuart's 'Lansdowne' portrait of Washington with Kehinde Wiley's portrait of Barack Obama; list how each uses pose and background.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Intro* (40 min): Learn team handball's origins and rules (3 seconds, 3 steps, goal area); play a passing keep-away game.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Media lens* (25 min): Analyze three ads or posts for unrealistic body messages and photo editing.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 8* (75 min): Read chapter 8 and write about how Johnny's condition and words ('Useless...fighting's no good') affect Ponyboy.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Graphing proportional relationships* (60 min): Graph 4 relationships, choosing scales for the axes, and identify the unit rate as the slope on each graph.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Pendulum lab* (50 min): Build a string-and-washer pendulum and observe energy changes; explain why it eventually stops (thermal energy).
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Hamilton vs Jefferson* (50 min): Debate the National Bank and the assumption of state debts; create a T-chart of the two visions.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Regular tú commands* (45 min): Learn that the affirmative tú command equals the él form (limpia, lava, come) and write commands for each chore.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Frida Kahlo* (40 min): Analyze Frida Kahlo's 'Self-Portrait with Thorn Necklace and Hummingbird' and explain the meaning of three symbols.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Passing* (40 min): Practice shoulder, bounce and wrist passes on the move in triangle passing drills.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Body image* (25 min): Discuss influences on body image (family, peers, culture, sports) and write positive self-talk statements.

- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Irregular commands* (45 min): Learn di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, ven and complete a 20-item command practice.

- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Shooting* (40 min): Practice the step shot and jump shot from outside a taped 6-meter arc at a cone goal.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: The Outsiders, chapters 7–9: conflict and consequences
- Objective: Analyze how incidents and dialogue lead to decisions
- Objective: Use active and passive voice for effect
- Vocabulary: rumble, conflict, consequence, Randy, perspective
- Materials: The Outsiders
- Assessment: Quiz and voice rewrite.

### Mathematics 8: Proportional relationships and graphs
- Objective: Graph proportional relationships and interpret unit rate as slope
- Objective: Compare two proportional relationships shown in different ways
- Vocabulary: proportional relationship, unit rate, constant of proportionality, slope, origin
- Materials: graph paper, stopwatch, Desmos
- Assessment: Quiz plus walking-rate lab graph.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Energy transfer and transformation
- Objective: Track energy transfer between kinetic and potential forms
- Objective: Argue that changes in kinetic energy mean energy was transferred
- Vocabulary: energy transfer, energy transformation, conservation of energy, thermal energy
- Materials: foam pipe insulation cut in half (or paper track), marbles, tape, string, washers
- Assessment: Roller coaster design and energy CER.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Washington's presidency and the first parties
- Objective: Explain Hamilton's financial plan and Jefferson's opposition
- Objective: Analyze Washington's Farewell Address
- Vocabulary: cabinet, National Bank, Federalist Party, Democratic-Republican Party, Whiskey Rebellion, neutrality
- Materials: Farewell Address excerpts
- Assessment: Quiz and Farewell Address analysis.

### Spanish 1B: Los quehaceres and affirmative tú commands
- Objective: Name household chores
- Objective: Give informal commands
- Vocabulary: hacer la cama, lavar los platos, sacar la basura, pasar la aspiradora, poner la mesa, limpiar, haz, pon
- Materials: chore chart
- Assessment: Commands quiz and chore role-play.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Portraits and identity
- Objective: Analyze how portraits use symbols, pose and setting to express identity
- Objective: Plan a symbolic identity portrait
- Vocabulary: symbolism, identity, pose, setting, patron, commission
- Materials: Smithsonian National Portrait Gallery images (via Learning Lab or Google Arts & Culture), sketchbook
- Assessment: Identity portrait plan with symbol key.

### Physical Education 8: Team handball: skills
- Objective: Perform the jump shot, bounce pass and three-step rule
- Objective: Understand the goal area
- Vocabulary: goal area, three-step rule, jump shot, pivot
- Materials: playground ball or small handball, cones or a goal, tape
- Assessment: Handball skills checklist.

### Health Education 8: Body image, media and eating disorders
- Objective: Analyze media influences on body image
- Objective: Recognize signs of eating disorders and how to get help
- Vocabulary: body image, eating disorder, anorexia, bulimia, binge eating disorder, body neutrality
- Materials: magazine ads or social posts, NEDA or KidsHealth fact sheets
- Assessment: Media-literacy response.
