## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Chapter 10* (75 min): Read chapter 10 and write a journal entry analyzing Dally's death and why Ponyboy refuses to accept Johnny is gone.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Stacking cups* (60 min): Measure the height of a stack of 1 to 8 paper cups, graph the data, and find the slope (height per cup) and intercept (base height).
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Bounce test* (50 min): Drop tennis, rubber and clay balls from 1 m; measure rebound heights and calculate the percent energy retained.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Adams and the Alien and Sedition Acts* (50 min): Read the Sedition Act and argue whether it violated the First Amendment.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Present progressive* (45 min): Learn estar + -ando/-iendo, including leyendo and durmiendo, and describe what each family member is doing right now.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Underpainting* (40 min): Transfer the sketch and block in large areas with thin underpainting in your chosen scheme.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Give-and-go* (40 min): Practice the give-and-go and the cut to open space in 2-v-1 drills.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Label study* (25 min): Compare caffeine and sugar in three energy drinks with the recommended limit for teens.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~385 min)

- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Fast break* (40 min): Practice transitioning from defense to a 3-on-2 fast break after a goalkeeper save.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Supplement claims* (25 min): Evaluate two protein or 'fat-burner' supplement ads with a claim-checking checklist.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: The Outsiders, chapters 10–12: resolution and theme
- Objective: Analyze how the ending resolves conflicts and develops theme
- Objective: Plan a literary analysis essay with a defensible thesis
- Vocabulary: resolution, circular structure, thesis, literary analysis, embedded quotation
- Materials: The Outsiders, essay planning sheet, highlighters
- Assessment: Essay outline and chapter quiz.

### Mathematics 8: Representing linear relationships
- Objective: Recognize nonproportional linear relationships
- Objective: Interpret slope and vertical intercept in context
- Vocabulary: linear relationship, vertical intercept, y-intercept, rate of change, initial value
- Materials: cups, marbles or coins, graduated measuring cup, graph paper
- Assessment: Quiz on interpreting slope and intercept; lab equation.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Collisions and energy review
- Objective: Analyze energy transfers in collisions
- Objective: Synthesize unit learning about kinetic and potential energy
- Vocabulary: elastic collision, inelastic collision, energy dissipation
- Materials: balls of different materials, meter stick, phone camera (slow motion)
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: Adams, Jefferson and the Louisiana Purchase
- Objective: Analyze the Alien and Sedition Acts and the election of 1800
- Objective: Explain the significance of the Louisiana Purchase and Lewis and Clark
- Vocabulary: Alien and Sedition Acts, Marbury v. Madison, judicial review, Louisiana Purchase, Corps of Discovery
- Materials: U.S. map, Lewis and Clark journal excerpts
- Assessment: Map quiz on 1803 territories and expedition route.

### Spanish 1B: Present progressive and unit 2 assessment
- Objective: Describe actions in progress
- Objective: Present a home tour
- Vocabulary: estar + -ando/-iendo, estoy limpiando, está leyendo, durmiendo, el patio
- Materials: camera or phone
- Assessment: Home tour video rubric and unit 2 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: Painting the identity portrait
- Objective: Paint a portrait using an intentional color scheme
- Objective: Evaluate and defend artistic choices in an artist statement
- Vocabulary: underpainting, layering, glazing, artist statement, critique
- Materials: paints, canvas board or heavy paper, brushes
- Assessment: Finished portrait and artist statement.

### Physical Education 8: Team handball: transition and game play
- Objective: Execute fast breaks
- Objective: Use a give-and-go and defensive positioning
- Vocabulary: fast break, give-and-go, transition, screen
- Materials: handball, cones, pinnies
- Assessment: Invasion-games strategy comparison.

### Health Education 8: Supplements, energy drinks and diet claims
- Objective: Evaluate supplement and diet claims
- Objective: Explain risks of energy drinks for teens
- Vocabulary: supplement, FDA, caffeine, claim, evidence
- Materials: supplement and energy-drink labels, internet access
- Assessment: Consumer review.
