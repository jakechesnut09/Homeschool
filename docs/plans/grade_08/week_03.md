## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Read 'The Gift of the Magi'* (75 min): Read the story and track each character's sacrifice and what the reader knows that the characters do not.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *What is congruence?* (60 min): Sort 8 pairs of figures into congruent / not congruent and justify each answer with a rigid-motion argument rather than 'looks the same'.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Inertia* (50 min): Do the coin-on-card flick and tablecloth-style demonstrations; explain Newton's first law in your notebook.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Common Sense* (50 min): Read excerpts from Thomas Paine's Common Sense and identify his three strongest arguments for independence.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Rules* (45 min): Learn the DOCTOR (ser) and PLACE (estar) memory aids and sort 20 sentence cards.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Perspective basics* (40 min): Draw boxes above, on and below the horizon line using one vanishing point.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Motivation* (40 min): Sort a list of reasons people exercise into intrinsic and extrinsic, then list your own top three motivators; finish with a 20-minute jog/walk interval.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Continuum* (25 min): Learn the mental health continuum and place everyday examples on it.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Types of irony* (75 min): Sort 10 examples from the three stories studied so far into situational, verbal and dramatic irony; explain the effect of each.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Sequences of moves* (60 min): Find a sequence of two or three transformations mapping figure A to figure B for 6 grid problems; compare two different valid sequences.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Planning the investigation* (50 min): Write a plan to test how added mass changes the speed of a car released down a ramp: question, variables, procedure, data table.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *The Declaration* (50 min): Close-read the preamble of the Declaration; paraphrase each key phrase and list 5 grievances against the king.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Meaning changes* (45 min): Study adjectives that change meaning (es aburrido vs. está aburrido; es rico vs. está rico) and write 6 contrasting pairs.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Block letters* (40 min): Draw your initials in one-point perspective block letters.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Barriers* (40 min): Identify three barriers to being active (time, weather, screens) and write a strategy for each; complete a 15-minute body-weight circuit.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Common conditions* (25 min): Research one condition (anxiety disorder, ADHD, depression, OCD) and list symptoms and treatments.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Theme & summary* (75 min): Write an objective summary (no opinions) and a theme statement for the story supported by two quotations.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Congruent polygons* (60 min): Cut out cardstock quadrilaterals and test congruence by moving one onto the other; write the sequence used.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Carrying out the investigation* (50 min): Run 3 trials each with 0, 2, 4 and 6 washers taped to the car; calculate average times and speeds.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Winning the war* (50 min): Map key battles (Saratoga, Valley Forge, Yorktown) and explain the role of French alliance and of Black and women patriots such as James Armistead and Mercy Otis Warren.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *Food & feelings* (45 min): Describe a meal: how the food is (ser) and how it tastes and is today (estar), plus how diners feel.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Hallway or road* (40 min): Draw a hallway, road or railroad track receding to a vanishing point.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *SMART goals* (40 min): Write two SMART goals (one health-related, one skill-related) with a weekly action step for each.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Stigma* (25 min): Analyze stigmatizing language in media and rewrite it with person-first language.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~385 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 8** — *Grammar: infinitives* (75 min): Identify infinitives and infinitive phrases used as nouns, adjectives and adverbs; write a paragraph using all three verbal types correctly.
- [ ] **Mathematics 8** — *Points, segments and circles* (60 min): Show that two segments of the same length and two circles of the same radius are congruent by describing the moves.
- [ ] **Science 8 (Integrated)** — *Force & mass math* (50 min): Graph your results; solve 8 F = ma problems and relate the math to your data.
- [ ] **U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict** — *Global impact* (50 min): Compare the Declaration with the French Declaration of the Rights of Man (1789) in a Venn diagram.
- [ ] **Spanish 1B** — *StudySpanish practice* (45 min): Read the StudySpanish.com ser/estar lesson and complete its practice exercises.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 8** — *Room interior* (40 min): Draw your bedroom in one-point perspective with furniture.
- [ ] **Physical Education 8** — *Activity log* (40 min): Begin a 4-day-per-week MVPA log and do a 30-minute activity of your choice outside of class.
- [ ] **Health Education 8** — *Treatment* (25 min): Learn what counselors, psychologists and psychiatrists do and how to access them.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 8: 'The Gift of the Magi': irony and theme
- Objective: Distinguish situational, verbal and dramatic irony
- Objective: Determine a theme and write an objective summary
- Vocabulary: situational irony, verbal irony, dramatic irony, theme, objective summary, infinitive, magi
- Materials: O. Henry, 'The Gift of the Magi' (Project Gutenberg), irony chart
- Assessment: Verbals quiz and theme statement.

### Mathematics 8: Congruence through sequences of rigid motions
- Objective: Define congruence using rigid motions
- Objective: Describe a sequence of moves that maps one figure onto a congruent figure
- Vocabulary: congruent, sequence of transformations, corresponding parts
- Materials: tracing paper, graph paper, cardstock shapes, scissors
- Assessment: Quiz on congruence sequences with written justifications.

### Science 8 (Integrated): Newton's second law: force, mass and acceleration
- Objective: Plan an investigation of how mass and force affect acceleration
- Objective: Use F = ma qualitatively and quantitatively
- Vocabulary: acceleration, mass, inertia, Newton's first law, Newton's second law
- Materials: toy car or small cart, coins or washers for added mass, ramp (board and books), meter stick, stopwatch
- Assessment: Lab report on mass and motion; quiz.

### U.S. History and Geography: Growth and Conflict: The Declaration of Independence and the Revolution
- Objective: Analyze the philosophy of government in the Declaration
- Objective: Explain how the Revolution was won and its effect on France and other nations
- Vocabulary: Declaration of Independence, unalienable rights, grievance, consent of the governed, Treaty of Paris
- Materials: Declaration of Independence transcript (National Archives)
- Assessment: Unit 1 test with constructed response.

### Spanish 1B: Ser vs. estar
- Objective: Distinguish permanent traits from conditions and location
- Objective: Use ser and estar correctly in context
- Vocabulary: ser, estar, está rico, es rico, está caliente, está frío, DOCTOR, PLACE
- Materials: sorting cards
- Assessment: Ser vs. estar quiz (80%+).

### Visual and Performing Arts 8: One-point perspective
- Objective: Use horizon line, vanishing point and orthogonals
- Objective: Draw a room or hallway in one-point perspective
- Vocabulary: horizon line, vanishing point, orthogonal, one-point perspective, foreshortening
- Materials: ruler, pencils, drawing paper
- Assessment: One-point perspective room drawing.

### Physical Education 8: Goals, motivation and activity tracking
- Objective: Write SMART goals for one health- and one skill-related component
- Objective: Identify personal barriers and motivators
- Vocabulary: intrinsic motivation, extrinsic motivation, barrier, SMART goal
- Materials: fitness journal, step counter or phone app (optional)
- Assessment: Signed SMART goal sheet and first week of activity log.

### Health Education 8: Mental illness and reducing stigma
- Objective: Distinguish mental health from mental illness
- Objective: Explain stigma and its effects
- Vocabulary: mental illness, stigma, therapy, treatment
- Materials: NAMI or KidsHealth fact sheets
- Assessment: Anti-stigma infographic.
