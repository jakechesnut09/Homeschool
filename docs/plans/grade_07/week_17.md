## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *The sonnet* (75 min): Read Sonnet 18, map its three quatrains and couplet, and explain how the couplet turns the argument.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Interpreting negatives* (60 min): Describe temperatures, elevations and bank balances with signed numbers and order them on a number line.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Food chains* (50 min): Build food chains for a California chaparral ecosystem and label producers, consumers and decomposers.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Feudal pyramid* (45 min): Draw the feudal pyramid and explain the exchange of land for loyalty.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *-ar endings* (45 min): Learn infinitive, stem, and endings (-o, -as, -a, -amos, -áis, -an) and conjugate hablar and estudiar in a chart.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Harmonize* (40 min): Add I-IV-V chords under the melody.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Taking a pulse* (40 min): Measure resting heart rate at the wrist and neck for 3 mornings.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Calling 911* (25 min): Practice what to say when calling 911.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Frost* (75 min): Read 'The Road Not Taken' and 'Nothing Gold Can Stay' and write a theme statement for each.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Adding with arrows* (60 min): Model sums such as -5 + 8 and 3 + (-7) with arrows on a number line and with two-color counters.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Yarn web* (50 min): Connect organism cards with yarn into a food web and observe the effect of 'removing' one organism.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Oath of fealty* (45 min): Read a medieval oath of fealty (Fordham) and role-play a homage ceremony.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Drill* (45 min): Complete Conjuguemos -ar verb practice until you score 90%, then write 10 original sentences.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *B section* (40 min): Compose a contrasting 8-bar B section to create ABA form.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Target zone* (40 min): Calculate maximum heart rate (220 − age) and a 60–85% target zone.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Cuts and burns* (25 min): Practice treating cuts, scrapes and minor burns.

- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Owl pellet* (50 min): Dissect an owl pellet (or virtual pellet) and identify prey bones to build the owl's food web.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *The manor* (45 min): Label a manor diagram and calculate how three-field rotation increased crop yields.

- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Arrange* (40 min): Add a rhythm part or bass line.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Zone workout* (40 min): Do a 20-minute workout, checking pulse every 5 minutes to stay in zone.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Choking* (25 min): Learn the universal choking sign and abdominal thrust technique on a pillow.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Form and meaning: sonnets, free verse and Frost
- Objective: Analyze how form contributes to meaning
- Objective: Determine theme in poems
- Vocabulary: sonnet, quatrain, couplet, free verse, stanza
- Materials: Shakespeare's Sonnet 18, Frost: 'The Road Not Taken', 'Nothing Gold Can Stay', Whitman: 'O Captain! My Captain!'
- Assessment: Poetry analysis page.

### Mathematics 7: Positive and negative numbers; adding rational numbers
- Objective: Represent signed numbers in context
- Objective: Add integers and rational numbers on a number line
- Vocabulary: rational number, opposite, absolute value, additive inverse
- Materials: two-color counters or coins, thermometer
- Assessment: Addition quiz and number-line explanations.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Food webs and energy flow
- Objective: Build food webs
- Objective: Explain why energy decreases up trophic levels
- Vocabulary: food web, trophic level, decomposer, energy pyramid
- Materials: yarn, index cards, owl pellet (optional)
- Assessment: Food-web model and quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Feudalism and manor life
- Objective: Explain feudal obligations
- Objective: Describe the manorial economy
- Vocabulary: feudalism, vassal, fief, serf, manor, guild
- Materials: manor diagram, oath of fealty text
- Assessment: Diary entry rubric.

### Spanish 1A: Present tense of -ar verbs
- Objective: Conjugate regular -ar verbs
- Objective: Describe daily activities using -ar verbs
- Vocabulary: hablar, estudiar, trabajar, usar, necesitar, enseñar, practicar, tocar
- Materials: verb chart, Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: -ar verb quiz (80%+).

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Composition: arranging
- Objective: Add harmony and form to a composition
- Objective: Rehearse with criteria
- Vocabulary: arrangement, accompaniment, ABA form
- Materials: MuseScore or Song Maker, instrument
- Assessment: ABA composition draft.

### Physical Education 7: Heart rate and intensity
- Objective: Measure resting and working heart rate
- Objective: Calculate target heart-rate zones
- Vocabulary: resting heart rate, maximum heart rate, target zone, RPE
- Materials: stopwatch, heart-rate monitor (optional)
- Assessment: Heart-rate data and explanation.

### Health Education 7: First aid and unit assessment
- Objective: Perform basic first aid
- Objective: Respond to emergencies
- Vocabulary: first aid, 911, Good Samaritan, Heimlich maneuver
- Materials: first aid kit, bandages
- Assessment: First-aid skills check and unit 3 test.
