## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 10* (75 min): Read Chapter 10 and explain how the Logans try to keep their land.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Building with straws* (60 min): Cut straws to given lengths and test which sets of three form a triangle; state the triangle inequality.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Scale chart* (50 min): Create a chart of Earth processes from fast/small (landslide) to slow/large (mountain building).
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Roots* (45 min): Trace ideas from Greek, Muslim and Renaissance scholars that set up the Scientific Revolution.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Ir* (45 min): Learn voy, vas, va, vamos, vais, van and the contraction a + el = al.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Study* (40 min): Watch short clips of stop-motion and analyze how frames create motion.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Pacing* (40 min): Run 4 × 400m at an even pace, recording splits.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Review 1* (25 min): Review changes, hygiene and sleep concepts.

- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Review 2* (25 min): Review boundaries and communication skills.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Roll of Thunder, chapters 10–12
- Objective: Analyze the climax and resolution
- Objective: Evaluate characters' choices
- Vocabulary: climax, sacrifice, resolution
- Materials: Roll of Thunder, Hear My Cry
- Assessment: Novel test.

### Mathematics 7: Drawing triangles
- Objective: Construct triangles from given conditions
- Objective: Determine when conditions give a unique triangle
- Vocabulary: unique, triangle inequality, included angle
- Materials: straws, ruler, protractor, compass
- Assessment: Construction quiz and straw-lab table.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Changes at many scales and unit assessment
- Objective: Explain processes at different time and spatial scales
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: uplift, time scale, spatial scale
- Materials: poster paper
- Assessment: Landform history and unit 6 test.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Scientific Revolution
- Objective: Explain roots of the Scientific Revolution
- Objective: Describe theories of Copernicus, Galileo, Kepler and Newton
- Vocabulary: geocentric, heliocentric, telescope, gravity, hypothesis
- Materials: flashlight, ball, Galileo's Starry Messenger excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1A: ¿Adónde vas? The verb ir and question words
- Objective: Conjugate ir
- Objective: Say where people go and when
- Vocabulary: ir, voy, vas, al, a la, ¿Adónde?, ¿Con quién?, los fines de semana
- Materials: weekly planner
- Assessment: Ir and question words quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Stop-motion animation planning
- Objective: Storyboard an animation
- Objective: Build characters and sets
- Vocabulary: storyboard, frame, frame rate, animation
- Materials: clay or LEGO figures, free stop-motion app, storyboard template
- Assessment: Storyboard and test animation.

### Physical Education 7: Track: distance and pacing
- Objective: Pace a distance run
- Objective: Track splits
- Vocabulary: pace, split, negative split
- Materials: track, stopwatch
- Assessment: Mile time comparison.

### Health Education 7: Unit 5 review and assessment
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: review
- Materials: review cards
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.
