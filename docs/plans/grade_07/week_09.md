## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 7* (75 min): Read Chapter 7 'The Sounding of the Call' and discuss the ending's meaning.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Collecting data* (60 min): Measure how much water drips from a faucet in 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 minutes and record in a table.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Measuring change* (50 min): Measure temperature change for baking soda + vinegar every 30 seconds and graph it.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Confucian values* (45 min): Read selected Analects passages and explain filial piety and the five relationships.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Personality words* (45 min): Learn 16 personality adjectives with flashcards and describe yourself in five sentences using soy and no soy.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Feldman critique* (40 min): Use the describe-analyze-interpret-judge method to critique a famous landscape.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Cutting* (40 min): Practice cutting to get open and receiving passes in a 2-v-1 drill.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Mental illness facts* (25 min): Read KidsHealth articles on depression and anxiety and list symptoms.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Objective summary* (75 min): Write a 200-word objective summary of the whole novel without opinions.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Modeling* (60 min): Graph the drip data, find k, write an equation, and predict water wasted in a day and a year.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Hot reaction* (50 min): Measure temperature change for yeast + hydrogen peroxide and for steel wool + vinegar in a jar.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Civil service exams* (45 min): Take a mock civil service exam with Analects questions and discuss fairness of the system.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Agreement* (45 min): Learn masculine/feminine and singular/plural adjective endings (-o/-a/-os/-as, -e, consonant) and complete a 20-item agreement worksheet.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Self-critique* (40 min): Critique your own painting with both personal and established criteria and note differences.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Marking* (40 min): Practice marking a thrower with a 10-second stall count.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Warning signs* (25 min): Learn suicide warning signs and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Theme tracking* (75 min): Trace a theme (survival of the fittest, nature vs. civilization or loyalty) through 6 quotations from different chapters.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Report* (60 min): Write a one-page report explaining whether the relationship is proportional and what the constant means.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Comparing reactions* (50 min): Compare graphs and classify each reaction as exothermic or endothermic.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Scholar-officials* (45 min): Explain how the exam created a scholar-official class and compare to modern government hiring.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Intensifiers* (45 min): Add muy, un poco, bastante, and pero/y to describe three family members in connected sentences.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Revise* (40 min): Make one revision to strengthen the focal point.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Rules & Spirit* (40 min): Learn ultimate rules and the Spirit of the Game, then play a 3-v-3 game.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Trusted adults* (25 min): Identify five trusted adults and how to reach them.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 44, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Film comparison* (75 min): Watch a clip from a film adaptation and note two ways it changes Buck's story and why.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a mixed review on tables, equations, graphs and unit rates with fractions.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Hand warmers & cold packs* (50 min): Read how commercial hand warmers and instant cold packs work and identify their chemicals.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Neo-Confucianism* (45 min): Read about Zhu Xi and how Neo-Confucianism blended Buddhist and Daoist ideas.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Entrevista* (45 min): Interview a parent: ¿Cómo eres? ¿Eres...? and write a five-sentence description of them from the answers.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Artist statement* (40 min): Write an artist statement for the painting.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Game day* (40 min): Play a full game of ultimate and self-officiate calls.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Helping a friend* (25 min): Role-play encouraging a friend to talk to an adult.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 45, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Essay planning* (75 min): Choose a thesis for a literary analysis essay on how setting shapes Buck and outline three body paragraphs with evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Unit 2 test* (60 min): Take the unit 2 test covering all representations of proportional relationships.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on exothermic and endothermic reactions with graph interpretation.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph evaluating whether merit-based exams strengthened China.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take an adjective agreement quiz and record a 30-second self-description.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Mini show* (40 min): Display drawings and painting for family and explain one choice in each.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Rate your own and teammates' spirit and strategy on a scorecard.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Create a help card with resources to keep in your wallet or phone.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: The Call of the Wild, chapter 7 and theme
- Objective: Determine and trace themes across the novel
- Objective: Write an objective summary
- Vocabulary: theme, naturalism, objective summary
- Materials: The Call of the Wild
- Assessment: Objective summary and essay outline.

### Mathematics 7: Proportional reasoning project & unit test
- Objective: Model a real situation proportionally
- Objective: Demonstrate mastery of unit 2
- Vocabulary: model, representation
- Materials: phone timer, graph paper
- Assessment: Unit 2 test and dripping-faucet modeling report.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Energy in reactions: hot and cold
- Objective: Classify reactions as exothermic or endothermic
- Objective: Measure temperature changes
- Vocabulary: exothermic, endothermic, thermal energy, temperature
- Materials: thermometer, baking soda, vinegar, hydrogen peroxide, yeast, steel wool, calcium chloride (ice melt)
- Assessment: Temperature graphs and quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Confucianism and the scholar-officials
- Objective: Explain Confucian values and the civil service exam
- Objective: Analyze Neo-Confucianism
- Vocabulary: Confucianism, filial piety, bureaucracy, civil service exam, Neo-Confucianism
- Materials: Analects excerpts, mock exam
- Assessment: Evaluation paragraph.

### Spanish 1A: ¿Cómo eres? Personality adjectives and agreement
- Objective: Describe personality
- Objective: Make adjectives agree in gender and number
- Vocabulary: simpático/a, gracioso/a, serio/a, trabajador/a, perezoso/a, inteligente, deportista, muy
- Materials: adjective flashcards
- Assessment: Agreement quiz and oral self-description.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Painting critique
- Objective: Critique with personal vs. established criteria
- Objective: Revise artwork
- Vocabulary: critique, criteria, describe-analyze-interpret-judge
- Materials: finished painting
- Assessment: Written critique and mini show.

### Physical Education 7: Ultimate frisbee: game play and Spirit of the Game
- Objective: Apply cutting and marking
- Objective: Self-officiate fairly
- Vocabulary: cut, stack, stall count, Spirit of the Game
- Materials: flying disc, cones
- Assessment: Game play and spirit scorecard.

### Health Education 7: Depression, anxiety and getting help
- Objective: Recognize warning signs
- Objective: Know how to get help
- Vocabulary: depression, anxiety, warning sign, 988 Lifeline
- Materials: KidsHealth articles
- Assessment: Help card and role-play.
