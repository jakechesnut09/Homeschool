## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Shakespeare's world* (75 min): Watch a short video on the Globe Theatre and Elizabethan England and take notes on how plays were staged.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Position and velocity* (60 min): Model multiplication with a toy car moving at positive or negative velocities for positive or negative time.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Build a terrarium* (50 min): Build a bottle terrarium with plants, soil, water and small invertebrates and diagram its biotic and abiotic parts.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Pope Urban II* (45 min): Read accounts of Pope Urban II's 1095 speech and identify his reasons for the First Crusade.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Breakfast foods* (45 min): Learn 12 breakfast foods and drinks and label a picture of your own breakfast.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Context* (40 min): View Alhambra and mosque tilework and discuss why geometric patterns are central in Islamic art.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Stretching* (40 min): Learn 8 static stretches and when to use static vs. dynamic stretching.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Tobacco effects* (25 min): List short- and long-term effects of smoking on the body.

- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Yoga* (40 min): Complete a 30-minute beginner yoga session.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Vaping* (25 min): Read CDC facts on e-cigarettes and nicotine content.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Shakespeare's world and Act 1 of A Midsummer Night's Dream
- Objective: Understand Elizabethan theater and language
- Objective: Analyze how conflicts are introduced
- Vocabulary: comedy, Globe Theatre, iambic pentameter, thou/thee
- Materials: Folger edition of A Midsummer Night's Dream, character map
- Assessment: Character map, translation and Act 1 quiz.

### Mathematics 7: Multiplying rational numbers
- Objective: Explain sign rules for multiplication
- Objective: Multiply signed fractions and decimals
- Vocabulary: product, sign rule
- Materials: number line, toy car (optional)
- Assessment: Multiplication quiz and written justification of sign rules.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Ecosystem model project
- Objective: Build and monitor an ecosystem model
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: terrarium, biotic, abiotic
- Materials: 2-liter bottle, soil, gravel, small plants, pill bugs or snails
- Assessment: Terrarium diagram and unit 4 test.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Crusades and the Reconquista
- Objective: Analyze causes and effects of the Crusades
- Objective: Describe the Reconquista
- Vocabulary: Crusade, Jerusalem, Saladin, Reconquista, convivencia
- Materials: Urban II speech, Muslim chronicle excerpt
- Assessment: Perspective paragraph.

### Spanish 1A: El desayuno y el almuerzo: food vocabulary
- Objective: Name breakfast and lunch foods
- Objective: Say what you eat and drink
- Vocabulary: el desayuno, el almuerzo, el pan, los huevos, la fruta, el sándwich, el jugo, la leche
- Materials: grocery ads, food pictures
- Assessment: Food vocabulary quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Islamic geometric pattern art
- Objective: Construct geometric patterns
- Objective: Connect art to Islamic culture
- Vocabulary: tessellation, arabesque, symmetry, tile
- Materials: compass, ruler, colored pencils, Smarthistory Alhambra images
- Assessment: Geometric pattern artwork.

### Physical Education 7: Flexibility, injury prevention and mid-year plan
- Objective: Perform static and dynamic stretches
- Objective: Write a one-week fitness plan
- Vocabulary: static stretch, dynamic stretch, RICE
- Materials: mat, yoga video
- Assessment: One-week fitness plan.

### Health Education 7: Tobacco and vaping
- Objective: Describe effects of tobacco and e-cigarettes
- Objective: Analyze vape marketing
- Vocabulary: nicotine, e-cigarette, secondhand smoke, flavoring
- Materials: vape ad examples, CDC fact sheets
- Assessment: Vaping fact sheet.
