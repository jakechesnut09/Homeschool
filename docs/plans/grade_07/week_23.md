## Day 1 — Monday (school day 111, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Choosing a topic* (75 min): Brainstorm topics on an invention, scientist or environmental issue and narrow to one focused research question.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Relationships and diagrams* (60 min): Match 6 stories to tape diagrams showing px + q = r or p(x + q) = r.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Gold Rush geology* (50 min): Read about how California gold formed in quartz veins and concentrated in streams, then explain the geologic processes.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Cortés* (45 min): Read an excerpt of Cortés's letter to Charles V describing Tenochtitlan.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Menu design* (45 min): Design a bilingual café menu with 12 items and prices in pesos or euros.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Warm-ups* (40 min): Do vocal and physical warm-ups (tongue twisters, mirror exercise).
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Base running* (40 min): Practice running through first and rounding bases.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Refusal steps* (25 min): Learn refusal steps: say no, give a reason, suggest an alternative, leave.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 112, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Evaluating sources* (75 min): Apply the CRAAP test (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose) to five websites on the topic.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Writing equations* (60 min): Write equations for situations such as 4 friends each buying a $2.50 snack plus a shared $6 pizza.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Aquifer model* (50 min): Build an aquifer in a cup with gravel, sand and clay layers and add colored 'pollution' to see it spread.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Aztec perspective* (45 min): Read an Aztec account from the Florentine Codex (Broken Spears) and compare points of view.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Descriptions* (45 min): Write a one-sentence Spanish description for each menu item using adjectives.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Yes, and* (40 min): Play 'Yes, and' story-building improv with a family member.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Fielding decisions* (40 min): Practice where to throw in force-out situations.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Practice 1* (25 min): Role-play refusing a vape offer from a friend.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 113, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Library databases* (75 min): Search the public library's student databases and find three credible sources.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Hanger diagrams* (60 min): Use balanced hanger diagrams to reason about solving 3x + 2 = 11.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Oil and gas* (50 min): Read about how oil formed in California's Central Valley and map where fossil fuels are found.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Pizarro and the Inca* (45 min): Explain how civil war, disease and steel helped Pizarro defeat Atahualpa.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Ordering chunks* (45 min): Learn the chunks ¿Qué quieres comer? and Quisiera... and practice ordering from your menu.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Tableaux* (40 min): Create frozen tableaux for scenes from history (e.g., signing Magna Carta).
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Rules* (40 min): Learn basic rules and play a modified game of 3-pitch softball.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Practice 2* (25 min): Role-play refusing alcohol at a party.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 114, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Working bibliography* (75 min): Create MLA citations for at least four sources using Purdue OWL.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Solving with balance* (60 min): Solve 10 equations by doing the same to each side and checking solutions.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Resource map* (50 min): Analyze a USGS map of mineral and groundwater resources and explain uneven distribution.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *DBQ* (45 min): Analyze 6 documents to answer 'How were so few Spanish able to conquer so many?'
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Role-play* (45 min): Run a café role-play with a family member as customer, using numbers for prices.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Objectives* (40 min): Improvise short scenes where each character has a clear objective and obstacle.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Game day* (40 min): Play a full game with a homeschool group or family.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Exit plans* (25 min): Create a family code word for getting picked up.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 115, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Review & check* (75 min): Submit research question, source evaluations and working bibliography for feedback.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on writing equations from diagrams and stories.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on resource formation and distribution.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Unit 7 assessment* (45 min): Write the DBQ essay and take a short unit 7 test.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Unit 6 test* (45 min): Take the unit 6 test: food listening, -er/-ir verbs, and a short writing on your eating habits.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Reflect* (40 min): Journal on which game built confidence and why.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Write about one strategic decision you made in the game.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Perform a refusal-skills assessment scenario.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Research questions and sources
- Objective: Develop a focused research question
- Objective: Assess credibility of sources
- Vocabulary: research question, credible, primary source, secondary source, database
- Materials: public library online databases, CRAAP test checklist
- Assessment: Research proposal and bibliography.

### Mathematics 7: Tape diagrams and equations
- Objective: Represent situations with tape diagrams
- Objective: Write equations p(x + q) = r and px + q = r
- Vocabulary: tape diagram, equation, coefficient
- Materials: grid paper
- Assessment: Quiz on tape diagrams and equation writing.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Earth's resources: minerals, energy, groundwater
- Objective: Explain why resources are unevenly distributed
- Objective: Model groundwater
- Vocabulary: ore, fossil fuel, aquifer, groundwater, renewable
- Materials: clear cup, gravel, sand, clay, water, food coloring
- Assessment: Aquifer model and quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Spanish conquest
- Objective: Explain why the Aztec and Inca fell
- Objective: Evaluate multiple perspectives on conquest
- Vocabulary: conquistador, Cortés, Pizarro, smallpox, Malinche
- Materials: Florentine Codex excerpt, Cortés letter excerpt
- Assessment: DBQ essay and unit 7 test.

### Spanish 1A: Unit 6 assessment: a restaurant menu
- Objective: Create a menu
- Objective: Order and describe foods
- Vocabulary: el menú, ¿Qué quieres?, quisiera, la cuenta, el precio
- Materials: paper or slides for a menu
- Assessment: Menu project rubric and unit 6 test.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Theatre games and improvisation
- Objective: Build focus and ensemble skills
- Objective: Use 'yes, and' in improv
- Vocabulary: improvisation, ensemble, tableau, objective
- Materials: open space, prompt cards
- Assessment: Participation checklist and journal.

### Physical Education 7: Softball: game strategy
- Objective: Apply base-running and fielding strategy
- Objective: Play a modified game
- Vocabulary: force out, tag up, cutoff, inning
- Materials: softball equipment, bases
- Assessment: Game play and reflection.

### Health Education 7: Refusal skills
- Objective: Demonstrate refusal skills
- Objective: Plan exit strategies
- Vocabulary: refusal skills, assertive, exit strategy
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Refusal skills rubric.
