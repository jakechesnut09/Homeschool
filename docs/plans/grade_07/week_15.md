## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Revision* (75 min): Revise for logical order and transitions using a color-coding check (claim, evidence, reasoning).
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Simple interest* (60 min): Compute simple interest on savings and loans using I = Prt for different rates and years.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Oh Deer! simulation* (50 min): Play a tabletop version of 'Oh Deer!' with beans for food, water and shelter over 15 rounds and graph the deer population.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Rise of the shogun* (45 min): Explain how the Minamoto clan created the Kamakura shogunate in 1192.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Objects* (45 min): Label 20 school supplies and furniture at home with sticky notes in Spanish with el/la.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Strumming patterns* (40 min): Learn down-down-up-up-down-up strumming and play it on C and Am.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Strategy chart* (40 min): Compare strategies in volleyball, badminton and pickleball.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Risk factors* (25 min): Identify risk and protective factors for violence.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Editing & citations* (75 min): Edit grammar and add in-text citations, then publish the final essay.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Comparing accounts* (60 min): Compare two savings accounts and one loan in a table and decide which is best for a goal.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Population graphs* (50 min): Interpret real data on the lynx-hare population cycle and describe the pattern.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Social pyramid* (45 min): Draw Japan's feudal pyramid and describe each class's duties.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Plurals* (45 min): Learn plural rules (-s, -es, lápiz→lápices) and write the plural of 20 nouns.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Chord changes* (40 min): Practice C-Am-F-G changes with a metronome at 60 bpm.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Favorite game* (40 min): Play your favorite net game for the full session.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *De-escalation* (25 min): Practice de-escalation phrases in role-plays.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 73, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Oral argument* (75 min): Deliver a 2-minute speech version of the argument with eye contact and adequate volume.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Percent error lab* (60 min): Estimate the masses of 5 objects, weigh them on a kitchen scale, and compute percent error for each estimate.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Crowding experiment* (50 min): Plant radish seeds at 3 different densities to investigate competition for resources.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Bushido* (45 min): Read excerpts on the samurai code and explain its values of loyalty and honor.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Hay* (45 min): Describe your backpack and desk using hay and numbers: En mi mochila hay dos cuadernos y un lápiz.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Song 1* (40 min): Learn the verse of a chosen song with the four chords.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Skills test* (40 min): Complete the net games skills test.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Weapons and gangs* (25 min): Discuss why to tell an adult about weapons and how gangs recruit.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 74, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Reflection* (75 min): Write a reflection on which revision made the biggest difference and why.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Error and tolerance* (60 min): Solve problems about manufacturing tolerance, e.g., a 500 g bag allowed 2% error.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Carrying capacity* (50 min): Analyze a graph of a population reaching carrying capacity and identify limiting factors.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Cultural legacy* (45 min): Research Noh theater, haiku or tea ceremony and present one to the family.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Classroom commands* (45 min): Learn and respond physically to 12 classroom expressions (Saquen una hoja, ¿Cómo se dice...?, Repitan) in a TPR game.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Song 2* (40 min): Learn a contrasting song (minor key or different tempo).
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Teach a skill* (40 min): Teach one net game skill to a sibling or parent.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Decision making* (25 min): Apply a decision-making model to two risky scenarios.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 75, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Unit 3 assessment* (75 min): Take a unit test on an unseen argument text (claims, evidence, author's purpose, structure).
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on simple interest and percent error.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on limiting factors and population graphs.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Unit 5 assessment* (45 min): Take the unit 5 test.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a vocabulary, plural, and hay quiz.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Check* (40 min): Record yourself playing and set practice goals with criteria.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Reflect* (40 min): Write about biomechanics (follow-through, base of support) in your best skill.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Quiz on violence prevention.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Argument revision and unit assessment
- Objective: Revise and publish an argument
- Objective: Present a claim orally
- Vocabulary: formal tone, citation
- Materials: revision checklist
- Assessment: Argumentative essay (rubric), speech and unit 3 test.

### Mathematics 7: Interest and percent error
- Objective: Compute simple interest
- Objective: Find percent error in measurements
- Vocabulary: simple interest, principal, percent error
- Materials: kitchen scale, measuring cups
- Assessment: Percent-error lab sheet and quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Resources and populations
- Objective: Analyze data on resource availability and population size
- Objective: Identify limiting factors
- Vocabulary: population, limiting factor, carrying capacity, resource
- Materials: dried beans, graph paper, radish seeds, cups of soil
- Assessment: Population graph and quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Shoguns, samurai and the warrior code
- Objective: Describe the shogun-daimyo-samurai system
- Objective: Compare Japanese and European feudalism
- Vocabulary: shogun, daimyo, samurai, bushido, Kamakura
- Materials: Bushido code excerpts, comparison chart
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Spanish 1A: En la clase: classroom objects, gender, plurals, and hay
- Objective: Name classroom objects
- Objective: Use hay and form plurals
- Vocabulary: el lápiz, la mochila, el cuaderno, la carpeta, la computadora, hay, ¿Cuántos?, los/las
- Materials: school supplies, sticky notes
- Assessment: Classroom vocabulary quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Instrument skills (ukulele or keyboard)
- Objective: Play chord changes in rhythm
- Objective: Develop practice habits
- Vocabulary: strum pattern, chord change, practice log
- Materials: ukulele or keyboard, chord charts, practice log
- Assessment: Recording and practice log.

### Physical Education 7: Net games review
- Objective: Compare net game strategies
- Objective: Demonstrate skills
- Vocabulary: placement, base position
- Materials: all net game equipment
- Assessment: Net games skills test.

### Health Education 7: Violence prevention
- Objective: Evaluate risky situations
- Objective: Use strategies to avoid violence
- Vocabulary: violence, weapon, gang, de-escalation
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Quiz and scenario responses.
