## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Revision: pacing* (75 min): Revise the narrative using a checklist: add transitions, cut one slow section, expand the climax.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *One of these things* (60 min): Compare recipes for lemonade that taste the same versus different and organize the amounts in tables.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Natural vs. synthetic* (50 min): Sort 15 household items as natural or synthetic and trace three synthetics back to their raw resources.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *The Qur'an* (45 min): Read short translated passages from the Qur'an on charity and justice and summarize their teachings.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Los días* (45 min): Learn the days of the week (lowercase, week starts on lunes) and write tomorrow/today/yesterday statements: Hoy es martes, mañana es miércoles.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Arranging* (40 min): Arrange 4–5 objects and use a paper viewfinder to make three thumbnail compositions.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Lead a warm-up* (40 min): Plan and lead the family through a 5-minute warm-up.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Activity guidelines* (25 min): Learn the 60-minutes-a-day guideline and log a week's activity and screen time.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Editing & publishing* (75 min): Edit for clauses, commas and spelling, then type and publish the final narrative.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Constant of proportionality* (60 min): Find k in tables of cost versus pounds and miles versus hours, writing k as a unit rate with units.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Milk plastic* (50 min): Make casein plastic from warm milk and vinegar, mold it and let it dry.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Five Pillars* (45 min): Create a graphic organizer of the Five Pillars with a description and example practice for each.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Los meses* (45 min): Learn the 12 months and fill in a blank Spanish calendar for this month with family events.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Drawing* (40 min): Draw the strongest composition lightly with accurate proportion and overlap.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Capture the flag* (40 min): Play capture the flag with a group and practice calling fair play.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Benefits and barriers* (25 min): List benefits of activity and personal barriers with solutions.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Read 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi'* (75 min): Read Kipling's 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi' and track how Rikki's courage develops through each conflict.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Both directions* (60 min): Find the constant of proportionality from x to y and y to x in a currency-conversion table and note they are reciprocals.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Polymer slime* (50 min): Make glue-and-borax slime and compare its properties to the starting materials.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Sunnah and law* (45 min): Explain how the Sunnah and Sharia guided daily life, commerce and family law.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *La fecha* (45 min): Learn the date format (el 5 de mayo, el primero de enero) and write 10 family birthdays: El cumpleaños de mi mamá es el....
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Shading* (40 min): Shade the still life fully with a range of values.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Conflict scenarios* (40 min): Role-play 4 game conflicts (disputed call, ball hog) and solve them respectfully.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Food safety* (25 min): Learn clean, separate, cook, chill and check the fridge temperature.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Theme statement* (75 min): Write an objective summary of the story and a theme statement supported by three pieces of evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Not proportional* (60 min): Sort 8 tables into proportional and not proportional, explaining with ratios that are or are not equivalent.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Research a material* (50 min): Research nylon, polyester or plastic bags and write how they are made and how they affect society and the environment.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Mosque architecture* (45 min): Examine images of the Dome of the Rock and Great Mosque of Córdoba and identify features like minaret, mihrab and arabesque.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Culture: días festivos* (45 min): Read about three holidays (16 de septiembre, 12 de octubre, 6 de enero Día de Reyes) and mark them on your calendar with a one-line note each.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Critique* (40 min): Analyze a Dutch still life (e.g., Claesz) and compare its composition to yours.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Create a game* (40 min): Design a new tag game with rules that include everyone, then teach and play it.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Cook a snack* (25 min): Prepare a healthy snack using food safety steps.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 25, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Unit 1 assessment* (75 min): Take a unit test on an unseen short story (CommonLit) with questions on plot, character, point of view and theme.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: identify proportional tables, find k, and fill in missing table values.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Unit 1 assessment* (50 min): Take the unit 1 test and present a one-page infographic on a synthetic material.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on Islamic beliefs and practices.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review & check* (45 min): Answer ¿Qué día es hoy? and ¿Cuál es la fecha? orally for five random dates and take a written dates quiz.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Artist statement* (40 min): Write a 150-word artist statement explaining your choices.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Self-assess sportsmanship with a checklist.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Quiz on food safety and activity guidelines.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Narrative revision, 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi' and unit assessment
- Objective: Revise for pacing and word choice
- Objective: Analyze theme in a short story
- Vocabulary: theme, revision, transition
- Materials: 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi' by Kipling (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Published personal narrative (rubric) and unit 1 test.

### Mathematics 7: Proportional relationships in tables
- Objective: Identify proportional relationships in tables
- Objective: Find the constant of proportionality
- Vocabulary: proportional relationship, constant of proportionality, unit rate
- Materials: measuring cups, water
- Assessment: Quiz on tables and constant of proportionality.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Natural resources to synthetic materials
- Objective: Trace synthetic materials back to natural resources
- Objective: Evaluate societal impact of a synthetic material
- Vocabulary: synthetic, natural resource, polymer, petroleum
- Materials: milk, vinegar, cornstarch, glue, borax (adult supervision)
- Assessment: Unit 1 test and synthetic-material infographic.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Beliefs and practices of Islam
- Objective: Explain the Qur'an and Sunnah as sources of belief and law
- Objective: Describe the Five Pillars
- Vocabulary: Qur'an, Sunnah, Five Pillars, Hajj, Sharia, mosque
- Materials: translated Qur'an excerpts (Fordham Sourcebook), Smarthistory mosque architecture
- Assessment: Five Pillars organizer and quiz.

### Spanish 1A: Los días, los meses y la fecha
- Objective: Name days and months
- Objective: Say and write dates and birthdays
- Vocabulary: lunes, domingo, enero, diciembre, ¿Qué día es hoy?, ¿Cuál es la fecha?, el cumpleaños, el primero de
- Materials: printable calendar, markers
- Assessment: Dates quiz and oral calendar questions.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Still life composition and artist statement
- Objective: Compose and render a still life
- Objective: Write an artist statement
- Vocabulary: composition, focal point, overlap, artist statement
- Materials: household objects, drawing paper, viewfinder
- Assessment: Still life drawing and artist statement (rubric).

### Physical Education 7: Leadership and fair play
- Objective: Take a leadership role
- Objective: Model sportsmanship
- Vocabulary: leadership, sportsmanship, conflict resolution
- Materials: cones, frisbee, pinnies
- Assessment: Game design and sportsmanship checklist.

### Health Education 7: Physical activity and food safety
- Objective: Explain benefits of physical activity
- Objective: Practice food safety
- Vocabulary: MVPA, sedentary, cross-contamination, foodborne illness
- Materials: food thermometer, kitchen
- Assessment: Activity log and quiz.
