## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Rhythm and story* (75 min): Read 'Paul Revere's Ride' aloud, tap the galloping meter, and mark rhyme scheme in two stanzas.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Shopping project* (60 min): Choose a $200 back-to-school budget and select items from ads, applying discounts and coupons.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Interaction types* (50 min): Sort 20 organism-pair cards into predation, competition, mutualism, commensalism and parasitism.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Europe's geography* (45 min): Map Europe's rivers, mountains, plains and seas and explain their effects on trade and settlement.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Subjects* (45 min): Learn the school subjects and ordinal numbers 1st–8th and fill out your weekly schedule in Spanish with times.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Motifs* (40 min): Create three 2-bar motifs using Song Maker and choose the strongest.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *FITT* (40 min): Learn the FITT principle and analyze three sample workout plans.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Earthquakes* (25 min): Practice Drop, Cover and Hold On and identify safe spots at home.

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Sound devices* (75 min): Annotate 'Annabel Lee' for repetition, internal rhyme and alliteration and explain how they create a haunting tone.

- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Backyard survey* (50 min): Observe a yard or park for 30 minutes and record organisms and any interactions seen.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Monasteries* (45 min): Read the Rule of St. Benedict excerpt and describe daily life in a monastery.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Números 32–100* (45 min): Learn tens and combinations (treinta y dos ... cien) and play number bingo with a parent.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Phrases* (40 min): Extend the motif into a 4-bar question phrase and 4-bar answer phrase.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Cardio session* (40 min): Complete a 25-minute jog/walk interval and record FITT details.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Wildfires* (25 min): Learn about wildfire evacuation and air-quality alerts.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Sound and figurative language in poetry
- Objective: Analyze rhyme, rhythm, alliteration and repetition
- Objective: Interpret figurative language and allusion
- Vocabulary: meter, alliteration, metaphor, personification, allusion
- Materials: 'Paul Revere's Ride' (Longfellow), 'Annabel Lee' (Poe), 'Mother to Son' (Hughes)
- Assessment: Annotations and poetry quiz.

### Mathematics 7: Percent project & unit test
- Objective: Apply percent reasoning to a budget
- Objective: Demonstrate mastery of unit 4
- Vocabulary: budget, discount
- Materials: online store pages or flyers
- Assessment: Unit 4 test and budget project.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Interactions among organisms
- Objective: Classify predation, competition and symbiosis
- Objective: Predict interaction patterns across ecosystems
- Vocabulary: predation, competition, mutualism, commensalism, parasitism
- Materials: ecosystem cards, outdoor observation area
- Assessment: Survey log and written prediction.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Geography of Europe and the early Middle Ages
- Objective: Describe Europe's physical geography
- Objective: Explain the role of monasteries and Charlemagne
- Vocabulary: Middle Ages, monastery, Charlemagne, Franks, missionary
- Materials: physical map of Europe, Einhard's Life of Charlemagne excerpt
- Assessment: Map and quiz.

### Spanish 1A: Mi horario: school subjects and numbers to 100
- Objective: Discuss class schedules
- Objective: Use numbers 32–100
- Vocabulary: las matemáticas, las ciencias, la clase de español, el almuerzo, primero/a, segundo/a, cuarenta, cien
- Materials: blank schedule, price ads
- Assessment: Schedule interview and number dictation.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Composition: melody
- Objective: Compose an 8-bar melody
- Objective: Use repetition and contrast
- Vocabulary: motif, phrase, cadence, unity, variety
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Song Maker or notation app (MuseScore)
- Assessment: Notated 8-bar melody.

### Physical Education 7: The FITT principle
- Objective: Explain FITT
- Objective: Apply overload, specificity and progression
- Vocabulary: frequency, intensity, time, type, overload, progression
- Materials: fitness journal
- Assessment: FITT quiz and activity log.

### Health Education 7: Emergency preparedness
- Objective: Prepare for California disasters
- Objective: Build an emergency kit
- Vocabulary: earthquake, wildfire, evacuation, emergency kit
- Materials: Ready.gov checklist, supplies for kit
- Assessment: Emergency plan and kit.
