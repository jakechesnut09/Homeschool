## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Select work* (75 min): Choose the best narrative, analysis, argument, research and poem pieces for the portfolio.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Plan an investigation* (60 min): Write a statistical question comparing two groups (e.g., reaction times of kids vs adults) and plan a random sample.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Prepare display* (50 min): Create a display board on the human-impact engineering project with problem, design, data and conclusion.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Themes across time* (45 min): Trace one theme (trade, religion or government) across all nine units on a chart.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Interpretive* (45 min): Read an authentic text (a pen-pal profile or town website) and listen to a short audio, answering comprehension questions.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Before & after* (40 min): Compare first and last drawings of the year and list three skills that improved.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Plan field day* (40 min): Plan field day stations for family or a homeschool group.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Wellness wheel* (25 min): Redo the wellness wheel and compare with week 1.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Revise one piece* (75 min): Revise one earlier piece using everything learned this year.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Collect and display* (60 min): Collect data from at least 10 people per group with a ruler-drop test and create dot plots or box plots.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Present* (50 min): Present the project to family or a homeschool group and answer questions.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Timeline review* (45 min): Complete the wall timeline and quiz yourself on 30 key events and dates.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Interpersonal* (45 min): Hold a 4-minute conversation with a parent about yourself, family, school, food, and plans.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Digital portfolio* (40 min): Photograph all work and create a digital portfolio slideshow.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Field day* (40 min): Run and participate in the field day.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Review* (25 min): Review all six units with a game.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Reflection letter* (75 min): Write a reflection letter describing growth, challenges and goals for grade 8.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Analyze and report* (60 min): Compute means and MADs, write conclusions, and present findings to the family.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Terrarium wrap-up* (50 min): Make final terrarium observations and write what the year-long model revealed about matter cycling.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Final review* (45 min): Review unit study guides with flashcards and map practice.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Presentational* (45 min): Write and present 'Mi vida' (12–15 sentences) covering all 1A topics.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Free choice* (40 min): Create one free-choice artwork in your favorite medium.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Favorite activity* (40 min): Spend the session on your favorite activity from the year.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Final exam* (25 min): Take the year-end health exam.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Reading celebration* (75 min): Total the year's reading log and give a 3-minute book talk on the favorite book of the year.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Final review* (60 min): Complete a cumulative review covering all 8 units.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Final review* (50 min): Review all seven units with a cumulative practice set.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Final exam* (45 min): Take the year-end world history exam with a map section and short essay.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Cultures* (45 min): Present one cultural comparison from the year (food, greetings, plazas, or celebrations).
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Reflection letter* (40 min): Write a letter to your future self about your artistic identity.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Portfolio* (40 min): Finalize the fitness portfolio with all scores and reflections.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Wellness plan* (25 min): Write a summer wellness plan covering nutrition, activity, sleep and stress.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Portfolio presentation* (75 min): Present the portfolio to family, explaining one piece in depth, and create a summer reading list.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Final exam* (60 min): Take the year-end grade 7 math exam and set goals for Math 8.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Final exam* (50 min): Take the year-end science exam.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Reflection* (45 min): Write a reflection on which civilization or idea most shaped today's world and present it.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Reflection* (45 min): Complete a can-do self-assessment and set goals for Spanish 1B.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Goals* (40 min): Set goals for VAPA 8 and choose a summer sketchbook challenge.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Reflect* (40 min): Write a year-end reflection on fitness and sportsmanship growth.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Reflect* (25 min): Share the plan with family and set check-in dates.
