## Day 1 — Monday (school day 161, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Paired reading* (75 min): Read two articles on lowering the voting age to 16 and identify each author's claim and key evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Likelihood* (60 min): Place 10 events on a 0-to-1 likelihood line from impossible to certain and justify placement.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Population growth* (50 min): Graph world population from 1800 to today and describe the pattern.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Columbian Exchange* (45 min): Sort foods, animals and diseases into Old World and New World origins and cook a dish using both.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Sports* (45 min): Learn 12 sports and jugar a and describe which sports family members play.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Plan* (40 min): Sketch a design for a soap carving from three views.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Compass skills* (40 min): Learn to take a bearing with a compass in the yard.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Checkups* (25 min): Learn what happens at medical, dental and vision checkups.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Comparing authors on one issue
- Objective: Analyze how two authors on the same topic differ
- Objective: Prepare debate evidence
- Vocabulary: bias, selective evidence, rebuttal
- Materials: paired articles on a youth issue (e.g., voting age at 16)
- Assessment: Comparison paragraph and debate case.

### Mathematics 7: Chance experiments and probability
- Objective: Describe probability as a number from 0 to 1
- Objective: Compare experimental and theoretical probability
- Vocabulary: probability, outcome, sample space, relative frequency
- Materials: coins, dice, paper bag with colored tiles
- Assessment: Probability quiz and experiment record.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Human population and resource consumption
- Objective: Analyze population and consumption data
- Objective: Argue how they affect Earth systems
- Vocabulary: per-capita consumption, ecological footprint, exponential growth
- Materials: online footprint calculator, household utility bills
- Assessment: Household audit and argument.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Columbian Exchange and mercantilism
- Objective: Analyze effects of the Columbian Exchange
- Objective: Explain mercantilism and the origins of capitalism
- Vocabulary: Columbian Exchange, mercantilism, joint-stock company, capitalism, Atlantic slave trade
- Materials: foods from the Columbian Exchange, trade simulation cards
- Assessment: Paragraph and simulation reflection.

### Spanish 1A: Los deportes e ir a + infinitive
- Objective: Name sports and leisure plans
- Objective: Talk about future plans with ir a + infinitive
- Vocabulary: jugar al fútbol, el básquetbol, el partido, ir a + infinitivo, mañana, esta noche, el próximo fin de semana
- Materials: sports schedule
- Assessment: Ir a + infinitive quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Subtractive sculpture and finishing
- Objective: Carve a subtractive form
- Objective: Finish sculptures
- Vocabulary: carving, patina, finish
- Materials: bar of soap, plastic knife, paint
- Assessment: Soap carving and finished clay sculpture.

### Physical Education 7: Hiking and orienteering
- Objective: Read a map and compass
- Objective: Practice trail safety
- Vocabulary: orienteering, bearing, trail etiquette, Leave No Trace
- Materials: compass, park map, water, daypack
- Assessment: Orienteering completion and hike log.

### Health Education 7: Health care and checkups
- Objective: Explain the importance of checkups
- Objective: Access health services
- Vocabulary: checkup, screening, health insurance, pharmacist
- Materials: community resource list
- Assessment: Personal health record.
