# Spanish 1A (Grade 7)

Spanish 1A is the first half of Spanish I, taking students from Novice Low to Novice Mid. Students learn greetings, numbers, dates, time, weather, personal description with ser, likes with gustar, school vocabulary, food, family, places in town, and present-tense regular -ar/-er/-ir verbs plus tener, estar, and ir (with ir a + infinitive). Culture threads include the 21 Spanish-speaking countries, greeting customs, school life, regional foods, family naming customs, and town plazas. Spanish 1B in grade 8 completes Spanish I.

*Framework:* 2019 California World Languages Standards; 2020 CA World Languages Framework (Novice Low to Novice Mid)  
*Time:* 45 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: ¡Hola! Greetings and the Spanish-Speaking World (weeks 1–4)
Greetings, names, and numbers open every conversation, and Spanish connects us to 21 countries.

- Week 1: ¡Hola! Greetings, farewells, and names
- Week 2: ¿Cómo estás? Feelings and formal vs. informal address
- Week 3: El alfabeto y los números 0–31
- Week 4: El mundo hispanohablante

### Unit 2: El calendario, la hora y el tiempo (weeks 5–7)
Dates, time, and weather organize daily life, and seasons differ across hemispheres.

- Week 5: Los días, los meses y la fecha
- Week 6: ¿Qué hora es? Telling time
- Week 7: ¿Qué tiempo hace? Weather and seasons

### Unit 3: ¿Cómo eres? Describing People (weeks 8–11)
Ser and agreeing adjectives let us describe who people are.

- Week 8: Subject pronouns and the verb ser
- Week 9: ¿Cómo eres? Personality adjectives and agreement
- Week 10: Physical descriptions, articles, and nationalities
- Week 11: Proyecto: Una persona famosa del mundo hispano

### Unit 4: Me gusta: Activities and Free Time (weeks 12–14)
Sharing likes and dislikes builds friendships across cultures.

- Week 12: ¿Qué te gusta hacer? Activities and gustar
- Week 13: A mí también, a mí tampoco: agreeing, disagreeing, and le gusta
- Week 14: Unit 4: Free time in the Spanish-speaking world

### Unit 5: La escuela (weeks 15–18)
Talking about school introduces regular -ar verbs and numbers to 100.

- Week 15: En la clase: classroom objects, gender, plurals, and hay
- Week 16: Mi horario: school subjects and numbers to 100
- Week 17: Present tense of -ar verbs
- Week 18: Semester 1 review and exam

### Unit 6: ¡A comer! Food and Healthy Eating (weeks 19–23)
Food reveals culture; -er and -ir verbs let us talk about what we eat and drink.

- Week 19: El desayuno y el almuerzo: food vocabulary
- Week 20: Present tense of -er and -ir verbs
- Week 21: Para mantener la salud: healthy eating and plural adjectives
- Week 22: Culture: food traditions and markets
- Week 23: Unit 6 assessment: a restaurant menu

### Unit 7: Mi familia (weeks 24–27)
Families and their celebrations are central to identity in Spanish-speaking cultures.

- Week 24: Mi familia: family members
- Week 25: The verb tener and possessive adjectives
- Week 26: Tener expressions and celebrations
- Week 27: Unit 7 project: Mi árbol genealógico

### Unit 8: Mi ciudad: Places and Directions (weeks 28–32)
Estar and ir help us locate places and move through a community.

- Week 28: ¿Dónde está? Estar and location
- Week 29: Lugares en la ciudad
- Week 30: ¿Adónde vas? The verb ir and question words
- Week 31: Las direcciones: getting around town
- Week 32: Unit 8 assessment: Mi ciudad

### Unit 9: El tiempo libre: Sports, Plans, and Invitations (weeks 33–34)
Ir a + infinitive lets us make plans and invite others.

- Week 33: Los deportes e ir a + infinitive
- Week 34: Invitations: accepting and declining

### Unit 10: Repaso y evaluación final (weeks 35–36)
We show what we can do in Spanish across all three modes.

- Week 35: Spanish 1A review
- Week 36: Final integrated performance assessment

## Standards

- **WL-COMM1-NL** Interpretive communication (Novice Low): recognize familiar words and memorized phrases in simple spoken and written texts.
- **WL-COMM1-NM** Interpretive communication (Novice Mid): understand words, phrases, and some simple sentences in texts on familiar topics.
- **WL-COMM2-NL** Interpersonal communication (Novice Low): greet, introduce oneself, and answer simple questions using memorized words and phrases.
- **WL-COMM2-NM** Interpersonal communication (Novice Mid): exchange information on familiar topics using practiced phrases and simple questions.
- **WL-COMM3-NL** Presentational communication (Novice Low): present basic information about oneself using memorized words and phrases.
- **WL-COMM3-NM** Presentational communication (Novice Mid): present information on familiar topics using practiced phrases and some simple sentences.
- **WL-CONN1-NM** Connections (Novice Mid): use Spanish to reinforce knowledge of geography, science, and health.
- **WL-CULT1-NM** Cultures (Novice Mid): identify products and practices of Spanish-speaking cultures and compare them with one's own.
- **WL-CULT2-NM** Intercultural interaction (Novice Mid): use culturally appropriate gestures, greetings, and forms of address in familiar situations.
- **WL-STRUC1-NL** Structures (Novice Low): use sound-symbol correspondence, numbers, and memorized patterns to communicate.
- **WL-STRUC1-NM** Structures (Novice Mid): use gender/number agreement, articles, and regular and common irregular present-tense verbs to communicate.
