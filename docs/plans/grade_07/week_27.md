## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Historical context* (75 min): Examine Library of Congress FSA photographs of 1930s Mississippi sharecroppers and list observations.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Visual patterns* (60 min): Write expressions for the nth step of two visual patterns and show the expressions are equivalent.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Making fossils* (50 min): Make mold and cast fossils of shells and leaves in salt dough and plaster.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Problems in the Church* (45 min): Read about the Great Schism of the papacy, corruption and indulgences and chart the causes of discontent.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Apellidos* (45 min): Learn how Spanish double surnames (apellido paterno y materno) work and write your name in that format.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Dress rehearsal* (40 min): Hold a full dress rehearsal.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Choose music* (40 min): Choose a song and map its sections.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Endocrine system* (25 min): Learn the endocrine glands and hormones that drive puberty.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Roll of Thunder, Hear My Cry: background and chapters 1–3
- Objective: Build historical context of 1930s Mississippi
- Objective: Analyze how setting shapes characters
- Vocabulary: sharecropping, Jim Crow, Great Depression, night men
- Materials: Roll of Thunder, Hear My Cry by Mildred D. Taylor (library), Library of Congress photos of 1930s South
- Assessment: Quiz and discussion notes.

### Mathematics 7: Expressions and equations in context
- Objective: Model real problems with expressions, equations and inequalities
- Objective: Choose an appropriate representation
- Vocabulary: model, constraint
- Materials: Visual Patterns website
- Assessment: Quiz and fundraiser model write-up.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Fossils and the history of life
- Objective: Explain how fossils form
- Objective: Analyze patterns in the fossil record
- Vocabulary: fossil, mold, cast, trace fossil, extinction
- Materials: plaster of Paris or salt dough, shells, leaves, petroleum jelly
- Assessment: Fossil models and data analysis quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Causes of the Reformation; Luther
- Objective: List causes of Church weakening
- Objective: Analyze Luther's ideas
- Vocabulary: indulgence, Reformation, Martin Luther, 95 Theses, Protestant
- Materials: 95 Theses excerpts
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1A: Unit 7 project: Mi árbol genealógico
- Objective: Present your family tree
- Objective: Explain Spanish naming customs
- Vocabulary: el apellido, el árbol genealógico, paterno/a, materno/a, casado/a
- Materials: poster board, family photos
- Assessment: Family tree presentation rubric and unit test.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Performance and theatre critique
- Objective: Perform for an audience
- Objective: Evaluate a performance with criteria
- Vocabulary: audience, critique, curtain call
- Materials: recorded play or local theatre performance
- Assessment: Performance and written review.

### Physical Education 7: Choreography project
- Objective: Create a group dance
- Objective: Perform for an audience
- Vocabulary: choreography, level, pathway, canon
- Materials: music, space
- Assessment: Choreography performance rubric.

### Health Education 7: Body systems and reproductive health (parent-led)
- Objective: Describe reproductive system structure and function
- Objective: Explain the role of hormones
- Vocabulary: hormone, endocrine system, reproductive system
- Materials: parent-selected resources, KidsHealth
- Assessment: Labeling worksheet.
