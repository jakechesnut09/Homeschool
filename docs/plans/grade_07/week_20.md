## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Act 2* (75 min): Read Act 2 and explain the conflict between Oberon and Titania and Puck's role.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Dividing signed numbers* (60 min): Use multiplication facts to determine sign rules for division and solve 15 division problems.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Mineral properties* (50 min): Test samples for luster, streak and hardness using a fingernail, penny and nail on the Mohs scale.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Spread of the plague* (45 min): Map the spread of the Black Death from Central Asia along trade routes to Europe, 1330s–1350s.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *-er verbs* (45 min): Learn the -er endings and conjugate comer, beber, and leer in a chart.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Design* (40 min): Design a print inspired by Japanese woodblock prints (Hokusai) with strong positive and negative space.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Dribbling* (40 min): Practice stationary and moving dribbles with each hand and crossovers through cones.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Alcohol effects* (25 min): Learn how alcohol affects the brain, liver and judgment.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 97, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Dramatic irony* (75 min): List moments when the audience knows more than the lovers and explain how this creates comedy.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Dividing by zero* (60 min): Explore why division by zero is undefined using related multiplication sentences and write an explanation.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Grow crystals* (50 min): Grow salt or sugar crystals from a supersaturated solution and connect to how minerals form.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Eyewitness* (45 min): Read Boccaccio's description of plague in Florence and list social effects.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *-ir verbs* (45 min): Learn the -ir endings, noting nosotros -imos, and conjugate vivir, escribir, compartir.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Transfer and carve* (40 min): Transfer the design onto a foam plate and incise lines.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Passing* (40 min): Practice chest, bounce and overhead passes with a partner, 20 each.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *BAC* (25 min): Learn what BAC is and factors that affect it.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 98, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Act 3* (75 min): Read Act 3 and track who loves whom on a 'love chart' before and after the potion.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Long division to decimals* (60 min): Convert 1/8, 3/11, 5/6 and 7/16 to decimals by long division and mark repeating parts with bar notation.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Rock types* (50 min): Classify yard and store-bought rocks into igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic with an ID key.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Consequences* (45 min): Analyze population data and explain how the plague weakened feudalism.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Drill* (45 min): Complete Conjuguemos -er/-ir practice to 90%, then write 10 sentences with frequency words (siempre, a veces, nunca).
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *First prints* (40 min): Ink with a brayer and pull test prints, adjusting pressure.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Triple threat* (40 min): Practice triple-threat position and jab steps.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Alcohol poisoning* (25 min): Learn signs of alcohol poisoning and when to call 911.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 99, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Soliloquy* (75 min): Analyze Helena's soliloquy at the end of Act 1 and what it reveals that dialogue does not.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Which fractions terminate?* (60 min): Investigate which denominators give terminating decimals and state a rule based on factors of 2 and 5.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Fizz test* (50 min): Test rocks with vinegar to identify limestone/calcite and explain the chemical reaction.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Universities and cathedrals* (45 min): Read about Thomas Aquinas and Gothic cathedrals and explain how the Church shaped learning and art.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Interview* (45 min): Interview a parent about their eating habits with eight questions using -er/-ir verbs.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Edition* (40 min): Pull an edition of 4 prints, numbering and signing each.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Keep-away* (40 min): Play 2-v-1 keep-away with dribble and pass.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Ad analysis* (25 min): Analyze alcohol ads and identify missing information.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Acts 2–3: the forest and the mix-ups
- Objective: Analyze how setting and characters interact
- Objective: Analyze how dramatic irony shapes points of view
- Vocabulary: dramatic irony, soliloquy, aside, mischief
- Materials: A Midsummer Night's Dream
- Assessment: Love chart and quiz.

### Mathematics 7: Dividing rational numbers and decimals
- Objective: Divide signed numbers
- Objective: Convert fractions to terminating or repeating decimals
- Vocabulary: quotient, terminating decimal, repeating decimal
- Materials: calculator
- Assessment: Division and decimal conversion quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Minerals and rocks
- Objective: Identify minerals by properties
- Objective: Classify rocks by formation
- Vocabulary: mineral, crystal, hardness, streak, igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic
- Materials: rock samples from yard, nail, penny, unglazed tile, vinegar
- Assessment: Rock and mineral ID chart and quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Black Death and the Church's influence
- Objective: Map the spread of the plague
- Objective: Explain the Church's role in learning
- Vocabulary: bubonic plague, pandemic, university, scholasticism, Gothic
- Materials: plague spread map, Boccaccio's Decameron excerpt
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Spanish 1A: Present tense of -er and -ir verbs
- Objective: Conjugate regular -er and -ir verbs
- Objective: Talk about eating and drinking habits
- Vocabulary: comer, beber, comprender, leer, escribir, compartir, vivir, siempre, nunca
- Materials: verb chart, Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: Regular present-tense quiz (80%+).

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Relief printmaking
- Objective: Carve and print a relief block
- Objective: Pull an edition of prints
- Vocabulary: relief print, brayer, edition, positive/negative space
- Materials: foam printing plates or erasers, pencil, water-based ink, brayer
- Assessment: Numbered print edition.

### Physical Education 7: Basketball: dribbling and passing
- Objective: Dribble with either hand
- Objective: Make chest, bounce and overhead passes
- Vocabulary: crossover, chest pass, bounce pass, triple threat
- Materials: basketball, hoop, cones
- Assessment: Dribble course time.

### Health Education 7: Alcohol
- Objective: Describe effects of alcohol
- Objective: Explain risks for teens
- Vocabulary: BAC, binge drinking, impairment, alcohol poisoning
- Materials: alcohol ads, fact sheets
- Assessment: Quiz.
