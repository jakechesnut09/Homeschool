## Day 1 — Monday (school day 106, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Watch a production* (75 min): Watch key scenes of a filmed production and note how costumes, setting and acting change your understanding.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Solving simple equations* (60 min): Solve one-step equations with negative numbers like x + 4.5 = -2 and -3x = 18.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Stream table* (50 min): Build a stream table in a baking pan with sand and observe erosion and delta formation as water flows.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Tenochtitlan* (45 min): Study a map of Tenochtitlan and explain how chinampas fed a city of 200,000.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Regional foods* (45 min): Research one traditional dish each from Mexico, Spain, Venezuela, and Peru and add photos and captions to your map.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Codex art* (40 min): Analyze Aztec codex images for color, line and storytelling.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Throwing* (40 min): Practice overhand throws with step and follow-through at 15 and 25 meters.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Medicine labels* (25 min): Read OTC labels for dosage, warnings and expiration.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 107, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Comparison essay* (75 min): Write a 5-paragraph comparison of the script and film's portrayal of Bottom or Puck, citing both.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Representing contexts* (60 min): Write and solve equations for situations involving temperature drops and debt.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Changing slope* (50 min): Repeat with steeper slope and record differences in erosion and deposition.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Aztec society* (45 min): Analyze Codex Mendoza tribute pages and describe Aztec social classes and warfare.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Tortilla: two meanings* (45 min): Compare the Mexican corn tortilla and the Spanish tortilla española; write five comparison sentences.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Adinkra* (40 min): Learn the meanings of 8 West African adinkra symbols.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Fielding* (40 min): Field grounders and fly balls hit or thrown by a partner.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Prescription misuse* (25 min): Learn why sharing prescriptions is dangerous.

- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Game day* (60 min): Play an integer card game (red = negative, black = positive) for sums and products and record 20 rounds.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Glaciers* (50 min): Drag an ice cube with embedded sand across clay to model glacial erosion and compare to Yosemite Valley.

- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *El mercado* (45 min): Watch a video of a Latin American market and list 10 foods you recognize in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Stamp design* (40 min): Design a personal symbol and carve it into a potato or foam stamp.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Batting tee* (40 min): Practice level swings off a tee, 40 swings.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Fentanyl* (25 min): Learn about counterfeit pills laced with fentanyl and the 'one pill can kill' message.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Page vs. stage and performance
- Objective: Compare the script to a filmed or staged version
- Objective: Perform a scene
- Vocabulary: staging, blocking, interpretation, director's choice
- Materials: filmed version (1999 film or a recorded stage production from library)
- Assessment: Comparison essay, performance rubric and unit 5 test.

### Mathematics 7: Rational numbers unit test
- Objective: Apply rational number operations to solving equations
- Objective: Demonstrate mastery of unit 5
- Vocabulary: solution, variable
- Materials: index cards
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Weathering, erosion and deposition shape the surface
- Objective: Investigate surface processes
- Objective: Compare fast and slow changes
- Vocabulary: deposition, delta, glacier, landslide
- Materials: baking pan, sand, soil, watering can, ice cubes
- Assessment: Stream-table data and CER.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Aztec and Inca
- Objective: Compare Aztec and Inca societies
- Objective: Describe social classes and warfare
- Vocabulary: Tenochtitlan, chinampa, tribute, quipu, Cuzco, terrace
- Materials: Codex Mendoza images, string for quipu
- Assessment: Comparison chart and quiz.

### Spanish 1A: Culture: food traditions and markets
- Objective: Identify traditional foods of several countries
- Objective: Compare food practices
- Vocabulary: la tortilla, el mercado, los churros, el gazpacho, las arepas, típico/a
- Materials: world map, ingredients for a simple recipe
- Assessment: Cultural comparison writing rubric.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Mesoamerican and African art connections
- Objective: Analyze art from Mesoamerica and West Africa
- Objective: Create a culturally inspired work
- Vocabulary: motif, symbol, adinkra, codex
- Materials: stamp materials, fabric or paper, images of Aztec codices and adinkra symbols
- Assessment: Stamped textile and reflection.

### Physical Education 7: Softball: throwing, catching and batting
- Objective: Throw overhand with accuracy
- Objective: Bat with level swing
- Vocabulary: crow hop, grounder, fly ball, batting stance
- Materials: softball or wiffle ball, bat, gloves, tee
- Assessment: Throwing and batting test.

### Health Education 7: Medicine safety and fentanyl awareness
- Objective: Use medicines safely
- Objective: Understand fentanyl risks
- Vocabulary: prescription, OTC, dosage, fentanyl, naloxone
- Materials: medicine labels
- Assessment: Medicine safety inventory.
