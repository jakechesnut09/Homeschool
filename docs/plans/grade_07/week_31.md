## Day 1 — Monday (school day 151, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Historical accounts* (75 min): Read a historical article and two primary sources on segregated schooling and sharecropping in the 1930s.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Slicing solids* (60 min): Shape clay into a cube, cylinder and pyramid and slice with floss parallel and perpendicular to the base; sketch each cross section.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Phenomenon: wildfire* (50 min): Compare before-and-after images of a California wildfire area and predict population changes.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Bacon and Descartes* (45 min): Compare Bacon's empiricism and Descartes's rationalism using short quotes.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Direction words* (45 min): Learn derecha, izquierda, todo derecho, cuadra, esquina and follow parent's spoken directions around the house.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Shoot scene 1* (40 min): Capture frames for the first scene of the animation.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Standing long jump* (40 min): Practice arm swing and takeoff and measure 3 jumps.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Pathogens* (25 min): Compare bacteria, viruses, fungi and parasites.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Fiction vs. history: comparative essay
- Objective: Compare a fictional portrayal with historical accounts
- Objective: Write an explanatory essay
- Vocabulary: historical account, fictional portrayal, accuracy
- Materials: Library of Congress primary sources on sharecropping and segregated schools, Roll of Thunder
- Assessment: Comparative essay (rubric).

### Mathematics 7: Cross sections and volume of prisms
- Objective: Describe cross sections of 3-D figures
- Objective: Find volume of right prisms
- Vocabulary: cross section, base, prism, volume
- Materials: clay or play dough, dental floss, cereal box
- Assessment: Cross-section sketches and volume quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Disrupting ecosystems
- Objective: Argue how changes to ecosystems affect populations
- Objective: Analyze invasive-species data
- Vocabulary: invasive species, disturbance, succession, biodiversity
- Materials: population data sets, graph paper
- Assessment: Data-based argument.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The scientific method and new instruments
- Objective: Explain Bacon's and Descartes's methods
- Objective: Describe new inventions' importance
- Vocabulary: scientific method, empiricism, rationalism, microscope, barometer
- Materials: magnifying glass, jar barometer materials
- Assessment: Written explanation.

### Spanish 1A: Las direcciones: getting around town
- Objective: Follow and give simple directions
- Objective: Read a city map in Spanish
- Vocabulary: a la derecha, a la izquierda, derecho, la cuadra, la esquina, cruzar, doblar
- Materials: printed city map
- Assessment: Direction-following listening quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Stop-motion production
- Objective: Produce and edit an animation
- Objective: Present a media work
- Vocabulary: editing, soundtrack, credits
- Materials: stop-motion app, tripod or stack of books
- Assessment: Completed stop-motion film.

### Physical Education 7: Field events
- Objective: Perform standing long jump and softball/shot throw
- Objective: Apply force and angle principles
- Vocabulary: takeoff, angle of release, momentum
- Materials: tape measure, softball or medicine ball, sand or grass
- Assessment: Field event marks.

### Health Education 7: Communicable diseases
- Objective: Explain disease transmission
- Objective: Practice prevention
- Vocabulary: pathogen, virus, bacteria, immune system, vaccine
- Materials: glitter or Glo Germ, soap
- Assessment: Quiz.
