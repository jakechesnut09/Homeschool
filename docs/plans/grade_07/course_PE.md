# Physical Education 7 (Grade 7)

Seventh-grade physical education emphasizing team-building, invasion and net games, striking and fielding, dance, track and field, and outdoor pursuits, all tied to personal fitness. Students take the FITNESSGRAM (California's grade 7 Physical Fitness Test), set SMART goals, learn the FITT principle and heart-rate training zones, and finish the year with a self-designed fitness plan. Most sessions include a dynamic warm-up, skill practice, game play and cool-down; they can be done in a yard, park or community center with siblings, parents or a homeschool group.

*Framework:* California Physical Education Model Content Standards (Grade 7); 2009 CA Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 40 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Fitness Assessment and Goal Setting (weeks 1–3)
Knowing your baseline fitness lets you set realistic goals.

- Week 1: Warm-ups and aerobic capacity baseline
- Week 2: Strength and flexibility baseline
- Week 3: SMART goals and circuit training

### Unit 2: Cooperative Challenges and Team Building (weeks 4–5)
Groups succeed through communication, trust and shared leadership.

- Week 4: Cooperative challenges
- Week 5: Leadership and fair play

### Unit 3: Invasion Games: Soccer and Ultimate (weeks 6–10)
Creating and denying space is the key to invasion games.

- Week 6: Soccer: passing and receiving
- Week 7: Soccer: dribbling, shooting and strategy
- Week 8: Ultimate frisbee: throwing and catching
- Week 9: Ultimate frisbee: game play and Spirit of the Game
- Week 10: Invasion games tournament

### Unit 4: Net and Wall Games: Volleyball, Badminton, Pickleball (weeks 11–15)
Placement, footwork and returning to base win points in net games.

- Week 11: Volleyball: forearm pass and set
- Week 12: Volleyball: serving and game play
- Week 13: Badminton
- Week 14: Pickleball
- Week 15: Net games review

### Unit 5: Principles of Training (weeks 16–19)
The FITT principle and heart-rate zones guide safe, effective training.

- Week 16: The FITT principle
- Week 17: Heart rate and intensity
- Week 18: Strength training with body weight
- Week 19: Flexibility, injury prevention and mid-year plan

### Unit 6: Basketball and Striking/Fielding Games (weeks 20–24)
Offense and defense strategies transfer across team sports.

- Week 20: Basketball: dribbling and passing
- Week 21: Basketball: shooting and game play
- Week 22: Softball: throwing, catching and batting
- Week 23: Softball: game strategy
- Week 24: Team sports review

### Unit 7: Dance and Rhythms (weeks 25–27)
Dance is cardiovascular exercise and cultural expression.

- Week 25: Line dances and rhythm
- Week 26: Cultural and folk dances
- Week 27: Choreography project

### Unit 8: Individual Pursuits: Jump Rope and Track & Field (weeks 28–32)
Individual events build personal bests through technique and practice.

- Week 28: Jump rope skills
- Week 29: Track: sprints and starts
- Week 30: Track: distance and pacing
- Week 31: Field events
- Week 32: Track meet

### Unit 9: Outdoor Pursuits and Personal Fitness Plan (weeks 33–36)
Lifelong fitness comes from enjoyable activity and a personal plan.

- Week 33: Hiking and orienteering
- Week 34: FITNESSGRAM post-test
- Week 35: Personal fitness plan
- Week 36: Field day and year reflection

## Standards

- **PE-7.1.1** Combine manipulative, locomotor and nonlocomotor skills into movement patterns in individual, dual and team activities.
- **PE-7.1.2** Demonstrate mature techniques for throwing, catching, striking and kicking in modified game situations.
- **PE-7.1.3** Demonstrate offensive and defensive skills (passing, dribbling, guarding, marking) in invasion and net games.
- **PE-7.1.4** Perform rhythmic and dance sequences with a partner or group, including line and cultural dances.
- **PE-7.1.5** Demonstrate basic skills of an individual or outdoor activity (orienteering, jump rope, track and field events).
- **PE-7.2.1** Explain and apply offensive and defensive strategies in invasion, net/wall and striking/fielding games.
- **PE-7.2.2** Analyze movement performance using biomechanical principles such as force, follow-through and base of support.
- **PE-7.2.3** Explain how to create space and use teamwork to achieve a goal in game play.
- **PE-7.3.1** Participate in moderate-to-vigorous physical activity a minimum of four days each week.
- **PE-7.3.2** Assess muscular strength, muscular endurance, aerobic capacity, flexibility and body composition using a fitness assessment.
- **PE-7.3.3** Meet age- and gender-specific fitness standards (Healthy Fitness Zone) for aerobic capacity, muscular strength and endurance, flexibility and body composition.
- **PE-7.3.4** Develop and implement a one-week personal physical fitness plan using the FITT principle.
- **PE-7.4.1** Explain the FITT principle (frequency, intensity, time, type) and the principles of overload, specificity and progression.
- **PE-7.4.2** Identify and calculate target heart-rate zones and monitor intensity during activity.
- **PE-7.4.3** Explain the components of a proper warm-up and cool-down and how they prevent injury.
- **PE-7.5.1** Demonstrate responsible personal and social behavior, including safety, fair play and sportsmanship.
- **PE-7.5.2** Accept responsibility for individual improvement and set SMART goals.
- **PE-7.5.3** Work cooperatively with group members to solve problems and accomplish goals; demonstrate leadership.
