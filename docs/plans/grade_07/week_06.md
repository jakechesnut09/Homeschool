## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Background: Klondike Gold Rush* (75 min): Read a short article on the 1897 Klondike Gold Rush and locate Santa Clara, Seattle, Skagway and Dawson on a map.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Walking rates* (60 min): Time how long it takes to walk 1/4 mile and compute miles per hour using a ratio of fractions.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Phenomenon: rusty nail* (50 min): Compare a new and rusted nail and list differences in properties; start a steel-wool rusting jar to observe all week.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *The caliphates* (45 min): Map the expansion under the Rashidun, Umayyad and Abbasid caliphates from 632 to 750.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *¿Qué hora es?* (45 min): Build a paper-plate clock and practice es la una / son las... with y cuarto, y media, and menos diez.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Color wheel* (40 min): Mix and paint a 12-part color wheel from primaries only.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Passing technique* (40 min): Practice inside-foot passes against a wall, 50 with each foot.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Food log* (25 min): Review a 3-day food log and compare it to MyPlate.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 1* (75 min): Read Chapter 1 'Into the Primitive' and list three lessons Buck learns from the man in the red sweater.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Complex fractions* (60 min): Simplify complex fractions like (1/2)/(3/4) by multiplying by the reciprocal and connect to unit rate.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Signs of reactions* (50 min): Run four mini-reactions (baking soda + vinegar, milk + lemon juice, Alka-Seltzer + water, steel wool + vinegar) and record evidence.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Sunni and Shia* (45 min): Explain the origins of the Sunni-Shia division over succession.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Times of day* (45 min): Add de la mañana, de la tarde, de la noche and write the time of 10 daily events on your own schedule.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Tints and shades* (40 min): Paint tint and shade scales for two colors.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Receiving* (40 min): Practice receiving with sole, inside foot and thigh and controlling in one touch.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *SMART goal* (25 min): Write a SMART nutrition goal (e.g., 'eat 3 servings of vegetables 5 days a week').

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Context clues* (75 min): Use context to define 10 challenging words from chapters 1–2 and check with a dictionary.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Recipe rates* (60 min): Find cups of flour per batch and batches per cup from a recipe using 2/3 cup for 1/2 batch.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Physical vs. chemical* (50 min): Test melting ice, dissolving sugar, burning a candle and toasting bread; classify each with evidence.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Baghdad* (45 min): Read a description of Baghdad's round city and explain why it became a world center of trade and learning.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *¿A qué hora?* (45 min): Contrast ¿Qué hora es? with ¿A qué hora es...?; ask a parent five questions about family events and record answers.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Color schemes* (40 min): Paint four small squares showing complementary, analogous, monochromatic and triadic schemes.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Passing on the move* (40 min): Pass with a partner while jogging through cones.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Healthy recipe* (25 min): Plan and cook a balanced family dinner.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: The Call of the Wild, chapters 1–2
- Objective: Analyze how setting changes Buck
- Objective: Use context clues for unfamiliar words
- Vocabulary: Klondike, primordial, club and fang, ascendancy
- Materials: The Call of the Wild (Project Gutenberg or library), map of Yukon and Alaska
- Assessment: Reading quiz and journal entries.

### Mathematics 7: Unit rates with fractions
- Objective: Compute unit rates from ratios of fractions
- Objective: Use complex fractions in context
- Vocabulary: complex fraction, rate per 1
- Materials: measuring tape, stopwatch
- Assessment: Problem set on unit rates with fractions.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Evidence of chemical reactions
- Objective: Identify evidence of chemical change
- Objective: Distinguish physical and chemical changes
- Vocabulary: chemical reaction, physical change, precipitate, reactant, product
- Materials: baking soda, vinegar, steel wool, milk, lemon juice, ice
- Assessment: Reaction evidence chart and CER.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Expansion of Muslim rule and trade
- Objective: Trace the expansion of Muslim empires
- Objective: Explain the role of trade and cities
- Vocabulary: caliph, Umayyad, Abbasid, Sunni, Shia, Baghdad
- Materials: expansion map, Ibn Battuta excerpts
- Assessment: Expansion map and paragraph.

### Spanish 1A: ¿Qué hora es? Telling time
- Objective: Tell time on the hour, half hour, and quarter hour
- Objective: Say at what time events happen
- Vocabulary: ¿Qué hora es?, es la una, son las dos, y media, y cuarto, menos, ¿A qué hora?, de la mañana
- Materials: paper-plate clock, TV or event schedule
- Assessment: Time listening quiz and oral clock reading.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Color theory
- Objective: Mix a color wheel
- Objective: Explain color schemes
- Vocabulary: primary, secondary, tertiary, complementary, analogous
- Materials: tempera or acrylic paint (red, yellow, blue, white, black), brushes, paper plate palette
- Assessment: Color wheel and scheme swatches.

### Physical Education 7: Soccer: passing and receiving
- Objective: Pass with the inside of the foot
- Objective: Receive and control the ball
- Vocabulary: inside-foot pass, first touch, trap
- Materials: soccer ball, cones, wall
- Assessment: Passing accuracy test.

### Health Education 7: Nutrition goal and unit assessment
- Objective: Set and track a nutrition goal
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: SMART goal, tracking
- Materials: food log
- Assessment: Unit 1 test and goal tracker.
