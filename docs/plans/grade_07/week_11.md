## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Text features & structure* (75 min): Preview three ReadWorks articles and identify each one's structure (chronological, compare-contrast, cause-effect, problem-solution).
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Estimating area* (60 min): Trace a circle on grid paper and estimate its area by counting squares, comparing to r squared.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Phenomenon: a growing tree* (50 min): Read about van Helmont's willow tree experiment and predict where the tree's mass came from.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Vegetation zones* (45 min): Color a map of West Africa's desert, Sahel, savanna and forest zones and list resources from each.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Choose and research* (45 min): Choose a figure (e.g., Frida Kahlo, Lionel Messi, Sonia Sotomayor, Ellen Ochoa) and collect five facts in English, then key Spanish words.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Treble clef* (40 min): Learn treble clef note names and complete a note-naming drill.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Ready position & bump* (40 min): Practice ready position and forearm pass to self 20 times, then against a wall.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Types of bullying* (25 min): Define physical, verbal, social and cyberbullying with examples.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Two central ideas* (75 min): Read an article on teen screen time and trace two central ideas through the text with a graphic organizer.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Cutting the circle* (60 min): Cut a paper plate into 16 sectors and rearrange them into a near-parallelogram to show area is pi r times r.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Floating leaf disks* (50 min): Use a syringe to infiltrate spinach leaf disks with baking-soda water and count how many float under a lamp every minute.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Gold and salt* (45 min): Explain why salt was as valuable as gold and simulate silent barter with a family member.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Write* (45 min): Write an 8–10 sentence profile in Spanish using ser, nationalities, personality, and physical description.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Bass clef* (40 min): Learn bass clef note names and complete a drill.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Set* (40 min): Practice overhead sets to self and to a wall target.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Upstander* (25 min): Learn upstander strategies and write responses to three scenarios.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 53, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Objective summary* (75 min): Write an objective summary of the article stating both central ideas and key details.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Using the formula* (60 min): Find areas of pizzas, sprinkler regions and circular gardens given radius or diameter.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Light variable* (50 min): Repeat the leaf-disk test at different lamp distances and graph floating rate versus light.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Ghana Empire* (45 min): Read al-Bakri's description of Ghana's king and court (primary source) and list signs of wealth and power.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Revise* (45 min): Check adjective agreement and articles with a checklist; look up three new words on SpanishDict.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Keyboard* (40 min): Locate notes on a keyboard and play C, G and F major scales.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Partner passing* (40 min): Bump and set with a partner, counting consecutive contacts.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Conflict resolution* (25 min): Learn a six-step conflict resolution model and apply it to a sibling conflict.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 54, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Academic vocabulary* (75 min): Learn 10 academic words (analyze, significant, evidence, impact, contrast…) and use each in a sentence about the article.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Area vs circumference* (60 min): Decide whether 8 situations need area or circumference (fencing vs sod, crust vs cheese) and solve each.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Photosynthesis equation* (50 min): Build a gumdrop model of 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2 and explain how atoms are rearranged.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Family and specialization* (45 min): Explain how family clans and specialized crafts like blacksmithing supported cities.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Present* (45 min): Create a poster or 3-slide presentation and practice presenting it without reading word for word.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Steps* (40 min): Build major scales using the whole-whole-half pattern.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Movement to ball* (40 min): Practice shuffling to the ball before passing.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Decision making* (25 min): Use the DECIDE model on a social situation.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 55, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Review & check* (75 min): Take a quiz on an unseen article identifying structure and central ideas.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on area of circles and choosing the correct measure.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a CER answering 'Where does a plant's mass come from?' using leaf-disk evidence.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on geography and Ghana.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Unit 3 assessment* (45 min): Present the profile (1–2 minutes) and take the unit 3 written test on ser and adjective agreement.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Check* (40 min): Take a note-reading and scale quiz.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Complete a consecutive-pass skill test (target 15).
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Write an action plan for responding to cyberbullying.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Reading informational text: central ideas and structure
- Objective: Determine two central ideas
- Objective: Analyze text structure
- Vocabulary: central idea, text structure, cause and effect, problem-solution
- Materials: ReadWorks articles on technology and teens
- Assessment: Summary and informational reading quiz.

### Mathematics 7: Area of a circle
- Objective: Derive the area formula informally
- Objective: Use A = pi r squared
- Vocabulary: area, squared, sector
- Materials: paper plates, scissors, grid paper
- Assessment: Quiz and written derivation explaining the plate rearrangement.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Photosynthesis: where does a plant's mass come from?
- Objective: Gather evidence about inputs and outputs of photosynthesis
- Objective: Model how plants make sugar
- Vocabulary: photosynthesis, chloroplast, glucose, carbon dioxide
- Materials: spinach leaves, baking soda, syringe, cups, lamp
- Assessment: Leaf-disk data and CER.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Geography and the gold-salt trade
- Objective: Relate vegetation zones to trade
- Objective: Explain the rise of Ghana
- Vocabulary: Sahara, Sahel, savanna, caravan, silent barter
- Materials: vegetation-zone map, salt and gold-painted rocks for simulation
- Assessment: Map and quiz.

### Spanish 1A: Proyecto: Una persona famosa del mundo hispano
- Objective: Research a notable Hispanic figure
- Objective: Present a description in simple sentences
- Vocabulary: el/la artista, el/la atleta, el/la escritor/a, famoso/a, importante
- Materials: poster board or slides, library books
- Assessment: Presentational rubric and unit 3 test.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Pitch, staff and keyboard
- Objective: Read notes on treble and bass clef
- Objective: Build major scales
- Vocabulary: staff, clef, ledger line, whole step, half step, major scale
- Materials: keyboard app or piano, Chrome Music Lab
- Assessment: Note-reading quiz.

### Physical Education 7: Volleyball: forearm pass and set
- Objective: Perform a forearm pass (bump)
- Objective: Perform an overhead set
- Vocabulary: forearm pass, set, ready position, platform
- Materials: volleyball or beach ball, wall, net or rope
- Assessment: Volleyball pass test.

### Health Education 7: Bullying, cyberbullying and conflict resolution
- Objective: Identify forms of bullying
- Objective: Resolve conflicts peacefully
- Vocabulary: bullying, cyberbullying, upstander, mediation
- Materials: Common Sense Education lessons
- Assessment: Action plan.
