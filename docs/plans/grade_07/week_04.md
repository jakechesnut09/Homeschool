## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Finding a story* (75 min): Brainstorm a 'heart map' of memorable moments and choose one with a clear conflict or turning point.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Dream space design* (60 min): Design a dream classroom or park at a chosen scale, listing at least six objects with actual and scaled dimensions.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Density* (50 min): Measure mass and volume of water, oil and corn syrup and calculate density for each.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Geography of Arabia* (45 min): Map the Arabian Peninsula's deserts, oases and trade routes and compare nomadic Bedouin and town life.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Mapa* (45 min): Label the 21 Spanish-speaking countries and their capitals on a blank map, color-coding Spain, Mexico/Central America, the Caribbean, South America, and Equatorial Guinea.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Face proportions* (40 min): Learn standard face proportions and draw a guideline face.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Hula-hoop pass* (40 min): Pass a hoop around a hand-held circle without letting go and improve time over 5 tries.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Source check* (25 min): Evaluate 4 nutrition websites/posts for author, evidence and purpose.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Planning* (75 min): Plan the narrative on a story mountain with exposition, rising action, climax and reflection.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Actual area* (60 min): Compute actual area of each region in the design using the scale and record in a table.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Density column* (50 min): Build a density column with corn syrup, water and oil and predict where a grape, cork and coin will settle.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Mecca as a trade center* (45 min): Explain why Mecca was a crossroads of caravans and a religious center before Islam.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *¿De dónde eres?* (45 min): Practice ¿De dónde eres? / Soy de... and learn that Spanish is spoken widely in California and the U.S.; list five California place names of Spanish origin.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Features* (40 min): Practice drawing eyes, nose and mouth from a mirror, one page each.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Blind walk* (40 min): Guide a blindfolded partner through an obstacle course using only verbal cues.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Fad diets* (25 min): Compare two fad diet claims with MyPlate guidance and explain the risks.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Drafting the opening* (75 min): Draft an opening that starts with dialogue, action or a vivid setting detail, trying two versions.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Redraw at new scale* (60 min): Redraw one part of the design at half the original scale and compare the two drawings' areas.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Solubility* (50 min): Test how many teaspoons of salt, sugar and baking soda dissolve in 100 mL of water and graph results.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Life of Muhammad* (45 min): Create an illustrated timeline of Muhammad's life from 570 CE to 632 CE, including the hijra.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Map quiz practice* (45 min): Use an online map game or flashcards to drill countries and capitals until you can place all 21 in under five minutes.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Self-portrait sketch* (40 min): Sketch a self-portrait from a mirror using guidelines.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Group juggle* (40 min): Keep several balls moving in a set pattern with family members, adding balls each round.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Body image* (25 min): Discuss how media images affect body image and list healthy self-talk statements.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Drafting the body* (75 min): Finish the draft (600–900 words), slowing down the climax with sensory details and internal thoughts.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Unit review* (60 min): Work a mixed review set of 12 problems on scaled copies, scale factor, maps and area.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Mystery powders* (50 min): Identify three unlabeled white powders using solubility, texture and reaction with vinegar or iodine.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Connections to Judaism and Christianity* (45 min): Compare monotheism, prophets and holy books across Judaism, Christianity and Islam in a chart.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Presentación* (45 min): Write and record a self-introduction: greeting, name, how you are, age in numbers, where you are from, and farewell.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Self-portrait shading* (40 min): Add value to the self-portrait using the light source.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Human knot* (40 min): Untangle a human knot or complete 'toxic waste' transfer with rope.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Eating disorders* (25 min): Learn warning signs of eating disorders and how to help a friend get support.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Peer conference* (75 min): Read the draft aloud to a parent who gives two 'glows' and one 'grow'; record revision goals.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Unit 1 test* (60 min): Take the unit 1 test and present the design poster aloud, explaining how the scale was used.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a lab report on the mystery-powder investigation with a data table and conclusion.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz on geography and the origins of Islam.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Unit 1 assessment* (45 min): Take the unit test (greetings listening, numbers, alphabet, map of 21 countries) and submit the recorded introduction.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Reflect* (40 min): Compare the self-portrait to an early-year sketch and list improvements.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Reflect* (40 min): Discuss which communication strategies worked and write a reflection.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Take a quiz on valid information and healthy weight practices.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Personal narrative drafting
- Objective: Plan and draft a personal narrative with conflict
- Objective: Use dialogue, pacing and sensory detail
- Vocabulary: narrative arc, pacing, sensory detail, reflection
- Materials: mentor text: 'Thank You, Ma'am', writing notebook
- Assessment: Complete narrative draft and revision goals.

### Mathematics 7: Scale drawing project & unit test
- Objective: Apply scale to a real design
- Objective: Demonstrate mastery of unit 1
- Vocabulary: blueprint, actual area
- Materials: poster paper, ruler
- Assessment: Unit 1 test and scale design project.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Properties of substances
- Objective: Measure characteristic properties
- Objective: Use density, melting point and solubility to identify substances
- Vocabulary: density, solubility, melting point, characteristic property
- Materials: kitchen scale, measuring cup, water, oil, corn syrup, salt, sugar, baking soda
- Assessment: Density calculations and mystery-powder lab report.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Arabia and the origins of Islam
- Objective: Describe Arabian geography and ways of life
- Objective: Trace the life of Muhammad
- Vocabulary: Bedouin, oasis, Mecca, Medina, hijra, Kaaba
- Materials: physical map of Arabia, Khan Academy video on Islam
- Assessment: Timeline and quiz.

### Spanish 1A: El mundo hispanohablante
- Objective: Identify the 21 Spanish-speaking countries and capitals
- Objective: Present a short self-introduction
- Vocabulary: el país, la capital, ¿De dónde eres?, soy de, España, México, Argentina, Guinea Ecuatorial
- Materials: blank world map, colored pencils
- Assessment: Unit 1 test and presentational recording rubric.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Proportion and portrait drawing
- Objective: Apply facial proportion guidelines
- Objective: Draw a self-portrait from observation
- Vocabulary: proportion, guidelines, symmetry
- Materials: mirror, pencils
- Assessment: Self-portrait.

### Physical Education 7: Cooperative challenges
- Objective: Communicate to solve group challenges
- Objective: Demonstrate trust and safety
- Vocabulary: cooperation, communication, trust
- Materials: hula hoops, blindfold, rope, balls
- Assessment: Teamwork rubric and reflection.

### Health Education 7: Valid nutrition information and fad diets
- Objective: Evaluate nutrition claims
- Objective: Recognize fad diets and unhealthy weight practices
- Vocabulary: fad diet, credible source, body image, eating disorder
- Materials: websites and social media posts on diets
- Assessment: Source evaluation and quiz.
