## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Introduction* (75 min): Draft an introduction with a hook, brief novel context and a clear thesis statement.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Parts of a circle* (60 min): Use a compass to draw circles and label center, radius, diameter and circumference; note d = 2r.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Define the problem* (50 min): Write criteria (e.g., warms 10 °C for 5 minutes) and constraints (safe, cheap, under 50 g) for a chemical warmer or cooler.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Mongol conquests* (45 min): Map the Mongol Empire and explain how the Pax Mongolica revived Silk Road trade.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Physical traits* (45 min): Learn alto, bajo, rubio, moreno, pelirrojo, tiene el pelo/los ojos... and describe 6 magazine photos.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Note values* (40 min): Complete musictheory.net lessons on note and rest durations and clap 8 rhythms.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Compare games* (40 min): Make a chart comparing strategies in soccer, ultimate and basketball.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Healthy relationships* (25 min): Sort relationship behaviors into healthy, unhealthy and abusive.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 47, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Body paragraphs* (75 min): Draft three body paragraphs using claim-evidence-commentary with embedded quotations and page citations.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Measuring around* (60 min): Measure diameter and circumference of 6 household circles with string, record in a table and graph circumference versus diameter.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Prototype 1* (50 min): Build and test two designs with different amounts of reactants and record temperature over time.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Marco Polo* (45 min): Read Marco Polo's description of Kublai Khan's court and evaluate it as a source.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Articles* (45 min): Learn el/la/los/las and un/una/unos/unas and complete a sorting activity with 20 nouns.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Time signatures* (40 min): Identify meter in 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4 excerpts and conduct each pattern.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Tournament 1* (40 min): Play a mini soccer tournament with a homeschool group or family.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *I-messages* (25 min): Rewrite five blaming statements as I-messages.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 48, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Conclusion & revision* (75 min): Draft a conclusion and revise transitions between paragraphs for cohesion.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Discovering pi* (60 min): Compute C/d for each object, average the ratios, and compare to 3.14 and 22/7.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Analyze and improve* (50 min): Compare test data in a decision matrix and redesign to better meet criteria.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Zheng He* (45 min): Map Zheng He's seven voyages and compare his ships with Columbus's.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Nationalities* (45 min): Learn 12 nationality adjectives and their agreement (español/española) and write who each famous person is and where they are from.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Sixteenth notes* (40 min): Count and clap sixteenth-note rhythms with 1-e-and-a syllables.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Tournament 2* (40 min): Play a mini ultimate tournament.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Active listening* (25 min): Practice active listening with a family member and summarize what they said.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 49, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Editing* (75 min): Edit for sentence variety, quotation punctuation and spelling, then publish the essay.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Applying circumference* (60 min): Solve problems such as how far a bike wheel travels in 10 rotations and finding diameter from circumference.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Prototype 2* (50 min): Test the improved design three times and calculate the average temperature change.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Why stop?* (45 min): Analyze reasons the Ming ended the voyages and built the Great Wall, and debate the decision.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *¿Quién es?* (45 min): Play a guess-who game with a parent using only Spanish yes/no questions about appearance and personality.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Bucket drumming* (40 min): Perform a 16-bar bucket drumming rhythm with a metronome.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Skills test* (40 min): Complete a unit skills test (passing, dribbling, throwing, catching).
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Peer pressure* (25 min): Role-play responding to peer pressure with assertive communication.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Literary analysis essay
- Objective: Write a literary analysis with embedded quotations
- Objective: Revise for cohesion
- Vocabulary: thesis, embedded quotation, commentary
- Materials: essay outline, Purdue OWL guide on quotations
- Assessment: Literary analysis essay (rubric) and unit 2 test.

### Mathematics 7: Circumference
- Objective: Discover pi as the ratio of circumference to diameter
- Objective: Use C = pi d
- Vocabulary: circle, radius, diameter, circumference, pi
- Materials: string, ruler, cans and lids of different sizes
- Assessment: Measurement lab table and circumference quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Engineering challenge: design a hand warmer or cold pack
- Objective: Define criteria and constraints
- Objective: Test, compare and iterate a design
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraints, prototype, iteration
- Materials: zip bags, calcium chloride, baking soda, citric acid, thermometer
- Assessment: Design portfolio and unit 2 test.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Mongols and Ming: open and closed
- Objective: Analyze Mongol rule and trade
- Objective: Explain Zheng He's voyages and Ming isolation
- Vocabulary: Genghis Khan, Kublai Khan, Yuan, Zheng He, isolation
- Materials: Marco Polo excerpts, map of Zheng He's voyages
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Spanish 1A: Physical descriptions, articles, and nationalities
- Objective: Describe physical appearance
- Objective: Use definite/indefinite articles and nationalities
- Vocabulary: alto/a, bajo/a, pelirrojo/a, el pelo, los ojos, un/una, el/la, mexicano/a
- Materials: magazine photos, famous-person photo cards
- Assessment: Reading comprehension quiz and guess-who participation.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Rhythm reading
- Objective: Read rhythms with whole through sixteenth notes
- Objective: Count in simple meter
- Vocabulary: beat, meter, time signature, dotted note, rest
- Materials: musictheory.net, metronome app, hand drum or bucket
- Assessment: Rhythm-reading quiz and performance.

### Physical Education 7: Invasion games tournament
- Objective: Transfer strategies across invasion games
- Objective: Demonstrate unit skills
- Vocabulary: transfer, transition, offense, defense
- Materials: soccer ball, frisbee, cones
- Assessment: Unit skills test and strategy reflection.

### Health Education 7: Healthy relationships and communication
- Objective: Distinguish healthy and unhealthy relationships
- Objective: Use I-messages and active listening
- Vocabulary: respect, trust, I-message, active listening, peer pressure
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Role-play and quiz.
