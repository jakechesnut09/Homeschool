## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Introduction* (75 min): Draft an introduction that hooks the reader and states the focus of the report.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Representing situations* (60 min): Write inequalities for situations like 'at least 13 to ride' and graph them on number lines.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Layers in a jar* (50 min): Pour sand, soil and gravel into a jar of water and observe how layers settle.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Linear perspective* (45 min): Learn one-point perspective and draw a hallway or street using a vanishing point.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Tener* (45 min): Learn the forms of tener (tengo, tienes...) and complete a Conjuguemos tener drill.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Read the script* (40 min): Read a short one-act (e.g., the Pyramus and Thisbe scene) and summarize plot and characters.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Counting music* (40 min): Clap and step to 8-counts in three songs of different tempos.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Physical changes* (25 min): Read about physical changes of puberty and discuss with a parent.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Body section 1* (75 min): Draft the first body section integrating at least two sources with citations.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Finding solutions* (60 min): Test values in inequalities and discover the reverse when multiplying or dividing by a negative.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Play-dough strata* (50 min): Build play-dough layers, add a 'fault' and an 'intrusion', and have a family member sequence the events.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Leonardo* (45 min): Study Leonardo's notebooks and anatomical sketches and design an invention page in his style.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Ages* (45 min): Ask and answer ¿Cuántos años tiene...? for 10 family members and friends.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Playwright intent* (40 min): Research how the play was originally staged and discuss the playwright's intentions.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Basic steps* (40 min): Learn grapevine, step-touch, pivot turn and box step.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Emotional changes* (25 min): List emotional changes like mood swings and strategies to manage them.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Body sections 2–3* (75 min): Draft remaining body sections, adding a chart, image or diagram with caption.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Solving inequalities* (60 min): Solve 10 inequalities like 5x - 3 ≥ 22 and -2x + 4 < 10 and graph the solutions.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Grand Canyon* (50 min): Analyze a Grand Canyon cross-section and order the layers and events from oldest to youngest.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Michelangelo and Raphael* (45 min): Analyze the Sistine Chapel and The School of Athens for classical and humanist themes.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Possessives* (45 min): Learn mi/tu/su/nuestro/vuestro and their plurals and complete a 20-item practice.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Character analysis* (40 min): Write a character profile with objectives and subtext for your role.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Line dance 1* (40 min): Learn and perform the 'Cupid Shuffle' or 'Electric Slide'.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Social changes* (25 min): Discuss changing friendships and independence.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Conclusion & works cited* (75 min): Draft a conclusion and format the Works Cited page.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Inequalities in context* (60 min): Solve a budget problem: earning $8 per hour with $40 saved, how many hours to have more than $150? Interpret the solution.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Index fossils* (50 min): Use index fossils to correlate layers between two cliff diagrams.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Literature* (45 min): Read an excerpt from Dante's Inferno or Machiavelli's The Prince and discuss its ideas.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Conversation* (45 min): Discuss your family with a parent: how many siblings, cousins, and pets, and who is older or younger.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Design plan* (40 min): Sketch set, costume and prop designs using household materials.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Line dance 2* (40 min): Learn and perform a second line dance and check heart rate afterward.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Timing* (25 min): Learn that puberty timing varies and why comparisons aren't helpful.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 125, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Self-check* (75 min): Highlight every citation and verify each matches a Works Cited entry.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on writing, solving and graphing inequalities.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on relative dating principles.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Review & check* (45 min): Create a Renaissance 'museum card' for one artwork explaining its significance.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a tener and possessives quiz.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Patterns* (40 min): Compare comic patterns in this play with a sitcom episode.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Perform a line dance for family without the video.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Journal on one change and a healthy way to handle it.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Drafting the research report
- Objective: Write an informative report integrating sources
- Objective: Use formatting and graphics
- Vocabulary: synthesis, in-text citation, works cited
- Materials: outline, word processor
- Assessment: Full research report draft.

### Mathematics 7: Inequalities
- Objective: Write and graph inequalities
- Objective: Solve and interpret px + q > r
- Vocabulary: inequality, solution set, open circle, closed circle
- Materials: number line
- Assessment: Inequality quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Rock layers and relative dating
- Objective: Apply superposition and cross-cutting relationships
- Objective: Sequence events from rock layers
- Vocabulary: strata, superposition, relative age, unconformity, index fossil
- Materials: colored play dough, clear jar, sand, soil, gravel
- Assessment: Strata-sequencing quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Renaissance art, science and literature
- Objective: Analyze Renaissance art techniques
- Objective: Describe achievements of key figures
- Vocabulary: perspective, fresco, realism, vernacular
- Materials: Smarthistory images, ruler, sketchbook
- Assessment: Perspective drawing and museum card.

### Spanish 1A: The verb tener and possessive adjectives
- Objective: Conjugate tener
- Objective: Use possessive adjectives and ages
- Vocabulary: tener, tengo, mi/mis, tu/tus, su/sus, nuestro/a, ¿Cuántos años tienes?, mayor/menor
- Materials: Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: Tener and possessives quiz (80%+).

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Script analysis and design
- Objective: Analyze a script for character and staging
- Objective: Plan technical elements
- Vocabulary: script, subtext, set design, props, costume
- Materials: short play script (e.g., a public-domain one-act or the Pyramus and Thisbe scene)
- Assessment: Character profile and design sketches.

### Physical Education 7: Line dances and rhythm
- Objective: Perform line dances to music
- Objective: Count music in 8s
- Vocabulary: tempo, 8-count, grapevine, line dance
- Materials: music player, dance videos
- Assessment: Line dance performance.

### Health Education 7: Adolescent changes
- Objective: Describe changes during adolescence
- Objective: Normalize differences in timing
- Vocabulary: adolescence, puberty, growth spurt
- Materials: KidsHealth articles
- Assessment: Journal.
