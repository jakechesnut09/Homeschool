## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Text A* (75 min): Read an article arguing zoos protect endangered species and highlight evidence types (statistics, expert, anecdote).
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Lots of fractions* (60 min): Use tape diagrams to show 1/4 more and 1/4 less than a quantity and connect to 125% and 75%.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Nutrient tests* (50 min): Test foods for starch with iodine and for fat with brown paper and record results.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Griots* (45 min): Listen to a recorded griot performance with kora and explain the griot's role as historian.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *También/tampoco* (45 min): Learn a mí también, a mí tampoco, a mí sí/no and practice reacting to 15 statements read by a parent.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Dynamics and tempo* (40 min): Learn Italian dynamic and tempo terms and mark them while listening to a piece.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Grips and serve* (40 min): Learn forehand and backhand grips and practice short and long serves.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Leading causes* (25 min): Analyze CDC data on leading causes of teen injury.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Text B* (75 min): Read an article arguing zoos harm animals and highlight its evidence the same way.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Percent increase* (60 min): Compute new prices after increases of 10%, 15% and 120% using multiplication by 1.10, 1.15 and 2.20.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Nutrition labels* (50 min): Analyze five nutrition labels to compare calories from carbohydrates, fats and proteins.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Proverbs* (45 min): Interpret 8 West African proverbs and write two of your own family proverbs.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Le gusta* (45 min): Use a él/a ella le gusta to report what three relatives like; write a paragraph about each.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Rondo* (40 min): Map the form of 'Für Elise' (ABACA) with colored blocks.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Clear* (40 min): Practice overhead clears to the back of the court.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Home safety audit* (25 min): Complete a home safety audit for falls, fire and poisoning.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 63, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Compare authors* (75 min): Write a comparison paragraph on how each author emphasizes different evidence and facts.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Percent decrease* (60 min): Compute sale prices after discounts and find the original price given the sale price.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Energy in a peanut (demo)* (50 min): With an adult, burn a cheese puff under a can of water and calculate the temperature change as evidence of stored energy.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Oral history project* (45 min): Interview an older relative about family history and retell it as a griot would.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Negatives* (45 min): Practice no...nada, ni...ni, and tampoco in 10 written sentences.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Variations* (40 min): Listen to Mozart's 'Twelve Variations on Ah vous dirai-je, Maman' and describe how each variation changes.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Drop shot* (40 min): Practice drop shots and discuss when to use them.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Sports safety* (25 min): Check helmet fit and learn about concussion signs.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Paired texts: same topic, different authors
- Objective: Compare how two authors present the same topic
- Objective: Gather evidence for an argument
- Vocabulary: perspective, emphasis, interpretation
- Materials: two articles on zoos (pro and con)
- Assessment: Comparison paragraph and evidence organizer.

### Mathematics 7: Percent increase and decrease
- Objective: Represent percent change with tape diagrams and equations
- Objective: Compute percent increase and decrease
- Vocabulary: percent increase, percent decrease, tape diagram
- Materials: store ads or flyers
- Assessment: Percent change quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Food, energy and the body
- Objective: Explain food as fuel and building material
- Objective: Measure energy in food
- Vocabulary: calorie, carbohydrate, protein, fat, digestion
- Materials: nutrition labels, iodine, bread, potato, brown paper bag
- Assessment: Food-testing results and written explanation.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Oral traditions and griots
- Objective: Explain the importance of oral and written traditions
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: griot, oral tradition, proverb, manuscript
- Materials: recorded griot performance, kora music clip
- Assessment: Unit 4 test and oral history performance.

### Spanish 1A: A mí también, a mí tampoco: agreeing, disagreeing, and le gusta
- Objective: Agree and disagree with others' likes
- Objective: Report others' likes with le gusta
- Vocabulary: a mí también, a mí tampoco, a mí no, a mí sí, le gusta, ni...ni, tampoco
- Materials: survey sheet
- Assessment: Interpersonal conversation rubric and writing check.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Musical form and expression
- Objective: Identify forms (AB, ABA, rondo, theme and variations)
- Objective: Read dynamics and tempo markings
- Vocabulary: form, rondo, theme and variations, dynamics, tempo, articulation
- Materials: recordings of Mozart 'Twinkle' variations, Beethoven 'Für Elise'
- Assessment: Form maps and quiz.

### Physical Education 7: Badminton
- Objective: Use forehand and backhand grips
- Objective: Perform clears, drops and serves
- Vocabulary: clear, drop shot, smash, shuttlecock
- Materials: badminton rackets, shuttlecocks, net or rope
- Assessment: Rally test and game strategy observation.

### Health Education 7: Injury prevention
- Objective: Identify common injuries and causes
- Objective: Plan prevention strategies
- Vocabulary: unintentional injury, risk, protective gear
- Materials: home safety checklist, helmet
- Assessment: Home safety audit and plan.
