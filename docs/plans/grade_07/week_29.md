## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 7* (75 min): Read Chapter 7 and analyze Papa's story about the fig tree as a symbol.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Angles on a line* (60 min): Measure angles formed by intersecting lines with a protractor and record which pairs sum to 180 or are equal.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Plate boundaries* (50 min): Model convergent, divergent and transform boundaries with graham crackers on frosting.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Council of Trent* (45 min): Read summaries of the Council of Trent's reforms and chart what changed and what stayed.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Places* (45 min): Learn 16 places in a town and label a hand-drawn map of your neighborhood.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Plan* (40 min): Plan a 6–8 photo essay about a day in your family or neighborhood.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Sprint drills* (40 min): Practice A-skips, B-skips and high knees for sprint form.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Values* (25 min): Rank 20 values cards and identify your top five.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 142, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 8* (75 min): Read Chapter 8 and explain Cassie's plan against Lillian Jean and what it reveals.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Complementary and supplementary* (60 min): Find unknown complements and supplements and write equations like x + 47 = 90.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Earthquake map* (50 min): Plot recent earthquakes from the USGS map and identify plate boundaries from the patterns.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Jesuits* (45 min): Research Ignatius of Loyola and Jesuit schools and missions to Asia and the Americas.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Map questions* (45 min): Answer ¿Dónde está la biblioteca? questions using prepositions on your map.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Shoot* (40 min): Take photos for the essay, asking permission before photographing people.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Starts* (40 min): Practice standing and three-point starts over 20 meters.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Identity map* (25 min): Create an identity map with interests, culture and goals.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 143, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 9* (75 min): Read Chapter 9 and track the boycott and its consequences.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Vertical angles* (60 min): Build intersecting lines with paper strips and a brad to show vertical angles stay equal as you rotate.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *San Andreas Fault* (50 min): Study how the Pacific and North American plates slide along the San Andreas and calculate how far LA moves in 1,000 years at 5 cm/yr.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Missions* (45 min): Analyze Calisphere images of early California missions and discuss impacts on Native Californians.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *La plaza* (45 min): Read about the central plaza in Spanish and Latin American towns and compare it with a California downtown.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Edit* (40 min): Crop and adjust exposure in a free photo editor.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Timed 50m* (40 min): Run and time three 50-meter sprints.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Family roles* (25 min): Discuss family responsibilities and how they change in adolescence.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 144, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Theme tracking* (75 min): Add evidence to a theme tracker for 'family strength' and 'standing up to injustice'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Angle equations* (60 min): Solve 10 problems like (2x + 10) + 3x = 180 from diagrams.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Mountains and volcanoes* (50 min): Explain the formation of the Sierra Nevada and Mount Shasta in terms of plate motions.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Unit review* (45 min): Review Renaissance and Reformation with a timeline sorting game.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Virtual tour* (45 min): Use Google Maps Street View to tour a plaza in Oaxaca or Salamanca and write 8 sentences about what is there.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Roll of Thunder, chapters 7–9
- Objective: Trace themes of family and justice
- Objective: Cite evidence from multiple chapters
- Vocabulary: theme, symbol, injustice
- Materials: Roll of Thunder, Hear My Cry
- Assessment: Theme tracker and quiz.

### Mathematics 7: Angle relationships
- Objective: Identify complementary, supplementary, vertical and adjacent angles
- Objective: Write and solve equations for unknown angles
- Vocabulary: complementary, supplementary, vertical angles, adjacent
- Materials: protractor, paper strips, brads
- Assessment: Angle relationships quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Plate tectonics and California
- Objective: Explain plate boundary types
- Objective: Connect plate motion to California earthquakes and mountains
- Vocabulary: convergent, divergent, transform, fault, San Andreas
- Materials: graham crackers, frosting, USGS earthquake map
- Assessment: Plotted earthquake map and quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Counter-Reformation and missionaries
- Objective: Explain the Counter-Reformation
- Objective: Analyze missionary impact
- Vocabulary: Council of Trent, Jesuits, Inquisition, missionary
- Materials: Council of Trent excerpts, California missions images (Calisphere)
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Spanish 1A: Lugares en la ciudad
- Objective: Name places in a town
- Objective: Ask and say where places are
- Vocabulary: la biblioteca, el parque, el centro comercial, la iglesia, la plaza, el cine, el gimnasio, la playa
- Materials: town map, Google Maps
- Assessment: Places vocabulary and map quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Photo essay and digital ethics
- Objective: Tell a story with photographs
- Objective: Practice ethical sharing online
- Vocabulary: photo essay, consent, copyright, Creative Commons
- Materials: camera, free photo editor
- Assessment: Photo essay.

### Physical Education 7: Track: sprints and starts
- Objective: Use proper sprint mechanics
- Objective: Practice starts
- Vocabulary: drive phase, arm swing, stride, start
- Materials: track or open field, stopwatch, cones
- Assessment: 50m times and video analysis.

### Health Education 7: Identity and family
- Objective: Explore personal identity and values
- Objective: Strengthen family communication
- Vocabulary: identity, values, family roles
- Materials: values cards
- Assessment: Identity map and reflection.
