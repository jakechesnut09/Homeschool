# Visual & Performing Arts 7 (Grade 7)

A rotating arts course covering visual art (drawing, color, printmaking, sculpture), music (notation, listening, ukulele or keyboard performance, composition), theatre (improvisation, scene work, a short play) and media arts (photography and stop-motion animation). Projects connect to the medieval and early-modern cultures studied in history, and students keep a sketchbook/process journal and finish with a family exhibition and performance.

*Framework:* 2019 California Arts Standards for Public Schools (Visual Arts, Music, Theatre, Media Arts); 2020 CA Arts Framework  
*Time:* 40 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Drawing Foundations (weeks 1–5)
Careful observation, value and perspective let artists create the illusion of form and space.

- Week 1: Sketchbook habits and contour drawing
- Week 2: Value and shading
- Week 3: Linear perspective
- Week 4: Proportion and portrait drawing
- Week 5: Still life composition and artist statement

### Unit 2: Color and Painting (weeks 6–9)
Color relationships and composition communicate mood and meaning.

- Week 6: Color theory
- Week 7: Color and mood
- Week 8: Landscape painting
- Week 9: Painting critique

### Unit 3: Music Literacy and Listening (weeks 10–14)
Reading notation and listening analytically reveal how composers structure music.

- Week 10: Rhythm reading
- Week 11: Pitch, staff and keyboard
- Week 12: Listening: medieval to Renaissance music
- Week 13: Musical form and expression
- Week 14: Chords and harmony

### Unit 4: Music Performance and Composition (weeks 15–18)
Musicians rehearse with criteria and compose with unity and variety.

- Week 15: Instrument skills (ukulele or keyboard)
- Week 16: Composition: melody
- Week 17: Composition: arranging
- Week 18: Winter recital

### Unit 5: Printmaking and Art Across Cultures (weeks 19–22)
Artists across times and cultures—Islamic, Japanese, medieval European—use pattern, line and printmaking to share ideas.

- Week 19: Islamic geometric pattern art
- Week 20: Relief printmaking
- Week 21: Illuminated manuscripts and calligraphy
- Week 22: Mesoamerican and African art connections

### Unit 6: Theatre: From Improv to Performance (weeks 23–27)
Actors use voice, body and focused rehearsal to bring scripts to life for an audience.

- Week 23: Theatre games and improvisation
- Week 24: Voice and movement for actors
- Week 25: Script analysis and design
- Week 26: Rehearsal process
- Week 27: Performance and theatre critique

### Unit 7: Media Arts: Photography and Animation (weeks 28–31)
Media artists plan, capture and edit images and motion to tell stories responsibly.

- Week 28: Photography composition
- Week 29: Photo essay and digital ethics
- Week 30: Stop-motion animation planning
- Week 31: Stop-motion production

### Unit 8: Sculpture and Exhibition (weeks 32–36)
Three-dimensional form and thoughtful presentation complete an artist's year of work.

- Week 32: Additive sculpture
- Week 33: Subtractive sculpture and finishing
- Week 34: Curating a portfolio
- Week 35: Exhibition and showcase
- Week 36: Year reflection

## Standards

- **MA:Cn10.1.7** Access, evaluate and integrate personal and external resources to inform the creation of original media artworks.
- **MA:Cr1.1.7** Generate ideas, goals and solutions for original media artworks through application of chosen generative methods.
- **MA:Cr2.1.7** Design, propose and evaluate artistic ideas, plans, prototypes and production processes for media arts productions.
- **MA:Cr3.1.7** Coordinate production processes to integrate content and stylistic conventions for determined purpose and meaning in media arts productions.
- **MA:Pr5.1.7** Exhibit an increasing set of artistic, design, technical and soft skills through performing various roles in producing media artworks.
- **MA:Pr6.1.7** Evaluate various presentation formats in order to fulfill various tasks and defined processes in the presentation and distribution of media artworks.
- **MA:Re7.1.7** Describe, compare and analyze the qualities of and relationships between the components and style in media artworks.
- **MU:Cn11.0.7** Demonstrate understanding of relationships between music and the other arts, other disciplines, varied contexts and daily life.
- **MU:Cr1.1.7** Generate rhythmic, melodic and harmonic phrases and variations over harmonic accompaniments within expanded forms.
- **MU:Cr2.1.7** Select, organize and document personal musical ideas for arrangements, songs and compositions within expanded forms.
- **MU:Cr3.1.7** Evaluate their own work, applying selected criteria such as appropriate application of elements of music, including style, form and use of sound sources.
- **MU:Cr3.2.7** Present the final version of their documented personal composition, song or arrangement, demonstrating unity and variety.
- **MU:Pr4.2.7** When analyzing selected music, read and identify by name or function standard symbols for rhythm, pitch, articulation, dynamics, tempo and form.
- **MU:Pr4.3.7** Perform contrasting pieces of music, demonstrating as well as explaining how the music's intent is conveyed by interpretation of the elements of music.
- **MU:Pr5.1.7** Identify and apply collaboratively developed criteria to rehearse, refine and determine when a piece is ready to perform.
- **MU:Pr6.1.7** Perform music with technical accuracy and stylistic expression to convey the creator's intent.
- **MU:Re7.2.7** Classify and explain how the elements of music and expressive qualities relate to the structure of contrasting pieces.
- **MU:Re8.1.7** Describe a personal interpretation of contrasting works and explain how creators' and performers' application of the elements of music convey expressive intent.
- **MU:Re9.1.7** Select from teacher-provided criteria to evaluate musical works or performances.
- **TH:Cn11.2.7** Research and discuss how a playwright might have intended a drama/theatre work to be produced.
- **TH:Cr1.1.7** Investigate multiple perspectives and solutions to staging problems in a drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Cr2.1.7** Examine and justify original ideas and artistic choices in a drama/theatre work based on critical analysis, background knowledge and historical and cultural context.
- **TH:Cr3.1.7** Demonstrate focus and concentration in the rehearsal process to analyze and refine choices in a devised or scripted drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Pr4.1.7** Consider multiple planned technical elements during the rehearsal process for a devised or scripted drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Pr5.1.7** Participate in a variety of acting exercises and techniques that can be applied in a rehearsal or drama/theatre performance.
- **TH:Pr6.1.7** Participate in rehearsals for a drama/theatre work that will be shared with an audience.
- **TH:Re7.1.7** Compare recurring patterns in drama/theatre works.
- **TH:Re8.1.7** Identify the artistic choices made in a drama/theatre work through participation and observation.
- **TH:Re9.1.7** Explain preferences, using supporting evidence and criteria, to evaluate drama/theatre work.
- **VA:Cn10.1.7** Individually or collaboratively create art that reflects ideas and values from various cultures or time periods.
- **VA:Cn11.1.7** Analyze how response to art is influenced by understanding the time and place in which it was created.
- **VA:Cr1.1.7** Apply methods to overcome creative blocks.
- **VA:Cr1.2.7** Develop criteria to guide making a work of art or design to meet an identified goal.
- **VA:Cr2.1.7** Demonstrate persistence in developing skills with various materials, methods and approaches in creating works of art or design.
- **VA:Cr2.2.7** Demonstrate awareness of ethical responsibility to oneself and others when posting and sharing content online.
- **VA:Cr2.3.7** Apply visual organizational strategies to design and produce a work of art, design or media that clearly communicates information or ideas.
- **VA:Cr3.1.7** Reflect on and explain important information about personal artwork in an artist statement or another format.
- **VA:Pr4.1.7** Compare and contrast how technologies have changed the way artwork is preserved, presented and experienced.
- **VA:Pr5.1.7** Based on criteria, analyze and evaluate methods for preparing and presenting art.
- **VA:Pr6.1.7** Compare and contrast viewing and experiencing collections and exhibitions in different venues.
- **VA:Re7.1.7** Explain how the method of display, the location and the experience of an artwork influence how it is perceived and valued.
- **VA:Re7.2.7** Analyze multiple ways that images influence specific audiences.
- **VA:Re8.1.7** Interpret art by analyzing art-making approaches, the characteristics of form and structure, relevant contextual information, subject matter and use of media to identify ideas and mood conveyed.
- **VA:Re9.1.7** Compare and explain the difference between an evaluation of an artwork based on personal criteria and one based on a set of established criteria.
