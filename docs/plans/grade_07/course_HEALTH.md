# Health Education 7 (Grade 7)

A skills-based health course for early adolescence covering nutrition and physical activity, mental, emotional and social health, injury prevention and safety (including online safety), alcohol, tobacco/vaping and other drugs, growth and development, and personal and community health. Each unit pairs essential concepts with the eight CA health skills—analyzing influences, accessing valid information, communication, decision making, goal setting, practicing healthy behaviors and advocacy. Sensitive topics in the growth and development unit are designed for parent-led discussion consistent with family values.

*Framework:* 2008 California Health Education Content Standards (Grades 7–8); 2019 CA Health Education Framework  
*Time:* 25 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Nutrition and Physical Activity (weeks 1–6)
Balanced eating and regular activity fuel a growing body; media and marketing influence choices.

- Week 1: Wellness and the six nutrients
- Week 2: MyPlate and reading labels
- Week 3: Influences on food choices
- Week 4: Valid nutrition information and fad diets
- Week 5: Physical activity and food safety
- Week 6: Nutrition goal and unit assessment

### Unit 2: Mental, Emotional and Social Health (weeks 7–12)
Managing stress, building healthy relationships and knowing when to seek help support well-being.

- Week 7: Emotions and mental health
- Week 8: Stress and coping
- Week 9: Depression, anxiety and getting help
- Week 10: Healthy relationships and communication
- Week 11: Bullying, cyberbullying and conflict resolution
- Week 12: Promoting positive climate; unit assessment

### Unit 3: Injury Prevention and Safety (weeks 13–17)
Preparation and good decisions prevent injuries and keep people safe online and offline.

- Week 13: Injury prevention
- Week 14: Online safety and digital citizenship
- Week 15: Violence prevention
- Week 16: Emergency preparedness
- Week 17: First aid and unit assessment

### Unit 4: Alcohol, Tobacco, Vaping and Other Drugs (weeks 18–24)
Substances change the developing brain; knowing the facts and refusal skills supports drug-free choices.

- Week 18: Drugs and the brain
- Week 19: Tobacco and vaping
- Week 20: Alcohol
- Week 21: Cannabis and other drugs
- Week 22: Medicine safety and fentanyl awareness
- Week 23: Refusal skills
- Week 24: Drug-free advocacy and unit assessment

### Unit 5: Growth and Development (weeks 25–30)
Adolescence brings physical, emotional and social changes; sleep, hygiene, boundaries and trusted adults support healthy growth.

- Week 25: Adolescent changes
- Week 26: Hygiene and sleep
- Week 27: Body systems and reproductive health (parent-led)
- Week 28: Boundaries and consent
- Week 29: Identity and family
- Week 30: Unit 5 review and assessment

### Unit 6: Personal and Community Health (weeks 31–36)
Preventing disease, using health services and advocating for a healthy environment protect individuals and communities.

- Week 31: Communicable diseases
- Week 32: Noncommunicable diseases
- Week 33: Health care and checkups
- Week 34: Environmental health
- Week 35: Community health advocacy project
- Week 36: Year review and wellness plan

## Standards

- **H-7.1.1.A** Describe the short- and long-term effects of alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarettes, cannabis and other drugs on the body and brain.
- **H-7.1.1.G** Describe physical, social, mental and emotional changes during adolescence and puberty.
- **H-7.1.1.M** Describe characteristics of mental and emotional health and the relationship among stress, emotions and physical health.
- **H-7.1.1.N** Identify the six major nutrients and their functions; describe how to use MyPlate and nutrition labels.
- **H-7.1.1.P** Explain how communicable and noncommunicable diseases are caused, transmitted and prevented.
- **H-7.1.2.S** Explain the causes of common injuries and ways to prevent them at home, outdoors and in sports.
- **H-7.1.3.A** Explain the addictive nature of nicotine and other substances and the process of dependency.
- **H-7.1.3.M** Identify signs of depression, anxiety and suicide risk and describe ways to get help from trusted adults.
- **H-7.1.3.P** Describe the importance of regular medical and dental checkups, vaccinations and health screenings.
- **H-7.1.4.G** Explain the structure and function of the reproductive systems and the role of hormones (parent-led).
- **H-7.1.4.N** Describe the benefits of and barriers to physical activity and healthy eating, including the relationship among diet, activity and healthy weight.
- **H-7.1.5.S** Describe safe and responsible use of the internet and social media, including privacy and dealing with online predators.
- **H-7.1.6.A** Describe safe use and storage of prescription and over-the-counter medicines, including risks of misuse such as fentanyl-laced pills.
- **H-7.1.6.M** Describe characteristics of healthy and unhealthy relationships, including bullying and cyberbullying.
- **H-7.1.6.P** Explain how environmental factors such as air and water quality affect personal and community health.
- **H-7.1.7.G** Describe the importance of sleep, hygiene and personal responsibility for health during adolescence.
- **H-7.1.7.S** Describe emergency preparedness for earthquakes, wildfires and other disasters in California.
- **H-7.2.1.A** Analyze how marketing, media, peers and family influence decisions about alcohol, tobacco and other drugs.
- **H-7.2.1.N** Analyze internal and external influences (media, culture, peers, advertising) on food choices and physical activity.
- **H-7.3.1.G** Identify trusted adults and valid resources for questions about growth and development.
- **H-7.3.1.N** Access and evaluate valid nutrition information, including identifying fad diets and false claims.
- **H-7.3.1.P** Access valid health information and health services in the community.
- **H-7.4.1.G** Demonstrate communication skills to set personal boundaries and respect the boundaries of others.
- **H-7.4.1.M** Use effective verbal and nonverbal communication, active listening and I-messages to resolve conflicts.
- **H-7.4.2.A** Demonstrate refusal skills to avoid alcohol, tobacco, vaping and drug use.
- **H-7.5.1.M** Use a decision-making process to address social and emotional situations.
- **H-7.5.1.S** Use a decision-making process to evaluate risky situations, including violence, weapons and gang involvement.
- **H-7.6.1.N** Set a personal goal to improve dietary practices and physical activity and track progress.
- **H-7.7.1.M** Practice healthy strategies to manage stress, anger and difficult emotions.
- **H-7.7.1.N** Practice healthy eating, including planning balanced meals and snacks and proper food safety.
- **H-7.7.1.S** Demonstrate basic first aid and how to respond to emergencies, including calling 911.
- **H-7.8.1.A** Advocate for a tobacco- and drug-free lifestyle and community.
- **H-7.8.1.M** Encourage others to seek help and promote a positive, inclusive school and community climate.
- **H-7.8.1.P** Advocate for a personal, family or community health issue such as clean air, recycling or disease prevention.
