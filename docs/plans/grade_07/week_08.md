## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 5* (75 min): Read Chapter 5 'The Toil of Trace and Trail' and list choices Hal, Charles and Mercedes make that show ignorance.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Graphing tables* (60 min): Graph three tables on the coordinate plane and identify which form straight lines through the origin.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Open system* (50 min): Mass baking soda and vinegar before and after reacting in an open cup and calculate the apparent mass loss.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Geography and reunification* (45 min): Map China's rivers, the Grand Canal and Silk Road and explain how the Sui and Tang reunified China.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Subject pronouns* (45 min): Learn the subject pronouns (including vosotros in Spain and ustedes in Latin America) and assign the correct pronoun to 20 pictures of people.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Underpainting* (40 min): Block in background, middle ground and foreground with a thin underpainting.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Backhand* (40 min): Practice backhand throws to a partner at 5, 10 and 15 meters.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Stress response* (25 min): Learn how the body responds to stress and list personal stressors.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Foils* (75 min): Compare Hal's treatment of the dogs with John Thornton's in a Venn diagram and explain how they act as foils.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Meaning of points* (60 min): Explain what (0,0), (1,k) and (5,y) mean on a graph of distance versus time for a cyclist.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Closed system* (50 min): Repeat the reaction in a bottle sealed with a balloon and compare before-and-after masses.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Spread of Buddhism* (45 min): Trace Buddhism's spread from India through China to Korea and Japan on a map.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Ser* (45 min): Learn soy, eres, es, somos, sois, son; complete a Conjuguemos ser drill until you score 90%.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Background* (40 min): Paint the sky and distant areas lighter and cooler for atmospheric perspective.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Forehand* (40 min): Learn the forehand (flick) grip and throw 30 times.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Breathing* (25 min): Practice box breathing and progressive muscle relaxation.

- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Catching* (40 min): Practice pancake and rim catches at different heights.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: The Call of the Wild, chapters 5–6
- Objective: Analyze how characters contrast
- Objective: Distinguish connotation of similar words
- Vocabulary: connotation, denotation, foil
- Materials: The Call of the Wild
- Assessment: Venn diagram, quiz and discussion.

### Mathematics 7: Graphs of proportional relationships
- Objective: Recognize proportional graphs
- Objective: Interpret (0,0) and (1,k) on a graph
- Vocabulary: origin, coordinate plane, slope (informal)
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Graph-interpretation quiz and card-match accuracy.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Conservation of mass
- Objective: Collect evidence that mass is conserved
- Objective: Explain open vs. closed systems
- Vocabulary: conservation of mass, closed system, open system
- Materials: kitchen scale, plastic bottle, balloon, baking soda, vinegar, Alka-Seltzer
- Assessment: Conservation of mass lab report.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Tang and Song China
- Objective: Describe reunification and the spread of Buddhism
- Objective: Explain Tang-Song innovations
- Vocabulary: dynasty, Buddhism, Grand Canal, porcelain, woodblock printing
- Materials: map of China, potato or foam stamp
- Assessment: Invention chart and quiz.

### Spanish 1A: Subject pronouns and the verb ser
- Objective: Use subject pronouns
- Objective: Conjugate ser in the present
- Vocabulary: yo, tú, él/ella/usted, nosotros, vosotros, ellos/ustedes, ser, soy/eres/es
- Materials: pronoun chart, Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: Ser and subject pronoun quiz (80%+).

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Landscape painting
- Objective: Create depth with atmospheric perspective
- Objective: Apply a chosen color scheme
- Vocabulary: foreground, middle ground, background, atmospheric perspective
- Materials: paint, canvas board or heavy paper
- Assessment: Landscape painting progress.

### Physical Education 7: Ultimate frisbee: throwing and catching
- Objective: Throw backhand and forehand
- Objective: Catch with pancake and rim catches
- Vocabulary: backhand, forehand, pancake catch, pivot
- Materials: flying disc, cones
- Assessment: Throwing accuracy test.

### Health Education 7: Stress and coping
- Objective: Explain the stress response
- Objective: Practice coping strategies
- Vocabulary: stress, stressor, fight-or-flight, coping
- Materials: breathing app or timer
- Assessment: Stress plan.
