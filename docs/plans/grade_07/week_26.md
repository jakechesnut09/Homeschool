## Day 1 — Monday (school day 126, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Revision* (75 min): Revise the report for clarity, precise vocabulary and smooth integration of quotes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Subtraction in expressions* (60 min): Rewrite expressions with subtraction as adding the opposite and evaluate to test equivalence.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Toilet-paper timeline* (50 min): Build a 46-meter (or 46-square) timeline of Earth's history and mark major events to scale.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Gutenberg* (45 min): Read about Gutenberg's press and compare the time to copy a book by hand vs. by press.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Expressions* (45 min): Learn tener hambre, sed, frío, calor, sueño, miedo, prisa, razón and caption 10 pictures.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Read-through* (40 min): Hold a read-through with family members playing roles.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Mexican folk dance* (40 min): Learn basic steps of a Mexican folk dance like 'La Raspa'.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Hygiene* (25 min): Create a daily hygiene routine (skin, hair, teeth, deodorant).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 127, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Final edit* (75 min): Edit and publish the final 1,000–1,200-word report.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Distributive property* (60 min): Expand and factor expressions like 6(2x - 5) and 12x + 18 using area models.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Reflecting on scale* (50 min): Calculate what fraction of the timeline includes humans and write a reflection.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Movable type* (45 min): Use letter stamps to print a short message and reflect on the process.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Tener que* (45 min): Write a to-do list using tener que + infinitive for each family member this week.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Blocking* (40 min): Block the scene and write blocking notes in the script.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Israeli/European folk dance* (40 min): Learn 'Hora' or 'Virginia Reel' formations.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Acne* (25 min): Learn causes of acne and myths vs. facts.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 128, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Build presentation* (75 min): Create a 6–8 slide presentation with images and minimal text emphasizing key findings.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Combining like terms* (60 min): Simplify expressions such as 3x - 4 - 7x + 2.5 and check by substituting values.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Absolute dating* (50 min): Model radioactive half-life with 100 pennies flipped and removed each round, and graph the decay.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Spread of ideas* (45 min): Map printing presses in Europe by 1500 and explain effects on literacy and vernacular texts.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Celebrations* (45 min): Read about the quinceañera and the cumpleaños tradition of las mañanitas; listen to Las mañanitas and write down five words you hear.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Memorize* (40 min): Work toward off-book with cue-line drills.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *West African dance* (40 min): Follow a West African dance video and learn about its connection to drumming.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Sleep needs* (25 min): Learn teens need 8–10 hours and start a one-week sleep log.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 129, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Present* (75 min): Deliver a 5-minute presentation and answer two audience questions.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Rewriting for meaning* (60 min): Explain why 'increase by 5%' can be written as a + 0.05a or 1.05a and solve 4 similar problems.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Eras and extinctions* (50 min): Read about the five mass extinctions and label them on the timeline.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Northern artists and Shakespeare* (45 min): Compare Dürer and van Eyck with Italian artists and preview Shakespeare's place in the Renaissance.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Mi fiesta* (45 min): Describe your last family party in present-tense sentences: who is there, what they eat, what they have.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Run-through* (40 min): Run the full scene with props and give notes.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Compare* (40 min): Compare formations and purposes of the three dances.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Screens and sleep* (25 min): Analyze how screens affect sleep and set a screen curfew.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 130, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Reflection & check* (75 min): Write a reflection on the research process and listen to a TED-Ed talk, summarizing its main ideas and supporting details.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on equivalent expressions.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write an explanation of how rock strata and fossils were used to build the geologic time scale.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a cause-effect paragraph on the printing press.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a tener expressions quiz with a listening section.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Tech* (40 min): Add costumes, sound effects or lighting cues and run again.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Perform one folk dance with family members.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Evaluate the sleep log and set a sleep goal.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Research revision and multimedia presentation
- Objective: Revise and publish research
- Objective: Present findings with multimedia
- Vocabulary: slide design, salient point
- Materials: Google Slides or poster board
- Assessment: Research report and presentation rubrics.

### Mathematics 7: Equivalent expressions
- Objective: Use the distributive property
- Objective: Combine like terms
- Vocabulary: term, like terms, equivalent expressions, factor
- Materials: area model templates
- Assessment: Equivalent expressions quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): The geologic time scale
- Objective: Construct a scaled model of geologic time
- Objective: Explain how time scale is organized by major events
- Vocabulary: eon, era, period, mass extinction, radiometric dating
- Materials: roll of receipt paper or toilet paper, meter stick, markers
- Assessment: Timeline project and written explanation.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Printing press and Northern Renaissance
- Objective: Explain the effects of the printing press
- Objective: Describe the Northern Renaissance
- Vocabulary: Gutenberg, movable type, literacy, Erasmus
- Materials: letter stamps or potato stamps, ink pad
- Assessment: Cause-effect paragraph.

### Spanish 1A: Tener expressions and celebrations
- Objective: Use idiomatic tener expressions
- Objective: Describe a family celebration
- Vocabulary: tener hambre, tener sed, tener frío, tener calor, tener sueño, tener que, la fiesta, el pastel
- Materials: party photos
- Assessment: Tener expressions quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Rehearsal process
- Objective: Rehearse with focus
- Objective: Refine choices
- Vocabulary: read-through, run-through, cue, off-book
- Materials: script, props, costumes
- Assessment: Rehearsal journal.

### Physical Education 7: Cultural and folk dances
- Objective: Perform folk dances from different cultures
- Objective: Explain their cultural context
- Vocabulary: folk dance, partner dance, formation
- Materials: music, dance videos
- Assessment: Folk dance performance.

### Health Education 7: Hygiene and sleep
- Objective: Practice adolescent hygiene
- Objective: Improve sleep habits
- Vocabulary: hygiene, acne, circadian rhythm, sleep hygiene
- Materials: sleep log
- Assessment: Sleep log and goal.
