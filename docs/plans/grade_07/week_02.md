## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Ship-Trap Island* (75 min): Read the first third of 'The Most Dangerous Game' and list details of setting that create a sense of danger.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Finding scale factors* (60 min): Find the scale factor between pairs of figures, including factors less than 1, and use them to compute missing side lengths.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Element hunt* (50 min): Find 10 elements in household items (aluminum foil, copper wire, carbon pencil lead) and locate them on a periodic table.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Internal problems* (45 min): Read about political corruption, inflation and reliance on mercenaries and sort them into political, economic and military causes.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *¿Cómo estás?* (45 min): Learn bien, muy bien, regular, mal, así así and practice responding to ¿Cómo estás? with emoji cards drawn at random.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Value scale* (40 min): Create a 9-step value scale with pencil and a second using hatching and cross-hatching.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Curl-up form* (40 min): Practice cadence curl-ups with correct form, then test to maximum.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *MyPlate* (25 min): Explore MyPlate.gov and get a personalized MyPlate Plan for your age and activity level.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Rising action* (75 min): Read the middle section and chart Rainsford's three tricks on a plot diagram, noting foreshadowing clues.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Reversing a scale* (60 min): Scale a triangle up by 4 then back down by 1/4 and explain why the reciprocal undoes the scaling.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Reading the table* (50 min): Identify atomic number, symbol and mass for 15 elements and color-code metals, nonmetals and metalloids.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Invasions* (45 min): Map the routes of the Huns, Visigoths and Vandals and the sack of Rome in 410 CE.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Tú vs. usted* (45 min): Sort 12 people (best friend, grandmother, doctor, teacher, cousin) into tú or usted and write the matching greeting for each.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Shading spheres* (40 min): Draw and shade three spheres with highlight, core shadow, reflected light and cast shadow.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Push-up form* (40 min): Practice 90-degree push-ups at cadence, then test to maximum.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Label reading* (25 min): Compare labels of two cereals on serving size, added sugar and fiber.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Climax and ending* (75 min): Finish the story and write a paragraph explaining how the jungle setting drives the conflict, with three cited details.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Scaling area* (60 min): Build squares and triangles from pattern blocks at scale factors 2 and 3, count pieces, and record that area grows by 4 and 9.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Mixtures lab* (50 min): Separate a mixture of salt, sand and paper-clip bits using a magnet, filtering and evaporation.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Primary source: Jerome* (45 min): Read St. Jerome's letter reacting to the sack of Rome and analyze his point of view and tone.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Listening* (45 min): Watch a Spanish Proficiency Exercises (UT Austin) greeting clip twice and note each speaker's name and how they feel.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Egg study* (40 min): Light an egg with a lamp and draw it realistically for 25 minutes.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Trunk lift & sit-and-reach* (40 min): Test trunk lift and back-saver sit-and-reach and record results.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Sugar math* (25 min): Convert grams of sugar in five drinks to teaspoons and measure them out.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Suspense: 'The Most Dangerous Game' by Richard Connell
- Objective: Analyze how setting shapes conflict and plot
- Objective: Use Greek/Latin roots to define words
- Vocabulary: suspense, foreshadowing, external conflict, palpable, indolently
- Materials: 'The Most Dangerous Game' (public domain), plot diagram template
- Assessment: Plot diagram, setting paragraph, reading and root quizzes.

### Mathematics 7: Scale factor, distance and area
- Objective: Use scale factors to find unknown lengths
- Objective: Explain how area changes by the square of the scale factor
- Vocabulary: scale factor, reciprocal, area
- Materials: graph paper, pattern blocks or paper squares
- Assessment: Quiz and written explanation of why area scales by the square of the factor.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Elements and the periodic table
- Objective: Distinguish elements, compounds and mixtures
- Objective: Read basic periodic-table information
- Vocabulary: element, compound, mixture, periodic table, symbol
- Materials: periodic table printout, salt, sand, iron filings or paper clips, magnet
- Assessment: Separation lab write-up and quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Why did Rome fall?
- Objective: Analyze internal and external causes of Rome's decline
- Objective: Distinguish long- and short-term causes
- Vocabulary: inflation, mercenary, Visigoths, Huns, division
- Materials: cause-effect organizer, document set
- Assessment: Mini-DBQ essay.

### Spanish 1A: ¿Cómo estás? Feelings and formal vs. informal address
- Objective: Ask how someone is and respond
- Objective: Choose tú or usted appropriately
- Vocabulary: ¿Cómo estás?, ¿Cómo está usted?, bien, mal, regular, cansado/a, tú, usted
- Materials: emoji feeling cards, index cards
- Assessment: Quiz (80%+) and oral check-in rubric.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Value and shading
- Objective: Create a value scale
- Objective: Shade forms with light source
- Vocabulary: value, highlight, cast shadow, hatching, blending
- Materials: pencils, blending stump, egg or ball, lamp
- Assessment: Value scale and shaded egg study.

### Physical Education 7: Strength and flexibility baseline
- Objective: Complete curl-up, push-up, trunk lift and sit-and-reach tests
- Objective: Use correct form
- Vocabulary: muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility
- Materials: mat, measuring strip, sit-and-reach box or yardstick
- Assessment: Complete FITNESSGRAM baseline record.

### Health Education 7: MyPlate and reading labels
- Objective: Use MyPlate to plan meals
- Objective: Interpret nutrition facts labels
- Vocabulary: MyPlate, serving size, % Daily Value, added sugars
- Materials: MyPlate.gov, food labels
- Assessment: Day meal plan.
