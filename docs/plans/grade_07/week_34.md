## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Debate rehearsal* (75 min): Rehearse the opening statement and cross-examination questions with a parent.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Tree diagrams* (60 min): Draw tree diagrams for outfit choices and coin-die combinations and count outcomes.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Problem definition* (50 min): Choose a local impact (stormwater runoff, plastic waste, air quality) and write criteria and constraints for a solution.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Roots of the Enlightenment* (45 min): Create a flowchart linking Greek, Roman, Christian, Renaissance, Reformation and Scientific Revolution ideas to the Enlightenment.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Invitation chunks* (45 min): Learn ¿Quieres ir...?, ¡Claro que sí!, Lo siento, no puedo, tengo que... and practice in pairs.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Virtual museum* (40 min): Take a Google Arts & Culture virtual tour and note how works are displayed and labeled.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *PACER* (40 min): Retake the PACER or mile.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Air quality* (25 min): Check the Air Quality Index for a week and learn health actions for each level.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 167, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Debate* (75 min): Hold a structured debate with family (opening, rebuttal, cross-examination, closing).
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Tables of outcomes* (60 min): Make a 6x6 table of sums of two dice and find P(sum = 7) and P(doubles).
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Water filter design* (50 min): Build a water filter from sand, gravel, charcoal and coffee filters and test it on muddy water.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Locke* (45 min): Read excerpts from Locke's Second Treatise and explain natural rights.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Texting* (45 min): Write a Spanish text-message exchange inviting a friend to a game, including time and place.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Select* (40 min): Select 8–10 works representing each unit.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Strength tests* (40 min): Retake curl-ups and push-ups.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Water* (25 min): Learn where your tap water comes from and read your local water report.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 168, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Evaluate the debate* (75 min): Watch a recording of the debate and evaluate both sides' reasoning with a rubric.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Designing a simulation* (60 min): Design a coin-flip simulation for 'a family with 3 children has at least 2 girls' and run 30 trials.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Monitoring tool* (50 min): Design a method to monitor the impact (e.g., petroleum-jelly air particle cards placed around the house).
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Montesquieu* (45 min): Read Montesquieu on separation of powers and diagram the three branches.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Phone call* (45 min): Make a simulated phone call to invite a family member to an event and respond to their excuse.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Prepare* (40 min): Mat or mount 2-D work and create pedestals for sculptures.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Flexibility tests* (40 min): Retake trunk lift and sit-and-reach.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Noise and sun* (25 min): Learn about noise-induced hearing loss and sun protection.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 169, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Persuasive speech* (75 min): Write a 3-minute persuasive speech on a community issue using ethos, pathos and logos.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Simulating with technology* (60 min): Use a random number generator to simulate a 30% free-throw shooter taking 5 shots and estimate P(at least 2 makes).
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Testing* (50 min): Collect data from the filter and monitoring cards and record results.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Enlightenment salon* (45 min): Role-play a salon conversation among Locke, Voltaire, Rousseau and Montesquieu.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Cultural norms* (45 min): Read about punctuality and social plans in Latin America and Spain and discuss differences.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Labels* (40 min): Write museum-style labels for each piece.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Active play* (40 min): Play a favorite game from the year.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Reduce, reuse, recycle* (25 min): Audit household waste and plan reductions.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Debate and persuasive speech
- Objective: Participate in a structured debate
- Objective: Use formal English and evidence orally
- Vocabulary: opening statement, cross-examination, closing
- Materials: debate timer, evidence cards
- Assessment: Debate and speech rubrics.

### Mathematics 7: Compound events and simulation
- Objective: Find probabilities of compound events
- Objective: Design simulations
- Vocabulary: compound event, tree diagram, simulation
- Materials: two dice, coins, random number generator
- Assessment: Compound probability quiz and simulation write-up.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Engineering to monitor and reduce human impact
- Objective: Define a human-impact problem
- Objective: Design a monitoring or mitigation method
- Vocabulary: mitigation, monitoring, runoff, pollution
- Materials: coffee filters, sand, gravel, charcoal, plastic bottles, muddy water
- Assessment: Design notebook entries.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Enlightenment
- Objective: Trace roots of Enlightenment ideas
- Objective: Compare Locke, Montesquieu, Rousseau and Voltaire
- Vocabulary: Enlightenment, natural rights, social contract, separation of powers
- Materials: Locke's Second Treatise excerpt, Montesquieu's Spirit of the Laws excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz and salon performance.

### Spanish 1A: Invitations: accepting and declining
- Objective: Extend, accept, and decline invitations
- Objective: Give excuses politely
- Vocabulary: ¿Quieres...?, ¡Claro que sí!, lo siento, no puedo, estoy ocupado/a, ¡Qué pena!, ¿A qué hora?
- Materials: phone
- Assessment: Interpersonal invitation rubric.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Curating a portfolio
- Objective: Select and prepare work for exhibition
- Objective: Evaluate presentation methods
- Vocabulary: curate, mat, label, exhibition
- Materials: all year's artwork, poster board, cardstock
- Assessment: Curated portfolio.

### Physical Education 7: FITNESSGRAM post-test
- Objective: Retake fitness tests
- Objective: Compare to baseline
- Vocabulary: Healthy Fitness Zone, growth
- Materials: PACER audio, mat, measuring tools
- Assessment: Post-test results and graph.

### Health Education 7: Environmental health
- Objective: Explain environmental effects on health
- Objective: Reduce personal impact
- Vocabulary: air quality index, pollution, lead, recycling
- Materials: AirNow website, water test strips (optional)
- Assessment: Quiz.
