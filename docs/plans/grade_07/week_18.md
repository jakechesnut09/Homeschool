## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Imagery poem* (75 min): Write a free-verse poem about a place using imagery of all five senses.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Subtracting with counters* (60 min): Model 3 - 5 and -2 - (-6) with counters, adding zero pairs when needed.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Decomposition jars* (50 min): Set up decomposition jars with bread, apple and leaves under moist and dry conditions to observe over weeks.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Popes and kings* (45 min): Read about Henry IV and Pope Gregory VII at Canossa and explain the investiture controversy.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review: units 1–2* (45 min): Review greetings, numbers, dates, time, and weather with a self-made flashcard set and a practice listening quiz.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Final rehearsal* (40 min): Run the full recital program twice and fix problem spots.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Muscle groups* (40 min): Match 10 body-weight exercises to the muscle groups they train.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *The teen brain* (25 min): Learn how the prefrontal cortex develops into the mid-20s.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Form poem* (75 min): Write a sonnet, haiku sequence or ballad stanza modeled on a mentor poem.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Add the opposite* (60 min): Rewrite 15 subtraction problems as addition of the opposite and solve.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Mold growth* (50 min): Observe moldy bread with a magnifier and draw hyphae and spores.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Magna Carta* (45 min): Read clauses 39 and 40 of the Magna Carta and restate them in modern English.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review: units 3–5* (45 min): Review ser, adjectives, gustar, classroom vocabulary, and -ar verbs with a practice test.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Program* (40 min): Design a recital program with titles and short program notes.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Form* (40 min): Practice squats, lunges, push-ups, planks and glute bridges with a form checklist.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Reward pathway* (25 min): Diagram the dopamine reward pathway and how drugs hijack it.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 88, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Figurative poem* (75 min): Write an extended-metaphor poem modeled on 'Mother to Son'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Distance* (60 min): Find distances between elevations like Death Valley (-282 ft) and Mt. Whitney (14,505 ft) using |a - b|.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Nitrogen and carbon* (50 min): Diagram how carbon and nitrogen cycle among plants, animals, decomposers and soil.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Parliament* (45 min): Trace the development of Parliament and habeas corpus from Magna Carta.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Speaking exam* (45 min): Do a 3-minute interpersonal interview with a parent covering name, origin, personality, likes, and schedule.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Recital* (40 min): Perform two contrasting songs and the original composition for family.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Design* (40 min): Design a 6-station strength circuit for all major muscle groups.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Addiction* (25 min): Explain tolerance, dependence and withdrawal.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 89, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Recitation practice* (75 min): Memorize 14+ lines of a studied poem and rehearse with attention to pacing and emphasis.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Changes in temperature* (60 min): Compute temperature changes over a week from a weather table and graph them.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Compost design* (50 min): Design a small compost bin and explain how it returns matter to the soil.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Legacy* (45 min): Connect Magna Carta clauses to the U.S. Bill of Rights in a chart.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Written exam* (45 min): Take the semester written exam (listening, reading, grammar, and a 10-sentence writing prompt).
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Evaluate* (40 min): Watch the recording and evaluate with a performance rubric.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Perform* (40 min): Perform your circuit for 2 sets and record reps.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Myths vs facts* (25 min): Sort 10 drug myths and facts.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Writing and performing poetry
- Objective: Write original poems using devices and forms
- Objective: Perform a poem with expression
- Vocabulary: imagery, line break, recitation
- Materials: poetry notebook
- Assessment: Poetry portfolio (3 poems), recitation rubric and semester checkpoint.

### Mathematics 7: Subtracting rational numbers
- Objective: Understand subtraction as adding the opposite
- Objective: Find distance between numbers as absolute value of difference
- Vocabulary: difference, distance, change
- Materials: number line, two-color counters
- Assessment: Semester 1 cumulative checkpoint.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Decomposers and cycling matter
- Objective: Investigate decomposition
- Objective: Model how matter cycles in ecosystems
- Vocabulary: decomposition, nutrient cycle, compost, fungi
- Materials: clear jars, soil, food scraps, leaves, bread
- Assessment: Decomposition observations and semester checkpoint.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Church and state; Magna Carta
- Objective: Analyze conflict between popes and kings
- Objective: Explain the significance of Magna Carta
- Vocabulary: pope, excommunication, investiture, Magna Carta, Parliament, habeas corpus
- Materials: Magna Carta translation (National Archives/British Library)
- Assessment: Magna Carta chart and semester checkpoint.

### Spanish 1A: Semester 1 review and exam
- Objective: Review all semester 1 content
- Objective: Demonstrate proficiency across three modes
- Vocabulary: repaso, el examen
- Materials: unit tests from semester 1, flashcards
- Assessment: Semester 1 exam (written and oral).

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Winter recital
- Objective: Perform with accuracy and expression
- Objective: Evaluate performances
- Vocabulary: recital, stage presence, expression
- Materials: instrument, program paper
- Assessment: Recital performance and self-evaluation.

### Physical Education 7: Strength training with body weight
- Objective: Perform body-weight exercises safely
- Objective: Design a strength circuit
- Vocabulary: repetition, set, muscle group, form
- Materials: mat, resistance band (optional)
- Assessment: Circuit design and mid-year re-test.

### Health Education 7: Drugs and the brain
- Objective: Explain how drugs affect the brain
- Objective: Understand addiction
- Vocabulary: drug, dopamine, addiction, tolerance, withdrawal
- Materials: NIDA for Teens materials
- Assessment: Quiz.
