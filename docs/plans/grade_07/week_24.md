## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Note cards* (75 min): Take 15 note cards organized by subtopic, each with source and page.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Two methods* (60 min): Solve 2(x + 3) = 14 by dividing first and by distributing first, comparing steps.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Cookie mining* (50 min): Mine chocolate chips from a cookie with toothpicks, track 'costs' and 'profits', and discuss environmental damage.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Why Italy?* (45 min): Map Italian city-states and explain how trade, wealth and Roman ruins sparked the Renaissance.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Family words* (45 min): Learn 20 family words and label a photo collage of your family.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Projection* (40 min): Practice breath support and projection reading lines across a room.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Coach a drill* (40 min): Plan and lead a drill for basketball or softball.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *PSA planning* (25 min): Plan a 60-second PSA promoting a drug-free lifestyle.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Note-taking, paraphrasing and avoiding plagiarism
- Objective: Take organized notes
- Objective: Paraphrase and quote correctly
- Vocabulary: paraphrase, plagiarism, direct quotation, subtopic
- Materials: note cards or digital notes
- Assessment: Note cards, paraphrase set and outline.

### Mathematics 7: Solving two-step equations
- Objective: Solve px + q = r and p(x + q) = r fluently
- Objective: Compare arithmetic and algebraic solutions
- Vocabulary: inverse operation, distributive property
- Materials: algebra tiles (paper)
- Assessment: Two-step equation quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Resource use and unit assessment
- Objective: Evaluate how resource extraction affects people and environment
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: extraction, mining, groundwater depletion
- Materials: chocolate chip cookies, toothpicks, graph paper
- Assessment: Cookie-mining analysis and unit 5 test.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Origins of the Renaissance
- Objective: Explain humanism and the revival of classical learning
- Objective: Explain why the Renaissance began in Italy
- Vocabulary: Renaissance, humanism, patron, Medici, city-state
- Materials: map of Italian city-states, Petrarch letter excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish 1A: Mi familia: family members
- Objective: Name family members
- Objective: Describe family relationships
- Vocabulary: el padre, la madre, el hermano, los abuelos, el tío, la prima, el padrastro, la mascota
- Materials: family photos
- Assessment: Family vocabulary quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Voice and movement for actors
- Objective: Use projection, diction and inflection
- Objective: Develop character physicality
- Vocabulary: projection, diction, inflection, blocking, stage directions
- Materials: monologue excerpts
- Assessment: Monologue performance.

### Physical Education 7: Team sports review
- Objective: Compare team sports
- Objective: Demonstrate leadership
- Vocabulary: captain, strategy
- Materials: mixed equipment
- Assessment: Unit skills test and leadership reflection.

### Health Education 7: Drug-free advocacy and unit assessment
- Objective: Advocate for drug-free choices
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: advocacy, PSA
- Materials: video recording device
- Assessment: PSA and unit 4 test.
