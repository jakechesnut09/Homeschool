## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Listen and read* (75 min): Read along with a LibriVox recording of 'The Tell-Tale Heart' and mark every time the narrator insists he is not mad.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Reading a scale* (60 min): Use the scale 1 cm to 50 km on a California map to find road distances between five cities.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Chemical formulas* (50 min): Interpret formulas H2O, CO2, O2, CH4 and C6H12O6 by counting atoms of each element.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Constantinople's location* (45 min): Analyze a map of Constantinople and explain three geographic advantages for trade and defense.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *El alfabeto* (45 min): Learn the Spanish alphabet with a song, then practice the five pure vowel sounds and the letters ñ, ll, rr, j, and h (silent).
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *One-point* (40 min): Draw block letters of your initials in one-point perspective.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *SMART goals* (40 min): Write two SMART goals based on baseline scores (e.g., 'add 8 PACER laps by January').
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Influence map* (25 min): Map internal (hunger, emotions) and external (family, culture, media) influences on your food choices.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Unreliable narrator* (75 min): Make a T-chart of what the narrator claims versus what his actions show and explain why he is unreliable.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Creating a scale drawing* (60 min): Measure a bedroom or kitchen with a tape measure and plan a scale drawing using 1 inch to 2 feet.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Gumdrop molecules* (50 min): Build gumdrop-and-toothpick models of water, carbon dioxide, methane and ammonia using a color key.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Justinian and Theodora* (45 min): Read Procopius's account of Theodora during the Nika riots (primary source) and evaluate his reliability.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *¿Cómo se escribe?* (45 min): Spell your full name, your town, and five family members' names aloud; a parent writes what they hear to check accuracy.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Finding vanishing points* (40 min): Find and trace orthogonals in The School of Athens to locate its vanishing point.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Circuit 1* (40 min): Complete an 8-station circuit (jumping jacks, squats, planks, mountain climbers) 40 seconds on, 20 off, 2 rounds.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Ad analysis* (25 min): Analyze three food ads aimed at teens for techniques like celebrity endorsement and bright packaging.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Mood and word choice* (75 min): Identify repetition, dashes and sound words that build tension and explain their effect on mood in a paragraph.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Scales without units* (60 min): Rewrite scales like 1 in to 10 ft as 1 to 120 and compare which of two map scales shows more detail.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *PhET Build a Molecule* (50 min): Use PhET 'Build a Molecule' to assemble 8 molecules and record formulas and names.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Justinian's Code* (45 min): Read selected laws from Justinian's Code and compare them with the Twelve Tables.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Números 0–15* (45 min): Learn numbers 0–15 and play a card game (draw two cards, say the sum in Spanish) for 15 minutes.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *One-point room* (40 min): Draw your bedroom or a hallway in one-point perspective.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Goal action plan* (40 min): Plan weekly activities to reach each goal and post them on a calendar.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Cultural foods* (25 min): Interview a family member about a traditional family food and its meaning.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Grammar: coordinate adjectives* (75 min): Learn the comma rule for coordinate adjectives (the 'and' test) and edit 12 sentences describing the old man's room.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Equivalent scales* (60 min): Decide which of six scales are equivalent and redraw a basketball court at a new scale.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Crystals* (50 min): Examine salt and sugar crystals with a magnifier and build a repeating NaCl lattice model to show an extended structure.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Great Schism* (45 min): Compare Eastern Orthodox and Roman Catholic practices in a Venn diagram and explain the 1054 split.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Números 16–31* (45 min): Learn 16–31 (dieciséis, veintiuno), then do a listening dictation of 15 phone-style number strings.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Two-point* (40 min): Draw a street corner of buildings in two-point perspective.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Circuit 2* (40 min): Repeat the circuit and try to increase reps at each station.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Counter-ad* (25 min): Design a counter-ad promoting a healthy food using the same persuasive techniques.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 15, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Rewrite & check* (75 min): Rewrite the climax from the police officers' point of view in 300 words and take a point-of-view quiz.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Finish the room floor plan in scale with labeled actual dimensions and answer 5 map distance questions.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Draw labeled models of three molecules and one extended structure and explain the difference.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Unit 1 assessment* (45 min): Take the unit 1 test and examine the Hagia Sophia mosaics, writing how art reflected Byzantine power.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a spelling dictation of 8 Spanish words and a numbers 0–31 listening quiz.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Reflect* (40 min): Explain in writing how perspective changed art in the Renaissance.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Journal on how the circuit felt and rate perceived exertion.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Write a paragraph on which influence affects you most.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Point of view: 'The Tell-Tale Heart' by Edgar Allan Poe
- Objective: Analyze an unreliable first-person narrator
- Objective: Compare points of view
- Vocabulary: unreliable narrator, first person, mood, dissimulation
- Materials: 'The Tell-Tale Heart' (Project Gutenberg), LibriVox audio
- Assessment: Point-of-view rewrite and quiz.

### Mathematics 7: Scale drawings and maps
- Objective: Use scales with units to find actual distances
- Objective: Convert between scales with and without units
- Vocabulary: scale drawing, scale, scale without units
- Materials: printed city map, ruler, tape measure
- Assessment: Floor-plan scale drawing scored with a rubric (accuracy, labels, scale) plus map quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Modeling molecules and extended structures
- Objective: Build models of simple molecules
- Objective: Contrast molecules with extended structures like salt and diamond
- Vocabulary: molecule, chemical formula, bond, crystal, extended structure
- Materials: gumdrops or mini marshmallows, toothpicks, salt and sugar crystals, magnifying glass
- Assessment: Molecule models and labeled drawings.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Byzantine Empire
- Objective: Explain Constantinople's importance
- Objective: Compare Eastern Orthodox and Roman Catholic churches
- Vocabulary: Byzantine, Justinian's Code, icon, schism, patriarch
- Materials: Smarthistory Hagia Sophia video, Venn diagram
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Spanish 1A: El alfabeto y los números 0–31
- Objective: Spell names using the Spanish alphabet
- Objective: Count and use numbers 0–31
- Vocabulary: el alfabeto, la ñ, la ll, la rr, ¿Cómo se escribe?, cero, quince, treinta y uno
- Materials: alphabet chart, playing cards, dice
- Assessment: Spelling dictation and number listening quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Linear perspective
- Objective: Draw in one- and two-point perspective
- Objective: Analyze perspective in Renaissance art
- Vocabulary: horizon line, vanishing point, orthogonal, foreshortening
- Materials: ruler, pencil, print of Raphael's School of Athens
- Assessment: Perspective drawings.

### Physical Education 7: SMART goals and circuit training
- Objective: Write SMART fitness goals
- Objective: Complete a fitness circuit
- Vocabulary: SMART goal, circuit, station
- Materials: jump rope, cones, task cards
- Assessment: SMART goals and circuit log.

### Health Education 7: Influences on food choices
- Objective: Analyze food marketing
- Objective: Identify internal and external influences
- Vocabulary: marketing, target audience, influence, culture
- Materials: food ads, magazines or online ads
- Assessment: Ad analysis and counter-ad.
