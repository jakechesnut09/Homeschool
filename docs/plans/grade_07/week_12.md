## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Anatomy of an argument* (75 min): Read an op-ed on a school-start-time debate and label claim, reasons, evidence and counterclaim.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Semicircles and quarters* (60 min): Find perimeter and area of semicircles and quarter circles, including the straight edges.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Yeast balloons* (50 min): Feed yeast different amounts of sugar in bottles topped with balloons and measure balloon circumference every 10 minutes.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Sundiata* (45 min): Read a retelling of the Epic of Sundiata and identify the qualities of a leader it celebrates.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Actividades* (45 min): Learn 18 activity infinitives with picture cards and act them out for a parent to guess.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Chant* (40 min): Listen to Gregorian chant and Hildegard von Bingen and describe texture and melody.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Underhand serve* (40 min): Practice underhand serves over a net, 20 attempts.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Empathy* (25 min): Practice perspective-taking with three scenarios.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Sound reasoning?* (75 min): Evaluate whether each piece of evidence in the op-ed is relevant and sufficient using a rating chart.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Composite figures* (60 min): Find areas of a running track shape and a window shaped like a rectangle topped with a semicircle.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Breath test* (50 min): Blow through a straw into red-cabbage indicator before and after exercise and compare color changes as evidence of CO2.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Mansa Musa's hajj* (45 min): Analyze the Catalan Atlas image of Mansa Musa and explain how his 1324 hajj affected Cairo's economy.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Me gusta / no me gusta* (45 min): Write 10 sentences: Me gusta nadar, No me gusta cantar, Me gusta más leer.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Polyphony* (40 min): Listen to a Palestrina mass movement and identify the interweaving voices.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Overhand serve* (40 min): Try overhand serves and compare success rates.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Advocacy campaign* (25 min): Design a kindness or anti-bullying campaign poster or video.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Author's point of view* (75 min): Analyze how the author distinguishes her view from opponents' using specific phrases.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Expressing in terms of pi* (60 min): Write answers both as exact expressions with pi and as decimal approximations and compare.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Respiration equation* (50 min): Compare photosynthesis and respiration equations and draw a diagram of how they are linked.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Timbuktu* (45 min): Research Timbuktu's mosques and the Sankore scholars and its manuscript tradition.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *¿Te gusta...?* (45 min): Ask a family member ten ¿Te gusta...? questions and record answers in a chart.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Secular music* (40 min): Listen to a troubadour song and an English madrigal and compare to sacred music.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Rules* (40 min): Learn rotation and scoring and play 2-v-2 with three contacts.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Gratitude* (25 min): Start a gratitude practice and write three letters of thanks.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Listening to arguments* (75 min): Watch a short persuasive speech or ad and delineate its claims, noting any bandwagon or loaded-language fallacies.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Unit review* (60 min): Solve a review set of 10 problems on circumference, area and composite shapes.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Food to body* (50 min): Model how digested food molecules are rearranged to build body tissue or release energy.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Ibn Battuta in Mali* (45 min): Read Ibn Battuta's account of Mali and evaluate what he admired and criticized.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Listening* (45 min): Listen to two teens on a Spanish podcast or video describe hobbies and note each one's likes and dislikes.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Global connections* (40 min): Listen to Japanese gagaku and West African kora music and compare to European music of the same era.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Game day* (40 min): Play doubles volleyball games.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Review* (25 min): Review unit 2 concepts and skills.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Evaluating arguments
- Objective: Trace an argument's claims
- Objective: Evaluate evidence for relevance and sufficiency
- Vocabulary: claim, reason, evidence, counterclaim, fallacy
- Materials: CommonLit argument texts, persuasive video
- Assessment: Argument evaluation chart and quiz.

### Mathematics 7: Composite circular figures & unit test
- Objective: Find areas and perimeters of composite shapes with circles
- Objective: Demonstrate mastery of unit 3
- Vocabulary: semicircle, quarter circle, composite figure
- Materials: grid paper, calculator
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Cellular respiration
- Objective: Explain how organisms release energy from food
- Objective: Measure carbon dioxide production
- Vocabulary: cellular respiration, mitochondria, energy, fermentation
- Materials: yeast, sugar, warm water, balloons, bottles, bromothymol blue or red cabbage indicator
- Assessment: Yeast data table and quiz.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Mali and the spread of Islam
- Objective: Describe Mali's rise under Sundiata and Mansa Musa
- Objective: Explain Islam's influence in West Africa
- Vocabulary: Mansa Musa, Timbuktu, Sankore, hajj
- Materials: Catalan Atlas image (Library of Congress/BnF), Ibn Battuta on Mali
- Assessment: Paragraph and source analysis.

### Spanish 1A: ¿Qué te gusta hacer? Activities and gustar
- Objective: Name leisure activities
- Objective: Say what you and others like to do
- Vocabulary: nadar, bailar, cantar, dibujar, leer, montar en bicicleta, me gusta, te gusta
- Materials: activity picture cards
- Assessment: Activities vocabulary and gustar quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Listening: medieval to Renaissance music
- Objective: Identify features of early music
- Objective: Connect music to history
- Vocabulary: Gregorian chant, monophony, polyphony, troubadour, madrigal
- Materials: recordings of Hildegard von Bingen, a troubadour song, Palestrina and a madrigal
- Assessment: Listening log.

### Physical Education 7: Volleyball: serving and game play
- Objective: Serve underhand or overhand
- Objective: Use three contacts in game play
- Vocabulary: serve, rotation, bump-set-spike
- Materials: volleyball, net or rope
- Assessment: Serving test and game observation.

### Health Education 7: Promoting positive climate; unit assessment
- Objective: Advocate for kindness and inclusion
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: advocacy, inclusion, empathy
- Materials: poster paper
- Assessment: Campaign product and unit 2 test.
