## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Timed argument* (75 min): Write a 45-minute argument essay responding to a prompt with two short sources.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Populations and samples* (60 min): Identify populations and samples in 8 survey scenarios and judge which are biased.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Case study* (50 min): Research solutions to protect a California species (e.g., wildlife crossings for mountain lions, fish ladders for salmon).
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *English Bill of Rights* (45 min): Read key clauses of the 1689 English Bill of Rights and compare with Magna Carta.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review: units 1–5* (45 min): Review greetings, calendar, ser, gustar, school, and -ar verbs with a practice test.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Install* (40 min): Install the exhibition in the home or community space.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Plan* (40 min): Design a 4-week summer plan using FITT for all fitness components.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Choose an issue* (25 min): Choose a community health issue and research with valid sources.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Self-scoring* (75 min): Score the timed essay with the rubric and revise one paragraph.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Random sampling* (60 min): Use random page numbers to sample word lengths in a novel and estimate the mean word length.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Decision matrix* (50 min): Rate three competing solutions on cost, effectiveness, community impact and ecosystem services.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Declaration of Independence* (45 min): Read the Declaration's preamble and find Locke's ideas.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review: units 6–9* (45 min): Review food, -er/-ir verbs, family, tener, estar, ir, and ir a + infinitive with Conjuguemos drills.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Rehearse* (40 min): Rehearse a song, monologue and gallery talk for showcase night.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Trial day 1* (40 min): Complete day 1 of the plan.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Plan* (25 min): Plan an advocacy product (letter to city council, poster or video).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 173, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Grammar review* (75 min): Complete a mixed review of phrases, clauses, sentence types, modifiers and commas.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Variation in samples* (60 min): Take 5 random samples of 10 words, compare sample means and describe the variability.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Filter redesign* (50 min): Redesign the water filter based on test data and compare versions.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *DBQ planning* (45 min): Plan an essay answering 'How did ideas from 1215 to 1776 shape democracy?' using 5 documents.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Reading practice* (45 min): Read three short Spanish texts (menu, family profile, town brochure) and answer questions.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Showcase* (40 min): Host the arts showcase: gallery, music performance and animation screening.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Trial day 2* (40 min): Complete day 2 of the plan and adjust intensity.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Create* (25 min): Create the advocacy product.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 174, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Word study review* (75 min): Review Greek/Latin roots and figurative language with a 40-item review game.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Comparing groups* (60 min): Compare two dot plots (e.g., heights of two plant groups) using means and MAD and decide if the difference is meaningful.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Stakeholder views* (50 min): Role-play a town meeting with a farmer, developer and biologist debating a solution.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *DBQ writing* (45 min): Write the DBQ essay with a thesis and evidence from each document.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Speaking practice* (45 min): Do a mock 3-minute interview covering all topics of the year.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Feedback* (40 min): Collect visitor comments on response cards.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Barriers* (40 min): Identify barriers to staying active and plan solutions.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Present* (25 min): Present or send the product.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 175, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Final exam* (75 min): Take the year-end ELA exam (reading passages, grammar and a short written response).
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on sampling and comparing populations.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a recommendation letter to a city council supporting the best solution with evidence.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Review & check* (45 min): Revise and submit the DBQ essay.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Writing practice* (45 min): Write a practice paragraph about yourself, your family, and your weekend plans.

- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Present the plan to a parent.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Reflect* (25 min): Reflect on the impact of advocacy.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Timed writing and year review
- Objective: Write an argument under timed conditions
- Objective: Review grammar and vocabulary
- Vocabulary: prompt, timed writing
- Materials: practice prompts
- Assessment: Timed essay and final exam.

### Mathematics 7: Sampling and comparing populations
- Objective: Explain representative and random samples
- Objective: Compare two populations with measures of center and variability
- Vocabulary: population, sample, random sample, mean absolute deviation, bias
- Materials: book with many pages, dot plots
- Assessment: Sampling quiz and word-length sampling report.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Evaluating competing solutions for biodiversity
- Objective: Evaluate design solutions systematically
- Objective: Compare test data
- Vocabulary: decision matrix, trade-off, stakeholder
- Materials: research articles, decision matrix template
- Assessment: Decision matrix and recommendation letter.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: From Magna Carta to the Declaration of Independence
- Objective: Trace democratic ideas across documents
- Objective: Write a document-based essay
- Vocabulary: English Bill of Rights, Declaration of Independence, consent of the governed
- Materials: Magna Carta, English Bill of Rights, Declaration of Independence (National Archives)
- Assessment: DBQ essay.

### Spanish 1A: Spanish 1A review
- Objective: Review all Spanish 1A content
- Objective: Prepare for the final assessment
- Vocabulary: repaso
- Materials: flashcards, unit tests
- Assessment: Self-scored practice test.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Exhibition and showcase
- Objective: Present an exhibition and performance
- Objective: Discuss how display affects viewing
- Vocabulary: showcase, gallery talk
- Materials: exhibition space, instrument, animation file
- Assessment: Showcase and reflection.

### Physical Education 7: Personal fitness plan
- Objective: Design a summer fitness plan
- Objective: Implement and adjust it
- Vocabulary: lifelong fitness, adherence
- Materials: fitness journal
- Assessment: Personal fitness plan.

### Health Education 7: Community health advocacy project
- Objective: Advocate for a health issue
- Objective: Use valid information
- Vocabulary: advocacy, stakeholder
- Materials: poster or slides
- Assessment: Advocacy project.
