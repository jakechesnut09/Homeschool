## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Introduction* (75 min): Draft an introduction with background and a clear claim.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Sales tax* (60 min): Compute California sales tax (use local rate) on five purchases and find the tax rate from a receipt.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Sealed ecosystem* (50 min): Analyze data from a sealed jar containing a plant (and optionally a snail) and explain how both survive.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Geography* (45 min): Map Japan's islands, mountains and proximity to Korea and China and explain how geography shaped settlement.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Culture reading* (45 min): Read about popular pastimes (fútbol in Argentina, béisbol in the Dominican Republic, paseo in Spain) and list three products and practices.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Triads* (40 min): Build C, F and G major triads on keyboard or ukulele.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Groundstrokes* (40 min): Practice forehand and backhand groundstrokes against a wall.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Digital footprint* (25 min): Search your name online and discuss what a digital footprint is.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 67, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Reasons and evidence* (75 min): Draft two body paragraphs, each with a reason, two pieces of cited evidence and explanation.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Tips* (60 min): Compute 15%, 18% and 20% tips on menu orders using mental math (10% then adjust).
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Carbon atom journey* (50 min): Write a story following one carbon atom from the air into a leaf, a rabbit and back into the air.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Shotoku's constitution* (45 min): Read Prince Shotoku's Seventeen-Article Constitution and identify Confucian and Buddhist ideas.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Señor Wooly* (45 min): Watch a free Señor Wooly music video, identify every activity word you hear, and summarize the story in five English sentences.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Major vs minor* (40 min): Play major and minor triads and describe the difference in sound.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Dinks and volleys* (40 min): Practice dinking at the kitchen line and volleys with a partner.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Privacy settings* (25 min): Review privacy settings on a device or app with a parent.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 68, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Counterclaim* (75 min): Draft a counterclaim and rebuttal paragraph using phrases like 'Some argue… however…'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Markup and markdown* (60 min): Solve problems where a store marks up wholesale cost by 40% then discounts the retail price by 25%.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Model poster* (50 min): Create a poster model linking photosynthesis and respiration with matter and energy arrows.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Heian court* (45 min): Read an excerpt from The Tale of Genji or The Pillow Book and describe court life.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Pen-pal email* (45 min): Write a 10-sentence email to a pen pal introducing yourself and your likes and asking three questions.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *I-IV-V* (40 min): Play a I-IV-V-I progression and identify it in a 12-bar blues recording.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Rules* (40 min): Learn the serve, two-bounce rule and kitchen rule, then play doubles.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Online predators* (25 min): Learn grooming warning signs and how to report.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 69, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Concise language* (75 min): Cut wordiness and redundancy from 12 practice sentences, then from your own draft.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Commission and fees* (60 min): Calculate salesperson commission and service fees, then solve 'which deal is better' comparisons.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Unit review* (50 min): Review with flashcards and a practice set on unit 3 concepts.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Japanese Buddhism* (45 min): Compare Pure Land and Zen Buddhism and create a simple Zen rock garden in a shoebox.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Comparación* (45 min): Make a Venn diagram comparing your free time with a teen in Mexico or Spain, then write three comparison sentences in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Song chords* (40 min): Strum or play chords to a simple folk song like 'This Land Is Your Land'.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Game day* (40 min): Play a pickleball round robin at a local court.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Scams* (25 min): Identify phishing attempts in 6 sample messages.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 70, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Conclusion* (75 min): Draft a conclusion that restates the claim and leaves a call to action.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Plan a restaurant meal for a family within $60 including tax and tip and show all calculations.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Unit 3 assessment* (50 min): Take the unit 3 test.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on early Japan.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Unit 4 assessment* (45 min): Take the unit test: listening to likes, reading a pen-pal letter, and writing a reply.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Check* (40 min): Take a chord identification quiz by ear and on paper.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Analyze your follow-through using a phone video.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Create a family media agreement.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Drafting the argumentative essay
- Objective: Write a claim-based essay with counterclaim and rebuttal
- Objective: Use precise, concise language
- Vocabulary: rebuttal, concession, formal style
- Materials: evidence organizer, argument essay template
- Assessment: Complete argument draft.

### Mathematics 7: Tax, tips, commission and markup
- Objective: Solve multistep percent problems
- Objective: Use percent equations in shopping contexts
- Vocabulary: sales tax, tip, commission, markup, markdown
- Materials: restaurant menu, receipts
- Assessment: Restaurant budget task and short quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Matter and energy in organisms: synthesis
- Objective: Integrate photosynthesis and respiration in one model
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: carbon cycle, producer, consumer
- Materials: sealed jar with plant and snail (optional), poster paper
- Assessment: Carbon story, model poster and unit 3 test.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Japan's geography, Shotoku and Heian culture
- Objective: Explain Chinese and Korean influence on Japan
- Objective: Describe Heian court culture
- Vocabulary: archipelago, Shinto, Prince Shotoku, Heian, Tale of Genji
- Materials: map of Japan, Seventeen-Article Constitution excerpt, Tale of Genji excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz and source analysis.

### Spanish 1A: Unit 4: Free time in the Spanish-speaking world
- Objective: Compare teen free-time activities across cultures
- Objective: Write a pen-pal email about likes
- Vocabulary: el fútbol, el béisbol, el pasatiempo, el/la amigo/a por correspondencia, querido/a
- Materials: sample pen-pal email
- Assessment: Unit 4 test and pen-pal email rubric.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Chords and harmony
- Objective: Build triads
- Objective: Hear I-IV-V progressions
- Vocabulary: chord, triad, harmony, progression
- Materials: keyboard or ukulele, Chrome Music Lab Chords
- Assessment: Chord quiz and play-along.

### Physical Education 7: Pickleball
- Objective: Perform dinks, groundstrokes and volleys
- Objective: Apply kitchen rules
- Vocabulary: dink, volley, kitchen, two-bounce rule
- Materials: pickleball paddles, wiffle balls, net or court
- Assessment: Video self-analysis.

### Health Education 7: Online safety and digital citizenship
- Objective: Protect privacy online
- Objective: Recognize online predators and scams
- Vocabulary: digital footprint, privacy settings, grooming, phishing
- Materials: Common Sense Education lessons
- Assessment: Family media agreement.
