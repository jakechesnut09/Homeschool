## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 3* (75 min): Read Chapter 3 and chart each clash between Buck and Spitz leading to their final fight.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *From table to equation* (60 min): Write y = kx equations for tables of gas cost, hourly pay and unit price taken from real receipts.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Reading equations* (50 min): Identify reactants, products, coefficients and subscripts in five chemical equations.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *House of Wisdom* (45 min): Read about the House of Wisdom and how scholars translated Greek texts into Arabic.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *El tiempo* (45 min): Learn hace calor/frío/sol/viento, llueve, nieva, está nublado and give a daily weather report for your town.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Analyze mood* (40 min): Compare Van Gogh's 'Starry Night' and Picasso's 'The Old Guitarist' and describe how color creates mood.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Dribbling* (40 min): Dribble through a cone slalom using both feet and three different touches.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *What is mental health?* (25 min): Define mental health and list traits of emotionally healthy people.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Character tracker* (75 min): Update a Buck character tracker with traits gained and lost, citing a page for each.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Using equations* (60 min): Use y = 3.25x to answer forward and backward questions about cost and quantity.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Modeling with LEGO* (50 min): Use colored LEGO bricks to model methane burning (CH4 + 2O2 → CO2 + 2H2O) and count atoms before and after.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Math and science* (45 min): Solve a simple equation al-Khwarizmi's way and research Ibn Sina's Canon of Medicine.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Las estaciones* (45 min): Learn the four seasons and match months to seasons for California and for Argentina, explaining why they are reversed.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Same scene, two moods* (40 min): Paint the same simple landscape twice, once warm and once cool.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Shooting* (40 min): Practice instep shots at targets from 12 meters, focusing on plant foot and follow-through.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Emotion wheel* (25 min): Use an emotion wheel to name precise feelings from three recent situations.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Grammar: sentence types* (75 min): Identify simple, compound, complex and compound-complex sentences in a London passage, then write one of each about Buck.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Two equations* (60 min): Write both y = kx and x = (1/k)y for a recipe and explain what each tells you.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Balancing simulation* (50 min): Complete PhET 'Balancing Chemical Equations' intro and game levels 1–2.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Astrolabe* (45 min): Build a paper-plate astrolabe and use it to measure the angle of the North Star.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Weather report* (45 min): Read a Spanish-language weather forecast for Mexico City and Buenos Aires and record the temperature and conditions for both.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Plan* (40 min): Choose a personal place and sketch three thumbnails for a landscape painting.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Creating space* (40 min): Play 2-v-1 games learning to spread wide and move into open space.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Self-esteem* (25 min): List 10 personal strengths and ask family to add three.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 34, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 4* (75 min): Read Chapter 4 'Who Has Won to Mastership' and explain how Buck leads differently than Spitz.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Proportional or not?* (60 min): Decide whether equations like y = 4x, y = x + 4 and y = 4/x are proportional and justify with tables.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Baking soda and vinegar equation* (50 min): Model the baking soda + vinegar reaction and explain where the gas comes from.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Art and geometry* (45 min): Draw an Islamic geometric star pattern using compass and ruler.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *El pronóstico* (45 min): Write and record a 45-second TV-style weather report for three Spanish-speaking capitals, using the date and time.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Set criteria* (40 min): Write criteria for the painting (color scheme, focal point, depth).
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Small-sided game* (40 min): Play 3-v-3 soccer applying width and support.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Resilience* (25 min): Read a story of someone overcoming adversity and identify resilience skills.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 35, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Review & check* (75 min): Take a quiz on chapters 3–4 and revise five choppy sentences into compound-complex sentences.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz writing and using equations for proportional relationships.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz: count atoms and balance three simple equations with models.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Unit 2 assessment* (45 min): Take the unit 2 test with a short constructed response on Golden Age contributions.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Unit 2 assessment* (45 min): Take the unit test on days, months, dates, time, and weather, including a listening section.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Reflect* (40 min): Discuss which thumbnail best meets the criteria and why.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Check* (40 min): Self-assess dribbling and shooting with a skill rubric.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Journal about a time you bounced back from a setback.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: The Call of the Wild, chapters 3–4
- Objective: Trace conflict between Buck and Spitz
- Objective: Combine sentences for variety
- Vocabulary: dominant primordial beast, atavism, compound-complex sentence
- Materials: The Call of the Wild, character tracker
- Assessment: Quiz and sentence-combining exercise.

### Mathematics 7: Equations for proportional relationships
- Objective: Write equations of form y = kx
- Objective: Use equations to solve problems
- Vocabulary: equation, independent variable, dependent variable
- Materials: grocery receipts
- Assessment: Quiz on writing and interpreting y = kx.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Atoms rearrange: chemical equations
- Objective: Model reactions as rearrangements of atoms
- Objective: Read simple chemical equations
- Vocabulary: chemical equation, coefficient, subscript, balanced
- Materials: LEGO bricks or gumdrops, PhET Balancing Chemical Equations
- Assessment: Balancing quiz and model photos.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Islamic Golden Age
- Objective: Describe Muslim contributions to science, math, medicine and the arts
- Objective: Demonstrate unit mastery
- Vocabulary: House of Wisdom, algebra, astrolabe, calligraphy
- Materials: paper plate astrolabe template, geometric pattern grid
- Assessment: Unit 2 test and astrolabe/pattern projects.

### Spanish 1A: ¿Qué tiempo hace? Weather and seasons
- Objective: Describe weather and seasons
- Objective: Compare seasons in the northern and southern hemispheres
- Vocabulary: hace calor, hace frío, hace sol, llueve, nieva, está nublado, la primavera, el invierno
- Materials: weather website, world map
- Assessment: Unit 2 test and weather report recording.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Color and mood
- Objective: Analyze how color creates mood
- Objective: Plan a painting with criteria
- Vocabulary: warm, cool, mood, expressionism
- Materials: images of Van Gogh and Picasso Blue Period works, sketchbook
- Assessment: Mood studies and painting plan.

### Physical Education 7: Soccer: dribbling, shooting and strategy
- Objective: Dribble with control
- Objective: Shoot with the laces
- Objective: Create space in small-sided games
- Vocabulary: dribble, shooting, width, depth
- Materials: soccer ball, cones, goal or targets
- Assessment: Skill rubric and game observation.

### Health Education 7: Emotions and mental health
- Objective: Describe mental and emotional health
- Objective: Identify and name emotions
- Vocabulary: emotion, self-esteem, resilience, mental health
- Materials: emotion wheel, journal
- Assessment: Journal entries.
