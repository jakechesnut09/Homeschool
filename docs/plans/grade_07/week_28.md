## Day 1 — Monday (school day 136, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 4* (75 min): Read Chapter 4 and explain T.J.'s choices and how others react to him.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Equation sort* (60 min): Sort 15 equations and inequalities by solution and solving strategy.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Puzzle continents* (50 min): Cut out continents with fossil and rock evidence marked and fit them into Pangaea.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Reformers compared* (45 min): Compare the ideas of Erasmus, Luther, Calvin and Tyndale in a four-column chart.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Estar* (45 min): Learn estoy, estás, está, estamos, estáis, están and when to use estar for location and feelings.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Analyze photos* (40 min): Analyze Dorothea Lange's 'Migrant Mother' and a modern ad for composition and audience influence.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Basics* (40 min): Practice basic bounce, alternate foot and side swing for 10 minutes.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Types of boundaries* (25 min): Identify physical, emotional and digital boundaries.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 137, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 5* (75 min): Read Chapter 5 (the trip to Strawberry) and write a journal entry from Cassie's perspective about the Barnett Mercantile.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Create a problem* (60 min): Write two original word problems—one equation and one inequality—and solve them with full work.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Wegener's evidence* (50 min): Read about Alfred Wegener and list four lines of evidence and why scientists first rejected his idea.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Henry VIII* (45 min): Explain how Henry VIII created the Church of England and why the decision was political.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Prepositions* (45 min): Place a stuffed animal in 12 locations and describe each: El gato está debajo de la mesa.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Rule of thirds* (40 min): Take 10 photos using the rule of thirds.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Tricks* (40 min): Learn criss-cross and boxer step.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Saying no* (25 min): Practice assertive ways to state boundaries.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 138, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Chapter 6* (75 min): Read Chapter 6 and compare how Big Ma, Mama and Uncle Hammer each respond to injustice.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Partner swap* (60 min): Solve a parent's or sibling's version of the original problems and check each other's answers.

- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Church self-government* (45 min): Explain how Protestant congregations' self-government influenced democratic practices.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Where is it?* (45 min): Hide five objects and give a parent Spanish clues to find them; then switch roles.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Lines and framing* (40 min): Take 10 photos using leading lines and natural frames.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Endurance* (40 min): Complete 5 one-minute jump rounds with 30-second rests.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Respecting others* (25 min): Practice asking permission and accepting 'no'.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Roll of Thunder, chapters 4–6
- Objective: Analyze developing conflicts
- Objective: Contrast characters' perspectives
- Vocabulary: boycott, credit, dignity
- Materials: Roll of Thunder, Hear My Cry
- Assessment: Perspective chart and quiz.

### Mathematics 7: Unit 6 review & test
- Objective: Demonstrate mastery of unit 6
- Vocabulary: review
- Materials: index cards
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Continental drift and seafloor evidence
- Objective: Analyze evidence for past plate motions
- Objective: Model seafloor spreading
- Vocabulary: continental drift, Pangaea, seafloor spreading, mid-ocean ridge
- Materials: printed continent outlines, scissors, paper strips, shoebox
- Assessment: CER on continental drift.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Spread of Protestantism
- Objective: Compare Calvin, Tyndale, Erasmus and Luther
- Objective: Map Catholic and Protestant Europe
- Vocabulary: Calvinism, Anglican, Tyndale, congregation, self-government
- Materials: religious map of Europe c. 1600
- Assessment: Map and paragraph.

### Spanish 1A: ¿Dónde está? Estar and location
- Objective: Conjugate estar
- Objective: Describe location with prepositions
- Vocabulary: estar, encima de, debajo de, al lado de, delante de, detrás de, cerca de, lejos de
- Materials: stuffed animal, household objects
- Assessment: Estar and prepositions quiz.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Photography composition
- Objective: Apply composition rules in photos
- Objective: Analyze photographs
- Vocabulary: rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, depth of field
- Materials: phone or digital camera
- Assessment: Photo portfolio of 5.

### Physical Education 7: Jump rope skills
- Objective: Perform jump rope tricks
- Objective: Build cardiovascular endurance
- Vocabulary: basic bounce, criss-cross, double under
- Materials: jump rope
- Assessment: Jump rope routine.

### Health Education 7: Boundaries and consent
- Objective: Set and respect personal boundaries
- Objective: Communicate clearly
- Vocabulary: boundary, consent, respect, assertive
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Role-play.
