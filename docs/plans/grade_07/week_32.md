## Day 1 — Monday (school day 156, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Ethos, pathos, logos* (75 min): Learn the three appeals and find examples in five advertisements.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Nets* (60 min): Draw and cut out nets of a triangular and a rectangular prism and fold them to check.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Ecosystem services* (50 min): List services ecosystems provide (pollination, water filtering, flood control) and give a California example of each.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Motives* (45 min): Explain the motives for exploration: God, gold and glory, and the new technologies that enabled it.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Plan* (45 min): Choose five places in your town and write two sentences about each: where it is and what people do there.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Sculpture study* (40 min): Compare Donatello's 'David' and Michelangelo's 'David' from multiple viewpoints online.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Prep* (40 min): Plan events and warm-up for the meet.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Chronic diseases* (25 min): Research heart disease, type 2 diabetes, cancer and asthma.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 157, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Gettysburg Address* (75 min): Read and listen to the Gettysburg Address and annotate parallelism and purpose.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Surface area* (60 min): Find surface area of prisms from nets and from dimensions, including a prism with a hole.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Biodiversity survey* (50 min): Survey a 1-square-meter plot in two locations and count species and individuals.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Mapping voyages* (45 min): Map the voyages of Dias, da Gama, Columbus, Magellan and Cabrillo on a world map.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Record* (45 min): Film or photograph the places and record a narrated tour in Spanish (2 minutes).
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Armature* (40 min): Build a foil and wire armature for a small figure or animal.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Meet day 1* (40 min): Compete in sprints and long jump.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Risk factors* (25 min): Sort risk factors into controllable and uncontrollable.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Famous speeches: rhetoric and purpose
- Objective: Analyze speakers' purpose and rhetoric
- Objective: Evaluate reasoning in speeches
- Vocabulary: rhetoric, ethos, pathos, logos, parallelism
- Materials: Lincoln's Gettysburg Address, Susan B. Anthony's 'On Women's Right to Vote', Chief Joseph's 'I Will Fight No More Forever'
- Assessment: Rhetoric quiz and comparison paragraph.

### Mathematics 7: Surface area & unit test
- Objective: Find surface area of prisms using nets
- Objective: Distinguish surface area from volume problems
- Vocabulary: surface area, net, lateral face
- Materials: cardstock, tape
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Science 7 (Integrated): Biodiversity and ecosystem services
- Objective: Explain why biodiversity matters
- Objective: Measure local biodiversity
- Vocabulary: ecosystem services, biodiversity index, pollination, habitat
- Materials: hula hoop or string, field guide or iNaturalist app
- Assessment: Biodiversity survey report.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Age of Exploration
- Objective: Map major voyages
- Objective: Explain motives and technology of exploration
- Vocabulary: caravel, Prince Henry, da Gama, Columbus, Magellan, cartography
- Materials: world map, Columbus's journal excerpt
- Assessment: Voyage map and quiz.

### Spanish 1A: Unit 8 assessment: Mi ciudad
- Objective: Create a guided tour of your town
- Objective: Integrate estar, ir, and places
- Vocabulary: el turista, la visita, visitar
- Materials: camera or phone, map
- Assessment: Town tour video rubric and unit test.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Additive sculpture
- Objective: Build a form using additive methods
- Objective: Consider form from all sides
- Vocabulary: additive, subtractive, armature, form, in the round
- Materials: air-dry clay, aluminum foil, wire
- Assessment: Clay sculpture progress.

### Physical Education 7: Track meet
- Objective: Compete in personal events
- Objective: Set personal records
- Vocabulary: personal record, heat
- Materials: track, stopwatch, tape measure
- Assessment: Track meet results.

### Health Education 7: Noncommunicable diseases
- Objective: Describe chronic diseases
- Objective: Identify risk factors
- Vocabulary: chronic disease, diabetes, heart disease, risk factor, asthma
- Materials: family health history form
- Assessment: Prevention plan.
