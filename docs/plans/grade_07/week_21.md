## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Act 4* (75 min): Read Act 4 and explain how each conflict is resolved.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Money in and out* (60 min): Track a month of deposits and withdrawals in a check register using signed decimals.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Crayon rock cycle* (50 min): Shave crayons into sediment, press into 'sedimentary rock', heat-press into 'metamorphic', and melt into 'igneous'.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Geography* (45 min): Map the Yucatán, highlands and rainforest and explain how the Maya farmed with slash-and-burn and terraces.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Comida sana* (45 min): Learn 12 vegetable and protein words and classify foods as bueno/malo para la salud.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Manuscripts* (40 min): Study images of the Book of Kells and a Persian manuscript and compare them.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Shooting form* (40 min): Practice BEEF shooting form from close range, 50 shots.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Cannabis* (25 min): Learn effects of THC on the developing brain and California law for minors.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Pyramus and Thisbe* (75 min): Read a summary of Ovid's myth and explain how Shakespeare's allusion parodies the lovers' story.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Order of operations* (60 min): Evaluate expressions like -2(3 - 7)^2 / 4 + (-1.5) step by step.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Energy sources* (50 min): Label a rock cycle diagram with energy from the sun (weathering, erosion) and Earth's interior (melting, metamorphism).
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *City-states* (45 min): Explore images of Tikal and Chichén Itzá and describe pyramids, ball courts and plazas.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Plural adjectives* (45 min): Describe 10 foods with ser and agreeing adjectives: Las zanahorias son sabrosas.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Calligraphy* (40 min): Practice a gothic or italic alphabet with a chisel-tip marker.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Layups* (40 min): Practice right- and left-handed layups.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Drug categories* (25 min): Sort drugs into stimulants, depressants, hallucinogens and opioids with effects.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Act 5* (75 min): Read Act 5 and analyze the audience's comments during the mechanicals' play.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Rate problems* (60 min): Solve problems about a diver descending at -1.5 m/s and a tank draining at -2.25 gal/min.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Weathering lab* (50 min): Shake sugar cubes in a jar for different times to model mechanical weathering and graph the results.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Maya math* (45 min): Write numbers 1–100 in Maya base-20 notation, including zero.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Nutrition labels* (45 min): Read a Spanish nutrition label (información nutricional) and answer questions about grasa, azúcar, and calorías.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Illuminated letter* (40 min): Design an illuminated initial with border decoration.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Give-and-go* (40 min): Practice give-and-go and defensive slides.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Inhalants* (25 min): Learn about the dangers of inhalants.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 104, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Theme* (75 min): Write a theme statement about love or imagination with three supporting quotes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Estimation* (60 min): Estimate answers to 8 multistep problems before solving and compare estimate to exact answer.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *California rocks* (50 min): Research California's state rock serpentine and granite of the Sierra Nevada and place each in the rock cycle.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Calendar and glyphs* (45 min): Calculate your birthday in the Maya Tzolk'in calendar and write your name in glyphs.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Mi plato* (45 min): Draw a balanced plate and write a 6-sentence description of a healthy lunch, using para mantener la salud debes...
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Finish page* (40 min): Write a short quote in calligraphy and add metallic accents.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Game* (40 min): Play 2-v-2 or 3-v-3 half-court games.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Drug-free benefits* (25 min): List benefits of staying drug-free for goals and activities.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Acts 4–5 and play-within-a-play
- Objective: Analyze how structure resolves the plot
- Objective: Interpret allusions
- Vocabulary: resolution, play-within-a-play, Pyramus and Thisbe
- Materials: A Midsummer Night's Dream, summary of Ovid's Pyramus and Thisbe
- Assessment: Theme paragraph and quiz.

### Mathematics 7: Four operations with rational numbers in context
- Objective: Solve multistep problems with rational numbers
- Objective: Use estimation to check answers
- Vocabulary: order of operations, estimate
- Materials: bank statement template
- Assessment: Multistep problem quiz.

### Science 7 (Integrated): The rock cycle and energy that drives it
- Objective: Model the rock cycle
- Objective: Identify the energy sources driving it
- Vocabulary: rock cycle, weathering, erosion, magma, compaction
- Materials: crayons, pencil sharpener, foil, hot water
- Assessment: Rock-cycle model.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: The Maya
- Objective: Relate geography to Maya development
- Objective: Describe Maya achievements in math and astronomy
- Vocabulary: Maya, city-state, glyph, codex, zero
- Materials: map of Mesoamerica, Maya number chart
- Assessment: Math and calendar work, quiz.

### Spanish 1A: Para mantener la salud: healthy eating and plural adjectives
- Objective: Discuss healthy and unhealthy foods
- Objective: Use plural adjectives and ser with foods
- Vocabulary: la salud, las verduras, los guisantes, la grasa, sabroso/a, horrible, malo/a para la salud, deber
- Materials: MyPlate graphic, nutrition labels
- Assessment: Reading quiz and oral presentation.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Illuminated manuscripts and calligraphy
- Objective: Practice calligraphy
- Objective: Create an illuminated page
- Vocabulary: illumination, calligraphy, gold leaf, manuscript
- Materials: calligraphy or chisel marker, metallic paint or gold marker
- Assessment: Illuminated page.

### Physical Education 7: Basketball: shooting and game play
- Objective: Shoot with BEEF technique
- Objective: Apply give-and-go and defense
- Vocabulary: BEEF, layup, give-and-go, man-to-man
- Materials: basketball, hoop
- Assessment: Shooting test.

### Health Education 7: Cannabis and other drugs
- Objective: Describe effects of cannabis and other drugs
- Objective: Explain legal issues for minors
- Vocabulary: cannabis, THC, stimulant, depressant, hallucinogen
- Materials: NIDA for Teens
- Assessment: Drug category chart and quiz.
