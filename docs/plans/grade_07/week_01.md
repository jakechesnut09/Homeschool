## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Reader inventory* (75 min): Complete a reading interest survey, set a goal of 25 independent books for the year, and write a journal entry about a memorable story.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Pre-assessment & math autobiography* (60 min): Take a 20-question grade 6 readiness check (ratios, fractions, decimals, area) and write a half-page math autobiography describing strengths and goals.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Safety & notebook* (50 min): Set up a science notebook with a table of contents and sign a home lab safety contract after reviewing goggles, heat and chemical rules.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Course launch & timeline* (45 min): Build a wall timeline from 300 to 1800 CE and preview the nine regions studied this year on a world map.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Saludos* (45 min): Watch a short greeting dialogue, then practice hola, buenos días/tardes/noches aloud and label a time-of-day chart with the right greeting.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Sketchbook setup* (40 min): Design a sketchbook cover and fill one page with 20 different kinds of lines.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Dynamic warm-up* (40 min): Learn a 10-move dynamic warm-up (high knees, butt kicks, carioca, leg swings) and a static cool-down stretch routine.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Wellness wheel* (25 min): Complete a wellness wheel rating physical, mental, social and emotional health and write one goal.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Read 'Thank You, Ma'am'* (75 min): Read Hughes' 'Thank You, Ma'am' aloud and annotate Mrs. Jones's actions and words with sticky notes labeled 'trait' and 'evidence'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Scaled copies* (60 min): Compare five versions of a letter 'F' drawn on grid paper and decide which are scaled copies, explaining which lengths changed and how.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Phenomenon: food coloring* (50 min): Drop food coloring into hot and cold water, time how long it takes to spread, and sketch a particle model of what is happening.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Mapping the empire* (45 min): Map the Roman Empire in 117 CE, labeling Rome, Constantinople, the Rhine and Danube frontiers and major provinces.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *¿Cómo te llamas?* (45 min): Practice the exchange ¿Cómo te llamas? / Me llamo... / Mucho gusto / Igualmente with a parent playing a new classmate; record it twice.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Blind contour* (40 min): Draw your non-dominant hand in blind contour three times for 3 minutes each.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *PACER practice* (40 min): Practice the 20-meter PACER pacing for 5 minutes with the audio cadence.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Six nutrients* (25 min): Make a chart of the six nutrients with functions and two food sources each.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Direct vs. indirect characterization* (75 min): Build a two-column chart of direct and indirect characterization for Roger and Mrs. Jones and write a paragraph on how she changes him, citing two quotes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Corresponding parts* (60 min): Label corresponding points, segments and angles on pairs of polygons; measure angles with a protractor to see that angles stay the same.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Scent in a balloon* (50 min): Put vanilla extract inside a balloon, inflate and tie it, then smell the outside and explain using particles.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Roman law and citizenship* (45 min): Read excerpts of the Twelve Tables (primary source) and list three laws still reflected in modern legal ideas.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Despedidas* (45 min): Learn adiós, hasta luego, hasta mañana, nos vemos; write and act out three 4-line mini-dialogues that open with a greeting and close with a farewell.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Modified contour* (40 min): Draw a shoe in modified contour, glancing at the paper only at corners.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *PACER test* (40 min): Complete the 20-meter PACER (or timed mile) and record laps or time.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Water* (25 min): Track water intake for a day and calculate how much a teen needs.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Grammar: phrases vs. clauses* (75 min): Learn phrases (prepositional, participial) versus independent and dependent clauses and label 15 sentences from the story.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Making scaled copies* (60 min): Draw a scaled copy of a grid polygon with scale factor 3, then 1/2, and write how each side length changed.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Atoms and elements* (50 min): Use the PhET 'Build an Atom' simulation to build hydrogen, carbon and oxygen atoms and record protons, neutrons and electrons.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Engineering* (45 min): Study Roman roads, aqueducts and the Pantheon dome, and build a cardboard arch to test why arches carry weight.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Culture: el saludo* (45 min): Read about greeting customs (handshake, cheek kiss in Spain and much of Latin America) and compare them with greetings in your family in a two-column chart.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Beating creative block* (40 min): Try three idea-starters (random object, word prompt, 'draw it 5 ways') and fill a page.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Active recovery* (40 min): Take a 30-minute brisk walk or bike ride and log it in a fitness journal.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Nutrient detective* (25 min): Find nutrients in five pantry foods using their labels.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 7** — *Discussion & check* (75 min): Discuss with a parent whether Mrs. Jones's choice was wise, building on each other's points, then take a short quiz on characterization and clauses.
- [ ] **Mathematics 7** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a 6-problem quiz identifying scale factors and drawing a scaled copy; correct mistakes in a different color.
- [ ] **Science 7 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a claim-evidence-reasoning paragraph answering 'Is matter continuous or made of particles?' and take a vocabulary quiz.
- [ ] **World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph on Rome's most important legacy and take a map and vocabulary quiz.
- [ ] **Spanish 1A** — *Review & check* (45 min): Complete Duolingo's first unit and perform an unscripted greet-introduce-farewell exchange with a parent.
- [ ] **Visual & Performing Arts 7** — *Reflect* (40 min): Choose your best contour drawing and write three sentences about line quality.
- [ ] **Physical Education 7** — *Reflect* (40 min): Compare your PACER score to the Healthy Fitness Zone chart and write one observation.
- [ ] **Health Education 7** — *Check* (25 min): Take a nutrient quiz and journal about one change to try.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 7: Launching the year; 'Thank You, Ma'am' by Langston Hughes
- Objective: Analyze how characters' actions reveal traits
- Objective: Identify phrases and clauses
- Vocabulary: characterization, dialogue, phrase, clause
- Materials: CommonLit: 'Thank You, Ma'am', reading journal
- Assessment: Evidence paragraph, grammar labeling and discussion checklist.

### Mathematics 7: What are scaled copies?
- Objective: Distinguish scaled copies from non-scaled copies
- Objective: Find corresponding parts and scale factors
- Vocabulary: scaled copy, corresponding, scale factor
- Materials: graph paper, ruler, colored pencils
- Assessment: Quiz on scale factor and scaled-copy drawing; readiness check used to plan review.

### Science 7 (Integrated): What is everything made of? Particles and atoms
- Objective: Use evidence that matter is made of tiny particles
- Objective: Set up a science notebook and lab safety habits
- Vocabulary: matter, particle, atom, mass, volume
- Materials: food coloring, hot and cold water, clear cups, balloon, vanilla extract
- Assessment: CER paragraph and particle model sketches.

### World History & Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times: Rome at its height: strengths and legacies
- Objective: Describe Rome's contributions in law, citizenship and engineering
- Objective: Map the empire at its height
- Vocabulary: republic, empire, citizenship, aqueduct, Pax Romana
- Materials: blank map of the Mediterranean, colored pencils, timeline strip
- Assessment: Map quiz and legacy paragraph.

### Spanish 1A: ¡Hola! Greetings, farewells, and names
- Objective: Greet and say goodbye appropriately
- Objective: Ask for and give names
- Vocabulary: hola, buenos días, buenas tardes, adiós, hasta luego, ¿Cómo te llamas?, me llamo, mucho gusto
- Materials: Spanish notebook, mirror, Duolingo account
- Assessment: Oral exchange checklist: greets, gives name, responds to mucho gusto, uses a farewell.

### Visual & Performing Arts 7: Sketchbook habits and contour drawing
- Objective: Start a sketchbook practice
- Objective: Draw blind and modified contours
- Vocabulary: contour, blind contour, line quality
- Materials: sketchbook, pencils 2H–6B, felt-tip pen
- Assessment: Sketchbook pages and reflection.

### Physical Education 7: Warm-ups and aerobic capacity baseline
- Objective: Perform a dynamic warm-up
- Objective: Complete the PACER or mile run baseline
- Vocabulary: dynamic warm-up, aerobic capacity, PACER
- Materials: cones, PACER audio (free online), stopwatch
- Assessment: Recorded PACER/mile baseline.

### Health Education 7: Wellness and the six nutrients
- Objective: Describe the dimensions of wellness
- Objective: Identify the six nutrients and their functions
- Vocabulary: wellness, nutrient, carbohydrate, protein, fat, vitamin, mineral
- Materials: health journal, nutrition labels
- Assessment: Nutrient chart and quiz.
