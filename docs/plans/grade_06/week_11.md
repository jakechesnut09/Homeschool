## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Meet Odysseus* (90 min): Read the opening of the Odysseus section of The Children's Homer, map the start of his journey, and list epic hero traits.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *What is a percent?* (60 min): Shade 10 x 10 grids to show 25%, 40%, 7% and 120%, and connect percents to fractions and decimals out of 100.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Air mass types* (50 min): Learn the four air mass types (maritime/continental, tropical/polar) and identify which ones affect California.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Hieroglyphs and the Rosetta Stone* (45 min): Learn how the Rosetta Stone was deciphered and write your name in hieroglyphs in a cartouche.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Two-point boxes* (30 min): Draw boxes using two vanishing points on a long sheet of paper.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Ball handling* (40 min): Complete ball-handling drills (around the waist, figure 8, finger taps).
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Types of pressure* (20 min): Identify direct, indirect and online peer pressure in scenarios.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *The Cyclops* (90 min): Read the Polyphemus episode and cite evidence that Odysseus is both clever and proud.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Benchmark percents* (60 min): Find 10%, 25%, 50% and 75% of 8 different quantities using mental math and double number lines.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Front model* (50 min): Model a front with warm red water and cold blue water separated by a divider; remove it and sketch what happens.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Egyptian art rules* (45 min): Analyze tomb paintings for composite view, hierarchical scale and symbolism, then draw a figure in Egyptian style.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Buildings* (30 min): Turn boxes into buildings with doors, windows and roofs.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Dribbling* (40 min): Practice control and speed dribbles with each hand through cones.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Positive pressure* (20 min): Write examples of positive peer pressure you have experienced or given.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 53, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Aeolus and Circe* (90 min): Read the bag of winds and Circe episodes and explain how the crew's choices cause new problems.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Percent of a quantity* (60 min): Use tables to find percents such as 35% of $80 and 15% of 60 minutes (10 problems).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Reading weather maps* (50 min): Interpret front symbols, highs and lows on three daily NOAA weather maps.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Hatshepsut* (45 min): Read about Hatshepsut's reign, trade expedition to Punt and temple, and why her images were later erased.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Cityscape* (30 min): Draw a street corner cityscape in two-point perspective.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Crossover* (40 min): Learn the crossover dribble and practice it against a partner defender.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Family roles* (20 min): Interview a family member about responsibilities and how communication works at home.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 54, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Root review* (90 min): Find 10 words in the reading with Greek or Latin parts (e.g., 'journey', 'monstrous') and analyze them.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Sales and tips* (60 min): Calculate sale prices from real ads (20% off, 30% off) and a 15% tip on three restaurant bills.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Weather data* (50 min): Collect a week of local temperature, pressure, wind and humidity data and connect changes to fronts on maps.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Ramses the Great* (45 min): Examine Abu Simbel and the Battle of Kadesh accounts and decide what is history and what is propaganda.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Atmospheric perspective* (30 min): Color the cityscape using brighter, sharper colors in front and lighter, cooler colors far away.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Peer feedback* (40 min): Use a critical-elements checklist to give a partner feedback on dribbling.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Family meeting* (20 min): Lead a short family meeting using I-messages to plan an activity.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 55, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a paragraph analyzing which episode best shows Odysseus's greatest flaw; reading quiz.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on percent meaning and finding a percent of a quantity.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a CER explaining a weather change you observed using air mass and front data.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph comparing the legacies of Hatshepsut and Ramses II.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Revise one area of your cityscape based on a parent's critique.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Skill check* (40 min): Complete a timed dribble course with both hands.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take the Relationships unit test.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: The Odyssey, part 1
- Objective: Understand epic hero traits
- Objective: Analyze how episodes reveal character
- Vocabulary: epic, epic hero, episode, cunning, Cyclops, hospitality
- Materials: 'The Children's Homer' by Padraic Colum (Project Gutenberg), map of the Mediterranean
- Assessment: Analytical paragraph and quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Percentages
- Objective: Understand percent as a rate per 100
- Objective: Find percents of quantities using double number lines and tables
- Vocabulary: percent, per hundred, benchmark percent
- Materials: 10 x 10 grids, store sale ads
- Assessment: Percent quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Air masses and fronts
- Objective: Identify air mass types
- Objective: Explain how fronts change weather
- Vocabulary: air mass, cold front, warm front, stationary front, high pressure, low pressure
- Materials: two containers, warm water, cold blue-dyed water, cardboard divider, weather maps from NOAA
- Assessment: Weather data analysis CER.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Art, writing and great rulers
- Objective: Describe Egyptian art and hieroglyphs
- Objective: Evaluate Hatshepsut and Ramses II
- Vocabulary: hieroglyphics, papyrus, Rosetta Stone, obelisk, temple, propaganda
- Materials: hieroglyph alphabet chart, paper bag (for papyrus effect), images of Abu Simbel and Deir el-Bahri
- Assessment: Comparison paragraph.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Two-point perspective
- Objective: Draw forms with two vanishing points
- Objective: Create a cityscape
- Vocabulary: two-point perspective, leading edge, atmospheric perspective
- Materials: ruler, long paper, colored pencils
- Assessment: Two-point cityscape.

### Physical Education 6: Basketball: ball handling and dribbling
- Objective: Dribble with either hand
- Objective: Protect the ball
- Vocabulary: crossover, control dribble, speed dribble, pivot foot
- Materials: basketball, cones, hoop if available
- Assessment: Dribble course time and checklist.

### Health 6: Peer pressure and family relationships
- Objective: Respond to negative peer pressure
- Objective: Describe healthy family communication
- Vocabulary: peer pressure, positive peer pressure, family roles, responsibility
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Unit test.
