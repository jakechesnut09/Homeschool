## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Evaluate a speech* (90 min): Watch a short persuasive speech and note the speaker's claims and whether each is supported.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Decimals in money* (60 min): Add and subtract prices from a shopping list, lining up place values, and find change from $50.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Shake table* (50 min): Build a simple shake table from cardboard and rubber bands and test a sugar-cube tower.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Forms of government* (45 min): Create a chart defining monarchy, aristocracy, oligarchy, tyranny and democracy with Greek examples.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Dynamics* (30 min): Listen to Ravel's 'Bolero' and chart how dynamics grow; learn p, mp, mf, f symbols.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Grip and serve* (40 min): Learn the handshake grip and practice short and long serves.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Resources* (20 min): List school, community and crisis resources including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Prepare a speech* (90 min): Turn your essay into a 2-minute opening statement with note cards.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Adding and subtracting decimals* (60 min): Solve 20 decimal sums and differences with up to thousandths, including regrouping across zeros.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Earthquake-resistant design* (50 min): Redesign the tower with cross-bracing or base isolation and test again, recording results.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Athenian democracy* (45 min): Read about Solon and Cleisthenes and explain who could and could not participate as citizens.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Tempo* (30 min): Learn Italian tempo terms and match them to five recorded excerpts.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Forehand and backhand* (40 min): Rally with forehand and backhand strokes with a partner.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Helping a friend* (20 min): Learn the steps: notice, ask, listen, tell a trusted adult, and practice with a scenario.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 83, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Rehearse* (90 min): Practice delivery (eye contact, volume, pace) while recording yourself and critique the video.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Multiplying decimals* (60 min): Use area diagrams and fractions (0.3 x 0.4 = 3/10 x 4/10) to explain decimal placement, then compute 12 products.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Early warning* (50 min): Learn how California's MyShake/ShakeAlert early warning system uses data and write how seconds of warning help.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Ostracism* (45 min): Hold a mock ostracism vote with family using paper 'ostraka' and discuss its purpose.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Articulation* (30 min): Play a melody legato and staccato and explain how it changes character.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Clear and drop* (40 min): Practice clears to the back and drop shots near the net.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *When to tell* (20 min): Discuss why some secrets must be shared with an adult to keep someone safe.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 84, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Debate* (90 min): Hold a structured debate with a family member or co-op partner: opening statements, rebuttals and closings.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Decimal applications* (60 min): Calculate total cost of buying fabric at $4.75 per yard for 2.5 yards and 5 similar problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Family hazard plan* (50 min): Create a family emergency plan and kit list for an earthquake or wildfire.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Pericles' Funeral Oration* (45 min): Read an excerpt and identify three values Pericles praised about Athenian democracy.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Expressive performance* (30 min): Add dynamics and articulation to your melody and practice following markings.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Singles game* (40 min): Play singles badminton games using placement strategy.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Self-care plan* (20 min): Write a personal self-care plan for hard days.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 85, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a reflection on the debate and take the unit test on argument terms and analysis.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on decimal addition, subtraction and multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Present your shake-table results and family plan; take the Unit 5 test.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a comparison of Athenian direct democracy and U.S. representative democracy; quiz on government forms.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & check* (30 min): Quiz on musical terms and perform your melody with expression.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Skill check* (40 min): Count longest rally and number of successful clears out of 10.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Create a resource card and take the Mental Health unit quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Speaking: debate
- Objective: Present claims with evidence orally
- Objective: Evaluate a speaker's argument
- Vocabulary: debate, opening statement, rebuttal, delivery
- Materials: essay, timer, a recorded student speech or TED-Ed talk
- Assessment: Debate performance rubric and unit test.

### Mathematics 6: Decimals: adding, subtracting and multiplying
- Objective: Add and subtract decimals fluently
- Objective: Multiply decimals and explain decimal placement
- Vocabulary: decimal, place value, tenth, hundredth, thousandth
- Materials: base-ten blocks or grid paper, receipts
- Assessment: Decimal operations quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Mitigating hazards
- Objective: Design solutions to reduce hazard damage
- Objective: Evaluate technologies such as early warning systems
- Vocabulary: mitigation, early warning, retrofit, base isolation, defensible space
- Materials: sugar cubes or blocks, cardboard, rubber bands, pan of gelatin or tray
- Assessment: Shake-table design report and Unit 5 test.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: From tyranny to democracy
- Objective: Trace forms of Greek government
- Objective: Compare direct and representative democracy
- Vocabulary: monarchy, oligarchy, tyranny, democracy, citizen, assembly, ostracism
- Materials: pottery shards or paper ostraka, excerpt of Pericles' Funeral Oration (Thucydides)
- Assessment: Comparison essay and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Dynamics, tempo and expression
- Objective: Read dynamic and tempo markings
- Objective: Perform with expression
- Vocabulary: dynamics, piano, forte, crescendo, tempo, allegro, adagio, legato, staccato
- Materials: instrument, recordings of orchestral works
- Assessment: Terms quiz and expressive performance.

### Physical Education 6: Badminton
- Objective: Use forehand and backhand strokes
- Objective: Perform the clear and drop shot
- Vocabulary: racket, shuttlecock, clear, drop shot, forehand, backhand
- Materials: badminton rackets and shuttlecocks, net or rope
- Assessment: Rally and clear check.

### Health 6: Getting help and supporting others
- Objective: Identify mental health resources
- Objective: Practice helping a friend
- Vocabulary: counselor, crisis line, 988, confidentiality
- Materials: resource card template
- Assessment: Resource card and quiz.
