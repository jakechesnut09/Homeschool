# Physical Education 6 (Grade 6)

Grade 6 PE emphasizes working cooperatively toward common goals while refining skills in invasion, net/wall, striking/fielding, rhythmic and individual activities. Students learn fitness concepts (FITT, heart-rate zones, components of health-related fitness), take FITNESSGRAM-style baseline and end-of-year assessments, and build a personal fitness plan. Lessons are designed for a backyard, park or co-op with minimal equipment; every day includes a warm-up and at least 20 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous activity.

*Framework:* Physical Education Model Content Standards for California Public Schools (Grade 6); 2009 CA Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 40 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Fitness Baseline and Goal Setting (weeks 1–3)
Knowing your current fitness lets you set meaningful goals and train safely.

- Week 1: Warm-ups, safety and heart rate
- Week 2: Fitness testing: aerobic capacity and strength
- Week 3: Fitness testing: flexibility and goal setting

### Unit 2: Cooperative Challenges and Team Building (weeks 4–6)
Teams succeed when members communicate, plan together and include everyone.

- Week 4: Communication challenges
- Week 5: Group problem solving
- Week 6: Cooperative games and fitness

### Unit 3: Invasion Games: Soccer and Ultimate (weeks 7–10)
Invasion games share strategies: create space on offense, deny space on defense.

- Week 7: Soccer: dribbling and passing
- Week 8: Soccer: shooting and team play
- Week 9: Ultimate: throwing and catching a disc
- Week 10: Ultimate: game play and Spirit of the Game

### Unit 4: Invasion Games: Basketball (weeks 11–14)
Ball control, passing and spacing make team offense effective.

- Week 11: Basketball: ball handling and dribbling
- Week 12: Basketball: passing and shooting
- Week 13: Basketball: offense and defense
- Week 14: Basketball: games and officiating

### Unit 5: Net and Wall Games: Volleyball and Badminton (weeks 15–18)
Net games reward controlled contacts and placing the object where opponents are not.

- Week 15: Volleyball: forearm pass and set
- Week 16: Volleyball: serving and game play
- Week 17: Badminton
- Week 18: Net games tournament and mid-year check

### Unit 6: Striking and Fielding: Softball and Kickball (weeks 19–22)
Fielding teams cooperate to prevent runs; batters aim to place the ball.

- Week 19: Throwing and catching for fielding
- Week 20: Batting
- Week 21: Kickball and base running
- Week 22: Modified softball games

### Unit 7: Rhythms and Dance (weeks 23–25)
Dance builds coordination and fitness and expresses culture.

- Week 23: Rhythm and line dances
- Week 24: Folk dances from around the world
- Week 25: Create a fitness dance

### Unit 8: Individual Activities: Track, Jump Rope and Orienteering (weeks 26–30)
Individual activities let you measure personal progress and can be done for a lifetime.

- Week 26: Sprinting and relays
- Week 27: Distance running and pacing
- Week 28: Jumping and throwing events
- Week 29: Jump rope fitness
- Week 30: Orienteering

### Unit 9: Personal Fitness Plan and Reassessment (weeks 31–36)
Applying FITT principles to a personal plan improves fitness you can measure.

- Week 31: FITT principle
- Week 32: Designing a personal fitness plan
- Week 33: Following the plan: week 2
- Week 34: Following the plan: week 3
- Week 35: End-of-year fitness reassessment
- Week 36: Field day and lifetime activity goals

## Standards

- **PE-6.1.1** Throw, kick and strike objects with accuracy and appropriate force using mature form in cooperative and individual activities.
- **PE-6.1.2** Combine locomotor, non-locomotor and manipulative skills to work cooperatively toward a common goal.
- **PE-6.1.3** Perform rhythmic patterns and folk, line and cooperative dances with accuracy and coordination.
- **PE-6.1.4** Demonstrate mature technique for dribbling, passing, volleying, setting and receiving in game settings.
- **PE-6.2.1** Explain how to modify skills and strategies to work effectively with a partner or team.
- **PE-6.2.2** Use practice, feedback and critical elements to analyze and improve one's own and others' skill performance.
- **PE-6.2.3** Explain and apply basic offensive and defensive strategies in cooperative, invasion, net and fielding games.
- **PE-6.3.1** Participate in moderate to vigorous physical activity a minimum of four days each week, and track activity.
- **PE-6.3.2** Assess aerobic capacity, muscular strength and endurance, flexibility and body composition using a standard fitness test.
- **PE-6.3.3** Set and monitor short- and long-term goals to improve health-related fitness.
- **PE-6.4.1** Explain and apply the FITT principles (frequency, intensity, time, type) to a personal fitness plan.
- **PE-6.4.2** Measure heart rate and explain target heart-rate zones and recovery.
- **PE-6.4.3** Explain the purpose of warm-up and cool-down, safe practices, hydration and nutrition for activity.
- **PE-6.5.1** Cooperate with others, communicate positively and accept roles to accomplish a group goal.
- **PE-6.5.2** Demonstrate responsible behavior, sportsmanship and conflict-resolution skills during activity.
- **PE-6.5.3** Include and encourage others of different skill levels and abilities.
