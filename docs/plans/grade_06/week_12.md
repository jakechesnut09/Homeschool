## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Sirens, Scylla and Charybdis* (90 min): Read the Sirens and Scylla episodes and discuss what they suggest about temptation and leadership.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Finding the whole* (60 min): Solve problems like '12 is 30% of what number?' with tape diagrams and tables (10 problems).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Make a forecast* (50 min): Use your barometer, cloud and wind observations to forecast tomorrow's weather and check the next day.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Trade routes* (45 min): Map Egyptian trade with Punt, Nubia, the Levant and Crete, listing goods exchanged.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Interview a user* (30 min): Interview a family member about what they would need in a dream reading nook or community space.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Passing* (40 min): Practice chest, bounce and overhead passes to a wall target, 20 each.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Identity map* (20 min): Create an identity map (culture, interests, family, abilities) and compare with a family member's.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Return to Ithaca* (90 min): Read Odysseus's return in disguise and trace how Penelope tests him.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *What percent?* (60 min): Find what percent one quantity is of another using nutrition labels (e.g., grams of sugar out of total carbohydrates).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Thunderstorms* (50 min): Diagram the stages of a thunderstorm and explain the roles of rising warm air and condensation.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Kush rises* (45 min): Read about Kush and its resources (gold, ivory, ebony) and its conquest of Egypt under Piye.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Floor plan* (30 min): Draw a floor plan to scale that meets those needs, including accessibility features.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Shooting form* (40 min): Practice form shooting close to the hoop using BEEF (balance, eyes, elbow, follow-through).
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Stereotypes* (20 min): Identify stereotypes in 5 media examples and explain their harm.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Themes across the epic* (90 min): Write two theme statements for the Odyssey (e.g., about loyalty or pride) with evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Battery and progress bars* (60 min): Use a device battery percentage and download bars to solve find-the-part, find-the-percent and find-the-whole problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Atmospheric rivers* (50 min): Read about atmospheric rivers that bring California winter storms and trace one on a satellite image.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Meroe* (45 min): Compare the pyramids of Meroe with Giza and explain Meroe's ironworking industry.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Perspective view* (30 min): Draw a perspective view of your design.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Layups* (40 min): Practice right- and left-hand layups with the correct footwork.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Inclusion* (20 min): Read a story about exclusion and rewrite the ending to show inclusion.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Text vs. media* (90 min): Watch or listen to a retelling of the Sirens episode and compare it with the text in a chart of what was added, cut or changed.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a mixed review of unit rates, conversions, speed and percents.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Weather report* (50 min): Write and record a 2-minute TV-style weather report for your town using real data.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Kush and Egypt* (45 min): Create a timeline showing changing relations between Egypt and Kush (trade, conquest, cultural borrowing).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Model* (30 min): Build a small cardboard model of the space.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Video analysis* (40 min): Record your shot, compare it with the BEEF checklist and set a correction goal.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Respect in action* (20 min): List ways to include people with disabilities, new students or different family structures.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 60, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a comparison of how a Greek myth and the Odyssey both treat pride (RL.6.9) and take the Odyssey quiz.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit 3 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 3 end-of-unit assessment and correct missed problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Take the Unit 3 test on water cycle, air pressure, air masses and forecasting.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Unit 3 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 3 test on Egypt and Kush.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Present your design and explain how it meets the user's needs.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Skill check* (40 min): Shoot 10 shots from 3 spots and record your percentage.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a paragraph on how respect supports mental health; complete a check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: The Odyssey, part 2: homecoming
- Objective: Determine the epic's themes
- Objective: Compare text with a film or audio version
- Vocabulary: Sirens, loyalty, disguise, suitor, homecoming
- Materials: The Children's Homer, audio or animated clip of the Sirens episode
- Assessment: Comparison response and quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Finding the whole and percent problems
- Objective: Find the whole given a part and percent
- Objective: Find what percent one quantity is of another
- Vocabulary: part, whole, percentage
- Materials: phone or tablet battery indicator, nutrition labels
- Assessment: Unit 3 end-of-unit assessment.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Forecasting and severe weather
- Objective: Use data to make forecasts
- Objective: Explain how storms form
- Vocabulary: forecast, meteorologist, thunderstorm, hurricane, tornado, atmospheric river
- Materials: weather log, National Weather Service website
- Assessment: Weather report and Unit 3 test.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Trade and the Kingdom of Kush
- Objective: Describe Egyptian trade
- Objective: Explain Kush's relations with Egypt
- Vocabulary: Nubia, Kush, Meroe, Napata, iron, trade network
- Materials: map of Egypt and Kush, images of Meroe pyramids
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Design for people: architecture
- Objective: Design a space that meets users' needs
- Objective: Explain design choices
- Vocabulary: architecture, floor plan, accessibility, user, elevation
- Materials: graph paper, cardboard, tape, scissors
- Assessment: Design presentation.

### Physical Education 6: Basketball: passing and shooting
- Objective: Execute chest, bounce and overhead passes
- Objective: Shoot with BEEF form
- Vocabulary: chest pass, bounce pass, BEEF, layup, follow-through
- Materials: basketball, wall target, hoop
- Assessment: Shooting percentage and form checklist.

### Health 6: Respecting differences
- Objective: Explain why respecting differences matters
- Objective: Identify stereotypes and prejudice
- Vocabulary: diversity, stereotype, prejudice, inclusion, discrimination
- Materials: picture books or articles
- Assessment: Paragraph and check.
