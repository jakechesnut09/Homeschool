## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Choose a question* (90 min): Choose a debatable question from a list, read two articles on it, and take a position.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Fractional lengths* (60 min): Measure a ribbon and find how many 3/8-yard pieces can be cut from 4 1/2 yards; solve 6 similar length problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Greenhouse model* (50 min): Place thermometers in a covered and an uncovered jar under a lamp and record temperatures every 2 minutes for 20 minutes.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Babylonian exile* (45 min): Read about the destruction of the First Temple and exile to Babylon and map the movement.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Treble clef* (30 min): Learn line and space notes (EGBDF, FACE) and complete a note-naming drill.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Ready position* (40 min): Learn ready position and footwork; practice forearm passes off a self-toss.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Cyberbullying* (20 min): Analyze how cyberbullying differs from in-person bullying (audience, permanence, anonymity).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Evidence notes* (90 min): Take notes on cards, separating quotations and paraphrases, with source titles.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Rectangles with fractional sides* (60 min): Find a missing side when area and one side are fractions (e.g., area 2 1/2 sq ft, width 3/4 ft).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Reading the graphs* (50 min): Analyze NASA global temperature and atmospheric CO2 graphs and describe trends since 1900.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Return and Second Temple* (45 min): Read about Cyrus of Persia permitting return and the rebuilding of the Temple.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *First notes* (30 min): Learn to play B, A and G (recorder) or C-D-E (keyboard) and play 'Hot Cross Buns' from notation.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Partner bumping* (40 min): Bump with a partner and count consecutive passes.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Stop, block, tell* (20 min): Practice responses: don't reply, screenshot, block, tell a trusted adult.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 73, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Vague pronouns* (90 min): Lesson on vague pronouns ('they say', 'this'); fix 12 sentences with unclear antecedents.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Recipes* (60 min): Scale a recipe calling for 2/3 cup sugar to fit a 1/2-cup measuring scoop and solve 5 recipe-division problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Asking questions* (50 min): Write 8 questions to clarify evidence about causes of warming (solar output, volcanoes, CO2) and research answers on NASA Climate Kids.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Roman destruction* (45 min): Analyze the Arch of Titus relief showing the Temple's treasures being carried away in 70 CE.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Bass clef* (30 min): Learn bass clef notes and name 20 notes in a timed drill.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Setting* (40 min): Learn the overhead set with a triangle hand shape and set to yourself 20 times.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Kindness campaign* (20 min): Design a kindness or anti-bullying poster or short video.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 74, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Outline* (90 min): Outline an essay with a claim, two or three reasons, evidence for each and a counterclaim with rebuttal.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Write your own* (60 min): Write and solve three fraction-division story problems and trade with a family member to solve.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *California impacts* (50 min): Read about changes to California's snowpack and wildfire seasons and discuss solutions families and communities use.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Yavneh and survival* (45 min): Read about Yohanan ben Zaccai's academy at Yavneh and explain how study, synagogues and rabbis preserved Judaism.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Expanding range* (30 min): Add two new notes and play 'Mary Had a Little Lamb' and 'Ode to Joy' (first phrase).
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Pass to set* (40 min): Practice pass-to-set sequences with a partner.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Present* (20 min): Share the campaign with family or co-op.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 75, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Check-in* (90 min): Explain your outline orally to a parent in 3 minutes and revise based on their questions; quiz on vague pronouns.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on fraction word problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Take the Unit 4 test on climate, circulation and global temperature change.

- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take the Respect and Bullying Prevention unit test.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Planning an argument essay
- Objective: Research a debatable question
- Objective: Write a clear claim and gather evidence
- Vocabulary: thesis, paraphrase, quotation, source
- Materials: two to three articles on a chosen issue (e.g., Should zoos exist? Should kids have phones?), note cards
- Assessment: Completed outline and pronoun quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Fractions in real-world problems
- Objective: Solve multi-step fraction division word problems
- Objective: Use division to find lengths and areas
- Vocabulary: fractional length, fractional area
- Materials: ribbon or string, ruler, recipe card
- Assessment: Word problem quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Global temperature rise
- Objective: Analyze data on global temperatures
- Objective: Ask questions about factors causing warming
- Vocabulary: greenhouse effect, greenhouse gas, carbon dioxide, fossil fuels, climate change
- Materials: two jars, thermometers, plastic wrap, lamp, NASA Climate Kids graphs
- Assessment: Greenhouse lab write-up and Unit 4 test.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Exile and diaspora
- Objective: Trace the movements of the Jewish people
- Objective: Explain how Judaism survived dispersion
- Vocabulary: exile, Babylonian Captivity, Diaspora, synagogue, rabbi, Yavneh
- Materials: map of the Mediterranean and Near East, image of the Arch of Titus
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Reading pitch on the staff
- Objective: Read notes on the treble clef
- Objective: Begin an instrument (recorder, keyboard or ukulele)
- Vocabulary: staff, treble clef, bass clef, pitch, ledger line
- Materials: recorder, keyboard or ukulele, note-naming flashcards
- Assessment: Note-naming quiz and playing check.

### Physical Education 6: Volleyball: forearm pass and set
- Objective: Perform a forearm pass (bump)
- Objective: Perform an overhead set
- Vocabulary: forearm pass, platform, set, ready position
- Materials: volleyball or beach ball, net or rope
- Assessment: Passing accuracy check.

### Health 6: Cyberbullying and advocacy for kindness
- Objective: Respond to cyberbullying
- Objective: Create an anti-bullying message
- Vocabulary: cyberbullying, block, report, advocacy
- Materials: poster paper
- Assessment: Campaign and unit test.
