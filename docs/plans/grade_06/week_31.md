## Day 1 — Monday (school day 151, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Colin's tantrum* (90 min): Read chapters 16-19 and explain how Mary's response to Colin shows her growth.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Reaction time data* (60 min): Measure reaction time 25 times with the ruler-drop test and record the catch distance in centimeters.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Digestion in a bag* (50 min): Model chemical and mechanical digestion by mashing crackers with water and vinegar in a bag, then squeezing through a 'small intestine' stocking.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Patricians and plebeians* (45 min): Read about the Conflict of the Orders and explain how plebeians gained rights.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Elements of dance* (30 min): Explore levels, pathways and energy by moving across the room in 10 different ways.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *FITT* (40 min): Learn the FITT principle and identify FITT in three sample workouts.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Chronic diseases* (20 min): Research one chronic disease (asthma, diabetes, heart disease, cancer) and make a fact card.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 152, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Into the garden* (90 min): Read chapters 20-22 and identify the garden as a symbol of healing.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Making histograms* (60 min): Group your reaction data into equal bins and draw a histogram with labeled axes.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Length of the gut* (50 min): Measure out 9 meters of string to represent the digestive tract and label each organ's section.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Three branches* (45 min): Diagram consuls, Senate and assemblies and show checks and balances among them.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Greek folk dance* (30 min): Learn a basic Greek line dance (e.g., Kalamatianos steps) from a video.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Cardio workout* (40 min): Complete a cardio workout and write its FITT.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Asthma* (20 min): Learn asthma triggers and how an inhaler helps; discuss how to help a friend having an attack.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 153, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Magic* (90 min): Read chapters 23-25 and discuss what the children mean by 'Magic'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Dot plot or histogram?* (60 min): Compare a dot plot and histogram of the same data and write when each display is more useful.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Saliva enzyme* (50 min): Chew a plain cracker for 1 minute and note the change in taste; explain how enzymes break starch into sugar.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Twelve Tables* (45 min): Read selected laws of the Twelve Tables and explain why written law mattered (compare with Hammurabi).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Mexican folk dance* (30 min): Learn the basic zapateado step from ballet folklorico and discuss its regional origin.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Strength workout* (40 min): Complete a body-weight strength circuit (squats, lunges, planks, push-ups) and write its FITT.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Risk factors* (20 min): Sort controllable and uncontrollable risk factors for chronic disease.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 154, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Homecoming* (90 min): Read chapters 26-27 and compare the ending with the opening chapters.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Interpreting histograms* (60 min): Answer questions about histograms of California city temperatures and dog weights, describing shape and spread.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *System connections* (50 min): Map how nutrients travel from the small intestine through blood to cells and how kidneys remove waste.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Rome and the U.S.* (45 min): Compare the Roman Republic with the U.S. government in a chart (veto, Senate, written law).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Indian dance* (30 min): Watch a Bharatanatyam excerpt and try three hand gestures (mudras) that tell a story.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Flexibility workout* (40 min): Complete a yoga-based flexibility session and write its FITT.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Family history* (20 min): Complete a simple family health history with a parent.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 155, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write two theme statements for the novel and take the final novel test.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on building and reading histograms.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on the digestive and excretory systems.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write an essay paragraph on the Roman Republic's influence on American government; quiz on vocabulary.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Write how one dance communicates its community's history or purpose.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Check* (40 min): Quiz on FITT and the principle of overload.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a paragraph on habits that lower chronic disease risk; complete a check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: The Secret Garden, chapters 16-27
- Objective: Determine theme
- Objective: Analyze resolution
- Vocabulary: Magic, tantrum, symbol, renewal, theme
- Materials: The Secret Garden
- Assessment: Novel test and theme statements.

### Mathematics 6: Histograms
- Objective: Organize data into bins
- Objective: Make and interpret histograms
- Vocabulary: histogram, bin, frequency, skewed
- Materials: stopwatch, graph paper
- Assessment: Histogram quiz and reaction-time display.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Digestive and excretory systems
- Objective: Model digestion
- Objective: Explain how digestion connects to circulation and cells
- Vocabulary: digestion, enzyme, stomach, small intestine, nutrient, kidney
- Materials: zip bag, crackers, water, vinegar, old pantyhose or sieve, string 9 m long
- Assessment: Digestion model write-up and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: The Roman Republic
- Objective: Describe the structure of the Republic's government
- Objective: Compare it to the U.S. government
- Vocabulary: patrician, plebeian, consul, Senate, tribune, veto, Twelve Tables, checks and balances
- Materials: Twelve Tables excerpts (Avalon or Fordham), U.S. Constitution summary
- Assessment: Comparison chart and paragraph.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Elements of dance and cultural dance
- Objective: Use body, energy, space and time in movement
- Objective: Interpret the cultural meaning of dances
- Vocabulary: levels, pathways, tempo, energy, folk dance
- Materials: open space, music player, videos of folk dances
- Assessment: Movement participation and written interpretation.

### Physical Education 6: FITT principle
- Objective: Explain the FITT principle
- Objective: Apply FITT to each fitness component
- Vocabulary: frequency, intensity, time, type, overload, progression
- Materials: fitness log, stopwatch
- Assessment: FITT quiz.

### Health 6: Noncommunicable diseases and prevention
- Objective: Describe chronic diseases
- Objective: Identify lifestyle risk factors
- Vocabulary: chronic disease, diabetes, asthma, heart disease, risk factor
- Materials: family health history form
- Assessment: Paragraph and check.
