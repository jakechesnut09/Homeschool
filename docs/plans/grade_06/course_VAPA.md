# Visual and Performing Arts 6 (Grade 6)

A year-long arts survey aligned to the 2019 California Arts Standards. Students keep a sketchbook, study the elements and principles of art, create works inspired by the ancient civilizations they study in history, learn one- and two-point perspective, read and compose music, improvise and perform theatre scenes, make a stop-motion animation, choreograph a short dance and curate a final portfolio exhibition.

*Framework:* 2019 California Arts Standards for Public Schools, Prekindergarten Through Grade Twelve; 2020 CA Arts Education Framework  
*Time:* 30 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Sketchbook and the Elements of Art (weeks 1–4)
Artists build images from line, shape, form, value, color, texture and space, and develop ideas in a sketchbook.

- Week 1: Sketchbook launch and line
- Week 2: Shape, form and value
- Week 3: Color theory
- Week 4: Texture, space and principles of design

### Unit 2: Art of the Ancient World (weeks 5–9)
Art reveals how ancient peoples lived and what they valued.

- Week 5: Prehistoric and Mesopotamian art
- Week 6: Egyptian art
- Week 7: Greek pottery
- Week 8: Art of India and China
- Week 9: Roman mosaics and art preservation

### Unit 3: Drawing Space: Perspective and Design (weeks 10–13)
Perspective creates the illusion of depth; designers plan spaces for real users.

- Week 10: One-point perspective
- Week 11: Two-point perspective
- Week 12: Design for people: architecture
- Week 13: Visual persuasion: posters

### Unit 4: Music Literacy and Performance (weeks 14–19)
Reading notation and practicing with clear criteria lets musicians perform accurately and expressively.

- Week 14: Rhythm and meter
- Week 15: Reading pitch on the staff
- Week 16: Scales and melodies
- Week 17: Dynamics, tempo and expression
- Week 18: Singing and ensemble
- Week 19: Music of world cultures

### Unit 5: Listening and Composing (weeks 20–22)
Composers organize musical ideas into forms like AB and ABA to express intent.

- Week 20: Form in music
- Week 21: Composing an ABA piece
- Week 22: Music and other arts: film scoring

### Unit 6: Theatre: Improvisation and Scene Work (weeks 23–26)
Actors use voice, body and objectives to create believable characters for an audience.

- Week 23: Theatre games and improvisation
- Week 24: Voice and movement for character
- Week 25: Adapting a story for the stage
- Week 26: Rehearsal and performance

### Unit 7: Media Arts: Photography and Stop-Motion (weeks 27–30)
Media artists use composition, timing and editing to tell stories with images.

- Week 27: Photography composition
- Week 28: Photo story and editing
- Week 29: Stop-motion animation: planning
- Week 30: Stop-motion animation: production

### Unit 8: Dance and Movement (weeks 31–32)
Dance communicates ideas and cultural meaning through movement, space and time.

- Week 31: Elements of dance and cultural dance
- Week 32: Choreography

### Unit 9: Portfolio and Exhibition (weeks 33–36)
Artists select, reflect on and present their work to communicate growth and meaning.

- Week 33: Personal investigation artwork
- Week 34: Museums and curation
- Week 35: Portfolio and artist statement
- Week 36: Arts showcase

## Standards

- **DA:Cn11.1.6** Interpret how the movement and qualities of a dance communicate its cultural, historical and/or community purpose or meaning.
- **DA:Cr1.1.6** Relate similar or contrasting ideas to develop choreography using a variety of stimuli (music, observed dance, literary forms, natural phenomena, personal experience).
- **DA:Pr4.1.6** Refine partner and ensemble skills in the ability to judge distance and spatial design; establish diverse pathways, levels and patterns.
- **MA:Cr1.1.6** Formulate variations of goals and solutions for media artworks by practicing chosen generative methods.
- **MA:Cr3.1.6** Refine and modify media artworks, using identified principles such as movement, balance and timing.
- **MA:Pr5.1.6** Develop and practice a variety of artistic, design, technical and soft skills in producing media artworks.
- **MU:Cn11.0.6** Demonstrate understanding of relationships between music and other arts, disciplines, contexts and daily life.
- **MU:Cr1.1.6** Generate simple rhythmic, melodic and harmonic phrases within AB and ABA forms that convey expressive intent.
- **MU:Cr2.1.6** Select, organize, construct and document personal musical ideas for arrangements and compositions within AB or ABA form.
- **MU:Cr3.2.6** Present the final version of a documented personal composition or arrangement, demonstrating craftsmanship.
- **MU:Pr4.2.6** Explain how understanding the structure and elements of music are used in performance, reading standard notation.
- **MU:Pr5.1.6** Identify and apply teacher-provided criteria to rehearse, refine and determine when a piece is ready to perform.
- **MU:Pr6.1.6** Perform music with technical accuracy and stylistic expression to convey the creator's intent.
- **MU:Re7.1.6** Select or choose contrasting music to listen to and compare connections to interest, purpose or context.
- **MU:Re7.2.6** Describe how the elements of music and expressive qualities relate to the structure of the pieces.
- **MU:Re9.1.6** Apply teacher-provided criteria to evaluate musical works or performances.
- **TH:Cr1.1.6** Identify possible solutions to staging challenges and explore a character's inner thoughts and objectives in a drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Cr2.1.6** Use critical analysis to improve, refine and evolve original ideas and artistic choices in a devised or scripted drama/theatre work.
- **TH:Pr4.1.6** Identify the essential events in a story or script that make up the dramatic structure; experiment with vocal and physical choices to develop a believable character.
- **TH:Pr6.1.6** Adapt a drama/theatre work and present it informally for an audience.
- **TH:Re7.1.6** Describe and record personal reactions to artistic choices in a drama/theatre work.
- **VA:Cn10.1.6** Generate a collection of ideas reflecting current interests and concerns that could be investigated in art-making.
- **VA:Cn11.1.6** Analyze how art reflects changing times, traditions, resources and cultural uses.
- **VA:Cr1.1.6** Combine concepts collaboratively to generate innovative ideas for creating art.
- **VA:Cr1.2.6** Formulate an artistic investigation of personally relevant content for creating art.
- **VA:Cr2.1.6** Demonstrate openness in trying new ideas, materials, methods and approaches in making works of art and design.
- **VA:Cr2.2.6** Explain environmental implications of conservation, care and clean-up of art materials, tools and equipment.
- **VA:Cr2.3.6** Design or redesign objects, places or systems that meet the identified needs of diverse users.
- **VA:Cr3.1.6** Reflect on whether personal artwork conveys the intended meaning and revise accordingly.
- **VA:Pr4.1.6** Analyze similarities and differences associated with preserving and presenting two-dimensional, three-dimensional and digital artwork.
- **VA:Pr5.1.6** Individually or collaboratively, develop a visual plan for displaying works of art.
- **VA:Pr6.1.6** Assess, explain and provide evidence of how museums or other venues reflect history and values of a community.
- **VA:Re7.1.6** Identify and interpret works of art that reveal how people live around the world and what they value.
- **VA:Re7.2.6** Analyze ways that visual components and cultural associations suggested by images influence ideas, emotions and actions.
- **VA:Re8.1.6** Interpret art by distinguishing between relevant and non-relevant contextual information and analyzing subject matter, form and use of media.
- **VA:Re9.1.6** Develop and apply relevant criteria to evaluate a work of art.
