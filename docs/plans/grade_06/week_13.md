## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Hero's journey* (90 min): Map Odysseus's and Brian's stories onto the hero's journey and plan your own hero's journey story or modern myth.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Size of divisor and quotient* (60 min): Predict and then compute what happens to quotients like 12 / 6, 12 / 1, 12 / 1/2, and write a rule about divisors less than 1.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Flashlight angles* (50 min): Shine a flashlight on graph paper straight down and at an angle; count squares lit to model why the equator is warmer.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Geography of Canaan* (45 min): Map Abraham's journey from Ur to Canaan as described in Genesis and the location of ancient Israel.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Analyze posters* (30 min): Analyze three public service posters (e.g., WPA National Park posters) for focal point, color and text.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Give-and-go* (40 min): Practice the give-and-go with a partner and cut to the basket.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Defining bullying* (20 min): Distinguish bullying (repeated, power imbalance) from conflict in 8 scenarios.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Draft beginning* (90 min): Draft your story's opening and call to adventure using vivid setting details.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Two meanings* (60 min): Write two different story problems for 6 / 2 (how many groups vs. how much per group) and draw a diagram for each.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Convection cells* (50 min): Draw global convection cells (Hadley, Ferrel, polar) and explain how they form from uneven heating.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Monotheism* (45 min): Compare monotheism and polytheism and explain why historians consider Judaism's belief in one God significant.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Plan a message* (30 min): Choose a message (water conservation, reading) and sketch three thumbnail layouts.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Defense* (40 min): Practice defensive slides and staying between your player and the basket.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Forms* (20 min): Chart physical, verbal, social/relational and cyber bullying with examples and effects.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 63, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Draft trials* (90 min): Draft two trials your hero faces, using dialogue and transitions that show time shifts.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *How many groups?* (60 min): Use measuring cups to find how many 1/4 cups fit in 3 cups and how many 3/4 cups fit in 3 cups; record as division equations.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Coriolis effect* (50 min): Draw a straight line on a spinning paper plate to model the Coriolis effect and explain curved winds.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *The Exodus* (45 min): Read the Exodus account and map the route; explain the significance of the Exodus in Jewish tradition and Passover.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Lettering* (30 min): Practice block and decorative lettering for your headline.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Rebounding* (40 min): Practice boxing out and rebounding in a 1v1 drill.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Bystander roles* (20 min): Learn bystander, upstander and reporter roles and practice safe upstander responses.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 64, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Revise and edit* (90 min): Revise the climax and return; edit for pronoun case and consistent verb tense, then type the final draft.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Using diagrams* (60 min): Draw tape diagrams to solve 8 'how many groups' fraction problems, including answers that are not whole numbers.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Global wind map* (50 min): Label trade winds, westerlies and polar easterlies on a world map and explain which affects California.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *The Ten Commandments* (45 min): Read Exodus 20 and categorize the commandments as duties to God and to other people; connect to later legal traditions.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Final poster* (30 min): Create the final poster with a clear focal point and limited color palette.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *2v2 games* (40 min): Play 2v2 half-court games applying offensive and defensive strategies.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Reporting* (20 min): Write a script for reporting bullying to a trusted adult.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 65, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Share and check* (90 min): Read your story aloud and score it with the narrative rubric; take the unit test on myths and the Odyssey.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: match equations, diagrams and stories for fraction division.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on uneven heating and global winds.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on early Hebrew history and beliefs.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & critique* (30 min): Ask two viewers what the message is and revise if they misread it.

- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a bullying scenario quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Writing: modern myth or hero's journey story
- Objective: Write a narrative using hero's-journey structure
- Objective: Use precise language and transitions to show time shifts
- Vocabulary: hero's journey, call to adventure, mentor, trial, return
- Materials: hero's journey diagram, computer
- Assessment: Narrative scored on rubric; unit test.

### Mathematics 6: Meanings of division
- Objective: Interpret division as 'how many groups' or 'how much in each group'
- Objective: Relate multiplication and division equations
- Vocabulary: dividend, divisor, quotient, equal groups
- Materials: fraction strips, measuring cups
- Assessment: Quiz on interpreting division and fraction diagrams.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Uneven heating and global winds
- Objective: Explain why the equator receives more energy than the poles
- Objective: Model global wind patterns and the Coriolis effect
- Vocabulary: latitude, angle of insolation, global winds, Coriolis effect, jet stream
- Materials: flashlight, graph paper, globe or ball, lazy Susan or paper plate, marker
- Assessment: Global wind map and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Origins of the ancient Hebrews
- Objective: Describe monotheism and its significance
- Objective: Explain the roles of Abraham and Moses as described in the Hebrew Bible
- Vocabulary: monotheism, covenant, Torah, Exodus, patriarch, Canaan
- Materials: map of ancient Canaan and Egypt, Hebrew Bible excerpts: Genesis 12 and Exodus 20
- Assessment: Map and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Visual persuasion: posters
- Objective: Analyze how images influence ideas
- Objective: Design a persuasive poster
- Vocabulary: typography, focal point, message, audience
- Materials: poster paper, markers, sample public service posters
- Assessment: Persuasive poster and viewer feedback.

### Physical Education 6: Basketball: offense and defense
- Objective: Use give-and-go and pick-and-roll concepts
- Objective: Play person-to-person defense
- Vocabulary: give-and-go, screen, help defense, box out, rebound
- Materials: basketball, hoop
- Assessment: Diagrammed play and observation.

### Health 6: Bullying and harassment
- Objective: Define forms of bullying
- Objective: Explain bystander roles
- Vocabulary: bullying, harassment, bystander, upstander, power imbalance
- Materials: StopBullying.gov materials
- Assessment: Scenario quiz.
