## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Shelter and food* (90 min): Read chapters 5-6 and list Brian's survival decisions with page numbers; rate each decision smart or risky.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Defining equivalent ratios* (60 min): Generate five ratios equivalent to 3:4 by multiplying both quantities, then decide whether 6 lists of ratio pairs are equivalent.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Plan the investigation* (50 min): Plan an investigation comparing temperature change of 100 g vs. 200 g of water given equal heating time; identify variables.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *City-states* (45 min): Read about Ur and Uruk and describe how a Sumerian city-state was organized.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Rules of Egyptian art* (30 min): Analyze a tomb painting for composite view, hierarchical scale and registers.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Sheet games* (40 min): Play bedsheet or parachute games (popcorn, mushroom) that require timing together.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Strengths inventory* (20 min): List 10 personal strengths and ask a family member to add three.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Self-pity* (90 min): Read chapters 7-8; write a paragraph explaining Brian's lesson that self-pity 'didn't work', citing two quotes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Double number lines* (60 min): Draw double number lines for a 2-mile per 30-minute walking pace and answer 6 questions about time and distance.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Mass investigation* (50 min): Run the mass investigation under a lamp, recording temperature every 2 minutes.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Ziggurats* (45 min): Build a model ziggurat from blocks or sugar cubes and explain its religious and political role.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Figure study* (30 min): Draw a figure on a grid using Egyptian proportions.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Cooperative tag* (40 min): Play 'everybody's it' and 'freeze tag with rescue' and track steps.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Self-talk* (20 min): Rewrite 6 negative self-talk statements as realistic positive ones.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Fire* (90 min): Read chapter 9 and annotate how Paulsen describes fire as a 'friend'; identify personification.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Price per item* (60 min): Use real grocery receipts to build double number lines for cost and quantity and find the cost of 1, 5 and 12 items.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Sand vs. water* (50 min): Heat equal masses of sand and water for 15 minutes and compare temperature changes; connect to beaches and coastal climates.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Standard of Ur* (45 min): Analyze the 'War' and 'Peace' sides of the Standard of Ur and infer the social hierarchy.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Tomb painting* (30 min): Plan a 'tomb painting' of your own daily life in registers.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Team circuit* (40 min): Complete a partner fitness circuit where partners must sync reps.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Growth mindset* (20 min): Read a short article on brain growth with practice and write about something you improved with effort.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Survival nonfiction* (90 min): Read a wilderness-survival article and the Ten Essentials list, then summarize the central idea objectively in 4-5 sentences.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Same speed?* (60 min): Time yourself walking 20 steps, then use a double number line to predict time for 60 and 100 steps; test one prediction.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Analyze* (50 min): Graph both investigations and write how mass and type of matter affect temperature change.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Social pyramid* (45 min): Create a social pyramid of Sumerian society and explain how religion supported the rulers' power.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Color and finish* (30 min): Complete the painting with Egyptian colors and a hieroglyph cartouche of your name.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Design a game* (40 min): Design a cooperative game with rules and teach it to family members.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Resilience story* (20 min): Read a biography excerpt of someone who overcame adversity and identify resilience traits.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Hatchet chapters 5-9: first lessons
- Objective: Explain how Brian's attitude changes
- Objective: Read informational text on survival priorities
- Vocabulary: self-pity, adaptation, shelter, priority, hypothermia
- Materials: Hatchet, ReadWorks or CommonLit wilderness survival article, National Park Service 'Ten Essentials' list
- Assessment: Objective summary and reading quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Equivalent ratios and double number lines
- Objective: Generate equivalent ratios by multiplying or dividing
- Objective: Use double number lines to solve problems
- Vocabulary: equivalent ratios, double number line, scale factor, per
- Materials: grid paper, grocery receipts
- Assessment: Quiz on equivalent ratios and double number lines.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Mass, material and temperature change
- Objective: Plan an investigation of how mass and type of matter affect temperature change
- Vocabulary: specific heat, mass, energy transferred
- Materials: two cups, water and sand (or vegetable oil), kitchen scale, thermometer, lamp or sunny window
- Assessment: Lab report with graphs and CER.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Sumerian city-states and religion
- Objective: Describe Sumerian city-states
- Objective: Explain the link between religion and political order
- Vocabulary: city-state, ziggurat, polytheism, priest, king, social hierarchy
- Materials: images of the Ziggurat of Ur and Standard of Ur, sugar cubes or blocks
- Assessment: Artifact analysis paragraph.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Egyptian art
- Objective: Identify the conventions of Egyptian art
- Objective: Create a profile figure in Egyptian style
- Vocabulary: composite view, hierarchical scale, register, cartouche
- Materials: paper bag or kraft paper, colored pencils, gold marker
- Assessment: Egyptian-style painting with explanation.

### Physical Education 6: Cooperative games and fitness
- Objective: Combine skills in cooperative games
- Objective: Maintain moderate-to-vigorous activity
- Vocabulary: cooperative game, MVPA
- Materials: parachute or bedsheet, balls, pedometer or phone step counter
- Assessment: Original cooperative game.

### Health 6: Self-esteem and growth mindset
- Objective: Describe self-esteem and resilience
- Objective: Practice positive self-talk
- Vocabulary: self-esteem, resilience, growth mindset, self-talk
- Materials: index cards, health journal
- Assessment: Goal and check.
