## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Introduction* (90 min): Draft an introduction with a hook, background and a precise claim.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Packing cubes* (60 min): Pack a small box with 1/2-inch cubes, count them, and show that the count x 1/8 cubic inch equals l x w x h.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Earthquake data* (50 min): Use the USGS 'Latest Earthquakes' map to record 20 California earthquakes from the past month and plot them on a map.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Mountains and seas* (45 min): Make a salt-dough relief map of Greece showing mountains and coastlines.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Whole and half steps* (30 min): Find whole and half steps on a keyboard diagram and build the C major scale.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Underhand serve* (40 min): Practice underhand serves over the net from 6 and then 9 meters.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Mental health continuum* (20 min): Place examples on a mental health continuum (thriving to struggling).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 77, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Body paragraphs* (90 min): Draft body paragraphs that explain how each piece of evidence supports your reason.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Volume formulas* (60 min): Compute volumes of 10 prisms with fractional edges using V = lwh and V = Bh.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Patterns along faults* (50 min): Compare your map with a map of the San Andreas fault system and describe the pattern.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *The polis* (45 min): Explain how mountains led to independent city-states and diagram a polis with acropolis and agora.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Major vs. minor* (30 min): Listen to major and minor versions of the same tune in Chrome Music Lab and describe the mood.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Overhand serve* (40 min): Learn the overhand serve toss and contact.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Depression and anxiety* (20 min): Read KidsHealth articles and list warning signs of each.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 78, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Counterclaim* (90 min): Draft a counterclaim-and-rebuttal paragraph and a conclusion that follows from the argument.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Missing dimensions* (60 min): Use fraction division to find a missing edge when volume and two edges are known (6 problems).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Landslide tray* (50 min): Build a sand slope in a tray and add water gradually to observe when a landslide happens; relate to wildfire burn scars and storms.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Minoans and Mycenaeans* (45 min): Read about Knossos and Mycenae and compare the two earlier Aegean cultures.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Practice with criteria* (30 min): Practice 'Ode to Joy' using criteria (steady tempo, correct notes, good tone) and mark trouble spots.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Cooperative rally* (40 min): Play a cooperative rally game aiming for 3 contacts per side.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Stigma* (20 min): Discuss myths about mental illness and rewrite them as facts.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 79, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise for formal style (no slang, no 'I think') and add transitions like 'furthermore' and 'however'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit review* (60 min): Mixed review of fraction division and volume, including one aquarium or garden-bed design problem.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Volcano monitoring* (50 min): Read how scientists monitor Mount Shasta and Lassen with seismometers and gas sensors.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Trade and colonies* (45 min): Map Greek colonies around the Mediterranean and Black Sea and list traded goods (olive oil, wine, pottery).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Refine* (30 min): Slow-practice the trouble spots with a metronome and gradually increase tempo.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Game play* (40 min): Play modified volleyball with rotation and placement strategy.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Treatment works* (20 min): Learn about counseling and treatment and why getting help early matters.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 80, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Edit and publish* (90 min): Edit for pronoun use, commas and spelling and type the final essay; self-score with the argument rubric.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit 4 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 4 assessment and reflect on the meaning of division.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on hazard patterns and forecasting.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a CER-style paragraph: How did geography shape Greek life?
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & check* (30 min): Record a performance of the scale and melody and evaluate it with the criteria.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Reflect* (40 min): Write about where to place the ball to win points and why.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a signs-and-symptoms matching check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Drafting and revising the argument
- Objective: Draft an argument essay with formal style
- Objective: Revise for logic and transitions
- Vocabulary: formal style, rebuttal, transition
- Materials: outline, computer
- Assessment: Argument essay scored on rubric.

### Mathematics 6: Volume with fractional edge lengths
- Objective: Find volume of prisms with fractional edges
- Objective: Show V = lwh by packing unit cubes of fractional size
- Vocabulary: volume, cubic unit, rectangular prism, base area
- Materials: small box, 1/2-inch cubes or sugar cubes, ruler
- Assessment: Unit 4 end-of-unit assessment.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Earthquakes, volcanoes and landslides
- Objective: Analyze patterns in natural hazard data
- Objective: Describe how forecasts are made
- Vocabulary: natural hazard, fault, magnitude, epicenter, forecast, landslide
- Materials: USGS earthquake map, graph paper, tray of sand and water
- Assessment: Earthquake map analysis and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Geography and the Greek city-state
- Objective: Explain how geography encouraged independent city-states
- Objective: Describe Greek trade and colonization
- Vocabulary: peninsula, polis, acropolis, agora, colony, Minoan, Mycenaean
- Materials: relief map of Greece, clay or salt dough
- Assessment: Relief map and paragraph.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Scales and melodies
- Objective: Play a major scale
- Objective: Perform a melody with correct rhythm
- Vocabulary: scale, whole step, half step, major, minor, melody
- Materials: instrument, Chrome Music Lab, sheet music for 'Ode to Joy'
- Assessment: Recorded scale and melody.

### Physical Education 6: Volleyball: serving and game play
- Objective: Serve underhand and overhand
- Objective: Play cooperative and competitive volleyball
- Vocabulary: underhand serve, overhand serve, rotation, rally
- Materials: volleyball, net or rope
- Assessment: Serve accuracy and game observation.

### Health 6: Mental health conditions
- Objective: Recognize signs of depression and anxiety
- Objective: Reduce stigma
- Vocabulary: mental health, depression, anxiety, stigma, treatment
- Materials: KidsHealth teen articles
- Assessment: Matching check.
