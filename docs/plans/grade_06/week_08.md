## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *The moose and the tornado* (90 min): Read chapters 14-15 and explain how these setbacks test the 'new Brian'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Tape diagrams* (60 min): Use tape diagrams to solve 'ratio of boys to girls is 2:3 and there are 30 students' style problems (8 problems).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Water cycle in a bowl* (50 min): Build a mini water cycle (bowl with warm salty water, cup in middle, plastic wrap and ice on top) and observe after an hour.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *First empires* (45 min): Read about Sargon of Akkad and Hammurabi of Babylon and map their empires.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Mandalas* (30 min): Study mandala designs and create a radially symmetric mandala using a compass and protractor.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Shooting* (40 min): Practice instep shots at a target from 10 meters, aiming for the corners.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Qualities* (20 min): Rank 12 friendship qualities and explain your top three.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Back to the plane* (90 min): Read chapters 16-17; list the items in the survival pack and predict how they will change his life.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Trail mix lab* (60 min): Make trail mix in a 3:2 nuts-to-raisins ratio for a total of 2 cups, using a tape diagram to plan amounts before measuring.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Transpiration* (50 min): Tie a plastic bag around a leafy branch and record water collected after 24 hours; explain transpiration.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Reading the Code* (45 min): Read 20 laws from Hammurabi's Code and sort them into categories (property, family, trade, injury).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Buddhist sculpture* (30 min): Analyze images of Gandharan and Chinese Buddha sculptures and note how style changed as Buddhism spread.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Offensive spacing* (40 min): Learn to create passing triangles and move to open space in a 3v1 drill.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Healthy vs. unhealthy* (20 min): Sort friendship behaviors (support, gossip, exclusion, honesty, control) into a spectrum chart.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 38, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Rescue* (90 min): Read chapters 18-19 and the epilogue; discuss how the epilogue changes our understanding of the story's ending.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Choosing representations* (60 min): Solve the same 4 problems with a table, a double number line and a tape diagram, and write which was most efficient and why.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Energy in the cycle* (50 min): Draw a water cycle model labeling where energy from the sun and the force of gravity drive each process.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Fair or not?* (45 min): Analyze how punishments differed by social class and debate whether the code was 'just' by Babylonian and by modern standards.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Brush strokes* (30 min): Practice basic Chinese brush strokes and paint bamboo stalks and leaves.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Defense* (40 min): Practice defensive stance, jockeying and staying goal-side.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Boundaries* (20 min): Practice setting a boundary with a friend in 3 role-plays.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 39, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Listen and compare* (90 min): Listen to a chapter of the audiobook while following along and note what the narrator's voice adds or changes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a mixed ratio review set covering language, equivalence, tables, graphs and tape diagrams.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *California's water* (50 min): Trace how Sierra Nevada snowmelt reaches California homes using a map; discuss droughts and the snowpack.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Stele analysis* (45 min): Examine the stele image and explain why carving laws in public mattered.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Ink landscape* (30 min): Paint a vertical scroll landscape with mountains and mist using ink wash values.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Game day* (40 min): Play small-sided soccer, rotating positions and practicing fair play.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Making friends* (20 min): Plan ways to make new friends through clubs, sports or co-ops.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 40, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take the final Hatchet comprehension test with short constructed responses.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit 2 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 2 end-of-unit assessment and write a reflection on the strategy you rely on most.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Explain why the water in the bowl's center cup was not salty (CER) and quiz on water cycle processes.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Unit 2 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 2 test with a document-based short essay on Hammurabi's Code.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Compare your mandala and scroll and write how each reflects its culture's values.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Reflect* (40 min): Write about one offensive and one defensive strategy you used.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a letter describing what kind of friend you want to be; complete a check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Hatchet chapters 14-19 and epilogue
- Objective: Analyze the resolution and epilogue
- Objective: Compare text and audio versions
- Vocabulary: resolution, epilogue, tornado, survival pack
- Materials: Hatchet, library audiobook of Hatchet (optional)
- Assessment: Hatchet novel test.

### Mathematics 6: Part-part-whole ratios and tape diagrams
- Objective: Use tape diagrams for part-part-whole problems
- Objective: Choose an efficient representation
- Vocabulary: tape diagram, part-part-whole, total
- Materials: paper strips, trail-mix ingredients
- Assessment: Unit 2 end-of-unit assessment.

### Science 6 (Integrated): The water cycle
- Objective: Model how sun and gravity drive water through Earth's systems
- Vocabulary: evaporation, transpiration, condensation, precipitation, runoff, groundwater
- Materials: large bowl, plastic wrap, small cup, ice, plastic bag, a houseplant or leafy branch
- Assessment: Water cycle model and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Empires and Hammurabi's Code
- Objective: Describe Sargon's and Hammurabi's empires
- Objective: Analyze Hammurabi's Code as a primary source
- Vocabulary: empire, Akkad, Babylon, code of law, stele
- Materials: Code of Hammurabi excerpts (Avalon Project), image of the Hammurabi stele
- Assessment: Unit 2 test with document-based question.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Art of India and China
- Objective: Interpret mandalas, Buddhist art and Chinese brush painting
- Objective: Practice brush techniques
- Vocabulary: mandala, radial symmetry, calligraphy, ink wash, scroll
- Materials: compass, colored markers, black ink or watercolor, round brush
- Assessment: Mandala and ink landscape.

### Physical Education 6: Soccer: shooting and team play
- Objective: Shoot with the instep
- Objective: Apply spacing and support in small games
- Vocabulary: instep drive, support, spacing, goalkeeper
- Materials: soccer ball, goal or cones
- Assessment: Game-play observation checklist.

### Health 6: Healthy friendships
- Objective: Describe qualities of healthy friendships
- Objective: Recognize unhealthy friendship behaviors
- Vocabulary: trust, loyalty, boundaries, peer, manipulation
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Friendship letter and check.
