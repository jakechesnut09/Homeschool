## Day 1 — Monday (school day 156, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Claim* (90 min): Write a claim answering: Who changes more, Mary or Colin, and what causes the change? Gather five pieces of evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Fair share* (60 min): Use stacks of snap cubes to level out unequal towers and connect the result to adding and dividing.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Neurons and messages* (50 min): Diagram a neuron and the pathway from receptor to spinal cord to brain to muscle.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Punic Wars* (45 min): Map the Punic Wars and Hannibal's route and explain how victory made Rome a Mediterranean power.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Stimulus* (30 min): Choose a stimulus (a poem, the water cycle, a storm) and brainstorm movements.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Review data* (40 min): Review baseline and mid-year scores to choose focus areas.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Ad claims* (20 min): Analyze 4 health-product ads for claims, techniques and missing information.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 157, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Embedding quotes* (90 min): Lesson on embedding quotations smoothly; draft two body paragraphs with embedded quotes and analysis.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Balance point* (60 min): Show on a dot plot that the mean is the point where distances above and below balance, using 4 small data sets.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Reaction time* (50 min): Run the ruler-drop reaction test 10 times with each hand and compare averages.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Crisis in the Republic* (45 min): Read about the gap between rich and poor, the Gracchi and Cicero's defense of the Republic.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Motifs* (30 min): Create three movement motifs and vary them in level and speed.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Write plan* (40 min): Write a 4-week plan with FITT for cardio, strength and flexibility.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Comparison shopping* (20 min): Compare name-brand and generic versions of a product by ingredients and unit price.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 158, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Finish draft* (90 min): Draft the introduction, a counterclaim paragraph and conclusion.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Mean absolute deviation* (60 min): Compute the MAD for two basketball players' points per game and decide who is more consistent.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Touch receptors* (50 min): Use a two-point test with paper clips on fingertip, palm and forearm to map receptor density.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Julius Caesar* (45 min): Read Plutarch's account of Caesar crossing the Rubicon and his assassination; debate whether he was a hero or threat.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Structure* (30 min): Arrange motifs into a 1-minute dance with a beginning, middle and end.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Week 1 workout* (40 min): Complete day 1 of your plan and log it.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Quackery* (20 min): Identify red flags of health fraud (miracle cure, secret formula, testimonials only).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 159, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Revise and edit* (90 min): Revise for sentence variety and consistent formal tone; edit and type the final essay.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Comparing groups* (60 min): Compare two data sets (e.g., plant heights with and without fertilizer) using mean and MAD.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Taste and smell* (50 min): Blindfolded, taste jelly beans with and without holding your nose and record identification accuracy.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Augustus* (45 min): Read excerpts of the Res Gestae and identify how Augustus presented his power while keeping republican forms.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse with a partner using unison and canon.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Nutrition and rest* (40 min): Add hydration, nutrition and sleep goals to your plan and complete day 2.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Decide* (20 min): Use DECIDE to choose a sunscreen or toothpaste and justify the choice.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 160, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Unit check* (90 min): Submit the essay with a self-assessment and take the unit vocabulary test.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on mean and MAD.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a CER explaining why smell affects taste, using your data.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Create a timeline from 509 BCE to 27 CE and take a quiz on the republic's fall.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Perform* (30 min): Perform and record the dance, then explain how it connects to the stimulus.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Check* (40 min): Complete day 3 and have a parent sign off on the plan.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a consumer-health worksheet.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Literary argument essay
- Objective: Write an argument about a literary text
- Objective: Use embedded quotations
- Vocabulary: literary claim, embedded quotation, analysis
- Materials: Secret Garden notes, computer
- Assessment: Literary argument essay rubric.

### Mathematics 6: Mean and mean absolute deviation
- Objective: Understand mean as fair share and balance point
- Objective: Calculate and interpret MAD
- Vocabulary: mean, average, balance point, mean absolute deviation
- Materials: snap cubes, ruler and pencil for balance
- Assessment: Mean and MAD quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): The nervous system and senses
- Objective: Explain how sensory receptors send messages to the brain
- Objective: Investigate reaction time and senses
- Vocabulary: neuron, stimulus, sensory receptor, reflex, brain, spinal cord
- Materials: ruler, paper clips, blindfold, jelly beans or flavored candy
- Assessment: Sense investigation data and CER.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: From republic to empire
- Objective: Explain the expansion of Roman territory
- Objective: Analyze the roles of Julius Caesar and Augustus
- Vocabulary: Punic Wars, Hannibal, dictator, Julius Caesar, Augustus, Pax Romana, Cicero
- Materials: map of Roman expansion, Plutarch's Life of Caesar excerpt, Res Gestae of Augustus excerpt
- Assessment: Timeline and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Choreography
- Objective: Choreograph a short dance from a stimulus
- Objective: Perform with spatial awareness
- Vocabulary: choreography, motif, canon, unison, transition
- Materials: music, poem or natural phenomenon as inspiration
- Assessment: Choreography performance.

### Physical Education 6: Designing a personal fitness plan
- Objective: Write a 4-week personal fitness plan
- Objective: Include warm-up, cool-down and progression
- Vocabulary: fitness plan, progression, rest day
- Materials: fitness log, planning template
- Assessment: Written personal fitness plan.

### Health 6: Consumer health: products and advertising
- Objective: Analyze health product advertising
- Objective: Compare products using criteria
- Vocabulary: consumer, advertising claim, generic, unit price, quackery
- Materials: shampoo, toothpaste or sunscreen samples, price tags
- Assessment: Consumer worksheet.
