## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Reading assessment* (90 min): Read an unfamiliar story and article and answer evidence-based constructed-response questions.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review: ratios and number* (60 min): Complete a review set on ratios, percents, fraction division and decimals; star problems to revisit.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Prepare presentation* (50 min): Create a presentation of your solar oven design process with photos, graphs and conclusions.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Choose a legacy* (45 min): Choose one ancient legacy (writing, law, democracy, mathematics, religion, engineering) to trace across at least three civilizations.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Install* (30 min): Install your exhibition following your display plan.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Plan field day* (40 min): Plan a family or co-op field day with stations from each unit.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Present* (20 min): Present your advocacy project to family, co-op or a community group.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *On-demand writing* (90 min): Write a timed argument or explanatory essay from a prompt with two short sources.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review: expressions and geometry* (60 min): Complete a review set on equations, expressions, area, surface area and volume.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Present* (50 min): Present to your family or co-op and answer questions about the science behind your design.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Research* (45 min): Gather evidence including at least two primary sources from the year and take notes.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse a short music performance and dance or monologue for the showcase.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Field day* (40 min): Run the field day, leading stations and encouraging others.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review game* (20 min): Play a review game on mental health, safety and personal health.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Grammar and vocabulary* (90 min): Take a cumulative test on pronouns, punctuation, roots and figurative language.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review: rational numbers and data* (60 min): Complete a review set on negatives, the coordinate plane and statistics; then take the Khan Academy 6th grade course challenge.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Year review: energy* (50 min): Make a concept map showing how energy connects thermal energy, weather, forces and living systems.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Create* (45 min): Create a museum exhibit poster or slide show connecting the legacy to life in California today.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Showcase* (30 min): Host the showcase: guide visitors as a docent and perform.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Reflection* (40 min): Write a reflection on your favorite unit and biggest improvement.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Final assessment* (20 min): Take the end-of-year health assessment.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Portfolio* (90 min): Choose your best three pieces from the year and write a cover letter explaining your growth.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Final exam* (60 min): Take a 40-problem cumulative final assessment covering all grade 6 domains.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Final assessment* (50 min): Take a cumulative assessment covering all units.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Present* (45 min): Present your exhibit to family or co-op as a museum docent and answer questions.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Feedback* (30 min): Collect written comments from visitors and summarize them.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Summer plan* (40 min): Write a summer activity plan with at least 60 minutes of activity per day.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Goal review* (20 min): Review your wellness wheel from week 1 and re-rate each area.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Looking ahead* (90 min): Create a summer reading list of 6 books and share your favorite read of the year in a book talk.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Reflection and goals* (60 min): Correct the final, write a reflection on your growth this year, and set goals for Math 7.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Reflection* (50 min): Write a reflection on your favorite investigation and a question you want to explore in grade 7.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Final assessment* (45 min): Complete the year-end timeline and take a cumulative final assessment on all seven civilizations.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Final reflection* (30 min): Write a final reflection on your favorite project and set an arts goal for grade 7.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Final check* (40 min): Complete a year-end quiz on fitness concepts and sportsmanship.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Reflect* (20 min): Write a reflection on your growth and a summer health and safety plan.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Year-end reflection and assessment
- Objective: Demonstrate grade 6 reading and writing skills
- Objective: Reflect on growth as a reader and writer
- Materials: writing portfolio, summer reading list
- Assessment: Final reading, writing and language assessments; portfolio letter.

### Mathematics 6: Cumulative review and final assessment
- Objective: Review all grade 6 standards
- Objective: Demonstrate mastery on a final assessment
- Materials: review packets, Khan Academy course challenge
- Assessment: Cumulative final exam.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Capstone presentation and year review
- Objective: Communicate design results
- Objective: Review major ideas of grade 6 science
- Materials: science notebook, slides or poster
- Assessment: Capstone presentation rubric and cumulative assessment.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Legacies of the ancient world: final project
- Objective: Synthesize learning across civilizations
- Objective: Present a legacy project
- Vocabulary: legacy, diffusion, synthesis
- Materials: year-long timeline, poster board or slides
- Assessment: Legacy project rubric and cumulative final.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Arts showcase
- Objective: Present an exhibition and performance
- Objective: Reflect on artistic growth
- Vocabulary: showcase, docent, reflection
- Materials: exhibition space, instrument, audience
- Assessment: Showcase rubric and final reflection.

### Physical Education 6: Field day and lifetime activity goals
- Objective: Demonstrate skills and sportsmanship in field day events
- Objective: Set summer activity goals
- Vocabulary: lifetime activity, summer plan
- Materials: field day equipment (sacks, balls, cones)
- Assessment: Year-end quiz and summer activity plan.

### Health 6: Presentation and year review
- Objective: Present the advocacy project
- Objective: Reflect on health goals
- Vocabulary: reflection, advocacy, goal
- Materials: project materials
- Assessment: Presentation and final assessment.
