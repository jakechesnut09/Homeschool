## Day 1 — Monday (school day 161, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Choose a topic* (90 min): Choose a topic connected to ancient civilizations or science (e.g., how the pyramids were built) and write a focused research question.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Median* (60 min): Line up 11 index cards of data to find the median and see why it resists outliers compared with the mean.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Memory tray* (50 min): Study a tray of 20 objects for 1 minute, cover it, and list what you remember; repeat with a memory strategy and compare.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Roads and trade* (45 min): Map Roman roads and sea trade routes and list goods from each province.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Big idea* (30 min): Review your sketchbook ideas page and choose a personally meaningful theme.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Cardio day* (40 min): Complete your plan's cardio workout and record heart rate.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Providers* (20 min): Match 10 health-care providers to what they do.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 162, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Evaluate sources* (90 min): Evaluate six websites with a credibility checklist (author, date, purpose, evidence) and keep the best three.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Five-number summary* (60 min): Find minimum, Q1, median, Q3 and maximum for 6 data sets and compute range and IQR.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Optical illusions* (50 min): Investigate three optical illusions and explain how the brain interprets signals from the eyes.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Roman coins* (45 min): Analyze images of Roman coins and explain how a common currency helped the economy and spread imperial messages.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Plan* (30 min): Make three thumbnail sketches and choose media.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Strength day* (40 min): Complete your strength workout with progression (add reps or sets).
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Check-up prep* (20 min): Write questions to ask at your next check-up and learn what a well-child visit includes.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 163, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Library visit* (90 min): Find at least one print book and one database article at the public library on your topic.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Box plots* (60 min): Draw box plots for your reaction-time data and a family height data set.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Research* (50 min): Read two sources about how memories form and summarize them in a one-page infographic.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Daily life* (45 min): Read about family life, slavery, schools and entertainment and compare a rich and a poor Roman's day.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Create* (30 min): Begin the culminating artwork.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Active play* (40 min): Play a favorite sport from the year for 30 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Pharmacist role* (20 min): Read a prescription label and list questions to ask a pharmacist.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 164, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Note-taking* (90 min): Take paraphrased notes on cards, each labeled with its source and subtopic.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Comparing box plots* (60 min): Compare two box plots (e.g., Los Angeles vs San Francisco monthly temperatures) and write 3 conclusions.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Body systems poster* (50 min): Create a poster showing how at least four body systems work together when you catch a ball.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Build an aqueduct* (45 min): Build a cardboard aqueduct with a gentle slope that carries water 1 meter, measuring the drop.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Create and revise* (30 min): Continue the artwork and get feedback halfway through.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Flexibility day* (40 min): Complete your flexibility workout and a sit-and-reach check.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Community services* (20 min): Locate a local clinic, dentist and public health department online.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 165, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Check-in* (90 min): Create an MLA-style works-cited list for your sources and refocus your question if needed.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on median, IQR and box plots.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Take the Unit 8 test on body systems and senses.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on the Roman economy and daily life.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Finish* (30 min): Complete the artwork and reflect on whether it conveys your intended meaning.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Log review* (40 min): Review your log and rate exertion for each day.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take the Personal and Consumer Health unit test.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Research questions and sources
- Objective: Develop a focused research question
- Objective: Evaluate credibility of sources
- Vocabulary: research question, credible, .gov / .edu, primary source, bibliography
- Materials: library card, online databases (public library), Library of Congress and Smithsonian websites
- Assessment: Source evaluation sheets and works-cited list.

### Mathematics 6: Median, IQR and box plots
- Objective: Find median and quartiles
- Objective: Make box plots and interpret IQR
- Vocabulary: median, quartile, interquartile range, range, five-number summary, box plot, outlier
- Materials: index cards, graph paper
- Assessment: Box plot quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Memory and the brain
- Objective: Gather information on how the brain stores memories
- Objective: Synthesize body systems learning
- Vocabulary: short-term memory, long-term memory, perception, optical illusion
- Materials: tray of 20 objects, towel, optical illusion images
- Assessment: Infographic, poster and Unit 8 test.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Life and economy in the Roman Empire
- Objective: Describe how the empire fostered trade
- Objective: Describe daily life in Rome
- Vocabulary: Pax Romana, denarius, aqueduct, forum, gladiator, province
- Materials: images of Roman coins (Smithsonian), map of Roman roads and trade, cardboard and tape for aqueduct
- Assessment: Aqueduct model and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Personal investigation artwork
- Objective: Formulate a personal artistic investigation
- Objective: Create a culminating artwork
- Vocabulary: artist statement, concept, series
- Materials: choice of media
- Assessment: Culminating artwork.

### Physical Education 6: Following the plan: week 2
- Objective: Follow and log a personal plan
- Objective: Monitor intensity
- Vocabulary: log, perceived exertion
- Materials: fitness log, stopwatch
- Assessment: Fitness log review.

### Health 6: Health care services and providers
- Objective: Identify types of health-care providers
- Objective: Access valid health services
- Vocabulary: pediatrician, specialist, pharmacist, check-up, health insurance
- Materials: family health records (with parent)
- Assessment: Unit test.
