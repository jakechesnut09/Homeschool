## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Reader's notebook launch* (90 min): Set up a reader's notebook, write a reading-history page, and read the first third of 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi', jotting predictions about the cobras.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Tiling the plane* (60 min): Compare areas of tangram shapes by covering them with smaller pieces, then explain in writing why two different-looking shapes can have equal area.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Notebook setup* (50 min): Set up a science notebook with table of contents, safety rules and a page explaining what scientists and engineers do differently.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *What is history?* (45 min): Examine three family artifacts (photo, letter, object) and write what each reveals and what it cannot tell you.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Sketchbook cover* (30 min): Design a sketchbook cover with your name in a decorative style and an 'ideas I want to explore' page.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Dynamic warm-up* (40 min): Learn a 10-move dynamic warm-up (high knees, butt kicks, carioca, arm circles) and a static-stretch cool-down.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Wellness wheel* (20 min): Rate yourself 1–5 on a wellness wheel (physical, emotional, social, intellectual, environmental) and shade it in.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Character and evidence* (90 min): Finish the story; list three traits of Rikki with a quoted line of evidence for each, and practice the 'claim + quote + explain' paragraph frame.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Composing and decomposing* (60 min): Find the area of L-shaped and staircase regions on graph paper three different ways (cut apart, enclose and subtract, rearrange) and record each method.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Testable questions* (50 min): Turn 10 'wonder' questions into testable questions and identify independent and dependent variables in each.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Primary vs. secondary* (45 min): Sort 12 sources (a diary, an encyclopedia article, a coin, a textbook) as primary or secondary and justify each.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Line types* (30 min): Fill a page with 20 line types (zigzag, hatched, wavy, broken) and label each with a mood word.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Finding your pulse* (40 min): Find your carotid and radial pulse, measure resting heart rate for 15 seconds x 4, and record it.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Health skills* (20 min): Match the eight CA health skills to middle-school scenarios and write one example for each.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Plot mountain* (90 min): Draw a plot diagram of the story and mark where Kipling builds suspense; discuss with a parent which moment is the climax and why.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Parallelograms to rectangles* (60 min): Cut a paper parallelogram along its height, slide the triangle to form a rectangle, and use this to write A = b x h; solve 8 parallelogram area problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Paper towel investigation* (50 min): Design and run a fair test of which paper towel absorbs the most water, keeping water amount and soaking time constant.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Timelines and BCE/CE* (45 min): Build a classroom-length timeline from 10,000 BCE to 500 CE that you will add to all year; practice calculating years between BCE and CE dates.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Contour drawing* (30 min): Make three blind contour and three modified contour drawings of your hand.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Heart rate response* (40 min): Measure heart rate after walking, jogging and sprinting for 1 minute each and graph the results.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Risk and protective factors* (20 min): Sort 12 behaviors into risk or protective factors and explain two.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Pronoun case* (90 min): Lesson on subjective, objective and possessive pronouns; correct 15 sentences (e.g., 'Him and me went') and find 5 pronouns in the story.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Bases and heights* (60 min): Identify every base-height pair on tilted parallelograms, including heights that fall outside the shape, and compute areas of 10 drawn figures.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Data and graphs* (50 min): Organize results in a data table, calculate averages of three trials and draw a bar graph.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Map skills* (45 min): Locate the seven continents, major rivers (Nile, Tigris, Euphrates, Indus, Huang He) and practice latitude/longitude coordinates.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Gesture drawing* (30 min): Do ten 30-second gesture drawings of a family member in different poses.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Safety and hydration* (40 min): Play a tag game, then discuss heat safety, hydration and proper shoes; set a daily water goal.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *SMART goal* (20 min): Write a SMART goal to strengthen the lowest section of your wellness wheel and make a tracker.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a one-paragraph response explaining how Rikki changes from beginning to end, citing two quotations; take a 10-item pronoun-case quiz.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a 10-problem quiz on rectangle and parallelogram area and write one sentence correcting any error you made.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a claim-evidence-reasoning conclusion for the paper towel test and quiz on variables.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on sources, timelines and map skills.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Choose your best line drawing and write two sentences on what line choices made it successful.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Check* (40 min): Lead the warm-up yourself and complete a short quiz on heart rate and warm-up purpose.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a quick check on wellness dimensions and share your goal with a parent.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Plot and suspense: 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi'
- Objective: Map the plot structure of a short story
- Objective: Cite evidence about character traits
- Objective: Identify subjective and objective pronouns
- Vocabulary: exposition, rising action, climax, resolution, suspense, cower, revive
- Materials: 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi' by Rudyard Kipling (from The Jungle Book), reading journal
- Assessment: Evidence paragraph on character plus pronoun-case quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Area of rectangles and parallelograms
- Objective: Find area by decomposing and rearranging
- Objective: Derive and use A = bh for parallelograms
- Vocabulary: area, base, height, parallelogram, decompose
- Materials: graph paper, scissors, tangram set or paper tangrams
- Assessment: Quiz: area of composite rectangles and parallelograms; explanation of why A = bh works.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Scientific investigations and the science notebook
- Objective: Ask testable questions
- Objective: Identify variables and plan a fair test
- Objective: Write claim-evidence-reasoning conclusions
- Vocabulary: hypothesis, independent variable, dependent variable, control, claim, evidence, reasoning
- Materials: paper towels of 3 brands, water, measuring cup, notebook
- Assessment: CER conclusion and variables quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Tools of the historian and geographer
- Objective: Use BCE/CE timelines
- Objective: Distinguish primary and secondary sources
- Objective: Read maps with latitude and longitude
- Vocabulary: archaeology, artifact, primary source, secondary source, BCE, CE, latitude, longitude
- Materials: world map, long strip of paper for timeline, family artifacts
- Assessment: Skills quiz and timeline setup.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Sketchbook launch and line
- Objective: Set up a sketchbook
- Objective: Use line types to show movement and mood
- Vocabulary: line, contour, gesture, sketchbook
- Materials: sketchbook, pencils (HB, 2B, 4B), eraser, fine marker
- Assessment: Sketchbook check with written reflection.

### Physical Education 6: Warm-ups, safety and heart rate
- Objective: Perform a dynamic warm-up and cool-down
- Objective: Measure resting and active heart rate
- Vocabulary: dynamic stretch, static stretch, pulse, resting heart rate
- Materials: stopwatch, cones, water bottle
- Assessment: Student-led warm-up and heart-rate log.

### Health 6: Dimensions of wellness and health skills
- Objective: Describe physical, mental/emotional and social health
- Objective: Name the CA health skills
- Vocabulary: wellness, health skill, risk behavior, protective factor
- Materials: health journal
- Assessment: Quick check and SMART goal.
