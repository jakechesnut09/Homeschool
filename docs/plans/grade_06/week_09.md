## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Thesis and plan* (90 min): Write a thesis explaining three ways Brian survives (mental and physical) and fill an essay organizer with evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Measuring with different units* (60 min): Measure the same objects in inches and centimeters and in cups and milliliters, and record why the numbers differ.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Layers of air* (50 min): Create a scaled drawing of atmospheric layers and note where weather happens.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Mapping the Nile* (45 min): Label the Nile, delta, cataracts, Upper and Lower Egypt, and surrounding deserts on a map.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Roman mosaics* (30 min): Study Pompeii mosaics (e.g., 'Cave Canem') and identify how tesserae create shading.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Backhand* (40 min): Practice backhand throws to a partner, focusing on a flat release.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Three styles* (20 min): Watch or act out passive, aggressive and assertive responses to the same situation and compare.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Body paragraphs* (90 min): Draft three body paragraphs, each with a quote from Hatchet and one fact from the survival article.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Converting units* (60 min): Use the ratio 1 in : 2.54 cm and 1 ft : 12 in to convert 12 measurements with ratio tables.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Air has pressure* (50 min): Demonstrate air pressure with the index-card-on-a-cup-of-water trick and the crushed bottle (warm air cooling inside a capped bottle).
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Hymn to the Nile* (45 min): Read the 'Hymn to the Nile' and list what Egyptians thanked the river for.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Plan a mosaic* (30 min): Sketch a mosaic design and plan colors on a grid.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Catching* (40 min): Practice pancake and rim catches at different heights.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *I-messages* (20 min): Rewrite 6 blaming statements as I-messages (I feel... when... because... I need...).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Intro and conclusion* (90 min): Draft an introduction with a hook and a conclusion that explains what Brian's survival teaches readers.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Two unit rates* (60 min): For 'every 4 cups of water makes 3 servings' find both unit rates (cups per serving, servings per cup) and explain when each is useful.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Build a barometer* (50 min): Build a jar-and-balloon barometer with a straw pointer and begin recording daily readings.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Farming calendar* (45 min): Make a circular calendar of the flood, planting and harvest seasons and explain the shaduf.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Build the mosaic* (30 min): Glue paper or bean tesserae onto cardboard, leaving even grout lines.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Forehand* (40 min): Learn the forehand (flick) throw and practice 30 throws.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Active listening* (20 min): Practice paraphrasing in a 5-minute conversation with a family member.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 44, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Revise and edit* (90 min): Revise for transitions and precise words; edit for pronoun shifts and intensive pronouns, then type the final essay.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Rates at home* (60 min): Time how many seconds it takes to fill a 1-liter bottle at the faucet and calculate liters per minute and minutes per liter.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Warm air rises* (50 min): Hold a paper spiral above a warm lamp or radiator and explain the motion using density.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Unification* (45 min): Read about Narmer (Menes) and the double crown and explain how the Nile helped unify Egypt.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Preservation* (30 min): Watch a museum conservation video and compare how 2D, 3D and digital works are preserved.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Pivot and pass* (40 min): Practice pivoting to avoid a marker and passing to a moving receiver.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Nonverbal* (20 min): Practice assertive body language and tone in a mirror.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 45, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Unit check* (90 min): Submit the essay, self-score with the explanatory rubric, and take the unit vocabulary test.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on unit conversion and finding unit rates.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on atmosphere and air pressure; compare your barometer readings with the weather.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Compare the Nile with the Tigris-Euphrates in a Venn diagram; quiz on Egyptian geography.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & critique* (30 min): Display your ancient-art pieces together and write a critique of your strongest work using your criteria.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Skill check* (40 min): Complete 10 backhand throws to a target 10 meters away and record successes.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Perform an assertive communication role-play scored with a rubric.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Explanatory essay: how Brian survives
- Objective: Write an explanatory essay with evidence from fiction and nonfiction
- Objective: Use transitions and precise vocabulary
- Vocabulary: thesis, topic sentence, evidence, transition, conclusion
- Materials: Hatchet notes, survival article, essay organizer
- Assessment: Explanatory essay scored on rubric; vocabulary test.

### Mathematics 6: Units of measure and unit rates
- Objective: Convert measurement units using ratios
- Objective: Find and interpret both unit rates in a ratio
- Vocabulary: unit rate, conversion, inch, centimeter, liter, quart
- Materials: ruler with inches and cm, measuring cups and a liter bottle, bathroom scale
- Assessment: Quiz on conversions and unit rates.

### Science 6 (Integrated): The atmosphere and air pressure
- Objective: Describe atmospheric layers
- Objective: Investigate air pressure and its relationship to temperature
- Vocabulary: atmosphere, troposphere, air pressure, barometer, density
- Materials: balloon, jar, straw, tape, empty plastic bottle, hot water
- Assessment: Quiz and barometer log.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: The gift of the Nile
- Objective: Describe the Nile's geography and flooding
- Objective: Explain how the Nile supported agriculture and unity
- Vocabulary: delta, cataract, inundation, shaduf, Upper Egypt, Lower Egypt
- Materials: map of the Nile, Herodotus 'Egypt is the gift of the Nile' quote, 'Hymn to the Nile' (Fordham Sourcebook)
- Assessment: Venn diagram and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Roman mosaics and art preservation
- Objective: Create a mosaic
- Objective: Analyze how museums preserve ancient art
- Vocabulary: mosaic, tessera, conservation, curator
- Materials: colored paper squares or dried beans, glue, cardboard
- Assessment: Mosaic and unit critique.

### Physical Education 6: Ultimate: throwing and catching a disc
- Objective: Throw backhand and forehand
- Objective: Catch with pancake and rim catches
- Vocabulary: backhand, forehand, pancake catch, pivot
- Materials: flying disc, cones
- Assessment: Disc throwing accuracy check.

### Health 6: Communication skills
- Objective: Use I-messages and active listening
- Objective: Distinguish passive, aggressive and assertive communication
- Vocabulary: I-message, active listening, assertive, passive, aggressive, nonverbal
- Materials: scenario cards, mirror
- Assessment: Communication rubric.
