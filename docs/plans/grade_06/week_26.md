## Day 1 — Monday (school day 126, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Shakespeare's theater* (90 min): Learn about the Globe Theatre and Elizabethan stage; read the Lambs' prose retelling of the play for an overview.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Comparing signed numbers* (60 min): Write inequality statements comparing pairs like -7 and -3, and explain them as positions on a number line.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Organelles* (50 min): Learn the function of each major organelle and complete a labeled diagram of an animal cell.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Barriers and rivers* (45 min): Label China's natural barriers (Himalayas, Gobi, Taklamakan, Pacific) and the Huang He and Chang Jiang rivers.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Blocking* (30 min): Block the play: mark entrances, exits and positions.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Sprint form* (40 min): Practice sprint drills (A-skips, high knees, arm drive).
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Types of violence* (20 min): Discuss physical, verbal and emotional violence and their effects.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 127, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *The lovers* (90 min): Read Act 1 Scene 1 in the original (with glosses); chart the four lovers and their conflicts.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Ordering* (60 min): Order sets of rational numbers from least to greatest using a card sort, including fractions and decimals.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Plant vs. animal* (50 min): Compare plant and animal cells in a Venn diagram and relate differences to how plants make food.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *River of sorrow* (45 min): Explain why the Huang He was called 'China's Sorrow' and how loess soil helped farming.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse with scripts, then off book for the first half.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Starts* (40 min): Practice standing and three-point starts and time 40-meter dashes.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Warning signs* (20 min): List warning signs of potential violence and why to tell an adult.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 128, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *The mechanicals* (90 min): Read Act 1 Scene 2 aloud in parts and identify what makes the actors' rehearsal funny.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Absolute value* (60 min): Find |x| for 12 numbers and interpret absolute value as distance, e.g., a debt of $30 means balance -30.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Edible cell model* (50 min): Build a cell model in a bag of gelatin using food items for organelles, with a key explaining each choice.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Oracle bones* (45 min): Study images of Shang oracle bones, write a question in the oracle-bone style, and connect them to Chinese writing.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Dress rehearsal* (30 min): Hold a dress rehearsal with costumes and props.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Baton exchange* (40 min): Practice upsweep baton exchanges in an exchange zone.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Weapons* (20 min): Learn the rule if you see a gun: don't touch, leave, tell an adult.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 129, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *The fairies* (90 min): Read key speeches from Act 2 (Puck, Oberon) and paraphrase three passages into modern English.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Comparing statements* (60 min): Distinguish statements about order and about absolute value (e.g., -24 < -14 but |-24| > |-14|) in 8 contexts.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Cell analogy* (50 min): Write a 'cell is like a city' analogy explaining the role of at least 7 organelles.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Shang bronzes* (45 min): Analyze Shang bronze vessels and explain what they reveal about technology and ancestor worship.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Performance* (30 min): Perform the play for an audience and record it.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Relay races* (40 min): Run shuttle and 4x50 m relays.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Gangs* (20 min): Read about gang recruitment and list positive alternatives.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 130, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Complete a plotline web connecting the lovers, fairies and mechanicals; quiz on drama terms.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on ordering and absolute value.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Shakespeare's world and A Midsummer Night's Dream, Act 1-2
- Objective: Understand the elements of drama
- Objective: Summarize the four plotlines
- Vocabulary: act, scene, stage directions, soliloquy, aside, comedy
- Materials: 'Tales from Shakespeare' by Charles and Mary Lamb (Project Gutenberg), Folger Shakespeare text of A Midsummer Night's Dream (free online)
- Assessment: Drama terms quiz and plot web.

### Mathematics 6: Comparing and absolute value
- Objective: Order rational numbers
- Objective: Interpret absolute value as distance from zero
- Vocabulary: absolute value, greater than, less than, magnitude
- Materials: index cards with rational numbers
- Assessment: Ordering and absolute value quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Cell parts and their functions
- Objective: Identify organelles and their functions
- Objective: Compare plant and animal cells
- Vocabulary: nucleus, cell membrane, cytoplasm, mitochondria, chloroplast, cell wall, vacuole
- Materials: plastic bag, gelatin or cornstarch, small food items as organelles
- Assessment: Cell model, analogy and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Geography and the Shang Dynasty
- Objective: Explain how geography isolated China
- Objective: Describe Shang civilization
- Vocabulary: Huang He, loess, Gobi, dynasty, oracle bone, bronze
- Materials: map of China, images of oracle bones and Shang bronzes
- Assessment: Map and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Rehearsal and performance
- Objective: Rehearse and present an adapted play
- Objective: Respond to a theatre work
- Vocabulary: blocking, cue, dress rehearsal, critique
- Materials: script, simple costumes and props, video camera
- Assessment: Performance and written critique.

### Physical Education 6: Sprinting and relays
- Objective: Use proper sprint form and starts
- Objective: Pass a baton
- Vocabulary: sprint, stance, acceleration, baton exchange
- Materials: stopwatch, cones, baton or paper towel tube
- Assessment: Sprint times.

### Health 6: Violence prevention
- Objective: Identify warning signs of violence
- Objective: Describe dangers of weapons and gangs
- Vocabulary: violence, weapon, gang, warning sign, de-escalation
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Role-play and check.
