## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Ode* (90 min): Write an ode to an ordinary object using at least three types of figurative language.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *True or false?* (60 min): Test whether given values make 10 equations true using substitution.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Energy of motion* (50 min): Roll a ball down a ramp into a paper cup and measure how far the cup slides as evidence of kinetic energy.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Socrates, Plato, Aristotle* (45 min): Read about the three philosophers and practice the Socratic method by questioning a family member about 'What is fairness?'.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Motifs* (30 min): Improvise and record five short motifs on your instrument or Song Maker.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Base running* (40 min): Practice running through first base and rounding bases.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Check-Call-Care* (20 min): Learn the Check-Call-Care steps and practice scene safety checks.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Haiku and concrete poems* (90 min): Write three haiku about nature observations and one concrete (shape) poem.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Hanger diagrams* (60 min): Use hanger diagrams (or a homemade coat-hanger balance with paper clips) to model and solve equations like x + 3 = 7 and 3x = 12.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Mass investigation* (50 min): Use balls of different mass from the same ramp height and record cup distance in three trials each.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Scientists* (45 min): Research Euclid, Archimedes, Hippocrates and Hypatia and make trading cards for each.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Section A* (30 min): Compose an 8-measure A section that expresses a chosen mood.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Outs* (40 min): Practice force outs and tag outs in a fielding drill.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Bleeding* (20 min): Practice direct pressure and bandaging on a family member with gloves.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Narrative poem* (90 min): Write a short narrative poem with a rhyme scheme, modeled on Longfellow.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Solving addition equations* (60 min): Solve 12 equations of the form x + p = q with fractions and decimals, checking by substitution.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Speed investigation* (50 min): Release the same ball from three ramp heights (different speeds) and record cup distance.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Alexander the Great* (45 min): Map Alexander's conquests and explain how he spread Greek culture into Egypt and Asia.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Section B* (30 min): Compose a contrasting B section that changes rhythm, register or dynamics.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Kickball game* (40 min): Play kickball applying fielding positions.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Burns* (20 min): Learn first aid for minor burns (cool water, cover) and when to get help.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 104, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Compile* (90 min): Revise and compile 6-8 poems into an illustrated anthology with a table of contents and author's note.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Solving multiplication equations* (60 min): Solve 12 equations of the form px = q, including 2/3 x = 10, and write stories for two of them.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Graph it* (50 min): Graph cup distance vs. mass and vs. speed and describe which relationship is steeper.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Alexandria* (45 min): Read about the Library and Lighthouse of Alexandria and do one of Euclid's compass-and-straightedge constructions.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Notate* (30 min): Write the composition in standard notation or save it in Song Maker.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Relay throws* (40 min): Practice relay throws from the outfield through a cutoff.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Nosebleeds and sprains* (20 min): Practice nosebleed care and RICE for sprains.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 105, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Poetry cafe* (90 min): Host a family poetry reading performing three poems; take the unit test on poetry terms.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on solving one-step equations and writing equations for word problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a CER explaining why a speeding car is much more dangerous than a slow one (speed squared relationship).
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Unit 5 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 5 test on Ancient Greece.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Peer feedback* (30 min): Play your draft for a listener and record their feedback using criteria.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Reflect* (40 min): Explain where fielders should throw with a runner on first and why.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Inventory and restock the home first aid kit and complete a skills check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Poetry anthology
- Objective: Write poems in several forms
- Objective: Present poetry orally
- Vocabulary: haiku, ode, limerick, concrete poem, anthology
- Materials: notebook, art supplies, computer
- Assessment: Anthology rubric, performance and unit test.

### Mathematics 6: Solving one-step equations
- Objective: Understand a solution makes an equation true
- Objective: Solve x + p = q and px = q
- Vocabulary: equation, solution, inverse operation, balanced
- Materials: balance scale or hanger diagram printouts, paper clips
- Assessment: One-step equation quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Kinetic energy
- Objective: Graph the relationship of kinetic energy to mass and speed
- Vocabulary: kinetic energy, speed, mass, collision
- Materials: ramp (board and books), toy car, balls of different masses, paper cup
- Assessment: Graphs and CER.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Philosophers, scientists and Alexander
- Objective: Describe contributions of Greek thinkers
- Objective: Trace Alexander's empire and Hellenistic culture
- Vocabulary: philosophy, Socratic method, Hellenistic, Alexandria, geometry
- Materials: Plato's Apology excerpt, map of Alexander's empire, compass and straightedge
- Assessment: Trading cards and Unit 5 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Composing an ABA piece
- Objective: Compose and document an ABA composition
- Objective: Use expressive intent
- Vocabulary: compose, motif, phrase, cadence
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Song Maker, staff paper
- Assessment: Documented draft composition.

### Physical Education 6: Kickball and base running
- Objective: Run bases efficiently
- Objective: Make force and tag outs
- Vocabulary: force out, tag out, rounding a base, relay throw
- Materials: playground ball, bases
- Assessment: Fielding decision quiz.

### Health 6: First aid basics I
- Objective: Check, call, care
- Objective: Treat minor wounds, burns and nosebleeds
- Vocabulary: first aid, check-call-care, bleeding, burn, universal precautions
- Materials: first aid kit, gloves, gauze
- Assessment: First aid skills check.
