## Day 1 — Monday (school day 136, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *View a performance* (90 min): Watch a filmed scene of the play and compare the director's choices with your mental picture from reading.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Four quadrants* (60 min): Plot 20 points including negative and fractional coordinates and name each point's quadrant.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Specialized cells* (50 min): Compare nerve, muscle, red blood and skin cells and explain how shape fits function.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Unifying China* (45 min): Read about Qin Shi Huangdi's unification and list his standardization policies (writing, coins, weights, axles).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Visual storytelling* (30 min): Analyze a photo essay and identify beginning, middle and end.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Standing long jump* (40 min): Practice arm swing and two-foot takeoff; measure three jumps.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Hygiene review* (20 min): Review skin, hair and body odor care and update your hygiene routine for middle school.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 137, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Cut and cast* (90 min): Choose and cut a 2-3 minute scene (e.g., the mechanicals' rehearsal), assign roles with family members, and mark blocking.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Reflections* (60 min): Reflect points over each axis and describe how the signs of the coordinates change.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Levels of organization* (50 min): Create a nested diagram from cell to tissue to organ to organ system to organism with examples.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Legalism* (45 min): Compare Legalist ideas with Confucianism and discuss the burning of books.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Storyboard* (30 min): Storyboard a 6-photo story about a family routine or recipe.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Running long jump* (40 min): Practice approach run and one-foot takeoff into grass or sand.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Acne myths* (20 min): Sort acne myths and facts using KidsHealth and plan a gentle skin routine.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 138, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Rehearse* (90 min): Rehearse lines with attention to iambic rhythm, projection and gestures.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Distances* (60 min): Find distances between points like (-3, 4) and (5, 4) using absolute value, in 10 problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Cell division* (50 min): Make a flip book showing a cell dividing and explain why cells divide for growth and repair.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Great Wall* (45 min): Map the early walls and explain the costs to the workers who built them.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Shoot* (30 min): Take the photos following your storyboard.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Scissor high jump* (40 min): Practice the scissor-kick high jump over a pool noodle.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Dental health* (20 min): Learn about gingivitis and practice proper flossing.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 139, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Perform* (90 min): Perform and record the scene for an audience.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Coordinate map* (60 min): Draw a neighborhood map on a coordinate grid with home at the origin and solve walking-distance problems along the streets.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Unit review* (50 min): Review cells with a concept map linking all unit vocabulary.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Terracotta army* (45 min): Study the terracotta army and sculpt a small clay soldier showing individual features.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Edit* (30 min): Crop and adjust contrast; discuss when photo editing becomes misleading manipulation.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Softball throw* (40 min): Throw for distance using a step-and-throw and measure the best of three.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Sports hygiene* (20 min): List hygiene rules for sports gear, showers and preventing athlete's foot.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 140, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Reflect and check* (90 min): Write a reflection explaining your interpretive choices with textual evidence; take the unit test.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on plotting, reflecting and distance.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Unit 7 test* (50 min): Take the Unit 7 test on cells and their parts.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write an evaluation: was Shi Huangdi a great leader or a tyrant? Use evidence for both sides.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & share* (30 min): Present your photo story and gather feedback.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Mini track meet* (40 min): Hold a family track meet with sprint, jump and throw events.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a hygiene self-assessment and quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Performing a scene
- Objective: Perform a scene with appropriate voice and movement
- Objective: Compare live or filmed performance to the text
- Vocabulary: blocking, projection, props, interpretation
- Materials: scene script, simple costumes and props, filmed production clip
- Assessment: Performance rubric, reflection and unit test.

### Mathematics 6: The coordinate plane
- Objective: Plot points in all four quadrants
- Objective: Find distances between points with a common coordinate
- Vocabulary: quadrant, x-axis, y-axis, origin, reflection
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Coordinate plane quiz and map project.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Cells to organisms
- Objective: Explain levels of organization
- Objective: Explain cell division for growth and repair
- Vocabulary: tissue, organ, organ system, specialized cell, cell division
- Materials: images of specialized cells, paper for flip book
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: The Qin Dynasty and Shi Huangdi
- Objective: List Shi Huangdi's policies and achievements
- Objective: Evaluate Legalism
- Vocabulary: Qin, Legalism, standardization, Great Wall, terracotta army
- Materials: images of the terracotta army, clay
- Assessment: Evidence-based evaluation.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Photo story and editing
- Objective: Sequence photos to tell a story
- Objective: Edit images ethically
- Vocabulary: sequence, crop, contrast, saturation, manipulation
- Materials: camera, free photo editor
- Assessment: Photo story.

### Physical Education 6: Jumping and throwing events
- Objective: Perform standing long jump and high jump technique
- Objective: Throw for distance safely
- Vocabulary: takeoff, landing, approach, release angle
- Materials: measuring tape, soft shot (bean bag or softball), foam noodle as bar
- Assessment: Track meet results.

### Health 6: Personal hygiene and dental care for adolescents
- Objective: Explain adolescent hygiene needs
- Objective: Practice dental care
- Vocabulary: hygiene, acne, plaque, gingivitis, orthodontics
- Materials: toothbrush, floss, hygiene checklist
- Assessment: Self-assessment and quiz.
