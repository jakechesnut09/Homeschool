## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Rhythm* (90 min): Read 'Paul Revere's Ride' aloud and tap the galloping meter; explain how rhythm matches content.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Greatest common factor* (60 min): Find the largest square tile that exactly covers a 24 x 36 rectangle, then find GCF for 10 pairs of numbers.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Static electricity* (50 min): Rub a balloon on hair and test its pull on tissue bits and a stream of tap water at different distances.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Golden Age* (45 min): Read about Pericles' Athens and the building of the Parthenon and list achievements.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *India* (30 min): Listen to a sitar raga and identify the drone and tala rhythm cycle.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Overhand throw* (40 min): Practice the overhand throw with step, hip rotation and follow-through at a target.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Water safety* (20 min): Learn about drowning prevention, rip currents and life-jacket fit for pools, lakes and beaches.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 92, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Rhyme scheme* (90 min): Label rhyme schemes of three poems and identify alliteration and internal rhyme in an excerpt of 'The Raven'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Least common multiple* (60 min): Solve 'hot dogs come in 10s, buns in 8s' problems and find LCM for 10 pairs.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Charges* (50 min): Model positive and negative charges and explain attraction and repulsion between two charged balloons.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Delian League* (45 min): Explain how the Delian League turned into an Athenian empire and why Sparta felt threatened.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *China* (30 min): Listen to guzheng music and play a pentatonic melody on black keys of a keyboard or a xylophone app.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Ground balls* (40 min): Field 30 ground balls using alligator hands and throw to a target.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Concussions* (20 min): Read about concussion signs and why to stop playing and tell an adult.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 93, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Heard vs. read* (90 min): Listen to a professional recording of 'The Raven' excerpt and compare it with your silent reading.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *GCF or LCM?* (60 min): Sort 8 word problems as GCF or LCM situations and solve each.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Build an electromagnet* (50 min): With adult supervision, wrap wire around a nail, connect to a battery and count paper clips picked up.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *War and plague* (45 min): Read Thucydides' description of the plague of Athens and explain its effect on the war.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *West Africa* (30 min): Learn a West African polyrhythm with two household percussion instruments.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Fly balls* (40 min): Catch fly balls with two hands above the head, calling 'mine'.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Protective gear* (20 min): Inspect sports gear and check helmet fit using the 2-V-1 rule.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 94, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Structure* (90 min): Analyze how one stanza of 'Paul Revere's Ride' builds suspense within the whole poem.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Distributive property* (60 min): Rewrite sums like 36 + 8 as 4(9 + 2) using the GCF and use area models to show why it works.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Variables* (50 min): Test how the number of coils (10, 20, 30) affects electromagnet strength and graph results.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Consequences* (45 min): Create a cause-and-effect chart of the Peloponnesian War and its result: a weakened Greece.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Mexico* (30 min): Listen to mariachi music and identify instruments and the son jalisciense rhythm.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Crow hop* (40 min): Learn the crow hop to throw farther and measure distance.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Heat illness* (20 min): Learn signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke and prevention steps.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 95, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Memorize and recite 12+ lines of a chosen poem with expression; quiz on sound devices.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit 5 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 5 assessment on decimals, long division, GCF/LCM.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on electric and magnetic forces and write questions you would investigate next.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on the Golden Age and Peloponnesian War.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & check* (30 min): Take a listening quiz identifying four world music traditions by sound.

- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Create a sports safety checklist and take a quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Sound and structure
- Objective: Analyze rhyme, rhythm and repetition
- Objective: Explain how stanza structure contributes to meaning
- Vocabulary: rhyme scheme, meter, stanza, alliteration, onomatopoeia, refrain
- Materials: 'Paul Revere's Ride' by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow, 'The Raven' (excerpt) by Edgar Allan Poe, audio recordings
- Assessment: Recitation rubric and sound-device quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Factors, multiples and the distributive property
- Objective: Find GCF and LCM
- Objective: Factor sums with the distributive property
- Vocabulary: factor, multiple, greatest common factor, least common multiple, distributive property
- Materials: colored tiles, grid paper
- Assessment: Unit 5 end-of-unit assessment.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Electric forces and electromagnets
- Objective: Investigate static electric forces
- Objective: Determine factors affecting electromagnet strength
- Vocabulary: static electricity, charge, electromagnet, current, coil
- Materials: balloons, tissue paper bits, iron nail, insulated copper wire, D battery, paper clips
- Assessment: Electromagnet data and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Golden Age and the Peloponnesian War
- Objective: Describe Athens' Golden Age
- Objective: Explain causes and effects of the Peloponnesian War
- Vocabulary: Delian League, Parthenon, Peloponnesian War, plague, alliance
- Materials: images of the Parthenon, Thucydides excerpt on the plague
- Assessment: Cause-effect chart and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Music of world cultures
- Objective: Identify instruments and features of world music
- Objective: Perform a rhythm from another culture
- Vocabulary: raga, tala, pentatonic, polyrhythm, ostinato
- Materials: recordings of Indian, Chinese, West African and Mexican music, household percussion
- Assessment: Listening quiz.

### Physical Education 6: Throwing and catching for fielding
- Objective: Throw overhand with hip and shoulder rotation
- Objective: Field ground balls and fly balls
- Vocabulary: crow hop, ground ball, fly ball, glove side
- Materials: softball glove or soft ball, tennis balls, bucket target
- Assessment: Throwing accuracy check.

### Health 6: Water, sports and recreation safety
- Objective: Describe water and sports safety
- Objective: Recognize concussion signs
- Vocabulary: drowning, life jacket, concussion, protective gear, rip current
- Materials: helmet, life jacket (if available)
- Assessment: Checklist and quiz.
