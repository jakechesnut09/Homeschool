## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *What is an argument?* (90 min): Compare a fact, an opinion and an argument; identify the claim in 6 short advertisements and op-ed excerpts.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *How much in each group?* (60 min): Solve problems like '3/4 of a pound fills 1/2 a bag; how much fills a whole bag?' with tape diagrams (6 problems).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Wind-driven currents* (50 min): Blow across water in a baking dish with pepper flakes to model wind-driven currents and sketch the pattern.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Naomi and Ruth* (45 min): Read the Book of Ruth and explain what it teaches about loyalty and kindness to strangers.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Beat vs. rhythm* (30 min): Tap the steady beat and then the rhythm of three songs and explain the difference.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Rules* (40 min): Learn traveling, double dribble and foul calls and practice referee signals.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Digital footprint* (20 min): Search a made-up name scenario and discuss what a digital footprint reveals and how long it lasts.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 67, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Claim, reasons, evidence* (90 min): Read an article on whether middle schools should start later and annotate the claim, reasons and evidence in three colors.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Dividing by unit fractions* (60 min): Notice that dividing by 1/3 is the same as multiplying by 3, and test the pattern with 10 examples.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *California Current* (50 min): Compare San Francisco and Washington, D.C. temperatures at similar latitudes and explain the role of the cold California Current.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *David and Solomon* (45 min): Read about King David and Solomon's Temple and add the united monarchy to the timeline.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Note values* (30 min): Complete musictheory.net lessons on note and rest durations and clap 8 written rhythm patterns.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *3v3 game* (40 min): Play 3v3 games, rotating a student referee.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Privacy settings* (20 min): Review privacy settings on a family device or app with a parent.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 68, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Supported or not?* (90 min): Evaluate each piece of evidence in a second article as relevant, sufficient or unsupported and explain why.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Algorithm for fraction division* (60 min): Use the reciprocal method to compute 15 quotients including mixed numbers, and check each with multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Rain shadow* (50 min): Model a rain shadow with a cardboard mountain and spray bottle; compare rainfall on both sides of the Sierra Nevada using data.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Prophets and justice* (45 min): Read Micah 6:8 and Amos 5:24 and explain the prophets' teachings on justice and righteousness.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Time signatures* (30 min): Identify 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4 in recordings by counting and conducting patterns.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Knockout and HORSE* (40 min): Play shooting games to practice under pressure.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Scams and phishing* (20 min): Identify red flags in 5 sample messages (phishing, prize scams, stranger requests).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 69, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Author's purpose and bias* (90 min): Determine each author's point of view and identify loaded words that reveal bias.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Explain it* (60 min): Write a short explanation, with a diagram, of why 2/3 / 1/6 = 4, and critique a sample student's incorrect solution.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Climographs* (50 min): Make climographs for San Diego, Sacramento and Death Valley and explain their differences.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Western traditions* (45 min): Find connections between Jewish ethical teachings (Sabbath, care for the poor, rule of law) and later Western traditions.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Bucket drumming* (30 min): Play a 4-measure bucket-drum rhythm with sixteenth notes from notation.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Tournament* (40 min): Hold a mini-tournament with family or co-op, focusing on sportsmanship.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Online strangers* (20 min): Role-play refusing and reporting an online stranger asking for photos or to meet.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 70, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Read a new short argument and complete an argument-analysis organizer independently as a quiz.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on computing fraction quotients and explaining the algorithm.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a CER on why two places at the same latitude have different climates.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph explaining one key Jewish ethical teaching and its influence, citing a source.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform a rhythm reading test of 4 patterns with a metronome.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Unit check* (40 min): Take a basketball rules quiz and redo the shooting check to compare with week 12.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a Common Sense digital citizenship lesson and quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Analyzing arguments
- Objective: Identify claims, reasons and evidence
- Objective: Distinguish supported from unsupported claims
- Vocabulary: claim, reason, evidence, counterclaim, credible, bias
- Materials: CommonLit argument articles (e.g., on school start times, homework, screen time), highlighters
- Assessment: Independent argument analysis.

### Mathematics 6: Dividing fractions by fractions
- Objective: Divide fractions using diagrams and the reciprocal
- Objective: Explain why multiplying by the reciprocal works
- Vocabulary: reciprocal, multiplicative inverse, mixed number
- Materials: fraction strips, grid paper
- Assessment: Fraction division computation quiz with one written explanation.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Ocean currents and regional climate
- Objective: Explain how ocean currents distribute energy
- Objective: Explain how geography shapes regional climates
- Vocabulary: ocean current, California Current, upwelling, rain shadow, climate, El Nino
- Materials: baking dish, warm and cold colored water, hair dryer or straw, climate data for two cities
- Assessment: Climographs and CER.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Kingdom, teachings and ethics
- Objective: Describe the kingdom of David and Solomon
- Objective: Identify sources of Jewish ethical teachings
- Vocabulary: Hebrew Bible, Talmud, prophet, righteousness, justice, Temple
- Materials: Book of Ruth (short reading), excerpts from the prophets (Micah 6:8, Amos 5:24)
- Assessment: Evidence paragraph.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Rhythm and meter
- Objective: Read and perform rhythms with whole through sixteenth notes
- Objective: Identify time signatures
- Vocabulary: beat, rhythm, meter, time signature, measure, rest
- Materials: musictheory.net, pencil and paper, drum or bucket
- Assessment: Rhythm reading performance.

### Physical Education 6: Basketball: games and officiating
- Objective: Play modified games with rules
- Objective: Officiate fairly
- Vocabulary: traveling, double dribble, foul, referee
- Materials: basketball, hoop, whistle
- Assessment: Rules quiz and shooting improvement.

### Health 6: Digital citizenship and online safety
- Objective: Protect personal information online
- Objective: Recognize online risks
- Vocabulary: digital footprint, privacy settings, phishing, online predator, screenshot
- Materials: Common Sense Education lessons, computer
- Assessment: Digital citizenship quiz.
