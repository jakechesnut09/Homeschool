## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Plan* (90 min): Brainstorm moments when you learned something difficult and plan a memoir chapter with a turning point.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Temperature and elevation* (60 min): Record temperatures in different places (e.g., Death Valley highs, Lake Tahoe winter lows) and elevations above and below sea level, and explain the meaning of 0.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Is it alive?* (50 min): Sort 15 items as living, once living or nonliving using characteristics of life.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Maurya Empire* (45 min): Map the Maurya Empire under Chandragupta and describe how it was governed.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Essential events* (30 min): Choose a fable or myth and identify the essential events that make up its dramatic structure.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Plan* (40 min): Choose music and plan a 2-minute routine using at least six steps learned.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Wildfire and smoke* (20 min): Learn evacuation zones and how to read the Air Quality Index during wildfire smoke.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Draft* (90 min): Draft the chapter, combining narration of events with adult-like reflection, as Keller does.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Opposites* (60 min): Mark 12 numbers and their opposites on a number line, including -2.5 and 3/4, and explain why -(-4) = 4.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Yeast are alive* (50 min): Mix yeast, sugar and warm water in a bottle with a balloon on top, and record balloon size every 10 minutes.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Ashoka's edicts* (45 min): Read three Rock Edicts of Ashoka and identify the values he promoted after the Kalinga war.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Scripting* (30 min): Write a 2-3 page script with dialogue and stage directions.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Build* (40 min): Build the routine in 8-counts and write it down.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Go-bag* (20 min): Pack or list a 72-hour go-bag using the Ready.gov checklist.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise for voice and add at least three nonrestrictive elements punctuated with commas, parentheses or dashes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Money and signed numbers* (60 min): Represent deposits, withdrawals and debt with positive and negative numbers in 8 situations.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Pond water* (50 min): Observe pond water or a hay infusion under the microscope and sketch single-celled organisms.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Lion Capital* (45 min): Analyze the Lion Capital and explain why it appears on India's flag and currency today.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Staging problems* (30 min): Solve staging challenges (a flying horse, a storm) with simple props, sound or movement.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Practice* (40 min): Practice the routine and check heart rate to confirm moderate-to-vigorous intensity.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Family plan* (20 min): Complete a family communication plan with an out-of-area contact.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Edit and publish* (90 min): Edit for conventions and publish the chapter typed.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Rational numbers on the line* (60 min): Place fractions and decimals between -3 and 3 on a carefully scaled number line.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Cheek cells* (50 min): Gently swab your inner cheek, stain with food coloring and observe human cells; compare with onion cells.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Indian achievements* (45 min): Research Indian contributions (zero and Hindu-Arabic numerals, metallurgy, medicine, the Bhagavad Gita) and make an illustrated chart.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Table read* (30 min): Do a table read with family and revise the script based on what did not work.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Teach* (40 min): Teach your routine to family members.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Help others* (20 min): Plan how to help a neighbor, pet or elderly relative in an emergency.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 125, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Share and check* (90 min): Read your chapter aloud and take the unit test on informational reading skills.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on signed numbers in context and number-line placement.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a CER that yeast is a living, single-celled organism using balloon data.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Unit 6 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 6 test on ancient India.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & check* (30 min): Finalize the script and plan props and costumes.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Perform* (40 min): Perform the routine together and evaluate with a rubric.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Present the family emergency plan and take the Preparedness unit quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Writing a memoir chapter
- Objective: Write a memoir chapter about a time you learned something hard
- Objective: Use reflection and parenthetical punctuation
- Vocabulary: reflection, turning point, voice
- Materials: notebook, computer
- Assessment: Memoir chapter rubric and unit test.

### Mathematics 6: Positive and negative numbers
- Objective: Use signed numbers in context
- Objective: Locate rational numbers and opposites on the number line
- Vocabulary: positive, negative, integer, opposite, rational number
- Materials: outdoor thermometer, number line strip
- Assessment: Signed-number quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Living things are made of cells
- Objective: Provide evidence that living things are unicellular or multicellular
- Objective: Distinguish living from nonliving
- Vocabulary: unicellular, multicellular, organism, yeast, bacteria
- Materials: dry yeast, sugar, warm water, balloon, bottle, pond water (optional), cotton swab
- Assessment: Yeast CER.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: The Maurya Empire and Indian achievements
- Objective: Describe Ashoka's political and moral achievements
- Objective: Identify lasting Indian contributions
- Vocabulary: Maurya, Ashoka, edict, stupa, zero, decimal system, Gupta
- Materials: Rock Edicts of Ashoka excerpts, images of the Lion Capital of Ashoka
- Assessment: Edict analysis and Unit 6 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Adapting a story for the stage
- Objective: Identify essential events in a story
- Objective: Adapt a story into a script
- Vocabulary: script, dialogue, stage directions, adaptation, conflict
- Materials: Aesop's fable or Greek myth text, notebook
- Assessment: Original adapted script.

### Physical Education 6: Create a fitness dance
- Objective: Choreograph a dance that raises heart rate
- Objective: Teach it to others
- Vocabulary: routine, transition, aerobic
- Materials: music, paper for routine notes
- Assessment: Fitness dance rubric.

### Health 6: Wildfire, flood and emergency kits
- Objective: Build an emergency kit
- Objective: Plan for evacuation
- Vocabulary: evacuation, go-bag, air quality index, emergency kit
- Materials: Ready.gov kit checklist, backpack
- Assessment: Family plan and quiz.
