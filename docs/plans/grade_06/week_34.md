## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Outline* (90 min): Sort note cards into 3-4 subtopics and build an outline with headings.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Mean or median?* (60 min): Decide for 6 data sets whether mean/MAD or median/IQR better describes them, based on shape and outliers.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *The problem* (50 min): Research how solar ovens are used around the world and define criteria (reach 60 C) and constraints (materials, cost).
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Judea and Rome* (45 min): Read about Roman rule in Judea, the Jewish revolts, and Rome's ban on Jews living in Jerusalem; map Jewish communities around the Mediterranean.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Museum visit* (30 min): Visit a local or virtual museum (e.g., the Getty or LACMA online) and note how works are grouped and labeled.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Cardio intervals* (40 min): Complete interval training with increased intensity.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Air quality* (20 min): Check the local AQI for a week and graph it.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 167, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Draft sections* (90 min): Draft two sections, synthesizing information from at least two sources in each.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Plan an investigation* (60 min): Write a statistical question, plan how to collect at least 30 data points, and begin collecting.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Science ideas* (50 min): List how reflection, absorption, insulation and the greenhouse effect could each help the oven.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Jesus of Nazareth* (45 min): Read the Sermon on the Mount and summarize key teachings of Jesus as described in the New Testament.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Community values* (30 min): Write how the museum's choices reflect its community's history and values.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Strength* (40 min): Complete strength workout and test max push-ups in 1 minute.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Water and noise* (20 min): Learn about safe drinking water and noise pollution in your community.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 168, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Paraphrase practice* (90 min): Practice paraphrasing vs. plagiarism with three passages, then draft the remaining sections with in-text citations.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Analyze* (60 min): Display your data with two plot types and calculate center and spread measures.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Sketch designs* (50 min): Sketch three designs and evaluate them with a decision matrix.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Paul's journeys* (45 min): Map Paul's missionary journeys and explain his role in spreading Christian beliefs to non-Jews.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Select works* (30 min): Select 8-10 of your works from the year across visual art and media arts.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Team game* (40 min): Play a team game (ultimate or soccer) with family or co-op.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Trash audit* (20 min): Conduct a one-day household trash audit and plan reduce/reuse/recycle steps.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 169, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Text features* (90 min): Add an introduction, conclusion and at least one graphic (map, diagram or timeline) with a caption.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Present* (60 min): Create a poster or slides answering your question with data, and present it to your family.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Build prototype* (50 min): Build the chosen solar oven from a pizza box and household materials.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *From persecution to official religion* (45 min): Create a timeline from early persecution to Constantine's Edict of Milan and Theodosius, and explain why Christianity spread.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Display plan* (30 min): Draw a display plan for your exhibition showing placement and flow.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Flexibility and yoga* (40 min): Complete a 20-minute yoga flow.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Public health* (20 min): Research what your county public health department does.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 170, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Check-in* (90 min): Type the full draft (at least 3 pages in one sitting) and conference with a parent using the research rubric.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit 8 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 8 end-of-unit assessment on data and distributions.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Check-in* (50 min): Write a design brief explaining your choices and predict the maximum temperature.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a short explanation of three factors that helped Christianity spread through the Roman Empire.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & check* (30 min): Share the plan and revise based on feedback.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Adjust* (40 min): Adjust next week's plan based on results and how you felt.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Choose a community health issue for your advocacy project and justify it.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Drafting the research report
- Objective: Organize information into a report
- Objective: Quote and paraphrase without plagiarism
- Vocabulary: plagiarism, citation, heading, synthesize
- Materials: note cards, computer
- Assessment: Complete typed draft.

### Mathematics 6: Statistics project
- Objective: Choose appropriate measures of center and spread
- Objective: Carry out a statistical investigation
- Vocabulary: investigation, summary, context
- Materials: survey or measurement tools, poster board or slides
- Assessment: Unit 8 assessment and statistics project rubric.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Solar oven: define and design
- Objective: Define criteria and constraints for a solar oven
- Objective: Apply thermal energy ideas to a design
- Vocabulary: reflect, absorb, insulate, solar energy
- Materials: pizza box, foil, black paper, plastic wrap, tape, newspaper
- Assessment: Design brief and decision matrix.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Judea under Rome and the origins of Christianity
- Objective: Trace Jewish migration and conflict with Rome
- Objective: Describe the origins and spread of Christianity
- Vocabulary: Judea, Messiah, Jesus of Nazareth, New Testament, apostle, Paul, persecution, Constantine
- Materials: Sermon on the Mount excerpt (Matthew 5-7), map of Paul's journeys
- Assessment: Explanation paragraph and map.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Museums and curation
- Objective: Evaluate how museums reflect community values
- Objective: Plan a display
- Vocabulary: curator, exhibition, wall label, gallery
- Materials: virtual or local museum visit, graph paper
- Assessment: Museum reflection and display plan.

### Physical Education 6: Following the plan: week 3
- Objective: Apply progression
- Objective: Adjust the plan using data
- Vocabulary: plateau, adjustment
- Materials: fitness log
- Assessment: Plan adjustment notes.

### Health 6: Environmental and community health
- Objective: Describe environmental effects on health
- Objective: Identify community health agencies
- Vocabulary: air quality, pollution, public health, recycling, environmental health
- Materials: AQI website, trash audit sheet
- Assessment: Project proposal.
