# Health 6 (Grade 6)

Grade 6 health education aligned to the 2008 California Health Education Content Standards, which focus sixth grade on Mental, Emotional and Social Health; Injury Prevention and Safety; and Personal and Community Health. Students learn to identify emotions, manage stress, build self-esteem, communicate assertively, resolve conflict, prevent bullying and stay safe online, and recognize when to seek mental-health help. They then practice injury prevention, first aid, 911 calls and California earthquake/wildfire preparedness, study disease prevention, immunity and consumer health, and finish with a community health advocacy project.

*Framework:* 2008 California Health Education Content Standards (Grade 6: Mental, Emotional and Social Health; Injury Prevention and Safety; Personal and Community Health); 2019 CA Health Education Framework  
*Time:* 20 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Wellness and Health Skills (weeks 1–2)
Health has physical, mental/emotional and social sides that we strengthen with skills.

- Week 1: Dimensions of wellness and health skills
- Week 2: Decision making and valid health information

### Unit 2: Emotions, Stress and Self-Esteem (weeks 3–7)
Understanding emotions and managing stress builds resilience.

- Week 3: Understanding emotions
- Week 4: Stress and its effects
- Week 5: Coping and stress management
- Week 6: Self-esteem and growth mindset
- Week 7: Media and social media influences on emotions

### Unit 3: Healthy Relationships and Communication (weeks 8–11)
Respectful communication keeps friendships and families strong.

- Week 8: Healthy friendships
- Week 9: Communication skills
- Week 10: Conflict resolution
- Week 11: Peer pressure and family relationships

### Unit 4: Respect, Bullying Prevention and Digital Citizenship (weeks 12–15)
Everyone deserves respect online and offline, and bystanders can make a difference.

- Week 12: Respecting differences
- Week 13: Bullying and harassment
- Week 14: Digital citizenship and online safety
- Week 15: Cyberbullying and advocacy for kindness

### Unit 5: Mental Health and Getting Help (weeks 16–17)
Mental health concerns are common and treatable; asking for help is a strength.

- Week 16: Mental health conditions
- Week 17: Getting help and supporting others

### Unit 6: Injury Prevention and First Aid (weeks 18–22)
Most injuries are preventable, and first aid skills can save lives.

- Week 18: Injury prevention at home
- Week 19: Water, sports and recreation safety
- Week 20: Pedestrian, bike and passenger safety
- Week 21: First aid basics I
- Week 22: First aid basics II

### Unit 7: Emergency and Disaster Preparedness (weeks 23–25)
Preparing before an emergency keeps families safe in California's earthquakes, fires and floods.

- Week 23: Calling 911 and emergency resources
- Week 24: Earthquake and fire preparedness
- Week 25: Wildfire, flood and emergency kits

### Unit 8: Violence Prevention and Personal Safety (weeks 26–27)
Recognizing danger, using refusal skills and telling trusted adults prevent violence.

- Week 26: Violence prevention
- Week 27: Personal safety and boundaries

### Unit 9: Personal Health, Disease Prevention and Consumer Health (weeks 28–33)
Daily habits, immunity and smart consumer choices protect lifelong health.

- Week 28: Personal hygiene and dental care for adolescents
- Week 29: Sleep, screens, eyes and hearing
- Week 30: Communicable diseases and the immune system
- Week 31: Noncommunicable diseases and prevention
- Week 32: Consumer health: products and advertising
- Week 33: Health care services and providers

### Unit 10: Community Health and Advocacy (weeks 34–36)
Individuals can improve the health of their families, schools and communities.

- Week 34: Environmental and community health
- Week 35: Advocacy project
- Week 36: Presentation and year review

## Standards

- **H-6.1.1.M** Identify a wide range of emotions and describe healthy ways to express them.
- **H-6.1.1.P** Describe personal hygiene and dental health practices for adolescents.
- **H-6.1.1.S** Identify ways to prevent injuries at home, in water, in sports and in the community.
- **H-6.1.2.M** Describe the causes and effects of stress and identify positive coping strategies.
- **H-6.1.2.P** Explain the importance of adequate sleep and healthy screen-time habits.
- **H-6.1.2.S** Describe basic first aid procedures for common injuries and emergencies.
- **H-6.1.3.M** Describe characteristics of healthy and unhealthy friendships and relationships.
- **H-6.1.3.P** Distinguish between communicable and noncommunicable diseases and describe how the immune system works.
- **H-6.1.3.S** Describe emergency and disaster preparedness procedures, including earthquakes, fires and wildfires.
- **H-6.1.4.M** Describe bullying, cyberbullying, harassment and their effects on individuals and communities.
- **H-6.1.4.P** Describe ways to prevent the spread of communicable diseases, including handwashing and immunizations.
- **H-6.1.4.S** Describe safe and responsible use of the internet, social media and digital devices.
- **H-6.1.5.M** Explain the importance of respecting individual differences, including culture, ability, gender and family structure.
- **H-6.1.5.P** Describe sun safety and the protection of eyes and hearing.
- **H-6.1.5.S** Identify ways to prevent violence and recognize the dangers of weapons and gangs.
- **H-6.1.6.M** Recognize signs of depression, anxiety and other mental health concerns and when to seek help.
- **H-6.1.6.P** Describe how environmental factors (air, water, noise) affect personal and community health.
- **H-6.1.6.S** Describe pedestrian, bicycle, skateboard and passenger safety practices.
- **H-6.1.7.M** Explain how self-esteem, resilience and a growth mindset contribute to mental health.
- **H-6.2.1.M** Analyze how family, peers, social media and culture influence emotions, self-image and behavior.
- **H-6.2.1.P** Analyze how advertising influences the purchase of health products and services.
- **H-6.2.1.S** Analyze how peers, media and the desire for thrills influence risk-taking behavior.
- **H-6.3.1.M** Identify trusted adults, school and community resources for mental health support, including crisis lines.
- **H-6.3.1.P** Access valid health information, products and services, and identify health-care providers.
- **H-6.3.1.S** Identify emergency resources and how to access them, including calling 911.
- **H-6.4.1.M** Use effective communication skills (I-messages, active listening) and conflict-resolution strategies.
- **H-6.4.1.S** Demonstrate communication and refusal skills to avoid or escape unsafe situations.
- **H-6.5.1.M** Use a decision-making process to address social situations and peer pressure.
- **H-6.5.1.P** Use a decision-making process to choose health products and services.
- **H-6.5.1.S** Use a decision-making process to evaluate safety risks.
- **H-6.6.1.M** Set and monitor a goal to improve mental, emotional or social health.
- **H-6.6.1.P** Set a goal to improve a personal health practice.
- **H-6.7.1.M** Practice stress-management and self-care techniques.
- **H-6.7.1.P** Practice behaviors that reduce the risk of disease and protect personal health.
- **H-6.7.1.S** Practice injury-prevention, first aid and emergency-preparedness skills.
- **H-6.8.1.M** Advocate for respect, kindness and bullying prevention.
- **H-6.8.1.P** Advocate for a healthy home, school and community environment.
- **H-6.8.1.S** Encourage others to follow safety practices.
