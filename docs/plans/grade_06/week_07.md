## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Foolbirds and fish* (90 min): Read chapters 10-11 and record what Brian learns from failure while hunting; connect to the idea of 'tough hope'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Ratio tables* (60 min): Convert three double number lines into ratio tables, and fill in missing values in 8 tables using multiplication and scaling.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Define the problem* (50 min): Define criteria (an ice cube loses the least mass in 60 minutes) and constraints (materials list, size) for an ice cube keeper.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *From tokens to writing* (45 min): Read how clay tokens and pictographs evolved into cuneiform and make a three-stage chart.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Vase shapes* (30 min): Learn the shapes and uses of the amphora, krater and kylix and sketch each.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Dribbling* (40 min): Dribble through a cone course using inside, outside and sole touches.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Comparison trap* (20 min): Discuss how curated posts lead to comparison and list three edited-reality examples.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *The plane passes* (90 min): Read chapter 12 and write a journal entry analyzing how the missed rescue plane is a turning point.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Tables and graphs* (60 min): Plot ratio-table pairs (e.g., cups of rice to cups of water) on the first quadrant and notice that the points line up through (0,0).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Design and build* (50 min): Sketch two designs, choose one using science ideas about conduction, convection and radiation, and build it.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Write cuneiform* (45 min): Press your name and a short message in cuneiform wedges into a clay tablet using a stick.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Black vs. red figure* (30 min): Compare black-figure and red-figure vases from the Met's collection and list differences.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Passing* (40 min): Practice inside-foot passes with a partner or against a wall at 5 and 10 meters.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Screen-time log* (20 min): Log screen use and mood for two days and look for patterns.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *New Brian* (90 min): Read chapter 13 and complete a 'Brian then vs. now' character-change chart with evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Which is faster?* (60 min): Compare two runners and two recipes using tables with a common row; justify which is faster or stronger in writing.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Test* (50 min): Weigh an ice cube, keep it in your device for 60 minutes alongside a control cup, then weigh the remaining ice.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Scribe school* (45 min): Read about scribal schools and write a letter from a student scribe describing a day.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Design* (30 min): Design a vase showing a myth or a modern event with a Greek key border.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Receiving* (40 min): Trap balls with the inside of the foot and thigh, then pass in one motion.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Algorithms* (20 min): Learn how algorithms keep users scrolling and identify design features that do this.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 34, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Pronoun shifts* (90 min): Lesson on shifts in pronoun number and person; correct 12 sentences such as 'If a person gets lost, you should stay calm'.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Problem solving* (60 min): Solve 6 multi-step ratio word problems from Illustrative Math Grade 6 Unit 2, showing a table for each.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Redesign* (50 min): Analyze which features worked, modify the design and retest.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Gilgamesh* (45 min): Read the flood episode of the Epic of Gilgamesh and discuss what it reveals about Mesopotamian beliefs.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Create* (30 min): Create the vase in black-figure style with cut paper or on an air-dry clay pot.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Small-sided game* (40 min): Play 2v1 keep-away to practice passing into open space.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Media plan* (20 min): Write a family media plan with screen-free times and zones.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 35, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Draft a theme statement for Hatchet (not just a topic word) and support it with three details; quiz on chapters 10-13.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on completing tables, graphing ratio pairs and comparing ratios.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Present and check* (50 min): Present your data and final design, explaining how it reduces each type of heat transfer; take the unit test.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on writing and short response on why writing mattered for government and trade.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Present the story on your vase and evaluate your use of border patterns.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Skill check* (40 min): Complete a skill check: 10 passes through a 1-meter cone gate and a timed dribble course.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take the Emotions and Stress unit test.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Hatchet chapters 10-13: mistakes and growth
- Objective: Trace character development
- Objective: Identify the novel's emerging theme
- Vocabulary: theme, perseverance, motivation, resilience
- Materials: Hatchet, character-change chart
- Assessment: Theme statement paragraph and quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Tables of equivalent ratios
- Objective: Organize ratios in tables
- Objective: Graph ratio pairs on the coordinate plane
- Objective: Compare ratios
- Vocabulary: table, row, column, coordinate plane, ordered pair
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos (optional)
- Assessment: Quiz on ratio tables and graphs.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Design challenge: keep it cold
- Objective: Design, build and test a device that minimizes thermal energy transfer
- Objective: Analyze test data to improve a design
- Vocabulary: insulation, prototype, optimize, trade-off
- Materials: small boxes or cups, foil, cotton balls, bubble wrap, newspaper, ice cubes, kitchen scale
- Assessment: Design challenge rubric and Unit 2 test.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Writing and the Epic of Gilgamesh
- Objective: Trace the development of cuneiform
- Objective: Analyze a literary primary source
- Vocabulary: cuneiform, scribe, stylus, pictograph, epic
- Materials: air-dry clay or play dough, chopstick or craft stick, excerpts of the Epic of Gilgamesh (Fordham Sourcebook)
- Assessment: Clay tablet and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Greek pottery
- Objective: Distinguish black-figure and red-figure pottery
- Objective: Design a vase that tells a story
- Vocabulary: amphora, black-figure, red-figure, meander (Greek key), frieze
- Materials: orange and black paper, white pencil, scissors, air-dry clay (optional)
- Assessment: Greek vase project.

### Physical Education 6: Soccer: dribbling and passing
- Objective: Dribble with control using inside and outside of foot
- Objective: Pass accurately with the inside of the foot
- Vocabulary: dribble, inside-foot pass, trap, first touch
- Materials: soccer ball, cones
- Assessment: Soccer skill check.

### Health 6: Media and social media influences on emotions
- Objective: Analyze social media's effect on mood and self-image
- Objective: Plan healthy media habits
- Vocabulary: social media, comparison, algorithm, FOMO, digital wellness
- Materials: screen-time log, magazines
- Assessment: Unit test.
