## Day 1 — Monday (school day 106, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Early childhood* (90 min): Read chapters 1-2 and list how Keller introduces her illness and frustrations; note how the adult Keller looks back on her child self.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Same value, different form* (60 min): Substitute several values into pairs like 3(x + 2) and 3x + 6 to test equivalence, recording results in a table.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Height and energy* (50 min): Drop a ball into flour or sand from 3 heights and measure crater depth as evidence of stored energy.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Geography and monsoons* (45 min): Map the Indus and Ganges rivers, Himalayas and Deccan Plateau and explain the monsoon cycle.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Film scores* (30 min): Watch a film scene with and without sound and describe how music changes the meaning.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Positions* (40 min): Learn infield and outfield positions and backing-up responsibilities.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Choking* (20 min): Learn signs of choking and practice abdominal-thrust hand position on a pillow (no thrusts on people).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 107, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *The 'water' moment* (90 min): Read chapters 3-4 and analyze the water-pump moment: how does Keller's word choice show it was life-changing?
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Distributive property with variables* (60 min): Use area diagrams to expand 4(x + 3) and factor 6x + 18 (12 problems).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Elastic energy* (50 min): Launch rubber bands from a ruler at different stretch lengths and measure distance traveled.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Planned cities* (45 min): Study a plan of Mohenjo-daro and list evidence of planning (grid streets, drains, Great Bath).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Leitmotif* (30 min): Identify leitmotifs in a film score example and create a leitmotif for a character.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Game 1* (40 min): Play a modified softball game with every player batting each inning.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Allergic reactions* (20 min): Learn signs of anaphylaxis and how an epinephrine auto-injector trainer is used.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 108, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Commas, parentheses, dashes* (90 min): Lesson on setting off nonrestrictive and parenthetical elements; punctuate 15 sentences about Keller three ways and discuss the effect.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Combining like terms* (60 min): Use paper algebra tiles to simplify expressions like 2x + 5 + 3x + 1 and explain why x + x is 2x.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Magnetic potential energy* (50 min): Hold two magnets at different separations and explain where energy is stored in the system.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Mystery of the seals* (45 min): Examine Indus seals and explain why the script is still undeciphered.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Score a scene* (30 min): Create music or sound effects to accompany a short public-domain silent film clip.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Game 2* (40 min): Play again, rotating positions.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Hands-only CPR intro* (20 min): Watch an American Heart Association Hands-Only CPR video and practice compression rhythm on a pillow.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 109, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Point of view* (90 min): Write a paragraph on Keller's purpose in writing her autobiography, with two quotations.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Prove or disprove* (60 min): Decide whether 8 pairs of expressions are equivalent and justify with properties or a counterexample.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Energy Skate Park* (50 min): Use PhET Energy Skate Park to track potential and kinetic energy bar graphs as the skater moves.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Decline theories* (45 min): Evaluate three theories for the decline of Harappan cities and choose the best supported.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Final composition* (30 min): Revise your ABA composition and perform or play back the final version.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Home run derby* (40 min): Hold a placement derby where points depend on hitting target zones.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *911 or not?* (20 min): Decide in 10 scenarios whether to call 911, call a parent, or give first aid.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 110, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Reading quiz on chapters 1-4 and punctuation quiz.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on generating and identifying equivalent expressions.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Draw energy models for a roller coaster at four points and take a quiz on potential energy.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on Indian geography and the Indus civilization.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & critique* (30 min): Evaluate your composition and film score with criteria and write a reflection.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Unit check* (40 min): Take a quiz on striking and fielding rules and strategies.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take the Injury Prevention and First Aid unit test.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: The Story of My Life, chapters 1-4
- Objective: Determine how an author introduces key ideas and events
- Objective: Analyze point of view in autobiography
- Vocabulary: autobiography, memoir, affliction, tantrum, perspective
- Materials: 'The Story of My Life' by Helen Keller (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Quiz and purpose paragraph.

### Mathematics 6: Equivalent expressions
- Objective: Use properties to write equivalent expressions
- Objective: Combine like terms
- Vocabulary: equivalent, like terms, commutative, associative, distributive
- Materials: algebra tiles (paper)
- Assessment: Equivalent-expressions quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Potential energy
- Objective: Model how changing arrangement changes stored potential energy
- Vocabulary: potential energy, gravitational potential energy, elastic potential energy, system
- Materials: rubber bands, ruler, ball, magnets, PhET Energy Skate Park
- Assessment: Energy model and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: The Indus Valley civilization
- Objective: Describe the geography of the Indian subcontinent
- Objective: Analyze Harappan city planning
- Vocabulary: subcontinent, monsoon, Himalayas, Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, citadel
- Materials: map of South Asia, images of Mohenjo-daro and Indus seals
- Assessment: Quiz and decline-theory evaluation.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Music and other arts: film scoring
- Objective: Refine and present a composition
- Objective: Connect music to film and storytelling
- Vocabulary: film score, leitmotif, soundtrack, mood
- Materials: silent film clip (e.g., Charlie Chaplin, public domain), composition
- Assessment: Final composition and film score presentation.

### Physical Education 6: Modified softball games
- Objective: Apply striking and fielding skills in games
- Objective: Cooperate in fielding positions
- Vocabulary: infield, outfield, inning, backing up
- Materials: whiffle ball and bat, bases, gloves
- Assessment: Rules and strategy quiz.

### Health 6: First aid basics II
- Objective: Respond to choking and allergic reactions
- Objective: Recognize when to call 911
- Vocabulary: choking, abdominal thrusts, anaphylaxis, epinephrine auto-injector, CPR
- Materials: training video, pillow for practice
- Assessment: Unit test.
