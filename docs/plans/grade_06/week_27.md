## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Love potion chaos* (90 min): Read selected scenes from Act 3 and chart who loves whom before and after the potion.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Writing inequalities* (60 min): Write inequalities for constraints like 'you must be at least 48 inches to ride' and 'fewer than 30 students' (10 situations).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Naked egg* (50 min): Soak a raw egg in vinegar to dissolve the shell, measuring circumference before and after.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Mandate of Heaven* (45 min): Draw the dynastic cycle and explain how the Zhou used the Mandate of Heaven to justify rule.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Composition rules* (30 min): Study example photos for rule of thirds, leading lines and framing.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Target heart rate* (40 min): Calculate your target heart-rate zone and check it during a 10-minute jog.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Trust your gut* (20 min): Discuss intuition and practice identifying uh-oh feelings in scenarios.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 132, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Bottom's transformation* (90 min): Read the Bottom and Titania scene aloud and identify puns and wordplay.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Graphing solutions* (60 min): Graph x > c, x < c, x >= c and x <= c on number lines and test values with substitution.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Egg osmosis* (50 min): Move the shell-less egg into corn syrup and later water, measuring and explaining size changes with osmosis.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Warring States* (45 min): Read about disorder in the Warring States period and list problems Confucius saw in society.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Rule of thirds* (30 min): Take 10 photos using the rule-of-thirds grid and choose the best two.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Pacing* (40 min): Run four laps trying to keep each lap within 5 seconds of the others.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Boundaries* (20 min): Learn personal-boundary rights and practice saying no to unwanted touch or requests.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 133, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Resolution* (90 min): Read Act 4 and the Pyramus and Thisbe performance in Act 5; explain how each plotline resolves.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Infinitely many solutions* (60 min): Explain why x > 3 has infinitely many solutions and identify whether given values are solutions.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Potato osmosis* (50 min): Soak potato slices in plain and salty water and compare their mass and stiffness after an hour.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *The Analects* (45 min): Read 10 sayings from the Analects and explain the five relationships and filial piety.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Angles* (30 min): Photograph the same object from bird's-eye, eye-level and worm's-eye views.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Interval run* (40 min): Alternate 1 minute fast and 1 minute easy for 16 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Grooming warning signs* (20 min): Learn warning signs such as secrets, gifts and isolation from adults or online contacts.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 134, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Play vs. retelling* (90 min): Compare the Lambs' prose version with Shakespeare's play: what is lost and gained? Write two paragraphs.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Inequalities in context* (60 min): Solve real problems like saving at least $120 and decide which values make sense in context (whole people, decimals of money).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Diffusion* (50 min): Place a scented extract in a balloon and detect diffusion through the membrane; model particle movement.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Daoism* (45 min): Read passages of the Dao De Jing and compare Daoist ideas about nature and government with Confucian ideas.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Photo walk* (30 min): Take a neighborhood photo walk and capture leading lines, patterns and textures.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Long run* (40 min): Run or run-walk 20 continuous minutes and record distance.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Escape plan* (20 min): Practice No-Go-Tell and create a code word with your family.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 135, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a comprehension quiz and write a short analysis of how one scene contributes to the play's comedy.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on writing, graphing and interpreting inequalities.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a lab report explaining how the egg membrane acts like a cell membrane.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a dialogue between Confucius and Laozi about how to fix a troubled kingdom.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & critique* (30 min): Select your best 5 photos and explain the composition technique in each.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Check* (40 min): Run a timed mile and compare with baseline.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take the Violence Prevention and Personal Safety unit quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: A Midsummer Night's Dream, Acts 3-5
- Objective: Analyze how scenes build comic conflict
- Objective: Compare prose retelling and play
- Vocabulary: mistaken identity, pun, resolution, play within a play
- Materials: Folger text of the play, Lambs' Tales
- Assessment: Quiz and scene analysis.

### Mathematics 6: Inequalities
- Objective: Write inequalities for real constraints
- Objective: Graph solutions on a number line
- Vocabulary: inequality, solution set, open circle, closed circle
- Materials: number line strips
- Assessment: Inequality quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): The cell membrane and getting materials
- Objective: Model how the membrane controls what enters and leaves
- Objective: Investigate osmosis
- Vocabulary: diffusion, osmosis, selectively permeable, concentration
- Materials: raw egg, vinegar, corn syrup, water, potato slices, salt
- Assessment: Osmosis lab report.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Zhou, Mandate of Heaven, Confucius and Laozi
- Objective: Explain the Mandate of Heaven
- Objective: Describe Confucianism and Daoism and the problems they addressed
- Vocabulary: Mandate of Heaven, dynastic cycle, Warring States, Confucianism, filial piety, Daoism, yin and yang
- Materials: Analects of Confucius excerpts, Dao De Jing excerpts
- Assessment: Philosophers' dialogue.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Photography composition
- Objective: Apply rule of thirds, leading lines and framing
- Objective: Take intentional photos
- Vocabulary: rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, angle, depth of field
- Materials: phone or tablet camera
- Assessment: Curated 5-photo set with captions.

### Physical Education 6: Distance running and pacing
- Objective: Pace a run evenly
- Objective: Use heart rate to monitor intensity
- Vocabulary: pace, split, target heart rate zone, endurance
- Materials: stopwatch, measured loop
- Assessment: Mile time and pacing splits.

### Health 6: Personal safety and boundaries
- Objective: Recognize unsafe situations
- Objective: Use refusal and escape skills
- Vocabulary: boundaries, consent, grooming, trusted adult, intuition
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Unit quiz.
