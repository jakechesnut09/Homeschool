## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Inside the garden* (90 min): Read chapters 8-10 and note sensory images of the garden coming alive; compare to Mary's changes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Statistical questions* (60 min): Sort 12 questions as statistical or not (e.g., 'How old am I?' vs 'How old are students at my co-op?') and write 3 of your own.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Heart rate investigation* (50 min): Measure resting heart rate and heart rate after 1 minute of jumping jacks, three trials each, and graph.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Italy's geography* (45 min): Map the Italian peninsula, Tiber River, seven hills and Mediterranean location, and explain advantages for Rome.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Test shots* (30 min): Shoot a 2-second test and adjust lighting and camera stability.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Map reading* (40 min): Draw a map of your yard or a park with a legend and north arrow.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Pathogens* (20 min): Compare bacteria, viruses, fungi and protozoa and give a disease example for each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 147, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Dickon* (90 min): Read chapters 11-12 and analyze Dickon's role and how others respond to him.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Collect data* (60 min): Survey 20 or more people (family, friends, co-op) with your statistical question and record results in a frequency table.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Path of blood* (50 min): Trace the path of blood through the heart, lungs and body on a diagram.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Legends* (45 min): Read Livy's account of Romulus and Remus and the Aeneas legend; separate legend from archaeological evidence.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Shoot part 1* (30 min): Shoot the first half of the animation, moving figures in small increments.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Compass* (40 min): Learn to take a bearing and walk to a target 20 paces away.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *How germs spread* (20 min): Model germ spread with glitter on hands and objects around the house.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 148, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Colin* (90 min): Read chapters 13-14 and compare Colin with Mary at the beginning of the book.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Dot plots* (60 min): Make a dot plot of your survey data and of a provided data set on daily screen time.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Lung model* (50 min): Build a bottle-and-balloon lung model and explain how the diaphragm moves air.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Etruscans and Greeks* (45 min): Identify what Rome borrowed from Etruscans and Greeks (arches, alphabet, gods).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Shoot part 2* (30 min): Shoot the second half.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Score-O* (40 min): Find as many control points as possible in 20 minutes on a parent-made course.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Handwashing test* (20 min): Test glitter removal with water only vs. 20-second soap wash and record results.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 149, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Narrator* (90 min): Read chapter 15; find places where the omniscient narrator comments directly, and discuss how that shapes our view.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Describing distributions* (60 min): Describe the center, spread and shape (symmetric, skewed, clusters, gaps) of three dot plots in writing.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Systems interact* (50 min): Explain in writing how the circulatory and respiratory systems interact during exercise using your heart rate data.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Cincinnatus* (45 min): Read the story of Cincinnatus and explain the Roman value of civic duty; connect to George Washington.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Edit and sound* (30 min): Edit timing, add titles, credits and sound or music.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Design a course* (40 min): Design an orienteering course for a family member.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Immune defenses* (20 min): Draw the body's lines of defense (skin, mucus, white blood cells, antibodies) and explain how vaccines work.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 150, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Book-club style discussion of three questions you prepared; quiz on chapters 8-15.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on statistical questions and dot plots.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a CER arguing that the body is a system of interacting subsystems using exercise data.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on early Rome and legend vs. history.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Screening* (30 min): Screen the animation for your family and reflect on what you would improve.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Check* (40 min): Complete a timed orienteering course and reflect on navigation strategy.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a communicable disease quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: The Secret Garden, chapters 8-15
- Objective: Trace character change
- Objective: Explain how the narrator develops point of view
- Vocabulary: omniscient narrator, transformation, wick, hysterics
- Materials: The Secret Garden
- Assessment: Discussion rubric and quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Statistical questions and dot plots
- Objective: Recognize statistical questions
- Objective: Make and describe dot plots
- Vocabulary: statistical question, variability, data, dot plot, distribution
- Materials: family survey sheet, graph paper
- Assessment: Quiz plus survey dot plot.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Circulatory and respiratory systems
- Objective: Explain how the heart and lungs work together
- Objective: Measure heart rate and breathing rate
- Vocabulary: heart, artery, vein, capillary, lungs, alveoli, oxygen
- Materials: stopwatch, plastic bottle, balloons, straw, clay
- Assessment: Heart rate graph and CER.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Founding of Rome
- Objective: Describe Rome's geography
- Objective: Distinguish legend from history in Rome's founding
- Vocabulary: Tiber River, Etruscans, legend, Aeneas, Romulus and Remus, republic
- Materials: map of Italy, Livy's account of Romulus and Remus (excerpt)
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Stop-motion animation: production
- Objective: Shoot and edit a stop-motion animation
- Objective: Refine timing and movement
- Vocabulary: timing, easing, soundtrack, credits
- Materials: phone on a stand, stop-motion app, set and characters
- Assessment: Completed stop-motion animation.

### Physical Education 6: Orienteering
- Objective: Read a map and use a compass
- Objective: Navigate a course
- Vocabulary: orienteering, compass bearing, control point, map legend
- Materials: compass or phone compass, hand-drawn park map, control markers
- Assessment: Course completion time.

### Health 6: Communicable diseases and the immune system
- Objective: Explain how pathogens spread
- Objective: Describe immune defenses
- Vocabulary: pathogen, bacteria, virus, immune system, antibody, vaccine
- Materials: glitter or flour, soap, timer
- Assessment: Disease quiz.
