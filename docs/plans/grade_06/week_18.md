## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Images* (90 min): Read 'Fog' and sketch the image it creates; identify the metaphor and explain what it suggests about fog.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Partial quotients* (60 min): Divide 3- and 4-digit numbers by 2-digit divisors using partial quotients, then compare to the standard algorithm.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Magnet exploration* (50 min): Test which household objects are attracted to a magnet and how poles interact; record results.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Sparta vs. Athens* (45 min): Read about education, women's roles and government in Sparta and Athens and complete a comparison chart.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Vocal warm-ups* (30 min): Learn breathing and vocal warm-ups and sing a major scale on solfege.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Four square and paddle games* (40 min): Play four square and paddle games to practice placement.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Injury facts* (20 min): Read data on the leading causes of injury for kids 10–14 and graph the top five.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Extended metaphor* (90 min): Read Dickinson's 'Hope is the thing with feathers' and annotate each stanza's development of the bird metaphor.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Long division* (60 min): Complete 12 long-division problems with the standard algorithm, checking each by multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Distance and strength* (50 min): Measure how many paper clips a magnet holds at different distances through layers of paper; graph the data.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Persian Empire* (45 min): Map the Persian Empire under Cyrus and Darius and describe satrapies, the Royal Road and tolerance policies.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Rounds* (30 min): Sing 'Row, Row, Row Your Boat' as a round with family members.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Mid-year fitness check* (40 min): Retake the PACER or mile and the push-up test and compare to baseline.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Home audit* (20 min): Complete a home safety audit with a parent (smoke and CO alarms, cords, chemicals, stairs).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 88, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Hughes's dreams* (90 min): Read Hughes's 'Dreams' and 'Mother to Son'; compare their metaphors and tones.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Dividing decimals* (60 min): Divide decimals by whole numbers and by decimals by multiplying both numbers by powers of 10 (12 problems).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Mapping fields* (50 min): Map the magnetic field around a bar magnet using a compass or iron filings under paper and draw field lines.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Marathon and Thermopylae* (45 min): Read Herodotus's accounts and evaluate him as a source (what bias might he have?).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Two-part harmony* (30 min): Learn 'Dona Nobis Pacem' and sing one part while a recording or family member sings another.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Tournament day 1* (40 min): Play a family volleyball or badminton tournament round.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Kitchen safety* (20 min): List rules for preventing burns and cuts and practice safe stove use with an adult.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 89, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Connotation* (90 min): Sort word pairs by connotation (thrifty/stingy, slender/skinny) and rewrite a stanza changing connotations to alter tone.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Decimal word problems* (60 min): Solve problems such as splitting a $47.85 bill among 3 people and finding miles per gallon.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Earth's field* (50 min): Make a compass by magnetizing a needle and floating it on a cork; explain Earth's magnetic field.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Salamis* (45 min): Explain how Athenian naval power won at Salamis and diagram the battle.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Rehearsal* (30 min): Rehearse a song and an instrumental piece for a mini-concert using performance criteria.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Tournament day 2* (40 min): Play final rounds and practice handshakes and positive comments.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Poison prevention* (20 min): Check chemical storage and post the Poison Control number.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 90, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write your own poem using an extended metaphor; quiz on figurative language identification.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Semester 1 cumulative review quiz covering area, ratios, percents, fraction division and decimals.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a CER on how distance affects magnetic force using your graph.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Semester 1 cumulative review quiz covering early humans through the Persian Wars.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Mid-year concert* (30 min): Perform a mid-year family concert (one song, one instrumental piece) and self-evaluate.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Goal check* (40 min): Update your SMART goals based on mid-year results.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a home safety action plan and complete a check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Figurative language in poetry
- Objective: Identify simile, metaphor, personification and hyperbole
- Objective: Explain how figurative language affects meaning
- Vocabulary: simile, metaphor, personification, hyperbole, imagery, connotation
- Materials: 'Fog' by Carl Sandburg, 'Hope is the thing with feathers' by Emily Dickinson, 'Dreams' by Langston Hughes
- Assessment: Figurative language quiz and original poem.

### Mathematics 6: Long division and dividing decimals
- Objective: Divide multi-digit whole numbers with the standard algorithm
- Objective: Divide decimals
- Vocabulary: long division, partial quotients, remainder
- Materials: graph paper for aligning digits
- Assessment: Semester 1 cumulative quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Magnetic forces and fields
- Objective: Investigate factors affecting magnetic force
- Objective: Map magnetic fields
- Vocabulary: magnet, magnetic field, pole, attract, repel, field lines
- Materials: bar magnets, paper clips, iron filings or compass, ruler, paper
- Assessment: Lab data graph and CER.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Athens, Sparta and the Persian Wars
- Objective: Compare Athens and Sparta
- Objective: Outline the Persian Empire
- Objective: Explain the Persian Wars
- Vocabulary: Sparta, helot, phalanx, satrapy, Marathon, Thermopylae, Salamis
- Materials: map of the Persian Empire, Herodotus excerpts on Marathon and Thermopylae
- Assessment: Semester 1 cumulative quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Singing and ensemble
- Objective: Sing with good posture and breath support
- Objective: Perform in parts
- Vocabulary: round, harmony, unison, breath support, posture
- Materials: lyrics for rounds ('Dona Nobis Pacem', 'Row, Row, Row Your Boat')
- Assessment: Mid-year concert rubric.

### Physical Education 6: Net games tournament and mid-year check
- Objective: Apply net-game strategies
- Objective: Check fitness progress
- Vocabulary: strategy, placement, progress
- Materials: volleyball, badminton set, fitness log
- Assessment: Mid-year fitness comparison and updated goals.

### Health 6: Injury prevention at home
- Objective: Identify home hazards
- Objective: Plan prevention for falls, burns, poisonings
- Vocabulary: hazard, injury, prevention, poison, carbon monoxide
- Materials: home safety checklist, flashlight
- Assessment: Home safety plan.
