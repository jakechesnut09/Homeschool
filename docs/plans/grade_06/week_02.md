## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Situational irony* (90 min): Read 'The Ransom of Red Chief' and chart what the kidnappers expect vs. what happens; write two sentences explaining the irony.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Two copies make a parallelogram* (60 min): Trace and cut two identical triangles, join them into a parallelogram, and explain why a triangle's area is half of b x h.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Defining problems* (50 min): Analyze three everyday products (e.g., a lunch box) and list their criteria and constraints.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Life on the move* (45 min): Read about hunter-gatherer daily life and create a chart of food, shelter, tools and social roles.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Value scale* (30 min): Draw a 9-step value scale from white to black using pressure and layering.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *PACER or mile* (40 min): Complete the 20-meter PACER test or a timed one-mile run/walk and record laps or time.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *DECIDE model* (20 min): Learn the DECIDE model (Define, Explore, Consider, Identify values, Decide, Evaluate) and apply it to a scenario about staying up late.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Narrator's voice* (90 min): Analyze how Sam's first-person narration and fancy vocabulary create humor; rewrite one paragraph in plain third person and compare the effect.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Right triangles* (60 min): Find areas of 10 right triangles on a grid and write a rule using the two legs as base and height.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Index card bridge* (50 min): Design and build a bridge from 10 index cards and 30 cm of tape that spans 20 cm and holds the most pennies.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Tool technology* (45 min): Analyze images of Oldowan and Acheulean hand axes and explain how tools improved over time.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Shading techniques* (30 min): Practice hatching, cross-hatching, stippling and blending in four boxes.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Curl-ups* (40 min): Learn correct curl-up form and complete the cadence curl-up test.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Source detective* (20 min): Compare a .gov site, a health blog and an influencer video using a CRAAP-style checklist.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Twist endings* (90 min): Read 'After Twenty Years' and 'The Open Window'; mark foreshadowing clues on a second read and discuss which twist was fairer to the reader.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Any base, any height* (60 min): For acute and obtuse triangles, draw all three heights with a ruler and show the area comes out the same using each base.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Test and redesign* (50 min): Test the bridge, record the penny count, then redesign and retest once.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Fire* (45 min): Write a paragraph explaining at least four ways controlling fire changed early human life.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Shapes to forms* (30 min): Turn a circle, square and triangle into a sphere, cube and cone with shading.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Push-ups* (40 min): Learn 90-degree push-up form and complete the cadence push-up test.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Bias and ads* (20 min): Identify who profits from two health claims online and how that creates bias.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 9, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Context clues* (90 min): Use context clues to define 10 challenging words from the O. Henry stories, then verify with a dictionary.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Triangle formula practice* (60 min): Solve 12 triangle-area problems including missing-base and missing-height problems (e.g., area 24 sq cm and base 8 cm; find height).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Decision matrix* (50 min): Score three bridge designs (yours and two sketches) in a weighted decision matrix and choose the best.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Cave art* (45 min): Study images of the Lascaux and Chauvet cave paintings and infer what they show about beliefs and daily life.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Still life* (30 min): Draw an egg lit by a lamp, showing highlight, core shadow, reflected light and cast shadow.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Active recovery* (40 min): Play a low-intensity game (frisbee catch) and record test results in a fitness log.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Practice decision* (20 min): Use DECIDE on a scenario of a friend daring you to jump off a high wall.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 10, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a short comparison of how two of the three stories use point of view to surprise readers; quiz on irony and pronoun case.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz mixing triangle and parallelogram area, then correct mistakes and explain one in a short paragraph.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a short engineering report: problem, criteria, constraints, designs tested and results.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a 'day in the life' diary entry of a hunter-gatherer using at least six accurate details.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Critique your egg drawing with a checklist and redraw one area to improve it.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Check* (40 min): Compare your results with the Healthy Fitness Zone charts for your age.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Evaluate a new health website in writing and take a 6-question quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Irony and point of view: O. Henry and Saki
- Objective: Identify situational irony
- Objective: Explain how narration affects what readers know
- Vocabulary: irony, narrator, first person, third person, foreshadowing, ransom
- Materials: 'The Ransom of Red Chief' and 'After Twenty Years' by O. Henry, 'The Open Window' by Saki
- Assessment: Comparison paragraph and quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Area of triangles
- Objective: Derive A = 1/2 bh from two copies of a triangle
- Objective: Find any base and height of a triangle
- Vocabulary: triangle, opposite vertex, formula, right triangle
- Materials: paper triangles, ruler, scissors
- Assessment: Quiz on triangle area including finding missing dimensions.

### Science 6 (Integrated): The engineering design process
- Objective: Define criteria and constraints
- Objective: Evaluate design solutions with a decision matrix
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraints, prototype, iterate, decision matrix
- Materials: index cards, tape, pennies, straws
- Assessment: Engineering report.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Hunter-gatherers and early tools
- Objective: Describe Paleolithic life
- Objective: Explain the importance of tools and fire
- Vocabulary: Paleolithic, hominin, hunter-gatherer, nomad, flint, Stone Age
- Materials: photos of stone tools (Smithsonian Human Origins), smooth stones, clay
- Assessment: Historically accurate diary entry.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Shape, form and value
- Objective: Turn shapes into forms using value
- Objective: Create a value scale
- Vocabulary: shape, form, value, shading, highlight, cast shadow
- Materials: pencils, blending stump or tissue, egg or ball, lamp
- Assessment: Value scale and still-life drawing.

### Physical Education 6: Fitness testing: aerobic capacity and strength
- Objective: Complete baseline fitness tests
- Objective: Record results accurately
- Vocabulary: aerobic capacity, PACER, muscular endurance, curl-up, push-up
- Materials: PACER audio (free online) or measured 20 m course, mat, measuring tape
- Assessment: Baseline aerobic and strength scores.

### Health 6: Decision making and valid health information
- Objective: Apply a decision-making model
- Objective: Evaluate health information sources
- Vocabulary: decision-making model, valid, bias, credentials
- Materials: computer, source checklist
- Assessment: Website evaluation and quiz.
