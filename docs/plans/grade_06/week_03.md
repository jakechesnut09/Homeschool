## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Finding a story* (90 min): Brainstorm a heart map of memorable moments, choose one small moment, and tell it aloud to a listener in two minutes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Decomposing polygons* (60 min): Split a trapezoid, a kite and a pentagon into triangles and rectangles and find each area; compare two different decompositions.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Food coloring in hot and cold water* (50 min): Add a drop of food coloring to cups of hot and cold water at the same time, time how long each takes to mix, and record observations.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Out of Africa* (45 min): Map the major human migration routes out of Africa with approximate dates.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Color wheel* (30 min): Paint a 12-part color wheel by mixing only red, yellow and blue.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Sit-and-reach* (40 min): Complete the back-saver sit-and-reach test on both legs.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Feelings wheel* (20 min): Use a feelings wheel to identify 15 emotions beyond happy/sad/mad and give a situation for each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Strong leads* (90 min): Study leads from the unit's stories and write three possible openings for your narrative (action, dialogue, setting).
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Trapezoid strategies* (60 min): Use two copies of a trapezoid to make a parallelogram and solve 8 trapezoid-area problems with your own reasoning.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Particle model* (50 min): Draw particle models of the two cups and explain the difference using particle motion.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Ice Age land bridges* (45 min): Explain how lower sea levels during the Ice Age created Beringia and allowed people to reach the Americas.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Tints and shades* (30 min): Paint a tint and shade strip of one color with 7 steps; practice proper brush cleaning and water conservation.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Trunk lift* (40 min): Complete the trunk-lift test and learn safe back-strengthening exercises.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Body cues* (20 min): Map where you feel anger, anxiety and excitement in your body on an outline.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Drafting with dialogue* (90 min): Draft the middle of your narrative, including at least four lines of correctly punctuated dialogue.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Measure a room* (60 min): Measure a room or yard at home, sketch it to scale on graph paper, and calculate its area in square feet.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Simulation* (50 min): Use the PhET States of Matter simulation to heat and cool a substance and record how particle speed changes with temperature.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Adapting to environments* (45 min): Compare how people adapted clothing, shelter and food in the Arctic, desert and rainforest.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Color schemes* (30 min): Paint the same simple landscape twice, once in warm analogous colors and once in cool colors.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Components of fitness* (40 min): Learn the five components of health-related fitness and match each test to a component; play a circuit game.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Triggers* (20 min): Track emotional triggers in a journal for two days.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Slow-motion moment* (90 min): Expand the most important moment with sensory detail and inner thoughts so it takes up at least a full paragraph.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Covering a surface* (60 min): Determine how many 1 ft square tiles and how many boxes of tiles (10 per box) would cover the room you measured, including irregular parts.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *How thermometers work* (50 min): Build a simple thermometer from a bottle, colored water and a straw, and test it in warm and cool water baths.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *First Californians* (45 min): Read about early evidence of people in California (e.g., Channel Islands sites) and add it to the timeline.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Complementary pop* (30 min): Create a small abstract painting using a complementary color pair to make a focal point.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *SMART goals* (40 min): Write two SMART goals based on your baseline results.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Healthy expression* (20 min): Sort responses to anger into healthy and unhealthy and role-play two healthy responses.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 15, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Ending and reflection* (90 min): Draft an ending that reflects on why the moment mattered and read the full draft aloud for a family member's feedback.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a 6-problem composite-figure assessment and present your floor plan calculation to a family member.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a CER explaining why food coloring spreads faster in hot water; quiz on temperature vs. thermal energy.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Map quiz on migration routes and a short answer on human adaptation.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Compare your two landscapes and write how color changed the mood.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Check* (40 min): Share goals with a parent and do a 20-minute circuit targeting your goal areas.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a journal entry analyzing one emotion, its trigger and your response; complete a check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Personal narrative: drafting
- Objective: Plan a personal narrative around a meaningful moment
- Objective: Use dialogue, pacing and sensory detail
- Vocabulary: narrative, dialogue, pacing, sensory detail, reflection
- Materials: mentor text: opening of 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi', writing notebook
- Assessment: Complete draft checked against a narrative checklist.

### Mathematics 6: Polygons and real-world area
- Objective: Find area of trapezoids and other polygons by decomposing
- Objective: Solve real-world area problems
- Vocabulary: polygon, trapezoid, rhombus, kite, composite figure
- Materials: graph paper, measuring tape, home floor plan sketch
- Assessment: Composite polygon quiz plus floor-plan area project.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Temperature and particle motion
- Objective: Model matter as moving particles
- Objective: Relate temperature to average kinetic energy
- Vocabulary: particle, kinetic energy, temperature, thermal energy, thermometer
- Materials: food coloring, cups of hot and cold water, thermometer, PhET States of Matter
- Assessment: CER and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Peopling the world
- Objective: Trace human migration out of Africa
- Objective: Explain adaptations to different environments
- Vocabulary: migration, Ice Age, land bridge, adaptation, Beringia
- Materials: world outline map, colored pencils
- Assessment: Migration map and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Color theory
- Objective: Mix primary, secondary and tertiary colors
- Objective: Use color schemes to create mood
- Vocabulary: hue, tint, shade, complementary, analogous, warm, cool
- Materials: tempera or watercolor paints, brushes, paper plate palette
- Assessment: Color wheel and mood landscapes.

### Physical Education 6: Fitness testing: flexibility and goal setting
- Objective: Complete flexibility tests
- Objective: Set SMART fitness goals
- Vocabulary: flexibility, sit-and-reach, trunk lift, SMART goal
- Materials: box and ruler for sit-and-reach, fitness log
- Assessment: Written SMART goals.

### Health 6: Understanding emotions
- Objective: Identify a wide range of emotions
- Objective: Describe healthy expression of emotions
- Vocabulary: emotion, trigger, body cue, empathy, emotional regulation
- Materials: feelings wheel, health journal
- Assessment: Journal analysis and check.
