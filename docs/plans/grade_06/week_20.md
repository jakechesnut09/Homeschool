## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *The speaker* (90 min): Read 'The Road Not Taken' and describe the speaker's situation and attitude with evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Letters for numbers* (60 min): Write expressions for situations such as 'x more than 5' and 'a number divided by 3'; translate 15 phrases into expressions.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Falling objects* (50 min): Drop objects of different masses (a book and a crumpled paper) and test whether they fall at the same rate.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Gods and daily life* (45 min): Read about the Olympian gods, festivals and the Olympic Games and explain how religion shaped Greek life.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *AB and ABA* (30 min): Listen to songs and label AB and ABA sections using shapes and colors.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Stance and grip* (40 min): Learn batting stance and grip and practice dry swings.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Road rules* (20 min): Learn CA rules for cyclists and pedestrians, including helmet law under 18.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 97, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Theme in Frost* (90 min): Debate the poem's theme (is it really about choosing the 'less traveled' road?) and write a theme paragraph.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Parts of expressions* (60 min): Label terms, coefficients and constants in 10 expressions and describe 2(x + 5) as 'a product of two factors'.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Mass vs. weight* (50 min): Calculate your weight on the Moon, Mars and Jupiter using gravity ratios and explain why mass stays the same.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Aesop's Fables* (45 min): Read five of Aesop's Fables and write the moral of each, then write your own fable.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Rondo* (30 min): Map the ABACA form of Beethoven's 'Fur Elise'.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Tee hitting* (40 min): Hit 30 whiffle balls off a tee aiming for line drives.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Bike check* (20 min): Do an ABC quick check (air, brakes, chain) and ride a safe route with a parent.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 98, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Tone words* (90 min): Read Rossetti and Stevenson poems, choose precise tone words for each, and cite the lines that create that tone.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Expression match* (60 min): Play a card-matching game linking verbal descriptions, expressions and tape diagrams.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Orbits simulation* (50 min): Use PhET Gravity and Orbits to change the Sun's and Earth's masses and record effects on the orbit.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Greek theater* (45 min): Learn the parts of a Greek theater, tragedy vs. comedy, and make a Greek drama mask.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Theme and variations* (30 min): Listen to Mozart's 'Twelve Variations on Ah vous dirai-je, Maman' and describe how three variations change the theme.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Soft toss* (40 min): Hit soft-tossed balls from a partner.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Distractions* (20 min): Discuss phone and headphone distraction while walking or riding.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 99, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Free verse* (90 min): Read a free-verse poem from CommonLit and compare its structure with Frost's rhymed stanzas.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Evaluating expressions* (60 min): Evaluate 12 expressions for given variable values, e.g., 3x + 4 when x = 2.5.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Ball on a string* (50 min): Swing a ball on a string to model an orbit and explain what happens when the string (gravity) is removed.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Columns hunt* (45 min): Identify Doric, Ionic and Corinthian columns and find examples on buildings in your community (courthouses, banks).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Compare* (30 min): Choose two contrasting pieces you like and compare their form, purpose and mood in writing.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Placement* (40 min): Practice hitting to left, center and right field targets.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Risk influences* (20 min): Analyze stunt videos and peer dares that encourage risky behavior.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 100, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Analyze an unseen poem independently for speaker, tone and theme as a quiz.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on writing, reading and evaluating expressions.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write an argument with evidence that gravitational force depends on mass and distance.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): List 10 modern words, brands or symbols from Greek mythology and explain their origins.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & check* (30 min): Take a listening quiz on musical forms.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Skill check* (40 min): Hit 10 pitched balls and record contacts.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Map a safe route to a local destination and explain hazards.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Speaker, tone and theme
- Objective: Distinguish poet and speaker
- Objective: Determine theme in poetry
- Vocabulary: speaker, tone, mood, theme, free verse
- Materials: 'The Road Not Taken' by Robert Frost, 'Who Has Seen the Wind?' by Christina Rossetti, 'The Land of Counterpane' by Robert Louis Stevenson
- Assessment: Independent poetry analysis.

### Mathematics 6: Variables and writing expressions
- Objective: Write expressions with variables from words
- Objective: Identify parts of an expression
- Vocabulary: variable, expression, term, coefficient, sum, product, quotient
- Materials: index cards
- Assessment: Expression quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Gravity
- Objective: Argue that gravity is attractive and depends on mass
- Objective: Explain orbits
- Vocabulary: gravity, mass, weight, orbit, gravitational force
- Materials: PhET Gravity and Orbits simulation, ball on a string, objects of different masses
- Assessment: Written argument.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Greek myths, literature and the arts
- Objective: Explain the role of mythology in Greek life
- Objective: Identify Greek words and symbols in modern life
- Vocabulary: myth, Olympian, fable, tragedy, comedy, column orders
- Materials: Aesop's Fables (Project Gutenberg), photos of Doric, Ionic and Corinthian columns
- Assessment: Fable and Greek legacy list.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Form in music
- Objective: Identify AB, ABA and rondo forms
- Objective: Compare contrasting musical works
- Vocabulary: form, binary, ternary, rondo, theme, variation
- Materials: recordings of Mozart 'Twinkle Variations', Beethoven 'Fur Elise'
- Assessment: Form listening quiz.

### Physical Education 6: Batting
- Objective: Swing with a level path
- Objective: Place hits to open space
- Vocabulary: stance, level swing, contact point, bunt
- Materials: bat or foam bat, tee or cone, whiffle balls
- Assessment: Batting contact check.

### Health 6: Pedestrian, bike and passenger safety
- Objective: Practice road safety
- Objective: Analyze risk-taking influences
- Vocabulary: pedestrian, right of way, seat belt, distraction, risk-taking
- Materials: bicycle, helmet, neighborhood map
- Assessment: Safe route map.
