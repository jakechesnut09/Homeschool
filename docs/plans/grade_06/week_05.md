## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Before the crash* (90 min): Read chapter 1, locate the flight route on a map of Canada, and note what we learn about Brian and the Secret.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Introducing ratios* (60 min): Describe collections of beans or counters with ratio language in three forms (3:2, 3 to 2, 3 for every 2) and write 10 ratio sentences.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Spoon conduction race* (50 min): Put a dab of butter on metal, wooden and plastic spoons standing in hot water and record which melts first.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Land between rivers* (45 min): Label a map of the Fertile Crescent with rivers, deserts, mountains and modern countries.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Cave painting study* (30 min): Study images of Lascaux and discuss what the animals may have meant to the artists.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Lava river* (40 min): Cross a 'lava river' using a limited number of poly spots as a group, planning before trying.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Breathing* (20 min): Practice box breathing and 4-7-8 breathing; record pulse before and after.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Crash landing* (90 min): Read chapters 2-3; list sensory words Paulsen uses during the crash and explain how short sentences build tension.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Ratio diagrams* (60 min): Draw discrete diagrams for recipe ratios (e.g., 2 cups flour for every 3 eggs) and answer which-ratio-matches questions.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Convection currents* (50 min): Drop cold blue-dyed water into warm water in a clear container and sketch the convection current.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Irrigation model* (45 min): Build an irrigation model in a tray of soil with a canal and test how water reaches 'fields'.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Cave art* (30 min): Crumple a brown paper bag for a rock texture and draw animals with charcoal and earth-tone pastels.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Group juggle* (40 min): Pass several balls in a set pattern around a circle and add balls as success grows.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Muscle relaxation* (20 min): Follow a progressive muscle relaxation script and journal how you feel.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *First night* (90 min): Read chapter 4; make a T-chart of Brian's physical and emotional problems and discuss which is worse.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Recipes and batches* (60 min): Make one batch and a double batch of a drink mix (e.g., 2 scoops per 1 cup water), taste-test that they match, and explain why the ratio is equivalent.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Radiation* (50 min): Place thermometers under black and white paper in sunlight or under a lamp and record temperatures every 2 minutes for 20 minutes.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Floods and silt* (45 min): Read about unpredictable Tigris-Euphrates floods and how Sumerians managed them with levees and canals.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Mesopotamian relief* (30 min): Study the Ishtar Gate and Assyrian lion reliefs and identify how they show power.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Keep it up* (40 min): Keep a balloon or beach ball in the air as long as possible with each person touching it in order.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Time management* (20 min): Plan the coming week in a planner, blocking schoolwork, activity, chores and rest.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Intensive pronouns* (90 min): Lesson on intensive pronouns (himself, myself); write 8 sentences about Brian using them correctly and fix 6 misuse errors.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Color mixing* (60 min): Mix food coloring drops with water in 1:5 and 2:10 ratios to check that equivalent ratios make the same color; record observations in a table.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Reaching equilibrium* (50 min): Mix equal amounts of hot and cold water and measure the final temperature; explain energy flow from hot to cold.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Surplus and cities* (45 min): Draw a cause-and-effect chain from irrigation to surplus to specialization to cities.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Cylinder seal* (30 min): Carve a design into a clay cylinder and roll it onto flat clay to make an impression.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Modify for all* (40 min): Redesign one challenge so a person using a wheelchair or with limited vision could fully take part.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Coping toolbox* (20 min): Build a coping toolbox of 8 strategies (exercise, music, talking, art, nature, etc.).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 25, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Reading check quiz on chapters 1-4 and a journal entry from Brian's point of view after the first night.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: write and interpret ratios from pictures and stories; explain the difference between a part-to-part and part-to-whole ratio.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Label conduction, convection and radiation in a diagram of a campfire and a kitchen; quiz on transfer methods.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on Mesopotamian geography and agriculture.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Write how your cave art and seal connect to the purposes of ancient art.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Reflect* (40 min): Record your group's best times and explain which strategy change helped most.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a personal stress-management plan and use one strategy daily for a week.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Hatchet chapters 1-4: the crash
- Objective: Analyze how setting creates conflict
- Objective: Track Brian's thoughts and the 'Secret'
- Vocabulary: conflict, person vs. nature, flashback, rudder, altimeter
- Materials: Hatchet by Gary Paulsen (library copy), map of Canada
- Assessment: Reading quiz and point-of-view journal entry.

### Mathematics 6: What are ratios?
- Objective: Use ratio language a:b, a to b, a for every b
- Objective: Represent ratios with diagrams
- Vocabulary: ratio, for every, part, quantity
- Materials: colored counters or beans, measuring cups, drink mix or food coloring
- Assessment: Ratio language quiz and color-mixing lab write-up.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Energy transfer: conduction, convection, radiation
- Objective: Distinguish the three methods of thermal energy transfer
- Objective: Explain transfer from hot to cold
- Vocabulary: conduction, convection, radiation, conductor, insulator, equilibrium
- Materials: metal, wooden and plastic spoons, butter, hot water, lamp, black and white paper
- Assessment: Transfer diagram and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Geography of the Fertile Crescent
- Objective: Locate the Tigris and Euphrates
- Objective: Explain irrigation and surplus
- Vocabulary: Mesopotamia, Fertile Crescent, irrigation, silt, levee, surplus
- Materials: baking tray, soil, water, map of Mesopotamia
- Assessment: Map and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Prehistoric and Mesopotamian art
- Objective: Interpret cave art and Mesopotamian art
- Objective: Create work inspired by ancient techniques
- Vocabulary: cave painting, pigment, relief, cylinder seal
- Materials: brown paper bag, charcoal, chalk pastels, clay, pencil
- Assessment: Cave art and seal with reflection.

### Physical Education 6: Group problem solving
- Objective: Plan and test strategies as a group
- Objective: Adapt roles for different abilities
- Vocabulary: strategy, role, plan, adapt
- Materials: poly spots or paper plates, ball, rope
- Assessment: Strategy reflection.

### Health 6: Coping and stress management
- Objective: Practice coping strategies
- Objective: Build a personal stress plan
- Vocabulary: coping strategy, mindfulness, time management, relaxation
- Materials: timer, planner
- Assessment: Stress-management plan.
