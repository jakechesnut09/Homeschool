## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Why myths?* (90 min): Read the myth of Prometheus and list what it explains (fire, human suffering); discuss why cultures created myths.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Constant speed* (60 min): Solve 8 problems using distance = rate x time reasoning with tables (e.g., a cyclist at 12 miles per hour).
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Cloud in a jar* (50 min): With adult help, make a cloud in a jar using warm water, ice and a little smoke, and explain the role of cooling and particles.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *God-king* (45 min): Read about the pharaoh as a divine ruler and draw Egypt's social pyramid.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Finding vanishing points* (30 min): Trace orthogonal lines on photos of roads and hallways to locate vanishing points.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Rules* (40 min): Learn ultimate rules and practice a stall count while marking.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Conflict causes* (20 min): List common causes of conflict among middle schoolers and family members.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 47, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Pandora's box* (90 min): Read Hawthorne's 'The Paradise of Children' (Pandora) and write the theme in one sentence with supporting evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Speed lab* (60 min): Measure a 20-meter course, time yourself walking, jogging and hopping, and compute meters per second for each.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Dew point* (50 min): Add ice slowly to water in a metal can, stirring, and record the temperature when dew forms.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Mummify an apple* (45 min): Begin an apple mummification experiment with salt and baking soda, weighing the slices first.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Floating boxes* (30 min): Draw 8 boxes above, below and on the horizon line using one vanishing point.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Cutting* (40 min): Practice cuts to get open and the 'give and go'.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Resolution steps* (20 min): Learn steps: cool down, take turns telling, listen, brainstorm solutions, agree, follow up.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 48, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Midas and hubris* (90 min): Read 'The Golden Touch' (King Midas) and analyze how Midas's choices drive the plot to its resolution.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit price* (60 min): Compare 6 pairs of grocery items in different sizes using unit price, and decide the better buy for each.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Cloud types* (50 min): Observe and photograph clouds daily, identify them with a cloud chart and relate them to weather that followed.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Book of the Dead* (45 min): Read the 'Weighing of the Heart' scene and explain what it shows about Egyptian ethics.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Hallway* (30 min): Draw a hallway with doors and windows in one-point perspective.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Game* (40 min): Play ultimate, resolving calls through Spirit of the Game discussion.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Sibling scenario* (20 min): Use the steps to resolve a realistic sibling or friend conflict in a role-play.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 49, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Words from myths* (90 min): Study roots and words from myths (narcissist, echo, panic, tantalize, chronology, psyche) and build a 20-word root map.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Rate problems* (60 min): Solve mixed rate problems (pages per minute, heartbeats per minute, cost per ounce) and justify one answer to a family member.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Precipitation* (50 min): Read about how droplets grow into rain, snow, sleet and hail, and make a diagram of each.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Pyramids* (45 min): Research how the Great Pyramid was built and who built it, then write a short explanation using two sources.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Your room* (30 min): Draw your bedroom in one-point perspective with furniture.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Compare strategies* (40 min): Compare soccer and ultimate strategies on a chart and play a modified game.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Mediation* (20 min): Act as a mediator for a scripted peer conflict.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 50, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Quiz on myth themes and roots; retell a myth orally to a family member in two minutes.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on constant speed and unit price problems.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on humidity, clouds and precipitation types.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Weigh the apple slices, explain the results, and take a quiz on Egyptian religion and government.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Self-check orthogonals with a ruler and fix any errors.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Unit check* (40 min): Take a quiz on invasion strategies and self-assess sportsmanship.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a solution for a new conflict scenario using every step.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Greek myths and Greek/Latin roots
- Objective: Identify the purposes of myths
- Objective: Use Greek and Latin roots to determine meaning
- Vocabulary: myth, deity, hubris, chaos, narcissism, echo
- Materials: 'A Wonder-Book' and 'Tanglewood Tales' by Nathaniel Hawthorne (Project Gutenberg), D'Aulaires' Book of Greek Myths (library, optional)
- Assessment: Roots quiz and oral retelling.

### Mathematics 6: Speed and unit price
- Objective: Solve constant speed problems
- Objective: Compare unit prices to find the better buy
- Vocabulary: constant speed, unit price, better deal, miles per hour
- Materials: store flyer or online grocery prices, stopwatch, tape measure
- Assessment: Quiz plus speed lab data table.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Humidity, clouds and precipitation
- Objective: Explain cloud formation
- Objective: Measure humidity and dew point
- Vocabulary: humidity, dew point, cloud, cumulus, stratus, cirrus
- Materials: clear jar, warm water, ice, match (with adult) or aerosol, cloud chart
- Assessment: Cloud log and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Pharaohs, religion and the afterlife
- Objective: Explain the pharaoh's religious and political roles
- Objective: Describe beliefs about the afterlife
- Vocabulary: pharaoh, dynasty, mummification, afterlife, Book of the Dead, pyramid
- Materials: apple slices, salt and baking soda, images of Giza and tomb paintings
- Assessment: Mummification lab notes and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: One-point perspective
- Objective: Use a horizon line and vanishing point
- Objective: Draw a room in one-point perspective
- Vocabulary: perspective, horizon line, vanishing point, orthogonal, foreshortening
- Materials: ruler, pencil, photos of hallways and roads
- Assessment: One-point perspective room drawing.

### Physical Education 6: Ultimate: game play and Spirit of the Game
- Objective: Play ultimate using rules and self-officiating
- Objective: Compare invasion game strategies
- Vocabulary: Spirit of the Game, stall count, end zone, self-officiating
- Materials: flying disc, cones for end zones
- Assessment: Strategy quiz and sportsmanship self-assessment.

### Health 6: Conflict resolution
- Objective: Apply conflict-resolution steps
- Objective: Practice compromise and negotiation
- Vocabulary: conflict, compromise, negotiation, mediation, win-win
- Materials: conflict-resolution steps chart
- Assessment: Written conflict solution.
