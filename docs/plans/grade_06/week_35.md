## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise for precise domain-specific vocabulary and logical order of ideas.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Choose a destination* (60 min): Pick a California destination, find the distance, and use a car's miles-per-gallon rate to calculate fuel needed.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *First test* (50 min): Test the oven in direct sun, recording internal temperature every 5 minutes for 45 minutes.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Architecture and engineering* (45 min): Compare the Pantheon and Colosseum with modern buildings and identify Roman arches, domes and concrete.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Artist statement* (30 min): Write a one-paragraph artist statement about your growth and interests.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *PACER or mile* (40 min): Retake the PACER or mile run.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Research* (20 min): Gather 4 facts from valid sources about your chosen issue (kindness, emergency preparedness, sun safety, clean air).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Edit and publish* (90 min): Edit for conventions, check every citation, and publish the final report.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Budget* (60 min): Build a budget with lodging, food and tickets, compute totals with decimals and add 8% sales tax.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Analyze* (50 min): Graph results and compare with a classmate's, family member's or online design data to find the best features.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Latin everywhere* (45 min): Find 20 English words with Latin roots and translate common Latin mottos (e.g., California's 'Eureka' is Greek; the U.S. 'E pluribus unum' is Latin).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Wall labels* (30 min): Write wall labels (title, media, year, one sentence) for each piece.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Curl-ups and push-ups* (40 min): Retake curl-up and push-up tests.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Plan* (20 min): Identify audience, message and call to action.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 173, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Build slides* (90 min): Create 6-8 slides with images and minimal text that clarify your key findings.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Time and speed* (60 min): Use average speed to plan driving time and a schedule of stops.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Modify* (50 min): Make one change (extra reflector, insulation, angle) and record why you chose it.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Roman law* (45 min): Identify Roman legal principles still used today (innocent until proven guilty, rights of citizens).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Mat and mount* (30 min): Mat and mount 2D works and prepare stands for 3D works.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Flexibility* (40 min): Retake sit-and-reach and trunk-lift tests.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Create* (20 min): Produce a poster, brochure, slide show or short video.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 174, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Rehearse* (90 min): Rehearse a 5-minute presentation focusing on eye contact, volume and clear pronunciation.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Packing volume* (60 min): Determine whether your luggage (measure boxes or bags) fits a car trunk of given volume, using fractional dimensions.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Second test* (50 min): Retest and try to melt a s'more; compare the two graphs.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Decline and fall* (45 min): Read about the division of the empire and the fall of the West in 476 CE and rank causes by importance.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Digital portfolio* (30 min): Photograph all works and assemble a digital portfolio including your animation and music recordings.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Analyze* (40 min): Graph baseline, mid-year and final scores and check whether goals were met.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Rehearse* (20 min): Practice a 3-minute presentation and get feedback.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 175, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Present* (90 min): Present to family or co-op and answer audience questions.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Present the plan* (60 min): Present your trip plan with all calculations and answer family questions about your choices.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Check-in* (50 min): Write a paragraph explaining how the modification affected thermal energy transfer.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Unit review* (45 min): Take the Unit 8 test on Ancient Rome.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & check* (30 min): Evaluate portfolio completeness with a checklist.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Celebrate* (40 min): Play a favorite game and share one fitness accomplishment.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Revise using an advocacy rubric.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Revising and presenting research
- Objective: Revise for clarity and precise vocabulary
- Objective: Plan a multimedia presentation
- Vocabulary: multimedia, visual aid, audience
- Materials: draft, slides or poster software
- Assessment: Research report and presentation rubrics.

### Mathematics 6: Modeling project: plan a trip
- Objective: Apply ratios, percents and decimals to a real plan
- Objective: Communicate a mathematical model
- Vocabulary: model, budget, estimate
- Materials: map of California, online prices, spreadsheet or paper
- Assessment: Trip-planning project rubric (accuracy, representations, explanation).

### Science 6 (Integrated): Solar oven: test and iterate
- Objective: Collect test data
- Objective: Modify the design based on data
- Vocabulary: iteration, optimize, data
- Materials: solar oven, oven thermometer, s'mores ingredients
- Assessment: Test data and iteration analysis.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Roman legacy and the decline of the West
- Objective: Identify legacies of Roman culture
- Objective: Explain causes of the Western Empire's decline
- Vocabulary: Latin, Romance languages, arch, dome, concrete, civil law, decline
- Materials: Latin roots list, images of the Pantheon and Colosseum
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Portfolio and artist statement
- Objective: Write an artist statement
- Objective: Prepare works for exhibition
- Vocabulary: portfolio, artist statement, mat, label
- Materials: selected artworks, construction paper for mats, labels
- Assessment: Portfolio and artist statement.

### Physical Education 6: End-of-year fitness reassessment
- Objective: Retake fitness tests
- Objective: Compare results to baseline
- Vocabulary: reassessment, growth
- Materials: PACER audio or measured course, mat, sit-and-reach box
- Assessment: Fitness growth graph.

### Health 6: Advocacy project
- Objective: Plan and create an advocacy project
- Objective: Use valid information to persuade
- Vocabulary: advocacy, audience, call to action, evidence
- Materials: poster paper, computer, camera
- Assessment: Advocacy rubric draft score.
