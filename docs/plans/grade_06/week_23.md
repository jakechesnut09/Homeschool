## Day 1 — Monday (school day 111, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Learning language* (90 min): Read chapters 5-7 and trace how Anne Sullivan teaches Keller, listing each method.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Paper-folding powers* (60 min): Fold a sheet of paper in half repeatedly, record layers as 2^1, 2^2 ... and predict layers after 10 folds.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Where does energy go?* (50 min): Rub hands together and bounce a ball until it stops; explain the energy transfers to thermal energy and sound.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Indo-Aryan migrations* (45 min): Read about the Indo-Aryan migrations and the Vedas and explain their effect on language and religion.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Warm-ups* (30 min): Do vocal and physical warm-ups (tongue twisters, mirror game) and learn theatre etiquette.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Finding the beat* (40 min): Step to the beat of songs at different tempos and count 8-counts.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *911 calls* (20 min): Learn what dispatchers ask and practice a mock 911 call with a parent playing dispatcher.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 112, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Braille and reading* (90 min): Read about Keller learning to read and write; use a Braille chart to write your name with glue dots on cardstock.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Evaluating powers* (60 min): Evaluate 15 expressions such as 3^4, (1/2)^3 and 0.2^2 and compare 2^5 with 5^2.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Roller coaster design* (50 min): Design a marble roller coaster from foam pipe insulation with a loop, defining criteria and constraints.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Brahmanism* (45 min): Read the Rig Veda 'Hymn of Creation' and discuss the role of Brahmin priests and rituals.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Yes, and* (30 min): Play 'Yes, and' storytelling and 'Freeze' improv with family members.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Basic steps* (40 min): Learn grapevine, step-touch, pivot turn and kick-ball-change.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Know your info* (20 min): Memorize your address and parents' numbers and set up an ICE contact.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 113, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Learning to speak* (90 min): Read chapter 13 on learning to speak and explain how this section contributes to the central idea.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Order of operations* (60 min): Evaluate 15 expressions like 2 + 3 x 4^2 and (2 + 3)^2 and find the errors in three worked examples.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Build and test* (50 min): Build and test the coaster, adjusting starting height so the marble completes the loop.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Hindu beliefs* (45 min): Create a graphic organizer explaining Brahman, karma, dharma, reincarnation and moksha.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Objectives* (30 min): Improvise scenes where each character wants something different and identify the objective.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Line dance* (40 min): Learn the Cha Cha Slide or Cupid Shuffle with correct counts.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Local resources* (20 min): Locate nearest fire station, hospital and police station on a map.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 114, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Objective summary* (90 min): Write a 150-word objective summary of the selections read so far.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Formulas* (60 min): Evaluate V = s^3, SA = 6s^2 and A = 1/2 bh for given values, and choose a formula to solve a packaging problem.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Energy argument* (50 min): Write an argument explaining energy transfers at each part of your coaster, including friction.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Social structure* (45 min): Diagram the four varnas and jati system, and discuss how the system shaped daily life and limited mobility.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Tableaux* (30 min): Create frozen tableaux of three moments from a myth studied in history.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Heart rate* (40 min): Dance for 10 minutes continuously and check heart rate.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Home alone safety* (20 min): Write home-alone rules with a parent.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 115, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Quiz on central idea and structure; discuss with a parent what surprised you most.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on exponents, order of operations and formulas.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Take the Unit 6 test on forces, fields and energy.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph explaining how Brahmanism evolved into Hinduism with two pieces of evidence.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & reflect* (30 min): Write about what made an improv scene work and what you would change.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Check* (40 min): Perform the line dance from memory for a family member.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a mock-call rubric and contact card.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: The Story of My Life, selected chapters 5-23
- Objective: Trace the development of a central idea
- Objective: Summarize objectively
- Vocabulary: central idea, Braille, perseverance, eloquent
- Materials: The Story of My Life, Braille alphabet chart
- Assessment: Objective summary and quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Exponents and order of operations
- Objective: Evaluate expressions with whole-number exponents
- Objective: Use order of operations including exponents
- Objective: Evaluate formulas
- Vocabulary: exponent, base, power, order of operations
- Materials: sheet of paper for folding, calculator
- Assessment: Exponent and order of operations quiz.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Energy transfer and conservation
- Objective: Argue that changing kinetic energy means energy is transferred
- Objective: Design a marble roller coaster
- Vocabulary: energy transfer, conservation of energy, friction, thermal energy
- Materials: foam pipe insulation cut in half, marbles, tape, rubber bands
- Assessment: Coaster argument and Unit 6 test.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: Indo-Aryan migrations, Brahmanism and early Hinduism
- Objective: Explain the significance of Indo-Aryan migrations
- Objective: Describe beliefs of early Hinduism and the varna/caste system
- Vocabulary: Vedas, Sanskrit, Brahman, varna, caste, karma, dharma, reincarnation
- Materials: Rig Veda Hymn of Creation excerpt, Bhagavad Gita excerpt
- Assessment: Organizer and paragraph.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Theatre games and improvisation
- Objective: Build ensemble skills
- Objective: Improvise characters and situations
- Vocabulary: improvisation, ensemble, yes, and, objective, tableau
- Materials: open space, a few props
- Assessment: Participation rubric and reflection.

### Physical Education 6: Rhythm and line dances
- Objective: Move to a beat
- Objective: Perform a line dance
- Vocabulary: beat, tempo, grapevine, step-touch, count
- Materials: music player, GoNoodle videos
- Assessment: Line dance performance.

### Health 6: Calling 911 and emergency resources
- Objective: Make an effective 911 call
- Objective: Know local emergency resources
- Vocabulary: dispatcher, address, emergency contact, ICE
- Materials: phone (practice, not live), contact card
- Assessment: Mock call rubric.
