## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Biography account* (90 min): Read a biography's account of the water-pump scene and annotate differences from Keller's own account.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Two changing quantities* (60 min): For situations like 'earning $9 per hour', name the independent and dependent variables and write an equation d = 9h.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Cell theory history* (50 min): Read about Hooke, Leeuwenhoek and the cell theory and make a timeline of discoveries.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Siddhartha's life* (45 min): Read the story of Siddhartha Gautama and create a storyboard of key events leading to enlightenment.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Vocal choices* (30 min): Read one line in 6 different emotions and change pitch, pace and volume.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Mayim Mayim* (40 min): Learn the Israeli circle dance Mayim Mayim.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Earthquake science* (20 min): Review why California has earthquakes and what to do indoors, outdoors and in a car.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 117, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Compare authors* (90 min): Complete a comparison chart: what each author includes, omits and emphasizes, and why.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Tables to graphs* (60 min): Make tables and first-quadrant graphs for three equations (y = 4x, y = x + 3, y = 2.5x) and compare their shapes.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Microscope skills* (50 min): Learn microscope parts and practice focusing on a newspaper letter 'e', noting how the image flips.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Teachings* (45 min): Explain the Four Noble Truths and Eightfold Path in your own words.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Physical choices* (30 min): Walk as different characters (king, child, old sailor) leading with different body parts.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Virginia Reel* (40 min): Learn the Virginia Reel with partners.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Drill* (20 min): Practice Drop, Cover and Hold On in 3 rooms and identify safe spots.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 118, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Film scene* (90 min): Watch the water-pump scene from 'The Miracle Worker' and note how actors, sound and camera change the experience.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Real data* (60 min): Fill a cup with water a few tablespoons at a time and weigh it, then make a table, graph and equation for the relationship.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Cork cells* (50 min): Observe a thin slice of cork as Hooke did and sketch what you see with magnification noted.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Dhammapada* (45 min): Read selected verses of the Dhammapada and connect them to Buddhist teachings on the Middle Way.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Character profile* (30 min): Choose a monologue and write a character profile (age, wants, fears, inner thoughts).
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Tinikling* (40 min): Learn Tinikling (Philippines) steps using jump ropes on the ground in place of poles.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Fire escape plan* (20 min): Draw a home floor plan with two exits from each room and a meeting place.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 119, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Primary sources* (90 min): Look at Library of Congress photographs and letters related to Helen Keller and explain what they add.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit review* (60 min): Mixed review of expressions, equations, exponents and variable relationships.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Onion cells* (50 min): Prepare an onion skin wet mount with iodine or food coloring and draw the cells.

- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Practice* (40 min): Practice all three dances and discuss where each comes from.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Fire drill* (20 min): Run a family fire drill and time it; practice stop, drop and roll and crawling low.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Memoir vs. biography vs. drama
- Objective: Compare how different authors present the same events
- Objective: Compare text with a filmed or staged version
- Vocabulary: biography, primary source, secondary source, dramatization
- Materials: a children's biography of Helen Keller (library), film clip of the water-pump scene from 'The Miracle Worker' (1962)
- Assessment: Comparison essay.

### Mathematics 6: Relationships between quantities
- Objective: Identify independent and dependent variables
- Objective: Represent a relationship with a table, graph and equation
- Vocabulary: independent variable, dependent variable, input, output
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 6 end-of-unit assessment.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Discovering cells and using a microscope
- Objective: Describe the history of cell discovery
- Objective: Use a microscope or magnifier to observe cells
- Vocabulary: cell, cell theory, microscope, magnification, specimen
- Materials: microscope (or smartphone clip-on microscope), onion, cork, slides or clear tape
- Assessment: Observation drawings and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: The Buddha and the spread of Buddhism
- Objective: Describe the life and teachings of the Buddha
- Objective: Trace the spread of Buddhism
- Vocabulary: Siddhartha Gautama, enlightenment, Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, nirvana, missionary
- Materials: Dhammapada excerpts (Fordham Sourcebook), map of Asia
- Assessment: Venn diagram and quiz.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Voice and movement for character
- Objective: Use vocal and physical choices to create character
- Vocabulary: projection, articulation, inflection, posture, gesture
- Materials: short monologues (e.g., from Aesop adaptations)
- Assessment: Monologue performance.

### Physical Education 6: Folk dances from around the world
- Objective: Perform folk dances with partners
- Objective: Describe dances' cultural origins
- Vocabulary: folk dance, partner, formation, promenade
- Materials: music for Mayim Mayim, Virginia Reel, Tinikling
- Assessment: Folk dance performance.

### Health 6: Earthquake and fire preparedness
- Objective: Practice drop, cover and hold on
- Objective: Create a fire escape plan
- Vocabulary: drop cover hold on, aftershock, escape plan, meeting place, smoke alarm
- Materials: graph paper, flashlight
- Assessment: Escape plan and quiz.
