## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Sentence variety* (90 min): Lesson on varying sentence beginnings and lengths; revise ten sentences in your draft so no two consecutive sentences start the same way.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Polyhedra and faces* (60 min): Sort household objects into prisms and pyramids, count faces, edges and vertices, and record them in a table.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Heating curve lab* (50 min): With adult help, heat ice in a pot, recording temperature every 30 seconds until the water boils.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Domestication* (45 min): Read about the domestication of wheat, rice, maize and animals and map where each began.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Texture rubbings* (30 min): Make crayon rubbings of 10 textures around the house and then draw implied versions of three.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Blindfold maze* (40 min): Guide a blindfolded partner through a cone maze using only verbal directions, then switch.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Fight or flight* (20 min): Learn the body's stress response and label hormone and body effects on a diagram.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Word choice* (90 min): Replace weak verbs and vague nouns with precise ones; highlight five improvements in a different color.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unfold a box* (60 min): Cut open a cereal box along its edges to make a net, measure each face and compute its surface area; compare with a partner's or parent's estimate.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Graph the data* (50 min): Graph temperature vs. time and identify the flat sections where the state changes.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Plant an experiment* (45 min): Plant bean seeds and begin a growth log, reflecting on why early farmers chose to settle near their crops.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Negative space* (30 min): Draw only the negative spaces around a chair and see the chair appear.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Hula hoop pass* (40 min): Pass a hula hoop around a hand-holding circle without letting go; time and improve.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Good vs. bad stress* (20 min): Classify 10 situations as eustress or distress.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Editing conference* (90 min): Edit with a checklist for pronoun case, dialogue punctuation, capitalization and spelling, then conference with a parent.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Nets to surface area* (60 min): Draw nets for a cube, a rectangular prism and a square pyramid on grid paper, fold one from cardstock, and calculate surface area of each.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Why the plateau?* (50 min): Use particle models to explain why temperature stays constant while ice melts and water boils.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Catalhoyuk* (45 min): Study an artist's reconstruction of Catalhoyuk and list evidence of permanent settlement and specialization.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Principles hunt* (30 min): Find magazine images that show balance, emphasis, rhythm, contrast and unity and label them.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Human knot* (40 min): Untangle a human knot with family or co-op members and discuss communication strategies.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Stress inventory* (20 min): Complete a middle-school stress inventory and identify your top three stressors.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Publish* (90 min): Type the final narrative (at least 2 pages) and add a title.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Squares and cubes* (60 min): Build cubes with snap cubes to model 2^3, 3^3, 4^3; write area of a square as s^2, volume of a cube as s^3, and surface area as 6s^2; evaluate for 8 side lengths.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Condensation and freezing* (50 min): Observe condensation on a cold glass and freeze water in a cup; model particles during cooling.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Costs and benefits* (45 min): Create a T-chart of the benefits and costs of farming vs. hunting and gathering.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Design collage* (30 min): Create a collage that uses at least three principles of design deliberately.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Encouragement game* (40 min): Play a relay where teams earn points for encouraging words as well as speed.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Stress and health* (20 min): Read about how long-term stress affects sleep, immunity and concentration.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Author's chair and check* (90 min): Read your narrative aloud with expression to an audience and score it with the narrative rubric; take a unit vocabulary quiz.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit 1 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 1 assessment (area of polygons, nets, surface area, exponent notation) and redo two missed problems with explanation.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Draw and label a complete particle model of all state changes and take a quiz on heating curves.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Unit 1 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 1 assessment and write a paragraph on how farming changed human life.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Review & critique* (30 min): Write a criteria checklist for a good composition and use it to evaluate your collage.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Reflect* (40 min): Write about one way you helped your team and one way to improve.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a stress-concepts quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: Personal narrative: revising and publishing
- Objective: Revise for sentence variety and word choice
- Objective: Edit for pronoun use and punctuation
- Vocabulary: revise, edit, transition, sentence variety
- Materials: narrative draft, computer for typing
- Assessment: Published personal narrative scored on a 4-point rubric.

### Mathematics 6: Nets, surface area, squares and cubes
- Objective: Draw nets of prisms and pyramids
- Objective: Calculate surface area
- Objective: Write s^2 and s^3 with exponents
- Vocabulary: face, edge, vertex, net, surface area, prism, pyramid, exponent
- Materials: cereal box, cardstock, tape, sugar cubes or snap cubes
- Assessment: Unit 1 end-of-unit assessment.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Changes of state
- Objective: Model what happens to particles during melting, freezing, evaporating and condensing
- Objective: Interpret a heating curve
- Vocabulary: melting point, boiling point, condensation, evaporation, heating curve
- Materials: ice cubes, pot, kitchen thermometer, stopwatch, graph paper
- Assessment: Particle model poster and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: The agricultural revolution
- Objective: Explain how climate change and domestication led to farming
- Objective: Analyze effects of farming on society
- Vocabulary: Neolithic, domestication, agricultural revolution, surplus, Catalhoyuk, specialization
- Materials: seeds (beans or wheat berries), cup of soil, images of Catalhoyuk
- Assessment: Unit 1 test with constructed response.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Texture, space and principles of design
- Objective: Create actual and implied texture
- Objective: Apply principles of design
- Vocabulary: texture, positive space, negative space, balance, emphasis, rhythm, unity
- Materials: crayons, textured household objects, magazines, glue
- Assessment: Collage and self-made criteria evaluation.

### Physical Education 6: Communication challenges
- Objective: Communicate clearly to solve group problems
- Objective: Encourage teammates
- Vocabulary: cooperation, communication, encouragement, trust
- Materials: blindfold, cones, hula hoop, rope
- Assessment: Teamwork self-assessment.

### Health 6: Stress and its effects
- Objective: Explain the stress response
- Objective: Identify personal stressors
- Vocabulary: stress, stressor, eustress, distress, fight-or-flight
- Materials: stress inventory, pulse timer
- Assessment: Stress quiz.
