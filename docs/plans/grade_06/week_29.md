## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Mistress Mary* (90 min): Read chapters 1-2 and list Mary's traits and their causes with evidence.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Drawing polygons* (60 min): Plot and connect vertices to draw rectangles, triangles and trapezoids in all four quadrants.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Bones* (50 min): Identify 15 major bones on a diagram and feel the bones of your hand and arm.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Han government* (45 min): Read about the Han civil service exams and explain how Confucian ideas shaped the bureaucracy.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Flip book* (30 min): Make a 20-page flip book of a bouncing ball on sticky notes.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Basic skills* (40 min): Practice basic bounce, alternate foot step and skier for 1-minute intervals.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Sleep needs* (20 min): Learn why 9–12 hours matter for preteens and keep a 3-night sleep log.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 142, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Across the moor* (90 min): Read chapters 3-4 and describe Misselthwaite Manor and the moor; explain how the setting reflects Mary's mood.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Side lengths* (60 min): Use coordinates to find horizontal and vertical side lengths and perimeters of 8 polygons.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Joints* (50 min): Test range of motion of hinge, ball-and-socket and pivot joints on your body and record findings.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Han inventions* (45 min): Make recycled paper with scrap paper, a blender and a screen, and list other Han inventions (seismograph, wheelbarrow).
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Animation history* (30 min): Watch early animation (zoetrope, Muybridge's horse) and explain persistence of vision.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Tricks* (40 min): Learn criss-cross and side swing; attempt double unders.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Screens and sleep* (20 min): Discuss blue light and phones in bedrooms and plan a device curfew.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 143, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Yorkshire dialect* (90 min): Read chapter 5; translate five lines of Martha's dialect and discuss why Burnett uses dialect.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Area on the grid* (60 min): Find areas of coordinate polygons by decomposing or enclosing in rectangles.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Muscle pairs* (50 min): Build a cardboard arm model with string 'muscles' to show biceps and triceps working in pairs.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Silk Roads* (45 min): Map the Silk Roads from Chang'an to Rome and list goods and ideas exchanged in each direction.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Story idea* (30 min): Develop a 20-30 second animation idea with a character, problem and solution.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Endurance* (40 min): Jump in 30-second intervals for 10 rounds and record total jumps.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Hearing* (20 min): Use a sound meter app to measure common noises and learn safe headphone volume (60/60 rule).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 144, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *The key* (90 min): Read chapters 6-7 and predict how finding the garden will change Mary.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Design challenge* (60 min): Design a garden plot with 4-6 vertices on Desmos having area exactly 48 square units and list its coordinates.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Chicken wing* (50 min): With gloves, examine a chicken wing to find muscles, tendons and bone and sketch them.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Buddhism comes to China* (45 min): Trace how Buddhism traveled to China along the Silk Roads and how it blended with Chinese traditions.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Storyboard* (30 min): Draw an 8-panel storyboard with camera angles.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Routine* (40 min): Create a 1-minute jump rope routine with at least five skills.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Sun and eyes* (20 min): Read a sunscreen label, check the UV index and learn the 20-20-20 eye rule.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 145, ~335 min)

- [ ] **English Language Arts 6** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a character sketch of Mary with three quotations; reading quiz on chapters 1-7.
- [ ] **Mathematics 6** — *Unit 7 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 7 end-of-unit assessment on rational numbers, inequalities and the coordinate plane.
- [ ] **Science 6 (Integrated)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on bones, joints and muscle pairs.
- [ ] **World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations** — *Unit 7 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 7 test on ancient China.
- [ ] **Visual and Performing Arts 6** — *Build set* (30 min): Build characters and a simple set from clay or LEGO.
- [ ] **Physical Education 6** — *Check* (40 min): Perform your routine and count jumps in 1 minute.
- [ ] **Health 6** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a personal health practice goal and take a quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English Language Arts 6: The Secret Garden, chapters 1-7
- Objective: Analyze how setting shapes character
- Objective: Describe Mary's character at the start
- Vocabulary: contrary, moor, Yorkshire dialect, sour, neglect
- Materials: 'The Secret Garden' by Frances Hodgson Burnett (Project Gutenberg or library)
- Assessment: Character sketch and quiz.

### Mathematics 6: Polygons in the coordinate plane
- Objective: Draw polygons from vertices
- Objective: Find side lengths, perimeter and area using coordinates
- Vocabulary: vertex, perimeter, coordinates
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 7 end-of-unit assessment.

### Science 6 (Integrated): Skeletal and muscular systems
- Objective: Explain how bones and muscles work together
- Objective: Model a joint
- Vocabulary: skeleton, joint, ligament, tendon, skeletal muscle, contract
- Materials: cardboard, straws, string, chicken wing (cooked or raw with gloves)
- Assessment: Arm model and quiz.

### World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations: The Han Dynasty and the Silk Roads
- Objective: Describe Han government and inventions
- Objective: Explain the significance of the Silk Roads and spread of Buddhism to China
- Vocabulary: Han, civil service, bureaucracy, Silk Roads, caravan, cultural diffusion
- Materials: map of Eurasia, paper-making supplies (scrap paper, blender, screen)
- Assessment: Silk Roads map and Unit 7 test.

### Visual and Performing Arts 6: Stop-motion animation: planning
- Objective: Understand persistence of vision
- Objective: Plan an animation
- Vocabulary: frame, frames per second, flip book, storyboard, persistence of vision
- Materials: sticky notes, clay or LEGO figures, free stop-motion app
- Assessment: Flip book and storyboard.

### Physical Education 6: Jump rope fitness
- Objective: Perform basic and advanced jump rope skills
- Objective: Combine skills into a routine
- Vocabulary: basic bounce, criss-cross, double under, skier
- Materials: jump rope
- Assessment: Jump rope routine and count.

### Health 6: Sleep, screens, eyes and hearing
- Objective: Explain sleep needs
- Objective: Protect eyes, hearing and skin
- Vocabulary: sleep debt, blue light, decibel, UV index, SPF
- Materials: sleep log, sound meter app, sunscreen
- Assessment: Goal and quiz.
