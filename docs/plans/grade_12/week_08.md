## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Shakespeare's stage* (60 min): Read background on the Globe, James I, the Gunpowder Plot, and witchcraft beliefs. Learn to scan a line of iambic pentameter and mark stresses in three lines.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Exponential graphs* (60 min): Graph growth and decay functions including base e; identify asymptotes and transformations.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Seismic waves* (60 min): Use a Slinky to model P-waves and S-waves; explain why S-waves cannot pass through liquid.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Article III* (55 min): Read Article III; diagram district courts, circuit courts, and the Supreme Court; locate California in the Ninth Circuit.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Color wheel* (50 min): Mix a 12-step color wheel from three primaries plus white.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Deload* (45 min): Do a light deload session at 60% of usual load.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Card terms* (50 min): Compare the Schumer boxes of three real credit card offers (student, secured, rewards) for APR, fees, and penalties.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Act I, scenes 1–3* (60 min): Read I.1–I.3 aloud with a parent taking parts. Analyze 'Fair is foul, and foul is fair' as the play's motif of equivocation.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Compound interest and e* (60 min): Compute compound interest for annual through continuous compounding and watch the values approach e.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Shadow zones* (60 min): Analyze a diagram of seismic shadow zones and infer the liquid outer core; draw Earth's layers to scale.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *How cases reach the Court* (55 min): Learn the certiorari process and the 'rule of four'; analyze how few petitions are granted.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Value in color* (50 min): Paint a grayscale and then a full-color version of the same simple object.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Retest strength* (45 min): Retest 10-rep maxes and push-up/curl-up scores.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Minimum payment trap* (50 min): Calculate how long a $2,000 balance at 24% APR takes to repay with minimum payments vs. $150/month.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 38, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Act I, scenes 4–5* (60 min): Read I.4–I.5. Paraphrase Lady Macbeth's 'unsex me here' soliloquy line by line and identify her strategy.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Logarithms* (60 min): Convert between exponential and log form; graph y = log_b(x) as the inverse of y = b^x.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Convection lab* (60 min): Heat water in a glass pan over a low flame and add drops of food coloring to observe convection cells; sketch and relate to the mantle.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Selecting justices* (55 min): Watch a segment of a recent confirmation hearing and evaluate the questions senators asked.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Temperature* (50 min): Paint the same landscape twice: once warm-dominant, once cool-dominant.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Analyze* (45 min): Calculate percent improvement and write what worked.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Auto and personal loans* (50 min): Use an amortization spreadsheet to compare a 36- vs. 72-month auto loan for total interest paid.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 39, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Act I, scenes 6–7* (60 min): Read I.6–I.7. Annotate Macbeth's 'If it were done' soliloquy for imagery and reasoning; chart his arguments for and against the murder.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Log properties* (60 min): Expand and condense 15 expressions using product, quotient, and power rules; use change of base.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Hot spots* (60 min): Use Hawaiian island ages to calculate Pacific Plate speed and direction over the Hawaii hot spot.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Reading an opinion* (55 min): Read the syllabus of a recent Supreme Court opinion and identify the question, holding, and reasoning.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Limited palette* (50 min): Paint a still life with a Zorn palette (yellow ochre, red, black, white).
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Teach a lift* (45 min): Teach a family member one lift with correct cues.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Predatory products* (50 min): Research payday loans, rent-to-own, and buy-now-pay-later; compute the APR of a $15-per-$100 two-week payday loan.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Macbeth Act I – prophecy and temptation
- Objective: Understand Elizabethan/Jacobean context
- Objective: Analyze how Shakespeare establishes ambition and the supernatural
- Vocabulary: tragedy, soliloquy, aside, iambic pentameter, equivocation
- Materials: Macbeth (Folger free text)
- Assessment: Recitation and Act I quiz.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Exponential functions and logarithms
- Objective: Graph exponential and log functions
- Objective: Apply log properties
- Vocabulary: base e, logarithm, natural log, change of base
- Materials: Desmos, calculator
- Assessment: Exponent/log quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Earth's interior and mantle convection
- Objective: Use seismic evidence to model Earth's layers
- Objective: Model mantle convection
- Vocabulary: seismic wave, P-wave, S-wave, shadow zone, convection, mantle plume
- Materials: clear pot or glass pan, water, food coloring, candle or stovetop, Slinky
- Assessment: Interior/convection model and quiz.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): The judicial branch
- Objective: Describe Article III and the federal court system
- Objective: Explain how justices are selected
- Vocabulary: jurisdiction, appellate, writ of certiorari, precedent, majority/dissenting opinion
- Materials: Article III text, Senate Judiciary hearing video clip
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Color theory in paint
- Objective: Mix accurate colors
- Objective: Use color schemes intentionally
- Vocabulary: hue, saturation, temperature, complementary, limited palette
- Materials: acrylic or gouache (primary colors + white), brushes, palette, canvas paper
- Assessment: Color study sheets.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Strength retest and program evaluation
- Objective: Evaluate the program with data
- Vocabulary: evaluation, adaptation
- Materials: weights or bands, fitness log
- Assessment: Program evaluation and quiz.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Credit cards, loans & the cost of borrowing
- Objective: Calculate the cost of carrying a balance
- Objective: Compare credit products and identify predatory lending
- Vocabulary: APR, minimum payment, grace period, secured card, payday loan, buy now, pay later
- Materials: credit card agreement (Schumer box) samples, CFPB credit card calculator
- Assessment: Borrowing-cost calculations and first credit card plan.
