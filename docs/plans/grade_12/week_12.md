## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Age of Reason* (60 min): Read a short overview of the Enlightenment and the Irish famine context. Read 'A Modest Proposal' paragraphs 1–10 and mark the persona's calm, logical tone.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Tangent graphs* (60 min): Graph y = tan x and transformations; identify asymptotes and period.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Energy budget* (60 min): Label NASA's Earth energy budget diagram and confirm incoming and outgoing energy balance.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Register* (55 min): Learn California's voter pre-registration at 16–17 and registration at 18; complete or walk through the registertovote.ca.gov process.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Study examples* (50 min): Review three AP sample Sustained Investigations and note how each inquiry guided the work.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Race pace* (45 min): Run 3 × 1 km at goal race pace.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *How insurance works* (50 min): Play an NGPF risk-pooling simulation and explain in writing why insurers can pay large claims.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *The proposal* (60 min): Finish 'A Modest Proposal.' Chart each satirical technique (statistics, understatement, mock-logic) and identify Swift's real proposals in paragraph 29.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Reciprocal functions* (60 min): Graph secant and cosecant as reciprocals of cosine and sine and identify their asymptotes.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Greenhouse jar* (60 min): Compare temperatures in a covered vs. uncovered jar under a lamp for 30 minutes and graph results; discuss limits of the model.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Ballot measures* (55 min): Read a recent California ballot proposition in the Official Voter Guide, including LAO analysis and pro/con arguments, and decide how you would vote.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Brainstorm* (50 min): Brainstorm 10 inquiry questions rooted in your life, culture, or community.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Easy* (45 min): Run 20 minutes easy plus strides.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Insurance math* (50 min): Calculate what you pay for three medical scenarios under a high-deductible vs. low-deductible plan.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Mock epic* (60 min): Read an excerpt from Pope's 'The Rape of the Lock' and explain how epic conventions applied to a trivial event create Horatian satire.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Ferris wheel* (60 min): Write a height-versus-time function for a rider on a Ferris wheel with given diameter, center height, and rotation period.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Greenhouse simulation* (60 min): Use PhET Greenhouse Effect to vary greenhouse gas concentration and record equilibrium temperature.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Recall and referendum* (55 min): Compare the 2003 and 2021 gubernatorial recall elections and the process for qualifying measures.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Narrow* (50 min): Narrow to one question and write why it matters to you.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Cross-train* (45 min): Do 30 minutes of easy swimming or cycling.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Health coverage options* (50 min): Compare staying on a parent's plan to age 26, student health plans, employer plans, Covered California, and Medi-Cal.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Modern satire* (60 min): Analyze one modern satirical article or sketch: identify the target, technique, and real argument; compare with Swift.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Tides* (60 min): Use NOAA tide predictions for a California station to build and test a sinusoidal model.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Long-term drivers* (60 min): Model Milankovitch cycles (eccentricity, tilt, precession) and match them to ice-age cycles in Vostok ice-core data.

- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Planning* (50 min): Make 15 thumbnail ideas responding to your question.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Race prep* (45 min): Plan race-day nutrition, gear, and warm-up; do a 15-minute shakeout.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Choosing a plan* (50 min): Use Covered California's shop-and-compare tool for a sample 19-year-old and choose a plan with a written justification.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Satire: Swift and Pope
- Objective: Analyze satirical techniques
- Objective: Distinguish stated from intended meaning
- Vocabulary: satire, verbal irony, hyperbole, persona, Juvenalian, Horatian
- Materials: Swift, 'A Modest Proposal' (Project Gutenberg), Pope, 'The Rape of the Lock' Canto 1 excerpt
- Assessment: Original satire scored on persona, technique, and clear target.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Other trig graphs and modeling
- Objective: Graph tangent, cotangent, secant, cosecant
- Objective: Model periodic phenomena
- Vocabulary: tangent, secant, cosecant, cotangent, frequency
- Materials: Desmos, tide tables (NOAA)
- Assessment: Ferris wheel and tide modeling tasks.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Earth's energy budget and climate drivers
- Objective: Model energy flow into and out of Earth
- Objective: Explain Milankovitch cycles and other climate drivers
- Vocabulary: energy budget, greenhouse effect, Milankovitch cycles, insolation, ocean circulation
- Materials: two jars, plastic wrap, thermometers, lamp, PhET Greenhouse Effect
- Assessment: Energy flow explanation and quiz.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Voting, direct democracy, and the Electoral College
- Objective: Register or pre-register to vote
- Objective: Analyze California ballot measures and the Electoral College
- Vocabulary: initiative, referendum, recall, redistricting, gerrymandering, Electoral College
- Materials: registertovote.ca.gov, California Official Voter Information Guide, Citizens Redistricting Commission maps
- Assessment: Ballot-measure analysis and Unit 5 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Defining an inquiry
- Objective: Write a Sustained Investigation inquiry question
- Vocabulary: inquiry, sustained investigation, practice, experimentation, revision
- Materials: sketchbook, AP sample portfolios (College Board)
- Assessment: Sustained Investigation proposal.

### Lifetime Fitness II: 5K peak and taper
- Objective: Apply tapering principles
- Vocabulary: taper, race pace, carbohydrate
- Materials: running shoes
- Assessment: Race plan.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Insurance basics & health insurance
- Objective: Explain premiums, deductibles, and out-of-pocket maximums
- Objective: Compare health coverage options in California
- Vocabulary: premium, deductible, copay, coinsurance, out-of-pocket maximum, Covered California
- Materials: Khan Academy insurance videos, Covered California plan comparison (sample)
- Assessment: Insurance quiz and plan-choice justification.
