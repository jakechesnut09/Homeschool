## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Act I* (60 min): Read Act I. Collect five epigrams and explain the paradox in each (e.g., 'The truth is rarely pure and never simple').
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Slicing a cone* (60 min): Shine a flashlight at a wall at different angles to see circle, ellipse, parabola, and hyperbola; derive a parabola from focus and directrix.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Fishing game* (60 min): Play a 'tragedy of the commons' fishing game with crackers (fish reproduce each round) and record the population collapse.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Law of demand* (55 min): Survey 10 people on how many coffees they'd buy at different prices and graph a market demand curve.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Material tests* (50 min): Test a new material in five sketchbook experiments.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Forehand* (45 min): Practice forehand grip and swing; hit 50 balls against a wall.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *UC and homeschoolers* (50 min): Read the UC admissions page for homeschooled students and summarize how a–g can be met (AP exams, college courses, a–g approved online courses, admission by examination).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Act II* (60 min): Read Act II. Analyze 'Bunburying' as a satire of the double life demanded by Victorian respectability.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Ellipses* (60 min): Draw an ellipse with string and two pins; derive its equation and graph ellipses from standard form.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Build the model* (60 min): Build a spreadsheet model of a fish population with growth rate, carrying capacity, and harvest.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Shifts* (55 min): Graph demand shifts caused by income, tastes, substitutes, complements, and expectations for 8 scenarios.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Piece 8* (50 min): Begin piece 8 using the new material.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Backhand* (45 min): Practice one- or two-handed backhand against a wall.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *CSU and homeschoolers* (50 min): Read CSU's homeschool guidance and list required documents and how to report courses in Cal State Apply.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~380 min)

- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Continue* (50 min): Continue piece 8.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Serve* (45 min): Practice the serve motion using kinetic chain cues.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Course list* (50 min): Build a four-year course list from your homeschool records with a–g letter, grade, and semester credits for each course.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: The Importance of Being Earnest
- Objective: Analyze comic satire of Victorian manners
- Objective: Compare two treatments of marriage and respectability
- Vocabulary: comedy of manners, epigram, paradox, farce
- Materials: Oscar Wilde, The Importance of Being Earnest (Project Gutenberg), filmed production
- Assessment: Timed comparison essay (RL.11-12.9).

### Precalculus with Statistics: Conic sections
- Objective: Derive and graph parabolas, ellipses, and hyperbolas
- Objective: Connect conics to applications
- Vocabulary: focus, directrix, ellipse, hyperbola, eccentricity
- Materials: string, two pushpins, cardboard, flashlight
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Simulating resources, population, and biodiversity
- Objective: Create a computational simulation of resource management
- Objective: Test management strategies
- Vocabulary: simulation, sustainable yield, tragedy of the commons, parameter
- Materials: spreadsheet (Google Sheets), dried beans or goldfish crackers
- Assessment: Simulation and recommendation report.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Demand
- Objective: Explain the law of demand
- Objective: Distinguish changes in quantity demanded from shifts
- Vocabulary: demand, substitute, complement, elasticity, normal good
- Materials: graph paper, grocery receipts
- Assessment: Demand quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Investigation: piece 8
- Objective: Experiment with a new material
- Vocabulary: material exploration, unconventional media
- Materials: a material you have not used (wax, thread, clay, coffee stain)
- Assessment: Piece 8.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Tennis fundamentals
- Objective: Perform forehand, backhand, and serve
- Vocabulary: forehand, backhand, topspin, baseline, deuce
- Materials: tennis racket and balls, public court or wall
- Assessment: Rally count and serve test.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Homeschool applicants: a–g, testing & records
- Objective: Explain how UC and CSU evaluate homeschool applicants
- Objective: Draft course descriptions for the transcript
- Vocabulary: a–g, course description, admission by examination, AP exam, dual enrollment
- Materials: UC and CSU homeschool admissions pages, list of completed courses and grades
- Assessment: Draft four-year course list and course descriptions.
