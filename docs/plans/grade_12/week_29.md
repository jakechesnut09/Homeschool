## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Reverse outline* (60 min): Create a reverse outline of your draft (one sentence per paragraph) and reorder or cut paragraphs that don't advance the thesis.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Distributions* (60 min): Define a random variable for the number of heads in 4 flips; build and graph its probability distribution.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Footprint* (60 min): Calculate your household carbon footprint with the EPA calculator and identify the three largest sources.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Measuring GDP* (55 min): Learn the expenditure approach (C+I+G+NX) and classify 10 transactions as counted or not.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Setup* (50 min): Set up even lighting with two lamps at 45° and a neutral backdrop.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Cumbia* (45 min): Learn basic cumbia steps and their cultural origins; dance for 30 minutes.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *CCC programs* (50 min): Explore a local college catalog and list three certificates, three AA/AS degrees, and their unit requirements.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Revising and editing the research paper
- Objective: Revise for argument and organization
- Objective: Edit for conventions and MLA
- Vocabulary: reverse outline, coherence, hanging indent
- Materials: rough draft, revision checklist
- Assessment: Revised draft with tracked changes.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Random variables and expected value
- Objective: Build probability distributions
- Objective: Compute expected value and binomial probabilities
- Vocabulary: random variable, expected value, binomial distribution, Bernoulli trial
- Materials: dice, coins, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Unit 9 test.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Mitigation and your carbon footprint
- Objective: Represent human modifications to Earth systems computationally
- Objective: Evaluate mitigation strategies
- Vocabulary: mitigation, carbon footprint, cap-and-trade, carbon sequestration, net zero
- Materials: EPA carbon footprint calculator, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Footprint reduction model and Unit 8 test.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): GDP and economic growth
- Objective: Calculate GDP and growth rates
- Objective: Distinguish nominal and real data
- Vocabulary: GDP, real vs. nominal, business cycle, recession, GDP per capita
- Materials: FRED, BEA GDP release
- Assessment: GDP quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Photographing artwork
- Objective: Document work professionally
- Vocabulary: documentation, white balance, keystoning, resolution
- Materials: phone or camera, two lamps or window light, neutral backdrop
- Assessment: Digital archive.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Social and cultural dance
- Objective: Perform basic steps from several dance forms
- Vocabulary: tempo, partner frame, cumbia, swing, line dance
- Materials: music player, open floor
- Assessment: Dance performance.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): California Community Colleges
- Objective: Explain community college programs and costs
- Objective: Complete the CCCApply steps
- Vocabulary: CCCApply, associate degree, certificate, matriculation, priority registration
- Materials: CCCApply, local community college website and catalog
- Assessment: Community college education plan.
