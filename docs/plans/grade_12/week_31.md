## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: War poets and Modernist poetry
- Objective: Analyze WWI poetry's rejection of heroic tradition
- Objective: Identify Modernist techniques
- Vocabulary: Modernism, free verse, imagery, fragmentation, irony
- Materials: Owen 'Dulce et Decorum Est'; Brooke 'The Soldier'; Sassoon 'Suicide in the Trenches'; Eliot 'The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock'
- Assessment: Timed comparative poetry essay.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Simulation, margin of error, and comparing treatments
- Objective: Estimate a proportion with margin of error
- Objective: Use simulation to judge significance
- Vocabulary: sampling distribution, margin of error, confidence interval, statistical significance
- Materials: CODAP, index cards
- Assessment: Experiment lab report.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Designing and building a prototype
- Objective: Design and test a prototype solution
- Vocabulary: prototype, iteration, testing protocol, solar oven
- Materials: cardboard box, aluminum foil, plastic wrap, black paper, thermometer
- Assessment: Prototype test data and graphs.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Money, the Fed, and fiscal vs. monetary policy
- Objective: Explain the Fed's tools
- Objective: Compare fiscal and monetary policy
- Objective: Distinguish short- and long-term interest rates
- Vocabulary: federal funds rate, open market operations, yield curve, stimulus, quantitative easing
- Materials: latest FOMC statement (federalreserve.gov), FRED yield data
- Assessment: Unit 12 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Artist statement and online portfolio
- Objective: Write an artist statement
- Objective: Publish an online portfolio
- Vocabulary: artist statement, bio, CV
- Materials: free portfolio site (Google Sites, Adobe Portfolio)
- Assessment: Artist statement and published portfolio.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Evaluating fitness resources and claims
- Objective: Evaluate gyms, apps, and products as a consumer
- Vocabulary: contract, supplement, consumer claim, certification
- Materials: gym brochures, app reviews
- Assessment: Consumer guide.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Resumes & cover letters
- Objective: Write a targeted one-page resume
- Objective: Write a tailored cover letter
- Vocabulary: resume, action verb, accomplishment statement, ATS, cover letter, references
- Materials: resume template, real job posting, word processor
- Assessment: Final resume and cover letter scored with a rubric.
