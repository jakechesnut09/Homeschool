## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Drafting the research paper
- Objective: Draft an 8–10 page paper integrating sources
- Objective: Signal-phrase and cite correctly
- Vocabulary: signal phrase, integration, commentary
- Materials: outline, notes
- Assessment: Complete rough draft checkpoint.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Counting methods
- Objective: Use permutations and combinations
- Objective: Compute probabilities of compound events
- Vocabulary: factorial, permutation, combination, fundamental counting principle
- Materials: calculator
- Assessment: Counting quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Climate impacts on California
- Objective: Connect climate change to drought, fire, snowpack, and coastline
- Objective: Explain human responses
- Vocabulary: snowpack, atmospheric river, megadrought, coastal erosion, adaptation
- Materials: CA DWR snow survey data, ice cubes, pan with sand beach model
- Assessment: Impact quiz and paragraph.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Unions, minimum wage, and global labor
- Objective: Trace the history of labor unions
- Objective: Evaluate minimum wage and unemployment insurance
- Objective: Explain global capital and labor mobility
- Vocabulary: collective bargaining, strike, minimum wage, unemployment insurance, outsourcing
- Materials: Cesar Chavez speeches (UFW), Dept. of Industrial Relations minimum wage page
- Assessment: Unit 11 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Public art proposal
- Objective: Plan art that impacts community
- Vocabulary: public art, site-specific, proposal, mural
- Materials: photos of a local site, sketchbook
- Assessment: Public art proposal.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Outdoor adventure challenge
- Objective: Lead a group outdoor activity
- Vocabulary: leadership, risk management
- Materials: outdoor gear
- Assessment: Leadership outing and quiz.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Comparing financial aid offers
- Objective: Calculate net cost from aid offers
- Objective: Make a financially sound college decision
- Vocabulary: aid offer, net price, gap, work-study, appeal
- Materials: sample or real aid offers, spreadsheet, StudentAid.gov Loan Simulator
- Assessment: Unit 9 test and decision memo.
