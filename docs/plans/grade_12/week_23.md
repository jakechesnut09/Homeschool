## Day 1 — Monday (school day 111, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Exile in Mbanta* (60 min): Read chapters 14–19. Discuss how exile and the missionaries' arrival change Okonkwo's status and family.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *The normal curve* (60 min): Explore normal curves in CODAP; apply the 68-95-99.7 rule to heights and test scores.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Your watershed* (60 min): Use the EPA 'How's My Waterway' tool to identify your watershed and its listed impairments.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Price ceilings* (55 min): Graph the effect of rent control and analyze California's Costa-Hawkins Act debates.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Finish piece 9* (50 min): Complete piece 9.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Putting* (45 min): Practice putting from 3, 6, and 10 feet; record make percentage.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Prompts and planning* (50 min): Read all eight UC PIQ prompts and UC's guide, then brainstorm two stories for each and pick four.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 112, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Nwoye's choice* (60 min): Analyze why Nwoye converts, citing the hymn passage; compare father-son conflicts here and in Part One.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *z-scores* (60 min): Compute and compare z-scores (e.g., SAT vs. ACT scores) to decide which performance is more unusual.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Water testing* (60 min): Test tap water, a local stream or pond, and rainwater with test strips; record pH, nitrate, and hardness.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Price floors* (55 min): Graph a minimum wage as a price floor and discuss predicted vs. observed effects.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Art and society* (50 min): Research an artist or movement that changed public beliefs (e.g., the Chicano mural movement, Faith Ringgold) and note strategies.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Chipping* (45 min): Practice chipping to a target from 10–20 yards.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Draft 1 and 2* (50 min): Draft two PIQs of up to 350 words each using specific examples and reflection.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 113, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Part Three* (60 min): Read chapters 20–25. Annotate the final paragraph and the Commissioner's planned book title.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Things Fall Apart Parts Two and Three
- Objective: Analyze the arrival of missionaries and colonial government
- Objective: Interpret the ironic ending
- Vocabulary: exile, conversion, district commissioner, tragic hero
- Materials: Things Fall Apart, Yeats 'The Second Coming'
- Assessment: Debate rubric evaluating evidence and rebuttal.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Normal distributions
- Objective: Apply the Empirical Rule and z-scores
- Objective: Estimate population percentages
- Vocabulary: normal distribution, z-score, Empirical Rule, percentile
- Materials: CODAP, calculator
- Assessment: Normal distribution quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Water quality
- Objective: Test water quality indicators
- Objective: Trace sources of water pollution
- Vocabulary: watershed, point source, nonpoint source, eutrophication, turbidity
- Materials: water test strips (pH, nitrate, hardness), clear jars, coffee filters, sand, gravel, activated charcoal
- Assessment: Watershed report.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Price controls and market structures
- Objective: Analyze price ceilings and floors
- Objective: Compare competition, monopoly, and oligopoly
- Vocabulary: price ceiling, price floor, monopoly, oligopoly, antitrust
- Materials: rent control articles, Sherman Antitrust Act text
- Assessment: Price-control policy brief.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Investigation: pieces 9–10
- Objective: Deepen synthesis of ideas and materials
- Vocabulary: synthesis, social impact
- Materials: chosen media
- Assessment: Pieces 9–10.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Golf fundamentals
- Objective: Perform putting, chipping, and full swing
- Vocabulary: grip, stance, putt, chip, par
- Materials: golf clubs (borrowed or rented), practice balls, putting green or driving range
- Assessment: Short-game skills test.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Personal Insight Questions
- Objective: Choose four UC PIQs that showcase different strengths
- Objective: Draft and revise 350-word responses
- Vocabulary: Personal Insight Question, anecdote, reflection, specificity
- Materials: UC PIQ prompts and guide, word processor
- Assessment: Four polished PIQs scored with a rubric.
