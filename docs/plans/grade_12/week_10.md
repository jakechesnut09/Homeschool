## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *The apparitions* (60 min): Read IV.1. Explain how each apparition equivocates and how Macbeth's misreading drives his overconfidence.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *What is a radian?* (60 min): Wrap string the length of a paper plate's radius around its edge to see one radian; convert 15 angles between degrees and radians.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Properties of water* (60 min): Test water's high heat capacity by heating equal masses of water and sand under a lamp and recording temperature every 2 minutes.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Plessy to Brown* (55 min): Compare excerpts from Plessy v. Ferguson (including Harlan's dissent) and Brown v. Board; explain the reversal.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *California Impressionists* (50 min): Study California plein air painters (e.g., Granville Redmond, Guy Rose) and compare to French Impressionists.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Easy run* (45 min): Run 25–30 minutes easy and log pace and heart rate.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Why taxes* (50 min): List public goods funded by federal and California taxes and compare the share of revenue from income, payroll, and sales taxes.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 47, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Macduff's grief* (60 min): Read IV.2–IV.3. Compare Macduff's grief with Macbeth's response to loss; discuss what the play defines as true manhood.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Arc length & sector area* (60 min): Solve arc-length and sector-area problems (s = rθ, A = ½r²θ), including a bicycle-wheel application.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Chemical weathering* (60 min): Place chalk in water vs. vinegar and measure mass loss over 24 hours to model acid weathering of limestone.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Student rights* (55 min): Analyze Tinker v. Des Moines and a later student speech case; discuss limits on student speech.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Morning study* (50 min): Paint a 1-hour plein air study in morning light.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Strength* (45 min): Do a runner's strength circuit (single-leg squats, calf raises, glute bridges).
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Brackets* (50 min): Calculate federal income tax for three incomes using the current bracket table and find each marginal and effective rate.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 48, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Sleepwalking* (60 min): Read V.1. Analyze Lady Macbeth's prose speech and how her guilt reverses Act II's 'a little water clears us of this deed.'
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Building the unit circle* (60 min): Use 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 triangles to build the unit circle coordinates for all special angles.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Frost wedging* (60 min): Fill a small plaster block's drilled hole with water, freeze/thaw it several cycles, and record cracking.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Rights of the accused* (55 min): Analyze Miranda v. Arizona and Gideon v. Wainwright and their effect on policing and courts.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Afternoon study* (50 min): Paint the same scene in afternoon light and compare.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Fartlek* (45 min): Do a 30-minute fartlek run with 8 surges.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *California taxes* (50 min): Use the CA FTB rate schedule to compute state income tax for the same incomes and add local sales tax on a $1,000 purchase in your county.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 49, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Tomorrow and tomorrow* (60 min): Read V.2–V.8. Perform and analyze the 'Tomorrow, and tomorrow' soliloquy as Macbeth's final worldview.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Macbeth Acts IV–V – downfall
- Objective: Analyze the resolution of the tragic arc
- Objective: Evaluate film interpretations
- Vocabulary: catharsis, hubris, tragic flaw, denouement
- Materials: Macbeth text, two filmed productions (library/streaming)
- Assessment: Production comparison chart and discussion (RL.11-12.7).

### Precalculus with Statistics: Radian measure and the unit circle
- Objective: Convert between degrees and radians
- Objective: Use the unit circle to evaluate trig functions
- Vocabulary: radian, arc length, unit circle, reference angle, coterminal
- Materials: string, paper plate, protractor
- Assessment: Timed unit circle and evaluation quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Water as a geologic agent
- Objective: Investigate properties of water
- Objective: Measure water's effects on erosion and weathering
- Vocabulary: weathering, erosion, frost wedging, solubility, stream table
- Materials: chalk, vinegar, plaster of Paris, zip bags, baking pan stream table, sand, watering can
- Assessment: Water-investigation lab report (HS-ESS2-5).

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Civil rights and civil liberties cases
- Objective: Trace changing interpretations of civil rights
- Objective: Moot a landmark case
- Vocabulary: equal protection, separate but equal, affirmative action, Miranda rights, strict scrutiny
- Materials: Plessy and Brown opinions (excerpts), Tinker v. Des Moines, Regents of UC v. Bakke, Miranda v. Arizona
- Assessment: Moot court argument and Unit 4 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Plein air and landscape
- Objective: Paint outdoors capturing light
- Vocabulary: plein air, atmospheric perspective, Impressionism, California Plein Air
- Materials: portable paint kit, small panels
- Assessment: Plein air studies and interpretation.

### Lifetime Fitness II: 5K training: building base
- Objective: Follow and log an endurance plan
- Vocabulary: cadence, pacing, negative split
- Materials: running shoes, log
- Assessment: Running log.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): How taxes work
- Objective: Explain progressive income tax brackets
- Objective: Identify federal, state, and local taxes Californians pay
- Vocabulary: progressive tax, marginal rate, effective rate, sales tax, payroll tax, Franchise Tax Board
- Materials: IRS tax bracket table (current year), CA FTB tax rate schedule, calculator
- Assessment: Tax-calculation quiz.
