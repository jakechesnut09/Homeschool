## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Choose a topic* (60 min): Brainstorm five topics connected to a career interest or a literature theme; narrow one into an open, arguable research question.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Scatter plots* (60 min): Plot life expectancy vs. GDP per capita from public data and describe direction, form, and strength.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Waste audit* (60 min): Weigh and sort your household's trash, recycling, and organics for the week and record in a spreadsheet.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Public goods* (55 min): Classify 10 goods as private, public, common resource, or club goods and explain the free-rider problem.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Gap analysis* (50 min): Lay out all pieces and identify gaps in the investigation.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Ten Essentials* (45 min): Pack the Ten Essentials and learn Leave No Trace principles.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Which application* (50 min): Read CSAC's guidance and explain who files the CADAA (including AB 540 eligibility) versus the FAFSA, and why never to file both.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Library databases* (60 min): Use your public library's free databases (e.g., Gale, EBSCO) and Google Scholar to find six candidate sources; record full citation info.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Linear regression* (60 min): Fit a least-squares line in Desmos; interpret slope and intercept in context and compute r.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Microplastics* (60 min): Filter a sample of beach sand or laundry lint water through a coffee filter and look for microplastic fibers with a magnifier.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Externalities* (55 min): Graph a negative externality (air pollution) and analyze California's cap-and-trade as a solution.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Final pieces* (50 min): Create or revise pieces to fill the gaps.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Conditioning* (45 min): Do stair or hill repeats with a loaded daypack for 30 minutes.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *CADAA walkthrough* (50 min): Walk through the California Dream Act Application sections and compare them to the FAFSA.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Evaluate sources* (60 min): Apply the CRAAP test (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose) to each source and keep the four strongest.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Residuals* (60 min): Make a residual plot to judge fit; try a logarithmic model and compare residuals.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Composting* (60 min): Start a small compost bin and explain how California's SB 1383 organic waste law reduces methane.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Consumer protection* (55 min): Read about the FTC and a California consumer protection law (e.g., CCPA privacy) and explain the market problem it addresses.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Continue* (50 min): Continue final pieces.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Plan a hike* (45 min): Plan a 4–6 mile hike in a local or state park, including weather, elevation, and emergency plan.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *GPA verification* (50 min): Learn how Cal Grant GPA verification works for a private homeschool student and prepare the school's GPA certification or test-score alternative.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Proposal* (60 min): Write a one-page research proposal: question, why it matters, working hypothesis, and planned sources.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Correlation vs. causation* (60 min): Analyze 5 'spurious correlation' examples and identify lurking variables; explain why an experiment is needed for causation.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Evaluate technology* (60 min): Evaluate a pollution-reduction technology (catalytic converter, scrubbers, Ocean Cleanup) using cost, effectiveness, and side effects.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Costs vs. benefits* (55 min): Analyze a case where regulation costs may exceed benefits (permitting delays, housing) and propose improvements.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Finish* (50 min): Finish the body of work (12–15 pieces).
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Hike* (45 min): Complete the planned hike with a family member, logging time and heart rate.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *WebGrants* (50 min): Create a WebGrants student account, find where award status and messages appear, and set reminders to check monthly.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 125, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Conference* (60 min): Present the proposal in a 10-minute conference with a parent/mentor and revise the question based on feedback.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Unit 8 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 8 test on data analysis and regression.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Research question and preliminary sources
- Objective: Develop an arguable research question
- Objective: Locate and evaluate credible sources
- Vocabulary: research question, scholarly source, database, CRAAP test, primary vs. secondary
- Materials: public library card (for databases), research notebook
- Assessment: Approved research proposal and source evaluation chart.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Scatter plots, regression, and correlation
- Objective: Fit and interpret linear and nonlinear models
- Objective: Distinguish correlation from causation
- Vocabulary: least-squares regression, residual, correlation coefficient r, lurking variable
- Materials: CODAP, Desmos, public data (e.g., Gapminder)
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Waste, plastics, and pollution solutions
- Objective: Analyze waste streams
- Objective: Evaluate a technology that reduces pollution
- Vocabulary: microplastics, landfill, composting, circular economy, SB 1383
- Materials: kitchen scale, household trash for one week, compost container
- Assessment: Waste audit analysis and Unit 7 test.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Market failures and the role of government
- Objective: Identify public goods and externalities
- Objective: Evaluate government interventions
- Vocabulary: public good, externality, free rider, regulation, government failure
- Materials: CARB cap-and-trade overview, FTC consumer protection page
- Assessment: Government-role quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Investigation: completion
- Objective: Complete the Sustained Investigation body of work
- Vocabulary: cohesion, body of work
- Materials: all pieces
- Assessment: Completed Sustained Investigation.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Hiking safety and preparation
- Objective: Plan a safe day hike
- Vocabulary: Ten Essentials, elevation gain, Leave No Trace, trailhead
- Materials: daypack, water, trail map
- Assessment: Hike plan and trip report.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): California Dream Act Application & CSAC WebGrants
- Objective: Determine whether the FAFSA or CADAA is the right form
- Objective: Set up and monitor a WebGrants account
- Vocabulary: CADAA, AB 540, CSAC, WebGrants, GPA verification
- Materials: csac.ca.gov, WebGrants student account
- Assessment: State-aid checklist and WebGrants setup.
