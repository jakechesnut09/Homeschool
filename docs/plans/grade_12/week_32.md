## Day 1 — Monday (school day 156, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Part I* (60 min): Read Part I. Discuss why Gregor worries more about his job than his transformation; mark deadpan narration.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Insurance decisions* (60 min): Compare expected costs of low vs. high deductible car or health insurance plans.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Trade-offs* (60 min): Score your solution on cost, safety, reliability, aesthetics, and social/environmental impact.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Comparative advantage* (55 min): Calculate opportunity costs for two countries producing two goods and determine who should specialize in what.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Venue* (50 min): Secure a venue (library community room, co-op, café) and set a date.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: The Metamorphosis
- Objective: Analyze absurdism and alienation in Kafka
- Objective: Interpret symbolism in a Modernist novella
- Vocabulary: absurd, Kafkaesque, alienation, allegory
- Materials: Franz Kafka, The Metamorphosis (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Metamorphosis analytical essay.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Decisions with probability
- Objective: Use expected value to weigh decisions
- Objective: Make fair decisions using probability
- Vocabulary: expected payoff, insurance, deductible, fair decision
- Materials: sample insurance quotes, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Unit 10 test.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Evaluating trade-offs and simulating impacts
- Objective: Evaluate solutions using prioritized criteria and trade-offs
- Objective: Use a simulation to model impact
- Vocabulary: life-cycle, scalability, equity, sensitivity analysis
- Materials: spreadsheet, En-ROADS or spreadsheet model
- Assessment: Design brief and pitch.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Comparative advantage and trade
- Objective: Explain gains from trade
- Objective: Calculate comparative advantage
- Vocabulary: absolute advantage, comparative advantage, specialization, imports, exports
- Materials: Census trade data, Port of Los Angeles/Long Beach statistics
- Assessment: Comparative advantage quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Planning the exhibition
- Objective: Plan a public exhibition
- Vocabulary: exhibition, wall label, venue, installation plan
- Materials: venue (library, church hall, co-op, café), floor plan
- Assessment: Exhibition plan.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Fitting activity into adult life
- Objective: Plan activity around school, work, and family
- Vocabulary: active commute, workplace wellness, intramurals
- Materials: sample college or work schedule
- Assessment: Busy-day workouts and reflection.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Job applications & work permits
- Objective: Complete online job applications accurately
- Objective: Obtain a California work permit if under 18
- Vocabulary: work permit, Statement of Intent to Employ a Minor, I-9, background check, online application
- Materials: CA form B1-1 (Statement of Intent to Employ a Minor and Request for Work Permit), two real job postings
- Assessment: Completed application packet and work permit form.
