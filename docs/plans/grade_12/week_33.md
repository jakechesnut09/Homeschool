## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Orwell: language and power
- Objective: Evaluate the structure of an argumentative essay
- Objective: Apply Orwell's rules to your own writing
- Vocabulary: euphemism, propaganda, doublespeak, dying metaphor
- Materials: Orwell, 'Politics and the English Language' and 'Shooting an Elephant' (library or anthology), 1984 excerpt on Newspeak (Appendix)
- Assessment: Unit 9 test.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Limits preview: rates of change
- Objective: Estimate limits numerically and graphically
- Objective: Connect average rate of change to instantaneous rate
- Vocabulary: limit, instantaneous rate of change, tangent line, continuity
- Materials: Desmos, ball and phone video
- Assessment: Limits quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Capstone: research question and plan
- Objective: Pose a local environmental question
- Objective: Plan a data collection method
- Vocabulary: hypothesis, sampling plan, control, replicate
- Materials: field notebook, test kits from earlier units
- Assessment: Approved capstone plan.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Trade policy: tariffs and agreements
- Objective: Compare Smoot-Hawley with modern trade debates
- Objective: Evaluate effects of trade agreements
- Vocabulary: tariff, quota, protectionism, free trade, NAFTA/USMCA, WTO
- Materials: Smoot-Hawley Tariff primary sources, USMCA summary (USTR)
- Assessment: Trade policy debate.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Preparing and protecting work
- Objective: Prepare art for display and preservation
- Vocabulary: matting, archival, hanging hardware, conservation
- Materials: mat board, mat cutter or pre-cut mats, hanging wire, acid-free tape
- Assessment: Prepared works.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Writing the adult fitness plan
- Objective: Write a 12-week adult fitness plan
- Vocabulary: periodization, maintenance, adherence
- Materials: plan template
- Assessment: Draft adult fitness plan.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Interviews
- Objective: Answer common interview questions with STAR stories
- Objective: Perform a mock interview professionally
- Vocabulary: STAR method, behavioral question, elevator pitch, thank-you note, dress code
- Materials: phone or webcam for recording, list of common interview questions
- Assessment: Mock interview rubric score and thank-you email.
