# Precalculus with Statistics (Grade 12)

A college-preparatory course that deepens function analysis (polynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic, and trigonometric), adds vectors, matrices, complex numbers in polar form, sequences, series, and conics, and closes with a substantial statistics strand: data analysis, regression, probability, and inference. Students use Desmos and CODAP throughout and finish with a limits preview and an original statistical investigation.

*Framework:* California Common Core State Standards for Mathematics (Higher Mathematics, including + standards); 2023 California Mathematics Framework (Data Science and Precalculus pathways)  
*Time:* 60 min/day
*UC/CSU a–g:* c · *Credits:* 10.0

## Units

### Unit 1: Functions and Their Transformations (weeks 1–4)
A small family of parent functions, transformed and combined, models a huge range of situations.

- Week 1: Functions, domain, and key features
- Week 2: Parent functions and transformations
- Week 3: Combining and composing functions; average rate of change
- Week 4: Inverse functions and Unit 1 test

### Unit 2: Polynomial and Rational Functions (weeks 5–7)
Factors determine zeros, and zeros and asymptotes determine the shape of a graph.

- Week 5: Polynomial functions
- Week 6: Polynomial division and complex zeros
- Week 7: Rational functions

### Unit 3: Exponential and Logarithmic Functions (weeks 8–9)
Logarithms undo exponentials and let us solve for time in growth and decay.

- Week 8: Exponential functions and logarithms
- Week 9: Solving exponential and logarithmic equations; modeling

### Unit 4: Trigonometric Functions (weeks 10–13)
The unit circle turns angles into coordinates and creates functions that model anything periodic.

- Week 10: Radian measure and the unit circle
- Week 11: Graphs of sine and cosine
- Week 12: Other trig graphs and modeling
- Week 13: Inverse trig functions and trig equations

### Unit 5: Analytic Trigonometry, Triangles, and Polar Form (weeks 14–16)
Identities and the Laws of Sines and Cosines extend trigonometry to any triangle and to the complex plane.

- Week 14: Trigonometric identities
- Week 15: Law of Sines, Law of Cosines, and triangle area
- Week 16: Polar coordinates and complex numbers in polar form

### Unit 6: Vectors and Matrices (weeks 17–19)
Vectors capture magnitude and direction; matrices organize data and solve systems all at once.

- Week 17: Vectors
- Week 18: Semester 1 review and final exam
- Week 19: Matrices and systems

### Unit 7: Sequences, Series, and Conic Sections (weeks 20–21)
Patterns that repeat by addition or multiplication can be summed, and conics are defined by distance.

- Week 20: Sequences and series
- Week 21: Conic sections

### Unit 8: Data Analysis and Regression (weeks 22–25)
Distributions and models summarize variability, but correlation alone never proves cause.

- Week 22: Describing distributions
- Week 23: Normal distributions
- Week 24: Two-way tables and categorical data
- Week 25: Scatter plots, regression, and correlation

### Unit 9: Probability and Random Variables (weeks 26–29)
Probability quantifies uncertainty and expected value guides decisions.

- Week 26: Probability rules
- Week 27: Conditional probability and independence
- Week 28: Counting methods
- Week 29: Random variables and expected value

### Unit 10: Statistical Inference and Decisions (weeks 30–32)
Random samples and experiments let us draw conclusions about populations with known uncertainty.

- Week 30: Sampling, surveys, and experiments
- Week 31: Simulation, margin of error, and comparing treatments
- Week 32: Decisions with probability

### Unit 11: Limits Preview and Statistics Capstone (weeks 33–36)
Average rates of change point toward calculus, and a full investigation pulls the year's statistics together.

- Week 33: Limits preview: rates of change
- Week 34: Statistics capstone: design and data collection
- Week 35: Statistics capstone: analysis and presentation
- Week 36: Final review and exam
