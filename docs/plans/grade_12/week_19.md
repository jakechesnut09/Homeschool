## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Victorian age* (60 min): Read an overview of industrialization, empire, and Darwin's impact on faith. Read 'Dover Beach' and annotate imagery of retreating faith.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Matrices as data* (60 min): Organize store inventory and price data in matrices; add, subtract, and scalar-multiply them.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Biodiversity* (60 min): Define genetic, species, and ecosystem diversity and calculate a simple diversity index from sample data.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Scarcity* (55 min): List 10 scarce resources in your life (time, money, space) and identify a choice each forces.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Revisit inquiry* (50 min): Rewrite your inquiry question based on what you discovered in S1.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Rules and serve* (45 min): Learn rules and scoring; practice underhand serves (20 per side).
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Interest inventory* (50 min): Take the career interest assessment on CaliforniaColleges.edu and the O*NET Interest Profiler, then compare results.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 92, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *My Last Duchess* (60 min): Read Browning's 'My Last Duchess' aloud. Infer what happened to the Duchess and what the Duke unknowingly reveals about himself.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Matrix multiplication* (60 min): Multiply matrices by hand and compute total revenue by multiplying a sales matrix by a price matrix.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Quadrat survey* (60 min): Mark a 1 m² quadrat in a yard, park, or vacant lot and identify and count all organisms using iNaturalist.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Opportunity cost* (55 min): Calculate the opportunity cost of attending college vs. working full-time for four years.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Plan* (50 min): Plan pieces 6–15 with thumbnails and materials list.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Dinks* (45 min): Practice dinking at the kitchen line with a partner for 20 minutes.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Strengths and values* (50 min): Complete a work-values ranking and list five strengths with evidence from homeschool projects, jobs, or activities.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 93, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Ulysses* (60 min): Read Tennyson's 'Ulysses' and compare its ideal of striving with Beowulf's heroism.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Determinants & inverses* (60 min): Compute 2×2 determinants and inverses; verify AA⁻¹ = I; discuss why det = 0 means no inverse.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Keystone species* (60 min): Analyze sea otter–urchin–kelp data from the California coast as an example of a keystone species.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *PPF* (55 min): Graph a production possibilities frontier for a household (meals vs. cleaning) and identify efficient, inefficient, and unattainable points.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Piece 6* (50 min): Begin piece 6.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Volleys* (45 min): Practice volleys and blocks; play a short game.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Career research* (50 min): Research three careers in the Occupational Outlook Handbook: duties, education, pay, outlook, and California openings.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Victorian poetry
- Objective: Analyze dramatic monologue and Victorian themes of doubt and duty
- Vocabulary: dramatic monologue, Victorian, auditor, elegy
- Materials: Browning 'My Last Duchess'; Tennyson 'Ulysses'; Arnold 'Dover Beach'; Christina Rossetti 'Goblin Market' excerpt
- Assessment: Original dramatic monologue and poetry quiz.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Matrices and systems
- Objective: Operate on matrices
- Objective: Solve systems with inverse matrices
- Vocabulary: matrix, dimension, determinant, identity matrix, inverse matrix
- Materials: Desmos matrix calculator
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Ecosystem stability and change
- Objective: Evaluate claims about ecosystem stability
- Objective: Investigate a local ecosystem
- Vocabulary: biodiversity, carrying capacity, succession, disturbance, keystone species
- Materials: meter stick or 1 m² string quadrat, field notebook, iNaturalist app
- Assessment: Quadrat data and CER on ecosystem stability.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Scarcity, choice, and opportunity cost
- Objective: Explain scarcity and opportunity cost
- Objective: Use marginal analysis
- Vocabulary: scarcity, opportunity cost, production possibilities frontier, marginal benefit, marginal cost
- Materials: graph paper, Khan Academy microeconomics
- Assessment: Fundamentals quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Investigation: new directions
- Objective: Revise inquiry based on S1 reflection
- Vocabulary: revision, pivot, body of work
- Materials: sketchbook, chosen media
- Assessment: Updated plan and piece 6.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Pickleball fundamentals
- Objective: Perform serve, return, dink, and volley
- Vocabulary: kitchen, dink, third-shot drop, two-bounce rule
- Materials: pickleball paddles and balls, public court or driveway with tape lines
- Assessment: Pickleball skill test.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Self-assessment & career exploration
- Objective: Identify interests, strengths, and values
- Objective: Research careers with labor-market data
- Vocabulary: interest inventory, labor market information, career cluster, informational interview
- Materials: CaliforniaColleges.edu account, O*NET Interest Profiler, BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook
- Assessment: Career exploration report.
