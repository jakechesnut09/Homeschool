## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *The dagger* (60 min): Read II.1–II.2. Analyze the dagger soliloquy and the 'sleep no more' exchange; start a motif tracker for blood, sleep, and darkness.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Solving equations* (60 min): Solve 12 exponential and logarithmic equations, checking for extraneous solutions.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Boundary models* (60 min): Model divergent, convergent, and transform boundaries with graham crackers on frosting; record landforms each produces.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Marbury v. Madison* (55 min): Read excerpts of Marshall's opinion and explain how the Court established judicial review.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Underpainting* (50 min): Tone a canvas and block in a still life with a monochrome underpainting.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Energy systems* (45 min): Learn aerobic vs. anaerobic systems; do an easy 25-minute run.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Student loan basics* (50 min): Read the StudentAid.gov loan types page and make a comparison chart of Direct Subsidized, Unsubsidized, PLUS, and private loans.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *The porter and discovery* (60 min): Read II.3–II.4. Explain the comic relief of the Porter scene and how unnatural events in nature reflect the murder.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Half-life and doubling* (60 min): Solve half-life (carbon-14) and doubling-time (population, investment) problems with logarithms.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Global patterns* (60 min): Plot the last 30 days of M4.5+ earthquakes from USGS on a world map and outline plate boundaries.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *McCulloch v. Maryland* (55 min): Analyze 'the power to tax involves the power to destroy' and how McCulloch expanded federal power.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Block-in* (50 min): Block in large color shapes, focusing on value relationships.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Intervals* (45 min): Do 6 × 400 m intervals with 90-second recoveries.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Loan Simulator* (50 min): Use the StudentAid.gov Loan Simulator to estimate monthly payments on $27,000 under standard and income-driven plans.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Banquo's fear* (60 min): Read III.1–III.2. Compare Macbeth's reasoning for killing Banquo with his earlier hesitation about Duncan; write a paragraph citing two quotations.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Cooling lab* (60 min): Measure the temperature of a cup of hot water every 2 minutes for 30 minutes and record data for Newton's Law of Cooling.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *San Andreas* (60 min): Study the San Andreas transform fault and measure offset features (e.g., Wallace Creek) to calculate slip rate.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *U.S. v. Nixon* (55 min): Listen to Oyez audio excerpts and explain how the case limited executive privilege.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Develop* (50 min): Develop forms and edges; use glazing or impasto deliberately.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Cross-train* (45 min): Do 30 minutes of cycling or swimming.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Debt strategies* (50 min): Pay down a sample four-debt portfolio using both avalanche and snowball methods in a spreadsheet and compare interest paid.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 44, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *The banquet* (60 min): Read III.3–III.6. Watch the banquet scene in one filmed production and discuss how staging the ghost (visible or invisible) changes meaning.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Fit the model* (60 min): Fit an exponential model to the cooling data in Desmos, interpret parameters, and predict when it reaches 40 °C.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Cascadia & mountains* (60 min): Compare Cascadia subduction (Mt. Shasta, Lassen) with the Sierra Nevada's uplift; map California's landforms by process.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Activism vs. restraint* (55 min): Read short essays on judicial philosophy (originalism, living constitution) and apply each to one case.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Finish* (50 min): Add final details and highlights.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Tempo* (45 min): Run 20 minutes at comfortably hard tempo pace.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *When debt goes wrong* (50 min): Research collections, default, and wage garnishment, and list three resources (nonprofit credit counseling, CFPB) for help.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Macbeth Acts II–III – murder and guilt
- Objective: Track motifs of blood, sleep, and darkness
- Objective: Analyze Macbeth's transformation
- Vocabulary: motif, dramatic irony, hallucination, regicide
- Materials: Macbeth text, motif tracker chart
- Assessment: Motif analysis paragraph scored on evidence and commentary.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Solving exponential and logarithmic equations; modeling
- Objective: Solve exponential and log equations
- Objective: Fit exponential models to data
- Vocabulary: half-life, doubling time, exponential regression, extraneous
- Materials: Desmos, cup of hot water and thermometer
- Assessment: Unit 3 test and cooling-lab report.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Plate boundaries and California landforms
- Objective: Relate boundary types to landforms
- Objective: Analyze California earthquake data
- Vocabulary: divergent, convergent, transform, fault, subduction zone
- Materials: graham crackers, frosting, wax paper, USGS earthquake map
- Assessment: Annotated California cross-section.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Judicial review and federal power
- Objective: Evaluate Marbury, McCulloch, and U.S. v. Nixon
- Objective: Distinguish judicial activism from restraint
- Vocabulary: judicial review, implied powers, supremacy clause, judicial activism, judicial restraint
- Materials: Oyez case summaries, opinion excerpts
- Assessment: Case analysis essay.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Painting from life
- Objective: Complete an observational painting
- Vocabulary: underpainting, block-in, glazing, impasto
- Materials: acrylic or oil paints, canvas or panel
- Assessment: Observational painting.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Aerobic training principles
- Objective: Plan aerobic training using zones
- Vocabulary: aerobic, anaerobic, VO2 max, tempo, long slow distance
- Materials: running shoes, watch
- Assessment: 5K training plan.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Managing debt & student loans
- Objective: Compare federal and private student loans
- Objective: Apply debt snowball and avalanche strategies
- Vocabulary: subsidized loan, unsubsidized loan, income-driven repayment, debt avalanche, debt snowball, default
- Materials: StudentAid.gov Loan Simulator, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Unit 3 test and debt-repayment spreadsheet.
