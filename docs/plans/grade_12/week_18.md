## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Timeline review* (60 min): Build a literary timeline from Anglo-Saxon to Romantic periods, placing each text read with one defining trait of its period.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Review: functions* (60 min): Complete a review set on transformations, inverses, polynomials, rationals, and logs.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Concept map* (60 min): Build a semester concept map linking stars, Earth formation, plate tectonics, climate, and resources.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Review: foundations & rights* (55 min): Create a one-page study guide on founding principles and the Bill of Rights.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Image transfer* (50 min): Practice gel-medium image transfers of your own photos onto paper and wood.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Reflect* (45 min): Write a reflection on your semester training and what motivated you; do a 30-minute workout.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Rental search* (50 min): Find three rentals near a chosen college or job, apply the 30% rule, and calculate total move-in costs.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Archetype seminar* (60 min): Discuss the 'overreacher' archetype across Beowulf, Macbeth, and Victor Frankenstein; take notes for the final.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Review: trig* (60 min): Complete a review set on the unit circle, sinusoids, identities, and triangle laws.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Data practice* (60 min): Complete a data-analysis review set using graphs from each unit.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Review: branches & courts* (55 min): Complete a review set matching powers to branches and cases to holdings.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Mixed-media piece* (50 min): Create a mixed-media work for your investigation combining transfer, paint, and drawing.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *New goals* (45 min): Write new SMART goals for semester 2.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Reading a lease* (50 min): Annotate a sample California lease for rent, deposit, late fees, repairs, and termination; list your tenant rights and the legal security deposit return timeline.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 88, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Vocabulary & grammar review* (60 min): Review the semester's vocabulary logs and grammar lessons with a practice test on appositives, pronouns, and punctuation.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Practice exam* (60 min): Take a timed practice exam and analyze errors by topic.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Practice test* (60 min): Take a practice exam and target weak areas with CK-12 readings.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Civic action presentation* (55 min): Present your civic action project in 5 minutes.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Finish* (50 min): Complete the mixed-media piece.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Revise plan* (45 min): Revise your weekly plan to include strength, cardio, and a lifetime sport.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Roommates* (50 min): Write a roommate agreement covering rent split, utilities, guests, and chores.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 89, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Final exam* (60 min): Take the semester final: multiple choice on passages, short answers, and a 45-minute synthesis essay comparing two works.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Targeted practice* (60 min): Do Khan Academy practice on your two weakest topics from the practice exam.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Lab review* (60 min): Review your lab notebook and summarize the claim and evidence from five investigations.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Practice exam* (55 min): Retake the USCIS civics test and complete a practice exam.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Semester review* (50 min): Lay out all semester work and select your top 8 pieces.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Choice workout* (45 min): Lead a family member through a workout you designed.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Capstone budget* (50 min): Build a complete first-year independent life budget combining a job's net pay, rent, insurance, transportation, savings, and debt payments.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 90, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Portfolio reflection* (60 min): Select your two best pieces of writing and write a 400-word reflection on how your writing changed this semester.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Semester final* (60 min): Take the cumulative Semester 1 final exam.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Semester final* (60 min): Take the Semester 1 final exam.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Semester final* (55 min): Take the Government semester final with a DBQ essay on an enduring constitutional tension.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Semester critique* (50 min): Write a semester self-evaluation and present your top pieces.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Written exam* (45 min): Take the semester written exam on fitness concepts.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Semester final* (50 min): Take the NGPF post-assessment/semester final and present your capstone budget to a parent.
