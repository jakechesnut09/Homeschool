## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: The Pardoner's Tale and the Wife of Bath
- Objective: Analyze irony and moral in an exemplum
- Objective: Compare medieval texts on a common theme
- Vocabulary: exemplum, situational irony, hypocrisy, romance
- Materials: Canterbury Tales text, Sir Gawain and the Green Knight excerpt (library)
- Assessment: Unit test with comparative essay (RL.11-12.9).

### Precalculus with Statistics: Rational functions
- Objective: Identify asymptotes and holes
- Objective: Operate on rational expressions and solve rational equations
- Vocabulary: vertical asymptote, horizontal asymptote, slant asymptote, removable discontinuity
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Geologic time
- Objective: Interpret relative and absolute ages
- Objective: Build a scaled geologic timeline
- Vocabulary: superposition, unconformity, index fossil, eon, era
- Materials: adding-machine tape or 4.6 m string, meter stick, colored paper/sand layers
- Assessment: Unit 2 test and scaled timeline.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): The executive branch
- Objective: Describe Article II powers
- Objective: Analyze presidential decision-making
- Vocabulary: executive order, cabinet, bureaucracy, commander in chief, executive privilege
- Materials: Article II text, Federal Register executive orders, Truman's Korean War / Kennedy's Cuban Missile Crisis documents (DocsTeach)
- Assessment: Executive branch quiz and case-study analysis.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Portraiture and expressive drawing
- Objective: Create an expressive portrait
- Objective: Participate in critique
- Vocabulary: expressive line, likeness, critique
- Materials: charcoal, ink, mirror or photo reference
- Assessment: Expressive portrait and critique notes.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Program in progress
- Objective: Apply progressive overload
- Vocabulary: deload, plateau, DOMS
- Materials: weights or bands, training log
- Assessment: Training log progression.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Credit reports & credit scores
- Objective: Explain what goes into a credit score
- Objective: Read a credit report and dispute an error
- Vocabulary: credit report, FICO score, credit utilization, hard inquiry, AnnualCreditReport.com
- Materials: sample credit report (NGPF or CFPB), Khan Academy credit videos
- Assessment: Credit profile ranking and dispute letter.
