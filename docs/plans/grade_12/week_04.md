## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Anglo-Saxon world* (60 min): Read a short overview of Anglo-Saxon England and the Sutton Hoo ship burial; list five values a warrior culture would prize. Read 'The Seafarer' and mark where Christian and pagan ideas collide.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Inverses graphically* (60 min): Reflect graphs over y = x on patty paper and in Desmos; apply the horizontal-line test to 10 functions.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Kepler's first law* (60 min): Draw ellipses with string and pins at different focus spacings; calculate eccentricity and compare with planetary orbits.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Citizenship* (55 min): Take the USCIS 100-question naturalization civics test and research how people become U.S. citizens.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Value scale* (50 min): Make a 10-step value scale in graphite and in charcoal.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Movement patterns* (45 min): Learn the six patterns (squat, hinge, push, pull, carry, lunge) with a broomstick; film form.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Opportunity cost* (50 min): Analyze three of your own recent purchases for opportunity cost and compute what each would be worth saved at 5% for 10 years.

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Grendel attacks* (60 min): Read Beowulf lines 1–700 (Prologue through Beowulf's arrival). Collect 8 kennings and translate each into plain English.

- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Comparison shopping* (50 min): Compare unit prices for 10 grocery items across two stores or websites and calculate monthly savings.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Beowulf: the heroic code
- Objective: Identify features of epic poetry and the Anglo-Saxon heroic code
- Objective: Analyze Beowulf's character through his boasts and deeds
- Vocabulary: epic, kenning, caesura, comitatus, wergild
- Materials: Beowulf (Gummere translation, Project Gutenberg, or Heaney translation from library)
- Assessment: Reading quiz plus timed analytical paragraph scored for evidence.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Inverse functions and Unit 1 test
- Objective: Find and verify inverse functions
- Objective: Restrict domains to create invertible functions
- Vocabulary: inverse, one-to-one, horizontal-line test, restricted domain
- Materials: Desmos, patty paper
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Orbits and Kepler's laws
- Objective: Apply Kepler's laws and Newton's gravitation to orbits
- Objective: Predict orbital motion mathematically
- Vocabulary: ellipse, eccentricity, orbital period, semi-major axis, gravitation
- Materials: string, 2 pushpins, cardboard, PhET Gravity and Orbits
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Citizenship and civil society
- Objective: Describe obligations of citizenship
- Objective: Explain how civil society influences government
- Vocabulary: civic virtue, jury duty, naturalization, nonprofit, voluntary association
- Materials: USCIS naturalization civics test, jury summons sample (CA courts), Tocqueville on associations
- Assessment: Unit 2 test and position essay.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Value and form
- Objective: Render form with a full value range
- Vocabulary: value scale, chiaroscuro, core shadow, reflected light, cast shadow
- Materials: white eggs or objects, desk lamp, charcoal, toned paper
- Assessment: Chiaroscuro still-life drawing.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Resistance training safety and technique
- Objective: Perform foundational lifts with safe technique
- Vocabulary: hinge, squat, push, pull, spotting, neutral spine
- Materials: dumbbells or filled water jugs, resistance bands, broomstick
- Assessment: Form-check video rubric.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Smart spending & big purchases
- Objective: Use comparison shopping and opportunity cost
- Objective: Calculate the total cost of owning a car
- Vocabulary: opportunity cost, unit price, total cost of ownership, impulse buying, marketing
- Materials: grocery ads or store website, Kelley Blue Book or Edmunds (free), calculator
- Assessment: Unit 1 test and car-cost comparison project.
