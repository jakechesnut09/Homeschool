## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Defense day* (60 min): Present your research to a panel of at least two adults (family, co-op, or community mentor) and answer 5 minutes of questions.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Model* (60 min): Fit a regression or run a simulation-based comparison appropriate to your question.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Analyze* (60 min): Interpret patterns in the data and state whether evidence supports the hypothesis.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Choose a policy* (55 min): Choose a current California or federal economic policy (housing, minimum wage, gas tax, tariffs) and read an LAO or CBO analysis.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Final self-portrait* (50 min): Draw a new 45-minute self-portrait and compare it with Week 1.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Finalize* (45 min): Finalize the plan and do a workout from it.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *GPA and credits* (50 min): Calculate cumulative unweighted GPA and total credits (10 credits per year-long course) from your four-year record.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Evaluate a speaker* (60 min): Watch a TED talk or classmate presentation and write an evaluation of its reasoning, evidence, and rhetoric.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Interpret* (60 min): Interpret results in context and list at least three limitations.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Propose* (60 min): Propose a local solution with trade-offs evaluated against criteria.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Analyze* (55 min): Use supply-and-demand graphs to predict effects on prices, quantities, and different groups.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Growth analysis* (50 min): Write a growth analysis comparing early and late work with specific evidence.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Prepare* (45 min): Prepare a 5-minute presentation of your plan.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Final transcript* (50 min): Finalize the transcript with school name, CDS/affidavit info, courses by year, a–g letters, grades, GPA, and graduation date, signed by the administrator.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 173, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Panel feedback* (60 min): Review panel scoring sheets and write a response to the toughest question you received.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Write report* (60 min): Write a 4–6 page statistical report with graphs, methods, and conclusions.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Write* (60 min): Write the capstone report (introduction, methods, results, discussion, recommendation).
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Data* (55 min): Gather at least two data series from FRED or BLS supporting your analysis.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Evaluate with two lenses* (50 min): Evaluate your investigation with two different criteria sets (technical and conceptual).
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Present* (45 min): Present the plan to family and answer questions.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Diploma and graduation* (50 min): Learn that the private school (PSA) administrator issues the diploma, review graduation requirements, and compare with the CHSPE as an alternative.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 174, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Op-ed* (60 min): Turn your research into a 650-word op-ed for a general audience and submit it to a local paper or blog.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Build presentation* (60 min): Create a 7-minute presentation with your three most important visuals.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Stakeholder letter* (60 min): Write a letter to a city council member, school, or water district sharing your findings.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Write* (55 min): Write a 1,000-word policy analysis with a recommendation and acknowledgment of trade-offs.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Peer/mentor talk* (50 min): Discuss your year with an artist mentor or teacher.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Lead* (45 min): Lead family through one session from your plan.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Records packet* (50 min): Assemble a permanent records packet: transcript, course descriptions, reading lists, test scores, work samples, and copies of affidavits.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 175, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Reflection* (60 min): Write a reflection on the full research process from question to defense.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Present* (60 min): Present the capstone to family or co-op and answer questions.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Senior research defense
- Objective: Present and defend research to an audience
- Objective: Evaluate peers' or speakers' reasoning
- Vocabulary: defense, panel, rebuttal, credibility
- Materials: slides, invited audience (family, co-op, mentors)
- Assessment: Defense scored by panel rubric; op-ed draft.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Statistics capstone: analysis and presentation
- Objective: Model and analyze data
- Objective: Communicate conclusions and limitations
- Vocabulary: conclusion, limitation, inference
- Materials: slides, CODAP/Desmos
- Assessment: Capstone report and presentation.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Capstone: analysis and solution
- Objective: Analyze data and propose a local solution
- Vocabulary: conclusion, recommendation, stakeholder
- Materials: data, poster board or slides
- Assessment: Capstone report and stakeholder letter.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Economics capstone: policy analysis
- Objective: Apply economic reasoning to a real policy
- Objective: Present a cost-benefit analysis
- Vocabulary: cost-benefit analysis, unintended consequences, trade-off
- Materials: LAO reports, FRED, slides
- Assessment: Policy analysis paper and presentation.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Reflection on growth
- Objective: Analyze how your art and responses changed
- Vocabulary: growth, metacognition
- Materials: baseline self-portrait, mirror
- Assessment: Growth analysis.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Presenting the plan
- Objective: Present and defend the adult fitness plan
- Vocabulary: presentation
- Materials: slides or poster
- Assessment: Plan presentation.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Homeschool transcript & diploma
- Objective: Finalize an official homeschool transcript
- Objective: Understand the private-school diploma and graduation records
- Vocabulary: transcript, weighted GPA, credit, private school affidavit, diploma, CHSPE
- Materials: course list from Unit 8, transcript template, copy of filed Private School Affidavit
- Assessment: Signed final transcript and records packet.
