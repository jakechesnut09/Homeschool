## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Build slides* (60 min): Build 10–12 slides following the 'one idea per slide' rule with images, charts, and minimal text.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Pose a question* (60 min): Write a statistical question about your community using a public data set (data.ca.gov) or your own survey/experiment.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Field day 1* (60 min): Collect data at your first set of sites, recording date, time, conditions, and photos.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Exchange rates* (55 min): Convert a travel budget into three currencies and explain what determines exchange rates.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Install* (50 min): Install the exhibition, level and light the works, and place labels.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Mile* (45 min): Final timed mile.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Research presentation rehearsal
- Objective: Prepare a polished multimedia presentation
- Objective: Rehearse delivery and Q&A
- Vocabulary: delivery, eye contact, pacing, visual aid
- Materials: slides, timer, video recorder or phone
- Assessment: Rehearsal recording self-critique.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Statistics capstone: design and data collection
- Objective: Pose a statistical question
- Objective: Collect data responsibly
- Vocabulary: statistical question, data collection plan, variable
- Materials: CODAP, public data portal (e.g., data.ca.gov)
- Assessment: Capstone plan and data checkpoint.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Capstone: data collection
- Objective: Collect and organize field data
- Vocabulary: data table, metadata, uncertainty
- Materials: thermometer or infrared thermometer, test strips, camera
- Assessment: Organized data set and graphs.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Exchange rates and the global economy
- Objective: Explain exchange rates
- Objective: Analyze effects of a strong or weak dollar
- Vocabulary: exchange rate, appreciation, depreciation, balance of trade, foreign direct investment
- Materials: currency converter, FRED dollar index
- Assessment: Unit 13 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Senior exhibition
- Objective: Install and host an exhibition
- Objective: Engage the public in conversation
- Vocabulary: artist talk, reception, audience
- Materials: artwork, labels, guest book
- Assessment: Exhibition and artist talk.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Final fitness assessment
- Objective: Retest all components and compare
- Vocabulary: final assessment
- Materials: stopwatch, mat
- Assessment: Final fitness assessment.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Workplace skills & workers' rights
- Objective: Explain California workers' rights
- Objective: Practice professional communication
- Vocabulary: minimum wage, meal break, harassment, Labor Commissioner, professional email
- Materials: CA Labor Commissioner (DLSE) pages, email account
- Assessment: Unit 11 test.
