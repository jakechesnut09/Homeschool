## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Paired nonfiction* (60 min): Read a contemporary article on AI or gene editing. List three ways it echoes Victor's choices.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Vector basics* (60 min): Draw vectors, find magnitude and direction, and write them in component and i/j form.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Energy mix* (60 min): Analyze California's in-state electricity mix from the California Energy Commission and compare to 10 years ago.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Enduring tensions* (55 min): Read excerpts of King's 'Letter from Birmingham Jail' and identify tensions between law, order, liberty, and equality.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Composition* (50 min): Shoot 50 photos practicing rule of thirds, leading lines, and framing.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Mile retest* (45 min): Retest the mile run.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Scam spotting* (50 min): Analyze 10 real phishing texts, emails, and job-offer scams and label the red flags in each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Thesis & outline* (60 min): Write a nuanced thesis on 'Who is the real monster?' or 'What does Frankenstein warn about creators?' and outline with evidence.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Adding vectors* (60 min): Add and subtract vectors tip-to-tail, by parallelogram, and by components; scalar-multiply vectors.

- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Choose an issue* (55 min): Choose a local or state issue, research multiple perspectives, and identify the decision-makers.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Light* (50 min): Shoot the same subject in golden hour, midday, and window light.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Strength retest* (45 min): Retest push-ups and curl-ups.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Identity theft* (50 min): Complete the FDIC Money Smart identity-theft lesson and walk through the IdentityTheft.gov recovery plan steps.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Frankenstein literary argument
- Objective: Write a sustained literary argument
- Objective: Connect the novel to a modern issue
- Vocabulary: synthesis, warrant, nuance
- Materials: Frankenstein notes, one article on AI or bioethics (CommonLit or library)
- Assessment: Literary argument essay (major grade).

### Precalculus with Statistics: Vectors
- Objective: Represent and operate on vectors
- Objective: Solve velocity and force problems
- Vocabulary: vector, magnitude, component form, unit vector, resultant
- Materials: ruler, protractor, graph paper
- Assessment: Vectors quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Energy and mineral resource design solutions
- Objective: Evaluate competing energy solutions by cost-benefit
- Objective: Compute levelized cost and emissions
- Vocabulary: cost-benefit ratio, levelized cost, lithium, capacity factor, life-cycle assessment
- Materials: home utility bill, spreadsheet, small solar cell (optional)
- Assessment: Energy decision memo.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Enduring tensions and civic action project
- Objective: Analyze enduring tensions in constitutional democracy
- Objective: Take informed civic action
- Vocabulary: majority rule, minority rights, liberty vs. equality, advocacy
- Materials: Letter from Birmingham Jail (King), local issue research
- Assessment: Civic action project portfolio.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Photography and visual culture
- Objective: Compose strong photographs
- Objective: Analyze how images shape lives
- Vocabulary: rule of thirds, depth of field, exposure, visual culture
- Materials: phone camera, editing app
- Assessment: Photo essay.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Semester fitness reassessment
- Objective: Retest all fitness components
- Vocabulary: reassessment, progress
- Materials: stopwatch, mat, fitness log
- Assessment: Reassessment results.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Consumer protection, scams & identity theft
- Objective: Recognize common scams targeting young adults
- Objective: Respond to fraud and identity theft
- Vocabulary: phishing, identity theft, credit freeze, CFPB, FTC, DFPI
- Materials: FDIC Money Smart fraud module, IdentityTheft.gov
- Assessment: Scam-prevention guide and quiz.
