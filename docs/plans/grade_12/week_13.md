## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Blake's contraries* (60 min): Read 'The Lamb,' 'The Tyger,' and 'London.' Compare innocence and experience and annotate how 'London' critiques industrial society.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Restricted domains* (60 min): Explain why sine, cosine, and tangent must be restricted to be invertible; graph arcsin, arccos, arctan.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Reservoirs and fluxes* (60 min): Build a box-and-arrow carbon cycle model with reservoir sizes (GtC) and annual fluxes from NASA/NOAA data.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Dividing power* (55 min): Create a Venn diagram of federal, state, and concurrent powers and discuss the 9th and 10th Amendments.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Research* (50 min): Research three artists whose work connects to your inquiry and build a visual reference page for each.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *5K* (45 min): Run a timed 5K (local race or measured course) and record splits.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Auto insurance* (50 min): Explain liability, collision, comprehensive, and uninsured motorist coverage and look up California's minimum liability limits.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Wordsworth* (60 min): Read the preface definition of poetry as 'spontaneous overflow of powerful feelings' and lines from 'Tintern Abbey.' Write about a place that has changed for you.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Evaluate inverses* (60 min): Evaluate 15 inverse trig expressions exactly and with a calculator, including compositions like sin(arccos(3/5)).

- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Federal-state conflict* (55 min): Analyze a federal-state conflict (cannabis law, vehicle emissions waivers, immigration) and how courts resolved it.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Process study* (50 min): Do a process study imitating one artist's technique.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Recovery* (45 min): Walk 30 minutes and stretch.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Auto quote lab* (50 min): Get two practice online quotes (no purchase) changing deductible and car type, and record how premiums change.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Romantic poetry
- Objective: Identify Romantic ideals: nature, emotion, imagination, the individual
- Objective: Explicate a poem in writing
- Vocabulary: ode, sublime, apostrophe, lyric, explication
- Materials: Poems: Blake 'The Lamb' and 'The Tyger,' 'London'; Wordsworth 'Tintern Abbey' excerpt; Shelley 'Ozymandias'; Keats 'Ode on a Grecian Urn'
- Assessment: Timed poetry explication scored on an AP-style 6-point rubric.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Inverse trig functions and trig equations
- Objective: Evaluate inverse trig functions on restricted domains
- Objective: Solve trig equations in context
- Vocabulary: arcsin, arccos, arctan, principal value, general solution
- Materials: calculator, Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: The carbon cycle
- Objective: Build a quantitative carbon cycle model
- Objective: Measure carbon movement in a home experiment
- Vocabulary: carbon reservoir, flux, photosynthesis, ocean acidification, fossil fuel
- Materials: bromothymol blue (aquarium/pool kit) or red cabbage indicator, straw, aquatic plant (Elodea), baking soda, vinegar
- Assessment: Quantitative carbon cycle model.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Federalism and the California Constitution
- Objective: Distinguish delegated, reserved, and concurrent powers
- Objective: Describe California's state government
- Vocabulary: reserved powers, concurrent powers, Tenth Amendment, preemption, Legislature
- Materials: California Constitution (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov), LAO budget overview
- Assessment: Federalism quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Artist research and influences
- Objective: Research artists connected to the inquiry
- Vocabulary: influence, appropriation, homage
- Materials: Smithsonian Learning Lab, sketchbook
- Assessment: Artist research pages.

### Lifetime Fitness II: 5K time trial
- Objective: Complete a 5K and evaluate training
- Vocabulary: time trial, split
- Materials: measured course or local 5K event
- Assessment: 5K result and pacing analysis.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Auto, renters, disability & life insurance
- Objective: Compare auto coverage types and California minimums
- Objective: Decide which insurance a young adult needs
- Vocabulary: liability, collision, comprehensive, renters insurance, term life, disability insurance
- Materials: online auto insurance quote tool, CA Department of Insurance consumer pages
- Assessment: Unit 5 test and personal insurance plan.
