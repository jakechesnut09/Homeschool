## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *What makes a voice* (60 min): Read Brian Doyle's short essay 'Joyas Voladoras' (or any short personal essay from the library) and annotate three moments where a concrete detail carries emotion. Write a paragraph on the essay's tone.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *What is a function?* (60 min): Review function definition with mappings, tables, and the vertical-line test; classify 12 relations as functions or not and explain each.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *The Sun's layers* (60 min): Read about the Sun's structure and build a cross-section clay model labeling core, radiative zone, convection zone, photosphere, and corona.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Why government?* (55 min): Read Hobbes and Locke excerpts on the state of nature and write a paragraph comparing their views on why people form governments.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Studio setup* (50 min): Organize a home studio corner with good light and storage; photograph it for your process journal.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Mile run* (45 min): Warm up, then run/walk a timed mile; record time and heart rate.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Why personal finance* (50 min): Take the NGPF pre-assessment and write a money autobiography (one page) on how your family talked about money and one financial goal for age 25.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Life inventory* (60 min): Complete a 'life inventory' brainstorm: 10 places, 10 people, 10 challenges, 10 things you do without being asked. Star the three stories with the most specific memories.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Domain and range* (60 min): Find domain and range in interval notation for 15 functions including square-root, rational, and piecewise examples.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Fusion model* (60 min): Model the proton-proton chain with clay balls (protons/neutrons) showing 4 H → 1 He; calculate the mass lost and energy released with E = mc².
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Ancient roots* (55 min): Compare Athenian direct democracy and the Roman Republic; chart which features the Framers adopted or rejected.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Baseline drawing* (50 min): Draw a self-portrait from a mirror in 45 minutes as a baseline to compare at year's end.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Strength tests* (45 min): Do max 90° push-ups and curl-ups to cadence; record results.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Earnings and education* (50 min): Use BLS median-earnings-by-education data to graph weekly earnings and unemployment rates for six education levels, then write three conclusions.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Show, then reflect* (60 min): Study the narrative-to-reflection ratio by highlighting story sentences vs. insight sentences in a model essay. Freewrite 300 words on one starred story, ending with what it taught you.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Reading graphs* (60 min): Analyze 6 real-context graphs (e.g., temperature over a day) for intercepts, increasing/decreasing intervals, and relative extrema.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Observe sunspots* (60 min): Build a pinhole projector and safely observe the Sun's disk; compare with today's NASA SDO image and sketch sunspots.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *English roots* (55 min): Read excerpts of Magna Carta (1215) and the English Bill of Rights (1689); highlight limits on the monarch that reappear in the Constitution.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Skills inventory* (50 min): Fill two sketchbook pages with quick tests of each medium you own and rate your comfort level.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Flexibility* (45 min): Perform sit-and-reach and shoulder stretch tests; record results.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Career salary research* (50 min): Pick three careers on CaliforniaColleges.edu and record typical California salary, required training, and projected growth for each.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Grammar: sentence variety* (60 min): Mini-lesson on varying sentence openings (participial phrase, subordinate clause, prepositional phrase). Revise your freewrite so no two consecutive sentences begin the same way.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Piecewise functions* (60 min): Graph piecewise functions by hand, then model a cell-phone or tax-bracket plan as a piecewise function in Desmos.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Sun's life span* (60 min): Use the Sun's mass, luminosity, and fusion rate to estimate its main-sequence life (~10 billion years) and draw a model of its future (red giant, white dwarf).
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *The Declaration* (55 min): Annotate the Declaration's second paragraph, identifying Locke's ideas, and categorize the grievances by type.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Portfolio goals* (50 min): Review the AP 2-D Art and Design portfolio structure and write three goals for your senior portfolio.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Body measures* (45 min): Measure resting heart rate, waist circumference, and height/weight privately; learn limits of BMI.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Senior deadline calendar* (50 min): Mark real dates on a calendar: FAFSA/CADAA opens Oct 1, Cal State Apply and UC application windows (UC due Nov 30), and the March Cal Grant deadline; set reminders to submit applications this fall.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Read-aloud & check* (60 min): Read your freewrite aloud to a parent and get two 'I want to know more about…' questions. Submit the freewrite with a 3-sentence self-reflection on voice.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Quiz* (60 min): Take a quiz on domain, range, interval notation, and key features.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Check* (60 min): Write a CER explaining how energy from fusion in the core reaches Earth and take a short quiz.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Check* (55 min): Take a quiz and write a 300-word response: which thinker most influenced the founding, with evidence?
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Reflect* (50 min): Write a half-page reflection on what kind of artist you want to be by June.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Compare to standards* (45 min): Compare results with Healthy Fitness Zone standards and record in a fitness log.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Complete a 10-question quiz on income types and the education-earnings link and present your calendar to a parent.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Reading personal essays as models
- Objective: Analyze how essayists use specific detail and reflection
- Objective: Brainstorm a bank of personal stories
- Vocabulary: anecdote, reflection, voice, tone
- Materials: notebook, UC PIQ prompt list
- Assessment: Freewrite scored on specific detail and reflection; brainstorm inventory complete.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Functions, domain, and key features
- Objective: Determine domain and range from equations and graphs
- Objective: Describe key features of a graph in context
- Vocabulary: function, domain, range, interval notation, extrema
- Materials: Desmos, graph paper
- Assessment: Quiz on domain, range, and key features.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: The Sun: fusion and energy
- Objective: Model nuclear fusion in the Sun's core
- Objective: Explain how solar energy reaches Earth
- Vocabulary: nuclear fusion, photosphere, radiation, convection zone, solar wind
- Materials: clay or playdough in 2 colors, flashlight, solar-safe pinhole projector (cardboard, foil)
- Assessment: Fusion model and CER on solar energy transfer.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Roots of American government
- Objective: Trace Greek, Roman, English, and Enlightenment influences
- Objective: Analyze the Declaration's philosophy
- Vocabulary: natural rights, social contract, republic, rule of law, popular sovereignty
- Materials: Declaration of Independence (National Archives), Magna Carta excerpt, Locke, Second Treatise excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz and short response.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Setting up a studio practice
- Objective: Establish a sketchbook routine
- Objective: Assess current skills
- Vocabulary: visual journal, thumbnail, artist statement
- Materials: mixed-media sketchbook, graphite pencils 2H–6B, kneaded eraser, fineliner pens
- Assessment: Baseline self-portrait and goal sheet.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Baseline fitness testing
- Objective: Measure the five components of fitness
- Vocabulary: cardiorespiratory endurance, muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility, body composition
- Materials: stopwatch, measuring tape, yardstick, mat
- Assessment: Completed baseline fitness log.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Income, careers & senior-year money calendar
- Objective: Connect education and career choices to lifetime earnings
- Objective: Build a senior-year financial and application deadline calendar
- Vocabulary: income, salary, wage, human capital, opportunity cost
- Materials: NGPF account, calculator, planner or calendar app
- Assessment: Quiz on income concepts plus a completed senior deadline calendar.
