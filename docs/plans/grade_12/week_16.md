## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *The broken promise* (60 min): Read chapters 18–20. Analyze Victor's reasons for destroying the female creature and the creature's threat 'I shall be with you on your wedding-night.'
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Polar coordinates* (60 min): Plot points in polar form and convert 12 points between rectangular and polar coordinates.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Cookie mining* (60 min): 'Mine' chocolate chips from cookies with toothpicks under a budget and calculate profit, land damage, and reclamation cost.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *System types* (55 min): Compare federal, confederal, and unitary systems and presidential vs. parliamentary democracy (U.S. vs. UK).
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Copyright* (50 min): Learn copyright, fair use, and Creative Commons; analyze two appropriation-art court cases.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Food log* (45 min): Log your food for three days; do a 30-minute workout of choice.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Why retirement now* (50 min): Watch the Khan Academy 401(k) and IRA videos and chart tax treatment of traditional vs. Roth accounts.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 77, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Losses* (60 min): Read chapters 21–23. Chart every death in the novel and who Victor blames for each.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Polar graphs* (60 min): Graph roses, cardioids, and circles in Desmos and by hand; describe symmetry.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *California gold and oil* (60 min): Read about the Gold Rush and Kern County oil fields; explain how these resources shaped settlement.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Rise of tyranny* (55 min): Analyze conditions that gave rise to dictatorship in Germany (1933) and one Latin American case (e.g., Chile 1973).
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Digital tools* (50 min): Learn layers, masks, and brushes in Krita or Photopea through a guided exercise.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Fueling* (45 min): Learn pre- and post-workout fueling; plan a sample day of meals.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Employer match* (50 min): Calculate the free money from a 50% match up to 6% on a $50,000 salary over 10 years with growth.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 78, ~380 min)

- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Roth for teens* (50 min): Explain how a teen with earned income can open a custodial Roth IRA and project a $1,000 contribution to age 65.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Frankenstein: pursuit and ending
- Objective: Analyze doubling and the novel's resolution
- Objective: Trace two interacting themes
- Vocabulary: doppelgänger, retribution, hubris, ambiguity
- Materials: Frankenstein text
- Assessment: Theme map and reading quiz.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Polar coordinates and complex numbers in polar form
- Objective: Convert between rectangular and polar
- Objective: Multiply complex numbers in polar form
- Vocabulary: polar coordinates, modulus, argument, complex plane, De Moivre's Theorem
- Materials: polar graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Natural resources and civilization
- Objective: Explain how resource availability shapes human activity
- Objective: Map California's resources
- Vocabulary: renewable, nonrenewable, aquifer, ore, reserve
- Materials: chocolate chip cookies, toothpicks, graph paper, California water map
- Assessment: Regional resource explanation and quiz.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Comparative political systems
- Objective: Compare democratic and authoritarian systems
- Objective: Analyze the rise of tyrannies and new democracies
- Vocabulary: parliamentary, presidential, unitary, confederal, authoritarian, totalitarian
- Materials: Freedom House country reports, Mandela 'I Am Prepared to Die' speech excerpt
- Assessment: Country comparison and quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Digital art and ethics
- Objective: Create digital work
- Objective: Use images ethically
- Vocabulary: copyright, fair use, Creative Commons, raster, vector
- Materials: free software (Krita, GIMP, or Photopea), phone or tablet
- Assessment: Digital artwork and ethics statement.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Nutrition, hydration, sleep, recovery
- Objective: Evaluate habits that support training
- Vocabulary: macronutrients, hydration, sleep hygiene, overtraining
- Materials: food log app or notebook, MyPlate
- Assessment: Recovery habits evaluation.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Retirement accounts
- Objective: Compare 401(k), traditional IRA, Roth IRA, and CalSavers
- Objective: Calculate the value of an employer match
- Vocabulary: 401(k), Roth IRA, traditional IRA, employer match, vesting, CalSavers
- Materials: spreadsheet, Khan Academy retirement videos
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.
