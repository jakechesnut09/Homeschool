## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Claim and counterclaim* (60 min): Choose a prompt (e.g., 'Who is more responsible for Duncan's murder?') and draft a thesis plus a counterclaim you will rebut. Build an evidence outline.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Unwrapping the circle* (60 min): Use a Desmos animation to unwrap the unit circle into sine and cosine graphs; explain even/odd symmetry.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Four spheres* (60 min): Diagram interactions among geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, and biosphere for a wildfire event.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Origin of parties* (55 min): Read Washington's warning about factions in his Farewell Address and trace the development of the two-party system.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Abstraction research* (50 min): Compare works by Kandinsky, Helen Frankenthaler, and Mark Rothko; note how each conveys emotion without imagery.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Technique* (45 min): Swim: practice freestyle breathing and kicks for 30 minutes; or Bike: practice shifting and braking drills in a safe lot.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Reading a W-2* (50 min): Label each box on a sample W-2 and match boxes to the lines where they go on the 1040 and 540.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Draft body* (60 min): Draft the full argument essay (900–1,200 words) with embedded quotations and act/scene/line citations.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Amplitude & period* (60 min): Graph y = a sin(bx) and y = a cos(bx) for 8 cases by hand.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Albedo lab* (60 min): Melt ice cubes on black vs. white paper under a lamp and record melt time and temperature; relate to sea-ice feedback.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Nominations* (55 min): Explain how presidential primaries and conventions evolved and how California's top-two primary works.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Studies* (50 min): Make four small abstract studies based on a piece of music or an emotion.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Endurance* (45 min): Swim 20 continuous minutes or ride 45 minutes on a bike path.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Form 1040* (50 min): Complete a practice Form 1040 for a single filer with one W-2 and the standard deduction; compute the refund or amount owed.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 53, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Sonnets* (60 min): Read Sonnets 18, 73, and 130. Label the quatrains, volta, and couplet; explain how 130 parodies the Petrarchan blazon.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Phase shift & midline* (60 min): Graph fully transformed sinusoids y = a sin(b(x−h)) + k and write equations from graphs.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Volcanic feedback* (60 min): Analyze temperature data after the 1991 Mt. Pinatubo eruption and explain the cooling effect of aerosols.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Campaign ads* (55 min): Analyze three historical campaign ads for persuasive techniques and target audiences.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Final abstraction* (50 min): Begin a larger abstract painting developed from your best study.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Run* (45 min): Do a 30-minute run with 4 hill repeats.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *CA Form 540* (50 min): Complete a practice CA Form 540 using the same W-2 and check eligibility for the California Earned Income Tax Credit.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 54, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Revise essay* (60 min): Revise the argument essay for transitions between claims and to strengthen the rebuttal paragraph; edit for verb tense consistency (literary present).
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Modeling periodic data* (60 min): Model daylight hours for your California city over a year (data from timeanddate or NOAA) with a sinusoid.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Dams and rivers* (60 min): Analyze how a dam changes sediment, beaches, and ecosystems downstream (e.g., Matilija Dam) as a feedback chain.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Money in politics* (55 min): Use FEC.gov to compare fundraising for two candidates and analyze Citizens United v. FEC.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Finish* (50 min): Complete the painting and finish your studio landscape from Week 10.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Safety* (45 min): Learn California bike laws (helmet under 18, lights, lane use) or water-safety rescue skills; do a 30-minute session.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Filing options* (50 min): Compare IRS Free File, VITA sites, and the FTB's CalFile, and list documents to keep and how long to keep them.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 55, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Unit 3 check* (60 min): Submit final Macbeth essay and write a 14-line Shakespearean sonnet of your own in iambic pentameter.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Check* (60 min): Take a quiz on graphing and writing sinusoidal equations.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Macbeth argument essay and the sonnets
- Objective: Write a literary argument
- Objective: Analyze Shakespearean sonnet structure
- Vocabulary: counterclaim, volta, quatrain, couplet
- Materials: Macbeth notes, Sonnets 18, 29, 73, 116, 130
- Assessment: Argument essay (W.11-12.1 rubric) and original sonnet.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Graphs of sine and cosine
- Objective: Graph sinusoids with amplitude, period, phase shift, and midline
- Objective: Explain symmetry and periodicity
- Vocabulary: amplitude, period, phase shift, midline, sinusoid
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Sinusoid quiz and daylight model.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Feedbacks among Earth systems
- Objective: Analyze data showing feedbacks
- Objective: Distinguish positive and negative feedbacks
- Vocabulary: feedback, albedo, positive feedback, negative feedback, sphere interactions
- Materials: black and white paper, 2 thermometers, lamp, ice cubes
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Parties, campaigns, and money
- Objective: Analyze the role of political parties
- Objective: Evaluate campaign finance and polling
- Vocabulary: primary, caucus, PAC, super PAC, margin of error, Citizens United
- Materials: Washington's Farewell Address excerpt, FEC.gov data, campaign ads (Living Room Candidate archive)
- Assessment: Campaign ad analysis and quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Abstraction and expressive painting
- Objective: Create a nonrepresentational painting
- Vocabulary: abstraction, Abstract Expressionism, gesture, color field
- Materials: large paper or canvas, acrylics, palette knives
- Assessment: Abstract painting and unit critique.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Swimming or cycling skills
- Objective: Improve technique in a second endurance activity
- Vocabulary: freestyle, flip turn, cadence, gear ratio, bike safety
- Materials: public pool access or bicycle and helmet
- Assessment: Technique self-check.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Filing a return: W-2, 1040 & CA 540
- Objective: Complete a basic Form 1040 and CA 540 from a W-2
- Objective: Identify credits and free filing options
- Vocabulary: W-2, 1040, standard deduction, refund, CalEITC, CalFile
- Materials: sample W-2, IRS Form 1040 and CA Form 540 (practice copies), IRS Understanding Taxes / NGPF tax simulation
- Assessment: Accuracy of practice 1040 and 540 returns.
