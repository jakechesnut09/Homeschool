## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Abstract* (60 min): Write a 150-word abstract summarizing question, method, findings, and significance.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Populations and samples* (60 min): Distinguish parameters from statistics and practice simple random, stratified, and cluster sampling with a random number generator.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Choose a challenge* (60 min): Choose a global challenge (clean water access, urban heat, food waste) and research its scale with data.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Unemployment* (55 min): Calculate unemployment and labor-force participation rates from sample data; classify frictional, structural, and cyclical unemployment.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Criteria* (50 min): Write selection criteria for a college art portfolio and for a general exhibition.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Counts* (45 min): Learn to count music in 8s and follow a 30-minute dance fitness video.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Transfer basics* (50 min): Explain Associate Degree for Transfer (CSU guarantee) and UC Transfer Admission Guarantee eligibility.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Final paper and presentation prep
- Objective: Publish the final paper
- Objective: Begin converting research to a presentation
- Vocabulary: abstract, visual evidence, data display
- Materials: final draft, slide software
- Assessment: Final Senior Research Paper (major grade).

### Precalculus with Statistics: Sampling, surveys, and experiments
- Objective: Distinguish surveys, experiments, and observational studies
- Objective: Identify bias
- Vocabulary: population, sample, random assignment, bias, confounding
- Materials: news articles citing studies
- Assessment: Study design quiz and article critiques.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Defining a global challenge
- Objective: Specify criteria and constraints for a global challenge
- Objective: Break the problem into sub-problems
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraints, trade-off, decision matrix, sub-problem
- Materials: engineering notebook
- Assessment: Problem definition and criteria sheet.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Unemployment and inflation
- Objective: Calculate unemployment and inflation rates
- Objective: Explain their significance
- Vocabulary: unemployment rate, labor force, CPI, inflation, deflation, stagflation
- Materials: BLS Employment Situation report, BLS CPI inflation calculator
- Assessment: Personal CPI project and quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Selecting and sequencing
- Objective: Curate a portfolio for a specific audience
- Vocabulary: curation, selected works, sequence, SlideRoom
- Materials: digital archive, printed thumbnails
- Assessment: Curated portfolio selection.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Dance fitness choreography
- Objective: Choreograph a dance fitness routine
- Vocabulary: choreography, 8-count, Zumba, HIIT dance
- Materials: music, video recorder
- Assessment: Recorded routine.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Transfer paths: ADT, TAG & ASSIST
- Objective: Use ASSIST.org to find articulated courses
- Objective: Compare ADT and TAG transfer guarantees
- Vocabulary: ADT, TAG, ASSIST.org, IGETC, Cal-GETC, articulation
- Materials: ASSIST.org, UC Transfer Pathways pages, CSU transfer pages
- Assessment: Two-year transfer plan and cost comparison.
