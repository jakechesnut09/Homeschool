## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Chaucer's England* (60 min): Read about the three estates (clergy, nobility, commoners) and the Black Death. Listen to a recording of the opening 18 lines in Middle English and compare to a modern translation.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Long division* (60 min): Divide polynomials using long division for 8 problems including ones with missing terms.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Continental drift* (60 min): Fit cut-out continents together using fossil and rock-type evidence as Wegener did.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Lawmaking* (55 min): Flowchart how a bill becomes law, including committees, floor votes, conference, and veto override.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Gesture* (50 min): Do 30 one-minute gesture drawings from a family model or timed online poses.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Program design* (45 min): Write a 4-week full-body program (3 days/week) with exercises, sets, reps, and progression.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Emergency fund* (50 min): Calculate a 3-month emergency fund from your Unit 1 budget and plan how many months to reach it.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *The pilgrims I* (60 min): Read the General Prologue portraits of the Knight, Prioress, Monk, and Friar. Chart each character's ideal role vs. actual behavior.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Synthetic division & Remainder Theorem* (60 min): Use synthetic division and the Remainder Theorem to evaluate and test factors of 10 polynomials.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Magnetic striping* (60 min): Model seafloor spreading by pulling two paper strips through a slit and marking alternating magnetic polarity bands.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Track a bill* (55 min): Use congress.gov to track one current bill and record its sponsor, committee, and status.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Proportion* (50 min): Study figure proportions (head units) and draw three 10-minute poses.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Workout A* (45 min): Complete Workout A of your program and log it.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Simple vs. compound* (50 min): Compute simple and compound interest by hand for $1,000 at 4% over 5 years, then verify in a spreadsheet with =FV.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *The pilgrims II* (60 min): Read portraits of the Wife of Bath, Parson, Miller, Summoner, and Pardoner. Mark lines of verbal irony where Chaucer's narrator praises a flaw.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Rational Root Theorem* (60 min): List possible rational zeros and fully factor 6 cubics and quartics.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Seafloor age map* (60 min): Analyze the NOAA seafloor age map: measure distance vs. age from the Mid-Atlantic Ridge and calculate spreading rate in cm/yr.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Mock legislature* (55 min): Write a short bill, 'mark it up' with family as a committee, and simulate a floor vote with amendments.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Hands and feet* (50 min): Fill a page with studies of your own hands and feet from multiple angles.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Active recovery* (45 min): Do a 30-minute walk and mobility routine.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Rule of 72* (50 min): Use the Rule of 72 to estimate doubling times at 2%, 6%, and 10% and compare to spreadsheet results.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Language change* (60 min): Study how English changed from Old to Middle to Modern (vocabulary from French, loss of inflections). Translate four Middle English lines with a glossary.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: The Canterbury Tales: General Prologue
- Objective: Analyze Chaucer's characterization and estates satire
- Objective: Recognize Middle English features
- Vocabulary: frame narrative, estates satire, irony, pilgrimage, Middle English
- Materials: The Canterbury Tales (modern verse translation, Project Gutenberg or library)
- Assessment: Modern satirical portrait and vocabulary quiz.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Polynomial division and complex zeros
- Objective: Divide polynomials and apply the Remainder and Factor Theorems
- Objective: Find all real and complex zeros
- Vocabulary: synthetic division, Remainder Theorem, Rational Root Theorem, complex conjugate
- Materials: notebook
- Assessment: Zeros and division quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Seafloor spreading and the ages of rocks
- Objective: Analyze seafloor age and magnetic striping
- Objective: Explain why oceanic crust is younger than continental crust
- Vocabulary: seafloor spreading, paleomagnetism, mid-ocean ridge, subduction, craton
- Materials: paper strips, markers, NOAA seafloor age map
- Assessment: Evidence evaluation and quiz.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): How a bill becomes law; amending the Constitution
- Objective: Trace the lawmaking process
- Objective: Explain the amendment process
- Vocabulary: filibuster, cloture, conference committee, veto, ratification
- Materials: congress.gov bill tracker, Article V text
- Assessment: Mock bill and lawmaking quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: The figure
- Objective: Draw the figure with proportion and gesture
- Vocabulary: gesture, proportion, foreshortening, contour
- Materials: family model or online timed-pose site, charcoal, newsprint
- Assessment: Figure drawing set.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Designing a personal strength program
- Objective: Write a 4-week program using FITT and overload
- Vocabulary: FITT, split routine, full-body routine, periodization
- Materials: program template, weights or bands
- Assessment: Written 4-week program.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Savings, interest & digital payments
- Objective: Calculate simple and compound interest on savings
- Objective: Use digital payments safely
- Vocabulary: emergency fund, simple interest, compound interest, high-yield savings, P2P payment
- Materials: compound interest calculator (Investor.gov), spreadsheet
- Assessment: Savings plan with compound-interest projection and quiz.
