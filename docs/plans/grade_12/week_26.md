## Day 1 — Monday (school day 126, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Paraphrase practice* (60 min): Practice paraphrasing three passages without patchwriting; compare to originals and fix any borrowed phrasing.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Sample spaces* (60 min): List sample spaces for coin, die, and card experiments; describe events with unions, intersections, and complements using Venn diagrams.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Weather vs. climate* (60 min): Compare one week of local weather to 30-year normals and explain the difference between weather and climate.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Federal budget* (55 min): Analyze CBO charts of federal revenue and spending; identify the largest categories.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Printmaking history* (50 min): Study Japanese ukiyo-e and José Guadalupe Posada's prints; note how printmaking spreads ideas.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Topo maps* (45 min): Read contour lines on a USGS topo map and identify hills, valleys, and saddles.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Cal Grant types* (50 min): Chart Cal Grant A, B, and C: what each pays, at which schools, and eligibility requirements.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Reading, note-taking, and annotated bibliography
- Objective: Take accurate notes with paraphrase, summary, quotation
- Objective: Write an MLA annotated bibliography
- Vocabulary: paraphrase, summary, annotated bibliography, plagiarism
- Materials: Purdue OWL MLA guide, sources
- Assessment: Annotated bibliography scored for accuracy and evaluation quality.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Probability rules
- Objective: Use set language for events
- Objective: Apply addition and complement rules
- Vocabulary: sample space, event, union, intersection, complement, mutually exclusive
- Materials: dice, deck of cards, coins
- Assessment: Probability rules quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Evidence of climate change
- Objective: Analyze temperature, ice, and sea-level data
- Objective: Distinguish weather from climate
- Vocabulary: anomaly, proxy data, ice core, sea-level rise, baseline
- Materials: NASA GISS temperature data, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Data-interpretation quiz.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Taxes, spending, and the budget
- Objective: Describe federal and California revenue and spending
- Objective: Evaluate tax fairness
- Vocabulary: progressive tax, regressive tax, deficit, national debt, entitlement
- Materials: CBO budget infographics, California budget summary (ebudget.ca.gov)
- Assessment: Budget simulation and Unit 10 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Relief printmaking
- Objective: Carve and print a relief block
- Vocabulary: relief print, brayer, edition, registration
- Materials: soft-cut linoleum or foam sheets, carving tools, block ink, brayer, spoon
- Assessment: Print edition.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Map, compass, and orienteering
- Objective: Navigate with map and compass
- Vocabulary: bearing, declination, contour line, orienteering
- Materials: compass, topographic map (USGS), cones or markers
- Assessment: Navigation skills test.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Cal Grant & Middle Class Scholarship
- Objective: Compare Cal Grant A, B, and C
- Objective: Explain Middle Class Scholarship eligibility
- Vocabulary: Cal Grant A, Cal Grant B, Cal Grant C, Middle Class Scholarship, California College Promise Grant
- Materials: csac.ca.gov Cal Grant and MCS pages, calculator
- Assessment: State-aid estimate for three schools.
