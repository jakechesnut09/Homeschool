## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *The dragon* (60 min): Read the dragon episode and Beowulf's death. Annotate how the tone shifts from triumph to elegy.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *End behavior* (60 min): Determine end behavior from degree and leading coefficient for 12 polynomials; verify in Desmos.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Solar nebula* (60 min): Read about the solar nebula hypothesis and draw a storyboard of accretion from dust to planets.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Article I* (55 min): Read Article I and chart qualifications, terms, and unique powers of the House and Senate.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Perspective boxes* (50 min): Complete the Drawabox 250-box challenge warm-up (25 boxes today) in two-point perspective.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Find working loads* (45 min): Estimate 10-rep max for five lifts and record loads.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Why bank* (50 min): Complete the FDIC Money Smart module on bank accounts and list the costs of being unbanked (check-cashing and money-order fees).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Wiglaf and loyalty* (60 min): Analyze Wiglaf's speech to the cowardly thanes; write a paragraph on how the poem defines loyalty and what happens when the comitatus fails.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Zeros & multiplicity* (60 min): Investigate how multiplicity 1, 2, and 3 zeros look on a graph (cross, bounce, flatten).
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Crater lab* (60 min): Drop marbles from different heights into a pan of flour topped with cocoa and measure crater diameter vs. drop height; relate to the Moon's ancient surface.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Your representatives* (55 min): Look up your U.S. Representative and both U.S. Senators; summarize their committees and one recent vote each.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Interior* (50 min): Draw a room in your home in one- or two-point perspective from life.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Strength day* (45 min): Do 4×5 of squat and press at heavier load with 2-minute rests.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Compare institutions* (50 min): Compare fee schedules of a local bank and a credit union: minimum balance, monthly fees, overdraft fees, ATM network.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Another perspective* (60 min): Read the opening chapter of John Gardner's Grendel and compare how the monster is characterized. Complete a two-column comparison chart.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Sketching* (60 min): Sketch 8 polynomials from factored form, labeling intercepts, end behavior, and turning-point counts.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Meteorites* (60 min): Examine NASA images of chondrites and explain why meteorites date the solar system to 4.56 billion years.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Powers of Congress* (55 min): Sort Article I, Section 8 powers into categories (money, commerce, defense) and discuss the elastic clause.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Exterior* (50 min): Draw your street or a building from a dramatic three-point viewpoint.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Endurance day* (45 min): Do 3×15 of lunges, rows, and push-ups with 45-second rests.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Account management* (50 min): Practice a month of transactions in a sample check register, including a debit card purchase, direct deposit, and a pending charge.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Grammar: appositives* (60 min): Mini-lesson on appositive phrases (Beowulf's epithets are appositives). Write five sentences about the poem using appositives correctly punctuated.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Beowulf: fate, age, and legacy
- Objective: Analyze the theme of mortality and legacy
- Objective: Compare a modern retelling to the original
- Vocabulary: wyrd, elegy, alliteration, foil
- Materials: Beowulf text, excerpt of John Gardner's Grendel (library)
- Assessment: Explanatory essay scored on thesis, evidence, and organization.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Polynomial functions
- Objective: Analyze end behavior and multiplicity
- Objective: Sketch polynomials from factored form
- Vocabulary: degree, leading coefficient, multiplicity, end behavior, turning point
- Materials: Desmos, graph paper
- Assessment: Polynomial graphing quiz and box-volume model.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Early Earth and the solar system's formation
- Objective: Explain solar nebula formation
- Objective: Use meteorites and other planets as evidence for early Earth
- Vocabulary: solar nebula, accretion, differentiation, meteorite, radiometric dating
- Materials: flour, cocoa powder, baking pan, marbles or small rocks
- Assessment: Written account of early Earth (HS-ESS1-6).

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Congress: structure and powers
- Objective: Describe Article I powers and structure
- Objective: Identify your representatives
- Vocabulary: bicameral, enumerated powers, necessary and proper clause, apportionment, constituent
- Materials: Article I text, congress.gov, house.gov 'Find your representative'
- Assessment: Congress quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Perspective and space
- Objective: Draw interior and exterior spaces in perspective
- Vocabulary: one-point, two-point, three-point perspective, horizon line, vanishing point
- Materials: ruler, drawing paper, Drawabox lessons
- Assessment: Perspective drawing.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Program variables: sets, reps, load
- Objective: Choose reps and load for strength vs. endurance
- Vocabulary: repetition maximum, volume, rest interval, progressive overload
- Materials: weights or bands, training log
- Assessment: Training log with volume calculations.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Banks, credit unions & checking accounts
- Objective: Compare financial institutions and account features
- Objective: Explain FDIC and NCUA insurance
- Vocabulary: checking account, credit union, FDIC, overdraft, routing number, APY
- Materials: FDIC Money Smart for Young Adults, account fee schedules from two local institutions
- Assessment: Banking quiz and account recommendation memo.
