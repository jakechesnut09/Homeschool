## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Justine's trial* (60 min): Read chapters 6–8. Discuss Victor's silence during Justine's trial and what it shows about his sense of responsibility.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Area formula* (60 min): Derive A = ½ab sin C from base-height area and find areas of 6 triangles.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Earthquake risk* (60 min): Look up your address on Cal OES MyHazards; record earthquake, flood, fire, and tsunami risk.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Free press* (55 min): Analyze the Pentagon Papers case (New York Times v. U.S.) and explain prior restraint.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Finish piece 2* (50 min): Complete piece 2 and document it.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Breathing* (45 min): Practice box breathing and 4-7-8 breathing; do a 20-minute walk.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Diversification* (50 min): Simulate a single-stock vs. index portfolio through a market crash using historical returns and compare losses.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Mont Blanc* (60 min): Read chapters 9–10. Connect the sublime Alpine setting to Romantic poetry from Unit 4.

- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Fees matter* (50 min): Calculate the 40-year difference in a $100,000 balance invested at 0.05% vs. 1% annual fees.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Frankenstein: the creature's story
- Objective: Analyze the creature's narrative and education
- Objective: Evaluate nature vs. nurture
- Vocabulary: nature vs. nurture, sympathy, alienation, foil
- Materials: Frankenstein text, discussion question sheet
- Assessment: Seminar participation rubric and written reflection.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Law of Sines, Law of Cosines, and triangle area
- Objective: Solve oblique triangles
- Objective: Find triangle area using ½ab sin C
- Vocabulary: oblique triangle, ambiguous case, Law of Sines, Law of Cosines, Heron's formula
- Materials: tape measure, protractor or phone inclinometer app
- Assessment: Oblique triangle quiz and field report.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Natural hazards and human activity
- Objective: Explain how hazards have shaped settlement
- Objective: Prepare a household hazard plan
- Vocabulary: hazard, risk, liquefaction, wildland-urban interface, tsunami
- Materials: sand, water, pan, small weights, MyHazards (Cal OES) website
- Assessment: Family hazard plan and case-study summary.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Media and democracy
- Objective: Explain the role of a free press
- Objective: Evaluate media bias and misinformation
- Vocabulary: prior restraint, media bias, misinformation, lateral reading, agenda setting
- Materials: New York Times v. United States (Pentagon Papers) summary, two news sources on the same story
- Assessment: Media-literacy guide.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Sustained Investigation piece 3 and review
- Objective: Evaluate progress against criteria
- Vocabulary: rubric, synthesis, materials, processes, ideas
- Materials: chosen media, AP scoring guidelines
- Assessment: Self-scored progress review.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Yoga and stress management
- Objective: Use yoga and breathing to manage stress
- Vocabulary: mindfulness, box breathing, cortisol, vinyasa
- Materials: yoga mat, free yoga video
- Assessment: Mood log analysis.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Diversification, fees & investor behavior
- Objective: Build a diversified portfolio
- Objective: Calculate the long-term cost of fees and spot investment scams
- Vocabulary: diversification, asset allocation, expense ratio, dollar-cost averaging, Ponzi scheme
- Materials: NGPF stock market simulation, FINRA BrokerCheck
- Assessment: Asset allocation project and fraud evaluation.
