## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Act I* (60 min): Read Act I. List every pet name Torvald uses for Nora and what each suggests about their marriage.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Arithmetic sequences* (60 min): Write arithmetic sequences recursively and explicitly and find sums with the arithmetic series formula.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *HIPPO* (60 min): Learn the HIPPO threats (Habitat loss, Invasive species, Pollution, Population, Overharvesting) and sort 10 case examples.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Incentives* (55 min): Classify 10 incentives (bottle deposits, grades, fines, praise) as monetary or nonmonetary and predict behavior changes.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Scale studies* (50 min): Make one very small and one very large study of the same idea.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Positioning* (45 min): Learn doubles positioning and communication; drill transition-zone movement.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Systems compared* (50 min): Make a chart comparing the UC, CSU, California Community Colleges, and private colleges by mission, cost, size, and admission requirements.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: A Doll's House
- Objective: Analyze realist drama and social critique
- Objective: Track character development through dialogue
- Vocabulary: realism, dramatic irony, subtext, symbol
- Materials: Henrik Ibsen, A Doll's House (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Seminar rubric and character-development essay.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Sequences and series
- Objective: Write arithmetic and geometric sequences
- Objective: Find sums of finite and infinite geometric series
- Vocabulary: sequence, series, common ratio, sigma notation, convergence
- Materials: notebook
- Assessment: Sequences and series quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Human impacts on biodiversity
- Objective: Identify major threats (HIPPO)
- Objective: Analyze a California biodiversity case
- Vocabulary: habitat loss, invasive species, overharvesting, endemic, biodiversity hotspot
- Materials: CDFW species info, map of California hotspots
- Assessment: Quiz and regional-threat paragraph.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Incentives and economic systems
- Objective: Explain how incentives change behavior
- Objective: Compare market, command, and mixed economies
- Vocabulary: incentive, market economy, command economy, mixed economy, private property
- Materials: Adam Smith, Wealth of Nations excerpt (Project Gutenberg), Marx excerpt
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Investigation: piece 7
- Objective: Experiment with scale
- Vocabulary: scale, monumental, intimate
- Materials: large paper or canvas
- Assessment: Piece 7 and reflection.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Pickleball strategy and play
- Objective: Apply doubles strategy
- Vocabulary: stacking, transition zone, poach
- Materials: pickleball equipment
- Assessment: Game-play rubric.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): California postsecondary pathways
- Objective: Compare UC, CSU, CCC, private, apprenticeship, and military paths
- Objective: Build a balanced college list
- Vocabulary: a–g requirements, impacted major, apprenticeship, net price, reach/match/likely
- Materials: CaliforniaColleges.edu college search, College Scorecard, college net price calculators
- Assessment: Balanced college/pathway list with data.
