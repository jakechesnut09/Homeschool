## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Select work* (60 min): Choose five pieces for your senior portfolio: a PIQ, a literary analysis, a timed write, the research paper, and a creative piece.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Review: functions & trig* (60 min): Complete a mixed review of functions, logs, trig, vectors, and matrices.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Present* (60 min): Present your capstone to family, co-op, or a local group and answer questions.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Review micro* (55 min): Complete a review set on opportunity cost, supply and demand, and market structures.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Art careers* (50 min): Research five art careers (illustrator, art therapist, UX designer, conservator, teacher) and their pathways.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Favorite activity* (45 min): Spend 45 minutes on your favorite activity from the year.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Final decisions* (50 min): Complete enrollment or job acceptance steps (Statement of Intent to Register, housing, orientation) or a gap-year plan with milestones.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~380 min)

- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Review S2* (60 min): Review Units 6–9 with a concept map linking biodiversity, pollution, climate, and engineering.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Review macro* (55 min): Complete a review set on GDP, unemployment, inflation, fiscal and monetary policy.

- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Written exam* (45 min): Take the final written exam on fitness concepts and lifetime activities.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *First-year budget* (50 min): Update the Unit 7 life budget with actual aid, wages, and costs for your first year after graduation.
