## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Draft PIQs #3–4* (60 min): Draft the remaining two PIQ responses, choosing at least one that addresses academic interest or community contribution.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Function operations* (60 min): Find (f+g), (f−g), (fg), (f/g) and their domains for 8 pairs of functions.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *H-R diagram* (60 min): Plot 30 stars by temperature and luminosity on a Hertzsprung-Russell diagram and identify main sequence, giants, and white dwarfs.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *The First Amendment* (55 min): Read the First Amendment and classify 10 scenarios (school speech, protest permits, religious displays) as protected or not.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Artist video* (50 min): Watch an Art21 episode on a contemporary artist and take notes on their process.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *SMART goals* (45 min): Write three SMART goals based on baseline data; do a 30-minute activity of choice.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Needs vs. wants* (50 min): Sort 30 expense cards into needs and wants, then defend five borderline items (phone plan, car, streaming) in writing.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Cliché hunt* (60 min): Mini-lesson on diction: replace every cliché and vague abstraction ('passionate,' 'made me who I am') with a concrete image or action in all four drafts.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Composition* (60 min): Evaluate and simplify f(g(x)) and g(f(x)) and decompose 6 functions into compositions; model a sale price with stacked discounts.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Life cycles* (60 min): Create a branching flowchart of low-mass vs. high-mass star life cycles.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Amendments 2–8* (55 min): Create a chart of Amendments 2–8 with a modern example for each, focusing on rights of the accused.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Formal analysis* (50 min): Write a formal analysis of one artwork (elements, principles, meaning) in 300 words.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Barriers* (45 min): List personal barriers (time, cost, motivation) and two strategies each; complete a 25-minute jog.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Track your spending* (50 min): Begin a 7-day expense tracker logging every purchase, and watch the Khan Academy budgeting video, taking notes on the 50/30/20 rule.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Mechanics edit* (60 min): Edit all four PIQs for comma splices, run-ons, and apostrophe errors using a mechanics checklist; read each aloud backwards sentence by sentence to catch typos.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Average rate of change* (60 min): Compute average rates of change over intervals from tables, graphs, and equations; interpret units in context (miles per hour, dollars per year).
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Element factories* (60 min): Color-code a periodic table by origin (Big Bang, fusion in low-mass stars, massive stars, supernovae/neutron-star mergers).
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Incorporation* (55 min): Read about the 14th Amendment's due process clause and incorporation; explain how the Bill of Rights came to limit states.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Series thinking* (50 min): Study how an artist develops a series and sketch three variations on one of your own ideas.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Habit tracking* (45 min): Set up a weekly habit tracker; do a Darebee 30-minute full-body workout.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Budget spreadsheet* (50 min): Build a monthly budget spreadsheet with formulas for income, fixed and variable expenses, savings, and the balance for a $2,400 net income.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Mock interview* (60 min): Do a 10-minute mock admissions conversation with a parent, answering 'Tell me about yourself' and one question drawn from each PIQ.

- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Budget challenge* (50 min): Play the NGPF budget simulation (e.g., 'Budget Challenge' or SPENT) and record three decisions you would change.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: College essay revision and publishing
- Objective: Revise for clarity, specificity, and voice
- Objective: Finalize four PIQs and a Common App-style essay outline
- Vocabulary: revision, editing, diction, cliché
- Materials: PIQ drafts, Common App essay prompts (optional)
- Assessment: Final four PIQs scored with a college-essay rubric (specificity, insight, voice, conventions).

### Precalculus with Statistics: Combining and composing functions; average rate of change
- Objective: Perform operations and compositions on functions
- Objective: Compute and interpret average rate of change
- Vocabulary: composition, difference quotient, average rate of change, secant line
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Quiz on composition and rate of change.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Stellar life cycles and nucleosynthesis
- Objective: Trace the life cycles of stars of different masses
- Objective: Explain how stars produce elements
- Vocabulary: nebula, main sequence, supernova, neutron star, nucleosynthesis, H-R diagram
- Materials: star data table, colored pencils, periodic table
- Assessment: Element-origin infographic/podcast and quiz.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): The Bill of Rights
- Objective: Explain the protections in each amendment
- Objective: Apply rights to scenarios
- Vocabulary: due process, incorporation, establishment clause, free exercise, eminent domain
- Materials: Bill of Rights (National Archives), iCivics 'Do I Have a Right?'
- Assessment: Bill of Rights quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Looking at contemporary art
- Objective: Analyze how contemporary artists develop ideas
- Objective: Write a formal analysis
- Vocabulary: formal analysis, contemporary, concept, series
- Materials: Art21 videos (free online), sketchbook
- Assessment: Formal analysis and mini-work.

### Lifetime Fitness II: SMART goals and motivation
- Objective: Set SMART fitness goals
- Objective: Identify barriers and strategies
- Vocabulary: SMART goal, intrinsic motivation, barrier, habit
- Materials: fitness log, calendar
- Assessment: SMART goals and fitness contract.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Building a spending plan
- Objective: Distinguish needs, wants, and savings
- Objective: Create a balanced monthly budget
- Vocabulary: budget, fixed expense, variable expense, 50/30/20 rule, pay yourself first
- Materials: spreadsheet (Google Sheets or LibreOffice), receipts or bank statements (sample)
- Assessment: Graded budget spreadsheet with a balanced revision.
