## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Achebe's argument and postcolonial essay
- Objective: Evaluate a seminal critical argument
- Objective: Write a literary argument using a critical lens
- Vocabulary: critical lens, representation, Eurocentrism
- Materials: Achebe, 'An Image of Africa' (library or anthology), excerpt of Conrad's Heart of Darkness (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Postcolonial literary argument essay.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Two-way tables and categorical data
- Objective: Build and interpret two-way frequency tables
- Objective: Compute joint, marginal, and conditional relative frequencies
- Vocabulary: two-way table, marginal frequency, joint frequency, conditional relative frequency
- Materials: survey form, CODAP
- Assessment: Survey report.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Air quality
- Objective: Measure particulate pollution
- Objective: Explain smog formation in California
- Vocabulary: particulate matter (PM2.5), ozone, smog, inversion layer, AQI
- Materials: index cards, petroleum jelly, magnifying glass, AirNow app
- Assessment: Particulate lab report.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Entrepreneurship, financial markets, and location
- Objective: Explain profit as an incentive
- Objective: Describe financial markets
- Objective: Explain location decisions
- Vocabulary: entrepreneur, profit, stock, bond, venture capital, comparative location
- Materials: SEC Investor.gov, map of California agriculture and industry
- Assessment: Business plan and Unit 9 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Investigation: pieces 11–12
- Objective: Sustain productivity
- Vocabulary: studio habits, momentum
- Materials: chosen media
- Assessment: Pieces 11–12.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Golf course play and focus
- Objective: Play a short course with etiquette
- Objective: Use focus strategies
- Vocabulary: etiquette, pre-shot routine, handicap
- Materials: pitch-and-putt course or mini golf
- Assessment: Scorecard analysis.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Cost of attendance & the FAFSA
- Objective: Read a cost of attendance budget
- Objective: Complete the FAFSA with contributors and an FSA ID
- Vocabulary: cost of attendance, Student Aid Index, contributor, FSA ID, dependency status
- Materials: StudentAid.gov account, Federal Student Aid Estimator, family tax return (prior-prior year)
- Assessment: FAFSA completion/checklist and aid quiz.
