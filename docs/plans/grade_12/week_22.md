## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Empire: context and Things Fall Apart Part One
- Objective: Understand colonial context
- Objective: Analyze Okonkwo's character and Igbo society
- Vocabulary: colonialism, postcolonial, proverb, chi, patriarchy
- Materials: Chinua Achebe, Things Fall Apart (library), Kipling 'The White Man's Burden' (public domain)
- Assessment: Reading quiz and paired-text paragraph.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Describing distributions
- Objective: Display data with dot plots, histograms, and box plots
- Objective: Choose appropriate measures of center and spread
- Vocabulary: distribution, skew, IQR, standard deviation, outlier
- Materials: CODAP, stopwatch
- Assessment: Reaction-time comparison write-up and quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Designing solutions for biodiversity
- Objective: Design and evaluate a solution reducing human impact on biodiversity
- Vocabulary: wildlife corridor, restoration, native plants, pollinator
- Materials: native plant seeds or a pollinator plant, wood scraps for a bee hotel (optional)
- Assessment: Solution design portfolio and Unit 6 test.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Supply and market equilibrium
- Objective: Explain the law of supply
- Objective: Find equilibrium and analyze shifts
- Vocabulary: supply, equilibrium, surplus, shortage, price signal
- Materials: graph paper, index cards for market simulation
- Assessment: Equilibrium quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Mid-investigation critique
- Objective: Analyze differing interpretations of your work
- Vocabulary: interpretation, intent vs. reception
- Materials: all investigation pieces
- Assessment: Critique analysis.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Tennis play
- Objective: Play singles and doubles with strategy
- Vocabulary: approach shot, volley, lob, sportsmanship
- Materials: tennis equipment
- Assessment: Racket sports quiz and match.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): UC & CSU applications
- Objective: Complete UC and Cal State Apply application sections
- Objective: Report courses, activities, and awards accurately
- Vocabulary: UC Application, Cal State Apply, self-reported record, activities and awards, fee waiver
- Materials: UC Application (practice or live), Cal State Apply account, transcript draft
- Assessment: Application section audit checklist.
