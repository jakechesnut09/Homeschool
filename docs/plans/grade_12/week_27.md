## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Synthesis matrix* (60 min): Build a synthesis matrix mapping each subtopic to what each source says; identify a gap or debate.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Conditional probability* (60 min): Compute P(A|B) from two-way tables and Venn diagrams; explain in everyday language.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *How models work* (60 min): Read how climate models divide Earth into grid cells and test them by hindcasting the past.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Wages as prices* (55 min): Graph labor supply and demand for two occupations and explain wage differences.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Assemblage artists* (50 min): Study Betye Saar's assemblages and Louise Nevelson; note how found objects carry meaning.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Maintenance* (45 min): Learn the ABC check (air, brakes, chain) and fix a flat tire.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Finding scholarships* (50 min): Search local, community, employer, and online scholarships and list 10 with deadlines and requirements.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 132, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Thesis* (60 min): Draft three versions of a thesis and choose the most specific, arguable one.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Independence* (60 min): Test whether events are independent using P(A and B) = P(A)P(B) and P(A|B) = P(A) with your survey data.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Scenarios* (60 min): Compare low and high emission scenario projections for 2100 temperature and sea level.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Human capital* (55 min): Use BLS data to graph median earnings and unemployment by education level.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Maquette* (50 min): Build a small cardboard or clay maquette.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Route* (45 min): Plan a safe 10–15 mile route using bike lanes or trails.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Scam spotting* (50 min): Use FTC guidance to identify red flags of scholarship scams (fees, guaranteed winnings).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 133, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Primary research* (60 min): Conduct a short interview or survey (5+ responses) or analyze a primary document related to your question; record results.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Tree diagrams* (60 min): Use tree diagrams and the general multiplication rule for drawing cards without replacement.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Cal-Adapt* (60 min): Use Cal-Adapt to project extreme heat days and snowpack for your county under two scenarios.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Career research* (55 min): Research two careers in the Occupational Outlook Handbook and California EDD projections for your region.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Build* (50 min): Build the sculpture with an armature or assemblage.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Ride* (45 min): Complete the ride, logging time, distance, and heart rate.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Essay draft* (50 min): Draft a 500-word scholarship essay that can be adapted to multiple prompts.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 134, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Outline* (60 min): Write a full sentence outline with topic sentences, evidence assignments, and a counterargument section.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Medical testing* (60 min): Compute the probability of disease given a positive test (false positives) using a tree and a table; discuss why base rates matter.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *En-ROADS* (60 min): Use the En-ROADS climate simulator to test policies and record which changes most reduce warming.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Technology and work* (55 min): Analyze how automation and AI affect demand for different skills; read one current article.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Finish* (50 min): Finish surfaces and paint or patina.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Commute* (45 min): Plan a bike or walk route to a future job or campus and time it.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Applications* (50 min): Complete and submit (or fully prepare) two scholarship applications.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Thesis, outline, and primary research
- Objective: Craft a research thesis
- Objective: Organize evidence in a detailed outline
- Vocabulary: thesis, synthesis matrix, counterargument, interview
- Materials: notes, synthesis matrix template
- Assessment: Thesis and outline checkpoint.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Conditional probability and independence
- Objective: Compute conditional probabilities
- Objective: Test for independence
- Vocabulary: conditional probability, independent, tree diagram, multiplication rule
- Materials: two-way table from Week 24
- Assessment: Conditional probability quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Climate models and forecasts
- Objective: Interpret climate model projections
- Objective: Forecast regional impacts
- Vocabulary: climate model, emission scenario, projection, uncertainty
- Materials: Cal-Adapt website, En-ROADS simulator
- Assessment: Regional climate forecast.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Labor markets and wages
- Objective: Explain wage determination
- Objective: Analyze productivity and skills
- Vocabulary: labor supply, labor demand, human capital, productivity, derived demand
- Materials: BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, California EDD labor market data
- Assessment: Career research chart and quiz.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Sculpture and assemblage
- Objective: Translate an idea into three dimensions
- Vocabulary: armature, assemblage, in the round, found object
- Materials: cardboard, wire, air-dry clay, found objects, hot glue
- Assessment: Sculpture or assemblage.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Cycling for fitness and transport
- Objective: Ride safely and plan a route
- Vocabulary: ABC check, bike lane, cadence, route planning
- Materials: bicycle, helmet, repair kit
- Assessment: Ride log and route plan.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Scholarships
- Objective: Find legitimate scholarships
- Objective: Write a reusable scholarship essay
- Vocabulary: merit aid, need-based aid, outside scholarship, scholarship scam, award displacement
- Materials: scholarship search (Fastweb, Bold.org, local foundations), word processor
- Assessment: Two completed scholarship applications.
