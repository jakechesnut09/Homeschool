## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Origins* (60 min): Read Shelley's 1831 introduction about the ghost-story contest at Lake Geneva. Read Walton's letters 1–4 and note how Walton mirrors Victor.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Pythagorean identity* (60 min): Prove sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 from the unit circle and use it to find trig values given one ratio and the quadrant.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Oxygen revolution* (60 min): Read about cyanobacteria and the Great Oxidation Event; examine images of stromatolites and banded iron formations.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Local government* (55 min): Map who provides your water, schools, police, fire, roads, and parks (city, county, special district).
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Piece 1* (50 min): Continue and complete piece 1.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Dynamic warm-up* (45 min): Learn and perform a 10-minute dynamic warm-up, then jog 20 minutes.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Time is money* (50 min): Model two savers in a spreadsheet: one invests $200/month from 22–32, the other from 32–65, at 7%; graph the results.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: Frankenstein: frame and creation
- Objective: Analyze the frame narrative
- Objective: Connect the novel to Romantic and scientific contexts
- Vocabulary: epistolary, frame narrative, Gothic, galvanism, Promethean
- Materials: Mary Shelley, Frankenstein (1818 text, Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Reading quiz and analytical paragraph.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Trigonometric identities
- Objective: Prove the Pythagorean identity
- Objective: Verify identities and use sum/difference formulas
- Vocabulary: identity, Pythagorean identity, sum formula, double-angle
- Materials: identity reference sheet
- Assessment: Identities quiz.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Coevolution of Earth and life
- Objective: Argue from evidence that life and Earth systems coevolved
- Vocabulary: stromatolite, Great Oxidation Event, banded iron formation, coevolution, mass extinction
- Materials: steel wool, vinegar, jars, timeline from Week 7
- Assessment: Coevolution argument and Unit 4 test.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): Local and tribal government, budgets, and courts
- Objective: Identify local government responsibilities and revenue
- Objective: Describe California's court system
- Vocabulary: county board of supervisors, city council, special district, property tax, Proposition 13, tribal sovereignty
- Materials: your city/county budget, city council agenda, California Courts website
- Assessment: Meeting report and Unit 6 test.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Sustained Investigation pieces 1–2
- Objective: Produce and revise early investigation works
- Vocabulary: iteration, documentation
- Materials: chosen media
- Assessment: Pieces 1–2 progress.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Flexibility and mobility
- Objective: Distinguish static, dynamic, and PNF stretching
- Vocabulary: static stretch, dynamic stretch, PNF, range of motion
- Materials: yoga mat, strap or towel
- Assessment: Flexibility retest.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Compound growth & investment basics
- Objective: Model compound growth over decades
- Objective: Explain risk and return of stocks, bonds, and funds
- Vocabulary: stock, bond, mutual fund, index fund, ETF, risk-return tradeoff
- Materials: Investor.gov compound interest calculator, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Compound growth spreadsheet and asset-class quiz.
