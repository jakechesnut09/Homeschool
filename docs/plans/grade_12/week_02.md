## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Decoding the prompts* (60 min): Read all eight UC Personal Insight Questions and the UC tips page. Chart which of your starred stories fits each prompt and choose your four.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Parent library* (60 min): Sketch and memorize key points of 8 parent functions (linear, quadratic, cubic, absolute value, square root, cube root, reciprocal, exponential).
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *DIY spectroscope* (60 min): Build a spectroscope from a CD and cardboard tube; compare spectra of an incandescent bulb, LED, and fluorescent light and sketch the lines.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Articles to Constitution* (55 min): List weaknesses of the Articles of Confederation and match each to a fix in the Constitution.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Mind map* (50 min): Create a mind map around a single word (e.g., 'home') and branch to 30 visual ideas.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Calculate zones* (45 min): Calculate target heart rate zones with the Karvonen formula; do a 20-minute brisk walk checking pulse every 5 minutes.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Gross vs. net* (50 min): Calculate gross pay for hourly jobs at California minimum wage with overtime (over 8 hours/day or 40/week) for three sample schedules.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Draft PIQ #1* (60 min): Draft the first PIQ response (350-word limit) using a 'moment → action → result → meaning' structure.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Shifts* (60 min): Use Desmos sliders to discover the effects of f(x)+k and f(x+k); graph 10 shifted functions by hand.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Spectral fingerprints* (60 min): Match unknown spectra to element spectra and explain how astronomers determine star composition.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Federalist 10* (55 min): Read Federalist 10 and explain Madison's argument that a large republic controls the effects of faction.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *20 thumbnails* (50 min): Draw 20 two-inch thumbnails exploring compositions from your mind map.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Intervals* (45 min): Do jump-rope intervals (30 s on / 30 s off × 10) and record heart rate and RPE.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Reading a pay stub* (50 min): Label every line on an NGPF sample pay stub (federal and state withholding, Social Security, Medicare, CA SDI, YTD) and find net pay.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Draft PIQ #2* (60 min): Draft a second PIQ on a different prompt, making sure it reveals a different side of you than PIQ #1.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Stretches & reflections* (60 min): Explore k·f(x) and f(kx) with sliders; write equations for 8 transformed graphs.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Expanding balloon* (60 min): Draw dots on a balloon, inflate it in stages, and measure distances between dots to model Hubble's law; graph velocity vs. distance.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Federalist 51 and 78* (55 min): Read excerpts of Federalist 51 ('ambition must be made to counteract ambition') and 78 (independent judiciary); diagram checks and balances.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Image research* (50 min): Collect 10 reference images from Smithsonian Learning Lab or NGA open access that connect to your ideas.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Steady state* (45 min): Jog or cycle 25 minutes holding 60–70% heart rate reserve.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Form W-4* (50 min): Fill out a practice IRS Form W-4 for a single teen with one job and explain each step in writing.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 9, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Concision workshop* (60 min): Cut each draft by 15% by removing filler words (very, really, in order to), passive constructions, and repeated ideas. Track word counts before and after.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Even and odd* (60 min): Test 10 functions algebraically (f(−x)) and graphically for even/odd symmetry.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Evidence for the Big Bang* (60 min): Read about redshifted galaxies, the cosmic microwave background (COBE/Planck), and the H/He ratio; organize the three lines of evidence in a table.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Anti-Federalists and Tocqueville* (55 min): Read Brutus No. 1 and a Tocqueville excerpt on 'tyranny of the majority'; explain how the Bill of Rights answered these fears.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Collage brainstorm* (50 min): Make a quick magazine collage that combines unexpected images into a new idea.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Circuit* (45 min): Complete a 20-minute bodyweight circuit (squats, lunges, push-ups, planks) and track RPE.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Form DE 4 and benefits* (50 min): Complete a practice CA DE 4 and compare two job offers that include health insurance and 401(k) match to see total compensation.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 10, ~380 min)

- [ ] **English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research** — *Peer-style review* (60 min): Exchange drafts with a parent or mentor using a checklist (specific? answers the prompt? shows impact?). Write a revision plan for each response.
- [ ] **Precalculus with Statistics** — *Transformation challenge* (60 min): Complete a Desmos 'match the graph' set of 12 multi-step transformations and take a short check.
- [ ] **Earth & Space / Environmental Science** — *Explanation* (60 min): Write a 1-page scientific explanation of the Big Bang theory citing all three lines of evidence.
- [ ] **American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2)** — *Unit 1 test* (55 min): Take the Unit 1 test with a DBQ-style essay on how the Constitution limits government power.
- [ ] **Advanced Art & Portfolio** — *Share & check* (50 min): Present your three strongest ideas to a family member and record their feedback.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness II** — *Reflect* (45 min): Graph the week's heart-rate data and explain which workouts hit your zone.
- [ ] **Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Play NGPF 'Paycheck' scenarios and solve five net-pay problems on a short quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 12 – World & British Literature + Senior Research: UC Personal Insight Questions – drafting
- Objective: Match personal stories to UC PIQ prompts
- Objective: Draft two 350-word responses
- Vocabulary: prompt, concision, word limit, insight
- Materials: UC PIQ website, word processor
- Assessment: Two complete PIQ drafts under 350 words with a written revision plan.

### Precalculus with Statistics: Parent functions and transformations
- Objective: Apply vertical/horizontal shifts, stretches, and reflections
- Objective: Identify even and odd functions
- Vocabulary: parent function, translation, dilation, reflection, even/odd
- Materials: Desmos sliders
- Assessment: Transformation matching challenge.

### Earth & Space / Environmental Science: Light, spectra, and the Big Bang
- Objective: Interpret emission and absorption spectra
- Objective: Explain redshift and cosmic background evidence
- Vocabulary: spectrum, redshift, Doppler effect, cosmic microwave background, Hubble's law
- Materials: old CD or DVD, cardboard tube, balloon, marker
- Assessment: Big Bang explanation scored on evidence and reasoning.

### American Government & Civics (S1) / Economics (S2): The Constitution and the Federalist Papers
- Objective: Explain the Constitution's structural principles
- Objective: Analyze Federalist 10, 51, and 78
- Vocabulary: separation of powers, checks and balances, federalism, faction, Anti-Federalist
- Materials: U.S. Constitution (Interactive Constitution), Federalist Nos. 10, 51, 78, Brutus No. 1, Tocqueville, Democracy in America excerpt
- Assessment: Unit 1 test with document-based essay.

### Advanced Art & Portfolio: Idea generation strategies
- Objective: Use brainstorming methods to generate ideas
- Vocabulary: mind map, thumbnail sketch, visual research
- Materials: sketchbook, magazines, glue stick
- Assessment: Sketchbook idea pages.

### Lifetime Fitness II: Heart rate and intensity
- Objective: Calculate target heart rate zones
- Objective: Use RPE
- Vocabulary: target heart rate, Karvonen formula, RPE, maximum heart rate
- Materials: stopwatch or watch, jump rope
- Assessment: Heart rate data analysis.

### Personal Finance (S1) & College/Career Readiness (S2): Paychecks, pay stubs & the W-4
- Objective: Calculate gross and net pay
- Objective: Complete a W-4 and CA DE 4 correctly
- Vocabulary: gross pay, net pay, FICA, withholding, CA SDI, W-4
- Materials: sample pay stub (NGPF), IRS Form W-4 (blank), CA EDD Form DE 4 (blank)
- Assessment: Net-pay quiz and accuracy check of practice W-4/DE 4.
