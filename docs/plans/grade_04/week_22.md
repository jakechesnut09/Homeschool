## Day 1 — Monday (school day 106, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Choose a topic* (90 min): Choose a question (Should California ban single-use plastic bags?) and state an opinion.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Joining parts* (60 min): Add fractions with like denominators using fraction strips and write equations (10 problems).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Layer jar* (45 min): Make layers of sediment in a jar and observe settling.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Sutter's Mill* (45 min): Read about James Marshall's January 1848 discovery at Sutter's Mill in Coloma and John Sutter's reaction.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Improvise* (30 min): Improvise 4-beat answers on recorder using B, A and G to a parent's 4-beat questions.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *BEEF shooting* (30 min): Shoot 30 shots close to the hoop using BEEF cues.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Drug Facts label* (20 min): With an adult, find active ingredient, uses, warnings, directions and expiration date on a Drug Facts label.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 107, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Gather evidence* (90 min): Gather facts from two short articles and record them with sources.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Taking away* (60 min): Subtract like fractions using number lines including from 1 whole (1 - 3/8).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Superposition* (45 min): Order 6 rock layer drawings from oldest to youngest using the law of superposition and explain one.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *The news spreads* (45 min): Read the 1848 newspaper announcement excerpt (Sam Brannan's 'Gold! Gold from the American River!') and track how news spread worldwide.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Motive* (30 min): Create a 2-measure melodic motive and repeat it with changes.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Give-and-go* (30 min): Practice give-and-go: pass to a partner, cut toward the basket and receive the return pass.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Side effects* (20 min): Learn what side effects are and why you tell an adult if you feel unwell after medicine.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 108, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Modal auxiliaries* (90 min): Practice can, may, must, should in 12 sentences.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Word problems* (60 min): Solve 6 problems like 'Maya painted 3/6 of a fence and Leo 2/6; how much is painted?'
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Fossil casts* (45 min): Press shells and leaves into clay to make imprint fossils and fill one with plaster or glue to make a cast.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Mining methods* (45 min): Practice panning with sand and painted gravel and compare pans, rockers and sluices in a chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Song Maker* (30 min): Compose an 8-measure melody in Chrome Music Lab Song Maker.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Defense* (30 min): Practice defensive slide and keeping between the player and the basket.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Safe rules* (20 min): Write 5 safe medicine rules (only from a trusted adult, never share prescriptions, follow dose).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 109, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Outline* (90 min): Plan the essay: introduction, 3 reasons with evidence, conclusion.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Sums greater than 1* (60 min): Add like fractions with sums greater than 1 and rename as mixed numbers.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Grand Canyon & Painted Cave* (45 min): Analyze a photo of rock layers and fossils and infer past environments.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Primary source photos* (45 min): Analyze two daguerreotypes from the Calisphere set 'The Gold Rush Era: Everyday Life and People' using See-Think-Wonder.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Notate* (30 min): Notate the melody on staff paper with rhythm and pitch.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *2-on-1* (30 min): Play 2-on-1 with a passer, cutter and defender.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Scenario practice* (20 min): Act out what to do if a friend offers you a pill or you find medicine on the floor.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 110, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Debate the topic with a parent arguing the opposite side.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on adding and subtracting like fractions.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Explain changes in a landscape over time using layers.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a diary entry as a Forty-Niner arriving at the diggings, using five facts.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Play the 8-measure composition on recorder and record it for next week's revision.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Record 5 shots on video and self-assess each BEEF cue on a checklist.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Create a safe medicine checklist and explain it to a younger sibling or parent.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Planning an opinion essay
- Objective: Introduce an opinion and group reasons
- Objective: Use modal auxiliaries
- Vocabulary: claim, reason, modal, linking words
- Materials: writer's notebook, sources on topic
- Assessment: Essay outline.

### Math 4: Adding and subtracting fractions with like denominators
- Objective: Add and subtract fractions referring to the same whole
- Objective: Solve word problems
- Vocabulary: like denominators, sum, difference
- Materials: fraction strips, number lines
- Assessment: Fraction addition/subtraction quiz.

### Science 4: Rock layers and fossils
- Objective: Read rock layers
- Objective: Use fossils to infer past environments
- Vocabulary: sedimentary, layer, fossil, superposition
- Materials: jar, sand, soil, gravel, shells, clay
- Assessment: Rock layer explanation.

### California, A Changing State: Gold discovered!
- Objective: Describe the discovery of gold and the rush that followed
- Objective: Analyze primary sources
- Vocabulary: Forty-Niner, prospector, Sutter's Mill, placer mining
- Materials: Calisphere 'The Gold Rush Era: Everyday Life and People' set, pan, sand, small pebbles, gold-painted gravel
- Assessment: Forty-Niner diary entry.

### Art & Music 4: Composing rhythms and melodies
- Objective: Compose short melodies with standard notation
- Objective: Use Song Maker
- Vocabulary: compose, motive, phrase, notation
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Song Maker, staff paper, recorder
- Assessment: Composition draft.

### PE 4: Shooting and offense-defense
- Objective: Shoot using BEEF cues
- Objective: Create and reduce space
- Vocabulary: BEEF (balance, eyes, elbow, follow-through), give-and-go, guard
- Materials: basketball and lowered hoop or laundry basket
- Assessment: BEEF self-assessment.

### Health 4: Safe medicine use
- Objective: Read medicine labels for safe use
- Objective: Practice safe medicine rules
- Vocabulary: Drug Facts label, side effect, expiration date, dose
- Materials: OTC medicine box (empty or with adult), highlighter
- Assessment: Safe medicine checklist.
