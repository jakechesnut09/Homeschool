## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Compare quests* (90 min): Read chapter 1 of Alice's Adventures in Wonderland and compare its journey pattern with Oz.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Length* (60 min): Measure the hallway in meters and centimeters and make a conversion table for 1-10 meters.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Pinwheel turbine* (45 min): Build a pinwheel turbine that lifts a paper clip.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Who came?* (45 min): Map countries of origin of California immigrants 1850-1900 (China, Mexico, Ireland, Germany, Chile, Japan).
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Listen* (30 min): Listen to 'El Son de la Negra' and identify instruments: violin, trumpet, vihuela, guitarrón.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Hand volley* (30 min): Volley a beach ball upward with fingertips 20 times.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Family interview* (20 min): Interview a parent about family rules on alcohol, tobacco and medicine and why they exist.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 142, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Plan* (90 min): Plan a new adventure for Dorothy in a California setting.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Mass and weight* (60 min): Weigh 5 kitchen items in grams and ounces; build kg-g and lb-oz tables.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Water wheel* (45 min): Build a water wheel from a plastic plate and cups.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Diverse state* (45 min): Analyze images from the Calisphere set 'Diversity in the Changing State' and list communities that formed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Timbre* (30 min): Describe the timbre of each instrument with adjectives.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Forearm pass* (30 min): Practice the bump with a platform of forearms.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Influences* (20 min): List positive and negative influences from family, friends and media.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 143, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Draft* (90 min): Draft the episode with dialogue and description.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Liquid volume* (60 min): Fill a liter bottle with 250 ml cups and record a liter-milliliter table.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Solar* (45 min): Compare the temperature of water in a black container in sun vs shade every 10 minutes for 40 minutes.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Chinese Exclusion Act* (45 min): Read about the 1882 Chinese Exclusion Act and analyze a document from the Calisphere set 'Chinese Exclusion Act'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Context* (30 min): Read about mariachi at celebrations in California communities.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Partner rally* (30 min): Rally a beach ball with a partner using sets and bumps, counting hits.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Trusted adults* (20 min): Review trusted adults you can talk to about alcohol or drug concerns.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 144, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Revise & edit* (90 min): Revise the episode with the narrative checklist (hook, dialogue, sensory details, ending) and edit for quotation marks.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Time* (60 min): Convert hours to minutes and minutes to seconds and make a table for a soccer schedule.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Compare* (45 min): Compare wind, water and solar models in a chart by output, reliability and needs.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Accords* (45 min): Read about people and groups who worked together or defended immigrants' rights.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Rhythm* (30 min): Clap the 3/4 + 6/8 alternating rhythm (sesquialtera).
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Net game* (30 min): Play a cooperative over-the-net game, counting consecutive hits.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Family agreement* (20 min): Write a family agreement about staying alcohol- and drug-free.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 145, ~320 min)

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Retelling Oz: narrative and comparison
- Objective: Write a new narrative episode
- Objective: Compare journey stories across cultures
- Vocabulary: episode, quest, journey
- Materials: novel, Alice's Adventures in Wonderland chapter 1 (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: New-episode narrative.

### Math 4: Measurement units and conversions
- Objective: Know relative sizes of metric and customary units
- Objective: Convert larger to smaller units with tables
- Vocabulary: kilometer, gram, liter, ounce, conversion table
- Materials: meter stick, kitchen scale, measuring cups, clock
- Assessment: Conversion quiz with tables.

### Science 4: Renewable energy investigations
- Objective: Investigate solar, wind and water energy
- Vocabulary: solar, wind turbine, hydroelectric
- Materials: paper pinwheel, fan, cups, water
- Assessment: Energy comparison chart and explanation.

### California, A Changing State: Immigration and the Chinese Exclusion Act
- Objective: Discuss immigration from 1850 to 1900
- Objective: Analyze conflicts and accords among groups
- Vocabulary: immigrant, exclusion, discrimination, Angel Island
- Materials: Calisphere sets 'Chinese Exclusion Act' and 'Diversity in the Changing State', world map
- Assessment: Evidence-based response.

### Art & Music 4: Mariachi and Mexican music in California
- Objective: Explain how music connects to culture
- Objective: Identify instruments by timbre
- Vocabulary: mariachi, timbre, vihuela, guitarrón, son
- Materials: recordings of mariachi music, video of a mariachi performance
- Assessment: Listening response.

### PE 4: Volleying
- Objective: Volley a ball upward with hands and forearms
- Objective: Rally with a partner
- Vocabulary: volley, set, bump, forearm pass
- Materials: beach ball or volleyball trainer, rope as net
- Assessment: Rally count.

### Health 4: Family rules and influences about alcohol
- Objective: Describe family and peer influences
- Objective: Communicate family expectations
- Vocabulary: family rules, expectations, influence, peer
- Materials: family interview sheet
- Assessment: Unit 8 test and family agreement.
