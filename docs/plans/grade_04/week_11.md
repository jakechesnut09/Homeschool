## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Text features* (90 min): Interpret a diagram, a map and a chart in a nonfiction book and explain what each adds.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Area model 2x2* (60 min): Draw and shade area models for 23 x 14 and 36 x 25 showing four partial products.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Morse code* (45 min): Send messages with a flashlight using Morse code.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Why colonize?* (45 min): Read about Spain's reasons for colonizing Alta California.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Techniques* (30 min): Practice flat wash, gradated wash and wet-on-wet skies.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Skills stations* (30 min): Rotate through dribbling, passing and shooting stations.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Ad detectives* (20 min): Watch 3 food commercials and record techniques used (cartoons, prizes, celebrities, catchy songs).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Choose a topic* (90 min): Choose a California animal for an explanatory report and generate research questions.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Partial products list* (60 min): Solve 8 problems with the partial products method, labeling each product (20 x 10, 20 x 4...).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Binary pictures* (45 min): Encode a pixel picture in 1s and 0s and have a partner decode it.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Portolá and Serra* (45 min): Trace the 1769 Portolá-Serra expedition route to San Diego and Monterey.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Paint* (30 min): Paint the week 10 landscape in watercolor, painting the sky first and the foreground last.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Conflict resolution* (30 min): Practice resolving a disputed call with rock-paper-scissors.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Cereal box analysis* (20 min): Compare the front of a sugary cereal box with its Nutrition Facts label and note what the front leaves out.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 53, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Note-taking* (90 min): Take paraphrased notes on note cards from two sources, listing each source.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Estimate first* (60 min): Round factors to estimate 6 products, then compute and compare.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Drum signals* (45 min): Invent a drum-beat code for 10 words and send 3 messages to a family member in another room.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Crespí's diary* (45 min): Read an excerpt from Juan Crespí's expedition diary and list his observations of the land and people.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Ansel Adams* (30 min): Study Ansel Adams' 'Monolith, the Face of Half Dome' and discuss value and contrast.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Game day* (30 min): Play a family soccer game with kick-ins, no hands and fair-play rules for 15 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Influences web* (20 min): Make a web of influences on your food choices: family, culture, friends, media, cost, taste.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 54, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Progressive tenses* (90 min): Practice past, present and future progressive verbs in 12 sentences about the animal.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Word problems* (60 min): Solve 5 problems such as rows of seats in a theater or cases of oranges from a Central Valley farm.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Compare codes* (45 min): Compare which code is fastest and most accurate and why.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Perspectives* (45 min): Discuss how the Kumeyaay might have viewed the newcomers.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Photograph* (30 min): Take 5 black-and-white photos of your neighborhood using Adams' ideas of contrast.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Refereeing* (30 min): Referee a short game, making calls for out-of-bounds and handballs and explaining each.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Create an ad* (20 min): Design a cereal box or poster that advertises a healthy food using the same techniques honestly.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 55, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Paraphrase a 2-minute video segment aloud and sort notes into categories.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on 2-digit by 2-digit multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph about how patterns transfer information.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write about the goals of soldiers, missionaries and Native people.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Compare your painting and photos in a short written response.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Unit assessment* (30 min): Complete a foot skills test: cone-weave dribble, 10 gate passes and 5 traps scored by a parent.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a paragraph on how ads try to influence kids and how to think critically about them.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Visual information and note-taking
- Objective: Interpret charts, diagrams and timelines
- Objective: Take notes and categorize information
- Vocabulary: diagram, caption, timeline, paraphrase
- Materials: nonfiction books on a California animal, note cards
- Assessment: Notes and source list check.

### Math 4: Two-digit by two-digit multiplication
- Objective: Multiply 2-digit by 2-digit numbers with area models and partial products
- Objective: Estimate products
- Vocabulary: partial products, estimate, array
- Materials: grid paper, colored pencils
- Assessment: Two-digit multiplication quiz.

### Science 4: Patterns that carry information
- Objective: Use patterns to send messages
- Objective: Compare codes
- Vocabulary: code, pattern, signal, Morse code, binary
- Materials: flashlight, paper, Morse code chart
- Assessment: Code comparison paragraph.

### California, A Changing State: Spanish colonization begins: the Sacred Expedition
- Objective: Describe the 1769 expedition
- Objective: Explain roles of soldiers, missionaries and Indians
- Vocabulary: colonization, expedition, Franciscan, presidio
- Materials: map, Calisphere 'Hispanic Americans: Spanish Colonization' set
- Assessment: Perspectives writing.

### Art & Music 4: Watercolor landscape and Ansel Adams photography
- Objective: Use watercolor techniques
- Objective: Compare painting and photography of landscapes
- Vocabulary: wet-on-wet, wash, photograph, contrast
- Materials: watercolors, watercolor paper, Ansel Adams photos (online)
- Assessment: Watercolor and photo comparison.

### PE 4: Soccer skills assessment
- Objective: Demonstrate foot skills
- Objective: Apply rules and fair play
- Vocabulary: fair play, rules, referee
- Materials: soccer ball, cones
- Assessment: Foot skills test.

### Health 4: Advertising and food choices
- Objective: Analyze how food ads target kids
- Objective: Describe influences on food choices
- Vocabulary: advertising, persuasion, slogan, mascot, influence
- Materials: cereal boxes, recorded TV or online food ads
- Assessment: Healthy ad design and paragraph.
