## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Paul Revere's Ride* (90 min): Read Longfellow's poem and summarize the events stanza by stanza.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Benchmark 1/2* (60 min): Sort 12 fractions as less than, equal to, or greater than 1/2 and explain reasoning.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Ruler drop* (45 min): Measure reaction time with a ruler drop test 5 times.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Why come?* (45 min): List push and pull factors that brought emigrants in the 1840s (land, trade, adventure) and rank them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Look* (30 min): Study a classical Chinese bamboo painting and discuss balance and empty space.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Plan* (30 min): Plan a routine with a start shape, 2 balances, 1 roll, 1 jump and a finish, using high, medium and low levels.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Barriers* (20 min): List barriers to being active (weather, time, screens) and a solution for each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 92, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Compare to audio* (90 min): Listen to a recorded reading and note how the reader's pacing changes the effect.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Common numerators* (60 min): Compare 3/5 and 3/8 by thinking about piece size; solve 8 similar comparisons.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Smell and taste* (45 min): Taste apple and potato pieces blindfolded with nose plugged, then unplugged, and record how smell affects taste.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *John Bidwell* (45 min): Read about the 1841 Bidwell-Bartleson party, the first overland wagon group, and trace its route over the Sierra.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Strokes* (30 min): Practice the four basic strokes: bamboo stalk, leaf, dot and line.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Transitions* (30 min): Practice smooth transitions between each element.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Set goal* (20 min): Write a 3-week SMART activity goal (e.g., 30 minutes of outdoor play after school 5 days a week).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 93, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Fact vs poem* (90 min): Read a short factual account and list what Longfellow changed.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Common denominators* (60 min): Rename to compare 5/6 and 7/8; record with >, =, < (10 problems).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Touch test* (45 min): Identify 8 fabrics and textures by touch while blindfolded and record which were hardest.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *James Beckwourth* (45 min): Read about James Beckwourth, an African American mountain man who found Beckwourth Pass, and mark it on the map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Bamboo painting* (30 min): Paint a bamboo stalk with leaves using ink washes of different values.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse the routine 5 times, then record a final run-through on video.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Activity menu* (20 min): Create an 'activity menu' of indoor and outdoor choices for rainy and sunny days.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 94, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Rehearse* (90 min): Rehearse 3 stanzas with expression and record them.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Ordering* (60 min): Order sets of four fractions on a number line and justify in writing.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Animal senses* (45 min): Research owls, bats and sharks and their special senses.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *The Donner Party* (45 min): Read an age-appropriate account of the Donner Party and a letter excerpt by survivor Virginia Reed; list decisions that led to disaster.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Context* (30 min): Read about Chinese American artists in California (e.g., Tyrus Wong) and their influence.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Peer feedback* (30 min): Watch the recording with a parent and revise one transition.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Track* (20 min): Begin tracking the goal on a calendar with stickers and note how you feel each day.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 95, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Perform* (90 min): Perform with a background sound effect or slides; self-evaluate fluency.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on comparing fractions with explanations.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Unit 5 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 5 test and draw a sense-brain-response model.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Compare the sea route around Cape Horn, the Panama route and the overland trail in a chart (time, cost, dangers).
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Finish a painting with a seal-style signature and compare it to your earlier work.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Unit assessment* (30 min): Perform the final routine; scored on control, levels, transitions and safety.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Check progress after 5 days and adjust the plan if needed.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Narrative poetry and performance
- Objective: Read a narrative poem fluently
- Objective: Connect a poem to an oral presentation
- Vocabulary: narrative poem, meter, expression
- Materials: 'Paul Revere's Ride' (Longfellow), audio recording of the poem
- Assessment: Recorded performance with fluency self-evaluation.

### Math 4: Comparing fractions
- Objective: Compare fractions using benchmarks, common numerators and common denominators
- Objective: Justify with models
- Vocabulary: benchmark, compare, common numerator
- Materials: fraction strips, number lines
- Assessment: Comparing fractions quiz.

### Science 4: Senses and the brain
- Objective: Model how senses send information to the brain
- Objective: Investigate reaction time
- Vocabulary: senses, nerve, brain, reaction time, stimulus
- Materials: ruler, blindfold, spices, fabric samples
- Assessment: Sense model and Unit 5 test.

### California, A Changing State: Overland trails to California
- Objective: Compare how and why people traveled to California
- Objective: Trace major routes
- Vocabulary: emigrant, overland trail, wagon train, pass
- Materials: U.S. map, trail diaries excerpt, Calisphere 'Transportation in California History' set
- Assessment: Route comparison chart.

### Art & Music 4: Chinese brush painting
- Objective: Use brush techniques from Chinese painting
- Objective: Connect art to Chinese American heritage in California
- Vocabulary: brushstroke, ink wash, bamboo, calligraphy
- Materials: watercolor brush, black watercolor or ink, rice or newsprint paper
- Assessment: Brush painting.

### PE 4: Gymnastics routine
- Objective: Design a movement sequence with balance, rolls and changes in level
- Objective: Perform with control
- Vocabulary: sequence, level, pathway, transition, finish
- Materials: mat, routine planning sheet
- Assessment: Gymnastics routine (rubric).

### Health 4: Physical activity goal
- Objective: Set and track a physical-activity goal
- Objective: Overcome barriers to activity
- Vocabulary: goal, barrier, progress, motivation
- Materials: goal tracker, calendar
- Assessment: Goal tracker check-in.
