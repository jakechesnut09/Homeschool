## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Drama structure* (90 min): Read a reader's theater script and label cast list, scenes and stage directions.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Same whole?* (60 min): Compare 1/2 of a small pizza and 1/4 of a large one with paper circles and write why the whole matters.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Shake test* (45 min): Shake sugar cubes in a jar for 1, 2 and 3 minutes and observe rounding.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Tensions* (45 min): Read about tensions between Mexican officials and American settlers in the 1840s.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Mural study* (30 min): Look at photos of murals in Chicano Park (San Diego) or Balmy Alley (San Francisco) and discuss their messages.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Stationary* (30 min): Practice pound dribbles, low dribbles and figure-8s around the legs with each hand.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Family challenge* (20 min): Design a one-week family activity challenge with daily tasks (walk after dinner, dance party).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 97, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Prose vs drama* (90 min): Compare the script to the prose version and list structural differences.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Critique reasoning* (60 min): Analyze 4 student claims about fraction size and write agree/disagree arguments.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Chalk and vinegar* (45 min): Place chalk pieces in vinegar and in water overnight and compare them to model chemical weathering.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *John C. Frémont* (45 min): Read about Frémont's expeditions and his role in 1846.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Set goals* (30 min): With family members, choose a theme about your community and set goals for the mural.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Eyes up* (30 min): Dribble while calling out the number of fingers a parent holds up.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Lead activity* (20 min): Lead the family in a 15-minute active game you choose.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 98, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Write a scene* (90 min): Convert a scene from Island of the Blue Dolphins into a short script.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Fraction word problems* (60 min): Solve 6 comparison word problems about who ran farther or ate more.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Freeze-thaw* (45 min): Freeze a water-filled straw sealed with clay and observe expansion.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *The revolt* (45 min): Read about the June 1846 capture of Sonoma and the arrest of Mariano Vallejo; sequence the events.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Sketch* (30 min): Each person sketches ideas; combine the best into one plan.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Moving dribble* (30 min): Dribble down and back on a line with right, left, then alternating hands.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Advocacy poster* (20 min): Make a poster encouraging 60 minutes of play a day for kids in your neighborhood.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 99, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Rehearse* (90 min): Rehearse the script with family members using expression and blocking.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 5 review* (60 min): Review game covering equivalence and comparison.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Plants weather rock* (45 min): Observe roots breaking sidewalks on a neighborhood walk.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *The flag* (45 min): Analyze a photo of the original Bear Flag found on Calisphere: what symbols do you see and what do they mean?
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Paint/draw* (30 min): Create the mural on a large sheet of paper together.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Crossover* (30 min): Learn the crossover dribble, switching hands low in front of the body around 6 cones.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Goal check* (20 min): Review your 3-week goal tracker and reflect on progress.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 100, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Perform & assess* (90 min): Perform the play; take the Unit 5 test on poetry and drama structure.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 5 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 5 test on equivalent fractions and comparing fractions with explanations.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz sorting 10 examples into physical and chemical weathering.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Perspectives* (45 min): Write two short journal entries about the revolt: one as a settler and one as Vallejo.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Write an artist statement explaining the mural's message and display it.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Complete a timed cone dribble course using both hands.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Unit assessment* (20 min): Take the Unit 5 test on the benefits, types and recommended amount of physical activity and on goal setting.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Drama: reading and performing a play
- Objective: Identify drama structure (cast, scenes, stage directions)
- Objective: Compare drama and prose
- Vocabulary: script, cast, scene, stage directions, dialogue
- Materials: reader's theater script of an Aesop fable or 'The Emperor's New Clothes', props
- Assessment: Performance and Unit 5 test.

### Math 4: Fraction reasoning
- Objective: Recognize comparisons are valid only for the same whole
- Objective: Apply equivalence to problems
- Vocabulary: whole, reasoning, justify
- Materials: paper circles of different sizes
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Science 4: Weathering
- Objective: Observe physical and chemical weathering
- Objective: Measure weathering effects
- Vocabulary: weathering, physical, chemical, sediment
- Materials: sugar cubes, jar, chalk, vinegar, rocks
- Assessment: Weathering quiz.

### California, A Changing State: The Bear Flag Revolt
- Objective: Explain the causes and events of the Bear Flag Revolt
- Objective: Analyze multiple perspectives
- Vocabulary: revolt, republic, Californio, Sonoma
- Materials: image of the original Bear Flag (Calisphere search), map of Sonoma
- Assessment: Perspective journal entries.

### Art & Music 4: Community mural design
- Objective: Collaboratively plan an artwork with purpose
- Objective: Represent community cultures
- Vocabulary: mural, theme, collaboration, message
- Materials: large paper, markers, photos of murals (e.g., Chicano Park, Balmy Alley)
- Assessment: Collaborative mural and statement.

### PE 4: Stationary and moving hand dribble
- Objective: Dribble with fingerpads keeping eyes up
- Objective: Dribble while changing speed and direction
- Vocabulary: fingerpads, pound dribble, crossover, protect
- Materials: basketball or playground ball, cones
- Assessment: Dribble course time.

### Health 4: Encouraging active families
- Objective: Encourage others to be active
- Objective: Lead a family activity
- Vocabulary: advocate, encourage, role model
- Materials: poster paper, outdoor equipment
- Assessment: Unit 5 test and family challenge.
