## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Kansas vs Oz* (90 min): Read chapters 1-2 and contrast the settings with details.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Measure to 1/8 inch* (60 min): Measure 15 pencils or leaves to the nearest 1/8 inch and record the data.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *USGS map* (45 min): Plot 20 recent magnitude 4+ earthquakes from the USGS map on a world map with red dots.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Need for government* (45 min): Discuss why Gold Rush California needed a government (crime, land claims, no courts).
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Clay basics* (30 min): Learn safe clay handling and make a pinch pot.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Square dance calls* (30 min): Learn do-si-do, swing your partner, promenade and allemande left.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *What is vaping?* (20 min): Read an FDA fact sheet on e-cigarettes and list what is in the aerosol.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Scarecrow* (90 min): Read chapters 3-4 and describe the Scarecrow's traits.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Build the line plot* (60 min): Make a line plot of the measurements with a labeled fraction scale.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Volcanoes* (45 min): Plot 15 active volcanoes (including Mount Shasta and Lassen Peak) with orange triangles on the same map.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Colton Hall* (45 min): Read about the 1849 constitutional convention at Colton Hall in Monterey, including Californio delegates like Vallejo.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Coils* (30 min): Roll coils and add them to build a taller vessel or animal.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Virginia reel* (30 min): Dance the Virginia reel with family members, including the reel and the arch.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Myth or fact* (20 min): Sort 10 statements about vaping into myth or fact.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Tin Woodman* (90 min): Read chapters 5-6 and describe the Tin Woodman and Cowardly Lion.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Answer questions* (60 min): Use the plot to find the difference between longest and shortest and the total of the 1/2-inch items.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Pattern* (45 min): Describe the Ring of Fire pattern formed by the dots and triangles around the Pacific Ocean.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *The constitution* (45 min): Read simplified excerpts of the 1849 California Constitution (bilingual publication, ban on slavery) and list 4 key decisions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Texture* (30 min): Press found objects into clay to create textures.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Grapevine* (30 min): Learn the grapevine step and a 4-wall line dance (e.g., Cha Cha Slide).
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Flavors and marketing* (20 min): Analyze how flavors and colorful packaging target young people.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Prediction* (90 min): Write predictions about what the Wizard will grant each character.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 6 review* (60 min): Mixed review of fraction addition, subtraction, multiplication and line plots.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *San Andreas* (45 min): Locate the San Andreas Fault and explain why California has earthquakes.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Compromise of 1850* (45 min): Read about how California's admission as a free state was part of the Compromise of 1850 on September 9, 1850.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Sculpt* (30 min): Sculpt a California animal (sea otter, quail, bear) using score-and-slip joins.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Heart rate* (30 min): Measure heart rate after dancing and compare to jogging.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Brain effects* (20 min): Learn how nicotine affects the developing brain.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Oz chapters 1-6
- Objective: Describe characters and setting
- Objective: Make predictions
- Vocabulary: cyclone, Munchkin, prediction
- Materials: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (L. Frank Baum, Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Comprehension questions.

### Math 4: Line plots with fractional data
- Objective: Measure to the nearest 1/8 inch
- Objective: Make and interpret line plots
- Vocabulary: line plot, data, 1/8 inch, range
- Materials: ruler, 15 pencils or leaves
- Assessment: Unit 6 test plus line plot product.

### Science 4: Earthquakes and volcanoes patterns
- Objective: Map earthquake and volcano patterns
- Objective: Identify the Ring of Fire
- Vocabulary: earthquake, volcano, fault, Ring of Fire, plate
- Materials: world map, USGS earthquake map, colored dots
- Assessment: Map analysis writing.

### California, A Changing State: The road to statehood
- Objective: Explain how California became the 31st state
- Objective: Describe the 1849 constitutional convention
- Vocabulary: constitution, convention, delegate, Compromise of 1850
- Materials: 1849 California Constitution excerpt (Online Archive of California), timeline strip
- Assessment: Statehood timeline.

### Art & Music 4: Clay sculpture: form and texture
- Objective: Create a sculpture using pinch and coil methods
- Objective: Use tools safely
- Vocabulary: sculpture, form, texture, pinch, coil, score and slip
- Materials: air-dry clay, plastic knife, toothpicks, water cup
- Assessment: Clay sculpture.

### PE 4: American square and line dance
- Objective: Perform square dance calls and a line dance
- Objective: Move with a partner and group
- Vocabulary: do-si-do, promenade, allemande, grapevine
- Materials: square dance or Virginia reel music, open floor
- Assessment: Called sequence check.

### Health 4: Vaping and e-cigarettes
- Objective: Describe the risks of vaping
- Objective: Recognize misleading marketing
- Vocabulary: e-cigarette, vape, aerosol, flavoring, marketing
- Materials: FDA vaping fact sheets, sample ads
- Assessment: PSA message.
