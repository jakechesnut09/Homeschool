## Day 1 — Monday (school day 156, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Outline* (90 min): Outline the report with an introduction, 3-4 sections and conclusion.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Geometry vocabulary* (60 min): Draw and label points, lines, segments and rays in a geometry dictionary.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *The challenge* (45 min): Read the challenge: design a device that converts wind energy from a box fan into motion that lifts 10 pennies 30 cm; restate it in your own words.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *The Depression* (45 min): Read about the Great Depression in California and analyze images from the Calisphere set 'The Great Depression: Hard Times'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Film music* (30 min): Watch a film clip with and without sound and describe the mood change.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Dodging* (30 min): Practice dodging, faking and pivoting in a 'shadow' drill with a partner.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Assertive voice* (20 min): Compare passive, aggressive and assertive responses and practice an assertive voice and body language.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 157, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Draft intro* (90 min): Draft an introduction that hooks the reader with a surprising fact, then draft the first headed section from note cards.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Angle hunt* (60 min): Find 10 angles at home, test with an index card corner and classify them.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Criteria* (45 min): List measurable criteria for success (lift height, load, time to lift) in a table.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Dust Bowl migrants* (45 min): Analyze Dorothea Lange's 'Migrant Mother' and photos from the Calisphere set 'Dust Bowl Migration'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Kandinsky* (30 min): Draw in Chrome Music Lab's Kandinsky to turn art into sound.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Flag tag* (30 min): Play flag tag in a coned area, using fakes to keep your flags.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Refusal steps* (20 min): Learn refusal strategies: say no clearly, give a reason, suggest an alternative, walk away.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 158, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Draft body* (90 min): Draft the remaining 2-3 headed sections, paraphrasing notes and including one short quotation with its source.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Parallel and perpendicular* (60 min): Find parallel and perpendicular lines on a map of San Francisco streets and trace them.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Constraints* (45 min): List constraints: only household materials (cups, straws, skewers, cardstock, string, tape), 3 days of building, no batteries.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Home front* (45 min): Read about shipyards in Richmond, aircraft factories and women workers using the Calisphere set 'Life on the Home Front During World War II'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Soundtrack* (30 min): Create a soundtrack for a short story using recorder and percussion.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Capture the flag* (30 min): Play capture the flag with family or neighbors, assigning defenders and attackers.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Role-play 1* (20 min): Role-play refusing a vape offered by an older kid.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 159, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Commas* (90 min): Practice commas in series and introductory phrases; apply to the draft.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Angles as turns* (60 min): Model 1/4, 1/2 and 3/4 turns with paper circle fractions and connect to 90, 180 and 270 degrees.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Research* (45 min): Study photos and diagrams of historic windmills and modern turbines and note how blades and axles convert wind to rotation.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Japanese American incarceration* (45 min): Read about Executive Order 9066 and analyze photos from the Calisphere set 'Japanese American Internment'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Personal playlist* (30 min): Make a playlist of 5 songs that represent you and explain choices.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Strategy board* (30 min): Draw a strategy diagram for your team and test it in a rematch.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Role-play 2* (20 min): Role-play refusing alcohol at a party and leaving with help from a trusted adult.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Drafting the research report
- Objective: Organize information
- Objective: Use quotations and paraphrase
- Vocabulary: paraphrase, quotation, citation
- Materials: notes, computer
- Assessment: Complete draft with source list.

### Math 4: Points, lines, rays and angles
- Objective: Draw and identify geometric figures
- Objective: Classify angles as acute, right, obtuse
- Vocabulary: ray, line segment, vertex, acute, obtuse, parallel, perpendicular
- Materials: ruler, index card corner, colored pencils
- Assessment: Geometry vocabulary quiz.

### Science 4: Defining a design problem: the wind-powered lifter
- Objective: Define a design problem with criteria and constraints
- Objective: Research existing energy-converting devices
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraint, prototype, design brief
- Materials: science notebook, box fan, examples of windmills (photos)
- Assessment: Design brief with criteria and constraints.

### California, A Changing State: Hard times: the Great Depression, Dust Bowl and World War II
- Objective: Discuss effects of the Depression, Dust Bowl and WWII on California
- Vocabulary: Depression, Dust Bowl, migrant, home front, incarceration
- Materials: Calisphere sets 'Dust Bowl Migration', 'Life on the Home Front During World War II' and 'Japanese American Internment', Dorothea Lange 'Migrant Mother'
- Assessment: Historical paragraph.

### Art & Music 4: Music and connections
- Objective: Connect music to other arts and daily life
- Vocabulary: film score, soundtrack, mood
- Materials: film clip, Chrome Music Lab Kandinsky
- Assessment: Soundtrack and unit check.

### PE 4: Tag and invasion game strategy
- Objective: Use dodging and faking to create space
- Objective: Apply simple offensive and defensive strategies
- Vocabulary: invasion game, dodge, fake, zone, strategy
- Materials: flag belts or bandanas, cones, foam ball
- Assessment: Strategy diagram and reflection.

### Health 4: Refusal skills
- Objective: Demonstrate refusal skills
- Objective: Practice assertive communication
- Vocabulary: refusal, assertive, passive, aggressive, broken record
- Materials: refusal skills card, scenarios
- Assessment: Refusal skills performance check.
