## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Rontu* (90 min): Read chapters 15-17 and analyze how Karana's relationship with Rontu reveals her values.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Four-digit partial quotients* (60 min): Solve 6 problems like 3,624 / 6 using partial quotients.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Rainbow* (45 min): Make a rainbow with a glass of water and sunlight.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Perspectives* (45 min): Read short accounts from a Franciscan, a soldier and a Native leader (e.g., Toypurina).
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Dynamics* (30 min): Play 'Clementine' piano and then forte, and add a crescendo to one phrase.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Tabata jump* (30 min): Do a 4-minute Tabata (20 seconds jumping, 10 seconds rest) and record heart rate after.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Clean* (20 min): Use glitter on hands to model germ spread, then practice 20-second handwashing.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Context clues* (90 min): Use context to define 10 words from chapters 18-19.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Zeros in quotients* (60 min): Solve 6 problems like 1,824 / 6 where a quotient digit is zero and explain why.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Color heat* (45 min): Place thermometers under black and white paper in the sun and record temperature.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Toypurina* (45 min): Read about Toypurina's 1785 resistance at Mission San Gabriel.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Tempo* (30 min): Play 'Ode to Joy' at largo, moderato and allegro tempos with a metronome app.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Choreograph* (30 min): Choose a song and choreograph a 1-minute routine with at least 5 tricks matched to the beat.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Separate* (20 min): Learn why raw meat stays separate from produce and sort grocery items for safe storage.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 73, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Tutok* (90 min): Read chapters 18-20 and discuss friendship across conflict.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Which strategy?* (60 min): Solve the same 3 problems with area model and partial quotients and decide which is more efficient for each.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Solar energy* (45 min): Build a simple solar oven from a pizza box and foil.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Legacy* (45 min): Discuss the legacy of the missions today, including place names, architecture and Native descendants' perspectives.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Listening map* (30 min): Listen to 'In the Hall of the Mountain King' and draw a listening map of its growing tempo and dynamics.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse the routine 5 times, fixing any spot where you miss.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Cook* (20 min): Learn safe cooking temperatures and check one with a food thermometer.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 74, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Theme* (90 min): Read chapters 21-22 and draft a theme statement with supporting details.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Division puzzles* (60 min): Complete a 'missing digit' division puzzle sheet and an error analysis of 3 worked problems.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Test oven* (45 min): Melt chocolate or s'mores in the solar oven and record temperature.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Essay* (45 min): Write an informative essay on how the missions affected different groups.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Interpretation plan* (30 min): Mark dynamics and tempo on a folk song's music to match the story's mood.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Perform* (30 min): Perform the routine for family as a 'Jump Rope Show'.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Chill* (20 min): Check the refrigerator temperature (40°F or below) and list leftovers rules.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 75, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Answer comprehension questions and share the theme statement aloud.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz with 8 four-digit by one-digit division problems, including zeros in the quotient.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Unit 4 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 4 test on light and sight.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 4 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 4 test on Spanish colonization, mission placement, daily life and economic change.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform the folk song with your planned expression and explain each choice.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Unit assessment* (30 min): Complete the jump rope skills test: 1-minute speed count, 3 tricks and long-rope entry.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a Clean-Separate-Cook-Chill poster for the kitchen.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Island of the Blue Dolphins, chapters 15-22
- Objective: Determine theme
- Objective: Analyze relationships
- Vocabulary: theme, relationship, forgiveness
- Materials: novel
- Assessment: Theme statement and questions.

### Math 4: Four-digit dividends
- Objective: Divide 4-digit numbers by 1-digit divisors
- Objective: Handle zeros in the quotient
- Vocabulary: quotient, place value, remainder
- Materials: grid paper
- Assessment: Four-digit division quiz.

### Science 4: Light energy and color
- Objective: Show light carries energy
- Objective: Investigate color and heat
- Vocabulary: spectrum, prism, absorb, energy
- Materials: prism or water glass, thermometers, black and white paper
- Assessment: Solar oven data and Unit 4 test.

### California, A Changing State: Missions from many perspectives
- Objective: Evaluate the mission system from multiple perspectives
- Objective: Write an informative essay
- Vocabulary: perspective, legacy, impact
- Materials: notes, Calisphere sources
- Assessment: Essay and Unit 4 test.

### Art & Music 4: Expressive qualities: dynamics and tempo
- Objective: Use dynamics and tempo to convey intent
- Objective: Identify expressive choices in recordings
- Vocabulary: dynamics, piano, forte, crescendo, tempo, allegro, largo
- Materials: recorder, recording of Grieg's 'In the Hall of the Mountain King'
- Assessment: Expressive performance with explanation.

### PE 4: Jump rope fitness show
- Objective: Use jump rope for aerobic fitness
- Objective: Perform a rhythmic routine to music
- Vocabulary: routine, tempo, choreography, endurance
- Materials: jump rope, music with steady beat
- Assessment: Jump rope skills test.

### Health 4: Food safety
- Objective: Explain safe food handling and storage
- Objective: Prevent foodborne illness
- Vocabulary: bacteria, cross-contamination, refrigerate, foodborne illness
- Materials: glitter or GloGerm substitute, soap, thermometer
- Assessment: Food safety poster.
