## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Character traits* (90 min): Read 'The Elephant's Child' and chart the Elephant's Child's traits with evidence from his words and actions.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *10 ones = 1 ten* (60 min): Trade ten unit cubes for a rod and ten rods for a flat; write 'the 4 in 40 is 10 times the 4 in 4' sentences.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Ramp setup* (45 min): Build a ramp with a book stack and practice timing a marble over 1 meter.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Capital and regions* (45 min): Color a blank California map showing the coastal, mountain, Central Valley and desert regions and star Sacramento.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Value scale* (30 min): Create a 7-step value scale from white to black with a 4B pencil.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Five components* (30 min): Match 10 activities to the five health-related fitness components.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Decision steps* (20 min): Learn a decision model (Stop, Think of options, Consider consequences, Choose, Check) and apply it to a snack choice.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Prefixes un-, re-, dis-* (90 min): Sort 20 words by prefix, define each using the prefix meaning, and use 5 in sentences.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Calculator x10 chain* (60 min): Enter 3, then multiply by 10 repeatedly to 3,000,000; record each result and describe how the digits shift.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Height and speed* (45 min): Release a marble from 3 ramp heights, timing 3 trials each and recording data.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Region study* (45 min): Read about each region's climate, landforms and vegetation and make a 4-column comparison chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Light and shadow* (30 min): Shine a flashlight on a ball and identify highlight, midtone, core shadow and cast shadow.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *PACER or mile* (30 min): Complete the 20-meter PACER (or a timed 1-mile walk/run) and record laps or time.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Scenario practice* (20 min): Work through 3 scenarios (skipping breakfast, choosing an after-school activity, drink at a party) with the model.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Narrative planning* (90 min): Choose a small moment from the heart map and plan it on a story mountain (beginning, rising events, high point, ending).
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Millions* (60 min): Read populations of California's five largest cities and write each in word and expanded form.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Cup push test* (45 min): Measure how far the marble pushes a paper cup at each height as evidence of energy.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Living in a region* (45 min): Compare photos of Imperial Valley farms, a Sierra ski town, a Central Valley orchard and a coastal fishing harbor; explain how land shapes jobs.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Shade a sphere* (30 min): Draw and shade a sphere, cube and cylinder using the value scale.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Curl-ups & push-ups* (30 min): Complete cadence curl-ups and 90-degree push-ups tests and record results.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *SMART goals* (20 min): Rewrite 4 vague goals ('eat better') as SMART goals.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 9, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Complete sentences* (90 min): Fix 12 fragments and run-ons, then check the narrative plan's first sentences for completeness.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Compare values* (60 min): Solve 10 problems like 'How many times greater is the 5 in 5,200 than the 5 in 520?' and explain one in writing.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Graph and explain* (45 min): Graph height vs. cup distance and write a claim-evidence-reasoning paragraph.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Our region* (45 min): Describe the region you live in using five geographic details and one photo you take.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Hatching* (30 min): Practice hatching, cross-hatching and stippling in a 3-box chart.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Trunk lift & sit-and-reach* (30 min): Complete trunk lift and back-saver sit-and-reach tests and record results.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Your goal* (20 min): Write a personal 2-week SMART goal for eating or activity and make a tracking chart.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 10, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Draft the narrative's opening scene with a hook and share it aloud; take a quiz on prefixes and sentence types.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Exit ticket: explain with a drawing why the 2 in 2,000 is ten times the 2 in 200.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz: predict and explain which marble has more energy in 4 scenarios.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on regions and write a paragraph on how one region's environment affects people.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Shade a piece of fruit from observation and compare it with the week 1 contour drawing.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Compare results to Healthy Fitness Zone charts with a parent.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain your decision-making steps to a parent using a real choice from today.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Character and personal narrative beginnings
- Objective: Describe a character using thoughts, words and actions
- Objective: Plan a personal narrative
- Vocabulary: trait, motive, narrative, sequence
- Materials: Just So Stories, writer's notebook
- Assessment: Character chart and prefix/sentence quiz.

### Math 4: Ten times as much
- Objective: Explain that a digit is ten times the value of the same digit one place right
- Objective: Extend to millions
- Vocabulary: ten times, millions period, expanded form
- Materials: base-ten blocks, place value chart, calculator
- Assessment: Written explanation with drawing plus 10-problem practice set.

### Science 4: Speed and energy
- Objective: Measure speed using distance and time
- Objective: Relate speed to energy
- Vocabulary: speed, distance, time, ramp, variable
- Materials: cardboard ramp, books, marble or toy car, meter stick, stopwatch, paper cup
- Assessment: CER explanation relating speed and energy.

### California, A Changing State: California's regions
- Objective: Identify the capital and the four regions
- Objective: Explain how environments affect human activity
- Vocabulary: region, coast, Central Valley, desert, mountains
- Materials: California physical map, colored pencils
- Assessment: Region chart and paragraph.

### Art & Music 4: Shape and form: shading with value
- Objective: Turn shapes into forms with value
- Objective: Create a value scale
- Vocabulary: value, shading, form, highlight, cast shadow
- Materials: pencils, paper, flashlight, ball, cylinder object
- Assessment: Shaded fruit drawing.

### PE 4: Fitness pre-assessment
- Objective: Complete baseline fitness tests
- Objective: Understand the five fitness components
- Vocabulary: aerobic capacity, muscular strength, endurance, flexibility, body composition
- Materials: PACER audio (or 20 m measured course), mat, measuring stick, cadence app
- Assessment: Baseline fitness data recorded.

### Health 4: Decision making and goal setting
- Objective: Use a decision-making model
- Objective: Write a SMART health goal
- Vocabulary: decision, consequence, option, SMART goal
- Materials: decision-making worksheet, health journal
- Assessment: SMART goal and tracking chart.
