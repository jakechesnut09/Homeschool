## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Stevenson* (90 min): Read 'My Shadow' and 'The Land of Counterpane'; mark rhyme scheme and count beats.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Dividing by common factors* (60 min): Use factor knowledge to rename 6/8, 9/12, 10/100 in simpler form and check with fraction strips.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Skeleton* (45 min): Examine cleaned chicken bones and compare to human bones.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Rancho life* (45 min): Read about daily life on a rancho (hide and tallow trade, fiestas, household work) and list jobs of each family member.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Folk art tour* (30 min): Look at images of papel picado, alebrijes and Talavera pottery and discuss when and why they are made.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Counterbalance* (30 min): Practice partner sit-ups-to-stand back-to-back and lean-away holds with hands joined.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Activity log* (20 min): Log activity minutes for 3 days, labeling each activity as light, moderate or vigorous.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Imagery* (90 min): Read Sandburg's 'Fog' and explain the extended metaphor.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Common denominators* (60 min): Rename pairs like 1/3 and 1/4 as twelfths using multiples.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Lung model* (45 min): Build a lung model from a plastic bottle, two balloons and a straw and pull the diaphragm balloon to model breathing.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Vaqueros* (45 min): Read about vaqueros, many of them Native Californians, and their roping and riding skills that shaped the American cowboy.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Positive/negative space* (30 min): Practice folding and cutting symmetrical shapes and identify positive and negative space.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Partner stunts* (30 min): Try partner balances such as the 'thigh stand' with a spotter, holding for 3 seconds.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Screen time log* (20 min): Log recreational screen time (TV, games, videos) for the same 3 days with start and end times.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 88, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Frost* (90 min): Read 'Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening' and discuss mood and repetition.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Fraction match game* (60 min): Play a memory match game with 20 cards of equivalent fraction pairs and models.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Heart* (45 min): Measure pulse at rest and after exercise and explain the heart's job.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Vallejo and Pico* (45 min): Read biographies of Mariano Guadalupe Vallejo and Pío Pico.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Design* (30 min): Sketch a papel picado design with a theme (celebration, nature, family).
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Strength builders* (30 min): Do planks, superman holds and wall sits to build the strength stunts need.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Compare* (20 min): Compare activity to the 60-minute goal and screen time to family limits.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 89, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Write poems* (90 min): Write a metaphor poem and a rhyming quatrain about a California place.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Real-world equivalents* (60 min): Solve recipe problems: is 2/4 cup of flour the same as 1/2 cup? Explain with a drawing.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Digestion* (45 min): Model digestion with a plastic bag, crackers and water.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Settlements map* (45 min): Map Mexican settlements, Fort Ross and Sutter's Fort.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Cut* (30 min): Cut 3 tissue paper banners and string them together.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Group shape* (30 min): With 2-3 family members, create a group balance that has at least 4 points of contact with the floor.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Swap* (20 min): List 5 active alternatives to screen time that work indoors and outdoors and try one today.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 90, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Memorize and recite a poem; quiz on poetry terms.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on simplest form and common denominators.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Label a diagram of internal structures and functions.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 5 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 5 test on Mexican independence, secularization and rancho life.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Hang the banner and explain its symbols and the tradition it comes from.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform 2 partner stunts and the group balance; discuss what communication made them work.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a paragraph about patterns in your logs and one change you will make next week.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Poetry elements
- Objective: Identify verse, rhythm, meter and rhyme
- Objective: Interpret figurative language
- Vocabulary: stanza, verse, rhythm, meter, metaphor
- Materials: A Child's Garden of Verses (Stevenson, Gutenberg), 'Fog' (Sandburg), 'Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening' (Frost)
- Assessment: Recitation and poetry terms quiz.

### Math 4: Simplifying and finding common denominators
- Objective: Write fractions in simplest form
- Objective: Rename fractions with a common denominator
- Vocabulary: simplest form, common factor, common denominator
- Materials: fraction strips
- Assessment: Quiz on renaming fractions.

### Science 4: Animal internal structures
- Objective: Identify internal structures
- Objective: Explain their roles
- Vocabulary: heart, lungs, skeleton, stomach, brain
- Materials: chicken bones (cleaned), balloons, plastic bottle
- Assessment: Labeled diagram.

### California, A Changing State: Life on the ranchos
- Objective: Describe rancho life
- Objective: Identify key Californios
- Vocabulary: ranchero, vaquero, Californio, fiesta
- Materials: Calisphere 'Californio Society' set
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Art & Music 4: Papel picado and Mexican folk art
- Objective: Create a cut-paper design reflecting a community tradition
- Objective: Interpret symbols
- Vocabulary: papel picado, folk art, symmetry, positive and negative space
- Materials: tissue paper, scissors, string, images of papel picado and alebrijes
- Assessment: Papel picado banner.

### PE 4: Partner and group balance stunts
- Objective: Perform small-group balances by distributing weight
- Objective: Cooperate and communicate safely
- Vocabulary: partner balance, counterbalance, countertension, spotter
- Materials: mat, family members
- Assessment: Group balance performance.

### Health 4: Tracking activity and screen time
- Objective: Track activity and screen time
- Objective: Compare to recommendations
- Vocabulary: sedentary, screen time, log
- Materials: activity log, timer
- Assessment: Activity and screen time analysis.
