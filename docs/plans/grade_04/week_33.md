## Day 1 — Monday (school day 161, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Revise for clarity* (90 min): Reread the draft aloud, mark confusing sentences, and revise each section so its first sentence states the section's main idea.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Reading a protractor* (60 min): Measure 10 printed angles, deciding which scale to read.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Brainstorm* (45 min): Brainstorm at least 5 blade and axle ideas without judging, sketching each quickly.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Industries map* (45 min): Map aerospace (Southern California), electronics (Silicon Valley), agriculture (Central Valley) and Pacific trade ports.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review the year* (30 min): Lay out every artwork from the year, sort by unit, and note which skills each shows.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Plan* (30 min): Invent a game combining at least two skills (e.g., foot dribble and throwing) and list equipment and boundaries.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Decision model* (20 min): Apply the decision model to 3 ATOD scenarios and write consequences.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 162, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Edit conventions* (90 min): Edit for capitalization of proper nouns (Sierra Nevada, Mojave Desert), commas and spelling using a checklist and dictionary.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Drawing angles* (60 min): Draw angles of 35, 90, 125 and 160 degrees and have a parent check with the protractor.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Blade mini-test* (45 min): Test 3 blade shapes (flat, angled, cupped) on a straw axle in front of the fan and count rotations in 10 seconds.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Public education* (45 min): Read about California's schools, community colleges, CSU and UC systems and the 1960 Master Plan.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Select* (30 min): Select 6 pieces for an exhibition using one set of criteria (skill, idea, growth) and record a score for each.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Rules* (30 min): Write 5 rules, a scoring system and a safety rule on a poster.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Healthy alternatives* (20 min): List 10 healthy activities to have fun and manage stress.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 163, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Visuals* (90 min): Add a labeled map of the wonder's location and two captioned images, and explain in the caption what each shows.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Angle addition* (60 min): Split a right angle into two angles and write the equation; solve 6 unknown-angle problems.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Detailed sketches* (45 min): Draw three full designs with labels, materials and how energy changes from wind to motion to lifting.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Artists* (45 min): Study Ansel Adams' Yosemite photos, read about John Steinbeck and Dorothea Lange, and discuss their impact.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Digital portfolio* (30 min): Photograph each piece in good light and create a digital portfolio slideshow with titles.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Test* (30 min): Play-test the game with family and note problems.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Pledge* (20 min): Write a personal pledge to stay alcohol-, tobacco- and drug-free, with one action you will take to keep it.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 164, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Publish* (90 min): Type and print the final report with headings, visuals and a source list in a consistent format.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Pattern block angles* (60 min): Find the angles of pattern block shapes by fitting them around a point (360 degrees).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Decision matrix* (45 min): Score each design 1-3 on each criterion and constraint in a decision matrix.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Entertainment* (45 min): Read about Hollywood and Walt Disney and how film spread California culture.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Preservation* (30 min): Read how museums such as the Getty protect artworks (light, humidity, digital archives) and list 3 ways technology helps preserve art.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Revise* (30 min): Revise rules to make the game fairer and more active.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Share* (20 min): Share your pledge with a trusted adult and discuss support.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 165, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Self-assess the report against the research rubric and write two sentences on its strongest and weakest parts.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on measuring, drawing and adding angles.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Choose & justify* (45 min): Choose one design and write a paragraph explaining why it best meets the criteria.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 10 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 10 test on water projects, the Depression and WWII, industries, education and culture.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Curator's statement* (30 min): Write a one-paragraph curator's statement explaining the exhibition's theme and choices.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Teach & play* (30 min): Teach the final game to a new player and lead a full game.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Unit assessment* (20 min): Take the Unit 9 test on influences, refusal and decisions.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Revising and publishing the report
- Objective: Revise for clarity
- Objective: Publish with visuals
- Vocabulary: revise, caption, graphic
- Materials: computer, images
- Assessment: Published research report.

### Math 4: Measuring angles with a protractor
- Objective: Measure and draw angles to the nearest degree
- Objective: Add angle measures
- Vocabulary: protractor, degree, adjacent angles, additive
- Materials: protractor, ruler
- Assessment: Protractor quiz.

### Science 4: Generating and comparing solutions
- Objective: Generate several design solutions
- Objective: Compare solutions using criteria
- Vocabulary: brainstorm, decision matrix, trade-off, axle
- Materials: paper, ruler, colored pencils, sample materials
- Assessment: Decision matrix and justification paragraph.

### California, A Changing State: Industry, education and culture
- Objective: Describe new industries
- Objective: Analyze Californians' cultural impact
- Objective: Trace the growth of public education
- Vocabulary: aerospace, Silicon Valley, Hollywood, university
- Materials: map, Ansel Adams photographs, library books
- Assessment: Unit 10 test.

### Art & Music 4: Curating a portfolio
- Objective: Select artwork for an exhibition using criteria
- Objective: Analyze how technology preserves and presents art
- Vocabulary: curate, portfolio, digital archive, preserve
- Materials: all artwork from the year, camera or phone, folder
- Assessment: Curated selection, digital portfolio and statement.

### PE 4: Design-a-game
- Objective: Invent a game that uses grade 4 skills
- Objective: Write fair rules and teach them
- Vocabulary: rules, boundaries, scoring, fairness
- Materials: any equipment from the year, poster paper
- Assessment: Game poster and led game.

### Health 4: Decisions and the drug-free pledge
- Objective: Use decision-making to avoid ATOD
- Objective: Set a personal drug-free goal
- Vocabulary: pledge, goal, consequence, alternative
- Materials: decision-making worksheet, pledge card
- Assessment: Unit 9 test and pledge.
