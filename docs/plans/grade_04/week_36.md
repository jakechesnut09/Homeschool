## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Choose pieces* (90 min): Select the best narrative, explanatory report, opinion essay and poem from the writing folder and label why each was chosen.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Whole number review* (60 min): Complete a mixed review of place value, multiplication and division with a self-check key.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Energy & waves review* (45 min): Make concept-map flashcards for energy, collisions, circuits, waves and light, and quiz yourself.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Local governments* (45 min): Identify your city, county, school district and nearest rancheria or reservation.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Warm-up routine* (30 min): Lead your own 10-minute warm-up: long tones, a D-to-D scale and the toughest phrase from each piece.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Plan field day* (30 min): Plan 6 stations (sack race, accuracy throw, jump rope contest, dribble relay, tug-of-war, water relay) and make station signs.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Present* (20 min): Present the campaign to family or a community group.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Reflect* (90 min): Write a reflection letter comparing the week 4 narrative with a recent piece, naming two specific improvements.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Fraction & decimal review* (60 min): Complete a mixed review of fractions, decimals and measurement.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Life science review* (45 min): Redo the ruler-drop reaction test and explain the senses-brain-response model from memory.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *City council* (45 min): Watch part of a city council meeting online and list topics discussed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Exhibition opening* (30 min): Host the exhibition opening, giving guests a 3-minute tour and answering questions about techniques.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Set up* (30 min): Measure and set up stations safely and prepare a score card.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Display* (20 min): Display posters in a visible place (with permission).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Grammar review* (90 min): Take a cumulative grammar and conventions assessment (relative pronouns, progressive tenses, modals, commas, quotation marks).
- [ ] **Math 4** — *End-of-year assessment* (60 min): Take the end-of-year cumulative assessment and compare with the week 1 diagnostic.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Earth science review* (45 min): Explain a photo of California coastal cliffs using weathering, erosion and rock layer vocabulary.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Tribal government* (45 min): Read about a California tribal government and its services.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Concert* (30 min): Perform the 4-piece recorder concert with introductions and proper stage etiquette.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Field day* (30 min): Run field day with family or friends, rotating through all stations.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Year review* (20 min): Review nutrition, activity and ATOD key ideas with a game.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *On-demand writing* (90 min): Write an on-demand opinion piece in one 45-minute sitting on a choice of three prompts.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Math fair project* (60 min): Create a poster solving a multistep 'Plan a California road trip' problem with distance, cost and time.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Cumulative test* (45 min): Take the end-of-year science assessment covering all units.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Year timeline* (45 min): Build a California history timeline from first peoples to today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Reflection* (30 min): Write a one-page reflection comparing week 1 drawings and recorder playing to now, using the year's vocabulary.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Awards* (30 min): Make sportsmanship awards for every participant.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Final assessment* (20 min): Take the end-of-year health assessment covering nutrition, physical activity, medicines, tobacco and alcohol.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Celebration* (90 min): Share the portfolio with family in an author celebration and write three grade 5 reading and writing goals.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Present & reflect* (60 min): Present the project to family and write a reflection on the most improved skill.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Notebook showcase* (45 min): Choose your three favorite investigations from the notebook and explain them to family.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Final assessment* (45 min): Take the end-of-year HSS assessment covering geography, first peoples, missions, Gold Rush, statehood and government.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Goal setting* (30 min): Watch the concert recording, evaluate it with the class rubric, and set two art and music goals for grade 5.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Year reflection* (30 min): Write a reflection on your favorite unit and the skill you improved most.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Reflect* (20 min): Write a reflection on your health goals and set a goal for summer.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Writing portfolio and celebration
- Objective: Select and reflect on best work
- Objective: Set goals
- Vocabulary: portfolio, reflection, goal
- Materials: writing folder
- Assessment: Portfolio and on-demand writing sample.

### Math 4: Year in review and celebration
- Objective: Apply grade 4 skills to multistep problems
- Objective: Reflect on growth
- Vocabulary: review, strategy, reflection
- Materials: end-of-year test, poster paper
- Assessment: End-of-year cumulative assessment and project presentation.

### Science 4: Science year in review
- Objective: Connect big ideas across physical, life and Earth science
- Objective: Demonstrate mastery on a cumulative assessment
- Vocabulary: energy, wave, structure, weathering, resource
- Materials: science notebook, index cards
- Assessment: End-of-year cumulative assessment.

### California, A Changing State: Local government and year review
- Objective: Describe local governance structures
- Objective: Review the year
- Vocabulary: county, city council, rancheria, school district
- Materials: county map, local government website
- Assessment: End-of-year assessment.

### Art & Music 4: Exhibition opening and recorder concert
- Objective: Perform with expression and accuracy for an audience
- Objective: Reflect on artistic growth across the year
- Vocabulary: performance, audience, reflection, growth
- Materials: exhibition wall, recorder, concert programs
- Assessment: Concert and exhibition evaluated with rubric; written reflection.

### PE 4: Field day
- Objective: Apply the year's skills in a field day
- Objective: Demonstrate sportsmanship
- Vocabulary: field day, relay, sportsmanship
- Materials: cones, ropes, balls, water, sack or pillowcase
- Assessment: Field day participation and reflection.

### Health 4: Presenting the campaign and year review
- Objective: Present the campaign
- Objective: Reflect on health goals
- Vocabulary: advocacy, reflection, goal
- Materials: campaign materials
- Assessment: Campaign presentation and final assessment.
