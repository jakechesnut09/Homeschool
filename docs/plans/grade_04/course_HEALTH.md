# Health 4 (Grade 4)

Grade 4 health education aligned to the 2008 California Health Education Content Standards, which focus fourth grade on Nutrition and Physical Activity and on Alcohol, Tobacco and Other Drugs. Students learn nutrients and MyPlate, read Nutrition Facts labels, plan balanced meals and snacks, practice food safety, and set physical-activity goals; then they study safe medicine use, the effects of tobacco/vaping and alcohol, influences such as advertising and peers, and refusal and decision-making skills, finishing with a family health-promotion campaign.

*Framework:* 2008 California Health Education Content Standards (Grade 4: Nutrition and Physical Activity; Alcohol, Tobacco and Other Drugs); 2019 CA Health Education Framework  
*Time:* 20 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Health Skills and Wellness (weeks 1–3)
Good health comes from everyday choices and skills we can practice.

- Week 1: What is health?
- Week 2: Decision making and goal setting
- Week 3: Trusted adults and reliable information

### Unit 2: Nutrients and MyPlate (weeks 4–8)
Our bodies need a variety of nutrients from every food group.

- Week 4: Nutrients: carbohydrates, proteins and fats
- Week 5: Vitamins, minerals and water
- Week 6: MyPlate food groups
- Week 7: Breakfast, fruits and vegetables
- Week 8: Food tracking

### Unit 3: Food Labels and Influences (weeks 9–12)
Labels give reliable information, while ads and habits influence what we choose.

- Week 9: Reading the Nutrition Facts label
- Week 10: Comparing snacks and making choices
- Week 11: Advertising and food choices
- Week 12: Communicating about food at home

### Unit 4: Planning Healthy Meals and Food Safety (weeks 13–16)
Balanced meals and safe food handling keep families healthy.

- Week 13: Planning a balanced meal
- Week 14: Cooking a healthy recipe
- Week 15: Food safety
- Week 16: Eating out and family meals

### Unit 5: Physical Activity for Life (weeks 17–20)
Sixty minutes of daily activity strengthens the heart, muscles and mind.

- Week 17: Benefits of physical activity
- Week 18: Tracking activity and screen time
- Week 19: Physical activity goal
- Week 20: Encouraging active families

### Unit 6: Medicines and Household Safety (weeks 21–23)
Medicines help when used correctly; unsafe use and poisons can harm.

- Week 21: What medicines are
- Week 22: Safe medicine use
- Week 23: Household poisons and inhalants

### Unit 7: Tobacco and Vaping (weeks 24–26)
Nicotine is addictive and harms the body; most young people choose not to use it.

- Week 24: Tobacco and the body
- Week 25: Vaping and e-cigarettes
- Week 26: Secondhand smoke and social norms

### Unit 8: Alcohol (weeks 27–29)
Alcohol affects the brain and body and is especially harmful to growing kids.

- Week 27: Alcohol and the body
- Week 28: Alcohol and the growing brain
- Week 29: Family rules and influences about alcohol

### Unit 9: Influences, Decisions and Refusal Skills (weeks 30–33)
Recognizing influences and practicing refusal skills helps us stay drug-free.

- Week 30: Media influences on ATOD
- Week 31: Peer pressure
- Week 32: Refusal skills
- Week 33: Decisions and the drug-free pledge

### Unit 10: Health Promotion Campaign (weeks 34–36)
We can encourage our families and communities to make healthy choices.

- Week 34: Planning a health campaign
- Week 35: Creating the campaign
- Week 36: Presenting the campaign and year review

## Standards

- **H-4.1.1.A** Identify safe and unsafe uses of medicines; distinguish prescription and over-the-counter medicines.
- **H-4.1.1.N** Identify the key nutrients (carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water) and their functions.
- **H-4.1.2.A** Describe the short- and long-term effects of tobacco and nicotine use, including e-cigarettes/vaping and secondhand smoke.
- **H-4.1.2.N** Describe the food groups and the recommended daily amounts from each (MyPlate).
- **H-4.1.3.A** Describe the short- and long-term effects of alcohol use.
- **H-4.1.3.N** Explain the benefits of eating a healthy breakfast and a variety of fruits and vegetables.
- **H-4.1.4.A** Recognize that most young people do not use alcohol, tobacco or other drugs (social norms).
- **H-4.1.4.N** Explain the importance of drinking water and limiting sugar-sweetened beverages.
- **H-4.1.5.A** Identify dangers of inhalants and household poisons.
- **H-4.1.5.N** Describe the benefits of daily physical activity and the recommended 60 minutes per day for children.
- **H-4.1.6.N** Explain safe food handling and storage practices.
- **H-4.2.1.A** Analyze how advertising and the media influence decisions about alcohol, tobacco and other drugs.
- **H-4.2.1.N** Describe internal and external influences (family, culture, media, advertising) on food choices and physical activity.
- **H-4.2.2.A** Describe how family and peers influence choices about alcohol, tobacco and other drugs.
- **H-4.3.1.A** Identify trusted adults and community resources for help with alcohol, tobacco and other drug concerns.
- **H-4.3.1.N** Use the Nutrition Facts label to locate valid information about foods.
- **H-4.4.1.A** Demonstrate refusal skills to avoid alcohol, tobacco and other drugs.
- **H-4.4.1.N** Practice effective communication to ask for healthy food and activity options at home and in the community.
- **H-4.5.1.A** Use a decision-making process to avoid alcohol, tobacco and other drug use.
- **H-4.5.1.N** Use a decision-making process to choose healthy foods and snacks.
- **H-4.6.1.A** Set a personal goal to remain alcohol-, tobacco- and drug-free.
- **H-4.6.1.N** Set a short-term goal to improve nutrition or increase physical activity and track progress.
- **H-4.7.1.A** Practice safe use of medicines (read labels, take only with adult supervision).
- **H-4.7.1.N** Plan a nutritious meal and snack using MyPlate.
- **H-4.7.2.N** Track daily food intake and physical activity.
- **H-4.8.1.A** Encourage others to be alcohol-, tobacco- and drug-free.
- **H-4.8.1.N** Encourage others to make healthy food and physical activity choices.
