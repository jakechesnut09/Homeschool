## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Fact vs opinion* (90 min): Sort 15 statements about California (e.g., 'Yosemite is the best park') into fact and opinion and underline the clue words.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit fractions* (60 min): Write 5/8 as a sum of unit fractions and two other ways (e.g., 2/8 + 3/8).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Stream table* (45 min): Build a sand stream table and pour water to observe erosion and deposition.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *War begins* (45 min): Read about the causes of the Mexican-American War and the raising of the U.S. flag at Monterey in July 1846.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *AB form* (30 min): Listen to a two-part song and hold up shapes when the A and B sections change.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Chest pass* (30 min): Practice 30 chest passes to a wall target, stepping into each pass.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Why medicines?* (20 min): List reasons people take medicines (treat illness, prevent disease, relieve pain) and the risks of misuse.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Reasons* (90 min): Read an opinion article on school uniforms and outline its reasons and evidence.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Decomposition equations* (60 min): Write 3 decompositions each for 6 fractions and justify with fraction strips.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Slope test* (45 min): Test erosion at different slopes and measure sediment.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Battle of San Pasqual* (45 min): Read about the 1846 Battle of San Pasqual where Californio lancers under Andrés Pico fought U.S. troops.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *ABA form* (30 min): Listen to 'Twinkle, Twinkle' as ABA and label the sections in the lyrics.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Bounce pass* (30 min): Practice bounce passes that hit two-thirds of the way to the partner.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Rx vs OTC* (20 min): Compare a prescription label and an OTC drug facts label in a Venn diagram.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Evaluate evidence* (90 min): Rate each piece of evidence as strong or weak and explain.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Mixed numbers* (60 min): Convert between mixed numbers and fractions greater than 1 using number lines (10 problems).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Wind* (45 min): Blow through a straw at sand and measure distance moved.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Treaty* (45 min): Read key promises of the 1848 Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo about land and citizenship for Mexican residents.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Rondo* (30 min): Listen to Mozart's 'Rondo alla Turca' and track the ABACA form.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Overhead pass* (30 min): Practice overhead passes and when to use them.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Health helpers* (20 min): Learn the roles of doctors and pharmacists; write 3 questions you could ask a pharmacist.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 104, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Listen for reasons* (90 min): Watch a short persuasive video and record the speaker's reasons.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Number line jumps* (60 min): Show 2 1/4 as jumps of 1/4 on a number line and write the matching equation.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Ice* (45 min): Drag an ice cube with sand over clay and observe scratches like glaciers in Yosemite.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Map the cession* (45 min): Color the Mexican Cession on a map and compare U.S. borders before and after 1848.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Movement* (30 min): Create a different movement for each section of a rondo and perform it.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Moving passes* (30 min): Pass to a partner jogging across the yard and catch with 'target hands'.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Not candy* (20 min): Discuss why some medicines look like candy and why only adults should give medicine.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 105, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Read a new opinion article independently and fill in an organizer listing the author's claim, three reasons and the evidence for each.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on decomposing fractions and mixed numbers.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a CER comparing how fast water, wind and ice eroded material in this week's models, using measurements.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 6 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 6 test on trails, the Bear Flag Revolt and the war.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Listen to 3 new pieces and identify each form (AB, ABA or rondo) with a labeled diagram.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Score 10 chest, 10 bounce and 10 overhead passes to a partner's target hands.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Quiz on types of medicines and who can give them.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Reasons and evidence in opinion texts
- Objective: Explain how authors use reasons and evidence
- Objective: Distinguish fact from opinion
- Vocabulary: opinion, reason, evidence, fact
- Materials: opinion articles from CommonLit or newspaper kids' sections
- Assessment: Reasons and evidence analysis.

### Math 4: Decomposing fractions
- Objective: Decompose fractions into unit fractions in more than one way
- Objective: Write mixed numbers as fractions
- Vocabulary: unit fraction, decompose, mixed number, improper fraction
- Materials: fraction strips
- Assessment: Decomposition quiz.

### Science 4: Erosion by water, wind and ice
- Objective: Measure rates of erosion
- Objective: Compare agents
- Vocabulary: erosion, deposition, agent, rate
- Materials: baking pan, sand, soil, water bottle, straw, ice
- Assessment: Erosion CER.

### California, A Changing State: The Mexican-American War and the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo
- Objective: Explain how California became U.S. territory
- Objective: Identify effects of the treaty
- Vocabulary: war, treaty, cession, territory
- Materials: maps of North America 1846 and 1848
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Art & Music 4: Musical form: AB, ABA and rondo
- Objective: Identify musical forms by listening
- Objective: Show form with movement and symbols
- Vocabulary: form, section, AB, ABA, rondo, repeat
- Materials: recordings, colored paper shapes
- Assessment: Form identification check.

### PE 4: Passing and receiving
- Objective: Use chest, bounce and overhead passes
- Objective: Pass to a moving teammate
- Vocabulary: chest pass, bounce pass, overhead pass, target hands
- Materials: basketball, wall, partner
- Assessment: Pass accuracy score.

### Health 4: What medicines are
- Objective: Distinguish prescription and over-the-counter medicines
- Objective: Explain why medicines are used
- Vocabulary: medicine, prescription, over-the-counter, pharmacist, dosage
- Materials: empty OTC medicine box (with adult), prescription label sample
- Assessment: Medicine quiz.
