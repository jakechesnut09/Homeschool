## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Coyote tales* (90 min): Read a California Native Coyote story (e.g., how Coyote brought fire) and identify the trickster pattern.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Bar models* (60 min): Draw tape diagrams for 4 comparison and part-whole problems and write an equation with a letter for the unknown.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Light a bulb* (45 min): Use a battery, one wire and a bulb to find 4 ways to light the bulb and draw them.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Legends* (45 min): Read a Chumash or Miwok creation story and identify what it teaches about the natural world.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *High C and D* (30 min): Learn high C and D fingerings and play scales B-C-D.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Ultimate disc basics* (30 min): Learn ultimate disc rules (no running with the disc, pivot foot) and play a 2-on-2 game.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Breakfast power* (20 min): Read about how breakfast fuels the brain and list 5 quick healthy breakfasts.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Anansi* (90 min): Read an Anansi tale from West Africa and record its events and theme.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Two-step problems* (60 min): Solve 5 two-step problems (e.g., a library had 12,450 books, gave away 1,375, bought 2,060) and estimate first.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Conductor test* (45 min): Build a circuit tester and test 12 household objects as conductors or insulators.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Oral tradition* (45 min): Discuss how stories passed down orally preserve history and values.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Mary Had a Little Lamb* (30 min): Play 'Mary Had a Little Lamb' using B, A and G from notation, keeping a steady beat.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Sportsmanship talk* (30 min): List 5 sportsmanship behaviors and practice them in a game.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Make breakfast* (20 min): Prepare a healthy breakfast with 3 food groups.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Compare* (90 min): Complete a Venn diagram comparing the two tricksters' themes and patterns of events.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Write your own* (60 min): Write two multistep word problems using real data (state park visitors) and trade solutions with a parent.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Switches* (45 min): Make a paper clip switch and add it to a circuit.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Primary vs secondary* (45 min): Sort 10 sources (basket, photograph, textbook, oral story recording) as primary or secondary.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse 'Merrily We Roll Along' and 'Ode to Joy' excerpt.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Hot potato relay* (30 min): Play a timed throwing and catching relay with family.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Eat the rainbow* (20 min): Track the colors of fruits and vegetables you eat for 3 days.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 34, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Point of view* (90 min): Rewrite a short scene of the Coyote story in first person as Coyote and discuss what changes.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 2 review* (60 min): Mixed review of addition, subtraction and word problems with a self-check answer key.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *PhET circuits* (45 min): Build series and parallel circuits in the Circuit Construction Kit.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Ceremonies* (45 min): Read about a ceremony such as the World Renewal ceremony of the Karuk and Hupa.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Record and evaluate* (30 min): Record your playing and evaluate tone and rhythm accuracy.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Skill review* (30 min): Review throwing and catching cues by teaching them to a sibling or parent.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Taste test* (20 min): Taste-test 3 new fruits or vegetables and rate them.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 35, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a compare/contrast paragraph about the two tales citing details.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 2 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 2 test on algorithms and multistep problems.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Draw a circuit diagram and explain how energy travels.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a reflection on how legends connect people and place.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Play 'Mary Had a Little Lamb' and 'Merrily We Roll Along' for a parent, who checks tone and steady beat.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Unit assessment* (30 min): Complete a throwing and catching skills test and a sportsmanship self-rating.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Coyote and trickster tales across cultures
- Objective: Compare themes and patterns in tales from different cultures
- Objective: Compare first- and third-person narration
- Vocabulary: trickster, culture, compare, point of view
- Materials: California Indian Coyote stories (library collection, e.g., Coyote tales), Anansi tales (library), Venn diagram
- Assessment: Compare/contrast paragraph.

### Math 4: Multistep addition and subtraction problems
- Objective: Represent problems with equations using a letter for the unknown
- Objective: Assess reasonableness
- Vocabulary: equation, variable, unknown, reasonable
- Materials: bar model templates
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Science 4: Electric circuits
- Objective: Build simple circuits
- Objective: Show electricity transfers energy to light, sound and motion
- Vocabulary: circuit, battery, conductor, closed circuit, switch
- Materials: D-cell batteries, small bulbs or LEDs, wires, foil, paper clips, buzzer
- Assessment: Circuit diagram and conductor data.

### California, A Changing State: Beliefs, legends and oral tradition
- Objective: Explain legends and beliefs of California Indians
- Objective: Distinguish primary and secondary sources
- Vocabulary: legend, oral tradition, ceremony, primary source
- Materials: collection of California Indian stories (library), primary/secondary source cards
- Assessment: Source sort and reflection.

### Art & Music 4: Recorder songs with B-A-G and C, D
- Objective: Play songs with B, A, G, high C and D
- Objective: Read rhythms in songs
- Vocabulary: high C, high D, phrase, slur
- Materials: recorder, song sheets
- Assessment: Recorded songs and self-evaluation.

### PE 4: Throwing and catching games
- Objective: Apply skills in games
- Objective: Show sportsmanship
- Vocabulary: teamwork, sportsmanship, strategy
- Materials: soft balls, cones, flying disc
- Assessment: Skills test and sportsmanship rating.

### Health 4: Breakfast, fruits and vegetables
- Objective: Explain the benefits of breakfast
- Objective: Increase fruits and vegetables
- Vocabulary: breakfast, fiber, eat the rainbow, variety
- Materials: breakfast foods, fruits and vegetables, chart
- Assessment: Rainbow tracker and paragraph.
