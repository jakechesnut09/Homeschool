## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Organize* (90 min): Arrange notes into an outline with an introduction, 3 headed sections and a conclusion.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Choose a strategy* (60 min): Solve 6 problems choosing area model, partial products or algorithm and justify one choice in writing.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Define challenge* (45 min): Challenge: send a 5-word message across the yard without speaking.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Mission map* (45 min): Map all 21 missions from San Diego de Alcalá (1769) to Sonoma (1823) along El Camino Real and label founding dates.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Criteria* (30 min): Develop criteria for a successful landscape (depth, color, mood, technique).
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Rope sizing & basic bounce* (30 min): Size the rope (handles reach armpits when standing on its center) and practice the basic two-foot bounce for 4 sets of 30 seconds.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *I-messages* (20 min): Practice I-messages to ask for healthy food ('I feel more energy when we have fruit at breakfast; could we buy berries?').

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Draft sections* (90 min): Draft the introduction and two sections using linking words (also, for example, because).
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Multistep problems* (60 min): Solve 4 multistep problems combining multiplication with addition or subtraction.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Brainstorm* (45 min): Brainstorm 3 solutions and list pros and cons.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Site factors* (45 min): Identify why missions were placed near water, fertile soil and Native villages.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Evaluate masters* (30 min): Apply the criteria to 3 landscapes by different artists (Bierstadt, Hokusai, a California Impressionist).
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Skier and bell* (30 min): Learn the skier (side-to-side) and bell (front-to-back) jumps and do 20 of each without missing.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Role-play* (20 min): Role-play asking for a healthy option at a restaurant, a friend's house and the grocery store.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Finish draft* (90 min): Draft the last section and conclusion; add one diagram with a caption.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Real-world model* (60 min): Plan a class field trip budget: tickets for 24 students at $14 plus 3 buses at $215.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Test* (45 min): Test each solution and record speed and accuracy.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Spread of influence* (45 min): Explain how missions expanded Spain's and Catholicism's influence across New Spain.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Self-evaluate* (30 min): Apply the same criteria to your own landscape.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Criss-cross* (30 min): Practice the criss-cross by crossing arms at the waist on every third jump; count successful crosses.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Family traditions* (20 min): Interview a family member about a traditional family dish and identify its food groups.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Revise & publish* (90 min): Revise for precise vocabulary, edit, and type the final report.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 3 review* (60 min): Mixed review game: comparison, factors, multiplication strategies.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Improve* (45 min): Improve the most accurate solution to send the message faster, then retest it twice.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Calisphere images* (45 min): Study images from the Calisphere set 'California Missions' and compare two missions' layouts.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Revise* (30 min): Add finishing details to improve one criterion.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Endurance intervals* (30 min): Jump five 30-second intervals with 30-second rests, recording jumps per interval and heart rate.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Healthy twist* (20 min): Suggest one healthy twist to the family recipe and discuss it with the cook.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 60, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Present & assess* (90 min): Present the report to family; take the Unit 3 informational reading test.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 3 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 3 test on multiplicative comparison, factors and multiples, and multi-digit multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Unit 3 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 3 test on waves and information.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on mission locations and placement factors.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Present the landscape and evaluation to family.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform a 4-trick routine (bounce, skier, bell, criss-cross) for a parent who scores it.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Unit assessment* (20 min): Take the Unit 3 test on labels, influences and communication.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Writing an explanatory report
- Objective: Write an informative text with headings, facts and a conclusion
- Objective: Use precise vocabulary
- Vocabulary: introduction, heading, linking words, conclusion
- Materials: notes, computer
- Assessment: Explanatory report (rubric) and Unit 3 test.

### Math 4: Multiplication problem solving
- Objective: Solve multistep multiplication problems
- Objective: Explain strategies
- Vocabulary: strategy, multistep, equation
- Materials: grid paper
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Science 4: Designing an information system
- Objective: Generate and compare solutions to send information
- Objective: Test reliability
- Vocabulary: solution, reliability, criteria
- Materials: flashlight, string, cups, paper
- Assessment: Design comparison and Unit 3 test.

### California, A Changing State: Mapping the missions
- Objective: Map the 21 missions
- Objective: Explain why sites were chosen
- Vocabulary: El Camino Real, mission, site
- Materials: California map, mission list
- Assessment: Mission map quiz.

### Art & Music 4: Evaluating landscape art
- Objective: Apply criteria to evaluate multiple artworks
- Vocabulary: criteria, evaluate, interpret, mood
- Materials: artwork images, finished landscapes
- Assessment: Written evaluation.

### PE 4: Individual jump rope
- Objective: Perform basic and intermediate short-rope jumps
- Objective: Keep a steady rhythm
- Vocabulary: rhythm, rebound, skier, criss-cross
- Materials: jump rope sized to height, stopwatch
- Assessment: Scored 4-trick routine and interval log.

### Health 4: Communicating about food at home
- Objective: Ask for healthy options respectfully
- Objective: Appreciate cultural food traditions
- Vocabulary: I-message, request, culture, tradition
- Materials: health journal, family recipe
- Assessment: Unit 3 test and family interview.
