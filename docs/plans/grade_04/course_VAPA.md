# Art & Music 4 (Grade 4)

Grade 4 visual art and music aligned to the 2019 California Arts Standards. Units alternate between visual art (drawing, color and landscape, cultural art forms of California, sculpture and printmaking) and music (rhythm and meter, beginning soprano recorder, melody and notation, composition, musical traditions of California). The year ends with a curated art exhibition and a recorder concert for family.

*Framework:* 2019 California Arts Standards for Public Schools, Prekindergarten Through Grade Twelve (Visual Arts and Music); 2020 CA Arts Framework  
*Time:* 30 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Drawing: Line, Shape and Value (weeks 1–4)
Artists use line, shape and value to show what they observe and imagine.

- Week 1: Line and contour drawing
- Week 2: Shape and form: shading with value
- Week 3: Observational drawing: nature study
- Week 4: Drawing critique and revision

### Unit 2: Rhythm, Meter and Beginning Recorder (weeks 5–8)
Music is organized by steady beat, rhythm and meter, and the recorder lets us play melodies.

- Week 5: Steady beat, rhythm and meter
- Week 6: Recorder basics: B, A, G
- Week 7: Recorder songs with B-A-G and C, D
- Week 8: Rhythm ensemble performance

### Unit 3: Color and the California Landscape (weeks 9–12)
Artists use color, space and media to express feelings about places.

- Week 9: Color theory
- Week 10: Landscape space: foreground, middle ground, background
- Week 11: Watercolor landscape and Ansel Adams photography
- Week 12: Evaluating landscape art

### Unit 4: Melody, Notation and California Folk Songs (weeks 13–16)
Melodies move by step and skip, and songs carry the stories of their time and place.

- Week 13: Melody: steps, skips and leaps
- Week 14: Gold Rush songs
- Week 15: Expressive qualities: dynamics and tempo
- Week 16: Melody unit performance

### Unit 5: Art and Culture in California (weeks 17–20)
Art from many cultures in California reflects traditions, beliefs and community.

- Week 17: California Native basketry and pattern
- Week 18: Papel picado and Mexican folk art
- Week 19: Chinese brush painting
- Week 20: Community mural design

### Unit 6: Composing Music and Musical Form (weeks 21–24)
Composers organize musical ideas into forms like AB, ABA and rondo.

- Week 21: Musical form: AB, ABA and rondo
- Week 22: Composing rhythms and melodies
- Week 23: Composing in ABA form
- Week 24: Presenting compositions and evaluating music

### Unit 7: Sculpture, Printmaking and Built Environments (weeks 25–28)
Three-dimensional art and prints transform materials, and architecture shapes how communities look.

- Week 25: Clay sculpture: form and texture
- Week 26: Printmaking
- Week 27: Built environments: California architecture
- Week 28: Sculpture revision and installation

### Unit 8: Musical Traditions of California and the World (weeks 29–32)
Music connects to the cultures, purposes and experiences of the people who make it.

- Week 29: Mariachi and Mexican music in California
- Week 30: Taiko, gamelan and Asian American music
- Week 31: Jazz, blues and California sounds
- Week 32: Music and connections

### Unit 9: Exhibition and Concert (weeks 33–36)
Artists and musicians select, refine and present their best work to an audience.

- Week 33: Curating a portfolio
- Week 34: Preparing the exhibition
- Week 35: Concert preparation
- Week 36: Exhibition opening and recorder concert

## Standards

- **MU:Cn10.0.4** Demonstrate how interests, knowledge and skills relate to personal choices and intent when creating, performing and responding to music.
- **MU:Cn11.0.4** Demonstrate understanding of relationships between music and the other arts, other disciplines, varied contexts and daily life.
- **MU:Cr1.1.4** Improvise rhythmic, melodic and harmonic ideas, and explain connection to specific purpose and context.
- **MU:Cr2.1.4** Demonstrate selected and organized musical ideas for an improvisation, arrangement or composition to express intent; use standard and/or iconic notation to document them.
- **MU:Cr3.1.4** Evaluate, refine and document revisions to personal music, applying teacher-provided and collaboratively developed criteria and feedback.
- **MU:Cr3.2.4** Present the final version of personal created music to others, and explain connection to expressive intent.
- **MU:Pr4.1.4** Demonstrate and explain how the selection of music to perform is influenced by personal interest, knowledge, context and technical skill.
- **MU:Pr4.2.4** Demonstrate understanding of the structure and the elements of music (rhythm, pitch and form) in music selected for performance; read and perform using iconic and standard notation.
- **MU:Pr4.3.4** Demonstrate and explain how intent is conveyed through interpretive decisions and expressive qualities (dynamics, tempo, timbre, articulation/style).
- **MU:Pr5.1.4** Apply teacher-provided and collaboratively developed criteria and feedback to evaluate accuracy and expressiveness of ensemble and personal performances; rehearse to refine.
- **MU:Pr6.1.4** Perform music, alone or with others, with expression, technical accuracy and appropriate interpretation; demonstrate performance decorum and audience etiquette.
- **MU:Re7.1.4** Demonstrate and explain how selected music connects to and is influenced by specific interests, experiences, purposes or contexts.
- **MU:Re7.2.4** Demonstrate and explain how responses to music are informed by the structure, the use of the elements of music and context (social and cultural).
- **MU:Re8.1.4** Demonstrate and explain how the expressive qualities (dynamics, tempo, timbre, articulation) are used in performers' and personal interpretations to reflect expressive intent.
- **MU:Re9.1.4** Evaluate musical works and performances, applying established criteria, and explain appropriateness to the context.
- **VA:Cn10.1.4** Create works of art that reflect community cultural traditions.
- **VA:Cn11.1.4** Through observation, infer information about time, place and culture in which a work of art was created.
- **VA:Cr1.1.4** Brainstorm multiple approaches to a creative art or design problem.
- **VA:Cr1.2.4** Collaboratively set goals and create artwork that is meaningful and has purpose to the makers.
- **VA:Cr2.1.4** Explore and invent art-making techniques and approaches.
- **VA:Cr2.2.4** When making works of art, utilize and care for materials, tools and equipment in a manner that prevents danger to oneself and others.
- **VA:Cr2.3.4** Document, describe and represent regional constructed environments.
- **VA:Cr3.1.4** Revise artwork in progress on the basis of insights gained through peer discussion.
- **VA:Pr4.1.4** Analyze how past, present and emerging technologies have impacted the preservation and presentation of artwork.
- **VA:Pr5.1.4** Analyze the various considerations for presenting and protecting art in various locations, indoor or outdoor settings, in temporary or permanent forms.
- **VA:Pr6.1.4** Compare and contrast purposes of art museums, art galleries and other venues, as well as the types of personal experiences they provide.
- **VA:Re7.1.4** Compare responses to a work of art before and after working in similar media.
- **VA:Re7.2.4** Analyze components in visual imagery that convey messages.
- **VA:Re8.1.4** Interpret art by referring to contextual information and analyzing relevant subject matter, characteristics of form and use of media.
- **VA:Re9.1.4** Apply one set of criteria to evaluate more than one work of art.
