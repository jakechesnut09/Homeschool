## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *The Golden Touch* (90 min): Read Hawthorne's 'The Golden Touch' (King Midas) and summarize it in a 5-box comic strip.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Regrouping with blocks* (60 min): Model 5,203 - 1,476 with blocks, then record with the standard algorithm.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Hot water spoons* (45 min): Place metal, plastic and wooden spoons in hot water and feel which handle warms first.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Tomol canoes* (45 min): Read about the Chumash tomol plank canoe and draw a labeled diagram.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Recorder care* (30 min): Learn posture, left-hand-on-top hold and gentle 'doo' tonguing; play B.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Lead passes* (30 min): Throw to a partner jogging across the yard, aiming ahead of them.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *MyPlate* (20 min): Label a paper plate with the 5 MyPlate food groups and recommended proportions.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Pandora* (90 min): Read 'The Paradise of Children' (Pandora) and determine its theme.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Across zeros* (60 min): Solve 12 problems like 40,000 - 13,586 and explain the regrouping in one.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Ice cube race* (45 min): Wrap ice cubes in foil, cloth and paper and time melting.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Basketry* (45 min): Study basket images from the Calisphere set 'Native Americans: Arts and Traditions' and describe patterns and uses.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *B and A* (30 min): Play B and A in quarter and half notes from notation.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Disc throw* (30 min): Learn the backhand flying-disc throw and complete 20 partner throws.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Food group collage* (20 min): Cut magazine pictures and glue them into the correct food groups.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Allusions* (90 min): Explain 8 mythological allusions (Midas touch, Pandora's box, Achilles' heel, Herculean) and use 4 in sentences.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Check with addition* (60 min): Check each of 8 differences by adding the difference to the subtrahend.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Thermometer data* (45 min): Measure temperature of warm water every 2 minutes as it cools and graph.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Land stewardship* (45 min): Read about controlled burns and explain how they improved plant and animal resources.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Add G* (30 min): Add G and play 'Hot Cross Buns' from the staff.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Football spiral* (30 min): Practice gripping and throwing a junior football in a spiral.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Portions* (20 min): Measure portion sizes of cereal, milk and fruit and compare to what you usually eat.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Greek roots* (90 min): Learn roots tele, photo, graph, micro, scope and build 12 words.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Error analysis* (60 min): Find and fix the mistakes in 5 worked subtraction problems and write what the student did wrong.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Insulator design* (45 min): Design a cup cozy to keep water warm and test it.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Trade routes* (45 min): Map trade of shell beads, obsidian and salt between nations.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Note reading* (30 min): Write letter names for B, A, G on a staff worksheet.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Target game* (30 min): Play a 'hit the cone' accuracy game from three distances.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Cultural foods* (20 min): Identify food groups in 3 cultural dishes (tamales, stir-fry, pasta).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 30, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Quiz on allusions and Greek roots; 1-minute fluency read of a myth passage.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on subtraction including across zeros.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on conductors, insulators and heat transfer.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz on technology, trade and environmental management.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Play 'Hot Cross Buns' and 'Gently Sleep' for a parent.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Score accuracy on 10 throws to a moving partner.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take a quiz naming the five food groups, example foods and the plate proportions for each.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Greek myths and allusions
- Objective: Read Greek myths
- Objective: Explain words that allude to mythology
- Vocabulary: myth, allusion, Herculean, Pandora's box, Midas touch
- Materials: A Wonder-Book for Girls and Boys (Hawthorne, Gutenberg) or D'Aulaires' Book of Greek Myths (library)
- Assessment: Allusion and roots quiz.

### Math 4: Subtraction with the standard algorithm
- Objective: Subtract multi-digit numbers including across zeros
- Objective: Check with addition
- Vocabulary: difference, regroup, inverse operation
- Materials: base-ten blocks, grid paper
- Assessment: Subtraction quiz plus error-analysis task.

### Science 4: Heat energy
- Objective: Observe heat transfer
- Objective: Compare conductors and insulators
- Vocabulary: heat, conductor, insulator, temperature
- Materials: thermometer, ice cubes, metal spoon, plastic spoon, wooden spoon, cups, foil, cloth
- Assessment: Insulator design results and quiz.

### California, A Changing State: Trade, technology and the environment
- Objective: Describe how California Indians modified the environment
- Objective: Explain trade networks
- Vocabulary: trade, tomol, basketry, controlled burn, shell money
- Materials: Calisphere 'Native Americans: Arts and Traditions' set, paper, yarn
- Assessment: Trade and technology quiz.

### Art & Music 4: Recorder basics: B, A, G
- Objective: Hold and play the recorder with good tone
- Objective: Play B, A and G
- Vocabulary: recorder, embouchure, tonguing, fingering
- Materials: soprano recorder, fingering chart, music staff sheet
- Assessment: Recorder playing check.

### PE 4: Throwing to a moving target
- Objective: Lead a moving partner
- Objective: Combine throwing and catching
- Vocabulary: lead pass, target, accuracy
- Materials: flying disc, football, cones
- Assessment: Moving-target accuracy score.

### Health 4: MyPlate food groups
- Objective: Name the five food groups
- Objective: Describe recommended amounts
- Vocabulary: MyPlate, fruits, vegetables, grains, protein, dairy, portion
- Materials: paper plate, MyPlate Kids poster, magazines
- Assessment: MyPlate quiz.
