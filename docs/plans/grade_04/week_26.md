## Day 1 — Monday (school day 126, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *The journey* (90 min): Read chapters 7-9 and track obstacles and how friends solve them.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Money model* (60 min): Use dimes and pennies to show 3/10 and 30/100 of a dollar and write as decimals.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Shake table* (45 min): Build toothpick-and-marshmallow buildings and test on a gelatin shake table.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Comparing governments* (45 min): Compare who made laws and who had rights under Spanish, Mexican and early American rule in a 3-column chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Printmaking history* (30 min): Look at Japanese woodblock prints and discuss repetition.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Plan* (30 min): Choose music and plan 4 eight-count sections using changes in level, pathway, tempo and force.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Secondhand smoke* (20 min): Learn how secondhand smoke and vape aerosol affect others, especially children.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 127, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Idioms* (90 min): Learn 12 idioms and write a short dialogue for the characters using four.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Hundred grids* (60 min): Shade 10 hundred grids for given decimals and write each as a fraction and decimal.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Improve* (45 min): Add cross-bracing to the toothpick buildings and retest on the gelatin shake table, counting seconds until collapse.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Who was left out* (45 min): Discuss how statehood laws limited rights of Native Californians, Chinese immigrants and women.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Design* (30 min): Brainstorm 3 designs and choose one; remember it will print in reverse.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Teach* (30 min): Teach your dance to a family member one eight-count at a time.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Smoke-free laws* (20 min): Read about California's smoke-free laws for restaurants, parks and cars with kids.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 128, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *The Emerald City* (90 min): Read chapters 10-11 and discuss the Wizard's demands.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Tenths to hundredths* (60 min): Rename tenths as hundredths and add (3/10 + 4/100 = 34/100) in 10 problems.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Compare solutions* (45 min): Build a base-isolation version on rubber bands or marbles and compare its shaking with the braced design.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Moving capital* (45 min): Trace the capital's moves (San Jose, Vallejo, Benicia, Sacramento) and examine images from the Calisphere set 'Civic Buildings in California History'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Carve plate* (30 min): Incise the design into a foam plate with a dull pencil.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse the full dance with music and smooth out the transitions between sections.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Social norms data* (20 min): Read survey data showing most California students have never used tobacco and graph it.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 129, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Wicked Witch* (90 min): Read chapter 12 and describe the turning point.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Decimals on a number line* (60 min): Place 0.3, 0.45, 0.7 and 0.09 on a number line from 0 to 1.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Earthquake kit* (45 min): Plan a family earthquake kit and practice 'Drop, Cover and Hold On' as in California's Great ShakeOut drill.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Seal and symbols* (45 min): Analyze the Great Seal of California and explain each symbol.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Print* (30 min): Roll paint and pull an edition of 4 prints in different colors.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Perform* (30 min): Perform the dance for an audience and ask them to identify one level and one pathway change.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Avoiding smoke* (20 min): Role-play 3 polite ways to avoid secondhand smoke or vape aerosol (move away, ask to open a window, tell a trusted adult).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 130, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a quiz explaining 6 idioms and answering text-dependent questions on chapters 7-12.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz writing 10 fractions with denominators 10 and 100 as decimals and vice versa.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a comparison of bracing and base isolation using test data, cost and materials.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 8 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 8 test on the 1849 convention, the Compromise of 1850 and how the new government differed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Sign and number the edition and choose the best print.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Unit assessment* (30 min): Self-assess the dance with a rubric and complete a dance steps check.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Unit assessment* (20 min): Take the Unit 7 test on effects of tobacco, vaping and secondhand smoke and on social norms.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Oz chapters 7-12
- Objective: Analyze conflict
- Objective: Interpret idioms and figurative language
- Vocabulary: idiom, conflict, Kalidahs
- Materials: novel
- Assessment: Idioms and reading quiz.

### Math 4: Tenths and hundredths
- Objective: Write fractions with denominators 10 and 100 as decimals
- Objective: Convert tenths to hundredths
- Vocabulary: decimal, tenth, hundredth, decimal point
- Materials: hundred grids, dimes and pennies
- Assessment: Decimal notation quiz.

### Science 4: Reducing hazard impacts
- Objective: Compare solutions to reduce earthquake damage
- Objective: Test designs
- Vocabulary: hazard, retrofit, base isolation, engineer
- Materials: toothpicks, mini marshmallows, gelatin pan
- Assessment: Shake table results and comparison.

### California, A Changing State: New government compared
- Objective: Compare Spanish, Mexican and American governments in California
- Objective: Locate the capital's moves
- Vocabulary: governor, legislature, rights, capital
- Materials: Calisphere set 'Civic Buildings in California History', comparison chart
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Art & Music 4: Printmaking
- Objective: Create relief prints
- Objective: Explore repetition and pattern
- Vocabulary: relief print, brayer, plate, edition, reverse
- Materials: foam meat trays or styrofoam sheets, pencil, tempera paint, brayer or roller
- Assessment: Print edition.

### PE 4: Create a dance
- Objective: Design a dance using level, pathway, tempo and force
- Objective: Perform with a group
- Vocabulary: choreography, level, pathway, tempo, force
- Materials: music of your choice, planning sheet
- Assessment: Original dance (rubric).

### Health 4: Secondhand smoke and social norms
- Objective: Describe dangers of secondhand smoke
- Objective: Recognize that most youth do not use tobacco
- Vocabulary: secondhand smoke, social norm, smoke-free
- Materials: survey data chart, poster paper
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.
