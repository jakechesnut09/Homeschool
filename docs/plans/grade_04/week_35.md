## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Fluency check* (90 min): Do a final 1-minute fluency read of a grade 4 passage, graph words-correct-per-minute, and compare with the week 1 baseline.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Number patterns* (60 min): Generate patterns for 'start at 1, add 3' and describe why terms alternate odd and even.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Redesign* (45 min): Change one variable (blade angle, number of blades or axle material) to fix the biggest failure point.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Executive* (45 min): Read about the governor and other elected officials (lieutenant governor, attorney general).
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Choose program* (30 min): Choose 4 pieces (a folk song, a classical excerpt, your ABA composition and a free choice) and explain why each belongs.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Fitness components quiz* (30 min): Take a quiz on the five fitness components and heart rate concepts.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Slogan* (20 min): Write a catchy slogan and a one-sentence key message for the campaign and test it on a family member.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Reading reflection* (90 min): Write a one-page reflection on the favorite book of the year, citing two scenes that made it memorable.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Shape patterns* (60 min): Build growing patterns with pattern blocks and predict the 10th figure.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Retest* (45 min): Run 5 new trials under the same conditions and record results.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Legislature* (45 min): Read about the Assembly and Senate and how a bill becomes law.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Targeted practice* (30 min): Practice the two hardest passages slowly with a metronome, raising the tempo 10 beats at a time.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Favorite activities* (30 min): List 10 activities you enjoy and which fitness component each builds.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Poster* (20 min): Create a campaign poster with the slogan, three facts with sources and a clear visual.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 173, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Book talk* (90 min): Give a 2-minute book talk recommending a book read this year, including a short read-aloud excerpt.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Input/output tables* (60 min): Complete 6 tables and write the rule for each.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Compare results* (45 min): Graph before-and-after averages and decide whether the change helped.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Your legislators* (45 min): Look up your state Assembly member and senator and their committee roles.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Feedback round* (30 min): Record a run-through, get feedback from a family member, and mark dynamics changes on the music.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Summer plan* (30 min): Write a 4-week summer plan with 60 minutes of activity daily using frequency, intensity, time and type.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Video or skit* (20 min): Record a 1-minute public service video or rehearse a short skit that models the healthy behavior.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 174, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *End-of-year reading* (90 min): Take the end-of-year reading assessment with a literary and an informational passage.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Pattern project* (60 min): Design an original pattern with a rule and write 3 observations about it.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Present* (45 min): Present the device, explaining each energy conversion (wind to rotation to lifting) and the data.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Courts* (45 min): Read about the California Supreme Court and state courts and explain the job of the judicial branch.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Design program* (30 min): Design a printed concert program with piece titles, composers and short program notes.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Try something new* (30 min): Try a new activity (hiking trail, swimming, skating) and log heart rate.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Fact check* (20 min): Fact-check every statistic and claim against KidsHealth, MyPlate, FDA or NIDA and correct any errors.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 175, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Summer list* (90 min): Browse the library and build a summer reading list of 10 books across genres, with a reason for each.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on patterns and rules.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Reflect* (45 min): Write a reflection on what you would change with more time and materials.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a letter to your state legislator about an issue that matters to you.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Dress rehearsal* (30 min): Hold a full dress rehearsal including bows and spoken introductions, and fix one remaining problem.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Present your plan to family and post it on the fridge.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Show the materials to two people, collect one suggestion from each, and revise the poster and video.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Fluency and reading reflection
- Objective: Measure fluency growth
- Objective: Reflect on reading
- Vocabulary: fluency, reflection, goal
- Materials: grade 4 passages, reading log
- Assessment: End-of-year reading assessment.

### Math 4: Patterns and rules
- Objective: Generate number and shape patterns from a rule
- Objective: Explain features of patterns
- Vocabulary: rule, pattern, term, input/output
- Materials: pattern blocks, graph paper
- Assessment: Pattern quiz plus project.

### Science 4: Improving and presenting the design
- Objective: Improve a prototype based on test data
- Objective: Communicate the design process
- Vocabulary: iterate, redesign, improve, energy conversion
- Materials: prototype, materials, poster board or slides
- Assessment: Engineering presentation and reflection (rubric).

### California, A Changing State: State government
- Objective: Explain the structures and functions of state government
- Vocabulary: governor, legislature, Assembly, Senate, bill
- Materials: state government website, map of district
- Assessment: Letter to legislator.

### Art & Music 4: Concert preparation
- Objective: Select concert repertoire based on interest, context and skill
- Objective: Rehearse and refine using feedback
- Vocabulary: program, repertoire, rehearsal, dress rehearsal
- Materials: recorder, all music from the year, program paper
- Assessment: Dress rehearsal checklist.

### PE 4: Lifetime activity plan
- Objective: Explain how heart rate and fitness components connect
- Objective: Plan summer physical activity
- Vocabulary: lifetime activity, frequency, intensity, time, type
- Materials: calendar template, heart rate log
- Assessment: Summer activity plan.

### Health 4: Creating the campaign
- Objective: Create campaign materials
- Objective: Use accurate information
- Vocabulary: slogan, persuasion, accurate
- Materials: poster paper, markers, device for video
- Assessment: Campaign materials.
