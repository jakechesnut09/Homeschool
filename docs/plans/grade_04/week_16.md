## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Chapters 23-25* (90 min): Read and track how Karana's attitude toward animals changes.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Plan the steps* (60 min): Solve 4 multistep problems by first writing a plan and an equation with a letter for the unknown.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Celery transport* (45 min): Put celery in colored water and observe xylem transport after 24 hours.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Independence* (45 min): Read about the 1810-1821 Mexican War for Independence.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Choose repertoire* (30 min): Choose two recorder pieces learned this unit and explain why they fit your skill level and interests.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Bases of support* (30 min): Hold 8 balances on different body parts (two hands and one foot, one knee and one hand) for 5 seconds each.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Menu choices* (20 min): Read a restaurant kids' menu and choose a balanced meal and drink.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 77, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Chapters 26-29* (90 min): Finish the novel and discuss whether the ending is happy, sad or both.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Remainders in context* (60 min): Solve 'packing boxes' problems: 1,250 oranges packed 8 per bag; how many full bags and how many left?
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Flower dissection* (45 min): Dissect a flower and label petals, stamens and pistil.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Map boundaries* (45 min): Compare maps of North America before and after 1821.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Section rehearsal* (30 min): Rehearse the hardest 4 measures of each piece slowly, then at tempo.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Stable or not?* (30 min): Test narrow vs wide stances and explain which is more stable using 'center of gravity'.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Portions out* (20 min): Compare restaurant portion sizes with MyPlate recommendations.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 78, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Summary* (90 min): Write a one-paragraph summary of the whole novel.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Performance task* (60 min): Plan a 3-day camping trip at Yosemite for 7 people: food packs, tent spaces and cost per person.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Root systems* (45 min): Compare roots of grass and a carrot and explain their functions.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Effects* (45 min): List effects on Alta California's government and trade.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Record* (30 min): Record a full run-through of both pieces without stopping.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Yoga balances* (30 min): Practice tree, warrior III and boat pose, holding each for 15 seconds.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Fast food swaps* (20 min): Find 3 healthier swaps at a fast-food restaurant using online nutrition info.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 79, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Fluency* (90 min): Rehearse and record a 2-minute expressive reading of a favorite passage.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 4 review* (60 min): Mixed review of division strategies and multistep problems.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *California plants* (45 min): Research structures of a cactus and a redwood for their environments.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Trade opens* (45 min): Read about hide and tallow trade with Yankee ships (Two Years Before the Mast excerpt).
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Evaluate* (30 min): Listen back and score tone, rhythm, notes and expression with a 4-point rubric; fix two problems.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Safety* (30 min): Learn safe falling (tuck and roll sideways) and spotting rules.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Family meal* (20 min): Plan a screen-free family meal with conversation questions.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 80, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take the novel comprehension test.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 4 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 4 test on division strategies, remainders and multistep problems.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write an argument for how one plant structure supports survival.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Complete a cause-and-effect chart showing how Mexican independence changed Alta California's government, trade and borders.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Perform* (30 min): Perform both pieces for family with a spoken introduction for each.

- [ ] **Health 4** — *Unit assessment* (20 min): Take the Unit 4 test on meal planning and food safety.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Island of the Blue Dolphins, chapters 23-29
- Objective: Summarize the novel
- Objective: Evaluate the ending
- Vocabulary: summary, resolution, survival
- Materials: novel
- Assessment: Novel comprehension test.

### Math 4: Multistep problems with all four operations
- Objective: Solve multistep problems including remainders
- Objective: Represent with equations
- Vocabulary: multistep, interpret, equation
- Materials: bar model templates
- Assessment: Unit 4 test plus camping performance task.

### Science 4: Plant structures
- Objective: Identify internal and external plant structures
- Objective: Explain how they support survival
- Vocabulary: xylem, root, stem, flower, seed
- Materials: celery, food coloring, flowers, magnifying glass
- Assessment: Plant structure argument.

### California, A Changing State: Mexican independence and Alta California
- Objective: Explain effects of the Mexican War for Independence
- Objective: Identify new boundaries
- Vocabulary: independence, territory, boundary, Mexico
- Materials: maps of 1821 North America
- Assessment: Cause-and-effect chart.

### Art & Music 4: Melody unit performance
- Objective: Select, rehearse and perform melodies from notation
- Objective: Evaluate accuracy and expression
- Vocabulary: repertoire, rehearse, accuracy, expression
- Materials: recorder, song sheets, recording device
- Assessment: Recorded and live performance (rubric).

### PE 4: Balance and base of support
- Objective: Hold static balances with different bases of support
- Objective: Explain why wide, low bases are stable
- Vocabulary: base of support, center of gravity, static balance, counterbalance
- Materials: mat or carpet, pillows
- Assessment: Balance demonstration and explanation.

### Health 4: Eating out and family meals
- Objective: Make healthy choices when eating out
- Objective: Value family meals
- Vocabulary: menu, portion, family meal
- Materials: restaurant menu (online)
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.
