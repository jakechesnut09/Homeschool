## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Plan presentation* (90 min): Turn the report into 5 slides or a poster, using one key fact and one visual per section.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Triangles* (60 min): Sort 12 triangles into right, acute and obtuse and recognize right triangles.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Build day 1* (45 min): Build the base and axle of the lifter following the chosen sketch.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *U.S. Constitution* (45 min): Read the Preamble and identify the six goals of the U.S. government.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Venues compared* (30 min): Compare an art museum, a commercial gallery and a community art walk in a chart: purpose, who visits, what experience they give.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Warm-up & PACER* (30 min): Lead your dynamic warm-up and complete the PACER (or 1-mile run) and record the result.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Choose topic* (20 min): Choose a topic: drink water, eat the rainbow, be active, or stay vape-free.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 167, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Rehearse* (90 min): Rehearse the 3-minute talk with a timer, practicing eye contact, volume and pace.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Quadrilaterals* (60 min): Sort quadrilaterals by parallel and perpendicular sides into a hierarchy chart.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Build day 2* (45 min): Attach blades, string and the penny cup and do a first trial run.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Three branches* (45 min): Make a chart of the legislative, executive and judicial branches.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Mat and label* (30 min): Mount the 6 artworks on cardstock mats and write museum-style labels (title, artist, medium, year, one-sentence description).
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Curl-ups & push-ups* (30 min): Complete the cadence curl-up and 90-degree push-up tests and record results.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Audience* (20 min): Identify the audience (family, neighbors, younger kids) and what message will reach them.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 168, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Audio* (90 min): Record a 30-second narrated introduction or add a nature sound clip that fits the wonder.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Symmetry* (60 min): Fold paper shapes and letters to find lines of symmetry and check with a mirror.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Plan a fair test* (45 min): Write a test plan keeping fan distance, fan speed and load the same for every trial.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *California Constitution* (45 min): Read the California Constitution's preamble and Declaration of Rights excerpts.

- [ ] **Health 4** — *Facts* (20 min): Gather 5 facts from reliable sources for the campaign.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Presenting research
- Objective: Present findings with visuals
- Objective: Listen and ask questions
- Vocabulary: presentation, visual aid, eye contact
- Materials: slides or poster
- Assessment: Presentation and Unit 8 test.

### Math 4: Classifying shapes and symmetry
- Objective: Classify triangles and quadrilaterals by sides and angles
- Objective: Draw lines of symmetry
- Vocabulary: right triangle, trapezoid, parallelogram, line of symmetry
- Materials: paper shapes, mirror, scissors
- Assessment: Unit 9 test.

### Science 4: Building and testing the prototype
- Objective: Build a prototype
- Objective: Plan and carry out fair tests controlling variables
- Vocabulary: fair test, variable, trial, failure point
- Materials: cups, straws, skewers, cardstock, string, tape, pennies, box fan, meter stick, stopwatch
- Assessment: Test plan, data table and failure-point analysis.

### California, A Changing State: Constitutions: U.S. and California
- Objective: Explain the U.S. Constitution
- Objective: Compare it with the California Constitution
- Vocabulary: constitution, preamble, branch, rights
- Materials: U.S. Constitution (DocsTeach), California Constitution preamble
- Assessment: Constitution quiz.

### Art & Music 4: Preparing the exhibition
- Objective: Plan how to present and protect art in a chosen space
- Objective: Compare the purposes of museums, galleries and other venues
- Vocabulary: gallery, museum, label, mat, venue
- Materials: cardstock for mats, painter's tape, index cards for labels
- Assessment: Installed exhibition and venue comparison.

### PE 4: Fitness post-assessment
- Objective: Complete end-of-year fitness tests
- Objective: Compare results to baseline
- Vocabulary: post-test, improvement, Healthy Fitness Zone
- Materials: PACER audio or measured course, mat, measuring stick
- Assessment: Post-assessment results and comparison graph.

### Health 4: Planning a health campaign
- Objective: Choose a health message and audience
- Objective: Plan a campaign
- Vocabulary: campaign, message, audience, advocacy
- Materials: planning sheet
- Assessment: Campaign plan.
