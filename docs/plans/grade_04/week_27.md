## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Rescue* (90 min): Read chapters 13-14 and note each friend's contribution.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Grid comparisons* (60 min): Shade grids to compare 0.4 and 0.38 and explain which is greater.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Tsunami model* (45 min): Make a wave in a tub and test sea wall designs.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Overland Mail* (45 min): Read about the Butterfield Overland Mail stagecoach route and its 25-day trip.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Styles* (30 min): Compare images of a mission adobe, a San Francisco Victorian, a Craftsman bungalow and a mid-century modern house.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Grip & bumps* (30 min): Learn the shake-hands grip and bump a ball upward 20 times.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *What is alcohol* (20 min): Learn what alcohol is and why it's illegal for people under 21.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 132, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *The Wizard revealed* (90 min): Read chapters 15-16 and discuss what the Wizard really is.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Meter stick decimals* (60 min): Measure 6 objects in meters to hundredths using a meter stick and order them.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Landslides* (45 min): Model a hillside and test plants/stakes to reduce slides.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Pony Express* (45 min): Read about the 1860-61 Pony Express and calculate how long a letter took from Missouri to Sacramento.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Neighborhood walk* (30 min): Photograph and sketch 4 buildings in your neighborhood.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Forehand* (30 min): Practice forehand strikes against a wall from a sideways stance.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Short-term effects* (20 min): List short-term effects (slowed reaction time, poor balance, poor judgment).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 133, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Gifts* (90 min): Read chapters 17-18 and analyze whether the friends already had what they wanted.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Compare and order* (60 min): Compare 12 decimal pairs and order 3 sets of decimals.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Floods* (45 min): Build a clay or sand levee along a sand river in a pan and test how well it holds back a flood of poured water.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Telegraph* (45 min): Read about the 1861 transcontinental telegraph and send a Morse code message with a flashlight.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Details* (30 min): Draw close-up details (windows, roofs, doors) of one building.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Backhand* (30 min): Practice 30 backhand strikes against a wall, turning the shoulder toward the wall before each swing.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Coordination demo* (20 min): Walk a tape line while spinning first to feel how impaired balance makes tasks hard; discuss.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 134, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Theme* (90 min): Write a paragraph stating a theme (e.g., 'what you seek may already be inside you') supported by two quotations.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Sports times* (60 min): Compare race times from a track meet (e.g., 12.45 vs 12.5 seconds) and rank runners.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Warning systems* (45 min): Read about the NOAA tsunami warning system and tsunami evacuation signs on the California coast.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Compare speeds* (45 min): Make a chart comparing message times by ship, stagecoach, Pony Express and telegraph.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Design a building* (30 min): Design a building for your community and draw its facade.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Rally* (30 min): Rally with a partner over a low net (string between chairs).
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Long-term effects* (20 min): Learn long-term effects on liver, heart and brain.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 135, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Discuss the theme with a parent and revise the paragraph.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz comparing 10 decimal pairs to hundredths and explaining one with a grid model.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Unit 7 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 7 test on reading maps of Earth's features and comparing solutions to natural hazards.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph on why the Pony Express ended after only 18 months.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Explain how your design fits California's climate and community.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Count your longest wall rally with forehand and backhand.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Label a body outline with 6 short- and long-term effects of alcohol on the brain, liver, heart and stomach.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Oz chapters 13-18
- Objective: Determine theme
- Objective: Analyze how characters change
- Vocabulary: theme, humbug, character growth
- Materials: novel
- Assessment: Theme paragraph.

### Math 4: Comparing decimals
- Objective: Compare decimals to hundredths
- Objective: Recognize comparisons valid only for same whole
- Vocabulary: compare, hundredths, equivalent decimal
- Materials: hundred grids, meter stick
- Assessment: Comparing decimals quiz.

### Science 4: Other hazards: tsunamis, landslides, floods
- Objective: Generate solutions for other hazards
- Vocabulary: tsunami, landslide, flood, levee
- Materials: tub, sand, water, craft sticks
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### California, A Changing State: Pony Express, telegraph and mail
- Objective: Explain how communication connected California to the nation
- Vocabulary: Pony Express, telegraph, Overland Mail, stagecoach
- Materials: U.S. map, Morse code chart, stopwatch
- Assessment: Speed chart and paragraph.

### Art & Music 4: Built environments: California architecture
- Objective: Document and represent regional built environments
- Vocabulary: architecture, adobe, Victorian, mid-century, facade
- Materials: camera, sketchbook, images of California buildings
- Assessment: Building design.

### PE 4: Striking with paddles
- Objective: Strike a ball with a paddle using a side-facing stance
- Objective: Control force
- Vocabulary: paddle, forehand, backhand, sideways stance
- Materials: paddle or ping-pong paddle, foam or tennis ball, wall
- Assessment: Wall rally count.

### Health 4: Alcohol and the body
- Objective: Describe short- and long-term effects of alcohol
- Vocabulary: alcohol, intoxication, liver, coordination, reaction time
- Materials: body outline, balance beam (tape line)
- Assessment: Body outline.
