## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Mentor text study* (90 min): Reread 'How the Leopard Got His Spots' and highlight dialogue and description; note how Kipling shows feelings.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Compare by place* (60 min): Line up numbers by place value and compare 12 pairs, writing the symbol and the deciding place.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Marble collisions* (45 min): Roll one marble into a stationary marble in a ruler groove and observe what happens to motion.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Water features* (45 min): Trace the Sacramento and San Joaquin Rivers, the Delta and San Francisco Bay on a map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Nature walk* (30 min): Collect 5 natural objects (leaves, pinecone, feather) on a walk.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *SMART goals* (30 min): Write two SMART fitness goals based on pre-assessment results.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Trusted adults* (20 min): Make a 'helping hand' tracing listing 5 trusted adults and how to reach them.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Draft the middle* (90 min): Draft the narrative's middle events using at least two time-order transitions.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Ordering* (60 min): Order the heights of eight California mountains (e.g., Whitney 14,505 ft) from least to greatest.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Question generation* (45 min): Write 5 testable questions about collisions (mass, speed, number of marbles).
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Passes* (45 min): Locate Donner Pass, Tehachapi Pass and Cajon Pass and explain why travelers needed them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Viewfinder* (30 min): Make a cardstock viewfinder and sketch 4 small compositions of the objects.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Circuit design* (30 min): Set up a 6-station circuit (jumping jacks, plank, wall sit, jump rope, step-ups, crab walk) for 40 seconds each.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Reliable vs unreliable* (20 min): Compare a health ad with a KidsHealth article and list clues that show which is reliable.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Quotation marks* (90 min): Practice punctuating dialogue in 10 sentences and add 3 lines of dialogue to the draft.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Digit card war* (60 min): Play 'Place Value War' with digit cards: each player builds the largest 6-digit number and records the comparison.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Test a question* (45 min): Investigate one question with 3 trials and record results.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Town growth* (45 min): Mark 8 major cities and explain why each grew where it did (harbor, river, pass, valley).
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Detailed study* (30 min): Draw one object large, filling the page, with contour and value.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Circuit day* (30 min): Complete the circuit twice with 20-second rests and log repetitions.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Asking for help* (20 min): Role-play asking a trusted adult for help with a health question.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Show, don't tell* (90 min): Rewrite 4 'telling' sentences as 'showing' sentences with sensory details and revise one draft paragraph.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Number line placement* (60 min): Place 5-digit numbers on an open number line between 40,000 and 50,000 and justify positions.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Sound and heat* (45 min): Clap hands and bang blocks to observe that collisions also make sound and heat.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Primary source map* (45 min): Examine an 1850s map of California from the Library of Congress and compare place names to today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Artist look* (30 min): Look at John James Audubon bird drawings and compare your response before and after your own nature study.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Family challenge* (30 min): Teach a family member the circuit and complete it together.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Health helpers* (20 min): Identify community health helpers (school nurse, pharmacist, doctor, poison control 1-800-222-1222).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 15, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Draft the ending and do a timed fluency read of a passage from the independent book.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz with 10 comparisons using >, =, < and 2 sets of numbers to order.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Explain in writing where the energy went in a car crash test video.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph explaining why Sacramento grew where two rivers meet.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Write 3 sentences on what you noticed by drawing that you didn't notice by looking.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Repeat the circuit and compare repetitions to day 3.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a quiz on reliable sources and trusted adults; check your goal tracker.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Drafting narratives with dialogue and detail
- Objective: Use dialogue and description to develop events
- Objective: Punctuate dialogue correctly
- Vocabulary: dialogue, quotation marks, sensory details, transition
- Materials: writer's notebook, independent reading book
- Assessment: Complete narrative draft with dialogue.

### Math 4: Comparing and ordering large numbers
- Objective: Compare multi-digit numbers using >, =, <
- Objective: Order sets of numbers
- Vocabulary: greater than, less than, compare, order
- Materials: digit cards, number line strips
- Assessment: Comparison and ordering quiz.

### Science 4: Collisions
- Objective: Predict energy changes in collisions
- Objective: Ask testable questions
- Vocabulary: collision, transfer, force, prediction
- Materials: marbles of different sizes, ruler with groove, dominoes
- Assessment: Collision investigation write-up.

### California, A Changing State: Rivers, valleys and mountain passes
- Objective: Locate the Pacific Ocean, rivers, valleys and passes
- Objective: Explain their effect on the growth of towns
- Vocabulary: river, delta, mountain pass, harbor, tributary
- Materials: California physical map, tracing paper
- Assessment: Map analysis paragraph.

### Art & Music 4: Observational drawing: nature study
- Objective: Draw natural objects with detail
- Objective: Brainstorm compositions
- Vocabulary: observation, detail, composition, viewfinder
- Materials: sketchbook, viewfinder cut from cardstock, leaves, shells, pinecones
- Assessment: Nature study drawing and reflection.

### PE 4: Goal setting and circuit training
- Objective: Set SMART fitness goals
- Objective: Complete a fitness circuit
- Vocabulary: SMART goal, circuit, station, repetition
- Materials: cones, jump rope, mat, station cards
- Assessment: Goal sheet and circuit log.

### Health 4: Trusted adults and reliable information
- Objective: Identify trusted adults
- Objective: Recognize reliable health information sources
- Vocabulary: trusted adult, reliable source, advertisement
- Materials: health journal, sample websites and ads
- Assessment: Trusted adult tracing and quiz.
