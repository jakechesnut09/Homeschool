## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Theme* (90 min): Read 'The Cat That Walked by Himself' and determine a theme supported by two details; write a 5-sentence summary.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Rounding on a number line* (60 min): Round 3- and 4-digit numbers to the nearest ten and hundred by marking the two benchmark numbers and halfway point.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Newton's cradle* (45 min): Build a hanging-washer pendulum chain from string and observe energy transfer.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Urban, suburban, rural* (45 min): Compare photos of Los Angeles, Fresno suburbs and a Modoc County ranch and record land use, architecture and transportation.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Criteria* (30 min): Make a 4-point checklist for a strong drawing (fills page, line variety, value range, detail).
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Cues* (30 min): Learn the cues: side to target, arm back, step with opposite foot, rotate hips, follow through.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Nutrient jobs* (20 min): Learn what carbohydrates, proteins and fats do and make a 3-column chart.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise the narrative using a checklist: strong lead, clear sequence, dialogue, satisfying ending.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Round to any place* (60 min): Round 6-digit numbers to the nearest thousand, ten thousand and hundred thousand (15 problems).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Roller coaster design* (45 min): Build a foam pipe insulation marble coaster and measure speed at the bottom.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Population data* (45 min): Read a population density chart and rank 6 counties.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Critique* (30 min): Discuss two of your drawings with a parent using 'I notice / I wonder / I suggest'.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Wall throws* (30 min): Throw 30 balls at a taped wall target from 15 feet, focusing on stepping.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Food sort* (20 min): Sort 20 food pictures or packages by their main macronutrient.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Edit* (90 min): Edit for capitals, commas, quotation marks and spelling with a parent conference.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Estimation in context* (60 min): Round attendance at four California stadiums to the nearest thousand and estimate their total.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Loops and hills* (45 min): Test whether the marble makes it over hills of different heights and explain using energy.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Primary source photos* (45 min): Analyze 3 images from the Calisphere set 'City Scenes in California History' and describe what changed and stayed the same.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Revise* (30 min): Revise one drawing based on feedback, adding value or detail.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Video check* (30 min): Record your throw in slow motion and check each cue.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Whole grains* (20 min): Compare white and whole-wheat bread labels and explain why whole grains give longer-lasting energy.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Publish* (90 min): Type the final narrative (at least one page) and add a title.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 1 review* (60 min): Play a review game mixing number forms, comparison, ten-times reasoning and rounding.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *PhET simulation* (45 min): Use Energy Skate Park Basics to observe speed and height relationships.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Community profile* (45 min): Create a community profile poster for your town: climate, land use, services and transportation.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Final drawing* (30 min): Create a finished observational drawing of a household object using all skills.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Distance throws* (30 min): Throw for distance 10 times and measure the best three.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Healthy fats* (20 min): Learn the difference between fats in avocados and nuts and fats in fried foods.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Celebrate & assess* (90 min): Read the narrative aloud to family; take the Unit 1 reading check on a new Just So story.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 1 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 1 test on place value, comparison and rounding.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Unit 1 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 1 test on speed, energy and collisions.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 1 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 1 test on geography, regions and communities.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Self-assess the final drawing with the checklist and display it.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Demonstrate a throw while a parent scores the 5 cues.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take a quiz matching carbohydrates, proteins and fats to their jobs and to 10 example foods.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Revising, publishing and theme
- Objective: Revise and edit a narrative
- Objective: Determine a theme and summarize a story
- Vocabulary: theme, summary, revise, edit
- Materials: computer, editing checklist
- Assessment: Published personal narrative (rubric) and Unit 1 reading check.

### Math 4: Rounding multi-digit numbers
- Objective: Round to any place using place value and number lines
- Objective: Use rounding to estimate
- Vocabulary: round, estimate, nearest, halfway
- Materials: number line strips, place value chart
- Assessment: Unit 1 test (place value, comparison, rounding).

### Science 4: Energy in motion: Newton's cradle and roller coasters
- Objective: Model energy transfer in a system
- Objective: Apply ideas to a design
- Vocabulary: transfer, potential, system
- Materials: foam pipe insulation, marbles, tape, cups
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### California, A Changing State: Comparing California communities
- Objective: Use maps, charts and pictures to compare communities
- Vocabulary: population density, land use, urban, rural, suburban
- Materials: population chart, photos, Calisphere 'City Scenes' set
- Assessment: Community profile and Unit 1 test.

### Art & Music 4: Drawing critique and revision
- Objective: Give and receive feedback
- Objective: Revise artwork
- Vocabulary: critique, feedback, revise
- Materials: sketchbook, drawings from weeks 1-3
- Assessment: Final drawing scored with criteria.

### PE 4: Overhand throw mechanics
- Objective: Throw overhand with mature form
- Objective: Identify throwing cues
- Vocabulary: side to target, step with opposite foot, rotate, follow through
- Materials: tennis balls, wall target, tape
- Assessment: Throwing cue checklist.

### Health 4: Nutrients: carbohydrates, proteins and fats
- Objective: Identify macronutrients and their jobs
- Objective: Name foods rich in each
- Vocabulary: nutrient, carbohydrate, protein, fat, energy
- Materials: food packages, pictures of foods, chart paper
- Assessment: Macronutrient quiz.
