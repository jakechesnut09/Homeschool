## Day 1 — Monday (school day 111, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Introduction* (90 min): Draft an introduction with a hook and clear opinion statement.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Adding mixed numbers* (60 min): Add 2 3/8 + 1 2/8 by adding wholes and parts, then with renamed fractions (8 problems).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Yosemite* (45 min): Read about how glaciers carved Yosemite Valley.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Dame Shirley* (45 min): Read an excerpt of Louise Clappe's 'Shirley Letters' from Rich Bar and list details of camp life.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Plan contrast* (30 min): Plan an ABA piece: what will make B different (tempo, dynamics, pitch range)?
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Rules* (30 min): Learn rules for traveling, double dribble and fouls and demonstrate each.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Poison hunt* (20 min): With an adult, find 10 household products with warning labels and note signal words (Caution, Warning, Danger).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 112, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Body* (90 min): Draft three body paragraphs, each with a reason, two pieces of evidence and a linking phrase (for instance, in addition, because).
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Regrouping in sums* (60 min): Add mixed numbers where parts exceed 1 whole and regroup.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Coastal erosion* (45 min): Analyze photos of eroding cliffs at Pacifica and Big Sur and list the agents causing the erosion.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Biddy Mason* (45 min): Read about Biddy Mason, who won her freedom in a Los Angeles court in 1856 and became a landowner and philanthropist.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Write B* (30 min): Compose an 8-measure B section that contrasts with the A section from week 22.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Half-court game* (30 min): Play a 2-on-2 half-court basketball game to 7 points using the rules learned.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Inhalants* (20 min): Learn why sniffing fumes from products like markers, glue or aerosols harms the brain and can be deadly.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 113, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Conclusion* (90 min): Draft a conclusion that restates the opinion and calls readers to action.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Subtracting mixed numbers* (60 min): Subtract mixed numbers, including renaming 1 whole as 4/4 (8 problems).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Sea caves model* (45 min): Model sea arch formation with sugar cubes and water.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Diverse miners* (45 min): Analyze images from the Calisphere sets 'African Americans in the Gold Rush' and 'Asian Americans: Gold Rush Era to 1890s'; note who came and from where.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Feedback* (30 min): Play the draft for a parent and gather feedback with a 3-question form.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Conflict scenarios* (30 min): Act out 3 disputed calls and resolve each with a fair strategy.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Safe storage* (20 min): Help organize cleaning products out of reach and away from food.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 114, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Revise* (90 min): Revise for precise words and varied sentences.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Measurement context* (60 min): Solve trail-length and rainfall problems using mixed numbers.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Sketch* (45 min): Create a before/after landscape sketch with explanation.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Conflict* (45 min): Read about the Foreign Miners' Tax and violence against Native Californians, Chinese and Mexican miners; discuss fairness.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Revise* (30 min): Revise two measures based on feedback and record the changes.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Skills stations* (30 min): Rotate through dribble, pass and shoot stations.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Poison Control* (20 min): Learn the Poison Control number (1-800-222-1222) and role-play calling it.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 115, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Peer conference* (90 min): Conference with a parent using the opinion rubric and plan edits.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on mixed number operations.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Unit 6 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 6 test on weathering, erosion and rock layers.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph explaining who profited most from the Gold Rush (miners or merchants like Levi Strauss) with evidence.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write a short description of the piece's form and mood.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Unit assessment* (30 min): Complete the hand dribble and passing skills test.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Unit assessment* (20 min): Take the Unit 6 test on medicines and household safety.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Drafting and revising the opinion essay
- Objective: Link opinion and reasons with words and phrases
- Objective: Write a concluding statement
- Vocabulary: for instance, in order to, in addition, conclusion
- Materials: outline, computer
- Assessment: Complete revised draft.

### Math 4: Adding and subtracting mixed numbers
- Objective: Add and subtract mixed numbers with like denominators
- Objective: Regroup when needed
- Vocabulary: mixed number, regroup, rename
- Materials: fraction strips
- Assessment: Mixed number quiz.

### Science 4: California landscapes over time
- Objective: Explain how Yosemite and the coast formed
- Objective: Synthesize unit learning
- Vocabulary: glacier, canyon, landform
- Materials: photos of Yosemite and coast
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### California, A Changing State: Gold Rush lives and communities
- Objective: Analyze how the Gold Rush affected daily life and diverse groups
- Objective: Study women who built early California
- Vocabulary: boomtown, merchant, Foreign Miners' Tax, entrepreneur
- Materials: Calisphere sets 'African Americans in the Gold Rush' and 'Asian Americans: Gold Rush Era to 1890s', Louise Clappe ('Dame Shirley') letter excerpt
- Assessment: Evidence paragraph on Gold Rush profits.

### Art & Music 4: Composing in ABA form
- Objective: Organize ideas into ABA form
- Objective: Revise with feedback
- Vocabulary: contrast, revise, A section, B section
- Materials: staff paper, Song Maker, recorder
- Assessment: Revised ABA composition.

### PE 4: Small-sided basketball games
- Objective: Apply dribbling, passing and shooting in games
- Objective: Resolve conflicts fairly
- Vocabulary: rules, fair play, foul, conflict resolution
- Materials: basketball, hoop or basket
- Assessment: Basketball skills test.

### Health 4: Household poisons and inhalants
- Objective: Identify household poisons
- Objective: Explain dangers of inhalants
- Vocabulary: poison, inhalant, Mr. Yuk, Poison Control
- Materials: household product labels (with adult)
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.
