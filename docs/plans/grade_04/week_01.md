## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Reading inventory* (90 min): Complete a reading interest survey, choose an independent library book, and do a 1-minute fluency read of a grade 4 passage to set a baseline.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Diagnostic & number talk* (60 min): Take a 20-question beginning-of-year diagnostic (facts, place value, 3rd-grade fractions) and do a number talk on 99 + 46.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Energy hunt* (45 min): Walk through the house listing 15 examples of energy (moving, sound, light, heat, electrical) in a notebook table.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Globe tour* (45 min): Mark the equator and prime meridian on an orange 'globe' and name the four hemispheres California lies in.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Sketchbook start* (30 min): Decorate a sketchbook cover and fill one page with 15 kinds of lines (zigzag, broken, thick-thin, spiral).
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Dynamic warm-up* (30 min): Learn a 5-minute warm-up (high knees, butt kicks, arm circles, lunges, inchworms) and lead it once.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Health triangle* (20 min): Draw a health triangle (physical, mental/emotional, social) and list 3 habits for each side.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *How the Whale Got His Throat* (90 min): Read Kipling's 'How the Whale Got His Throat' aloud and fill in a story map with character, setting, problem and solution.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Building big numbers* (60 min): Build 4- and 5-digit numbers with base-ten blocks and record them on a place value chart with period commas.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Notebook setup* (45 min): Set up a science notebook with table of contents and an investigation template (question, prediction, data, conclusion).
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Latitude lines* (45 min): Use a world map to find the latitude of Sacramento, San Diego and the North Pole and record them in a table.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Blind contour* (30 min): Draw your own hand three times without looking at the paper, then once looking; compare the results.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Find your pulse* (30 min): Count your pulse for 15 seconds at rest, multiply by 4, and log it; repeat after 2 minutes of jumping jacks.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Habit inventory* (20 min): Complete a 15-item checklist of daily habits (sleep, water, activity, fruits and vegetables) and circle strengths.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Details as evidence* (90 min): Reread 'How the Camel Got His Hump' and answer 4 questions citing a detail from the text for each.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Number names* (60 min): Write ten numbers up to 999,999 in standard form, word form and expanded form; read them aloud to a parent.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Stored and moving energy* (45 min): Stretch a rubber band, wind a toy, lift a ball; describe where energy is stored and when it becomes motion.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Longitude lines* (45 min): Find longitude for five California cities and explain why longitude lines meet at the poles.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Contour still life* (30 min): Draw a contour line drawing of three kitchen objects (mug, spoon, fruit) arranged on a table.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Heart rate zones* (30 min): Do 3 activities of different intensity (walk, jog, sprint) and record heart rate after each.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Health journal* (20 min): Set up a health journal and write about one healthy habit your family already has.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Writer's notebook launch* (90 min): Make a 'heart map' of memories in the writer's notebook and quick-write for 10 minutes about one memory.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Value of a digit* (60 min): Play 'Digit Detective': draw 6 digit cards, make a number, and state the value of the 7 or 3 in each number made.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Mystery question* (45 min): Watch a Mystery Science energy intro and discuss 'What makes things go?'
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Coordinate game* (45 min): Play 'Where in California?': a parent gives coordinates and you locate the place (e.g., 37.7 N, 122.4 W = San Francisco).
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Line in art* (30 min): Look at a Van Gogh reed-pen drawing online and list the lines he used for grass, sky and trees.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Cool-down & hydration* (30 min): Learn 4 static stretches for the cool-down and track water intake for the day.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Healthy day schedule* (20 min): Plan an ideal healthy day schedule with meals, activity, screen time and 9-12 hours of sleep.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Discuss the two stories with a parent using sentence stems ('I agree because...'), then answer a short story-element check.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: write 8 numbers in three forms and identify the value of underlined digits.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Draw and label a diagram of 4 energy examples and explain each in a sentence.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Quiz: plot 6 locations from coordinates and label the tropics, poles and hemispheres on a blank globe map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Choose the best contour drawing, add a title and explain your line choices to a family member.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain to a parent why heart rate rises with intensity and lead the full warm-up and cool-down.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Share the health triangle with family and answer 5 quick-check questions on health dimensions.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Story elements in Just So Stories
- Objective: Identify characters, setting, problem and resolution
- Objective: Establish reading and writing routines
- Vocabulary: character, setting, plot, resolution
- Materials: Just So Stories (Kipling, Gutenberg), reader's notebook, library book for independent reading
- Assessment: Story map, cited answers and fluency baseline.

### Math 4: Place value to the hundred thousands
- Objective: Read and write numbers to 999,999
- Objective: Name the value of each digit
- Vocabulary: digit, period, place value, value
- Materials: place value chart, base-ten blocks, index cards
- Assessment: Quiz on three forms of numbers and digit value (80% target).

### Science 4: What is energy?
- Objective: Identify forms of energy around us
- Objective: Set up a science notebook
- Vocabulary: energy, motion, kinetic, observe
- Materials: science notebook, ball, toy car, rubber band
- Assessment: Labeled energy diagram.

### California, A Changing State: Latitude, longitude and the globe
- Objective: Locate places using latitude and longitude
- Objective: Identify poles, equator, prime meridian, tropics and hemispheres
- Vocabulary: latitude, longitude, equator, prime meridian, hemisphere
- Materials: globe, world and California maps, orange, marker
- Assessment: Coordinate plotting quiz.

### Art & Music 4: Line and contour drawing
- Objective: Use varied lines to describe objects
- Objective: Practice contour and blind contour drawing
- Vocabulary: contour, line quality, blind contour
- Materials: sketchbook, drawing pencils (2B, 4B), kneaded eraser
- Assessment: Contour drawing and line explanation.

### PE 4: Warm-ups, safety and heart rate
- Objective: Perform a dynamic warm-up and cool-down
- Objective: Measure resting and active heart rate
- Vocabulary: warm-up, cool-down, pulse, heart rate, hydration
- Materials: stopwatch, water bottle, cones
- Assessment: Heart rate log and led warm-up.

### Health 4: What is health?
- Objective: Describe physical, mental and social health
- Objective: Identify healthy habits
- Vocabulary: wellness, physical health, mental health, social health, habit
- Materials: health journal, markers
- Assessment: Health triangle and quick check.
