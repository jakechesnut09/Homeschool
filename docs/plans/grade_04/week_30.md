## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Choose a wonder* (90 min): Choose a California natural wonder (Yosemite, Death Valley, redwoods, Lake Tahoe) and write research questions.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Elapsed time* (60 min): Solve 6 elapsed time problems with number lines (e.g., a train from Sacramento leaves 9:45 and arrives 12:20).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Oil spill* (45 min): Simulate an oil spill and test cleanup methods.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *LA boom* (45 min): Read about Los Angeles's 1880s land boom after the railroads arrived.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Taiko* (30 min): Watch San Jose Taiko and describe movement and sound.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Pickleball basics* (30 min): Learn pickleball serve and scoring with family.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Ad analysis* (20 min): Analyze 3 alcohol or tobacco ads for target audience and hidden messages.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 147, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Credible sources* (90 min): Evaluate 4 websites about your topic (.gov, .edu, a museum, a personal blog) for author, date and purpose, and rate each.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Mixed units* (60 min): Solve 5 problems with mixed units like 3 ft 4 in minus 1 ft 10 in.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Mining* (45 min): Model mining by extracting chocolate chips from a cookie with toothpicks and observe the damage to the 'land'.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Citrus and oil* (45 min): Read about orange groves and the discovery of oil in Los Angeles; analyze citrus crate label art.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Bucket taiko* (30 min): Learn a 4-beat taiko pattern on a bucket with kiai shouts.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Mini-tournament* (30 min): Play a family pickleball or paddle tournament.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Movies and TV* (20 min): Notice how alcohol or smoking is portrayed in a TV show or movie scene and whether consequences are shown.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 148, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Gather* (90 min): Find 3 sources including one with maps or charts.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Fractions of units* (60 min): Solve problems like 3/4 of an hour in minutes and 1/2 kg in grams.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Air pollution* (45 min): Smear petroleum jelly on index cards, place them near a road and in the yard for 3 days, and compare particles collected.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Everyday life* (45 min): Analyze images from the Calisphere set 'Everyday Life and People in Late 19th Century California'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Gamelan* (30 min): Listen to Balinese gamelan and identify layered patterns.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Newcomb ball* (30 min): Play Newcomb ball (catch-and-throw volleyball).
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Counter-ad* (20 min): Create a counter-ad that shows the truth about a product.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 149, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Notes* (90 min): Take paraphrased notes on note cards with source info.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Planning a day* (60 min): Make a timed schedule for a weekend trip using elapsed time and distances.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Trade-offs* (45 min): Discuss trade-offs of 4 energy sources (cost, pollution, reliability, land use) and rank them.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Graph growth* (45 min): Graph the populations of San Francisco and Los Angeles from 1850 to 1920 and compare.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Compose* (30 min): Create a layered rhythm piece with 3 repeating parts on bucket, pot and shaker and notate it.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Sportsmanship* (30 min): Write 3 sentences describing how you showed sportsmanship in the tournament.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Social media* (20 min): Discuss influencers and sponsored posts that promote vaping.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 150, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Check sources and notes with a parent and fix gaps.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on measurement word problems.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write about how one energy source affects the environment.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 9 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 9 test on transportation, immigration and urban growth.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform the taiko piece and compare taiko and gamelan.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Unit assessment* (30 min): Complete a striking and volleying skills test.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a media literacy checklist for evaluating ads.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Research questions and sources
- Objective: Generate research questions
- Objective: Locate credible sources
- Vocabulary: research question, source, credible, bibliography
- Materials: library, computer
- Assessment: Research questions and note check.

### Math 4: Measurement word problems
- Objective: Solve problems with distance, time, volume, mass and money
- Objective: Use number line diagrams
- Vocabulary: elapsed time, number line diagram, measurement
- Materials: number line diagrams, clock
- Assessment: Measurement problem quiz.

### Science 4: Environmental effects of energy use
- Objective: Describe environmental effects of resource use
- Vocabulary: pollution, habitat, oil spill
- Materials: feathers, oil, dish soap, water
- Assessment: Environmental effects writing.

### California, A Changing State: Growth of cities: Los Angeles and beyond
- Objective: Describe migration and the growth of towns and cities
- Vocabulary: boom, citrus, oil, migration
- Materials: Calisphere set 'Everyday Life and People in Late 19th Century California', population data
- Assessment: Unit 9 test.

### Art & Music 4: Taiko, gamelan and Asian American music
- Objective: Compare musical traditions
- Objective: Perform a rhythm piece
- Vocabulary: taiko, ensemble, gamelan, polyrhythm
- Materials: buckets, drumsticks or wooden spoons, videos
- Assessment: Taiko performance and comparison.

### PE 4: Striking and volleying games
- Objective: Apply striking skills in games
- Objective: Show sportsmanship
- Vocabulary: game play, sportsmanship, scoring
- Materials: paddles, balls, net
- Assessment: Striking skills test.

### Health 4: Media influences on ATOD
- Objective: Analyze ads and media messages about alcohol and tobacco
- Vocabulary: media literacy, target audience, hidden message
- Materials: magazine or online ads, movie clips
- Assessment: Counter-ad and checklist.
