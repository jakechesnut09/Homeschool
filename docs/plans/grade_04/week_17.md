## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Nonfiction* (90 min): Read a nonfiction account of the Lone Woman of San Nicolas Island (Juana Maria) and take notes.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Folding fractions* (60 min): Fold paper strips into halves, fourths and eighths and record equivalent fractions like 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Bird beak lab* (45 min): Use tweezers, spoons and clothespins as beaks to pick up seeds, rice and gummy worms.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Secularization* (45 min): Read about the 1834 secularization of the missions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Look closely* (30 min): Study Pomo and Chumash basket images on Calisphere and describe shapes, patterns and materials.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Rolls* (30 min): Practice log rolls and egg rolls, then a forward roll down a gentle incline with chin tucked.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Benefits* (20 min): List physical, mental and social benefits of physical activity.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Compare* (90 min): Make a two-column chart of what is historical fact and what Scott O'Dell invented, citing the novel and the article for each row.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Area models* (60 min): Draw area models to show 2/3 = 8/12 by partitioning each part into 4 equal pieces.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Camouflage* (45 min): Hide colored paper 'insects' outside and count which are found first.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Land grants* (45 min): Examine a diseño (rancho map) and explain how land was granted.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Infer* (30 min): Write what the baskets suggest about the makers' environment, skills and daily life.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Cartwheel progression* (30 min): Practice hand-hand-foot-foot along a line, then a lunge-to-cartwheel.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Activity types* (20 min): Sort activities into aerobic, muscle-strengthening and bone-strengthening.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 83, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Essay plan* (90 min): Plan an essay: 'How does Karana survive, and what does it show about her?'
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Number lines* (60 min): Mark 3/4, 6/8 and 9/12 on aligned number lines and explain why they share a point.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Shell protection* (45 min): Test how a shell structure (egg carton) protects an egg in a drop.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Native outcomes* (45 min): Discuss what happened to Native peoples after secularization.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Weaving loom* (30 min): Make a paper-plate loom and begin weaving a yarn pattern.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Flexibility* (30 min): Do bridge, straddle stretch and butterfly stretch to improve tumbling flexibility.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Heart rate* (20 min): Measure heart rate before and after 3 minutes of jumping.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 84, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Draft & revise* (90 min): Draft and revise the essay with at least 3 quotations.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Multiply by n/n* (60 min): Generate 3 equivalent fractions for 8 given fractions using n x a / n x b and explain the rule with a model.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *California animals* (45 min): Research external structures of the desert tortoise and sea otter.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Calisphere* (45 min): Analyze images from the Calisphere set 'Californio Society'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Pattern design* (30 min): Design a repeating geometric motif inspired by (not copied from) the baskets and finish the weaving.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Combine* (30 min): Link a forward roll, stand, and balance into a smooth sequence.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Mood check* (20 min): Rate your mood before and after a 15-minute walk.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 85, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Present & assess* (90 min): Share the essay in a book talk; scored with the literary essay rubric.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on generating and explaining equivalent fractions.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a claim-evidence-reasoning paragraph about how one external structure (e.g., a sea otter's dense fur) helps it survive.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz on mission secularization, diseños and rancho land grants.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Explain your pattern choices and what you learned about basketry as a living tradition.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform the forward roll and cartwheel for a parent who checks safety cues.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take a quiz on the 60-minute guideline, types of activity and three benefits of being active.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Fiction meets fact: the Lone Woman of San Nicolas
- Objective: Integrate information from novel and nonfiction
- Objective: Write a literary response essay
- Vocabulary: historical fiction, source, integrate
- Materials: novel, nonfiction article on Juana Maria (library/ReadWorks or National Park Service Channel Islands)
- Assessment: Literary response essay.

### Math 4: Equivalent fractions with models
- Objective: Explain equivalence with area models and number lines
- Objective: Generate equivalent fractions
- Vocabulary: equivalent, numerator, denominator, partition
- Materials: fraction strips, paper for folding
- Assessment: Equivalent fractions quiz with a model explanation.

### Science 4: Animal external structures
- Objective: Identify external animal structures
- Objective: Argue their function
- Vocabulary: adaptation, beak, claw, shell, camouflage
- Materials: pictures of animals, tweezers, spoons, clothespins
- Assessment: Beak lab data and CER.

### California, A Changing State: Secularization and land grants
- Objective: Explain mission secularization
- Objective: Describe land grants
- Vocabulary: secularization, land grant, diseño, rancho
- Materials: Calisphere 'Californio Society' set
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Art & Music 4: California Native basketry and pattern
- Objective: Infer culture from art objects
- Objective: Create a woven pattern
- Vocabulary: basketry, coil, twine, pattern, motif
- Materials: Calisphere set 'Native Americans: Arts and Traditions', paper strips, yarn, paper plate
- Assessment: Weaving and written inference.

### PE 4: Tumbling skills
- Objective: Perform log roll, forward roll and cartwheel progressions safely
- Vocabulary: tuck, forward roll, cartwheel, landing
- Materials: mat or soft lawn
- Assessment: Tumbling cue checklist.

### Health 4: Benefits of physical activity
- Objective: Describe benefits of daily activity
- Objective: Identify the 60-minute guideline
- Vocabulary: aerobic, muscle-strengthening, bone-strengthening, 60 minutes
- Materials: stopwatch, health journal
- Assessment: Activity benefits quiz.
