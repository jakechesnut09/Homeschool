## Day 1 — Monday (school day 151, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Firsthand* (90 min): Read a John Muir excerpt about Yosemite and note what he saw and felt.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Measure rooms* (60 min): Measure two rooms at home and compute area and perimeter with formulas.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Choose a source* (45 min): Choose one energy source used in California (Diablo Canyon nuclear, Altamont Pass wind, Central Valley solar, Hoover Dam hydro, oil from Kern County) and write 3 research questions.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Where water is* (45 min): Compare maps of where rain falls and where people live in California.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Blues form* (30 min): Listen to a 12-bar blues (e.g., 'C-Jam Blues') and clap on the first beat of each of the 12 bars.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Hula hoop pass* (30 min): Pass a hula hoop around a circle of family members without letting go of hands; time three tries and improve.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Types of pressure* (20 min): Learn spoken, unspoken, positive and negative peer pressure with examples.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 152, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Secondhand* (90 min): Read an encyclopedia article on Yosemite and note its focus.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unknown sides* (60 min): Find unknown side lengths given area or perimeter (10 problems).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Research notes* (45 min): Take notes from two sources on how the energy is made, where the resource comes from and its environmental effects.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *LA Aqueduct* (45 min): Read about William Mulholland and the 1913 Los Angeles Aqueduct from Owens Valley, including the conflict with Owens Valley residents.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Swing* (30 min): Practice swung eighth notes ('doo-ba doo-ba') on recorder on B, A and G.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Blindfold course* (30 min): Guide a blindfolded partner through a cone course using words only, then switch roles.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Scenario sort* (20 min): Sort 10 scenario cards by type of pressure (spoken, unspoken, positive, negative) and explain two choices.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 153, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Compare* (90 min): Complete a chart comparing the two accounts' focus and information.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Garden design* (60 min): Design a garden with 24 ft of fencing and find which rectangle gives the largest area.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Poster* (45 min): Create a poster with a labeled diagram of how the resource becomes electricity and a pros/cons chart.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Big projects* (45 min): Map Hetch Hetchy, Shasta Dam, the Central Valley Project and the State Water Project.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Improvise* (30 min): Improvise with B, A, G over a blues backing track.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Lava river* (30 min): Cross a 10-foot 'lava river' as a group using only 3 paper-plate stepping stones.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Good friends* (20 min): Make a list of qualities of friends who support healthy choices.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 154, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Integrate* (90 min): Write a paragraph integrating information from both.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 8 review* (60 min): Mixed review of conversions, measurement problems, area and perimeter.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Present* (45 min): Present the poster to family in 3 minutes and answer questions about trade-offs.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Primary sources* (45 min): Analyze photos from the Calisphere set 'Building Bridges, Dams and Power Plants'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *California jazz* (30 min): Listen to Dave Brubeck's 'Take Five' and identify the 5/4 meter.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Debrief* (30 min): List the words and actions that helped the group succeed and one thing that caused frustration.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Positive pressure* (20 min): Plan a way to use positive peer pressure to encourage a healthy habit.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 155, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Quiz identifying firsthand vs secondhand accounts.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 8 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 8 test on conversions, measurement problems, area and perimeter.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Unit 8 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 8 test on natural resources, renewable vs nonrenewable energy and environmental effects.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Firsthand and secondhand accounts
- Objective: Compare firsthand and secondhand accounts
- Objective: Explain differences in focus
- Vocabulary: firsthand, secondhand, primary source, account
- Materials: John Muir excerpt from 'The Yosemite' or 'My First Summer in the Sierra' (Gutenberg), encyclopedia article on Yosemite
- Assessment: Comparison chart and quiz.

### Math 4: Area and perimeter
- Objective: Apply area and perimeter formulas
- Objective: Find unknown side lengths
- Vocabulary: area, perimeter, formula, square unit
- Materials: grid paper, tape measure
- Assessment: Unit 8 test plus garden design task.

### Science 4: Energy research and presentation
- Objective: Obtain and combine information from multiple sources
- Objective: Present findings on an energy source
- Vocabulary: research, source, cite, trade-off
- Materials: library books, computer, poster board
- Assessment: Poster presentation and Unit 8 test.

### California, A Changing State: Water: dams, aqueducts and reservoirs
- Objective: Trace the evolution of California's water system
- Vocabulary: aqueduct, reservoir, dam, irrigation
- Materials: California water project map, Calisphere set 'Building Bridges, Dams and Power Plants'
- Assessment: Water flow diagram.

### Art & Music 4: Jazz, blues and California sounds
- Objective: Identify features of jazz and blues
- Objective: Improvise
- Vocabulary: jazz, blues, improvisation, swing, scat
- Materials: recordings (Dave Brubeck 'Take Five', Ella Fitzgerald scat), recorder
- Assessment: Improvisation and evaluation.

### PE 4: Cooperative challenges
- Objective: Solve movement problems with a group
- Objective: Communicate and encourage effectively
- Vocabulary: cooperation, communication, problem solving, encouragement
- Materials: hula hoops, rope, blindfold, paper plates
- Assessment: Cooperation self-rating and goal.

### Health 4: Peer pressure
- Objective: Recognize types of peer pressure
- Objective: Choose positive friends
- Vocabulary: peer pressure, positive pressure, negative pressure, dare
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Peer pressure reflection.
