## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Edit* (90 min): Edit for commas in compound sentences, spelling and capitalization.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Multiples of unit fractions* (60 min): Show 5/4 = 5 x 1/4 with fraction strips and write 6 similar equations.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Clay mountain* (45 min): Build a clay mountain and slice contour lines to make a topographic map.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Boom cities* (45 min): Compare images of San Francisco in 1847 and 1851 from the Calisphere set 'Growth of Cities in the Gold Rush Era' and list changes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Program note* (30 min): Write a program note explaining your composition's title and intent.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Watch & discuss* (30 min): Watch a ballet folklórico performance video and describe the footwork and costumes.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *What's in tobacco* (20 min): Learn about nicotine, tar and carbon monoxide and what each does to the body.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 117, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Publish* (90 min): Type the final opinion essay in one sitting, formatting the title and paragraphs, and proofread it once aloud.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Whole times fraction* (60 min): Solve 3 x 2/5 as 6 x 1/5 with number lines (10 problems).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Read a topo map* (45 min): Read a topographic map of Half Dome and find the steepest side by looking at contour line spacing.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Population data* (45 min): Graph California's non-Native population from 1848 to 1852 and explain the change.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse the ABA piece with dynamics and tempo markings.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *La Raspa steps* (30 min): Learn the La Raspa bleking step (alternating heels) in time with the music.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Lung model* (20 min): Model healthy vs smoker's lungs by soaking one sponge in dark syrup and comparing.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 118, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Formal vs informal* (90 min): Compare a text message and a speech on the same topic and rewrite informal sentences formally.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Recipe scaling* (60 min): Triple a muffin recipe with 3/4 cup amounts using measuring cups, then record the multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Ocean floor* (45 min): Read an ocean floor map and identify trenches and ridges.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Hydraulic mining model* (45 min): Spray water at a soil hillside in a tray to model hydraulic mining and observe the debris.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Premiere* (30 min): Perform the premiere of your composition for family.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Partner figure* (30 min): Learn the elbow-swing partner figure of La Raspa and connect it to the bleking section.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Straw breathing* (20 min): Jog in place, then breathe through a straw for 30 seconds to feel restricted breathing (stop if dizzy).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 119, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Speech* (90 min): Turn the essay into a 2-minute speech on note cards and rehearse.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Word problems* (60 min): Solve 6 problems like '5 students each drink 2/3 cup of juice; how much juice?' and find whole numbers between which the answer lies.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *World patterns* (45 min): Mark the Sierra Nevada, Andes, Rockies and Himalayas on a world map and note where they lie relative to coasts.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Environmental impact* (45 min): Analyze images from the Calisphere set 'Environmental Impact in the Gold Rush Era' and list effects on rivers and land.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Evaluate* (30 min): Watch two recorded youth performances online and evaluate them with criteria.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Full dance* (30 min): Perform La Raspa from start to finish with a family member three times.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Addiction* (20 min): Read how nicotine causes addiction and why quitting is hard.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 120, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Deliver & assess* (90 min): Deliver the 2-minute speech to family, then take the Unit 6 test on reasons and evidence and opinion writing.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on multiplying fractions by whole numbers.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz on reading contour lines, elevation and ocean-floor features.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 7 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 7 test and complete a cause-effect chart of the Gold Rush.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Self-evaluate your premiere using the same criteria.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform for family and explain where the dance comes from.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Draw a body diagram labeling 6 effects of tobacco.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Publishing and speaking: formal vs informal
- Objective: Publish an opinion essay
- Objective: Present formally
- Vocabulary: formal, informal, audience
- Materials: computer, note cards
- Assessment: Published essay, speech and Unit 6 test.

### Math 4: Multiplying a fraction by a whole number
- Objective: Understand a/b as a multiple of 1/b
- Objective: Multiply a fraction by a whole number
- Vocabulary: multiple, product, unit fraction
- Materials: fraction strips, measuring cups
- Assessment: Fraction multiplication quiz.

### Science 4: Maps of Earth's features
- Objective: Read topographic and relief maps
- Objective: Identify mountain ranges and trenches
- Vocabulary: topographic, contour, elevation, trench
- Materials: clay, string, ruler, marker
- Assessment: Map reading quiz.

### California, A Changing State: The Gold Rush transforms California
- Objective: Explain changes to towns, the economy and the environment
- Vocabulary: hydraulic mining, debris, population growth, economy
- Materials: Calisphere sets 'Growth of Cities in the Gold Rush Era' and 'Environmental Impact in the Gold Rush Era', population chart, tray of soil and spray bottle
- Assessment: Unit 7 test and cause-effect chart.

### Art & Music 4: Presenting compositions and evaluating music
- Objective: Present original music
- Objective: Evaluate performances with criteria
- Vocabulary: premiere, criteria, evaluate, program note
- Materials: recorder, recording device
- Assessment: Composition premiere and evaluation.

### PE 4: Mexican folk dance
- Objective: Perform steps of a Mexican folk dance
- Objective: Connect dance to California culture
- Vocabulary: folklórico, zapateado, step-close, tempo
- Materials: recording of 'La Raspa' or 'Jarabe Tapatío', hard floor, shoes
- Assessment: La Raspa performance.

### Health 4: Tobacco and the body
- Objective: Describe short- and long-term effects of tobacco
- Objective: Explain nicotine addiction
- Vocabulary: tobacco, nicotine, addiction, tar, carbon monoxide
- Materials: two sponges, molasses or dark syrup, straws, diagram of lungs
- Assessment: Body effects diagram.
