## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Fable reading* (90 min): Read 6 Aesop fables (e.g., 'The Ant and the Grasshopper', 'The Fox and the Grapes') and state each moral in your own words.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Partial sums to algorithm* (60 min): Add 4-digit numbers using partial sums, then the standard algorithm, and compare the two recordings.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Vibrations* (45 min): Stretch plastic wrap over a bowl, sprinkle rice and make sounds nearby to see the rice move.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Map of nations* (45 min): Label a map with Chumash, Tongva, Ohlone, Miwok, Pomo, Yurok, Mojave and Kumeyaay homelands.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Beat vs rhythm* (30 min): Pat the steady beat while saying the rhythm of 'Old MacDonald' and explain the difference.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Hand position* (30 min): Practice thumbs-together catches high and pinkies-together catches low (30 tosses).
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Vitamins and minerals* (20 min): Research one vitamin and one mineral (e.g., vitamin C, calcium) and their sources and jobs.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Adages & proverbs* (90 min): Match 12 adages ('Don't count your chickens...') to meanings and to the fable that fits.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Regrouping across places* (60 min): Solve 10 addition problems with multiple regroupings up to 6 digits using grid paper to align places.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Rubber band guitar* (45 min): Build a shoebox rubber band guitar and test how tightness changes pitch.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Living with the land* (45 min): Compare how coastal, valley, mountain and desert peoples obtained food and shelter in a chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Rhythm notation* (30 min): Read and clap quarter notes, eighth notes, half notes and rests from 8 rhythm cards.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Partner catch* (30 min): Play partner catch, stepping back after every 3 successful catches.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Water's job* (20 min): Learn how water regulates temperature and track your water intake for one day.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Suffixes -ful, -less, -ness* (90 min): Build and define 15 words with suffixes; note how suffixes change part of speech.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Estimate first* (60 min): Estimate each sum by rounding before computing and circle any answer that is not reasonable.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Sound travels* (45 min): Test how sound travels through air, water and a table by tapping and listening.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Primary sources* (45 min): Examine images from the Calisphere set 'Native Americans: Pre-Columbian' and describe tools and dwellings.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Meter* (30 min): Conduct 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4 patterns to recordings of a march and a waltz.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Moving catch* (30 min): Run a pattern and catch a ball tossed ahead of you.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Sugar in drinks* (20 min): Measure the teaspoons of sugar in a soda, juice drink and sports drink using labels and compare.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Discussion* (90 min): Discuss with a parent whether the grasshopper deserved help, citing the text.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Word problems* (60 min): Solve 5 two-step addition word problems about miles traveled on a California road trip.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *String telephone* (45 min): Build a cup telephone and test string length and tightness.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Acorns* (45 min): Read about acorn processing and sequence the steps from gathering to cooking.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Improvise* (30 min): Improvise 4-beat rhythms on a bucket drum in a call-and-response with a parent.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Bounce catch* (30 min): Play a reaction-bounce game with a playground ball.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Rethink your drink* (20 min): Make a chart of 6 healthier drink swaps (sparkling water with fruit instead of soda) with teaspoons of sugar saved.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 25, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write an original 1-paragraph fable with a moral; quiz on suffixes.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on multi-digit addition and estimation.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write how sound energy moved from source to ear in each investigation.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph on how one nation adapted to its environment.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Clap 4 rhythm patterns and identify the meter of 3 songs.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Complete a 20-catch test at three levels and record the score.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write 3 reasons water is the best drink choice.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Aesop's fables and morals
- Objective: Determine themes and morals of fables
- Objective: Explain adages and proverbs
- Vocabulary: fable, moral, adage, proverb
- Materials: Aesop's Fables (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Original fable and suffix quiz.

### Math 4: Addition with the standard algorithm
- Objective: Add multi-digit numbers with regrouping
- Objective: Estimate to check sums
- Vocabulary: sum, regroup, algorithm, estimate
- Materials: base-ten blocks, grid paper
- Assessment: Addition quiz with estimation check.

### Science 4: Sound energy
- Objective: Show sound carries energy
- Objective: Investigate vibrations
- Vocabulary: vibration, sound, pitch, volume
- Materials: rubber bands, plastic wrap, bowl, rice, tuning fork or spoon
- Assessment: Sound energy explanation.

### California, A Changing State: California Indian nations and regions
- Objective: Locate major California Indian nations
- Objective: Explain how environment shaped ways of life
- Vocabulary: nation, tribe, territory, culture area, acorn
- Materials: map of California Indian language groups, Calisphere 'Native Americans: Pre-Columbian' set
- Assessment: Adaptation paragraph.

### Art & Music 4: Steady beat, rhythm and meter
- Objective: Distinguish beat from rhythm
- Objective: Identify 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4 meter
- Vocabulary: beat, rhythm, meter, measure, bar line
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Rhythm, drum or bucket, rhythm cards
- Assessment: Rhythm reading and meter check.

### PE 4: Catching at different levels
- Objective: Catch above and below the waist
- Objective: Catch while moving
- Vocabulary: thumbs together, pinkies together, give with the ball
- Materials: tennis balls, playground ball, partner
- Assessment: Catching test score.

### Health 4: Vitamins, minerals and water
- Objective: Explain vitamins and minerals
- Objective: Explain why water is important
- Vocabulary: vitamin, mineral, calcium, iron, hydration
- Materials: measuring cup, clear cups, sugar, drink labels
- Assessment: Sugar-in-drinks chart and paragraph.
