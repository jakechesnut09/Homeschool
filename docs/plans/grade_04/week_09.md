## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Main idea* (90 min): Read a ReadWorks passage on sea otters and state the main idea with three key details.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Rectangle factor pairs* (60 min): Build every rectangle possible with 12, 18 and 24 square tiles and list the factor pairs.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Rope waves* (45 min): Shake a jump rope to make waves and change amplitude and wavelength.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Cabrillo* (45 min): Read about Juan Rodríguez Cabrillo's 1542 voyage and trace his route.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Color wheel* (30 min): Paint a 12-part color wheel mixing only red, yellow and blue.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Inside-foot pass* (30 min): Pass to a wall 30 times with each foot, plant foot beside the ball.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Label parts* (20 min): Label a printed Nutrition Facts panel: serving size, calories, fat, sodium, fiber, added sugars and % Daily Value.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Summarize* (90 min): Write a 4-sentence summary of a passage on California condors without opinions.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Prime or composite* (60 min): Use tiles to test numbers 1-30 and classify each as prime or composite.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Water waves* (45 min): Make waves in a tub of water and observe how a floating cork moves up and down, not along.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Drake* (45 min): Read about Francis Drake's 1579 landing and compare motives.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Tints and shades* (30 min): Make tint and shade strips by adding white and black to one color.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Trapping* (30 min): Trap balls rolled at different speeds with sole and inside of foot.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Serving size* (20 min): Compare the serving size to the whole package on 3 snacks and calculate how many servings each package holds.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Domain vocabulary* (90 min): Use context and glossaries to define 10 science words from the passages.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Sieve of Eratosthenes* (60 min): Shade multiples on a hundreds chart to find all primes under 100.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Wave drawings* (45 min): Draw and label waves with different amplitude and wavelength.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Vizcaíno* (45 min): Read about Sebastián Vizcaíno's 1602 voyage and the naming of Monterey.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Warm vs cool* (30 min): Paint two small abstract designs, one warm and one cool, and compare moods.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Partner passing* (30 min): Pass back and forth with a partner, increasing distance.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *5/20 rule* (20 min): Learn the 5/20 rule (5% DV or less is low, 20% or more is high) and mark 'low' and 'high' nutrients on 3 labels.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 44, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Latin roots* (90 min): Learn roots port, rupt, struct, dict and build 12 words.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Multiples* (60 min): List the first 10 multiples of 6 and 8, find common multiples, and solve 3 'which number am I?' riddles.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *PhET Wave on a String* (45 min): Use the simulation to change amplitude and frequency and record patterns.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Manila galleons* (45 min): Explain why Spain needed a port for the Manila galleon trade.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Complements* (30 min): Paint complementary color pairs side by side and observe contrast.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Gate passing* (30 min): Pass through cone gates scattered across the yard.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Pantry scavenger hunt* (20 min): Find the cereal or snack in the pantry with the most fiber and the least added sugar and record the numbers.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 45, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Read a new passage and write main idea, details and summary independently.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on factor pairs, multiples and prime/composite.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz labeling crest, trough, amplitude and wavelength on drawn waves.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Create a timeline of explorers with routes and motives.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Quiz on color vocabulary using the color wheel.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Count successful passes through a gate in 2 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take a label-reading quiz using a new food package.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Main idea and key details
- Objective: Determine main idea and supporting details
- Objective: Summarize informational text
- Vocabulary: main idea, key detail, summary, domain-specific
- Materials: ReadWorks grade 4 passages on California animals, highlighter
- Assessment: Independent main idea/summary task.

### Math 4: Factors, multiples, prime and composite
- Objective: Find all factor pairs for numbers to 100
- Objective: Determine prime or composite
- Vocabulary: factor pair, multiple, prime, composite
- Materials: square tiles, hundreds chart
- Assessment: Factor and prime quiz.

### Science 4: Wave patterns
- Objective: Describe amplitude and wavelength
- Objective: Model waves
- Vocabulary: wave, amplitude, wavelength, crest, trough
- Materials: jump rope or slinky, tub of water, ruler
- Assessment: Wave model diagram and quiz.

### California, A Changing State: Sea explorers of the North Pacific
- Objective: Identify early sea routes
- Objective: Explain motives of explorers
- Vocabulary: explorer, galleon, empire, route
- Materials: world map, timeline strip
- Assessment: Explorer timeline.

### Art & Music 4: Color theory
- Objective: Mix primary, secondary and tertiary colors
- Objective: Identify warm and cool colors
- Vocabulary: primary, secondary, tertiary, complementary, warm, cool
- Materials: watercolor or tempera paints, brushes, paper
- Assessment: Color wheel and quiz.

### PE 4: Passing and trapping
- Objective: Pass with the inside of the foot
- Objective: Trap a ball
- Vocabulary: pass, trap, plant foot, receive
- Materials: soccer ball, wall, cones
- Assessment: Gate pass count.

### Health 4: Reading the Nutrition Facts label
- Objective: Locate key information on the Nutrition Facts label
- Objective: Use % Daily Value to compare foods
- Vocabulary: Nutrition Facts, serving size, calories, sodium, added sugars, % Daily Value
- Materials: 5 food packages from the pantry, highlighter
- Assessment: Label-reading quiz.
