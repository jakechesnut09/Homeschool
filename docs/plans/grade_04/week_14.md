## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Alone* (90 min): Read chapters 7-9 and chart Karana's actions and feelings after Ramo's death.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Base-ten division* (60 min): Divide 156 / 3 by sharing base-ten blocks into 3 groups and trading as needed.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Dark box* (45 min): Look into a dark shoebox with an object, then open a small light hole and observe.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Presidios* (45 min): Read about the four presidios and their purpose.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Clementine* (30 min): Sing 'Oh My Darling, Clementine' and discuss what the lyrics tell about a forty-niner's family.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Partner jumping* (30 min): Jump side-by-side with a partner sharing one short rope, each turning one handle.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Kitchen safety* (20 min): Learn kitchen safety rules (handwashing, knife safety with adult, hot surfaces).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 67, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Point of view* (90 min): Discuss how first-person narration affects what we know; rewrite a paragraph in third person.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Area model division* (60 min): Use an area model with an unknown side length to solve 4 problems like 432 / 4.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Eye model* (45 min): Draw and label the eye and how light enters the pupil.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Pueblos* (45 min): Read about the founding of San José and Los Angeles.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Sweet Betsy* (30 min): Sing 'Sweet Betsy from Pike' and trace Betsy and Ike's overland journey on a map.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Double Dutch turning* (30 min): Practice 'eggbeater' turning of two ropes in alternating circles with a family member and a post.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Shop* (20 min): Help shop for the meal planned last week, reading labels to choose items.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 68, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Building a home* (90 min): Read chapters 10-12 and list the survival decisions Karana makes.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Partial quotients* (60 min): Solve 8 problems with partial quotients, subtracting friendly multiples of the divisor.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Pupil changes* (45 min): Observe pupil size in dim and bright light with a mirror.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Los Pobladores* (45 min): Read about the 44 settlers who founded Los Angeles in 1781 and their diverse backgrounds.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Play Clementine* (30 min): Learn the 'Clementine' melody on recorder in G, two phrases at a time.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Double Dutch entry* (30 min): Rock in time with the back rope, then enter and try 3 jumps; count best streak.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Prep* (20 min): Wash produce under running water and, with adult supervision, practice safe cutting with a claw grip.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 69, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Figurative language* (90 min): Find 5 similes/metaphors in chapters 13-14 and explain them.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Check with multiplication* (60 min): Check 6 quotients by multiplying and adding the remainder.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Seeing model* (45 min): Draw a model of light from a lamp reflecting off a book into the eye.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Compare* (45 min): Compare mission, presidio and pueblo in a chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Context writing* (30 min): Write 3 sentences on what these ballads reveal about Gold Rush life and why people sang them.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Routine design* (30 min): With a partner, design a 4-part routine mixing partner jumps and single-rope tricks.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Cook* (20 min): Cook the planned dinner with an adult and measure ingredients.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 70, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a paragraph on how Karana has changed so far, with two quotes.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on area-model and partial-quotient division.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Explain in writing how light allows us to see.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz comparing the purposes, people and locations of missions, presidios and pueblos.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform one Gold Rush song (sung or played) and explain why you chose it.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform the partner routine and give each other one compliment and one tip.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Island of the Blue Dolphins, chapters 7-14
- Objective: Trace character development
- Objective: Use first-person point of view
- Vocabulary: first person, narrator, character change, simile
- Materials: novel, reader's notebook
- Assessment: Character change paragraph.

### Math 4: Division with place value and area models
- Objective: Divide up to 4-digit dividends using area models and partial quotients
- Vocabulary: partial quotients, area model, inverse
- Materials: base-ten blocks, grid paper
- Assessment: Division strategies quiz.

### Science 4: Seeing: light and the eye
- Objective: Model how we see objects
- Objective: Explain why we cannot see in total darkness
- Vocabulary: pupil, retina, lens, optic nerve
- Materials: shoebox, small object, flashlight, diagram of eye
- Assessment: Seeing model and explanation.

### California, A Changing State: Presidios and pueblos
- Objective: Describe presidio and pueblo life
- Vocabulary: presidio, pueblo, settler, alcalde
- Materials: map, Calisphere images
- Assessment: Settlement quiz.

### Art & Music 4: Gold Rush songs
- Objective: Sing and play California folk songs
- Objective: Explain the songs' historical context
- Vocabulary: folk song, ballad, verse, refrain
- Materials: recorder, lyrics of 'Oh My Darling, Clementine' and 'Sweet Betsy from Pike', California map
- Assessment: Song performance and explanation.

### PE 4: Double Dutch and partner jumping
- Objective: Try Double Dutch turning and entry
- Objective: Jump with a partner in one rope
- Vocabulary: Double Dutch, eggbeater, partner jump, timing
- Materials: two long ropes, one long short-rope for partners
- Assessment: Partner routine performance.

### Health 4: Cooking a healthy recipe
- Objective: Follow a recipe safely
- Objective: Measure ingredients
- Vocabulary: recipe, measure, ingredient, kitchen safety
- Materials: simple recipe, measuring cups, knife (with adult), cutting board
- Assessment: Cooked meal and reflection.
