# Math 4 (Grade 4)

Fourth-grade mathematics aligned to the California Common Core State Standards. Students extend place value to 1,000,000, become fluent with multi-digit addition and subtraction, multiply up to four digits by one digit and two digits by two digits, and divide with remainders. The heart of the year is fractions (equivalence, comparison, adding/subtracting like denominators, multiplying a fraction by a whole number) and decimal notation, followed by measurement conversion, angle measurement and classification of two-dimensional figures.

*Framework:* California Common Core State Standards for Mathematics; 2023 CA Mathematics Framework  
*Time:* 60 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Place Value to One Million (weeks 1–4)
Each place is ten times the place to its right, which lets us read, compare and round big numbers.

- Week 1: Place value to the hundred thousands
- Week 2: Ten times as much
- Week 3: Comparing and ordering large numbers
- Week 4: Rounding multi-digit numbers

### Unit 2: Multi-Digit Addition & Subtraction (weeks 5–7)
The standard algorithm is place value regrouping written efficiently.

- Week 5: Addition with the standard algorithm
- Week 6: Subtraction with the standard algorithm
- Week 7: Multistep addition and subtraction problems

### Unit 3: Multiplication: Comparison, Factors & Multi-Digit (weeks 8–12)
Multiplication can compare quantities and is built from place-value partial products.

- Week 8: Multiplicative comparison
- Week 9: Factors, multiples, prime and composite
- Week 10: Multiplying by 10s, 100s and 1,000s; 1-digit by multi-digit
- Week 11: Two-digit by two-digit multiplication
- Week 12: Multiplication problem solving

### Unit 4: Division with Remainders (weeks 13–16)
Division shares or groups by place value; remainders must be interpreted in context.

- Week 13: Division concepts and mental division
- Week 14: Division with place value and area models
- Week 15: Four-digit dividends
- Week 16: Multistep problems with all four operations

### Unit 5: Fraction Equivalence & Comparison (weeks 17–20)
The same amount can be named by many fractions; size depends on numerator and denominator together.

- Week 17: Equivalent fractions with models
- Week 18: Simplifying and finding common denominators
- Week 19: Comparing fractions
- Week 20: Fraction reasoning

### Unit 6: Fraction Operations (weeks 21–25)
Fractions are built from unit fractions, so we can add, subtract and multiply them by whole numbers.

- Week 21: Decomposing fractions
- Week 22: Adding and subtracting fractions with like denominators
- Week 23: Adding and subtracting mixed numbers
- Week 24: Multiplying a fraction by a whole number
- Week 25: Line plots with fractional data

### Unit 7: Decimal Fractions (weeks 26–28)
Tenths and hundredths extend place value to the right of the ones.

- Week 26: Tenths and hundredths
- Week 27: Comparing decimals
- Week 28: Decimals and money problems

### Unit 8: Measurement, Area & Perimeter (weeks 29–31)
Larger units can be expressed in smaller units, and formulas describe area and perimeter.

- Week 29: Measurement units and conversions
- Week 30: Measurement word problems
- Week 31: Area and perimeter

### Unit 9: Angles & Two-Dimensional Geometry (weeks 32–34)
Angles are measured in degrees and help us classify shapes.

- Week 32: Points, lines, rays and angles
- Week 33: Measuring angles with a protractor
- Week 34: Classifying shapes and symmetry

### Unit 10: Patterns & Year in Review (weeks 35–36)
Rules generate patterns, and the year's skills combine to solve multistep problems.

- Week 35: Patterns and rules
- Week 36: Year in review and celebration

## Standards

- **4.G.A.1** Draw points, lines, line segments, rays, angles (right, acute, obtuse) and perpendicular and parallel lines.
- **4.G.A.2** Classify two-dimensional figures based on parallel or perpendicular lines or angles of a specified size; recognize right triangles.
- **4.G.A.3** Recognize a line of symmetry for a two-dimensional figure; identify line-symmetric figures and draw lines of symmetry.
- **4.MD.A.1** Know relative sizes of measurement units (km, m, cm; kg, g; lb, oz; l, ml; hr, min, sec) and express larger units in terms of smaller units.
- **4.MD.A.2** Use the four operations to solve word problems involving distances, time, liquid volume, masses and money, including fractions and decimals.
- **4.MD.A.3** Apply the area and perimeter formulas for rectangles in real-world and mathematical problems.
- **4.MD.B.4** Make a line plot to display measurements in fractions of a unit (1/2, 1/4, 1/8) and solve problems using it.
- **4.MD.C.5** Recognize angles as geometric shapes formed where two rays share an endpoint; understand degree measurement.
- **4.MD.C.6** Measure angles in whole-number degrees using a protractor; sketch angles of specified measure.
- **4.MD.C.7** Recognize angle measure as additive; solve addition and subtraction problems to find unknown angles.
- **4.NBT.A.1** Recognize that a digit in one place represents ten times what it represents in the place to its right.
- **4.NBT.A.2** Read and write multi-digit whole numbers in base-ten numerals, number names and expanded form; compare using >, =, <.
- **4.NBT.A.3** Use place value understanding to round multi-digit whole numbers to any place.
- **4.NBT.B.4** Fluently add and subtract multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm.
- **4.NBT.B.5** Multiply a whole number of up to four digits by a one-digit number and two two-digit numbers using place value strategies; illustrate with equations, arrays and area models.
- **4.NBT.B.6** Find whole-number quotients and remainders with up to four-digit dividends and one-digit divisors using place value strategies.
- **4.NF.A.1** Explain why a/b is equivalent to (n x a)/(n x b) using visual fraction models; generate equivalent fractions.
- **4.NF.A.2** Compare two fractions with different numerators and denominators using common denominators/numerators or a benchmark such as 1/2.
- **4.NF.B.3** Understand a fraction a/b with a > 1 as a sum of fractions 1/b; add and subtract fractions and mixed numbers with like denominators; solve word problems.
- **4.NF.B.4** Multiply a fraction by a whole number; solve word problems involving multiplication of a fraction by a whole number.
- **4.NF.C.5** Express a fraction with denominator 10 as an equivalent fraction with denominator 100 and add the two.
- **4.NF.C.6** Use decimal notation for fractions with denominators 10 or 100.
- **4.NF.C.7** Compare two decimals to hundredths by reasoning about their size; record with >, =, <.
- **4.OA.A.1** Interpret a multiplication equation as a comparison (35 = 5 x 7 is 5 times as many as 7).
- **4.OA.A.2** Multiply or divide to solve word problems involving multiplicative comparison.
- **4.OA.A.3** Solve multistep word problems with whole numbers using all four operations, including interpreting remainders; check reasonableness with estimation.
- **4.OA.B.4** Find all factor pairs for a whole number 1-100; recognize multiples; determine prime or composite.
- **4.OA.C.5** Generate a number or shape pattern that follows a given rule and identify features of the pattern.
- **MP.1** Make sense of problems and persevere in solving them.
- **MP.3** Construct viable arguments and critique the reasoning of others.
- **MP.4** Model with mathematics.
