## Day 1 — Monday (school day 136, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Finish* (90 min): Read chapters 19-24 (the journey to Glinda) and write a 5-sentence summary of how Dorothy gets home.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Money as decimals* (60 min): Write 10 amounts of money as decimals and fractions of a dollar.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Resource sort* (45 min): Sort 12 resources into renewable and nonrenewable.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *The Big Four* (45 min): Read about Stanford, Huntington, Hopkins and Crocker and the Central Pacific Railroad.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Feedback* (30 min): Get feedback on sculpture and prints using 'I notice / I wonder / I suggest'.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Stance & grip* (30 min): Practice batting stance and grip with knuckles aligned.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Brain parts* (20 min): Label brain parts and their jobs (thinking, memory, balance).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 137, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Film* (90 min): Watch selected scenes of the 1939 film and note differences (silver shoes vs ruby slippers).
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Store simulation* (60 min): Use grocery ads to compare prices and find which of two items costs less.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Fossil fuels* (45 min): Read about how coal, oil and natural gas formed from ancient living things and draw a 3-step diagram.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Chinese workers* (45 min): Read about Chinese workers who blasted tunnels through the Sierra Nevada and their unequal pay.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Revise* (30 min): Paint the dried clay sculpture with acrylic or tempera and add details suggested in feedback.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Tee hitting* (30 min): Hit 30 balls off a tee aiming for level swings.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Growing brain* (20 min): Read why young brains are more affected by alcohol.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 138, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Compare chart* (90 min): Complete a book-vs-film comparison chart covering characters, events, setting colors and the ending.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Money word problems* (60 min): Solve 5 problems adding and subtracting money amounts using whole-cent reasoning.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Home energy* (45 min): Read a family energy bill and identify energy sources.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Primary sources* (45 min): Analyze photographs from the Calisphere set 'The Transcontinental Railroad' and write captions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Display plan* (30 min): Plan where to display the sculpture safely (stable surface, away from edges and sunlight) and sketch the setup.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Soft toss* (30 min): Hit 20 soft-tossed balls from a parent, stepping toward the pitcher and swinging level.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Norms* (20 min): Read data showing most young people don't drink alcohol.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 139, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Opinion* (90 min): Write a paragraph: which version tells the story better and why?
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 7 review* (60 min): Mixed review of tenths, hundredths and comparison.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *California energy* (45 min): Read a pie chart of California's electricity sources (solar, natural gas, hydro, wind, nuclear) and list the top three.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Golden Spike* (45 min): Read about the May 10, 1869 ceremony at Promontory Summit and who was left out of the famous photo.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Labels* (30 min): Write gallery labels for the sculpture and best print with title, medium, date and a one-sentence description.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Fielding* (30 min): Practice fielding grounders and throwing to a base.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Safety* (20 min): Discuss drunk driving and what to do if an adult who has been drinking offers you a ride.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 140, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take the novel comprehension test.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Unit 7 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 7 test on tenths, hundredths, decimal comparison and money.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz classifying resources as renewable or nonrenewable and naming their uses.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph on how the railroad changed California's economy.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Install the sculpture and prints and give a 2-minute talk to family about your process.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Play a game of whiffle ball with family and track hits.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write 3 reasons kids choose not to drink alcohol, using facts from this week's readings.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Oz chapters 19-24 and the 1939 film
- Objective: Compare text with a film version
- Objective: Summarize the novel
- Vocabulary: adaptation, film, compare
- Materials: novel, The Wizard of Oz (1939 film, library DVD)
- Assessment: Novel test and comparison paragraph.

### Math 4: Decimals and money problems
- Objective: Solve money problems with decimals
- Objective: Connect fractions, decimals and money
- Vocabulary: dollar, cent, decimal
- Materials: play money, grocery ads
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Science 4: Natural resources and energy
- Objective: Identify natural resources used for energy
- Objective: Classify renewable and nonrenewable
- Vocabulary: natural resource, renewable, nonrenewable, fossil fuel
- Materials: picture cards, energy bill
- Assessment: Resource quiz.

### California, A Changing State: The transcontinental railroad
- Objective: Describe building the railroad and Chinese workers' contributions
- Vocabulary: Central Pacific, Big Four, Golden Spike, Chinese laborers
- Materials: Calisphere set 'The Transcontinental Railroad', map
- Assessment: Railroad paragraph.

### Art & Music 4: Sculpture revision and installation
- Objective: Revise artwork through peer feedback
- Objective: Plan a display
- Vocabulary: revision, installation, display
- Materials: sculpture, paint, labels
- Assessment: Installed sculpture and labels.

### PE 4: Striking with bats
- Objective: Strike a ball off a tee and from a toss
- Objective: Use proper batting stance
- Vocabulary: stance, grip, level swing, follow-through
- Materials: plastic bat, tee or cone, whiffle balls
- Assessment: Batting self-assessment.

### Health 4: Alcohol and the growing brain
- Objective: Explain why alcohol is more harmful to young people
- Objective: Use social norms data
- Vocabulary: brain development, judgment, social norm
- Materials: brain diagram, survey chart
- Assessment: Reasons paragraph.
