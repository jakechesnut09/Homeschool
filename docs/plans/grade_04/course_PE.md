# PE 4 (Grade 4)

Grade 4 physical education aligned to the California Physical Education Model Content Standards and Framework. Students refine manipulative skills (overhand throwing, catching, foot dribbling, hand dribbling, striking and volleying), perform long-rope jumping, balance stunts and folk dances, and apply movement concepts and simple strategies in small-sided games. Fitness is assessed with FITNESSGRAM-style tests at the start and end of the year, and students set personal goals, track heart rate and practice cooperation and sportsmanship.

*Framework:* California Physical Education Model Content Standards (Grade 4); 2009 California Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 30 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Fitness Foundations and Pre-Assessment (weeks 1–3)
Knowing our starting fitness lets us set goals and track growth.

- Week 1: Warm-ups, safety and heart rate
- Week 2: Fitness pre-assessment
- Week 3: Goal setting and circuit training

### Unit 2: Throwing and Catching (weeks 4–7)
Stepping with the opposite foot, rotating and following through produce accurate, powerful throws.

- Week 4: Overhand throw mechanics
- Week 5: Catching at different levels
- Week 6: Throwing to a moving target
- Week 7: Throwing and catching games

### Unit 3: Foot Skills: Dribble, Pass and Trap (weeks 8–11)
Soft touches and open space help a team keep and move the ball.

- Week 8: Foot dribbling
- Week 9: Passing and trapping
- Week 10: Creating space in small-sided soccer
- Week 11: Soccer skills assessment

### Unit 4: Jump Rope and Rhythm (weeks 12–15)
Timing and rhythm let us jump individually and with long ropes for fun fitness.

- Week 12: Individual jump rope
- Week 13: Long-rope jumping
- Week 14: Double Dutch and partner jumping
- Week 15: Jump rope fitness show

### Unit 5: Balance, Tumbling and Stunts (weeks 16–19)
A wide, low base of support makes balances stable, alone and in groups.

- Week 16: Balance and base of support
- Week 17: Tumbling skills
- Week 18: Partner and group balance stunts
- Week 19: Gymnastics routine

### Unit 6: Hand Dribbling and Passing (weeks 20–23)
Dribbling with fingerpads and eyes up lets us move and pass to open teammates.

- Week 20: Stationary and moving hand dribble
- Week 21: Passing and receiving
- Week 22: Shooting and offense-defense
- Week 23: Small-sided basketball games

### Unit 7: Folk and Line Dance (weeks 24–26)
Dance builds rhythm and connects us to the cultures of California.

- Week 24: Mexican folk dance
- Week 25: American square and line dance
- Week 26: Create a dance

### Unit 8: Striking and Volleying (weeks 27–30)
A side-facing stance, level swing and firm contact control where the object goes.

- Week 27: Striking with paddles
- Week 28: Striking with bats
- Week 29: Volleying
- Week 30: Striking and volleying games

### Unit 9: Cooperative and Strategy Games (weeks 31–33)
Teams win by communicating, creating space and playing fair.

- Week 31: Cooperative challenges
- Week 32: Tag and invasion game strategy
- Week 33: Design-a-game

### Unit 10: Fitness Post-Assessment and Field Day (weeks 34–36)
Comparing results shows growth and helps plan lifelong activity.

- Week 34: Fitness post-assessment
- Week 35: Lifetime activity plan
- Week 36: Field day

## Standards

- **PE-4.1.1** Perform simple small-group balance stunts by distributing weight and base of support.
- **PE-4.1.2** Perform intermediate-level, long-rope jumping skills (e.g., entering and exiting a turning rope, double turns).
- **PE-4.1.3** Perform locomotor and nonlocomotor movements in rhythmic patterns and folk/line dances.
- **PE-4.1.4** Throw a ball overhand with accuracy to a target and for distance using mature form.
- **PE-4.1.5** Catch a thrown or kicked ball above the waist, below the waist and while moving.
- **PE-4.1.6** Dribble and pass a ball with the feet (inside and outside of the foot) while moving and changing direction.
- **PE-4.1.7** Dribble a ball with the hands while moving, changing speed and direction, and pass to a moving partner.
- **PE-4.1.8** Strike an object with a paddle, racket or bat using a sideways orientation and controlled force.
- **PE-4.1.9** Volley an object upward and to a partner using hands and forearms.
- **PE-4.2.1** Explain how to use body position, force and follow-through to improve throwing, kicking and striking.
- **PE-4.2.2** Explain how to create and reduce space when moving with others in game situations (offense and defense).
- **PE-4.2.3** Design and refine a movement sequence or dance using changes in level, pathway, tempo and force.
- **PE-4.3.1** Participate in moderate-to-vigorous physical activity for increasing periods of time (aerobic capacity).
- **PE-4.3.2** Perform exercises that build muscular strength and endurance (curl-ups, push-ups).
- **PE-4.3.3** Perform exercises that improve flexibility (trunk lift, sit-and-reach).
- **PE-4.3.4** Assess personal fitness with standardized tests and set short-term fitness goals.
- **PE-4.3.5** Monitor heart rate before, during and after physical activity.
- **PE-4.4.1** Explain the relationship between heart rate, breathing and intensity of physical activity.
- **PE-4.4.2** Identify the five components of health-related fitness and activities that develop each.
- **PE-4.4.3** Explain the importance of warm-up, cool-down, hydration and safety in physical activity.
- **PE-4.5.1** Demonstrate sportsmanship and respect for others during activities, winning or losing.
- **PE-4.5.2** Work cooperatively with a partner or small group to solve movement problems and accomplish goals.
- **PE-4.5.3** Use conflict-resolution strategies (e.g., rock-paper-scissors, talk it out, take turns) during games.
