## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Plan a myth* (90 min): Choose a California phenomenon (fog over the Golden Gate, Half Dome's shape) and plan an origin myth.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Times as many* (60 min): Build 'Ana has 3 shells, Ben has 4 times as many' with counters and write 12 = 4 x 3 statements.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Conversion hunt* (45 min): List devices at home that convert energy (toaster, fan) and identify input and output.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Living communities* (45 min): Explore images from the Calisphere set 'Native Americans: 20th Century to Today' and list what has continued and changed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Ostinato* (30 min): Create a 4-beat rhythm ostinato to accompany a recorder song.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Touches* (30 min): Practice toe taps, inside-inside touches and sole rolls for 5 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Food log day 1* (20 min): Record everything you eat and drink today in a food log by food group.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Relative pronouns* (90 min): Combine sentences using who, whose, which, that, where, when, why (12 items).
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Tape diagrams* (60 min): Draw tape diagrams for 6 comparison problems and solve for the larger quantity or the multiplier.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Define problem* (45 min): Define a problem: a light-up alarm for a door; list criteria and constraints.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Language revival* (45 min): Read about a language revitalization program (e.g., Yurok language classes) and summarize it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Layering* (30 min): Play the recorder melody while a family member plays the ostinato.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Cone weave* (30 min): Dribble through 6 cones using inside and outside of the foot.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Food log day 2* (20 min): Continue the food log and add activity minutes.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 38, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Draft* (90 min): Draft the myth with a hero, a problem and an explanation of the phenomenon.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Additive vs multiplicative* (60 min): Sort 10 word problems into 'how many more' and 'how many times as many' and solve each.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Build* (45 min): Build the door alarm with a foil switch and buzzer or LED.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Questions* (45 min): Write 5 questions you would ask a tribal historian.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse the melody and ostinato together with a metronome at 80 beats per minute.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Speed changes* (30 min): Dribble at walking, jogging and running speed on a whistle.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Food log day 3* (20 min): Complete the third day of the food log, including a weekend meal, and total the servings per food group.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 39, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Revise & edit* (90 min): Revise to add at least two relative clauses and edit with a checklist.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Division comparison* (60 min): Solve comparison problems with an unknown smaller quantity (e.g., 'a redwood is 6 times as tall as a 50-ft oak...').
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Test and improve* (45 min): Test the alarm 5 times by opening the door, record each result and fix one failure point (loose foil, weak contact).
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Nation project* (45 min): Create a mini-book on one nation: land, food, shelter, beliefs and today's community.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Etiquette* (30 min): Practice performer and audience etiquette (bow, quiet listening, applause).
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Red light, green light* (30 min): Play dribbling red light, green light, stopping the ball with the sole.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Analyze* (20 min): Compare your 3-day log to MyPlate and identify one food group to increase.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 40, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Storytelling & assessment* (90 min): Tell the myth aloud as a storyteller; take the Unit 2 test on theme, allusions and comparison.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: write a comparison statement for 4 equations and solve 4 word problems.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Unit 2 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 2 test on energy transfer.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 2 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 2 test on California's first peoples.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Mini-concert* (30 min): Perform for family and evaluate with a 3-point rubric.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Time a cone-weave dribble 3 times and record the best.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Unit assessment* (20 min): Take the Unit 2 test on nutrients and MyPlate.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Writing an original myth
- Objective: Write a narrative explaining a natural phenomenon
- Objective: Use relative pronouns and adverbs
- Vocabulary: relative pronoun, relative adverb, phenomenon
- Materials: writer's notebook, computer
- Assessment: Published myth and Unit 2 test.

### Math 4: Multiplicative comparison
- Objective: Interpret multiplication as comparison
- Objective: Distinguish additive from multiplicative comparison
- Vocabulary: times as many, comparison, factor, product
- Materials: counters, tape diagram templates
- Assessment: Comparison quiz.

### Science 4: Energy conversion
- Objective: Identify energy conversions
- Objective: Design a device that converts energy
- Vocabulary: convert, energy conversion, criteria, constraint
- Materials: batteries, LEDs, buzzer, small motor, cardboard
- Assessment: Door alarm prototype and Unit 2 test.

### California, A Changing State: California Indians today
- Objective: Recognize California Indian nations as living communities
- Objective: Pose historical questions
- Vocabulary: sovereignty, rancheria, reservation, revitalization
- Materials: Calisphere 'Native Americans: 20th Century to Today' set, tribal websites
- Assessment: Nation mini-book and Unit 2 test.

### Art & Music 4: Rhythm ensemble performance
- Objective: Perform rhythm and recorder parts together
- Objective: Demonstrate performance etiquette
- Vocabulary: ensemble, ostinato, etiquette
- Materials: recorder, bucket drum
- Assessment: Mini-concert performance.

### PE 4: Foot dribbling
- Objective: Dribble with inside and outside of the foot
- Objective: Keep the ball close
- Vocabulary: dribble, inside of foot, outside of foot, close control
- Materials: soccer ball, cones
- Assessment: Cone-weave time.

### Health 4: Food tracking
- Objective: Track food intake for 3 days
- Objective: Compare intake to MyPlate
- Vocabulary: food log, serving, balance
- Materials: food log sheet, MyPlate chart
- Assessment: Food log analysis and Unit 2 test.
