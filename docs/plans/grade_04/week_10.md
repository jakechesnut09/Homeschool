## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Chronology* (90 min): Read an article on the building of the Golden Gate Bridge and make a timeline.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Patterns with zeros* (60 min): Use place value to solve 4 x 3, 4 x 30, 4 x 300, 4 x 3,000 and explain the pattern.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Wave pushes* (45 min): Show that larger amplitude waves move a toy boat more and measure distance.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Anza Trail* (45 min): Trace Juan Bautista de Anza's overland route from Sonora to San Francisco.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Depth tricks* (30 min): Look at a California landscape painting by a California Impressionist (e.g., Guy Rose) and find foreground, middle and background.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Open space* (30 min): Play keep-away 2-on-1, moving to open space to receive.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Whole vs processed* (20 min): Compare an apple, applesauce and an apple-flavored fruit snack label and rank them by nutrients.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 47, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Cause/effect* (90 min): Read about the 1906 San Francisco earthquake and chart causes and effects.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Area model* (60 min): Draw area models for 6 x 47 and 3 x 215 and add partial products.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Beach model* (45 min): Build a sand beach in a tub and make waves to observe sand movement.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Russians* (45 min): Read about Fort Ross and the Russian-American Company's sea otter trade.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Atmospheric perspective* (30 min): Draw three mountain ranges getting lighter as they recede.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Defense* (30 min): Practice defensive stance and staying goal-side.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Snack decision* (20 min): Use the decision model to choose an after-school snack from 4 options and explain the choice.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 48, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Compare/problem-solution* (90 min): Read about drought solutions and record the problem and solutions; compare two animals in another passage.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Four digits by one digit* (60 min): Solve 8 problems like 4 x 2,316 using area models and partial products.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Ocean waves* (45 min): Watch a video on California surf and tsunamis and record how waves carry energy.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Primary sources* (45 min): Analyze images from the Calisphere set 'Early California Exploration and Settlement'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Sketch* (30 min): Sketch a California landscape (Yosemite, coast or desert) with overlapping layers.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Small-sided game* (30 min): Play a 2-on-2 soccer game with small cone goals, calling for the ball when open.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Sodium check* (20 min): Compare sodium in chips, pretzels, popcorn and nuts and graph the results.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 49, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Structure sort* (90 min): Identify the structure of 6 short paragraphs by their signal words.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Standard algorithm intro* (60 min): Connect partial products to the standard algorithm for 3-digit by 1-digit problems (8 problems).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Sound as waves* (45 min): Connect sound waves to wave patterns with a slinky.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Compare empires* (45 min): Compare Spanish, English and Russian goals in a chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Color* (30 min): Add warm colors in front and cool colors behind.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Strategy talk* (30 min): Draw a diagram showing how to create space on offense.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Make a snack* (20 min): Prepare a nutrient-dense snack (veggies and hummus, yogurt parfait) and list its food groups.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 50, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Quiz: name the structure of 4 passages and draw the matching organizer.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on 1-digit by multi-digit multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a CER on how waves cause objects to move.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Unit 3 test* (45 min): Take the Unit 3 test on explorers, their routes and motives, and Fort Ross.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & share* (30 min): Explain to family how overlap, size, placement and color show depth in your landscape.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Self-assess moving to space using a 3-point checklist.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a 'Snack Smart' guide with 5 tips and 5 snack ideas.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Text structures
- Objective: Identify chronology, comparison, cause/effect and problem/solution
- Objective: Use signal words
- Vocabulary: text structure, chronology, cause and effect, problem/solution
- Materials: nonfiction articles, graphic organizers
- Assessment: Text structure quiz.

### Math 4: Multiplying by 10s, 100s and 1,000s; 1-digit by multi-digit
- Objective: Multiply by multiples of 10
- Objective: Multiply 2- to 4-digit numbers by 1-digit numbers with area models
- Vocabulary: area model, partial products, multiple of ten
- Materials: grid paper, base-ten blocks
- Assessment: Quiz with area model and algorithm items.

### Science 4: Waves cause motion
- Objective: Show waves move objects
- Objective: Relate ocean waves to coastal changes
- Vocabulary: energy, ocean wave, coast, erosion
- Materials: tub, sand, pebbles, plastic block
- Assessment: CER on waves causing motion.

### California, A Changing State: Land routes and the Russians
- Objective: Identify land routes and European settlements
- Objective: Explain Russian presence at Fort Ross
- Vocabulary: Fort Ross, otter pelt, colony, land route
- Materials: Calisphere 'Early California Exploration and Settlement' set, map
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Art & Music 4: Landscape space: foreground, middle ground, background
- Objective: Create depth in a landscape
- Objective: Study California landscapes
- Vocabulary: foreground, middle ground, background, horizon line, overlap
- Materials: photos of California landscapes, paper, colored pencils
- Assessment: Landscape sketch.

### PE 4: Creating space in small-sided soccer
- Objective: Move to open space
- Objective: Play offense and defense
- Vocabulary: open space, offense, defense, support
- Materials: soccer ball, cones, pinnies
- Assessment: Strategy diagram and self-assessment.

### Health 4: Comparing snacks and making choices
- Objective: Use labels and a decision model to choose snacks
- Objective: Compare whole and processed foods
- Vocabulary: processed, whole food, snack, nutrient-dense
- Materials: snack packages, fresh fruit, decision-making worksheet
- Assessment: Snack Smart guide.
