## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Setting* (90 min): Read chapters 1-2 and map San Nicolas Island; describe how the setting shapes the people's lives.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Sharing and grouping* (60 min): Model 23 / 4 with counters both as sharing and as grouping and record the remainder.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Straight lines* (45 min): Line up index cards with holes and shine a flashlight through to show light travels straight.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Daily schedule* (45 min): Read about a typical mission day (bells, prayer, work, meals) and make an hour-by-hour schedule.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Melodic contour* (30 min): Listen to 3 melodies ('Twinkle', 'Ode to Joy', 'Somewhere Over the Rainbow') and draw each melody's up-and-down shape.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Turning* (30 min): Practice turning a long rope with a partner, making full circles that tap the ground on each beat.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Meal analysis* (20 min): Analyze 3 sample dinners and decide which food groups are missing.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Inferences* (90 min): Read chapters 3-4 and write 3 inferences about Captain Orlov using text evidence.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Dividing multiples of 10* (60 min): Use basic facts to solve 240 / 6, 2,400 / 6 and 10 similar problems.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Mirror reflection* (45 min): Use mirrors to bounce a flashlight beam to a target.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Native experience* (45 min): Read accounts of Native life at missions, including forced labor and disease.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Low E and D* (30 min): Learn low E and D fingerings, covering holes fully, and play long tones on each.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Front door* (30 min): Enter the 'front door' as the rope comes toward you, jump 5 times and run out the far side.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Plan a dinner* (20 min): Plan a family dinner that includes all 5 food groups.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 63, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Conflict* (90 min): Read chapters 5-6 and discuss the battle and departure with a parent.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Interpreting remainders* (60 min): Solve 4 problems where the remainder is dropped, rounded up, or is the answer (e.g., vans needed for 29 kids).
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Periscope* (45 min): Build a periscope from a milk carton and two mirrors.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Economic change* (45 min): Explain how missions changed California from hunter-gatherer to agriculture and ranching.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Steps and skips* (30 min): Circle steps, skips and leaps in the written melody of 'Ode to Joy' and then play it.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Back door* (30 min): Enter through the 'back door' (rope moving away) by waiting for it to hit the ground and following it in.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Budget* (20 min): Use a grocery flyer to price the ingredients and stay within a $20 budget.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 64, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Word study* (90 min): Practice syllabication of 15 multisyllabic words from the novel.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Estimating quotients* (60 min): Use compatible numbers to estimate 6 quotients like 418 / 7.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Absorb vs reflect* (45 min): Compare how light reflects from foil, white paper and black paper.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Primary source analysis* (45 min): Analyze an image from the Calisphere set 'Native Americans and Contact' using See-Think-Wonder.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Note naming* (30 min): Name 20 notes from low D to high D on a treble staff worksheet.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Chants* (30 min): Jump to traditional rope chants like 'Teddy Bear, Teddy Bear' while doing the actions.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Snack plan* (20 min): Plan two healthy snacks for the week, each combining two food groups (apple with peanut butter, carrots with hummus).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 65, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 4** — *Review & check* (90 min): Answer 6 text-dependent questions on chapters 1-6.
- [ ] **Math 4** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on mental division and remainders.
- [ ] **Science 4** — *Review & check* (45 min): Draw light ray diagrams with arrows for the index card, mirror and periscope setups.
- [ ] **California, A Changing State** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph about how the missions changed Native lives.
- [ ] **Art & Music 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Play a D-to-D scale up and down and sight-read a 4-measure stepwise melody.
- [ ] **PE 4** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform a front-door entry, 10 jumps with a turn-around, and a clean exit.
- [ ] **Health 4** — *Review & check* (20 min): Present the menu to family and explain how it follows MyPlate.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 4: Island of the Blue Dolphins, chapters 1-6
- Objective: Describe the setting and its effect on characters
- Objective: Make inferences
- Vocabulary: inference, setting, Aleut, Ghalas-at
- Materials: Island of the Blue Dolphins (Scott O'Dell, library), map of the Channel Islands
- Assessment: Comprehension questions on chapters 1-6.

### Math 4: Division concepts and mental division
- Objective: Divide multiples of 10, 100, 1,000 by 1-digit numbers
- Objective: Interpret remainders
- Vocabulary: dividend, divisor, quotient, remainder
- Materials: counters, base-ten blocks
- Assessment: Division concepts quiz.

### Science 4: How light travels
- Objective: Show light travels in straight lines
- Objective: Observe reflection
- Vocabulary: reflect, light ray, mirror, absorb
- Materials: flashlight, mirrors, index cards with holes, clay
- Assessment: Light ray diagrams.

### California, A Changing State: Life in the missions
- Objective: Describe daily life at the missions
- Objective: Analyze Native experiences
- Vocabulary: neophyte, labor, adobe, disease
- Materials: Calisphere 'California Missions' and 'Native Americans and Contact' sets
- Assessment: Paragraph on change.

### Art & Music 4: Melody: steps, skips and leaps
- Objective: Identify melodic contour
- Objective: Add low E and D to the recorder
- Vocabulary: melody, step, skip, leap, contour, staff
- Materials: recorder, staff paper, fingering chart
- Assessment: Scale and sight-reading check.

### PE 4: Long-rope jumping
- Objective: Enter and exit a turning long rope
- Objective: Turn a long rope rhythmically
- Vocabulary: front door, back door, turner, rhythm
- Materials: long rope (16 ft), a partner or a post to tie one end
- Assessment: Long-rope entry/exit check.

### Health 4: Planning a balanced meal
- Objective: Plan a balanced meal and snack using MyPlate
- Objective: Consider budget and variety
- Vocabulary: balanced meal, menu, budget, variety
- Materials: MyPlate template, grocery flyer
- Assessment: Meal plan with budget.
