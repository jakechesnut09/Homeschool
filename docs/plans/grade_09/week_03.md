## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Situational irony* (60 min): Read "The Gift of the Magi"; explain in writing how the ending reverses expectations and what O. Henry suggests about love and sacrifice.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Dimensional analysis* (60 min): Convert 12 rates (mph to ft/s, gallons/day to L/year) using conversion-factor chains.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Interactions* (60 min): Classify 15 species pairs by interaction type with examples from California ecosystems.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Region types* (50 min): Classify 12 regions (Bay Area, Sun Belt, Corn Belt) by type.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Mis clases* (50 min): Describe your class schedule and opinions of each subject in 10 sentences.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Value scales* (50 min): Create 9-step value scales with pencil blending, hatching, cross-hatching, and stippling.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *PACER or mile* (50 min): Complete the 20-meter PACER (or timed 1-mile run) after a full warm-up and record results.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Credible or not?* (45 min): Use a CRAAP checklist (Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose) to evaluate four health websites, including a .gov, a .org and a product site.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Narrative techniques* (60 min): Study how O. Henry uses dialogue, a narrator's asides, and pacing; imitate one technique in a 150-word scene from your own life.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Measuring lab* (60 min): Measure a room, a book, and a mug with a tape; report each measurement with appropriate precision.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Predator–prey* (60 min): Graph the lynx–hare data and explain the cycles.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Perceptual regions* (50 min): Survey family members on where "Southern California" begins and map the results.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Stem-changers* (50 min): Practice e→ie, o→ue, e→i verbs with a card game and written drill.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Shading spheres* (50 min): Render a sphere, cube, and cylinder with highlight, midtone, core shadow, reflected light, and cast shadow.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Curl-ups* (50 min): Learn proper curl-up form and complete the cadence-based curl-up test.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Spotting misinformation* (45 min): Analyze three viral social-media health claims and trace each back to (or fail to find) its original research source.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Brainstorm a turning point* (60 min): Make a life map of 8 memorable moments; choose one turning point and draft a story arc (setup, conflict, climax, reflection).
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Defining quantities* (60 min): For a road-trip problem, define the quantities needed (distance, fuel efficiency, cost) and build an expression for total cost.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Keystone species* (60 min): Read about sea otters and kelp forests and draw the trophic cascade.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *World regions* (50 min): Label the world's major cultural regions (Latin America, Sub-Saharan Africa, East Asia, etc.) on a blank map and note one trait that unifies each.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Schools abroad* (50 min): Read about a school day in Mexico or Argentina and list five differences.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Light source lab* (50 min): Photograph an egg under a single lamp from three angles and draw the best lighting.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Push-ups* (50 min): Learn 90-degree push-up form and complete the push-up test.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Health care providers* (45 min): Make a chart of health care providers (primary care, specialists, urgent care, ER, school nurse, telehealth) and when to use each.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Grammar: clauses* (60 min): Learn independent, dependent, noun, adjective, and adverb clauses; write 8 sentences about your memory using each clause type.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Scales on graphs* (60 min): Choose axis scales and units for three data sets and explain your choices.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Diversity index* (60 min): Calculate Simpson's diversity index for your two quadrats and interpret.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Region essay* (50 min): Write a paragraph defining a region you belong to and its characteristics.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Conversation* (50 min): Hold a role-play conversation about after-school activities using preferir and querer.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Pen techniques* (50 min): Shade the same egg in pen with hatching only.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Active recovery* (50 min): Complete a 30-minute easy walk and 15 minutes of mobility work.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Local services map* (45 min): Research and list your local clinic, pharmacy, urgent care, Medi-Cal/Covered California options and teen confidential services with phone numbers.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Irony and craft in "The Gift of the Magi"; planning a personal narrative
- Objective: Explain situational irony
- Objective: Plan a narrative around a turning point
- Vocabulary: situational irony, narrative arc, sensory detail, dialogue, pacing
- Materials: "The Gift of the Magi" by O. Henry (public domain), mentor narrative excerpts
- Assessment: Narrative plan conference checklist; clause quiz (80%+).

### Algebra I: Units, quantities, and measurement accuracy
- Objective: Use units to guide problem solving
- Objective: Choose appropriate accuracy
- Vocabulary: unit rate, dimensional analysis, precision, significant digit, conversion factor
- Materials: measuring tape, kitchen scale, calculator
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Biodiversity and species interactions
- Objective: Describe predation, competition, and symbiosis
- Objective: Use math to compare biodiversity
- Vocabulary: predation, competition, mutualism, commensalism, parasitism, biodiversity, keystone species
- Materials: quadrat data, calculator
- Assessment: Quiz and diversity index calculation.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Regions
- Objective: Define formal, functional, and perceptual regions
- Objective: Map a region
- Vocabulary: formal region, functional region, perceptual region, boundary
- Materials: colored pencils, U.S. map
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Spanish II: La escuela y las actividades: stem-changing verbs and time expressions
- Objective: Talk about classes and activities
- Objective: Use stem-changing verbs accurately
- Vocabulary: asignatura, horario, almorzar, empezar, preferir, pedir
- Materials: school schedule template
- Assessment: Stem-changer quiz and role-play rubric.

### Visual Art I: Value: from light to dark
- Objective: Create value scales
- Objective: Render form with shading techniques
- Vocabulary: value, value scale, hatching, cross-hatching, stippling, blending
- Materials: pencils 2H–6B, blending stump, fine-tip pen
- Assessment: Value scales and shaded forms checked against a rubric.

### PE 9 (Course 1): FITNESSGRAM pre-assessment, part 1
- Objective: Complete baseline fitness tests
- Vocabulary: PACER, one-mile run, curl-up, push-up, Healthy Fitness Zone
- Materials: cones or tape, PACER audio (free online), measuring tape
- Assessment: Baseline FITNESSGRAM results recorded.

### Health & Wellness: Accessing valid health information and services
- Objective: Evaluate the credibility of health websites and claims
- Objective: Identify health care providers and services in the community
- Vocabulary: credible source, peer review, primary care provider, health insurance
- Materials: computer, source evaluation checklist
- Assessment: Unit 1 test and completed local health services directory.
