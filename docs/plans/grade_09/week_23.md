## Day 1 — Monday (school day 111, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Balcony scene* (60 min): Read 2.2 aloud in parts; collect light/dark imagery in a chart.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Linear vs. exponential* (60 min): Compare a $1/day plan and a doubling-penny plan in tables to see constant difference vs. constant ratio.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Beyond Mendel* (60 min): Solve problems on incomplete dominance and codominance (blood types).
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *1850 Act* (50 min): Read excerpts of the 1850 Act and explain how it allowed indenture of Native people.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Body vocabulary* (50 min): Label a body diagram with 30 parts and play a Simón dice game with family using the vocabulary.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Proportion guide* (50 min): Learn standard facial proportions (eyes halfway, etc.) and draw a front-view guide.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Streamline* (50 min): Practice streamline push-offs and measure how far you glide.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Abstinence* (45 min): Discuss abstinence as the only 100% effective method and reasons people choose it.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 112, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Juliet's caution* (60 min): Analyze how Juliet's speeches show more caution than Romeo's; cite two lines.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Decay simulation* (60 min): Shake 100 coins, remove heads each round, record the counts, and fit an exponential decay model.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Sex-linked traits* (60 min): Solve 8 sex-linked problems on color blindness and hemophilia, explaining why males are more often affected.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Burnett's address* (50 min): Analyze Gov. Burnett's 1851 message and identify its purpose and effects.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Doler* (50 min): Use doler with indirect object pronouns (Me duele la cabeza).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Eyes and nose* (50 min): Draw 6 studies of eyes and noses from a mirror.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Backstroke* (50 min): Learn backstroke arm rotation and flutter kick; swim 6 × 25 yards.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Contraceptive methods* (45 min): Learn about all FDA-approved methods using a CDC effectiveness chart.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 113, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Friar Laurence* (60 min): Read 2.3; explain the Friar's warning "Wisely and slow" and his reasons for helping.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Growth and decay factors* (60 min): Write functions like A=500(1.04)^t and identify percent rate of change.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Pedigrees* (60 min): Analyze pedigrees to determine inheritance patterns.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Unratified treaties* (50 min): Learn about the 18 unratified treaties of 1851–52 and their consequences for land.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Symptoms* (50 min): Write symptom descriptions for 10 illnesses (la gripe, una alergia) using tener, doler, and estar.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Mouth and ears* (50 min): Draw studies of mouths and ears.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Breaststroke kick* (50 min): Learn the whip kick holding the wall, then with a kickboard.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Comparing methods* (45 min): Compare methods by effectiveness, STI protection and access.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 114, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Nurse and marriage* (60 min): Read 2.4–2.6; perform a short scene with family focusing on vocal expression.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Romeo and Juliet, Act 2: the balcony and the secret marriage
- Objective: Analyze soliloquy and imagery
- Objective: Evaluate characters' haste
- Vocabulary: soliloquy, light/dark imagery, oxymoron, haste
- Materials: Romeo and Juliet, Act 2
- Assessment: Act 2 quiz and journal entry.

### Algebra I: Exponential growth and decay
- Objective: Write exponential functions from tables and contexts
- Objective: Interpret growth and decay factors
- Vocabulary: exponential function, growth factor, decay factor, initial value, geometric sequence, percent rate
- Materials: M&Ms or coins for decay simulation
- Assessment: Exponential quiz and simulation analysis.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Non-Mendelian inheritance and human traits
- Objective: Explain incomplete dominance, codominance, and sex-linked traits
- Objective: Analyze pedigrees
- Vocabulary: incomplete dominance, codominance, polygenic, sex-linked, pedigree
- Materials: pedigree worksheets
- Assessment: Non-Mendelian quiz and pedigree analysis.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Gold Rush, statehood, and Native survival
- Objective: Analyze state policies toward Native Californians
- Objective: Evaluate primary sources on violence and survival
- Vocabulary: genocide, indenture, treaty, reservation, Act for the Government and Protection of Indians
- Materials: 1850 Act for the Government and Protection of Indians (excerpt), Gov. Peter Burnett 1851 address (excerpt)
- Assessment: Document comparison.

### Spanish II: El cuerpo y la salud
- Objective: Name body parts and symptoms
- Objective: Describe how you feel
- Vocabulary: cabeza, garganta, fiebre, doler, tos, estornudar
- Materials: body diagram
- Assessment: Vocabulary quiz and role-play.

### Visual Art I: Facial proportions
- Objective: Draw the face with correct proportions
- Vocabulary: proportion, guidelines, features, three-quarter view, profile
- Materials: mirror, pencils, drawing paper
- Assessment: Proportion studies.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Swimming strokes: backstroke and breaststroke
- Objective: Perform backstroke and breaststroke
- Objective: Use streamline position
- Vocabulary: backstroke, breaststroke, whip kick, streamline, glide
- Materials: pool access, phone for video
- Assessment: Stroke demonstration and video self-analysis.

### Health & Wellness: Abstinence and contraception
- Objective: Explain abstinence and contraceptive methods
- Objective: Compare effectiveness of methods
- Vocabulary: abstinence, contraception, effectiveness, condom
- Materials: effectiveness chart
- Assessment: Quiz on contraception and decisions.
