## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Revise* (60 min): Revise the paper using a teacher conference and a checklist for synthesis and transitions.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Absolute value functions* (60 min): Graph 8 functions of the form y=a|x−h|+k and describe each transformation from y=|x|.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Trade-offs* (60 min): Evaluate your solution's costs, benefits, and trade-offs.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Local sources* (50 min): Find at least four sources on your issue (news article, city/county data, an agency report, a meeting agenda) and annotate each for credibility and bias.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Plan* (50 min): Outline a 4-minute video: your childhood (imperfect), a memorable event (preterite), your routine (present), and your future plans (future).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Exhibitions* (50 min): Take a virtual tour of a museum exhibition and analyze how works are grouped, labeled, and lit.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Cardio retest* (50 min): Retest the mile or PACER after a full warm-up and compare to weeks 3, 8, and 28.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Choose a topic* (45 min): Choose a teen health issue from the course (e.g., vaping, sleep, teen mental health access, wildfire smoke) and write a one-paragraph problem statement with a target audience.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Edit* (60 min): Edit for MLA format, parallel structure, and semicolon/colon use.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Piecewise functions* (60 min): Graph piecewise functions for tax brackets and shipping costs.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Stakeholders* (60 min): Consider perspectives of stakeholders and revise.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Stakeholder interview* (50 min): Interview a stakeholder (librarian, community organizer, council staffer, or neighbor) using prepared questions.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Script* (50 min): Write the script (500+ words) and self-edit with a checklist of tenses and agreement.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Select* (50 min): Select 10–12 works from the year for your exhibition, including the series.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Overload session* (50 min): Complete your strength session with one added set or heavier resistance and log it.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Research* (45 min): Gather three credible sources (.gov, .edu, peer-reviewed) and record two statistics and one expert recommendation on a note sheet with citations.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 173, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Presentation design* (60 min): Build a 6–8 slide presentation with images, a chart, and minimal text.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Square root functions* (60 min): Graph 6 functions of the form y=√(x−h)+k and state domain and range for each.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Prepare presentation* (60 min): Prepare a 6-minute presentation with the problem, data, design, simulation results, and trade-offs.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Evaluate arguments* (50 min): Summarize the strongest arguments on each side and flag any fallacious reasoning you found.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Conference* (50 min): Revise the script after a teacher conference focusing on accuracy and connectors (además, sin embargo).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Labels* (50 min): Write wall labels with title, medium, date, and a one-sentence description.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Motivation* (50 min): Read about intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation and write which strategies (music, partner, tracking app) kept you active this year; then do a 30-minute choice workout.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Create* (45 min): Build an advocacy product: a 60-second PSA video, an infographic poster, or a letter to a school board member or legislator with a clear call to action.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 174, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Speaker notes* (60 min): Write speaker notes and rehearse a 6-minute talk.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Comparing functions* (60 min): Compare function families from tables, graphs, and equations in a card sort.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Rehearse* (60 min): Rehearse the presentation twice with a timer and revise slides after feedback.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Action plan* (50 min): Choose an action (letter to an official, public comment, informational flyer, short video) and draft it with evidence and a clear request.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Record* (50 min): Record the video with visuals and subtitles in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Install* (50 min): Install the exhibition in your home or a community space, considering flow and lighting.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Lead a family workout* (50 min): Plan and lead a 30-minute workout for your family, including warm-up and cool-down.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Revise* (45 min): Get feedback from a parent using the advocacy rubric (accuracy, audience, persuasive appeal, call to action) and revise the product.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 175, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Submit the final research paper with Works Cited and a short reflection on your revision choices.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on other function families.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Present* (60 min): Present the solution to family or a co-op group.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Revise the draft action after parent/teacher feedback against a rubric for evidence and respectful tone.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Premiere* (50 min): Show the video to family and answer their questions in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Review & check* (50 min): Walk through the show and adjust placement.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a one-page evaluation of your plan: adherence percentage, results, and two adjustments.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Present* (45 min): Present the advocacy product to family or a co-op group, answer questions, and submit it with a works-cited list.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Revision and multimedia presentation
- Objective: Revise for clarity and evidence
- Objective: Design a multimedia presentation
- Vocabulary: revision, slide design, visual evidence
- Materials: research draft, slide software
- Assessment: Final research paper (W.9-10.7 rubric).

### Algebra I: Absolute value, piecewise, and square root functions
- Objective: Graph absolute value, piecewise, and square root functions
- Objective: Compare functions across representations
- Vocabulary: absolute value function, piecewise function, step function, square root function
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Function family quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Evaluating solutions and presenting
- Objective: Evaluate trade-offs
- Objective: Communicate a solution
- Vocabulary: trade-off, cost-benefit, stakeholder
- Materials: presentation software
- Assessment: Project presentation rubric.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Community action project: research and planning
- Objective: Research a local issue with multiple sources
- Objective: Plan a responsible civic action
- Vocabulary: stakeholder, advocacy, public comment, evidence, petition
- Materials: local newspaper archives, city council or school board agendas and minutes
- Assessment: Research notes and draft action product.

### Spanish II: Final project: "Mi vida en español" video
- Objective: Synthesize the year's structures in a personal video
- Objective: Use past, present, and future
- Vocabulary: pasado, presente, futuro, guion
- Materials: phone or camera, script template
- Assessment: Final video project rubric.

### Visual Art I: Curating an exhibition
- Objective: Curate and install an exhibition
- Vocabulary: curator, exhibition, label, installation, lighting
- Materials: artwork, tape or hooks, label cards
- Assessment: Installed exhibition.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Monitoring, retesting, and adjusting the plan
- Objective: Evaluate plan effectiveness with data
- Objective: Identify sources of self-motivation
- Vocabulary: evaluation, adherence, intrinsic motivation, extrinsic motivation
- Materials: activity log, stopwatch, PACER audio
- Assessment: Plan evaluation with data.

### Health & Wellness: Health advocacy project
- Objective: Choose a health issue and advocate for change
- Objective: Create an advocacy product
- Vocabulary: advocacy, target audience, call to action
- Materials: computer, poster board
- Assessment: Advocacy project rubric.
