## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Sound and form: sonnets and the Harlem Renaissance
- Objective: Identify meter, rhyme scheme, and volta
- Objective: Analyze how form supports meaning
- Vocabulary: sonnet, iambic pentameter, rhyme scheme, volta, couplet, refrain
- Materials: Shakespeare, Sonnets 18 and 130, Langston Hughes, "Harlem" and "Mother to Son", Paul Laurence Dunbar, "We Wear the Mask"
- Assessment: Poetic terms/scansion quiz and comparative paragraph.

### Algebra I: Linear modeling project
- Objective: Apply linear functions to a real data set
- Objective: Communicate a model
- Vocabulary: linear model, prediction, interpolation, extrapolation
- Materials: recorded data (e.g., candle burning or water draining), Desmos
- Assessment: Project report and Unit 4 test.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Natural selection and probability
- Objective: Apply statistics to trait frequency
- Objective: Explain adaptation in populations
- Vocabulary: allele frequency, selection pressure, adaptation, proportion
- Materials: HHMI BioInteractive rock pocket mouse film, spreadsheet
- Assessment: CER and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Resources, trade, and supply chains
- Objective: Explain the distribution and importance of resources
- Objective: Trace a global supply chain
- Vocabulary: renewable resource, nonrenewable resource, rare earth elements, supply chain, comparative advantage, export
- Materials: clothing and electronics labels, world map
- Assessment: Supply chain map and resource paragraph.

### Spanish II: En el restaurante: object pronouns
- Objective: Order at a restaurant
- Objective: Use direct and indirect object pronouns
- Vocabulary: mesero, la cuenta, pedir, traer, propina, lo, la, le
- Materials: menu from a Mexican or Spanish restaurant
- Assessment: Pronoun quiz and role-play rubric.

### Visual Art I: Principles of design
- Objective: Identify the principles of design
- Objective: Apply balance, emphasis, and rhythm
- Vocabulary: balance, emphasis, rhythm, unity, contrast, movement, pattern
- Materials: magazines, scissors, glue
- Assessment: Principles reference sheet and quiz.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Folk and cultural dances
- Objective: Perform dances from multiple cultures
- Vocabulary: folk dance, cultural dance, formation, partner
- Materials: music, videos
- Assessment: Cultural dance performance and explanation.

### Health & Wellness: The brain and addiction
- Objective: Explain how drugs affect the brain's reward system
- Objective: Describe why the teen brain is especially vulnerable
- Vocabulary: dopamine, tolerance, dependence, addiction, prefrontal cortex
- Materials: computer, brain diagram
- Assessment: Quiz and explanatory paragraph.
