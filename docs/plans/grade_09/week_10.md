## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *The occasion* (60 min): Read the clergymen's "A Call for Unity" and summarize their objections; note the date and context of the Birmingham campaign.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Visual patterns* (60 min): Build toothpick patterns and write how many toothpicks are in step n.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Carbon cycle model* (60 min): Draw a carbon cycle model across biosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, and geosphere with flux arrows.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Culture traits* (50 min): Define culture and identify traits of your own culture.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Irregular preterite* (50 min): Learn ser/ir, hacer, tener, estar, poder in the preterite.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Material safety* (50 min): Learn safe handling and cleanup of paints and brushes, including not pouring pigments down storm drains.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Rules and scoring* (50 min): Learn rally scoring and court boundaries; play practice games.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Stress response* (45 min): Diagram the fight-or-flight response and label the hormones and body changes involved.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 47, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *King's response, part 1* (60 min): Read the first third of the Letter; chart each criticism King answers and the reasoning he uses.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Arithmetic sequences* (60 min): Find common differences and write explicit formulas aₙ=a₁+(n−1)d for 10 sequences.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Keeling curve* (60 min): Graph Mauna Loa CO2 data and explain seasonal wiggles with photosynthesis and respiration.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Languages* (50 min): Color a world map of the major language families (Indo-European, Sino-Tibetan, Niger-Congo, Afro-Asiatic) and explain how Spanish spread to the Americas.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Día de los Muertos* (50 min): Read about Día de los Muertos traditions and their meanings.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Technique sampler* (50 min): Paint a sampler grid of wet-on-wet, gradient wash, glazing, salt, lifting, and wax resist.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Singles strategy* (50 min): Practice moving opponents with clears and drops.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Stressor inventory* (45 min): List your current stressors, rate each 1–5 and sort them into things you can and cannot control.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 48, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Just and unjust laws* (60 min): Read the middle section; explain King's definition of just vs. unjust laws in your own words with two quotations.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Recursive formulas* (60 min): Write recursive definitions and convert between recursive and explicit forms.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Quantitative model* (60 min): Use reservoir values (gigatons C) to calculate how burning fossil fuels changes atmospheric carbon.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Religions* (50 min): Map the hearths and spread of Hinduism, Buddhism, Judaism, Christianity, and Islam with arrows showing diffusion routes.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Ofrenda* (50 min): Build a small ofrenda (or digital one) honoring a family member or historical figure, labeled in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Sky study* (50 min): Paint three sky studies at different times of day.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Smash* (50 min): Learn the smash and when to use it.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Relaxation practice* (45 min): Practice box breathing, progressive muscle relaxation and a 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise, rating each afterward.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 49, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Tone and appeals* (60 min): Finish the Letter; analyze how King balances patience and urgency, then evaluate his strongest piece of evidence.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Sequences as functions* (60 min): Graph sequences as discrete points and compare to linear functions.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Ocean sink* (60 min): Model ocean acidification with carbonated water and red cabbage indicator.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Diffusion* (50 min): Trace the diffusion of a food (e.g., chili peppers via the Columbian Exchange) or music genre (hip-hop) on a world map with dates.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Narration* (50 min): Write a paragraph describing a holiday you celebrated using irregular preterite.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Botanical* (50 min): Paint a leaf or flower from observation with glazing.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Doubles* (50 min): Play doubles with family, practicing side-by-side and front-back formation.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Time management* (45 min): Build a weekly schedule that balances school, activities, sleep and downtime.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Argument structure: "Letter from Birmingham Jail"
- Objective: Trace claims, reasons, and evidence
- Objective: Evaluate how a writer answers counterclaims
- Vocabulary: claim, counterclaim, rebuttal, concession, evidence, reasoning
- Materials: Martin Luther King Jr., "Letter from Birmingham Jail" (1963), "A Call for Unity" statement by Alabama clergymen (1963)
- Assessment: One-page argument analysis scored for tracing claims and evaluating evidence.

### Algebra I: Sequences as functions
- Objective: Write arithmetic sequences explicitly and recursively
- Objective: Connect sequences to linear functions
- Vocabulary: sequence, term, common difference, recursive, explicit
- Materials: toothpicks or tiles for pattern building
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Biology – The Living Earth: The carbon cycle and Earth's spheres
- Objective: Model carbon cycling across spheres
- Objective: Quantify carbon reservoirs and fluxes
- Vocabulary: carbon cycle, reservoir, flux, carbon sink, sequestration, Keeling curve
- Materials: Keeling curve data, graph paper
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Culture, language, and religion
- Objective: Describe cultural traits and diffusion
- Objective: Map world languages and religions
- Vocabulary: culture, cultural diffusion, language family, religion, hearth
- Materials: world maps
- Assessment: Cultural geography quiz.

### Spanish II: Día de los Muertos and fiestas: irregular preterite
- Objective: Use irregular preterite verbs
- Objective: Describe cultural celebrations
- Vocabulary: Día de los Muertos, ofrenda, calavera, fui, hice, tuve, estuve
- Materials: ofrenda materials, marigold images
- Assessment: Irregular quiz and ofrenda labels.

### Visual Art I: Watercolor techniques
- Objective: Use watercolor techniques
- Vocabulary: wet-on-wet, wet-on-dry, wash, glaze, lifting, resist
- Materials: watercolor set, watercolor paper, salt, crayon
- Assessment: Technique sampler.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Badminton strategy and games
- Objective: Apply singles and doubles strategies
- Objective: Keep score
- Vocabulary: rally scoring, singles, doubles, court positioning, smash
- Materials: badminton set
- Assessment: Game play rubric including sportsmanship.

### Health & Wellness: Stress and coping
- Objective: Explain the stress response
- Objective: Practice healthy coping strategies
- Vocabulary: stressor, fight-or-flight, cortisol, coping strategy
- Materials: timer, journal
- Assessment: Stress toolkit card and quiz.
