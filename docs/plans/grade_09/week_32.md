## Day 1 — Monday (school day 156, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Halloween* (60 min): Read Chapters 27–28; analyze the attack and how the author builds suspense.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Completing the square* (60 min): Solve 10 equations by completing the square with tiles, then symbolically.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Greenhouse jar lab* (60 min): Compare temperature in covered and uncovered jars under a lamp.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Executive Order 9066* (50 min): Read Executive Order 9066 and identify words that hide its effect (e.g., "military areas"); note who it targeted without naming them.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Comparatives* (50 min): Learn comparisons of inequality and equality and write 10 comparing family members.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Contemporary artists* (50 min): Explore work by Kara Walker, El Anatsui, Ai Weiwei, and Judy Baca and note their themes.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Choose* (50 min): Choose a lifetime activity (tennis, cycling, hiking, rock climbing gym) and research rules and safety.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Chronic diseases* (45 min): Research heart disease, type 2 diabetes and cancer risk factors.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 157, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Boo Radley* (60 min): Read Chapters 29–31; explain how Scout standing on the Radley porch completes the empathy theme.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Deriving the formula* (60 min): Complete the square on ax²+bx+c=0 to derive the quadratic formula.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Climate models* (60 min): Use an online climate simulator (e.g., En-ROADS) to test emissions scenarios.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Lange photos* (50 min): Analyze three Dorothea Lange photos of Manzanar and the government's decision to impound many of them.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Superlatives* (50 min): Learn superlatives and irregular forms (mejor, peor, mayor, menor).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Artist research* (50 min): Research one artist in depth and write a 400-word profile with images.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Lesson* (50 min): Take a free community lesson or follow a tutorial video and practice for 40 minutes.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Family history* (45 min): Interview a family member to build a family health history.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 158, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Essay plan* (60 min): Plan an essay tracing one theme (empathy, courage, or justice) across the novel.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Using the formula* (60 min): Solve 15 equations with the quadratic formula, giving exact and decimal answers.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Ecosystem effects* (60 min): Analyze effects of warming on coral reefs and Sierra Nevada snowpack.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Korematsu* (50 min): Read about Fred Korematsu's case and the 1983 reversal.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Prado tour* (50 min): Take the Museo del Prado's online tour and choose works by Velázquez and Goya.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Mind map* (50 min): Make a mind map of personal themes (family, place, nature, technology).
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Practice* (50 min): Practice the activity for 40 minutes, filming one skill.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Prevention* (45 min): Make a chart of recommended screenings (blood pressure, cholesterol, skin checks, Pap/HPV, testicular self-exam) and the lifestyle habits that lower chronic-disease risk.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 159, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Draft and revise* (60 min): Draft and revise the 900-word theme essay with at least six quotations.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Discriminant* (60 min): Use b²−4ac to predict the number of real solutions and explain why some solutions are irrational.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Ecosystem change* (60 min): Evaluate claims about ecosystems shifting to new states (e.g., kelp to urchin barrens).
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Redress* (50 min): Analyze the Civil Liberties Act of 1988 and the apology.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Compare art* (50 min): Compare "Las Meninas" with Picasso's versions in a written paragraph using comparatives.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Proposal* (50 min): Write a proposal for a 3-piece body of work with media and sketches.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Analyze* (50 min): Compare the activity's fitness benefits using heart rate and the FITT principle.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Personal care* (45 min): Learn about dental care, vision, hearing protection and sun safety.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Resolution and theme essay: Chapters 27–31
- Objective: Analyze the resolution and Boo Radley
- Objective: Write an explanatory essay on theme
- Vocabulary: resolution, empathy, perspective, standing in someone's shoes
- Materials: To Kill a Mockingbird, Chapters 27–31
- Assessment: Theme essay and Unit 7 test.

### Algebra I: Completing the square and the quadratic formula
- Objective: Solve by completing the square
- Objective: Derive and use the quadratic formula
- Vocabulary: completing the square, quadratic formula, discriminant, irrational solution
- Materials: algebra tiles
- Assessment: Quadratic formula quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Climate change and Earth systems
- Objective: Use models of Earth systems and human activity
- Objective: Explain climate effects on ecosystems
- Vocabulary: greenhouse effect, feedback, climate model, sea level rise
- Materials: jars, thermometers, lamp, plastic wrap
- Assessment: Greenhouse lab report and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Japanese American incarceration
- Objective: Analyze Executive Order 9066
- Objective: Evaluate redress
- Vocabulary: Executive Order 9066, incarceration, Korematsu v. United States, redress, Civil Liberties Act of 1988
- Materials: Executive Order 9066 (National Archives), Dorothea Lange photographs, Densho archive
- Assessment: Argument paragraph.

### Spanish II: Comparing art: comparatives and superlatives
- Objective: Use comparatives and superlatives
- Objective: Compare works from Spain and Latin America
- Vocabulary: más...que, menos...que, tan...como, el/la más...de, mejor, peor
- Materials: Museo del Prado online collection
- Assessment: Comparatives quiz and conversation.

### Visual Art I: Contemporary art and finding your theme
- Objective: Research a contemporary artist
- Objective: Choose a theme for a body of work
- Vocabulary: contemporary art, body of work, theme, artist research
- Materials: Smithsonian Learning Lab, sketchbook
- Assessment: Artist profile and approved proposal.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Lifetime activity choice
- Objective: Try a new lifetime activity
- Objective: Analyze its fitness benefits
- Vocabulary: lifetime activity, skill progression
- Materials: equipment for chosen activity (tennis, hiking, cycling, skating)
- Assessment: Lifetime activity presentation.

### Health & Wellness: Chronic diseases and personal health care
- Objective: Identify risk factors for chronic disease
- Objective: Practice personal health habits
- Vocabulary: chronic disease, hypertension, diabetes, cancer screening, SPF
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: Personal prevention plan.
