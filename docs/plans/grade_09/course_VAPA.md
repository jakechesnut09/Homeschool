# Visual Art I (Grade 9)

Visual Art I is a foundational studio course covering drawing, perspective, color theory and painting, printmaking, design, sculpture and clay, portraiture, photography, collage, and digital art. Each unit pairs hands-on work with study of artists from many cultures and the critique process. The year ends with a personal body of work, an artist statement, and a curated home or community exhibition.

*Framework:* 2019 California Arts Standards for Public Schools (Visual Arts, High School Proficient); 2020 CA Arts Education Framework  
*Time:* 50 min/day
*UC/CSU a–g:* f · *Credits:* 10.0

## Units

### Unit 1: Drawing Foundations: Line, Shape, and Value (weeks 1–5)
Drawing is a way of seeing; line and value turn flat paper into form.

- Week 1: Sketchbook habits and contour line
- Week 2: Shape, gesture, and proportion
- Week 3: Value: from light to dark
- Week 4: Still life value drawing
- Week 5: Texture and drawing media exploration

### Unit 2: Space and Perspective (weeks 6–8)
Artists create the illusion of depth with perspective systems, overlap, and atmosphere.

- Week 6: One-point perspective
- Week 7: Two-point perspective
- Week 8: Atmospheric perspective and landscape

### Unit 3: Color Theory and Painting (weeks 9–13)
Color relationships create mood, light, and harmony; painting media each have their own behaviors.

- Week 9: Color theory
- Week 10: Watercolor techniques
- Week 11: Impressionism and landscape painting
- Week 12: Color and expression
- Week 13: Unit 3 painting critique

### Unit 4: Principles of Design and Printmaking (weeks 14–18)
Principles of design organize the elements; printmaking multiplies an image and has a long history as public art.

- Week 14: Principles of design
- Week 15: Relief printmaking: history and design
- Week 16: Relief printmaking: carving and printing
- Week 17: Graphic design: the public poster
- Week 18: Semester portfolio review and critique

### Unit 5: Three-Dimensional Art and Sculpture (weeks 19–22)
Sculpture and installation change how we experience space and place.

- Week 19: Form and space: paper sculpture
- Week 20: Clay: hand-building
- Week 21: Clay project: functional or sculptural vessel
- Week 22: Installation and site-specific art

### Unit 6: Portraiture and the Figure (weeks 23–27)
Portraits reveal identity through proportion, expression, and symbol.

- Week 23: Facial proportions
- Week 24: Self-portrait
- Week 25: The figure: gesture and proportion
- Week 26: Portraiture across cultures
- Week 27: Portrait project: someone who matters

### Unit 7: Photography, Collage, and Mixed Media (weeks 28–31)
Photography and collage let artists reframe everyday life and combine images into new meanings.

- Week 28: Photography: composition and light
- Week 29: Photo essay: my community
- Week 30: Collage and mixed media
- Week 31: Digital art basics

### Unit 8: Personal Voice, Portfolio, and Exhibition (weeks 32–36)
Artists develop a body of work around a personal theme and share it through a curated exhibition.

- Week 32: Contemporary art and finding your theme
- Week 33: Body of work: piece 1 and 2
- Week 34: Body of work: piece 3 and artist statement
- Week 35: Curating an exhibition
- Week 36: Exhibition opening and final reflection

## Standards

- **VA:Cn10.1.HSI** Document the process of developing ideas from early stages to fully elaborated ideas.
- **VA:Cn11.1.HSI** Describe how knowledge of culture, traditions, and history may influence personal responses to art.
- **VA:Cr1.1.HSI** Use multiple approaches to begin creative endeavors.
- **VA:Cr1.2.HSI** Shape an artistic investigation of an aspect of present-day life using a contemporary practice of art or design.
- **VA:Cr2.1.HSI** Engage in making a work of art or design without having a preconceived plan; experiment with materials and approaches.
- **VA:Cr2.2.HSI** Explain how traditional and nontraditional materials may impact human health and the environment, and demonstrate safe handling of materials, tools, and equipment.
- **VA:Cr2.3.HSI** Collaboratively develop a proposal for an installation, artwork, or space design that transforms the perception and experience of a particular place.
- **VA:Cr3.1.HSI** Apply relevant criteria from traditional and contemporary cultural contexts to examine, reflect on, and plan revisions for works of art in progress.
- **VA:Pr4.1.HSI** Analyze, select, and curate artifacts and/or artworks for presentation and preservation.
- **VA:Pr5.1.HSI** Analyze and evaluate the reasons and ways an exhibition is presented.
- **VA:Pr6.1.HSI** Analyze and describe the impact that an exhibition or collection has on personal awareness of social, cultural, or political beliefs.
- **VA:Re7.1.HSI** Hypothesize ways in which art influences perception and understanding of human experiences.
- **VA:Re7.2.HSI** Analyze how one's understanding of the world is affected by experiencing visual imagery.
- **VA:Re8.1.HSI** Interpret an artwork or collection of works, supported by relevant and sufficient evidence found in the work and its various contexts.
- **VA:Re9.1.HSI** Establish relevant criteria in order to evaluate a work of art or collection of works.
