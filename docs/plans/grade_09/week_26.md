## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Romeo and Juliet, Act 5 and film adaptation
- Objective: Analyze the tragic resolution
- Objective: Compare a key scene across two media
- Vocabulary: tragic flaw, catharsis, adaptation, cinematography
- Materials: Romeo and Juliet, Act 5, film versions (Zeffirelli 1968 or Luhrmann 1996, library)
- Assessment: Act 5 quiz; film comparison paragraph (RL.9-10.7).

### Algebra I: Factoring polynomials
- Objective: Factor GCF and trinomials
- Objective: Recognize special factoring patterns
- Vocabulary: factor, GCF, trinomial, difference of squares, perfect square trinomial
- Materials: algebra tiles, area models
- Assessment: Factoring quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Cell membranes and transport
- Objective: Explain passive and active transport
- Objective: Investigate osmosis
- Vocabulary: diffusion, osmosis, active transport, hypertonic, hypotonic, isotonic
- Materials: eggs, vinegar, corn syrup, water
- Assessment: Egg osmosis lab report and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Segregation, the Great Migration, and civil rights
- Objective: Analyze Jim Crow laws and the Great Migration
- Objective: Explain civil rights strategies
- Vocabulary: Jim Crow, Great Migration, redlining, civil rights, NAACP
- Materials: HOLC redlining maps (Mapping Inequality), Brown v. Board decision excerpt
- Assessment: Redlining analysis and quiz.

### Spanish II: Health and wellness across cultures
- Objective: Compare health practices
- Objective: Present health advice
- Vocabulary: remedio casero, medicina tradicional, bienestar
- Materials: articles on remedios caseros
- Assessment: PSA rubric and unit test.

### Visual Art I: Portraiture across cultures
- Objective: Analyze portraiture across cultures
- Vocabulary: court portrait, mask, idealization, realism
- Materials: museum images
- Assessment: Comparison essay and quiz.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Building a strength routine
- Objective: Design a full-body routine
- Objective: Apply progressive overload
- Vocabulary: full-body routine, split routine, rest interval, overload
- Materials: bands, dumbbells or jugs
- Assessment: Strength routine plan and log.

### Health & Wellness: Unintentional injuries: home, water, sports and concussion
- Objective: Identify causes of common unintentional injuries
- Objective: Explain concussion signs and return-to-play rules
- Vocabulary: unintentional injury, concussion, drowning, helmet
- Materials: home safety checklist
- Assessment: Home safety audit.
