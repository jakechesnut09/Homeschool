## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Historical context and Maycomb: To Kill a Mockingbird, Chapters 1–6
- Objective: Use historical context to interpret a novel
- Objective: Analyze narrative point of view
- Vocabulary: Jim Crow, Great Depression, retrospective narrator, setting
- Materials: Harper Lee, To Kill a Mockingbird (library), Library of Congress photos of 1930s Alabama; Scottsboro Boys trial overview
- Assessment: Reading quiz and setting paragraph.

### Algebra I: Graphing quadratic functions
- Objective: Identify key features of parabolas
- Objective: Graph from standard form
- Vocabulary: parabola, vertex, axis of symmetry, maximum, minimum, quadratic
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Parabola quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Homeostasis and feedback
- Objective: Investigate feedback mechanisms
- Objective: Explain negative and positive feedback
- Vocabulary: homeostasis, negative feedback, positive feedback, set point, thermoregulation
- Materials: thermometer, stopwatch, heart rate monitor (optional)
- Assessment: Homeostasis lab report.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Borders and the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo
- Objective: Analyze the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo
- Objective: Explain the impact on Mexican Californians
- Vocabulary: Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo, Californio, land grant, border
- Materials: Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo (National Archives), Land Act of 1851 summary
- Assessment: Treaty analysis.

### Spanish II: El medio ambiente: problems and solutions
- Objective: Discuss environmental problems
- Objective: Compare environmental issues across regions
- Vocabulary: contaminación, reciclar, deforestación, proteger, sequía, recursos naturales
- Materials: Spanish articles on the Amazon and Mexico City air quality, household recycling bins
- Assessment: Recycling report and reading check.

### Visual Art I: Photography: composition and light
- Objective: Apply composition rules in photographs
- Objective: Control natural light
- Vocabulary: rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, depth of field, golden hour
- Materials: phone or digital camera
- Assessment: Composition contact sheet.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Spring fitness assessment
- Objective: Complete the FITNESSGRAM
- Objective: Evaluate progress toward goals
- Vocabulary: post-test, growth, Healthy Fitness Zone
- Materials: PFT testing materials
- Assessment: Spring FITNESSGRAM results and progress analysis.

### Health & Wellness: Online safety and digital wellness
- Objective: Describe risks online including sextortion
- Objective: Protect privacy and digital footprint
- Vocabulary: digital footprint, sextortion, privacy settings, phishing
- Materials: phone, computer
- Assessment: Digital safety quiz.
