# PE 9 (Course 1) (Grade 9)

PE 9 follows the CA High School Course 1 model: fitness assessment and training principles, track and field, badminton and pickleball, rhythms and dance, balance/tumbling/yoga, aquatics and water safety, strength training, and lifetime individual activities like disc golf and jump rope. Students complete the California Physical Fitness Test (FITNESSGRAM) in fall and spring, log activity, and build a personal fitness plan. Aquatics uses a community pool where available, with land-based water-safety alternatives.

*Framework:* California Physical Education Model Content Standards (High School Course 1); 2009 CA Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 50 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Fitness Foundations and Pre-Assessment (weeks 1–4)
Health-related fitness can be measured, and the FITT principle guides how we improve it.

- Week 1: Components of fitness and safe warm-ups
- Week 2: Heart rate and the FITT principle
- Week 3: FITNESSGRAM pre-assessment, part 1
- Week 4: Pre-assessment, part 2, and goal setting

### Unit 2: Running and Track & Field (weeks 5–8)
Efficient mechanics and smart pacing make running faster and safer.

- Week 5: Running form and pacing
- Week 6: Sprinting and starts
- Week 7: Jumping and throwing events
- Week 8: Cardio endurance challenge

### Unit 3: Dual Activities: Badminton and Pickleball (weeks 9–12)
Racket sports reward consistent technique, shot selection, and good sportsmanship.

- Week 9: Badminton fundamentals
- Week 10: Badminton strategy and games
- Week 11: Pickleball fundamentals
- Week 12: Racket sports tournament

### Unit 4: Rhythms and Dance (weeks 13–16)
Dance builds coordination and fitness while expressing culture and creativity.

- Week 13: Line dance and rhythm basics
- Week 14: Folk and cultural dances
- Week 15: Choreography
- Week 16: Dance fitness and performance

### Unit 5: Balance, Tumbling, and Yoga (weeks 17–20)
Body control, flexibility, and core strength underlie every movement skill.

- Week 17: Balance and body management
- Week 18: Tumbling basics and semester fitness check
- Week 19: Yoga and flexibility
- Week 20: Core strength and stability

### Unit 6: Aquatics and Water Safety (weeks 21–24)
Knowing how to stay safe in and around water is a lifesaving skill.

- Week 21: Water safety on land
- Week 22: Swimming fundamentals: floating, treading, freestyle
- Week 23: Swimming strokes: backstroke and breaststroke
- Week 24: Aquatic fitness and survival skills

### Unit 7: Strength Training and Physical Fitness Test (weeks 25–28)
Safe resistance training builds muscular strength and endurance measured on the FITNESSGRAM.

- Week 25: Strength training safety and technique
- Week 26: Building a strength routine
- Week 27: California Physical Fitness Test preparation
- Week 28: Spring fitness assessment

### Unit 8: Individual Lifetime Activities: Disc Golf and Jump Rope (weeks 29–32)
Lifetime activities keep us moving long after school ends.

- Week 29: Jump rope skills and conditioning
- Week 30: Disc golf fundamentals
- Week 31: Disc golf rounds and course strategy
- Week 32: Lifetime activity choice

### Unit 9: Personal Fitness Plan (weeks 33–36)
A personal plan built on assessment data and goals keeps fitness going for life.

- Week 33: Designing a personal fitness plan
- Week 34: Implementing the plan
- Week 35: Monitoring, retesting, and adjusting the plan
- Week 36: Lifelong fitness portfolio and reflection

## Standards

- **PE-HS1-1.1** Combine and apply movement patterns, from simple to complex, in aquatics, rhythms/dance, and individual and dual activities.
- **PE-HS1-1.2** Demonstrate proficient skills in at least two individual or dual activities (e.g., badminton, pickleball, track and field, disc golf).
- **PE-HS1-1.3** Perform rhythmic sequences and dances from a variety of cultures, and create and perform original movement sequences.
- **PE-HS1-1.4** Demonstrate water safety and basic aquatic skills (floating, treading, swim strokes) or land-based water-safety knowledge.
- **PE-HS1-1.5** Demonstrate balance, tumbling, and body-management skills safely.
- **PE-HS1-2.1** Apply biomechanical principles (force, leverage, follow-through, center of gravity) to analyze and improve movement skills.
- **PE-HS1-2.2** Use practice principles and feedback, including video self-analysis, to improve performance.
- **PE-HS1-2.3** Explain and apply offensive, defensive, and scoring strategies in dual and individual activities.
- **PE-HS1-3.1** Participate in moderate-to-vigorous physical activity at least four days each week.
- **PE-HS1-3.2** Assess and meet the Healthy Fitness Zone on the FITNESSGRAM components of the California Physical Fitness Test.
- **PE-HS1-3.3** Develop, implement, and monitor a personal physical fitness plan.
- **PE-HS1-4.1** Explain the components of health-related fitness and apply the FITT principle (frequency, intensity, time, type).
- **PE-HS1-4.2** Calculate target heart rate zones and use heart rate and perceived exertion to monitor intensity.
- **PE-HS1-4.3** Explain warm-up, cool-down, injury prevention, hydration, and nutrition as they relate to physical activity.
- **PE-HS1-5.1** Demonstrate responsible personal and social behavior, safety, and sportsmanship in physical activity.
- **PE-HS1-5.2** Work cooperatively and show leadership in partner and group activities.
- **PE-HS1-5.3** Set, monitor, and evaluate personal goals; identify sources of self-motivation for lifelong activity.
