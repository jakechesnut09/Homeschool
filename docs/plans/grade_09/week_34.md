## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~425 min)

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Note-taking, outlining, and drafting the research paper
- Objective: Synthesize information from multiple sources
- Objective: Avoid plagiarism through paraphrase and citation
- Vocabulary: paraphrase, synthesis, plagiarism, works cited, signal phrase
- Materials: annotated bibliography, note cards or digital notes
- Assessment: Full research draft with MLA Works Cited.

### Algebra I: Linear-quadratic systems and Unit 10 test
- Objective: Solve linear-quadratic systems
- Objective: Synthesize quadratic solving
- Vocabulary: linear-quadratic system, intersection points
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 10 test.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Designing and simulating solutions
- Objective: Design a solution
- Objective: Test a solution with a simulation
- Vocabulary: prototype, simulation, iteration
- Materials: spreadsheet, recycled materials
- Assessment: Prototype or design plan with simulation results.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Coalitions and multiracial social movements
- Objective: Compare strategies of social movements
- Objective: Explain how coalitions form across groups
- Vocabulary: coalition, solidarity, social movement, intersectionality, nonviolent direct action
- Materials: 1968 Third World Liberation Front demands (SF State), movement timeline cards
- Assessment: Approved project proposal and strategies chart.

### Spanish II: Unit 8 project: virtual museum exhibit
- Objective: Curate and present a virtual exhibit
- Objective: Use descriptive language and comparisons
- Vocabulary: museo, exposición, curador/a, obra
- Materials: slides software, museum websites
- Assessment: Exhibit rubric and unit test.

### Visual Art I: Body of work: piece 3 and artist statement
- Objective: Complete a series
- Objective: Write an artist statement
- Vocabulary: artist statement, cohesion
- Materials: chosen media
- Assessment: Completed series and artist statement.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Implementing the plan
- Objective: Follow and monitor the plan
- Vocabulary: monitoring, adherence, adjustment
- Materials: log, heart rate app
- Assessment: Activity log.

### Health & Wellness: Environmental and community health
- Objective: Explain how the environment affects health
- Objective: Identify community health resources
- Vocabulary: air pollution, water quality, public health, environmental justice
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.
