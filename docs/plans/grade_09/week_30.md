## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Calpurnia's church* (60 min): Read Chapters 12–13; compare Calpurnia's two worlds and Aunt Alexandra's ideas of family.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Projectile lab* (60 min): Toss a ball straight up, time the flight, and use h(t)=−16t²+v₀t+h₀ to estimate initial velocity.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Enzymes* (60 min): Model enzyme-substrate specificity with a lock-and-key drawing.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Mendez v. Westminster* (50 min): Read the 1946 ruling and connect to Brown v. Board.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Choose a problem* (50 min): Choose a local environmental problem (e.g., plastic on beaches, water waste) and list facts in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Romare Bearden* (50 min): Study Bearden's collages of Harlem life and Hannah Höch's photomontage.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Backhand throw* (50 min): Learn backhand grip, reach-back, and follow-through; throw 30 discs at a target.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Emergency steps* (45 min): Learn the Check–Call–Care steps and practice a mock 911 call giving location, situation and number of victims.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 147, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *The mob* (60 min): Read Chapters 14–15; analyze how Scout disperses the mob outside the jail.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Max height* (60 min): Find maximum height and when it occurs for several launch scenarios.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Catalase lab* (60 min): Test catalase in potato with hydrogen peroxide at three temperatures and measure foam height.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Walkouts* (50 min): Analyze the 1968 East LA walkouts and students' demands.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Slogans and recommendations* (50 min): Write three slogans and eight recommendations using formal commands and the subjunctive.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Gather* (50 min): Gather images, textures, and papers around a theme (home, memory, the future).
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Forehand throw* (50 min): Learn the forehand (sidearm) throw; throw 30 discs.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Bleeding and burns* (45 min): Practice treating cuts and burns using a first aid kit.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 148, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Testimony* (60 min): Read Chapters 16–18; record each witness's claims and the evidence against them.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Area models* (60 min): Maximize the area of a garden with 40 ft of fencing using a quadratic function.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Enzyme data* (60 min): Graph foam height vs. temperature for the catalase lab and explain the drop at high temperature as denaturation.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Chicano art* (50 min): Analyze Chicano murals, such as Judith Baca's Great Wall of Los Angeles.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Create* (50 min): Design a bilingual poster or 60-second video campaign.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Compose* (50 min): Experiment with arrangements without gluing; photograph three options.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Putting* (50 min): Practice putting from 10, 15, and 20 feet into a basket or target.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Hands-Only CPR* (45 min): Practice Hands-Only CPR on a pillow to the beat of 'Stayin' Alive'.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 149, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Tom's testimony* (60 min): Read Chapter 19; analyze the moment Tom says he felt sorry for Mayella.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Interpreting* (60 min): Answer context questions about domain, range, and intercepts for projectile models.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a Unit 5 review guide covering cells, transport, body systems, homeostasis, and enzymes.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Latino diversity* (50 min): Research the diversity of Latino communities in California.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Present* (50 min): Present the campaign and answer two questions in Spanish.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: The trial: Chapters 12–19
- Objective: Evaluate evidence presented at trial
- Objective: Analyze characterization through testimony
- Vocabulary: testimony, cross-examination, prejudice, evidence
- Materials: To Kill a Mockingbird, Chapters 12–19
- Assessment: Mock closing argument and evidence chart.

### Algebra I: Quadratic modeling: projectiles and area
- Objective: Model projectile motion
- Objective: Interpret key features in context
- Vocabulary: projectile, initial velocity, gravity, h(t)=−16t²+v₀t+h₀
- Materials: ball, video recording or stopwatch, Desmos
- Assessment: Lab write-up and Unit 9 test.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Nutrition, enzymes, and Unit 5 test
- Objective: Investigate enzyme activity
- Objective: Connect biomolecules to body function
- Vocabulary: enzyme, substrate, active site, denature, catalyst
- Materials: hydrogen peroxide, potato or liver, thermometer
- Assessment: Catalase lab and Unit 5 test.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Education rights and Chicano movement
- Objective: Analyze education rights cases
- Objective: Explain the Chicano movement
- Vocabulary: Mendez v. Westminster, East LA walkouts, Chicano movement
- Materials: Mendez v. Westminster ruling excerpt, 1968 walkout demands
- Assessment: Unit 10 assessment.

### Spanish II: Unit 7 project: environmental awareness campaign
- Objective: Create a campaign with recommendations
- Objective: Present persuasively in Spanish
- Vocabulary: campaña, cartel, eslogan, persuadir
- Materials: poster board or video app
- Assessment: Campaign rubric and unit test.

### Visual Art I: Collage and mixed media
- Objective: Create a mixed-media collage that combines images into new meaning
- Vocabulary: collage, photomontage, mixed media, juxtaposition
- Materials: magazines, old photos (copies), glue, acrylic paint
- Assessment: Mixed-media collage and interpretation.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Disc golf fundamentals
- Objective: Throw backhand and forehand
- Objective: Explain disc golf rules
- Vocabulary: backhand, forehand, putter, driver, hyzer, anhyzer
- Materials: flying discs, local disc golf course or targets
- Assessment: Disc golf skills test.

### Health & Wellness: Basic first aid and Hands-Only CPR
- Objective: Describe basic first aid
- Objective: Demonstrate Hands-Only CPR
- Vocabulary: first aid, CPR, AED, Good Samaritan
- Materials: pillow or manikin, first aid kit
- Assessment: Unit 6 test and CPR skills check.
