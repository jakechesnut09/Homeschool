## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Beggar in the hall* (60 min): Read Books 17–18; note how the suitors' treatment of the beggar justifies what follows.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Comparing distributions* (60 min): Compare reaction times with dominant vs. non-dominant hand using box plots.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Central dogma* (60 min): Draw and explain the central dogma (DNA → RNA → protein) with arrows labeled replication, transcription, translation.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Media analysis* (50 min): Analyze stereotypes in vintage advertisements and mascots; describe the message each sends.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Map study* (50 min): Label 21 Spanish-speaking countries and capitals on a blank map.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Clay safety* (50 min): Learn clay safety (wet cleanup to avoid silica dust) and wedge clay.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Core anatomy* (50 min): Learn the core muscles and do a 15-minute core circuit.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Endocrine system* (45 min): Learn how hormones drive puberty and label the endocrine glands.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 97, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Penelope's test* (60 min): Read Book 19 and Book 21 (the bow); analyze Penelope's intelligence and caution.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Outliers* (60 min): Identify outliers with the 1.5×IQR rule and discuss their effect on mean and SD.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Transcription* (60 min): Transcribe 5 DNA template sequences into mRNA and mark start and stop codons.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Doll study* (50 min): Read about the Clarks' doll experiments cited in Brown v. Board (1954) and explain internalized bias.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Geography vocabulary* (50 min): Describe landforms in Spanish (los Andes, la selva amazónica, el desierto de Atacama).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Pinch pot* (50 min): Make a pinch pot with even walls.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Planks* (50 min): Practice plank, side plank, and bird-dog progressions.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Female anatomy* (45 min): Label female reproductive anatomy and describe the menstrual cycle.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 98, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Slaughter of the suitors* (60 min): Read Book 22; debate in writing whether Odysseus's revenge is justice or excess.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Two-way tables* (60 min): Survey 20 people on two categorical questions and build a two-way table.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Translation* (60 min): Translate mRNA into amino acid chains using a codon chart; build paper protein chains.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Four levels* (50 min): Sort 12 scenarios into ideological, institutional, interpersonal, and internalized levels.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Reading* (50 min): Read a Spanish article about Machu Picchu or the Galápagos and answer questions.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Coil* (50 min): Build a coil vessel using score and slip.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Stability* (50 min): Combine balance and core in a stability circuit.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Male anatomy* (45 min): Label male reproductive anatomy and describe its functions.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: The Odyssey: the suitors and the bow (Books 17–23)
- Objective: Evaluate justice and revenge in the epic
- Objective: Analyze Penelope as a complex character
- Vocabulary: justice, revenge, fidelity, test, denouement
- Materials: The Odyssey, Books 17–23 (selected)
- Assessment: Discussion notes and Penelope character analysis chart.

### Algebra I: Comparing distributions and categorical data
- Objective: Compare data sets using shape, center, and spread
- Objective: Analyze two-way tables
- Vocabulary: skewed, symmetric, outlier, two-way table, relative frequency, conditional frequency
- Materials: survey results, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Quiz and survey analysis.

### Biology – The Living Earth: From DNA to protein
- Objective: Model transcription and translation
- Objective: Explain how DNA determines protein structure
- Vocabulary: gene, mRNA, transcription, translation, codon, amino acid, ribosome
- Materials: codon chart, bead or paper protein chains
- Assessment: Protein synthesis quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Stereotypes, bias, and the levels of oppression
- Objective: Identify stereotypes in media
- Objective: Distinguish ideological, institutional, interpersonal, and internalized racism
- Vocabulary: stereotype, prejudice, discrimination, bias, institutional racism, internalized oppression
- Materials: advertisements and film clips, Kenneth and Mamie Clark doll study summary
- Assessment: Scenario quiz and counter-story review.

### Spanish II: Geography of the Spanish-speaking world
- Objective: Locate Spanish-speaking countries and capitals
- Objective: Describe geography in Spanish
- Vocabulary: país, capital, montaña, selva, desierto, costa
- Materials: blank map of Latin America and Spain
- Assessment: Map quiz.

### Visual Art I: Clay: hand-building
- Objective: Use pinch, coil, and slab methods
- Objective: Handle clay safely
- Vocabulary: pinch pot, coil, slab, score and slip, leather-hard, air-dry clay
- Materials: air-dry clay, plastic knife, water cup, rolling pin
- Assessment: Three hand-building samples.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Core strength and stability
- Objective: Build core strength
- Vocabulary: core, plank, stability, isometric
- Materials: mat
- Assessment: Core assessment.

### Health & Wellness: Reproductive anatomy, puberty and development
- Objective: Describe reproductive anatomy and physiology
- Objective: Explain changes during adolescence
- Vocabulary: hormone, ovary, testes, menstrual cycle, puberty
- Materials: anatomy diagrams
- Assessment: Anatomy quiz.
