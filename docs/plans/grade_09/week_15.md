## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Choose a recitation poem* (60 min): Browse the Poetry Out Loud anthology and choose a 14–25 line poem; begin memorizing and mark pauses and emphasis.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Graphing systems* (60 min): Solve 8 systems by graphing; classify each as one, none, or infinitely many solutions.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Genetic drift* (60 min): Simulate bottlenecks with beads in a cup and compare small vs. large populations.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Owens Valley primary sources* (50 min): Examine Calisphere photos of Owens Lake before and after the 1913 Los Angeles Aqueduct and list observable changes.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Affirmative tú commands* (50 min): Learn regular and irregular forms (pon, haz, ven).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Hokusai* (50 min): Study Hokusai's "The Great Wave" and ukiyo-e woodblock printing.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Choreography elements* (50 min): Learn levels, pathways, and dynamics.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Nicotine effects* (45 min): Research short- and long-term health effects of nicotine and vaping, including lung injury.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Analysis essay* (60 min): Write a 600-word analysis of your recitation poem explaining how two devices build its theme.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Intersections as solutions* (60 min): Explain why the intersection of f(x) and g(x) solves f(x)=g(x); approximate solutions with a table.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Founder effect* (60 min): Read about the Amish population and Ellis–van Creveld syndrome.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Aqueduct story* (50 min): Read about William Mulholland and Owens Valley farmers' resistance; build a cause-and-effect chart.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Negative commands* (50 min): Learn negative tú commands (no cortes, no pongas) and rewrite 10 kitchen safety rules as negative commands.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Posada and the Taller de Gráfica Popular* (50 min): Study José Guadalupe Posada's calaveras and Mexican political prints.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Create* (50 min): Create 32 counts of choreography to a song.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *What's in a vape?* (45 min): Analyze an ingredient list of e-cigarette aerosol and identify harmful chemicals.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 73, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Original poem* (60 min): Write an original sonnet or free-verse poem using an extended metaphor; revise line breaks for effect.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Substitution* (60 min): Solve 12 systems by substitution and check solutions in both equations.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Gene flow* (60 min): Model gene flow by moving beads between two cup populations each generation and record how allele frequencies converge.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Groundwater and subsidence* (50 min): Analyze USGS images of Central Valley land subsidence and explain the link to pumping.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Cook* (50 min): Follow a Spanish recipe to make guacamole or tortilla española.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Design* (50 min): Draw 3 thumbnails for a print about your community, thinking in black and white shapes.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Expand* (50 min): Expand to 64 counts, adding a formation change with a partner.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Marketing to teens* (45 min): Analyze vape and tobacco ads for techniques targeting youth (flavors, design, influencers).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 74, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Rehearse* (60 min): Practice the recitation with attention to volume, pacing, and eye contact; record and self-critique.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Systems in context* (60 min): Write and solve systems for ticket sales and two-plan comparisons.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Comparing mechanisms* (60 min): Create a chart comparing mechanisms with examples.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Policy evaluation* (50 min): Evaluate the Sustainable Groundwater Management Act (2014) by listing who benefits and who bears costs.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Video* (50 min): Record a 2-minute cooking-show video in Spanish giving at least 8 tú commands while preparing the recipe.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Transfer* (50 min): Transfer the design in reverse to a foam plate.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Rehearse* (50 min): Rehearse and film; adjust timing.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *California laws* (45 min): Learn California's Tobacco 21 law and flavored tobacco ban; research quitting resources (Kick It California).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 75, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Recite the poem for family (or record it) and submit the analysis and original poem; Unit 4 test.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on graphing and substitution.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on mechanisms of evolution.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Unit 5 test* (50 min): Take the Unit 5 test on economic geography and resources.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Poetry analysis, original poem, and recitation
- Objective: Write a poetry analysis
- Objective: Compose an original poem in a chosen form
- Objective: Recite with expression
- Vocabulary: recitation, enjambment, caesura, free verse
- Materials: Poetry Foundation / Poetry Out Loud anthology, recording device
- Assessment: Recitation scored on Poetry Out Loud criteria; analysis essay; original poem; Unit 4 test.

### Algebra I: Solving systems by graphing and substitution
- Objective: Solve systems by graphing
- Objective: Solve systems by substitution
- Vocabulary: system, solution, intersection, substitution, consistent, inconsistent
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Systems quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Mechanisms of evolution beyond selection
- Objective: Explain genetic drift, gene flow, and mutation
- Objective: Compare mechanisms
- Vocabulary: genetic drift, bottleneck, founder effect, gene flow, mutation
- Materials: M&Ms or beads, cups
- Assessment: Mechanisms quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Human impact on environments: California water case studies
- Objective: Analyze how human actions modify environments
- Objective: Evaluate environmental policy trade-offs
- Vocabulary: aqueduct, groundwater, subsidence, watershed, environmental policy
- Materials: Owens Valley maps and photos (Calisphere), Los Angeles Aqueduct timeline
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Spanish II: La cocina: tú commands and recipes
- Objective: Give instructions with tú commands
- Objective: Follow a recipe in Spanish
- Vocabulary: mandato, cortar, mezclar, hervir, freír, añadir
- Materials: ingredients for guacamole or tortilla española
- Assessment: Cooking video and commands quiz.

### Visual Art I: Relief printmaking: history and design
- Objective: Explain relief printing history
- Objective: Design a print
- Vocabulary: relief print, brayer, edition, reverse image
- Materials: foam printing plates (or soft-cut linoleum), pencil
- Assessment: Print design.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Choreography
- Objective: Create an original dance sequence
- Vocabulary: choreography, sequence, levels, pathways, canon
- Materials: music, phone
- Assessment: Choreography performance rubric.

### Health & Wellness: Tobacco, nicotine and vaping
- Objective: Describe the health effects of nicotine and vaping
- Objective: Analyze tobacco marketing to teens
- Vocabulary: nicotine, e-cigarette, secondhand smoke, Tobacco 21
- Materials: computer, ads
- Assessment: Counter-marketing post and quiz.
