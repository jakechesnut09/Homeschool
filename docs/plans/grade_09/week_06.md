## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Foreshadowing* (60 min): Read "The Monkey's Paw" and track every warning sign in a foreshadowing log; predict before each wish.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Inequality basics* (60 min): Graph 12 inequalities on number lines and test values to verify solution sets.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Earth's timeline* (60 min): Build a scaled toilet-paper timeline of Earth's 4.6 billion years marking the origin of life and oxygen.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Biomes* (50 min): Create a biome chart with climate, vegetation, and location.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Traditional games* (50 min): Learn about rayuela, trompo, lotería, and canicas through short Spanish readings.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Perspective in art* (50 min): Analyze Leonardo's "The Last Supper" and trace its orthogonals on a printout to find the vanishing point.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Sprint drills* (50 min): Do wall drills, high knees, and acceleration runs; learn the drive phase.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Energy balance* (45 min): Learn how calories in and calories out relate to growth during adolescence and calculate the energy cost of three activities.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Three-part structure* (60 min): Diagram how Jacobs divides the story into three scenes and explain why the final knock is never answered on the page.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Solving inequalities* (60 min): Solve multi-step inequalities, explaining why the sign reverses when multiplying by a negative.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Rusting model* (60 min): Soak steel wool in vinegar and observe rusting to model how oxygen created banded iron formations.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Adaptations* (50 min): Compare how people adapt to living in a desert vs. Arctic.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Lotería* (50 min): Play lotería with family, calling cards in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Boxes in space* (50 min): Draw floating boxes above, below, and on the horizon line in one-point perspective.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Starts* (50 min): Practice three-point and standing starts over 20 m.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Body image and media* (45 min): Compare edited and unedited images from a media-literacy lesson and write about how filtered images affect body image.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Bierce's timeline* (60 min): Read "An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge"; draw a timeline separating real time from imagined time in Part III.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Compound inequalities* (60 min): Solve and graph "and" and "or" inequalities such as −3 < 2x+1 ≤ 9.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Stromatolites* (60 min): Examine images of stromatolites at Shark Bay and explain their role in the oxygen revolution.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Natural hazards* (50 min): Analyze drought and wildfire effects on California communities.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Interview* (50 min): Ask a family member what games they played as a child and take notes in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Hallway* (50 min): Draw a hallway or room interior in one-point perspective.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Timed sprints* (50 min): Time 6 × 40 m sprints with full recovery.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Eating disorders* (45 min): Read a KidsHealth article on eating disorders and make a chart of types, warning signs and health effects.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Compare structure* (60 min): Write a comparison paragraph: How do Jacobs and Bierce each use structure to create surprise? Practice colons introducing lists and explanations.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Inequality word problems* (60 min): Write and solve inequalities for budgets, grades needed, and minimum sales.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Coevolution argument* (60 min): Write an argument: How did life and Earth's atmosphere change each other?
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Current events* (50 min): Relate a current news event to physical geography.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Comparison* (50 min): Compare games across cultures in a Venn diagram.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Street scene* (50 min): Draw a one-point street scene with buildings, windows, and sidewalk.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Video feedback* (50 min): Film sprints and use the video to correct arm action.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Diet culture* (45 min): Evaluate three fad diets (keto, detox teas, intermittent fasting for teens) for safety and evidence and explain why crash diets backfire.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Structure and time: "The Monkey's Paw" and "An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge"
- Objective: Analyze foreshadowing and structure
- Objective: Explain how manipulating time creates surprise
- Vocabulary: foreshadowing, flashback, stream of consciousness, frame, surprise ending
- Materials: "The Monkey's Paw" by W. W. Jacobs (public domain), "An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge" by Ambrose Bierce (public domain)
- Assessment: Comparison paragraph and structure quiz.

### Algebra I: Linear inequalities in one variable
- Objective: Solve and graph inequalities
- Objective: Write inequalities from constraints
- Vocabulary: inequality, solution set, reverse the inequality, compound inequality, interval
- Materials: number lines
- Assessment: Inequality quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Early Earth and the oxygen revolution
- Objective: Explain how life changed Earth's atmosphere
- Objective: Interpret evidence from banded iron formations and stromatolites
- Vocabulary: cyanobacteria, Great Oxidation Event, stromatolite, banded iron formation, coevolution
- Materials: steel wool, vinegar, jars
- Assessment: Argument paragraph and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Biomes and human-environment interaction
- Objective: Describe biome distribution
- Objective: Analyze how physical systems affect people
- Vocabulary: biome, tundra, rainforest, desert, savanna, adaptation
- Materials: biome maps
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Spanish II: Los juguetes y los juegos: childhood games around the Spanish-speaking world
- Objective: Describe childhood games
- Objective: Compare cultural practices
- Vocabulary: la rayuela, el trompo, la piñata, las canicas, esconderse
- Materials: videos of traditional games
- Assessment: Reading check and interview notes.

### Visual Art I: One-point perspective
- Objective: Draw with one-point perspective
- Vocabulary: horizon line, vanishing point, orthogonal, one-point perspective, eye level
- Materials: ruler, pencil, drawing paper
- Assessment: One-point drawing assessed for correct convergence.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Sprinting and starts
- Objective: Apply sprint mechanics
- Objective: Use a three-point start
- Vocabulary: acceleration, drive phase, three-point start, stride frequency
- Materials: cones, stopwatch, phone camera
- Assessment: 40 m sprint test and written biomechanics explanation.

### Health & Wellness: Energy balance, body image and eating disorders
- Objective: Explain energy balance
- Objective: Recognize warning signs of eating disorders and where to get help
- Vocabulary: energy balance, metabolism, body image, anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, binge-eating disorder
- Materials: health journal
- Assessment: Supportive letter scored for accurate information and resources; quiz.
