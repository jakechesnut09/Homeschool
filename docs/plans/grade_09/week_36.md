## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Present* (60 min): Deliver the research presentation to family or a co-op group and answer questions.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review: linear and systems* (60 min): Complete a cumulative review on Units 1–5 (equations, functions, linear models, systems).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review Units 1–2* (60 min): Redraw the food web, energy pyramid, photosynthesis, respiration, and carbon cycle models from memory and check them.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Finalize* (50 min): Finalize the action product and build 5 slides summarizing the issue, history, evidence, and your proposal.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Review* (50 min): Review with a mixed grammar drill and a vocabulary game covering all units.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Opening* (50 min): Host an exhibition opening for family and friends; give a short talk.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Favorite activity* (50 min): Spend 45 minutes on the activity you enjoyed most this year and record heart rate and RPE.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Review: units 1–4* (45 min): Complete a review grid for wellness, nutrition, mental health and substance use, then quiz yourself with 30 flashcards built from unit vocabulary.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Listen and evaluate* (60 min): Evaluate another speaker (or a TED-Ed talk) for reasoning and evidence using a rubric.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review: statistics and exponentials* (60 min): Complete a cumulative review on Units 6–7 (statistics and exponential functions).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review Units 3–4* (60 min): Practice 20 genetics and evolution problems (Punnett squares, selection data, cladograms).
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Present* (50 min): Present the project (8 minutes) to family or a co-op group and answer questions.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Listening and reading* (50 min): Answer questions on an authentic travel podcast clip and a newspaper article.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Audience feedback* (50 min): Collect feedback in a guest book and summarize responses.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Portfolio* (50 min): Assemble your portfolio: baseline and spring PFT data, graphs, goal sheets, logs, and best skill videos.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Review: units 5–7* (45 min): Answer 25 practice questions on relationships, injury prevention, disease prevention and consumer health, and correct missed items with notes.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Portfolio* (60 min): Assemble a portfolio of your best narrative, analysis, argument, and research writing.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review: polynomials and quadratics* (60 min): Complete a cumulative review on Units 8–11 (polynomials, factoring, quadratics, other functions).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review Units 5–6* (60 min): Review homeostasis feedback loops and human-impact case studies with flashcards and a concept map.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Take action* (50 min): Send the letter, submit the public comment, or share the flyer/video, and record any response.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Speaking interview* (50 min): Hold a 6-minute interview with the teacher, including a role-play at a pharmacy or hotel.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Impact reflection* (50 min): Write about how visiting a real or virtual exhibition this year changed your view of a social or cultural issue.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Reflection* (50 min): Write a 1-page reflection on your growth in skill, fitness, and sportsmanship.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Wellness plan* (45 min): Write a personal wellness plan with one SMART goal each for physical, mental/emotional and social health, including supports, obstacles and a tracking method.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Reflection letter* (60 min): Write a reflection letter describing your growth and goals for English 10.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Practice final* (60 min): Take a timed practice final and analyze errors.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Practice exam* (60 min): Take a practice exam.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Reflection essay* (50 min): Write a 600-word reflection on how your understanding of identity and community changed this semester, citing two primary sources.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Writing* (50 min): Write a 300-word letter to a future Spanish III classmate giving advice with commands and the subjunctive.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Digital portfolio* (50 min): Create a digital portfolio of the year's best work with captions.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Summer plan* (50 min): Write an 8-week summer activity plan with at least 4 active days per week and a backup for hot days.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Final exam* (45 min): Take the cumulative final exam (multiple choice, scenarios and a short decision-making essay).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Final exam* (60 min): Take the final exam: unseen literary and informational passages and a timed argument essay.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Final exam* (60 min): Take the final exam.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Final exam* (60 min): Take the final exam.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Final exam* (50 min): Take the Ethnic Studies final: key terms, primary source analysis, and a short essay on a movement's strategies.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Reflection* (50 min): Complete can-do self-assessment and write Spanish III goals.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Final reflection* (50 min): Write a final reflection on growth and goals for next year's art course.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Final activity day* (50 min): Play a family game day (pickleball, disc golf, or dance) and self-assess sportsmanship one last time.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Reflection* (45 min): Compare your week-1 wellness self-inventory to a retake today and write a one-page reflection on how your health knowledge and habits changed.
