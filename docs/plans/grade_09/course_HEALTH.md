# Health & Wellness (Grade 9)

A one-year high school health course aligned to the 2008 California Health Education Content Standards and the 2019 Health Education Framework. Students build the eight health skills (accessing information, analyzing influences, communication, decision making, goal setting, self-management and advocacy) while studying nutrition and physical activity, mental and emotional health, alcohol, tobacco and other drugs (including fentanyl and naloxone), injury and violence prevention, and personal and community health. Unit 5 (weeks 19–25) delivers comprehensive sexual health and HIV prevention education under the California Healthy Youth Act (Ed. Code 51930–51939); parents/guardians may review materials and opt the student out in writing, in which case the student completes an alternate health research project for those weeks.

*Framework:* 2008 California Health Education Content Standards (Grades 9–12); 2019 CA Health Education Framework; California Healthy Youth Act (Ed. Code 51930–51939)  
*Time:* 45 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Foundations of Wellness and Health Skills (weeks 1–3)
Health has physical, mental and social sides, and the eight health skills let you protect all three.

- Week 1: Wellness and the health triangle
- Week 2: Decision making and goal setting
- Week 3: Accessing valid health information and services

### Unit 2: Nutrition and Physical Activity (weeks 4–8)
Everyday food and movement choices build long-term health, and media messages shape those choices.

- Week 4: Nutrients and MyPlate
- Week 5: Food labels and the Dietary Guidelines
- Week 6: Energy balance, body image and eating disorders
- Week 7: Food marketing, supplements and food safety
- Week 8: Physical activity, sleep and a personal nutrition plan

### Unit 3: Mental, Emotional and Social Health (weeks 9–13)
Mental health is health; coping skills, connection and help-seeking keep it strong.

- Week 9: Mental wellness, self-esteem and resilience
- Week 10: Stress and coping
- Week 11: Mental health conditions and stigma
- Week 12: Suicide prevention and help-seeking
- Week 13: Bullying, social media and communication

### Unit 4: Alcohol, Tobacco and Other Drugs (weeks 14–18)
Substances change the developing brain; accurate facts and refusal skills protect your future.

- Week 14: The brain and addiction
- Week 15: Tobacco, nicotine and vaping
- Week 16: Alcohol
- Week 17: Cannabis, prescription drugs, fentanyl and naloxone
- Week 18: Influences, refusal skills and semester review

### Unit 5: Healthy Relationships and Sexual Health (CA Healthy Youth Act) (weeks 19–25)
Medically accurate, inclusive information and strong communication skills support healthy relationships and decisions. Parents/guardians may opt the student out of this unit (Ed. Code 51938); opted-out students complete an alternate project.

- Week 19: Healthy relationships, consent and boundaries
- Week 20: Reproductive anatomy, puberty and development
- Week 21: Gender identity, sexual orientation and respect
- Week 22: Conception, pregnancy and parenting
- Week 23: Abstinence and contraception
- Week 24: Sexually transmitted infections and HIV
- Week 25: Sexual violence, trafficking and accessing services

### Unit 6: Injury and Violence Prevention and Safety (weeks 26–30)
Most injuries are predictable and preventable, and prepared people help others in emergencies.

- Week 26: Unintentional injuries: home, water, sports and concussion
- Week 27: Violence prevention and dating violence
- Week 28: Online safety and digital wellness
- Week 29: Emergency preparedness: earthquakes, wildfires and smoke
- Week 30: Basic first aid and Hands-Only CPR

### Unit 7: Personal and Community Health (weeks 31–34)
Disease prevention, smart health-care use and healthy environments protect individuals and communities.

- Week 31: Communicable diseases and immunity
- Week 32: Chronic diseases and personal health care
- Week 33: Consumer health and the health care system
- Week 34: Environmental and community health

### Unit 8: Health Advocacy and Personal Wellness Plan (weeks 35–36)
Health-literate people advocate for themselves and their communities.

- Week 35: Health advocacy project
- Week 36: Final review and personal wellness plan

## Standards

- **H-HS.1.1.A** Explain how alcohol and other drugs affect the developing adolescent brain, including tolerance, dependence, and addiction.
- **H-HS.1.1.G** Describe human reproductive anatomy and physiology, puberty, conception, and pregnancy (CA Healthy Youth Act).
- **H-HS.1.1.M** Describe characteristics of mental and emotional wellness, including self-esteem, resilience, and emotional regulation.
- **H-HS.1.1.N** Explain the relationship between nutrition, physical activity, and health, and interpret food labels.
- **H-HS.1.1.P** Explain the interrelationships of physical, mental/emotional, and social health across the lifespan (wellness).
- **H-HS.1.2.A** Explain the short- and long-term effects of alcohol, cannabis, nicotine/vaping, and other drugs, including fentanyl.
- **H-HS.1.2.G** Describe adolescent development, gender, gender identity, gender expression, and sexual orientation in an inclusive, medically accurate way.
- **H-HS.1.2.M** Describe the signs, symptoms, and treatment of common mental health conditions, including depression and anxiety.
- **H-HS.1.2.N** Describe the functions and sources of macronutrients, micronutrients, and water, and the Dietary Guidelines/MyPlate recommendations.
- **H-HS.1.2.P** Describe how communicable diseases are transmitted and prevented, including hand hygiene and immunization.
- **H-HS.1.2.S** Identify ways to reduce the risk of unintentional injuries at home, in water, during recreation and sports, including concussion.
- **H-HS.1.3.A** Describe the dangers of counterfeit pills and fentanyl, signs of overdose, naloxone use, and California's 911 Good Samaritan law.
- **H-HS.1.3.G** Describe characteristics of healthy and unhealthy relationships, consent, and how to prevent dating violence.
- **H-HS.1.3.M** Explain the effects of stress on the body and mind and identify common stressors.
- **H-HS.1.3.N** Explain energy balance and healthy weight management, and the risks of eating disorders and unsafe weight-control practices.
- **H-HS.1.3.P** Explain risk factors for and prevention of chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes, and cancer.
- **H-HS.1.3.S** Describe causes and prevention of violence, including dating violence, gang violence, and weapons.
- **H-HS.1.4.A** Explain the legal, social, and economic consequences of substance use, including California laws for minors.
- **H-HS.1.4.G** Explain abstinence and all FDA-approved methods of preventing pregnancy and sexually transmitted infections, including effectiveness.
- **H-HS.1.4.M** Describe bullying, cyberbullying, and harassment, their effects, and the role of bystanders.
- **H-HS.1.4.N** Describe safe food handling and the prevention of foodborne illness.
- **H-HS.1.4.P** Describe personal health practices including sleep, dental, vision, hearing, and sun protection.
- **H-HS.1.5.G** Describe sexually transmitted infections including HIV: transmission, prevention (including PrEP/PEP), testing, and treatment.
- **H-HS.1.5.M** Identify warning signs of suicide and appropriate ways to seek help for self and others.
- **H-HS.1.5.N** Explain the benefits of regular physical activity and the components of health-related fitness.
- **H-HS.1.5.S** Describe safe online behavior, including privacy, digital footprint, sextortion, and reporting.
- **H-HS.1.6.G** Explain how to access reproductive and sexual health services and accurate information (CA Healthy Youth Act).
- **H-HS.1.6.P** Explain how environmental factors such as air quality and water affect personal and community health.
- **H-HS.1.6.S** Describe emergency preparedness for earthquakes, wildfires, smoke, and other disasters.
- **H-HS.1.7.G** Explain pregnancy outcomes and options, the importance of prenatal care, and California's Safely Surrendered Baby Law.
- **H-HS.1.8.G** Describe sexual harassment, sexual assault, and human trafficking, including prevention strategies and how to seek help.
- **H-HS.1.9.S** Describe the procedures for basic first aid, CPR, choking, and use of an AED.
- **H-HS.2.1.A** Analyze influences such as media, peers, and advertising on substance use.
- **H-HS.2.1.G** Analyze how media, peers, and culture influence relationships, gender norms, and sexual health decisions.
- **H-HS.2.1.M** Analyze how social media, family, peers, and culture influence emotional health and self-image.
- **H-HS.2.1.N** Analyze how media, culture, and marketing influence food choices and body image.
- **H-HS.2.1.P** Evaluate health products, services, and advertising claims as an informed consumer.
- **H-HS.3.1.M** Identify trusted adults and community resources (including 988) for mental health support.
- **H-HS.3.1.N** Evaluate the validity of nutrition information and dietary supplement claims.
- **H-HS.3.1.P** Access valid health information, products, and services, including health care providers and coverage options.
- **H-HS.3.2.A** Locate valid information and services for substance use prevention and treatment, including naloxone access.
- **H-HS.4.1.G** Demonstrate communication skills for setting boundaries, asking for and giving consent, and refusing unwanted pressure.
- **H-HS.4.1.M** Use effective communication and conflict-resolution skills.
- **H-HS.4.2.A** Demonstrate refusal and negotiation skills to avoid substance use.
- **H-HS.5.1.A** Use a decision-making process to avoid or leave situations involving alcohol, tobacco, and other drugs.
- **H-HS.5.1.G** Use a decision-making process to evaluate choices that affect sexual health.
- **H-HS.5.1.M** Use a decision-making process to address emotional and social challenges.
- **H-HS.5.1.P** Use a decision-making process to make health-enhancing choices about personal and community health.
- **H-HS.5.1.S** Use a decision-making process to evaluate risky situations.
- **H-HS.6.1.N** Develop a personal plan for healthy eating and physical activity.
- **H-HS.6.1.P** Set a personal wellness goal, develop an action plan, and monitor progress.
- **H-HS.7.1.M** Practice stress management and coping strategies.
- **H-HS.7.1.N** Plan and prepare healthy, affordable meals and snacks.
- **H-HS.7.1.P** Practice behaviors that prevent disease, such as self-exams, sleep routines, and hygiene.
- **H-HS.7.1.S** Practice injury-prevention behaviors and develop a household emergency plan.
- **H-HS.8.1.A** Advocate for tobacco-, alcohol-, and drug-free environments.
- **H-HS.8.1.M** Advocate for mental health awareness and the reduction of stigma.
- **H-HS.8.1.P** Advocate for a personal, family, or community health issue using accurate information.
- **H-HS.8.1.S** Encourage others to adopt safe behaviors.
