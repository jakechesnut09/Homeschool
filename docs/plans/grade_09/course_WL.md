# Spanish II (Grade 9)

Spanish II moves students from Novice High toward Intermediate Low proficiency. Students review the present tense and reflexive verbs, then narrate in the preterite and imperfect, give commands, talk about travel with the future, discuss health with the present perfect, and make recommendations with the present subjunctive. Cultural study includes Día de los Muertos, regional cuisines, Latin American geography, environmental issues, and the art and music of the Spanish-speaking world, assessed through integrated performance assessments.

*Framework:* 2019 California World Languages Standards; 2020 CA World Languages Framework (Novice High to Intermediate Low)  
*Time:* 50 min/day
*UC/CSU a–g:* e · *Credits:* 10.0

## Units

### Unit 1: ¡Bienvenidos de nuevo! Daily Routines and Review (weeks 1–4)
Describing who we are and what we do every day reactivates the present tense and adds reflexive verbs.

- Week 1: ¿Quién soy yo? Reintroductions and present tense review
- Week 2: Mi rutina diaria: reflexive verbs
- Week 3: La escuela y las actividades: stem-changing verbs and time expressions
- Week 4: Unit 1 integrated performance assessment

### Unit 2: Mi niñez: Childhood Memories (weeks 5–8)
The imperfect tense lets us describe how things used to be.

- Week 5: Cuando era niño/a: the imperfect tense
- Week 6: Los juguetes y los juegos: childhood games around the Spanish-speaking world
- Week 7: Describing people and places in the past
- Week 8: Unit 2 project: Mi niñez

### Unit 3: Celebraciones: Telling What Happened (weeks 9–12)
The preterite narrates completed events, like the celebrations that mark the year in Spanish-speaking cultures.

- Week 9: Las celebraciones: preterite of regular verbs
- Week 10: Día de los Muertos and fiestas: irregular preterite
- Week 11: Preterite vs. imperfect
- Week 12: Unit 3 assessment: telling a story

### Unit 4: ¡A comer! Food, Cooking, and Restaurants (weeks 13–18)
Food connects culture and community; we combine past tenses and give instructions with commands and pronouns.

- Week 13: La comida: food vocabulary and gustar-type verbs
- Week 14: En el restaurante: object pronouns
- Week 15: La cocina: tú commands and recipes
- Week 16: Regional cuisines of the Spanish-speaking world
- Week 17: Semester review: tenses, pronouns, and commands
- Week 18: Semester exam (integrated performance assessment)

### Unit 5: De viaje: Travel and Geography (weeks 19–22)
Travel requires planning in the future, asking for directions, and understanding the Spanish-speaking world's geography.

- Week 19: ¿Adónde vamos? Travel vocabulary and the future with ir a
- Week 20: Geography of the Spanish-speaking world
- Week 21: En la ciudad: directions and por vs. para
- Week 22: Unit 5 project: travel itinerary

### Unit 6: La salud y el cuerpo: Health and Wellness (weeks 23–26)
Talking about health requires body vocabulary, formal commands, and what we have done.

- Week 23: El cuerpo y la salud
- Week 24: En el consultorio: formal (usted) commands
- Week 25: ¿Qué has hecho? The present perfect
- Week 26: Health and wellness across cultures

### Unit 7: Nuestra comunidad y el medio ambiente (weeks 27–30)
Communities solve problems together; we express recommendations and wishes with the subjunctive.

- Week 27: Mi comunidad: places, jobs, and volunteering
- Week 28: El medio ambiente: problems and solutions
- Week 29: Introduction to the present subjunctive: recommendations and wishes
- Week 30: Unit 7 project: environmental awareness campaign

### Unit 8: El arte y la música del mundo hispano (weeks 31–34)
Art and music express identity; comparing works builds description and opinion skills.

- Week 31: Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera: describing art
- Week 32: Comparing art: comparatives and superlatives
- Week 33: Music of the Spanish-speaking world
- Week 34: Unit 8 project: virtual museum exhibit

### Unit 9: Proyecto final y repaso (weeks 35–36)
We show what we can do in Spanish across all three modes.

- Week 35: Final project: "Mi vida en español" video
- Week 36: Final exam and reflection

## Standards

- **WL-COMM1-IL** Interpretive communication (Intermediate Low): understand the main idea and some details in simple authentic texts on familiar and some new topics.
- **WL-COMM1-NH** Interpretive communication (Novice High): understand and infer meaning from simple authentic oral, written, and signed texts on familiar topics.
- **WL-COMM2-IL** Interpersonal communication (Intermediate Low): create with language to handle short social interactions and everyday transactions, asking and answering a variety of questions.
- **WL-COMM2-NH** Interpersonal communication (Novice High): exchange information in conversations on familiar topics using phrases and simple sentences, asking and answering questions.
- **WL-COMM3-IL** Presentational communication (Intermediate Low): present information on familiar topics using connected sentences in speaking and writing.
- **WL-COMM3-NH** Presentational communication (Novice High): present information on familiar topics using simple sentences in speaking and writing.
- **WL-CONN1-IL** Connections (Intermediate Low): use Spanish to build and reinforce knowledge in other disciplines (geography, science, art, health).
- **WL-CULT1-IL** Cultures (Intermediate Low): compare products, practices, and perspectives of Spanish-speaking cultures with one's own.
- **WL-CULT1-NH** Cultures (Novice High): identify and describe products and practices of Spanish-speaking cultures and relate them to perspectives.
- **WL-CULT2-IL** Intercultural interaction (Intermediate Low): use culturally appropriate behavior and language in familiar situations.
- **WL-STRUC1-IL** Structures (Intermediate Low): use language structures (preterite/imperfect, commands, future, present perfect, subjunctive expressions) to communicate at the Intermediate Low level.
- **WL-STRUC1-NH** Structures (Novice High): use language structures (present tense, reflexives, past tenses) to communicate in the modes at the Novice High level.
