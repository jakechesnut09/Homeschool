## Day 1 — Monday (school day 161, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Choosing a question* (60 min): Brainstorm topics connected to a text from this year (e.g., Greek hospitality, juvenile justice); narrow to one researchable question.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Choosing methods* (60 min): Solve 16 mixed equations, choosing factoring, square roots, completing the square, or the formula.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Choosing a problem* (60 min): Choose a local biodiversity problem (e.g., native pollinator decline).
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Filipino Americans* (50 min): Read about manongs and Stockton's Little Manila.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Genre tour* (50 min): Listen to salsa, cumbia, flamenco, mariachi, and son jarocho and note instruments and rhythm in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Piece 1 start* (50 min): Begin piece 1, documenting process with photos.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Review data* (50 min): Review your fall and spring fitness data and identify two priorities.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Advertising claims* (45 min): Evaluate three health product ads (supplement, acne cream, fitness gadget) for claims, evidence and red flags of quackery.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 162, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Lateral reading* (60 min): Learn to check sources by reading laterally; evaluate five websites with a credibility checklist.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Projectile problems* (60 min): Solve when a launched object hits the ground or reaches a height.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Criteria and constraints* (60 min): Define qualitative and quantitative criteria and constraints.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Southeast Asian refugees* (50 min): Read accounts of Vietnamese, Cambodian, and Hmong refugees.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Lyrics* (50 min): Analyze the lyrics of "La Bamba" (son jarocho) or "Latinoamérica" (Calle 13): identify the theme and figurative language.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Piece 1 finish* (50 min): Finish piece 1 and write process notes.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Plan design* (50 min): Write a 4-week plan with FITT for cardio, strength, and flexibility.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Health insurance* (45 min): Learn insurance terms and how Medi-Cal and Covered California work.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 163, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Finding sources* (60 min): Locate six sources (two print or database) and write annotated bibliography entries for three.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Linear, quadratic, exponential* (60 min): Compare growth of 10x, x², and 2^x in a table and explain which eventually wins.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Research* (60 min): Research existing solutions and their effectiveness.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Pacific Islanders* (50 min): Research Native Hawaiian sovereignty or Samoan communities.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Origins* (50 min): Research one genre's African, Indigenous, and European roots and write a paragraph.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Piece 2 start* (50 min): Begin piece 2 with a variation on the theme.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Barriers* (50 min): Identify barriers (time, weather, cost) and strategies to overcome them.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Medications* (45 min): Compare brand-name vs. generic drugs and read an OTC drug label.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 164, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Summarizing sources* (60 min): Write objective summaries of the central idea of two key sources.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Rejecting solutions* (60 min): Solve applications where one solution isn't viable (negative time or length).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Breaking down* (60 min): Break the biodiversity problem into 3–4 smaller sub-problems (habitat, food source, public awareness) and rank them.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Model minority myth* (50 min): Analyze income and poverty data for different Asian American groups and explain why averages hide differences (the model minority myth).
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Playlist* (50 min): Create a 6-song playlist with a Spanish description of each song.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Piece 2 finish* (50 min): Finish piece 2.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Start plan* (50 min): Complete day 1 of your plan and log it.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Being a patient* (45 min): Prepare a list of questions and a medication/allergy list for a doctor visit, then role-play checking in and describing symptoms.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 165, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Submit research question and annotated bibliography for approval.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on applications.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review & check* (60 min): Submit a problem statement with criteria and constraints for feedback and revise it.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Unit 11 assessment* (50 min): Complete a unit assessment: key terms and a document-based paragraph using EO 9066 and the Civil Liberties Act of 1988.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Review & check* (50 min): Listening quiz identifying genres and lyric comprehension.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Review & check* (50 min): Mid-project critique using your criteria.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Submit the plan for feedback.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Check* (45 min): Take a consumer health quiz on insurance terms, OTC labels and evaluating claims, including calculating a cost with a deductible and copay.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Research questions and credible sources
- Objective: Write a focused research question
- Objective: Evaluate source credibility
- Vocabulary: research question, primary source, secondary source, credibility, lateral reading
- Materials: library databases, Purdue OWL research guide
- Assessment: Annotated bibliography (6 sources) and approved research question.

### Algebra I: Choosing methods and quadratic applications
- Objective: Choose efficient solving methods
- Objective: Solve applied quadratic problems
- Vocabulary: efficient method, viable solution
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Applications quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Engineering solutions for biodiversity: defining the problem
- Objective: Define criteria and constraints
- Objective: Research a local biodiversity problem
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraints, trade-off, mitigation
- Materials: local environmental reports
- Assessment: Problem statement and criteria list.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Filipino, Southeast Asian, and Pacific Islander communities
- Objective: Analyze diverse AAPI experiences
- Objective: Connect to contemporary issues
- Vocabulary: manong, refugee, Pacific Islander, model minority myth
- Materials: oral histories, Hmong and Vietnamese refugee accounts
- Assessment: Unit 11 assessment.

### Spanish II: Music of the Spanish-speaking world
- Objective: Interpret song lyrics
- Objective: Compare origins of musical genres
- Vocabulary: canción, letra, ritmo, salsa, cumbia, flamenco, mariachi, son jarocho
- Materials: songs on a streaming service or YouTube
- Assessment: Listening quiz and annotated playlist.

### Visual Art I: Body of work: piece 1 and 2
- Objective: Develop artworks around a theme
- Vocabulary: series, consistency, variation, process documentation
- Materials: chosen media
- Assessment: Two finished pieces with process documentation.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Designing a personal fitness plan
- Objective: Use assessment data to design a plan
- Objective: Apply FITT to all components
- Vocabulary: personal fitness plan, FITT, periodization, barriers
- Materials: fitness portfolio, calendar
- Assessment: Personal fitness plan.

### Health & Wellness: Consumer health and the health care system
- Objective: Evaluate health products and services
- Objective: Understand health insurance basics
- Vocabulary: premium, deductible, copay, generic drug, quackery
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: Consumer health quiz.
