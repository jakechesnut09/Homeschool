## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Rhetorical triangle* (60 min): Learn speaker–audience–purpose and the three appeals; identify appeals in 10 modern advertisements.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Graph stories* (60 min): Match 8 graphs to stories (e.g., filling a bathtub) and sketch graphs for 4 new stories.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Macromolecules* (60 min): Build a chart of the four macromolecules, their monomers, and functions.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Site and situation* (50 min): Analyze why cities like San Francisco and Sacramento grew where they did.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Preterite forms* (50 min): Learn regular preterite endings and conjugate 20 verbs.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Color wheel* (50 min): Mix a 12-step color wheel from primaries.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Grips and footwork* (50 min): Learn forehand and backhand grips and ready position; rally 100 hits with a partner.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Mental wellness* (45 min): Brainstorm signs of good mental health and compare to a list from a mental health organization.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Patrick Henry* (60 min): Read "Give me liberty or give me death"; annotate rhetorical questions and emotional appeals and explain the speech's purpose.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Key features* (60 min): Identify intercepts, intervals of increase/decrease, and maxima/minima on 10 graphs.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Food testing* (60 min): Test foods for starch with iodine and lipids with the brown-paper test; record results.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Settlement patterns* (50 min): Compare clustered and dispersed rural settlement.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Time expressions* (50 min): Write 10 sentences about what you did yesterday or last weekend.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Tints and shades* (50 min): Create tint and shade scales for one color.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Serves* (50 min): Practice short and long serves into targets.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Emotions* (45 min): Use an emotion wheel to name emotions felt this week and identify body cues for each.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Kennedy's Inaugural* (60 min): Watch and read JFK's 1961 Inaugural; find examples of antithesis and parallelism ("Ask not...").
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Context domain* (60 min): Determine reasonable domains for functions modeling a phone battery, a ball's height, and a savings plan.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *From sugar to protein* (60 min): Explain how carbon skeletons from glucose combine with nitrogen to form amino acids.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Urban models* (50 min): Sketch the concentric zone and sector models, then test each against a map of Sacramento or Los Angeles and decide which fits better.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Spelling changes* (50 min): Practice -car/-gar/-zar yo forms (busqué, llegué, empecé).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Color schemes* (50 min): Paint four small squares in complementary, analogous, triadic, and monochromatic schemes.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Clears* (50 min): Practice overhead forehand clears, focusing on follow-through.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Self-esteem* (45 min): Write a list of 10 personal strengths and ask a family member to add three more.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 44, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *SOAPSTone analysis* (60 min): Complete a SOAPSTone chart for one speech and write a rhetorical analysis paragraph.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Comparing functions* (60 min): Compare two functions given as a table and a graph (e.g., two runners) and decide which grows faster.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Modeling polymers* (60 min): Build polymer chains with paper clips to model dehydration synthesis and hydrolysis.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Local study* (50 min): Use Google Maps satellite view to map your town's downtown, residential areas, and commercial strips, and explain the pattern in a paragraph.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Birthday story* (50 min): Write a 120-word paragraph about your last birthday celebration: who came, what you ate, and what you did.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Color and mood* (50 min): Analyze how Picasso's Blue Period paintings use color for mood.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Drop shots* (50 min): Practice drop shots and net play.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Resilience stories* (45 min): Read a short biography of someone who overcame adversity and identify the protective factors that helped them.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Rhetorical appeals in famous speeches
- Objective: Identify ethos, pathos, logos
- Objective: Analyze how rhetoric advances purpose
- Vocabulary: ethos, pathos, logos, rhetorical question, anaphora, parallelism
- Materials: Patrick Henry, "Speech to the Virginia Convention" (1775), John F. Kennedy, Inaugural Address (1961) text and video
- Assessment: Rhetorical analysis paragraph and oral explanation; term quiz.

### Algebra I: Interpreting graphs and key features
- Objective: Identify key features of graphs in context
- Objective: Relate domain to context
- Vocabulary: intercept, increasing, decreasing, maximum, minimum, interval
- Materials: graph stories cards
- Assessment: Graph interpretation paragraph and quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Building biomolecules from sugar
- Objective: Explain how sugar's atoms build macromolecules
- Objective: Test foods for biomolecules
- Vocabulary: carbohydrate, lipid, protein, nucleic acid, monomer, polymer
- Materials: iodine (tincture), brown paper bag, foods to test
- Assessment: Food test data table and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Settlement patterns
- Objective: Explain settlement site and situation
- Objective: Compare rural and urban settlement
- Vocabulary: site, situation, urban, rural, suburb, settlement
- Materials: topographic maps
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Spanish II: Las celebraciones: preterite of regular verbs
- Objective: Form preterite of regular verbs
- Objective: Narrate a past celebration
- Vocabulary: pretérito, celebrar, cumpleaños, ayer, anoche, el año pasado
- Materials: calendar
- Assessment: Preterite quiz.

### Visual Art I: Color theory
- Objective: Mix and organize colors
- Objective: Explain color relationships
- Vocabulary: primary, secondary, tertiary, complementary, analogous, hue, tint, shade
- Materials: tempera or acrylic primaries plus white and black, palette, brushes
- Assessment: Color wheel and quiz.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Badminton fundamentals
- Objective: Use correct grip and basic strokes
- Vocabulary: forehand grip, backhand grip, clear, drop shot, serve, shuttlecock
- Materials: badminton rackets, shuttlecocks, net or string
- Assessment: Badminton skills test.

### Health & Wellness: Mental wellness, self-esteem and resilience
- Objective: Describe characteristics of mental wellness
- Objective: Practice strategies that build resilience
- Vocabulary: self-esteem, resilience, emotional intelligence, growth mindset
- Materials: health journal
- Assessment: Resilience reflection scored with a rubric.
