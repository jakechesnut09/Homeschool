## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Course launch & reading survey* (60 min): Set up a reader's notebook with sections for vocabulary, quotes, and quick-writes; write a one-page reading autobiography describing three books that shaped you.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Number system* (60 min): Sort 30 numbers (√2, −3/4, π, 0.333..., √49) into a nested real-number diagram and justify each placement.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Lab safety & notebooks* (60 min): Set up a lab notebook (claim–evidence–reasoning format) and review home lab safety rules with a parent.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Five themes* (50 min): Learn the five themes of geography and apply each to your own neighborhood in a one-page chart.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Presentaciones* (50 min): Write and record a 1-minute self-introduction (name, age, personality, likes) using ser, tener, and gustar.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Sketchbook launch* (50 min): Decorate a sketchbook cover with your name in three lettering styles and draw your non-dominant hand for 10 minutes.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Fitness components* (50 min): Learn the five health-related fitness components, then do a 20-minute circuit with one station for each.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *What is health?* (45 min): Read the WHO definition of health, then draw and label your own health triangle with three current strengths and one weakness on each side.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Plot and conflict* (60 min): Learn the plot diagram and four conflict types; read the first half of "The Most Dangerous Game" and annotate every moment that raises suspense.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Properties* (60 min): Use commutative, associative, and distributive properties to compute 12 mental-math problems and name the property used.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Ecosystem levels* (60 min): Read about organization from organism to biosphere and build a concept map with local examples.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Absolute location* (50 min): Locate 15 world cities by latitude and longitude on a blank map.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Present tense review* (50 min): Conjugate 30 regular -ar/-er/-ir verbs on Conjuguemos and write 10 sentences about your week.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Blind contour* (50 min): Do five 3-minute blind contour drawings of a shoe, a plant, and your face in a mirror without looking at the paper.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Dynamic warm-up* (50 min): Learn and perform a 10-minute dynamic warm-up (high knees, butt kicks, leg swings, inchworms) and a 15-minute brisk walk/jog.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Wellness continuum* (45 min): Place 10 scenario cards on a wellness continuum from premature death to high-level wellness and explain each placement in writing.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Close reading Rainsford* (60 min): Finish the story; record three quotations that show Rainsford's attitude toward hunting changing and explain each in two sentences.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Rational vs. irrational* (60 min): Explore sums and products like 3+√2 and √2·√8; write an explanation of when results are rational.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Quadrat survey* (60 min): Mark a 1 m² quadrat in a yard or park with string; record every organism and abiotic factor you observe.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Relative location* (50 min): Describe the relative location of your home and three world capitals.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Irregular verbs* (50 min): Review yo-go verbs (tengo, hago, salgo, pongo) and stem-changers; complete a timed drill.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Modified contour* (50 min): Draw a kitchen utensil grouping with modified contour, glancing at the paper only at corners.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Activity log* (50 min): Set up a weekly activity log and complete a 30-minute bike ride, hike, or jog at moderate intensity.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Risk and protective factors* (45 min): Sort a list of 20 behaviors and conditions into risk factors and protective factors, then identify which you can control.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Suspense techniques* (60 min): Chart how Connell uses foreshadowing, setting (Ship-Trap Island), and pacing; write a paragraph explaining which technique creates the most tension.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Order and estimate* (60 min): Estimate square roots between integers and order 15 real numbers on a number line.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Data sharing* (60 min): Graph quadrat species counts and compare to a second quadrat in a different spot (sun vs. shade).
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Mental maps* (50 min): Draw a world map from memory, then compare to an atlas and list three surprises.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Entrevista* (50 min): Interview a family member (or Spanish-speaking pen pal) in Spanish with 10 questions and record answers.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Cross-contour* (50 min): Draw a banana and a crumpled paper bag using cross-contour lines to show form.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Static stretching* (50 min): Learn when to use static stretching; complete a 20-minute circuit and a cool-down stretch routine.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Self-assessment* (45 min): Complete a 30-question wellness self-inventory and graph your scores by area in your journal.

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a plot diagram for the story and take a 10-question reading quiz; discuss aloud whether Rainsford is a hero.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Plot, conflict, and suspense in "The Most Dangerous Game"
- Objective: Map plot structure of a short story
- Objective: Identify types of conflict
- Objective: Set up a reader's notebook
- Vocabulary: exposition, rising action, climax, resolution, external conflict, internal conflict
- Materials: "The Most Dangerous Game" by Richard Connell (public domain), reader's notebook, highlighters
- Assessment: Plot diagram accuracy and reading quiz (80%+); paragraph scored for evidence and explanation.

### Algebra I: Real numbers and properties
- Objective: Classify numbers in the real number system
- Objective: Explain why rational + irrational is irrational
- Vocabulary: rational, irrational, integer, closure, real number
- Materials: number line, calculator, Khan Academy account
- Assessment: Quiz plus written explanation of why rational + irrational is irrational.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Science practices and ecosystem components
- Objective: Use lab safety and science notebooks
- Objective: Identify biotic and abiotic factors
- Vocabulary: ecosystem, biotic, abiotic, population, community, biome
- Materials: science notebook, meter stick, string, hand lens
- Assessment: CER paragraph and vocabulary quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Thinking like a geographer: the five themes
- Objective: Apply the five themes of geography
- Objective: Use latitude and longitude
- Vocabulary: location, place, region, movement, human-environment interaction, latitude, longitude
- Materials: world atlas, blank world map
- Assessment: Coordinates quiz and five-themes chart.

### Spanish II: ¿Quién soy yo? Reintroductions and present tense review
- Objective: Introduce yourself in detail
- Objective: Review regular and irregular present-tense verbs
- Vocabulary: presentarse, personalidad, gustar, ser, estar, tener
- Materials: Spanish notebook, Conjuguemos account
- Assessment: Present-tense quiz (80%+) and speaking recording rubric.

### Visual Art I: Sketchbook habits and contour line
- Objective: Establish a daily sketchbook practice
- Objective: Draw with contour and blind contour line
- Vocabulary: contour line, blind contour, cross-contour, sketchbook, observation
- Materials: sketchbook (9x12), graphite pencils 2H–6B, kneaded eraser
- Assessment: Sketchbook check for 10+ contour drawings and written reflection.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Components of fitness and safe warm-ups
- Objective: Identify the five components of health-related fitness
- Objective: Lead a dynamic warm-up
- Vocabulary: cardiorespiratory endurance, muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility, body composition, dynamic warm-up
- Materials: athletic shoes, water bottle, activity log
- Assessment: Warm-up demonstration and fitness components quiz.

### Health & Wellness: Wellness and the health triangle
- Objective: Define health and wellness
- Objective: Assess personal wellness across physical, mental and social health
- Vocabulary: wellness, health triangle, risk factor, protective factor
- Materials: health journal, colored pencils
- Assessment: Vocabulary quiz and wellness reflection scored on a 4-point rubric.
