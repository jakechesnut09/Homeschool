## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Friar's plan* (60 min): Read 4.1; list every step of the plan and what could go wrong.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Polynomial vocabulary* (60 min): Classify 15 polynomials by degree and number of terms; write in standard form.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Cell theory* (60 min): Read about cell theory and compare prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Douglass* (50 min): Read an excerpt of Douglass's Narrative on learning to read and analyze it as resistance.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Present perfect* (50 min): Learn haber + participle and irregular participles (hecho, dicho, escrito).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Figure proportions* (50 min): Learn the 7.5-heads rule and draw a proportion chart.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Muscle groups* (50 min): Label major muscle groups and pair each with one bodyweight exercise; complete a 20-minute circuit.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Harassment and assault* (45 min): Define sexual harassment and assault and learn your rights under Title IX.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Juliet's fears* (60 min): Read 4.3 soliloquy; annotate her fears and analyze her courage.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Add and subtract* (60 min): Add and subtract polynomials with algebra tiles, then symbolically.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Microscope lab* (60 min): Prepare an onion skin slide stained with iodine and sketch observations.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Forms of resistance* (50 min): Categorize forms of resistance (daily, escape, revolt).
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Habits* (50 min): Write about healthy habits you have practiced this week.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Gesture* (50 min): Do 20 one-minute gestures of a family member posing.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Squat and hinge* (50 min): Learn bodyweight squat, lunge, and hip hinge with mirror feedback; 3 sets of 10 each.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Human trafficking* (45 min): Learn how traffickers recruit teens online and in person, and warning signs.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Irony on the wedding morning* (60 min): Read 4.4–4.5; chart what the audience knows vs. what characters know.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Multiply* (60 min): Multiply monomials by polynomials and binomials by binomials with area models.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Organelle functions* (60 min): Create an organelle analogy chart comparing a cell to a city (nucleus = city hall) and justify each match.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *California and slavery* (50 min): Study Biddy Mason's 1856 freedom suit in Los Angeles.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Interview* (50 min): Interview a family member: ¿Has corrido un maratón?
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Contrapposto* (50 min): Study the Greek "Doryphoros" and draw a contrapposto pose.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Push and pull* (50 min): Learn push-ups (with modifications) and band rows; 3 sets of 10–12.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Getting help* (45 min): Identify hotlines (RAINN, National Human Trafficking Hotline) and trusted adults.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Vocabulary in context* (60 min): Use context and footnotes to define 15 archaic words from Acts 3–4.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Special products* (60 min): Find patterns in (a+b)², (a−b)², and (a+b)(a−b).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Specialized cells* (60 min): Compare nerve, muscle, and blood cells and connect structure to function.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Archy Lee case* (50 min): Analyze the 1858 Archy Lee case and the California Fugitive Slave Law.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Bucket list* (50 min): Write a 10-item bucket list of things you have and haven't done yet (Nunca he visto..., Ya he aprendido...).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Long pose* (50 min): Draw a 30-minute seated figure from life.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Strength vs. endurance* (50 min): Compare low-rep heavy and high-rep light sets and record how each feels.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Minor consent* (45 min): Learn which health services California minors can access confidentially.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 125, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Act 4 quiz and prediction letter written from Friar John's perspective.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on polynomial operations; explain why polynomials are closed under multiplication.

- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a source analysis on Biddy Mason or Archy Lee.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Review & check* (50 min): Present perfect quiz.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Review & check* (50 min): Critique proportion with a partner.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Romeo and Juliet, Act 4: plans and dramatic irony
- Objective: Track dramatic irony
- Objective: Analyze Juliet's soliloquy
- Vocabulary: dramatic irony, soliloquy, potion, foreshadowing
- Materials: Romeo and Juliet, Act 4
- Assessment: Act 4 quiz and creative letter.

### Algebra I: Adding, subtracting, and multiplying polynomials
- Objective: Classify polynomials
- Objective: Add, subtract, and multiply polynomials
- Vocabulary: polynomial, monomial, binomial, trinomial, degree, standard form
- Materials: algebra tiles, area model templates
- Assessment: Polynomial operations quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Cell structure and function
- Objective: Compare prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells
- Objective: Relate organelles to function
- Vocabulary: prokaryote, eukaryote, organelle, cell membrane, nucleus, mitochondria
- Materials: onion, iodine, microscope or phone microscope clip
- Assessment: Microscope sketch and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): African resistance to slavery and the meaning of freedom
- Objective: Analyze primary sources on slavery and resistance
- Objective: Connect California's history to slavery
- Vocabulary: enslavement, resistance, abolition, fugitive slave law
- Materials: Frederick Douglass narrative excerpt, Biddy Mason biography
- Assessment: Source analysis.

### Spanish II: ¿Qué has hecho? The present perfect
- Objective: Form the present perfect
- Objective: Talk about healthy habits
- Vocabulary: haber, participio, he comido, he hecho, ya, todavía no
- Materials: habit tracker
- Assessment: Present perfect quiz.

### Visual Art I: The figure: gesture and proportion
- Objective: Draw the human figure in proportion
- Objective: Capture movement
- Vocabulary: figure, head-to-body ratio, contrapposto, gesture, foreshortening
- Materials: wooden manikin (optional), pencils
- Assessment: Figure drawing.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Strength training safety and technique
- Objective: Use safe lifting technique
- Objective: Explain muscular strength vs. endurance
- Vocabulary: repetition, set, resistance, progressive overload, spotter, major muscle groups
- Materials: resistance bands, dumbbells or water jugs, mat
- Assessment: Technique check and quiz.

### Health & Wellness: Sexual violence, trafficking and accessing services
- Objective: Recognize sexual harassment, assault and trafficking
- Objective: Identify how to get help
- Vocabulary: sexual harassment, sexual assault, human trafficking, grooming, mandated reporter
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: Unit 5 test or alternate project.
