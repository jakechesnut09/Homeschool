## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Gifts in the tree* (60 min): Read Chapters 7–8; analyze the knot-hole gifts and the fire as signs of Boo's character.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Factored form* (60 min): Find zeros from factored form and sketch the graph.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Differentiation* (60 min): Explain how cells with the same DNA become different through gene expression.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Bracero Program* (50 min): Read about the Bracero Program (1942–1964) and analyze a bracero contract and photographs for working conditions.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Forms* (50 min): Learn the "opposite vowel" rule from the yo form and conjugate 20 verbs, including irregulars (sea, vaya, tenga).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Documentary photography* (50 min): Study Gordon Parks's photo essays and how sequence creates narrative.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Basics* (50 min): Size the rope and practice basic bounce and alternate step for 10 × 1 minute.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Earthquakes* (45 min): Practice Drop, Cover and Hold On and identify safe spots at home.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 142, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Atticus takes the case* (60 min): Read Chapter 9; explain Atticus's reasons for defending Tom Robinson.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Vertex form* (60 min): Graph from vertex form and convert to standard form.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Plant cuttings* (60 min): Root a pothos cutting in water to observe growth from undifferentiated cells.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Delano grape strike* (50 min): Learn how Filipino workers (Larry Itliong) began the 1965 strike and joined with Cesar Chavez and Dolores Huerta.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Recommendations* (50 min): Write 10 recommendations for your family about saving water (Recomiendo que tomemos duchas cortas).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Plan* (50 min): Choose a subject in your community (a market, a family recipe, a local business) and write a shot list.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Tricks* (50 min): Learn criss-cross, side swing, and boxer step.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Wildfire and smoke* (45 min): Learn evacuation steps and how to read the Air Quality Index.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 143, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *The mad dog* (60 min): Read Chapter 10; explain the mockingbird quote and what Atticus's shooting reveals.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Completing the square to vertex form* (60 min): Rewrite x²+6x+5 in vertex form by completing the square.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Stem cells* (60 min): Read about stem cell types and their medical uses.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Plan de Delano* (50 min): Read the 1966 Plan de Delano and identify its demands, religious imagery, and intended audiences.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Impersonal expressions* (50 min): Complete 15 sentences using es importante/necesario/mejor que + subjunctive.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Shoot* (50 min): Shoot 50+ photos following the shot list and permission guidelines.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Intervals* (50 min): Complete a 20-minute jump rope interval workout and track heart rate.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Go-bag* (45 min): Using the Ready.gov list, write a go-bag checklist for your household and gather water, flashlight, medications, N95 masks and copies of documents.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 144, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Mrs. Dubose* (60 min): Read Chapter 11; write about Atticus's definition of real courage.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Courage and conscience: Chapters 7–11
- Objective: Analyze how minor characters develop theme
- Objective: Interpret symbols
- Vocabulary: symbol, courage, mockingbird, moral education
- Materials: To Kill a Mockingbird, Chapters 7–11
- Assessment: Quiz and theme tracker.

### Algebra I: Forms of quadratic functions
- Objective: Use vertex, standard, and factored forms
- Objective: Find zeros from factored form
- Vocabulary: vertex form, factored form, standard form, zero, root, x-intercept
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Forms quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Growth, differentiation, and stem cells
- Objective: Explain cell differentiation
- Objective: Evaluate stem cell research
- Vocabulary: differentiation, stem cell, gene expression, cancer, cell cycle checkpoint
- Materials: plant cuttings (pothos), water
- Assessment: Differentiation quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Labor and the farmworker movement
- Objective: Analyze the farmworker movement
- Objective: Explain coalition building
- Vocabulary: bracero program, United Farm Workers, boycott, strike, coalition
- Materials: Plan de Delano (1966), photos of the Delano grape strike
- Assessment: Coalition paragraph.

### Spanish II: Introduction to the present subjunctive: recommendations and wishes
- Objective: Form the present subjunctive
- Objective: Make recommendations and express wishes
- Vocabulary: subjuntivo, recomendar que, sugerir que, es importante que, es necesario que, ojalá
- Materials: subjunctive conjugation chart
- Assessment: Subjunctive quiz.

### Visual Art I: Photo essay: my community
- Objective: Tell a story through a photo series
- Vocabulary: photo essay, sequence, caption, documentary photography
- Materials: camera, notebook
- Assessment: Photo essay.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Jump rope skills and conditioning
- Objective: Perform jump rope skills
- Objective: Use jump rope for cardio
- Vocabulary: basic bounce, alternate step, criss-cross, double under
- Materials: jump rope
- Assessment: Jump rope routine and speed test.

### Health & Wellness: Emergency preparedness: earthquakes, wildfires and smoke
- Objective: Prepare for natural disasters common in California
- Objective: Build a household emergency plan
- Vocabulary: Drop, Cover, Hold On, go-bag, AQI, evacuation
- Materials: household items for kit
- Assessment: Family emergency plan and drill.
