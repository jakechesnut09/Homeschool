## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Essay plan* (60 min): Choose who bears most responsibility for the deaths and outline a claim, reasons, and counterclaim.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Factoring flowchart* (60 min): Build a decision flowchart and factor 20 mixed polynomials completely.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Hierarchy* (60 min): Build a model from cell to organism using the digestive system.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Harlem Renaissance* (50 min): Read Hughes and listen to Duke Ellington; analyze themes of identity.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Community vocabulary* (50 min): Draw a map of your town and label 15 places and the jobs done there in Spanish.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Interview* (50 min): Interview your subject and photograph them in a meaningful setting.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *PACER practice* (50 min): Practice the PACER with a pacing strategy and record laps.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Causes of violence* (45 min): Identify risk factors and protective factors for violence.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 132, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Draft* (60 min): Draft the argument essay with at least five quotations cited by act, scene, and line.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Structure* (60 min): Factor expressions like 4x²−9y² and (x+2)²−25 by seeing them as patterns.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Systems interaction* (60 min): Trace how circulatory, respiratory, and muscular systems interact during exercise.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Black Californians* (50 min): Research a Black Californian leader (e.g., Tom Bradley, Maya Angelou).
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Reading* (50 min): Read an article about teen volunteers in Latin America (e.g., Techo in Chile) and answer main-idea and detail questions.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Sketch* (50 min): Make a composition sketch including setting objects.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Upper body* (50 min): Practice push-up cadence and complete a push-up improvement circuit.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *De-escalation* (45 min): Practice de-escalation techniques (calm voice, space, active listening, walking away) in three role-plays and rate which worked best.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 133, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Revise* (60 min): Revise for counterclaim strength and edit quotation punctuation.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Area applications* (60 min): Write factored expressions for areas of rectangles and borders.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Heart dissection* (60 min): Dissect a store-bought chicken or pig heart and identify chambers.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Contemporary issues* (50 min): Analyze data on an equity issue (education, housing, health).
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Interview* (50 min): Interview a family member or neighbor about volunteer work they have done, using the present perfect and preterite.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Create* (50 min): Begin the final portrait.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Core and flexibility* (50 min): Practice curl-ups, trunk lift, and sit-and-reach with flexibility work.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Dating violence* (45 min): Learn the cycle of abuse and warning signs using loveisrespect.org.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 134, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Rehearse a scene* (60 min): Memorize 15–20 lines from a scene; plan blocking and vocal choices.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review* (60 min): Complete a mixed Unit 8 review.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Plant systems* (60 min): Compare plant transport systems with celery and food coloring.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Reflection* (50 min): Write a reflection connecting history to contemporary issues.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Writing* (50 min): Write a 150-word paragraph about a volunteer project you have done or could do, explaining who it helps.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Finish* (50 min): Finish the portrait.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Targeted workout* (50 min): Complete a 35-minute workout aimed at your lowest component.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Weapons and gangs* (45 min): Discuss the dangers of weapons and gang involvement and how to report threats.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 135, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Perform the scene (live or recorded), submit the essay, and take the Unit 6 test.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Romeo and Juliet argument essay and scene performance
- Objective: Write an argument about responsibility for the tragedy
- Objective: Perform a scene with formal diction
- Vocabulary: argument, responsibility, staging, blocking
- Materials: Romeo and Juliet full text, props for performance
- Assessment: Argument essay, scene performance rubric, Unit 6 test.

### Algebra I: Factoring practice and Unit 8 test
- Objective: Choose factoring strategies
- Objective: Use structure to rewrite expressions
- Vocabulary: factor completely, prime polynomial
- Materials: factoring flowchart
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Levels of organization and body systems
- Objective: Model hierarchical organization
- Objective: Explain interacting body systems
- Vocabulary: tissue, organ, organ system, organism, interaction
- Materials: body system cards
- Assessment: Systems quiz and dissection sheet.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): African American culture and contemporary issues
- Objective: Analyze African American contributions to culture
- Objective: Evaluate contemporary issues
- Vocabulary: Harlem Renaissance, cultural wealth, equity
- Materials: Langston Hughes poems, jazz recordings
- Assessment: Unit 9 assessment.

### Spanish II: Mi comunidad: places, jobs, and volunteering
- Objective: Describe community places and roles
- Objective: Talk about volunteering experiences
- Vocabulary: comunidad, voluntario, ayudar, comedor de beneficencia, ayuntamiento, bombero
- Materials: map of your town, Spanish article on youth volunteering
- Assessment: Vocabulary quiz and oral map description.

### Visual Art I: Portrait project: someone who matters
- Objective: Create a portrait of someone in your community
- Vocabulary: commission, likeness, setting
- Materials: painting or drawing materials, camera
- Assessment: Final portrait.

### PE 9 (Course 1): California Physical Fitness Test preparation
- Objective: Practice PFT components
- Objective: Use data to target weaknesses
- Vocabulary: Healthy Fitness Zone, practice test, pacing strategy
- Materials: PACER audio, measuring tape, mat
- Assessment: PFT practice results.

### Health & Wellness: Violence prevention and dating violence
- Objective: Describe causes and prevention of violence
- Objective: Identify warning signs of dating violence
- Vocabulary: dating violence, gang, de-escalation, restraining order
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: Safety plan scenario.
