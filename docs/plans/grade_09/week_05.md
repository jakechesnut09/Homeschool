## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Meet Mathilde* (60 min): Read "The Necklace" through the ball; list Mathilde's desires and the details Maupassant uses to show her dissatisfaction.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Modeling with equations* (60 min): Write and solve equations for 8 word problems (consecutive integers, perimeter, ticket sales).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Group behavior* (60 min): Read about bee swarming, fish schooling, and wolf pack hunting; list the survival advantage of each.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Climate factors* (50 min): Learn latitude, elevation, ocean currents, and prevailing winds as climate controls.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Imperfect forms* (50 min): Learn -aba and -ía endings and conjugate 20 verbs.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Frottage* (50 min): Make crayon rubbings of 10 textures around the house and label them.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Running form* (50 min): Learn posture, arm swing, and midfoot strike; do 6 form drills (A-skips, B-skips) and a 15-minute run.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Reading a label* (45 min): Annotate a Nutrition Facts label for serving size, calories, %DV, added sugar and sodium using the 5/20 rule.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Finish and summarize* (60 min): Finish the story and write an objective 6-sentence summary with no opinions.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Rate and mixture problems* (60 min): Solve distance-rate-time and mixture problems with tables to organize information.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Ant observation* (60 min): Place sugar water near an ant trail and record recruitment over 30 minutes.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Climographs* (50 min): Build climographs for Sacramento and Singapore and compare.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Childhood habits* (50 min): Write 10 sentences about what you used to do as a child using frequency words.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Texture study* (50 min): Draw close-up studies of tree bark, fabric, and metal.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Video analysis* (50 min): Film yourself running and compare to a form checklist; note two corrections.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Label comparison* (45 min): Compare five pantry items in pairs (two cereals, two drinks, etc.) and choose the healthier option with evidence from the labels.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *From topic to theme* (60 min): Turn topics (pride, appearance, class) into full theme statements and support the strongest with three quotations.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Literal equations* (60 min): Solve formulas like A=½bh, d=rt, and C=5/9(F−32) for a specified variable.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Stability* (60 min): Analyze a case study of ecosystem recovery after a California wildfire.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Rain shadow* (50 min): Model a rain shadow with the Sierra Nevada and explain Death Valley's aridity.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Irregulars* (50 min): Learn ser, ir, and ver in the imperfect and use each in sentences.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Charcoal* (50 min): Draw a crumpled paper bag in charcoal using subtractive eraser drawing.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Pacing* (50 min): Run 4 × 400 m (or 2-minute repeats) trying to keep each within 3 seconds.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Sugar and sodium* (45 min): Measure the teaspoons of sugar in a can of soda and a sports drink using a sugar bowl and the 4 g = 1 tsp conversion.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Cultural lens & punctuation* (60 min): Discuss how 19th-century French class expectations shape Mathilde's choices; practice semicolons joining related clauses in 8 sentences.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Formulas in context* (60 min): Rearrange the BMI and simple-interest formulas to answer questions about real data.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Evidence evaluation* (60 min): Write a CER evaluating whether group behavior increases survival using two data sources.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Climate regions* (50 min): Map Köppen climate regions and explain human adaptations.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Photo description* (50 min): Describe a childhood photo orally using the imperfect.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Ink wash* (50 min): Paint a quick ink-wash study of a houseplant with three dilutions.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Long easy run* (50 min): Complete a 25–30 minute continuous easy run or run/walk.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Dietary Guidelines* (45 min): Read the Dietary Guidelines for Americans key recommendations and summarize four in your own words.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Character and theme in "The Necklace"
- Objective: Analyze how a complex character drives theme
- Objective: Write an objective summary
- Vocabulary: theme, objective summary, static character, dynamic character, motivation
- Materials: "The Necklace" by Guy de Maupassant (public domain translation)
- Assessment: Theme paragraph (claim + 2 quotations + commentary) and quiz.

### Algebra I: Writing equations to solve problems; literal equations
- Objective: Create one-variable equations from contexts
- Objective: Rearrange formulas
- Vocabulary: literal equation, formula, solve for, model
- Materials: formula sheet
- Assessment: Modeling/literal equations quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Group behavior and ecosystem stability
- Objective: Evaluate evidence on group behavior and survival
- Objective: Explain ecosystem resilience
- Vocabulary: group behavior, herding, flocking, cooperation, resilience, disturbance
- Materials: ants and sugar water (outdoor observation)
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Weather, climate, and climate regions
- Objective: Explain factors affecting climate
- Objective: Interpret climographs
- Vocabulary: climate, weather, climograph, rain shadow, ocean current, Mediterranean climate
- Materials: climate data, graph paper
- Assessment: Climograph analysis and quiz.

### Spanish II: Cuando era niño/a: the imperfect tense
- Objective: Form the imperfect of regular verbs
- Objective: Describe childhood habits
- Vocabulary: imperfecto, de niño, siempre, a menudo, juguete, jugar
- Materials: childhood photos
- Assessment: Imperfect quiz and photo description.

### Visual Art I: Texture and drawing media exploration
- Objective: Render implied texture
- Objective: Experiment with charcoal and ink
- Vocabulary: implied texture, actual texture, charcoal, ink wash, frottage
- Materials: vine and compressed charcoal, India ink, brush, textured objects
- Assessment: Unit 1 portfolio review.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Running form and pacing
- Objective: Use efficient running mechanics
- Objective: Run at an even pace
- Vocabulary: cadence, stride, foot strike, pace, negative split
- Materials: phone for video, stopwatch
- Assessment: Mile time trial and form video analysis.

### Health & Wellness: Food labels and the Dietary Guidelines
- Objective: Interpret Nutrition Facts labels and ingredient lists
- Objective: Compare foods using % Daily Value
- Vocabulary: % Daily Value, added sugars, sodium, serving size, whole grain
- Materials: 5 packaged food labels from the pantry
- Assessment: Food-label practical with at least 8/10 correct.
