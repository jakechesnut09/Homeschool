## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Narrator and point of view* (60 min): Read "The Cask of Amontillado" aloud; list every claim Montresor makes about Fortunato's "thousand injuries" and judge whether he is reliable.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Parts of expressions* (60 min): Label terms, coefficients, factors, and constants in 15 expressions such as 3(x+2)−5x².
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Growth models* (60 min): Graph exponential vs. logistic growth from a sample data set and identify carrying capacity.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Projections* (50 min): Peel an orange to see why flat maps distort; compare Mercator, Robinson, and Peters projections.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Reflexive verbs* (50 min): Learn reflexive pronouns and conjugate 10 routine verbs; label pictures of a routine.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Shapes underneath* (50 min): Block in a still life of three household objects using basic geometric shapes before refining edges.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Heart rate* (50 min): Measure resting heart rate for three mornings; calculate maximum (220 − age) and a 60–85% target zone.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Values inventory* (45 min): Rank 15 personal values (family, honesty, fitness, faith, achievement…) and explain how your top three guide health choices.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Irony hunt* (60 min): Find five examples of verbal or dramatic irony (the trowel, "I drink to your long life") and explain the effect of each in a two-column chart.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Expressions in context* (60 min): Interpret expressions like 1.08p and 50+12h in shopping and phone-plan contexts.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Yeast balloon lab* (60 min): Mix yeast, warm water, and 0/1/2 tsp sugar in three bottles with balloons; measure balloon circumference every 10 minutes.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Map types* (50 min): Analyze a historical map from the Library of Congress and identify its purpose and point of view.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Mi horario* (50 min): Write your weekday routine with times (A las seis y media me despierto...).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Sighting and measuring* (50 min): Learn pencil sighting to measure proportions; draw a chair accurately using sighting.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Intensity test* (50 min): Jog, walk, and sprint intervals for 20 minutes, checking heart rate after each and recording RPE.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *DECIDE model* (45 min): Learn the DECIDE steps (Define, Explore, Consider, Identify, Decide, Evaluate) and apply them to a scenario about staying up late before a test.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Diction and mood* (60 min): Highlight words in the catacomb scenes that create dread; rewrite one paragraph in a cheerful tone to see how diction controls mood.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Order of operations and evaluating* (60 min): Evaluate 20 expressions with integers, fractions, and exponents for given values.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Limiting factors* (60 min): Analyze the yeast data: which factor limited growth? Classify density-dependent and independent factors.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *GIS* (50 min): Use an online GIS map (ArcGIS or Google Earth) to layer population and rivers.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Listening* (50 min): Listen to a Spanish speaker describing a routine (SpanishDict or a Spanish YouTube vlog) and fill a timeline.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Gesture* (50 min): Do 20 thirty-second gesture drawings of a family member doing household tasks or of figures in a sports video.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *FITT* (50 min): Design a 30-minute cardio workout labeling frequency, intensity, time, and type, then complete it.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Decision practice* (45 min): Work through three more scenarios (skipping breakfast, a party invite, a sports injury) with the DECIDE model, writing pros and cons for each option.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 9, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Grammar: phrases* (60 min): Learn prepositional, appositive, and participial phrases; combine 10 choppy sentences from your own notebook using phrases.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Simplifying* (60 min): Combine like terms and use the distributive property to simplify 20 expressions; verify equivalence by substitution.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Case study* (60 min): Analyze the St. Matthew Island reindeer data and explain the crash using carrying capacity.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Scale* (50 min): Calculate real distances using map scales for three trips.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Comparing routines* (50 min): Compare your routine with a teen in Spain (late dinner, siesta) in a Venn diagram.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Negative space* (50 min): Draw only the negative spaces around a chair or plant to correct shape errors.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Interval training* (50 min): Complete a 25-minute interval workout (1 min hard, 2 min easy) staying in your target zone.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *SMART goals* (45 min): Rewrite five vague goals as SMART goals, then write one SMART health goal for yourself with a 6-week tracker.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 10, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Write a timed paragraph (25 min) answering: How does Poe make Montresor's revenge chilling? Take a phrases mini-quiz.

- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on projections and map types.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Review & check* (50 min): Reflexive verb quiz and oral description of your routine.

- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on heart rate calculations and FITT; turn in your activity log.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Point of view and mood: Poe's "The Cask of Amontillado"
- Objective: Analyze unreliable first-person narration
- Objective: Explain how diction builds mood
- Vocabulary: unreliable narrator, mood, diction, verbal irony, dramatic irony
- Materials: "The Cask of Amontillado" by Edgar Allan Poe (public domain), reader's notebook
- Assessment: Timed analytical paragraph scored on claim, two cited quotations, and commentary; phrase identification quiz.

### Algebra I: Interpreting algebraic expressions
- Objective: Identify terms, factors, and coefficients
- Objective: Interpret expressions in context
- Vocabulary: term, factor, coefficient, constant, expression, variable
- Materials: expression cards, Desmos
- Assessment: Expression quiz (85%+).

### Biology – The Living Earth: Population growth and carrying capacity
- Objective: Model exponential and logistic growth
- Objective: Explain limiting factors
- Vocabulary: carrying capacity, limiting factor, exponential growth, logistic growth, density-dependent
- Materials: dried beans, graph paper, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Lab report on yeast growth and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Maps, projections, and geospatial technology
- Objective: Compare map projections
- Objective: Use GIS and online tools
- Vocabulary: map projection, distortion, Mercator, Robinson, GIS, scale
- Materials: orange (for projection activity), computer
- Assessment: Projection quiz and GIS screenshot analysis.

### Spanish II: Mi rutina diaria: reflexive verbs
- Objective: Describe a daily routine
- Objective: Use reflexive verbs and pronouns
- Vocabulary: despertarse, levantarse, ducharse, cepillarse, vestirse, acostarse
- Materials: routine picture cards, clock
- Assessment: Reflexive quiz and oral routine description.

### Visual Art I: Shape, gesture, and proportion
- Objective: Use geometric and organic shapes to block in drawings
- Objective: Capture motion with gesture drawing
- Vocabulary: geometric shape, organic shape, gesture, proportion, sighting
- Materials: sketchbook, pencil, timer
- Assessment: Proportion still life evaluated for accurate shapes and proportions.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Heart rate and the FITT principle
- Objective: Calculate target heart rate
- Objective: Apply FITT to a workout
- Vocabulary: resting heart rate, maximum heart rate, target heart rate zone, FITT, rate of perceived exertion
- Materials: stopwatch, heart rate app (optional)
- Assessment: Heart rate calculations and FITT workout plan.

### Health & Wellness: Decision making and goal setting
- Objective: Apply a step-by-step decision-making model
- Objective: Write a SMART health goal
- Vocabulary: decision-making model, SMART goal, consequence, values
- Materials: health journal, goal tracker sheet
- Assessment: Completed DECIDE worksheet and SMART goal action plan.
