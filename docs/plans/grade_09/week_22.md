## Day 1 — Monday (school day 106, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Elizabethan theater* (60 min): Learn about the Globe, Elizabethan audiences, and Shakespeare's language; translate 15 common words (wherefore, anon, hie).
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Exponent rules* (60 min): Derive product, quotient, and power rules with expanded form; simplify 20 expressions.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Mendel's peas* (60 min): Read about Mendel's experiments and define key terms.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Native California* (50 min): Map California Native nations (100+ languages) and identify the people whose land you live on.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Research* (50 min): Choose a Spanish-speaking city and research sites, food, and lodging on Spanish-language websites.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Installation art* (50 min): Study Christo and Jeanne-Claude's "Running Fence" (Sonoma/Marin, 1976) and Yayoi Kusama's infinity rooms.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Floating* (50 min): At a community pool, practice back and front floats for 30 seconds each and push-off glides. (No pool: practice freestyle arm motion and breathing on a bench.)
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Conception* (45 min): Describe fertilization and implantation with a diagram.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 107, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *The Prologue* (60 min): Read and paraphrase the Prologue sonnet; predict how knowing the ending affects suspense.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Zero and negative exponents* (60 min): Explain patterns that define a⁰=1 and a⁻ⁿ=1/aⁿ; simplify 15 expressions.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Monohybrid crosses* (60 min): Solve 10 monohybrid Punnett squares for pea and human traits and state genotype and phenotype ratios.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Traditional ecological knowledge* (50 min): Read about Native land management such as cultural burning and acorn processing.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Itinerary* (50 min): Write a 5-day itinerary in the future tense with a budget in local currency.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Site study* (50 min): Choose a space (yard, hallway, park) and photograph and measure it.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Treading water* (50 min): Practice eggbeater and scissor kicks while treading water for 3 × 1 minute.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Fetal development* (45 min): Create an illustrated timeline of embryonic and fetal development by trimester, noting when major organs form and why early prenatal care matters.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 108, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Act 1 scenes 1–3* (60 min): Read the street brawl and meet Romeo and Juliet; mark puns and insults.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Rational exponents* (60 min): Show why 8^(1/3)=∛8 using the power rule; convert between forms.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Coin-toss genetics* (60 min): Flip coins to simulate allele inheritance and compare to expected ratios.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Missions* (50 min): Analyze mission-era primary sources, including Pablo Tac's writings, and identify perspectives.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Slides* (50 min): Create 8 slides with images and Spanish captions for each day of the itinerary.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Proposal* (50 min): Collaborate with a sibling or friend to sketch a proposal that transforms the space.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Flutter kick* (50 min): Kick 8 lengths with a kickboard, keeping legs straight and kicks from the hip.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Prenatal care* (45 min): Research prenatal care and how substances affect fetal development.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 109, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Act 1 scenes 4–5* (60 min): Read Mercutio's Queen Mab speech and the shared sonnet at the party; analyze the first meeting.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Simplifying radicals* (60 min): Simplify radicals like √72 and expressions with rational exponents.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Dihybrid crosses* (60 min): Solve dihybrid crosses and predict 9:3:3:1 ratios.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Resistance* (50 min): Read about the 1785 Toypurina-led uprising at Mission San Gabriel and explain its causes.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Present* (50 min): Present the itinerary in Spanish for 3 minutes and answer questions about budget and plans.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Model* (50 min): Build a scale model in a shoebox.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Freestyle* (50 min): Swim freestyle with bilateral breathing every three strokes for 8 × 25 yards with rest.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Options and laws* (45 min): Learn about parenting, adoption and abortion as legal options, and California's Safely Surrendered Baby Law.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 110, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Act 1 quiz and a paraphrase of 20 lines of your choice into modern English.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on exponents and radicals.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on Mendelian genetics.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Write a paragraph evaluating the Mission system's impacts using two sources.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Unit 5 test* (50 min): Take the Unit 5 test.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Review & check* (50 min): Present the proposal and model and explain how it changes a viewer's experience.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Skills check: float 1 minute, tread 2 minutes, and swim 50 yards freestyle.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Shakespeare's world; Romeo and Juliet, Prologue and Act 1
- Objective: Read and paraphrase Shakespearean language
- Objective: Analyze the sonnet Prologue
- Vocabulary: tragedy, prologue, blank verse, pun, aside, foil
- Materials: Romeo and Juliet (Folger Shakespeare free online edition)
- Assessment: Act 1 quiz and paraphrase.

### Algebra I: Properties of exponents and radicals
- Objective: Apply exponent rules
- Objective: Rewrite rational exponents as radicals
- Vocabulary: exponent, power, zero exponent, negative exponent, rational exponent, radical
- Materials: exponent rule cards
- Assessment: Exponent quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Mendelian genetics and probability
- Objective: Use Punnett squares
- Objective: Apply probability to inheritance
- Vocabulary: allele, genotype, phenotype, dominant, recessive, Punnett square
- Materials: coins, Punnett square worksheets
- Assessment: Genetics quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): California Native peoples before and during the Mission era
- Objective: Describe diversity of California Native nations
- Objective: Analyze effects of the Mission system
- Vocabulary: tribe, sovereignty, Mission system, Ohlone, Tongva, Chumash, colonization
- Materials: map of California Native territories, Calisphere mission-era images
- Assessment: Source-based paragraph.

### Spanish II: Unit 5 project: travel itinerary
- Objective: Plan a trip and present it
- Objective: Use future tense in a presentation
- Vocabulary: itinerario, presupuesto, alojamiento
- Materials: travel websites in Spanish, slides
- Assessment: Itinerary presentation and unit test.

### Visual Art I: Installation and site-specific art
- Objective: Propose an installation that transforms a space
- Vocabulary: installation, site-specific, environmental art, proposal, scale model
- Materials: shoebox, found materials, camera
- Assessment: Installation proposal and model.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Swimming fundamentals: floating, treading, freestyle
- Objective: Float and tread water
- Objective: Swim freestyle with rhythmic breathing
- Vocabulary: flutter kick, rhythmic breathing, treading water, front float, back float
- Materials: community pool access (or land-based alternative), goggles, kickboard
- Assessment: Swim skills check (or land-based technique check).

### Health & Wellness: Conception, pregnancy and parenting
- Objective: Describe conception, fetal development and prenatal care
- Objective: Explain pregnancy options and the Safely Surrendered Baby Law
- Vocabulary: conception, embryo, fetus, prenatal care, Safely Surrendered Baby Law
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: Budget activity and quiz.
