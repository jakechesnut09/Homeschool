## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Book 9: Cyclops* (60 min): Read the Cyclops episode; list each clever choice and each mistake Odysseus makes.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Half-planes* (60 min): Graph 10 inequalities in two variables using dashed/solid boundaries and test points.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Mass extinctions* (60 min): Analyze graphs of the five mass extinctions and their causes.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Globalization pros and cons* (50 min): Read two opposing articles on globalization and chart claims about jobs, culture, and environment.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Tense review* (50 min): Complete a mixed Conjuguemos drill on present, reflexive, preterite, and imperfect, then rewrite a short paragraph from present into past.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *WPA posters* (50 min): Analyze five 1930s WPA posters from the Library of Congress and note how each uses hierarchy, color, and lettering.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Center of gravity* (50 min): Experiment with base of support and center of gravity in one-foot and partner balances.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Cannabis* (45 min): Research cannabis effects on memory, motivation and mental health, including high-potency products.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Hubris* (60 min): Analyze Odysseus's boast to Polyphemus and its consequences in a cause-effect chain.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Systems of inequalities* (60 min): Graph systems and shade the overlapping solution region.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *K-Pg boundary* (60 min): Evaluate evidence for the asteroid impact (iridium layer, Chicxulub crater).
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *California's regions* (50 min): Map California's eight geographic regions (North Coast, Central Valley, Sierra Nevada, Deserts, etc.) with economy and climate notes.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Pronouns and commands* (50 min): Rewrite a recipe using pronouns and commands (Córtalo. No lo frías.) and correct a 15-item error-analysis sheet.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Thumbnails* (50 min): Sketch six thumbnails for a poster promoting a real community event (library program, farmers market, park cleanup).
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Static balances* (50 min): Hold 10 balances (V-sit, arabesque, tripod) for 10 seconds each.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Prescription misuse* (45 min): Learn why sharing or misusing prescription drugs is dangerous, and how to dispose of medicine safely.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 83, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Book 10: Aeolus and Circe* (60 min): Read Book 10; compare how Odysseus leads his men here vs. in Book 9.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Constraints* (60 min): Model a bake-sale problem (time, ingredients) with inequalities and identify viable combinations.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Recovery* (60 min): Explain how mammals radiated after the dinosaurs.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Then and now* (50 min): Compare 1950 and present-day California census data on population, industries, and diversity.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Speaking practice* (50 min): Answer 10 recorded conversation prompts (¿Qué hacías de niño? ¿Qué hiciste el fin de semana?) and self-score with the rubric.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Lettering* (50 min): Practice hand-lettering the title in two styles and choose one that matches the event's mood.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Dynamic balance* (50 min): Walk a line forward/backward and perform turns on a balance line.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Fentanyl* (45 min): Learn about fentanyl, counterfeit pills and why 'one pill can kill', using CDPH/DEA materials.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 84, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Seminar prep* (60 min): Prepare three open-ended discussion questions about leadership with text references.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: The Odyssey: the Cyclops and Circe (Books 9–10)
- Objective: Analyze Odysseus's strengths and flaws
- Objective: Explain how episodes develop theme
- Vocabulary: hubris, cunning, temptation, flashback, characterization
- Materials: The Odyssey, Books 9–10
- Assessment: Seminar participation rubric and written reflection.

### Algebra I: Linear inequalities in two variables and systems of inequalities
- Objective: Graph linear inequalities as half-planes
- Objective: Solve constraint problems with systems of inequalities
- Vocabulary: half-plane, boundary line, solution region, constraint, feasible
- Materials: colored pencils, graph paper
- Assessment: Inequality systems quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Extinction and changing environments
- Objective: Evaluate evidence of environmental change and extinction
- Objective: Connect Earth's changes to biodiversity
- Vocabulary: mass extinction, background extinction, adaptive radiation, climate change
- Materials: data on extinction rates
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Globalization and California as a global region
- Objective: Evaluate effects of globalization
- Objective: Describe California's physical and human regions
- Vocabulary: globalization, interdependence, outsourcing, cultural convergence, Pacific Rim
- Materials: California relief map, Census data
- Assessment: California regional report.

### Spanish II: Semester review: tenses, pronouns, and commands
- Objective: Review all semester structures
- Objective: Self-assess proficiency with can-do statements
- Vocabulary: repaso, presente, pretérito, imperfecto, mandatos
- Materials: semester review packet, Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Practice exam and can-do self-assessment.

### Visual Art I: Graphic design: the public poster
- Objective: Apply principles of design to a poster with text and image
- Objective: Analyze historical posters
- Vocabulary: typography, layout, visual hierarchy, negative space, silkscreen
- Materials: poster board or free design app, markers, Library of Congress WPA poster images
- Assessment: Poster evaluated on hierarchy, readability, and design principles.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Balance and body management
- Objective: Perform balances and body-management skills
- Vocabulary: center of gravity, base of support, static balance, dynamic balance
- Materials: mat or carpet
- Assessment: Balance sequence.

### Health & Wellness: Cannabis, prescription drugs, fentanyl and naloxone
- Objective: Describe effects of cannabis and prescription misuse
- Objective: Recognize opioid overdose and how to respond
- Vocabulary: THC, opioid, fentanyl, counterfeit pill, naloxone, Good Samaritan law
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: Overdose response scenario quiz.
