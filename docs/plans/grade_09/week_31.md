## Day 1 — Monday (school day 151, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Atticus's closing* (60 min): Read Chapters 20–21; analyze rhetorical appeals in Atticus's closing argument.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Zero product property* (60 min): Solve 15 equations by factoring after writing in standard form.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *HIPPO* (60 min): Learn the HIPPO framework (habitat, invasive species, pollution, population, overharvesting).
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Gold Rush and railroads* (50 min): Read about Chinese workers on the Transcontinental Railroad.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Art vocabulary* (50 min): Learn 20 art terms and describe colors, shapes, and placement in one painting.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Digital tools* (50 min): Learn layers, brushes, selection, and opacity in a free app.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Rules and etiquette* (50 min): Learn scoring, order of play, and etiquette; play 9 holes.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Pathogens* (45 min): Compare bacteria, viruses, fungi and parasites with examples of diseases.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 152, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *After the verdict* (60 min): Read Chapters 22–23; compare Jem's, Miss Maudie's, and Atticus's reactions.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Square roots* (60 min): Solve equations like 3(x−2)²=48 by square roots.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Invasive species* (60 min): Research a California invasive species (e.g., quagga mussel) and its impacts.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Exclusion Act* (50 min): Read the 1882 Chinese Exclusion Act (DocsTeach) and list its provisions and the reasons given for it.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Frida Kahlo* (50 min): Read a short Spanish biography of Kahlo and explain how her life appears in "Las dos Fridas."
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Digital copy* (50 min): Recreate one of your earlier drawings digitally to learn the tools.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Course management* (50 min): Plan shots on each hole and play 9 holes, noting choices.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Transmission* (45 min): Do a glitter handwashing experiment to show how germs spread.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 153, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Tom's fate* (60 min): Read Chapters 24–25; analyze Mr. Underwood's editorial and the mockingbird symbol.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Word problems* (60 min): Solve 8 area and number problems by factoring (e.g., a rectangle whose length is 3 more than its width has area 70).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Pollution test* (60 min): Test local water samples with pH strips and compare to standards.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Angel Island* (50 min): Read Angel Island poems and analyze detainees' experiences.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Diego Rivera* (50 min): Analyze Rivera's Detroit Industry murals or the San Francisco "Pan American Unity" mural and describe the workers shown.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Original* (50 min): Create an original digital illustration combining a photo and drawing.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Practice* (50 min): Practice the shots that cost you the most strokes.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Immune system* (45 min): Diagram the immune response and how vaccines train immunity.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 154, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Miss Gates* (60 min): Read Chapter 26; identify hypocrisy in the current-events lesson.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Graphs and solutions* (60 min): Graph 6 quadratics on Desmos and verify that factored solutions match the x-intercepts.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Data analysis* (60 min): Analyze data on Monarch butterfly population decline.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Wong Kim Ark* (50 min): Analyze the 1898 Wong Kim Ark case on birthright citizenship.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Opinions* (50 min): Write opinions on two works using me parece que, creo que, and me gusta porque.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Refine* (50 min): Refine color and layer effects.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Full round* (50 min): Play 18 holes (or two 9-hole rounds) and walk the course.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Vaccines* (45 min): Review the California school immunization schedule and teen vaccines (Tdap, HPV, meningococcal, flu).

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Verdict and aftermath: Chapters 20–26
- Objective: Analyze how the verdict shapes theme
- Objective: Evaluate characters' responses to injustice
- Vocabulary: verdict, injustice, hypocrisy, empathy
- Materials: To Kill a Mockingbird, Chapters 20–26
- Assessment: Seminar rubric and quiz.

### Algebra I: Solving quadratics by factoring and square roots
- Objective: Solve by factoring with the zero product property
- Objective: Solve by taking square roots
- Vocabulary: zero product property, square root method, standard form equation
- Materials: factoring flowchart
- Assessment: Solving quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Human impacts on ecosystems
- Objective: Identify major human impacts
- Objective: Analyze data on biodiversity loss
- Vocabulary: habitat loss, invasive species, pollution, overexploitation, climate change
- Materials: local news articles, data sets
- Assessment: Human impacts quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Chinese immigration and exclusion
- Objective: Analyze the Chinese Exclusion Act
- Objective: Evaluate primary sources
- Vocabulary: Chinese Exclusion Act, Angel Island, paper sons, nativism
- Materials: Chinese Exclusion Act (1882, National Archives), Angel Island poetry
- Assessment: Source analysis.

### Spanish II: Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera: describing art
- Objective: Describe artworks with art vocabulary
- Objective: Express opinions about art
- Vocabulary: cuadro, mural, autorretrato, pintor/a, fondo, me parece que
- Materials: images of Kahlo self-portraits and Rivera murals (museum websites)
- Assessment: Vocabulary quiz and oral description.

### Visual Art I: Digital art basics
- Objective: Create digital artwork using layers
- Vocabulary: digital art, layers, pixel, vector, opacity
- Materials: computer or tablet, free app (e.g., Krita or a browser-based editor)
- Assessment: Digital illustration.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Disc golf rounds and course strategy
- Objective: Play a round using strategy and etiquette
- Vocabulary: par, stroke, course management, etiquette
- Materials: discs, scorecard, local course
- Assessment: Scorecards and strategy reflection.

### Health & Wellness: Communicable diseases and immunity
- Objective: Explain how pathogens spread
- Objective: Describe how vaccines work
- Vocabulary: pathogen, immunity, vaccine, herd immunity
- Materials: glitter or lotion for handwashing demo
- Assessment: Quiz.
