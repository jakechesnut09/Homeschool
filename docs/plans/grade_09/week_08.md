## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Introductions* (60 min): Study three model introductions; write a hook, context, and thesis for your literary analysis essay.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Relations and functions* (60 min): Sort 16 mappings, tables, and graphs into functions/non-functions and explain the rule.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Respiration model* (60 min): Model C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + energy with molecule cards, showing bonds broken and formed.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Push and pull* (50 min): Classify push and pull factors in 10 migration cases.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Plan* (50 min): Plan a 6-page memory book: choose photos or drawings for home, family, school, games, food, and holidays.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Chinese landscape painting* (50 min): Study a Song dynasty landscape and identify how mist shows depth.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Fartlek* (50 min): Complete a 25-minute fartlek run with surges.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Activity guidelines* (45 min): Learn the 60-minutes-per-day guideline for teens and log your activity minutes and type for today.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Body paragraphs* (60 min): Draft two body paragraphs using the claim–evidence–commentary pattern with embedded quotations and MLA citations.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Function notation* (60 min): Evaluate f(x)=3x²−2x for 10 inputs and interpret f(5)=120 in a context.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *CO2 indicator lab* (60 min): Blow through a straw into red-cabbage indicator before and after exercise and compare color change.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Family interview* (50 min): Interview a family member about your family's migration history.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Write* (50 min): Write 3–4 imperfect-tense captions for each page (Vivíamos en..., Siempre jugaba...).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Plein air* (50 min): Draw a landscape outdoors with foreground, middle ground, and background.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Hill repeats* (50 min): Do 6 hill repeats with walk-down recovery.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Sleep and health* (45 min): Read about teen sleep needs (8–10 hours) and design a sleep-hygiene routine for school nights.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 38, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Finish and conclude* (60 min): Draft the final body paragraph and a conclusion that extends the idea; aim for 900–1,100 words.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Graphs as solution sets* (60 min): Plot points from an equation and explain why every point on the graph makes it true.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Fermentation* (60 min): Compare yeast fermentation with and without oxygen and connect to bread and yogurt.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Migration flows* (50 min): Analyze maps of the Great Migration and Dust Bowl migration to California.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Edit* (50 min): Edit captions with a checklist for imperfect endings, ser/ir/ver forms, and noun-adjective agreement.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Value layers* (50 min): Redo the landscape with value fading in the distance.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Cross-training* (50 min): Swim, bike, or hike for 40 minutes at target heart rate.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Meal planning* (45 min): Plan three days of balanced meals and snacks on a budget using a grocery flyer and MyPlate targets.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 39, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Revise and edit* (60 min): Revise for transitions and precise verbs ("reveals," "underscores"); edit punctuation of quotations.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Domain and range* (60 min): Find domain and range from graphs, tables, and context (e.g., tickets sold can't be negative).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *ATP* (60 min): Explain how ATP transfers energy in cells with an analogy and diagram.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Refugees* (50 min): Read about a current refugee crisis and its causes.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Present* (50 min): Present the memory book orally for 2 minutes without reading word-for-word, then answer two questions.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Compare* (50 min): Compare Chinese and Hudson River School landscapes in writing.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Easy run* (50 min): Complete a 20-minute recovery run and stretching.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Cook a meal* (45 min): Prepare one meal from your plan with family, photograph it and label its food groups.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 40, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Submit the final essay and complete a 45-minute timed write on an unseen short poem-length story excerpt.

- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Review & check* (50 min): Unit 2 review of perspective drawings.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Writing the literary analysis essay
- Objective: Write a multi-paragraph analysis with embedded quotations
- Objective: Use MLA in-text citations
- Vocabulary: thesis, embedded quotation, commentary, MLA citation, transition
- Materials: outline from week 7, MLA guide (Purdue OWL)
- Assessment: Literary analysis essay (W.9-10.2 rubric) and timed write.

### Algebra I: What is a function?
- Objective: Determine whether a relation is a function
- Objective: Use function notation
- Vocabulary: function, domain, range, input, output, function notation, vertical line test
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Function quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Cellular respiration and fermentation
- Objective: Model aerobic respiration
- Objective: Compare aerobic and anaerobic processes
- Vocabulary: cellular respiration, mitochondria, ATP, fermentation, aerobic, anaerobic
- Materials: bromothymol blue (or red cabbage indicator), straw, yeast, sugar
- Assessment: Indicator lab write-up and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Migration
- Objective: Explain push and pull factors
- Objective: Analyze migration flows
- Vocabulary: migration, push factor, pull factor, refugee, emigration, immigration
- Materials: migration maps, family interviews
- Assessment: Migration case study.

### Spanish II: Unit 2 project: Mi niñez
- Objective: Create a childhood memory book
- Objective: Present orally
- Vocabulary: recuerdo, álbum
- Materials: photos, construction paper or slides
- Assessment: Memory book rubric and unit test.

### Visual Art I: Atmospheric perspective and landscape
- Objective: Use atmospheric perspective
- Objective: Draw landscape from observation
- Vocabulary: atmospheric perspective, foreground, middle ground, background, overlap
- Materials: pencils, landscape photos
- Assessment: Landscape drawing and comparison.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Cardio endurance challenge
- Objective: Apply training principles to improve endurance
- Vocabulary: progressive overload, recovery, endurance
- Materials: stopwatch, route map
- Assessment: Mile/PACER retest compared to baseline.

### Health & Wellness: Physical activity, sleep and a personal nutrition plan
- Objective: Explain the benefits of physical activity and sleep
- Objective: Build a one-week healthy eating and activity plan
- Vocabulary: aerobic activity, FITT, sleep hygiene, meal plan
- Materials: grocery flyer, activity log
- Assessment: Unit 2 test and personal nutrition/activity plan.
