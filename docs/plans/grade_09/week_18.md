## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Book 11* (60 min): Read the Underworld episode; explain what Tiresias's prophecy and the meeting with Achilles reveal about glory.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review: equations and inequalities* (60 min): Complete mixed review on Units 1–2 and correct errors.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review ecosystems* (60 min): Create a one-page summary of ecosystem concepts with diagrams.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Profile research* (50 min): Choose a country and gather physical geography, climate, population, culture, and economic data.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Listening section* (50 min): Listen to two authentic audio clips (a restaurant order and a childhood memory) and answer comprehension questions.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Select* (50 min): Lay out all semester work and select six pieces that show range (drawing, perspective, painting, print, design).
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Rolls* (50 min): Practice log rolls, forward rolls, and backward shoulder rolls on a mat.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Influences* (45 min): Map the influences on teen substance use (peers, family, media, community) and identify protective factors.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Book 12* (60 min): Read the Sirens and Scylla and Charybdis; discuss what each hazard represents.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review: functions and linear models* (60 min): Complete a mixed review on Units 3–4 (function notation, slope, writing linear equations) and correct errors.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review energy and matter* (60 min): Redraw photosynthesis, respiration, and carbon cycle models from memory.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Profile maps* (50 min): Create two maps (physical and population/thematic) and a population pyramid for your country.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Reading section* (50 min): Read an authentic article about a Latin American festival and answer main-idea and detail questions.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Document* (50 min): Photograph each piece squarely in natural light with a neutral backdrop and crop/label the files.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Cartwheel* (50 min): Progress to a cartwheel with spotting.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Refusal skills* (45 min): Practice five refusal strategies (say no, give a reason, suggest an alternative, leave, use humor) in role-plays.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 88, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Structure* (60 min): Explain how Homer's use of Odysseus's flashback (Books 9–12) builds suspense for the return.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review: systems* (60 min): Complete mixed review on Unit 5, including a context problem.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review evolution* (60 min): Make flashcards of evidence for evolution and natural selection.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Present* (50 min): Present a 5-minute country profile connecting physical geography to how people live there.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Speaking interview* (50 min): Hold a 5-minute interview with the teacher on routines, childhood, and food.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Reflect* (50 min): Write a 100-word reflection for each piece: goal, process, what you'd change.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Fitness check* (50 min): Retake push-up and curl-up tests and compare to baseline.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Advocacy* (45 min): Create a short PSA promoting a substance-free lifestyle.
