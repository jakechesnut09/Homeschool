## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Draft the opening* (60 min): Write the first half of your personal narrative, opening in the middle of the action with dialogue or a vivid image.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Balance model* (60 min): Use balance-scale drawings to solve 10 two-step equations and write the property justifying each step.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Food webs* (60 min): Build a food web for a California chaparral ecosystem with at least 12 organisms.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Plate boundaries* (50 min): Model convergent, divergent, and transform boundaries with crackers and frosting.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Interpretive task* (50 min): Read an authentic school-schedule article from a Spanish-language site and answer comprehension questions.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Set up* (50 min): Arrange 3–5 white objects under a strong lamp; make three thumbnail compositions and pick one.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Trunk lift and flexibility* (50 min): Complete the trunk lift and back-saver sit-and-reach tests.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Six nutrients* (45 min): Watch a short nutrition video and make a chart of the six nutrient classes with functions and three food sources each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Finish the draft* (60 min): Complete the narrative through the climax and a reflective ending that shows what you learned (800–1,200 words).
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Distributive equations* (60 min): Solve 15 equations involving parentheses and fractions (e.g., ⅔(x−6)=4).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Owl pellet dissection* (60 min): Dissect an owl pellet (or virtual pellet), identify bones, and estimate prey eaten.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *California faults* (50 min): Map the San Andreas Fault and recent earthquakes using the USGS live earthquake map.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Interpersonal task* (50 min): Hold a 3-minute conversation about routines and school with a parent or tutor.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Block-in* (50 min): Lightly block in the composition with sighting.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Body composition* (50 min): Learn what body composition measures and why it's one part of health, then complete a 30-minute workout of choice.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *MyPlate* (45 min): Use the MyPlate Plan tool to find your personal daily food-group targets and record them.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Revise for craft* (60 min): Highlight telling sentences and rewrite five as showing details; vary sentence openings using phrases and clauses from weeks 2–3.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Variables on both sides* (60 min): Solve 15 equations with variables on both sides, including ones with no solution or infinitely many.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Energy pyramids* (60 min): Use the 10% rule to calculate energy at each trophic level from 10,000 kcal of producers.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Landforms* (50 min): Identify landforms formed by plates, water, wind, and ice in photos.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Presentational task* (50 min): Write a 150-word email to a new exchange student describing your routine.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Value* (50 min): Lay in the darkest darks and lightest lights, then build midtones.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *SMART goals* (50 min): Write two SMART fitness goals based on baseline results.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Portion lab* (45 min): Measure out recommended portions of cereal, rice, juice and nuts with measuring cups and compare them to the amounts you usually serve.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Edit and publish* (60 min): Edit with a checklist (commas after introductory elements, dialogue punctuation, verb tense) and type a final copy.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Error analysis* (60 min): Find and correct the error in 8 worked solutions, explaining each mistake in writing.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Biomass* (60 min): Explain why biomass pyramids narrow and where energy is lost as heat.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Hazards* (50 min): Analyze how earthquakes and volcanoes affect human settlement (e.g., 1906 San Francisco).
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Vocabulary review* (50 min): Review Unit 1 vocabulary with Quizlet or flashcards and correct errors.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Refine* (50 min): Refine edges, textures, and cast shadows.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Goal workout* (50 min): Complete a 30-minute workout targeting your weakest component.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Food log* (45 min): Begin a 3-day food log, recording everything you eat and drink with food groups and approximate portions.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Review & check* (60 min): Read your narrative aloud to family; take the Unit 1 test on plot, conflict, irony, point of view, and phrases/clauses.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on multi-step equations with step justifications.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on energy flow calculations.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Quiz on tectonics and landforms.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Unit 1 test* (50 min): Take the Unit 1 written test.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Critique* (50 min): Write criteria for a successful still life and use them to self-evaluate your drawing.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Review & check* (50 min): Submit a goal sheet with baseline data, goals, and a plan to reach them.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Check* (45 min): Compare your food log to your MyPlate targets and take a 15-question nutrient quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Drafting, revising, and publishing a personal narrative
- Objective: Draft a narrative using dialogue, pacing, and sensory detail
- Objective: Revise for show-don't-tell and sentence variety
- Vocabulary: revision, show don't tell, flashback, reflection
- Materials: narrative plan, computer or notebook, revision checklist
- Assessment: Personal narrative scored with W.9-10.3 rubric; Unit 1 test.

### Algebra I: Solving multi-step linear equations
- Objective: Solve equations and justify each step
- Objective: Solve equations with variables on both sides
- Vocabulary: equation, inverse operation, solution, equivalent equations, justify
- Materials: algebra tiles or balance drawings
- Assessment: Equation quiz with justifications.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Energy flow and matter cycling in food webs
- Objective: Build food webs
- Objective: Calculate energy transfer between trophic levels
- Vocabulary: producer, consumer, decomposer, trophic level, energy pyramid, 10% rule
- Materials: owl pellet (purchased) or online virtual dissection, tweezers
- Assessment: Energy calculations quiz and dissection data sheet.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Plate tectonics and landforms
- Objective: Explain plate boundaries
- Objective: Connect landforms to processes
- Vocabulary: plate tectonics, fault, subduction, earthquake, volcano, erosion
- Materials: crackers, frosting, USGS earthquake map
- Assessment: Tectonics quiz.

### Spanish II: Unit 1 integrated performance assessment
- Objective: Demonstrate interpretive, interpersonal, and presentational skills
- Vocabulary: repaso
- Materials: IPA task sheet
- Assessment: Integrated performance assessment and unit test.

### Visual Art I: Still life value drawing
- Objective: Compose and render a still life with a full value range
- Vocabulary: composition, focal point, cast shadow, full value range
- Materials: still life objects (white objects work best), lamp, drawing paper
- Assessment: Still life drawing scored against self-written and teacher criteria.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Pre-assessment, part 2, and goal setting
- Objective: Complete flexibility and trunk tests
- Objective: Set SMART fitness goals
- Vocabulary: trunk lift, sit-and-reach, SMART goal, baseline
- Materials: yardstick, box for sit-and-reach, ruler
- Assessment: SMART goal sheet.

### Health & Wellness: Nutrients and MyPlate
- Objective: Describe the six classes of nutrients and their functions
- Objective: Apply MyPlate recommendations to daily intake
- Vocabulary: carbohydrate, protein, fat, vitamin, mineral, MyPlate
- Materials: food log, measuring cups
- Assessment: Nutrient quiz and food log comparison.
