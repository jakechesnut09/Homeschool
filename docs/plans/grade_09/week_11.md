## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Founding ideals* (60 min): Read the Declaration's preamble and the Gettysburg Address; list ideas Lincoln echoes and how he redefines them.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Stair slope lab* (60 min): Measure rise and run of household stairs and a ramp; compute and compare slopes.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Rock layers* (60 min): Build a layered jar of soil and sand to model stratigraphy and relative dating.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *States and nations* (50 min): Distinguish state, nation, and nation-state with examples.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Rules* (50 min): Learn when to use each tense (background vs. completed action).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Impressionism* (50 min): Study Monet's "Haystacks" series and explain how light changes color.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Basics* (50 min): Learn grip, forehand, backhand, and underhand serve at a public court.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Common conditions* (45 min): Research anxiety, depression, ADHD and PTSD and complete a chart of symptoms and treatments.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Douglass's challenge* (60 min): Read the Douglass excerpt; analyze how he uses the Declaration's ideals to indict slavery.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Slope from two points* (60 min): Calculate slope from points, tables, and graphs for 15 problems.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Radiometric dating* (60 min): Use a coin half-life simulation to estimate the age of a sample.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Borders* (50 min): Classify borders (physical, geometric, cultural).
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Practice* (50 min): Choose preterite or imperfect in 25 sentences and label each with the rule (habit, description, completed action, interruption).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Thumbnails* (50 min): Paint 3 small studies of the same view at different times of day.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Rules* (50 min): Learn the two-bounce rule and the non-volley zone; play practice points.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Myths and facts* (45 min): Sort 12 mental health statements into myths and facts and correct each myth.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 53, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Fallacies* (60 min): Learn eight common fallacies and identify them in 12 sample arguments from letters to the editor and ads.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Rate of change in context* (60 min): Interpret slopes like −2.5 gallons/hour and $12/ticket.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Transitional fossils* (60 min): Analyze Tiktaalik and whale fossil sequences (Pakicetus to modern whales) and list the traits that changed.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Colonial borders* (50 min): Analyze the 1884 Berlin Conference map and its lasting effects on Africa.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Story* (50 min): Read a short story and identify why each tense is used.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Painting* (50 min): Start a landscape painting in broken color.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Dinking* (50 min): Practice dinks and volleys at the kitchen line.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Treatment options* (45 min): Learn about counseling, therapy types (CBT), medication and support groups.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 54, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Choose your issue* (60 min): Pick a debatable issue (e.g., later school start times); find two credible sources and record claims and evidence in a research log.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Average rate of change* (60 min): Find average rate of change of nonlinear data over intervals (e.g., population table).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Fossil timeline* (60 min): Place major fossil groups on the geologic time scale.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Conflict* (50 min): Analyze a current border conflict using multiple news sources for bias.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Comic* (50 min): Narrate a 6-panel comic strip using both tenses.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Seminal U.S. documents and evaluating arguments
- Objective: Compare how seminal documents treat liberty and equality
- Objective: Identify logical fallacies
- Vocabulary: fallacy, ad hominem, straw man, false dilemma, bandwagon, seminal document
- Materials: Declaration of Independence (preamble), Lincoln, Gettysburg Address, Frederick Douglass, "What to the Slave Is the Fourth of July?" (excerpt)
- Assessment: Document comparison chart; fallacy quiz; approved claim and research log.

### Algebra I: Slope and rate of change
- Objective: Calculate and interpret slope
- Objective: Compute average rate of change
- Vocabulary: slope, rate of change, rise over run, constant rate
- Materials: graph paper, stairs and tape measure
- Assessment: Slope quiz and lab write-up.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Evidence for evolution: fossils and the geologic record
- Objective: Interpret fossil evidence
- Objective: Use relative and absolute dating
- Vocabulary: fossil, stratigraphy, relative dating, radiometric dating, transitional fossil
- Materials: layered sand/soil jar, clay
- Assessment: Fossil evidence quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Political geography: states, borders, and conflict
- Objective: Explain states, nations, and borders
- Objective: Analyze boundary conflicts
- Vocabulary: state, nation, sovereignty, boundary, colonialism
- Materials: political maps
- Assessment: Political geography quiz.

### Spanish II: Preterite vs. imperfect
- Objective: Distinguish preterite and imperfect uses
- Objective: Narrate a story
- Vocabulary: mientras, de repente, una vez, trasfondo, acción
- Materials: comic strips
- Assessment: Tense quiz and comic narration.

### Visual Art I: Impressionism and landscape painting
- Objective: Paint a landscape
- Objective: Explain Impressionist ideas
- Vocabulary: Impressionism, broken color, plein air, light
- Materials: paints, canvas board or heavy paper
- Assessment: Landscape painting and written interpretation.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Pickleball fundamentals
- Objective: Use pickleball strokes
- Objective: Explain the two-bounce rule and kitchen
- Vocabulary: dink, volley, kitchen, two-bounce rule, third-shot drop
- Materials: pickleball paddles, ball, public court
- Assessment: Pickleball skills test and rules quiz.

### Health & Wellness: Mental health conditions and stigma
- Objective: Describe common mental health conditions and treatments
- Objective: Explain how stigma prevents help-seeking
- Vocabulary: anxiety disorder, depression, ADHD, therapy, stigma
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: Anti-stigma poster scored on accuracy and message.
