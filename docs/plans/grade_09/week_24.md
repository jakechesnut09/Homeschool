## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *The duel* (60 min): Read 3.1; explain how Mercutio's death and "A plague o' both your houses" turn comedy to tragedy.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Compound interest* (60 min): Model savings at 5% compounded yearly and monthly; compare results.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Mutation types* (60 min): Model point and frameshift mutations by changing a sentence of three-letter words.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Termination and relocation* (50 min): Read about 1950s federal termination and relocation to Los Angeles and the Bay Area.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Formal commands* (50 min): Learn usted/ustedes command forms, including irregulars (vaya, sea, dé), and conjugate 20 verbs.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Self-portrait history* (50 min): Compare self-portraits by Rembrandt, Frida Kahlo, and Kehinde Wiley and note symbols they use.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Interval swim* (50 min): Complete a 30-minute interval set (e.g., 8 × 50 yards on 1:30) and record heart rate.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Common STIs* (45 min): Research chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, HPV and herpes: symptoms, testing and treatment.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 117, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Juliet's reaction* (60 min): Read 3.2; analyze Juliet's oxymorons about Romeo.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Eventually exceeds* (60 min): Graph y=100x and y=1.1^x and find where exponential overtakes linear.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Environmental mutagens* (60 min): Read about UV and mutation; test UV beads in sunlight vs. sunscreen.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Alcatraz Proclamation* (50 min): Analyze the 1969 Proclamation's satire and claims.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Health pamphlet* (50 min): Read a CDC en español pamphlet and list commands it uses.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Plan* (50 min): Sketch thumbnails including 3 personal symbols.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Water aerobics* (50 min): Do a 25-minute water-aerobics routine of jogs, jumping jacks, and cross-country skis in chest-deep water.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *HIV* (45 min): Learn how HIV attacks the immune system and how it is and isn't transmitted.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 118, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Banishment* (60 min): Read 3.3–3.4; compare Romeo's despair with the Friar's plan.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Rewriting exponentials* (60 min): Rewrite 1.15^t as (1.15^(1/12))^(12t) to find monthly rate.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Sickle cell* (60 min): Explain how one base change causes sickle cell disease and malaria resistance.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Self-determination* (50 min): Read about the 1970 end of termination policy and the 1975 Indian Self-Determination Act and explain how Alcatraz activism contributed.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Advice* (50 min): Write 10 health tips for a doctor's office poster using formal commands (Lávese las manos).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Draw* (50 min): Draw the self-portrait from a mirror.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Survival skills* (50 min): Practice survival floating and the HELP position, and enter the water feet-first safely.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Prevention* (45 min): Learn prevention methods including abstinence, condoms, HPV vaccine, PrEP and PEP.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 119, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Parents and choice* (60 min): Read 3.5; analyze Lord Capulet's ultimatum and Juliet's isolation.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Half-life* (60 min): Model medicine leaving the body with a half-life function.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *CRISPR debate* (60 min): Research CRISPR and write a claim about its ethical use.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Contemporary sovereignty* (50 min): Research a California tribe today (e.g., land back, language revitalization) and write a profile.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Pharmacy* (50 min): Role-play buying medicine at a farmacia, describing symptoms and understanding the pharmacist's instructions.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Render* (50 min): Add value or color and the symbols.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Endurance swim* (50 min): Swim continuously for 15–20 minutes using any strokes.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Testing and services* (45 min): Locate confidential testing services for teens in California using the CDC GetTested locator.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Romeo and Juliet, Act 3: the turning point
- Objective: Analyze how a key scene shifts the plot
- Objective: Analyze character development under pressure
- Vocabulary: turning point, banishment, dramatic irony, climax
- Materials: Romeo and Juliet, Act 3
- Assessment: Act 3 quiz and turning-point paragraph.

### Algebra I: Exponential models and comparing growth
- Objective: Compare linear and exponential growth
- Objective: Model compound interest and population
- Vocabulary: compound interest, half-life, doubling time
- Materials: Desmos, calculator
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Mutations and genetic technology
- Objective: Explain mutation sources
- Objective: Evaluate genetic technologies
- Vocabulary: mutation, point mutation, frameshift, mutagen, CRISPR, genetic engineering
- Materials: mutation simulation worksheet
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Native activism and sovereignty
- Objective: Analyze Native activism in the 20th century
- Objective: Explain contemporary sovereignty issues
- Vocabulary: Indian termination, relocation, Alcatraz occupation, self-determination, sovereignty
- Materials: 1969 Alcatraz Proclamation, current tribal news
- Assessment: Unit 8 DBQ.

### Spanish II: En el consultorio: formal (usted) commands
- Objective: Give formal commands
- Objective: Understand health advice
- Vocabulary: usted, tome, descanse, beba, evite
- Materials: Spanish health pamphlets (CDC en español)
- Assessment: Commands quiz.

### Visual Art I: Self-portrait
- Objective: Create a self-portrait that expresses identity
- Vocabulary: self-portrait, symbolism, identity, expression
- Materials: mirror, drawing or painting materials
- Assessment: Self-portrait and symbolism statement.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Aquatic fitness and survival skills
- Objective: Use swimming as a fitness activity
- Objective: Perform survival floating
- Vocabulary: interval set, survival float, HELP position, water aerobics
- Materials: pool access
- Assessment: Final aquatics assessment.

### Health & Wellness: Sexually transmitted infections and HIV
- Objective: Describe STI transmission, symptoms, testing and treatment
- Objective: Explain HIV prevention including PrEP and PEP
- Vocabulary: STI, HIV, PrEP, PEP, asymptomatic
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: STI quiz.
