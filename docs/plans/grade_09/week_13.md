## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Extended metaphor* (60 min): Read Dickinson's "Hope is the thing with feathers"; map each line of the bird metaphor to a quality of hope.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Build a model* (60 min): Write a linear function for a phone plan, a gym membership, and a cooling cup of tea (table data).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Darwin's ideas* (60 min): Read about Darwin's finches and list the four factors of natural selection.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Economic sectors* (50 min): Classify 25 jobs (farmer, autoworker, nurse, software engineer) into primary–quaternary sectors and compare the U.S. and Ethiopia's workforce mix.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Food vocabulary* (50 min): Label 40 foods in your kitchen with Spanish sticky notes.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Feldman critique* (50 min): Learn describe–analyze–interpret–judge and critique a Frida Kahlo painting.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Finding the beat* (50 min): Clap and step to songs at different tempos; count 8-count phrases.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Forms of bullying* (45 min): Define physical, verbal, relational and cyberbullying and identify CA laws requiring schools to address bullying.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Frost's choices* (60 min): Read "The Road Not Taken" twice; debate the common misreading and support your interpretation with the final stanza.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Transformations* (60 min): Use Desmos sliders to describe how f(x)+k and kf(x) change the graph of f(x)=x.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Bean predation simulation* (60 min): Scatter colored beans on patterned fabric, act as predators for several rounds, and record survivors.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Measuring development* (50 min): Collect GDP per capita, HDI, literacy, and life expectancy for 8 countries from the World Factbook and rank them two ways.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Gustar-type verbs* (50 min): Practice gustar, encantar, interesar, molestar with indirect object pronouns.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Self-critique* (50 min): Critique your two paintings with the same method.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Basic steps* (50 min): Learn grapevine, step-touch, pivot turn, and kick-ball-change.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Social media audit* (45 min): Track your screen time for one day and evaluate how each app affects your mood.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 63, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Compressed meaning* (60 min): Read "Nothing Gold Can Stay"; annotate allusion and imagery and write a TP-CASTT analysis.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Inverse functions* (60 min): Find inverses of linear functions like the Celsius–Fahrenheit conversion and interpret them.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Data analysis* (60 min): Graph changes in bean color frequency across generations.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Gapminder trends* (50 min): Use Gapminder's bubble chart to trace China, India, and Nigeria from 1960 to today and describe what changed.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Grocery ads* (50 min): Read a Spanish grocery ad and create a shopping list with a budget.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Revise* (50 min): Revise one painting based on critique.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Line dance* (50 min): Learn a line dance such as the "Cupid Shuffle" or "Electric Slide."
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Communication skills* (45 min): Practice I-messages and active listening in three role-plays.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 64, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Connotation* (60 min): Sort 20 word pairs by connotation (e.g., "thrifty/stingy") and rewrite a stanza swapping connotations to change the tone.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Comparing models* (60 min): Compare two cell-plan functions and find when one becomes cheaper.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Real examples* (60 min): Analyze peppered moths and antibiotic resistance as natural selection.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Why uneven development?* (50 min): Read about colonial legacies, geography (landlocked states), and trade; write a paragraph linking one cause to a broader trend.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Survey* (50 min): Survey family about food preferences and report results.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Photograph* (50 min): Photograph and document work.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Cardio dance* (50 min): Complete a 30-minute dance-fitness video and track heart rate.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Conflict resolution* (45 min): Apply a six-step conflict-resolution model to a family disagreement scenario.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Figurative language and imagery
- Objective: Interpret metaphor, personification, and imagery
- Objective: Explain how word choice shapes tone
- Vocabulary: metaphor, extended metaphor, personification, imagery, tone, connotation
- Materials: Emily Dickinson, "Hope is the thing with feathers", Robert Frost, "The Road Not Taken" and "Nothing Gold Can Stay"
- Assessment: TP-CASTT chart and figurative-language paragraph.

### Algebra I: Linear models, transformations, and inverses
- Objective: Build linear models from contexts
- Objective: Describe transformations of f(x)=x
- Objective: Find inverses of linear functions
- Vocabulary: model, parameter, transformation, vertical shift, inverse function
- Materials: Desmos sliders
- Assessment: Modeling quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Natural selection
- Objective: Explain the four factors of natural selection
- Objective: Simulate selection
- Vocabulary: natural selection, variation, adaptation, fitness, overproduction, competition
- Materials: beans or pasta of different colors, fabric backgrounds
- Assessment: Simulation lab report and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Economic geography and development
- Objective: Classify economic activities by sector
- Objective: Compare development using GDP per capita and HDI
- Vocabulary: primary sector, secondary sector, tertiary sector, GDP per capita, Human Development Index, core and periphery
- Materials: Gapminder, CIA World Factbook
- Assessment: Development data analysis and quiz.

### Spanish II: La comida: food vocabulary and gustar-type verbs
- Objective: Name foods and express preferences
- Objective: Use gustar, encantar, and molestar
- Vocabulary: comida, encantar, molestar, fruta, verdura, picante
- Materials: grocery ads in Spanish
- Assessment: Quiz and survey report.

### Visual Art I: Unit 3 painting critique
- Objective: Critique paintings
- Objective: Revise work
- Vocabulary: critique, describe, analyze, interpret, judge
- Materials: finished paintings
- Assessment: Critique writing and revised painting.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Line dance and rhythm basics
- Objective: Move to a beat
- Objective: Perform a line dance
- Vocabulary: tempo, beat, count, line dance, grapevine
- Materials: music player, open space
- Assessment: Line dance performance.

### Health & Wellness: Bullying, social media and communication
- Objective: Identify forms of bullying and cyberbullying
- Objective: Use I-messages and conflict resolution
- Vocabulary: cyberbullying, bystander, upstander, I-message, conflict resolution
- Materials: phone, journal
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.
