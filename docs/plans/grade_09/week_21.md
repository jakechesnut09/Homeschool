## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Tennyson's "Ulysses"* (60 min): Read the dramatic monologue; compare the aging Ulysses's restlessness with Homer's homecoming.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Scatter plots* (60 min): Measure height and arm span of family members (or use a provided data set) and make a scatter plot.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Cell cycle* (60 min): Model the cell cycle and mitosis with pipe-cleaner chromosomes.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Oral history methods* (50 min): Learn interview ethics and write 12 open-ended questions.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Directions* (50 min): Learn direction phrases and trace routes on a map of Madrid.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Inspiration* (50 min): Study Pueblo pottery by Maria Martinez and Japanese Jomon pottery; sketch surface designs.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Drowning facts* (50 min): Watch an American Red Cross water-safety video and list the silent signs of drowning; then do a 30-minute cardio workout.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Key terms* (45 min): Learn medically accurate definitions of sex, gender identity, gender expression and sexual orientation.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Transformation* (60 min): Write a paragraph analyzing how Tennyson transforms Homer's hero and why.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Regression* (60 min): Use Desmos to find a linear regression and interpret slope and intercept.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Meiosis* (60 min): Model meiosis I and II and explain haploid gametes.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Conduct interview* (50 min): Interview an elder relative or community member for 30 minutes and record it with permission.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Por vs. para* (50 min): Learn por vs. para rules (duration, exchange, through vs. purpose, destination, deadline) and complete 20 sentences.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Build* (50 min): Build the vessel or sculpture using your chosen method.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Rip currents* (50 min): Learn to spot and escape a rip current (swim parallel to shore) using NOAA diagrams; draw an escape plan for a California beach.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Gender norms* (45 min): Analyze gender stereotypes in ads and media and how they affect relationships.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Essay plan* (60 min): Plan an essay: What qualities define Odysseus as an epic hero, and are they still admired today?
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Residuals and r* (60 min): Plot residuals and interpret the correlation coefficient.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Crossing over* (60 min): Demonstrate crossing over and independent assortment with colored pipe cleaners.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Transcribe* (50 min): Transcribe a 5-minute excerpt and highlight key memories.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Role-play* (50 min): Role-play a tourist asking for directions politely (Disculpe, ¿cómo llego a...?).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Surface* (50 min): Add texture or sgraffito designs at the leather-hard stage.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Reach-throw-don't go* (50 min): Practice reaching assists with a pole and throwing a rope or jug to a target 15 feet away, 10 times each.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Respect and inclusion* (45 min): Discuss how to treat all people with respect and identify school anti-discrimination protections.

- [ ] **English 9** — *Draft* (60 min): Draft the 800-word essay with evidence from at least four books of the Odyssey.

- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Mitosis vs. meiosis* (60 min): Complete a comparison table and explain differentiation.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Transforming source material: Tennyson's "Ulysses"; epic hero essay
- Objective: Analyze how a later author transforms Homer
- Objective: Write an informative essay on the epic hero
- Vocabulary: dramatic monologue, allusion, archetype, adaptation
- Materials: Alfred, Lord Tennyson, "Ulysses" (public domain)
- Assessment: Epic hero essay and Unit 5 test.

### Algebra I: Scatter plots, regression, and correlation
- Objective: Fit linear models to bivariate data
- Objective: Interpret correlation coefficients
- Objective: Distinguish correlation and causation
- Vocabulary: scatter plot, line of best fit, residual, correlation coefficient, causation
- Materials: Desmos regression tool, height/arm span data
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Mitosis, meiosis, and variation
- Objective: Model mitosis and meiosis
- Objective: Explain how meiosis creates variation
- Vocabulary: mitosis, meiosis, crossing over, independent assortment, haploid, diploid
- Materials: pipe cleaners or yarn, beads
- Assessment: Cell division quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Family history and oral history methods
- Objective: Conduct an oral history interview
- Objective: Connect a family story to larger historical trends
- Vocabulary: oral history, primary source, interview protocol, narrative
- Materials: recording device, interview question guide
- Assessment: Oral history transcript and reflection.

### Spanish II: En la ciudad: directions and por vs. para
- Objective: Ask for and give directions
- Objective: Distinguish por and para
- Vocabulary: doblar, seguir derecho, cuadra, esquina, por, para
- Materials: city map of Madrid or Mexico City
- Assessment: Por/para quiz and role-play.

### Visual Art I: Clay project: functional or sculptural vessel
- Objective: Design and build a ceramic piece inspired by a cultural tradition
- Vocabulary: vessel, surface design, sgraffito, Maria Martinez, Pueblo pottery
- Materials: air-dry clay, acrylic paint, sealant
- Assessment: Finished clay piece and statement.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Water safety on land
- Objective: Explain water safety rules and drowning signs
- Objective: Practice non-swimming rescues
- Vocabulary: drowning, rip current, life jacket, reach-throw-don't go, hypothermia
- Materials: life jacket, rope or garden hose, empty jug
- Assessment: Water safety quiz and throwing-assist demonstration.

### Health & Wellness: Gender identity, sexual orientation and respect
- Objective: Describe gender identity and sexual orientation accurately
- Objective: Analyze media influences on gender norms
- Vocabulary: gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, stereotype, inclusion
- Materials: media samples
- Assessment: Media analysis reflection.
