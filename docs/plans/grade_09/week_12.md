## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Draft the introduction and claim* (60 min): Write an introduction with context and a precise claim; draft one body paragraph with cited evidence.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Slope-intercept form* (60 min): Graph 12 equations y=mx+b and interpret m and b in two contexts.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Chicken wing dissection* (60 min): Dissect a chicken wing and compare its bones with a human arm.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Cooperation* (50 min): Research the UN and EU and their functions.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Interpretive* (50 min): Read a short folktale (e.g., La Llorona) and answer questions.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Fauvism* (50 min): Analyze Matisse's "Woman with a Hat" and list arbitrary colors.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Officiating* (50 min): Learn to officiate and call lines fairly.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Warning signs* (45 min): Learn the warning signs and risk factors of suicide from a credible prevention source.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Counterclaim paragraph* (60 min): Draft a paragraph that fairly states the opposing view and rebuts it with evidence and reasoning.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Point-slope form* (60 min): Write equations through given points and slopes; convert to slope-intercept form.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Homologous vs. analogous* (60 min): Sort 12 structures and explain why homologous structures indicate common ancestry.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Primary source* (50 min): Research the UN and EU: list members, main bodies, and two things each does, and compare them in a chart.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Interpersonal* (50 min): Ask and answer questions about a memorable event.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Expressionism* (50 min): Analyze Munch's "The Scream" and Kirchner's street scenes.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Tournament day 1* (50 min): Play badminton games against family/friends.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *ACT model* (45 min): Practice the Acknowledge–Care–Tell steps through role-play scenarios with a parent.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Grammar: parallel structure* (60 min): Fix 10 faulty parallel sentences, then revise your essay to include two deliberate parallel structures.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Standard form* (60 min): Graph using intercepts for equations like 3x+4y=24 (budget for two items).
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Molecular evidence* (60 min): Compare amino acid sequences of cytochrome c across species and rank relatedness.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Case study* (50 min): Read the UN Charter preamble (1945) and identify its four goals; explain how World War II shaped its wording.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Presentational* (50 min): Write a 200-word narrative about a memorable trip or event.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Expressive painting* (50 min): Paint a familiar place in arbitrary colors to express a mood.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Tournament day 2* (50 min): Play pickleball games.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Resources* (45 min): Save 988 and Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741) in your phone and list three trusted adults.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Final draft* (60 min): Finish, revise, and edit the 800–1,000-word argument essay; check citations.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Parallel and perpendicular* (60 min): Write equations of lines parallel or perpendicular to a given line.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Cladograms* (60 min): Build a cladogram for 6 animals from a table of shared derived traits and explain what each branch point means.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Debate* (50 min): Read a summary of the 1959 Antarctic Treaty and explain how countries agreed to share a continent without borders.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Review* (50 min): Review vocabulary and tenses with a self-made study guide and a Conjuguemos mixed-tense drill.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Finish* (50 min): Finish the painting and title it.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Teach a skill* (50 min): Teach a younger sibling or friend a basic stroke.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Self-care plan* (45 min): Complete a personal safety and support plan with coping strategies and people to contact.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Writing and debating an argument essay
- Objective: Write an argument with counterclaim and rebuttal
- Objective: Present a claim orally with evidence
- Vocabulary: counterargument, rebuttal, call to action, parallel structure
- Materials: research log, two credible sources
- Assessment: Argument essay (W.9-10.1 rubric), debate observation checklist, Unit 3 test.

### Algebra I: Writing and graphing linear equations
- Objective: Write equations in slope-intercept, point-slope, and standard form
- Objective: Graph linear functions
- Vocabulary: slope-intercept form, point-slope form, standard form, y-intercept, x-intercept
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Linear equation quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Evidence for evolution: anatomy, embryology, and molecules
- Objective: Compare homologous and analogous structures
- Objective: Use DNA and protein comparisons
- Vocabulary: homologous structure, analogous structure, vestigial structure, embryology, cladogram
- Materials: chicken wing (for dissection), gloves
- Assessment: Evidence brochure (HS-LS4-1).

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Supranational organizations and cooperation
- Objective: Explain international cooperation
- Objective: Evaluate the role of the UN and EU
- Vocabulary: supranational, United Nations, European Union, alliance
- Materials: UN website
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Spanish II: Unit 3 assessment: telling a story
- Objective: Narrate a past event
- Objective: Interpret a story
- Vocabulary: narración
- Materials: IPA tasks
- Assessment: IPA and unit test.

### Visual Art I: Color and expression
- Objective: Use expressive color
- Objective: Analyze Fauvism and Expressionism
- Vocabulary: Fauvism, Expressionism, arbitrary color, emotion
- Materials: paints, paper
- Assessment: Expressive painting and statement.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Racket sports tournament
- Objective: Compete with strategy and sportsmanship
- Objective: Lead a game
- Vocabulary: tournament, bracket, officiating, etiquette
- Materials: rackets/paddles, bracket sheet
- Assessment: Tournament play and reflection.

### Health & Wellness: Suicide prevention and help-seeking
- Objective: Recognize warning signs of suicide
- Objective: Practice how to get help for self and others
- Vocabulary: warning sign, crisis line, 988, trusted adult
- Materials: phone
- Assessment: Scenario response check and completed support plan.
