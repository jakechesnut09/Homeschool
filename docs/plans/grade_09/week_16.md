## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Greek world* (60 min): Read a background article on Homer, oral tradition, and the Trojan War; map Odysseus's journey on a Mediterranean map.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Elimination by adding* (60 min): Solve 10 systems where adding eliminates a variable.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Defining species* (60 min): Discuss the biological species concept and its limits.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Urbanization trends* (50 min): Graph the percent of world population living in cities from 1950 to 2050 (UN projections) and identify the fastest-urbanizing regions.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Regional dishes* (50 min): Research dishes like paella, arepas, ceviche, and mole.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Carving safety* (50 min): Learn tool safety (carve away from hands).
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Dance fitness* (50 min): Complete a 30-minute Zumba-style workout and compare heart rate to running.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Alcohol and the body* (45 min): Trace alcohol's path through the body and describe effects on each organ system.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Introduction to the epic: The Odyssey, Books 1 and 5
- Objective: Identify epic conventions
- Objective: Analyze how Homer introduces Odysseus indirectly
- Vocabulary: epic, epic hero, invocation, in medias res, epithet, xenia
- Materials: Homer, The Odyssey (Samuel Butler translation, public domain, or library Fagles/Fitzgerald), map of the Mediterranean
- Assessment: Epic conventions quiz; reader's notebook entry.

### Algebra I: Solving systems by elimination
- Objective: Solve systems by elimination
- Objective: Justify why elimination works
- Vocabulary: elimination, linear combination, equivalent system
- Materials: algebra tiles (optional)
- Assessment: Elimination quiz with written justification.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Speciation
- Objective: Explain how new species form
- Objective: Analyze reproductive isolation
- Vocabulary: speciation, reproductive isolation, geographic isolation, adaptive radiation
- Materials: maps of Ensatina salamander range
- Assessment: Speciation quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Urbanization and urban challenges
- Objective: Explain global urbanization trends
- Objective: Analyze urban problems and planning responses
- Vocabulary: urbanization, megacity, urban sprawl, informal settlement, gentrification, transit-oriented development
- Materials: Census QuickFacts, city maps
- Assessment: Urban planning page and quiz.

### Spanish II: Regional cuisines of the Spanish-speaking world
- Objective: Compare regional dishes
- Objective: Present on a dish
- Vocabulary: plato típico, ingrediente, región
- Materials: map of Latin America and Spain
- Assessment: Dish presentation.

### Visual Art I: Relief printmaking: carving and printing
- Objective: Carve and print an edition
- Vocabulary: carve, proof, edition, registration
- Materials: carving tools or pencil for foam, block printing ink, brayer
- Assessment: Print edition.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Dance fitness and performance
- Objective: Perform a polished dance
- Objective: Evaluate dance as fitness
- Vocabulary: performance, stamina, polish
- Materials: music
- Assessment: Final dance performance.

### Health & Wellness: Alcohol
- Objective: Explain how alcohol affects the body
- Objective: Analyze alcohol's role in injuries and violence
- Vocabulary: blood alcohol concentration, binge drinking, alcohol poisoning, zero tolerance
- Materials: computer
- Assessment: Decision-making scenario and quiz.
