## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Return to Ithaca* (60 min): Read Book 13; explain why Athena disguises Odysseus and what testing others will show.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Collect data* (60 min): Measure reaction time 20 times (ruler-drop test) for yourself and family members.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *DNA extraction* (60 min): Extract DNA from strawberries with soap, salt, and cold alcohol; observe the strands.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Origins of Ethnic Studies* (50 min): Read about the 1968 Third World Liberation Front student strikes at San Francisco State that created the first Ethnic Studies college.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Travel vocabulary* (50 min): Learn airport and hotel vocabulary with picture labels and a matching drill.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Sculpture survey* (50 min): Compare Michelangelo's "David", Barbara Hepworth's pierced forms, and Ruth Asawa's wire sculptures; note how each uses space.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Yoga basics* (50 min): Learn 10 foundational poses and breathing.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Unit introduction* (45 min): Parent reviews the CA Healthy Youth Act notice and opt-out option (Ed. Code 51938); student discusses family values and expectations for the unit.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 92, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Eumaeus* (60 min): Read Book 14; analyze the swineherd as an example of loyalty and xenia.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Plots* (60 min): Make a dot plot, histogram, and box plot of the data.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *DNA structure* (60 min): Build a paper DNA model showing base pairing and antiparallel strands.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Identity chart* (50 min): Create an identity chart (family, language, place, faith, interests) and write about which parts others see and which they don't.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Ir a + infinitive* (50 min): Write 10 sentences about plans for next weekend using ir a.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Paper techniques* (50 min): Practice scoring, folding, curling, tabbing, and slotting cardstock in a technique sampler.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Sun salutation* (50 min): Learn and repeat Sun Salutation A.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Healthy vs. unhealthy* (45 min): Sort relationship behaviors on a continuum from healthy to abusive and identify red flags.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 93, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Telemachus grows* (60 min): Read Books 15–16; chart how Telemachus has changed since Book 1.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Center* (60 min): Compute mean and median and explain which is more appropriate for skewed data.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *History* (60 min): Read about Rosalind Franklin, Watson, and Crick and Photo 51.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Race vs. ethnicity* (50 min): Read definitions and analyze how U.S. Census race categories changed from 1790 to 2020.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Simple future* (50 min): Learn regular and irregular future forms (tendré, saldré, haré) and complete a drill.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Abstract sculpture* (50 min): Build a freestanding abstract paper sculpture at least 10 inches tall that is interesting from every side.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Flexibility* (50 min): Complete a 30-minute flexibility routine and measure sit-and-reach.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Consent* (45 min): Learn what affirmative consent means under California law and analyze scenarios for whether consent was given.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: The Odyssey: homecoming (Books 13–16)
- Objective: Analyze recognition scenes and disguise
- Objective: Trace Telemachus's growth
- Vocabulary: disguise, recognition, coming of age, loyalty
- Materials: The Odyssey, Books 13–16
- Assessment: Reading quiz and scene analysis.

### Algebra I: Representing and summarizing one-variable data
- Objective: Create dot plots, histograms, and box plots
- Objective: Calculate measures of center and spread
- Vocabulary: mean, median, IQR, standard deviation, histogram, box plot
- Materials: stopwatch, spreadsheet or Desmos
- Assessment: Data display quiz.

### Biology – The Living Earth: DNA structure and replication
- Objective: Describe DNA structure
- Objective: Model DNA replication
- Vocabulary: DNA, nucleotide, double helix, base pairing, replication, chromosome
- Materials: strawberries, dish soap, salt, rubbing alcohol (cold), coffee filter
- Assessment: DNA extraction lab write-up and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): What is Ethnic Studies? Identity and community
- Objective: Define ethnic studies, race, ethnicity, and identity
- Objective: Reflect on personal and family identity
- Vocabulary: ethnic studies, race, ethnicity, identity, culture, social construct
- Materials: CDE Ethnic Studies Model Curriculum overview, journal
- Assessment: "Where I'm From" poem and vocabulary check.

### Spanish II: ¿Adónde vamos? Travel vocabulary and the future with ir a
- Objective: Talk about travel plans
- Objective: Use ir a + infinitive and the simple future
- Vocabulary: viajar, aeropuerto, pasaporte, maleta, boleto, haré
- Materials: travel brochures or websites
- Assessment: Future tense quiz.

### Visual Art I: Form and space: paper sculpture
- Objective: Create 3D form from 2D material
- Objective: Explore positive and negative space in sculpture
- Vocabulary: form, relief, freestanding, in the round, positive space, negative space
- Materials: cardstock, scissors, glue, craft knife, cutting mat
- Assessment: Paper sculpture and photo documentation.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Yoga and flexibility
- Objective: Perform yoga poses
- Objective: Explain flexibility benefits
- Vocabulary: flexibility, range of motion, yoga, breath, sun salutation
- Materials: yoga mat
- Assessment: Yoga flow leadership.

### Health & Wellness: Healthy relationships, consent and boundaries
- Objective: Identify characteristics of healthy and unhealthy relationships
- Objective: Practice asking for and giving consent
- Vocabulary: consent, boundary, affirmative consent, red flag
- Materials: opt-out notice to parents, journal
- Assessment: Relationship analysis and quiz.
