## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Dystopian satire* (60 min): Read "Harrison Bergeron"; list each handicap and explain what idea about equality Vonnegut is exaggerating.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Absolute value as distance* (60 min): Solve |x−3|=5 by reasoning about distance on a number line; then solve 12 equations algebraically.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Photosynthesis model* (60 min): Draw an input–output model of photosynthesis showing light energy stored in glucose bonds.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Distribution* (50 min): Map world population density and explain clusters.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Description* (50 min): Describe your childhood home and neighborhood using era, tenía, había.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Two-point boxes* (50 min): Draw boxes in two-point perspective at different heights.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Standing long jump* (50 min): Learn arm swing and two-foot takeoff; measure 5 jumps.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Marketing techniques* (45 min): Analyze three food ads or cereal boxes for techniques (celebrity, health halo, cartoon characters) and identify the target audience.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Satire discussion* (60 min): Hold a recorded 15-minute discussion with a parent on whether the story mocks equality or forced sameness, citing three lines.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Absolute value inequalities* (60 min): Solve and graph |2x+1|<7 and |x−4|≥3 type inequalities.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Floating leaf disk lab* (60 min): Punch spinach disks, infiltrate with baking-soda solution, and time disks floating under light.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Population pyramids* (50 min): Compare pyramids for Japan, Nigeria, and the U.S.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *People* (50 min): Write a 6-sentence description of a childhood friend or teacher (appearance, personality, what you used to do together).
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Building* (50 min): Draw your house or a local building in two-point.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Running long jump* (50 min): Practice a short approach and landing on grass or sand.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Supplements and energy drinks* (45 min): Research caffeine content and FDA regulation of supplements and energy drinks and write a consumer warning paragraph.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Chopin's hour* (60 min): Read "The Story of an Hour" twice; annotate imagery of the open window and the final line's irony.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Tolerance problems* (60 min): Write absolute value inequalities for manufacturing tolerances and thermostat ranges.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Light intensity variable* (60 min): Repeat the leaf disk lab at two lamp distances and graph results.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Demographic transition* (50 min): Learn the demographic transition model and place countries.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Listening* (50 min): Listen to a story told in the imperfect and answer questions.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *Worm's/bird's-eye* (50 min): Draw a box from a bird's-eye and worm's-eye view.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Throwing* (50 min): Practice overhand throwing with step and follow-through; measure distances.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Clean, separate, cook, chill* (45 min): Learn the four food safety steps and use a food thermometer to check safe internal temperatures while helping cook dinner.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 34, ~425 min)

- [ ] **English 9** — *Essay prompt and thesis* (60 min): Choose a story from Units 1–2 and draft three possible thesis statements about theme; select the strongest and outline body paragraphs.
- [ ] **Algebra I** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a mixed review of equations, formulas, and inequalities.
- [ ] **Biology – The Living Earth** — *Chloroplast structure* (60 min): Label the chloroplast and trace light reactions and the Calvin cycle in a flowchart.
- [ ] **World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2)** — *Gapminder* (50 min): Use Gapminder to compare life expectancy and income over time.
- [ ] **Spanish II** — *Conversation* (50 min): Compare childhoods with a partner in a guided conversation.
- [ ] **Visual Art I** — *City block* (50 min): Draw an imaginary city block in two-point perspective.
- [ ] **PE 9 (Course 1)** — *Biomechanics* (50 min): Experiment with release angles and record which gives the longest throw.
- [ ] **Health & Wellness** — *Kitchen audit* (45 min): Audit your home kitchen for food safety (fridge temperature, cutting boards, leftovers dates) and record fixes.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 9: Satire and voice: "Harrison Bergeron" and "The Story of an Hour"
- Objective: Analyze satire and its target
- Objective: Analyze irony in a short, dense text
- Vocabulary: satire, dystopia, exaggeration, situational irony, symbol
- Materials: "Harrison Bergeron" by Kurt Vonnegut (library/anthology), "The Story of an Hour" by Kate Chopin (public domain)
- Assessment: Outline/evidence chart reviewed for arguable thesis and relevant evidence; reading check.

### Algebra I: Absolute value equations and inequalities; unit review
- Objective: Solve absolute value equations and inequalities
- Objective: Synthesize Unit 2
- Vocabulary: absolute value, distance, tolerance
- Materials: number lines
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Biology – The Living Earth: Photosynthesis
- Objective: Model how light energy is stored in sugar
- Objective: Investigate factors affecting photosynthesis
- Vocabulary: photosynthesis, chloroplast, chlorophyll, glucose, light reactions, Calvin cycle
- Materials: spinach leaves, baking soda, dish soap, syringe, cups, lamp
- Assessment: Leaf disk lab report and quiz.

### World Geography (S1) & Ethnic Studies (S2): Population distribution and growth
- Objective: Analyze population density and distribution
- Objective: Interpret population pyramids
- Vocabulary: population density, demography, birth rate, death rate, population pyramid
- Materials: Census data, Gapminder
- Assessment: Population quiz.

### Spanish II: Describing people and places in the past
- Objective: Use imperfect for description
- Objective: Describe childhood people and places
- Vocabulary: vecindario, era, tenía, había, describir
- Materials: drawing paper
- Assessment: Descriptive writing quiz.

### Visual Art I: Two-point perspective
- Objective: Draw with two-point perspective
- Vocabulary: two-point perspective, vertical lines, worm's-eye view, bird's-eye view
- Materials: ruler, long paper
- Assessment: Two-point city drawing.

### PE 9 (Course 1): Jumping and throwing events
- Objective: Perform standing long jump and softball/frisbee throw technique
- Vocabulary: takeoff, landing, approach, release angle, follow-through
- Materials: measuring tape, soft ball or frisbee, sand or grass area
- Assessment: Mini-meet results and release-angle data.

### Health & Wellness: Food marketing, supplements and food safety
- Objective: Analyze food marketing techniques
- Objective: Apply the four steps to food safety
- Vocabulary: marketing, dietary supplement, foodborne illness, cross-contamination
- Materials: food thermometer, cereal boxes or online ads
- Assessment: Counter-ad and food safety quiz.
