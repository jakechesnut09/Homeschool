## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Storyboarding* (90 min): Storyboard 8 slides: title, 5 content slides, conclusion and sources.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Fraction add/subtract* (60 min): Solve 12 mixed number addition and subtraction problems and 2 word problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Choosing a question* (45 min): Choose a testable question connected to this year's science (e.g., Does temperature affect how fast compost decomposes?).
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Trails map* (45 min): Map the Oregon, California, Mormon and Santa Fe Trails and label rivers, mountains and landmarks like Chimney Rock.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Selecting repertoire* (30 min): Choose 4 pieces (a recorder song, a patriotic or folk song, a round and your composition) and explain each choice.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Aerobic test* (30 min): Complete the PACER or mile run post-test.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Final product* (20 min): Create the final poster, brochure or video.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Writing slide text* (90 min): Write concise slide text and a full speaker script for each slide.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Fraction multiply/divide* (60 min): Solve 12 fraction multiplication and unit-fraction division problems and explain 1 with a model.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Planning* (45 min): Write a hypothesis, list variables and controls and write a step-by-step procedure.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Packing the wagon* (45 min): Tape a 4-by-10-foot wagon outline on the floor and choose supplies that fit, explaining each choice.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Rehearsal 1* (30 min): Rehearse each piece, recording and noting problem spots.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Strength tests* (30 min): Complete curl-up and push-up post-tests.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Call to action* (20 min): Add a clear call to action and a source list.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 173, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Adding media* (90 min): Add a map, captioned photos and one audio or video element that supports a main idea, crediting sources.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Volume and geometry* (60 min): Find volumes of 6 prisms and composite figures and classify 6 quadrilaterals.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Running trials* (45 min): Conduct the investigation with at least 3 trials and record data.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Pioneer diaries* (45 min): Read excerpts of pioneer women's diaries and list hardships caused by terrain, rivers and climate.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Rehearsal 2* (30 min): Fix problem spots with slow practice and rehearse transitions.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Flexibility tests* (30 min): Complete trunk lift and sit-and-reach post-tests.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Rehearse* (20 min): Rehearse a 3-minute presentation of the campaign.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 174, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Editing* (90 min): Edit script and slides for spelling and conventions, and practice timing to 5 minutes.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Graphing* (60 min): Graph and interpret a pattern table and a real-world data set on a coordinate plane.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *More trials* (45 min): Finish trials and organize data in a table.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Arrival in California* (45 min): Read about the Donner Party crossing the Sierra Nevada and life at the end of the California Trail.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Concert program* (30 min): Design a printed concert program with piece titles, composers and short notes.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Comparing results* (30 min): Graph baseline vs. post-test results and identify improvements.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Goal check* (20 min): Review your nutrition, activity and drug-free goals from the year and record progress.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Building a multimedia presentation
- Objective: Write informative slide text and a script
- Objective: Integrate images, maps or audio to enhance main ideas
- Vocabulary: multimedia, slide, script, caption
- Materials: computer with free slideshow tool, images with credits
- Assessment: Storyboard, script and slide deck checklist.

### Math 5: Cumulative review: fractions, volume and geometry
- Objective: Perform all fraction operations accurately
- Objective: Apply volume and coordinate graphing skills
- Vocabulary: common denominator, scaling, volume, ordered pair
- Materials: fraction strips, graph paper, review packet
- Assessment: End-of-year cumulative assessment.

### Science 5: Capstone investigation: planning and testing
- Objective: Ask a testable question
- Objective: Plan and conduct a fair investigation
- Vocabulary: hypothesis, independent variable, dependent variable, control
- Materials: materials chosen for the investigation, science notebook
- Assessment: Investigation plan and data table.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: The overland trails
- Objective: Describe settlers' experiences on the Oregon, California and Santa Fe Trails
- Objective: Explain how geography affected the journey
- Vocabulary: Oregon Trail, California Trail, Santa Fe Trail, pioneer, Donner Party, prairie schooner
- Materials: overland trail maps, pioneer diary excerpts, masking tape to outline a wagon (4 x 10 ft)
- Assessment: Wagon-packing justification and Unit 9 test.

### Art & Music 5: Concert preparation
- Objective: Select and rehearse concert repertoire
- Objective: Apply feedback to improve performance
- Vocabulary: repertoire, rehearsal, program, stage presence, etiquette
- Materials: recorder, song sheets, composition
- Assessment: Dress rehearsal rubric.

### PE 5: FITNESSGRAM post-assessment
- Objective: Complete post-assessment fitness tests
- Objective: Compare results to baseline
- Vocabulary: post-test, improvement, Healthy Fitness Zone
- Materials: FITNESSGRAM HFZ chart, score sheet, PACER audio
- Assessment: FITNESSGRAM post-test and comparison graph.

### Health 5: Creating the campaign
- Objective: Produce campaign materials
- Objective: Practice presenting
- Vocabulary: persuasion, accuracy, call to action
- Materials: art supplies, camera
- Assessment: Rehearsal feedback.
