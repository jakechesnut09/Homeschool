## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Choosing and questioning* (90 min): Choose a CA national park (Yosemite, Joshua Tree, Redwood, Death Valley) and write 5 inquiry questions.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Multiplication fluency* (60 min): Complete a timed set of 10 multi-digit multiplication problems and track accuracy.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Night observation* (45 min): Go outside after dark with a star chart and sketch 2 constellations and their positions.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Tecumseh* (45 min): Read about Tecumseh's confederacy and an excerpt of his speech urging unity, and explain his goals.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Mounting* (30 min): Mount or mat each 2D artwork neatly.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Hydration* (30 min): Learn signs of dehydration and track water intake around a 30-minute workout.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Choose a topic* (20 min): Pick a topic (drug-free, vaping myths, healthy drinks, daily activity) and state a clear health-enhancing message.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 167, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Searching efficiently* (90 min): Practice keyword searching and evaluating credibility of 4 websites, then find answers on nps.gov.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Division review* (60 min): Solve 8 division problems with two-digit divisors and interpret remainders in 3 word problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Seasonal sky model* (45 min): Walk around a lamp (sun) with constellation pictures on the walls to model why we see different stars each season.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Sequoyah* (45 min): Examine the Cherokee syllabary and explain how Sequoyah's invention and the Cherokee Phoenix newspaper strengthened the nation.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Labels* (30 min): Write a museum label for each work (title, medium, date, short description).
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Fuel for activity* (30 min): Plan a pre-activity snack and post-activity snack and explain the choices.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Research* (20 min): Gather 3 facts from valid sources to support the message.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 168, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Note-taking* (90 min): Take paraphrased notes from 3 sources organized by question.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Decimal operations* (60 min): Complete a 20-problem decimal operation scavenger hunt with task cards around the house.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Data table* (45 min): Use a planetarium app to record which month Orion, Scorpius and the Big Dipper are visible at 9 p.m. and organize the data.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Marshall vs. Jackson* (45 min): Learn about Worcester v. Georgia (John Marshall) and Andrew Jackson's refusal to enforce it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Installation* (30 min): Install the exhibition on a wall or tables with even spacing and good lighting.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Recovery* (30 min): Practice a full cool-down and foam-roll or stretching routine.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Audience* (20 min): Identify the audience (younger siblings, family, community group) and plan how to reach them.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 169, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Synthesis* (90 min): Write a synthesis paragraph combining information from at least 2 sources on one question.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Expressions and conversions* (60 min): Evaluate 8 expressions with grouping symbols and solve 5 measurement conversion problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Second observation* (45 min): Observe the sky again 2 hours later and record how constellations moved, explaining rotation.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Trail of Tears* (45 min): Read John Ross's 1836 letter to Congress and a map of removal routes; discuss the human cost.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Gallery talk prep* (30 min): Prepare a 2-minute gallery talk about the exhibition's theme.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Practice test* (30 min): Do a practice round of the PACER and curl-ups to check progress.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Draft materials* (20 min): Draft a poster, brochure or short video script.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 170, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Conference on notes and source list; complete a source credibility checklist.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a cumulative quiz on number and operations in base ten.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Inquiry: researching a California national park or natural wonder
- Objective: Locate information efficiently in multiple sources
- Objective: Take notes and list sources
- Vocabulary: inquiry, database, keyword, credible
- Materials: National Park Service website (nps.gov), library books, note-taking template
- Assessment: Notes and source list; synthesis paragraph.

### Math 5: Cumulative review: whole numbers, decimals and expressions
- Objective: Maintain fluency with multi-digit multiplication and division
- Objective: Solve multi-step decimal problems
- Vocabulary: algorithm, estimate, reasonableness
- Materials: review task cards, timer
- Assessment: Cumulative quiz on NBT, OA and conversions.

### Science 5: Seasonal star patterns
- Objective: Observe and record constellations
- Objective: Explain why some stars are seen only in certain seasons
- Vocabulary: constellation, orbit, revolution, Orion, Big Dipper
- Materials: star chart or free planetarium app, red flashlight, lamp, constellation pictures
- Assessment: Star observation sketches and Unit 10 test.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Native nations and removal
- Objective: Explain leaders and resistance of Native nations
- Objective: Describe the Indian Removal Act and the Trail of Tears
- Vocabulary: Tecumseh, Sequoyah, syllabary, Indian Removal Act, Trail of Tears, Worcester v. Georgia
- Materials: Sequoyah's Cherokee syllabary chart, excerpt of John Ross's 1836 letter to Congress, Tecumseh speech excerpt
- Assessment: Primary-source paragraph.

### Art & Music 5: Preparing the exhibition
- Objective: Prepare and present artwork safely and effectively
- Objective: Write museum labels
- Vocabulary: mat, museum label, installation, lighting
- Materials: mats or construction paper, painter's tape, label cards
- Assessment: Installed exhibition and labels.

### PE 5: Hydration, nutrition and recovery
- Objective: Explain how hydration and nutrition support activity
- Objective: Practice recovery strategies
- Vocabulary: hydration, dehydration, fuel, recovery, rest
- Materials: water bottle, food labels, mat
- Assessment: Hydration log and written reflection.

### Health 5: Health advocacy campaign planning
- Objective: Choose a health message
- Objective: Plan an advocacy project
- Vocabulary: advocacy, audience, message, campaign
- Materials: poster paper, computer
- Assessment: Draft and checklist.
