## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 16–18* (90 min): Read chapters 16–18 and track Colin's attitude in a 'before and after' chart.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Building the plane* (60 min): Tape a floor grid with x- and y-axes and walk to ordered pairs called out, noting that x comes first.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Drop tests* (45 min): Drop balls of different masses from the same height and record which lands first.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Philadelphia 1787* (45 min): Read about the delegates and James Madison's role and list who was not represented.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Orchestra families* (30 min): Learn the four instrument families and sort 16 instrument pictures by family.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Ground balls* (30 min): Field 20 rolled ground balls with 'alligator hands' and get in front of the ball.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Rx vs. OTC* (20 min): Compare a prescription label and an OTC Drug Facts label and identify each part.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 142, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 19–20* (90 min): Read chapters 19–20 and explain what the children mean by 'Magic.'
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Plotting points* (60 min): Plot 20 points on graph paper and name the coordinates of 10 given points.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Plumb line* (45 min): Make a plumb line and test whether it always points the same direction in different places.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Great Compromise* (45 min): Compare the Virginia and New Jersey Plans and explain the Great Compromise with a diagram.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Young Person's Guide* (30 min): Listen to Britten's 'Young Person's Guide to the Orchestra' and list instruments as they are featured.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Fly balls* (30 min): Catch 20 tossed fly balls using two hands above the head.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Dosage math* (20 min): Use a Drug Facts chart to find the correct dose by age and weight in 3 practice problems.

- [ ] **Math 5** — *Coordinate pictures* (60 min): Plot and connect a list of ordered pairs to reveal a mystery picture, then design your own for someone else.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Round Earth* (45 min): Place figurines around a globe and draw arrows showing 'down' for people in California, Australia and Antarctica.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Slavery and the Constitution* (45 min): Read about the Three-Fifths Compromise and the slave trade clause and discuss why they were controversial.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Timbre* (30 min): Play the Classics for Kids instrument game and describe timbres with adjectives (bright, mellow, brassy).
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Throwing to bases* (30 min): Field and throw to a base target 30 ft away, recording accuracy.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Misuse* (20 min): Discuss why sharing prescriptions or taking extra doses is dangerous.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: The Secret Garden chapters 16–22: Colin's change
- Objective: Trace the theme of healing
- Objective: Analyze illustrations and film elements
- Vocabulary: Magic, healing, illustration, adaptation
- Materials: The Secret Garden, an illustrated edition or the 1949 or 1993 film adaptation
- Assessment: Theme paragraph; visual analysis notes.

### Math 5: The coordinate plane
- Objective: Identify axes, origin and ordered pairs
- Objective: Plot and name points in the first quadrant
- Vocabulary: coordinate plane, x-axis, y-axis, origin, ordered pair, quadrant
- Materials: graph paper, masking tape for floor grid
- Assessment: Quiz on coordinate plane vocabulary and plotting.

### Science 5: Gravity pulls down toward Earth's center
- Objective: Gather evidence that gravity acts downward everywhere on Earth
- Objective: Model gravity on a round Earth
- Vocabulary: gravity, force, mass, center, plumb line
- Materials: string, washer (plumb line), balls of different masses, globe, small figurines
- Assessment: Written argument about gravity's direction.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: The Constitutional Convention
- Objective: Describe the compromises of the Convention
- Objective: Analyze the Preamble
- Vocabulary: delegate, Great Compromise, Three-Fifths Compromise, Preamble, ratify
- Materials: Constitution transcript (National Archives), Preamble text
- Assessment: Preamble recitation and quiz.

### Art & Music 5: Instruments of the orchestra
- Objective: Identify orchestra families by sight and sound
- Objective: Describe timbre
- Vocabulary: strings, woodwinds, brass, percussion, timbre, conductor
- Materials: Britten's 'Young Person's Guide to the Orchestra' recording, Classics for Kids orchestra game
- Assessment: Instrument listening quiz.

### PE 5: Fielding and throwing
- Objective: Field ground balls and fly balls
- Objective: Throw to a base accurately
- Vocabulary: ground ball, fly ball, alligator hands, cutoff, base
- Materials: glove or bare hands with soft ball, bases
- Assessment: Fielding test score.

### Health 5: Using medicines safely
- Objective: Distinguish prescription and OTC medicines
- Objective: Explain medicine misuse
- Vocabulary: prescription, over-the-counter, dosage, side effect, misuse
- Materials: empty OTC medicine box, drug facts label
- Assessment: Scenario quiz.
