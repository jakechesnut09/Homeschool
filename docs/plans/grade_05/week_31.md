## Day 1 — Monday (school day 151, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *The Olympians* (90 min): Read the D'Aulaires' chapters on Zeus and the Olympians and make a family tree with each god's domain.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Two rules* (60 min): Generate patterns 'add 3' and 'add 6' starting at 0 in a table and describe how corresponding terms compare.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Toilet paper solar system* (45 min): Build a toilet-paper scale model of planet distances from the sun along a hallway.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Ratification debate* (45 min): Role-play a debate between a Federalist and an Anti-Federalist about ratifying the Constitution.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Motifs* (30 min): Study how Beethoven's Fifth Symphony grows from a 4-note motif and invent 3 motifs of your own.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Hit and run* (30 min): Play 'hit the bat and run' where batters run bases while fielders return the ball.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Influence web* (20 min): Make a web of influences (family, friends, media, culture, community) on decisions about drugs.

- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Bill of Rights* (45 min): Paraphrase the first 10 amendments and create an illustrated Bill of Rights booklet.

- [ ] **Health 5** — *Peer pressure types* (20 min): Identify spoken, unspoken and online peer pressure in 6 scenarios.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Greek myths: gods, heroes and lessons
- Objective: Determine the lesson of a myth
- Objective: Use Greek roots and myth-based words
- Vocabulary: myth, Olympian, hubris, allusion, Achilles' heel
- Materials: D'Aulaires' Book of Greek Myths (library), A Wonder-Book by Nathaniel Hawthorne (Project Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Myth lesson statements; allusion vocabulary quiz; oral summary.

### Math 5: Numerical patterns and ordered pairs
- Objective: Generate two patterns from rules
- Objective: Graph ordered pairs from corresponding terms and describe relationships
- Vocabulary: pattern, rule, corresponding terms, relationship
- Materials: graph paper, pattern tables
- Assessment: Unit 8 test covering 5.G.A.1–A.2 and 5.OA.B.3.

### Science 5: Scale of the solar system and stars
- Objective: Build a scale model of distances
- Objective: Argue about brightness and distance with data
- Vocabulary: scale model, astronomical unit, orbit, solar system
- Materials: toilet paper roll or measuring tape, markers, balls of different sizes
- Assessment: Unit 9 test; scale model reflection.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Ratification, the Bill of Rights and the American creed
- Objective: Explain the ratification debate and Bill of Rights
- Objective: Discuss the American creed and patriotic songs
- Vocabulary: Federalist, Anti-Federalist, amendment, Bill of Rights, rule of law
- Materials: Bill of Rights transcript, lyrics of 'The Star-Spangled Banner' and 'America the Beautiful'
- Assessment: Bill of Rights booklet and Unit 8 test.

### Art & Music 5: Composing a melody with Chrome Music Lab
- Objective: Compose an 8-measure melody
- Objective: Document music in notation or recording
- Vocabulary: compose, phrase, cadence, motif, repetition
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Song Maker, staff paper
- Assessment: Composition draft and notation.

### PE 5: Modified softball games
- Objective: Apply batting, fielding and base running in games
- Objective: Demonstrate sportsmanship in competition
- Vocabulary: inning, out, strategy, teamwork
- Materials: foam bat, whiffle balls, bases
- Assessment: Striking and fielding skills test.

### Health 5: Influences: family, peers and media
- Objective: Analyze influences on substance-use decisions
- Objective: Identify protective factors
- Vocabulary: influence, peer pressure, protective factor, risk factor
- Materials: influence web template, magazines
- Assessment: Analysis paragraph.
