## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Narrator's voice* (90 min): Read 'Eleven' aloud together and highlight every simile Rachel uses for being eleven; discuss what the comparisons reveal about her feelings.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Place value chart* (60 min): Build a place value chart from millions to thousandths and write 10 numbers dictated aloud into it, naming the value of each underlined digit.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Where did it go?* (45 min): Stir a spoonful of sugar into warm water, observe it 'disappear,' then taste a drop and record observations and questions in a science notebook.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Map skills* (45 min): Review map parts (title, compass rose, scale, legend) and find 5 places using latitude and longitude on a world map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Line sampler* (30 min): Fill a sketchbook page with 12 line types (zigzag, hatched, wavy, broken) and label the mood each suggests.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Dynamic warm-up* (30 min): Learn a 5-minute dynamic warm-up (high knees, butt kicks, arm circles, lunges, skips) and play a tag game.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Wellness triangle* (20 min): Draw a wellness triangle and list three current habits on each side; star the strongest side.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Quoting evidence* (90 min): Answer 4 inference questions about Rachel and Mrs. Price, quoting exact sentences with quotation marks; mini-lesson on conjunctions (and, but, because).
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Ten times as much* (60 min): Use place value disks to show 0.4 becoming 4 and 40 when multiplied by 10; record 8 'times 10' statements with a diagram showing digits shifting left.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Weighing the evidence* (45 min): Weigh water and sugar separately, mix them, and weigh again; record data in a table and discuss what it shows.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Landforms* (45 min): Label the Rocky Mountains, Appalachian Mountains, Great Plains, Mississippi River, Great Lakes and coasts on an outline map of North America.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Blind contour* (30 min): Draw your own hand three times with blind contour, never looking at the paper.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Components of fitness* (30 min): Do one station for each fitness component (jog, push-ups, plank, stretch) and match each to its definition.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Eight health skills* (20 min): Read a one-page list of the eight CA health skills (access info, analyze influences, communicate, decide, set goals, practice, advocate) and match each to a real-life example.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Writer's notebook* (90 min): Set up a writer's notebook and create a 'heart map' and 'memory timeline' of 10 small moments that could become narratives.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *One tenth of* (60 min): Model dividing 300, 30 and 3 by 10 with disks (trading for smaller units); write 8 '1/10 of' statements such as 0.07 is 1/10 of 0.7.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Evaporation clue* (45 min): Put a few drops of the sugar water on a dark plate in a sunny window to evaporate and predict what will be left.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Climate regions* (45 min): Shade climate and vegetation regions (desert, forest, grassland, tundra) and write how each might affect how people live.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Modified contour* (30 min): Draw a shoe or houseplant with modified contour, glancing at the paper only occasionally.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Safety and space* (30 min): Set up a safe activity area, review safety rules and play 'Sharks and Minnows' using space awareness.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Decision steps* (20 min): Learn a 5-step decision model (state the problem, list options, weigh consequences, decide, evaluate) and apply it to a bedtime vs. video-game scenario.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Discussion and fluency* (90 min): Hold a family book discussion on 'Is Rachel's reaction fair?' using sentence stems, then do a 1-minute fluency read of a passage and record words per minute.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Comparing digit values* (60 min): In numbers like 555.555, compare the value of each 5 to its neighbors and solve 10 'how many times greater' problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Modeling particles* (45 min): Check the evaporated plate for sugar crystals and draw a particle model of sugar dissolving in water, before and after.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *States strand: Northeast* (45 min): Begin the 50-states strand by learning the 11 Northeast states and capitals with a blank map and flashcards.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Gesture drawing* (30 min): Do ten 30-second gesture drawings of a family member in action poses.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Cool-down* (30 min): Learn a static stretching cool-down for major muscle groups after a 10-minute jog-walk.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *SMART goal* (20 min): Write one SMART health goal for the first month (e.g., drink water instead of soda at lunch 5 days a week) and make a tracking chart.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a paragraph explaining how first-person point of view affects the story, with two quotations, and complete a conjunction identification quiz.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a 12-question quiz on place value relationships and write one sentence explaining why the decimal point never moves (digits do).
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a claim-evidence-reasoning (CER) paragraph answering 'Is the sugar still there?' using weight and evaporation data.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a map quiz on North American landforms and Northeast states and capitals.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Choose your best contour drawing and write two sentences using art vocabulary about its line quality.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Lead the warm-up for a family member and explain the five components of fitness.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain the wellness triangle and decision steps to a parent and complete a 6-question quick check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Reading 'Eleven' by Sandra Cisneros; writer's notebook launch
- Objective: Quote accurately to explain a narrator's feelings
- Objective: Generate personal narrative ideas in a writer's notebook
- Vocabulary: narrator, first person, point of view, simile, inference
- Materials: 'Eleven' by Sandra Cisneros (in Woman Hollering Creek or online anthologies), composition notebook, highlighters
- Assessment: Evidence paragraph scored for accurate quoting; conjunction quiz; baseline fluency WPM.

### Math 5: Place value relationships to millions and thousandths
- Objective: Explain that each place is 10 times the place to its right
- Objective: Explain that each place is 1/10 of the place to its left
- Vocabulary: place value, digit, tenths, hundredths, thousandths
- Materials: place value chart, place value disks or paper squares, whiteboard
- Assessment: Friday quiz: 12 items on place value of whole numbers and decimals plus a written explanation of the 10x relationship.

### Science 5: The mystery of disappearing sugar: evidence for particles
- Objective: Observe that dissolved matter is still present
- Objective: Draw a first particle model
- Vocabulary: matter, particle, dissolve, model, evidence
- Materials: sugar, clear cups, warm water, kitchen scale, spoons, science notebook
- Assessment: CER paragraph and particle model drawing.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Geography of North America and map skills
- Objective: Identify major landforms, regions and bodies of water of North America
- Objective: Use latitude, longitude and map features
- Vocabulary: region, latitude, longitude, landform, climate
- Materials: blank outline maps of North America and the U.S., atlas or online map, colored pencils
- Assessment: Map quiz on landforms, latitude/longitude and Northeast states.

### Art & Music 5: Line and contour drawing
- Objective: Use different line types expressively
- Objective: Draw a blind and modified contour drawing
- Vocabulary: line, contour, blind contour, gesture
- Materials: sketchbook, pencils, fine-tip marker, household objects
- Assessment: Sketchbook check of contour and gesture drawings with written reflection.

### PE 5: Warm-ups, safety and the five components of fitness
- Objective: Perform a dynamic warm-up and static cool-down
- Objective: Name the five components of health-related fitness
- Vocabulary: aerobic capacity, muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility, body composition, dynamic stretch
- Materials: cones, water bottle, timer
- Assessment: Student-led warm-up and oral check of fitness components.

### Health 5: Health skills and the wellness triangle
- Objective: Describe physical, mental/emotional and social health
- Objective: Name the eight health skills
- Vocabulary: wellness, health skill, habit, goal
- Materials: health journal, markers
- Assessment: Quick check and SMART goal chart.
