## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *'The Road Not Taken'* (90 min): Read the poem twice and discuss what the two roads symbolize; write the theme in one sentence.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Benchmark estimation* (60 min): Estimate 15 sums and differences by rounding fractions to 0, 1/2 or 1 on a number line.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Roles in ecosystems* (45 min): Sort 20 organism cards from a California kelp forest into producers, consumers and decomposers.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Rights of Englishmen* (45 min): Read about the Magna Carta and English Bill of Rights and list rights colonists expected.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Erie Canal* (30 min): Learn 'Low Bridge, Everybody Down (Erie Canal)' and identify verse and chorus.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Pivoting* (30 min): Practice pivoting and faking before throwing to a partner.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Why sleep?* (20 min): Read about sleep and growth hormone, memory and mood, and list four effects of too little sleep.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 77, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *'Stopping by Woods'* (90 min): Analyze the speaker's point of view and why the last line repeats; mark the rhyme scheme.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Bar models for word problems* (60 min): Solve 6 word problems with bar models, such as a hiker who walked 2 1/3 miles then 1 3/4 miles.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Food chains* (45 min): Build 4 food chains from the cards with arrows showing the direction of matter and energy.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *House of Burgesses* (45 min): Learn how the House of Burgesses (1619) worked and who could vote.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Shenandoah* (30 min): Sing 'Shenandoah' and discuss how the melody's long phrases create a feeling of longing.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Ultimate rules* (30 min): Learn modified Ultimate rules and the 'spirit of the game' self-officiating tradition.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Sleep log* (20 min): Start a 4-night sleep log with bedtime, wake time and screen-off time.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 78, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Dickinson's metaphor* (90 min): Read 'Hope is the thing with feathers' and chart how hope is compared to a bird in each stanza.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Spotting unreasonable answers* (60 min): Judge 8 solutions (like 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7) as reasonable or not using benchmarks and correct the wrong ones.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Food web* (45 min): Connect the chains with yarn into a food web and identify organisms that connect to many others.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Town meeting* (45 min): Hold a family town meeting on a real household decision, using majority vote and taking minutes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Oh! Susanna* (30 min): Learn Stephen Foster's 'Oh! Susanna' and play its melody on recorder or virtual keyboard.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Game play* (30 min): Play a small-sided Ultimate game with family or friends.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Wind-down plan* (20 min): Design a 30-minute screen-free bedtime routine.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 79, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Idioms and proverbs* (90 min): Learn 12 common idioms, adages and proverbs ('cross that bridge when you come to it') and connect two to the poems.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Write your own* (60 min): Write and solve 4 fraction word problems about a garden or recipe, including one subtraction with regrouping.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *What if?* (45 min): Remove sea otters from the web and predict the effects on urchins and kelp; read about the real kelp forest decline.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Who was left out?* (45 min): Discuss who could and could not vote in colonial assemblies (women, enslaved people, Native Americans, non-landowners).
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Accompaniment* (30 min): Add a simple body-percussion or drum accompaniment to one folk song.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Including everyone* (30 min): Play a version where every player must touch the disc before scoring.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Daily routine* (20 min): Combine hygiene, dental care, sleep and activity into a daily health routine chart.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 80, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write an extended-metaphor poem of your own (e.g., 'Courage is...') and take an idioms quiz.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a word problem quiz requiring a model, equation and estimate check for each problem.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Draw a labeled food web for a local backyard ecosystem and take a vocabulary quiz.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Unit 4 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 4 test on colonial economy, slavery and self-government.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Unit 4 assessment* (30 min): Lead a family sing-along of 3 American songs and write a reflection on what the songs teach about history.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Unit 4 assessment* (30 min): Complete a throwing/catching skills test and write about how you showed spirit of the game.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Analyze your sleep log, set a sleep goal and take the Personal Health unit quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Lyric poetry: Frost and Dickinson
- Objective: Determine theme in poetry
- Objective: Interpret metaphors and the speaker's point of view
- Vocabulary: speaker, extended metaphor, symbol, adage, proverb
- Materials: 'The Road Not Taken' and 'Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening' by Robert Frost, 'Hope is the thing with feathers' by Emily Dickinson
- Assessment: Original metaphor poem; idioms/proverbs quiz; theme sentences.

### Math 5: Fraction word problems and estimation
- Objective: Solve fraction addition and subtraction word problems
- Objective: Use benchmark fractions to judge reasonableness
- Vocabulary: benchmark fraction, reasonable, estimate
- Materials: number lines, word problem cards
- Assessment: Word-problem quiz scored on model, equation, answer and estimate.

### Science 5: Food webs: producers, consumers and decomposers
- Objective: Identify roles of organisms in an ecosystem
- Objective: Build a food web model
- Vocabulary: producer, consumer, herbivore, carnivore, omnivore, decomposer, food web
- Materials: index cards, yarn, photos of a CA ecosystem (chaparral or kelp forest)
- Assessment: Backyard food web; vocabulary quiz.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Seeds of self-government
- Objective: Explain representative assemblies and town meetings
- Objective: Connect colonial practices to democracy
- Vocabulary: representative, assembly, town meeting, Magna Carta, rights of Englishmen
- Materials: Virginia House of Burgesses description, family town meeting agenda
- Assessment: Town meeting minutes and Unit 4 test.

### Art & Music 5: Folk songs of a growing nation
- Objective: Learn folk songs about westward travel
- Objective: Perform a family sing-along with accompaniment
- Vocabulary: folk song, ballad, accompaniment, verse-chorus form
- Materials: lyrics for 'Oh! Susanna,' 'Erie Canal,' 'Shenandoah,' 'Sweet Betsy from Pike'
- Assessment: Unit 4 sing-along performance and reflection.

### PE 5: Ultimate disc and team cooperation
- Objective: Apply throwing and catching in Ultimate
- Objective: Include and encourage all players
- Vocabulary: Ultimate, pivot, spirit of the game, end zone
- Materials: flying disc, cones for end zones
- Assessment: Skills test and spirit-of-the-game reflection.

### Health 5: Sleep and a daily health routine
- Objective: Explain why growing bodies need 9–12 hours of sleep
- Objective: Build a daily routine
- Vocabulary: sleep hygiene, growth hormone, routine, circadian rhythm
- Materials: sleep log, clock
- Assessment: Routine chart and unit quiz.
