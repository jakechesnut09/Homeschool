## Day 1 — Monday (school day 126, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Final copy* (90 min): Type and format the final opinion essay with a title and source list.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Composite figures* (60 min): Decompose 8 L-shaped and step-shaped figures into two prisms and add their volumes.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Resources* (45 min): Sort 15 resources as renewable or nonrenewable and discuss how each is used in your home.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Remember the Ladies* (45 min): Read Abigail Adams's 1776 letter and John's reply and discuss what she asked for.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Pinch pot* (30 min): Make a pinch pot and practice even wall thickness.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Grip and control* (30 min): Learn the shake-hands grip and bounce a ball on the paddle 25 times.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Historic ads* (20 min): Compare historical cigarette ads with today's vape marketing and identify persuasion techniques.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 127, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Debate prep* (90 min): Turn the essay into a 2-minute opening statement on note cards and prepare rebuttals to 2 likely counterarguments.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Real-world composite volume* (60 min): Find the volume of a two-level planter box and a staircase made of blocks.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Air quality* (45 min): Coat index cards with petroleum jelly, place them indoors and outdoors, and check particulates at week's end.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Women in the war* (45 min): Research Martha Washington, Molly Pitcher, Phillis Wheatley and Mercy Otis Warren and create a 'women of the Revolution' chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Coils and slabs* (30 min): Make a coil and a slab test piece and join pieces using score and slip.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Forehand* (30 min): Hit forehands against a wall above a tape line, counting consecutive hits.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Refusal steps* (20 min): Learn refusal strategies: say no clearly, give a reason, suggest an alternative, walk away.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 128, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Debate* (90 min): Debate the question with a family member or co-op partner using formal English and timed turns.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Design challenge* (60 min): Design 3 different boxes with a volume of exactly 72 cubic inches and choose the one using the least cardboard.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Trash audit* (45 min): Weigh and sort one day of household trash into recycling, compost, landfill and hazardous categories.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Inflation* (45 min): Use a simple price table to model how Continental dollars lost value ('not worth a Continental').
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Planning* (30 min): Sketch a sculpture (animal vessel or expressive figure) combining ideas from two inspirations.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Backhand* (30 min): Practice backhands against the wall and alternate forehand and backhand.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Role-play* (20 min): Role-play 4 scenarios refusing a vape from an older kid or friend.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 129, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Reflection* (90 min): Summarize the opponent's best reasons and write whether they changed your thinking.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Build and present* (60 min): Build the chosen box from cardstock and present its dimensions, volume calculation and design reasoning.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Reading about impacts* (45 min): Read two articles about human impacts (plastic in oceans, groundwater overuse) and take notes.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Freedom for whom?* (45 min): Read about Elizabeth Freeman (Mum Bett) suing for freedom in Massachusetts and discuss how Declaration ideals changed views on slavery.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Building* (30 min): Build the sculpture's main form using the chosen methods.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Partner rally* (30 min): Rally with a partner over a low rope, focusing on soft control.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Assertive vs. passive* (20 min): Practice assertive body language and tone compared with passive and aggressive responses.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 130, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Unit 6 assessment* (90 min): Take a cold-read assessment analyzing reasons and evidence and write an on-demand opinion paragraph in 30 minutes.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit 6 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 6 test on volume concepts, formulas and composite figures.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Examine the particulate cards with a magnifier and write a summary of human impacts on two spheres.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a letter as a family member on the home front describing hardships with accurate details.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Add texture and details and cover with plastic; note what to finish next week.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain how paddle angle changes the ball's direction and demonstrate.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Perform a refusal role-play for a parent and take the tobacco unit test.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Publishing and debating
- Objective: Publish a typed opinion essay
- Objective: Present an opinion orally and respond to counterarguments
- Vocabulary: rebuttal, formal speech, debate
- Materials: computer, debate note cards, timer
- Assessment: Opinion essay rubric; debate observation; Unit 6 on-demand writing.

### Math 5: Composite figures and volume design project
- Objective: Find volumes of figures composed of two rectangular prisms
- Objective: Design a box to meet volume requirements
- Vocabulary: composite figure, additive, decompose
- Materials: cardstock, tape, rulers, cubes
- Assessment: Unit 6 test and box design project rubric.

### Science 5: Human impact on land, water and air
- Objective: Identify ways human activities affect Earth's systems
- Objective: Obtain information from multiple sources
- Vocabulary: pollution, resource, renewable, nonrenewable, impact
- Materials: articles from NASA Climate Kids and EPA kids pages, coffee filter, trash audit gloves
- Assessment: Trash audit data and impact summary.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Women, families and the home front
- Objective: Identify roles women played
- Objective: Describe wartime hardships
- Vocabulary: home front, inflation, Continental currency, camp follower
- Materials: Abigail Adams's 'Remember the Ladies' letter (1776), image of Continental currency
- Assessment: Home-front letter and women's chart.

### Art & Music 5: Clay or salt dough sculpture: planning and building
- Objective: Use pinch, coil and slab methods
- Objective: Plan a functional or expressive form
- Vocabulary: pinch pot, coil, slab, score and slip, texture
- Materials: air-dry clay or homemade salt dough, plastic knife, fork, water cup
- Assessment: Technique test pieces and sculpture plan.

### PE 5: Paddle and racket skills
- Objective: Strike with forehand and backhand
- Objective: Control force to keep a rally
- Vocabulary: forehand, backhand, grip, paddle face, rally
- Materials: paddles or homemade paddles (paper plates on paint sticks), foam or wiffle balls, wall
- Assessment: Wall-hit counts and demonstration.

### Health 5: Tobacco advertising and refusal skills
- Objective: Analyze tobacco marketing
- Objective: Demonstrate refusal skills
- Vocabulary: refusal skill, marketing, assertive, walk away
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Refusal role-play and unit test.
