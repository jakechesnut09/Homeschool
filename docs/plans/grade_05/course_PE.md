# PE 5 (Grade 5)

Fifth-grade physical education that refines throwing, catching, dribbling, striking and rhythmic movement skills through lead-up games for soccer, flying disc, basketball, volleyball and softball, plus jump rope and folk dance. Fifth grade is a California Physical Fitness Test (FITNESSGRAM) year, so the course opens with a baseline fitness check, teaches the components of health-related fitness and heart-rate monitoring, and closes with a personal fitness plan and post-test. Every week includes a warm-up and cool-down, skill practice with critical cues, a game or challenge, and a sportsmanship focus; most activities need only a backyard, park or driveway.

*Framework:* California Physical Education Model Content Standards (2005); 2009 California Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 30 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Fitness Foundations and Baseline Assessment (weeks 1–4)
Knowing the components of fitness and where you start lets you set meaningful goals.

- Week 1: Warm-ups, safety and the five components of fitness
- Week 2: Heart rate and aerobic capacity
- Week 3: Muscular strength, endurance and flexibility
- Week 4: Baseline FITNESSGRAM assessment

### Unit 2: Jump Rope and Locomotor Rhythms (weeks 5–8)
Rhythm and timing connect footwork, jump rope tricks and heart health.

- Week 5: Individual jump rope skills
- Week 6: Advanced rope tricks and long rope
- Week 7: Locomotor patterns and rhythmic sequences
- Week 8: Jump rope routine and unit assessment

### Unit 3: Soccer Skills and Invasion Games (weeks 9–12)
Ball control and open space help a team keep possession and score.

- Week 9: Dribbling and ball control with feet
- Week 10: Passing and receiving
- Week 11: Shooting, punting and goalkeeping
- Week 12: Small-sided soccer games and strategy

### Unit 4: Throwing, Catching and Flying Disc (weeks 13–16)
Accurate throws depend on stepping, rotating and following through toward the target.

- Week 13: Overhand throwing for accuracy and distance
- Week 14: Catching on the move
- Week 15: Flying disc skills
- Week 16: Ultimate disc and team cooperation

### Unit 5: Basketball Skills (weeks 17–20)
Dribbling with control and passing to open teammates make the offense work.

- Week 17: Basketball dribbling
- Week 18: Basketball passing and shooting
- Week 19: Offense and defense in basketball
- Week 20: Basketball games and fair play

### Unit 6: Folk and Cultural Dance (weeks 21–23)
Dances from many cultures use patterns, rhythm and cooperation to tell stories and celebrate.

- Week 21: Square dance and line dance basics
- Week 22: Dances from around the world
- Week 23: Creating a dance

### Unit 7: Net and Wall Games: Volleyball and Paddle Skills (weeks 24–27)
Controlling force and angle sends the ball where the opponent is not.

- Week 24: Volleyball: forearm pass
- Week 25: Volleyball: set, serve and rally
- Week 26: Paddle and racket skills
- Week 27: Net games and strategy

### Unit 8: Striking and Fielding: Softball and Kickball (weeks 28–31)
Batting, fielding and base running combine skills with quick decisions.

- Week 28: Batting fundamentals
- Week 29: Fielding and throwing
- Week 30: Kickball and base running
- Week 31: Modified softball games

### Unit 9: Personal Fitness Plan and Post-Assessment (weeks 32–36)
A personal plan using the FITT principle turns fitness into a lifelong habit.

- Week 32: FITT principle and planning
- Week 33: Training week: aerobic and strength
- Week 34: Hydration, nutrition and recovery
- Week 35: FITNESSGRAM post-assessment
- Week 36: Summer activity plan and field day

## Standards

- **PE-5.1.1** Perform a teacher- or student-designed rhythmic routine with correct response to simple rhythms.
- **PE-5.1.10** Strike a pitched or tossed ball with a bat using correct grip, stance and follow-through.
- **PE-5.1.11** Kick, trap and punt a ball with control for distance and accuracy.
- **PE-5.1.12** Combine locomotor and manipulative skills in modified game situations.
- **PE-5.1.2** Jump rope combining a variety of tricks and footwork patterns, individually and with a long rope.
- **PE-5.1.3** Throw overhand to a moving partner or target with accuracy and appropriate force.
- **PE-5.1.4** Catch a thrown object while moving and immediately transition to a throw or pass.
- **PE-5.1.5** Throw a flying disc with accuracy for distance using backhand technique.
- **PE-5.1.6** Hand-dribble while changing speed and direction and while guarded by an opponent.
- **PE-5.1.7** Foot-dribble and pass a ball to a moving partner using the inside and outside of the foot.
- **PE-5.1.8** Strike an object with a paddle or racket using forehand and backhand strokes.
- **PE-5.1.9** Volley a ball using the forearm pass and overhead set to a target or partner.
- **PE-5.2.1** Explain and apply offensive and defensive strategies such as creating and denying open space.
- **PE-5.2.2** Explain how force, angle of release and follow-through affect distance and accuracy.
- **PE-5.2.3** Analyze a skill using its critical elements and provide feedback to self and others to improve performance.
- **PE-5.2.4** Explain how rhythm, tempo and patterns are used to design movement sequences and dances.
- **PE-5.3.1** Participate in appropriate warm-up and cool-down exercises for specific physical activities.
- **PE-5.3.2** Engage in moderate-to-vigorous physical activity for increasing periods to build aerobic capacity.
- **PE-5.3.3** Improve muscular strength and endurance through exercises such as curl-ups and push-ups.
- **PE-5.3.4** Improve flexibility through regular stretching (shoulder stretch, sit-and-reach, trunk lift).
- **PE-5.3.5** Assess health-related fitness and work toward the age-appropriate Healthy Fitness Zone.
- **PE-5.3.6** Monitor and log physical activity done outside of school toward 60 minutes daily.
- **PE-5.4.1** Identify and define the five components of health-related fitness.
- **PE-5.4.2** Measure heart rate before, during and after activity and explain the effect of exercise on the heart.
- **PE-5.4.3** Develop and monitor a personal fitness goal and plan based on fitness assessment results.
- **PE-5.4.4** Explain the FITT principle (frequency, intensity, time, type) for improving fitness.
- **PE-5.4.5** Explain the role of hydration and nutrition in physical activity.
- **PE-5.5.1** Cooperate and include others of all skill levels in group activities.
- **PE-5.5.2** Demonstrate good sportsmanship and resolve conflicts respectfully during games.
- **PE-5.5.3** Follow safety rules and use equipment safely.
- **PE-5.5.4** Encourage and give constructive feedback to others.
