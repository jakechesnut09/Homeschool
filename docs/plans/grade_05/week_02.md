## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Reading the story* (90 min): Read 'The Ransom of Red Chief' (partner reading with an adult; the story contains dated language, which is discussed) and mark the moments where the plan backfires.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Multiply by powers of 10* (60 min): Solve 20 problems like 3.42 x 100 on a place value chart, then describe the pattern of how many places digits shift.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Air takes up space* (45 min): Push an upside-down cup with a paper towel inside into a bowl of water and explain why the towel stays dry.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Cliff dwellers* (45 min): Explore NPS photos of Mesa Verde cliff dwellings and list how the people used the land, water and stone.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Value scale* (30 min): Create a 7-step value scale using pencil pressure and another using hatching and cross-hatching.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Finding a pulse* (30 min): Find your pulse at wrist and neck and count resting heart rate for 15 seconds x 4.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Body map* (20 min): Trace your body on butcher paper and sketch the skeletal, muscular and circulatory systems with labels.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Word study: prefixes* (90 min): Study prefixes un-, re-, dis-, mis-, pre- with 20 multisyllabic words, sorting and defining them; read 10 tricky words from the story by syllables.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Divide by powers of 10* (60 min): Solve 20 problems like 45 / 1,000 = 0.045 and explain why zeros must be added as placeholders.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Air has weight* (45 min): Build a balance from a ruler and two balloons, then pop one and observe the balance tip; record findings.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Pueblo life* (45 min): Read about Pueblo farming of corn, beans and squash and build a model adobe dwelling from salt dough.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Shading forms* (30 min): Shade a sphere, cube and cylinder showing highlight, core shadow and cast shadow.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Heart rate experiment* (30 min): Measure heart rate after walking, jogging and sprinting for 1 minute each and graph the results.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Heart and lungs* (20 min): Take your resting pulse, do 2 minutes of jumping jacks, retake it and explain how the circulatory and respiratory systems responded.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Comparing characters* (90 min): Use a Venn diagram to compare Bill and Sam with Red Chief, citing three details for each, and write a 5-sentence summary of the plot.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Exponents* (60 min): Write 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000 as 10^1 through 10^4; expand numbers like 6 x 10^3 and solve 15 mixed exponent problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Compressing air* (45 min): Pump air into a deflated ball and weigh it before and after; compress air in a capped syringe and describe the particles.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Pacific Northwest* (45 min): Read about the Tlingit and Kwakwaka'wakw use of salmon and cedar and analyze a photo of a totem pole as a primary source.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Observing light* (30 min): Light an egg with a flashlight and draw it from observation, matching values.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *PACER practice* (30 min): Practice the 20-meter PACER shuttle run with pacing strategy for 10 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Nervous system* (20 min): Do a ruler-drop reaction-time test 5 times and record results; write how brain, nerves and muscles worked together.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 9, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Narrative planning* (90 min): Choose a small moment from the notebook and fill a story arc (beginning, rising action, climax, resolution); mini-lesson on prepositions and prepositional phrases.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Metric connection* (60 min): Measure 5 household objects in centimeters and convert to meters and millimeters by multiplying or dividing by powers of 10.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Particle simulation* (45 min): Use the PhET 'States of Matter: Basics' simulation to compare particle motion in solids, liquids and gases and sketch each.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Comparing regions* (45 min): Complete a comparison chart of shelter, food, economy, government and customs for the two regions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Artist study* (30 min): Look at a Leonardo da Vinci drawing on Google Arts & Culture and identify how he used value.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Steady pace jog* (30 min): Jog-walk a measured quarter mile four times, trying to hold an even pace.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Endocrine system* (20 min): Read a KidsHealth article on glands and hormones and add the pituitary, thyroid and adrenal glands to your body map.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 10, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a reading check on situational irony and plot sequence and a prefix quiz; share the story arc aloud.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a 15-problem quiz with powers of 10 and exponents and correct any errors using the place value chart.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Revise last week's particle model to include gases and explain how particles too small to see can still have weight.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph explaining how environment shaped daily life in one region, using 3 details.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Complete a shaded still life of 2 objects and self-assess using a value checklist.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain to a parent why heart rate increases with exercise and record a heart rate chart.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Use the body map to explain to family how three systems work together when you run; complete a labeling quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: 'The Ransom of Red Chief' by O. Henry; character and plot
- Objective: Summarize plot and compare characters
- Objective: Plan a personal narrative with a clear sequence of events
- Vocabulary: irony, ransom, summarize, contrast, prefix
- Materials: 'The Ransom of Red Chief' (Project Gutenberg, O. Henry 'Whirligigs'), story map, Venn diagram
- Assessment: Summary and Venn diagram; prefix quiz; narrative plan check-in.

### Math 5: Powers of 10 and exponents
- Objective: Multiply and divide whole numbers and decimals by 10, 100 and 1,000
- Objective: Write powers of 10 using exponents
- Vocabulary: power of 10, exponent, base, decimal point
- Materials: place value chart, metric ruler, calculator for checking
- Assessment: Quiz on multiplying/dividing by powers of 10 and exponent notation; check of the metric measuring table.

### Science 5: Gases are matter too
- Objective: Gather evidence that air has weight and takes up space
- Objective: Model gas particles
- Vocabulary: gas, volume, mass, compress, air pressure
- Materials: balloons, ruler or dowel, string, syringe without needle, plastic bottle, bicycle pump or ball pump, kitchen scale
- Assessment: Revised particle model with written explanation; investigation data tables.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Peoples of the desert Southwest and Pacific Northwest
- Objective: Explain how the Ancestral Pueblo and Pacific Northwest peoples adapted to their environments
- Objective: Compare economies and customs
- Vocabulary: Ancestral Pueblo, adobe, mesa, potlatch, totem pole, cedar
- Materials: photos of Mesa Verde and Taos Pueblo (NPS), images of Kwakwaka'wakw or Tlingit art, clay or salt dough
- Assessment: Comparison chart and explanatory paragraph.

### Art & Music 5: Value and shading
- Objective: Create a value scale
- Objective: Shade geometric forms to show light
- Vocabulary: value, highlight, shadow, cast shadow, hatching, cross-hatching
- Materials: pencils (HB, 2B, 4B), blending stump or tissue, egg or ball, flashlight
- Assessment: Value scales and shaded still life.

### PE 5: Heart rate and aerobic capacity
- Objective: Measure resting and active heart rate
- Objective: Begin PACER or mile run practice
- Vocabulary: pulse, heart rate, resting heart rate, PACER, pace
- Materials: stopwatch, cones 20 m apart, PACER audio (FITNESSGRAM)
- Assessment: Heart rate graph and explanation.

### Health 5: Body systems working together
- Objective: Identify major body systems
- Objective: Explain how systems cooperate during exercise and growth
- Vocabulary: body system, organ, skeletal, muscular, circulatory, endocrine
- Materials: body outline paper, stopwatch
- Assessment: Labeled body map and quiz.
