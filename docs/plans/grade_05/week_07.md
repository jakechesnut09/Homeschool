## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 10–11* (90 min): Read chapters 10–11 and compare 'old Brian' and 'new Brian' on a T-chart with quotations.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Division by multiples of 10* (60 min): Use basic facts and place value to solve 15 problems like 2,400 / 60 and 420 / 30.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Making mixtures* (45 min): Make a trail-mix and a sand-and-salt mixture, weigh each part and the whole, and record whether weight adds up.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *John Cabot and Jacques Cartier* (45 min): Trace Cabot's and Cartier's voyages and record their sponsors and findings.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Recorder basics* (30 min): Learn posture, left-hand position and gentle 'too' tonguing; play B with steady tone.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Locomotor review* (30 min): Practice skip, slide, gallop, leap and hop to changing music tempos.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Breakfast matters* (20 min): Read a short article on breakfast and concentration, then plan three quick balanced breakfasts.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Roots: spect, port, struct, rupt* (90 min): Learn 4 Latin roots and build 16 words; find any of them in Hatchet and define them in context.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Area model division* (60 min): Solve 8 problems such as 1,512 / 24 by building an area model with known parts of the dividend.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Separation plan* (45 min): Plan how to separate sand, salt, paper clips and rice using properties (magnetism, size, solubility).
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Champlain and Hudson* (45 min): Trace Champlain's and Hudson's routes and explain the search for the Northwest Passage.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *A and G* (30 min): Learn A and G and play 4-note patterns from a fingering chart.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Rhythm sticks* (30 min): Learn a lummi stick routine in 4/4 time with a partner.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Snack sorting* (20 min): Sort 20 snack cards into everyday vs. sometimes foods and explain your reasoning with nutrients.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 12–13* (90 min): Read chapters 12–13 (the plane passing, the low point) and discuss how this turning point changes Brian.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Partial quotients* (60 min): Use partial quotients with friendly multiples (10x, 5x, 2x the divisor) to solve 8 four-digit by two-digit problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Separating* (45 min): Carry out the separation plan with a magnet, sieve, water and coffee filter, then set salt water to evaporate.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Explorer comparison* (45 min): Complete an explorer comparison matrix (sponsor, goal, obstacles, accomplishments) for 6 explorers.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Reading the staff* (30 min): Identify B, A and G on the staff with flashcards and play 'Hot Cross Buns' and 'Gently Sleep.'
- [ ] **PE 5** — *8-count sequences* (30 min): Combine four 8-count locomotor sequences into one pattern.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Make a snack* (20 min): Prepare a yogurt parfait or veggie-and-hummus plate with adult help and record its food groups.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 34, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Narrative scene* (90 min): Write a one-page scene from the point of view of an animal watching Brian, avoiding shifts in verb tense.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Checking with multiplication* (60 min): Solve 6 division problems and check each with multiplication plus remainder; write how the check confirms the answer.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Recovering the salt* (45 min): Weigh the recovered salt and compare with the starting amount; explain any differences.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Land claims map* (45 min): Color a map showing land claimed by Spain, France, England, Portugal, the Netherlands, Sweden and Russia.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Practice and record* (30 min): Practice 10 minutes and record a performance of 'Hot Cross Buns' to listen back.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Designing a routine* (30 min): Design a 32-count routine mixing locomotor moves and jump rope tricks.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Hydration* (20 min): Track your water intake for a day and design a water-bottle reminder chart.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 35, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Correct 10 sentences with inappropriate tense shifts and take a roots quiz.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz of 8 division problems showing a model or partial quotients for each.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a procedure for separating a new mixture and take a quiz on mixtures vs. solutions.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a map quiz on explorer routes and European land claims.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Self-evaluate the recording for tone and rhythm, then play a B-A-G song for an audience.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform the routine to music and explain how it follows the beat.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a one-week healthy snack menu and justify three choices using label data.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Hatchet chapters 10–13: the new Brian
- Objective: Analyze character change over time
- Objective: Use Greek and Latin roots to determine meaning
- Vocabulary: transformation, patience, root, tense shift
- Materials: Hatchet, character change chart
- Assessment: Character change T-chart; animal-POV scene; roots and tense-shift quiz.

### Math 5: Division with two-digit divisors: models
- Objective: Divide using area models and partial quotients
- Objective: Use multiplication to check division
- Vocabulary: dividend, divisor, quotient, remainder, partial quotients
- Materials: grid paper, base-ten blocks or drawings
- Assessment: Quiz on division with two-digit divisors using models.

### Science 5: Mixtures and solutions
- Objective: Distinguish mixtures from solutions
- Objective: Separate mixtures by their properties
- Vocabulary: mixture, solution, filter, separate, sieve
- Materials: sand, salt, paper clips, rice, coffee filter, sieve, magnet, water, kitchen scale
- Assessment: Separation procedure and data; mixtures quiz.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: French, English and Dutch explorers
- Objective: Trace routes of northern explorers searching for the Northwest Passage
- Objective: Compare aims and accomplishments of explorers
- Vocabulary: Northwest Passage, Cabot, Cartier, Champlain, Hudson, fur trade
- Materials: outline map of North America, explorer cards
- Assessment: Explorer matrix and land claims map quiz.

### Art & Music 5: Beginning recorder: B, A, G
- Objective: Hold and play the recorder with good tone
- Objective: Read B, A and G on the treble staff
- Vocabulary: treble clef, staff, tonguing, embouchure, breath support
- Materials: soprano recorder (inexpensive) or free virtual recorder/keyboard app, fingering chart
- Assessment: Recorded B-A-G performance with self-evaluation.

### PE 5: Locomotor patterns and rhythmic sequences
- Objective: Combine locomotor movements to a rhythm
- Objective: Design a movement sequence
- Vocabulary: locomotor, slide, gallop, leap, tempo, 8-count
- Materials: music, cones, lummi sticks or rhythm sticks (or wooden spoons)
- Assessment: Rhythmic routine performance.

### Health 5: Healthy breakfasts, snacks and beverages
- Objective: Plan nutrient-dense snacks
- Objective: Choose water and milk over sugary drinks
- Vocabulary: nutrient-dense, empty calories, hydration
- Materials: kitchen tools, fruit, vegetables, yogurt
- Assessment: Snack menu with justifications.
