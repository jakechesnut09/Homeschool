## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Reading the biography* (90 min): Read the Jean Fritz biography and make a timeline of Paul Revere's life events.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Which operation?* (60 min): Sort 12 decimal word problems by operation and solve 6 of them with a bar model.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Initial ideas* (45 min): Write and draw initial ideas about where a giant tree's material comes from.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Pennsylvania* (45 min): Read about William Penn and the Quakers and analyze an excerpt of Penn's letter to the Lenape about fair dealings.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Refining* (30 min): Add final details and adjust values to strengthen depth in the landscape.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Keep-away* (30 min): Play 2-v-1 keep-away and discuss how to move to open space.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *What is body image?* (20 min): Discuss what body image means and write three things your body lets you do that you appreciate.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Relationships* (90 min): Explain how three events or people (Sons of Liberty, the British, Revere's trade) influenced Revere's actions in a cause/effect chart.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit price* (60 min): Compare unit prices for 4 pairs of grocery items (e.g., 12 oz for $3.48 vs 16 oz for $4.32) by dividing.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Van Helmont's experiment* (45 min): Read about Jan van Helmont's 5-year willow experiment and analyze his data on tree mass and soil mass.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *New York, New Jersey, Delaware* (45 min): Learn how New Netherland became New York and why the Middle Colonies were diverse 'breadbasket' colonies.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Family critique* (30 min): Lead a family critique describing, analyzing, interpreting and judging your painting.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Defense* (30 min): Practice marking a player and staying goal-side in 1-v-1 drills.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Many shapes and sizes* (20 min): Look at family photos to see how genetics shapes body type and list factors that affect size.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Choosing a topic* (90 min): Choose a research topic about colonial life or an explorer and write 4 research questions about different aspects of it.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Recipe scaling* (60 min): Multiply a recipe's decimal measurements by 2.5 and divide by 2 to make a double-and-a-half batch and a half batch.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Set up investigation* (45 min): Weigh dry soil and plant bean seeds in cups, recording starting soil mass for a multi-week investigation.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Maryland and the South* (45 min): Read about Lord Baltimore's Catholic haven in Maryland and the founding of the Carolinas and Georgia.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Revision* (30 min): Make one revision based on critique feedback.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Small-sided game* (30 min): Play 2-v-2 or 3-v-3 soccer (family or park) applying offensive and defensive strategies.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Dieting dangers* (20 min): Read a KidsHealth article on dieting and list why skipping meals or fad diets harm growing kids.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Finding sources* (90 min): Locate 3 sources (library book, encyclopedia, reliable website) and record them in a source list with titles italicized or underlined.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a mixed decimal operations review and a 'my mistakes' log of errors to watch.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Hydroponic test* (45 min): Start seeds on wet paper towels in a bag with no soil to see if plants can grow without soil.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Regions chart* (45 min): Complete a 3-region chart comparing geography, economy, religion and founders of the colonies.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Matting* (30 min): Mount the painting on a black paper mat with clean edges.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Sportsmanship* (30 min): Play a game where players self-officiate and resolve disputes with rock-paper-scissors.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Kind words* (20 min): Practice replacing body-shaming comments with respectful statements in 5 scenario cards.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 60, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a quiz on the biography and on title punctuation, and conference with a parent on research questions.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit 3 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 3 test on all four decimal operations and word problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a preliminary claim about where plant mass comes from, citing van Helmont's evidence.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Label all 13 colonies and take a quiz on Middle and Southern colony founders.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write an artist statement and add the painting to your portfolio.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Unit 3 assessment* (30 min): Play a game while a parent uses a skills and strategy checklist, then reflect on sportsmanship.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a paragraph on healthy body image and answer a 5-question check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Biography: 'And Then What Happened, Paul Revere?' by Jean Fritz; research launch
- Objective: Explain relationships between individuals and events
- Objective: Choose a research topic and generate questions
- Vocabulary: biography, research question, source, paraphrase, citation
- Materials: 'And Then What Happened, Paul Revere?' by Jean Fritz (library), note cards, library catalog
- Assessment: Cause/effect chart; research questions and source list; title punctuation quiz.

### Math 5: Decimal word problems and unit assessment
- Objective: Choose the correct operation for decimal word problems
- Objective: Solve multi-step decimal problems
- Vocabulary: multi-step, unit price, operation
- Materials: recipe card, grocery ads
- Assessment: Unit 3 test covering 5.NBT.B.7.

### Science 5: Where does a plant's mass come from?
- Objective: Investigate whether plants get mass from soil
- Objective: Analyze historical evidence (van Helmont's willow)
- Vocabulary: mass, photosynthesis, carbon dioxide, nutrient
- Materials: bean or radish seeds, potting soil, cups, kitchen scale, water
- Assessment: Initial model and preliminary claim; investigation setup check.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Middle and Southern colonies
- Objective: Identify founders and reasons for the Middle and Southern colonies
- Objective: Compare regional economies
- Vocabulary: Quaker, proprietor, Catholic, plantation, breadbasket, cash crop
- Materials: William Penn's 1681 charter or letter excerpt, map of 13 colonies
- Assessment: 13 colonies map and regions chart.

### Art & Music 5: Finishing and critiquing the landscape
- Objective: Refine artwork using feedback
- Objective: Explain choices in an artist statement
- Vocabulary: critique, refine, artist statement, mat
- Materials: landscape painting, fine brushes, black paper for a mat
- Assessment: Landscape painting rubric and artist statement.

### PE 5: Small-sided soccer games and strategy
- Objective: Create open space on offense
- Objective: Mark and deny space on defense
- Vocabulary: offense, defense, open space, marking, support
- Materials: soccer ball, pinnies or colored shirts, cones
- Assessment: Game-play checklist and sportsmanship reflection.

### Health 5: Healthy body image
- Objective: Describe healthy body image
- Objective: Recognize risks of unhealthy dieting
- Vocabulary: body image, self-esteem, diet culture, genetics
- Materials: health journal
- Assessment: Paragraph and quick check.
