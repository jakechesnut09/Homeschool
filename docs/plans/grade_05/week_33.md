## Day 1 — Monday (school day 161, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Planning* (90 min): Choose a phenomenon (why the moon changes shape, why cats purr) and plan a myth with characters, conflict and explanation.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Quadrilateral family* (60 min): Build each quadrilateral type on a geoboard and list its defining properties.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Daylight data* (45 min): Collect sunrise and sunset times for the first day of each month and calculate hours of daylight.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Louisiana Purchase* (45 min): Read about Jefferson's 1803 purchase and calculate its size compared to the existing U.S.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *What curators do* (30 min): Take a virtual museum tour on Google Arts & Culture and list a curator's responsibilities.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Interval run* (30 min): Do a 20-minute interval run (1 min jog, 1 min walk) and check heart rate.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Drug-free goal* (20 min): Write a personal drug-free goal with reasons and supports.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 162, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Drafting* (90 min): Draft the myth with dialogue and a god, hero or trickster character.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Hierarchy diagram* (60 min): Create a hierarchy diagram from quadrilateral down to square with arrows and property labels.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Graphing day length* (45 min): Graph daylight hours across the year and identify the longest and shortest days.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Lewis and Clark journals* (45 min): Read journal excerpts describing animals and landscapes and sketch one like a naturalist.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Selecting work* (30 min): Review the year's artwork and select 6 pieces that show growth and variety.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Strength circuit* (30 min): Complete a 3-round strength circuit targeting curl-ups and push-ups.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Refusal toolbox* (20 min): Practice 6 refusal strategies (broken record, humor, change subject, leave, give facts, suggest alternative).

- [ ] **Math 5** — *Always, sometimes, never* (60 min): Decide whether 12 statements like 'a rhombus is a square' are always, sometimes or never true, with drawings as proof.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Tilt model* (45 min): Use a lamp and tilted globe to model why California has longer days in summer.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Sacagawea and York* (45 min): Research the contributions of Sacagawea and York to the expedition.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Reflection* (30 min): Write a reflection for each piece on what was learned and what you would change.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Active game* (30 min): Play a vigorous game (capture the flag) for 20 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Record a skit* (20 min): Write and record a 1-minute refusal skit.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Writing an original myth
- Objective: Write a myth that explains a natural phenomenon
- Objective: Use narrative techniques including pacing and dialogue
- Vocabulary: pourquoi tale, phenomenon, pacing, resolution
- Materials: myth planning sheet, art supplies for illustrations
- Assessment: Original myth narrative rubric; roots cumulative quiz.

### Math 5: Hierarchy of quadrilaterals
- Objective: Classify quadrilaterals in a hierarchy
- Objective: Explain why a figure belongs to multiple categories
- Vocabulary: quadrilateral, trapezoid, parallelogram, rectangle, rhombus, square, kite, hierarchy
- Materials: geoboard or Math Learning Center Geoboard app, chart paper
- Assessment: Unit 9 test covering 5.G.B.3–B.4; hierarchy diagram.

### Science 5: Day length and seasons
- Objective: Graph day length data across the year
- Objective: Explain patterns in daylight hours
- Vocabulary: sunrise, sunset, daylight, tilt, solstice, equinox
- Materials: sunrise/sunset data table for your city, graph paper, globe, lamp
- Assessment: Day length graph and quiz.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: The Louisiana Purchase and western explorers
- Objective: Explain the Louisiana Purchase
- Objective: Trace the journeys of Lewis and Clark, Pike and Frémont
- Vocabulary: Louisiana Purchase, Corps of Discovery, Sacagawea, York, expedition
- Materials: excerpts from the Lewis and Clark journals (Library of Congress), map of expedition routes
- Assessment: Journal entry and route map.

### Art & Music 5: Curating the art portfolio
- Objective: Select and reflect on artworks
- Objective: Explain the role of a curator
- Vocabulary: curator, portfolio, selection, label, theme
- Materials: year's artwork, index cards
- Assessment: Portfolio selection and reflections.

### PE 5: Training week: aerobic and strength
- Objective: Follow a personal training plan
- Objective: Monitor intensity using heart rate
- Vocabulary: target heart rate, interval, set, repetition
- Materials: stopwatch, mat, activity log
- Assessment: Activity log and heart-rate entries.

### Health 5: Drug-free goal and refusal practice
- Objective: Set a drug-free goal
- Objective: Demonstrate multiple refusal strategies
- Vocabulary: commitment, goal, refusal strategy
- Materials: goal card, video recorder (optional)
- Assessment: Refusal skill rubric.
