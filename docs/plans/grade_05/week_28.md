## Day 1 — Monday (school day 136, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 8–10* (90 min): Read chapters 8–10 and compare the garden's condition to Mary's condition at the start.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Metric length* (60 min): Measure objects in mm, cm and m and convert 15 measurements by multiplying or dividing by 10, 100 and 1,000.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Define the problem* (45 min): Choose a problem (oil spill cleanup or water-saving planter) and define criteria and constraints.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *A weak union* (45 min): Read about the Articles of Confederation and list 5 powers Congress did and did not have.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Artist study* (30 min): Look at Andy Goldsworthy's nature art and Louise Nevelson's assemblages and discuss materials and meaning.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Grip and stance* (30 min): Learn the batting grip (knuckles aligned) and athletic stance, and practice dry swings.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Cannabis facts* (20 min): Read a NIDA for kids article on marijuana and list effects on memory, learning and reaction time.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 137, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 11–12* (90 min): Read chapters 11–12 and write a character sketch of Dickon with 3 quotations.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Metric mass and capacity* (60 min): Measure liquids in mL and L and masses in g and kg; solve 12 conversion problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Design options* (45 min): Sketch two possible solutions and compare their trade-offs.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Simulation* (45 min): Play a simulation where each 'state' prints its own money and taxes trade, then discuss problems.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Collecting* (30 min): Collect natural or recycled materials and sort them by color, shape and texture.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Tee hitting* (30 min): Hit 30 balls off a tee, aiming for line drives.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Edibles danger* (20 min): Learn why cannabis edibles can look like candy and what to do if you find one.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 138, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Sentence combining* (90 min): Combine, expand and reduce 12 sentences from the novel to improve style.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Multi-step problems* (60 min): Solve 6 multi-step problems such as how many 250 mL cups can be filled from three 2 L bottles.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Build and test* (45 min): Build a prototype from recycled materials and test it (e.g., cleaning a vegetable-oil spill from water with cotton, feathers, sponge).
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Shays' Rebellion* (45 min): Read about Shays' Rebellion and explain why it alarmed leaders like Washington.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Environmental sculpture* (30 min): Build an ephemeral outdoor sculpture from natural materials and photograph it.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Soft toss* (30 min): Hit soft tosses from a partner, tracking the ball to the bat.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Inhalants* (20 min): Read warning labels on household products and explain why sniffing them can cause sudden death.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 139, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 13–15* (90 min): Read chapters 13–15 and compare Mary and Colin as two 'contrary' children in a chart.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a mixed customary and metric review and make a conversion reference card.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Improve* (45 min): Identify a failure point, change one variable, and retest.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Critics' views* (45 min): Read quotes from critics of the Articles and match each to a weakness.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Assemblage* (30 min): Assemble a small found-object sculpture in a shoebox lid painted one color.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Bat safety* (30 min): Review bat safety (safe zone, dropping not throwing the bat) and play a hitting-target game.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Laws and norms* (20 min): Learn that cannabis is illegal under 21 in CA and review data showing most youth don't use it.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: The Secret Garden chapters 8–15: the garden and Dickon
- Objective: Analyze how relationships change Mary
- Objective: Combine and expand sentences for style
- Vocabulary: wick, transformation, companionship, compound sentence
- Materials: The Secret Garden, sentence combining cards
- Assessment: Character sketch; descriptive paragraph; comparison chart.

### Math 5: Metric conversions and multi-step measurement problems
- Objective: Convert metric units using powers of 10
- Objective: Solve multi-step conversion problems
- Vocabulary: millimeter, centimeter, meter, kilometer, gram, kilogram, liter, milliliter
- Materials: meter stick, liter bottle, kitchen scale
- Assessment: Unit 7 test covering 5.MD.A.1.

### Science 5: Engineering a solution: reduce, reuse or protect
- Objective: Design a solution to an environmental problem
- Objective: Test and improve a prototype
- Vocabulary: prototype, criteria, constraints, trade-off
- Materials: recycled materials (bottles, cardboard, cans), tape, scissors, water
- Assessment: Engineering notebook and presentation; Unit 8 test.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: The Articles of Confederation
- Objective: List weaknesses of the Articles
- Objective: Explain Shays' Rebellion
- Vocabulary: Articles of Confederation, confederation, Shays' Rebellion, tariff, unanimous
- Materials: Articles of Confederation excerpt, simulation cards
- Assessment: Weaknesses chart and paragraph.

### Art & Music 5: Found-object and environmental sculpture
- Objective: Create sculpture from found or natural materials
- Objective: Interpret how artists use materials to communicate
- Vocabulary: assemblage, found object, environmental art, ephemeral
- Materials: recycled objects, natural materials (leaves, stones, sticks), camera
- Assessment: Unit 7 sculpture portfolio entry.

### PE 5: Batting fundamentals
- Objective: Bat with correct grip, stance and swing
- Objective: Hit off a tee and from a soft toss
- Vocabulary: grip, stance, swing plane, follow-through, batting tee
- Materials: plastic or foam bat, whiffle balls, batting tee or cone
- Assessment: Batting cue checklist.

### Health 5: Cannabis and inhalants
- Objective: Describe the effects of cannabis on young people
- Objective: Identify dangers of inhalants
- Vocabulary: cannabis, THC, edible, inhalant, poison control
- Materials: NIDA articles, household product labels
- Assessment: Matching quiz.
