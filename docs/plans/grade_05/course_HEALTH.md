# Health 5 (Grade 5)

Grade 5 health education aligned to the 2008 California Health Education Content Standards, which focus fifth grade on Growth and Development, Nutrition and Physical Activity, and Alcohol, Tobacco and Other Drugs, with supporting Personal and Community Health (hygiene, sleep, dental care). Students study body systems, nutrition labels and fitness, healthy body image and media literacy, then a seven-week puberty unit covering hormones, the reproductive systems, menstruation and emotional changes (parents preview materials and may opt out, consistent with CA Education Code). The year closes with tobacco, vaping, alcohol, cannabis and medicine safety, refusal skills, and a health-advocacy campaign.

*Framework:* 2008 California Health Education Content Standards (Grade 5: Growth and Development; Nutrition and Physical Activity; Alcohol, Tobacco and Other Drugs; plus Personal and Community Health); 2019 CA Health Education Framework  
*Time:* 20 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Health Skills and Body Systems (weeks 1–3)
Healthy choices are skills we practice, and our body systems work together as we grow.

- Week 1: Health skills and the wellness triangle
- Week 2: Body systems working together
- Week 3: Growing and changing; reliable health information

### Unit 2: Nutrition for Growing Bodies (weeks 4–8)
A growing body needs balanced nutrients and fewer added sugars, sodium and saturated fats.

- Week 4: Nutrients and MyPlate for a growing body
- Week 5: Added sugars, sodium and saturated fat
- Week 6: Reading Nutrition Facts labels
- Week 7: Healthy breakfasts, snacks and beverages
- Week 8: Meal planning and food tracking

### Unit 3: Physical Activity and Fitness (weeks 9–11)
Daily activity builds every component of fitness and supports healthy growth.

- Week 9: Components of physical fitness
- Week 10: 60 minutes a day: planning activity
- Week 11: Activity, communication and safety

### Unit 4: Body Image and Media (weeks 12–13)
Healthy bodies come in many shapes and sizes; media images are often not real.

- Week 12: Healthy body image
- Week 13: Media, ads and body image

### Unit 5: Personal Health and Hygiene (weeks 14–16)
Daily hygiene, sleep and check-ups keep a changing body healthy.

- Week 14: Skin, hair and body hygiene
- Week 15: Dental health and check-ups
- Week 16: Sleep and a daily health routine

### Unit 6: Puberty: Growth and Development (weeks 17–23)
Puberty is a normal stage of growth that happens to everyone at their own pace.

- Week 17: Introduction to puberty (parent notification and opt-out honored)
- Week 18: Hormones and the endocrine system
- Week 19: The reproductive systems
- Week 20: Menstruation and other normal changes
- Week 21: Emotional and social changes
- Week 22: Talking with trusted adults
- Week 23: Puberty review and respect for differences

### Unit 7: Tobacco, Nicotine and Vaping (weeks 24–26)
Nicotine is addictive, and tobacco and vaping products harm the lungs, heart and brain.

- Week 24: Tobacco and nicotine effects
- Week 25: E-cigarettes and vaping
- Week 26: Tobacco advertising and refusal skills

### Unit 8: Alcohol, Cannabis, Inhalants and Medicines (weeks 27–30)
Alcohol and other drugs affect the developing brain; medicines are safe only when used correctly.

- Week 27: Alcohol and the body
- Week 28: Cannabis and inhalants
- Week 29: Using medicines safely
- Week 30: Getting help and community resources

### Unit 9: Influences, Refusal and Decisions (weeks 31–33)
Recognizing pressure and practicing refusal skills helps us stay drug-free.

- Week 31: Influences: family, peers and media
- Week 32: Decision making in risky situations
- Week 33: Drug-free goal and refusal practice

### Unit 10: Advocacy and Year Review (weeks 34–36)
We can use what we know to help family and friends make healthy choices.

- Week 34: Health advocacy campaign planning
- Week 35: Creating the campaign
- Week 36: Presentation and year review

## Standards

- **H-5.1.1.A** Describe the short- and long-term effects of alcohol on the brain and body, including impaired judgment.
- **H-5.1.1.G** Describe how body systems (endocrine, circulatory, skeletal, muscular, reproductive) work together and change as people grow.
- **H-5.1.1.N** Describe the food groups, key nutrients and the Dietary Guidelines recommendations for a growing body.
- **H-5.1.1.P** Describe hygiene practices for skin, hair, teeth and body odor, including care during puberty.
- **H-5.1.2.A** Describe the effects of tobacco and nicotine, including e-cigarettes/vaping and secondhand smoke and aerosol.
- **H-5.1.2.G** Describe the physical, social and emotional changes that occur during puberty.
- **H-5.1.2.N** Explain the importance of limiting added sugars, sodium and saturated fat.
- **H-5.1.2.P** Explain the importance of adequate sleep (9–12 hours) and regular dental and medical check-ups.
- **H-5.1.3.A** Describe the effects of cannabis (marijuana) and inhalants on the developing brain and body.
- **H-5.1.3.G** Explain the role of hormones and the endocrine system in puberty.
- **H-5.1.3.N** Describe the components of physical fitness and the benefits of 60 minutes of daily physical activity.
- **H-5.1.4.A** Distinguish between the safe use and misuse of prescription and over-the-counter medicines.
- **H-5.1.4.G** Identify the structures and functions of the male and female reproductive systems.
- **H-5.1.4.N** Explain the relationship between food intake, physical activity, growth and healthy body weight.
- **H-5.1.5.A** Explain addiction and why young people's developing brains are especially vulnerable.
- **H-5.1.5.G** Explain menstruation, nocturnal emissions and other normal pubertal changes.
- **H-5.1.5.N** Recognize that healthy bodies come in many shapes and sizes and describe the dangers of unhealthy dieting.
- **H-5.1.6.A** Recognize that most young people do not use alcohol, tobacco, cannabis or other drugs.
- **H-5.1.6.G** Recognize that the timing of puberty varies and that each person grows at their own rate.
- **H-5.1.7.G** Explain why personal hygiene becomes more important during puberty.
- **H-5.2.1.A** Analyze how advertising, media, flavors and packaging promote alcohol, tobacco and vaping products.
- **H-5.2.1.G** Analyze how media, family and peers influence feelings about growth, body image and gender roles.
- **H-5.2.1.N** Analyze how media, advertising, peers and culture influence food choices, physical activity and body image.
- **H-5.2.2.A** Explain how family, peers and community norms influence decisions about alcohol, tobacco and other drugs.
- **H-5.3.1.A** Identify trusted adults and community resources for help with alcohol, tobacco and other drug concerns.
- **H-5.3.1.G** Identify trusted adults and valid sources of information about puberty and growth.
- **H-5.3.1.N** Use Nutrition Facts labels and valid sources to access accurate nutrition and fitness information.
- **H-5.4.1.A** Demonstrate refusal and negotiation skills to avoid alcohol, tobacco and other drugs.
- **H-5.4.1.G** Demonstrate how to talk with a parent/guardian or trusted adult about puberty and personal concerns.
- **H-5.4.1.N** Use effective communication to express needs for healthy food and physical activity.
- **H-5.5.1.A** Use a decision-making process in situations involving alcohol, tobacco and other drugs.
- **H-5.5.1.G** Use a decision-making process to solve problems related to growth, hygiene and changing friendships.
- **H-5.5.1.N** Use a decision-making process to choose healthy meals, snacks and beverages.
- **H-5.6.1.A** Set a personal goal to remain alcohol-, tobacco- and drug-free.
- **H-5.6.1.N** Set and track a goal to improve eating habits or physical activity.
- **H-5.7.1.G** Practice personal hygiene and self-care routines appropriate to puberty.
- **H-5.7.1.N** Plan healthy meals and snacks and a weekly physical activity schedule.
- **H-5.7.1.P** Develop and follow a daily personal health routine (hygiene, sleep, dental care).
- **H-5.8.1.A** Encourage others to be alcohol-, tobacco- and drug-free.
- **H-5.8.1.G** Support others and show respect for individual differences in growth and development.
- **H-5.8.1.N** Advocate for healthy eating and physical activity at home and in the community.
