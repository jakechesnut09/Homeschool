## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Finishing the novel* (90 min): Read chapters 18–19 and the epilogue; discuss how the epilogue changes our view of Brian's experience.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Modeling decimal addition* (60 min): Shade hundredths grids to add 10 pairs like 0.46 + 0.38 and record with a place value equation.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Open reaction* (45 min): Weigh vinegar and baking soda in an open cup before and after reacting and record the weight change.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Founding Jamestown* (45 min): Read about the Virginia Company's goals and the 1607 founding and list the problems settlers faced.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Color wheel* (30 min): Paint a 12-part color wheel mixing secondary and tertiary colors from primaries.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Close control* (30 min): Dribble through a cone line using small touches with both feet.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Five components* (20 min): Learn the five health-related components of fitness and match an activity to each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Essay planning* (90 min): Choose a prompt (How does Brian change?) and fill an organizer with a thesis, three reasons and quotations.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Adding with regrouping* (60 min): Add 15 decimals including different numbers of places (4.7 + 12.35) by lining up place values and using zero placeholders.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Closed reaction* (45 min): Repeat the reaction in a bottle sealed with a balloon and compare the before and after weights.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Primary source: John Smith's map* (45 min): Analyze John Smith's 1612 map of Virginia, noting Powhatan towns and what the map reveals about the land.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Tints and shades* (30 min): Paint a tint and shade strip for one color, adding white and black in steps.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Changing direction* (30 min): Practice inside and outside cuts around cones and a figure-8 pattern.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Endurance test* (20 min): Time a 10-minute walk/run and record distance and heart rate.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Drafting* (90 min): Draft a five-paragraph literary essay with an introduction, three body paragraphs and a conclusion.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Subtracting decimals* (60 min): Subtract 15 problems like 8 - 2.36 using models and then the written method, checking by adding.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Explaining the difference* (45 min): Graph both trials and explain why weight seemed to decrease in the open cup using particles of gas escaping.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Powhatan and Pocahontas* (45 min): Read about the Powhatan Confederacy and the real Pocahontas and separate fact from legend.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Color schemes* (30 min): Paint the same simple design three times with complementary, analogous and monochromatic schemes.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Speed dribbling* (30 min): Dribble 20 m at speed with longer touches and stop the ball with the sole.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Strength tests* (20 min): Count curl-ups and push-ups in 30 seconds each and record them.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 44, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Revising and editing* (90 min): Revise for transition words and evidence explanation; edit for perfect-tense verbs and spelling.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Shopping problems* (60 min): Use a store flyer to plan purchases under $50, adding prices and subtracting from the budget for 5 scenarios.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Rust results* (45 min): Examine the rust jars, describe evidence of a new substance, and connect the result to particles rearranging.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Tobacco and Anglicanism* (45 min): Explain how tobacco saved the colony and describe the role of the Anglican Church in Virginia.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Brush care* (30 min): Practice proper brush cleaning and palette care, then compare how each color scheme changes mood.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Dribble tag* (30 min): Play dribble tag where players must keep control while avoiding a tagger.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Flexibility test* (20 min): Do a sit-and-reach test with a yardstick on a box and record the best of three.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 45, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Unit 2 assessment* (90 min): Take the Unit 2 assessment on Hatchet (character, theme, figurative language, roots) and submit the final essay.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a 12-problem quiz on decimal addition and subtraction including 2 word problems.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Hatchet chapters 18–19 and epilogue; literary essay
- Objective: Analyze how the epilogue affects meaning
- Objective: Write a literary response essay using evidence
- Vocabulary: epilogue, resolution, thesis, evidence
- Materials: Hatchet, essay organizer
- Assessment: Literary essay rubric; Unit 2 test.

### Math 5: Adding and subtracting decimals
- Objective: Add and subtract decimals to hundredths using models and place value
- Objective: Estimate decimal sums and differences
- Vocabulary: sum, difference, regroup, align
- Materials: hundredths grids, play money, store flyer
- Assessment: Quiz on decimal addition and subtraction; budget scenarios checked for accuracy.

### Science 5: Conservation of weight in reactions
- Objective: Measure weight in open and closed reactions
- Objective: Explain conservation using a particle model
- Vocabulary: open system, closed system, rearrange, conserve
- Materials: plastic bottle, balloon, baking soda, vinegar, kitchen scale, graph paper
- Assessment: Unit 2 test; CER on conservation in chemical reactions.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Jamestown and the Virginia Colony
- Objective: Explain why Jamestown was founded and why it struggled
- Objective: Describe relations with the Powhatan Confederacy
- Vocabulary: Jamestown, Powhatan, charter, tobacco, House of Burgesses
- Materials: John Smith's 1612 map of Virginia (Library of Congress), Jamestown site photos
- Assessment: Map analysis sheet and cause-effect chart.

### Art & Music 5: Color theory
- Objective: Mix primary, secondary and tertiary colors
- Objective: Create tints, shades and color schemes
- Vocabulary: primary, secondary, tertiary, complementary, tint, shade, analogous
- Materials: tempera or watercolor paints (red, yellow, blue, white, black), brushes, water cups, palette
- Assessment: Color wheel, scheme studies and quiz.

### PE 5: Dribbling and ball control with feet
- Objective: Dribble with inside and outside of the foot
- Objective: Change speed and direction with control
- Vocabulary: dribble, inside of foot, outside of foot, touch, cut
- Materials: soccer ball, cones
- Assessment: Cone course time and control checklist.

### Health 5: Components of physical fitness
- Objective: Define the five health-related components of fitness
- Objective: Measure personal baselines
- Vocabulary: cardiorespiratory endurance, muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility, body composition
- Materials: stopwatch, yardstick, fitness log
- Assessment: Fitness baseline chart and goals.
