## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Theme from actions* (90 min): Read 'Thank You, Ma'am' and track what Mrs. Jones does and says at three points; write a theme statement supported by those actions.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Three forms* (60 min): Convert 12 decimals among standard form, word form (three and fifty-two thousandths) and expanded form including fraction form like 3 x 1 + 5 x (1/100).
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Describing properties* (45 min): Observe 8 material samples and record color, texture, hardness (scratch test) and flexibility in a table.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Plains nations* (45 min): Read about the Lakota and other Plains nations and diagram the many uses of the bison.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Showing depth* (30 min): Draw a landscape that uses overlapping, size change and placement to show foreground, middle ground and background.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Curl-up form* (30 min): Learn FITNESSGRAM curl-up form with a cadence and practice 3 sets.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Growth timeline* (20 min): Make a timeline of your own growth using family photos and recorded heights, and note skills gained at each stage.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Dialogue craft* (90 min): Study how Hughes uses dialogue to show character; punctuate 10 lines of dialogue and learn interjections (Oh!, Well,).
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Modeling thousandths* (60 min): Shade 1,000-square grids to show 0.4, 0.40 and 0.400 and explain in writing why these are equivalent.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Magnetism and reflectivity* (45 min): Test each sample with a magnet and a flashlight beam and add results to the table.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Woodlands life* (45 min): Read about Eastern Woodlands peoples (Haudenosaunee, Cherokee) and compare a longhouse to a tipi.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *One-point perspective* (30 min): Draw 3D boxes in one-point perspective with a horizon line and vanishing point.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Push-up form* (30 min): Learn the 90-degree push-up and practice with modified versions (wall, knee) to build up.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Life cycle* (20 min): Compare stages of the human life cycle (infancy, childhood, adolescence, adulthood, older adulthood) in a 5-column chart.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Drafting the lead* (90 min): Draft the opening of the personal narrative three ways (action, dialogue, sensory description) and choose the strongest.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Comparing decimals* (60 min): Compare 20 pairs of decimals (like 0.305 vs 0.35) by lining up place values and record with symbols.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Electrical conductivity* (45 min): Build a simple circuit with a battery and bulb and test which samples complete the circuit.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Primary source: Great Law of Peace* (45 min): Analyze an excerpt of the Great Law of Peace and the Hiawatha wampum belt and explain how the five (later six) nations governed together.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Perspective road* (30 min): Draw a road or hallway disappearing to a vanishing point with trees or doors along it.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Trunk lift and flexibility* (30 min): Practice the trunk lift and back-saver sit-and-reach and shoulder stretch with correct technique.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Is it reliable?* (20 min): Compare a .gov health page, a product ad and a social-media post on the same topic using a 4-question reliability checklist.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Drafting the middle* (90 min): Draft the rest of the narrative, including at least 4 lines of dialogue and commas in a series of sensory details.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Ordering game* (60 min): Play 'Decimal Line-Up': draw 5 index cards with decimals and order them least to greatest on a number line, then order Olympic race times.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Thermal conductivity* (45 min): Place metal, plastic and wooden spoons in hot water and compare which handle warms fastest after 3 minutes.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Folklore* (45 min): Read a Haudenosaunee or Lakota traditional story and identify what it teaches about values or nature.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Place of significance* (30 min): Begin a perspective drawing of your street, room or a favorite place.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Strength circuit* (30 min): Complete a 6-station circuit (squats, planks, curl-ups, push-ups, wall sit, lunges) 30 seconds each.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Trusted adults* (20 min): List at least four trusted adults and two valid sources you could use for health questions and discuss with a parent.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 15, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Discuss theme with a partner using 'I think... because in the text...' stems, then take a quiz on dialogue punctuation and commas in a series.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz writing decimals in three forms and comparing/ordering 10 sets, then explain one error a student might make.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Use the property table to identify 3 'mystery' materials and explain which properties gave them away.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz on Plains and Woodlands peoples and explain one feature of Haudenosaunee government.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Finish the place drawing and label its horizon line and vanishing point.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Watch a partner perform each test and give feedback on form using a checklist.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a source-sorting activity (reliable/unreliable) and write a paragraph on how you will check health information.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: 'Thank You, Ma'am' by Langston Hughes; drafting narratives
- Objective: Determine theme from how a character responds to a challenge
- Objective: Draft a narrative using dialogue and description
- Vocabulary: theme, dialogue, sensory detail, interjection
- Materials: 'Thank You, Ma'am' (CommonLit), dialogue punctuation anchor chart
- Assessment: Theme statement with evidence; complete narrative draft; punctuation quiz.

### Math 5: Reading, writing and comparing decimals to thousandths
- Objective: Read and write decimals in standard, word and expanded form
- Objective: Compare decimals using >, = and <
- Vocabulary: expanded form, word form, standard form, equivalent decimals
- Materials: thousandths grid paper, index cards, place value chart
- Assessment: Quiz on decimal forms and comparisons; observation of ordering game reasoning.

### Science 5: Properties of materials
- Objective: Measure and describe physical properties
- Objective: Use properties to identify materials
- Vocabulary: property, hardness, reflectivity, conductivity, magnetism, solubility
- Materials: samples: aluminum foil, paper clip, plastic spoon, wooden stick, rubber band, glass marble, salt, baking soda, magnet, flashlight, battery, bulb and wires (simple circuit kit), cups of water
- Assessment: Property data table and mystery-material identification with explanations.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Peoples of the Great Plains and the Eastern Woodlands
- Objective: Explain how Plains nations relied on the bison
- Objective: Describe the Haudenosaunee Confederacy's government
- Vocabulary: nomadic, bison, tipi, longhouse, confederacy, Haudenosaunee, wampum
- Materials: excerpt of the Haudenosaunee Great Law of Peace (NMAI Native Knowledge 360), image of the Hiawatha Belt, chart paper
- Assessment: Quiz and primary-source analysis sheet.

### Art & Music 5: Perspective and space
- Objective: Use overlapping, size and placement to show depth
- Objective: Draw in one-point perspective
- Vocabulary: perspective, horizon line, vanishing point, foreground, background
- Materials: ruler, pencil, sketchbook
- Assessment: One-point perspective drawing of a personally significant place.

### PE 5: Muscular strength, endurance and flexibility
- Objective: Perform curl-ups, push-ups and trunk lifts with correct form
- Objective: Practice sit-and-reach and shoulder stretch
- Vocabulary: curl-up, 90-degree push-up, trunk lift, sit-and-reach, form
- Materials: mat or towel, curl-up strip (cardboard 4.5 in wide), ruler, box for sit-and-reach
- Assessment: Form checklist for each fitness test item.

### Health 5: Growing and changing; reliable health information
- Objective: Describe how bodies grow from childhood to adolescence
- Objective: Evaluate the reliability of health sources
- Vocabulary: growth, adolescence, valid source, trusted adult
- Materials: baby photos, computer
- Assessment: Source-sorting activity and paragraph.
