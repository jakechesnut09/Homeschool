## Day 1 — Monday (school day 111, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *What is an argument?* (90 min): Read a short opinion article and map its claim, reasons and evidence on a graphic organizer.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Serving sizes* (60 min): Use a 1/3 cup measure to find how many servings are in 2 cups of rice, then solve 5 similar problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Case study* (45 min): Read about a California wildfire followed by winter mudslides and list each sphere involved.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Common Sense* (45 min): Read a short excerpt of Common Sense and explain how Paine persuaded ordinary colonists.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Rounds* (30 min): Sing 'Row, Row, Row Your Boat' and 'Frère Jacques' as rounds with family members.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Choreography tools* (30 min): Learn to vary levels, formations, unison and canon and try each in short phrases.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Concept map* (20 min): Build a concept map linking hormones, physical changes, emotions, hygiene and trusted adults.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 112, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Evaluating evidence* (90 min): Rate 10 pieces of evidence as fact, statistic, expert quote or opinion and decide which are strongest.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Multiply or divide?* (60 min): Sort 10 fraction word problems as multiplication or division and solve them with diagrams.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Cause-effect chain* (45 min): Build a cause-and-effect chain showing how the fire changed the biosphere and how rain then affected the geosphere.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Drafting the Declaration* (45 min): Learn about the Committee of Five (Jefferson, Adams, Franklin, Sherman, Livingston) and how the Declaration was written.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Partner songs* (30 min): Sing two partner songs together ('This Old Man' and 'Skip to My Lou') to create harmony.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Planning* (30 min): Plan a 64-count dance with a partner or family member and write the counts.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Stop teasing* (20 min): Role-play responding to teasing about early or late development.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 113, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Two sides* (90 min): Read paired articles on whether zoos help animals and compare which reasons each author uses.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Multi-step problems* (60 min): Solve 4 multi-step problems combining fraction multiplication, division and addition.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Model poster* (45 min): Create a poster model of the event with arrows labeling each sphere interaction.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *The Preamble* (45 min): Paraphrase the Declaration's second paragraph and explain unalienable rights and consent of the governed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Chords* (30 min): Use Chrome Music Lab Chords to build I and V chords in C and listen for when they change in a song.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Rehearsing* (30 min): Rehearse the dance, cleaning up timing and transitions.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Self-care kit* (20 min): Assemble or list a personal self-care kit for school or travel.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 114, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Listening for claims* (90 min): Watch a short student speech or news segment and summarize the speaker's claims and supporting reasons.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a cumulative review of fractions as division, multiplication, scaling and division.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Presentation* (45 min): Present the model to family and answer questions about how humans are part of the biosphere.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Grievances* (45 min): Match 5 grievances against King George III to events studied earlier.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Two-chord song* (30 min): Accompany a familiar two-chord song (e.g., 'Clementine') by playing I and V on a virtual keyboard or ukulele.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Recording* (30 min): Record the dance and revise one section.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Advice column* (20 min): Write answers to three advice-column questions from fictional 5th graders about puberty.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 115, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a quiz identifying claims and matching reasons to points in a new article.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit 5 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 5 test on multiplying and dividing fractions.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Unit 6 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 6 assessment on Earth's spheres and their interactions.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Unit 6 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 6 test on the causes of the Revolution and the Declaration.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Analyzing arguments: reasons and evidence
- Objective: Identify an author's points and supporting reasons and evidence
- Objective: Summarize a speaker's claims
- Vocabulary: claim, reason, evidence, counterclaim, persuade
- Materials: paired opinion articles from CommonLit or ReadWorks (e.g., on homework or zoos), argument mapping sheet
- Assessment: Argument maps; reasons-and-evidence quiz.

### Math 5: Fraction division word problems and unit assessment
- Objective: Solve real-world problems with fraction division
- Objective: Choose between fraction multiplication and division
- Vocabulary: equal shares, serving, operation
- Materials: measuring cups, rice or water
- Assessment: Unit 5 test covering 5.NF.B.3–B.7.

### Science 5: Sphere interactions in a California event
- Objective: Develop a model of sphere interactions in a real event
- Objective: Communicate the model to others
- Vocabulary: wildfire, mudslide, drought, system model
- Materials: articles on California wildfires and mudslides, poster board
- Assessment: Model poster rubric and Unit 6 test.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Common Sense and the Declaration of Independence
- Objective: Analyze key ideas in the Declaration
- Objective: Describe the people who drafted and signed it
- Vocabulary: unalienable rights, consent of the governed, grievance, independence, Common Sense
- Materials: Declaration of Independence transcript (National Archives), excerpt of Thomas Paine's Common Sense
- Assessment: Declaration paraphrase and Unit 6 test.

### Art & Music 5: Harmony: rounds, partner songs and chords
- Objective: Sing in harmony in a round
- Objective: Identify I and V chords
- Vocabulary: harmony, round, partner song, chord, I chord, V chord
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Chords, song sheets
- Assessment: Round performance and chord-change analysis.

### PE 5: Creating a dance
- Objective: Design a dance using patterns and formations
- Objective: Collaborate with others
- Vocabulary: choreography, formation, canon, unison, level
- Materials: music of choice, recording device
- Assessment: Original dance rubric.

### Health 5: Puberty review and respect for differences
- Objective: Summarize physical, emotional and social changes
- Objective: Show respect for differences in development
- Vocabulary: respect, empathy, teasing, self-care
- Materials: poster paper
- Assessment: Growth and Development unit test.
