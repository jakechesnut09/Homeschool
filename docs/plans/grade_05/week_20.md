## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Las Ciruelas* (90 min): Read 'Las Ciruelas' and list the arguments of Marta and Miguel about the strike.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Folding paper* (60 min): Fold paper to show 1/2 of 1/3 and 2/3 of 3/4 and record the product for 6 problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Four spheres* (45 min): Watch a NASA Space Place video on Earth's systems and create a four-quadrant chart with examples of each sphere.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Britain's debt* (45 min): Role-play Parliament and colonists debating who should pay for the French and Indian War.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Quilts as stories* (30 min): Study Harriet Powers's Bible Quilt and a Gee's Bend quilt and discuss how quilts tell stories.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Rules* (30 min): Learn traveling, double dribble and fouls and practice calling them fairly.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Menstrual cycle* (20 min): Watch an AMAZE video and draw a simple 28-day cycle diagram with labels.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 97, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Las Papas* (90 min): Read 'Las Papas' and explain how Mama's illness changes Esperanza's responsibilities.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Area models* (60 min): Shade overlapping regions to solve 10 fraction x fraction problems, then notice the numerator x numerator / denominator x denominator rule.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Outdoor survey* (45 min): Survey a yard or park and list 10 things you observe, labeling each with its sphere.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Stamp Act* (45 min): Examine an image of a tax stamp and a protest broadside and list items that were taxed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Block patterns* (30 min): Draw 3 traditional quilt block patterns (Log Cabin, Flying Geese, Star) on graph paper.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Skill stations* (30 min): Rotate through dribble, pass and shoot stations as a warm-up, then play 3-on-3.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Period supplies* (20 min): Learn about pads, tampons and period underwear, and pack a period-preparedness kit list.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 98, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Informational connection* (90 min): Read an informational article on 1930s farmworkers and Mexican repatriation and note 3 facts the novel reflects.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Fractional side lengths* (60 min): Tile a 3/4 inch by 2/3 inch rectangle with unit fraction squares and compute areas of 8 rectangles by multiplying sides.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Finding interactions* (45 min): Identify 5 interactions from the survey (rain soaking soil, roots breaking rock) and draw arrows between spheres.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Resistance* (45 min): Read about the Stamp Act Congress, boycotts and the Sons and Daughters of Liberty.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Original square* (30 min): Combine ideas to design an original paper quilt square that tells something about your family.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Modified game* (30 min): Play a modified game where every player must receive a pass before a shot.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Male changes* (20 min): Read about voice changes, erections and nocturnal emissions as normal parts of puberty.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 99, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Los Aguacates* (90 min): Read 'Los Aguacates' and practice synonyms, antonyms and homographs (wind/wind, lead/lead) with 15 words.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Mixed numbers* (60 min): Multiply 8 mixed number problems like 2 1/2 x 1 1/3 using area models with partial products.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Satellite views* (45 min): Examine NASA satellite images of a hurricane and a wildfire and identify which spheres are interacting.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Townshend Acts* (45 min): Explain the Townshend Acts and the boycott of British goods; calculate the cost of taxed items in a simple chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Family quilt* (30 min): Join squares from family members into a paper 'quilt' showing unity and variety.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Conflict resolution* (30 min): Role-play resolving three common game disputes respectfully.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Hygiene connection* (20 min): Write a hygiene plan for managing periods or sweat, odor and shaving.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 100, ~320 min)

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Esperanza Rising: Las Ciruelas, Las Papas, Los Aguacates
- Objective: Analyze conflict between characters' viewpoints on the strike
- Objective: Distinguish synonyms, antonyms and homographs
- Vocabulary: strike, union, repatriation, homograph
- Materials: Esperanza Rising, ReadWorks or CommonLit article on the Great Depression and farmworkers
- Assessment: Two-sides paragraph; informational notes; word relationships quiz.

### Math 5: Multiplying fractions by fractions and area
- Objective: Multiply fractions by fractions using area models
- Objective: Find areas of rectangles with fractional side lengths
- Vocabulary: area model, numerator, denominator, tiling
- Materials: grid paper, colored pencils, rulers
- Assessment: Quiz on fraction multiplication and fractional area.

### Science 5: Introducing Earth's four spheres
- Objective: Define the geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere and biosphere
- Objective: Find sphere interactions in a local place
- Vocabulary: geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, biosphere, interaction
- Materials: clipboard, colored pencils, NASA Earth images
- Assessment: Sphere interaction chart and quiz.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Taxation without representation
- Objective: Explain British tax policies after 1763
- Objective: Describe colonial resistance
- Vocabulary: Stamp Act, Townshend Acts, boycott, repeal, Sons of Liberty, Parliament
- Materials: image of a 1765 tax stamp, Stamp Act protest broadside (Library of Congress)
- Assessment: Colonist letter and broadside analysis.

### Art & Music 5: Quilts and collaborative design
- Objective: Analyze quilts as art and history
- Objective: Combine ideas into an original quilt square design
- Vocabulary: quilt, block, radial symmetry, repetition, unity
- Materials: images of historic quilts (Gee's Bend, Harriet Powers's Bible Quilt), colored paper, glue, ruler
- Assessment: Quilt square and Unit 5 reflection.

### PE 5: Basketball games and fair play
- Objective: Apply skills in a modified game
- Objective: Demonstrate sportsmanship and rule knowledge
- Vocabulary: traveling, double dribble, foul, fair play
- Materials: basketball, hoop
- Assessment: Basketball skills test and sportsmanship self-rating.

### Health 5: Menstruation and other normal changes
- Objective: Explain the menstrual cycle
- Objective: Explain erections and nocturnal emissions as normal
- Vocabulary: menstruation, menstrual cycle, pad, tampon, nocturnal emission, erection
- Materials: menstrual-cycle diagram, product samples (optional)
- Assessment: Myth-or-fact quiz.
