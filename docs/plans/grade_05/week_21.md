## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Los Espárragos* (90 min): Read 'Los Espárragos' and discuss the betrayal and Esperanza's decision about Abuelita's money.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Scaling predictions* (60 min): Without calculating, decide whether 15 products like 3/4 x 12 or 1 1/2 x 12 are greater than, less than or equal to 12, and justify.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Shake test* (45 min): Shake sugar cubes or small rocks in a jar with water for 3 minutes and observe physical weathering.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Boston Massacre engraving* (45 min): Analyze Paul Revere's engraving, listing details that make the British look guilty.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Melodic contour* (30 min): Listen to 4 melodies and draw their contour shapes with lines.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Basic figures* (30 min): Learn do-si-do, promenade, swing and allemande with family partners.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Feelings wheel* (20 min): Use a feelings wheel to name emotions common in puberty and write about one.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Los Duraznos* (90 min): Read 'Los Duraznos' and track the symbol of the phoenix and the blanket through the book.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Resizing pictures* (60 min): Redraw a simple grid picture at 1/2 size and 1 1/2 size and explain how the scale factor changed it.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Stream table* (45 min): Build a sloped sand-and-soil pan and pour water slowly to observe erosion and deposition; sketch the channel and delta.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Another account* (45 min): Compare the engraving with Captain Preston's account and decide why the stories differ.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Steps, skips and leaps* (30 min): Identify steps, skips and leaps in 'Ode to Joy' and 'Twinkle, Twinkle' notation.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Virginia Reel* (30 min): Learn and perform the Virginia Reel (a colonial-era dance tied to HSS).
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Changing friendships* (20 min): Read two scenarios about friends drifting apart and use the decision steps to solve them.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Las Uvas* (90 min): Read the final chapter and explain why the novel ends with the same fruit it begins with.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Real-world problems* (60 min): Solve 6 problems such as scaling a recipe by 2/3 or finding 3/8 of a 2 1/2 mile trail.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Variables* (45 min): Repeat the stream table test with a steeper slope and with plants (grass clumps) and compare erosion.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Boston Tea Party* (45 min): Read about the Tea Act and Tea Party and write a newspaper headline from both Patriot and Loyalist views.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Note naming* (30 min): Practice naming notes on the treble staff with musictheory.net exercises until scoring 90%.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Line dance* (30 min): Learn grapevine, step-touch and pivot turns in a beginner line dance.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Crushes and respect* (20 min): Discuss crushes as normal and list ways to treat others respectfully, including respecting 'no'.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 104, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Theme* (90 min): Write 2 theme statements and support each with 3 pieces of evidence from across the novel.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Mixed practice* (60 min): Complete 15 mixed fraction multiplication problems and simplify answers.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Wind erosion* (45 min): Use a hair dryer on dry sand to model wind erosion and compare to wet sand.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Coercive Acts and Committees* (45 min): List the Coercive Acts and explain how Committees of Correspondence helped colonies unite.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Recorder: F-sharp and low D* (30 min): Learn low D and F-sharp on recorder and play a D-major warm-up.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Calling* (30 min): Take turns being the caller and following calls on the beat.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Gender stereotypes* (20 min): Sort statements about how boys and girls 'should' act into stereotypes vs. facts and discuss respect for all.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 105, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a whole-novel reading assessment on plot, character change and symbolism.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz with scaling questions and multi-step fraction multiplication word problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write about how the sphere interactions changed the land in the models and connect to a CA example (Yosemite Valley or coastal cliffs).
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz and create a cause-and-effect chain from the Stamp Act to the Coercive Acts.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Take a note-naming and interval quiz and play an 8-measure melody.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform the Virginia Reel and line dance for a family audience.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a journal entry on a coping strategy for emotional changes and complete a check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Esperanza Rising: Los Espárragos, Los Duraznos, Las Uvas
- Objective: Determine the novel's theme
- Objective: Explain how chapter titles structure the novel
- Vocabulary: symbol, cycle, rising, phoenix
- Materials: Esperanza Rising, theme organizer
- Assessment: Theme organizer; novel assessment.

### Math 5: Multiplication as scaling and real-world problems
- Objective: Predict product size without multiplying
- Objective: Solve real-world fraction multiplication problems
- Vocabulary: scaling, resize, greater than 1, less than 1
- Materials: photo or drawing to resize, grid paper
- Assessment: Quiz on scaling and real-world fraction multiplication.

### Science 5: Weathering, erosion and deposition
- Objective: Model how water and wind shape land
- Objective: Explain geosphere-hydrosphere interactions
- Vocabulary: weathering, erosion, deposition, sediment, delta
- Materials: baking pan, sand and soil, watering can, rocks, jar with lid, sugar cubes, hair dryer
- Assessment: Stream table sketches and written explanation.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Boston Massacre, Boston Tea Party and the Coercive Acts
- Objective: Analyze Paul Revere's engraving as propaganda
- Objective: Explain how the Tea Party led to the Coercive Acts
- Vocabulary: propaganda, massacre, Tea Act, Coercive (Intolerable) Acts, Committees of Correspondence
- Materials: Paul Revere's 1770 engraving 'The Bloody Massacre', excerpt of Captain Preston's account
- Assessment: Engraving analysis and cause-effect chain.

### Art & Music 5: Melody: steps, skips and leaps
- Objective: Identify melodic contour and intervals
- Objective: Read notes on the treble staff
- Vocabulary: melody, contour, step, skip, leap, interval
- Materials: musictheory.net note identification, recorder or virtual keyboard
- Assessment: Note-naming quiz and melody performance.

### PE 5: Square dance and line dance basics
- Objective: Perform basic square and line dance figures
- Objective: Respond to calls and rhythm
- Vocabulary: promenade, do-si-do, allemande, grapevine, caller
- Materials: music for 'Virginia Reel' or a simple square dance and a line dance, open space
- Assessment: Dance performance checklist.

### Health 5: Emotional and social changes
- Objective: Describe emotional changes of puberty
- Objective: Practice handling changing friendships and crushes
- Vocabulary: emotions, independence, peer pressure, crush, stereotype
- Materials: scenario cards, health journal
- Assessment: Journal entry and check.
