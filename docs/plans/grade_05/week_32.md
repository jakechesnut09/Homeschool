## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Myths across cultures: comparing themes
- Objective: Compare myths from different cultures on similar themes
- Objective: Explain adages and proverbs from folklore
- Vocabulary: origin myth, trickster, culture, universal theme
- Materials: Greek myth of Prometheus, Native American and West African trickster or origin tales (library collections or CommonLit), comparison matrix
- Assessment: Comparison paragraphs; cold-read quiz.

### Math 5: Attributes of two-dimensional figures
- Objective: Identify attributes of polygons
- Objective: Classify triangles by sides and angles
- Vocabulary: polygon, parallel, perpendicular, acute, obtuse, right, equilateral, isosceles, scalene
- Materials: protractor, rulers, shape cut-outs
- Assessment: Quiz on attributes and classifying triangles.

### Science 5: Shadows during the day
- Objective: Measure and graph shadow length and direction
- Objective: Relate shadow patterns to Earth's rotation
- Vocabulary: shadow, rotation, axis, sundial, noon
- Materials: stick or pencil in clay, chalk, measuring tape, compass, flashlight, globe
- Assessment: Shadow graph and written explanation.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Crossing the Appalachians and new immigrants
- Objective: Describe routes and transportation west
- Objective: Discuss immigration waves 1789–1850
- Vocabulary: Cumberland Gap, Wilderness Road, Erie Canal, flatboat, steamboat, immigrant
- Materials: map of the U.S. in 1850, Erie Canal song lyrics ('Low Bridge, Everybody Down')
- Assessment: 1850 map and quiz.

### Art & Music 5: Revising and presenting the composition
- Objective: Revise a composition using feedback
- Objective: Present and explain expressive intent
- Vocabulary: revision, intent, title, program note
- Materials: Song Maker composition, recorder or keyboard
- Assessment: Final composition and program note rubric.

### PE 5: FITT principle and planning
- Objective: Explain the FITT principle
- Objective: Draft a personal fitness plan
- Vocabulary: FITT, frequency, intensity, time, type, overload
- Materials: baseline fitness scores, planning template, activity log
- Assessment: Personal fitness plan draft.

### Health 5: Decision making in risky situations
- Objective: Apply the decision process to ATOD scenarios
- Objective: Evaluate consequences
- Vocabulary: consequence, alternative, values, evaluate
- Materials: scenario cards, decision chart
- Assessment: Decision-making assessment.
