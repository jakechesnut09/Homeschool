# Math 5 (Grade 5)

Fifth-grade mathematics following the CA CCSS sequence: place value through thousandths, fluency with the multi-digit multiplication algorithm, division with two-digit divisors, all four operations with decimals, and the big shift to adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing fractions. Students also build volume concepts, convert measurement units, graph on the coordinate plane, and classify two-dimensional figures in a hierarchy. Lessons move from concrete models (place value disks, area models, fraction strips, unit cubes) to written strategies and real-world problem solving.

*Framework:* California Common Core State Standards for Mathematics; 2023 CA Mathematics Framework  
*Time:* 60 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Place Value and Decimal Fractions (weeks 1–4)
Our base-ten system extends to the right of the decimal point; each place is 10 times the place to its right.

- Week 1: Place value relationships to millions and thousandths
- Week 2: Powers of 10 and exponents
- Week 3: Reading, writing and comparing decimals to thousandths
- Week 4: Rounding decimals and unit assessment

### Unit 2: Multi-Digit Whole Number Multiplication and Division (weeks 5–8)
Place value and the properties of operations make efficient algorithms for multiplying and dividing large numbers.

- Week 5: Multiplying multi-digit whole numbers
- Week 6: Numerical expressions and multiplication word problems
- Week 7: Division with two-digit divisors: models
- Week 8: Estimating quotients, interpreting remainders, unit assessment

### Unit 3: Decimal Operations (weeks 9–12)
The same place value reasoning used for whole numbers works for adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing decimals.

- Week 9: Adding and subtracting decimals
- Week 10: Multiplying decimals
- Week 11: Dividing decimals
- Week 12: Decimal word problems and unit assessment

### Unit 4: Adding and Subtracting Fractions (weeks 13–17)
Fractions can only be combined when they refer to same-sized parts, so we rename them with common denominators.

- Week 13: Equivalent fractions and common denominators
- Week 14: Adding and subtracting fractions with unlike denominators
- Week 15: Adding and subtracting mixed numbers
- Week 16: Fraction word problems and estimation
- Week 17: Line plots with fractional data and unit assessment

### Unit 5: Multiplying and Dividing Fractions (weeks 18–23)
A fraction is a division, and multiplying by a fraction scales a quantity up or down.

- Week 18: Fractions as division
- Week 19: Multiplying a fraction and a whole number
- Week 20: Multiplying fractions by fractions and area
- Week 21: Multiplication as scaling and real-world problems
- Week 22: Dividing unit fractions and whole numbers
- Week 23: Fraction division word problems and unit assessment

### Unit 6: Volume (weeks 24–26)
Volume measures how many unit cubes fill a solid, and it can be found by multiplying layers.

- Week 24: Understanding volume with unit cubes
- Week 25: Volume formulas
- Week 26: Composite figures and volume design project

### Unit 7: Measurement Conversions (weeks 27–28)
Units within a system are related by multiplication, so converting means multiplying or dividing.

- Week 27: Converting customary units
- Week 28: Metric conversions and multi-step measurement problems

### Unit 8: Coordinate Plane and Numerical Patterns (weeks 29–31)
Ordered pairs locate points on a plane and let us see relationships between two patterns.

- Week 29: The coordinate plane
- Week 30: Graphing real-world problems
- Week 31: Numerical patterns and ordered pairs

### Unit 9: Classifying Two-Dimensional Figures (weeks 32–33)
Shapes belong to families; a square is a rectangle, a rhombus, a parallelogram and a quadrilateral all at once.

- Week 32: Attributes of two-dimensional figures
- Week 33: Hierarchy of quadrilaterals

### Unit 10: Year Review and Capstone (weeks 34–36)
Fifth-grade math tools combine to solve real, multi-step problems.

- Week 34: Cumulative review: whole numbers, decimals and expressions
- Week 35: Cumulative review: fractions, volume and geometry
- Week 36: Capstone: Plan a backyard garden

## Standards

- **5.G.A.1** Use a pair of perpendicular number lines (axes) to define a coordinate system and understand ordered pairs.
- **5.G.A.2** Represent real-world and mathematical problems by graphing points in the first quadrant and interpret coordinate values in context.
- **5.G.B.3** Understand that attributes belonging to a category of two-dimensional figures also belong to all subcategories of that category.
- **5.G.B.4** Classify two-dimensional figures in a hierarchy based on properties.
- **5.MD.A.1** Convert among different-sized standard measurement units within a system and use conversions in multi-step real-world problems.
- **5.MD.B.2** Make a line plot to display a data set of measurements in fractions of a unit and use fraction operations to solve problems from it.
- **5.MD.C.3** Recognize volume as an attribute of solid figures measured in unit cubes.
- **5.MD.C.4** Measure volumes by counting unit cubes, using cubic cm, cubic in, cubic ft, and improvised units.
- **5.MD.C.5** Relate volume to multiplication and addition; apply V = l x w x h and V = B x h, and find volumes of figures composed of two right rectangular prisms.
- **5.NBT.A.1** Recognize that a digit in one place represents 10 times what it represents in the place to its right and 1/10 of what it represents to its left.
- **5.NBT.A.2** Explain patterns in the number of zeros and decimal point placement when multiplying or dividing by powers of 10; use whole-number exponents for powers of 10.
- **5.NBT.A.3** Read, write, and compare decimals to thousandths using base-ten numerals, number names, and expanded form.
- **5.NBT.A.4** Use place value understanding to round decimals to any place.
- **5.NBT.B.5** Fluently multiply multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm.
- **5.NBT.B.6** Find whole-number quotients of up to four-digit dividends and two-digit divisors using place value strategies, properties, and models.
- **5.NBT.B.7** Add, subtract, multiply, and divide decimals to hundredths using models, place value strategies, and properties; explain the reasoning.
- **5.NF.A.1** Add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators (including mixed numbers) by replacing them with equivalent fractions.
- **5.NF.A.2** Solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions; use benchmark fractions and number sense to check reasonableness.
- **5.NF.B.3** Interpret a fraction as division of the numerator by the denominator and solve word problems with quotients that are fractions or mixed numbers.
- **5.NF.B.4** Apply and extend understanding of multiplication to multiply a fraction or whole number by a fraction, including area of rectangles with fractional side lengths.
- **5.NF.B.5** Interpret multiplication as scaling (resizing) by comparing the size of a product to the size of one factor.
- **5.NF.B.6** Solve real-world problems involving multiplication of fractions and mixed numbers.
- **5.NF.B.7** Divide unit fractions by whole numbers and whole numbers by unit fractions, and solve related word problems.
- **5.OA.A.1** Use parentheses, brackets, or braces in numerical expressions and evaluate expressions with these symbols.
- **5.OA.A.2** Write simple expressions that record calculations with numbers and interpret numerical expressions without evaluating them.
- **5.OA.B.3** Generate two numerical patterns from two given rules, identify relationships between corresponding terms, and graph the ordered pairs.
