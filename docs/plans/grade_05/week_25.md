## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Introduction* (90 min): Draft an introduction with a hook, background and opinion statement.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *V = l x w x h* (60 min): Connect layers of cubes to the formula and find volumes of 12 prisms with given dimensions.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Water cycle in a bag* (45 min): Draw the water cycle on a zip bag with a little water, tape it to a sunny window, and observe evaporation and condensation.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *France joins* (45 min): Read about Franklin's negotiations in Paris and the French alliance of 1778.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Paper techniques* (30 min): Practice curling, folding, scoring, fringing and tab joints to make a paper techniques sampler.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Overhead set* (30 min): Practice the set with 'triangle hands' using self-sets and partner tosses.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *What is a vape?* (20 min): Read the FDA fact sheet on e-cigarettes and list what is in vape aerosol.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Body paragraphs* (90 min): Draft three body paragraphs linking reasons and evidence with words like 'consequently' and 'specifically'.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *V = B x h* (60 min): Find the base area and multiply by height for 10 prisms; show the associative property gives the same volume.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *California snowpack* (45 min): Read about the Sierra Nevada snowpack and trace on a map how it reaches cities through rivers and aqueducts.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Foreign heroes* (45 min): Create biography cards for Lafayette, von Steuben, Kosciuszko and Bernardo de Gálvez.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Sculptor study* (30 min): Look at Alexander Calder and Ruth Asawa sculptures and discuss positive and negative space.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Underhand serve* (30 min): Practice the underhand serve over a rope net from short, then longer distances.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Myth vs. fact* (20 min): Sort 10 vaping statements (e.g., 'it's just water vapor') into myths and facts with evidence.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Counterclaim and conclusion* (90 min): Add a sentence addressing the other side and draft a conclusion that restates the opinion and calls readers to act.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Measuring real boxes* (60 min): Measure 4 household boxes to the nearest inch and calculate their volumes in cubic inches.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Groundwater* (45 min): Build an aquifer model in a clear cup with gravel, sand and clay and pour 'rain' to show how groundwater forms.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Native alliances* (45 min): Read about the Oneida who supported the Patriots and Joseph Brant (Mohawk) who supported the British, and explain each nation's reasons.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Abstract sculpture* (30 min): Build a freestanding abstract paper sculpture at least 8 inches tall.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Cooperative rally* (30 min): Rally with a partner over the net using bumps and sets and count your best rally.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Flavors and packaging* (20 min): Analyze images of vape packaging and explain how flavors and colors target young people.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Commas for yes/no, tags and address* (90 min): Learn commas for yes/no, tag questions and direct address and edit dialogue examples, then revise the essay with a checklist.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Missing dimensions* (60 min): Solve 8 problems where volume and two dimensions are known and find the third.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Home water audit* (45 min): Measure how much water your family uses in one day by estimating faucet, shower and toilet use.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *African American soldiers* (45 min): Read about the 1st Rhode Island Regiment and Lord Dunmore's Proclamation and explain why Black Americans fought on both sides.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Viewing from all sides* (30 min): Photograph the sculpture from 4 angles and adjust any weak side.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Serve targets* (30 min): Serve to marked zones and explain how force and contact point changed placement.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Real norms* (20 min): Read survey data showing most youth do not vape and make a bar graph.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 125, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Read the draft aloud to a family member who gives two 'glows' and one 'grow', and revise accordingly.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz on volume formulas and missing dimensions.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Unit 7 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 7 assessment on water distribution and graph reading.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz on allies and individuals who shaped the war.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write about how your sculpture uses positive and negative space.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Record serve success rate and best cooperative rally.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write a short public service announcement correcting one vaping myth.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Drafting and revising the opinion essay
- Objective: Draft an opinion essay with linking words
- Objective: Revise for clear reasoning and a strong conclusion
- Vocabulary: consequently, specifically, in addition, conclusion, tag question
- Materials: opinion organizer, revision checklist
- Assessment: Complete revised draft; comma quiz embedded in editing.

### Math 5: Volume formulas
- Objective: Apply V = l x w x h
- Objective: Apply V = B x h
- Vocabulary: length, width, height, base area, formula
- Materials: rulers, cereal and shoe boxes
- Assessment: Quiz on V = lwh and V = Bh; box measurement table.

### Science 5: California's water: sources and the water cycle
- Objective: Trace where California's water comes from
- Objective: Model the water cycle connecting reservoirs
- Vocabulary: snowpack, aqueduct, aquifer, watershed, water cycle
- Materials: map of California water systems, zip bag, marker, tape
- Assessment: Home water audit; Unit 7 test.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Allies, heroes and Native nations in the war
- Objective: Describe foreign contributions to victory
- Objective: Explain Native alliances on both sides
- Vocabulary: ally, Lafayette, von Steuben, Kosciuszko, Bernardo de Gálvez, Oneida
- Materials: biography cards, map of Spanish campaigns on the Gulf Coast
- Assessment: Biography cards and quiz.

### Art & Music 5: Form and space: paper sculpture
- Objective: Transform 2D materials into 3D forms
- Objective: Use positive and negative space
- Vocabulary: form, positive space, negative space, relief, freestanding
- Materials: cardstock, scissors, glue, tape
- Assessment: Paper sculpture and written reflection.

### PE 5: Volleyball: set, serve and rally
- Objective: Perform an overhead set and underhand serve
- Objective: Sustain a cooperative rally
- Vocabulary: set, underhand serve, rally, triangle hands
- Materials: beach ball or soft volleyball, rope net
- Assessment: Serve success rate and rally count.

### Health 5: E-cigarettes and vaping
- Objective: Describe the risks of vaping
- Objective: Identify vaping myths
- Vocabulary: e-cigarette, vape, aerosol, flavoring, social norm
- Materials: FDA vaping fact sheet, computer
- Assessment: PSA script.
