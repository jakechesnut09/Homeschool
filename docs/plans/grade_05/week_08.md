## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 14–15* (90 min): Read chapters 14–15 and plot the major events of the novel so far on a plot mountain.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Compatible numbers* (60 min): Estimate 15 quotients like 3,476 / 58 ≈ 3,600 / 60 and decide whether the actual quotient is greater or less.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Signs of reaction* (45 min): Learn the signs of a new substance (gas, color change, temperature change, new solid) and mix baking soda and vinegar, recording observations.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Atlantic trade routes* (45 min): Draw the Atlantic trade routes and label goods carried on each leg.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *High C and D* (30 min): Learn high C and D and play 'Merrily We Roll Along' and 'Old MacDonald' sections.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Routine planning* (30 min): Plan a 1-minute jump rope routine with at least 6 tricks and transitions.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Food log* (20 min): Log everything you eat and drink for one day by food group.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapter 16* (90 min): Read chapter 16 (the tornado) and list what Brian lost and what he still has, explaining his reaction.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Adjusting estimates* (60 min): Divide 8 problems where the first estimate is too high or too low and adjust the trial quotient.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Milk and lemon* (45 min): Add lemon juice to warm milk and observe curds forming; compare to milk mixed with water.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *The Middle Passage* (45 min): Read an age-appropriate excerpt from Olaudah Equiano's narrative (1789) and discuss what it reveals about the Middle Passage.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Low E* (30 min): Learn low E and play a 5-note warm-up scale with a metronome.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Practice* (30 min): Practice the routine with music, focusing on staying on beat.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Analyze the log* (20 min): Compare your log with MyPlate recommendations and highlight food groups that were over or under.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 38, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapter 17* (90 min): Read chapter 17 and discuss the theme of resilience in a Socratic-style conversation, citing two page numbers.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Interpreting remainders* (60 min): Solve 8 word problems (buses for 230 students, packs of 24 pencils) and decide whether to round up, drop or use the remainder.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Rust investigation* (45 min): Set up steel wool samples (dry, wet, wet with vinegar) in jars to observe rusting over the week.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *States strand: Midwest* (45 min): Learn the 12 Midwest states and capitals with a blank map and flashcards.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Ensemble* (30 min): Play 'Ode to Joy' (simplified) with a sibling, parent or recording, staying together.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Record and review* (30 min): Record the routine and identify two spots to improve.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Plan a day* (20 min): Plan breakfast, lunch, dinner and two snacks for one day that meet MyPlate amounts within a set budget using grocery flyers.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 39, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Fluency and expression* (90 min): Practice reading a dramatic passage from chapter 16 three times with expression and record the final reading.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a mixed review of multiplication, expressions and division and write your own word problem for each.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Cabbage indicator* (45 min): Make red cabbage juice indicator and test 6 household liquids, recording color changes.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Unit review* (45 min): Create a timeline of key explorations from 1492 to 1610 and review vocabulary.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Practice log* (30 min): Practice the unit songs and write a practice log noting a tricky measure and how you fixed it.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Jump-a-thon* (30 min): Do a family 'Jump Rope for Heart' style challenge, counting total minutes jumped.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Cook a meal* (20 min): Help prepare one planned meal with an adult, practicing handwashing and safe knife use.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 40, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Write a theme paragraph: 'What does Hatchet teach about mistakes?' with two quotations and explanation.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit 2 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 2 test on multi-digit multiplication, division and numerical expressions.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Sort 10 changes as making new substances or not, using evidence from the investigations, and check the rust jars.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Unit 2 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 2 test on exploration and the Midwest states.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Unit 2 assessment* (30 min): Perform two recorder songs and a rhythm reading for family, then evaluate with a performance rubric.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Unit 2 assessment* (30 min): Perform the routine for an audience and give feedback on another performer's routine.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Present the meal plan and complete the Nutrition unit test.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Hatchet chapters 14–17: the moose and the tornado
- Objective: Explain how chapters build toward the climax
- Objective: Determine theme from Brian's response to setbacks
- Vocabulary: climax, setback, resilience, theme
- Materials: Hatchet, plot mountain diagram
- Assessment: Theme paragraph scored on claim, evidence and explanation; fluency recording.

### Math 5: Estimating quotients, interpreting remainders, unit assessment
- Objective: Estimate quotients using compatible numbers
- Objective: Interpret remainders in word problems
- Vocabulary: compatible numbers, interpret, remainder
- Materials: word problem cards
- Assessment: Unit 2 test covering 5.NBT.B.5, 5.NBT.B.6, 5.OA.A.1–A.2.

### Science 5: Chemical reactions: making new substances
- Objective: Identify evidence of a new substance
- Objective: Test whether mixing produces new substances
- Vocabulary: chemical change, reaction, gas, precipitate, evidence
- Materials: baking soda, vinegar, milk, lemon juice, steel wool, jar, balloons, red cabbage juice indicator
- Assessment: Observation tables and change sorting with evidence.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: The Atlantic world and unit review
- Objective: Trace Atlantic trade routes linking Africa, the West Indies, the colonies and Europe
- Objective: Summarize the impact of exploration
- Vocabulary: triangular trade, Middle Passage, colony, export, import
- Materials: map of Atlantic trade routes, states flashcards
- Assessment: Unit 2 test and timeline.

### Art & Music 5: Recorder: C, D and E; ensemble playing
- Objective: Play C', D' and low E
- Objective: Perform with others keeping a steady beat
- Vocabulary: ensemble, unison, tempo, phrase
- Materials: recorder, song sheets, metronome app
- Assessment: Unit 2 recorder and rhythm performance rubric.

### PE 5: Jump rope routine and unit assessment
- Objective: Perform a jump rope routine to music
- Objective: Give constructive feedback
- Vocabulary: routine, transition, feedback
- Materials: jump ropes, music, recording device
- Assessment: Routine rubric (tricks, rhythm, transitions).

### Health 5: Meal planning and food tracking
- Objective: Plan a balanced day of meals
- Objective: Track and evaluate food intake
- Vocabulary: meal plan, balance, variety, portion
- Materials: food log, grocery flyers
- Assessment: Meal plan presentation and unit test.
