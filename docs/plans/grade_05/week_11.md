## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Primary source* (90 min): Read an excerpt of Columbus's journal from October 1492 and note how he describes the land and the Taíno people.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Decimal / whole number* (60 min): Use disks to share 4.8 among 4 and solve 12 problems like 6.24 / 3 with the written method.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Fair test* (45 min): Run the same amount of dirty water through the prototype, measuring clarity, time and volume recovered.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Roger Williams* (45 min): Read about Roger Williams's banishment and the founding of Rhode Island on ideas of religious freedom.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Watercolor techniques* (30 min): Practice wash, wet-on-wet, dry brush and salt texture on a technique sampler.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Instep shot* (30 min): Practice the instep drive at a goal from 10 m, aiming for corners.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Ask for activity* (20 min): Role-play asking a parent for a family walk or park trip using a clear, respectful request.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Another perspective* (90 min): Read a secondary source about Taíno life and the effects of contact, noting the author's point of view.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Decimal / whole number with zeros* (60 min): Solve 10 problems like 7.5 / 6 where zeros must be annexed to the dividend to finish.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Failure points* (45 min): Identify where the filter failed (leaks, cloudy water, slow flow) and plan one change.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Anne Hutchinson and Thomas Hooker* (45 min): Compare Anne Hutchinson's and Thomas Hooker's disagreements with Puritan leaders.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Planning* (30 min): Sketch a landscape from a photo or outdoors with foreground, middle ground and background.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Punting* (30 min): Practice punting for distance and height, measuring the best of 5.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Exercise safety* (20 min): List safety rules for heat, hydration, sun and protective gear; check your own helmet fit.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 53, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Comparing accounts* (90 min): Complete a chart comparing what each account emphasizes and leaves out, quoting both.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Dividing by tenths* (60 min): Model 2 / 0.5 as 'how many halves in 2' and solve 10 problems like 3.6 / 0.4 using grids.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Redesign* (45 min): Build version 2 changing only one variable and test it under the same conditions.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Geography and economy* (45 min): Map New England's rocky soil, forests and harbors and explain how they led to fishing, shipbuilding and trade.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Background washes* (30 min): Paint the sky and distant layers with light, cool washes.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Goalkeeping* (30 min): Practice the W catch and scoop as a partner shoots gently.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Family challenge* (20 min): Design a one-week family activity challenge with a points chart.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 54, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Discussion* (90 min): Discuss 'Why do accounts of the same event differ?' and learn Greek roots graph, tele, geo, chron.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Equivalent division* (60 min): Multiply dividend and divisor by 10 or 100 to rewrite 1.44 / 0.12 as 144 / 12 and solve 10 such problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Comparing results* (45 min): Graph results of both versions and discuss why filtered water is still not safe to drink without disinfection.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Colonial diary* (45 min): Write a diary entry as a child in a New England town, using 5 accurate details.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Foreground detail* (30 min): Paint the middle ground and foreground with warmer, darker colors and detail.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Shooting game* (30 min): Play a 1-v-1 shooting game, rotating goalkeeper.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Retest* (20 min): Retest one fitness baseline from week 9 and compare results.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Multiple accounts: Columbus and the Taíno
- Objective: Analyze similarities and differences in point of view across accounts
- Objective: Quote accurately from primary and secondary sources
- Vocabulary: primary source, secondary source, perspective, account, bias
- Materials: excerpt of Columbus's 1492 journal (Library of Congress / National Archives sites), Smithsonian NMAI article on the Taíno, comparison chart
- Assessment: Point-of-view comparison paragraph; Greek roots quiz.

### Math 5: Dividing decimals
- Objective: Divide decimals by whole numbers
- Objective: Divide by decimals using equivalent division problems
- Vocabulary: quotient, equivalent, dividend, divisor
- Materials: place value disks, hundredths grids
- Assessment: Quiz on decimal division with multiplication checks.

### Science 5: Testing and improving the filter
- Objective: Plan and carry out fair tests
- Objective: Identify failure points and improve a prototype
- Vocabulary: fair test, variable, failure point, iterate
- Materials: filter prototype, dirty water, measuring cup, timer
- Assessment: Engineering presentation and data comparison graph.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: New England colonies and religious dissent
- Objective: Explain why Rhode Island and Connecticut were founded
- Objective: Describe New England geography and economy
- Vocabulary: dissenter, religious freedom, banishment, town meeting, shipbuilding
- Materials: map of New England colonies, colonial economy cards
- Assessment: Diary entry and map quiz.

### Art & Music 5: Painting a landscape
- Objective: Plan and paint a landscape with atmospheric perspective
- Objective: Use brushes and materials with care
- Vocabulary: wash, wet-on-wet, dry brush, layering
- Materials: watercolor paper, watercolors, brushes, masking tape, salt for texture
- Assessment: Technique sampler and painting progress check.

### PE 5: Shooting, punting and goalkeeping
- Objective: Shoot with the instep
- Objective: Punt and catch as a goalkeeper
- Vocabulary: instep drive, punt, goalkeeper, W catch
- Materials: soccer ball, goal or two cones, targets
- Assessment: Shooting accuracy record.

### Health 5: Activity, communication and safety
- Objective: Ask for active options at home
- Objective: Exercise safely
- Vocabulary: I-message, hydration, heat illness, safety gear
- Materials: helmet, water bottle
- Assessment: Fitness unit quiz and reflection.
