## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Langston Hughes* (90 min): Read 'Dreams' and 'April Rain Song', identify the metaphor and personification, and write a free-verse weather poem.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Collect data* (60 min): Measure 12 pencils or leaves to the nearest 1/8 inch and record the data in a table.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Mold investigation* (45 min): Set up sealed bags with bread under different conditions (dry, damp, fridge, warm) to observe mold growth (do not open).
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Agriculture and survival* (45 min): Read about how Native peoples taught colonists to grow corn and use local resources.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Looking at art* (30 min): Examine images of Pueblo pottery, Navajo weavings and Haudenosaunee beadwork and describe their patterns.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Stationary dribbling* (30 min): Do ball-handling drills (pound, low, crossover, around the leg) for 1 minute each.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Family kickoff* (20 min): Parent reviews the unit outline and materials together with the student; set up an anonymous question box.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *'Jabberwocky'* (90 min): Read 'Jabberwocky' and use grammar clues to decide the part of speech and meaning of 8 nonsense words.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Build the line plot* (60 min): Draw a line plot of the measurement data with a scale in eighths and title and label it.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Bottle compost* (45 min): Build a mini compost column in a clear bottle with soil, leaves and fruit scraps and draw day 1.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *The fur trade* (45 min): Map the French and Dutch fur trade networks and explain what each side gained.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Symbols and meaning* (30 min): Read museum labels explaining what certain designs mean and why some symbols are sacred and should not be copied.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Moving dribble* (30 min): Dribble through cones with the right hand, left hand and crossover at each cone.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *What is puberty?* (20 min): Watch an AMAZE.org video on puberty and list changes that happen to everyone.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 83, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Varieties of English* (90 min): Compare formal and informal registers in poems and rewrite a stanza in a different register.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Questions from the plot* (60 min): Use the line plot to find the total length, the difference between longest and shortest, and how much liquid each beaker would hold if redistributed equally.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Decomposer research* (45 min): Read about fungi, bacteria and worms and explain how each breaks down dead matter.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Primary source: painting* (45 min): Analyze Benjamin West's painting of Penn's treaty and discuss how a painting made 90 years later can shape memory.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Pattern design* (30 min): Design your own symmetrical geometric pattern on graph paper using personal symbols.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Speed and control* (30 min): Alternate speed dribbles and control dribbles on a whistle.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Different timelines* (20 min): Read about how puberty can start between ages 8 and 14 and write why comparing yourself to friends isn't useful.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 84, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Anthology* (90 min): Compile 5 original poems (narrative stanza, metaphor, free verse, rhyme, nonsense) into an illustrated anthology.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a mixed review of unlike fractions, mixed numbers and line plot problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Observations* (45 min): Record and sketch changes in the mold bags and compost column with dates.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Cultural exchange* (45 min): List words, foods and technologies exchanged between Native nations and colonists.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Paper weaving* (30 min): Weave a paper mat in two colors to understand over-under structure.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Protecting the ball* (30 min): Practice dribbling while a partner tries to tap the ball away, keeping the body between.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Growth spurts* (20 min): Graph your height over the past few years and learn about growth spurts and growing pains.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 85, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Unit 4 assessment* (90 min): Hold a poetry café reading of 2 original poems and take a cold-read poetry assessment on theme and figurative language.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit 4 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 4 test on adding and subtracting fractions and line plots.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Add decomposers to last week's food web with arrows back to soil and air and explain the cycle.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a summary of three forms of cooperation with examples.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write how art preserves culture and explain the meaning of your own symbols.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Complete a timed dribble course with both hands and record time.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Answer questions from the question box with a parent and complete a 5-question check.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Poets play with language: Hughes and Carroll; poetry anthology
- Objective: Compare varieties of English and invented language in poems
- Objective: Compile and perform an original poetry anthology
- Vocabulary: dialect, nonsense word, free verse, anthology
- Materials: 'Dreams' and 'April Rain Song' by Langston Hughes, 'Jabberwocky' by Lewis Carroll
- Assessment: Poetry anthology rubric; performance; Unit 4 cold-read assessment.

### Math 5: Line plots with fractional data and unit assessment
- Objective: Make line plots of measurements in fractions of a unit
- Objective: Solve problems using data from line plots
- Vocabulary: line plot, data, redistribute
- Materials: ruler marked in eighths, pencils or leaves to measure, measuring cups
- Assessment: Unit 4 test covering 5.NF.A.1–A.2 and 5.MD.B.2; graded line plot.

### Science 5: Decomposers and the matter cycle
- Objective: Observe decomposition
- Objective: Explain how decomposers return matter to soil and air
- Vocabulary: decompose, fungi, bacteria, nutrients, compost
- Materials: bread slices, zip bags, fruit scraps, clear plastic bottle, soil, leaves, magnifying glass
- Assessment: Observation log and revised food web with decomposers.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Cooperation: trade, alliances and exchange
- Objective: Describe cooperation between colonists and Native nations
- Objective: Explain the fur trade and alliances
- Vocabulary: fur trade, alliance, treaty, cultural exchange
- Materials: Penn's Treaty with the Lenape painting (Benjamin West, 1771), fur trade map
- Assessment: Painting analysis and cooperation summary.

### Art & Music 5: Native American art: pattern and symbolism
- Objective: Analyze symbols and patterns in Native American art
- Objective: Create a design inspired by traditional pattern principles
- Vocabulary: symmetry, geometric pattern, symbol, motif, cultural respect
- Materials: images of Navajo weaving, Pueblo pottery and Haudenosaunee beadwork (Smithsonian NMAI), graph paper, colored pencils
- Assessment: Pattern design and written reflection.

### PE 5: Basketball dribbling
- Objective: Dribble with either hand while changing speed and direction
- Objective: Protect the ball from a defender
- Vocabulary: crossover, fingertips, protect, speed dribble, control dribble
- Materials: basketball or playground ball, cones
- Assessment: Dribble course time and cue checklist.

### Health 5: Introduction to puberty (parent notification and opt-out honored)
- Objective: Define puberty
- Objective: Recognize that puberty is normal and timing varies
- Vocabulary: puberty, adolescence, growth spurt, hormone
- Materials: parent letter, AMAZE videos, question box
- Assessment: Quick check and discussion.
