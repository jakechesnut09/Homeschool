## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Setting and mood* (90 min): Read 'All Summer in a Day' and list Bradbury's descriptions of rain and sun; explain how the Venus setting causes the conflict.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Rounding on a number line* (60 min): Round 12 decimals to the nearest tenth by placing them on a vertical number line between two benchmarks and marking the midpoint.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Observing powders* (45 min): Examine 5 known white powders with a magnifying glass and record texture, crystal shape and feel.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Research* (45 min): Choose one nation (e.g., Hopi, Chinook, Lakota, Cherokee) and research its homeland, food, shelter, government and stories.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Choosing a view* (30 min): Use a cardstock viewfinder to choose a composition of a corner of your home and make 3 thumbnail sketches.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Aerobic test* (30 min): Complete the PACER (or one-mile run) and record laps or time.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Six nutrients* (20 min): Make a 6-panel chart of carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals and water with each job and two food sources.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Revising for pacing* (90 min): Revise the narrative by slowing down the most important moment with thoughts, feelings and sensory details (stretch it to at least a full paragraph).
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Rounding to any place* (60 min): Round the same 10 numbers (like 4.386) to the nearest whole, tenth and hundredth and compare results in a table.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Water test* (45 min): Add each powder to water and record whether it dissolves, clumps or settles.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Today's nations* (45 min): Learn that these nations still exist today and find one fact about the nation's present-day government or culture.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Drawing* (30 min): Lightly block in the composition with contour lines and perspective.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Strength tests* (30 min): Complete the curl-up and push-up tests and record scores.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Nutrients for growth* (20 min): Read about calcium, iron and protein needs during growth spurts and list foods rich in each.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Editing* (90 min): Edit with a checklist for dialogue punctuation, commas in series, spelling and verb tense, then read it aloud to catch errors.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Rounding in context* (60 min): Round prices on a grocery receipt to the nearest dollar and estimate the total, then compare to the actual total.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Vinegar and indicator tests* (45 min): Test each powder with vinegar (fizzing) and an indicator (adult-supervised iodine or cabbage juice) and record results.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Create the project* (45 min): Create an illustrated poster or museum card set showing how geography shaped the nation's life.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Shading* (30 min): Add value and texture to the drawing, establishing a focal point.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Flexibility tests* (30 min): Complete the trunk lift and sit-and-reach and record scores.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *MyPlate check* (20 min): Use MyPlate Kids to find daily food-group amounts for your age and activity level and record them.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Publishing* (90 min): Type the final narrative (aim for two pages in one sitting) and read it aloud to family as an author's chair.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit review* (60 min): Complete a mixed review of place value, powers of 10, comparing and rounding, and make a one-page 'decimal place value' reference sheet.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Mystery mixture* (45 min): Identify an adult-prepared mystery powder (or mixture of two) using the test results chart.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *States strand: Southeast* (45 min): Learn Southeast states and capitals and locate the homelands of the Cherokee, Creek and Seminole on a map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Critique and revise* (30 min): Get feedback using 'I notice, I wonder' and make one revision.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Comparing to HFZ* (30 min): Compare your scores to the Healthy Fitness Zone chart for your age and highlight strengths and areas to improve.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Plate collage* (20 min): Cut magazine pictures to build a balanced MyPlate collage that meets the recommended amounts.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Unit 1 assessment* (90 min): Take a cold-read assessment on an unfamiliar short story (theme, character, quoting evidence) and a root/prefix vocabulary quiz.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit 1 test* (60 min): Take the Unit 1 test (place value, exponents, comparing, rounding) and complete a self-reflection on the hardest item.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a CER identifying the mystery powder and take a quiz on material properties.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Unit 1 assessment* (45 min): Present the project and take the Unit 1 test on First Peoples and Southeast states.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write an artist statement describing your choices and revision, and file the drawing in your portfolio.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write one fitness goal for the year and an activity to support it, then play an active game of choice.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Answer a 10-question nutrients quiz and explain your collage to a family member.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: 'All Summer in a Day' by Ray Bradbury; revising and publishing narratives
- Objective: Analyze how setting shapes events
- Objective: Revise and publish a personal narrative
- Vocabulary: setting, mood, revise, edit, pacing
- Materials: 'All Summer in a Day' (library anthology or CommonLit), revision checklist, colored pens
- Assessment: Published personal narrative scored with a narrative rubric; Unit 1 cold-read assessment.

### Math 5: Rounding decimals and unit assessment
- Objective: Round decimals to the nearest whole, tenth and hundredth
- Objective: Use a number line to justify rounding
- Vocabulary: round, benchmark, midpoint, estimate
- Materials: vertical number line template, grocery receipt
- Assessment: Unit 1 test covering 5.NBT.A.1–A.4.

### Science 5: Identifying mystery powders
- Objective: Plan tests to identify white powders
- Objective: Use solubility and reactions as properties
- Vocabulary: solubility, reaction, identify, fair test, variable
- Materials: baking soda, salt, sugar, cornstarch, flour, vinegar, iodine or tincture (adult supervised) or red cabbage juice, magnifying glass, muffin tin
- Assessment: Powder test chart, CER and properties quiz.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Comparing First Peoples; unit project
- Objective: Compare the four major regions of American Indian nations
- Objective: Present findings about one nation
- Vocabulary: culture region, trade network, sovereignty
- Materials: poster board, research sources from NMAI Native Knowledge 360
- Assessment: Unit 1 project rubric and test.

### Art & Music 5: Drawing project: a detailed observational drawing
- Objective: Combine line, value and space in a finished drawing
- Objective: Explain revisions using art vocabulary
- Vocabulary: composition, focal point, revision, critique
- Materials: drawing paper, pencils, viewfinder made from cardstock
- Assessment: Drawing project rubric and artist statement.

### PE 5: Baseline FITNESSGRAM assessment
- Objective: Complete baseline fitness tests
- Objective: Compare results to the Healthy Fitness Zone
- Vocabulary: Healthy Fitness Zone, baseline, goal
- Materials: FITNESSGRAM HFZ chart, stopwatch, PACER audio, score sheet
- Assessment: Baseline FITNESSGRAM score sheet and goal statement.

### Health 5: Nutrients and MyPlate for a growing body
- Objective: Name the six nutrients and their jobs
- Objective: Describe MyPlate amounts for a 10–11 year old
- Vocabulary: nutrient, protein, carbohydrate, calcium, fiber
- Materials: MyPlate printout, food magazines
- Assessment: Nutrients quiz and MyPlate collage.
