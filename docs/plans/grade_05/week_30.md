## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Finishing the novel* (90 min): Read chapters 23–27 and summarize how the ending resolves Mary's, Colin's and Mr. Craven's stories.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Graphing data* (60 min): Record outdoor temperature every hour for 6 hours and graph (hour, temperature) points.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Sun as a star* (45 min): Read NASA Space Place 'What is a Star?' and compare the sun to other stars in a chart.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Legislative branch* (45 min): Read Article I basics and diagram how a bill becomes a law.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Peter and the Wolf* (30 min): Listen to 'Peter and the Wolf' and match each character to its instrument and theme.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Kicking* (30 min): Kick rolled balls to left, center and right field on purpose.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Who can help?* (20 min): List trusted adults and community resources (doctor, school counselor, Poison Control, faith leader) for drug concerns.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 147, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Story vs. drama* (90 min): Study a sample script's scenes, cast list and stage directions, and compare its structure to chapters.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Choosing scales* (60 min): Graph 3 data sets (savings over weeks, plant height over days) choosing appropriate axis scales.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Flashlight investigation* (45 min): In a dark room, compare the brightness of identical flashlights at 1 m, 3 m and 6 m and record observations.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Executive and judicial* (45 min): Read about the president's and Supreme Court's powers and complete a three-branch tree.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Mountain King* (30 min): Listen to 'In the Hall of the Mountain King' and graph how tempo and dynamics build.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Base running* (30 min): Practice running through first base and rounding bases efficiently.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Family affected* (20 min): Read a story about a family member's alcohol problem and discuss feelings and where kids can get support.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 148, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Writing a script* (90 min): Adapt one key chapter (finding the garden or Colin's first visit) into a 2-scene readers' theater script.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Interpreting graphs* (60 min): Answer 10 questions about what specific points mean on a graph of a bike trip's time and distance.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Measuring brightness* (45 min): Use a light meter app or a paper-layer test to measure brightness at different distances and graph the data.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Checks and balances* (45 min): Play an iCivics checks-and-balances game and list 4 checks between branches.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Sound story plan* (30 min): Choose a short story (from ELA) and assign sounds and instruments to characters and events.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Rules* (30 min): Learn force outs and tagging up and practice decisions in scenarios.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Help script* (20 min): Write a script for telling a trusted adult you found a vape or pills.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 149, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Rehearsal* (90 min): Rehearse the script with family members, focusing on expression, pacing and dialect.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Map coordinates* (60 min): Draw a neighborhood map on a coordinate grid and write directions between locations using coordinates.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Star distances* (45 min): Compare the distances of the sun, Sirius and Betelgeuse with a scale model using a long hallway or sidewalk.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Federalism* (45 min): Sort 15 powers into federal, state or shared (e.g., printing money, schools, taxes).
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Improvising* (30 min): Improvise and record the sound story with household instruments.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Kickball game* (30 min): Play a family or neighborhood kickball game.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Resource card* (20 min): Design a wallet-size resource card with key numbers.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 150, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Unit 7 assessment* (90 min): Perform the readers' theater and take an assessment on the novel's structure, characters and theme.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz graphing and interpreting a real-world data set.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a CER explaining why the sun looks brighter than other stars.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz on branches, checks and balances and federalism.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: The Secret Garden chapters 23–27 and readers' theater
- Objective: Explain how scenes structure a drama
- Objective: Perform a readers' theater adaptation
- Vocabulary: script, scene, stage directions, cast, adaptation
- Materials: The Secret Garden, script template
- Assessment: Script and performance rubric; Unit 7 novel assessment.

### Math 5: Graphing real-world problems
- Objective: Represent real-world situations as points on a graph
- Objective: Interpret coordinates in context
- Vocabulary: interpret, scale, data point, trend
- Materials: graph paper, thermometer, plant or shadow to measure
- Assessment: Quiz on graphing and interpreting real-world points.

### Science 5: The sun is a star
- Objective: Compare the sun to other stars
- Objective: Investigate how distance affects apparent brightness
- Vocabulary: star, apparent brightness, distance, light-year, luminosity
- Materials: two identical flashlights, measuring tape, dark room, light meter app (optional)
- Assessment: Brightness graph and CER.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Three branches, checks and balances, and federalism
- Objective: Explain the three branches and their powers
- Objective: Compare federal and state powers
- Vocabulary: legislative, executive, judicial, checks and balances, separation of powers, federalism
- Materials: Ben's Guide to U.S. Government, iCivics game, three-branch tree diagram
- Assessment: Three-branch diagram and quiz.

### Art & Music 5: Program music: telling stories with sound
- Objective: Explain how composers use expressive qualities to tell stories
- Objective: Improvise a sound story
- Vocabulary: program music, leitmotif, dynamics, tempo, crescendo
- Materials: Prokofiev's 'Peter and the Wolf', Grieg's 'In the Hall of the Mountain King', household sound makers
- Assessment: Sound story recording and explanation.

### PE 5: Kickball and base running
- Objective: Kick a rolled ball for distance and placement
- Objective: Run bases with correct technique
- Vocabulary: base running, tag up, force out, placement
- Materials: playground ball, bases or cones
- Assessment: Kick placement accuracy and strategy diagram.

### Health 5: Getting help and community resources
- Objective: Identify trusted adults and resources
- Objective: Explain how to help someone affected by substance use
- Vocabulary: resource, counselor, hotline, support
- Materials: resource card template
- Assessment: Unit test.
