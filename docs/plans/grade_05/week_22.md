## Day 1 — Monday (school day 106, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Planning the comparison* (90 min): Use an organizer to compare how Brian and Esperanza face loss and change, with 2 quotations from each book.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Unit fraction / whole* (60 min): Share 1/2 of a pan of brownies among 3 people with a drawing and solve 8 problems like 1/3 / 4.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Cloud in a jar* (45 min): Make a cloud in a jar with hot water and ice and explain the role of water vapor and cooling.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *First Continental Congress* (45 min): Read about the 1774 Congress and list its decisions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Major vs. minor* (30 min): Listen to 6 excerpts and identify major or minor, describing the mood of each.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *La Raspa* (30 min): Learn the Mexican folk dance 'La Raspa' with its hop-step and elbow swing sections.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Conversation starters* (20 min): Write three conversation starters for talking with a parent about puberty.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 107, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Drafting* (90 min): Draft a compare-contrast essay using point-by-point or block structure and contrast transitions.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Whole / unit fraction* (60 min): Use number lines to find how many 1/4s are in 3 and solve 8 problems like 5 / 1/3.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Ocean currents* (45 min): Model warm and cold water currents with colored hot and cold water in a clear container and observe.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Patrick Henry* (45 min): Read and perform an excerpt of Patrick Henry's 1775 speech and explain its argument.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Scale patterns* (30 min): Build a C major scale using whole and half steps on a virtual keyboard.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *The Hora* (30 min): Learn the Israeli hora circle step and dance to music.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Practice talk* (20 min): Have a 10-minute conversation with a parent or guardian using a starter and record one thing you learned.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 108, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Dialect and language* (90 min): Compare the use of Spanish words and dialect in Esperanza Rising with Paulsen's plain style and add a paragraph on language.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Checking with multiplication* (60 min): Check 10 division answers with multiplication (1/12 x 4 = 1/3) and explain the relationship.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Weather tracking* (45 min): Record temperature, cloud cover and precipitation daily for the week in a weather log.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Lexington and Concord* (45 min): Map Revere's and Dawes's rides and the battles at Lexington and Concord.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Playing scales* (30 min): Play the G major scale on recorder (G-A-B-C-D-E-F#-G) slowly and evenly.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Tinikling* (30 min): Learn the Philippine tinikling steps with ropes or sticks on the ground, then with movement.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Valid sources* (20 min): Compare KidsHealth, AMAZE and a random video comment on a puberty question and judge reliability.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 109, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Revising and editing* (90 min): Revise with a peer/parent checklist, edit, and type the final essay.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Story contexts* (60 min): Write story contexts for 1/4 / 2 and 6 / 1/2, then solve both with models.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Coastal climate* (45 min): Compare average temperatures of San Francisco and Sacramento and explain how the ocean affects climate.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Comparing stories: Esperanza Rising and Hatchet; compare-contrast essay
- Objective: Compare how two novels approach a similar theme
- Objective: Write a compare-contrast informative essay
- Vocabulary: compare, contrast, similarly, whereas, on the other hand
- Materials: Esperanza Rising, Hatchet, compare-contrast organizer
- Assessment: Compare-contrast essay rubric; Unit 5 cold-read assessment.

### Math 5: Dividing unit fractions and whole numbers
- Objective: Divide a unit fraction by a whole number
- Objective: Divide a whole number by a unit fraction
- Vocabulary: unit fraction, divide, how many groups
- Materials: fraction strips, paper strips, number lines
- Assessment: Quiz on unit fraction division.

### Science 5: Atmosphere and weather interactions
- Objective: Model how the atmosphere and hydrosphere interact in weather
- Objective: Explain how ocean temperatures affect climate
- Vocabulary: atmosphere, water vapor, cloud, climate, current
- Materials: jar, hot water, ice, plate, food coloring, thermometer
- Assessment: Rainstorm sphere-interaction model; weather log.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: The Continental Congresses and the first shots
- Objective: Explain the significance of the First and Second Continental Congresses
- Objective: Describe Lexington and Concord
- Vocabulary: Continental Congress, militia, minutemen, Loyalist, Patriot
- Materials: Patrick Henry's 'Give me liberty' speech excerpt (1775), map of Lexington and Concord
- Assessment: Speech performance and T-chart.

### Art & Music 5: Major and minor; scales
- Objective: Hear the difference between major and minor
- Objective: Play a major scale
- Vocabulary: major, minor, scale, tonic, whole step, half step
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Piano Roll or Chords, recorder
- Assessment: Listening quiz and scale performance.

### PE 5: Dances from around the world
- Objective: Perform folk dances from different cultures
- Objective: Describe each dance's cultural context
- Vocabulary: folk dance, tinikling, hora, La Raspa, culture
- Materials: two long sticks or ropes for tinikling, music for the hora and 'La Raspa'
- Assessment: World dance performance and cultural fact.

### Health 5: Talking with trusted adults
- Objective: Communicate about puberty with a parent or trusted adult
- Objective: Locate valid information
- Vocabulary: communication, trusted adult, privacy, valid information
- Materials: question box, computer
- Assessment: Role-play and pocket card.
