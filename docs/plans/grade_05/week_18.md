## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Las Uvas* (90 min): Read 'Las Uvas' and describe the setting of El Rancho de las Rosas with 5 details; locate Aguascalientes on a map.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Sharing wholes* (60 min): Cut paper circles to share 3 pizzas among 4 people and record 3 / 4 = 3/4; repeat for 4 other sharing situations.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Build a terrarium* (45 min): Build a closed terrarium with gravel, soil, plants and (optionally) pill bugs, and diagram its parts.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Powhatan Wars* (45 min): Read about the Powhatan Wars in Virginia and list causes, including land and tobacco expansion.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Reading a portrait* (30 min): Analyze Copley's 'Paul Revere' portrait and list clues about his job and status.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Passing* (30 min): Practice chest, bounce and overhead passes against a wall, 20 each.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Messengers* (20 min): Learn how hormones act as chemical messengers and model it with a message-passing game.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Las Papayas* (90 min): Read 'Las Papayas' and discuss how Papa's death changes everything; mark Spanish words and use context to define them.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Division as fraction* (60 min): Write 15 division expressions as fractions and fractions as division (7 / 3 = 7/3 = 2 1/3).
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Predicting cycles* (45 min): Predict how water, oxygen, carbon dioxide and nutrients will move in the sealed jar.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Pequot War* (45 min): Read about the Pequot War and the Mystic massacre and discuss how colonists and Native allies fought.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Folk art* (30 min): Compare a professional portrait with a folk-art portrait by Ammi Phillips and discuss different criteria for judging them.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Form shooting* (30 min): Shoot close to the hoop using BEEF cues, 30 shots.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Pituitary gland* (20 min): Add the pituitary gland to your body map and explain how it signals the ovaries or testes.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 88, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Point of view* (90 min): Explain how the third-person limited narrator shows events through Esperanza's eyes, using 2 quotations.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Between which wholes?* (60 min): Decide between which two whole numbers each of 10 quotients like 47 / 6 lies, and write each as a mixed number.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Observing* (45 min): Observe condensation on the jar walls and plant health and record observations.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *King Philip's War* (45 min): Read Metacom's reported grievances (1675) and explain why he resisted colonial expansion.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Facial proportions* (30 min): Learn basic facial proportions and draw a guided self-portrait from a mirror.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Layups* (30 min): Practice the right-hand layup footwork (right-left-up) slowly, then at speed.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Hormone effects* (20 min): Make a cause-and-effect chart linking estrogen and testosterone to body changes.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 89, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Los Higos and Las Guayabas* (90 min): Read 'Los Higos' and 'Las Guayabas' and create a character trait web for Esperanza with evidence.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Word problems* (60 min): Solve 6 sharing problems such as 50 pounds of rice shared among 8 families.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Mold and compost update* (45 min): Record final observations of mold bags and compost column and compare to day 1.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Native rivalries* (45 min): Read about rivalries among Native nations (Haudenosaunee and Huron) and how Europeans used them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Symbolic objects* (30 min): Add objects to your self-portrait that show your interests, as colonial portraitists did.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Shooting game* (30 min): Play 'Around the World' at 5 spots.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Mood and hormones* (20 min): Read about how hormones can affect moods and brainstorm healthy ways to handle mood swings.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Esperanza Rising: 'Aguascalientes, Mexico 1924' – Las Uvas through Las Guayabas
- Objective: Analyze the setting and its historical context
- Objective: Describe Esperanza's character at the start
- Vocabulary: rancho, privilege, historical fiction, third-person limited
- Materials: Esperanza Rising by Pam Muñoz Ryan (library), map of Mexico and California, character trait list
- Assessment: Character trait web; journal entry; chapter quiz.

### Math 5: Fractions as division
- Objective: Interpret a/b as a divided by b
- Objective: Solve sharing problems with fractional answers
- Vocabulary: quotient, fraction, share equally
- Materials: paper 'pizzas' or tortillas, scissors
- Assessment: Quiz on interpreting fractions as division.

### Science 5: Modeling matter cycling in an ecosystem
- Objective: Build a closed terrarium
- Objective: Model movement of matter among organisms and the environment
- Vocabulary: terrarium, ecosystem, cycle, balance
- Materials: large jar with lid, gravel, soil, small plants or moss, pill bugs (optional), spray bottle
- Assessment: Terrarium matter-cycle model.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Conflict: Powhatan Wars, Pequot War and King Philip's War
- Objective: Explain causes and effects of early colonial wars
- Objective: Analyze broken treaties and their consequences
- Vocabulary: Metacom, encroachment, massacre, treaty, rivalry
- Materials: excerpt of Mary Rowlandson's narrative or Metacom's 1675 grievances, timeline template
- Assessment: Timeline and quiz.

### Art & Music 5: Colonial portraits and folk art
- Objective: Interpret messages in portraits
- Objective: Compare folk art and academic art
- Vocabulary: portrait, folk art, proportion, symbolism, status
- Materials: John Singleton Copley's 'Paul Revere' portrait, Ammi Phillips folk portrait, mirror, pencils
- Assessment: Self-portrait with symbolic objects and interpretation comparison.

### PE 5: Basketball passing and shooting
- Objective: Perform chest, bounce and overhead passes
- Objective: Shoot using BEEF cues
- Vocabulary: chest pass, bounce pass, overhead pass, BEEF (balance, eyes, elbow, follow-through), layup
- Materials: basketball, hoop or bucket target, wall
- Assessment: Shooting form self-assessment.

### Health 5: Hormones and the endocrine system
- Objective: Explain how the pituitary gland starts puberty
- Objective: Name the hormones involved
- Vocabulary: pituitary gland, endocrine system, estrogen, testosterone, gland
- Materials: body map from week 2
- Assessment: Endocrine flowchart and quiz.
