## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Revising* (90 min): Revise the report for precise domain vocabulary and add one quotation and one text feature (map, diagram, timeline).
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Adding with models* (60 min): Use fraction strips to add 1/2 + 1/3 and 8 other pairs, recording the renamed equation for each.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Tracing breakfast* (45 min): Trace every food in your breakfast back to a plant and the sun with arrows.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Mercantilism* (45 min): Play a simple trade simulation showing raw materials going to England and finished goods returning, then discuss who benefits.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Yankee Doodle* (30 min): Learn the history of 'Yankee Doodle' as a British insult turned Patriot anthem and play it on recorder.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Target hands* (30 min): Practice catching above and below the waist with proper hand position.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Sweat and oil* (20 min): Learn how sweat and oil glands become more active in puberty and why body odor and acne happen.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 67, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Typing and editing* (90 min): Type the final report in one sitting and edit for commas, titles and spelling.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Adding with equations* (60 min): Add 15 pairs of unlike fractions by renaming, writing sums greater than 1 as mixed numbers.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *How bodies use energy* (45 min): Measure your pulse and body temperature at rest and after exercise and discuss how food energy powers motion and warmth.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Free enterprise roots* (45 min): Read how colonists ran farms, shops and ships for profit and explain the start of a free-market economy.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Star-Spangled Banner* (30 min): Read about Francis Scott Key and Fort McHenry and sing the first verse with attention to the wide range.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Moving catches* (30 min): Run routes (slant, out, curl) and catch passes from a partner.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Skin care* (20 min): Read about gentle face washing and acne myths; write a simple morning and evening skin routine.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 68, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Visual display* (90 min): Create a poster or 4-slide display highlighting the report's main ideas with captioned images.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Subtracting with models* (60 min): Model 3/4 - 1/6 on a number line and solve 8 more subtraction problems with models.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Food labels* (45 min): Compare calories on 5 food labels and explain that calories measure stored energy originally from sunlight.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Spanish and French colonies* (45 min): Read about Spanish missions and presidios (including California) and French fur-trading posts.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *America the Beautiful* (30 min): Learn 'America the Beautiful' and list the geographic images Katharine Lee Bates used.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Catch and throw* (30 min): Catch on the move and immediately throw to a second partner or target.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Deodorant vs. antiperspirant* (20 min): Compare product labels for deodorant and antiperspirant and explain the difference.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 69, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Presenting* (90 min): Present the report in 3–4 minutes to family, speaking clearly and answering two audience questions.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Subtracting with equations* (60 min): Subtract 15 unlike-fraction problems and simplify answers.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Energy model* (45 min): Create an energy flow diagram from sun to grass to cow to human, labeling uses of energy at each step.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Comparing systems* (45 min): Complete a chart comparing British, Spanish and French colonies in government, economy, religion and relations with Native peoples.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Expressive singing* (30 min): Choose dynamics and tempo to express the meaning of one song and record a performance.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Team passing game* (30 min): Play 'Ultimate ball' (no running with the ball) with teammates of all skill levels.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Hair and nails* (20 min): Learn how often to wash hair as it gets oilier and practice nail care.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 70, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Unit 3 assessment* (90 min): Take a cold-read assessment comparing two informational texts on the same topic (structure, point of view, integration).
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a 12-problem quiz on adding and subtracting unlike fractions.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write an explanation of how the energy used to run a race was once energy from the sun.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph explaining one major difference between British and Spanish colonies.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform two songs and explain the ideal each expresses.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Complete 10 moving catches and record success rate.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Build a personal hygiene checklist and complete a hygiene myth-or-fact quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Publishing and presenting the research report
- Objective: Revise and type a research report
- Objective: Present findings with a visual display
- Vocabulary: revise, visual aid, caption, eye contact
- Materials: computer, poster board or slideshow, presentation checklist
- Assessment: Research report rubric; presentation checklist; Unit 3 assessment.

### Math 5: Adding and subtracting fractions with unlike denominators
- Objective: Add fractions with unlike denominators
- Objective: Subtract fractions with unlike denominators
- Vocabulary: unlike denominators, sum, difference, renaming
- Materials: fraction strips, Math Learning Center Fractions app
- Assessment: Quiz on adding/subtracting unlike fractions.

### Science 5: Energy in food comes from the sun
- Objective: Trace energy from the sun to food to animals
- Objective: Describe how animals use food energy
- Vocabulary: energy, food chain, calorie, body warmth, growth
- Materials: food labels, thermometer, jump rope, drawing supplies
- Assessment: Energy flow diagram and written explanation.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Colonial economy and the British system
- Objective: Explain how the colonial economy worked
- Objective: Compare British, Spanish and French colonial systems
- Vocabulary: mercantilism, free market, raw materials, mission, New France
- Materials: comparison chart, colonial trade goods list
- Assessment: Colonial systems chart and paragraph.

### Art & Music 5: Songs of the Revolution and the nation
- Objective: Sing patriotic songs with accurate pitch
- Objective: Explain the history and ideals behind each song
- Vocabulary: anthem, lyrics, fife, verse, refrain
- Materials: lyrics for 'Yankee Doodle,' 'The Star-Spangled Banner,' 'America the Beautiful,' 'My Country, 'Tis of Thee'
- Assessment: Patriotic song performance and explanation.

### PE 5: Catching on the move
- Objective: Catch while moving and transition to a throw
- Objective: Lead a moving receiver
- Vocabulary: receiver, lead, target hands, transition
- Materials: foam football or tennis ball, cones
- Assessment: Moving-catch success rate.

### Health 5: Skin, hair and body hygiene
- Objective: Explain why sweat and oil increase with growth
- Objective: Practice a hygiene routine
- Vocabulary: hygiene, sweat glands, oil glands, acne, deodorant, antiperspirant
- Materials: hygiene product samples, checklist
- Assessment: Hygiene checklist and quiz.
