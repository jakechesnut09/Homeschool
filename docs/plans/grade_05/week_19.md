## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Los Melones* (90 min): Read 'Los Melones' (the train journey) and discuss how Esperanza treats Isabel and the peasant girl.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Fraction of a set* (60 min): Use 24 counters to find 1/3, 2/3, 3/4 and 5/6 of the set and write each as multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Invasive species* (45 min): Read about an invasive species in California and diagram how it disrupts the food web.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Competition for the Ohio Valley* (45 min): Map British, French and Native claims to the Ohio Valley and explain why war began in 1754.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Prints that persuade* (30 min): Study Paul Revere's Boston Massacre engraving and Franklin's 'Join, or Die' and explain how prints spread messages.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Defensive stance* (30 min): Practice defensive slides and stance for 5 x 30 seconds.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Correct names* (20 min): Learn medically accurate names for body parts and why using them matters for health and safety.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 92, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Las Cebollas* (90 min): Read 'Las Cebollas' and compare Esperanza's old and new homes in a Venn diagram.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Tape diagrams* (60 min): Draw tape diagrams to solve 10 problems like 3/5 x 40.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Population data* (45 min): Graph sample population data for predators and prey over 10 years and describe the pattern.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Primary source: 'Join, or Die'* (45 min): Analyze Franklin's 'Join, or Die' cartoon and explain its message about colonial unity.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Designing a print* (30 min): Design a simple image with a message about a cause you care about, remembering the print will be reversed.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Give-and-go* (30 min): Practice the give-and-go with a partner and finish with a layup.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Female system* (20 min): Label a diagram of the female reproductive system and write the function of each structure.

- [ ] **Math 5** — *Whole x fraction* (60 min): Solve 12 problems like 6 x 2/3 using repeated addition and then (6 x 2)/3.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Ecosystem game* (45 min): Play an 'Oh Deer' style game with family modeling food, water and shelter and record population changes each round.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Alliances and outcome* (45 min): Chart which Native nations allied with France or Britain and compare 1754 and 1763 maps.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Carving* (30 min): Press the design into a foam plate with a dull pencil, checking line depth.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *2-v-1* (30 min): Play 2-v-1 drills deciding when to pass or shoot.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Male system* (20 min): Label a diagram of the male reproductive system and write the function of each structure.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Esperanza Rising: arrival in California – Los Melones through Las Almendras
- Objective: Compare Esperanza's life in Mexico and California
- Objective: Use correlative conjunctions
- Vocabulary: labor camp, migrant, dignity, correlative conjunction
- Materials: Esperanza Rising, Venn diagram
- Assessment: Venn diagram; paragraph with evidence; grammar quiz.

### Math 5: Multiplying a fraction and a whole number
- Objective: Multiply a whole number by a fraction
- Objective: Find a fraction of a set
- Vocabulary: fraction of a set, product, unit fraction
- Materials: counters, tape diagrams
- Assessment: Quiz on multiplying fractions and whole numbers.

### Science 5: Ecosystem balance and invasive species
- Objective: Explain how changes affect an ecosystem
- Objective: Argue using evidence about ecosystem health
- Vocabulary: invasive species, balance, population, native
- Materials: articles on California invasive species (e.g., quagga mussels, Argentine ants), graph paper
- Assessment: Population graph; Unit 5 test.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: The French and Indian War
- Objective: Explain the causes, alliances and outcome of the French and Indian War
- Objective: Connect the war's debt to future conflict with Britain
- Vocabulary: Ohio Valley, Proclamation of 1763, Pontiac, debt, Treaty of Paris (1763)
- Materials: Benjamin Franklin's 'Join, or Die' cartoon (1754), before-and-after maps of 1754 and 1763
- Assessment: Cartoon analysis and Unit 5 test.

### Art & Music 5: Printmaking and persuasive images
- Objective: Analyze how prints spread ideas
- Objective: Make a relief print
- Vocabulary: relief print, brayer, edition, propaganda, reverse image
- Materials: foam trays or craft foam sheets, dull pencil, washable ink or tempera, brayer or small roller, paper
- Assessment: Print edition and message explanation.

### PE 5: Offense and defense in basketball
- Objective: Use give-and-go and moving to open space
- Objective: Play person-to-person defense
- Vocabulary: give-and-go, defensive stance, help defense, open lane
- Materials: basketball, hoop
- Assessment: Strategy diagram and game observation.

### Health 5: The reproductive systems
- Objective: Identify structures of the female reproductive system
- Objective: Identify structures of the male reproductive system
- Vocabulary: ovary, uterus, vagina, testes, penis, sperm, egg (ovum)
- Materials: labeled diagrams, AMAZE videos
- Assessment: Diagram quiz.
