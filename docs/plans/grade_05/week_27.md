## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 1–2* (90 min): Read chapters 1–2 and list 5 details showing Mary is 'contrary'; discuss why she behaves that way.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Length conversions* (60 min): Measure the room in feet and convert to inches and yards; complete 15 length conversion problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Case studies* (45 min): Read about two California conservation projects and record the problem, science used and results.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Yorktown* (45 min): Map the Yorktown campaign showing the French fleet and armies trapping Cornwallis.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Smoothing* (30 min): Smooth rough edges of the dry sculpture carefully with fine sandpaper or a damp sponge.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Placement* (30 min): Play a 1-v-1 paddle game scoring only when the ball lands in open space.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Alcohol pathway* (20 min): Trace alcohol's path through the body on a diagram and note effects on the brain, liver and stomach.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: The Secret Garden chapters 1–7: Mary arrives at Misselthwaite
- Objective: Describe how the setting reflects Mary's character
- Objective: Compare dialects in the novel
- Vocabulary: contrary, moor, dialect, Yorkshire, manor
- Materials: The Secret Garden by Frances Hodgson Burnett (Project Gutenberg or library), reading journal
- Assessment: Setting drawing; dialect translation; chapter quiz.

### Math 5: Converting customary units
- Objective: Convert length, weight and capacity in customary units
- Objective: Solve conversion word problems
- Vocabulary: inch, foot, yard, mile, ounce, pound, cup, pint, quart, gallon
- Materials: measuring tape, kitchen scale, measuring cups and jugs
- Assessment: Quiz on customary unit conversions.

### Science 5: Communities protecting the environment
- Objective: Research how communities use science to protect resources
- Objective: Combine information from multiple sources
- Vocabulary: conservation, recycling, restoration, watershed protection
- Materials: articles on California efforts (e.g., Monterey Bay sanctuary, water-wise landscaping, condor recovery), note sheet
- Assessment: Fact sheet from multiple sources; quiz.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Victory at Yorktown and new governments
- Objective: Explain how the war ended
- Objective: Describe state constitutions and land ordinances
- Vocabulary: Yorktown, Treaty of Paris (1783), state constitution, Northwest Ordinance, township
- Materials: map of the U.S. in 1783, Northwest Ordinance excerpt
- Assessment: Township grid and Unit 7 test.

### Art & Music 5: Finishing and presenting sculpture
- Objective: Finish a sculpture with quality craftsmanship
- Objective: Argue for safe and effective presentation
- Vocabulary: craftsmanship, glaze, pedestal, display
- Materials: dried sculpture, acrylic or tempera paint, clear glue or Mod Podge
- Assessment: Sculpture craftsmanship rubric and display plan.

### PE 5: Net games and strategy
- Objective: Place the ball into open space
- Objective: Play modified net games safely and fairly
- Vocabulary: open space, placement, rotation, court
- Materials: rope net, beach ball, paddles, balls
- Assessment: Net skills test.

### Health 5: Alcohol and the body
- Objective: Describe effects of alcohol on the brain and body
- Objective: Explain impaired judgment
- Vocabulary: alcohol, depressant, blood alcohol, impaired, liver
- Materials: body diagram, goggles or blindfold (optional)
- Assessment: Alcohol quiz.
