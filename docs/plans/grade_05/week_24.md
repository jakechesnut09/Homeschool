## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Choosing a position* (90 min): Choose a debatable question (e.g., Should schools have longer recess?) and write a clear opinion statement.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *What is volume?* (60 min): Fill 3 small boxes with sugar cubes and compare which holds the most; define a unit cube and cubic unit.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Model all the water* (45 min): Use 1 liter of water to model Earth's water, measuring out 97% ocean, then dividing the remaining 3% into ice, groundwater and surface water.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Strengths and weaknesses* (45 min): Compare the strengths and weaknesses of the British and Continental armies in a chart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *AB and ABA* (30 min): Listen to two short pieces and label sections A and B using colored shapes.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Ready position* (30 min): Practice ready position and shuffling to a tossed ball.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *What's in a cigarette* (20 min): Read about nicotine, tar and carbon monoxide and label a body diagram with their effects.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 117, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Gathering evidence* (90 min): Gather 6 facts or quotes from at least 2 sources and record them with source names.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Building prisms* (60 min): Build 6 rectangular prisms with given dimensions from cubes and record the number of cubes in each.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *USGS data* (45 min): Read USGS Water Science School data on water distribution and record percentages in a table.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Bunker Hill to Trenton* (45 min): Map Bunker Hill, the loss of New York and Washington's crossing to Trenton.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Rondo* (30 min): Listen to Mouret's 'Rondeau' and map the ABACA form with a listening map.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Making a platform* (30 min): Practice the forearm platform with self-bumps of a beach ball.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Straw breathing* (20 min): Jog in place 1 minute, then breathe through a thin straw for 30 seconds to simulate damaged lungs; write a reflection.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 118, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Organizing reasons* (90 min): Group evidence under 3 reasons and order them from least to most convincing.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Layers* (60 min): Count cubes in one layer and multiply by number of layers for 8 prism pictures.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Graphing* (45 min): Make a circle graph and a bar graph of salt water vs. fresh water and fresh-water reservoirs.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Saratoga* (45 min): Read about Saratoga and explain why it convinced France to join the war.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Theme and variations* (30 min): Listen to Mozart's 'Twelve Variations on Ah vous dirai-je, Maman' and describe how 3 variations change the theme.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Partner bumps* (30 min): Bump tosses from a partner back to them, counting consecutive good passes.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Addiction and the brain* (20 min): Learn how nicotine changes the brain's reward system and why teens get addicted faster.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 119, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Introductory elements* (90 min): Learn commas after introductory words, phrases and clauses and write 10 sentences for the essay using them.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Nets and boxes* (60 min): Cut out and fold box nets, predict how many centimeter cubes fill each, then test.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Thinking about the data* (45 min): Answer questions like 'What fraction of fresh water is easily available?' using the graphs.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Valley Forge* (45 min): Read an excerpt of Washington's letter describing Valley Forge hardships and list soldiers' needs.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Movement map* (30 min): Create a movement routine that changes with each section of 'Für Elise' to show its form.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Bump to target* (30 min): Bump to a hula hoop target from tosses at different heights.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Secondhand smoke* (20 min): Research secondhand smoke effects and CA smoke-free laws.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 120, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Conference on the organizer and take a quiz on commas with introductory elements.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz finding volume of prisms by counting cubes and layers.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write an explanation, using the graphs as evidence, for why fresh water is a limited resource.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Create an annotated battle map with 5 battles and their significance.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Unit 6 assessment* (30 min): Take a listening assessment identifying form, major/minor and melodic contour.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Self-assess bump form with cues (flat platform, legs lift, no swing).
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a tobacco-effects quiz.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Planning an opinion essay
- Objective: Choose an opinion and gather reasons from multiple sources
- Objective: Organize reasons logically
- Vocabulary: thesis, logical order, introductory phrase, source
- Materials: sources on chosen topic, opinion organizer
- Assessment: Completed opinion organizer; comma quiz.

### Math 5: Understanding volume with unit cubes
- Objective: Define volume as cubic units filling a solid
- Objective: Measure volume by counting cubes
- Vocabulary: volume, unit cube, cubic unit, rectangular prism
- Materials: sugar cubes or centimeter cubes, small boxes, net templates
- Assessment: Quiz on volume by counting unit cubes; observation during building.

### Science 5: Where is Earth's water?
- Objective: Describe the amounts of water in Earth's reservoirs
- Objective: Graph the percentages of salt water and fresh water
- Vocabulary: reservoir, salt water, fresh water, glacier, groundwater, percentage
- Materials: 1 L water bottle, measuring cups and spoons, eyedropper, graph paper, USGS water distribution data
- Assessment: Graphs and written explanation.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Early battles and turning points
- Objective: Map major battles and campaigns
- Objective: Explain why Saratoga was a turning point
- Vocabulary: campaign, turning point, Saratoga, Valley Forge, strategy
- Materials: Revolutionary War battle map, Washington's letter from Valley Forge (excerpt)
- Assessment: Annotated battle map.

### Art & Music 5: Musical form: AB, ABA, rondo and theme and variations
- Objective: Identify musical forms by listening
- Objective: Map the form of a piece
- Vocabulary: form, binary, ternary, rondo, theme and variations
- Materials: recordings: Mozart 'Twinkle Variations,' Beethoven 'Für Elise,' Mouret 'Rondeau'
- Assessment: Listening map and Unit 6 assessment.

### PE 5: Volleyball: forearm pass
- Objective: Perform the forearm pass (bump) to a target
- Objective: Move feet to get under the ball
- Vocabulary: forearm pass, platform, ready position, shuffle
- Materials: beach ball or volleyball trainer, rope or net between two chairs
- Assessment: Bump consecutive-count and cue self-assessment.

### Health 5: Tobacco and nicotine effects
- Objective: Describe effects of tobacco on the body
- Objective: Explain nicotine addiction
- Vocabulary: nicotine, tar, carbon monoxide, addiction, secondhand smoke
- Materials: straws, jar and cotton balls
- Assessment: Tobacco quiz.
