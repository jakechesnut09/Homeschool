## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *First read* (90 min): Read 'Paul Revere's Ride' aloud and summarize what happens in each stanza in one line.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Mixed numbers and improper fractions* (60 min): Convert 15 numbers between mixed and improper form using fraction circles and then multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Harvest* (45 min): Carefully remove plants, dry the soil and weigh it; compare to starting soil mass.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *1619 and beyond* (45 min): Read about the arrival of enslaved Africans in Virginia in 1619 and how slavery became law over the following decades.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Spirituals* (30 min): Listen to 'Swing Low, Sweet Chariot' and learn about the origins of spirituals among enslaved African Americans.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Backhand throw* (30 min): Practice the backhand grip, stance and wrist snap with short throws.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Plaque check* (20 min): Use a disclosing tablet or grape juice to see plaque, then brush for two minutes and recheck.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Structure and rhythm* (90 min): Mark the rhyme scheme and galloping rhythm of the first three stanzas and explain how the sound matches the action.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Adding mixed numbers* (60 min): Add 12 mixed numbers like 2 3/4 + 1 2/3, renaming and regrouping fraction sums greater than 1.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Plant mass* (45 min): Weigh the plants and compare the gain in plant mass to the change in soil mass in a table.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Lives of enslaved families* (45 min): Read about enslaved families' work, culture, music and faith and how they kept traditions alive.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Code songs* (30 min): Read the lyrics of 'Follow the Drinking Gourd' and 'Wade in the Water' and discuss their possible hidden meanings.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Catching* (30 min): Practice pancake and rim catches at different heights.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Flossing* (20 min): Practice flossing technique and explain how sugar and plaque cause cavities.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 73, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Poem vs. history* (90 min): Compare the poem's events with the Jean Fritz biography and list what Longfellow changed and why.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Subtracting mixed numbers* (60 min): Subtract 12 mixed numbers like 5 1/4 - 2 2/3, regrouping a whole when needed.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Graphing* (45 min): Graph soil mass change vs. plant mass gain for each cup.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Resistance* (45 min): Learn about forms of resistance including slowdowns, escape and the Stono Rebellion, and record examples.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Work songs* (30 min): Learn a call-and-response work song and explain how rhythm helped people work together.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Accuracy* (30 min): Throw through a hula hoop target from increasing distances.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Check-ups* (20 min): Interview a parent about your last dental and medical check-up and list what was checked.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 74, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Imagery* (90 min): Illustrate the three most vivid images from the poem and label the words that created each picture.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Kitchen fractions* (60 min): Use measuring cups to combine 1 1/2 cups and 2/3 cup of water, then compute the total and how much more is needed to make 3 cups.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Argument writing* (45 min): Write a scientific argument with claim, evidence from your data and van Helmont, and reasoning about air and water.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Opposing slavery* (45 min): Analyze the 1688 Germantown Quaker Petition and Phillis Wheatley's poem as early voices in the debate.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Expressive performance* (30 min): Compare two performances of the same spiritual and note differences in tempo, dynamics and timbre.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Disc golf* (30 min): Set up a 6-hole backyard or park disc golf course and play a round, counting throws.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Orthodontics and sports* (20 min): Read about braces and mouth guards and when they are needed.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 75, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Recite two memorized stanzas with expression and take a quiz on stanza, rhyme scheme and imagery.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz on adding and subtracting mixed numbers.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Unit 4 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 4 assessment and share the argument aloud with family.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph explaining how slavery became institutionalized in the South, using two sources.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Sing a spiritual with chosen expressive qualities and write about the performers' intent.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain how angle of the disc affects its flight and demonstrate a straight throw.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Create a dental care poster and answer 5 questions on oral health.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Narrative poetry: 'Paul Revere's Ride' by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow
- Objective: Explain how stanzas build a narrative poem
- Objective: Read poetry aloud with rhythm and expression
- Vocabulary: stanza, rhyme scheme, meter, narrative poem, imagery
- Materials: 'Paul Revere's Ride' (Poetry Foundation), Jean Fritz biography for comparison
- Assessment: Recitation; poetry terms quiz; poem vs. history chart.

### Math 5: Adding and subtracting mixed numbers
- Objective: Add mixed numbers with unlike denominators
- Objective: Subtract mixed numbers including regrouping
- Vocabulary: mixed number, improper fraction, regroup
- Materials: fraction circles, measuring cups
- Assessment: Quiz on mixed number addition and subtraction.

### Science 5: Plant investigation results and argument
- Objective: Analyze soil mass data from the plant investigation
- Objective: Argue that plants gain mass chiefly from air and water
- Vocabulary: argument, claim, reasoning, data analysis
- Materials: plant cups, kitchen scale, graph paper
- Assessment: Written scientific argument; Unit 4 test.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Slavery in the colonies
- Objective: Describe the introduction and growth of slavery
- Objective: Explain how enslaved people resisted and preserved their culture
- Vocabulary: slavery, enslaved, resistance, plantation, abolition
- Materials: Phillis Wheatley poem 'On Being Brought from Africa to America', 1688 Germantown Quaker Petition Against Slavery (excerpt), excerpt from Olaudah Equiano's narrative
- Assessment: Source-based paragraph.

### Art & Music 5: Spirituals and work songs
- Objective: Explain the history and hidden meanings of spirituals
- Objective: Perform with expressive qualities
- Vocabulary: spiritual, work song, code song, syncopation, dynamics
- Materials: recordings of 'Follow the Drinking Gourd,' 'Swing Low, Sweet Chariot,' 'Wade in the Water'
- Assessment: Listening comparison and performance.

### PE 5: Flying disc skills
- Objective: Throw a backhand with accuracy
- Objective: Catch a disc with pancake and rim catches
- Vocabulary: backhand, forehand, pancake catch, flight, spin
- Materials: flying disc, hula hoops or targets
- Assessment: Disc golf scorecard and demonstration.

### Health 5: Dental health and check-ups
- Objective: Practice proper brushing and flossing
- Objective: Explain the purpose of dental and medical check-ups
- Vocabulary: plaque, fluoride, orthodontist, check-up, immunization
- Materials: toothbrush, floss, disclosing tablets (optional)
- Assessment: Dental poster and questions.
