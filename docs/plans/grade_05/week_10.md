## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Text structure overview* (90 min): Learn five text structures and their signal words, then sort 10 short paragraphs about exploration by structure.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Decimal x whole number* (60 min): Solve 12 problems like 4 x 0.36 using repeated addition, disks, and then multiplication, comparing the results.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *The problem* (45 min): Read about communities without clean water and define the design problem with criteria (clearest water) and constraints (materials, budget of play money).
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Pilgrims and Wampanoag* (45 min): Read about the Pilgrims' voyage and the Wampanoag people, including Massasoit and Tisquantum (Squanto).
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Looking closely* (30 min): Study Thomas Cole's 'The Oxbow' for 5 minutes and list what you see, think and wonder.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Push pass* (30 min): Practice the inside-of-foot push pass against a wall 50 times, alternating feet.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Activity benefits* (20 min): Read about how activity helps the heart, bones, sleep and mood, and list five benefits.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 47, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Comparing structures* (90 min): Read two articles on navigation tools (one chronological, one problem/solution) and compare their structures in a chart.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Tenths x tenths* (60 min): Shade overlapping areas on a hundredths grid to model 0.3 x 0.6 = 0.18 for 8 problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Testing materials* (45 min): Test each filter material alone and record how clear the dirty water becomes on a 1–5 turbidity scale.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Primary source: Mayflower Compact* (45 min): Read the Mayflower Compact, paraphrase it, and explain why it matters to self-government.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Atmospheric perspective* (30 min): Identify how Cole and Bierstadt make distant mountains lighter and bluer, then practice with a 3-layer paint study.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Trapping* (30 min): Trap passes with the sole, inside of foot and thigh, then pass back.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Moderate vs. vigorous* (20 min): Try walking, jumping rope and dancing for 3 minutes each and use the talk test to classify intensity.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 48, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Domain vocabulary* (90 min): Use context and a dictionary to define 10 navigation terms (compass, sextant, latitude) and draw a labeled picture of two.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Placing the decimal point* (60 min): Multiply 12 problems like 2.4 x 1.3 as whole numbers, then place the decimal by estimating and by counting place values.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Brainstorming designs* (45 min): Sketch 3 different filter designs with labeled layers and estimated cost.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Bradford's account* (45 min): Read an excerpt of Bradford's Of Plymouth Plantation on the first winter and the 1621 harvest and compare it to a Wampanoag perspective.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *California connection* (30 min): Compare Bierstadt's Yosemite painting with a photograph of Yosemite and discuss how the artist exaggerated for effect.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Passing gates* (30 min): Pass through cone gates to a partner, scoring a point for each gate.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Weekly plan* (20 min): Create a 7-day plan with at least 60 minutes daily including muscle- and bone-strengthening activity 3 days.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 49, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Main ideas* (90 min): Identify two main ideas in an article about the Age of Exploration and summarize it in 5 sentences.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Decimal products in context* (60 min): Solve 6 word problems such as the cost of 3.5 pounds of apples at $1.29 per pound.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Choosing a design* (45 min): Compare the 3 designs in a decision matrix against criteria and constraints and choose one to build.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Puritans* (45 min): Read about John Winthrop's 'city upon a hill' and Puritan life and write what Puritans valued.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Comparing styles* (30 min): Compare a Hudson River School painting to a Native American landscape design or a modern landscape and discuss how each would be judged.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Lead passes* (30 min): Pass ahead of a moving partner so they receive the ball in stride.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Screen time swap* (20 min): Track screen time for a day and plan two active swaps.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 50, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a text structure quiz and a domain vocabulary check.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz on 10 decimal multiplication problems and explain why 0.5 x 0.4 is smaller than both factors.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Build the first prototype and write a prediction of how it will perform.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz on the New England founding and write a two-sentence summary of the Mayflower Compact.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write a paragraph interpreting the mood of one landscape using evidence from color, light and composition.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Analyze a partner's pass using cues (plant foot, ankle locked, follow-through) and give feedback.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Follow day one of your plan and complete a short quiz on activity guidelines.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Informational text structures: navigating the oceans
- Objective: Identify text structures in informational articles
- Objective: Determine meanings of domain-specific words
- Vocabulary: chronology, cause and effect, problem and solution, compare and contrast, astrolabe, latitude
- Materials: ReadWorks articles on exploration and navigation, text structure graphic organizers, dictionary
- Assessment: Text structure quiz; vocabulary check; written summary.

### Math 5: Multiplying decimals
- Objective: Multiply decimals by whole numbers
- Objective: Multiply decimals by decimals using area models and place value reasoning
- Vocabulary: decimal product, area model, estimate
- Materials: hundredths grids, colored pencils
- Assessment: Quiz on decimal multiplication with written explanation.

### Science 5: Designing a water filter: define and generate
- Objective: Define criteria and constraints for a design problem
- Objective: Generate and compare multiple solutions
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraints, prototype, filter, turbidity
- Materials: plastic bottles, sand, gravel, cotton balls, coffee filters, activated charcoal (aquarium), dirty water (soil, leaves, food coloring)
- Assessment: Design notebook: criteria/constraints, sketches and decision matrix.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Plymouth and the Massachusetts Bay Colony
- Objective: Analyze the Mayflower Compact as an early form of self-government
- Objective: Describe Puritan beliefs and life
- Vocabulary: Pilgrim, Puritan, Mayflower Compact, Wampanoag, covenant
- Materials: text of the Mayflower Compact, excerpt of William Bradford's Of Plymouth Plantation
- Assessment: Mayflower Compact paraphrase and quiz.

### Art & Music 5: The Hudson River School and American landscapes
- Objective: Analyze mood and meaning in landscape paintings
- Objective: Compare criteria across styles
- Vocabulary: landscape, atmospheric perspective, Romanticism, sublime, mood
- Materials: images of Thomas Cole's 'The Oxbow' and Albert Bierstadt's Yosemite paintings (Google Arts & Culture)
- Assessment: Written interpretation paragraph and atmospheric perspective study.

### PE 5: Passing and receiving
- Objective: Pass with the inside of the foot to a moving partner
- Objective: Trap the ball to control it
- Vocabulary: push pass, trap, lead pass, first touch
- Materials: soccer ball, cones for gates, wall or rebounder
- Assessment: Passing gates score and peer feedback form.

### Health 5: 60 minutes a day: planning activity
- Objective: Plan a weekly activity schedule
- Objective: Explain benefits of activity for body and mind
- Vocabulary: moderate activity, vigorous activity, warm-up, cool-down, screen time
- Materials: calendar, jump rope
- Assessment: Weekly activity plan and quiz.
