## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Note-taking* (90 min): Take 15 paraphrased note cards from 3 sources, coding each card to a research question and its source.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Equivalent fractions review* (60 min): Fold paper strips to show 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8 and generate 3 equivalent fractions for each of 10 fractions using multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Floating leaf disks* (45 min): Do the floating leaf disk investigation with spinach in baking soda water under a lamp and count disks rising over time.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Great Awakening* (45 min): Read about Jonathan Edwards and George Whitefield and Franklin's account of hearing Whitefield preach; explain how revivals encouraged questioning authority.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Native American music* (30 min): Listen to a Smithsonian Folkways recording of a Native American song and describe the instruments, vocables and steady drum.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Throwing cues* (30 min): Practice the overhand throw cues (side to target, step with opposite foot, rotate, follow through) at a wall target.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Ad techniques* (20 min): Learn 6 advertising techniques (bandwagon, celebrity, fun, health claims, giveaways, beautiful people) and find examples in magazines.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Paraphrase vs. plagiarism* (90 min): Practice paraphrasing 5 passages and compare to the originals; finish note cards.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Simplifying* (60 min): Simplify 15 fractions by dividing by common factors and match simplified and unsimplified fraction cards.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Light vs. dark* (45 min): Repeat the leaf disk test in darkness and compare results to show plants need light.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Colonial work* (45 min): Read about apprentices and artisans (blacksmith, cooper, printer) and choose a trade to research.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Purposes of music* (30 min): Discuss and chart purposes of songs (ceremony, storytelling, celebration, work) with examples.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Distance throws* (30 min): Throw for distance at 3 different release angles and measure to find the best angle.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Edited images* (20 min): Watch a short before-and-after photo-retouching video and discuss how it affects body image.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 63, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Outlining* (90 min): Sort note cards into 3–4 subtopics and build an outline with headings.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Common denominators* (60 min): List multiples to find common denominators for 12 pairs like 2/3 and 3/4 and rename both fractions.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Stomata peel* (45 min): Paint clear nail polish on a leaf, peel it with tape, and look for stomata with a magnifier or phone microscope.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Hands-on colonial life* (45 min): Try a colonial craft or chore (dipping a candle with adult help, making butter in a jar, writing with a quill).
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Colonial psalms* (30 min): Listen to a 'lining out' psalm from colonial New England and try call-and-response singing.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Accuracy targets* (30 min): Throw at targets from 5, 10 and 15 m and record hits.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Food ads* (20 min): Analyze two kids' food commercials: target audience, techniques and what they leave out.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 64, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Drafting* (90 min): Draft the introduction and two body sections of the report using transition words like 'in addition' and 'however'.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Comparing unlike fractions* (60 min): Compare 12 pairs of unlike fractions by renaming with common denominators and by benchmark reasoning.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Plant growth check* (45 min): Measure plant heights in the soil cups and paper towel bags and record growth data.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *States strand: South* (45 min): Review Southeast states and learn the South Central states and capitals (TX, OK, AR, LA).
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Making an instrument* (30 min): Make a simple drum or rattle from household items and play a steady-beat pattern.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Video analysis* (30 min): Record your throw in slow motion and compare to the cues.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Gender messages* (20 min): Compare toy or clothing ads aimed at boys vs. girls and note stereotypes about how bodies should look.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 65, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Finish the draft with a conclusion and a list of sources; self-check against the informative rubric.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Take a quiz on equivalent fractions, simplifying and common denominators.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Draw a model showing air and water entering a plant and sugar being made, then take a quiz.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Unit 3 assessment* (45 min): Take the Unit 3 test on founding the colonies and colonial life.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write how music connected to daily life in one culture, citing a recording.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain in writing how force and angle affected your distance results.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Create a counter-ad that promotes healthy body image or a healthy food honestly.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Research: note-taking and drafting an informative report
- Objective: Paraphrase information in notes
- Objective: Integrate information from several texts in an informative draft
- Vocabulary: paraphrase, plagiarism, heading, subtopic
- Materials: note cards, sources from week 12, outline template
- Assessment: Note cards and outline; complete informative draft.

### Math 5: Equivalent fractions and common denominators
- Objective: Generate equivalent fractions
- Objective: Find common denominators for unlike fractions
- Vocabulary: equivalent fraction, common denominator, multiple, simplify
- Materials: fraction strips, paper for folding
- Assessment: Quiz on equivalent fractions and common denominators.

### Science 5: Plants need air and water
- Objective: Gather evidence that plants use carbon dioxide and water
- Objective: Explain the role of light
- Vocabulary: leaf, stomata, chlorophyll, glucose, oxygen
- Materials: growing bean plants, spinach leaves, baking soda, syringe, cup of water, lamp, clear nail polish and tape
- Assessment: Leaf disk data and plant model diagram; quiz.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: The First Great Awakening and colonial daily life
- Objective: Explain the significance of the Great Awakening
- Objective: Describe daily life, work and education in the colonies
- Vocabulary: Great Awakening, revival, apprentice, artisan, hornbook
- Materials: excerpt describing George Whitefield's preaching (Benjamin Franklin's Autobiography), colonial games or crafts
- Assessment: Unit 3 test; trade research card.

### Art & Music 5: Native American and early colonial music
- Objective: Describe the functions of music in Native American cultures
- Objective: Compare instruments and song forms
- Vocabulary: vocable, drum, flute, hymn, psalm, call and response
- Materials: Smithsonian Folkways recordings (free streaming excerpts), hand drum or homemade drum
- Assessment: Listening log and written response.

### PE 5: Overhand throwing for accuracy and distance
- Objective: Use correct overhand throwing mechanics
- Objective: Explain how force and angle affect distance
- Vocabulary: opposition, hip rotation, follow-through, angle of release
- Materials: tennis balls, targets (hula hoop or chalk circles), measuring tape
- Assessment: Throwing data and written explanation.

### Health 5: Media, ads and body image
- Objective: Analyze how media edits images
- Objective: Analyze ads targeting kids
- Vocabulary: media literacy, photo editing, target audience, advertising technique
- Materials: magazines, computer
- Assessment: Counter-ad with technique explanation.
