## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 5–6* (90 min): Read chapters 5–6 and mark three moments where Brian makes a decision; rate each as smart or risky with evidence.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Grouping symbols* (60 min): Evaluate 15 expressions with parentheses such as (6 + 4) x 3 vs 6 + 4 x 3 and discuss why the answers differ.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Prediction* (45 min): Predict whether a sealed bag of ice will weigh more, less or the same after melting, and explain the prediction.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Primary source: Columbus's journal* (45 min): Read the October 12, 1492 journal entry and note what Columbus observed and what he wanted.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Dotted rhythms* (30 min): Learn dotted half and dotted quarter notes and clap patterns from 'America the Beautiful.'
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Criss-cross* (30 min): Learn the criss-cross and side swing open, practicing with feedback cues.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Label anatomy* (20 min): Label every part of a Nutrition Facts panel on a printout and define each in your own words.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Figurative language* (90 min): Collect 6 similes, metaphors or personifications from chapters 5–7 and explain what each shows about Brian's situation.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Brackets and braces* (60 min): Evaluate nested expressions like 2 x {5 + [12 - (3 x 2)]}, working from the inside out on 12 problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Melting test* (45 min): Weigh 3 sealed bags of ice, let them melt and reweigh, recording data in a table.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Taíno perspective* (45 min): Read about Taíno society and the effects of disease and forced labor after contact, and compare to Columbus's account.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Syncopation* (30 min): Clap and say syncopated patterns (ta ti-ta ti) and find syncopation in a song like 'Ragtime' by Scott Joplin.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Double under attempts* (30 min): Practice high jumps with fast wrists to attempt double unders.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Serving size test* (20 min): Pour your usual cereal, measure it, and compare it to the label's serving size; recalculate sugar for your real portion.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Paired informational text* (90 min): Read a ReadWorks wilderness survival article, identify two main ideas and compare its advice to Brian's choices.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Writing expressions* (60 min): Translate 12 phrases like 'add 8 and 7, then multiply by 2' into expressions and write phrases for given expressions.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Freezing test* (45 min): Weigh sealed bags of water, freeze overnight and reweigh; also melt chocolate chips in a sealed bag in warm water and reweigh.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Coronado and de Soto* (45 min): Trace the routes of Coronado and de Soto on a map and record their goals, obstacles and results.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Body percussion ostinato* (30 min): Create a 4-beat body percussion ostinato (stomp, pat, clap, snap) and perform it with a recording.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Long rope* (30 min): With two turners (family or a tree tie-off), practice entering, jumping 10 and exiting a long rope.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Ingredient lists* (20 min): Read ingredient lists in order of weight and identify whole grains and different names for sugar.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 8–9* (90 min): Read chapters 8–9 (the porcupine and fire) and write a journal entry on why the hatchet becomes so important.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Interpreting expressions* (60 min): Explain without calculating why 3 x (18,932 + 921) is three times as large as 18,932 + 921, then solve 5 multi-step multiplication word problems.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Graphing* (45 min): Create a bar graph comparing before and after weights for all trials and calculate any differences.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Columbian Exchange* (45 min): Sort plants, animals and diseases that crossed the Atlantic in each direction and explain one lasting effect.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Bucket drumming* (30 min): Improvise 4-measure rhythm solos on a bucket or pot in a call-and-response with a family member.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Peer coaching* (30 min): Coach a family member on one trick using critical cues and encouragement.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Cereal compare* (20 min): Compare 5 cereals for fiber, added sugar and sodium and use the decision steps to choose the healthiest.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 30, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Take a quiz on chapters 5–9 and figurative language, and practice verb tense to show sequence in 5 sentences.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Play 'Target 24' with dice to build expressions, then take a 12-item quiz on evaluating and writing expressions.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a CER on whether weight is conserved during melting and freezing, explaining small measurement errors.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Write a paragraph evaluating Columbus's legacy from two perspectives.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform the ostinato and an improvised solo and evaluate using a steady-beat rubric.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform 5 different tricks and record which ones you mastered.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Take a label-reading test using two unfamiliar products.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Hatchet chapters 5–9: first days of survival
- Objective: Trace Brian's changing mindset
- Objective: Interpret figurative language
- Vocabulary: self-pity, metaphor, personification, mindset
- Materials: Hatchet, ReadWorks article on wilderness survival, sticky notes
- Assessment: Figurative language chart; journal entry using evidence; chapters 5–9 quiz.

### Math 5: Numerical expressions and multiplication word problems
- Objective: Evaluate expressions with parentheses, brackets and braces
- Objective: Write and interpret expressions without evaluating
- Vocabulary: expression, parentheses, brackets, braces, evaluate, order of operations
- Materials: expression cards, dice
- Assessment: Quiz on evaluating and writing numerical expressions; word problem solutions with labeled answers.

### Science 5: Conservation of weight when melting and freezing
- Objective: Measure weight before and after a change of state
- Objective: Graph data as evidence of conservation
- Vocabulary: conservation, weight, closed system, physical change
- Materials: kitchen scale (grams), ice cubes, sealed zip bags, chocolate chips, graph paper
- Assessment: Bar graph and CER on conservation.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Columbus and the Spanish in the Americas
- Objective: Evaluate Columbus's voyages from multiple perspectives
- Objective: Describe Spanish exploration of North America
- Vocabulary: Taíno, conquistador, Columbian Exchange, encomienda
- Materials: excerpt of Columbus's journal, October 12, 1492, map of Coronado's and de Soto's routes
- Assessment: Perspective paragraph and route map.

### Art & Music 5: Syncopation, dotted rhythms and body percussion
- Objective: Perform dotted and syncopated rhythms
- Objective: Improvise a rhythm pattern
- Vocabulary: dotted half note, dotted quarter note, syncopation, ostinato, improvise
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Rhythm, buckets or pots for drumming
- Assessment: Performance of ostinato and improvisation.

### PE 5: Advanced rope tricks and long rope
- Objective: Perform crosses, double unders (attempt) and side swings
- Objective: Enter and exit a long rope
- Vocabulary: criss-cross, double under, long rope, turner, entry
- Materials: jump ropes, long rope (or two ropes tied)
- Assessment: Trick mastery chart.

### Health 5: Reading Nutrition Facts labels
- Objective: Use each part of a Nutrition Facts label
- Objective: Compare foods using serving size
- Vocabulary: serving size, calories, ingredient list, whole grain
- Materials: 5 cereal boxes, measuring cup
- Assessment: Label-reading test.
