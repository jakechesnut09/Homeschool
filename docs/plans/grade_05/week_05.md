## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapters 1–2* (90 min): Read chapters 1–2 and list sentences Paulsen uses to create suspense (short fragments, repetition); explain the effect of one.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Estimating products* (60 min): Round factors to estimate 15 products (like 389 x 42 ≈ 400 x 40) and do a 3-minute multiplication fact fluency check.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Melting race* (45 min): Time ice cubes melting in different places (hand, counter, sunny window) and graph the times.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Gold, God and glory* (45 min): Read about the reasons for European exploration (trade, wealth, religion, Reconquista, competition) and rank them with evidence.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Feel the beat* (30 min): Pat the steady beat to 3 recordings (a march, a waltz, a pop song) and decide whether each feels in 2, 3 or 4.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Rope basics* (30 min): Size the rope and practice the basic bounce and alternate step for 3 x 1-minute sets.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Sugar in drinks* (20 min): Measure the teaspoons of sugar in 5 drinks (soda, juice, sports drink, chocolate milk, water) from their labels and display them with sugar or sand.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Flashbacks* (90 min): Read chapter 3 and identify the flashback to 'the Secret'; explain how this chapter fits the overall structure of the opening.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Area model* (60 min): Solve 8 problems such as 236 x 34 with an area model and add the partial products.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Food coloring diffusion* (45 min): Drop food coloring into hot and cold water and compare how quickly it spreads, linking speed to particle motion.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Navigation tools* (45 min): Build a simple astrolabe from cardboard and a protractor and measure the angle of a tall object.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Note values* (30 min): Complete the musictheory.net lessons on note durations and clap 8 rhythm cards with counting (1-and-2-and).
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Footwork tricks* (30 min): Learn skier, bell and side-straddle tricks and practice each 20 times.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Sodium hunt* (20 min): Compare sodium on labels of canned soup, chips, crackers and fresh vegetables and rank them.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Perfect tenses* (90 min): Learn present, past and future perfect tenses with examples from Hatchet and write 9 sentences about the crash using each tense.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Standard algorithm 3x2* (60 min): Connect partial products to the standard algorithm and solve 10 three-digit by two-digit problems on grid paper to keep columns aligned.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Evaporation and condensation* (45 min): Observe water boiling and condensing on a cold lid (adult handles the stove) and draw particle diagrams for each step.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Ships* (45 min): Compare a caravel to earlier ships and explain how its design made long voyages possible.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Time signatures* (30 min): Draw bar lines into 6 rhythm lines in 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Jumping to music* (30 min): Jump to a song with a steady beat, changing tricks every 8 counts.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Fats* (20 min): Sort food cards into mostly unsaturated vs. saturated fat sources and explain why plant oils and nuts are better choices.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Chapter 4 and conflict* (90 min): Read chapter 4 and create a two-column chart of Brian's internal and external conflicts with page numbers.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Standard algorithm 4x2 and 3x3* (60 min): Solve 10 larger problems like 2,407 x 56, checking each against an estimate.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Model poster* (45 min): Create a poster showing particle arrangements in solid, liquid and gas with arrows for melting, freezing, evaporating and condensing.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Entrepreneurs* (45 min): Analyze how explorers had to convince sponsors to fund voyages; write a short 'pitch' as an explorer.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Rhythm dictation* (30 min): Listen to 6 clapped rhythms and write them in notation.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Heart rate check* (30 min): Measure heart rate after 2 minutes of continuous jumping and compare to jogging.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *5/20 rule* (20 min): Learn the 5% low / 20% high Daily Value rule and apply it to 4 pantry labels.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 25, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Review & check* (90 min): Discuss 'What would you do first?' using text evidence, then take a quiz on chapters 1–4 and perfect tenses.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a 10-problem multiplication quiz and an error analysis: find and fix the mistake in 3 worked examples.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Unit 1 assessment* (45 min): Take a Unit 1 assessment on particles and properties and present the model poster.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Review & check* (45 min): Take a quiz on motives and navigation technology.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Read and clap a 4-measure rhythm at sight and take a note-value quiz.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform a 30-second trick sequence and count successful jumps in 1 minute.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Review & check* (20 min): Complete a label-reading worksheet and write three swaps that cut added sugar or sodium.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Hatchet chapters 1–4: the crash
- Objective: Analyze how the author builds suspense
- Objective: Identify Brian's internal and external conflicts
- Vocabulary: suspense, conflict, flashback, the Secret, perfect tense
- Materials: Hatchet by Gary Paulsen (library), reading journal, map of Canadian wilderness
- Assessment: Reading journal entries; chapters 1–4 quiz including perfect tense items.

### Math 5: Multiplying multi-digit whole numbers
- Objective: Estimate products by rounding
- Objective: Multiply multi-digit numbers using area models and the standard algorithm
- Vocabulary: product, factor, partial products, standard algorithm, estimate
- Materials: grid paper, multiplication fact cards
- Assessment: Quiz on standard-algorithm multiplication with estimation checks.

### Science 5: Particle model and states of matter
- Objective: Explain melting, freezing and evaporation with particle models
- Objective: Communicate a model of matter
- Vocabulary: solid, liquid, melt, freeze, evaporate, condense
- Materials: ice cubes, zip bags, pot with lid (adult help), food coloring, hot and cold water
- Assessment: Unit 1 test and particle model poster rubric.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: Why explore? Motives and technology
- Objective: Explain the reasons Europeans explored
- Objective: Describe navigation technologies
- Vocabulary: Reconquista, spices, caravel, astrolabe, compass, sextant
- Materials: images of an astrolabe and caravel, cardboard, string and protractor to make an astrolabe
- Assessment: Quiz and explorer pitch.

### Art & Music 5: Beat, meter and note values
- Objective: Identify steady beat and meter in 2, 3 and 4
- Objective: Read whole, half, quarter and eighth notes and rests
- Vocabulary: beat, meter, time signature, measure, bar line, rest
- Materials: musictheory.net, rhythm cards, pencil and paper
- Assessment: Note-value quiz and sight-clapping check.

### PE 5: Individual jump rope skills
- Objective: Jump rope with basic and alternate foot steps
- Objective: Monitor heart rate during jump rope
- Vocabulary: basic bounce, skier, bell, side swing, rhythm
- Materials: jump rope sized to height, music with steady beat
- Assessment: Jump rope trick checklist and 1-minute count.

### Health 5: Added sugars, sodium and saturated fat
- Objective: Explain why to limit added sugars, sodium and saturated fat
- Objective: Find them on labels
- Vocabulary: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, % Daily Value
- Materials: sugar cubes or teaspoons, drink containers, food labels
- Assessment: Label-reading worksheet.
