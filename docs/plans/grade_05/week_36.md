## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Presentation day* (90 min): Deliver the multimedia presentation and answer audience questions.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Design the layout* (60 min): Draw a garden plan on a coordinate grid, labeling the corners of each bed with ordered pairs.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Analysis* (45 min): Analyze data, describe patterns and decide whether the hypothesis was supported.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Western states* (45 min): Learn the remaining Mountain and Pacific states and capitals, including Alaska and Hawaii.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Invitations* (30 min): Make and send invitations to family or friends for the exhibition and concert.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Summer plan* (30 min): Create a summer activity plan with weekly goals and favorite activities.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Present* (20 min): Present the campaign to family, a co-op or community group.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Listening and summarizing* (90 min): Watch a family member's or online presentation and summarize its main points and supporting details.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Planter volume* (60 min): Calculate the soil volume for 2 raised planter boxes (including one composite shape) and convert to cubic feet.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Conclusion* (45 min): Write a conclusion with sources of error and a new question for further investigation.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *All 50 practice* (45 min): Practice all 50 states and capitals with a blank map game and online puzzle.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Final rehearsal* (30 min): Run the full concert once and practice bows and introductions.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Field day planning* (30 min): Plan 6 field day stations using skills from this year.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Year review game* (20 min): Play a review game covering nutrition, fitness, hygiene, puberty and ATOD.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Portfolio selection* (90 min): Choose the best narrative, informative and opinion pieces from the year and write a reflection card for each.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Fractions of space* (60 min): Decide what fraction of each bed gets each plant and compute the area for each crop with fraction multiplication.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Display* (45 min): Create a science fair board or slideshow with question, hypothesis, procedure, data, graph and conclusion.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Timeline review* (45 min): Build a class timeline from first peoples to 1850 with 20 key events from the year.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Exhibition and concert* (30 min): Host the exhibition with a gallery talk and perform the concert.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Field day* (30 min): Host a family or co-op field day, leading the stations and encouraging others.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Final assessment* (20 min): Take the end-of-year health assessment.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *Reading reflection* (90 min): List all books read this year, rate them, and write a letter to next year's self about reading goals; do a final fluency check.
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Budget* (60 min): Use decimal prices to total seeds, soil and boards, staying under a $150 budget.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Science fair* (45 min): Present the investigation to family or a co-op science fair and answer questions.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Being a citizen* (45 min): Write a reflection on the American creed: one right you value and one responsibility you will practice.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Evaluating* (30 min): Watch a recording of the concert and evaluate it using established criteria.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Lifetime activities* (30 min): Try a new lifetime activity (hiking, biking, swimming) for 30 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Summer plan* (20 min): Write a summer health plan with activity, sleep and hygiene goals.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~320 min)

- [ ] **ELA 5** — *End-of-year assessment* (90 min): Take an end-of-year reading and writing assessment (cold read with evidence questions and an on-demand essay).
- [ ] **Math 5** — *Present* (60 min): Present the garden plan to family, explaining each calculation, and complete a year-end math reflection.
- [ ] **Science 5** — *Year reflection* (45 min): Review the science notebook and write about the three most important ideas learned this year.
- [ ] **United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation** — *Final assessment* (45 min): Take the 50 states and capitals map test and a year-end history review.
- [ ] **Art & Music 5** — *Year reflection* (30 min): Write a final reflection on your growth in art and music and set a goal for next year.
- [ ] **PE 5** — *Year reflection* (30 min): Write a reflection on your favorite unit, biggest improvement and how you will stay active.
- [ ] **Health 5** — *Reflect* (20 min): Write a reflection on how you have grown this year and what health skill helps you most.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 5: Presentation and year-end portfolio
- Objective: Deliver a multimedia presentation
- Objective: Reflect on growth as a reader and writer
- Vocabulary: portfolio, reflection, growth
- Materials: slideshow, writing portfolio folder
- Assessment: Presentation rubric; portfolio reflections; end-of-year assessment and fluency WPM compared to week 1.

### Math 5: Capstone: Plan a backyard garden
- Objective: Apply fractions, decimals, volume and graphing to a real project
- Objective: Communicate mathematical reasoning in a presentation
- Vocabulary: budget, scale drawing, planter, area, volume
- Materials: graph paper, garden store flyer or website prices, calculator for checking
- Assessment: Capstone project rubric: accuracy of calculations, use of grid and units, clarity of explanation.

### Science 5: Capstone: analyzing and presenting
- Objective: Analyze data and draw conclusions
- Objective: Communicate findings in a science fair format
- Vocabulary: conclusion, analysis, error, display board
- Materials: tri-fold board or slides, graphs, photos
- Assessment: Science fair rubric and year-end reflection.

### United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation: 50 states and capitals; year review
- Objective: Locate all 50 states and name their capitals
- Objective: Connect the year's inquiry questions
- Vocabulary: capital, region, territory, citizen
- Materials: blank U.S. map, state capital flashcards, timeline from all units
- Assessment: 50 states and capitals test and year-end review.

### Art & Music 5: Exhibition and concert night
- Objective: Perform and present with confidence and decorum
- Objective: Evaluate the year's growth
- Vocabulary: audience etiquette, bow, reflection, growth
- Materials: exhibition, concert program, invitations
- Assessment: Exhibition and concert performance; year-end reflection.

### PE 5: Summer activity plan and field day
- Objective: Create a summer activity plan
- Objective: Lead and participate in a field day
- Vocabulary: lifetime activity, field day, leadership
- Materials: field day equipment (ropes, balls, cones, sacks), planning template
- Assessment: Summer plan and field day leadership observation.

### Health 5: Presentation and year review
- Objective: Present the campaign
- Objective: Reflect on growth and health goals
- Vocabulary: reflection, advocacy, goal
- Materials: campaign materials
- Assessment: Campaign presentation and final assessment.
