## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Gather poems* (90 min): Choose 5 favorite poems from the unit (yours and published) for a personal anthology and copy them neatly.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Subtract by place* (60 min): Subtract 586 − 243 with blocks; solve 8 problems without decomposing.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Habitat needs* (40 min): Learn that a habitat provides food, water, shelter, and space; walk around the block and list 4 small habitats (tree, lawn, garden bed, puddle) with one animal that lives in each.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Milk's journey* (30 min): Trace milk from cow to dairy to truck to store to your fridge and draw a flowchart.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Plan* (40 min): Sketch an animal or creature sculpture from 2 views and list the materials needed.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Basket toss* (30 min): Throw beanbags into laundry baskets at 3 distances for points.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Heart pump* (20 min): Squeeze a tennis ball to feel how hard the heart works; learn it pumps blood.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 97, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Rehearse* (90 min): Practice reading each poem at the right pace with expression; mark pauses with slashes. Phonics review game: two-syllable and compound-word bingo.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Decompose a ten* (60 min): Subtract 462 − 128, breaking a ten into 10 ones; record with quick pictures.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Hoop survey 1* (40 min): Lay a hoop on a lawn or garden bed, search it for 15 minutes with a magnifier, and tally every different kind of plant and animal (ants, clover, beetles, grass) in a survey sheet.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Cereal journey* (30 min): Trace a box of cereal from wheat field to factory to store.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Build* (40 min): Build the main form from clay or recyclables.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Clean your room* (30 min): Play a family game throwing soft balls over a 'net' (couch cushions) before the timer ends.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Lung model* (20 min): Build a balloon lung model in a plastic bottle.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 98, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Record* (90 min): Make an audio recording of 3 poems and listen back to self-assess pace and volume. Re-record one to improve it.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Decompose a hundred* (60 min): Subtract 531 − 274, breaking a hundred and a ten; explain each trade.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Hoop survey 2* (40 min): Repeat the same 15-minute hoop survey in a very different spot (sidewalk crack, gravel, or under a tree) and tally kinds of living things the same way.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Grocery visit* (30 min): Visit a grocery store and find 5 foods grown in California.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Details* (40 min): Add texture and details (eyes, fur, scales) and paint.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Hot potato* (30 min): Play hot potato with quick catch-and-throw passes.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Breathing count* (20 min): Count breaths per minute at rest and after jumping.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 99, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Illustrate* (90 min): Add illustrations and a table of contents to the anthology. Write one sentence for each poem explaining why you chose it.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Zeros in the middle* (60 min): Subtract across zeros (400 − 165, 302 − 147) using blocks and counting up; compare strategies.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Graph the data* (40 min): Make a bar graph comparing the number of different kinds of living things found in each spot, and circle any organism found in both places.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Jobs* (30 min): Match food jobs to their roles (farmer, processor, distributor, grocer).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Critique* (40 min): Hold a family 'art critique': say one thing you like and one question about each other's sculptures.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Practice graph* (30 min): Retake the wall-target test from week 17 and graph both scores.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Healthy heart* (20 min): List ways to keep heart and lungs healthy (exercise, no smoking).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 100, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Unit 5 assessment* (90 min): Poetry café: perform 2 poems for family. Unit test: identify rhyme, alliteration, and repetition in a new poem; spelling test of 15 unit words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Solve 10 subtraction problems within 1,000 and check 3 with addition.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Write a claim with evidence: 'The ___ spot had more kinds of living things because it had more ___ (water, shade, plants),' and share it aloud.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Explain the farm-to-table steps for one food.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Unit 5 check* (40 min): Explain texture, form, and repurposing in your sculpture.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain with your graph how practice changed your throwing accuracy.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain how heart and lungs work together to move oxygen.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Poetry recording and anthology
- Objective: Create an audio recording of poems
- Objective: Read poetry fluently with expression
- Objective: Use new vocabulary to describe
- Vocabulary: anthology, expression, pace, recording
- Materials: phone or tablet for audio recording, folder for anthology
- Assessment: Poetry recording and anthology (rubric), unit test on poetic elements.

### Math 2: Subtracting within 1,000
- Objective: Subtract three-digit numbers with models
- Objective: Decompose hundreds and tens
- Objective: Check with addition
- Vocabulary: decompose, trade, difference
- Materials: base-ten blocks, place-value mat
- Assessment: Subtraction within 1,000 quiz.

### Science 2: What is a habitat? Surveying living things nearby
- Objective: Define habitat and its parts
- Objective: Observe and count kinds of living things in a local area
- Objective: Compare two local areas with data
- Vocabulary: habitat, biodiversity, survey, organism
- Materials: hula hoop or 1-meter string loop, magnifier, clipboard and tally sheet, field guide or iNaturalist Seek app (optional)
- Assessment: Hoop-survey data, bar graph, and claim-evidence sentence.

### People Who Make a Difference: From farm to table: today
- Objective: Trace food from farm to table
- Objective: Identify farmers, processors, distributors, and sellers
- Objective: Explain the role of each
- Vocabulary: farmer, processor, distributor, grocer
- Materials: milk carton, cereal box, flowchart paper
- Assessment: Farm-to-table flowchart.

### Art & Music 2: Sculpture project and unit reflection
- Objective: Plan and create an original sculpture
- Objective: Reflect on choices with peers
- Vocabulary: plan, sculpture, critique
- Materials: clay or recyclables, sketchbook
- Assessment: Original sculpture and critique participation.

### PE 2: Throwing and catching games
- Objective: Apply throwing and catching in games
- Objective: Show how practice helps
- Vocabulary: game, rules, practice
- Materials: beanbags, laundry baskets, balls
- Assessment: Before/after accuracy graph.

### Health 2: Heart, lungs and blood
- Objective: Describe the circulatory and respiratory systems
- Vocabulary: heart, lungs, blood, oxygen
- Materials: balloons, straw, plastic bottle
- Assessment: Oral explanation using models.
