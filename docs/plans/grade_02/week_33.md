## Day 1 — Monday (school day 161, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Pooh and the bees* (90 min): Read Chapter 1 of Winnie-the-Pooh (Pooh and the honey bees) and describe Pooh's plan and why it fails. Start a book-club journal page for the chapter.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Halves* (60 min): Fold paper rectangles and circles into halves different ways; decide which folds make equal shares.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *California map* (40 min): Study a physical map of California and find the Sierra Nevada, Central Valley, Sacramento River, Lake Tahoe, and Pacific coast; mark them on a blank outline.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Sally Ride* (30 min): Read a biography of Sally Ride, the first American woman in space (1983), who grew up in Los Angeles, and study a NASA photo of her on the shuttle.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Lyrics* (40 min): Write 4 short lines about your community (places, people, sounds).
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Hopscotch* (30 min): Play hopscotch with correct hopping and jumping and fair turns.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Health helpers* (20 min): Learn about doctors, nurses, dentists, pharmacists and school nurses.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 162, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Pooh gets stuck* (90 min): Read Chapter 2 (Pooh visits Rabbit and gets stuck) and discuss how Pooh and Rabbit each respond to the problem. Read Pooh's dialogue in his voice.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Thirds and fourths* (60 min): Cut play-dough 'pizzas' and paper rectangles into thirds and fourths and label each share.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Map key* (40 min): Create a map key with colors and symbols for mountains, valleys, rivers, lakes, and ocean.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Ellen Ochoa* (30 min): Read about Ellen Ochoa from La Mesa, California, the first Hispanic woman in space and later director of Johnson Space Center.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Rhythm* (40 min): Clap the rhythm of the lyrics and write it in ta and ti-ti notation.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Four square* (30 min): Learn and play four square or a two-square version with a partner.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Interview* (20 min): Interview a health helper or family member about their job.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 163, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Character traits* (90 min): Make a character-trait web for Pooh, Piglet, Rabbit, Owl, and Eeyore with one example from the text for each.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Different shapes, same size* (60 min): Cut identical squares into fourths in three different ways and explain why each piece is still a fourth.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Map the model* (40 min): Use the bird's-eye photo of last week's model to draw a map with the key and a compass rose.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Paper rockets* (30 min): Build and launch straw paper rockets and discuss how Ride and Ochoa used science and engineering.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Melody* (40 min): Set the words to a sol-mi-la-do melody using Song Maker or a xylophone.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Kickball* (30 min): Play a mini kickball game using kicking, throwing and catching.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Checkups* (20 min): Learn what happens at a checkup and why vaccines matter.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 164, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Hunting the Woozle* (90 min): Read Chapter 3 (Pooh and Piglet hunt a Woozle) and explain the joke. Book club: discuss with a family member, taking turns and asking questions.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Snack shares* (60 min): Share a tortilla or sandwich equally among 2, 3, or 4 people and describe the shares ('a third of the tortilla').
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Neighborhood map* (40 min): Draw a map of your neighborhood or a nearby park showing hills, creeks, ponds, or the coast, with a key.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Inspire others* (30 min): Discuss how Sally Ride started programs to get girls interested in science; write one way you could inspire someone.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Feedback* (40 min): Perform for a family member, get a 'star and wish,' and revise one line or melody spot.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Activity plan* (30 min): Make a list of 5 games you can play at home or the park and schedule two.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Thank-you card* (20 min): Write a thank-you card to a health helper.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 165, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 33 review* (90 min): Answer written who/what/why questions on Chapters 1–3 and do a one-minute fluency read of a Pooh passage.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 10 test* (60 min): Identify shapes, partition rectangles, and show halves, thirds, fourths (12 items).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Compare the model and the map: what does the map show well and what does the model show well (height)?
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Add both astronauts to the hero timeline and chart their traits.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Record* (40 min): Record the revised song and add a percussion ostinato.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Play one game from your list and tell why it is fun and good exercise.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Match 6 helpers to their jobs.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Winnie-the-Pooh: following characters across chapters
- Objective: Follow characters and plot across chapters of a chapter book
- Objective: Read with expression in dialogue
- Objective: Discuss books in a book club
- Vocabulary: chapter, dialogue, character trait, book club
- Materials: Winnie-the-Pooh (A. A. Milne, public domain in the U.S.), book club journal
- Assessment: Book-club journal entries and written comprehension; fluency record.

### Math 2: Equal shares: halves, thirds, fourths
- Objective: Partition circles and rectangles into equal shares
- Objective: Describe shares using halves, thirds, fourths
- Objective: Recognize equal shares can look different
- Vocabulary: half, third, fourth, equal share, whole
- Materials: paper circles and rectangles, play dough, crackers or tortillas
- Assessment: Unit 10 test.

### Science 2: Mapping land and water
- Objective: Draw a map of land and water from a model
- Objective: Use a map key and compass rose
- Objective: Read a map of California
- Vocabulary: map, key, symbol, compass rose, bird's-eye view
- Materials: photo of clay model, colored pencils, California physical map
- Assessment: Model map and California map with key.

### People Who Make a Difference: Sally Ride and Ellen Ochoa: reaching for the stars
- Objective: Explain how Sally Ride and Ellen Ochoa made history in space
- Objective: Analyze NASA photographs
- Objective: Connect science and courage
- Vocabulary: astronaut, space shuttle, NASA, engineer
- Materials: NASA photos of Sally Ride on STS-7 (1983), biographies of Sally Ride and Ellen Ochoa, paper rocket supplies
- Assessment: Hero-traits chart and timeline entries.

### Art & Music 2: Compose a song about my community
- Objective: Compose a short song with rhythm and melody
- Objective: Document it with notation or recording
- Objective: Revise using feedback
- Vocabulary: lyrics, verse, melody, rhythm
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Song Maker, recording device, percussion
- Assessment: Recorded community song with notation.

### PE 2: Classic playground and backyard games
- Objective: Play traditional games with skill and fair play
- Objective: Choose activities to do outside of school
- Vocabulary: traditional game, active play
- Materials: chalk, ball, jump rope
- Assessment: Activity plan and game play.

### Health 2: Community health helpers
- Objective: Identify community health helpers
- Vocabulary: doctor, nurse, pharmacist, public health
- Materials: 'Community Helpers' books (library)
- Assessment: Matching and interview notes.
