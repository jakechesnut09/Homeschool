## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Perrault's Cinderella* (90 min): Read Perrault's Cinderella and list the story parts: kind girl, mean family, magic helper, event, lost item, happy ending. Phonics: prefix un- (unhappy, unlock).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *10 and 100 patterns* (60 min): Use a 'number path' to show 10 more/less and 100 more/less for 10 numbers and describe which digit changes.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Forest* (40 min): Read a library book or National Geographic Kids article about a redwood forest and list 8 plants and animals that live there, noting where each finds shelter.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Farms long ago* (30 min): Look at Calisphere photos of early California farms and list tools and animals used.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Four families* (40 min): Watch an orchestra instrument demonstration and sort picture cards into strings, woodwinds, brass, and percussion.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Fingertip pads* (30 min): Dribble in place with the dominant hand using fingertips, ball at waist height, 20 times.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Food journey* (20 min): Learn the path: mouth, esophagus, stomach, intestines.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Yeh-Shen* (90 min): Read Yeh-Shen, a Cinderella story from China, and use the illustrations to describe the setting. Phonics: prefix re- (reread, retell, rebuild).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Number line jumps* (60 min): Add 345 + 230 by jumping 200, then 30 on an open number line; solve 6 more.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Desert* (40 min): Read about California's Mojave Desert and list the living things (Joshua tree, tortoise, roadrunner, cactus); discuss how each survives with little water.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Farms today* (30 min): Compare with photos of modern farms (tractors, irrigation).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *How sound is made* (40 min): Make a rubber-band guitar (string), a straw oboe (woodwind), and a tube trumpet (brass buzz) and compare how each makes sound.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Switch hands* (30 min): Dribble with the weaker hand, then alternate hands every 5 bounces.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Stomach model* (20 min): Mash crackers and water in a zip bag to model the stomach.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Compare* (90 min): Fill a Venn diagram comparing Cinderella and Yeh-Shen (magic fish vs. fairy godmother, golden slipper vs. glass slipper). Phonics: prefix dis- (dislike, disagree).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Mental math race* (60 min): Answer 30 quick '+10, −10, +100, −100' problems orally with a timer; record time.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Grassland* (40 min): Watch a short video about California grasslands or the prairie and list the plants and animals; tally how many kinds each source names.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Food memories* (30 min): Ask a grandparent how food was bought and cooked when they were young.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Timbre game* (40 min): Listen to 10 instrument sound clips and name the family of each.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Walking dribble* (30 min): Dribble while walking a straight line, then a curved chalk path.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Intestine model* (20 min): Squeeze mush through a stocking to model the intestines.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 104, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Prefix meaning* (90 min): Use prefixes to define 12 new words and use 4 in sentences. Discuss in a partner talk which magic helper is more interesting and why.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Missing number puzzles* (60 min): Solve chains like 472 → □ (+100) → □ (−10) → □ and create 2 puzzles.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Compare chart* (40 min): Make a three-column chart and a bar graph of how many kinds of living things each habitat has; describe the pattern you see.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Preserving food* (30 min): Learn how food was preserved long ago (canning, drying) and make dried apple slices with an adult.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Favorite instrument* (40 min): Research one instrument and draw it, labeling its parts and family.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Eyes up* (30 min): Dribble while calling out the number of fingers a parent holds up.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Fiber & water* (20 min): Discuss why fiber and water help digestion.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 105, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 21 review* (90 min): Write 3 sentences: one way the stories are alike and two ways they are different. Spelling check of 12 prefix words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: 15 mental math items and 4 number-line problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Answer in 3–4 sentences: 'Which habitat has the most diversity, and why?' using evidence from the chart (more water and plants support more animals).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Venn diagram of food production long ago and today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Identify instrument families from sound and picture (10 items).
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Dribble while walking around 4 cones and back, switching hands once.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Draw and label the digestive system.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Cinderella from France and China
- Objective: Compare two versions of the same story
- Objective: Use illustrations to understand setting
- Objective: Read words with prefixes un-, re-, dis-
- Vocabulary: version, compare, contrast, prefix
- Materials: Cinderella (Perrault, in Andrew Lang's The Blue Fairy Book, public domain), Yeh-Shen (Ai-Ling Louie), Venn diagram
- Assessment: Venn diagram and written compare/contrast sentences; prefix spelling check.

### Math 2: Mental math with 10 and 100
- Objective: Mentally add and subtract 10 and 100
- Objective: Use number lines for three-digit numbers
- Objective: Explain place-value patterns
- Vocabulary: mental math, pattern, number line
- Materials: number cards, open number line paper, hundreds flats
- Assessment: Mental math quiz.

### Science 2: Comparing land habitats: forest, desert, grassland
- Objective: Compare diversity in three land habitats
- Objective: Gather information from books and videos
- Objective: Organize data in a chart
- Vocabulary: forest, desert, grassland, adapt, diversity
- Materials: library habitat books (e.g., A Walk in the Desert, Rebecca L. Johnson), National Geographic Kids habitat articles, chart paper
- Assessment: Habitat comparison chart and written answer with evidence.

### People Who Make a Difference: Food long ago
- Objective: Compare food production long ago and today
- Objective: Use primary sources about farms
- Objective: Interview about food memories
- Vocabulary: long ago, harvest, preserve, technology
- Materials: Calisphere photos of California farms in the early 1900s, family recipes
- Assessment: Long ago/today Venn diagram.

### Art & Music 2: Instrument families
- Objective: Identify the four orchestra families
- Objective: Describe timbre
- Objective: Recognize instruments by sound
- Vocabulary: orchestra, strings, woodwinds, brass, percussion, timbre
- Materials: instrument picture cards, orchestra videos (PBS or BBC Ten Pieces)
- Assessment: Instrument family identification check.

### PE 2: Hand dribbling
- Objective: Dribble with fingertips at waist height
- Objective: Dribble while walking
- Vocabulary: dribble, fingertips, control
- Materials: playground or basketball
- Assessment: Dribble checklist: fingertips, waist height, eyes up.

### Health 2: Digestion
- Objective: Describe the path of food through the body
- Vocabulary: digestion, stomach, intestine, nutrients
- Materials: zip bag, crackers, water, stocking
- Assessment: Labeled diagram.
