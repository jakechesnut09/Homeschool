## Day 1 — Monday (school day 136, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Draft paragraphs 2–3* (90 min): Write the 'big work' and 'why we remember them' paragraphs with facts from notes and a concluding sentence.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Pairing* (60 min): Pair up counters or socks for numbers 1–20; if every item has a partner, it's even. Record results.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Water erosion* (40 min): Build a sand hill in a tray, sprinkle it with a watering can for 1 minute, and draw where sand was removed and where it was deposited.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Louis Pasteur* (30 min): Read a short biography of Louis Pasteur and find the word 'pasteurized' on a milk or juice carton; explain how heating kills germs.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *California murals* (40 min): Look at photos of Chicano Park murals and the Great Wall of Los Angeles and discuss what stories they tell and who they are for.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Rope sizing & turns* (30 min): Size the rope (handles to armpits), then practice turn-stop-jump over the rope.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Safe vs unsafe* (20 min): Discuss safe touches (hugs from family) and unsafe ones, and that your body belongs to you.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 137, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Revise and edit* (90 min): Revise for order and clarity; edit with checklist for capitals, apostrophes, and spelling. Make a final copy.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Equal addends* (60 min): Write each even number to 20 as a doubles fact (12 = 6 + 6) and build each with cubes.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Wind erosion* (40 min): Make a flat sand pile on newspaper, blow through a straw from 10 cm away for 30 seconds, and measure how far sand traveled.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Germs experiment* (30 min): With an adult, leave a slice of bread out and keep another sealed; observe mold over the week and connect to Pasteur's discoveries about germs.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Local public art* (40 min): Find a mural, statue, or sculpture in your community (in person or online), photograph or sketch it, and write who might enjoy it and why.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Continuous forward* (30 min): Jump forward continuously, counting jumps in 30 seconds.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *No-Go-Tell* (20 min): Practice saying No, getting away and telling a trusted adult.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 138, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Visual display* (90 min): Make a poster with a portrait, a 5-event timeline, and a quote or fact box to go with the report.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Patterns* (60 min): Color evens on a hundreds chart and describe the ones-digit pattern; predict whether 47 and 86 are odd or even.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Erosion walk* (40 min): Walk the neighborhood or a park to photograph or sketch erosion (gullies, exposed tree roots, washed-out paths) and note whether water or wind caused it.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Marie Curie* (30 min): Read a biography of Marie Curie and learn how she became the first woman to win a Nobel Prize and the first person to win two.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Design* (40 min): Sketch a mural design that tells a story about your family or community, including people, places, and a pattern border.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Backward jumps* (30 min): Practice turning the rope backward and jumping over it.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Strangers & tricky people* (20 min): Learn about tricky people and never going anywhere without permission.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 139, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Living museum* (90 min): Rehearse a 1-minute 'living museum' speech in first person as your research person ('I am Sally Ride…'), speaking audibly in complete sentences.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Odd + even* (60 min): Investigate even + even, odd + odd, and even + odd with 10 examples each; write rules.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Coastal erosion* (40 min): Read about California cliff erosion (e.g., Pacifica) and look at photos of homes near eroding cliffs; discuss why it is a problem for people.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Perseverance* (30 min): Look at a photo of Marie Curie in her laboratory and discuss how she kept working when people said women could not be scientists.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Paint* (40 min): Transfer the design to butcher paper and paint it, using a color palette chosen for mood.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Long rope* (30 min): Jump a long rope turned by two family members; try running in.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Secrets vs surprises* (20 min): Distinguish surprises (fun, temporary) from unsafe secrets.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Writing and presenting a biography report
- Objective: Write a 3-paragraph biography report
- Objective: Use a visual display to clarify ideas
- Objective: Recount information in an oral presentation
- Vocabulary: report, visual display, presentation, legacy
- Materials: poster board or slides, dress-up items for 'living museum'
- Assessment: Biography report and poster (research rubric), presentation, unit test.

### Math 2: Odd and even numbers
- Objective: Determine if a number of objects is odd or even
- Objective: Write even numbers as sums of equal addends
- Objective: Find patterns of odd and even
- Vocabulary: odd, even, pair, equal addends
- Materials: counters, socks or shoes for pairing
- Assessment: Odd/even quiz.

### Science 2: How wind and water change land
- Objective: Observe how wind and water move soil and sand
- Objective: Model erosion
- Objective: Find erosion in the neighborhood
- Vocabulary: erosion, sediment, gully, wind, deposit
- Materials: sand and soil, baking pan or tray, watering can, drinking straws, newspaper
- Assessment: Before/after erosion drawings with labels.

### People Who Make a Difference: Science heroes: Louis Pasteur and Marie Curie
- Objective: Explain how Louis Pasteur's work keeps food safe
- Objective: Explain Marie Curie's discoveries and perseverance
- Objective: Connect heroes to daily life
- Vocabulary: germ, pasteurize, vaccine, scientist, Nobel Prize
- Materials: milk carton with 'pasteurized' label, biographies of Pasteur and Curie, photo of Marie Curie in her lab
- Assessment: Written comparison of two scientists' contributions.

### Art & Music 2: Murals and public art in communities
- Objective: Explain how public art contributes to communities
- Objective: Design and paint a mural about home or community
- Objective: Present the mural
- Vocabulary: mural, public art, community, muralist
- Materials: photos of California murals (Chicano Park in San Diego, the Great Wall of Los Angeles), butcher paper, tempera paints
- Assessment: Mural presentation with explanation of public art.

### PE 2: Jump rope skills
- Objective: Jump a self-turned rope forward and backward
- Vocabulary: turn, rhythm, jump
- Materials: jump rope sized to height
- Assessment: Jump-count log.

### Health 2: Safe and unsafe touch and strangers
- Objective: Distinguish safe and unsafe situations
- Objective: Use No-Go-Tell
- Vocabulary: safe touch, unsafe touch, No-Go-Tell, trusted adult
- Materials: scenario cards
- Assessment: Scenario responses.
