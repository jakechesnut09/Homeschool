## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Chrysanthemum* (90 min): Read Chrysanthemum and use the pictures to show how her feelings change each day of school. Discuss why words can hurt or help.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Bar models* (60 min): Draw bar models for add-to, take-from, and compare problems within 100; solve 6 problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Final measurements* (40 min): Take final measurements of all plants and complete the data tables.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Canada* (30 min): Learn how Canada's Parliament makes laws and compare to Congress.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *De Colores* (40 min): Learn the first verse of the Mexican folk song 'De Colores' and discuss what it celebrates.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Push-up practice* (30 min): Practice knee push-ups and wall push-ups with a straight back; count good-form reps.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Where food comes from* (20 min): Trace a food (e.g., strawberries, milk) from a California farm to the table; draw the steps.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Shades of meaning* (90 min): Order feeling words on paint-chip strips (glad, happy, thrilled; sad, upset, miserable) and verbs (toss, throw, hurl). Use 3 in sentences about Chrysanthemum.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Two-step problems* (60 min): Solve two-step problems ('There were 45 apples. 18 were sold, then 26 more were picked. How many now?') with labeled steps.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Conclusion* (40 min): Write a conclusion: 'Plants need ___ and ___ to grow. My evidence is…'
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Mexico* (30 min): Learn how Mexico's Congress and president work and compare to the U.S.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Funga Alafia* (40 min): Learn the West African welcome song 'Funga Alafia' with movements and sing it as a greeting.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Curl-ups* (30 min): Do curl-ups sliding fingertips along the mat; count reps in 1 minute.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Farmers market* (20 min): Visit a farmers market or grocery produce section and list 5 local foods.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 73, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Adjectives and adverbs* (90 min): Grammar: adjectives describe nouns (a long name) and adverbs describe verbs (she walked slowly); sort 12 words and write 4 sentences. Phonics: soft c and g (city, gem, face, page).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unknown start* (60 min): Solve problems where the start is unknown using bar models and related equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Rescue* (40 min): Move the closet and unwatered plants to light and water; predict whether they recover.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Compare* (30 min): Make a chart comparing lawmakers and leaders in 3 countries.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Sakura* (40 min): Listen to 'Sakura Sakura' from Japan, describe its mood, and draw cherry blossoms while listening.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Playground strength* (30 min): At a park, try hanging from bars, climbing a ladder and doing monkey-bar swings.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Seasonal foods* (20 min): Learn which fruits and vegetables grow in each season in California.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 74, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Draft my story* (90 min): Draft the fiction story from the plan, using at least 3 adjectives, 2 adverbs, and dialogue. Write a beginning that introduces the character and setting.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Reasonable?* (60 min): Estimate answers to 6 problems before solving and decide if the final answers make sense.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Poster* (40 min): Make a poster of the investigation (question, prediction, test, results, conclusion).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Primary source* (30 min): Look at the Preamble to the U.S. Constitution (National Archives) and discuss 'promote the general welfare.'
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Singing game* (40 min): Play the American singing game 'Bluebird, Bluebird' with family, keeping the beat while moving.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Animal strength circuit* (30 min): Do 30 seconds each of bear crawl, crab walk, plank and frog jumps.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Grow a snack* (20 min): Plant a quick herb or sprout lettuce seeds in a cup.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 75, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 15 review* (90 min): Spelling check of soft c/g words, adjective-or-adverb quiz, and reread the draft aloud to find one place to add a feeling word.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Solve 6 mixed one- and two-step word problems showing a model and equation for each.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Unit 4 check* (40 min): Present the poster and complete a quiz on plant needs and fair tests.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Unit 3 check* (30 min): Quiz on making, carrying out, and judging laws.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Rehearse and check* (40 min): Choose a favorite folk song, practice the hard parts, and sing it for family, telling where it comes from and why you like it.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Record push-ups, curl-ups and hang time in your fitness log and name the muscles each works.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain the farm-to-table path for one food.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Feelings, shades of meaning, adjectives and adverbs
- Objective: Describe characters' feelings with precise words
- Objective: Use adjectives and adverbs
- Objective: Draft a fictional narrative
- Vocabulary: adjective, adverb, shades of meaning, soft c, soft g
- Materials: Chrysanthemum (Kevin Henkes), paint chips for word ladders
- Assessment: Adjective/adverb quiz, spelling check, story draft checked for beginning and descriptive words.

### Math 2: One- and two-step word problems within 100
- Objective: Solve one- and two-step word problems
- Objective: Use bar models and equations with unknowns
- Objective: Check reasonableness
- Vocabulary: two-step, bar model, reasonable, unknown
- Materials: bar model mats, word problem cards
- Assessment: Word problem set with models and equations.

### Science 2: Plant needs conclusions
- Objective: Draw conclusions from data
- Objective: Communicate findings
- Objective: Connect to real gardens
- Vocabulary: conclusion, evidence, results
- Materials: plant data, poster paper
- Assessment: Investigation poster and unit quiz.

### People Who Make a Difference: Government in other countries and unit check
- Objective: Compare how other countries make and carry out laws
- Objective: Review government roles
- Vocabulary: country, parliament, leader, democracy
- Materials: world map, photos of Canada's Parliament and Mexico's Congress
- Assessment: Government comparison chart and unit quiz.

### Art & Music 2: Folk songs and singing games
- Objective: Sing folk songs from different cultures
- Objective: Play singing games
- Objective: Explain why people sing these songs
- Vocabulary: folk song, verse, refrain, tradition
- Materials: recordings of 'De Colores,' 'Funga Alafia,' 'Sakura Sakura,' 'Bluebird, Bluebird'
- Assessment: Folk song performance with spoken introduction.

### PE 2: Muscle strength and endurance
- Objective: Perform modified push-ups, curl-ups and hangs
- Vocabulary: strength, push-up, curl-up
- Materials: mat, playground bars, fitness log
- Assessment: Baseline strength scores in fitness log.

### Health 2: Farm to table
- Objective: Describe how food gets from farms to our plates
- Vocabulary: farm, producer, market
- Materials: local produce, 'From Seed to Plant' by Gail Gibbons (library)
- Assessment: Farm-to-table drawing.
