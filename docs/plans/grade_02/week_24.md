## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Comparatives* (90 min): Phonics/grammar: add -er and -est (tall, taller, tallest; happy, happier, happiest) and compare Cinderella characters ('Yeh-Shen's fish was kinder than…').
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Dollars* (60 min): Learn $1, $5, $10, $20 bills and count mixed bills; show that 100¢ = $1.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Mount St. Helens* (40 min): Compare before-and-after photos of Mount St. Helens (1980) and read a short passage about the eruption; record how much the mountain changed in one day.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Barter* (30 min): Try to trade toys or snacks with family members without money and record what problems happen (not everyone wants what you have).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Music everywhere* (40 min): List all the music you hear in one day (ads, video games, stores, church, car radio) and its purpose.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Pass and move* (30 min): Pass to a partner, then move to a new open spot to receive the next pass.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Street smart* (20 min): Practice stop, look left-right-left, and listen at a crosswalk with a parent.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 117, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Revise the opinion* (90 min): Strengthen reasons with examples from the books and add a closing that restates the opinion. Check that each reason starts with a linking word.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Money problems* (60 min): Solve 6 problems like 'Jo has 2 dimes and 3 nickels. How many cents?' and 'Sam has $5 and $10 bills…'.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Model volcano* (40 min): Build a clay volcano around a cup, erupt it with baking soda and vinegar, and draw how the 'lava' changed the land around it.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Money makes it easier* (30 min): Read about how people long ago traded with shells, salt, and coins, and explain how money solves the barter problems.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Purpose sort* (40 min): Listen to a lullaby, a march, a dance tune, and a jingle and explain how each piece's tempo and dynamics fit its purpose.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Dribble tag* (30 min): Play dribble tag where everyone dribbles (by hand) and tries to tag others' balls out.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Helmet fit* (20 min): Check helmet fit with the 2-V-1 rule.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 118, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Edit and publish* (90 min): Edit for capitals, end marks, and spelling of prefix/suffix words; publish as a poster with a book cover drawing.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Making change* (60 min): Pay $1 for items costing 65¢, 80¢, 42¢ and count up to find the change.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Earthquake shake table* (40 min): Build a block tower on a tray resting on marbles, shake the tray, and record what happens; read about a California earthquake and how quickly it changed roads.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Market prep* (30 min): Make 5 items to sell (bookmarks or drawings), decide prices, and make a sign for your market stall.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Family playlist* (40 min): Interview family about their favorite songs and why they like them; make a family playlist.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *2 vs. 1 soccer* (30 min): Play a short 2 vs. 1 game with a family member defending.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Bike skills* (20 min): Practice hand signals and stopping on a chalk course.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 119, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Present* (90 min): Present the opinion poster to family and answer one question. Listen to a family member's opinion and ask a question about their reasons.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Budget challenge* (60 min): Choose items from a pretend catalog under $20 total and show the addition.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Landslide* (40 min): Pack damp soil on a tilted tray, pour water at the top, and time how fast the soil slides; compare to photos of a California coastal landslide.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Market day* (30 min): Run a family market: sell your goods as a producer, then buy from a sibling or parent as a consumer; record sales.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Music and art* (40 min): Paint while listening to two contrasting pieces and compare the paintings.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Basketball stations* (30 min): Rotate: stationary dribble, walking dribble, bounce pass, chest pass.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Car safety* (20 min): Learn why kids ride in booster seats in the back seat with seat belts.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 120, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Unit 6 assessment* (90 min): Unit test: compare two short versions of a folktale in a chart and sentences; decode 15 words with prefixes and suffixes; identify point of view.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Solve 8 money word problems using $ and ¢ correctly.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Fill a 3-row chart (event, how long it took, evidence and source) for the volcano, earthquake, and landslide.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Write 3 sentences: what you produced, what you consumed, and how buyers and sellers needed each other.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Unit 6 check* (40 min): Tell your favorite piece from the unit, using music words to explain why.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Show a hand dribble, foot dribble and pass and tell how you helped your teammate.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Demonstrate crosswalk safety and helmet fit.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Publishing opinions and comparatives
- Objective: Revise and edit an opinion piece
- Objective: Use comparative endings -er and -est
- Objective: Present an opinion to an audience
- Vocabulary: comparative, superlative, conclusion, persuade
- Materials: opinion drafts, poster paper
- Assessment: Published opinion poster (opinion rubric) and unit test.

### Math 2: Dollars and money word problems
- Objective: Count bills and coins
- Objective: Solve money word problems
- Objective: Make change from $1
- Vocabulary: dollar, bill, change, total
- Materials: play bills and coins, price tags
- Assessment: Money word-problem quiz.

### Science 2: Fast Earth changes: volcanoes, earthquakes, landslides
- Objective: Describe Earth events that happen quickly
- Objective: Model fast changes
- Objective: Gather evidence from several sources
- Vocabulary: volcano, earthquake, landslide, fast, evidence
- Materials: clay or play dough, baking soda and vinegar, tray, blocks, soil, Mount St. Helens before/after photos (USGS)
- Assessment: Fast-change evidence chart citing at least two sources.

### People Who Make a Difference: Money, trade, and markets
- Objective: Explain how money makes trade easier than barter
- Objective: Act as producer and consumer
- Objective: Explain the role of prices
- Vocabulary: barter, money, market, price, profit
- Materials: play money, homemade goods (bookmarks, drawings, snacks), price tags
- Assessment: Market sales record and reflection.

### Art & Music 2: Music in daily life and unit reflection
- Objective: Identify uses of music in daily life
- Objective: Explain musical preferences
- Objective: Connect music to other subjects
- Vocabulary: purpose, preference, jingle, lullaby
- Materials: family playlist, recordings of a lullaby, a march, a jingle
- Assessment: Purpose sort and explanation of preferences.

### PE 2: Dribble, pass and team games
- Objective: Use dribbling and passing in small games
- Objective: Cooperate with a partner
- Vocabulary: teammate, pass, open space
- Materials: balls, cones
- Assessment: Skill station checklist; self-reflection on teamwork.

### Health 2: Pedestrian, bike and car safety
- Objective: Follow safety rules walking, biking and riding
- Vocabulary: pedestrian, helmet, booster seat, crosswalk
- Materials: bike, helmet, chalk
- Assessment: Safety demonstration checklist.
