## Day 1 — Monday (school day 151, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Read a procedure* (90 min): Read the steps on a seed packet and a simple recipe; discuss why order matters and what happens if a step is skipped.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Sides and angles* (60 min): Sort pattern blocks and cut-out shapes by number of sides and angles; name each group.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Improve* (40 min): Change one feature of the better design (taller wall, added 'plants') and retest; record whether results improved.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Rosa Parks* (30 min): Read a short biography of Rosa Parks and look at her 1955 arrest record from the National Archives; discuss why breaking an unfair rule took courage.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Choose the story* (40 min): Choose a scene from 'Peter and the Wolf' or 'Carnival of the Animals' and list its characters and events.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Hoop pass* (30 min): Pass a hula hoop around a hand-holding circle without letting go.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Label hunt* (20 min): Find warning labels and the poison symbol on household products with a parent.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 152, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Follow the steps* (90 min): Make trail mix following written steps, then rewrite the steps in your own words with numbers and time-order words. Phonics: multisyllable words (ingredient, measure).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Quadrilaterals* (60 min): Explore quadrilaterals (square, rectangle, rhombus, trapezoid) on a geoboard and list what they share.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Poster* (40 min): Make a poster showing the problem, both designs, test data, and the improved design.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Dr. King* (30 min): Listen to an excerpt of Dr. King's 'I Have a Dream' speech and write your own dream for a fair world.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Create movement* (40 min): Create a movement for each character that matches its music (slinky clarinet cat, heavy elephant).
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Blanket ball* (30 min): Use a blanket with a partner to toss and catch a ball together.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Safe storage* (20 min): Help check that cleaners are stored up high or locked.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 153, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Plan a how-to* (90 min): Choose something you know how to do (make a sandwich, care for a pet, build a block tower) and list materials and steps.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Draw to rules* (60 min): Draw shapes with given attributes ('a shape with 5 sides,' 'a quadrilateral with no square corners').
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Present* (40 min): Present the poster to family, explaining why the improved design worked better, and answer 2 questions.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Ruby Bridges* (30 min): Read The Story of Ruby Bridges and discuss how a 6-year-old helped integrate a New Orleans school in 1960.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Add sound* (40 min): Add homemade percussion sound effects and a narrator line for each part of the scene.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Lava crossing* (30 min): Cross the yard on 'stepping stones' (paper plates) as a team without touching the 'lava.'
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Poison Control* (20 min): Learn the Poison Control number (1-800-222-1222) and add it to the phone.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 154, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Draft and test* (90 min): Draft the how-to with an introduction, numbered steps, and a closing; have a family member follow it exactly and note any missing steps.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Straw shapes* (60 min): Build shapes with straws and connectors or clay; count sides and vertices.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Real world* (40 min): Read about sand dunes and dune grass protecting California beaches and compare them to your designs.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Timeline* (30 min): Place 1947 (Jackie Robinson), 1955 (bus boycott), 1960 (Ruby Bridges), and 1963 (March on Washington) on the hero timeline.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Rehearse* (40 min): Rehearse the whole piece twice, fixing one problem each time.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Team juggle* (30 min): Toss a ball in the same order around the family, adding a second ball.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Saying no* (20 min): Practice saying no to tasting unknown substances.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 155, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 31 review* (90 min): Revise the how-to after the test, add diagrams, and publish. Spelling check of 12 multisyllable science words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Identify and draw shapes from attribute clues (10 items).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Unit 8 check* (40 min): Quiz: identify erosion in 4 photos and choose the best solution for 3 situations with a reason.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Explain how each person used peaceful action to make a difference.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Unit 8 check* (40 min): Perform the moving story for family and explain how dance, drama, and music worked together.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain one way your team cooperated and one thing you would do differently.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Identify 5 dangerous products and explain what to do if someone swallows one.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: How-to writing: steps in a procedure
- Objective: Describe connections between steps in a procedure
- Objective: Write an informative how-to text
- Objective: Read multisyllable words
- Vocabulary: procedure, steps, materials, sequence
- Materials: simple recipe (e.g., trail mix), a seed-planting how-to from a seed packet, paper
- Assessment: Published how-to text tested by a reader; spelling check.

### Math 2: Two-dimensional shapes
- Objective: Identify triangles, quadrilaterals, pentagons, hexagons by attributes
- Objective: Draw shapes with given attributes
- Objective: Count sides and angles
- Vocabulary: triangle, quadrilateral, pentagon, hexagon, angle, side, vertex
- Materials: geoboard or dot paper, pattern blocks, straws
- Assessment: Shape attribute quiz.

### Science 2: Improve, communicate, and unit check
- Objective: Improve a design using test data
- Objective: Communicate results
- Objective: Connect to real erosion solutions
- Vocabulary: improve, communicate, results
- Materials: designs from last week, poster paper
- Assessment: Design poster presentation and unit quiz.

### People Who Make a Difference: Rosa Parks, Martin Luther King Jr., and Ruby Bridges
- Objective: Explain how civil rights leaders worked for fairness
- Objective: Analyze primary sources
- Objective: Sequence civil rights events
- Vocabulary: civil rights, boycott, peaceful protest, equal
- Materials: Rosa Parks' arrest record/fingerprint card (National Archives), excerpt of 'I Have a Dream' (1963), The Story of Ruby Bridges (Robert Coles)
- Assessment: Civil rights timeline and written explanation.

### Art & Music 2: Moving stories: dance, drama, and music together
- Objective: Combine movement, music, and drama to tell a story
- Objective: Rehearse and perform
- Objective: Connect arts disciplines
- Vocabulary: perform, rehearse, sound effects, scene
- Materials: recording of 'Peter and the Wolf' or 'Carnival of the Animals', props and percussion
- Assessment: Moving-story performance and explanation.

### PE 2: Cooperative challenges
- Objective: Work with others to solve movement challenges
- Vocabulary: cooperate, teamwork, challenge
- Materials: hula hoops, blanket, balls
- Assessment: Cooperation reflection.

### Health 2: Poisons and household products
- Objective: Identify poisonous products and warning labels
- Vocabulary: poison, warning label, Poison Control
- Materials: product labels, stickers
- Assessment: Identification check.
