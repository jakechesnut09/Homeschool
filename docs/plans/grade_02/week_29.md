## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Volcanoes* (90 min): Read Volcanoes by Franklyn Branley and study the diagram of a volcano; label magma, crater, lava. Phonics: homophones to/two/too and there/their.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Equal groups* (60 min): Make equal groups of counters (4 groups of 3) and write repeated addition (3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Real solutions* (40 min): Look at photos of retaining walls, rows of trees as windbreaks, levees, and sand fences, and write what problem each solves.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Albert Einstein* (30 min): Read a picture-book biography of Albert Einstein (e.g., On a Beam of Light by Jennifer Berne) and list the questions he asked as a child.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Movement words* (40 min): Practice locomotor movements (walk, skip, gallop, slide) and nonlocomotor movements (twist, bend, stretch, swing) and call them out as a family game.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Hoop skills* (30 min): Practice waist hooping, arm hooping and rolling a hoop and chasing it.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Personal info* (20 min): Learn which information stays private online (name, address, school, photos).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 142, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Author's points* (90 min): Find the point 'Volcanoes can build new land' and list the reasons the author gives (Hawaii, Surtsey). Write: The author says ___ because ___.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Arrays at home* (60 min): Find arrays at home (egg carton, muffin tin, window panes) and write repeated addition for rows and columns.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Plants vs. bare soil* (40 min): Pour 2 cups of water down a tray of bare soil and a tray with grass growing; collect and compare the muddy runoff in cups.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Curiosity* (30 min): Write 3 'I wonder' questions of your own about the world, as Einstein did, and choose one to investigate.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Levels and shapes* (40 min): Freeze in high, middle, and low shapes (curved, straight, twisted) when the music stops; make 6 different statues.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Ribbon shapes* (30 min): Make circles, figure eights, snakes and spirals with a ribbon stick to music.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Online rules* (20 min): Make family online rules: ask first, tell an adult about anything uncomfortable.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 143, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Model eruption* (90 min): Build a clay volcano and erupt it with baking soda and vinegar; write 3 steps of what happened. Phonics: homophone pairs (see/sea, right/write).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Build arrays* (60 min): Build arrays up to 5 × 5 with counters and draw them on grid paper; write two equations for each (by rows and by columns).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Barriers* (40 min): Build a craft-stick wall and a pebble wall on a sand hill and test each with the same amount of water; measure how much sand moves.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Golda Meir* (30 min): Read about Golda Meir, who grew up in Milwaukee, taught school, and became prime minister of Israel; locate Ukraine, the U.S., and Israel on a map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Pathways* (40 min): Lay masking-tape straight, curved, and zigzag pathways and travel each with a different locomotor movement.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Pattern build* (30 min): Create an 8-count pattern with the hoop or ribbon and repeat it 4 times.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Weapon safety* (20 min): Learn: if you see a gun, don't touch it, leave the area, tell an adult.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 144, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Earthquakes* (90 min): Read a ReadWorks or library passage on earthquakes and identify one point and two reasons. Use context to define 'fault' and 'plates.'
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Array stories* (60 min): Solve 6 array word problems ('There are 4 rows of chairs with 5 chairs each…').
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Data table* (40 min): Record results of all four tests (bare, grass, sticks, pebbles) in a table and rank them from least to most sand lost.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Compare* (30 min): Make a Venn diagram comparing Einstein and Meir (both immigrants, different kinds of work).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Images to movement* (40 min): Create movement inspired by pictures of a storm, a seed growing, and a melting snowman, using scarves.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Jump rope chant* (30 min): Learn a jump rope rhyme (e.g., 'Teddy Bear, Teddy Bear') and jump to it.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Practice* (20 min): Role-play scenarios about online messages and finding a weapon.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 145, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 29 review* (90 min): Reasons-and-points organizer on a new science passage; homophone quiz (12 items).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Write repeated addition for 8 arrays and draw arrays for 4 equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Write which solution worked best and one weakness of it, using the data as evidence.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Add both to the hero timeline and explain which trait each showed most.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Perform a 30-second dance with 3 levels, 2 pathways, and a beginning and ending shape.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform your hoop or ribbon pattern and tell how it made you feel.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Unit quiz on safety rules from weeks 24–29.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Volcanoes and earthquakes: reasons support points
- Objective: Describe how reasons support an author's points
- Objective: Use homophones correctly
- Objective: Use diagrams to understand processes
- Vocabulary: volcano, magma, erupt, reason, homophone
- Materials: Volcanoes (Franklyn M. Branley, Let's-Read-and-Find-Out), baking soda, vinegar, clay
- Assessment: Point-and-reasons organizer, homophone quiz, written eruption steps.

### Math 2: Equal groups and arrays
- Objective: Make equal groups
- Objective: Use repeated addition to find totals
- Objective: Build arrays up to 5 by 5
- Vocabulary: array, row, column, equal groups, repeated addition
- Materials: counters, egg carton, grid paper
- Assessment: Array quiz.

### Science 2: Testing solutions that slow erosion
- Objective: Learn real solutions to erosion
- Objective: Test plant cover and barriers
- Objective: Compare results in a data table
- Vocabulary: retaining wall, windbreak, levee, vegetation, barrier
- Materials: two trays of soil (one planted with grass seed two weeks earlier or covered with moss), craft sticks, pebbles, measuring cup
- Assessment: Solution test data table and written conclusion.

### People Who Make a Difference: Albert Einstein and Golda Meir
- Objective: Describe Albert Einstein's curiosity and ideas
- Objective: Describe Golda Meir's leadership
- Objective: Compare two people who made a difference
- Vocabulary: curious, imagination, immigrant, prime minister
- Materials: biographies of Einstein and Golda Meir, world map
- Assessment: Venn diagram and hero timeline entries.

### Art & Music 2: Creative movement: levels, shapes, and pathways
- Objective: Perform locomotor and nonlocomotor movements
- Objective: Change levels, shapes, and pathways
- Objective: Create movement from images and music
- Vocabulary: locomotor, nonlocomotor, level, pathway, shape
- Materials: open floor space, scarves, masking tape, instrumental music
- Assessment: Short dance sequence meeting the movement checklist.

### PE 2: Hoops, ribbons and rhythm
- Objective: Create rhythmic patterns with hoops and ribbons
- Vocabulary: rhythm, pattern, twirl
- Materials: hula hoop, ribbon on a stick, music
- Assessment: Pattern performance and reflection.

### Health 2: Online safety and weapons
- Objective: Stay safe online
- Objective: Know what to do if a weapon is found
- Vocabulary: online, personal information, weapon
- Materials: computer, scenario cards
- Assessment: Safety unit quiz.
