# PE 2 (Grade 2)

PE 2 refines fundamental movement skills: traveling over, under and through obstacles, changing speed and direction, skipping, leaping and jumping, balancing, transferring weight and forward rolls, overhand throwing, catching high and low, hand and foot dribbling, kicking, striking with paddles, jump rope and dance. Children measure their heart rate, track fitness scores in a log, and practice cooperation, fair play and problem solving, ending with a skill showcase and family field day.

*Framework:* California Physical Education Model Content Standards; 2009 CA Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 30 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Moving in Space: Direction, Speed and Obstacles (weeks 1–4)
Changing direction, speed and level helps us move safely and cleverly.

- Week 1: Safe spaces and signals
- Week 2: Changing speed and direction
- Week 3: Over, under, around and through
- Week 4: Movement challenge course

### Unit 2: Skip, Leap and Jump (weeks 5–8)
Each locomotor skill has cues that make it smoother, farther and higher.

- Week 5: Skipping
- Week 6: Leaping
- Week 7: Jumping for distance and height
- Week 8: Locomotor mix and relay

### Unit 3: Balance, Weight Transfer and Rolling (weeks 9–12)
A tight core and wide base let us balance, transfer weight and roll safely.

- Week 9: Balances on different body parts
- Week 10: Balance beam and weight transfer
- Week 11: Rolling safely
- Week 12: Balance-and-roll routine

### Unit 4: Fitness and My Heart (weeks 13–16)
My heart is a muscle, and exercise makes it, my lungs and my muscles stronger.

- Week 13: My heart is a muscle
- Week 14: Aerobic endurance
- Week 15: Muscle strength and endurance
- Week 16: Flexibility and fitness review

### Unit 5: Overhand Throwing and Catching on the Move (weeks 17–20)
Side to target, step and follow through to throw; reach, give and watch to catch.

- Week 17: Overhand throw
- Week 18: Catching high and low
- Week 19: Partner throw and catch
- Week 20: Throwing and catching games

### Unit 6: Dribbling and Kicking (weeks 21–24)
Soft touches keep the ball close; controlled force sends it where we aim.

- Week 21: Hand dribbling
- Week 22: Foot dribbling
- Week 23: Kicking to a target
- Week 24: Dribble, pass and team games

### Unit 7: Striking and Volleying (weeks 25–27)
A flat paddle, watching eyes and the right amount of force control a struck ball.

- Week 25: Balloon and hand striking
- Week 26: Paddle striking
- Week 27: Striking games

### Unit 8: Jump Rope, Hoops and Dance (weeks 28–30)
Moving to a beat builds rhythm, coordination and endurance.

- Week 28: Jump rope skills
- Week 29: Hoops, ribbons and rhythm
- Week 30: Dance creation

### Unit 9: Cooperative Games and Fair Play (weeks 31–33)
Teams succeed when everyone follows rules, encourages others and solves problems fairly.

- Week 31: Cooperative challenges
- Week 32: Rules, fairness and sportsmanship
- Week 33: Classic playground and backyard games

### Unit 10: Fitness Check and Field Day (weeks 34–36)
We measure our growth and celebrate how active and skillful we have become.

- Week 34: Fitness retest
- Week 35: Skill showcase
- Week 36: Family field day

## Standards

- **PE-2.1.1** Travel over, under, in front of, behind, and through objects and obstacles.
- **PE-2.1.10** Strike a ball or balloon with a paddle or the hand, sending it upward and forward with control.
- **PE-2.1.11** Dribble a ball with one hand while walking, changing hands and direction.
- **PE-2.1.12** Create and perform rhythmic movement sequences and simple dances, including hoop and ribbon patterns.
- **PE-2.1.2** Change speed and direction in response to various rhythms and signals.
- **PE-2.1.3** Balance on a variety of body parts and transfer weight from one body part to another.
- **PE-2.1.4** Perform a forward roll and combine rolls and balances into a short sequence.
- **PE-2.1.5** Skip, leap, and jump for distance and height using a mature motor pattern.
- **PE-2.1.6** Jump a self-turned rope forward and backward, and jump a long rope with a partner.
- **PE-2.1.7** Throw a ball overhand to a target and to a partner, stepping with the opposite foot.
- **PE-2.1.8** Catch a thrown ball above the waist and below the waist, including while moving.
- **PE-2.1.9** Kick a rolling ball and dribble a ball with the feet while walking and jogging.
- **PE-2.2.1** Explain how practice improves the performance of a motor skill.
- **PE-2.2.2** Describe the critical elements (cues) of the skip, leap, and horizontal and vertical jump.
- **PE-2.2.3** Describe the critical elements of the overhand throw, catching, kicking, and striking.
- **PE-2.2.4** Identify and use pathways, levels, and force (strong/light) when moving and manipulating objects.
- **PE-2.3.1** Participate in enjoyable and challenging physical activities in and outside of school.
- **PE-2.3.2** Participate in moderate to vigorous physical activity for increasing periods of time.
- **PE-2.3.3** Perform muscular strength and endurance activities (modified push-ups, curl-ups, climbing, hanging).
- **PE-2.3.4** Perform flexibility exercises that stretch the major muscle groups.
- **PE-2.3.5** Walk and jog continuously for increasing distances to build aerobic capacity.
- **PE-2.3.6** Measure heart rate by placing a hand on the chest or neck before and after activity.
- **PE-2.4.1** Explain that the heart is a muscle that grows stronger with physical activity.
- **PE-2.4.2** Describe how heart rate and breathing change during and after physical activity.
- **PE-2.4.3** Identify healthy foods and the need for water before, during, and after physical activity.
- **PE-2.4.4** Explain that muscles pull on bones to move the body and that stretching keeps muscles flexible.
- **PE-2.4.5** Identify safety practices for physical activity, including sun protection and proper equipment use.
- **PE-2.5.1** Work cooperatively with a partner or small group to complete a task or game.
- **PE-2.5.2** Follow rules, take turns, and accept the results of a game with good sportsmanship.
- **PE-2.5.3** Give and accept positive feedback and encouragement.
- **PE-2.5.4** Use simple problem-solving steps to resolve conflicts that arise during play.
- **PE-2.5.5** Identify positive feelings and challenges that come from participating in physical activity.
