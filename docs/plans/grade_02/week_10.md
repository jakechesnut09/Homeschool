## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Monarch life cycle* (90 min): Read Monarch Butterfly by Gail Gibbons and list the 4 stages (egg, larva, pupa, adult). Phonics: er/ir/ur words (her, bird, turn) — same sound, different spellings.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Centimeters* (60 min): Measure 10 small objects to the nearest centimeter and record; use a fingertip as a 1-cm benchmark.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Cooking an egg* (40 min): Observe a raw egg, then cook it with an adult; discuss whether it can become raw again.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Ask about moves* (30 min): Interview a family member about where ancestors lived and when and why the family moved to your community.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Warm and cool sort* (40 min): Cut 12 pictures from magazines and sort them into warm-color and cool-color piles; describe how each pile makes you feel.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Beam walk* (30 min): Walk a 2x4 board or chalk line forward and sideways with arms out; then try with a beanbag on your head.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Habit check* (20 min): Review habits from this unit and rate yourself on each with a smiley scale.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 47, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Connecting steps* (90 min): Make a paper-plate life-cycle wheel and explain how each stage leads to the next using because and then. Learn the subject words larva and chrysalis from context.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Meters* (60 min): Measure the length of rooms and a hallway with a meter stick; estimate first using a giant step as about 1 meter.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Toast* (40 min): Toast a slice of bread and compare to untoasted bread; try to 'untoast' it (cooling) and record.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Map the journey* (30 min): Mark ancestors' homes on a world or U.S. map and connect them to your town with yarn or lines.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *The Starry Night* (40 min): Look closely at Van Gogh's 'The Starry Night' for 3 minutes, then describe its mood, colors, and swirling lines using 'I see… I think… I wonder…'.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Backward and turns* (30 min): Walk the beam backward and practice a half-turn in the middle.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Pick a goal* (20 min): Choose one habit to improve (brush twice daily, wash hands before meals) and write it as a goal.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 48, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Taking notes* (90 min): Learn to take notes in words, not sentences; record 6 facts about monarchs on a note sheet. Phonics: er/ir/ur spelling sort.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Two units* (60 min): Measure the same 5 objects in inches and centimeters and explain why there are more centimeters (smaller unit).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Pancakes* (40 min): Make pancakes and describe how liquid batter becomes solid when heated.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *How they traveled* (30 min): Learn how people traveled long ago (ship, wagon, train) and draw your family's journey.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Cool night painting* (40 min): Paint a night sky over your own house using only cool colors and swirling brushstrokes like Van Gogh.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Feet to hands* (30 min): Practice bunny hops (feet to hands to feet) over a rope and a cartwheel-shape step-over on the grass.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Make a tracker* (20 min): Make a 2-week sticker tracker for your goal.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 49, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Migration question* (90 min): Read the migration section and answer in writing 'Why do monarchs fly to California and Mexico?' using notes. Share answers and ask a partner a clarifying question.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Benchmark estimates* (60 min): Estimate 8 lengths in cm or m using benchmarks, then measure; color estimates that were within 5 cm.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Sort changes* (40 min): Sort 12 picture cards (melting snow, baking a cake, freezing juice, burning wood) into reversible/irreversible.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Why people move* (30 min): Discuss reasons people move (jobs, family, safety, land) and write one sentence about why your family moved.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Warm sunset* (40 min): Paint a sunset over the same house using only warm colors.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Muscles at work* (30 min): Feel your leg and stomach muscles tighten while balancing; learn that muscles pull on bones to move us.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Hygiene kit* (20 min): Pack a personal hygiene kit (toothbrush, tissues, travel soap) and explain each item.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 50, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 10 review* (90 min): Sequence the life cycle in a labeled drawing, spell 10 r-controlled words, and answer 4 questions on a ReadWorks butterfly passage.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: measure in cm, compare inch vs. cm results for 3 objects, and explain the unit-size relationship in a sentence.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Write an argument: 'Some changes caused by heating can be reversed and some cannot,' with two pieces of evidence each.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Add the map to the family history book and present it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Compare* (40 min): Hang both paintings side by side and tell how changing the palette changed the mood, using the words warm, cool, and mood.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Cross the beam three ways without falling off and show one weight-transfer move.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Quiz: germs, teeth, sleep and sun protection (10 oral questions); begin tracking your goal.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Life cycles: connecting steps in a process
- Objective: Describe the connection between stages of a life cycle
- Objective: Read er, ir, ur words
- Objective: Take notes from a source
- Vocabulary: life cycle, larva, pupa, stage, note
- Materials: Monarch Butterfly (Gail Gibbons), note-taking sheet, paper plates
- Assessment: Labeled life-cycle drawing, written migration answer from notes, and spelling check.

### Math 2: Measuring in centimeters and meters
- Objective: Measure in centimeters and meters
- Objective: Compare measurements in two units
- Objective: Estimate metric lengths
- Vocabulary: centimeter, meter, metric, estimate
- Materials: centimeter ruler, meter stick or string cut to 1 meter, sticky notes
- Assessment: Metric measuring quiz and written unit-size explanation.

### Science 2: Irreversible changes
- Objective: Observe changes that cannot be reversed
- Objective: Compare reversible and irreversible changes
- Objective: Construct an argument with evidence
- Vocabulary: irreversible, cook, burn, evidence, argument
- Materials: eggs, bread and toaster, pancake batter, paper
- Assessment: Change-sort and written argument with evidence.

### People Who Make a Difference: Where my family came from
- Objective: Locate places ancestors lived
- Objective: Tell when and how the family moved
- Objective: Explain reasons people move
- Vocabulary: ancestor, migrate, immigrant, journey
- Materials: world map, yarn and pins or stickers, family interview notes
- Assessment: Family migration map and explanation.

### Art & Music 2: Warm and cool colors and mood
- Objective: Sort warm and cool colors
- Objective: Describe how colors create mood
- Objective: Paint with a warm or cool palette
- Vocabulary: warm, cool, mood, palette
- Materials: image of Van Gogh's 'The Starry Night', tempera paints, old magazines
- Assessment: Warm and cool paintings with oral mood comparison.

### PE 2: Balance beam and weight transfer
- Objective: Walk a narrow surface forward, backward and sideways
- Objective: Transfer weight from feet to hands
- Vocabulary: weight transfer, beam
- Materials: 2x4 board or chalk line, mat
- Assessment: Beam and weight-transfer checklist.

### Health 2: My healthy habits goal
- Objective: Set and track a personal hygiene goal
- Vocabulary: goal, habit, track
- Materials: goal chart, stickers
- Assessment: Unit quiz and goal tracker.
