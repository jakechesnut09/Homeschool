## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Finish Pooh* (90 min): Read Chapter 10 (the party for Pooh) and discuss how the book ends. Rate Winnie-the-Pooh with stars and give one reason.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Measurement review* (60 min): Measure 5 objects in inches and centimeters and solve 2 compare-length problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Define the problem* (40 min): Problem: build a bridge across a 20-cm 'river' between two book stacks that holds a cup of pennies; list criteria and limits (10 straws, 1 sheet of paper, 50 cm of tape).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Choose and research* (30 min): Choose one hero from the unit (or another approved hero) and gather 6 facts from 2 sources: early life, challenge, action, and legacy.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Program* (40 min): Choose 3 musical pieces for the showcase (a folk song, your rhythm composition, your community song) and write a program.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Locomotor test* (30 min): Perform skip, leap, gallop, slide and standing long jump; parent uses checklist.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Choose a message* (20 min): Choose a health topic from this year to teach others.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Plan a review* (90 min): Choose the favorite book of the year and plan a review: summary without spoiling, opinion, 3 reasons, recommendation.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Money and time* (60 min): Count coin sets and tell time at 6 moments today; solve 3 money problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Sketch and choose* (40 min): Sketch two bridge designs (flat, folded accordion, arch) and choose materials using your strength-test data from Unit 2.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Timeline and traits* (30 min): Make a 5-event timeline and a list of 3 character traits with evidence for your hero.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Rehearse* (40 min): Rehearse each piece and mark one tricky spot to fix.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Throw & catch* (30 min): Show overhand throw to a target and catches high and low.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Plan* (20 min): Plan a poster or short video message.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 173, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Draft* (90 min): Draft the book review using linking words and at least 4 strong describing words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Graph project* (60 min): Survey family about favorite summer activity and make a bar graph with 3 questions.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build* (40 min): Build the chosen bridge and check that it spans the gap without touching the 'water.'
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Poster* (30 min): Create a biography poster with a portrait, timeline, traits, and one primary source image or quote.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Feedback* (40 min): Perform for a family member and apply one piece of feedback.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Dribble & kick* (30 min): Show hand dribble around cones and kick to a target.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Create* (20 min): Create the poster or video.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 174, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Type and publish* (90 min): Type the review on a computer or tablet with help, add a picture of the cover, and print or share it.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Shapes and shares* (60 min): Draw shapes from clues and partition shapes into equal shares.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Test* (40 min): Hang a cup from the bridge and add pennies one at a time, recording the count when it sags or falls.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Write and practice a 1-minute speech in first person ('I am Sally Ride…').
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Invitations* (40 min): Design invitations with artwork for family and friends.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Strike & rope* (30 min): Show paddle up-ups and jump rope forward.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Share* (20 min): Share your message with family or neighbors.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 175, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 35 review* (90 min): Read the review aloud and listen to a family member's review; spelling test of 20 review words from the year.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Mixed practice set of 15 items across measurement, data, geometry.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Improve* (40 min): Change one part (fold the paper, add a straw truss) and retest; record the new penny count.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Living museum* (30 min): Present the living museum in costume to family and answer audience questions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Dress rehearsal* (40 min): Run the whole showcase (gallery tour plus music) from start to finish.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Self-rate each skill with a smiley scale and pick one to keep practicing.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain why your message matters.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Book review: opinion writing about a favorite book
- Objective: Write an opinion book review with reasons
- Objective: Use digital tools to publish writing
- Objective: Use vivid adjectives and adverbs
- Vocabulary: review, recommend, rating, publish
- Materials: Winnie-the-Pooh, favorite books from the year, computer or tablet
- Assessment: Published typed book review (opinion rubric) and cumulative spelling test.

### Math 2: Review: measurement, data, money, time
- Objective: Review measurement, graphs, money, and time
- Objective: Apply skills in real contexts
- Vocabulary: review, apply
- Materials: rulers, coins, clock, graph paper
- Assessment: Mixed review set.

### Science 2: Engineering challenge: design a bridge
- Objective: Define a problem and criteria
- Objective: Choose materials using property data
- Objective: Build, test, and improve a bridge
- Vocabulary: bridge, load, support, span
- Materials: paper, straws, craft sticks, tape, two stacks of books 20 cm apart, pennies, paper cup
- Assessment: Bridge design sketches and load-test data.

### People Who Make a Difference: Hero project: living museum
- Objective: Research one person who made a difference
- Objective: Create a biography poster
- Objective: Present as the hero in a living museum
- Vocabulary: research, biography, living museum, legacy
- Materials: library biographies, poster board, costume items
- Assessment: Biography poster and living-museum presentation (rubric).

### Art & Music 2: Rehearsing the showcase
- Objective: Rehearse music performance pieces
- Objective: Apply feedback to refine performance
- Objective: Present final versions of compositions
- Vocabulary: rehearse, program, audience etiquette
- Materials: recordings, program paper, invitation materials
- Assessment: Dress rehearsal with checklist.

### PE 2: Skill showcase
- Objective: Show mastery of locomotor and manipulative skills
- Vocabulary: showcase, skill
- Materials: balls, cones, jump rope
- Assessment: End-of-year skill checklist and self-rating.

### Health 2: Health advocacy
- Objective: Encourage others to make healthy choices
- Vocabulary: advocate, encourage, message
- Materials: poster paper, markers
- Assessment: Advocacy project rubric.
