## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *The Little Red Hen* (90 min): Read The Little Red Hen and discuss why the hen would not share the bread; decide if that was fair. Phonics: oa/ow/oe words (boat, snow, toe).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Expanded form* (60 min): Use place-value arrow cards to show 427 = 400 + 20 + 7 and write expanded form for 10 numbers.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Define the problem* (40 min): Design a mini umbrella to keep a small toy dry; list criteria (waterproof, sturdy, light).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Grid game* (30 min): Play 'grid battleship' on a 5x5 letter-number grid to practice naming squares like C3.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Body percussion* (40 min): Learn four body-percussion sounds (stomp, pat, clap, snap) and echo 10 four-beat patterns led by a parent or video.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Swing and spring* (30 min): Practice 'swing arms back, bend knees, swing forward, jump' for standing long jumps; mark each landing with chalk.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Tooth parts* (20 min): Learn tooth parts (enamel, gum) and how plaque causes cavities; count your teeth in a mirror.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Stone Soup* (90 min): Read Stone Soup and compare its lesson about sharing with The Little Red Hen. Phonics: igh/ie/y words (night, pie, fly) in a sentence-building game.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Number words* (60 min): Practice spelling number words (twenty, forty, hundred) and write 8 numbers in word form (six hundred thirty-two).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Choose materials* (40 min): Use data from earlier tests to choose materials and sketch the design with labels.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Neighborhood map* (30 min): Look at a printed map of your neighborhood with a grid overlay and find home, school, park, and store by grid square.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Ostinato* (40 min): Perform a repeated rhythm ostinato while a parent sings 'Row, Row, Row Your Boat.'
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Measure it* (30 min): Do 5 standing long jumps, measure the best one in inches, and record it.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Egg experiment* (20 min): Soak hard-boiled eggs in water, soda and vinegar overnight to model how drinks affect enamel.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Voices for characters* (90 min): Practice reading Little Red Hen dialogue using a different voice for the hen, dog, cat, and duck. Make paper-bag puppets of each character.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Many ways* (60 min): Show 245 four ways: blocks, quick picture, expanded form, and as 24 tens 5 ones.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build* (40 min): Build the umbrella and test it under a spray bottle for 30 seconds.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Relative location* (30 min): Describe places using relative location ('The library is next to the park, north of my house').
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Bucket drumming* (40 min): Use a bucket or pot to play two layered rhythms with a family member.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Reach high* (30 min): Stand by a wall and jump to tap a sticky note; move the note up when you reach it 3 times.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Check the eggs* (20 min): Observe and record the eggs; discuss why limiting sugary drinks helps teeth.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 34, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Retell in writing* (90 min): Write a 5-sentence retelling of Stone Soup with a beginning, middle, and end and the lesson in the last sentence. Underline time-order words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Number riddles* (60 min): Solve riddles ('I have 3 hundreds, 0 tens, and 9 ones') and write 3 riddles for a family member.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Improve* (40 min): Improve one part and retest; record how the change affected results.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Draw a grid map* (30 min): Draw your own neighborhood map on grid paper with a key and list the grid square of 6 places.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Improvise* (40 min): Improvise 4-beat answers to rhythm 'questions' clapped by a parent.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Jump patterns* (30 min): Jump a chalk hopscotch and a 'jump ladder' (two feet, one foot, side to side).
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Brush & floss* (20 min): Practice 2-minute brushing in circles and flossing with a parent; start a brushing chart.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 35, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 7 review* (90 min): Perform The Little Red Hen as a puppet show using character voices. Spelling check of 12 long-o and long-i words and a written central-message question.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Convert 12 numbers among standard, word, and expanded forms.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Unit 2 check* (40 min): Present the umbrella and explain material choices; complete a short quiz on classifying materials by properties.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Answer 8 grid-location questions on a new community map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Perform a layered body-percussion piece (stomp-pat-clap-snap ostinato under a chanted rhyme) for the family and keep a steady beat throughout.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Retest the long jump and wall jump, compare with Day 2 and explain how practice helped.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain the egg experiment results and demonstrate proper brushing.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Folktales from many cultures and character voices
- Objective: Recount folktales and identify their central message
- Objective: Read oa/ow/oe and igh/ie/y long-vowel spellings
- Objective: Read with a different voice for each character
- Vocabulary: folktale, point of view, culture, dialogue
- Materials: The Little Red Hen (any version), Stone Soup (Marcia Brown or Jon J Muth), puppet supplies
- Assessment: Puppet-show performance (voice and fluency), spelling check, and written central message.

### Math 2: Reading and writing three-digit numbers
- Objective: Write numbers in standard form, word form, and expanded form
- Objective: Read number names
- Objective: Represent numbers in different ways
- Vocabulary: standard form, expanded form, word form, number name
- Materials: place-value cards, number word list
- Assessment: Form-conversion quiz (12 items).

### Science 2: Materials unit project: build a better umbrella
- Objective: Apply property data to a design challenge
- Objective: Build and test a model
- Objective: Communicate results
- Vocabulary: design, criteria, evaluate
- Materials: craft sticks, straws, foil, plastic bag, fabric, tape, spray bottle, small toy
- Assessment: Umbrella design presentation and properties quiz.

### People Who Make a Difference: Letter-number grids in my neighborhood
- Objective: Locate places on a letter-number grid
- Objective: Describe absolute and relative location
- Objective: Map the neighborhood
- Vocabulary: grid, coordinate, relative location, absolute location
- Materials: grid paper, neighborhood map (printed online map), colored pencils
- Assessment: Grid neighborhood map and location quiz.

### Art & Music 2: Body percussion and rhythm ostinatos
- Objective: Perform a repeated rhythm (ostinato)
- Objective: Layer rhythms
- Objective: Improvise rhythm patterns
- Vocabulary: ostinato, body percussion, layer, improvise
- Materials: recordings of 'We Will Rock You' style beats, household percussion (pots, buckets)
- Assessment: Body-percussion performance.

### PE 2: Jumping for distance and height
- Objective: Jump with a two-foot take-off and landing
- Objective: Use arms to jump farther and higher
- Vocabulary: standing long jump, vertical jump, swing
- Materials: tape measure, sidewalk chalk, sticky note
- Assessment: Recorded jump scores and oral explanation of cues and practice.

### Health 2: Healthy teeth
- Objective: Brush and floss correctly
- Objective: Explain how sugar affects teeth
- Vocabulary: plaque, cavity, enamel, floss
- Materials: toothbrush, floss, eggs, vinegar, soda, water, cups
- Assessment: Experiment record and brushing demonstration.
