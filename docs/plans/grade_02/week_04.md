## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Henry and Mudge* (90 min): Read Henry and Mudge: The First Book, chapters 1–2, and describe how Henry feels before and after he gets Mudge. Phonics: read initial blends (bl, cr, st, fl) in 15 words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Missing addends* (60 min): Solve equations with a box for the unknown (8 + □ = 15, □ − 4 = 9) using counters and a number line.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Plan a test* (40 min): Plan a fair test of which material absorbs water best: same amount of water, same size pieces. Write predictions.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Plan the interview* (30 min): Write 6 interview questions for a grandparent or older relative (What toys did you play with? How did you get to school? What did you eat? What chores did you do?).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Look at Mondrian* (40 min): Compare Mondrian's 'Broadway Boogie Woogie' with Kandinsky's circles; list how each artist used lines and shapes.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Add a twist* (30 min): Add direction changes to your course: sideways shuffle between cones and backward walk on a chalk line.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Read aloud* (20 min): Read 'Stand Tall, Molly Lou Melon' and talk about how Molly handled being teased.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Henry gets lost* (90 min): Read chapter 3 and discuss how Henry and Mudge each respond when Mudge gets lost. Phonics: final blends (-nd, -st, -mp) word building with letter tiles.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Start unknown* (60 min): Solve 'Some birds were on a tree. 6 more came. Now there are 14. How many were there at first?' using bar models; solve 5 like it.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Absorbency test* (40 min): Drop 10 drops (or spoonfuls) of water on each material and observe what happens; record in a data table.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Interview* (30 min): Conduct and record the interview by phone, video, or in person; take notes in drawings and words.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Neighborhood map art* (40 min): Sketch the streets near your home as vertical and horizontal lines on paper.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Follow the leader* (30 min): Play follow the leader with a sibling or parent, taking turns leading with speed and level changes.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *What is bullying* (20 min): Learn the difference between rude, mean and bullying (repeated, on purpose, hurtful).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Revise with details* (90 min): Writing: reread the narrative draft and add 2 details and a feeling sentence using a colored pencil ('My heart was pounding'). Edit for capitals and end marks with a checklist.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Write your own* (60 min): Write 3 word problems (one with the unknown at the start, middle, end) for a family member to solve and check their answers.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Waterproof test* (40 min): Test which materials keep a paper doll dry under drips (foil, plastic wrap, cloth, paper).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Compare* (30 min): Make a Venn diagram comparing your daily life with the interviewee's childhood (school, games, technology, food).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Mondrian city* (40 min): Turn the sketch into a Mondrian-style artwork with black lines and red, yellow, and blue rectangles.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Animal moves* (30 min): Move through the course as animals (bear crawl, crab walk, frog jump, flamingo balance).
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Upstander moves* (20 min): Practice 3 upstander moves: say 'Stop', walk away with the target, tell a trusted adult.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Publish* (90 min): Writing: copy the revised narrative neatly, add an illustration for each part, and give it a title. Practice reading it aloud at a steady pace with expression.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Fluency stations* (60 min): Rotate through fact games: fact-triangle flash, 'Salute!' card game, and an online fact practice session.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Analyze* (40 min): Make a bar graph of results and decide the best material for a raincoat and for cleaning a spill.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Games from long ago* (30 min): Play one game the interviewee played as a child (jacks, hopscotch, marbles) and write about it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Mini gallery* (40 min): Hang the unit's artworks in a hallway 'gallery' and label each with title and materials.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Encouragement* (30 min): Cheer a family member through your course using 3 positive phrases, then switch roles.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Trusted adults* (20 min): Draw a hand and write a trusted adult on each finger.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Unit 1 assessment* (90 min): Author's chair: read the published narrative aloud to the family and answer listeners' questions. Complete a unit check: blends/digraph spelling (12 words) and a short story-element quiz on a new Frog and Toad story.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 1 test* (60 min): Fluency quiz (40 facts within 20, compare to Week 1 baseline) and 6 one-step word problems with unknowns in different positions.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Write a claim and evidence sentence: 'The best material for a raincoat is ___ because ___.'
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Add the interview and Venn diagram to the family history book.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Unit 1 check* (40 min): Give a gallery tour naming 5 kinds of lines and both kinds of shapes in your work.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Run the full course and compare with your week-3 time; tell how practice changed your time.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Respond correctly to 3 bullying scenarios and explain the difference between telling and tattling.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Henry and Mudge; revising and sharing stories
- Objective: Read words with consonant blends
- Objective: Revise a narrative by adding details and feelings
- Objective: Tell a story audibly in complete sentences
- Vocabulary: blend, revise, detail, audience
- Materials: Henry and Mudge: The First Book (Cynthia Rylant), colored pencils, author chair
- Assessment: Published personal narrative scored with a simple rubric (sequence, details, closing, conventions) plus unit spelling and story-element quiz.

### Math 2: Unknowns in all positions and fluency check
- Objective: Solve problems with unknown start or change
- Objective: Use symbols for unknowns
- Objective: Show fluency within 20
- Vocabulary: unknown, missing addend, start unknown, bar model
- Materials: bar model mats, counters, timer
- Assessment: Unit 1 test with fluency comparison to baseline.

### Science 2: Testing materials: absorbency and waterproofing
- Objective: Plan and conduct a fair test
- Objective: Collect and compare data
- Objective: Decide which material is best for a purpose
- Vocabulary: absorb, waterproof, fair test, data
- Materials: paper towel, napkin, sponge, cloth, plastic wrap, foil, eyedropper or spoon, water, cup
- Assessment: Data table, bar graph, and claim-evidence sentence.

### People Who Make a Difference: Interviewing family: then and now
- Objective: Conduct an oral history interview
- Objective: Compare daily life with a parent or grandparent
- Objective: Record findings
- Vocabulary: interview, oral history, compare, contrast
- Materials: interview question sheet, audio recorder or phone, Venn diagram
- Assessment: Interview notes and then/now Venn diagram.

### Art & Music 2: Line and shape project: Mondrian and my neighborhood
- Objective: Use lines and shapes to plan an artwork
- Objective: Compare artists' use of line and shape
- Objective: Present and talk about art
- Vocabulary: vertical, horizontal, primary colors, grid
- Materials: image of Mondrian's 'Broadway Boogie Woogie', black tape or marker, red, yellow, blue paper
- Assessment: Mondrian-style artwork and gallery tour using art vocabulary.

### PE 2: Movement challenge course
- Objective: Combine direction, speed and obstacle skills
- Objective: Encourage others
- Vocabulary: combine, course, encourage
- Materials: home obstacle course, stopwatch or phone timer
- Assessment: Course time comparison and observation of encouraging words.

### Health 2: Standing up to bullying
- Objective: Recognize bullying
- Objective: Respond safely and help others
- Vocabulary: bullying, bystander, upstander, report
- Materials: 'Stand Tall, Molly Lou Melon' by Patty Lovell (library)
- Assessment: Scenario responses.
