## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Meet Peter* (90 min): Read The Tale of Peter Rabbit and fill a story map; discuss what the beginning tells us (Mother's warning). Phonics: oi/oy words (coin, boy, soil, toy).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Subtract tens and ones* (60 min): Subtract 68 − 25 with blocks and quick pictures; solve 10 problems without decomposing.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Plan the light test* (40 min): Plan a fair test: 2 plants in sunlight, 2 in a dark closet, same water and soil. Write predictions.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Lawmakers* (30 min): Learn that city councils, the California State Legislature in Sacramento, and Congress in Washington, D.C., make laws; look at photos of each building.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *High or low* (40 min): Listen to pairs of notes on a piano app or xylophone and show high (hands up) or low (hands on knees); hear high and low animal sounds.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Find your heart* (30 min): Place a hand on your chest, feel your heartbeat resting, and learn that the heart is a muscle pumping blood.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Breakfast power* (20 min): Learn how breakfast fuels the brain; plan 3 balanced breakfasts.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Peter responds* (90 min): Reread and describe how Peter responds to Mr. McGregor chasing him, using the illustrations as evidence. Write 3 sentences about his feelings.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Break a ten* (60 min): Subtract 52 − 17 by trading a ten for 10 ones; solve 8 problems with drawings.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Set up* (40 min): Set up the four plants, label each cup (light/dark), and measure and record each plant's starting height in centimeters.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *How a bill becomes a law* (30 min): Follow a simple flow chart of how an idea becomes a law (idea → bill → vote → signed).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Water glass scale* (40 min): Fill 5 glasses with different water levels, tap them, and order them from lowest to highest pitch.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Before and after* (30 min): Count heartbeats for 15 seconds at rest, then after 1 minute of jumping jacks; record both.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Snack sort* (20 min): Sort snacks into 'go' (every day), 'slow' (sometimes) and 'whoa' (once in a while).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 63, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Past tense irregular verbs* (90 min): Grammar: learn ran, went, ate, hid, sat, told, found from the story and complete 10 sentences. Phonics: oi/oy spelling sort.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Count up* (60 min): Solve 83 − 48 by counting up on a number line (48 → 50 → 80 → 83 = 35) and compare to the take-away method.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Observe* (40 min): Measure each plant's height and note its leaf color and stem shape; record the numbers in the data table.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Family vote* (30 min): Propose a 'family law' (e.g., a new pet rule), debate it, and vote on it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Melody maps* (40 min): Draw lines showing the melody of 'Twinkle, Twinkle' going up, down, and staying the same, then follow the map while singing.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Breathing check* (30 min): Notice and describe breathing at rest, after walking and after running in place.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Decision steps* (20 min): Use a 3-step decision process (choices, think about results, choose) to pick an after-school snack.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 64, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *How it ends* (90 min): Discuss how the ending (chamomile tea vs. blackberries) concludes the story and teaches a lesson. Rewrite the ending in 4 sentences as if Peter had listened.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Check with addition* (60 min): Solve 10 subtraction problems and check each with addition; explain why addition checks subtraction.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Midweek check* (40 min): Draw both groups and compare color (green vs. yellow) and stem shape.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Local leaders* (30 min): Find the names of your mayor and governor and write one question you would ask them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Scarf melody* (40 min): Move a scarf up and down to match the melody of 'Frère Jacques' and sing it in tune with a recording.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Heart-healthy moves* (30 min): Sort activities (sleeping, walking, soccer, dancing, video games) into 'slow heart' and 'fast heart' groups.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Snack lab* (20 min): Make a healthy snack like apple 'nachos' or veggie wraps and wash produce first.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 65, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 13 review* (90 min): Spelling check of 10 oi/oy words, irregular past-tense quiz, and a story-map check on 'The Tale of Benjamin Bunny' read aloud.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz of 12 subtraction problems within 100 and one written explanation of a strategy.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Explain why only light was changed and what we keep the same in a fair test.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Draw a flow chart of how a law is made.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Identify high/low in 8 note pairs and draw the contour of a short melody played by a parent.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain with your chart how exercise changes your heart rate and breathing.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain your snack choice using the decision steps.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Peter Rabbit: story structure and irregular past tense
- Objective: Describe how the beginning introduces and the ending concludes a story
- Objective: Use irregular past-tense verbs
- Objective: Read oi/oy words
- Vocabulary: beginning, middle, ending, past tense
- Materials: The Tale of Peter Rabbit (Beatrix Potter, public domain), story map
- Assessment: Story map for a new Potter tale, irregular past-tense quiz, spelling check.

### Math 2: Subtracting within 100 using place value
- Objective: Subtract two-digit numbers with and without decomposing a ten
- Objective: Subtract on a number line
- Objective: Relate subtraction to addition
- Vocabulary: decompose, trade, difference, count up
- Materials: base-ten blocks, open number line paper
- Assessment: Subtraction quiz with explanation.

### Science 2: Fair test: Do plants need light?
- Objective: Plan and conduct a fair test about light
- Objective: Keep variables the same
- Objective: Record growth data
- Vocabulary: variable, fair test, control, measure
- Materials: 4 bean plants or seedlings in cups, dark closet, ruler
- Assessment: Investigation plan and data table.

### People Who Make a Difference: Who makes laws?
- Objective: Explain how laws are made
- Objective: Identify local, state, and national lawmakers
- Objective: Model a vote
- Vocabulary: legislature, city council, Congress, vote, bill
- Materials: photos of the U.S. Capitol and California State Capitol, ballot slips
- Assessment: Lawmaking flow chart.

### Art & Music 2: High and low: pitch
- Objective: Distinguish high and low pitches
- Objective: Show melodic direction with body and drawings
- Objective: Sing in tune with a model
- Vocabulary: pitch, high, low, melody, contour
- Materials: xylophone, piano app, or water glasses, scarves
- Assessment: Pitch identification and melody-contour drawing.

### PE 2: My heart is a muscle
- Objective: Locate the heart and pulse
- Objective: Describe how heart rate changes with exercise
- Vocabulary: heart rate, pulse, beats per minute
- Materials: timer, chart paper
- Assessment: Heart-rate chart and oral explanation.

### Health 2: Breakfast and healthy snacks
- Objective: Explain why breakfast matters
- Objective: Choose snacks low in added sugar, fat and salt
- Vocabulary: breakfast, snack, added sugar, decide
- Materials: snack foods, paper
- Assessment: Decision-making worksheet and explanation.
