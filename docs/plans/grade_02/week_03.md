## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *The List* (90 min): Read 'A List' from Frog and Toad Together and make your own to-do list for the day. Phonics: read and write sh/ch/th words (ship, chop, thin, wish, much, bath).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Fact families* (60 min): Build fact families with fact triangles (7, 8, 15) and write the 4 related equations for 6 families.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Material hunt* (40 min): Find 15 household objects and identify what material each is made of (wood, metal, plastic, glass, fabric, paper, rubber).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Artifact museum* (30 min): Collect 3–5 family artifacts and set up a home 'museum'; write a label for each telling what it is and who used it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Look at Matisse* (40 min): Look at Matisse's 'The Snail' and learn that he 'drew with scissors' when he was ill; describe the shapes and colors.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Obstacle words* (30 min): Practice going over a pool noodle, under a broom across two chairs, around a cone and through a hoop; name each word.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Read aloud* (20 min): Read 'The Invisible Boy' by Trudy Ludwig and discuss how Brian felt left out.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Cookies and willpower* (90 min): Read 'Cookies' and answer why Frog and Toad cannot stop eating cookies and how they try to solve it. Phonics: wh and ck words (when, whip, duck, sock) in a word ladder.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Think addition* (60 min): Solve 14 − 6 by asking '6 + ? = 14'; practice 20 subtraction facts using the related addition fact.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Properties* (40 min): Describe each material's properties (hard/soft, rough/smooth, bendy/stiff, shiny/dull) in a table.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Ask the artifact* (30 min): Use the questions 'What is it? Who used it? When? Why was it important?' to study one artifact and draw it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Free cutting* (40 min): Practice safe scissor use and cut 10 organic shapes freehand without drawing first, inspired by leaves and seaweed.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Levels* (30 min): Travel the course at high (tiptoes), medium and low (crawl) levels; freeze in a shape at each level.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Friend recipe* (20 min): Write a 'Recipe for a Good Friend' listing ingredients like kindness, sharing and listening.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Collective nouns* (90 min): Grammar: match collective nouns to groups (a flock of birds, a herd of cows, a team of players, a class of students) and write 4 sentences using them. Read 'The Garden' aloud with a partner.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Back through ten* (60 min): Use a 0–20 number line to subtract through ten (15 − 7: back 5 to 10, back 2 more to 8).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Sort and classify* (40 min): Sort the objects two different ways (by material, then by a property) and explain the rule for each sort.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Documents* (30 min): Look at a family document (birth certificate copy, old letter, recipe card, or report card) and record 3 facts it tells.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Compose* (40 min): Arrange the shapes on a background in three different ways before gluing; take a photo of each arrangement and choose the best.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Build a course* (30 min): Design and draw a 6-station obstacle course with a parent and set it up in the yard or living room.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Listening skills* (20 min): Practice active listening: eyes on speaker, nod, ask a question; take turns telling about your weekend.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Draft my story* (90 min): Writing: draft the small moment chosen last week using temporal words (first, next, then, after that, finally) to show the order. Include what you saw, heard, and felt.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Compare problems* (60 min): Solve 'how many more/fewer' problems with bar models (e.g., 'Leo has 13 stickers, Mia has 8. How many more does Leo have?').
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Mystery bag* (40 min): Identify objects in a bag by touch alone and name their texture and hardness.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Family tree* (30 min): Draw a family tree with yourself, parents, and grandparents (and great-grandparents if known) with names and birthplaces.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Positive and negative* (40 min): Use the leftover paper (negative space) to make a second collage and compare the two.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Timed course* (30 min): Run your course three times, timing each try and recording the times on a chart.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *I-messages* (20 min): Learn 'I feel ___ when ___ because ___' and use it in 3 friend-problem role-plays.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 15, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 3 review* (90 min): Spelling check of 10 digraph words, collective-noun matching quiz, and read the narrative draft aloud to a family member who asks one question about it.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Subtraction fact quiz (30 facts) and 4 mixed word problems with drawings and equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Classify 10 new objects by material and properties and justify one classification aloud.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Start a family history book with the family tree and artifact pages.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Reflect* (40 min): Present both collages and explain why you chose your arrangement.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Teach the course to a family member using the words over, under, around, through and explain which level each part used.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Name 3 qualities of a good friend and show one I-message.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Digraphs and drafting a personal narrative
- Objective: Read words with sh, ch, th, wh, ck
- Objective: Use collective nouns
- Objective: Draft a personal narrative with temporal words
- Vocabulary: digraph, collective noun, temporal word, narrative
- Materials: Frog and Toad Together (Arnold Lobel), digraph cards, writing paper
- Assessment: Spelling check, collective-noun quiz (8 items), and narrative draft with at least 3 temporal words.

### Math 2: Subtraction strategies within 20
- Objective: Subtract by thinking addition
- Objective: Subtract by counting back and through ten
- Objective: Solve take-from and compare problems
- Vocabulary: fact family, related facts, count back, compare
- Materials: number line 0–20, fact triangles, counters
- Assessment: Subtraction fact quiz and mixed word problems.

### Science 2: Describing materials by properties
- Objective: Describe materials by color, texture, hardness, flexibility
- Objective: Classify objects by material
- Objective: Record observations in a table
- Vocabulary: material, property, texture, flexible, hard
- Materials: wood, metal, plastic, fabric, paper, rubber objects, sorting trays
- Assessment: Material/property classification table.

### People Who Make a Difference: Family artifacts and documents
- Objective: Use artifacts and documents to learn family history
- Objective: Ask questions about sources
- Objective: Begin a family history book
- Vocabulary: artifact, document, heirloom, ancestor
- Materials: family artifacts (old photos, letters, recipe cards, tools), family history notebook
- Assessment: Family tree and artifact pages.

### Art & Music 2: Matisse cut-outs: drawing with scissors
- Objective: Create organic shapes by cutting
- Objective: Arrange shapes into a composition
- Objective: Use scissors safely
- Vocabulary: collage, cut-out, positive space, negative space
- Materials: images of Matisse's 'The Snail' and 'Icarus', colored paper, scissors and glue sticks
- Assessment: Matisse-style collage and explanation of choices.

### PE 2: Over, under, around and through
- Objective: Travel over, under, around and through obstacles
- Objective: Change level while traveling
- Vocabulary: obstacle, level, over, under, through
- Materials: pool noodles, chairs, broom, hula hoop, blanket
- Assessment: Course completed safely; correct use of position and level words.

### Health 2: Being a good friend
- Objective: Describe qualities of a good friend
- Objective: Include others
- Vocabulary: friend, include, respect, empathy
- Materials: 'The Invisible Boy' by Trudy Ludwig (library), paper
- Assessment: Friend recipe and I-message role-play.
