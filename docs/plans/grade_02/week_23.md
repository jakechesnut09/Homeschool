## Day 1 — Monday (school day 111, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Traditional pigs* (90 min): Read The Three Little Pigs and retell it from the pigs' point of view. Practice voices for each pig and the wolf.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Coin values* (60 min): Sort real coins and learn values (1¢, 5¢, 10¢, 25¢); skip-count dimes by 10 and nickels by 5.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Choose and research* (40 min): Choose one habitat (redwood forest, Mojave Desert, kelp forest, tide pool, or pond) and use 2 sources to list 6 living things and what each needs.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Scarcity* (30 min): Share 5 crackers among 7 people and discuss what to do when there is not enough; define scarcity in your own words.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Dynamics* (40 min): Learn forte (loud) and piano (soft) and sing 'Bingo' changing dynamics on signal.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Plant and kick* (30 min): Learn cues: plant foot beside ball, kick with inside of foot, follow through to target.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Then and now* (20 min): Compare a baby photo to now; list what you can do now.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 112, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *The wolf's side* (90 min): Read The True Story of the 3 Little Pigs! and discuss how the wolf tells it differently. Chart what the pigs think vs. what the wolf claims.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Count mixed coins* (60 min): Count mixed coins starting with the greatest value (25, 35, 40, 41); count 10 handfuls and record.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build the background* (40 min): Paint or line a shoebox to show the habitat's land, water, and sky, and add plants made from paper, twigs, or clay.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Menu choices* (30 min): With $10 of play money, order from a pretend menu and write what you chose and what you gave up (the trade-off).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Tempo* (40 min): Learn fast (allegro) and slow (largo) and move like animals to 'The Elephant' and 'Wild Horses' from Carnival of the Animals.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Rolling ball kick* (30 min): Kick a ball rolled slowly toward you back to the roller.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Measure me* (20 min): Measure height, hand span and foot; compare with family members.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 113, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Stepsister's view* (90 min): Retell a scene from Cinderella in 4–5 sentences from a stepsister's point of view. Read it aloud in her voice.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Ways to make* (60 min): Show 50¢ four different ways with coins and record with drawings.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Add animals and labels* (40 min): Model at least 4 animals in clay or paper and add index-card labels telling what each eats and where it shelters.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Farmer's choice* (30 min): Plan a 20-square grid-paper farm with limited land and water; decide how many squares of almonds, tomatoes, and lettuce to plant and explain why.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Listening map* (40 min): Listen to 'The Swan' and 'Aquarium' and describe dynamics and tempo for each.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Force control* (30 min): Kick to cones at 5, 10 and 20 feet, using light, medium and strong kicks.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Life stages* (20 min): Make a life-stage timeline (infant to older adult) and note needs at each.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 114, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Opinion draft* (90 min): Draft the opinion piece 'The Best Cinderella Story' with an opening, opinion, 3 reasons using because, also, and another reason, and a concluding sentence.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Coin store* (60 min): Run a pretend store with items under $1; pay with exact change and record each purchase.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Present* (40 min): Present the diorama, naming how many kinds of living things it shows, and compare it with one habitat from the chart (more or less diversity and why).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Family grocery list* (30 min): Help plan a real grocery list within a budget, crossing out wants that do not fit.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Your animal* (40 min): Choose an animal and play it on a homemade instrument with dynamics and tempo that fit; family guesses the animal.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Mini goal* (30 min): Shoot at a 2-cone goal from 3 spots; count goals out of 9.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Everyone is unique* (20 min): Discuss how kids grow at different rates and that differences are normal.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 115, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 23 review* (90 min): Read a short new fable and tell how two characters see the event differently. Peer/parent check of the opinion draft for linking words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Count 8 coin collections and solve 3 word problems with cents.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Unit 6 check* (40 min): Quiz: match 8 organisms to habitats and explain which of two pictured habitats has more diversity.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Write about one choice you made this week, what you gave up, and why.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Identify dynamics and tempo in 4 excerpts and say which piece you prefer and why.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Pass back and forth with a partner 10 times and name the 3 kick cues.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Present your timeline and one way you have grown.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Point of view: whose story is it?
- Objective: Acknowledge different characters' points of view
- Objective: Read aloud in different voices
- Objective: Draft an opinion piece with linking words
- Vocabulary: point of view, narrator, linking word, because
- Materials: The Three Little Pigs (any traditional version), The True Story of the 3 Little Pigs! (Jon Scieszka)
- Assessment: Point-of-view retelling and opinion draft with at least 3 linking words.

### Math 2: Coins and counting money
- Objective: Identify coins and their values
- Objective: Count mixed coins
- Objective: Use ¢ and $ symbols
- Vocabulary: penny, nickel, dime, quarter, cent
- Materials: real or play coins, coin purse
- Assessment: Coin-counting quiz and word problems.

### Science 2: Habitat diorama project
- Objective: Build a model of a habitat
- Objective: Show the diversity of living things in it
- Objective: Present and compare habitats
- Vocabulary: diorama, model, diversity, shelter
- Materials: shoebox, construction paper, clay, twigs, sand, cotton, index-card labels
- Assessment: Diorama presentation (rubric) and unit quiz.

### People Who Make a Difference: Limited resources and choices
- Objective: Explain scarcity
- Objective: Make choices when resources are limited
- Objective: Identify the trade-off in a choice
- Vocabulary: scarcity, choice, trade-off, limited
- Materials: play money, pretend restaurant menu, grid paper 'farm'
- Assessment: Trade-off explanations and grid-farm plan.

### Art & Music 2: Dynamics and tempo: Carnival of the Animals
- Objective: Identify loud/soft and fast/slow
- Objective: Explain how composers use dynamics and tempo
- Objective: Perform with dynamics
- Vocabulary: dynamics, forte, piano, tempo, allegro, largo
- Materials: Saint-Saëns 'Carnival of the Animals' recordings, drawing paper
- Assessment: Dynamics and tempo listening check with preference.

### PE 2: Kicking to a target
- Objective: Kick a rolling ball with inside of foot
- Objective: Use the right force
- Vocabulary: pass, plant foot, force
- Materials: ball, cones for goal
- Assessment: Kick cue checklist and goal count.

### Health 2: Growing and changing
- Objective: Explain that people grow at different rates
- Objective: Identify life stages
- Vocabulary: growth, life stage, unique
- Materials: baby photos, measuring tape
- Assessment: Life-stage timeline.
