## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Main topic* (90 min): Read From Tadpole to Frog and name the main topic; reread 3 paragraphs and tell what each one is mostly about. Phonics: y as long e and long i (happy, sky).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *How much longer* (60 min): Measure two pencils and find how much longer one is by counting on from the shorter length.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Plan* (40 min): Collect clean recyclables and plan a new object (toy, organizer) to build from them; sketch it.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Three communities* (30 min): Look at photos of Los Angeles, a suburb, and a Central Valley farm and list what land is used for in each.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Value strip* (40 min): Make a 7-step value strip from one color by adding white (tints) on one side and black (shades) on the other.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Rolling review* (30 min): Practice log rolls and egg rolls on a mat, keeping arms in and body tight.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *MyPlate* (20 min): Learn the 5 food groups using MyPlate and name 3 foods in each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Diagrams help* (90 min): Study the diagram of the frog life cycle and explain what it shows that the words do not. Draw your own labeled diagram of a tadpole.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Ribbon compare* (60 min): Cut ribbons of different lengths, measure each in cm, and write 5 'how much longer' sentences.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build* (40 min): Build the object with glue and tape, counting how many of each piece you used, and label its parts.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Compare* (30 min): Make a three-column chart comparing housing, jobs, transportation, and land use.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Monet's Water Lilies* (40 min): Look at Monet's 'Water Lilies' and notice the short brushstrokes and many tints of blue and green; describe the time of day you think it shows.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Rocking chair* (30 min): Rock back and forth in a tuck, chin on chest, to learn the rounded back for a forward roll.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Flyer sort* (20 min): Cut foods from grocery flyers and glue them onto a MyPlate poster.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 53, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Choose an animal* (90 min): Choose an animal for an 'All About' report, read 2 library books on it, and take notes in 3 boxes: body, home, food.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Toy car race* (60 min): Roll toy cars down a ramp, measure distances, and find the difference between the farthest and shortest rolls.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Rebuild* (40 min): Disassemble and rebuild into a second, different object; photograph or draw both.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Map it* (30 min): Mark 3 urban, 3 suburban, and 3 rural places on a California map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Paint outside* (40 min): Sit outside like Monet and paint a garden, tree, or sky using short dabbed brushstrokes.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Forward roll on a slope* (30 min): Roll forward down a gentle grass slope or folded mattress: hands down, chin tucked, push with feet.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Plate check* (20 min): Draw your dinner and check which food groups it had and which were missing.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 54, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Topic sentences* (90 min): Learn that an informative paragraph starts with a topic sentence; draft paragraph 1 (body) with a topic sentence and 3 facts. Phonics: y word sort.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Family lengths* (60 min): Measure family members' arm spans in inches and write compare sentences ('Dad's arm span is 14 inches longer than mine').
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Ice pop test* (40 min): Freeze juice pops and let one melt and refreeze; discuss if it is the same as before.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *My community* (30 min): Decide whether your community is urban, suburban, or rural and give 3 reasons.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Add light and shadow* (40 min): Finish the landscape by adding tints where the sun hits and shades where shadows fall.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Forward roll on flat* (30 min): Practice forward rolls on the flat mat with a parent spotting at the hips.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Food group game* (20 min): Play 'food group freeze': run to the corner for the food group called.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 55, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 11 review* (90 min): Read a new 3-paragraph passage and write the main topic and the focus of each paragraph. Spelling check of y-as-vowel words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 3 test* (60 min): Measure objects in two unit systems, estimate lengths, and solve 4 'how much longer' problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Unit 3 check* (40 min): Quiz on pieces/new objects and reversible/irreversible changes; present the recycled builds.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Unit 2 check* (30 min): Quiz on map elements, grids, North America labels, and land use types.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Reflect* (40 min): Point to 3 tints and 3 shades in your painting and explain how you mixed them.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform 2 forward rolls with chin tucked and say the 3 safety cues.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Sort 15 food cards into groups and plan one balanced lunch.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Main topic, paragraphs, and diagrams
- Objective: Identify the main topic and paragraph focus
- Objective: Explain how a diagram clarifies a text
- Objective: Draft an informative paragraph
- Vocabulary: main topic, paragraph, diagram, label, topic sentence
- Materials: From Tadpole to Frog (Wendy Pfeffer), animal books from library, drawing paper
- Assessment: Main-topic and paragraph-focus organizer on a cold read; first report paragraph.

### Math 2: Comparing lengths
- Objective: Find how much longer one object is than another
- Objective: Measure and record differences
- Objective: Solve compare-length problems
- Vocabulary: difference, longer, shorter, compare
- Materials: rulers, ribbon or string, toy cars
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Science 2: Matter unit project: recycled materials
- Objective: Apply understanding of pieces and changes to a project
- Objective: Design a new object from old pieces
- Vocabulary: recycle, reuse, design
- Materials: clean recyclables (boxes, tubes, caps), glue and tape
- Assessment: Recycled-object presentation and unit quiz.

### People Who Make a Difference: Urban, suburban, and rural California
- Objective: Compare urban, suburban, and rural land use
- Objective: Use photos as evidence
- Objective: Map land use in California
- Vocabulary: urban, suburban, rural, land use
- Materials: Calisphere photos of California cities, suburbs, and farms, California map
- Assessment: Land-use chart and unit quiz.

### Art & Music 2: Tints, shades, and Monet's gardens
- Objective: Make tints and shades of a color
- Objective: Describe Impressionist brushwork
- Objective: Paint a landscape from observation
- Vocabulary: tint, shade, value, Impressionism, landscape
- Materials: image of Monet's 'Water Lilies', white and black paint, watercolor paper
- Assessment: Value strip and observational landscape.

### PE 2: Rolling safely
- Objective: Perform log, egg and forward rolls safely
- Vocabulary: log roll, forward roll, tuck, chin to chest
- Materials: soft mat, mattress or grass
- Assessment: Forward-roll checklist: hands placed, chin tucked, rounded back.

### Health 2: MyPlate food groups
- Objective: Classify foods into the five food groups
- Vocabulary: MyPlate, grains, protein, dairy, serving
- Materials: MyPlate printable, grocery flyers, scissors, glue
- Assessment: Food sort and balanced lunch plan.
