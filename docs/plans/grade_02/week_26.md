## Day 1 — Monday (school day 126, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Rosa Parks* (90 min): Read I Am Rosa Parks and discuss why her choice on the bus mattered. Answer how and why questions in complete sentences.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Ruler to number line* (60 min): Compare a measuring tape and a number line; make a 0–100 number line on adding-machine tape with tick marks every 5.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Sort events* (40 min): Sort 12 picture cards (flood, eruption, canyon, earthquake, sand dune, landslide, glacier valley) into fast and slow and justify 3 choices aloud.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *What is a hero* (30 min): List people you admire and the traits that make them heroes (courage, honesty, kindness, perseverance); make a 'hero traits' chart to use all unit.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Gyotaku history* (40 min): Learn that Japanese fishermen in the 1800s made gyotaku ('fish rubbing') prints to record the size of their catches, and look at museum examples.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Make a paddle* (30 min): Make a paddle from a paper plate and paint stick; practice ready position.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Fire facts* (20 min): Learn about smoke alarms and test one with a parent.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 127, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Jackie Robinson* (90 min): Read I Am Jackie Robinson and compare the courage of Jackie and Rosa. Grammar: contractions with apostrophes (can't, didn't, he's, they're).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Show lengths* (60 min): Mark numbers as lengths from 0 (e.g., 35) and show 35 + 20 and 35 − 12 with jumps.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Time scale strip* (40 min): Make a long paper timeline with marks for seconds, hours, days, years, and millions of years, and place each event card where it belongs.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Lincoln's life* (30 min): Read a picture-book biography of Abraham Lincoln and put 5 events on a timeline (log cabin, self-taught reader, lawyer, president, ended slavery).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *First prints* (40 min): Brush a thin layer of paint on a rubber fish, lay paper over it, and rub gently from head to tail; make 2 prints and compare which shows more scale texture.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Up-ups* (30 min): Strike a balloon upward with the paddle 10 times, flat face up.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Stop, drop, roll* (20 min): Practice stop, drop and roll and crawling low under smoke.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 128, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Choose a person* (90 min): Choose a person who made a difference to research (e.g., Cesar Chavez, Sally Ride, Abraham Lincoln, Helen Keller, Marie Curie). Find 2 books or passages at the library.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Equal intervals* (60 min): Fill in missing numbers on number lines counting by 2s, 5s, and 10s.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Yosemite Valley* (40 min): Read two short sources about how glaciers carved Yosemite Valley slowly and how a rockfall can happen in seconds; list evidence from each.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Grace Bedell's letter* (30 min): Read the 1860 letter from 11-year-old Grace Bedell suggesting Lincoln grow a beard and Lincoln's reply; discuss what it shows about him.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Color experiments* (40 min): Make 3 more prints using different paint colors and paper, noting which combination shows the most detail.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Down-ups* (30 min): Bounce a foam ball down with the paddle, then try alternating up and down.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Escape map* (20 min): Draw a home escape map with two ways out of each room.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 129, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Research questions* (90 min): Write 3 research questions (When and where was this person born? What problem did they work on? How did they change things?). Start notes from the first source.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Solve on lines* (60 min): Solve 8 addition and subtraction problems within 100 on open number lines.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Compare in writing* (40 min): Write a paragraph comparing one fast change and one slow change: what happened, how long it took, and how you know.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Lincoln's legacy* (30 min): Look at Lincoln on the penny and five-dollar bill and the Lincoln Memorial photo; discuss why we still honor him and which traits he showed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Finish the best print* (40 min): Choose the best print, paint in the eye with a fine brush, and add a name stamp or signature as Japanese printmakers do.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Wall rally* (30 min): Hit a foam ball to a wall after one bounce and track how many in a row.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Fire drill* (20 min): Hold a family fire drill to the outside meeting place.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 130, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 26 review* (90 min): Contractions quiz (12 items), read-aloud fluency check on a biography page, and research notes check.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: label 4 number lines and solve 6 problems with number-line jumps.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Read the paragraph aloud and answer a family member's questions; check that each claim names a source.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Write a letter to a leader, as Grace did, giving one idea and a reason.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Reflect* (40 min): Explain in 3 sentences how gyotaku started as a record and became art, and how it connects to the texture rubbings from Unit 5.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Do 10 balloon up-ups and 5 ball bounces and name the paddle cues.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain your escape plan and fire rules.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Biographies of courage: Rosa Parks and Jackie Robinson
- Objective: Ask and answer questions about key details in biographies
- Objective: Use apostrophes in contractions
- Objective: Begin shared research
- Vocabulary: courage, contraction, apostrophe, research
- Materials: I Am Rosa Parks and I Am Jackie Robinson (Brad Meltzer), research folder
- Assessment: Contractions quiz, fluency record, research question sheet and notes.

### Math 2: Number line diagrams
- Objective: Represent numbers as lengths on a number line
- Objective: Show sums and differences on a number line
- Objective: Connect rulers and number lines
- Vocabulary: number line, interval, tick mark, distance
- Materials: measuring tape, adding-machine tape for number lines
- Assessment: Number line quiz.

### Science 2: Comparing fast and slow changes on a timeline
- Objective: Sort Earth events by how long they take
- Objective: Build a timeline of time scales
- Objective: Compare a fast and a slow change in writing
- Vocabulary: timeline, minutes, years, millions
- Materials: Earth event picture cards, long paper strip, photos of Yosemite Valley
- Assessment: Time-scale strip and comparison paragraph.

### People Who Make a Difference: What makes a hero? Abraham Lincoln
- Objective: Identify character traits of people who make a difference
- Objective: Learn about Abraham Lincoln's life
- Objective: Analyze a primary-source letter
- Vocabulary: hero, character trait, honest, president, primary source
- Materials: Abraham Lincoln biography (library), copy of Grace Bedell's 1860 letter and Lincoln's reply, penny and five-dollar bill
- Assessment: Lincoln timeline and letter to a leader.

### Art & Music 2: Gyotaku fish printing (Japan)
- Objective: Learn the history and purpose of gyotaku
- Objective: Make relief prints
- Objective: Compare a tradition's past and present uses
- Vocabulary: print, gyotaku, relief, brayer
- Materials: rubber fish replica (or a whole fish from the market), washable tempera paint and brush, newsprint or rice paper
- Assessment: Finished gyotaku print and written reflection.

### PE 2: Paddle striking
- Objective: Strike a balloon and ball with a short-handled paddle
- Vocabulary: paddle, flat face, ready position
- Materials: short paddles (or paper plate on a paint stick), balloons, foam ball
- Assessment: Paddle skill counts.

### Health 2: Fire safety
- Objective: Know fire safety rules and a home escape plan
- Vocabulary: smoke alarm, escape plan, meeting place
- Materials: paper, smoke alarm
- Assessment: Escape map and drill.
