## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Alliteration* (90 min): Read tongue twisters ('Peter Piper') and Mother Goose rhymes; underline repeated beginning sounds. Write your own alliterative sentence about your name.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Measure a collection* (60 min): Measure 15 pencils or crayons to the nearest inch and list the lengths.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Flower parts* (40 min): Dissect a flower (or use a diagram) to find petals and pollen; draw and label.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Diplomacy* (30 min): Learn that diplomats talk to solve problems between nations; role-play two 'countries' solving a water-sharing problem.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *2-D vs. 3-D* (40 min): Compare shapes (flat) and forms (sphere, cube, cylinder) using household objects; draw a circle and roll a clay sphere.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Scarf catching* (30 min): Toss and catch scarves high and low to practice tracking with your eyes.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Screen count* (20 min): Track screen time for 2 days and compare with active minutes.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Repeated lines* (90 min): Read Christina Rossetti's 'Who Has Seen the Wind?' and talk about how the repeated question gives the poem its rhythm and meaning.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Line plot* (60 min): Draw a number line scale and place an X above each length to make a line plot; title and label it.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Pollinator observation* (40 min): Watch flowers outside for 15 minutes and tally bees, butterflies, and birds.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *United Nations* (30 min): Learn about the United Nations, founded in San Francisco in 1945; look at a photo of the signing.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Pinch pot* (40 min): Make a pinch pot by pressing a thumb into a clay ball and pinching walls evenly; follow cleanup steps.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Thumbs and pinkies* (30 min): Catch tossed balls above the waist with thumbs together and below with pinkies together.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Swap list* (20 min): Make a list of 10 active swaps for screen time.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 88, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Two-syllable words* (90 min): Phonics: split two-syllable words with long vowels (re-peat, pa-per, rain-coat, ex-plain) and read 20 words. Clap syllables in poem lines.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Hand spans* (60 min): Measure hand spans of family members in centimeters and make a second line plot.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Cheese puff model* (40 min): Use fingers dusted with cheese puff powder to 'visit' paper flowers; observe how 'pollen' moves.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Conflict resolution* (30 min): Practice peaceful problem-solving steps with scenario cards.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Add texture* (40 min): Use forks and toothpicks to press textures into the pot and add a small clay animal handle.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Soft hands* (30 min): Practice 'giving' with your arms to pull the ball in; play egg-toss with a water balloon or bean bag.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Screen-free day* (20 min): Plan and enjoy a screen-free afternoon outdoors.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 89, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *List poem* (90 min): Write a list poem about 'Things I Hear at Night' using describing words and a repeated line. Illustrate it.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Interpret* (60 min): Answer questions about the line plots: most common length, longest, shortest, and difference between them.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Pollinator research* (40 min): Read about bees, hummingbirds, and bats as pollinators; record one fact each.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *When talking fails* (30 min): Discuss briefly that sometimes nations use military force and why peace is preferred.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Dry and paint* (40 min): Let the pot dry, then paint it with a pattern using colors from Unit 3.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Self-toss* (30 min): Toss and catch to yourself, adding a clap, then a spin, before the catch.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Goal check* (20 min): Check progress on last week's activity goal and update the log.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 90, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 18 mid-year check* (90 min): Mid-year assessment: phonics inventory (vowel teams, r-controlled, two-syllable words), fluency read, and a poem comprehension question about rhythm and repetition.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 5 test* (60 min): Make a line plot from given measurements and a bar graph from given data; answer 6 questions.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Explain why plants need pollinators and pollinators need plants.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Unit 4 check* (30 min): Quiz on trade, cultural contacts, treaties, and diplomacy.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Reflect* (40 min): Show the pot and describe its form and texture and one thing that was hard to make.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Catch 8 of 10 tosses at mixed heights and explain thumbs vs. pinkies.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain how balancing screens and movement helps your health.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Alliteration, repetition, and two-syllable words
- Objective: Identify alliteration and repeated lines
- Objective: Decode two-syllable words with long vowels
- Objective: Write a list poem
- Vocabulary: alliteration, repetition, syllable, line
- Materials: Where the Sidewalk Ends (Shel Silverstein) or a library poetry anthology, Mother Goose rhymes
- Assessment: Mid-year phonics inventory and fluency record; list poem.

### Math 2: Measurement data and line plots
- Objective: Generate measurement data
- Objective: Make a line plot with whole-number units
- Objective: Interpret line plots
- Vocabulary: line plot, measurement data, scale, X mark
- Materials: inch ruler, 15 pencils or crayons of different lengths
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Science 2: Pollination
- Objective: Describe how animals pollinate flowers
- Objective: Identify flower parts
- Objective: Model pollination
- Vocabulary: pollen, pollinator, nectar, flower
- Materials: cheese puffs or chalk dust, paper flowers, cotton swabs, flowers to observe
- Assessment: Pollination model and explanation.

### People Who Make a Difference: Diplomacy and solving conflicts
- Objective: Explain diplomacy
- Objective: Describe the United Nations
- Objective: Practice peaceful problem solving
- Vocabulary: diplomacy, United Nations, peace, conflict
- Materials: photo of United Nations headquarters, problem scenario cards
- Assessment: Role-play and unit quiz.

### Art & Music 2: Clay forms: pinch pots
- Objective: Create a three-dimensional form
- Objective: Use clay tools safely
- Objective: Add texture to clay
- Vocabulary: form, three-dimensional, pinch pot, sculpture
- Materials: air-dry clay, forks, toothpicks, placemat
- Assessment: Finished pinch pot and reflection.

### PE 2: Catching high and low
- Objective: Catch above the waist (thumbs together) and below (pinkies together)
- Vocabulary: thumbs together, pinkies together, give
- Materials: soft ball, beanbag, scarf
- Assessment: Catch count out of 10 and cue explanation.

### Health 2: Screens and balance
- Objective: Balance screen time and active time
- Vocabulary: screen time, balance, sedentary
- Materials: activity log, timer
- Assessment: Activity goal progress and explanation.
