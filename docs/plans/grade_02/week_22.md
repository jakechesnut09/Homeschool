## Day 1 — Monday (school day 106, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Mufaro's Beautiful Daughters* (90 min): Read Mufaro's Beautiful Daughters (Zimbabwe) and add it to a class comparison chart: main character, helper, test, ending. Phonics: suffixes -ful and -less (helpful, careless).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Story problems* (60 min): Solve 5 story problems within 1,000 (e.g., 'A library had 425 books and got 138 more…') with drawings.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Kelp forest* (40 min): Watch the Monterey Bay Aquarium kelp forest live cam or a kelp video for 10 minutes and list every kind of fish, plant, and animal you see.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Weather and crops* (30 min): Read short scenarios (a frost on orange trees, heavy rain at strawberry harvest, a heat wave) and tell how each changes how much food farmers can sell.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Meet the characters* (40 min): Listen to the theme for each character in 'Peter and the Wolf' (bird = flute, duck = oboe, cat = clarinet, wolf = French horns, Peter = strings).
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Toe taps* (30 min): Do toe taps on top of the ball and inside-foot passes between feet.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Senses stations* (20 min): Do touch, smell and taste mystery stations with a blindfold.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 107, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *The Rough-Face Girl* (90 min): Read The Rough-Face Girl (Algonquin) and add it to the chart; discuss what 'seeing' the Invisible Being means. Phonics: suffix -ly (kindly, quickly).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Estimate* (60 min): Estimate sums and differences using nearest hundred before solving 6 problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Pond sample* (40 min): Scoop pond or puddle water into a clear jar with an adult, let it settle, and look for tiny swimming organisms with a magnifier; draw 3 you find (or use pond-life photos).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Water for farms* (30 min): Look at photos of the California Aqueduct and Central Valley irrigation canals and trace on a map how Sierra snowmelt reaches farms.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Listen to the story* (40 min): Listen to the full piece and hold up a character card whenever that theme plays.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Walking dribble* (30 min): Dribble the ball with soft touches while walking, keeping it close.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Brain messages* (20 min): Learn that sense organs send messages to the brain.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 108, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Adelita* (90 min): Read Adelita (Mexico) and add it to the chart. Phonics: suffix -er (teacher, farmer, painter) and its meaning 'one who.'
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Explain it* (60 min): Write an explanation for how you solved 563 − 287, using place-value words.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Tide pool* (40 min): Read about California tide pools and sketch a tide pool with sea stars, anemones, crabs, and snails, labeling where each lives (rock, water, crack).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Water test* (30 min): Plant beans in two cups, giving one plenty of water and one very little, and record growth twice this week to see why farmers need water.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Why that instrument* (40 min): Explain why Prokofiev chose each instrument for its character (high, quick flute for the bird).
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Cone weave* (30 min): Dribble through a zigzag of 6 cones using inside and outside of foot.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Eyes & ears check* (20 min): Do a simple eye chart and whisper test; learn about vision and hearing checkups.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 109, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Which is best* (90 min): Discuss which Cinderella story is your favorite, giving reasons from the text, while practicing taking turns and building on each other's ideas. Write the opinion sentence and 2 reasons.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Game: Race to 1,000* (60 min): Roll dice to build 2-digit and 3-digit numbers and add until reaching 1,000 exactly; record equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Land vs. water* (40 min): Add the three water habitats to last week's chart and compare totals with the land habitats; note which habitat had the most kinds overall.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Drought choices* (30 min): Read about a California drought and discuss why some farmers left fields empty or planted crops that need less water.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Draw a scene* (40 min): Draw your favorite scene and label the instrument playing.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Red light/green light* (30 min): Dribble on green and stop the ball with the sole of your foot on red.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Protect senses* (20 min): Make a list of ways to protect each sense organ.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 110, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 22 review* (90 min): Complete the comparison chart for 5 versions and write which two are most alike. Spelling check of 12 suffix words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 6 test* (60 min): Add and subtract within 1,000 (14 items), mental 10/100 items, and one written explanation.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Tell a family member which habitat surprised you most and give two facts as evidence; answer 2 questions they ask.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Write 3 sentences explaining how weather, land, and water affect the food we can buy.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Match 7 themes to characters and instruments.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Dribble through the cone course without losing control and explain strong vs. light touches.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Match each sense to its organ and one protection tip.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Cinderella stories from Africa and North America
- Objective: Compare and contrast multiple versions across cultures
- Objective: Read words with suffixes -ful, -less, -ly, -er
- Objective: State an opinion with a reason
- Vocabulary: suffix, culture, opinion, reason
- Materials: Mufaro's Beautiful Daughters (John Steptoe), The Rough-Face Girl (Rafe Martin), Adelita (Tomie dePaola), comparison chart
- Assessment: Completed comparison chart, opinion sentence with reasons, suffix spelling check.

### Math 2: Problem solving within 1,000
- Objective: Solve word problems within 1,000
- Objective: Choose and explain strategies
- Objective: Show fluency with three-digit computation
- Vocabulary: strategy, explain, estimate
- Materials: word problem cards, base-ten blocks
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Science 2: Comparing water habitats: kelp forest, pond, tide pool
- Objective: Compare diversity in ocean and freshwater habitats
- Objective: Observe a water sample
- Objective: Compare water and land habitats
- Vocabulary: ocean, pond, tide pool, kelp forest, freshwater
- Materials: clear jar, pond or puddle water sample (optional), magnifier, Monterey Bay Aquarium kelp cam or ocean video
- Assessment: Updated habitat chart and oral explanation with evidence.

### People Who Make a Difference: Weather, land, and water resources
- Objective: Explain how weather affects farming
- Objective: Identify land and water resources farms need
- Objective: Describe how drought affects farmers
- Vocabulary: weather, drought, irrigation, aqueduct, resource
- Materials: California physical map, bean seeds and cups, photos of the California Aqueduct (Calisphere)
- Assessment: Written explanation linking weather, land, and water to food production.

### Art & Music 2: Peter and the Wolf
- Objective: Identify instruments that represent characters
- Objective: Describe how music tells a story
- Vocabulary: theme, character, narrator
- Materials: recording of Prokofiev's 'Peter and the Wolf', character cards
- Assessment: Theme-matching check and labeled drawing.

### PE 2: Foot dribbling
- Objective: Dribble a ball with soft touches using inside and outside of foot
- Vocabulary: soft touch, inside of foot, outside of foot
- Materials: soccer or playground ball, cones
- Assessment: Cone-weave dribble observation.

### Health 2: Senses and protecting them
- Objective: Describe how senses work and how to protect them
- Vocabulary: senses, nerve, brain
- Materials: mystery bags, blindfold, foods for tasting
- Assessment: Senses matching.
