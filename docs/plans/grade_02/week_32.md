## Day 1 — Monday (school day 156, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Formal vs. informal* (90 min): Compare how you talk to a friend vs. how you would talk to a museum guide; rewrite 5 informal sentences in formal language.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Cubes* (60 min): Examine dice, sugar cubes, and boxes; count faces, edges, and vertices of a cube.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Landforms* (40 min): Sort photos of mountains, hills, valleys, plains, islands, and peninsulas and write a one-line definition for each.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Cesar Chavez* (30 min): Read Harvesting Hope and discuss what farmworkers' lives were like and why Chavez organized them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Memory list* (40 min): List 5 favorite events from this year at home or in the community (a birthday, a festival, a hike, a holiday meal) and sketch two.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Rules matter* (30 min): Play tag with no rules, then with rules; discuss why rules make games fair and safe.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Harmful effects* (20 min): Learn how tobacco and vaping harm lungs, heart and teeth.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 157, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Letter parts* (90 min): Learn the 5 parts of a friendly letter (date, greeting, body, closing, signature) with commas in the greeting and closing; label a sample letter.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Shape hunt* (60 min): Hunt for 3-D shapes at home and record which have square faces.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Bodies of water* (40 min): Learn ocean, lake, river, pond, stream, and bay, and find a California example of each on a map (Pacific Ocean, Lake Tahoe, Sacramento River, San Francisco Bay).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *The march* (30 min): Trace the 1966 march from Delano to Sacramento on a California map (about 300 miles) and look at Calisphere photos of the marchers.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Plan the artwork* (40 min): Choose one event and plan an artwork using lines, shapes, colors, and textures from earlier units.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Win or lose* (30 min): Play capture the flag; practice saying 'good game' whether you win or lose.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Lung model* (20 min): Model clean and smoker lungs with sponges and discuss secondhand smoke.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 158, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Write a letter* (90 min): Write a letter to a relative explaining 3 things you learned about volcanoes, mountains, or earthquakes using science words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Partition rectangles* (60 min): Cover rectangles with square tiles and draw rows and columns; count the total.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build a model* (40 min): Build a clay landscape in a pan with a mountain, valley, island, and peninsula, then add water to form a lake, river, and ocean.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Dolores Huerta* (30 min): Read about Dolores Huerta and the motto 'Sí, se puede' (Yes, we can); discuss her role as a leader and teacher.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Create* (40 min): Create the artwork with mixed media.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Solve it* (30 min): Learn problem-solving steps (stop, talk, choose a fair fix like rock-paper-scissors or a redo) and role-play an 'out or not out' dispute.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Influences* (20 min): Discuss how friends, family and ads influence choices.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 159, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Mail it* (90 min): Edit the letter, address an envelope (capitalize city and state names), and mail it. Read a nonfiction passage independently and log it.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Draw grids* (60 min): Partition rectangles into 2 rows of 4, 3 rows of 3, etc. on paper and connect to repeated addition.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Label* (40 min): Label each feature with toothpick flags and photograph the model from above and from the side.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Connect to food* (30 min): Connect Chavez and Huerta to the farm-to-table unit: who picks the grapes and lettuce we buy?
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Second piece* (40 min): Create a quick second piece about a place in your community (park, library, market) using a different material.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Make a game* (30 min): Invent a game with 3 rules and teach it to the family.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Refusal skills* (20 min): Practice saying no, giving a reason and walking away.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 160, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Unit 8 assessment* (90 min): Unit test: cold read of two short science texts with questions comparing them and identifying the author's reasons; letter-format and comma check.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: cube attributes and 6 partition-and-count problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Explain the model to a family member, pointing to each landform and body of water and telling how they differ.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Write a short paragraph on how Chavez and Huerta made a difference for workers.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Exhibit theme* (40 min): Lay out all your artworks from the year and sort them into possible exhibit themes (Nature, Me and My Family, Around the World).
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Play your invented game and show good sportsmanship and fair problem solving.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a 'Smoke-Free Me' poster and role-play a refusal.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Friendly letters, formal and informal language
- Objective: Write a friendly letter with commas in greeting and closing
- Objective: Compare formal and informal English
- Objective: Use precise science vocabulary
- Vocabulary: greeting, closing, formal, informal
- Materials: stationery and envelope, stamp
- Assessment: Friendly letter (format rubric) and unit test on comparing texts.

### Math 2: Three-dimensional shapes and partitioning rectangles
- Objective: Identify cubes and count faces
- Objective: Partition rectangles into rows and columns of squares
- Objective: Count same-size squares
- Vocabulary: cube, face, edge, row, column
- Materials: boxes and dice, grid paper, square tiles
- Assessment: Cube and partitioning quiz.

### Science 2: Landforms and bodies of water
- Objective: Name and describe landforms and bodies of water
- Objective: Build a model of land and water
- Vocabulary: mountain, valley, plain, island, peninsula, lake, river
- Materials: play dough or clay, baking pan, water, toothpick flags
- Assessment: Labeled landform model and oral explanation.

### People Who Make a Difference: Cesar Chavez and Dolores Huerta
- Objective: Explain how Chavez and Huerta helped California farmworkers
- Objective: Analyze photographs from Calisphere
- Objective: Connect to food and farming unit
- Vocabulary: farmworker, union, march, nonviolence
- Materials: Harvesting Hope (Kathleen Krull), Calisphere photos of the 1966 Delano-to-Sacramento march, California map
- Assessment: Paragraph and map of the march.

### Art & Music 2: Art about my life: drawing home and community
- Objective: Create artwork about events in home and community life
- Objective: Choose materials on purpose
- Objective: Plan an exhibit theme
- Vocabulary: subject, event, memory, exhibit theme
- Materials: sketchbook, mixed media (pastels, watercolor, collage paper)
- Assessment: Two community-life artworks and exhibit theme sort.

### PE 2: Rules, fairness and sportsmanship
- Objective: Follow rules and accept results
- Objective: Solve conflicts fairly
- Vocabulary: sportsmanship, fair, rock-paper-scissors
- Materials: balls, cones, flags
- Assessment: Observation of sportsmanship and problem-solving steps.

### Health 2: Tobacco, vaping and alcohol
- Objective: Describe harmful effects
- Objective: Use refusal skills
- Vocabulary: tobacco, vape, secondhand smoke, refuse
- Materials: paper, sponges
- Assessment: Poster and refusal role-play.
