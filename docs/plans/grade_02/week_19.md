## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Five senses poem* (90 min): Go outside and list what you see, hear, smell, feel, and taste; turn the list into a 5-line senses poem. Read Stevenson's 'Rain' and 'Windy Nights' for models.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Add hundreds, tens, ones* (60 min): Add 324 + 153 with blocks and quick pictures by place; solve 8 problems without regrouping.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Plan* (40 min): Design a 'hand pollinator' tool that moves colored powder from flower to flower; sketch.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Goods and services* (30 min): Sort 12 picture cards into goods (bread, shoes) and services (haircut, doctor visit).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Look at Nevelson* (40 min): Look at a Louise Nevelson wall sculpture and describe how she turned scraps of wood into art painted all one color.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Target hands* (30 min): Throw overhand to a partner's 'target hands'; step back one big step after 3 good catches.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Skeleton* (20 min): Learn major bones (skull, ribs, spine) and their jobs; feel them on your body.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 92, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Compound sentences* (90 min): Grammar: join two short sentences with and, but, or, so and a comma ('The wind blew, and the leaves fell.'); combine 8 pairs. Phonics: doubling before -ing (run/running).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Compose a ten* (60 min): Add 247 + 136, trading 10 ones for a ten; record with quick pictures and partial sums.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build* (40 min): Build the hand pollinator from pipe cleaners, cotton balls, and craft sticks, shaping the tip to reach into a paper flower.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Producers* (30 min): Interview a family member about their job: do they produce goods or provide services?
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Brainstorm* (40 min): Brainstorm with family 5 ways to turn recyclables into a sculpture, sketching ideas.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Moving catch* (30 min): Shuffle sideways to catch a ball tossed to the side.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Paper skeleton* (20 min): Build a paper skeleton with brad joints and label 6 bones.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 93, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Endings -ed* (90 min): Phonics: read -ed words with three sounds (jumped, played, wanted) and sort them. Use 3 in compound sentences.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Compose a hundred* (60 min): Add 365 + 182, trading 10 tens for a hundred; explain the trade in words.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Test* (40 min): Test how well it picks up and drops powder on paper flowers; record results.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Consumers* (30 min): List things your family bought this week and identify each as a good or service.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Build* (40 min): Glue boxes, tubes, and caps into a shallow box to create a relief sculpture.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Count-up challenge* (30 min): With a partner, count consecutive throw-catch pairs and try to beat your record.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Muscles pull* (20 min): Feel the biceps and triceps when bending and straightening your arm.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 94, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Word choice* (90 min): Revise yesterday's senses poem by swapping 3 plain words for stronger words (said → whispered, big → enormous). Read it aloud to compare versions.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Partial sums* (60 min): Use the partial-sums method (300 + 100, 60 + 80, 5 + 2) for 8 problems and match each to block drawings.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Compare* (40 min): Compare two designs and decide which works better and why.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Interdependence* (30 min): Draw a web showing how a baker depends on farmers, customers, and truck drivers.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Paint* (40 min): Paint the whole sculpture one color (black, white, or gold) like Nevelson and notice how shadows show the forms.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Monkey in the middle* (30 min): Play a 3-person throw-and-catch game, practicing quick throws to an open partner.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Strong bones* (20 min): Learn how calcium and exercise build strong bones.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 95, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 19 review* (90 min): Spelling check of 12 -ed/-ing words, a compound-sentence quiz, and an oral reading of the revised poem.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Solve 10 three-digit addition problems and explain one regrouping step.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Unit 5 check* (40 min): Present the model and take a quiz on seed dispersal and pollination.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Write 3 sentences about how buyers and sellers need each other.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Reflect* (40 min): Discuss with family how painting it one color changed the look and what objects you repurposed.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Complete 10 throws and catches with a partner at 15 feet and set a record goal.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Label bones and explain how muscles and bones work together.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Sensory poems and compound sentences
- Objective: Use adjectives and adverbs in poems
- Objective: Form compound sentences with and, but, or, so
- Objective: Read words with -ed and -ing endings
- Vocabulary: senses, compound sentence, conjunction, ending
- Materials: poetry anthology, five-senses chart, rainstick or small bell
- Assessment: Compound-sentence quiz, spelling check, revised senses poem.

### Math 2: Adding within 1,000
- Objective: Add three-digit numbers with models and drawings
- Objective: Compose tens and hundreds
- Objective: Relate strategies to written methods
- Vocabulary: compose, hundreds, regroup, partial sums
- Materials: base-ten blocks, place-value mat
- Assessment: Addition within 1,000 quiz.

### Science 2: Build a pollinator model
- Objective: Develop a model that mimics a pollinator
- Objective: Compare designs
- Objective: Communicate
- Vocabulary: design, function, shape
- Materials: pipe cleaners, cotton balls, tape, craft sticks, powdered drink mix
- Assessment: Pollinator model presentation and unit quiz.

### People Who Make a Difference: Producers and consumers
- Objective: Define producers and consumers
- Objective: Identify goods and services
- Objective: Explain how producers and consumers depend on each other
- Vocabulary: producer, consumer, goods, services
- Materials: picture cards of jobs, play money
- Assessment: Goods/services sort and interdependence web.

### Art & Music 2: Repurposed sculpture: Louise Nevelson
- Objective: Look at assemblage sculpture
- Objective: Brainstorm ways to repurpose objects
- Objective: Build a sculpture from found objects
- Vocabulary: assemblage, repurpose, found object, monochrome
- Materials: image of a Louise Nevelson wall sculpture, clean recyclables, bottle caps, boxes, glue, one color of paint
- Assessment: Repurposed relief sculpture and discussion.

### PE 2: Partner throw and catch
- Objective: Throw accurately to a partner
- Objective: Catch after moving
- Vocabulary: accuracy, partner, target hands
- Materials: ball, cones
- Assessment: Partner record and observation of throw/catch cues.

### Health 2: Bones and muscles
- Objective: Describe the skeletal and muscular systems
- Vocabulary: skeleton, bone, muscle, joint
- Materials: paper bones template, brads, chicken bone (optional)
- Assessment: Labeled skeleton.
