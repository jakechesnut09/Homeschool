## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Eeyore's tail* (90 min): Read Chapter 4 (Eeyore loses a tail) and discuss how the beginning introduces the problem and the ending solves it.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Fact fluency* (60 min): Take the fluency quiz (40 facts within 20) and compare with the September baseline graph.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Water planet* (40 min): Spin a globe and stop it with a finger 20 times, tallying land vs. water; color oceans, ice caps, and large lakes on a world map.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Local heroes* (30 min): Brainstorm people who make a difference in your community (firefighters, librarians, volunteers, coaches, a family member).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Curate* (40 min): Choose 6 artworks for the exhibit under one theme and explain why each belongs.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Strength retest* (30 min): Retest push-ups, curl-ups and hang time and record them next to week-15 scores.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Three Rs* (20 min): Sort household items into reduce, reuse, recycle and trash.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 167, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Eeyore's birthday* (90 min): Read Chapter 6 (Eeyore has a birthday) and tell the gift story from Piglet's point of view and then from Eeyore's.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Within 100* (60 min): Solve 12 mixed problems within 100 and explain one strategy aloud.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Solid and liquid* (40 min): Observe ice melting in a cup, then list where water is solid on Earth (glaciers, snow, ice caps) and where it is liquid (oceans, rivers, lakes).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Interview* (30 min): Interview a community helper or volunteer in person or by phone using 5 prepared questions about why they do their work.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Mount and mat* (40 min): Compare framing options (mats, mounting on cardstock, hanging with clips) and mount each piece.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Flexibility retest* (30 min): Retest sit-and-reach and record.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Clean air & water* (20 min): Discuss how pollution affects health.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 168, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Irregular word review* (90 min): Review 30 irregularly spelled words (said, laugh, enough, friend, people, because) with a speed-read game and a spelling self-check.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Within 1,000* (60 min): Solve 10 mixed problems within 1,000 using quick pictures where needed.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Sierra snowpack* (40 min): Read about the Sierra Nevada snowpack melting in spring and filling rivers and reservoirs; draw the path of water from snow to faucet.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Fred Korematsu* (30 min): Read about Fred Korematsu of Oakland, honored on California's Fred Korematsu Day (Jan. 30), who stood up for fairness for Japanese Americans.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Labels* (40 min): Make museum-style labels with title, materials, and date.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Endurance retest* (30 min): Walk-jog continuously as long as possible up to 15 minutes; record minutes and heart rate.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Neighborhood cleanup* (20 min): Do a park or street cleanup with gloves and an adult.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 169, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Reader's theater* (90 min): Perform a scene from Eeyore's birthday with different voices for Pooh, Piglet, and Eeyore.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Two-step stories* (60 min): Solve 4 two-step word problems and write one of your own.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Fresh vs. salt* (40 min): Taste-test (with an adult) a cup of fresh water and a cup of salty water; sort water bodies into fresh and salt on a chart.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Be a hero* (30 min): Plan and do one helpful act for your community (pick up litter, donate books, make cards for a senior center).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Artist statement* (40 min): Write a 3-sentence artist statement about yourself and your favorite piece.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Graph progress* (30 min): Make a bar graph comparing fall and spring fitness scores.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Reuse craft* (20 min): Make something useful from recyclables.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 170, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 34 review* (90 min): Story-structure chart for one chapter and a written paragraph explaining how two characters felt differently about the same event.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Complete a number-skills self-check (place value, fact families, regrouping) and mark two topics to practice in next week's review stations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Unit 9 check* (40 min): Quiz: label landforms and water on a map, and identify where water is solid or liquid on Earth.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Write a thank-you card to your interviewee naming the trait you admire.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Plan the space* (40 min): Plan where each piece will hang in your home gallery and sketch a layout.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Set one summer fitness goal based on your graph.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain how a clean environment helps people stay healthy.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Winnie-the-Pooh: structure and points of view
- Objective: Describe how chapters begin and end
- Objective: Acknowledge different characters' points of view
- Objective: Review irregular high-frequency words
- Vocabulary: structure, point of view, conclusion, irregular word
- Materials: Winnie-the-Pooh, sticky notes
- Assessment: Story-structure chart and point-of-view paragraph; reader's theater performance.

### Math 2: Review: number and operations
- Objective: Review addition and subtraction within 100 and 1,000
- Objective: Solve two-step problems
- Objective: Show fact fluency
- Vocabulary: strategy, review
- Materials: fact cards, base-ten blocks
- Assessment: Fluency quiz and mixed problem review.

### Science 2: Where water is found: solid and liquid
- Objective: Identify where water is found on Earth
- Objective: Explain that water can be solid or liquid
- Objective: Distinguish fresh and salt water
- Vocabulary: glacier, snowpack, ice cap, fresh water, salt water
- Materials: globe or world map, ice cubes, salt, cups
- Assessment: Water-sources chart and unit quiz.

### People Who Make a Difference: Heroes in our community
- Objective: Identify people who make a difference locally
- Objective: Interview a community helper
- Objective: Recognize that ordinary people can be heroes
- Vocabulary: community, volunteer, helper, interview
- Materials: interview questions, thank-you card supplies
- Assessment: Interview notes, service act, and thank-you card.

### Art & Music 2: Preparing art for presentation
- Objective: Choose and prepare artwork for an exhibit
- Objective: Compare ways to mount and display art
- Objective: Write artist statements
- Vocabulary: mat, mount, label, artist statement, curate
- Materials: construction paper or cardstock for mats, tape, index cards
- Assessment: Mounted artworks with labels and artist statement.

### PE 2: Fitness retest
- Objective: Retest strength, flexibility and endurance and compare
- Vocabulary: retest, improve, goal
- Materials: fitness log, timer, ruler
- Assessment: Fitness retest and goal.

### Health 2: Healthy environment
- Objective: Reduce, reuse, recycle
- Objective: Keep air and water clean
- Vocabulary: recycle, pollution, environment
- Materials: recycling bins, trash bag, gloves
- Assessment: Cleanup reflection.
