## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *The Ant and the Grasshopper* (90 min): Read 'The Ant and the Grasshopper' and chart what each character does in summer and in winter. Phonics: ai/ay words (rain, train, play, day).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Counting across hundreds* (60 min): Count aloud from 87 to 132 and from 385 to 421, filling a blank chart section; notice what happens at 'hundreds.'
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Melting predictions* (40 min): Predict which wrapper (foil, cloth, newspaper, bubble wrap, none) will keep an ice cube from melting longest.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *What is a map* (30 min): Compare a photo of a room taken from above with a map of the room; discuss what a bird's-eye view is.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Ta and ti-ti* (40 min): Learn the quarter note (ta) and eighth-note pair (ti-ti) and clap 8 four-beat flash cards.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Run and reach* (30 min): Run 3 steps and leap over a rolled towel, reaching forward with the lead leg.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Glitter germs* (20 min): Put lotion and glitter on hands, shake hands with family and see how 'germs' spread.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *The Crow and the Pitcher* (90 min): Read 'The Crow and the Pitcher' and test the idea at home by dropping pebbles into a cup of water. Phonics: ee/ea words (tree, green, beach, read).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Skip by 5s and 10s* (60 min): Skip-count by 5s using nickels and by 10s using dimes; skip-count by 10s starting at 23 (23, 33, 43…).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Ice cube test* (40 min): Wrap identical ice cubes and check every 15 minutes; record observations and time to melt.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Map elements* (30 min): Find the title, legend (key), compass rose, scale, and date on three maps (zoo, park, city) and label them with sticky notes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Rest* (40 min): Learn the quarter rest (silent beat) and read patterns with rests; play 'rhythm echo' with a parent.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *River leap* (30 min): Draw a chalk 'river' that gets wider; leap across from the narrow end to the wide end.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Handwashing steps* (20 min): Learn the 5 handwashing steps (wet, lather, scrub 20 seconds, rinse, dry) and sing 'Happy Birthday' twice.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Irregular plurals* (90 min): Grammar: learn plurals that change (foot/feet, child/children, tooth/teeth, mouse/mice, fish/fish) and complete 10 fill-in sentences. Read a fable aloud using complete sentences to answer 'What is the lesson?'
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Skip by 100s* (60 min): Skip-count by 100s from 100 and from 250; use a number path to show 10 more, 10 less, 100 more, 100 less.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Design an ice keeper* (40 min): Design and build an 'ice cube keeper' from two materials and sketch it.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Directions* (30 min): Use a real compass to find north in your home and label N, S, E, W on the walls; play a directions game ('take 3 steps north').
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Word rhythms* (40 min): Match food words to rhythms (pie = ta, apple = ti-ti, watermelon = ti-ti ti-ti) and write 4 food-word rhythms.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Lead leg switch* (30 min): Practice leaping off the right foot and the left foot; note which is stronger.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Pepper experiment* (20 min): Sprinkle pepper on water and touch it with a soapy finger to see soap push 'germs' away.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Fable connection* (90 min): Write 3–4 sentences about a time in your life when one of this week's morals came true. Draw a picture to go with it.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Mental 10s and 100s* (60 min): Mental math: add or subtract 10 or 100 from numbers between 100 and 900 (e.g., 456 + 100, 702 − 10) — 20 problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Compare designs* (40 min): Test your keeper vs. the best single material; record data and compare strengths and weaknesses.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Map of my room* (30 min): Draw a map of your bedroom with a title, key, and compass rose.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Play rhythms* (40 min): Play written rhythms on rhythm sticks, pots, or a drum while keeping a steady beat.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Leap series* (30 min): Run-run-leap pattern down the yard to a drumbeat; land softly with bent knees.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Cover coughs* (20 min): Practice coughing and sneezing into an elbow and learn how vaccines help protect us.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 30, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 6 review* (90 min): Spelling check of 12 vowel-team words, an irregular-plurals quiz, and a fluency read of 'The Boy Who Cried Wolf' with an oral moral check.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Fill in missing numbers on 6 skip-count patterns and 10 '10 more/100 less' problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Write a notebook conclusion about which materials make good insulators and why.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Quiz: identify map elements on a new map and answer 4 direction questions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Read and clap 6 new four-beat rhythms accurately and write 2 rhythms that a parent claps.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Leap the widest river you can 3 times, record your best, and explain how a leap differs from a jump.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Demonstrate handwashing correctly and list 3 times you should wash hands.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Fables continued: vowel teams ai/ay and ee/ea
- Objective: Read words with vowel teams ai, ay, ee, ea
- Objective: Use irregular plural nouns
- Objective: Explain how a fable's lesson connects to life
- Vocabulary: vowel team, plural, irregular, character trait
- Materials: Aesop's Fables, vowel-team word cards, picture cards for plurals
- Assessment: Spelling check, 10-item irregular plural quiz, and written fable-to-life connection.

### Math 2: Counting and skip-counting to 1,000
- Objective: Count within 1,000 from any number
- Objective: Skip-count by 5s, 10s, and 100s
- Objective: Find patterns on a hundreds chart
- Vocabulary: skip-count, pattern, hundreds chart, sequence
- Materials: hundreds chart, blank 1–1,000 chart pages, nickels and dimes
- Assessment: Skip-counting patterns and mental 10/100 more or less check.

### Science 2: Hot and cold: insulators
- Objective: Test which materials keep things cold
- Objective: Compare results of two designs
- Objective: Use data to identify strengths and weaknesses
- Vocabulary: insulator, melt, temperature, compare
- Materials: ice cubes, small cups, foil, cloth, newspaper, bubble wrap, timer
- Assessment: Ice-keeper design test with data comparison.

### People Who Make a Difference: Map elements
- Objective: Identify title, legend, compass rose, scale, and date
- Objective: Use cardinal directions
- Objective: Read a simple map
- Vocabulary: map, title, legend, compass rose, scale
- Materials: compass, sample maps (park, zoo, city), sticky notes
- Assessment: Room map with all elements and map-elements quiz.

### Art & Music 2: Reading rhythm: ta, ti-ti, and rest
- Objective: Read quarter notes, eighth-note pairs, and quarter rests
- Objective: Clap and say rhythm syllables
- Objective: Write rhythms
- Vocabulary: quarter note, eighth notes, rest, ta, ti-ti
- Materials: rhythm flash cards, index cards, rhythm sticks
- Assessment: Rhythm reading and writing check.

### PE 2: Leaping
- Objective: Leap over a low obstacle with a run, take-off and soft landing
- Vocabulary: leap, take-off, landing
- Materials: rolled towel or jump rope, chalk
- Assessment: Leap cue checklist and recorded distance.

### Health 2: Germs and handwashing
- Objective: Explain how germs spread
- Objective: Wash hands correctly
- Vocabulary: germs, bacteria, virus, spread
- Materials: glitter, lotion, soap, timer
- Assessment: Handwashing demonstration checklist.
