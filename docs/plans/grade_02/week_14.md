## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Velveteen Rabbit part 1* (90 min): Read the first half of The Velveteen Rabbit aloud and discuss what the Skin Horse means by 'Real.' Phonics: oo words in two sounds (moon, book).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Three addends* (60 min): Add 23 + 15 + 17 by finding a friendly pair first (23 + 17 = 40); solve 8 problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Plan the water test* (40 min): Set up 2 watered plants and 2 unwatered plants in the same light; predict results.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Carrying out laws* (30 min): Learn how mayors, governors, the president, and police carry out laws.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Sol-mi* (40 min): Sing 'Rain, Rain, Go Away' and 'Star Light, Star Bright' with sol-mi hand signs.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Walk-jog intervals* (30 min): Alternate 1 minute jogging and 1 minute walking for 8 minutes; sip water after.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Label hunt* (20 min): Find the serving size and sugar on 3 cereal boxes.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 67, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Velveteen Rabbit part 2* (90 min): Finish the story and chart how the Rabbit changes from beginning to end. Phonics: au/aw words (sauce, draw, paw).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Four addends* (60 min): Add four two-digit numbers by adding tens, then ones (24 + 31 + 12 + 18); use blocks to check.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Measure light test* (40 min): Measure the light-test plants and graph height over time.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Courts* (30 min): Learn how judges and juries decide whether a law was broken; look at a photo of a courtroom.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Add la* (40 min): Add la with 'Ring Around the Rosie' and echo-sing 8 sol-mi-la patterns with hand signs.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Pacing game* (30 min): Jog laps at 'turtle' and 'cheetah' pace and decide which pace lasts longer.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Compare cereals* (20 min): Rank the cereals from least to most sugar per serving.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 68, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Past tense in writing* (90 min): Write 5 sentences retelling the Rabbit's adventures using irregular past-tense verbs (was, became, went, grew, saw). Read the sentences to check they sound right.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Order doesn't matter* (60 min): Show that 35 + 26 + 15 gives the same sum in any order and explain the property in your own words.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Observe water test* (40 min): Observe and record wilting, color, and height of the water-test plants.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Mock trial* (30 min): Role-play a mock trial of Goldilocks with judge, jury, and witnesses.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Penny staff* (40 min): Place pennies on a 3-line staff to show sol-mi-la patterns and sing them.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Neighborhood loop* (30 min): Walk-jog a neighborhood loop with a parent and record minutes moving without stopping.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Ad detectives* (20 min): Watch or look at food ads and identify tricks (cartoon characters, toys, bright colors).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 69, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Plan my story* (90 min): Plan an original fiction story with a toy or animal character on a story-mountain organizer: beginning, problem, events, solution, ending.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Shopping list* (60 min): Add prices of 3–4 items from a grocery flyer (rounded to whole cents under $1, e.g., 45¢ + 29¢ + 18¢).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Graph* (40 min): Make bar graphs comparing plants with and without light and water.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Consequences* (30 min): Discuss fair consequences for breaking rules and laws.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Improvise* (40 min): Improvise a 4-beat sol-mi-la answer to a sung question ('What did you eat today?').
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Fuel & water* (30 min): Choose a healthy pre-activity snack and drink water before, during and after a 10-minute jog.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Design an ad* (20 min): Design a cereal box or ad for a healthy food using honest claims.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 70, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 14 review* (90 min): Answer who/what/why questions about the Velveteen Rabbit in writing, spelling check of 12 oo/au/aw words, and share the story plan.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Solve 8 multi-addend problems and explain which numbers you grouped first and why.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Share the two graphs with a family member and explain which plants look healthiest and what surprised you.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Match roles (lawmaker, judge, police officer, president) to their jobs.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Sing 4 patterns from penny-staff notation and echo 4 patterns with hand signs.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Jog or walk-jog continuously for 10 minutes and record the time on your fitness log.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain how ads try to influence kids and how labels help you choose.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: The Velveteen Rabbit and planning a fiction story
- Objective: Track how a character changes across a longer story
- Objective: Read oo, au, aw words
- Objective: Plan a fictional narrative
- Vocabulary: real, character change, plan, plot
- Materials: The Velveteen Rabbit (Margery Williams, public domain), character change chart
- Assessment: Written comprehension answers, spelling check, completed story plan.

### Math 2: Adding several two-digit numbers
- Objective: Add three and four two-digit numbers
- Objective: Use properties to make friendly numbers
- Objective: Solve multi-addend problems
- Vocabulary: commutative, associative, friendly number, addend
- Materials: base-ten blocks, grocery flyer
- Assessment: Multi-addend quiz with reasoning.

### Science 2: Fair test: Do plants need water?
- Objective: Plan and conduct a fair test about water
- Objective: Continue light test
- Objective: Compare data from two tests
- Vocabulary: water, wilt, compare, conclusion
- Materials: 4 more seedlings, measuring cup, ruler
- Assessment: Two bar graphs of plant growth.

### People Who Make a Difference: Carrying out laws and courts
- Objective: Explain how laws are carried out
- Objective: Explain how courts decide if laws were broken
- Objective: Describe consequences
- Vocabulary: executive, police, judge, court, consequence
- Materials: photos of a courtroom, role-play props
- Assessment: Role-matching quiz and mock trial participation.

### Art & Music 2: Sol, mi, and la
- Objective: Sing and sign sol, mi, and la
- Objective: Read sol-mi-la patterns on a staff
- Objective: Improvise short melodies
- Vocabulary: solfège, sol, mi, la, staff
- Materials: Curwen hand-sign chart, 3-line staff paper, pennies as notes
- Assessment: Solfège singing and reading check.

### PE 2: Aerobic endurance
- Objective: Jog continuously for longer periods
- Objective: Pace yourself
- Vocabulary: endurance, pace, hydration
- Materials: water bottle, timer, route map
- Assessment: Fitness log of continuous minutes.

### Health 2: Food labels and advertising
- Objective: Compare foods with labels
- Objective: Recognize advertising tricks
- Vocabulary: label, serving size, advertisement, influence
- Materials: cereal boxes, food labels
- Assessment: Label comparison and ad design.
