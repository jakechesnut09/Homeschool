## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Meet Frog and Toad* (90 min): Read aloud 'Spring' from Frog and Toad Are Friends, then answer who/what/where questions orally and draw the setting. Start a reading log and practice the book-talk rule 'listen, then add on.'
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Number talk & ten-frames* (60 min): Use quick-image dot cards and ten-frames to name numbers 1–10 and the 'partners of 10' (7 and 3, 6 and 4); write all ten facts that make 10.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Science notebook* (40 min): Set up a science notebook with a cover, table of contents, and a page on 'What scientists do.' Draw and label a magnifying glass.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Yesterday, today, tomorrow* (30 min): Use a calendar to label yesterday, today, and tomorrow and write one thing you did yesterday; discuss what 'long ago' means (before you were born).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Line hunt* (40 min): Walk inside and outside to find 10 kinds of lines (fence, branches, stairs, waves on a rug) and sketch each in a new sketchbook.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Warm-up routine* (30 min): Learn a 5-move warm-up (jog in place, arm circles, jumping jacks, toe touches, high knees) to use all year; set up a cone boundary in the yard.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Feelings book* (20 min): Read 'In My Heart' by Jo Witek and list every feeling word in the book.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Short a and short i* (90 min): Phonics: blend and read short-a/short-i words (cap, cab, pin, fit), then sort 12 word cards into two columns. Reread 'Spring' to a parent for fluency and write 2 sentences telling what Frog wanted.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Count on* (60 min): Add 1, 2, or 3 by counting on from the larger addend (8 + 3: 9, 10, 11); play 'Roll and Count On' with two dice and record 15 equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Nature observation* (40 min): Collect 5 natural objects outside and observe each with a magnifier; record color, shape, texture, and size in a chart.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Then and now objects* (30 min): Compare pairs of objects (rotary phone vs. smartphone, washboard vs. washing machine photo) and sort 10 picture cards into 'long ago' and 'today.'
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Line sampler* (40 min): Fold paper into 12 boxes and fill each with a different line type using markers; label each line.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Signal game* (30 min): Walk, jog and skip in general space and freeze on the whistle; add 'down' (sit) and 'switch' (change direction) signals.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Feelings thermometer* (20 min): Make a feelings thermometer from calm to very upset and color where you are today.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *What makes a sentence* (90 min): Grammar: learn that a sentence tells a whole idea and starts with a capital and ends with a period, question mark, or exclamation point; fix 6 scrambled sentences. Read 'The Story' and answer 3 why/how questions.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Doubles* (60 min): Learn doubles 1+1 to 10+10 with pictures (spider legs 4+4, egg carton 6+6) and quiz with flash cards.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Asking questions* (40 min): Sort questions about the objects into 'can test' and 'can't test' and write 3 testable questions (Which rock is heaviest?).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Primary sources* (30 min): Look at a Library of Congress photo of children around 1910 and ask: Who? What? When? What clues tell us it is long ago? Record 3 clues.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Lines show feelings* (40 min): Listen to calm and energetic music and draw lines that match each; discuss which lines feel calm or excited. Practice art-tool cleanup routine.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Safety & sun* (30 min): Make a PE safety checklist: water bottle, sunscreen/hat, clear space, shoes tied; then play Simon Says with movement commands.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Same situation, different feelings* (20 min): Discuss how two people might feel differently about the same event (a thunderstorm, a new baby).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Character talk* (90 min): Partner talk: describe Toad using two words and evidence from the pictures in 'The Story'. Write 3 complete sentences about a friend you like to be with.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Subtraction within 10* (60 min): Act out take-away stories with counters and write subtraction equations; relate 9 − 4 to 4 + 5 = 9.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Measure and compare* (40 min): Use a ruler and kitchen scale to measure the objects and order them by size and weight.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *History detectives* (30 min): Learn the difference between a primary source (made at the time) and a secondary source (made later); sort 6 items (a diary, a textbook, a photo, a birth certificate).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Yarn lines* (40 min): Drop pieces of yarn on paper, trace the shapes they make, and turn them into an imaginative drawing.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Shadow tag* (30 min): Play shadow tag inside the cone boundary, practicing safe tagging (two-finger touch on shoulder).
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Healthy vs. unhealthy* (20 min): Sort cards of ways to show anger (yelling, hitting, talking, drawing, walking away) into healthy and unhealthy.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 1 review* (90 min): Spelling check of 10 short-a/short-i words, a one-minute timed read of a Frog and Toad page, and a 3-question oral comprehension check. Record books read in the reading log.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Fact check* (60 min): Take a 2-minute baseline fact quiz (40 facts within 10) and graph your score to track all year. Solve 4 short add-to/take-from story problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Share notebook observations aloud and explain the difference between an observation and an opinion.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Draw a 'long ago' and a 'today' picture of a child's day and label 3 differences.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Reflect* (40 min): Share the line sampler and say which line is your favorite using art words (zigzag, curved, thick, thin).
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Respond to 5 random signals without bumping cones and explain two safety rules and why warm-ups come first.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write 3 sentences: 'I feel ___ when ___. I can ___.'

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Reading routines with Frog and Toad
- Objective: Ask and answer who/what/where questions about a story
- Objective: Read short-a and short-i words
- Objective: Write complete sentences with capitals and end marks
- Vocabulary: character, setting, sentence, short vowel
- Materials: Frog and Toad Are Friends (Arnold Lobel), reading log, whiteboard, word cards
- Assessment: Spelling check (10 words), oral who/what/where answers, and correctly punctuated sentences in journal.

### Math 2: Facts within 10 and counting on
- Objective: Add and subtract within 10 fluently
- Objective: Count on to add
- Objective: Use doubles facts
- Vocabulary: sum, difference, addend, count on, doubles
- Materials: ten-frame, counters, dot cards, dice
- Assessment: Baseline fluency quiz and story problem check.

### Science 2: Scientists observe and ask questions
- Objective: Make observations using senses and tools
- Objective: Ask testable questions
- Objective: Set up a science notebook
- Vocabulary: observe, question, investigate, notebook
- Materials: composition notebook, magnifying glass, leaves, rocks, shells
- Assessment: Notebook observation chart and 3 testable questions.

### People Who Make a Difference: What is history? Long ago and yesterday
- Objective: Distinguish long ago, yesterday, today, and tomorrow
- Objective: Define history and primary source
- Objective: Sort objects from past and present
- Vocabulary: history, past, present, long ago, primary source
- Materials: old and new household objects, calendar, LOC photos of children around 1900
- Assessment: Then/now sort and labeled comparison drawing.

### Art & Music 2: Kinds of lines
- Objective: Identify and draw straight, curved, zigzag, wavy, spiral, and dotted lines
- Objective: Use lines to show movement and feeling
- Objective: Set up and clean up art tools safely
- Vocabulary: line, zigzag, spiral, wavy, contour
- Materials: sketchbook, pencils, markers, crayons, yarn
- Assessment: Line sampler with labeled line types and oral reflection.

### PE 2: Safe spaces and signals
- Objective: Move safely in general space
- Objective: Respond quickly to start/stop signals
- Vocabulary: general space, signal, warm-up
- Materials: cones or plastic cups, whistle or drum, water bottle
- Assessment: Observation: safe spacing and correct response to signals; oral safety rules.

### Health 2: Feelings and how we show them
- Objective: Name a wide range of feelings
- Objective: Describe healthy ways to express feelings
- Vocabulary: emotion, frustrated, proud, express
- Materials: feelings chart, 'In My Heart: A Book of Feelings' by Jo Witek (library)
- Assessment: Feeling sentences and sorting activity.
