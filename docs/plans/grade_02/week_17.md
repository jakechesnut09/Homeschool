## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *My Shadow* (90 min): Read Stevenson's 'My Shadow' aloud and tap the beat; highlight rhyming pairs at line ends. Discuss what the shadow does.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Survey* (60 min): Survey 10–20 family members, friends, or neighbors about their favorite fruit (4 choices) and record with tally marks.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Design challenge* (40 min): Design a model 'animal' that picks up and carries seeds like fur does; sketch two ideas.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Cherry trees* (30 min): Look at a Library of Congress photo of the 1912 planting of cherry trees given by Japan to Washington, D.C., and discuss why nations give gifts.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Texture walk* (40 min): Touch and describe 10 textures around home and yard (bark, carpet, brick, sponge) and record words for each.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *T-pose throw* (30 min): Learn cues: side to target, arm back in a 'T', step with opposite foot, throw and follow through.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *60 minutes* (20 min): Learn kids need 60 minutes of activity daily; brainstorm active things to do.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *The Swing* (90 min): Read 'The Swing' and act out the rhythm while swinging arms; discuss how the beat feels like swinging. Phonics: compound words (sunshine, rainbow, playground) — split and define.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Picture graph* (60 min): Make a picture graph of the survey with a title, labels, and key (each picture = 1 vote).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build* (40 min): Build the model 'animal' from a sock, fabric scraps, Velcro, or pipe-cleaner 'fur,' following your sketch.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Peace Arch* (30 min): Learn about the Peace Arch on the U.S.–Canada border and what it symbolizes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Rubbings* (40 min): Make crayon rubbings of 12 textured surfaces and label each.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Wall throws* (30 min): Throw tennis balls overhand at a taped square on a wall from 10 feet, 10 times.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Mood and movement* (20 min): Rate your mood before and after a 10-minute walk.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 83, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Compound word meaning* (90 min): Use the two smaller words to figure out meanings of 10 compound words (birdhouse, bookshelf, notebook). Write a rhyming couplet using one.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Bar graph* (60 min): Turn the same data into a bar graph with a single-unit scale and labeled axes.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Test* (40 min): Drag the model through a tray of seeds or rice and count seeds picked up and dropped.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Treaties* (30 min): Learn that a treaty is an agreement between nations; make a class 'treaty' with a sibling about sharing toys.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Texture collage* (40 min): Cut up rubbings and arrange them into an animal collage (bark for fur, mesh for scales).
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Distance throws* (30 min): Throw for distance in the yard and mark the best of 5 throws.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Activity log* (20 min): Start a week-long activity log of minutes moved.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 84, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Memorize* (90 min): Memorize the first stanza of 'My Shadow' and practice reciting with expression and clear volume. Read 'Rain' and 'Where Go the Boats?' for fun.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Graph questions* (60 min): Answer 'how many more/fewer' and 'how many in all' questions from 2 graphs and write 3 of your own.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Improve* (40 min): Change the model's shape or material and retest; record changes.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Cultural exchange* (30 min): Learn a song, food, or game from another country and share it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Implied texture* (40 min): Draw 4 objects (a pinecone, a sweater, a rock, a feather) using lines and dots to show texture.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Target game* (30 min): Knock down a pyramid of plastic cups with overhand throws; count throws needed.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Family challenge* (20 min): Plan a family active challenge (dance party, bike ride, hike).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 85, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 17 review* (90 min): Recite the stanza, find rhymes and beats in a new poem, and take a spelling check of 10 compound words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Read a new bar graph and answer 6 put-together, take-apart, and compare questions.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Explain how the model is like a real animal and how its shape helps it work.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Explain one way nations share culture and one way they make agreements.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Reflect* (40 min): Explain the difference between real and implied texture using your collage and drawings.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Throw overhand 5 times while a parent checks off the 4 cues.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Total your active minutes and set a goal for next week.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Poems with rhythm and rhyme
- Objective: Describe how rhyme and beat supply rhythm
- Objective: Read compound words
- Objective: Recite a poem with expression
- Vocabulary: poem, rhyme, rhythm, stanza, compound word
- Materials: A Child's Garden of Verses (Robert Louis Stevenson, public domain), drum or pot to tap beat
- Assessment: Poem recitation (memory, expression, volume), rhyme/beat identification on a new poem, spelling check.

### Math 2: Picture graphs and bar graphs
- Objective: Collect data with up to four categories
- Objective: Draw picture graphs and bar graphs
- Objective: Answer compare questions from graphs
- Vocabulary: data, survey, tally, picture graph, bar graph, key
- Materials: graph paper, tally sheet, stickers
- Assessment: Bar graph construction and graph-question quiz.

### Science 2: Modeling seed dispersal
- Objective: Develop a model that mimics an animal dispersing seeds
- Objective: Test and improve the model
- Vocabulary: model, mimic, fur, Velcro
- Materials: pom-poms, Velcro, fabric scraps, pipe cleaners
- Assessment: Model test data and explanation.

### People Who Make a Difference: Sharing cultures and making agreements
- Objective: Describe cultural contacts between nations
- Objective: Explain treaties and agreements
- Objective: Analyze a primary source
- Vocabulary: culture, treaty, agreement, gift
- Materials: LOC photo of 1912 cherry tree planting in Washington, D.C., Peace Arch photos
- Assessment: Primary source analysis and treaty.

### Art & Music 2: Texture: rubbings and implied texture
- Objective: Identify real and implied texture
- Objective: Make texture rubbings
- Objective: Draw textures
- Vocabulary: texture, rubbing, rough, smooth, implied texture
- Materials: crayons with paper removed, thin paper, leaves, coins, mesh, bark
- Assessment: Texture collage and implied-texture drawings.

### PE 2: Overhand throw
- Objective: Throw overhand with side to target and opposite step
- Vocabulary: overhand, side to target, follow through
- Materials: tennis balls or beanbags, wall target, tape
- Assessment: Overhand throw cue checklist.

### Health 2: Active every day
- Objective: Explain how activity helps body and mind
- Objective: Find ways to be active
- Vocabulary: physical activity, 60 minutes, energy
- Materials: activity log
- Assessment: Activity log and goal.
