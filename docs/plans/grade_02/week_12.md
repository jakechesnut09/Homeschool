## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Why did the author write it* (90 min): Compare the purpose of Frogs! (teach facts) to a Frog and Toad story (entertain); decide what question Monarch Butterfly answers. Finish report paragraph 2 (home).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Add tens and ones* (60 min): Add 34 + 25 with blocks: combine tens (5 tens), then ones (9 ones); solve 10 problems without regrouping.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Plant parts* (40 min): Dig up a weed and identify roots, stem, leaves, and flowers; draw and label it.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Rules at home* (30 min): List rules at home and explain why each one exists; discuss what would happen without them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Plan* (40 min): Choose a mood (calm, joyful, spooky, excited) and sketch a scene in the sketchbook, noting which colors will show that mood.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Plan it* (30 min): Draw a routine of start shape, balance, roll, balance, finish shape.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Eat the rainbow* (20 min): Make a color chart of fruits and vegetables and taste one new produce item.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Finish the draft* (90 min): Write paragraph 3 (food) and a concluding sentence. Add a labeled diagram and a glossary of 3 words to the report.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Hundreds chart jumps* (60 min): Add 46 + 23 on a hundreds chart by jumping down 2 rows (tens) and right 3 (ones); explain why it works.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Celery straw* (40 min): Put a celery stalk in colored water and observe how water travels up the stem over a day.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Laws in the community* (30 min): Go on a walk to find signs (stop sign, speed limit, no littering) and write the law each shows.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Paint the background* (40 min): Paint the background with your chosen palette, using tints and shades for depth.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Smooth transitions* (30 min): Practice moving from one part of the routine to the next without stopping.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Plant parts we eat* (20 min): Sort vegetables by plant part (root, stem, leaf, seed, fruit), connecting to the Science plant unit.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Revise and edit* (90 min): Read the report aloud and fix anything that does not make sense; check spelling of r-controlled and vowel-team words. Make a final copy on cardstock.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Open number line* (60 min): Add 38 + 27 on an open number line (38 + 20 = 58, + 2 = 60, + 5 = 65) and solve 8 more.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Seed in a bag* (40 min): Plant bean seeds in wet paper towels in a zip bag on a window to watch roots grow.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Rule or law* (30 min): Sort 10 examples into rules (home, school, game) and laws (community, state, country).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Paint the details* (40 min): Add the main subject and details with line and shape from Unit 1.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Add music* (30 min): Perform the routine to a short piece of calm music, holding each balance for 3 counts.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Water for life* (20 min): Learn why the body needs water; track water cups drunk today.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Present* (90 min): Present the animal report to family or a video call, speaking clearly and answering 2 questions from listeners. Listeners ask one question to gather more information.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Making a new ten* (60 min): Add 47 + 36 with blocks, trading 10 ones for 1 ten; draw quick pictures for 8 problems that need regrouping.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *What do plants need?* (40 min): List what you think plants need and write questions to test (Do plants need light? Water?).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Fair rules* (30 min): Make up a new game and write 3 fair rules; test whether they work.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Guess the mood* (40 min): Show the painting to family without telling the mood and have them guess; record their guesses and the clues they used.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Polish* (30 min): Video or have a parent watch; pick one part to improve and practice it 5 times.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Rethink your drink* (20 min): Compare sugar in juice, soda and milk by measuring teaspoons of sugar into cups.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 60, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Unit 3 assessment* (90 min): Cold read of a nonfiction passage with questions on text features, main topic, and author's purpose. Spelling test of 15 r-controlled and y words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Solve 12 two-digit addition problems using a chosen strategy and explain one in writing.

- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform the 5-part routine for the family and explain which part was hardest.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Write 3 reasons to eat fruits and vegetables and drink water.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Author's purpose and publishing an animal report
- Objective: Identify an author's main purpose
- Objective: Write and publish an informative report with a diagram
- Objective: Present facts and answer questions
- Vocabulary: author's purpose, explain, describe, conclusion
- Materials: animal research notes, cardstock, markers
- Assessment: Published 'All About' report (rubric: topic sentence, facts, diagram, conclusion) and unit reading test.

### Math 2: Adding within 100 using place value
- Objective: Add two-digit numbers by combining tens and ones
- Objective: Add using a number line
- Objective: Explain why strategies work
- Vocabulary: tens, ones, regroup, open number line
- Materials: base-ten blocks, open number line paper, hundreds chart
- Assessment: Addition quiz with written strategy explanation.

### Science 2: Parts of plants and plant needs
- Objective: Identify plant parts and their jobs
- Objective: Ask questions about what plants need
- Objective: Plan an investigation
- Vocabulary: root, stem, leaf, sunlight, investigation
- Materials: bean seeds, clear cups, soil, paper towels
- Assessment: Labeled plant drawing and observation log.

### People Who Make a Difference: Why we have rules and laws
- Objective: Explain the purpose of rules and laws
- Objective: Distinguish rules and laws
- Objective: Make fair rules
- Vocabulary: rule, law, fair, citizen
- Materials: home rules chart, photos of signs (stop, speed limit)
- Assessment: Rule/law sort and written explanation.

### Art & Music 2: Color project: a painting with a mood
- Objective: Plan an artwork to show a chosen mood
- Objective: Choose colors on purpose
- Objective: Reflect on artistic choices with others
- Vocabulary: mood, reflect, choice, background
- Materials: paints, watercolor paper, sketchbook
- Assessment: Mood painting, family guesses, and image-sorting check.

### PE 2: Balance-and-roll routine
- Objective: Link balances and rolls into a sequence
- Vocabulary: sequence, transition, finish
- Materials: mat, paper and crayons
- Assessment: Routine performance rubric (shapes held, safe roll, smooth transitions).

### Health 2: Fruits, vegetables and water
- Objective: Explain benefits of fruits, vegetables and water
- Vocabulary: vitamin, nutrient, hydrate
- Materials: fruits and vegetables, cups, water
- Assessment: Written reasons and rainbow chart.
