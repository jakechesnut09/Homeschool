## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *The Lion and the Mouse* (90 min): Listen to 'The Lion and the Mouse' from Aesop's Fables and retell it with 4 sequence cards. Discuss the moral: little friends can be great friends.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Bundling* (60 min): Bundle straws into tens with rubber bands, then 10 tens into a hundred; explain why 100 = 10 tens.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Strength test* (40 min): Hang a paper cup from strips of different materials and add pennies until each breaks; record the number.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *School long ago* (30 min): Compare a Library of Congress photo of a one-room schoolhouse with your learning space today; list 4 differences.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Heartbeat* (40 min): Feel your heartbeat and compare it to a steady beat; pat the beat to Sousa's 'The Stars and Stripes Forever.'
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Step-hop* (30 min): Break the skip into 'step-hop' slowly on one side, then the other, saying the cue out loud.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Read aloud* (20 min): Read 'The Family Book' by Todd Parr and list ways families are alike and different.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Magic e* (90 min): Phonics: change CVC to CVCe words (cap/cape, kit/kite, hop/hope, cub/cube) and read 20 long-vowel words. Write 3 sentences using CVCe words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Build numbers* (60 min): Build 10 three-digit numbers on a place-value mat with base-ten blocks (e.g., 346 = 3 hundreds, 4 tens, 6 ones) and draw quick pictures.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Flexibility test* (40 min): Bend strips of cardboard, plastic, wood craft sticks, and foil and rank them from most to least flexible.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Finish the book* (30 min): Add a cover, a page 'How my life is different from my grandparent's,' and a page with a favorite family tradition.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Beat chart* (40 min): Point to 8 hearts on a beat chart while chanting 'Engine, Engine Number Nine' and other rhymes.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Add arms* (30 min): Skip across the yard swinging opposite arm to leg; practice in front of a window or mirror.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Family portrait* (20 min): Draw your family and label how each person helps the family.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Tortoise and the Hare* (90 min): Read 'The Tortoise and the Hare' and compare how each character responds to the race. Write the moral in your own words: 'Slow and steady wins the race' means…
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Zeros matter* (60 min): Build and compare 305, 350, and 503; explain what the zero means in each.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Stretch test* (40 min): Measure how far rubber bands and fabric strips stretch with a ruler.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Practice presenting* (30 min): Practice telling your family story in 2 minutes using the book.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Beat vs. rhythm* (40 min): Pat the beat while clapping the words of 'Twinkle, Twinkle' and explain the difference between beat and rhythm.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Skip to music* (30 min): Skip to a steady song, changing direction each time the chorus starts.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Family jobs* (20 min): Make a chore chart and choose two jobs you will do this week.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Proper nouns* (90 min): Grammar: capitalize names of people, pets, and places; fix 8 sentences with missing capitals. Retell a fable to a family member using first, next, then, last.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Hundreds* (60 min): Count 100, 200, …, 900 with flats; show that 4 hundreds = 40 tens = 400 ones.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Best for the job* (40 min): Use data to pick the best material for a bag handle, a bridge deck, and a hair tie; explain with numbers.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Present* (30 min): Present the family history book to relatives (in person or by video).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Marches and waltzes* (40 min): March to music in 2 and sway to a waltz in 3 (Strauss's 'Blue Danube'); count 1-2 and 1-2-3.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Skip for height & distance* (30 min): Skip high (big lift) and then skip far (long steps); count how many skips it takes to cross the yard.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Family meeting* (20 min): Hold a short family meeting to share feelings and plan a fun activity together.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 25, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 5 review* (90 min): Spelling check of 10 CVCe words, match 4 fables to their morals, and read a new fable ('The Fox and the Grapes') independently and write its lesson in one sentence.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz: write the number shown by 8 block pictures and give the value of the underlined digit in 8 numbers.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Present test results in a chart and answer 'Why do engineers test materials?'
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Unit 1 check* (30 min): Quiz: sort sources as primary/secondary, order events on a timeline, and name 2 ways life has changed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Keep a steady beat on spoons with 3 songs while a parent checks; explain beat in one sentence.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Show a smooth skip for 20 meters and tell the 3 cues: step-hop, swing arms, land on toes.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Tell 3 ways your family supports you and 2 ways you support your family.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Aesop's fables: the lesson of the story
- Objective: Recount a fable in order
- Objective: Determine a fable's moral
- Objective: Read CVCe long-vowel words
- Vocabulary: fable, moral, lesson, silent e
- Materials: Aesop's Fables (Project Gutenberg or library), story sequence cards, index cards
- Assessment: Fable-moral matching, written moral for a new fable, and CVCe spelling check.

### Math 2: Hundreds, tens, and ones
- Objective: Understand 100 as ten tens
- Objective: Represent three-digit numbers with base-ten blocks
- Objective: Identify the value of each digit
- Vocabulary: hundred, ten, one, digit, value
- Materials: base-ten blocks (or paper cutouts), straws and rubber bands, place-value mat
- Assessment: Place-value quiz on block pictures and digit values.

### Science 2: Testing materials: strength and flexibility
- Objective: Test strength and flexibility of materials
- Objective: Analyze data to choose materials
- Objective: Explain choices with evidence
- Vocabulary: strength, flexibility, stretch, purpose
- Materials: strips of paper, foil, fabric, plastic bag, pennies, cup, rubber bands
- Assessment: Strength data chart and material-choice explanations.

### People Who Make a Difference: Family history book and unit check
- Objective: Complete the family history book
- Objective: Present family history to others
- Objective: Explain how sources tell us about the past
- Vocabulary: source, present, history
- Materials: family history book, LOC school photo from long ago
- Assessment: Family history book presentation and unit quiz.

### Art & Music 2: Steady beat
- Objective: Keep a steady beat with body and instruments
- Objective: Tell beat from rhythm
- Objective: Move to music in 2s and 3s
- Vocabulary: beat, steady, tempo, pulse
- Materials: recorded music (marches and waltzes), wooden spoons or rhythm sticks, heart-beat chart
- Assessment: Steady-beat performance check.

### PE 2: Skipping
- Objective: Skip with a step-hop and arm swing
- Vocabulary: skip, step-hop, opposition
- Materials: chalk, music
- Assessment: Skip checklist: step-hop pattern, opposition, rhythm.

### Health 2: Families are different and special
- Objective: Describe how families differ and support each other
- Vocabulary: family, support, responsibility
- Materials: family photos, 'The Family Book' by Todd Parr (library)
- Assessment: Family portrait and oral explanation.
