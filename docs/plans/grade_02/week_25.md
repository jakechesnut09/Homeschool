## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *What is a biography* (90 min): Read A Picture Book of Martin Luther King, Jr. and learn that a biography tells about a real person's life in order. List 5 important events.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Make a clock* (60 min): Make a paper-plate clock with hands and label minutes by 5s around the edge.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Shake and break* (40 min): Shake 5 sugar cubes in a jar for 2 minutes, then 2 more, and compare the corners each time to model how rocks bumping in rivers are worn down.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Choose a food* (30 min): Choose a favorite food (strawberries, tortillas, rice, oranges) and find where it is grown in California on a map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Papel picado* (40 min): Look at photos of papel picado banners used in Mexican celebrations like Día de los Muertos and weddings and discuss their purpose.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Balloon keep-up* (30 min): Keep a balloon up with flat hands for as many hits as possible.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Playground rules* (20 min): Visit a playground and identify safe and unsafe behaviors.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Timeline* (90 min): Put Dr. King's life events on a timeline strip and explain how one event led to the next (unfair laws → bus boycott → March on Washington).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Five-minute times* (60 min): Read and show 12 times to the nearest 5 minutes (3:25, 7:50); match analog to digital.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Rock rubbing* (40 min): Rub two rough rocks together over black paper for 2 minutes and look at the dust with a magnifier; discuss how long it would take to wear a rock away.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Research* (30 min): Use 2 sources to find out who grows, processes, transports, and sells it and what weather and water it needs.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Practice folds* (40 min): Practice folding and cutting small shapes from scrap paper to make symmetrical designs.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Body parts* (30 min): Strike the balloon with hand, elbow, knee and foot in a pattern.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Water safety* (20 min): Learn rules: swim with an adult, wear a life jacket, never swim alone.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Reflexive pronouns* (90 min): Grammar: learn myself, yourself, himself, herself, ourselves, themselves; complete 10 sentences. Phonics: two-syllable words ending in -le (table, little, simple).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *a.m. and p.m.* (60 min): Sort daily activities into a.m. or p.m. and make a schedule of your day with clock drawings.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Grand Canyon* (40 min): Read about how the Colorado River carved the Grand Canyon over millions of years and look at the rock layers in photos; write 2 facts.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Poster* (30 min): Make a farm-to-table poster with 5 steps, a map, and labels for each worker's job.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Make banners* (40 min): Fold and cut 4 tissue-paper flags with designs.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Partner volley* (30 min): Volley a balloon back and forth over a string 'net' with a partner.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Reach or throw* (20 min): Practice 'reach or throw, don't go' to help someone in water.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Paragraph focus* (90 min): Reread 2 pages and write what each paragraph is mostly about. Discuss why Dr. King believed in peaceful protest.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Quarter and half past* (60 min): Learn 'half past' and 'quarter past/to' language and tell the time on the house clock at 5 checkpoints today.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Ice cracks rock* (40 min): Fill a small plastic container to the top with water, cap it, freeze overnight, and observe how the ice bulged or cracked the container.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Present* (30 min): Present the poster to family, explaining one way the food is grown differently today than long ago.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Hang* (40 min): String the flags into a banner and hang it.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Beach ball* (30 min): Move to a beach ball and strike it upward with two hands (set) 10 times.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Sun & heat* (20 min): Review sun safety and signs of overheating.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 125, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 25 review* (90 min): Answer 5 who/what/when/why/how questions on a ReadWorks biography passage. Spelling check of 10 -le words and a reflexive-pronoun quiz.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 7 test* (60 min): Tell and write 10 times, choose a.m. or p.m., and solve 6 money problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Write 3 sentences explaining why weathering and erosion change land so slowly, using evidence from the sugar cube and ice tests.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Unit 5 check* (30 min): Quiz: goods vs. services, producers vs. consumers, farm-to-table steps, and a scarcity choice question.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Compare* (40 min): Compare papel picado to decorations your family uses for celebrations.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Keep a balloon up for 15 hits in your space and explain why a flat hand helps.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Sort scenario cards into safe and unsafe.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Biography basics: Martin Luther King Jr.
- Objective: Identify the main topic of a biography and paragraph focus
- Objective: Describe connections between events in a life
- Objective: Use reflexive pronouns
- Vocabulary: biography, timeline, event, reflexive pronoun
- Materials: A Picture Book of Martin Luther King, Jr. (David A. Adler), timeline strip
- Assessment: Timeline with cause/effect connections, comprehension questions, spelling and grammar quizzes.

### Math 2: Telling time to five minutes
- Objective: Tell time on analog and digital clocks to the nearest 5 minutes
- Objective: Use a.m. and p.m.
- Objective: Connect counting by 5s to the clock
- Vocabulary: hour hand, minute hand, a.m., p.m., quarter past
- Materials: paper-plate clock, analog clock
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Science 2: Slow Earth changes: weathering and erosion
- Objective: Describe Earth changes that happen slowly
- Objective: Model weathering
- Objective: Use evidence from sources
- Vocabulary: weathering, erosion, canyon, glacier, slow
- Materials: sugar cubes and jar with lid, two rough rocks, small plastic container with lid, Grand Canyon photos
- Assessment: Weathering observations and written explanation.

### People Who Make a Difference: Economics project: my food's journey
- Objective: Research one food's path from farm to table
- Objective: Explain the roles of producers and consumers
- Objective: Present findings
- Vocabulary: research, journey, producer, consumer
- Materials: poster paper, food packaging, Calisphere and library sources
- Assessment: Farm-to-table poster and unit quiz.

### Art & Music 2: Papel picado (Mexico)
- Objective: Learn the cultural use of papel picado
- Objective: Fold and cut paper designs
- Objective: Compare celebration decorations
- Vocabulary: papel picado, symmetry, celebration, tradition
- Materials: tissue paper, scissors, string, photos of papel picado
- Assessment: Papel picado banner and comparison.

### PE 2: Balloon and hand striking
- Objective: Strike a balloon upward with hands
- Objective: Keep it in personal space
- Vocabulary: strike, volley, flat hand
- Materials: balloons, beach ball
- Assessment: Keep-up count and observation.

### Health 2: Playground and water safety
- Objective: Follow playground, water and sun safety rules
- Vocabulary: lifeguard, life jacket, buddy
- Materials: life jacket, pictures
- Assessment: Scenario sort.
