## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *A Lost Button* (90 min): Read 'A Lost Button' and list on sticky notes each button Frog and Toad find; discuss how Toad feels as each wrong button is found. Phonics: read short-o words (hop, lot, pond).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Make a ten* (60 min): Solve 9 + 5 by moving one counter to make 10 + 4 on a double ten-frame; practice with 8 + and 9 + facts.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *What engineers do* (40 min): Look at everyday tools (can opener, clothespin, zipper) and discuss what problem each solves; record 3 in your notebook.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Order events* (30 min): Put 6 picture cards of a morning routine in order, then talk through the events of your own life using before and after.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Shape sort* (40 min): Sort cut-paper shapes into geometric (circles, squares, triangles) and organic (leaf, puddle, cloud) and find both kinds in your home.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Speed dial* (30 min): Move to a drumbeat: slow beats = slow walk, medium = jog, fast = quick feet; switch speeds with the beat.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Breathing tools* (20 min): Practice balloon breathing, five-finger breathing and counting to 10.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Problem and solution* (90 min): Fill in a problem/solution chart for 'A Lost Button' and tell how Toad responds at the end (he sews buttons on the jacket). Phonics: short-u and short-e word sort (bug, bed, cup, net).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Near doubles* (60 min): Use doubles to solve near doubles (6 + 7 = 6 + 6 + 1) and explain the strategy in words; sort 20 facts by best strategy.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Define the problem* (40 min): Problem: pencils keep rolling off the table. Ask questions and list what a solution must do.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Gather facts* (30 min): Ask a parent for your birth date and 6 important events (first steps, first tooth, starting school, a move, a sibling's birth) with years.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Look at Kandinsky* (40 min): Look at Kandinsky's 'Squares with Concentric Circles' and describe the shapes, colors, and patterns you see.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Direction drill* (30 min): Travel forward, backward and sideways between cones, looking over the shoulder when moving backward.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Calm-down jar* (20 min): Make a glitter calm-down jar and watch the glitter settle while breathing slowly.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Nouns are naming words* (90 min): Grammar: circle nouns (people, places, things) in 8 sentences from the story, then list 10 nouns you can see at home. Read 'A Swim' with expression.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Word problems* (60 min): Solve 6 put-together/take-apart problems (e.g., 'Ana has 8 red and 7 blue marbles…') with a drawing and an equation.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Sketch* (40 min): Sketch two designs for a pencil holder from paper and tape and label how the shape keeps pencils from rolling.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Build the timeline* (30 min): Make a timeline on adding-machine tape with one mark per year from birth to now and place each event with a drawing or photo.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Concentric circles* (40 min): Draw a 3x4 grid and fill each square with concentric circles in oil pastel, using different colors in each ring.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Zigzag & curve* (30 min): Draw straight, curved and zigzag pathways in chalk and travel each with three different locomotor skills.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Calm corner* (20 min): Set up a calm corner at home with a pillow, book and feelings chart.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 9, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Small moment ideas* (90 min): Writing: brainstorm 3 'small moments' from your own summer and pick one; sketch it in 3 boxes (beginning, middle, end). Tell the story aloud to a listener using details.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Fact games* (60 min): Play 'Make Ten Go Fish' and 'Doubles War' with cards; record 10 facts from each game.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build & test* (40 min): Build the best design and test it with 5 pencils; record results and one improvement.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Storyboard* (30 min): Make a 4-box storyboard of one year of your life and tell it aloud in order.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Finish and add* (40 min): Finish the Kandinsky-style grid and add a border of geometric shapes.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Traffic jam* (30 min): Play 'cars and traffic lights': green = fast, yellow = slow, red = stop, 'reverse' = backward, 'turn' = change direction.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Mindful movement* (20 min): Do a GoNoodle Flow or kids yoga video and notice how your body feels after.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 10, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 2 review* (90 min): Spelling check of 10 short-o/u/e words, retell 'A Lost Button' in order using first/next/last, and write one sentence about how Toad changed. Add titles to reading log.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Quiz on 30 addition facts within 20 and 3 word problems; explain one strategy aloud.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Explain the design steps (ask, imagine, plan, create, improve) using your pencil holder as the example.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Present the timeline and answer questions using 'first, next, before, after, last.'
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Reflect* (40 min): Tell which square you like best and why, using the words geometric, pattern, and contrast.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Follow a 6-part cue card (e.g., slow-zigzag-backward) and draw your favorite pathway.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Role-play a frustrating moment (losing a game) and use one calming strategy.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: How characters respond to problems
- Objective: Describe how a character responds to a major event
- Objective: Read short-o, short-u and short-e words
- Objective: Identify common nouns
- Vocabulary: problem, solution, respond, noun
- Materials: Frog and Toad Are Friends, sticky notes, drawing paper
- Assessment: Problem/solution chart, spelling check, and oral retell checked for correct sequence.

### Math 2: Make-a-ten and near doubles
- Objective: Use make-a-ten to add within 20
- Objective: Use near doubles
- Objective: Solve put-together word problems
- Vocabulary: make a ten, near doubles, equation, strategy
- Materials: double ten-frames, counters, fact cards
- Assessment: Fact quiz (30) and strategy explanation.

### Science 2: Engineers solve problems
- Objective: Define a simple problem
- Objective: Sketch a solution
- Objective: Build and test a model
- Vocabulary: engineer, problem, design, model, test
- Materials: paper, tape, paper clips, pennies, index cards
- Assessment: Labeled design sketch and test results.

### People Who Make a Difference: My timeline
- Objective: Place events of my life in order
- Objective: Use words like first, then, before, after
- Objective: Build a personal timeline
- Vocabulary: timeline, sequence, event, year
- Materials: baby photos, adding-machine tape, markers
- Assessment: Personal timeline with events in correct order.

### Art & Music 2: Geometric and organic shapes: Kandinsky
- Objective: Distinguish geometric and organic shapes
- Objective: Look at and describe Kandinsky's art
- Objective: Create a shape composition
- Vocabulary: shape, geometric, organic, abstract, composition
- Materials: image of Kandinsky's 'Squares with Concentric Circles', compass or cups for tracing, oil pastels
- Assessment: Kandinsky-inspired circles artwork and reflection.

### PE 2: Changing speed and direction
- Objective: Move fast, medium and slow on cue
- Objective: Change direction forward, backward and sideways
- Vocabulary: speed, direction, sideways, backward
- Materials: drum or pot and spoon, cones
- Assessment: Cue-card sequence performed accurately; pathway drawing.

### Health 2: Calming strategies
- Objective: Use strategies to manage strong feelings
- Vocabulary: calm down, deep breath, stress
- Materials: calm-down jar materials (jar, water, glitter), GoNoodle Flow videos
- Assessment: Role-play demonstration of a calming strategy.
