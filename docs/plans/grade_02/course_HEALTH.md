# Health 2 (Grade 2)

Health 2 helps children build healthy habits and safe choices: naming and managing feelings, friendship, standing up to bullying and appreciating families; germs, dental care, sleep and sun protection; MyPlate, breakfast, snacks, food labels, farm-to-table and food safety; daily activity and screen balance; body systems and growth; pedestrian, bike, water, fire, earthquake and personal safety; medicine, poison, tobacco and alcohol awareness; and community health helpers and a healthy environment.

*Framework:* 2008 California Health Education Content Standards; 2019 CA Health Education Framework  
*Time:* 20 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Feelings, Friends and Families (weeks 1–5)
Understanding feelings and treating others with kindness help everyone feel safe and included.

- Week 1: Feelings and how we show them
- Week 2: Calming strategies
- Week 3: Being a good friend
- Week 4: Standing up to bullying
- Week 5: Families are different and special

### Unit 2: Personal Health and Hygiene (weeks 6–10)
Daily habits like washing hands, caring for teeth and sleeping well protect our health.

- Week 6: Germs and handwashing
- Week 7: Healthy teeth
- Week 8: Sleep, rest and screen time
- Week 9: Protecting skin, eyes and ears
- Week 10: My healthy habits goal

### Unit 3: Nutrition: Fuel for Growing Bodies (weeks 11–16)
A variety of foods from every food group, plus water, fuels our bodies and brains.

- Week 11: MyPlate food groups
- Week 12: Fruits, vegetables and water
- Week 13: Breakfast and healthy snacks
- Week 14: Food labels and advertising
- Week 15: Farm to table
- Week 16: Food safety and meal planning

### Unit 4: Active Living (weeks 17–18)
Daily movement and less screen time help our bodies and minds.

- Week 17: Active every day
- Week 18: Screens and balance

### Unit 5: How My Body Works and Grows (weeks 19–23)
Body systems work together, and everyone grows in their own way.

- Week 19: Bones and muscles
- Week 20: Heart, lungs and blood
- Week 21: Digestion
- Week 22: Senses and protecting them
- Week 23: Growing and changing

### Unit 6: Injury Prevention and Safety (weeks 24–29)
Knowing safety rules and having a plan help us prevent injuries and act in emergencies.

- Week 24: Pedestrian, bike and car safety
- Week 25: Playground and water safety
- Week 26: Fire safety
- Week 27: Earthquakes and emergencies
- Week 28: Safe and unsafe touch and strangers
- Week 29: Online safety and weapons

### Unit 7: Medicines, Poisons, Tobacco and Alcohol (weeks 30–32)
Some substances help and some harm; we make safe choices and ask trusted adults.

- Week 30: Medicine safety
- Week 31: Poisons and household products
- Week 32: Tobacco, vaping and alcohol

### Unit 8: Healthy Communities (weeks 33–36)
Health helpers and caring citizens keep our community and environment healthy.

- Week 33: Community health helpers
- Week 34: Healthy environment
- Week 35: Health advocacy
- Week 36: My healthy year

## Standards

- **H-2.1.1.A** Explain the difference between medicines and other drugs and the rules for using medicines safely.
- **H-2.1.1.G** Describe the basic structure and functions of the skeletal, muscular, digestive, circulatory, and respiratory systems.
- **H-2.1.1.M** Identify a variety of feelings and describe healthy ways to express and cope with them.
- **H-2.1.1.N** Identify the five food groups on MyPlate and recommended daily amounts for children.
- **H-2.1.1.P** Explain how germs cause illness and how handwashing, covering coughs, and vaccines help prevent disease.
- **H-2.1.1.S** Identify safety rules for walking, biking, riding in vehicles, and using playgrounds.
- **H-2.1.2.A** Identify household products that are poisonous and how to read warning labels.
- **H-2.1.2.G** Explain that people grow and develop at different rates and that each person is unique.
- **H-2.1.2.M** Describe qualities of a good friend and ways to include others.
- **H-2.1.2.N** Describe the benefits of eating a variety of fruits and vegetables and drinking water.
- **H-2.1.2.P** Describe proper dental care, including brushing, flossing, limiting sugary foods, and the role of dentists.
- **H-2.1.2.S** Describe fire, earthquake, and emergency safety, including a family emergency plan and kit.
- **H-2.1.3.A** Describe the harmful effects of tobacco, vaping, secondhand smoke, and alcohol on the body.
- **H-2.1.3.G** Describe how the senses work and how to protect sense organs.
- **H-2.1.3.M** Explain how families and family members are different and how they support one another.
- **H-2.1.3.N** Explain the importance of eating breakfast and choosing healthy snacks low in added sugar, fat, and salt.
- **H-2.1.3.P** Explain why adequate sleep, rest, and limited screen time are important for health.
- **H-2.1.3.S** Explain water and sun safety rules.
- **H-2.1.4.G** Identify life stages (infant, child, teen, adult, older adult) and how needs change.
- **H-2.1.4.N** Identify where food comes from and how it gets from farms to the table.
- **H-2.1.4.P** Describe ways to protect skin, eyes, and ears (sun protection, eye safety, loud noise).
- **H-2.1.4.S** Distinguish between safe and unsafe touch and situations, including online and with strangers.
- **H-2.1.5.N** Explain how physical activity and limiting screen time benefit the body and mind.
- **H-2.1.5.S** Identify dangers of weapons and what to do if a weapon is found.
- **H-2.2.1.A** Identify how family, friends, and media influence decisions about alcohol, tobacco, and other drugs.
- **H-2.2.1.N** Explain how media and advertising influence food choices.
- **H-2.3.1.N** Use simple information on food labels (serving size, sugar) to compare foods.
- **H-2.3.1.P** Identify trusted adults and health professionals in the community who promote health.
- **H-2.4.1.A** Demonstrate refusal skills to avoid unsafe substances and situations.
- **H-2.4.1.M** Demonstrate listening skills and 'I-messages' to resolve conflicts and express feelings.
- **H-2.4.1.S** Demonstrate how to call 911, give accurate information, and ask a trusted adult for help.
- **H-2.4.2.M** Demonstrate how to respond to bullying, including telling a trusted adult and helping others.
- **H-2.5.1.N** Use a decision-making process to choose healthy meals and snacks.
- **H-2.6.1.N** Set a goal to increase daily physical activity or healthy food choices and monitor progress.
- **H-2.6.1.P** Set a personal health goal (e.g., brushing twice daily) and track progress.
- **H-2.7.1.M** Practice strategies to manage stress and strong emotions (deep breathing, counting, asking for help).
- **H-2.7.1.N** Practice food safety: handwashing, washing produce, and keeping foods at safe temperatures.
- **H-2.7.1.P** Demonstrate personal hygiene and dental-care practices.
- **H-2.7.1.S** Practice safety behaviors such as wearing a helmet, using a seat belt or booster seat, and following drills.
- **H-2.8.1.P** Encourage others to reduce, reuse, and recycle and to keep the environment healthy.
