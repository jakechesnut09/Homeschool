## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Text one* (90 min): Read How Mountains Are Made and list 4 important points about how mountains form. Use the headings to locate facts.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Tile arrays* (60 min): Use square tiles or crackers to build arrays and find totals; record 10 equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Define the problem* (40 min): A toy house sits on a sand hill near a 'river'; list the criteria (house stays put after 2 cups of water) and constraints (only 10 craft sticks, pebbles, clay).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Teammates* (30 min): Read Teammates by Peter Golenbock and discuss how Jackie Robinson and Pee Wee Reese showed courage in 1947.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Character bodies* (40 min): Read The Three Billy Goats Gruff and practice how each goat and the troll walk, stand, and speak (tiny trip-trap vs. great big stomp).
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Folk dance* (30 min): Learn a simple folk dance like the Chimes of Dunkirk or a children's line dance from a video.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Medicine vs drug* (20 min): Learn medicines help when used correctly and that drugs can harm.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 147, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Text two* (90 min): Read a second text about mountains and list its important points. Mark points that are the same in both texts with a star.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Garden plans* (60 min): Design a garden with 4 rows of 5 plants and other arrays; write equations and decide if totals are odd or even.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Sketch two designs* (40 min): Sketch two different solutions (e.g., a wall vs. a channel that moves water away) and label how each shape protects the house.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Unfair rules* (30 min): Learn what segregation meant and why it was unfair; discuss how Robinson stayed calm and kept playing.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Voices* (40 min): Practice reading the dialogue with a different voice for each character, changing volume and speed.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Freeze dance* (30 min): Play freeze dance, holding a balanced shape when the music stops.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Label reading* (20 min): Look at an empty medicine box label with a parent; find the dose and warnings.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 148, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Compare* (90 min): Complete a two-text chart and write 3 compound sentences comparing the texts ('Both texts say…, but only the second…').
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Same total* (60 min): Find all the arrays that make 12 (2 × 6 as 6+6, 3 × 4 as 4+4+4…) using repeated addition.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build & test design 1* (40 min): Build design 1, pour 2 cups of water, and measure how far the house moved and how much sand washed away.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Primary source letter* (30 min): Read parts of Jackie Robinson's 1958 letter to President Eisenhower (DocsTeach) and discuss what he asked the President to do.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *What if?* (40 min): Discuss choices characters could make (What if the troll were lonely? What if the goats shared the grass?) and choose one new plot idea.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Create a dance* (30 min): Create a 16-count dance using skips, slides, turns and a jump.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Only from trusted adults* (20 min): Discuss why only trusted adults give medicine.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 149, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Dictionary skills* (90 min): Alphabetize 10 science words to the second letter and use guide words to look them up in a children's dictionary; copy one definition for each.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Mixed problems* (60 min): Solve 6 word problems mixing arrays, equal groups, and odd/even.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build & test design 2* (40 min): Rebuild the hill, build design 2, and test it the same way; record results in the same table.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Play ball* (30 min): Play catch and learn that Jackie Robinson's number 42 is retired by every Major League team; design a 'Jackie Robinson Day' poster.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Act it out* (40 min): Act out the original story with family using a chair as the bridge, then act out the new version.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Practice your dance to music 3 times and add a beginning and ending pose.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Candy or medicine?* (20 min): Compare look-alike candies and pills and discuss why to never guess.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 150, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 30 review* (90 min): Compare two short passages on rocks in a chart, alphabetical order quiz, and a dictionary look-up check.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 9 test* (60 min): Odd/even and array test (12 items) with drawings and equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Compare the two designs in a T-chart of strengths and weaknesses and decide which to improve.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Write how Jackie Robinson made a difference for others, using one detail from the letter.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Share* (40 min): Share both versions informally and tell which choice changed the story most.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform your dance for family and explain which moves raised your heart rate.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain 3 rules for medicine safety.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Comparing two texts on one topic
- Objective: Compare the most important points of two texts on the same topic
- Objective: Use a dictionary and alphabetical order
- Objective: Write compound sentences
- Vocabulary: compare, source, dictionary, guide word
- Materials: How Mountains Are Made (Kathleen Weidner Zoehfeld), second mountain text from ReadWorks or library, children's dictionary
- Assessment: Two-text comparison chart and sentences; dictionary and ABC-order check.

### Math 2: Arrays and repeated addition problem solving
- Objective: Solve problems with arrays
- Objective: Connect arrays to partitioning rectangles
- Objective: Apply odd/even reasoning
- Vocabulary: array, total, equation
- Materials: grid paper, tiles or crackers
- Assessment: Unit 9 test.

### Science 2: Design challenge: protect the house on the hill
- Objective: Define a problem with criteria
- Objective: Design, build, and test two solutions
- Objective: Compare strengths and weaknesses
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraint, design, compare
- Materials: tray of sand, small toy house or block, craft sticks, foil, cloth, pebbles, clay, watering can
- Assessment: Design sketches and comparison T-chart with data.

### People Who Make a Difference: Jackie Robinson
- Objective: Explain how Jackie Robinson broke baseball's color barrier
- Objective: Analyze Jackie Robinson's letter to President Eisenhower
- Objective: Connect courage to fairness
- Vocabulary: color barrier, segregation, courage, civil rights
- Materials: Jackie Robinson biography (e.g., Teammates by Peter Golenbock), Jackie Robinson's 1958 letter to President Eisenhower (National Archives/DocsTeach), baseball and glove
- Assessment: Written response using evidence from the primary-source letter.

### Art & Music 2: Story drama: The Three Billy Goats Gruff
- Objective: Use voice and body to show characters
- Objective: Propose choices characters could make
- Objective: Collaborate in a guided drama
- Vocabulary: character, voice, gesture, plot, choice
- Materials: The Three Billy Goats Gruff (any version), simple costume pieces, a chair 'bridge'
- Assessment: Participation in guided drama and new-version performance.

### PE 2: Dance creation
- Objective: Learn a simple folk or line dance
- Objective: Create and perform a dance
- Vocabulary: dance, beat, folk dance
- Materials: music, open space
- Assessment: Dance performance rubric.

### Health 2: Medicine safety
- Objective: Explain safe medicine use
- Vocabulary: medicine, drug, dose, prescription
- Materials: empty medicine box, labels
- Assessment: Oral rules.
