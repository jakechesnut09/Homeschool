## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *End-of-year reading check* (90 min): Read an on-level passage for a fluency check and answer comprehension questions; compare with the fall and mid-year scores.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Year-end test part 1* (60 min): Number and operations: place value, add/subtract within 100 and 1,000, and word problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Compare bridges* (40 min): Make a T-chart comparing the first and improved bridges' strengths and weaknesses and explain which shape worked best.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Family history review* (30 min): Reread the family history book and add one new page about this year's events.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Set up* (40 min): Hang the exhibit and set up the performance space.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Plan stations* (30 min): Plan 6 field-day stations (sack race, beanbag toss, relay, long jump, three-legged race, water balloon toss).
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Look back* (20 min): Review your health portfolio from the year.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Phonics inventory* (90 min): Take the end-of-year phonics and spelling inventory (vowel teams, r-controlled, prefixes/suffixes, two-syllable words) and the reading of irregular words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Year-end test part 2* (60 min): Take part 2 of the year-end test: measure lengths, read a bar graph and line plot, count money, tell time, and identify shapes and equal shares.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Choose investigations* (40 min): Review the science notebook and choose 3 favorite investigations (e.g., plant needs, ice keeper, erosion design) to display.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Map from memory* (30 min): Label a blank map of North America from memory (countries, oceans, Great Lakes, rivers, mountains) and compare with the fall map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Showcase* (40 min): Give a gallery tour and perform the 3 music pieces for guests.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Make awards* (30 min): Make ribbons for effort, teamwork and sportsmanship.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Goal review* (20 min): Check progress on hygiene and activity goals.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Portfolio selection* (90 min): Choose 5 best pieces (narrative, fable, report, opinion, poem) and write a one-sentence reflection about each.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Math fair project* (60 min): Plan a 'Math in My Home' poster showing arrays, measurements, money, and a graph from your home.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build the display* (40 min): Make a science fair board with the question, test, data, and conclusion for each chosen investigation.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Government and economics* (30 min): Play a review game matching vocabulary (law, judge, producer, consumer, trade, treaty) to meanings.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Audience* (40 min): Practice good audience etiquette by watching a family member's performance or talent.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Field day* (30 min): Lead family or friends through all stations, with water breaks and sunscreen.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Summer plan* (20 min): Write a summer health plan with food, activity, sleep and safety goals.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Showcase* (90 min): Present the portfolio to family, reading one favorite piece aloud and explaining one way you grew as a writer.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Build the poster* (60 min): Finish the poster with labeled photos or drawings and practice explaining each math idea aloud in 3 minutes.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Present* (40 min): Present the science fair board to family or friends, demonstrating one investigation live.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Heroes reflection* (30 min): Write which hero inspired you most and how you could make a difference.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Reflect* (40 min): Compare a fall artwork with a spring artwork and describe your growth using art words.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Reflect* (30 min): Write or draw your favorite part of PE this year and how you got stronger.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Health fair* (20 min): Hold a family health fair showcasing projects.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Summer reading plan* (90 min): Make a summer reading list of 10 books (e.g., more Frog and Toad, Mercer Mayer, Magic Tree House) and set a reading goal. Celebrate the year.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Present & celebrate* (60 min): Present the math fair poster to family and review the fluency growth graph from the year.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Year-end check* (40 min): Take a year-end review quiz covering materials, heating and cooling, plant needs, pollination, habitats, and Earth changes.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Year-end check* (30 min): Take a year-end review quiz and share your favorite project from the year.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Celebrate* (40 min): Write a thank-you note to guests and choose one art and one music goal for summer.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Present your PE portfolio (fitness log, graphs, reflections) to family.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Year-end oral review of key health ideas.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Year-end showcase and assessment
- Objective: Show growth in reading, writing, and speaking
- Objective: Reflect on goals as a reader and writer
- Objective: Present a portfolio
- Vocabulary: portfolio, reflection, goal, growth
- Materials: writing portfolio from the year, summer reading list
- Assessment: End-of-year fluency and phonics inventory, portfolio presentation and reflections.

### Math 2: Year-end assessment and math fair
- Objective: Show mastery of grade 2 standards
- Objective: Explain mathematical thinking
- Objective: Celebrate growth
- Vocabulary: assessment, explain, project
- Materials: test booklet, poster board
- Assessment: Year-end test and math fair presentation.

### Science 2: Science fair and year review
- Objective: Compare designs with data
- Objective: Communicate a favorite investigation
- Objective: Review the year's big ideas
- Vocabulary: compare, present, display
- Materials: bridge data, science notebook, trifold or poster board
- Assessment: Science fair presentation and year-end quiz.

### People Who Make a Difference: Celebrating our stories: year review
- Objective: Review family history, maps, government, economics, and heroes
- Objective: Show map skills from memory
- Objective: Reflect on how people make a difference
- Vocabulary: review, reflect, history
- Materials: family history book, blank North America map, hero timeline
- Assessment: Map from memory and year-end quiz.

### Art & Music 2: Art and music showcase
- Objective: Present artwork in an exhibit
- Objective: Perform music with expression
- Objective: Reflect on growth using art and music vocabulary
- Vocabulary: exhibit, perform, reflect, growth
- Materials: home gallery, instruments, programs
- Assessment: Showcase performance, gallery tour, and written reflection.

### PE 2: Family field day
- Objective: Plan and lead a field day
- Objective: Show sportsmanship and enjoyment
- Vocabulary: field day, station, celebrate
- Materials: cones, sacks or pillowcases, water balloons, ribbons
- Assessment: Field-day leadership and PE portfolio.

### Health 2: My healthy year
- Objective: Reflect on health goals and plan for summer
- Vocabulary: reflect, goal, plan
- Materials: health portfolio
- Assessment: Portfolio and summer plan.
