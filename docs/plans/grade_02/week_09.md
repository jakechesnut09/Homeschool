## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Fiction or nonfiction* (90 min): Sort 10 library books into fiction and nonfiction and explain the clues. Take a text-feature tour of Frogs! and flag a heading, caption, bold word, and glossary.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Why units* (60 min): Measure a book with paper clips leaving gaps and overlaps, then correctly; discuss why units must be the same size with no gaps.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Freeze and melt* (40 min): Freeze water in ice trays and then melt the cubes; record that water becomes ice and back again.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Mountains* (30 min): Locate and label the Rocky Mountains, Appalachian Mountains, and Sierra Nevada on the map; build a clay model of the Rockies.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Primary colors* (40 min): Learn red, yellow, blue as primary colors and paint three blobs; practice brush washing routine.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Body part balances* (30 min): Balance on 5, 4, 3, 2, then 1 body part, holding each for 5 seconds.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Sun print* (20 min): Leave construction paper with objects on it in the sun to see how UV rays fade it; discuss skin.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Feature scavenger hunt* (90 min): Use the table of contents and index to answer 5 questions from Frogs! (e.g., 'What page tells what frogs eat?'). Phonics: ar words (car, farm, star).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Inches* (60 min): Measure 10 household objects (pencil, spoon, shoe) to the nearest inch with a ruler starting at 0; record in a table.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Chocolate change* (40 min): Melt chocolate chips in a bag in warm water, then cool them in the fridge; observe the shape change and reversal.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Rivers* (30 min): Trace and label the Mississippi, Missouri, Colorado, and Rio Grande rivers; discuss why rivers matter to people.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Mixing* (40 min): Mix orange, green, and purple on a paper plate palette and record each recipe.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Wide vs. narrow base* (30 min): Compare balancing with feet wide and feet together; explain which is easier and why.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Sun safety* (20 min): Learn 'Slip, Slop, Slap, Wrap' (shirt, sunscreen, hat, sunglasses) and read an SPF label.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Glossary words* (90 min): Look up 5 bold words in the glossary and draw a picture for each. Phonics: or words (corn, storm, horse) in a word sort.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Feet and yards* (60 min): Measure a table, a door, and a room with a yardstick or tape; choose the best tool for each and explain.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Crayon remake* (40 min): With an adult, melt broken crayon pieces in a muffin tin in the oven at low heat and cool them into new crayons.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Great Lakes* (30 min): Learn the five Great Lakes with the mnemonic HOMES and label them.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Color wheel* (40 min): Paint a 6-part color wheel on a paper plate.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Balance cards* (30 min): Draw 6 balance cards (stork, airplane, bridge, tripod, V-sit, frog) and perform them in a shuffled order.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Eye safety* (20 min): Discuss safety goggles, never looking at the sun and screen breaks (20-20-20 rule).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 44, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Nouns and verbs* (90 min): Grammar: sort words from Frogs! into nouns and verbs (frog, leap, pond, swim). Write 4 fact sentences, each with a noun and a verb.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Estimate first* (60 min): Estimate then measure 8 objects in inches or feet; find how close each estimate was.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Before and after* (40 min): Make a before/heated/cooled chart for the three investigations.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Memory practice* (30 min): Label the complete map from memory and check with an atlas.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Color hunt* (40 min): Find objects at home in each color of the wheel and photograph or draw them.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Partner balance* (30 min): Do partner balances (back-to-back sit-stand, hand-to-hand lean) safely with a family member.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Ear safety* (20 min): Compare quiet and loud sounds, and learn why loud music and noise can hurt hearing.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 45, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 9 review* (90 min): Use text features to answer 6 questions from a new ReadWorks animal passage. Spelling check of 10 ar/or words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Review & check* (60 min): Measure 6 pictured objects to the nearest inch and choose the right unit/tool for 6 situations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Explain which changes were reversible and give evidence from the chart.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Map quiz: label countries, oceans, Great Lakes, 2 rivers, and 2 mountain ranges from memory.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Check* (40 min): Name primary and secondary colors and explain how to mix each.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Hold 4 different balances for 5 seconds each and explain what a 'tight core' does.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a poster about protecting skin, eyes and ears.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Nonfiction text features
- Objective: Use headings, bold words, captions, and glossaries to find facts
- Objective: Read ar and or words
- Objective: Distinguish nouns from verbs
- Vocabulary: nonfiction, heading, caption, glossary, index
- Materials: National Geographic Readers: Frogs! (Elizabeth Carney) or any National Geographic Kids reader, sticky flags, magnifying glass
- Assessment: Text-feature scavenger hunt (6 questions on a new text) and spelling check.

### Math 2: Measuring in inches and feet
- Objective: Measure length with a ruler and yardstick
- Objective: Choose appropriate tools
- Objective: Understand units are equal with no gaps
- Vocabulary: inch, foot, yard, ruler, length
- Materials: 12-inch ruler, yardstick, measuring tape, paper clips
- Assessment: Measurement accuracy check and tool/unit choice quiz.

### Science 2: Reversible changes with heating and cooling
- Objective: Observe changes when materials are heated or cooled
- Objective: Identify changes that can be reversed
- Objective: Record before-and-after observations
- Vocabulary: heat, cool, melt, freeze, reversible
- Materials: ice cube trays, chocolate chips, crayons (old pieces), water
- Assessment: Before/after chart and reversible-change explanation.

### People Who Make a Difference: North America: rivers, lakes, and mountains
- Objective: Locate major rivers, the Great Lakes, and mountain ranges
- Objective: Complete a North America map from memory
- Vocabulary: river, Great Lakes, mountain range, landform
- Materials: blank North America map, atlas, clay
- Assessment: Complete North America map from memory.

### Art & Music 2: The color wheel
- Objective: Identify primary and secondary colors
- Objective: Mix secondary colors with paint
- Objective: Make a color wheel
- Vocabulary: primary, secondary, color wheel, mix
- Materials: tempera or watercolor paints (red, yellow, blue), brushes, paper plates
- Assessment: Painted color wheel and oral check.

### PE 2: Balances on different body parts
- Objective: Hold balances on 1–5 body parts
- Objective: Use a tight core for stability
- Vocabulary: balance, base of support, core
- Materials: mat or carpet, balance cards (drawn)
- Assessment: Balance hold checklist.

### Health 2: Protecting skin, eyes and ears
- Objective: Use sun protection
- Objective: Protect eyes and ears
- Vocabulary: sunscreen, SPF, UV rays, decibel
- Materials: sunscreen bottle, UV beads (optional), construction paper
- Assessment: Poster with three protection tips.
