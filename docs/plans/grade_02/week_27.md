## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Model biography* (90 min): Read Harvesting Hope and notice how the author shows events in order and why Chavez's march mattered to farmworkers. Identify the author's purpose.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Length stories* (60 min): Solve 6 length problems ('A ribbon is 45 cm. 18 cm is cut off. How long is it now?') with drawings and equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Notebook review* (40 min): Review notebook entries on fast and slow changes and highlight the strongest piece of evidence for each.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Sitting Bull* (30 min): Read a short biography of Sitting Bull, Lakota leader, and discuss how he worked to protect his people's land and way of life.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Kente cloth* (40 min): Look at images of kente cloth from Ghana and learn that weavers choose colors with meanings (gold for wealth, green for growth); describe the patterns.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Target strike* (30 min): Hit a foam ball off a cone/tee into a hula hoop zone with a paddle or bat.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Drop, cover, hold on* (20 min): Learn and practice the earthquake drill in several rooms.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 132, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Second source* (90 min): Take notes from a second source on your research person and add new facts to note cards (early life, challenge, actions, legacy).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Garden fence* (60 min): Measure the sides of a small rectangle (table or rug) and add to find the distance around.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Sand canyon model* (40 min): Tilt a pan of damp sand and let water trickle from a pinhole cup for 10 minutes; photograph or sketch the 'canyon' every 2 minutes.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Photo study* (30 min): Study a Library of Congress photograph of Sitting Bull and answer: What do you see? What do you wonder? What does it tell us about him?
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Paper weaving* (40 min): Cut slits in a paper 'loom' and weave contrasting paper strips over-under to make a kente-inspired pattern with colors chosen for meaning.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Rally count* (30 min): Rally a balloon with a partner over a string net, counting together.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Emergency kit* (20 min): Help build a family emergency kit.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 133, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Possessives* (90 min): Grammar: singular possessives with 's (Rosa's bus, Cesar's march) and capitals for holidays, places, and names (Labor Day, Delano, California). Fix 10 sentences.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Jump contest* (60 min): Measure standing long jumps in inches for family members and write compare problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Model vs. real* (40 min): Compare the sand canyon to Grand Canyon photos; list 2 ways the model is like the real canyon and 1 way it is different (time).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *George Washington Carver* (30 min): Read a biography of George Washington Carver and learn how he taught Southern farmers to rotate crops with peanuts to restore the soil.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Native California baskets* (40 min): Look at Calisphere photos of Pomo baskets, some woven so tightly they hold water, and describe their patterns and everyday and ceremonial uses.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Paddle golf* (30 min): Hit a ball along the ground with a paddle through cone 'holes.'
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Calling 911* (20 min): Role-play a 911 call stating name, address and the emergency.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 134, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Organize notes* (90 min): Sort note cards into 3 groups (early life, big work, why we remember them) and plan a paragraph for each.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Two-step lengths* (60 min): Solve 4 two-step length problems with number lines.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Poster* (40 min): Make a 'Fast vs. Slow' poster with drawings, time labels, and one piece of evidence from a source for each event.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Peanut products* (30 min): Look at a list of products Carver developed and taste peanuts and sweet potatoes (check allergies); look at the LOC photo of Carver in his lab.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Coil a basket* (40 min): Make a small coiled 'basket' by wrapping yarn around rope or a pipe cleaner and spiraling it into a dish shape.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Coach a partner* (30 min): Give a family member 2 positive tips while they practice striking.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Know your info* (20 min): Memorize your address and a parent's phone number.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 135, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Week 27 review* (90 min): Draft the first paragraph of the biography report. Spelling check of 12 words with apostrophes and capitalized place names.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 8 test* (60 min): Solve 8 length word problems using number lines and equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Unit 7 check* (40 min): Quiz: classify 8 events as fast or slow and explain one using evidence; present the poster.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Complete a hero-traits chart for Sitting Bull and Carver with evidence from the texts.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Compare* (40 min): Make a T-chart comparing kente cloth and Pomo baskets (materials, patterns, uses, who makes them) and share one similarity aloud.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Play a short rally game, record your best rally and reflect on your striking skill.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Do a surprise earthquake drill and recite your address and phone number.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Researching a person who made a difference
- Objective: Gather information from sources to answer questions
- Objective: Use possessive nouns with apostrophes
- Objective: Capitalize names, holidays, and places
- Vocabulary: source, fact, possessive, capital
- Materials: Harvesting Hope: The Story of Cesar Chavez (Kathleen Krull), research notes, index cards
- Assessment: Organized note cards, first report paragraph, capitalization/possessive check.

### Math 2: Length word problems
- Objective: Solve word problems involving lengths
- Objective: Use drawings and equations with a symbol for the unknown
- Objective: Measure to solve real problems
- Vocabulary: total length, difference, unknown
- Materials: rulers, string, yarn
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Science 2: Earth changes model and unit check
- Objective: Model a canyon forming
- Objective: Compare a model to the real world
- Objective: Show understanding of fast and slow changes
- Vocabulary: model, canyon, evidence
- Materials: sand or soil in a long pan, watering can or cup with pinhole, Grand Canyon photos, poster paper
- Assessment: Poster and unit quiz.

### People Who Make a Difference: Sitting Bull and George Washington Carver
- Objective: Explain how Sitting Bull led and protected his people
- Objective: Explain how George Washington Carver helped farmers
- Objective: Analyze historical photographs
- Vocabulary: leader, Lakota, scientist, crop rotation, inventor
- Materials: LOC photographs of Sitting Bull and of Carver at Tuskegee Institute, peanuts and sweet potatoes
- Assessment: Hero-traits chart with evidence for two people.

### Art & Music 2: Patterns in textiles and baskets: kente cloth and Native California baskets
- Objective: Identify patterns and meanings in textiles and baskets
- Objective: Weave a paper pattern
- Objective: Compare cultural uses of art
- Vocabulary: weaving, warp, weft, kente, basketry, pattern
- Materials: construction paper strips, images of Ghanaian kente cloth, Calisphere photos of Pomo baskets
- Assessment: Paper weaving, coiled basket, and comparison chart.

### PE 2: Striking games
- Objective: Strike toward a target with control
- Objective: Encourage your partner
- Vocabulary: aim, rally, encourage
- Materials: paddles, foam ball, cones, batting tee or cone
- Assessment: Best rally score and feedback observation.

### Health 2: Earthquakes and emergencies
- Objective: Prepare for earthquakes and emergencies
- Objective: Call 911
- Vocabulary: earthquake, drop cover hold on, emergency kit, 911
- Materials: backpack, flashlight, water bottles
- Assessment: Drill and memorization check.
