## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Ira's choice* (90 min): Read Ira Sleeps Over and discuss how Ira responds to his sister's teasing and how he decides about his teddy bear. Practice irregular words (could, would, should, through, though).
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Compensation* (60 min): Solve 39 + 26 as 40 + 25 and 72 − 29 as 72 − 30 + 1; explain why changing numbers works.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Seed collection* (40 min): Collect seeds outside or from fruits and sort them by how you think they travel (wind, water, animals, fur).
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Label hunt* (30 min): Look at labels on 10 household items to find where they were made; mark the countries on a world map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Song Maker* (40 min): Explore Chrome Music Lab Song Maker and make 3 short melodies that go up, go down, and repeat.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Stretch routine* (30 min): Learn 6 stretches (hamstring, quad, calf, shoulder, side, butterfly), holding each for 15 seconds.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Clean, separate, cook, chill* (20 min): Learn the 4 food safety steps and find examples in your kitchen.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 77, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Feedback* (90 min): Read your story draft to a family member who gives one 'star' (something good) and one 'wish' (something to improve). Revise based on the wish.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Strategy choice* (60 min): Sort 12 problems by best strategy (mental, number line, blocks, compensation) and solve them.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Sock walk* (40 min): Walk through a grassy area wearing an old sock over a shoe; observe and count seeds that stick.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Why trade* (30 min): Learn that nations trade to get things they cannot make or grow; discuss bananas from Central America and almonds exported from California.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Compose* (40 min): Compose an 8-beat melody using sol, mi, la, and do on Song Maker or a xylophone; write it on staff paper or with iconic notes.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Sit and reach* (30 min): Measure sit-and-reach with a ruler by a box and record the result.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Fridge check* (20 min): Check fridge temperature with a parent and see where foods are stored.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 78, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Edit* (90 min): Edit with a checklist: capitals, end marks, quotation marks for dialogue, and spelling of this unit's patterns. Fix past-tense verbs.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Target 100 game* (60 min): Make two 2-digit numbers from cards whose sum is closest to 100; play 8 rounds and record equations.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Wind test* (40 min): Use a fan to test which seeds travel farthest; measure distances.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Trade game* (30 min): Play a trading game where each player has different goods and must trade to make a complete set.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Add words* (40 min): Add words about a pet or favorite place to your melody.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Yoga for kids* (30 min): Follow a kid yoga video (tree, cat-cow, downward dog, cobra) and name which muscles stretch.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Plan a meal* (20 min): Plan a balanced family dinner using MyPlate.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 79, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Publish the book* (90 min): Publish the story as a stapled book with a cover, title page, and illustrations. Practice reading it aloud smoothly three times.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Error analysis* (60 min): Find and fix mistakes in 5 worked problems (e.g., forgot to regroup) and explain each error.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Animals eat fruit* (40 min): Read about birds eating berries and dropping seeds; draw a seed's journey from fruit to new plant.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *California exports* (30 min): Read about California products sent around the world (almonds, movies, technology).
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Feedback* (40 min): Play it for a family member, ask for one 'star' and one 'wish,' and revise one part.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Fitness circuit* (30 min): Do a 6-station circuit: jog, push-ups, curl-ups, jumping jacks, stretch, balance.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Cook together* (20 min): Help cook the planned meal, washing hands and produce first.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 80, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Unit 4 assessment* (90 min): Fluency check: one-minute read of an on-level passage (goal about 70–90 words correct) with expression. Unit test on a new story: character response, beginning/ending, and adjective/adverb use.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 4 test* (60 min): Add and subtract within 100 (16 items), add up to four two-digit numbers, and solve 3 two-step word problems.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Match 8 seeds to their dispersal method with reasons.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Write 3 sentences about why trade helps nations.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Unit 4 check* (40 min): Perform the final melody and explain why you chose its up and down shape.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain how cardio, strength and flexibility each help your body; share your fitness log.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Unit quiz on food groups, snacks, labels and food safety.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Ira Sleeps Over; revising and publishing fiction
- Objective: Revise and edit a narrative with peer and adult feedback
- Objective: Read irregular high-frequency words
- Objective: Read aloud with fluency
- Vocabulary: feedback, revise, edit, fluency
- Materials: Ira Sleeps Over (Bernard Waber), revision checklist, stapler and construction paper
- Assessment: Published fiction book (narrative rubric), fluency WCPM record, and unit test.

### Math 2: Fluency within 100 and unit review
- Objective: Choose efficient strategies for adding and subtracting within 100
- Objective: Explain strategies
- Objective: Show fluency
- Vocabulary: efficient, strategy, compensation
- Materials: strategy anchor chart, cards 0–9
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Science 2: Seeds on the move
- Objective: Observe different seed types
- Objective: Describe how seeds are dispersed
- Objective: Connect seed shape to dispersal
- Vocabulary: seed, disperse, burr, hitchhiker
- Materials: assorted seeds (maple, dandelion, burrs, acorns, fruit seeds), old sock, fan
- Assessment: Seed dispersal matching and journey drawing.

### People Who Make a Difference: Trade between nations
- Objective: Explain why nations trade
- Objective: Identify products from other countries
- Objective: Map trade partners
- Vocabulary: trade, import, export, goods
- Materials: labels from household products, world map, stickers
- Assessment: Trade map and written explanation.

### Art & Music 2: Compose a melody
- Objective: Create a short melody using sol, mi, la, and do
- Objective: Document the melody with notation or recording
- Objective: Revise using feedback
- Vocabulary: compose, notation, revise, phrase
- Materials: Chrome Music Lab Song Maker, staff paper, xylophone or piano app
- Assessment: Composed melody (notation or recording) and performance.

### PE 2: Flexibility and fitness review
- Objective: Stretch major muscle groups
- Objective: Explain why fitness matters
- Vocabulary: flexibility, stretch, hamstring
- Materials: mat, fitness log
- Assessment: Fitness log complete; oral explanation of 3 fitness parts.

### Health 2: Food safety and meal planning
- Objective: Practice food safety
- Objective: Plan balanced meals
- Vocabulary: food safety, cross-contamination, refrigerate
- Materials: kitchen, recipe card
- Assessment: Nutrition unit quiz.
