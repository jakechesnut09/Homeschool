## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Anansi the trickster* (90 min): Read Anansi and the Moss-Covered Rock (West African folktale) and explain how Little Bush Deer teaches Anansi a lesson. Note how the beginning introduces Anansi's trick and the ending wraps it up.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Compare with blocks* (60 min): Build two numbers (452 and 425) with blocks and compare hundreds first, then tens; record with > or <.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Build a house* (40 min): Build a small house from 20 blocks and draw it; count pieces of each type.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Continents* (30 min): Use a globe to find the 7 continents and 5 oceans; locate North America and the United States.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Measures* (40 min): Learn bar lines and measures in 4/4 and draw 4 measures with rhythms.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Locomotor dice* (30 min): Roll a die; each number is a skill (1 hop, 2 gallop, 3 slide, 4 skip, 5 leap, 6 jump) to travel to the next cone.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Why sleep* (20 min): Learn that kids need 9–12 hours of sleep and how sleep helps the brain and growth.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Plan a fable* (90 min): Writing: choose two animal characters with opposite traits and a lesson; plan beginning, problem, and ending on a fable organizer. Phonics review: vowel-team word hunt in the Anansi book.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Symbols* (60 min): Compare 15 pairs of numbers with >, =, < and explain one comparison in words ('362 > 326 because 6 tens is more than 2 tens').
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Take apart* (40 min): Take the house apart and build a new object (car or bridge) using the same 20 pieces; draw it.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Countries* (30 min): Label Canada, the United States, and Mexico on a blank map and color each; locate California.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Compose* (40 min): Compose a 4-measure rhythm piece using ta, ti-ti, and rest and write it on cards.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Pattern card* (30 min): Write a 4-skill pattern (skip-skip-leap-jump) and perform it 5 times without stopping.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Sleep log* (20 min): Start a 4-night sleep log noting bedtime and wake time.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 38, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Draft the fable* (90 min): Draft the original fable with dialogue and a moral as the last line. Read it to a listener and ask 'Was my lesson clear?'
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Order* (60 min): Order sets of 4 numbers from least to greatest; compare city populations or heights of landmarks in feet.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Same pieces, new shape* (40 min): Compare the two drawings and explain how the same pieces made different objects.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Oceans* (30 min): Label the Pacific, Atlantic, and Arctic Oceans and the Gulf of Mexico.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Digital rhythm* (40 min): Recreate your piece in Chrome Music Lab Rhythm and listen to it.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Relay* (30 min): Run a family relay where each leg uses a different locomotor skill; pass a beanbag baton.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Bedtime routine* (20 min): Draw a picture schedule for a calm bedtime routine (bath, teeth, book, lights out).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 39, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Edit and publish* (90 min): Edit with a checklist (capitals, end marks, spelling of vowel-team words) and publish with illustrations. Rehearse a reader's theater script of 'The Lion and the Mouse.'
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Greatest number game* (60 min): Draw 3 digit cards, make the greatest and least possible numbers, and compare with a partner — 10 rounds.
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Real world* (40 min): Look at pictures of a wooden fence turned into a table or bricks reused in a building; discuss.
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Map practice* (30 min): Label the map from memory, check against an atlas, and fix mistakes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Rehearse* (40 min): Practice your piece on a homemade shaker or pot drum, circling and slowly repeating the trickiest measure until it is steady.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Coach me* (30 min): Watch a partner skip and leap and tell them one thing they did well and one cue to try.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Screen limits* (20 min): Discuss how screens before bed affect sleep; make a family 'screens off' time.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 40, ~310 min)

- [ ] **ELA 2** — *Unit 2 assessment* (90 min): Perform the reader's theater and read your fable aloud. Unit test: read a new folktale, write its moral, sequence 4 events, and spell 12 long-vowel words.
- [ ] **Math 2** — *Unit 2 test* (60 min): Test on place value, counting patterns, number forms, and comparing three-digit numbers (20 items).
- [ ] **Science 2** — *Review* (40 min): Write an evidence-based account: 'I know an object made of pieces can become a new object because…'
- [ ] **People Who Make a Difference** — *Review* (30 min): Map quiz: label 3 countries and 3 oceans from memory.
- [ ] **Art & Music 2** — *Unit 2 check* (40 min): Perform your composition and explain why you chose your rhythms.
- [ ] **PE 2** — *Review & check* (30 min): Demonstrate all six locomotor skills in a row and name one cue for each.
- [ ] **Health 2** — *Review & check* (20 min): Total your sleep hours from the log and set a bedtime goal.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 2: Anansi tales and writing an original fable
- Objective: Determine the lesson of a trickster tale
- Objective: Plan, draft, and edit an original fable
- Objective: Perform reader's theater
- Vocabulary: trickster, beginning, ending, edit
- Materials: Anansi and the Moss-Covered Rock (Eric A. Kimmel), fable planning sheet, reader's theater script
- Assessment: Original fable (rubric: characters, problem, moral, conventions) and unit test on a cold-read folktale.

### Math 2: Comparing three-digit numbers
- Objective: Compare three-digit numbers using place value
- Objective: Use >, =, < symbols
- Objective: Order numbers
- Vocabulary: greater than, less than, equal to, compare, order
- Materials: base-ten blocks, number cards 0–9
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Science 2: Pieces make a whole
- Objective: Build objects from small pieces
- Objective: Disassemble and rebuild into a new object
- Objective: Describe evidence from observations
- Vocabulary: piece, assemble, disassemble, structure
- Materials: building blocks or LEGO, craft sticks, photos/drawings
- Assessment: Written evidence account with drawings.

### People Who Make a Difference: North America: countries and oceans
- Objective: Locate North American countries
- Objective: Name the oceans around North America
- Objective: Label a map of North America
- Vocabulary: continent, country, ocean, border
- Materials: blank North America map, globe, colored pencils
- Assessment: North America countries and oceans map quiz.

### Art & Music 2: Meter and rhythm composition
- Objective: Compose a rhythm piece in 4 beats
- Objective: Document rhythms with notation
- Objective: Perform for an audience
- Vocabulary: meter, measure, bar line, compose
- Materials: index cards, Chrome Music Lab Rhythm, percussion
- Assessment: Rhythm composition performance and notation.

### PE 2: Locomotor mix and relay
- Objective: Combine skip, leap, jump and gallop
- Objective: Give positive feedback
- Vocabulary: relay, combination
- Materials: cones, beanbag baton
- Assessment: Locomotor skill checklist (6 skills) and peer-feedback observation.

### Health 2: Sleep, rest and screen time
- Objective: Explain why sleep matters
- Objective: Plan a bedtime routine
- Vocabulary: sleep, routine, screen time
- Materials: sleep log, paper
- Assessment: Sleep log and bedtime goal.
