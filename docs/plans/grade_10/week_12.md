## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Act 4* (55 min): Read the quarrel scene (4.3) and analyze how Brutus and Cassius's friendship unravels.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Polygon angle sums* (60 min): Derive the interior angle sum (n – 2)·180° and find interior/exterior angles of regular polygons.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Drops on a penny* (60 min): Count drops of water, alcohol and oil that fit on a penny and relate to surface tension.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Opium War* (55 min): Read Commissioner Lin's letter to Queen Victoria and analyze its arguments.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Vocabulario* (50 min): Learn body, illness and symptom vocabulary with doler.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Set lyrics* (45 min): Sing or play your lyric over the chord progression, adjusting the melody so stressed syllables fall on strong beats.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Rules* (45 min): Learn pickleball rules and scoring.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Pedestrians & cyclists* (40 min): Learn the 3-foot passing rule for bicycles and how to share bike lanes when turning.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Variables* (50 min): Trace assignment statements by hand (a ← 3; b ← a + 2; a ← b) and predict final values.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Act 5* (55 min): Read Act 5 and record how each conspirator dies and what Antony says of Brutus.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Parallelogram properties* (60 min): Prove opposite sides of a parallelogram are congruent using a diagonal and ASA.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Evaporation race* (60 min): Time evaporation of water and alcohol on paper towel and measure cooling with a thermometer.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *China's responses* (55 min): Chart the Taiping, Self-Strengthening, Boxer Rebellion and 1911 Revolution.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *En el consultorio* (50 min): Role-play a doctor's visit describing symptoms.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Bridge* (45 min): Write a 4–8 bar bridge that contrasts the chorus by changing chords (e.g., starting on vi or IV) and melodic range.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Serve & return* (45 min): Practice the underhand serve and deep return (50 each).
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Motorcycles* (40 min): Learn about motorcycle visibility and lane splitting in California.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Expressions* (50 min): Write expressions with arithmetic and the MOD operator and test them in App Lab.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Medium comparison* (55 min): Compare the funeral scene in two film versions and write which interpretation is more persuasive.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Diagonals* (60 min): Prove diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Explain forces* (60 min): Rank liquids by intermolecular force strength using evidence.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Japan* (55 min): Compare Japan's Meiji modernization and its own imperial expansion (Korea, Taiwan).
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Farmacia* (50 min): Read pharmacy ads and instructions in Spanish.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Demo* (45 min): Record a rough demo in BandLab with a simple guitar/keyboard part and scratch vocal.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Dinks* (45 min): Practice cross-court dinking rallies.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Trucks & buses* (40 min): Learn about truck 'no-zones' and wide turns; sit in a parked car near a truck diagram to visualize blind spots.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Strings* (50 min): Build a 'Mad Libs' app that concatenates user input into a story.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Tragic hero* (55 min): Decide whether Caesar or Brutus is the tragic hero, completing an Aristotelian criteria chart.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Proving parallelograms* (60 min): Use the five tests to decide if a quadrilateral is a parallelogram.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Melting & boiling points* (60 min): Analyze a data table of boiling points to explain trends with molar mass and hydrogen bonding.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Latin America* (55 min): Study economic imperialism, the Panama Canal and the Roosevelt Corollary.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Diario* (50 min): Write a diary entry about a time you were sick (past tenses).
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Critique* (45 min): Play the demo for a listener and use the 'I like / I wonder / What if' protocol to collect feedback.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Volleys* (45 min): Practice punch volleys at the kitchen line.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Light rail & slow vehicles* (40 min): Learn light rail rules and slow-moving vehicle signs.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Counter app* (50 min): Build a clicker counter app that updates a label using a global variable.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Julius Caesar Acts 4–5: Consequences
- Objective: Trace the fall of the conspirators
- Objective: Determine theme
- Vocabulary: denouement, tragic hero, theme, nemesis
- Materials: Folger Julius Caesar, film clip (1953 or 2012 version)
- Assessment: Julius Caesar unit test.

### Geometry: Parallelograms
- Objective: Prove properties of parallelograms
- Objective: Prove a quadrilateral is a parallelogram
- Vocabulary: parallelogram, diagonal, consecutive angles
- Materials: GeoGebra, graph paper
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Intermolecular forces and properties
- Objective: Compare intermolecular forces
- Objective: Infer force strength from bulk properties
- Vocabulary: hydrogen bond, dipole-dipole, London dispersion, surface tension, evaporation
- Materials: water, rubbing alcohol, vegetable oil, pennies, eyedropper, paper towels, thermometer
- Assessment: Lab report (HS-PS1-3).

### Modern World History: China, Japan and Latin America; independence movements
- Objective: Analyze responses in China and Japan
- Objective: Describe early independence leaders
- Vocabulary: Opium War, unequal treaties, Boxer Rebellion, Sun Yat-sen, Monroe Doctrine
- Materials: Commissioner Lin's letter to Queen Victoria (1839), Sun Yat-sen's Three Principles excerpt
- Assessment: DBQ essay.

### Spanish III: El cuerpo y la salud
- Objective: Describe symptoms and illnesses
- Vocabulary: la fiebre, toser, estornudar, la receta, doler
- Materials: pharmacy ads in Spanish
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: Songwriting: putting it together
- Objective: Combine melody, harmony and lyrics
- Vocabulary: bridge, demo
- Materials: BandLab, microphone
- Assessment: Demo checkpoint.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Pickleball fundamentals
- Objective: Perform serves, dinks and volleys
- Vocabulary: dink, kitchen, third-shot drop, volley
- Materials: pickleball paddles, balls, court or driveway
- Assessment: Skill checklist.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Sharing the road
- Objective: Describe how to share the road with vulnerable users and large vehicles
- Vocabulary: vulnerable road user, 3-foot rule, no-zone, lane splitting, light rail
- Materials: handbook, bicycle
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Computer Science Principles: Variables, expressions and strings
- Objective: Use variables to store data
- Objective: Write expressions and string concatenation
- Vocabulary: variable, assignment, expression, string, concatenation
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Tracing quiz and Mad Libs app.
