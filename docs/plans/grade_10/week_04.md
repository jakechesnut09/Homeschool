## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Plan the story* (55 min): Brainstorm a 1,000–1,500 word suspense story and plan it on a plot mountain, choosing a narrator and one structural trick (flashback, twist, in medias res).
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Transformations as functions* (60 min): Compare transformations that preserve distance and angle with those that don't (stretch) on a grid.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Carbon accounting* (60 min): Calculate kg of CO₂ produced by burning 1 gallon of gasoline using atom counts.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Declaration* (55 min): Close-read the Declaration and trace phrases to Locke.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Pretérito regular* (50 min): Review regular preterite forms and write about last weekend in 8 sentences.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Expressive markings* (45 min): Learn dynamics, tempo and articulation markings.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Ten Essentials* (45 min): Pack the Ten Essentials and explain each item's purpose.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Shapes & colors* (40 min): Make flashcards for sign shapes and colors (octagon stop, triangle yield, pentagon school, round railroad, pennant no passing).
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Why compress?* (50 min): Calculate how long an uncompressed 4K video would take to download at 25 Mbps, then discuss why compression matters.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Draft opening* (55 min): Draft the opening scene starting in medias res, using at least three sensory details and one foreshadowing clue.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Translations* (60 min): Translate polygons using rules like (x, y) → (x + 3, y – 5) and vector notation.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Household audit* (60 min): Estimate your household's weekly combustion CO₂ from car miles and gas bills.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Unique character* (55 min): Discuss what made the American Revolution distinctive (federalism, written constitution).
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Irregulares* (50 min): Drill preterite irregulars (ser/ir, tener, estar, hacer, decir, poder, poner).
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Sight-reading* (45 min): Sight-read three 8-bar melodies on keyboard or voice.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Trail planning* (45 min): Use a trail map app to plan a local hike, calculating distance and elevation gain.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Regulatory signs* (40 min): Study regulatory signs and sort 20 sign images into must-do and must-not-do.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Lossless compression* (50 min): Compress a song lyric with the Code.org Text Compression widget and record your compression ratio.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Draft middle & end* (55 min): Complete the draft, writing a climax scene where sentences shorten to speed pacing.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Reflections* (60 min): Reflect figures over the axes, y = x and y = –x, and write coordinate rules.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Criteria & constraints* (60 min): Define criteria and constraints for a cleaner transportation fuel in a table.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Haiti* (55 min): Read about Toussaint L'Ouverture and the Haitian Revolution; analyze its 1801 constitution excerpt.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Cambios ortográficos* (50 min): Practice -car/-gar/-zar and i→y spelling changes.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Choose a piece* (45 min): Select a short piece (e.g., 'Ode to Joy' or a folk song) to perform.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Conditioning walk* (45 min): Do a 45-minute brisk walk with a loaded daypack, monitoring heart rate.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Warning & guide signs* (40 min): Study warning and guide signs and explain each to a family member.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Run-length encoding* (50 min): Write the run-length encoding for three pixel images and decode one back to the original grid.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Revise & edit* (55 min): Revise using a checklist (dialogue punctuation, varied sentence openers, show-don't-tell), then peer/parent-read aloud for confusing spots.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Reflection definition* (60 min): Use patty paper to show the line of reflection is the perpendicular bisector of segments joining corresponding points.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Revisit candle* (60 min): Revise your candle model with what you've learned and update the driving-question board.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Bolívar* (55 min): Analyze Bolívar's Jamaica Letter for Enlightenment ideas and Creole grievances.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Narración* (50 min): Write a 150-word narrative about a memorable trip using 15 preterite verbs.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Rehearse* (45 min): Use a practice log and slow-practice strategies on difficult measures.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Leave No Trace* (45 min): Learn the seven LNT principles and apply them to scenarios.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Sign hunt* (40 min): On a ride as a passenger, photograph or list 15 different signs and explain what a driver must do at each.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Lossy compression* (50 min): Save a photo as JPEG at quality 100, 50 and 10, compare file sizes and visible artifacts, and decide when lossy is acceptable.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Publish & unit test* (55 min): Type the final story in MLA format and take the Unit 1 test on literary terms and a cold-read short passage.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Check* (60 min): Quiz on translation and reflection rules.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Unit 1 test* (60 min): Unit 1 test on combustion, energy and measurement.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Comparison* (55 min): Complete a chart comparing the American, Haitian and Latin American revolutions.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Evaluación* (50 min): Quiz on preterite.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Perform* (45 min): Record a performance and self-evaluate with a rubric.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Hike* (45 min): Complete a 3–5 mile hike with family, logging time, distance and heart rate.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Quiz* (40 min): Sign identification quiz (40 signs, 90% goal).
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Check* (50 min): Write a short comparison of lossless vs. lossy compression for three scenarios (medical scan, streaming video, text document).

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Narrative writing workshop
- Objective: Write an original short story using suspense techniques
- Objective: Revise for pacing and dialogue punctuation
- Vocabulary: pacing, dialogue tag, in medias res, sensory detail
- Materials: writing notebook, mentor stories from Unit 1
- Assessment: Narrative rubric (W.9-10.3) and Unit 1 test.

### Geometry: Translations and reflections
- Objective: Define and perform translations and reflections
- Objective: Use function notation for transformations
- Vocabulary: transformation, pre-image, image, translation, reflection, rigid motion
- Materials: graph paper, patty paper, GeoGebra
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Combustion and the atmosphere
- Objective: Connect combustion to CO₂ emissions
- Objective: Define engineering criteria for cleaner fuels
- Vocabulary: emission, carbon footprint, criteria, constraint
- Materials: CA Air Resources Board data, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Modern World History: The American Revolution and Latin American independence
- Objective: Analyze the Declaration of Independence
- Objective: Explain spread to Latin America
- Vocabulary: unalienable rights, Creole, Haitian Revolution, Simón Bolívar
- Materials: Declaration of Independence (National Archives), Bolívar's Jamaica Letter excerpt
- Assessment: Comparison chart.

### Spanish III: El pretérito
- Objective: Narrate completed past actions
- Vocabulary: ayer, anoche, de repente, el verano pasado
- Materials: Conjuguemos, SpanishDict preterite guide
- Assessment: Quiz and narrative.

### Music & Media Arts: Sight-reading and performance
- Objective: Sight-read simple melodies
- Objective: Perform with expression
- Vocabulary: dynamics, tempo, articulation, phrase
- Materials: keyboard, voice, ukulele or recorder, sight-reading practice sheets
- Assessment: Performance rubric.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Hiking fundamentals and Leave No Trace
- Objective: Plan a safe hike
- Objective: Apply Leave No Trace
- Vocabulary: Ten Essentials, Leave No Trace, elevation gain, pace
- Materials: daypack, water, trail map
- Assessment: Hike log and plan.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Traffic signs
- Objective: Identify signs by shape, color and meaning
- Vocabulary: regulatory sign, warning sign, guide sign, octagon, pennant
- Materials: handbook sign pages, index cards
- Assessment: Sign quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Compression: lossless and lossy
- Objective: Compare lossless and lossy compression
- Objective: Evaluate tradeoffs of file size and quality
- Vocabulary: compression, lossless, lossy, run-length encoding, JPEG
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Compression comparison scored with rubric.
