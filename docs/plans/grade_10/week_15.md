## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Ch. 9–10* (55 min): Read chapters 9–10 on Ezinma and the egwugwu trial; note how justice works in Umuofia.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Dilation by construction* (60 min): Dilate a triangle from a center with scale factors 2 and 1/2 using a ruler.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Food coloring diffusion* (60 min): Drop food coloring into hot and cold water and record diffusion times.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Context* (55 min): Read about Armenians in the Ottoman Empire and the 1915 deportations.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Vocabulario* (50 min): Learn environment vocabulary and label infographics.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Spirituals & blues* (45 min): Listen to spirituals and Robert Johnson; identify call and response.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Skill circuit* (45 min): Warm up with a skill circuit: 20 serves, 20 dinks/drops, 20 volleys.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Effects* (40 min): Learn how alcohol, cannabis and medications affect judgment, vision and reaction time.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Why lists?* (50 min): Rewrite a program with five separate variables using one list and explain the benefit.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Things Fall Apart Part 1 (cont.): Tradition and change
- Objective: Analyze how characters develop through conflict
- Vocabulary: exile, ogbanje, ritual, tragic flaw
- Materials: Things Fall Apart
- Assessment: Analysis paragraph and vocabulary check.

### Geometry: Dilations
- Objective: Verify properties of dilations
- Objective: Perform dilations on the plane
- Vocabulary: dilation, scale factor, center of dilation, similar
- Materials: ruler, graph paper, GeoGebra
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Thermal energy and particle motion
- Objective: Model temperature as particle kinetic energy
- Objective: Distinguish heat and temperature
- Vocabulary: kinetic energy, temperature, heat, thermal equilibrium
- Materials: food coloring, hot and cold water, PhET States of Matter
- Assessment: Heating curve graph and quiz.

### Modern World History: The Armenian Genocide and human costs
- Objective: Analyze the Armenian Genocide
- Objective: Assess human costs of WWI
- Vocabulary: genocide, deportation, Armenian Genocide, casualties
- Materials: Ambassador Henry Morgenthau's reports, casualty statistics
- Assessment: Reflection and source analysis.

### Spanish III: El medio ambiente: vocabulario y problemas
- Objective: Discuss environmental problems
- Vocabulary: la contaminación, el reciclaje, la deforestación, la sequía, los recursos naturales
- Materials: infographics in Spanish
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: American music: spirituals, blues, jazz and rock
- Objective: Trace African American roots of popular music
- Vocabulary: spiritual, blues, swing, improvisation, rock and roll
- Materials: Library of Congress audio, Smithsonian jazz resources
- Assessment: Written response.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Net sports tournament
- Objective: Demonstrate skills in competition with sportsmanship
- Vocabulary: round robin, etiquette
- Materials: courts
- Assessment: Skill test.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Alcohol, drugs and driving
- Objective: Explain effects of alcohol and drugs on driving
- Objective: Know DUI laws
- Vocabulary: BAC, implied consent, zero tolerance, DUI
- Materials: handbook, impairment goggles video
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Lists
- Objective: Create and access lists
- Objective: Use lists to generalize solutions
- Vocabulary: list, element, index, append, length
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: List quiz and app.
