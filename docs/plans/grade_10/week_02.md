## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Poe* (55 min): Read 'The Cask of Amontillado' aloud and highlight every instance of verbal irony in Montresor's speech to Fortunato.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Copy segment & angle* (60 min): Construct a copy of a segment and an angle with compass and straightedge, writing the steps.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Candle under a jar* (60 min): Burn a tea light under jars of different sizes and time how long each burns; graph volume vs. time.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Judaism & law* (55 min): Read excerpts on the covenant and Ten Commandments and note ideas of law above rulers.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Lectura* (50 min): Read 'Mi nombre' and identify five details the narrator gives about her name and heritage.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Note values* (45 min): Learn note and rest values and clap a rhythm ladder.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Heart rate* (45 min): Measure resting heart rate three mornings and calculate max and target zones (Karvonen).
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Controls tour* (40 min): Sitting in a parked car with a parent, locate and explain every control: lights, wipers, hazards, defroster, parking brake and gear selector.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Binary place value* (50 min): Use five flip-cards (16-8-4-2-1) to build numbers 0–31 and convert 10 decimal numbers to binary.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Irony chart* (55 min): Build a three-column chart (quote / what it says / what it really means) for six ironic moments, labeling each verbal or dramatic.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Bisectors* (60 min): Construct a perpendicular bisector and an angle bisector, then verify by folding patty paper.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Products of combustion* (60 min): Hold a cool spoon over the flame to collect water and test exhaled air vs. candle air with limewater for CO₂.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Christian ethics* (55 min): Read the Sermon on the Mount excerpt and identify ideas of individual worth and conscience.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Ser vs. estar* (50 min): Practice ser vs. estar in 20 sentences describing identity and emotions.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Time signatures* (45 min): Count and clap rhythms in 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4 with a metronome at 80 bpm.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Interval run* (45 min): Do a 20-minute interval run, taking heart rate after each interval.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Dashboard* (40 min): Use the owner's manual to identify 10 dashboard warning lights and what action each requires.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Conversion practice* (50 min): Use the Code.org Binary Odometer widget, then convert 15 numbers between binary and decimal by hand.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Word study: Latin roots* (55 min): Study roots (ven-, mort-, cred-, jur-) from the story's vocabulary (impunity, immolation, retribution) and build a 12-word root web.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Perpendiculars & parallels* (60 min): Construct a perpendicular through a point and a parallel line through a point not on a line.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Steel wool burns* (60 min): Weigh steel wool, ignite it with a 9-V battery over a baking tray, re-weigh, and explain the mass gain.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Venn diagram* (55 min): Compare Greco-Roman and Judeo-Christian views of law, reason and duty in a Venn diagram.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Escuchar* (50 min): Listen to a short Radio Ambulante excerpt about Latino identity and answer comprehension questions.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Dictation* (45 min): Notate four 2-measure rhythms you hear (Chrome Music Lab Rhythm).
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *FITT* (45 min): Learn frequency, intensity, time and type and apply them to sample plans.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Pre-drive check* (40 min): Do a walk-around check (tires, lights, fluids under the car, windows) and a cockpit check (seat, mirrors, belt) and time yourself.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Overflow* (50 min): Model a 4-bit odometer rolling over from 1111 and research the Gangnam Style YouTube counter and Y2K overflow examples.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 9, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Analytical paragraph* (55 min): Write a TIQA paragraph (topic, intro to quote, quote, analysis) answering: Is Montresor a reliable narrator? Cite two pieces of evidence.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *GeoGebra constructions* (60 min): Recreate all four constructions in GeoGebra and drag points to confirm they hold.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Model it* (60 min): Draw particle models of methane combustion and write the word and symbol equation.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Constitutional influence* (55 min): Compare articles of Japan's 1947 constitution with the U.S. Bill of Rights.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Mi nombre* (50 min): Write a paragraph 'Mi nombre' about the meaning or story of your own name.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Body percussion* (45 min): Perform an 8-measure body-percussion piece reading notation.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Circuit* (45 min): Complete a 30-minute bodyweight circuit and record RPE.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Mirrors & blind spots* (40 min): Adjust mirrors using the BGE method and have a family member walk around the car to map blind spots.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Round-off* (50 min): In a Python or JavaScript console run 0.1 + 0.2 and explain why the result is not exactly 0.3.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Unreliable narrators: Poe's 'The Cask of Amontillado'
- Objective: Analyze point of view and irony
- Objective: Use textual evidence to infer motive
- Vocabulary: verbal irony, dramatic irony, unreliable narrator, connotation
- Materials: 'The Cask of Amontillado' (Gutenberg), highlighters
- Assessment: Analytical paragraph rubric (claim, evidence, analysis) and irony quiz.

### Geometry: Compass-and-straightedge constructions
- Objective: Copy segments and angles
- Objective: Bisect segments and angles
- Vocabulary: construction, bisector, perpendicular bisector, arc
- Materials: compass, straightedge, patty paper, GeoGebra
- Assessment: Construction performance check.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: What happens when things burn?
- Objective: Identify evidence of a chemical change
- Objective: Model combustion reactants and products
- Vocabulary: combustion, reactant, product, chemical change, oxidation
- Materials: candle, glass jar, limewater (pickling lime + water), steel wool, 9-volt battery
- Assessment: CER paragraph scored with rubric.

### Modern World History: Judeo-Christian ethics and the spread of constitutional ideas
- Objective: Analyze Judeo-Christian views of law and the individual
- Objective: Assess U.S. Constitution's global influence
- Vocabulary: covenant, natural law, constitution, individual worth
- Materials: Ten Commandments and Sermon on the Mount excerpts, Constitutions of Japan (1947) and Germany (Basic Law) excerpts
- Assessment: Unit 1 essay.

### Spanish III: Identidad bicultural
- Objective: Read an authentic text about identity
- Objective: Compare identity perspectives
- Vocabulary: la identidad, bilingüe, la herencia, sentirse orgulloso
- Materials: Sandra Cisneros 'My Name' (Spanish 'Mi nombre' from La casa en Mango Street), Radio Ambulante episode excerpt
- Assessment: Paragraph rubric.

### Music & Media Arts: Rhythm notation and meter
- Objective: Read and count rhythms in simple meter
- Vocabulary: whole note, quarter note, eighth note, rest, time signature, measure
- Materials: metronome app, musictheory.net
- Assessment: Recorded rhythm performance.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Heart rate and FITT
- Objective: Calculate target heart rate
- Objective: Apply FITT principles
- Vocabulary: target heart rate, resting heart rate, FITT, RPE
- Materials: stopwatch or heart-rate app
- Assessment: Quiz and HR log.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Vehicle controls and pre-drive routine
- Objective: Identify vehicle controls and gauges
- Objective: Perform a pre-drive check
- Vocabulary: ignition, gear selector, parking brake, blind spot, dashboard warning light
- Materials: family car (parked), owner's manual
- Assessment: Pre-drive checklist.

### Computer Science Principles: Binary numbers and overflow
- Objective: Convert between binary and decimal
- Objective: Explain overflow and round-off errors
- Vocabulary: binary, decimal, place value, overflow error, round-off error
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account, paper flip-cards labeled 1, 2, 4, 8, 16
- Assessment: Binary conversion practice set.
