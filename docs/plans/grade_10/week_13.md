## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Choose an issue* (55 min): Choose a contemporary community issue and research two credible facts and one quotation to support a position.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Rectangles* (60 min): Prove diagonals of a rectangle are congruent and use it to solve problems.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Metals* (60 min): Model metallic bonding as a sea of electrons and explain malleability and conductivity.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *MAIN causes* (55 min): Build a cause web of militarism, alliances, imperialism and nationalism.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Mandatos afirmativos* (50 min): Learn affirmative tú commands including irregulars (di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, ven).
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Record* (45 min): Record final vocal or lead instrument tracks, doing at least three takes and choosing the best.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Grip & forehand* (45 min): Practice grips and forehand against a wall (100 reps).
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Night driving* (40 min): Learn headlight rules (30 min after sunset; when wipers are on) and high-beam use.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Booleans* (50 min): Evaluate 15 Boolean expressions using comparison and logical operators and build a truth table for AND/OR.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Draft speech* (55 min): Draft a 3–4 minute speech using at least anaphora, antithesis, a rhetorical question and a tricolon.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Rhombi & squares* (60 min): Prove diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular and bisect angles.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Polymers* (60 min): Make slime with glue and borax and explain cross-linking of polymer chains.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Sarajevo* (55 min): Read accounts of Franz Ferdinand's assassination and map the July Crisis.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Negativos* (50 min): Learn negative tú commands.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Mix* (45 min): Mix the song: balance levels, pan supporting parts, and add light reverb.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Backhand* (45 min): Practice backhand technique.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Rain & fog* (40 min): Learn about slippery first rain, hydroplaning and fog driving with low beams.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *If-else* (50 min): Write a program that gives a different message for a user's age range.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 63, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Revise for audience* (55 min): Revise for audience and timing; mark the script for pauses and emphasis.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Trapezoids & kites* (60 min): Explore trapezoid midsegment and kite diagonal properties in GeoGebra.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Material testing* (60 min): Test the stretch of different plastic bags (HDPE vs. LDPE) and relate to chain branching.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Leaders' arguments* (55 min): Analyze the Willy–Nicky telegrams and the ultimatum to Serbia.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Pronombres* (50 min): Attach and place pronouns with commands.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Lead sheet* (45 min): Create a lead sheet in MuseScore or Noteflight with melody, chord symbols and lyrics.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Overhead* (45 min): Practice clears and smashes.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Skids* (40 min): Learn skid recovery: ease off pedals and steer where you want to go.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Nested conditionals* (50 min): Build a 'Which Pet Should You Get?' quiz app that uses nested and compound conditionals.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 64, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Rehearse* (55 min): Rehearse twice on video and self-score eye contact, pace and volume.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Quadrilateral hierarchy* (60 min): Build a Venn diagram/hierarchy of quadrilaterals and justify placements.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Designed materials research* (60 min): Research one designed material (Kevlar, Gore-Tex, nonstick coating) and how structure gives function.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Blame debate* (55 min): Debate: Which nation bears most responsibility for war?
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Póster* (50 min): Create a health-advice poster with 10 commands.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Present* (45 min): Present the song to family and explain how melody, harmony and form support the lyric's meaning.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Footwork* (45 min): Do footwork ladder drills.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Wildfire smoke & wind* (40 min): Learn California-specific hazards: wildfire smoke, high winds and mountain driving.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Flowcharts* (50 min): Draw a flowchart for a movie-ticket pricing algorithm (age, day, student ID) before coding it, then implement it and compare code to chart.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Speech writing: persuasive oration
- Objective: Write and deliver a persuasive speech using rhetorical devices
- Vocabulary: anaphora, antithesis, call to action, tricolon
- Materials: speech planning sheet, video recorder/phone
- Assessment: Speech rubric (content, rhetoric, delivery).

### Geometry: Rectangles, rhombi, squares, trapezoids and kites
- Objective: Classify special quadrilaterals by properties
- Vocabulary: rhombus, rectangle, square, trapezoid, kite, isosceles trapezoid
- Materials: GeoGebra
- Assessment: Quiz and hierarchy diagram.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Metallic bonding and designed materials
- Objective: Explain metallic bonding
- Objective: Connect molecular structure to designed materials
- Vocabulary: metallic bond, alloy, polymer, malleable, ductile
- Materials: aluminum foil, white glue, borax, plastic bags
- Assessment: Material presentation rubric.

### Modern World History: Causes of World War I
- Objective: Analyze MAIN causes
- Objective: Evaluate leaders' arguments for war
- Vocabulary: militarism, alliances, imperialism, nationalism, Schlieffen Plan, assassination
- Materials: Austrian ultimatum to Serbia, 'Willy–Nicky' telegrams (1914)
- Assessment: Debate notes and quiz.

### Spanish III: Mandatos informales y consejos
- Objective: Give tú commands for health advice
- Vocabulary: tú commands, haz, pon, no comas
- Materials: Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Poster and quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: Songwriting project final
- Objective: Produce and present an original song
- Vocabulary: production, performance
- Materials: BandLab
- Assessment: Song rubric.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Badminton or tennis strokes
- Objective: Perform forehand, backhand and overhead
- Vocabulary: forehand, backhand, clear, drop shot, smash
- Materials: racquets, shuttlecocks or tennis balls
- Assessment: Rally count.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Hazardous conditions
- Objective: Adjust driving for weather and night
- Vocabulary: hydroplaning, skid, fog, headlights, smoke
- Materials: handbook
- Assessment: Scenario quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Conditionals and Boolean logic
- Objective: Use if/else statements
- Objective: Write compound Boolean expressions
- Vocabulary: Boolean, conditional, if-else, AND, OR, NOT
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Conditionals quiz and quiz app.
