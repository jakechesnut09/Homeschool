# PE 10 (Course 2) (Grade 10)

High School Physical Education Course 2, following the CA model content standards for adventure/outdoor activities, combatives (self-defense), individual and dual activities, dance, aquatics/gymnastics, and team activities. Students take the Physical Fitness Test (FITNESSGRAM) items, design and monitor personal fitness plans using FITT principles, and study the psychological and social aspects of activity such as leadership, group dynamics, and risk management. Activities are adapted for home, parks, and community facilities.

*Framework:* California Physical Education Model Content Standards (High School Course 2); 2009 CA Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 45 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Fitness Assessment and Goal Setting (weeks 1–3)
Knowing your baseline lets you set meaningful fitness goals.

- Week 1: FITNESSGRAM baseline testing
- Week 2: Heart rate and FITT
- Week 3: SMART goals and a personal plan draft

### Unit 2: Adventure and Outdoor Activities (weeks 4–7)
Outdoor activity builds fitness, confidence and risk-management skill.

- Week 4: Hiking fundamentals and Leave No Trace
- Week 5: Map, compass and orienteering
- Week 6: Climbing and balance challenges
- Week 7: Outdoor activity assessment

### Unit 3: Self-Defense and Combatives (weeks 8–11)
Awareness, avoidance and basic techniques keep you safe.

- Week 8: Awareness and avoidance
- Week 9: Falls, stances and blocks
- Week 10: Escapes and leverage
- Week 11: Self-defense assessment

### Unit 4: Individual and Dual Activities: Net Sports (weeks 12–15)
Strategy and precise skills win rallies in lifelong net sports.

- Week 12: Pickleball fundamentals
- Week 13: Badminton or tennis strokes
- Week 14: Strategy in net games
- Week 15: Net sports tournament

### Unit 5: Training Principles and Semester Assessment (weeks 16–18)
Applying FITT and progression principles produces fitness gains.

- Week 16: Muscular strength and endurance training
- Week 17: Nutrition, hydration and recovery
- Week 18: Semester fitness retest

### Unit 6: Team Activities: Invasion Games (weeks 19–23)
Teams succeed by creating and denying space together.

- Week 19: Soccer skills
- Week 20: Soccer tactics
- Week 21: Flag football or team handball
- Week 22: Invasion games tournament and review
- Week 23: Invasion games assessment

### Unit 7: Dance and Rhythmic Activities (weeks 24–26)
Dance builds fitness and expresses culture through movement.

- Week 24: Cultural and social dance
- Week 25: Choreography
- Week 26: Dance fitness

### Unit 8: Gymnastics, Tumbling and Body Control (weeks 27–29)
Body control and biomechanics underlie every movement skill.

- Week 27: Tumbling fundamentals
- Week 28: Body control and yoga
- Week 29: Gymnastics routine assessment

### Unit 9: Team Activities: Volleyball and Basketball (weeks 30–33)
Communication and roles make team strategy work.

- Week 30: Volleyball skills
- Week 31: Volleyball strategy
- Week 32: Basketball skills and strategy
- Week 33: Team sports assessment and leadership

### Unit 10: Lifetime Fitness Plan and Final Assessment (weeks 34–36)
A personal plan turns knowledge into lifelong activity.

- Week 34: Lifetime fitness plan
- Week 35: Final fitness test
- Week 36: Final assessment and reflection

## Standards

- **PE-HS2-1.1** Demonstrate proficient movement skills in outdoor/adventure activities (e.g., hiking, orienteering, climbing).
- **PE-HS2-1.2** Explain and apply safety practices and risk management in adventure and outdoor activities.
- **PE-HS2-1.3** Demonstrate basic self-defense and combative skills, including escapes, falls and blocks.
- **PE-HS2-1.4** Explain and demonstrate strategies for avoiding dangerous situations and self-protection.
- **PE-HS2-1.5** Demonstrate proficient skills and strategies in individual and dual activities (e.g., tennis, badminton, pickleball).
- **PE-HS2-1.6** Demonstrate proficient skills in dance and rhythmic activities, including creating a movement sequence.
- **PE-HS2-1.7** Demonstrate proficient skills in gymnastics/tumbling or aquatics activities with correct technique and safety.
- **PE-HS2-1.8** Demonstrate advanced offensive, defensive, and transition strategies in team activities.
- **PE-HS2-1.9** Apply biomechanical principles (force, leverage, levers, center of gravity) to analyze and improve performance.
- **PE-HS2-2.1** Participate in moderate to vigorous physical activity at least four days each week.
- **PE-HS2-2.2** Meet health-fitness standards for aerobic capacity, body composition, muscular strength, endurance, and flexibility (FITNESSGRAM).
- **PE-HS2-2.3** Develop and implement a personal fitness plan using the FITT principles and monitor progress.
- **PE-HS2-2.4** Explain the relationship between nutrition, hydration, rest, and performance.
- **PE-HS2-2.5** Use heart-rate monitoring and perceived exertion to regulate training intensity.
- **PE-HS2-3.1** Explain and demonstrate leadership, cooperation and group dynamics in physical activity settings.
- **PE-HS2-3.2** Accept responsibility for personal safety and the safety of others; demonstrate ethical behavior and sportsmanship.
- **PE-HS2-3.3** Identify and evaluate the benefits of lifelong participation in physical activity, including community resources.
