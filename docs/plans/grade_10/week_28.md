## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Fahrenheit 451 Part 1: The Hearth and the Salamander
- Objective: Analyze dystopian setting and symbolism
- Vocabulary: dystopia, censorship, symbol, conformity
- Materials: Fahrenheit 451 (Ray Bradbury, library)
- Assessment: Quiz and symbolism log.

### Geometry: Three-dimensional figures and cross sections
- Objective: Identify cross sections
- Objective: Identify solids of revolution
- Vocabulary: prism, pyramid, cylinder, cone, sphere, cross section, solid of revolution
- Materials: clay or play dough, dental floss, paper cutouts, pencil
- Assessment: Lab sketches and quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Collision theory and reaction rates
- Objective: Explain factors affecting reaction rate
- Vocabulary: collision theory, reaction rate, catalyst, surface area, concentration
- Materials: Alka-Seltzer tablets, water at three temperatures, stopwatch, mortar or spoon
- Assessment: Rate lab report.

### Modern World History: Cold War crises and Eastern Europe
- Objective: Describe Cold War crises
- Objective: Describe uprisings in Eastern Europe
- Vocabulary: arms race, Cuban Missile Crisis, Hungarian Revolution, Prague Spring, Solidarity
- Materials: JFK's Cuban Missile Crisis address, Prague Spring photographs, Solidarity's 21 demands
- Assessment: Short constructed responses.

### Spanish III: El condicional
- Objective: Use the conditional to express hypotheticals and politeness
- Vocabulary: sería, tendría, podría, me gustaría
- Materials: Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: Shooting and editing video
- Objective: Shoot and edit a short video
- Vocabulary: B-roll, timeline, transition, color correction
- Materials: phone, free video editor (e.g., DaVinci Resolve or Clipchamp)
- Assessment: Video draft.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Body control and yoga
- Objective: Demonstrate body control
- Vocabulary: flexibility, isometric, mobility
- Materials: mat
- Assessment: Routine draft.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Cannabis, opioids and fentanyl
- Objective: Explain risks
- Vocabulary: cannabis, opioid, fentanyl, counterfeit pill, naloxone, overdose
- Materials: NIDA resources, naloxone training video
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Cryptography
- Objective: Encrypt and decrypt with symmetric ciphers
- Objective: Explain how public key encryption enables secure communication
- Vocabulary: encryption, Caesar cipher, Vigenère cipher, public key, private key, HTTPS
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Cryptography quiz.
