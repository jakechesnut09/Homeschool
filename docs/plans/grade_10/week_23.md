## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Comparing mediums: war in film and text
- Objective: Compare text and film representations
- Vocabulary: cinematography, montage, adaptation
- Materials: documentary clip (e.g., 'They Shall Not Grow Old' trailer), WWI poems
- Assessment: Multimedia presentation rubric.

### Geometry: Circle basics, arcs and similarity
- Objective: Prove all circles are similar
- Objective: Find arc measures
- Vocabulary: radius, chord, arc, central angle, minor arc, major arc
- Materials: compass, string, circular objects
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Bond energy and energy in reactions
- Objective: Model exothermic and endothermic reactions with bond energies
- Vocabulary: exothermic, endothermic, bond energy, activation energy, enthalpy
- Materials: instant cold pack or baking soda + vinegar, hand warmer or yeast + hydrogen peroxide, thermometer
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Modern World History: Home fronts, the atomic bomb and human costs
- Objective: Analyze civilian experiences and the atomic bomb decision
- Vocabulary: Manhattan Project, Hiroshima, Nagasaki, internment, rationing
- Materials: Truman's Hiroshima statement, Hiroshima survivor accounts, Executive Order 9066
- Assessment: Seminar rubric.

### Spanish III: Las profesiones
- Objective: Discuss careers and job skills
- Vocabulary: el currículum, la entrevista, el sueldo, la carrera, el/la ingeniero/a
- Materials: job ads in Spanish
- Assessment: Interview rubric.

### Music & Media Arts: Sound design and recording
- Objective: Record and edit audio
- Objective: Create sound effects
- Vocabulary: foley, waveform, noise reduction, microphone placement
- Materials: Audacity, phone microphone, household objects
- Assessment: Soundscape.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Invasion games assessment
- Objective: Demonstrate team strategy
- Vocabulary: assessment
- Materials: equipment
- Assessment: Written test and observation.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Environmental emergencies and certification
- Objective: Respond to heat, cold and water emergencies
- Vocabulary: heat stroke, hypothermia, drowning
- Materials: first aid kit
- Assessment: Unit 6 written and skills test.

### Computer Science Principles: Collecting and cleaning data
- Objective: Collect data with a survey
- Objective: Clean data for analysis
- Vocabulary: dataset, data cleaning, metadata, sample
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account, Google Sheets
- Assessment: Cleaned dataset.
