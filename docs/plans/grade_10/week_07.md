## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Scenes 4–5* (55 min): Read Antigone's farewell and Teiresias's warning; mark the moment Creon's reversal begins.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Congruence definition* (60 min): Decide whether pairs of figures are congruent by finding a rigid motion or showing none exists.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Organizing elements* (60 min): Sort 20 element cards by properties to recreate Mendeleev's reasoning.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Sadler Report* (55 min): Read child-labor testimony from the Sadler Committee and annotate conditions.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *La Llorona* (50 min): Read a version of La Llorona and sequence events.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Bass lines* (45 min): Write a bass line using root notes of a chord progression.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Risk plan* (45 min): Write a risk-management plan for a half-day outing (weather, water, emergency contacts).
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Basic Speed Law* (40 min): Read and explain the Basic Speed Law: never drive faster than is safe for conditions.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Routing* (50 min): Run a traceroute (tracert/traceroute) to two websites and map how many hops each path takes.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Exodos* (55 min): Finish the play and complete a cause-effect chain from Creon's decree to the deaths.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Corresponding parts* (60 min): Write congruence statements and list all corresponding congruent parts (CPCTC).
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Graph trends* (60 min): Graph atomic radius and first ionization energy vs. atomic number and describe the patterns.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Cities* (55 min): Read Engels's description of Manchester and compare to a modern city.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Los volcanes* (50 min): Read the legend of Popocatépetl and Iztaccíhuatl and analyze its values.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Loops* (45 min): Explore BandLab loop library and layer three loops.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Bike or kayak* (45 min): Do a 60-minute bike ride or paddle with proper safety gear.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Speed limits* (40 min): Make a chart of limits: 65 mph max on most highways, 55 for two-lane undivided roads and when towing, 25 in residential and school zones, 15 at blind intersections and railroad crossings.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Redundancy* (50 min): Build a string-and-sticky-note network model and count how many alternative paths exist between two nodes.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Compare a staging* (55 min): Watch one scene from a filmed production and compare how actors portray Creon versus your reading.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Triangle criteria* (60 min): Build triangles from given sides/angles with straws to test SSS, SAS, ASA, SSA and AAA.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Explain trends* (60 min): Explain trends using nuclear charge and shielding with diagrams.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Dickens* (55 min): Read the Coketown chapter of Hard Times and identify social criticism.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Perspectivas* (50 min): Discuss what each legend teaches and why it endures.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Song form* (45 min): Analyze form of a favorite song (intro, verse, chorus, bridge).
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Culminating hike* (45 min): Lead a family hike using map and compass.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Stopping distance* (40 min): Calculate reaction and braking distance at 25, 45 and 65 mph and graph how distance grows with speed.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Fault tolerance* (50 min): Remove links from your model one at a time and determine which removals disconnect the network.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Antigone: Downfall and the tragic hero
- Objective: Determine who is the tragic hero
- Objective: Analyze theme
- Vocabulary: anagnorisis, peripeteia, catastrophe, theme
- Materials: Antigone text, filmed production (library or streaming)
- Assessment: Seminar rubric and theme statements.

### Geometry: Congruence through rigid motion
- Objective: Define congruence using rigid motions
- Objective: Explain triangle congruence criteria
- Vocabulary: congruent, corresponding parts, SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS
- Materials: patty paper, straws or strips, GeoGebra
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Periodic table trends
- Objective: Predict properties from valence electrons
- Objective: Explain trends in radius and ionization energy
- Vocabulary: period, group, atomic radius, ionization energy, electronegativity, reactivity
- Materials: periodic table, graph paper or spreadsheet
- Assessment: Trend graphs and quiz.

### Modern World History: Industrial society: cities, labor and reform
- Objective: Analyze working and living conditions
- Objective: Trace labor reform and changing roles
- Vocabulary: urbanization, child labor, Sadler Report, union, tenement, Romanticism
- Materials: Sadler Committee testimony (1832), Engels on Manchester excerpt, Dickens' Hard Times ch. 5 (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Historical letter scored for evidence.

### Spanish III: Leyendas del mundo hispano
- Objective: Read and retell a legend
- Objective: Connect legend to cultural perspectives
- Vocabulary: la leyenda, el fantasma, llorar, el volcán
- Materials: 'La Llorona' and 'Leyenda de los volcanes (Popocatépetl e Iztaccíhuatl)' texts
- Assessment: Legend writing rubric and Unit 2 test.

### Music & Media Arts: Loops, bass lines and arrangement
- Objective: Arrange loops into a song form
- Vocabulary: loop, bass line, intro, verse, drop, arrangement
- Materials: BandLab
- Assessment: Draft track.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Outdoor activity assessment
- Objective: Apply outdoor skills in a culminating activity
- Vocabulary: risk assessment, emergency plan
- Materials: outdoor gear
- Assessment: Quiz and leadership checklist.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Speed laws and following distance
- Objective: Apply the Basic Speed Law
- Objective: Use the 3-second rule
- Vocabulary: Basic Speed Law, prima facie limit, 3-second rule, school zone
- Materials: handbook, stopwatch
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Computer Science Principles: Routing, redundancy and fault tolerance
- Objective: Explain how routers forward packets
- Objective: Describe how redundancy creates fault tolerance
- Vocabulary: router, routing, redundancy, fault tolerance, scalability
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account, string and sticky notes
- Assessment: Network diagram questions.
