## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Context* (55 min): Read a short history of Julius Caesar and the Roman Republic and note why senators feared him.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Vertical angles proof* (60 min): Write a two-column proof that vertical angles are congruent.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Fusion in the Sun* (60 min): Model the proton-proton chain and explain how mass converts to energy.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Motives* (55 min): Sort motives (raw materials, markets, nationalism, Social Darwinism, missionary) with examples.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Futuro regular* (50 min): Learn future forms and write 10 predictions for your trip.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Triads* (45 min): Build major and minor triads on the keyboard.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Breakfalls* (45 min): Practice back and side breakfalls on a mat.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Lane choice* (40 min): Learn which lane to use for through traffic, passing and turning.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Digital divide* (50 min): Use FCC broadband map data to compare internet access in a rural and an urban California county.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Iambic pentameter* (55 min): Scan five lines of blank verse, marking stressed syllables, and notice when prose is used instead.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Transversals* (60 min): Explore angle pairs formed by parallel lines and a transversal in GeoGebra and record conjectures.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Life cycle of stars* (60 min): Create a star life-cycle diagram for a Sun-like star and a massive star.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Map empires* (55 min): Color a 1914 world map showing British, French, German, Belgian, Dutch, Russian, Japanese and U.S. holdings.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Irregulares* (50 min): Practice future irregulars (tendr-, saldr-, har-, dir-, podr-).
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Roman numerals* (45 min): Label chords in a key with Roman numerals.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Blocks* (45 min): Practice high and low blocks with a partner holding a pool noodle.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Passing* (40 min): Learn when passing is illegal (curves, hills, intersections, solid lines) and the steps to pass safely.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Effects* (50 min): Read an article on how the digital divide affected students during remote learning and summarize three impacts.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Act 1 scenes 1–2* (55 min): Read 1.1–1.2 with the Folger audio and chart how Cassius manipulates Brutus.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Parallel line proofs* (60 min): Prove alternate interior angles congruent from the corresponding angles postulate.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Where elements come from* (60 min): Color-code a periodic table by element origin (Big Bang, stars, supernovae, mergers).
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Kipling & critics* (55 min): Read 'The White Man's Burden' and a response such as 'The Brown Man's Burden'; analyze perspectives.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Ir a + infinitivo* (50 min): Compare ir a + infinitivo with the future tense.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *I-V-vi-IV* (45 min): Identify the 'four-chord' progression in five pop songs.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Striking basics* (45 min): Practice palm-heel strikes and knee strikes on a pillow or pad.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Being passed* (40 min): Learn what to do when another driver passes you.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Governance* (50 min): Research ICANN and net neutrality and write a pro/con table on net neutrality.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Shakespeare's Rome: Julius Caesar Act 1
- Objective: Read Shakespearean language with fluency
- Objective: Analyze characterization through dialogue
- Vocabulary: blank verse, iambic pentameter, soliloquy, aside
- Materials: Folger Julius Caesar, historical background reading on Caesar
- Assessment: Act 1 quiz; scansion practice.

### Geometry: Proving theorems about lines and angles
- Objective: Prove vertical angles congruent
- Objective: Prove theorems about parallel lines and transversals
- Vocabulary: transversal, alternate interior, corresponding angles, same-side interior, theorem
- Materials: GeoGebra, protractor
- Assessment: Proof quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Stars forge elements
- Objective: Explain stellar nucleosynthesis
- Objective: Model the Sun's life span
- Vocabulary: nucleosynthesis, main sequence, supernova, red giant, Big Bang
- Materials: NASA star life cycle resources
- Assessment: Explainer and Unit 2 test.

### Modern World History: Motives and methods of New Imperialism
- Objective: Explain economic, political and ideological motives
- Objective: Locate colonial empires
- Vocabulary: imperialism, colony, protectorate, sphere of influence, Social Darwinism
- Materials: blank world map, Kipling's 'The White Man's Burden' and responses, imperialist political cartoons
- Assessment: Map and quiz.

### Spanish III: El futuro
- Objective: Use the future tense to discuss plans and predictions
- Vocabulary: mañana, el año que viene, dentro de, algún día
- Materials: Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: Chords and chord progressions
- Objective: Build triads
- Objective: Identify common progressions
- Vocabulary: triad, root position, I-IV-V, roman numerals, inversion
- Materials: keyboard app, ukulele or guitar (optional)
- Assessment: Quiz.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Falls, stances and blocks
- Objective: Demonstrate safe falling and blocks
- Vocabulary: breakfall, base, guard, block
- Materials: exercise mat
- Assessment: Skill video.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Passing and lane positioning
- Objective: Explain safe passing and lane choice
- Vocabulary: passing, no-passing zone, center left-turn lane, lane position
- Materials: handbook
- Assessment: Scenario quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: The digital divide and internet impacts
- Objective: Evaluate the causes and effects of the digital divide
- Objective: Analyze internet governance
- Vocabulary: digital divide, net neutrality, ICANN, broadband
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Unit 2 assessment.
