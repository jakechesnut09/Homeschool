## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Bierce* (55 min): Read 'An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge' and mark where time slows, jumps back, and resumes; sketch a timeline of real vs. imagined time.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Midpoint formula* (60 min): Derive and apply the midpoint formula, including finding an endpoint given a midpoint.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Energy units* (60 min): Convert between joules, calories and food Calories and read nutrition labels for energy content.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Scientific Revolution to Enlightenment* (55 min): Read an overview and create a timeline from Newton to Rousseau.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Gustar & co.* (50 min): Learn encantar, molestar, importar, interesar, fascinar; write 10 sentences about family members' preferences.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Whole & half steps* (45 min): Build major scales using W-W-H-W-W-W-H on a keyboard.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *SMART goals* (45 min): Write three SMART goals based on baseline results.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Crash physics* (40 min): Run an egg 'passenger' crash demo on a ramp with and without a rubber-band seat belt and explain inertia.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Encoding text* (50 min): Encode your name in ASCII binary and decode a partner's (or parent's) secret message.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Film comparison* (55 min): Watch the 1961 short film of Owl Creek Bridge (library/streaming) and list three choices the director made differently than Bierce (RL.9-10.7 preview).
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Distance formula* (60 min): Derive the distance formula from the Pythagorean theorem and compute perimeters of polygons on the grid.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Food calorimeter* (60 min): Build a soda-can calorimeter and burn a cheese puff and a cashew to heat 100 mL of water.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Magna Carta & Bill of Rights* (55 min): Analyze key clauses of the Magna Carta and English Bill of Rights and list the rights they protect.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Reflexivos* (50 min): Describe your morning routine using 10 reflexive verbs in order with transition words.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Key signatures* (45 min): Learn the circle of fifths and identify sharp key signatures.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Plan* (45 min): Draft a 4-week plan with cardio, strength and flexibility days.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Belt & airbag laws* (40 min): Read handbook rules on seat belts and child restraints (children under 2 rear-facing; under 8 in a car seat or booster in the back seat).
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Unicode and emoji* (50 min): Look up the Unicode code points for three emoji and explain why ASCII alone was not enough for the world's languages.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Maupassant* (55 min): Read 'The Necklace' and annotate details of 19th-century French class and status that drive Mathilde's choices.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Partitioning* (60 min): Find the point that divides a segment in ratios like 2:3 using the coordinate method.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Calculate q* (60 min): Use q = mcΔT to compute energy released per gram and compare to label values; explain error.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Locke & Hobbes* (55 min): Compare Hobbes's Leviathan and Locke's Second Treatise views of human nature and government.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Encuesta* (50 min): Survey three people (in Spanish or translating) about what bothers and interests them; report results.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Flat keys* (45 min): Identify flat key signatures.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Workout 1* (45 min): Do the first planned workout (40 minutes) and log it.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Airbag safety* (40 min): Explain safe seating distance from airbags and why children ride in back.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Black-and-white images* (50 min): Use the Code.org Pixelation widget to draw a 10×10 black-and-white image and write its bits.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Compare endings* (55 min): Write a 250-word comparison of the twist endings: how does each author plant clues so the surprise feels earned?
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Inscribed polygons* (60 min): Construct an equilateral triangle, square and regular hexagon inscribed in a circle.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Fuels of California* (60 min): Research CA's energy mix (CA Energy Commission) and compare energy per gram of gasoline, natural gas and hydrogen.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Montesquieu & Rousseau* (55 min): Read excerpts from The Spirit of Laws and The Social Contract and summarize each idea.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Video* (50 min): Record a 2-minute 'Un día en mi vida' video.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Intervals* (45 min): Identify and sing intervals using song references (e.g., 'Here Comes the Bride' = P4).
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Flexibility* (45 min): Complete a 20-minute static stretching and yoga routine.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Persuade* (40 min): Create a 30-second PSA script encouraging peers to buckle up every ride.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Color images* (50 min): Create a color image using hex RGB values and calculate the file size in bits and bytes at 24 bits per pixel.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Time and twist: 'An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge' and 'The Necklace'
- Objective: Analyze manipulation of time
- Objective: Compare situational irony in two stories
- Vocabulary: flashback, stream of consciousness, situational irony, denouement
- Materials: Bierce and Maupassant stories (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Comparison response scored on evidence and analysis of structure; discussion participation checklist.

### Geometry: Midpoint, distance, and inscribed polygons
- Objective: Use midpoint and distance formulas
- Objective: Construct inscribed regular polygons
- Vocabulary: midpoint formula, distance formula, inscribed, regular polygon
- Materials: compass, graph paper
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Energy from fuels
- Objective: Compare energy released by fuels
- Objective: Calculate energy with q = mcΔT
- Vocabulary: calorie, joule, specific heat, calorimetry, fossil fuel
- Materials: soda can, thermometer, paperclip, cheese puffs and nuts, matches
- Assessment: Calorimetry lab report.

### Modern World History: The Enlightenment and the English Revolution
- Objective: Compare Enlightenment thinkers
- Objective: Identify principles of the Magna Carta and English Bill of Rights
- Vocabulary: Enlightenment, natural rights, social contract, separation of powers, Glorious Revolution
- Materials: Magna Carta (1215) and English Bill of Rights (1689) excerpts (Avalon/British Library), Locke's Second Treatise excerpt
- Assessment: Dialogue assessed for accuracy of ideas.

### Spanish III: Gustar y verbos similares; reflexivos
- Objective: Use gustar-type verbs and reflexive verbs accurately
- Vocabulary: encantar, molestar, importar, interesar, despertarse
- Materials: Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Music & Media Arts: Scales, keys and intervals
- Objective: Build major scales
- Objective: Identify intervals
- Vocabulary: major scale, whole step, half step, key signature, interval
- Materials: keyboard app, musictheory.net
- Assessment: Quiz.

### PE 10 (Course 2): SMART goals and a personal plan draft
- Objective: Write SMART fitness goals
- Objective: Draft a 4-week plan
- Vocabulary: SMART goal, progression, overload
- Materials: fitness log
- Assessment: Plan and activity log.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Occupant protection
- Objective: Explain seat belt, airbag and child restraint laws
- Vocabulary: seat belt law, airbag, child safety seat, crash forces
- Materials: egg, toy car and rubber band (crash demo), handbook
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Computer Science Principles: Representing text and images
- Objective: Explain how characters are encoded with ASCII/Unicode
- Objective: Create pixel images with RGB values
- Vocabulary: ASCII, Unicode, pixel, RGB, resolution, metadata
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account, grid paper
- Assessment: Pixelation project and quiz.
