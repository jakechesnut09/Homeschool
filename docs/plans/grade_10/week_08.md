## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Thoreau excerpt* (55 min): Read an excerpt of Thoreau's 'Civil Disobedience' and note parallels with Antigone's position.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Inductive vs. deductive* (60 min): Make conjectures from patterns and find counterexamples.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Types of decay* (60 min): Write nuclear equations for alpha, beta and gamma decay.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Adam Smith* (55 min): Read the 'invisible hand' and pin factory excerpts and summarize Smith's arguments.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Vocabulario* (50 min): Learn airport and hotel vocabulary with flashcards.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Mixing basics* (45 min): Learn volume balance and panning.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Awareness* (45 min): Learn the color codes of awareness and practice scanning in public settings.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Signaling* (40 min): Learn hand and turn signals; signal at least 100 feet before turning and 5 seconds before a freeway lane change.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *TCP vs. UDP* (50 min): Compare TCP and UDP using a video call vs. file download example and list which each would use.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Outline* (55 min): Outline an argument essay: Is Antigone right to break Creon's law? Include claim, two reasons, a counterclaim and rebuttal.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Conditionals* (60 min): Rewrite statements in if-then form and write converse, inverse and contrapositive with truth values.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Half-life lab* (60 min): Shake 100 pennies in a box, remove heads each round, graph results and find half-life.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Marx & Engels* (55 min): Read a Communist Manifesto excerpt and summarize its critique of capitalism.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Sitios auténticos* (50 min): Navigate a Spanish-language airline or hotel website to find prices and schedules.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Refine* (45 min): Refine your track with automation and fades.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Boundaries* (45 min): Practice assertive verbal boundary setting with role-plays.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Right & left turns* (40 min): Diagram right turns (merging into bike lane within 200 feet) and left turns from two-way and one-way streets.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *DNS* (50 min): Use nslookup to look up IP addresses for three domains and explain the DNS lookup process as a diagram.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 38, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Draft* (55 min): Draft the 700–900 word essay, integrating quotes from both the play and Thoreau.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Laws of logic* (60 min): Apply the Law of Detachment and Syllogism to draw valid conclusions.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Radiometric dating* (60 min): Use half-life to calculate ages of rocks (U-238) and artifacts (C-14).
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Utopians & reformers* (55 min): Research Robert Owen's New Lanark and compare responses in a chart.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Diálogo* (50 min): Role-play checking in at an airport and hotel.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Artist statement* (45 min): Write a 150-word artist statement explaining your choices.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *De-escalation* (45 min): Learn de-escalation techniques and practice scenarios.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *U-turns* (40 min): List where U-turns are legal and illegal in California.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *HTTP and the web* (50 min): Open browser developer tools, load a web page, and record the HTTP requests, status codes and load time.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 39, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Grammar: parallel structure* (55 min): Mini-lesson on parallel structure; revise three sentences in your draft to use parallelism for emphasis.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Algebraic proof* (60 min): Write two-column proofs solving equations with properties of equality.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Fission vs. fusion* (60 min): Compare fission (Diablo Canyon power plant) and fusion and their energy sources (E = mc²).
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Industrialization spreads* (55 min): Compare industrialization in Germany, Japan (Meiji) and the U.S.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Itinerario* (50 min): Create a 5-day itinerary for a country of your choice.

- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Lane changes* (40 min): Learn the signal–mirror–over-shoulder–go sequence and practice the head check as a passenger.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Bandwidth and latency* (50 min): Run an internet speed test at two times of day and explain the difference between bandwidth and latency.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Argument essay: Antigone and civil disobedience
- Objective: Write a claim-driven argument
- Objective: Address counterclaims
- Vocabulary: claim, counterclaim, rebuttal, warrant
- Materials: Antigone, excerpt of Thoreau's 'Civil Disobedience' (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Argument essay rubric and Unit 2 test.

### Geometry: Logic and reasoning
- Objective: Write conditionals, converses and biconditionals
- Objective: Use deductive reasoning
- Vocabulary: conditional, converse, inverse, contrapositive, counterexample, deductive
- Materials: notebook
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Nuclear chemistry
- Objective: Model fission, fusion and radioactive decay
- Objective: Calculate half-life
- Vocabulary: alpha decay, beta decay, gamma, half-life, fission, fusion
- Materials: pennies or M&Ms, shoebox
- Assessment: Half-life lab graph and quiz.

### Modern World History: Capitalism and its critics; global industrialization
- Objective: Compare capitalism, socialism and communism
- Objective: Compare industrialization in Germany, Japan and the U.S.
- Vocabulary: capitalism, laissez-faire, socialism, communism, Meiji Restoration
- Materials: Adam Smith's Wealth of Nations excerpt, Communist Manifesto excerpt
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Spanish III: Planear un viaje
- Objective: Discuss travel plans and logistics
- Vocabulary: el pasaporte, la aduana, el boleto de ida y vuelta, facturar, el alojamiento
- Materials: airline and hotel websites in Spanish
- Assessment: Role-play rubric.

### Music & Media Arts: Beat-making project
- Objective: Create and refine an original instrumental track
- Vocabulary: mix, volume, panning, fade
- Materials: BandLab
- Assessment: Track and statement rubric.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Awareness and avoidance
- Objective: Identify strategies for avoiding danger
- Vocabulary: situational awareness, boundary setting, de-escalation
- Materials: video resources
- Assessment: Scenario quiz.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Turns, signals and lane changes
- Objective: Describe correct turning and lane-change procedures
- Vocabulary: signal 100 feet, bike lane turn, U-turn, SMOG lane change
- Materials: handbook diagrams, toy cars
- Assessment: Diagram quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Protocols: TCP, UDP, DNS and HTTP
- Objective: Describe the roles of TCP, UDP, DNS and HTTP
- Objective: Explain issues that affect network performance
- Vocabulary: TCP, UDP, DNS, HTTP, latency, protocol stack
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Protocol stack diagram and quiz.
