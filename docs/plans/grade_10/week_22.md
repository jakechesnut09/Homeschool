## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Informative essay on witness literature
- Objective: Write an informative essay synthesizing literary and informational texts
- Vocabulary: synthesis, paraphrase, attributive tag
- Materials: Night, WWI poems, USHMM articles
- Assessment: Informative essay rubric and Unit 5 test.

### Geometry: Laws of Sines and Cosines
- Objective: Derive area formula ½ab sin C
- Objective: Apply Laws of Sines and Cosines
- Vocabulary: Law of Sines, Law of Cosines, oblique triangle, ambiguous case
- Materials: calculator
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Limiting reactants
- Objective: Identify limiting and excess reactants
- Vocabulary: limiting reactant, excess reactant, percent yield
- Materials: baking soda, vinegar, balloons, PhET Reactants, Products and Leftovers
- Assessment: Balloon lab graph and quiz.

### Modern World History: The Holocaust
- Objective: Analyze the progression of Nazi persecution to genocide
- Vocabulary: Holocaust, Kristallnacht, ghetto, Einsatzgruppen, Wannsee Conference, Final Solution, resistance
- Materials: USHMM primary sources, Wannsee Conference minutes excerpt, survivor testimony (USC Shoah Foundation IWitness)
- Assessment: Testimony response.

### Spanish III: El cuento: un cuento corto
- Objective: Read and analyze a short story
- Vocabulary: el narrador, el desenlace, el personaje, el pluscuamperfecto
- Materials: Short story such as 'Continuidad de los parques' (Cortázar) or 'El Almohadón de plumas' (Quiroga, public domain)
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Music & Media Arts: Copyright, fair use and media ethics
- Objective: Explain copyright and fair use
- Objective: Analyze media bias
- Vocabulary: copyright, fair use, Creative Commons, public domain, bias
- Materials: Creative Commons website
- Assessment: Quiz.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Flag football or team handball
- Objective: Apply invasion-game strategy in a new sport
- Vocabulary: route, zone defense, fast break
- Materials: football or handball, flags
- Assessment: Written test and game-play observation checklist.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Injuries and sudden illness
- Objective: Treat bleeding, burns, fractures and sudden illness
- Vocabulary: tourniquet, burn, splint, stroke FAST, anaphylaxis
- Materials: bandages, first aid kit
- Assessment: Scenario quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Unsolvable problems, heuristics and parallel computing
- Objective: Identify unreasonable-time and undecidable problems
- Objective: Compare sequential, parallel and distributed computing
- Vocabulary: heuristic, undecidable, parallel computing, distributed computing, speedup
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Unit 5 assessment.
