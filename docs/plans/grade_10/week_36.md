## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Review* (55 min): Review key texts and terms with a self-made study guide.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Targeted review* (60 min): Review topics missed on the practice final.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Present capstone* (60 min): Present the capstone to family with Q&A.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Review* (55 min): Targeted review of weak topics.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Repaso* (50 min): Review all tenses and moods.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Curate* (45 min): Select 6–8 best works from both semesters (performance, song, beat, photo essay, podcast, video, capstone).
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Review* (45 min): Review course concepts: FITT, heart-rate zones, biomechanics, outdoor safety and team strategy.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Final exam* (40 min): Take the written final exam.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Review: data and networks* (50 min): Complete Khan Academy AP CSP practice on binary, compression, the internet and fault tolerance and correct every error.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Practice exam* (55 min): Complete a CAASPP-style practice performance task.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Final exam part 1* (60 min): Take Part 1 of the final (transformations through similarity).
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Review* (60 min): Review all units with practice problems.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Final part 1* (55 min): Multiple-choice and map sections of the final.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Lectura y audio* (50 min): Final interpretive section with an authentic article and podcast.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Portfolio site* (45 min): Build a free portfolio website or slide portfolio with captions and credits for each work.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Final exam* (45 min): Take the written final exam.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Skills final* (40 min): Demonstrate CPR/AED and one first aid skill.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Review: programming* (50 min): Trace 20 AP reference-sheet pseudocode problems on lists, loops, procedures and Boolean logic.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Final exam* (55 min): Take the final: cold reads (literary and informational), grammar, and a timed argument essay.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Final exam part 2* (60 min): Take Part 2 (trig, circles, volume, probability).
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Final exam* (60 min): Take the final exam.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Final part 2* (55 min): Timed DBQ essay on the final.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Escritura* (50 min): Final writing section: a 300-word essay.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Final exam* (45 min): Take the final exam on music theory, history, media arts vocabulary and copyright.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Favorite activity* (45 min): Choose and lead a favorite activity from the year for your family.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Personal health plan* (40 min): Write a personal health plan with goals in three areas.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Practice exam* (50 min): Take a timed 70-question AP-style practice exam under test conditions (120 minutes).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Portfolio presentation* (55 min): Present the portfolio in a 6-minute talk with slides.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Error analysis* (60 min): Analyze errors and correct them with explanations.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Error analysis* (60 min): Correct errors on the exam.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Error analysis* (55 min): Correct errors.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Entrevista oral* (50 min): Final 10-minute oral proficiency interview.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Reflection* (45 min): Write a final reflection on growth as a musician and media artist with evidence from your portfolio.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Portfolio* (45 min): Compile fitness logs, test results and reflections into a PE portfolio.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Permit check* (40 min): Confirm next steps toward the learner's permit.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Error analysis* (50 min): Categorize missed practice-exam questions by big idea and redo each with a written explanation.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Summer reading plan* (55 min): Create a summer reading list of five books and set reading goals.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Reflection* (60 min): Write a reflection on your favorite proof and how geometry is used in a career of interest.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Revisit phenomenon* (60 min): Write a final explanation of the burning candle using all you learned this year.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Reflection* (55 min): Write a reflection on how studying history changed your view of current events.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Metas* (50 min): Set Spanish IV goals and choose a summer book or series in Spanish.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Goals* (45 min): Set goals for next year's arts course (e.g., Theatre & Performing Arts) and share the portfolio link with family.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Reflection* (45 min): Write a 300-word reflection on how your activity habits changed this year.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Reflection* (40 min): Reflect on how the course will change your choices.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Final exam* (50 min): Take the cumulative course final and write a short reflection on your growth as a programmer.
