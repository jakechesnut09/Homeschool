## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Imitation poem* (55 min): Write an imitation of Tagore's structure ('Where…') about your own vision of freedom.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Multiplication rule* (60 min): Apply P(A and B) = P(A)P(B|A) to multi-stage problems.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Breathe into indicator* (60 min): Blow through a straw into cabbage indicator water and observe the pH drop from CO₂.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *SDGs* (55 min): Read the UN Sustainable Development Goals and choose one.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Elegir tema* (50 min): Choose a project: documentary, magazine, or podcast on a Spanish-speaking country.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Critique session* (45 min): Show the rough version to two viewers and gather feedback using your proposal's goals as criteria.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Interest inventory* (45 min): Rank 15 lifetime activities by enjoyment, cost and access.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Accurate information* (40 min): Review medically accurate information on preventing pregnancy and STIs as required by the CA Healthy Youth Act.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Interface* (50 min): Build the program's screens and event handlers in App Lab (or Python) and log your progress.
