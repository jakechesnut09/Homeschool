## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Select pieces* (55 min): Select three best pieces (narrative, analysis, argument/research) for a final portfolio.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Transformations & proof* (60 min): Mixed review of transformations, congruence and proof.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Choose challenge* (60 min): Choose a challenge (urban air quality, ocean acidification, home energy) and define criteria and constraints.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Big themes* (55 min): Create a thematic timeline of democracy, nationalism and imperialism across the course.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Producción* (50 min): Produce the final video, podcast or magazine.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Exhibition prep* (45 min): Plan the presentation: format (screening, listening session, web page), order and introduction.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Aerobic retest* (45 min): Complete the final PACER or 1-mile run.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Driver ed review* (40 min): Review Semester 1 driver education with a practice DMV knowledge test.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *User testing* (50 min): Have two users test the program, note usability and accessibility problems, and prioritize fixes.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Revise piece 1* (55 min): Deeply revise one essay based on earlier feedback, tracking changes.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Similarity & trig* (60 min): Mixed review of similarity and trigonometry.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Research* (60 min): Research the chemistry behind the problem with three sources.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Map review* (55 min): Complete a map review of key places.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Presentación* (50 min): Present the project and answer questions in Spanish.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Exhibit* (45 min): Present the capstone to family or a homeschool group with a 2-minute introduction.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Strength retest* (45 min): Complete final push-up and curl-up tests.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *First aid review* (40 min): Review first aid and CPR with scenario cards and a 2-minute CPR practice.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Refine* (50 min): Fix the top issues, add code comments, and acknowledge any code or media from others with correct license attribution.
