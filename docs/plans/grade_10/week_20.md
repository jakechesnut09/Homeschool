## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Context* (55 min): Use the U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum encyclopedia to create a timeline of the Holocaust 1933–1945.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Ratios from similarity* (60 min): Draw three different-size right triangles with a 35° angle and compute side ratios to see they're constant.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Decomposition* (60 min): Decompose hydrogen peroxide with yeast and test for oxygen with a glowing splint.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Japan* (55 min): Map Japan's invasion of Manchuria and China and read accounts of Nanjing.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Formación* (50 min): Learn present perfect with regular participles.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Editing* (45 min): Learn exposure, contrast, crop in a free editor.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Attack principles* (45 min): Learn width, depth and penetration and practice a 3v1 rondo.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Chain of survival* (40 min): Learn the chain of survival.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Libraries* (50 min): Read App Lab library documentation and use a library function.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Night by Elie Wiesel, sections 1–3
- Objective: Analyze memoir as testimony
- Objective: Trace Eliezer's faith
- Vocabulary: memoir, Holocaust, ghetto, deportation, Sighet
- Materials: Night (Elie Wiesel, library), USHMM website
- Assessment: Reading quiz and faith-tracking chart.

### Geometry: Trigonometric ratios
- Objective: Define sine, cosine, tangent through similarity
- Objective: Relate sine and cosine of complements
- Vocabulary: sine, cosine, tangent, opposite, adjacent, hypotenuse
- Materials: protractor, ruler, scientific calculator
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Types of chemical reactions
- Objective: Classify reactions
- Objective: Predict products using periodic trends
- Vocabulary: synthesis, decomposition, single replacement, double replacement, activity series, precipitate
- Materials: hydrogen peroxide, yeast, steel wool, copper sulfate (root killer) or vinegar, epsom salt, washing soda
- Assessment: Reaction lab notebook and quiz.

### Modern World History: Aggression and appeasement
- Objective: Compare drives for empire
- Objective: Evaluate appeasement
- Vocabulary: appeasement, Munich Agreement, Anschluss, Nanjing, isolationism, Nazi-Soviet Pact
- Materials: Chamberlain's 'Peace for our time' speech, Churchill's response, maps of 1930s aggression
- Assessment: Debate and exit ticket.

### Spanish III: El presente perfecto
- Objective: Use the present perfect to describe experiences
- Vocabulary: haber, participio, ya, todavía no, alguna vez
- Materials: Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: Photo editing and visual storytelling
- Objective: Edit photos
- Objective: Tell a story in images
- Vocabulary: exposure, contrast, crop, photo essay
- Materials: free photo editor (Photopea or Snapseed)
- Assessment: Photo essay.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Soccer tactics
- Objective: Apply offensive and defensive principles
- Vocabulary: width, depth, pressure, cover
- Materials: ball, cones
- Assessment: Tactical diagram.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): CPR and AED
- Objective: Perform adult CPR and use an AED
- Vocabulary: compressions, rescue breaths, AED, chain of survival
- Materials: CPR manikin or firm pillow, AHA Hands-Only CPR video
- Assessment: Skills check.

### Computer Science Principles: Libraries and APIs
- Objective: Use libraries and documentation
- Objective: Explain license limitations of libraries
- Vocabulary: library, API, documentation
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Library project and quiz.
