## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~460 min)

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Seminal speeches of the 20th century
- Objective: Analyze rhetoric in world documents
- Vocabulary: rhetoric, allusion, fallacy, seminal document
- Materials: FDR 'Four Freedoms' (1941), Churchill 'We Shall Fight on the Beaches', Gandhi 'Quit India', Mandela 'I Am Prepared to Die'
- Assessment: Rhetorical analysis chart.

### Geometry: Inscribed angles and tangents
- Objective: Use inscribed angle theorem
- Objective: Prove tangent is perpendicular to radius
- Vocabulary: inscribed angle, intercepted arc, tangent, cyclic quadrilateral
- Materials: GeoGebra, compass
- Assessment: Quiz and constructions.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Earth's energy budget
- Objective: Model incoming and outgoing radiation
- Objective: Explain the greenhouse effect
- Vocabulary: albedo, infrared, greenhouse effect, radiation budget
- Materials: two jars, plastic wrap, thermometers, lamp, black and white paper
- Assessment: Lab data and quiz.

### Modern World History: WWII assessment
- Objective: Synthesize WWII causes and consequences
- Vocabulary: synthesis
- Materials: unit documents
- Assessment: DBQ and unit test.

### Spanish III: Subjuntivo con emociones y duda
- Objective: Use subjunctive after emotion and doubt
- Vocabulary: alegrarse de que, dudar que, no creer que, es posible que
- Materials: Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: Podcasting: planning and scripting
- Objective: Plan and script a podcast episode
- Vocabulary: segment, script, interview, show notes
- Materials: Radio Ambulante or NPR podcast examples
- Assessment: Script.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Cultural and social dance
- Objective: Perform dances from various cultures
- Vocabulary: folk dance, salsa, line dance, tempo
- Materials: music, open space
- Assessment: Performance.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Understanding mental health
- Objective: Describe common conditions and treatments
- Vocabulary: depression, anxiety, stigma, therapy
- Materials: KidsHealth
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Data visualization
- Objective: Create visualizations that communicate patterns
- Objective: Choose an appropriate chart type for a question
- Vocabulary: chart, pivot table, correlation, trend, outlier
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account, Google Sheets
- Assessment: Data story presentation scored with rubric.
