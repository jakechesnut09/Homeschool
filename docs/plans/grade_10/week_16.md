## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Things Fall Apart Parts 2–3: Colonial arrival
- Objective: Analyze the colonial encounter from an Igbo perspective
- Objective: Connect title to Yeats
- Vocabulary: missionary, colonialism, District Commissioner, allusion
- Materials: Things Fall Apart, Yeats 'The Second Coming' (Poetry Foundation)
- Assessment: Quiz and final-paragraph analysis.

### Geometry: Similar triangles
- Objective: Establish AA similarity
- Objective: Solve problems with SAS~ and SSS~
- Vocabulary: AA similarity, proportion, indirect measurement
- Materials: mirror, tape measure
- Assessment: Lab report and quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Specific heat and the second law
- Objective: Measure specific heat
- Objective: Show thermal energy flows to equilibrium
- Vocabulary: specific heat capacity, second law of thermodynamics, entropy, closed system
- Materials: foam cups with lids, thermometer, metal washers, water
- Assessment: Spreadsheet model and lab report.

### Modern World History: Treaty of Versailles and the postwar world
- Objective: Evaluate the Fourteen Points and Versailles
- Objective: Explain border changes and disillusionment
- Vocabulary: Fourteen Points, reparations, League of Nations, mandate system, Lost Generation
- Materials: Wilson's Fourteen Points (National Archives), Treaty of Versailles Article 231, Keynes excerpt
- Assessment: Unit 5 test.

### Spanish III: El presente de subjuntivo
- Objective: Form the present subjunctive
- Objective: Use it after expressions of will and influence
- Vocabulary: querer que, recomendar que, es importante que, ojalá
- Materials: Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: World music and music criticism
- Objective: Analyze music from diverse cultures
- Objective: Write a critique
- Vocabulary: gamelan, raga, mariachi, timbre, criteria
- Materials: Smithsonian Folkways
- Assessment: Music review.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Muscular strength and endurance training
- Objective: Perform resistance exercises with correct form
- Vocabulary: repetition, set, progressive overload, antagonist
- Materials: resistance bands, dumbbells or water jugs
- Assessment: Training log.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Distracted and drowsy driving
- Objective: Explain distraction types and phone laws
- Vocabulary: visual distraction, manual distraction, cognitive distraction, drowsy driving
- Materials: handbook, reaction-time app
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Computer Science Principles: Loops
- Objective: Use while and for loops
- Objective: Choose the appropriate loop structure
- Vocabulary: loop, iteration, while, for, infinite loop
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Loop quiz.
