## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Sections 4–5* (55 min): Read sections 4–5 and record moments of dehumanization and resistance.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Inverse trig* (60 min): Find missing angles with inverse trig and completely solve right triangles.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Mole ratios* (60 min): Write mole ratios from balanced equations and solve mole-to-mole problems.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Blitzkrieg to Britain* (55 min): Map 1939–1941 Axis conquests and Battle of Britain.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Martí* (50 min): Read 'Yo soy un hombre sincero' and identify imagery.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Design principles* (45 min): Learn CRAP (contrast, repetition, alignment, proximity).
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Throwing & catching* (45 min): Practice the spiral pass (or handball jump shot) 50 reps at 10, 15 and 20 yards and catch with 'diamond' hands.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Adult choking* (40 min): Practice abdominal thrusts on a pillow-wrapped chair back and back blows, then self-rescue thrusts against a chair.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Linear search* (50 min): Search for a target card in a shuffled deck of 52 cards one at a time, counting steps over five trials.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Sections 6–7* (55 min): Read sections 6–7 (the march and train) and chart Eliezer's responsibility toward his father.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Build a clinometer* (60 min): Build a clinometer from a protractor, straw and weighted string.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Mass-to-mass* (60 min): Solve gram-to-gram stoichiometry problems.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Eastern Front* (55 min): Analyze Stalingrad as a turning point.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Neruda* (50 min): Read 'Oda al tomate' and list its metaphors.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Typography* (45 min): Compare serif, sans-serif and display fonts.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Design plays* (45 min): Draw three offensive plays (e.g., slant-flat, curl, post) with routes and timing on a play card.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Child CPR* (40 min): Learn child CPR differences (one or two hands, about 2 inches deep) and practice 5 cycles of 30:2 on a manikin or pillow.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Binary search* (50 min): Search a sorted deck using binary search and compare the steps.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Sections 8–9* (55 min): Finish the book and analyze the mirror image in the final paragraph.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Field lab* (60 min): Measure the height of a building or tree using the clinometer and trig; write a lab report.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Predict & test* (60 min): Predict the mass of CO₂ lost when 5.0 g baking soda reacts with excess vinegar; measure it.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Pacific* (55 min): Listen to FDR's 'Day of Infamy' speech and map Pearl Harbor, Midway and island hopping.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Mistral* (50 min): Read a Gabriela Mistral poem and discuss its themes.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Album cover* (45 min): Design an album cover for your Semester 1 song.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Defense* (45 min): Practice man-to-man vs. zone coverage with a partner, switching every 5 minutes.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Infant CPR* (40 min): Practice infant CPR on a doll using two fingers or two thumbs, about 1.5 inches deep, at 100–120 per minute.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Efficiency* (50 min): Graph worst-case steps vs. list size (8 to 1,024) for linear and binary search and describe the growth as linear vs. logarithmic.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 104, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Nobel speech* (55 min): Read Wiesel's 1986 Nobel acceptance speech and connect his call 'to take sides' to the memoir.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Applications* (60 min): Solve ramp-design problems meeting ADA slope requirements and navigation problems.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Percent yield* (60 min): Calculate percent yield and analyze sources of error.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *D-Day* (55 min): Analyze Eisenhower's order of the day and his unused failure note.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Mi oda* (50 min): Write an 'Oda a…' about an everyday object.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Feedback* (45 min): Get feedback and revise.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Game* (45 min): Play 3-on-3 or 4-on-4 games running your plays and tracking completions.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Infant choking* (40 min): Practice infant choking relief with 5 back blows and 5 chest thrusts on a doll, supporting the head.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Across disciplines* (50 min): Show how binary search applies to a dictionary, debugging and guessing games.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Night sections 4–9
- Objective: Analyze father-son relationship
- Objective: Determine central idea
- Vocabulary: dehumanization, selection, death march, liberation
- Materials: Night
- Assessment: Seminar participation and written exit response.

### Geometry: Solving right triangles and applications
- Objective: Use inverse trig to find angles
- Objective: Solve angle of elevation/depression problems
- Vocabulary: inverse sine, angle of elevation, angle of depression, clinometer
- Materials: clinometer (protractor, straw, string, washer), tape measure
- Assessment: Lab report and quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Stoichiometry
- Objective: Use mole ratios to predict product amounts
- Vocabulary: mole ratio, stoichiometry, theoretical yield
- Materials: baking soda, vinegar, balloons, kitchen scale
- Assessment: Lab and quiz.

### Modern World History: The course of World War II
- Objective: Identify turning points
- Objective: Locate Allied and Axis powers
- Vocabulary: blitzkrieg, Battle of Britain, Stalingrad, D-Day, Pearl Harbor, Midway
- Materials: WWII maps, FDR 'Day of Infamy' speech, Eisenhower's D-Day order
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish III: La poesía
- Objective: Read and interpret poems
- Objective: Write a poem
- Vocabulary: el verso, la estrofa, la rima, la metáfora
- Materials: José Martí 'Versos sencillos' I, Gabriela Mistral and Pablo Neruda 'Oda al tomate'
- Assessment: Recital and ode.

### Music & Media Arts: Graphic design and typography
- Objective: Apply design principles
- Objective: Create an album cover
- Vocabulary: typography, hierarchy, contrast, alignment, color theory
- Materials: free design tool (Canva or Photopea)
- Assessment: Album cover.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Flag football or team handball
- Objective: Apply invasion-game strategy in a new sport (flag football or team handball)
- Vocabulary: backhand, forehand flick, stall count, spirit of the game
- Materials: flying disc, cones
- Assessment: Spirit score and skills.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Choking and child/infant CPR
- Objective: Perform choking relief
- Objective: Adapt CPR for children and infants
- Vocabulary: abdominal thrusts, back blows, infant CPR
- Materials: doll
- Assessment: Choking and pediatric CPR skills checklist.

### Computer Science Principles: Searching and algorithm efficiency
- Objective: Compare linear and binary search
- Objective: Analyze algorithm efficiency
- Vocabulary: linear search, binary search, efficiency, reasonable time
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account, deck of cards
- Assessment: Efficiency questions.
