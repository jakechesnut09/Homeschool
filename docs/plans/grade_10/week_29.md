## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Fahrenheit 451 Parts 2–3
- Objective: Trace Montag's transformation
- Objective: Determine theme
- Vocabulary: allusion, Phoenix, dynamic character
- Materials: Fahrenheit 451, Dover Beach (Matthew Arnold)
- Assessment: Seminar rubric and outline.

### Geometry: Volume
- Objective: Derive volume formulas using Cavalieri's principle
- Objective: Solve volume problems
- Vocabulary: Cavalieri's principle, volume, oblique
- Materials: stack of coins, paper cone and cylinder, rice
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Dynamic equilibrium
- Objective: Describe reversible reactions and equilibrium
- Vocabulary: equilibrium, reversible reaction, dynamic, equilibrium constant
- Materials: two cups, water, straws of different sizes
- Assessment: Model graph and quiz.

### Modern World History: The end of the Cold War
- Objective: Analyze the collapse of the Soviet Union
- Vocabulary: glasnost, perestroika, Berlin Wall, command economy
- Materials: Reagan's 'Tear Down This Wall' speech, Gorbachev's resignation speech
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Spanish III: Los medios de comunicación
- Objective: Analyze news and advertising in Spanish
- Vocabulary: el noticiero, el titular, la publicidad, la noticia falsa
- Materials: BBC Mundo or Univision news articles
- Assessment: Newscast rubric.

### Music & Media Arts: Film scoring
- Objective: Analyze and compose film music
- Vocabulary: leitmotif, underscore, diegetic, non-diegetic
- Materials: film clips, BandLab
- Assessment: Scored scene.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Gymnastics routine assessment
- Objective: Perform a routine
- Vocabulary: routine, transition
- Materials: mat
- Assessment: Routine rubric.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Refusal skills and advocacy
- Objective: Demonstrate refusal skills
- Objective: Advocate for substance-free choices
- Vocabulary: refusal skill, advocacy, peer pressure
- Materials: scenario cards, poster materials or phone
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Computer Science Principles: Cybersecurity threats and protection
- Objective: Identify common cyberattacks
- Objective: Compare security measures for different threats
- Vocabulary: phishing, malware, ransomware, DDoS, multifactor authentication
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Family cybersecurity guide.
