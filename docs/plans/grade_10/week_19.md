## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Poetry of the Great War
- Objective: Analyze tone and imagery in WWI poetry
- Objective: Compare perspectives on war
- Vocabulary: imagery, tone, irony, elegy, caesura
- Materials: Owen 'Dulce et Decorum Est', 'Anthem for Doomed Youth'; Brooke 'The Soldier'; McCrae 'In Flanders Fields'; Sassoon 'Suicide in the Trenches'
- Assessment: Comparative poetry response.

### Geometry: Pythagorean theorem and special right triangles
- Objective: Apply Pythagorean theorem and converse
- Objective: Use 45-45-90 and 30-60-90 ratios
- Vocabulary: Pythagorean triple, converse, special right triangle
- Materials: square tiles, calculator
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Chemical equations and conservation of mass
- Objective: Balance chemical equations
- Objective: Show mass conservation
- Vocabulary: balanced equation, coefficient, subscript, law of conservation of mass
- Materials: baking soda, vinegar, zip bag, balloon, kitchen scale
- Assessment: CER and balancing quiz.

### Modern World History: Fascism in Italy and Nazi Germany
- Objective: Compare Fascist and Communist regimes
- Objective: Explain how Hitler rose to power
- Vocabulary: fascism, totalitarianism, Weimar Republic, hyperinflation, Nuremberg Laws, Great Depression
- Materials: Mussolini's 'Doctrine of Fascism' excerpt, Nazi propaganda (USHMM), 1923 hyperinflation photographs
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Spanish III: El arte hispano
- Objective: Describe and interpret art
- Vocabulary: el cuadro, el mural, el autorretrato, el fondo, representar
- Materials: images of works by Frida Kahlo, Diego Rivera, Velázquez, Picasso
- Assessment: Museum label.

### Music & Media Arts: Photography: composition
- Objective: Apply composition principles
- Vocabulary: rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, depth of field
- Materials: phone camera
- Assessment: Photo set.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Soccer skills
- Objective: Dribble, pass and shoot
- Vocabulary: first touch, through ball, give-and-go
- Materials: soccer ball, cones
- Assessment: Skill test.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Emergency action steps
- Objective: Apply check-call-care
- Vocabulary: check-call-care, scene safety, Good Samaritan law, consent
- Materials: Red Cross First Aid app, first aid kit
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Procedures with parameters and return values
- Objective: Write procedures with parameters
- Objective: Use return values
- Vocabulary: procedure, parameter, argument, return, procedural abstraction
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Procedure quiz.
