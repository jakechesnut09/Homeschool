# Music & Media Arts (Grade 10)

A year-long arts course that combines music literacy and creation with digital media arts. Semester 1 builds notation, rhythm, harmony, songwriting and critical listening across world and American music traditions, ending in a recorded showcase. Semester 2 applies those skills to media arts: photography and design, audio production and podcasting, video editing and film scoring, culminating in an original multimedia capstone. All production uses free browser tools (BandLab, Chrome Music Lab, Audacity, free video editors).

*Framework:* 2019 California Arts Standards for Public Schools (Music and Media Arts, High School Proficient)  
*Time:* 45 min/day
*UC/CSU a–g:* f · *Credits:* 10.0

## Units

### Unit 1: Music Literacy Foundations (weeks 1–4)
Notation is a code for sound that lets musicians share ideas across time.

- Week 1: Pitch, staff and clefs
- Week 2: Rhythm notation and meter
- Week 3: Scales, keys and intervals
- Week 4: Sight-reading and performance

### Unit 2: Rhythm, Groove and Beat-Making (weeks 5–8)
Rhythm organizes time and defines musical styles across cultures.

- Week 5: Rhythms of the world
- Week 6: Drum patterns and beat-making
- Week 7: Loops, bass lines and arrangement
- Week 8: Beat-making project

### Unit 3: Melody, Harmony and Songwriting (weeks 9–13)
Melodies and chord progressions create emotion and form in songs.

- Week 9: Chords and chord progressions
- Week 10: Melody writing
- Week 11: Lyrics and songwriting
- Week 12: Songwriting: putting it together
- Week 13: Songwriting project final

### Unit 4: Listening Across Time and Cultures; Showcase (weeks 14–18)
Music reflects and shapes the history and values of the people who make it.

- Week 14: Western art music: Baroque to Romantic
- Week 15: American music: spirituals, blues, jazz and rock
- Week 16: World music and music criticism
- Week 17: Showcase preparation
- Week 18: Semester showcase and exam

### Unit 5: Media Arts Foundations: Image and Design (weeks 19–22)
Visual composition guides how viewers read meaning in images.

- Week 19: Photography: composition
- Week 20: Photo editing and visual storytelling
- Week 21: Graphic design and typography
- Week 22: Copyright, fair use and media ethics

### Unit 6: Audio Production and Podcasting (weeks 23–26)
Sound design and storytelling create immersive audio experiences.

- Week 23: Sound design and recording
- Week 24: Podcasting: planning and scripting
- Week 25: Podcasting: production
- Week 26: Podcast publish and critique

### Unit 7: Video and Film Scoring (weeks 27–31)
Picture, sound and music combine to shape a story's emotion.

- Week 27: Film language
- Week 28: Shooting and editing video
- Week 29: Film scoring
- Week 30: Music video
- Week 31: Video unit assessment

### Unit 8: Multimedia Capstone (weeks 32–36)
Artists integrate music and media to communicate a personal vision.

- Week 32: Capstone proposal
- Week 33: Capstone production I
- Week 34: Capstone production II
- Week 35: Capstone exhibition
- Week 36: Final portfolio and reflection

## Standards

- **MA:Cn10.1.HSP** Access, evaluate, and integrate personal and external resources to inform the creation of original media artworks.
- **MA:Cn11.1.HSP** Demonstrate and explain how media artworks and ideas relate to various contexts, purposes, and values, including ethics, copyright and fair use.
- **MA:Cr1.1.HSP** Use identified generative methods to formulate multiple ideas, develop artistic goals, and problem solve in media arts creation processes.
- **MA:Cr2.1.HSP** Apply aesthetic criteria in developing, proposing, and refining artistic ideas, plans, prototypes, and production processes for media arts productions.
- **MA:Cr3.1.HSP** Consolidate production processes to demonstrate deliberate choices in organizing and integrating content and stylistic conventions to show purpose and meaning.
- **MA:Pr4.1.HSP** Integrate various arts, media arts forms, and content into unified media arts productions that convey consistent perspectives and narratives.
- **MA:Pr5.1.HSP** Develop and refine a range of artistic, design, technical, and soft skills through performing various roles in producing media artworks.
- **MA:Pr6.1.HSP** Design the presentation and distribution of media artworks for intentional impacts through a variety of contexts.
- **MA:Re7.1.HSP** Analyze and describe the reactions and interpretations of media artworks, considering their intent and context.
- **MA:Re8.1.HSP** Analyze the intent, meanings, and impacts of diverse media artworks, considering complex factors of context and bias.
- **MA:Re9.1.HSP** Evaluate media art works and production processes with developed criteria, considering context and artistic goals.
- **MU:Cn10.1.HSP** Demonstrate how interests, knowledge, and skills relate to personal choices and intent when creating, performing, and responding to music.
- **MU:Cn11.1.HSP** Demonstrate understanding of relationships between music and the other arts, other disciplines, varied contexts, and daily life.
- **MU:Cr1.1.HSP** Compose and improvise melodic and rhythmic ideas or motives that reflect characteristic(s) of music from a variety of historical periods.
- **MU:Cr2.1.HSP** Assemble and organize sounds or short musical ideas to create initial expressions of selected experiences, moods, images, or storylines.
- **MU:Cr3.1.HSP** Evaluate and refine draft compositions and improvisations based on knowledge, skill, and teacher-provided criteria.
- **MU:Cr3.2.HSP** Share music through the use of notation, performance, or technology, and demonstrate how the elements of music have been employed.
- **MU:Pr4.1.HSP** Select music to perform based on interest, knowledge, technical skill, and context.
- **MU:Pr4.2.HSP** Analyze the structure, context, and performance decisions of selected works and how they inform interpretation, including reading notation.
- **MU:Pr4.3.HSP** Demonstrate understanding and application of expressive qualities in a varied repertoire.
- **MU:Pr5.1.HSP** Develop strategies to address technical challenges and evaluate the success of rehearsal strategies.
- **MU:Pr6.1.HSP** Perform with technical accuracy and expression, demonstrating an understanding of context and audience.
- **MU:Re7.1.HSP** Explain reasons for selecting music, citing characteristics found in the music and connections to interest, purpose, and context.
- **MU:Re7.2.HSP** Explain how the analysis of structures and context informs the response to music.
- **MU:Re8.1.HSP** Explain and support interpretations of the expressive intent and meaning of musical works, citing elements, contexts, and personal research.
- **MU:Re9.1.HSP** Evaluate works and performances based on personally or collaboratively developed criteria.
