## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Draft body* (55 min): Draft body paragraphs with at least two sources per paragraph.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Area review* (60 min): Review area formulas for triangles, parallelograms and trapezoids with derivations.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Solutions survey* (60 min): Research three solutions (solar, EVs, carbon capture) and their chemistry.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *India* (55 min): Read Gandhi's letter to Lord Irwin and Nehru's 'Tryst with Destiny'; analyze partition.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Vocabulario* (50 min): Learn technology vocabulary.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Shot types* (45 min): Watch a 3-minute scene from a classic film (e.g., Casablanca airport scene) and log each shot as wide, medium, close-up or extreme close-up.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Safety & landings* (45 min): Learn safe landing positions, mat setup and spotting roles for tumbling.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Alcohol* (40 min): Learn how alcohol affects the brain and body, BAC factors, and signs of alcohol poisoning (call 911).
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *PII inventory* (50 min): List the personally identifiable information you have shared with apps and websites and rate the risk of each item.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 132, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Intro & conclusion* (55 min): Draft the introduction hook and conclusion that addresses 'so what?'
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Regular polygons* (60 min): Find the area of regular polygons using apothem and trig.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Trade-off matrix* (60 min): Build a decision matrix weighing cost, CO₂ reduction and feasibility.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Africa* (55 min): Analyze Nkrumah's 1957 speech and map African independence dates.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Encuesta* (50 min): Survey family on tech use and graph the results with Spanish labels.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Camera angles* (45 min): Photograph the same object at high, low, eye-level and Dutch angles and write what feeling each angle creates.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Rolls* (45 min): Practice forward and backward rolls (and log roll progressions) 10 times each with a spotter.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Nicotine & vaping* (40 min): Read NIDA information on nicotine addiction and vaping ingredients; analyze a vape ad for targeting of teens.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Tracking* (50 min): Inspect cookies and site permissions in your browser, then use a tracker-visualizer extension or privacy report to count third-party trackers on a news site.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Research paper: drafting and presenting
- Objective: Write a 1,500-word research-based argument
- Objective: Present findings
- Vocabulary: Works Cited, hanging indent, signal phrase
- Materials: outline and notes, word processor
- Assessment: Research paper rubric and presentation rubric.

### Geometry: Area of polygons and composite figures
- Objective: Find areas of regular polygons and composite figures
- Vocabulary: apothem, composite figure, regular polygon
- Materials: graph paper, floor plan
- Assessment: Floor plan project and quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Climate solutions
- Objective: Evaluate technological solutions to reduce emissions
- Vocabulary: mitigation, carbon capture, renewable energy, trade-off
- Materials: research sources
- Assessment: Position brief and unit test.

### Modern World History: Decolonization in South Asia, Africa and the Middle East
- Objective: Explain independence movements
- Objective: Analyze creation of Israel and nationalism in the Middle East
- Vocabulary: decolonization, partition, Pan-Africanism, Zionism, apartheid
- Materials: Gandhi's Salt March letter, Nehru 'Tryst with Destiny', Nkrumah independence speech, Balfour Declaration
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish III: La tecnología
- Objective: Discuss technology use
- Vocabulary: la red social, descargar, la aplicación, la contraseña
- Materials: Spanish tech articles
- Assessment: Quiz and debate.

### Music & Media Arts: Film language
- Objective: Analyze camera shots and editing
- Vocabulary: shot, angle, cut, montage, continuity
- Materials: film clips
- Assessment: Quiz and storyboard.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Tumbling fundamentals
- Objective: Perform rolls and balances safely
- Vocabulary: forward roll, balance, center of gravity, spotting
- Materials: mats
- Assessment: Skill checklist.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Alcohol, nicotine and vaping
- Objective: Explain effects
- Vocabulary: nicotine, vaping, addiction, BAC, alcohol poisoning
- Materials: NIDA for Teens articles, vape advertisement examples
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Privacy and personal data
- Objective: Identify personally identifiable information
- Objective: Evaluate the tradeoffs of automated data collection
- Vocabulary: PII, cookies, tracking, privacy policy, CCPA
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Privacy argument essay.
