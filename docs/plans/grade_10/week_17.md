## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Literary analysis essay: Things Fall Apart
- Objective: Write a multi-paragraph literary analysis
- Objective: Use MLA citations
- Vocabulary: thesis, MLA in-text citation, embedded quotation, semicolon
- Materials: Things Fall Apart, Purdue OWL MLA guide
- Assessment: Draft checkpoint conference.

### Geometry: Proportionality theorems
- Objective: Prove the Triangle Proportionality Theorem
- Objective: Prove the Pythagorean theorem using similarity
- Vocabulary: Triangle Proportionality Theorem, geometric mean, angle bisector theorem
- Materials: GeoGebra
- Assessment: Exit tickets and proof write-up.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Earth's interior and convection
- Objective: Model mantle convection
- Objective: Explain Earth's internal heat sources
- Vocabulary: convection, mantle, radioactive heating, plate tectonics, seismic wave
- Materials: clear pot, water, food coloring, stove, rice grains
- Assessment: Device test data and model diagram.

### Modern World History: The Russian Revolution and Stalin
- Objective: Explain causes and consequences of the Russian Revolution
- Objective: Analyze Stalin's totalitarian rule
- Vocabulary: Bolshevik, Lenin, collectivization, Holodomor, Great Purge, five-year plan
- Materials: Lenin's April Theses excerpt, Holodomor survivor testimony, Soviet propaganda posters
- Assessment: Quiz and photo analysis.

### Spanish III: Soluciones verdes
- Objective: Propose solutions using the subjunctive
- Vocabulary: proteger, la energía renovable, el voluntariado
- Materials: article on Costa Rica's renewable energy
- Assessment: PSA rubric.

### Music & Media Arts: Showcase preparation
- Objective: Prepare a performance and portfolio
- Vocabulary: program notes, stage presence
- Materials: instrument/voice, recordings
- Assessment: Rehearsal log.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Nutrition, hydration and recovery
- Objective: Connect nutrition and sleep to performance
- Vocabulary: macronutrient, hydration, recovery, sleep
- Materials: food log
- Assessment: Food and sleep analysis.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Collisions, insurance and emergencies
- Objective: Explain what to do after a collision
- Objective: Know financial responsibility
- Vocabulary: SR-1, financial responsibility, point count, negligent operator
- Materials: handbook
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Traversals: filter, map and reduce
- Objective: Traverse a list to process every element
- Objective: Write filter, map and reduce patterns
- Vocabulary: traversal, filter, map, reduce, FOR EACH
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Traversal problem set.
