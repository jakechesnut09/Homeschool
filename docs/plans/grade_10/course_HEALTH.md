# Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2) (Grade 10)

Semester 1 is a classroom Driver Education course built on the California DMV Driver Handbook, covering the topics required before a minor may apply for an instruction permit at 15½: licensing and the provisional (GDL) rules, vehicle controls, signs and pavement markings, right-of-way, speed laws, lane use and parking, freeway driving, sharing the road, hazardous conditions, impaired and distracted driving, and collisions and insurance; it ends with full-length practice knowledge tests. Semester 2 combines First Aid, CPR and AED skills with Health II topics: mental and emotional health, substance use prevention (including fentanyl awareness), nutrition and consumer health, healthy relationships, and community and environmental health.

*Framework:* California DMV Driver Handbook (current edition) and CA Education Code driver-education requirements; 2008 California Health Education Content Standards (Grades 9–12); 2019 CA Health Education Framework  
*Time:* 40 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Licensing, the Vehicle and Driver Readiness (weeks 1–3)
Driving is a privilege earned step by step through California's graduated licensing system.

- Week 1: Graduated driver licensing in California
- Week 2: Vehicle controls and pre-drive routine
- Week 3: Occupant protection

### Unit 2: Signs, Signals, Markings and Right-of-Way (weeks 4–7)
Shared rules make traffic predictable and safe.

- Week 4: Traffic signs
- Week 5: Traffic signals and pavement markings
- Week 6: Right-of-way at intersections
- Week 7: Speed laws and following distance

### Unit 3: Navigating Roadways and Sharing the Road (weeks 8–12)
Safe drivers position, signal and communicate with every road user.

- Week 8: Turns, signals and lane changes
- Week 9: Passing and lane positioning
- Week 10: Parking
- Week 11: Freeway driving
- Week 12: Sharing the road

### Unit 4: Hazards, Impairment and Distraction (weeks 13–16)
Most collisions are preventable when drivers manage risk, stay sober and stay focused.

- Week 13: Hazardous conditions
- Week 14: Defensive driving and the Move Over law
- Week 15: Alcohol, drugs and driving
- Week 16: Distracted and drowsy driving

### Unit 5: Collisions, Insurance and Permit Test Prep (weeks 17–18)
Knowing the law and what to do in emergencies prepares you for the permit test and the road.

- Week 17: Collisions, insurance and emergencies
- Week 18: Permit test preparation and semester final

### Unit 6: First Aid, CPR and AED (weeks 19–23)
Trained bystanders save lives in the first minutes of an emergency.

- Week 19: Emergency action steps
- Week 20: CPR and AED
- Week 21: Choking and child/infant CPR
- Week 22: Injuries and sudden illness
- Week 23: Environmental emergencies and certification

### Unit 7: Mental and Emotional Health (weeks 24–26)
Mental health is health; skills and support help everyone cope.

- Week 24: Understanding mental health
- Week 25: Stress, coping and communication
- Week 26: Suicide prevention

### Unit 8: Substance Use Prevention (weeks 27–29)
Accurate information and refusal skills protect health and futures.

- Week 27: Alcohol, nicotine and vaping
- Week 28: Cannabis, opioids and fentanyl
- Week 29: Refusal skills and advocacy

### Unit 9: Nutrition, Consumer and Environmental Health (weeks 30–32)
Informed choices about food, products and environment shape lifelong health.

- Week 30: Nutrition for health and performance
- Week 31: Consumer health and health care access
- Week 32: Environmental health in California

### Unit 10: Healthy Relationships and Final Assessment (weeks 33–36)
Respect, consent and communication are the foundation of healthy relationships.

- Week 33: Healthy relationships and consent
- Week 34: Sexual health and services
- Week 35: Cumulative review
- Week 36: Final exam and personal health plan

## Standards

- **DE-1** Licensing: instruction permit requirements (age 15½, driver ed, knowledge and vision tests), provisional license rules (6-month permit, 50 supervised hours incl. 10 at night, passenger and 11 p.m.–5 a.m. restrictions), and license classes.
- **DE-10** Alcohol and drugs: effects on driving, BAC limits (0.08 adults; 0.01 under 21), implied consent, open-container and DUI penalties.
- **DE-11** Distracted and drowsy driving: California cell-phone laws (minors may not use any wireless device), and fatigue management.
- **DE-12** Collisions and emergencies: what to do after a collision, reporting (SR-1), insurance and financial responsibility, vehicle emergencies, and the point count system.
- **DE-13** Railroad crossings, school buses, work zones, and special road situations.
- **DE-14** Preparation for the DMV knowledge test: test-taking strategies and full practice examinations.
- **DE-2** Vehicle readiness: controls, instruments, pre-drive checks, seat/mirror adjustment, seat belts, airbags, and child passenger restraints.
- **DE-3** Traffic signs (shapes and colors), traffic signals, and pavement markings and lane lines; curb colors.
- **DE-4** Right-of-way rules at intersections, roundabouts, crosswalks, and for pedestrians and emergency vehicles.
- **DE-5** Turning, signaling, lane changes, passing, U-turns, lane positioning, and parking (including hills and parallel parking).
- **DE-6** Speed laws: the Basic Speed Law, maximum and posted limits, school and residential zones, and following distance.
- **DE-7** Sharing the road with pedestrians, bicyclists (3-foot rule), motorcyclists, large trucks, buses, light rail, and slow-moving vehicles.
- **DE-8** Freeway driving: entering, merging, exiting, carpool/HOV lanes, and interchanges.
- **DE-9** Hazardous conditions and defensive driving: night, rain, fog, wind, smoke, skids, space cushions, scanning, and the Move Over law.
