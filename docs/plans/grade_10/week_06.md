## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Scene 2* (55 min): Read Scene 2 (the confrontation) and annotate each rhetorical move Antigone and Creon make.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Compositions* (60 min): Perform a translation followed by a reflection and compare to reversing the order.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Build a spectroscope* (60 min): Build a spectroscope from a CD and cereal box and observe incandescent, LED and fluorescent lights.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Agricultural revolution* (55 min): Read about enclosure and crop rotation and explain links to population growth.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Reglas* (50 min): Learn the rules of preterite vs. imperfect (background vs. action) with a timeline graphic.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *DAW tour* (45 min): Set up a BandLab account and learn the interface.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Grip & core* (45 min): Do a grip and core strength workout (dead hangs, planks, farmer carries).
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Intersections* (40 min): Use toy cars on paper intersections to model four-way stops, uncontrolled and T-intersections.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *What is the internet?* (50 min): Watch the Code.org 'How the Internet Works' video on wires, cables and Wi-Fi, then draw your home network.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Stichomythia practice* (55 min): Perform the rapid line exchange aloud with a partner and explain how the form heightens conflict.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Two reflections* (60 min): Show reflecting over two parallel lines is a translation and over intersecting lines is a rotation.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Emission spectra* (60 min): Watch flame-test video demonstrations and match colors to elements.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Why Britain?* (55 min): Map Britain's coal, iron, rivers and ports and list five factors that favored industrialization.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Práctica* (50 min): Complete a 30-item cloze passage choosing preterite or imperfect.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Rock beat* (45 min): Program a basic rock beat at 100 bpm.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Climbing technique* (45 min): Learn footwork and three points of contact via video and practice on low structures.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Roundabouts* (40 min): Study roundabout rules (yield to traffic in the circle, travel counterclockwise) and diagram entering and exiting.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *IP addresses* (50 min): Find your device's IP address and compare IPv4 (32-bit) and IPv6 (128-bit) address spaces mathematically.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Ode 2 & figurative language* (55 min): Read Ode 2 and identify the extended metaphor of the curse as a storm; explain its effect in a short paragraph.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Find the sequence* (60 min): Given a figure and its image, write a sequence of rigid motions mapping one onto the other (8 problems).
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Energy levels* (60 min): Use the Bohr model to explain why each element has a unique spectrum.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Technology* (55 min): Create an illustrated chart of inventions (spinning jenny, water frame, steam engine, railroad).
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Cómic* (50 min): Narrate a six-panel comic strip in the past using both tenses.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Hip-hop beat* (45 min): Program a hip-hop beat with swing.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Bouldering or traverse* (45 min): Visit a climbing gym or practice low traverses with spotting.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Pedestrians* (40 min): Learn pedestrian right-of-way at marked and unmarked crosswalks and blind pedestrians with white canes or guide dogs.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Packets* (50 min): Use the Code.org Internet Simulator to send a message split into packets and observe packets arriving out of order.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Scene 3 – Haemon* (55 min): Read Scene 3 and evaluate Haemon's arguments to his father; list his three strongest points.

- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Emergency vehicles & school buses* (40 min): Learn to pull right and stop for emergency vehicles and to stop for flashing red school bus lights.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Packet reassembly* (50 min): Design a packet header with sequence numbers and send a 'postcard' message to a family member split across 6 cards.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Antigone: Creon vs. Antigone
- Objective: Analyze how conflict develops character
- Objective: Track the role of the chorus
- Vocabulary: foil, stichomythia, decree, conscience
- Materials: Antigone text, character chart
- Assessment: Quiz and annotation check.

### Geometry: Compositions of transformations
- Objective: Specify sequences of rigid motions
- Objective: Predict effects of compositions
- Vocabulary: composition, glide reflection, mapping
- Materials: graph paper, GeoGebra
- Assessment: Tessellation and quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Electrons and light
- Objective: Relate electron energy levels to emitted light
- Objective: Write electron configurations
- Vocabulary: energy level, emission spectrum, photon, electron configuration, valence electron
- Materials: DIY spectroscope (CD + cereal box), PhET Models of the Hydrogen Atom
- Assessment: Spectroscope observations and quiz.

### Modern World History: Origins of the Industrial Revolution
- Objective: Explain why Britain industrialized first
- Objective: Describe new technologies and energy
- Vocabulary: enclosure, textile, steam engine, capital, factory system
- Materials: maps of British coal and rivers, images of Watt's steam engine
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish III: Pretérito vs. imperfecto
- Objective: Choose preterite or imperfect for storytelling
- Vocabulary: mientras, cuando, de repente, interrumpir
- Materials: short story text, comic strips
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: Drum patterns and beat-making
- Objective: Program drum patterns in a DAW
- Vocabulary: DAW, kick, snare, hi-hat, quantize, BPM
- Materials: BandLab
- Assessment: Beat portfolio.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Climbing and balance challenges
- Objective: Demonstrate climbing technique and safety
- Vocabulary: belay, spotting, three points of contact, bouldering
- Materials: local climbing gym or playground structures, slackline (optional)
- Assessment: Skill checklist.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Right-of-way at intersections
- Objective: Apply right-of-way rules
- Vocabulary: right-of-way, uncontrolled intersection, four-way stop, T-intersection, roundabout
- Materials: toy cars, intersection diagrams
- Assessment: Scenario quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Networks, IP addresses and packets
- Objective: Describe how devices are addressed on the internet
- Objective: Explain how data is broken into packets
- Vocabulary: network, IP address, packet, protocol, bandwidth
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Internet Simulator log and quiz.
