## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Rhetorical appeals* (55 min): Learn ethos/pathos/logos and identify each in three modern ads or speeches.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *SSS & SAS proofs* (60 min): Write four two-column proofs using SSS and SAS.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Lewis structures* (60 min): Draw Lewis structures for H₂O, CO₂, CH₄, NH₃, O₂ and N₂.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *East India Company* (55 min): Trace the East India Company's rise and the 1857 rebellion.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Situaciones* (50 min): Role-play lost luggage, missed flight and hotel complaint scenarios.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Lyric analysis* (45 min): Analyze lyrics of a classic song for hook and rhyme scheme.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: The funeral orations: rhetoric in action
- Objective: Identify ethos, pathos, logos
- Objective: Compare speeches' persuasive techniques
- Vocabulary: ethos, pathos, logos, repetition, rhetorical question, verbal irony
- Materials: Folger Julius Caesar 3.2, video of Antony's speech
- Assessment: Performance rubric and timed response.

### Geometry: Triangle congruence proofs
- Objective: Write congruence proofs using SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, HL
- Objective: Use CPCTC
- Vocabulary: CPCTC, hypotenuse-leg, overlapping triangles
- Materials: proof templates
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Covalent bonding and molecular shape
- Objective: Draw Lewis structures
- Objective: Predict molecular geometry with VSEPR
- Vocabulary: covalent bond, Lewis structure, VSEPR, polar, nonpolar, double bond
- Materials: gumdrops and toothpicks, PhET Molecule Shapes
- Assessment: Model photos and quiz.

### Modern World History: Imperialism in India and Southeast Asia
- Objective: Analyze British rule in India
- Objective: Compare imperial rule in Southeast Asia and the Philippines
- Vocabulary: Raj, Sepoy Mutiny, Indian National Congress, Philippine-American War
- Materials: Macaulay's Minute on Indian Education, Dadabhai Naoroji on drain of wealth, Aguinaldo proclamation
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish III: Problemas en el viaje y mandatos formales
- Objective: Ask for help and give advice
- Vocabulary: perder, la maleta, el retraso, quejarse
- Materials: role-play cards
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Music & Media Arts: Lyrics and songwriting
- Objective: Write song lyrics with structure
- Vocabulary: verse, chorus, hook, rhyme scheme, prosody
- Materials: lyric notebook
- Assessment: Lyric draft.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Self-defense assessment
- Objective: Combine skills in scenarios
- Vocabulary: scenario, escape route
- Materials: mat
- Assessment: Skills test.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Freeway driving
- Objective: Explain merging, exiting and HOV lanes
- Vocabulary: acceleration lane, merge, deceleration lane, HOV/carpool lane, interchange
- Materials: handbook, local freeway map
- Assessment: Freeway quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Event-driven programming in App Lab
- Objective: Create UI elements in App Lab
- Objective: Write event handlers
- Vocabulary: event, event handler, onEvent, UI element, ID
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Multi-screen app checklist.
