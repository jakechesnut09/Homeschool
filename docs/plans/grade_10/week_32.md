## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: A Doll's House Acts 1–2
- Objective: Analyze character and social roles in realist drama
- Vocabulary: realism, stage directions, dramatic irony, gender roles
- Materials: A Doll's House (Henrik Ibsen, Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Quiz and character analysis.

### Geometry: Independence and conditional probability
- Objective: Determine independence
- Objective: Compute conditional probabilities
- Vocabulary: independent, dependent, conditional probability
- Materials: deck of cards
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Water's properties and Earth's surface
- Objective: Investigate water's properties and their effects on Earth materials
- Vocabulary: polarity, solvent, weathering, frost wedging, erosion
- Materials: plastic bottle, water, freezer, chalk, sugar cubes, sand
- Assessment: Investigation report (HS-ESS2-5).

### Modern World History: Nation-building research project
- Objective: Research and present a contemporary nation-building case
- Vocabulary: research, citation
- Materials: library databases, CIA World Factbook
- Assessment: Research report rubric.

### Spanish III: Herencia mixta: indígena, africana y europea
- Objective: Explain cultural blending
- Vocabulary: el mestizaje, indígena, afrodescendiente, el sincretismo
- Materials: readings on Afro-Latino culture
- Assessment: Essay.

### Music & Media Arts: Capstone proposal
- Objective: Propose a multimedia capstone
- Vocabulary: proposal, timeline
- Materials: notebook
- Assessment: Proposal.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Basketball skills and strategy
- Objective: Apply offense and defense
- Vocabulary: pick and roll, zone, fast break
- Materials: basketball, hoop
- Assessment: Skill test.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Environmental health in California
- Objective: Explain how air, water and heat affect health
- Vocabulary: AQI, contaminant, carbon monoxide, heat illness
- Materials: AirNow website, local water quality report
- Assessment: Unit 9 test.

### Computer Science Principles: Python lists and functions
- Objective: Use lists and functions in Python
- Objective: Decompose a problem into functions
- Vocabulary: list, def, return, parameter, module
- Materials: computer with internet, Trinket or Replit account
- Assessment: Python mini-project rubric.
