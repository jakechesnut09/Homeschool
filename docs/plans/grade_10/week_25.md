## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Letter part 1* (55 min): Read the first half of the Letter and outline King's refutations of the clergymen's claims.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Circumference & area* (60 min): Give informal arguments for C = 2πr and A = πr² by slicing a paper plate into sectors.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Reservoirs* (60 min): Map carbon reservoirs and fluxes in gigatons using NOAA data.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Yalta & UN* (55 min): Analyze Yalta agreements and read the UN Charter preamble.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *ONGs* (50 min): Research a nonprofit serving Spanish speakers in California.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Record host* (45 min): Record host segments in a quiet, soft-surfaced room, re-recording any flubbed lines.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Counts & elements* (45 min): Learn eight-counts, levels, pathways and formations by breaking down a 30-second music-video dance.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Stress log* (40 min): Track stressors and physical stress responses for three days and identify patterns.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Why simulate?* (50 min): Read the Code.org simulations lesson and list three real simulations (weather, traffic, epidemics) and what each simplifies.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Letter part 2* (55 min): Finish the Letter and analyze King's definition of just vs. unjust laws; connect to Antigone.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Arc length* (60 min): Derive arc length as a fraction of circumference and solve problems.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Quantitative model* (60 min): Build a simple spreadsheet box model of atmospheric carbon over 50 years.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Iron Curtain* (55 min): Read Churchill's speech and Stalin's response.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Lectura* (50 min): Read about César Chávez and Dolores Huerta and the UFW.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Interview* (45 min): Record the interview with phone or USB mic, capturing a 30-second room-tone sample.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Create* (45 min): Choreograph a 32-count sequence to a song of choice, using at least two levels and one turn.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Coping strategies* (40 min): Practice box breathing, progressive muscle relaxation and a 10-minute mindfulness exercise; rate which helped most.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Random numbers* (50 min): Write an App Lab program that flips a coin 1,000 times with randomNumber(0,1) and compare the result to the expected 50%.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *UDHR* (55 min): Read the 30 articles of the UDHR and choose three that King's letter supports.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Radians* (60 min): Measure a radius-length string around a plate to define one radian; convert degrees and radians.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Human flux* (60 min): Add fossil fuel emissions to your model and compare to Keeling Curve data.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Containment* (55 min): Analyze the Truman Doctrine and Marshall Plan.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Discusión* (50 min): Discuss what community means in Spanish.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Edit* (45 min): Edit the interview down to its best 3 minutes, removing filler and long pauses.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Rehearse* (45 min): Rehearse the sequence slowly, then at tempo, fixing transitions that break rhythm.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *I-statements* (40 min): Practice assertive communication by rewriting five blaming statements as I-statements and role-playing two.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Build a model* (50 min): Build a checkout-line simulation where customers arrive randomly and one cashier serves them; record the average wait.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Fallacies* (55 min): Learn eight logical fallacies and identify fallacies in sample op-eds.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Sector area* (60 min): Find sector and segment areas, e.g., pizza slices and sprinkler coverage.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Carbon in rocks* (60 min): Drop vinegar on limestone/chalk and connect to geosphere carbon storage.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Alliances* (55 min): Map NATO, Warsaw Pact and SEATO members.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Propuesta* (50 min): Write a proposal for a community project.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Music & mix* (45 min): Add your original intro music from Unit 2, crossfades between segments, and balanced levels.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Record* (45 min): Record a full performance from the front and review timing against the beat.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Conflict resolution* (40 min): Use a five-step conflict-resolution model to role-play a disagreement with a sibling or friend.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Refine the model* (50 min): Time real arrivals at a store or drive-through for 15 minutes, then change your model's arrival rate and add a second cashier to match.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Letter from Birmingham Jail and the UDHR
- Objective: Evaluate argument and reasoning
- Objective: Connect documents across time
- Vocabulary: just law, unjust law, refutation, human rights
- Materials: MLK 'Letter from Birmingham Jail', Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UN)
- Assessment: Timed rhetorical analysis.

### Geometry: Arc length, sectors and radians
- Objective: Derive arc length and sector area
- Objective: Define radian measure
- Vocabulary: arc length, sector, radian, circumference
- Materials: string, paper plates, ruler
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: The carbon cycle
- Objective: Quantify carbon reservoirs and fluxes
- Vocabulary: reservoir, flux, carbon sink, photosynthesis, respiration
- Materials: carbon cycle diagram, calculator
- Assessment: Spreadsheet carbon model.

### Modern World History: Origins of the Cold War
- Objective: Explain causes of the Cold War
- Objective: Analyze Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan and alliances
- Vocabulary: Iron Curtain, containment, Marshall Plan, NATO, Warsaw Pact, United Nations
- Materials: Churchill's 'Iron Curtain' speech, Truman Doctrine speech, UN Charter preamble
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish III: El servicio comunitario
- Objective: Discuss volunteering and community
- Vocabulary: la ONG, el voluntario, ayudar, la comunidad
- Materials: community organization websites in Spanish
- Assessment: Proposal presentation.

### Music & Media Arts: Podcasting: production
- Objective: Record and edit a podcast
- Vocabulary: intro music, levels, transitions
- Materials: Audacity
- Assessment: Draft episode.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Choreography
- Objective: Create a movement sequence
- Vocabulary: choreography, formation, counts
- Materials: music
- Assessment: Choreography video.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Stress, coping and communication
- Objective: Practice stress management
- Vocabulary: coping, mindfulness, I-statement
- Materials: journal
- Assessment: Wellness plan.

### Computer Science Principles: Simulations and computational models
- Objective: Build a simulation using random numbers
- Objective: Refine a model so it better matches real data
- Vocabulary: simulation, model, random, assumption, abstraction
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Simulation program and model-limitations paragraph.
