## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Greek theater background* (55 min): Watch a short video on Greek theater (masks, chorus, Dionysia) and take Cornell notes on Aristotle's elements of tragedy.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Rotations about origin* (60 min): Rotate figures 90°, 180°, 270° about the origin and derive the coordinate rules.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Atomic history timeline* (60 min): Make an illustrated timeline of Dalton, Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr and the quantum model.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Causes* (55 min): Analyze the 'Third Estate carrying the clergy and nobility' cartoon and list causes of the revolution.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Imperfecto* (50 min): Learn imperfect forms (regular, ser, ir, ver) and write 8 sentences about childhood habits.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Syncopation* (45 min): Clap and count syncopated rhythms.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Compass parts* (45 min): Learn compass parts and take bearings to five objects in the yard.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Signals* (40 min): Study solid, flashing and arrow signals and what to do when a signal is out (treat as all-way stop).
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Copyright basics* (50 min): Read about copyright and fair use and sort 10 scenarios as likely allowed or not allowed.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Oedipus family backstory* (55 min): Read a summary of the Theban myth and create a family tree of Oedipus, Jocasta, Antigone, Ismene, Polyneices, Eteocles and Creon.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Rotations about a point* (60 min): Rotate a triangle about a point that isn't the origin with protractor and compass.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Rutherford analogy* (60 min): Roll marbles at a hidden shape under cardboard to infer its shape, modeling the gold-foil experiment.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Rights of Man* (55 min): Compare the Declaration of the Rights of Man with de Gouges's response.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Fotos* (50 min): Describe three childhood photos: where you lived, what you were like, what you used to do.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Clave* (45 min): Learn the son clave and play it with salsa recordings.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Map reading* (45 min): Read contour lines and symbols on a topographic map.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Lane lines* (40 min): Diagram yellow vs. white lines, broken vs. solid, double solid and two-way left-turn lanes.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Creative Commons* (50 min): Find three images with different Creative Commons licenses and write the correct attribution for each.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Prologue* (55 min): Read the Prologue aloud in two voices and chart how Antigone and Ismene differ in values, citing three lines each.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Symmetry* (60 min): Find all lines of symmetry and rotation angles for regular polygons, rectangles and rhombi.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Build an Atom* (60 min): Use PhET Build an Atom to construct atoms and ions and record charge and mass.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Terror* (55 min): Read Robespierre's speech on virtue and terror and evaluate his justification.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Entrevista* (50 min): Interview a parent or grandparent about their childhood using 10 imperfect questions.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *West African polyrhythm* (45 min): Layer 3-against-2 polyrhythms with family on household drums.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Pace count* (45 min): Measure your pace count over 100 m.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Curb colors* (40 min): Learn curb colors: red no stopping, yellow loading, white passenger loading, green limited time, blue disabled parking.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Open source* (50 min): Compare MIT, GPL and proprietary software licenses and explain how each could help or hurt innovation.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Parodos & Scene 1* (55 min): Read the Parodos and Scene 1 and paraphrase Creon's opening speech on loyalty to the state.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Real-world symmetry* (60 min): Photograph three objects at home with symmetry (hubcap, tile, logo) and describe each symmetry.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Isotopes* (60 min): Calculate average atomic mass from isotope abundances using 'beanium' (three kinds of beans).
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Napoleon* (55 min): Weigh Napoleon's achievements and abuses; locate his empire on a map; explain the Congress of Vienna.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Comparación* (50 min): Write a paragraph comparing your childhood with theirs.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Listening log* (45 min): Listen to rhythms from Brazil, Ghana and India (tala) and note features.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Orienteering course* (45 min): Complete a homemade park orienteering course with 8 controls at a jog.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Street view* (40 min): Use an online street-view map to identify markings at three local intersections.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Project* (50 min): Create a one-page explainer (infographic or slide) on how one type of digital information is represented, using only properly licensed images.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Greek theater and Antigone, Prologue–Ode 1
- Objective: Explain conventions of Greek tragedy
- Objective: Identify the central conflict
- Vocabulary: tragic hero, chorus, ode, hubris, catharsis
- Materials: Antigone (Sophocles; Gutenberg or library translation)
- Assessment: Reading quiz; character-values chart.

### Geometry: Rotations and symmetry
- Objective: Rotate figures about a point
- Objective: Describe symmetries of polygons
- Vocabulary: rotation, center of rotation, rotational symmetry, line symmetry
- Materials: protractor, patty paper, GeoGebra
- Assessment: Quiz and symmetry photo set.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Atomic structure and history of the atom
- Objective: Describe protons, neutrons, electrons
- Objective: Trace models from Dalton to Bohr
- Vocabulary: proton, neutron, electron, isotope, atomic number, mass number
- Materials: PhET Build an Atom
- Assessment: Beanium lab and quiz.

### Modern World History: The French Revolution and Napoleon
- Objective: Trace the course of the French Revolution
- Objective: Explain Napoleon's rise and the Congress of Vienna
- Vocabulary: Estates-General, Reign of Terror, Declaration of the Rights of Man, Napoleonic Code, Congress of Vienna, nationalism
- Materials: Declaration of the Rights of Man (Avalon), Olympe de Gouges' Declaration of the Rights of Woman excerpt, political cartoons
- Assessment: DBQ essay.

### Spanish III: El imperfecto y la niñez
- Objective: Describe habitual past and childhood
- Vocabulary: de niño/a, siempre, a menudo, los juguetes, la guardería
- Materials: childhood photos
- Assessment: Interview presentation.

### Music & Media Arts: Rhythms of the world
- Objective: Identify rhythmic patterns from world cultures
- Vocabulary: syncopation, polyrhythm, clave, ostinato
- Materials: Smithsonian Folkways recordings, buckets or pots for drums
- Assessment: Ensemble performance.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Map, compass and orienteering
- Objective: Navigate with map and compass
- Vocabulary: bearing, declination, contour line, orienteering
- Materials: compass, printed park map
- Assessment: Orienteering completion time and accuracy.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Traffic signals and pavement markings
- Objective: Interpret signals, lane lines and curb colors
- Vocabulary: flashing red, protected turn, solid yellow line, double solid line, curb color
- Materials: handbook, street view maps
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Intellectual property and digital information project
- Objective: Explain copyright, Creative Commons and open-source licensing
- Objective: Apply licenses correctly in a project
- Vocabulary: copyright, Creative Commons, open source, public domain, license
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Unit 1 assessment and explainer project.
