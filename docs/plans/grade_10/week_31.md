## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Dystopian writing and Unit 7 assessment
- Objective: Write a literary analysis or dystopian narrative
- Vocabulary: world-building, satire
- Materials: Fahrenheit 451, notes
- Assessment: Writing rubric and unit test.

### Geometry: Sample spaces and set operations
- Objective: Describe events with unions, intersections, complements
- Vocabulary: sample space, event, union, intersection, complement, Venn diagram
- Materials: dice, coins, cards
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Engineering an equilibrium system
- Objective: Refine a chemical system design
- Vocabulary: yield, optimization, industrial chemistry
- Materials: notes
- Assessment: Design proposal and Unit 7 test.

### Modern World History: Nation-building in Africa and Latin America
- Objective: Analyze challenges in Africa and Mexico/Latin America
- Vocabulary: Rwandan genocide, Truth and Reconciliation Commission, NAFTA/USMCA, PRI
- Materials: Mandela inaugural speech (1994), Mexican Constitution 1917 excerpt
- Assessment: Comparison outline.

### Spanish III: Fiestas del mundo hispano
- Objective: Compare celebrations
- Vocabulary: el Día de los Muertos, la ofrenda, las Fallas, Inti Raymi, la quinceañera
- Materials: Smithsonian/authentic videos on festivals
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Music & Media Arts: Video unit assessment
- Objective: Evaluate video projects
- Vocabulary: screening
- Materials: editor
- Assessment: Video rubric.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Volleyball strategy
- Objective: Apply rotation and offense
- Vocabulary: pass-set-hit, rotation, communication
- Materials: volleyball
- Assessment: Observation.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Consumer health and health care access
- Objective: Evaluate health products and claims
- Objective: Explain how to access health services
- Vocabulary: health claim, supplement, insurance, confidentiality
- Materials: product ads, health websites
- Assessment: Consumer guide.

### Computer Science Principles: Python basics
- Objective: Write Python programs with variables, input, conditionals and loops
- Objective: Compare block-based and text-based syntax
- Vocabulary: syntax, print, input, indentation, type conversion
- Materials: computer with internet, Trinket or Replit account
- Assessment: Python practice set.
