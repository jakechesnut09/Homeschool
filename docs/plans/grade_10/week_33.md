## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: A Doll's House Act 3 and poetry of identity
- Objective: Evaluate the ending
- Objective: Compare poems of identity
- Vocabulary: epiphany, free verse, persona
- Materials: A Doll's House, Langston Hughes 'Let America Be America Again', Rabindranath Tagore 'Where the Mind is Without Fear', Claude McKay 'If We Must Die'
- Assessment: Comparative response.

### Geometry: Two-way tables
- Objective: Construct and interpret two-way frequency tables
- Vocabulary: two-way table, relative frequency, marginal, joint
- Materials: survey data, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Survey report and quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Solutions, acids and bases
- Objective: Calculate concentration
- Objective: Classify acids and bases with pH
- Vocabulary: solute, solvent, molarity, pH, acid, base, neutralization
- Materials: red cabbage indicator, household solutions (lemon, soap, vinegar, baking soda), measuring cups
- Assessment: pH chart and titration data.

- Objective: Analyze integration into the world economy
- Objective: Evaluate technological revolutions
- Vocabulary: globalization, outsourcing, WTO, internet, satellite
- Materials: trade data (World Bank), household product labels
