## Day 1 — Monday (school day 126, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Question* (55 min): Choose a human-rights topic from the 20th–21st century and draft a focused research question.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Circle equation* (60 min): Derive (x – h)² + (y – k)² = r² from the distance formula.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Keeling Curve* (60 min): Graph Mauna Loa CO₂ data and calculate the average yearly increase.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Civil war* (55 min): Trace the Nationalist–Communist conflict and the Long March.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Cláusulas adverbiales* (50 min): Learn cuando, tan pronto como, hasta que + subjunctive for future events.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Revise from feedback* (45 min): Re-edit the podcast using the three most important notes from the draft feedback, tightening any segment that runs over 2 minutes.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Dance workout 1* (45 min): Complete a 30-minute aerobic dance workout video and record peak heart rate.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Warning signs* (40 min): Learn suicide warning signs and risk and protective factors from a reliable source.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Bias in data* (50 min): Identify the sampling bias in five survey scenarios (e.g., an online-only poll about internet access) and rewrite one to fix it.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 127, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Source search* (55 min): Locate six sources using library databases and evaluate each with the CRAAP test.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Completing the square* (60 min): Convert general form to standard form to find center and radius.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Ice core data* (60 min): Analyze Vostok ice core CO₂ and temperature data for correlation.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Korea* (55 min): Map the Korean War and its outcome.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Plan* (50 min): Write a plan for your future career using future and subjunctive.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Show notes & credits* (45 min): Write show notes with a 50-word summary, timestamps, and Creative Commons attributions for any music or sound effects used.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Dance workout 2* (45 min): Complete a Latin-style dance fitness workout and compare RPE to Day 1.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Ask, care, tell* (40 min): Practice asking directly and caringly ('Are you thinking about suicide?') and listening without judgment through scripted role-plays.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Machine learning* (50 min): Train an image classifier in Teachable Machine with deliberately unbalanced training data, then retrain with balanced data and compare accuracy.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Research paper: question, sources and notes
- Objective: Develop a research question
- Objective: Evaluate source credibility
- Vocabulary: research question, credibility, database, annotated bibliography
- Materials: public library databases, Purdue OWL
- Assessment: Annotated bibliography check.

### Geometry: Equations of circles and parabolas
- Objective: Derive circle equation
- Objective: Derive parabola from focus and directrix
- Vocabulary: standard form, completing the square, focus, directrix
- Materials: Desmos, graph paper
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Climate data and feedbacks
- Objective: Analyze climate data
- Objective: Describe feedbacks between Earth systems
- Vocabulary: feedback loop, positive feedback, ice core, proxy data
- Materials: NOAA and NASA data sets, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Forecast write-up and quiz.

### Modern World History: China's revolution and the Cold War in Asia
- Objective: Analyze the Chinese Civil War and Mao's China
- Vocabulary: Mao Zedong, Great Leap Forward, Cultural Revolution, Korean War, Deng Xiaoping
- Materials: Mao's Little Red Book excerpts, Cultural Revolution posters
- Assessment: Comparison chart.

### Spanish III: Mi futuro profesional
- Objective: Present future career plans using multiple tenses
- Vocabulary: cuando + subjuntivo, tan pronto como, después de que
- Materials: notes
- Assessment: Video and unit test.

### Music & Media Arts: Podcast publish and critique
- Objective: Publish and evaluate media
- Vocabulary: distribution, audience
- Materials: Audacity
- Assessment: Podcast rubric.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Dance fitness
- Objective: Use dance for cardiovascular fitness
- Vocabulary: aerobic dance, Zumba
- Materials: music, video
- Assessment: Dance rubric.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Suicide prevention
- Objective: Recognize warning signs and get help
- Vocabulary: warning signs, 988, trusted adult
- Materials: 988 information
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Computer Science Principles: Bias in data and machine learning
