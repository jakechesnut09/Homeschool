## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Orwell essay* (55 min): Read 'Politics and the English Language' and list Orwell's six rules.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Density* (60 min): Compute population density of CA counties and BTU-per-cubic-foot problems.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Le Chatelier* (60 min): Predict shifts for changes in concentration, temperature and pressure.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Geography & resources* (55 min): Map oil reserves, water sources and religious groups.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Imperfecto de subjuntivo* (50 min): Learn the imperfect subjunctive from preterite ellos form.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Concept* (45 min): Choose a song (your original or a Creative Commons track) and write a one-paragraph music-video concept: narrative, performance or abstract.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Forearm pass* (45 min): Pass against a wall 100 times using a flat platform, then pass to a partner.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Food labels* (40 min): Read three food labels and compare serving size, added sugars, sodium and fiber.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Choose an innovation* (50 min): Pick a computing innovation (generative AI, self-driving cars, facial recognition, ride-sharing apps) and write three research questions.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Orwell and language
- Objective: Analyze how language can control thought
- Vocabulary: euphemism, propaganda, doublespeak, jargon
- Materials: Orwell 'Politics and the English Language', excerpts of Animal Farm
- Assessment: Response essay.

### Geometry: Modeling with density and design
- Objective: Apply density concepts
- Objective: Solve design optimization problems
- Vocabulary: density, population density, optimization
- Materials: kitchen scale, measuring cup, household objects
- Assessment: Design project and Unit 8 test.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Le Chatelier's principle
- Objective: Predict shifts in equilibrium
- Objective: Design changes to increase product
- Vocabulary: Le Chatelier's principle, stress, shift, Haber process
- Materials: red cabbage indicator, vinegar, baking soda, ice water, warm water
- Assessment: Design proposal and quiz.

### Modern World History: Nation-building in the Middle East
- Objective: Analyze challenges in the contemporary Middle East
- Vocabulary: OPEC, Iranian Revolution, Arab Spring, Arab-Israeli conflict
- Materials: maps of Middle East resources, news analysis sources
- Assessment: Written analysis.

### Spanish III: Cláusulas con si
- Objective: Use si clauses for hypotheticals
- Vocabulary: si tuviera, imperfecto de subjuntivo
- Materials: Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Music & Media Arts: Music video
- Objective: Create a music video
- Vocabulary: concept, lip sync, rhythm editing
- Materials: phone, editor
- Assessment: Draft.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Volleyball skills
- Objective: Pass, set, serve
- Vocabulary: forearm pass, set, overhand serve, rotation
- Materials: volleyball, net or rope
- Assessment: Skill test.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Nutrition for health and performance
- Objective: Interpret food labels
- Objective: Plan balanced meals for an active teen
- Vocabulary: added sugar, sodium, macronutrient, hydration, eating disorder
- Materials: food labels, MyPlate website
- Assessment: Meal plan.

### Computer Science Principles: Emerging technologies and computing innovations
- Objective: Research a computing innovation's beneficial and harmful effects
- Objective: Use credible sources to support claims
- Vocabulary: computing innovation, artificial intelligence, automation, unintended consequence
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Unit 7 assessment and impact essay.
