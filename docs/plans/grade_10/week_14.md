## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Context* (55 min): Read about Nigeria, the Igbo people and British colonization; locate Igboland on a map.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Slope criteria* (60 min): Show parallel lines have equal slopes and perpendicular slopes are negative reciprocals using rotation.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Counting by weighing* (60 min): Estimate the number of rice grains in a cup by weighing 100 grains, as an analogy for the mole.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Fronts* (55 min): Map the Western, Eastern and Ottoman fronts and key battles (Marne, Verdun, Somme, Gallipoli).
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Lectura* (50 min): Read an article about health care in a Spanish-speaking country.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Baroque* (45 min): Listen to Bach's Brandenburg Concerto No. 3 and identify texture.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Singles strategy* (45 min): Learn singles base position and practice a cross-court/down-the-line pattern to move an opponent side to side.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Scanning* (40 min): Practice looking 10–15 seconds ahead while a passenger and call out hazards.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Debugging guide* (50 min): Write a debugging checklist (read the error, isolate, print/console.log, rubber duck) for others to use.

- [ ] **English 10** — *Ch. 1–4* (55 min): Read chapters 1–4 and record Okonkwo's fears and their roots in his father Unoka.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Things Fall Apart Part 1: Igbo society
- Objective: Analyze cultural perspective in a non-U.S. novel
- Objective: Characterize Okonkwo
- Vocabulary: proverb, chi, egwugwu, patriarchy
- Materials: Things Fall Apart (Chinua Achebe, library), map of Nigeria
- Assessment: Quiz and proverb log.

### Geometry: Coordinate proofs
- Objective: Prove slope criteria for parallel/perpendicular
- Objective: Use coordinates to prove theorems
- Vocabulary: slope, coordinate proof, perpendicular slopes
- Materials: graph paper, Desmos
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Naming, moles and molar mass
- Objective: Calculate molar mass
- Objective: Convert between grams, moles and particles
- Vocabulary: mole, Avogadro's number, molar mass, formula unit
- Materials: kitchen scale, rice, sugar, salt
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Modern World History: Fighting the Great War
- Objective: Describe theaters and geography of the war
- Objective: Analyze total war
- Vocabulary: trench warfare, no man's land, Gallipoli, total war, propaganda, Zimmermann Telegram
- Materials: Library of Congress WWI posters, soldier letters, Zimmermann Telegram (National Archives)
- Assessment: Quiz and poster analysis.

### Spanish III: Sistemas de salud y medicina tradicional
- Objective: Compare health practices across cultures
- Vocabulary: la medicina tradicional, el curandero, las hierbas, el seguro médico
- Materials: article on Cuban or Mexican health systems, herbal remedies text
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Music & Media Arts: Western art music: Baroque to Romantic
- Objective: Identify features of historical periods
- Vocabulary: Baroque, Classical, Romantic, sonata form, symphony, fugue
- Materials: recordings of Bach, Mozart, Beethoven, Chopin (free on YouTube/IMSLP)
- Assessment: Listening quiz.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Strategy in net games
- Objective: Apply singles and doubles strategy
- Vocabulary: offense, defense, court positioning, shot selection
- Materials: racquets
- Assessment: Match analysis.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Defensive driving and the Move Over law
- Objective: Apply scanning and space-management
- Vocabulary: scanning, space cushion, Move Over law, work zone
- Materials: handbook, dash-cam videos
- Assessment: Hazard perception test.

### Computer Science Principles: Debugging and pair programming project
- Objective: Apply systematic debugging strategies
- Objective: Collaborate in pair programming roles
- Vocabulary: debugging, syntax error, logic error, driver, navigator
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account
- Assessment: Unit 3 app project rubric.
