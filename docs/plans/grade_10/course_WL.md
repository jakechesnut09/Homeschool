# Spanish III (Grade 10)

Spanish III moves students from Novice High toward Intermediate Mid proficiency through themed units on identity, childhood memories, travel, health, the environment, art and literature, community and work, technology, and celebrations across the Spanish-speaking world. Grammar focuses on preterite vs. imperfect, commands, the future and conditional, perfect tenses, and the present and imperfect subjunctive. Each week balances interpretive reading/listening with authentic sources, interpersonal conversation, presentational speaking and writing, and cultural comparison.

*Framework:* 2019 California World Languages Standards; ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines (target: Intermediate Low–Intermediate Mid)  
*Time:* 50 min/day
*UC/CSU a–g:* e · *Credits:* 10.0

## Units

### Unit 1: ¿Quién soy? Repaso e identidad (weeks 1–3)
Who we are is shaped by family, community, and culture.

- Week 1: Repaso: presentaciones y el presente
- Week 2: Identidad bicultural
- Week 3: Gustar y verbos similares; reflexivos

### Unit 2: Recuerdos de la niñez y leyendas (weeks 4–7)
Stories from the past—personal and legendary—connect generations.

- Week 4: El pretérito
- Week 5: El imperfecto y la niñez
- Week 6: Pretérito vs. imperfecto
- Week 7: Leyendas del mundo hispano

### Unit 3: ¡Vamos de viaje! (weeks 8–11)
Travel opens us to new places, people, and plans.

- Week 8: Planear un viaje
- Week 9: El futuro
- Week 10: Destinos: España y Latinoamérica
- Week 11: Problemas en el viaje y mandatos formales

### Unit 4: La salud y el bienestar (weeks 12–14)
Healthy choices and health systems differ across cultures.

- Week 12: El cuerpo y la salud
- Week 13: Mandatos informales y consejos
- Week 14: Sistemas de salud y medicina tradicional

### Unit 5: El medio ambiente (weeks 15–18)
Protecting the environment requires collective hopes, wishes, and actions.

- Week 15: El medio ambiente: vocabulario y problemas
- Week 16: El presente de subjuntivo
- Week 17: Soluciones verdes
- Week 18: Repaso y examen del semestre

### Unit 6: El arte y la literatura (weeks 19–22)
Art and literature express a culture's history and values.

- Week 19: El arte hispano
- Week 20: El presente perfecto
- Week 21: La poesía
- Week 22: El cuento: un cuento corto

### Unit 7: La comunidad y el trabajo (weeks 23–26)
Work and service build communities and futures.

- Week 23: Las profesiones
- Week 24: Subjuntivo con emociones y duda
- Week 25: El servicio comunitario
- Week 26: Mi futuro profesional

### Unit 8: La tecnología y los medios (weeks 27–30)
Technology changes how we communicate and what we imagine.

- Week 27: La tecnología
- Week 28: El condicional
- Week 29: Los medios de comunicación
- Week 30: Cláusulas con si

### Unit 9: Celebraciones y tradiciones (weeks 31–33)
Celebrations reveal shared values and blended heritages.

- Week 31: Fiestas del mundo hispano
- Week 32: Herencia mixta: indígena, africana y europea
- Week 33: Cultura en los Estados Unidos

### Unit 10: Proyecto final y evaluación (weeks 34–36)
Proficiency grows through sustained, meaningful communication.

- Week 34: Proyecto final: planificación
- Week 35: Proyecto final: presentación
- Week 36: Examen final y entrevista oral

## Standards

- **WL-COMM1-IL** Interpretive communication (Intermediate Low): understand main ideas and some details in simple authentic texts and audio on familiar topics.
- **WL-COMM1-IM** Interpretive communication (Intermediate Mid): understand main ideas and supporting details in a variety of authentic texts on familiar and some unfamiliar topics.
- **WL-COMM2-IL** Interpersonal communication (Intermediate Low): ask and answer questions and maintain simple conversations on familiar topics using sentences.
- **WL-COMM2-IM** Interpersonal communication (Intermediate Mid): sustain conversations, handle uncomplicated situations, and exchange information using strings of sentences.
- **WL-COMM3-IL** Presentational communication (Intermediate Low): present information on familiar topics using connected sentences.
- **WL-COMM3-IM** Presentational communication (Intermediate Mid): present and write connected paragraphs on familiar and researched topics in various time frames.
- **WL-CONN1-IL** Connections: build, reinforce, and expand knowledge of other disciplines through the language.
- **WL-CULT1-IL** Cultures: use the language to investigate, explain, and reflect on the relationship between cultural practices and perspectives.
- **WL-CULT2-IL** Cultures: use the language to investigate, explain, and reflect on the relationship between cultural products and perspectives.
- **WL-CULT3-IL** Cultures: interact with cultural competence and understanding in authentic or simulated situations.
- **WL-STRUC1-IL** Structures: use knowledge of the language's sound, writing, and grammar systems (including verb tenses and moods) to comprehend and communicate.
- **WL-STRUC2-IL** Structures: compare the language's structures and cultural features with those of English.
