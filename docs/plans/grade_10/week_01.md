## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Course launch & reading survey* (55 min): Complete a reading-history survey, set up a reading journal, and write a one-page 'reader autobiography' as a writing baseline.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Undefined terms* (60 min): Sketch and label points, lines and planes, then write definitions of segment, ray and angle using only undefined terms.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Lab safety* (60 min): Complete a home-lab safety contract, locate a fire extinguisher, and identify hazards in five lab scenarios.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Course launch* (55 min): Set up a history notebook and practice SOURCING a document (author, date, audience, purpose) with a modern photograph.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Diagnóstico* (50 min): Take a placement review of Spanish I–II grammar and write a 10-sentence self-introduction as a baseline.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Musical autobiography* (45 min): Write a one-page musical autobiography listing the music that shaped you and set goals for the year.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Warm-up & safety* (45 min): Learn a dynamic warm-up routine (10 moves) and safety expectations for home and park workouts.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Why GDL?* (40 min): Read teen crash statistics and explain why California uses graduated licensing; write three questions you want answered this semester.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *What is computing?* (50 min): Set up a Code.org CSP account and journal; list five computing devices you used today and the input, processing and output of each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Connell, part 1* (55 min): Read the first half of 'The Most Dangerous Game' and annotate every foreshadowing clue on Ship-Trap Island with sticky notes or margin marks.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Segment measure* (60 min): Use the Segment Addition Postulate and the distance on a number line to solve 10 problems like AB + BC = AC with algebra.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Measurement* (60 min): Measure mass, volume and temperature of water samples three times each and calculate mean and percent error.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Athens* (55 min): Read Pericles' Funeral Oration excerpt and list what Athenians valued about democracy.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Presente irregular* (50 min): Drill stem-changing and yo-go verbs on Conjuguemos and write 10 sentences about your daily routine.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Treble clef* (45 min): Complete the musictheory.net treble-clef lessons and drill until 90% accuracy.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Aerobic test* (45 min): Complete the 20-m PACER or 1-mile run and record laps/time.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Permit requirements* (40 min): Read the handbook licensing section and list every requirement to get a permit at 15½ (driver ed, application, vision and knowledge tests, parent signature).
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Representing with symbols* (50 min): Invent a code using a flashlight (on/off) to send a 4-choice answer to a partner and record how many on/off signals each message needed.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Read Connell, part 2* (55 min): Finish the story and chart the rising tension on a plot diagram, quoting one sentence for each stage.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Angles* (60 min): Measure and classify angles with a protractor and solve angle-addition problems with expressions like (3x + 5)°.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Sig figs & scientific notation* (60 min): Practice significant figures and scientific notation with 20 problems using your measurement data.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Plato & Aristotle* (55 min): Read the Allegory of the Cave and Aristotle's classification of governments; chart good and corrupt forms.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Árbol genealógico* (50 min): Create a labeled family tree and describe five relatives' personalities using ser and adjectives.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Bass clef* (45 min): Complete bass-clef lessons and drill; label notes on a printed grand staff.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Strength tests* (45 min): Complete curl-ups and 90° push-up tests with correct cadence.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Provisional rules* (40 min): Create a one-page chart of provisional license restrictions: 11 p.m.–5 a.m. limit, no passengers under 20 without a 25+ licensed adult for 12 months, and exceptions.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Abstraction* (50 min): Compare a subway map to a real street map and write how each hides details; then describe two abstractions in your phone's interface.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Grammar: phrases* (55 min): Identify prepositional, participial and appositive phrases in ten sentences from the story, then write five sentences imitating Connell's participial openers.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Angle pairs* (60 min): Find complementary, supplementary, vertical and linear-pair angles in diagrams and write equations to find unknowns.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Anchoring phenomenon* (60 min): Observe a candle burning and write 10 questions: where does the wax go? Start a driving-question board.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Rome* (55 min): Read about the Roman Republic, Twelve Tables and Cicero; diagram checks and balances in Rome.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Entrevista* (50 min): Record a 3-minute interview with a family member or tutor asking 8 questions about likes and routines.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Keyboard* (45 min): Find all C's, F's and G's on a keyboard app and play a C-major scale with correct fingering.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Flexibility & trunk lift* (45 min): Complete back-saver sit-and-reach and trunk lift; measure height/weight for BMI.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Practice hours plan* (40 min): With a parent, draft a 50-hour supervised driving log template including 10 night hours and behind-the-wheel training sessions.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Bits and patterns* (50 min): Calculate how many patterns 1–8 bits can make (2^n) and complete a table showing why 8 bits make 256 values.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Summary & discussion* (55 min): Write a 150-word objective summary (no opinion) and discuss with a parent: is Rainsford changed by the end? Cite two lines.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Quiz* (60 min): Quiz on definitions, segment and angle addition, and angle pairs.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Check* (60 min): Quiz on safety, units, sig figs and dimensional analysis.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Check* (55 min): Quiz and paragraph: Which Greek or Roman idea most shapes U.S. government today?
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Evaluación* (50 min): Quiz on present tense and give a 1-minute spoken self-introduction.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Check* (45 min): Note-naming quiz (30 notes in 2 minutes).
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Analyze* (45 min): Compare results to Healthy Fitness Zones and write a strengths/needs summary.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Quiz* (40 min): Take a 15-question handbook-style quiz on licensing rules.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Check* (50 min): Take a 10-question check on bits and abstraction and write a paragraph explaining how a touchscreen tap is an abstraction.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Suspense and structure: 'The Most Dangerous Game'
- Objective: Analyze how Connell builds suspense
- Objective: Write an objective summary
- Vocabulary: suspense, foreshadowing, antagonist, objective summary
- Materials: 'The Most Dangerous Game' (Richard Connell, CommonLit), reading journal
- Assessment: Objective summary scored for accuracy and no opinion; plot diagram with quoted evidence.

### Geometry: Points, lines, planes and precise definitions
- Objective: Use undefined terms to define geometric figures
- Objective: Measure segments and angles
- Vocabulary: point, line, plane, segment, ray, angle, congruent
- Materials: compass, straightedge, protractor, notebook
- Assessment: Quiz on vocabulary and angle relationships.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Lab safety, measurement and the phenomenon of fire
- Objective: Use lab safety practices
- Objective: Make precise measurements with significant figures
- Vocabulary: significant figures, precision, accuracy, SI units
- Materials: kitchen scale, measuring cups, thermometer, safety goggles
- Assessment: Quiz and measurement lab sheet.

### Modern World History: Greek and Roman roots of democracy
- Objective: Explain rule of law and illegitimacy of tyranny
- Objective: Compare Greek and Roman political thought
- Vocabulary: democracy, republic, rule of law, tyranny, philosopher
- Materials: excerpts: Pericles' Funeral Oration, Plato's Republic (Allegory of the Cave), Aristotle's Politics (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Quiz and constructed response.

### Spanish III: Repaso: presentaciones y el presente
- Objective: Introduce self and family in detail
- Objective: Review present-tense regular, irregular and stem-changing verbs
- Vocabulary: la personalidad, el apodo, los parientes, llevarse bien/mal
- Materials: notebook, SpanishDict, Conjuguemos
- Assessment: Quiz and speaking checklist.

### Music & Media Arts: Pitch, staff and clefs
- Objective: Read notes on treble and bass clefs
- Objective: Identify pitch on keyboard
- Vocabulary: staff, clef, ledger line, octave, pitch
- Materials: musictheory.net, keyboard app or piano
- Assessment: Note-naming quiz.

### PE 10 (Course 2): FITNESSGRAM baseline testing
- Objective: Complete baseline fitness tests
- Objective: Record results
- Vocabulary: aerobic capacity, PACER, Healthy Fitness Zone
- Materials: measuring tape, cones, PACER audio, stopwatch
- Assessment: Baseline FITNESSGRAM log.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Graduated driver licensing in California
- Objective: Explain permit and provisional license requirements
- Vocabulary: instruction permit, provisional license, GDL, supervised driving
- Materials: CA Driver Handbook, DMV Driver Licenses page
- Assessment: Licensing quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: Representing information and abstraction
- Objective: Explain how information can be represented with symbols
- Objective: Define abstraction in computing
- Vocabulary: bit, abstraction, computing device, binary
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account, index cards, flashlight
- Assessment: Quiz and abstraction paragraph.
