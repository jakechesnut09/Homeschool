## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Brutus's soliloquy* (55 min): Read 2.1 and paraphrase Brutus's 'It must be by his death' soliloquy line by line, identifying his flawed reasoning.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Angle sum* (60 min): Prove that triangle angles sum to 180° using a parallel line, and apply the Exterior Angle Theorem.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Ion formation* (60 min): Use Lewis dot diagrams to show electron transfer in NaCl, MgO and CaCl₂.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Africa before 1880* (55 min): Study a map of African states and societies before colonization.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Investigación* (50 min): Research a UNESCO site in a Spanish-speaking country in Spanish.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Motifs* (45 min): Analyze motifs in Beethoven's Fifth and a pop song.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Leverage* (45 min): Learn how levers and base explain escapes.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Parking rules* (40 min): List places you may never park (within 15 feet of a fire hydrant, in crosswalks, bike lanes, etc.).
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Design process* (50 min): Learn the design process (empathize, define, prototype, test) and analyze three apps you use for good and bad design.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 47, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Portia and Calpurnia* (55 min): Read 2.2–2.4 and compare the two wives' roles and warnings.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Isosceles triangles* (60 min): Prove the base angles of an isosceles triangle are congruent using a reflection or SAS.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Formulas & names* (60 min): Write formulas and names for 30 ionic compounds including polyatomic ions.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Berlin Conference* (55 min): Analyze the Berlin Conference and draw how borders cut across ethnic groups.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Clima y geografía* (50 min): Compare climate and geography with California.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Contour* (45 min): Sketch melodic contours and turn them into notation.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Wrist releases* (45 min): Practice wrist-grab releases toward the thumb.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Hill parking* (40 min): Model hill parking with a toy car on a book ramp: uphill/downhill, with and without curb.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *User personas* (50 min): Interview two family members about a daily problem and write a user persona for each.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 48, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Act 3.1* (55 min): Read the assassination scene aloud and track stage directions; discuss why Brutus lets Antony speak.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Midsegment* (60 min): Explore and prove the midsegment is parallel to the third side and half as long (coordinate proof).
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Salt crystals* (60 min): Grow salt crystals from saturated solution and examine shapes with a magnifier.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Congo* (55 min): Examine photographs and the Casement Report on King Leopold's Congo.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Comida* (50 min): Research two regional dishes and their ingredients.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Compose* (45 min): Compose an 8-bar melody over I-IV-V-I.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Bear hug escapes* (45 min): Practice front and rear bear-hug escapes slowly with a partner.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Parallel parking* (40 min): Watch a demonstration and write the step-by-step parallel parking procedure.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *Paper prototype* (50 min): Sketch a paper prototype for an app that solves one persona's problem with at least three screens.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 49, ~460 min)

- [ ] **English 10** — *Motive debate* (55 min): Debate with a parent: Was the assassination justified? Each side cites three lines.
- [ ] **Geometry** — *Concurrency* (60 min): Construct medians, angle bisectors and perpendicular bisectors and locate centroid, incenter and circumcenter.
- [ ] **Chemistry in the Earth System** — *Conductivity* (60 min): Build an LED conductivity tester and test dry salt, salt water and sugar water.
- [ ] **Modern World History** — *Resistance* (55 min): Compare Ethiopia's victory at Adwa with the Herero and Zulu resistance.
- [ ] **Spanish III** — *Folleto* (50 min): Create a travel brochure in Spanish.
- [ ] **Music & Media Arts** — *Revise* (45 min): Revise for cadence and singability.
- [ ] **PE 10 (Course 2)** — *Ground escapes* (45 min): Practice the shrimp and bridge-and-roll from the ground.
- [ ] **Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2)** — *Accessible parking* (40 min): Learn disabled-parking rules and penalties.
- [ ] **Computer Science Principles** — *User testing* (50 min): Test the paper prototype with a user, record feedback, and revise the design.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 10: Julius Caesar Acts 2–3.1: Conspiracy and assassination
- Objective: Analyze Brutus's internal conflict
- Objective: Evaluate motives
- Vocabulary: motive, internal conflict, tragic flaw, foreshadowing
- Materials: Folger Julius Caesar
- Assessment: Quiz and debate notes.

### Geometry: Proving theorems about triangles
- Objective: Prove the triangle angle sum theorem
- Objective: Prove isosceles triangle theorem
- Vocabulary: exterior angle, isosceles, base angles, midsegment, median, centroid
- Materials: patty paper, GeoGebra
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Chemistry in the Earth System: Ionic bonding
- Objective: Explain ionic bond formation
- Objective: Name and write formulas for ionic compounds
- Vocabulary: ion, cation, anion, ionic bond, crystal lattice, polyatomic ion
- Materials: table salt, magnifier, battery conductivity tester (9V, LED, wires)
- Assessment: Conductivity data table and quiz.

### Modern World History: The Scramble for Africa
- Objective: Analyze the Berlin Conference
- Objective: Explain African resistance
- Vocabulary: Berlin Conference, Congo Free State, Battle of Adwa, Herero, Zulu
- Materials: Berlin Conference General Act excerpt, photographs and reports on the Congo Free State, Things Fall Apart link from English
- Assessment: Paragraph scored for sourcing.

### Spanish III: Destinos: España y Latinoamérica
- Objective: Research and present a destination
- Vocabulary: el patrimonio, la arquitectura, el clima, la gastronomía
- Materials: UNESCO World Heritage site pages, country tourism websites
- Assessment: Brochure and presentation.

### Music & Media Arts: Melody writing
- Objective: Compose a melody over a progression
- Vocabulary: motif, contour, step, leap, cadence
- Materials: notation software (MuseScore or Noteflight free)
- Assessment: Melody composition.

### PE 10 (Course 2): Escapes and leverage
- Objective: Demonstrate escapes using leverage
- Vocabulary: leverage, wrist release, bear hug escape
- Materials: mat, partner
- Assessment: Skill checklist.

### Driver Education (S1) & First Aid/CPR + Health II (S2): Parking
- Objective: Explain parking rules and hill parking
- Vocabulary: parallel parking, curb wheel, hill parking, fire hydrant
- Materials: toy car, book ramp, handbook
- Assessment: Parking quiz.

### Computer Science Principles: User-centered design
- Objective: Apply a design process to identify user needs
- Objective: Prototype an app on paper
- Vocabulary: user interface, prototype, user persona, design process
- Materials: computer with internet, Code.org account, paper, markers
- Assessment: Design log scored with rubric.
