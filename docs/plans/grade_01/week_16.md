## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Topic sentence* (90 min): Write a topic sentence for your animal report ('Owls are birds that hunt at night.').
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Sort it* (60 min): Sort a bag of buttons by color, then by number of holes; name your categories.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Start a moon journal* (30 min): Go outside after dinner and draw the moon's shape and position in a calendar-style journal; continue nightly for four weeks.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Read* (30 min): Read 'Martin's Big Words' and list 3 'big words' Dr. King used.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Songs* (30 min): Learn 'Dreidel Song' and 'Feliz Navidad'; talk about where they come from.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Walk-jog* (30 min): Walk-jog for 8 minutes around a park.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Set a goal* (20 min): Choose a goal like 'eat a vegetable at every dinner.'

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 77, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Facts* (90 min): Turn 3 fact cards into 3 complete sentences in the report booklet, each with a capital and end mark.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Tally* (60 min): Record sorted buttons with tally marks in a three-row chart.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Read The Moon Book* (30 min): Read Gail Gibbons' 'The Moon Book' and list the names of 4 moon shapes (phases).
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Primary source* (30 min): Look at a Library of Congress photo of the 1963 March on Washington; describe what you see and wonder.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Paper chains* (30 min): Make a patterned paper chain decoration.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Fuel* (30 min): Discuss foods that give energy and why water matters during activity.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Track* (20 min): Track the goal with stickers each day.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 78, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Closing & diagram* (90 min): Write a closing sentence and draw a labeled diagram of the animal with 4 labels.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Picture graph* (60 min): Make a picture graph of favorite fruits from a family survey (3 choices).
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Cookie phases* (30 min): Scrape frosting from sandwich cookies to model new, crescent, half and full moons; label them on a paper plate.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Dream cloud* (30 min): Write and illustrate your own dream for a fairer world.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Art at home* (30 min): Identify art made at home for celebrations (cards, decorations).
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Longer jog* (30 min): Walk-jog for 10 minutes, taking water breaks.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Plan a menu* (20 min): Plan one day's meals using MyPlate.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 79, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Revise* (90 min): Read the report to a family member; add one describing adjective to each fact sentence.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Ask questions* (60 min): Write questions about the graph: how many in all? how many more chose apples than bananas?
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Daytime moon* (30 min): Look for the moon in the morning or afternoon sky and record the time and shape; discuss why it can be seen in daytime.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Peaceful problem-solving* (30 min): Role-play solving a disagreement with words, like Dr. King taught.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse songs for a family sing-along.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Rest* (30 min): Discuss why sleep helps muscles grow.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Grocery list* (20 min): Write a grocery list with foods from each group.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 80, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Unit 4 check* (90 min): Present the report aloud; long-vowel silent-e reading assessment (20 words).
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Read a given graph and answer 5 questions.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Put 6 moon-phase picture cards in order and compare them with your journal so far.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Tell why we celebrate Martin Luther King Jr. Day in January and name one thing Dr. King worked for.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Perform a seasonal sing-along with good audience manners.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Unit 4 check* (30 min): Fitness journal: record jog time, push-ups and stretch.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Unit 3 check* (20 min): Food-group assessment (sort and label a MyPlate) and tell whether you met your eating goal.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Writing an animal report
- Objective: Write an informative piece that names a topic, gives facts and a closing
- Objective: Revise by adding details
- Vocabulary: topic sentence, fact, closing
- Materials: fact cards from last week, report booklet paper, crayons
- Assessment: Animal report scored with a simple informative rubric (topic, 3 facts, closing, conventions).

### Math 1: Sorting and organizing data
- Objective: Sort objects into up to three categories
- Objective: Represent data in a tally chart and picture graph
- Vocabulary: data, category, tally, graph
- Materials: mixed buttons or toy collection, chart paper
- Assessment: Graph reading questions.

### Science 1 (NGSS): The moon's changing shape
- Objective: Observe and record the moon's shape over time
- Vocabulary: moon, phase, full moon, crescent, half moon
- Materials: moon journal (stapled calendar page), sandwich cookies and a plastic knife, 'The Moon Book' by Gail Gibbons (library)
- Assessment: Moon-phase card order and journal entries.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Heroes behind our holidays: Martin Luther King Jr.
- Objective: Explain the achievements of Dr. King and why we honor him
- Vocabulary: civil rights, equal, peaceful, dream
- Materials: 'Martin's Big Words' by Doreen Rappaport (library), LOC photo of the 1963 March on Washington
- Assessment: Dream writing and oral explanation.

### Art & Music 1: Songs and art of winter celebrations
- Objective: Sing songs from different winter traditions
- Objective: Make patterned decorations
- Vocabulary: tradition, celebration, perform
- Materials: paper strips, lyrics for 'Dreidel Song', 'Feliz Navidad', 'Jingle Bells'
- Assessment: Sing-along performance.

### PE 1: Fuel, water and endurance
- Objective: Walk/jog for longer distances
- Objective: Explain the body needs water, food and rest
- Vocabulary: endurance, fuel, hydrate
- Materials: water bottle, stopwatch
- Assessment: Unit 4 fitness journal.

### Health 1: Healthy eating goal
- Objective: Set and track a healthy eating goal
- Vocabulary: goal, track, progress
- Materials: goal chart, stickers
- Assessment: Unit 3 assessment.
