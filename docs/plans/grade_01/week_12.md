## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *The Gingerbread Man* (90 min): Read the tale; chant the repeating line and list everyone who chased him.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Teen numbers* (60 min): Show 13 as 10 + 3 on a double ten-frame; do this for 11–19.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Mirror bounce* (30 min): Bounce a flashlight beam off a mirror to hit a target.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *The Pledge* (30 min): Read the Pledge of Allegiance line by line and discuss what allegiance, liberty and justice mean.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Plan the animal* (30 min): Observe a photo of a real animal and sketch its shapes.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Plan* (30 min): Choose two rolls and one balance for your routine.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Taste test* (20 min): Taste 4 fruits/vegetables of different colors and describe them.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Town Mouse and Country Mouse* (90 min): Read the fable; tell which home you would choose and why.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Make ten* (60 min): Solve 9 + 4 by moving 1 counter to make 10 + 3.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Shiny vs. dull* (30 min): Compare reflection from foil, mirror and paper.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Words to pictures* (30 min): Draw a picture for each key word (liberty, justice, indivisible).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Cut and glue* (30 min): Build an Eric Carle-style collage animal from textured papers.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Practice* (30 min): Practice transitions between moves.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Wash produce* (20 min): Wash produce properly before eating.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 58, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Compare* (90 min): Compare the Gingerbread Man and the Country Mouse: where they went, what problems they had, how each ended.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *8 plus* (60 min): Solve 8 + 5, 8 + 6, 8 + 7 by making ten; record your thinking.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Periscope* (30 min): Build a simple periscope from a milk carton and two mirrors.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *My Country 'Tis of Thee* (30 min): Learn the first verse and talk about 'sweet land of liberty'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Sol-mi* (30 min): Sing 'Rain, Rain, Go Away' using sol-mi hand signs.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Add beginning and end* (30 min): Start and finish with a still shape.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Rainbow chart* (20 min): Track eating red, orange, yellow, green and purple foods this week.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 59, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Write a retell* (90 min): Choose a favorite fable and write a 4-sentence retelling with first, next, then, finally, using past-tense verbs.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Practice game* (60 min): Play 'Make Ten and Go' with cards; explain moves aloud.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Mirror maze* (30 min): Use two mirrors to redirect light around a corner.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *America the Beautiful* (30 min): Learn the first verse and match lines ('purple mountain majesties') to photos of American landscapes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Make it up* (30 min): Make up a sol-mi call ('Where are you?') and have family echo it.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Practice the routine 5 times, holding the balance for a 5-count each time.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Read* (20 min): Read 'Eating the Alphabet' by Lois Ehlert (library).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 60, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Unit 3 check* (90 min): Blends assessment (30 words); read your retelling aloud and state the moral.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): 10 make-ten problems with drawings.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Comparing folktales and writing a fable retell
- Objective: Compare adventures of characters in two folktales
- Objective: Write a retelling with beginning, middle, end
- Vocabulary: compare, version, beginning, middle, end
- Materials: 'The Gingerbread Man' and 'The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse' (public domain), story map
- Assessment: Blends assessment and scored written fable retelling.

### Math 1: Making ten to add within 20
- Objective: Use make-ten to add 8 + 5, 9 + 6
- Objective: Understand teen numbers as 10 and some ones
- Vocabulary: make ten, teen number
- Materials: double ten-frame, counters
- Assessment: Make-ten problems with drawings.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Mirrors and reflecting light
- Objective: Investigate how mirrors redirect light
- Vocabulary: reflect, mirror, beam
- Materials: small mirrors, flashlight, foil
- Assessment: Light path drawing.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: The Pledge of Allegiance and patriotic songs
- Objective: Recite the Pledge and explain its key words
- Objective: Sing songs that express American ideals
- Vocabulary: pledge, allegiance, liberty, justice, republic
- Materials: U.S. flag, lyrics to 'My Country 'Tis of Thee' and 'America the Beautiful'
- Assessment: Recitation and explanation of one key word.

### Art & Music 1: Collage animals; sol and mi
- Objective: Build a collage from textured papers
- Objective: Sing sol-mi patterns
- Vocabulary: sol, mi, echo
- Materials: textured papers from last week, glue
- Assessment: Collage animal and sol-mi singing.

### PE 1: Balance and roll routine
- Objective: Create a routine with two rolls and a balance
- Vocabulary: routine, sequence, beginning, end
- Materials: mat
- Assessment: Unit 3 routine performance.

### Health 1: Fruits and vegetables
- Objective: Explain why we eat a rainbow of fruits and vegetables
- Vocabulary: vitamin, rainbow, produce
- Materials: fruits and vegetables of many colors, colander
- Assessment: Rainbow chart.
