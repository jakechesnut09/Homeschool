# Health 1 (Grade 1)

Health 1 teaches young children to understand and care for themselves and others: naming and managing feelings, kindness and conflict resolution, handwashing, tooth care and sleep, MyPlate food groups and healthy snacks, daily physical activity, the senses and major organs, and safety at home, at play and on the road. Students also learn medicine and poison safety and meet community health helpers.

*Framework:* 2008 California Health Education Content Standards; 2019 CA Health Education Framework  
*Time:* 20 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: My Feelings and My Friends (weeks 1–5)
All feelings are okay; how we express them and treat others matters.

- Week 1: Naming feelings
- Week 2: Calm-down strategies
- Week 3: Using I-messages and asking for help
- Week 4: Kindness, respect and differences
- Week 5: Solving conflicts and stopping bullying

### Unit 2: Germs, Hygiene and Healthy Habits (weeks 6–10)
Clean hands, clean teeth and enough sleep keep our bodies healthy.

- Week 6: What are germs?
- Week 7: Handwashing
- Week 8: Caring for my teeth
- Week 9: Sleep and rest
- Week 10: Healthy habits champion

### Unit 3: Food Groups and Healthy Eating (weeks 11–16)
Eating a variety of foods from every food group gives our bodies what they need.

- Week 11: MyPlate and food groups
- Week 12: Fruits and vegetables
- Week 13: Water, breakfast and healthy snacks
- Week 14: Influences on food choices
- Week 15: Food safety in the kitchen
- Week 16: Healthy eating goal

### Unit 4: Active Bodies (weeks 17–19)
Moving every day keeps our heart, muscles and minds strong.

- Week 17: Why move every day?
- Week 18: Screen time and active play
- Week 19: Activity goal

### Unit 5: My Amazing, Growing Body (weeks 20–23)
Our senses and organs work together, and we keep growing and changing.

- Week 20: My five senses
- Week 21: Inside my body: organs
- Week 22: Growing and changing
- Week 23: Taking care of my body

### Unit 6: Staying Safe (weeks 24–29)
Knowing safety rules and trusted adults helps us stay safe at home, at play and on the road.

- Week 24: Pedestrian and car safety
- Week 25: Bike and playground safety
- Week 26: Fire safety
- Week 27: Emergencies and 911
- Week 28: Safe and unsafe touch; trusted adults
- Week 29: Water and sun safety

### Unit 7: Medicines and Harmful Substances (weeks 30–32)
Some things can help or harm our bodies; we ask trusted adults before touching or taking anything.

- Week 30: Medicines: help or harm
- Week 31: Harmful household products
- Week 32: Tobacco smoke and vaping

### Unit 8: Health Helpers and a Healthy Community (weeks 33–36)
Many people help keep us healthy, and we can help others be healthy too.

- Week 33: Health helpers
- Week 34: Healthy community
- Week 35: My health goals
- Week 36: Health review and celebration

## Standards

- **H-1.1.1.A** Explain that medicines can help or harm and should be given only by trusted adults.
- **H-1.1.1.G** Identify the five senses and the body parts associated with them.
- **H-1.1.1.M** Identify a range of feelings and how they can be expressed in healthy ways.
- **H-1.1.1.N** Classify foods into the food groups shown on MyPlate.
- **H-1.1.1.P** Explain how germs spread and how handwashing and covering coughs prevent illness.
- **H-1.1.1.S** Identify safe practices as a pedestrian, bike rider, and car passenger (crosswalks, helmets, car/booster seats, seat belts).
- **H-1.1.2.A** Identify household products that are harmful if swallowed, touched, or inhaled, and the poison symbol.
- **H-1.1.2.G** Describe how people grow and change over time.
- **H-1.1.2.M** Describe ways to show caring, kindness, and respect for others, including people who are different.
- **H-1.1.2.N** Explain why the body needs a variety of healthy foods and water.
- **H-1.1.2.P** Describe the importance of caring for teeth, including brushing, flossing, and dental checkups.
- **H-1.1.2.S** Explain fire safety rules, including stop-drop-and-roll and a home escape plan.
- **H-1.1.3.A** Explain that tobacco smoke and vaping are harmful to the body.
- **H-1.1.3.G** Identify major body organs (heart, lungs, brain, stomach) and their basic jobs.
- **H-1.1.3.M** Recognize teasing and bullying behaviors and why they are harmful.
- **H-1.1.3.N** Identify healthy snacks and the importance of eating breakfast.
- **H-1.1.3.P** Explain why sleep and rest are important for health.
- **H-1.1.3.S** Distinguish safe and unsafe touch and situations, and identify trusted adults.
- **H-1.1.4.N** Describe the benefits of daily physical activity for the heart, muscles, and mood.
- **H-1.1.4.S** Identify water, sun, and playground safety rules.
- **H-1.2.1.N** Identify how family, friends, and advertising influence food choices.
- **H-1.3.1.P** Identify health helpers in the community (doctor, nurse, dentist, pharmacist) and how they keep us healthy.
- **H-1.4.1.A** Demonstrate saying 'no' and telling a trusted adult about unsafe substances.
- **H-1.4.1.M** Use 'I-messages' and listening skills to express needs and feelings and to ask a trusted adult for help.
- **H-1.4.1.S** Demonstrate how to call 911 and give your name and address in an emergency.
- **H-1.4.2.M** Demonstrate simple ways to resolve conflicts with friends and family.
- **H-1.5.1.N** Use a simple decision-making process to choose healthy snacks and drinks.
- **H-1.6.1.N** Set a short-term goal to eat more fruits and vegetables or be more physically active and track progress.
- **H-1.7.1.M** Practice healthy ways to calm down and manage strong feelings.
- **H-1.7.1.N** Practice healthy eating and food-safety habits (washing hands and produce before eating).
- **H-1.7.1.P** Demonstrate proper handwashing, toothbrushing, and personal hygiene practices.
- **H-1.7.1.S** Practice safety behaviors at home, at play, and on the road.
- **H-1.8.1.P** Encourage others to practice healthy habits and to keep the environment clean (reduce, reuse, recycle).
