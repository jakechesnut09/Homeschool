## Day 1 — Monday (school day 106, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *er/ir/ur* (90 min): Read her, bird, girl, fur, turn, nurse; learn all three spell the same sound.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *On the chart* (60 min): Move up and down columns on the 120 chart to find 10 more/less.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Update data* (30 min): Record this week's sunrise/sunset and add it to your daylight graph; notice daylight growing since winter.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Old games* (30 min): Learn and play marbles, jacks and hopscotch.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Clay animal* (30 min): Sculpt a clay animal with pinch and coil techniques.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Trap and kick* (30 min): Stop a rolling ball with your foot, then kick.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Baby photos* (20 min): Compare baby photos and current photos.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 107, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Tricky words* (90 min): Practice irregular words (said, were, there, could, would) with a flash-card race.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *With blocks* (60 min): Add or remove a ten rod; tell the new number.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *NASA Space Place seasons* (30 min): Complete a NASA Space Place seasons activity and write one sentence about what you learned.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Toys* (30 min): Make a simple toy like a whirligig (button on a string) or a cup-and-ball.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Finish* (30 min): Add details and let it dry; paint when dry.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Rolling kick* (30 min): Kick a ball rolled by a partner.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Measure* (20 min): Measure height and hand size and compare with family.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 108, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Author's reasons* (90 min): Read a short passage on why we should recycle; underline two reasons the author gives.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Mental math* (60 min): Answer 10 more/10 less for 20 numbers without a chart.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Seasons wheel* (30 min): Make a paper-plate seasons wheel with a spinner showing daylight length and a picture for each season.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Clothes* (30 min): Compare photos of children's clothing from 1900 and today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Listening quiz* (30 min): Identify 6 instruments by sound alone.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Foot dribble* (30 min): Dribble with soft touches around cones.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Timeline* (20 min): Make a life timeline: baby, toddler, now, grown-up.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 109, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Conjunctions* (90 min): Join sentence pairs with and, but, or, so; write 4 compound sentences.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Number puzzles* (60 min): Fill 'plus-shaped' 120 chart pieces.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Present* (30 min): Use the wheel to teach a family member how daylight changes through the year.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Manners* (30 min): Discuss manners of long ago (bowing, curtsying, 'Yes, ma'am') and which we still use.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Sound story* (30 min): Create a short sound story using different instruments for characters.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Mini game* (30 min): Play 5 minutes of 1-on-1 soccer with a family member using small cone goals and soft kicks.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *What I can do now* (20 min): List things you can do now that you couldn't as a baby.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 110, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): er/ir/ur sort; name the author's point and two reasons.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: er, ir, ur words; authors' reasons
- Objective: Read er, ir, ur words
- Objective: Identify reasons an author gives
- Vocabulary: point, support, conjunction
- Materials: a ReadWorks persuasive/informational passage (e.g., about recycling), conjunction cards: and, but, or, so, because
- Assessment: Author's point/reasons organizer and conjunction sentences.

### Math 1: 10 more and 10 less
- Objective: Mentally find 10 more and 10 less
- Vocabulary: 10 more, 10 less
- Materials: 120 chart, base-ten blocks
- Assessment: Unit 6 assessment.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Daylight and seasons wrap-up
- Objective: Explain the yearly pattern of daylight with evidence
- Vocabulary: year, pattern, evidence
- Materials: daylight data, paper plates, brad fastener
- Assessment: Unit 5 assessment.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Games, toys, clothes and manners long ago
- Objective: Compare play, dress and manners of earlier generations
- Vocabulary: toy, manners, custom, folklore
- Materials: marbles, jacks, hoop or jump rope, photos of children's clothing long ago
- Assessment: Old-game demonstration.

### Art & Music 1: Form and timbre review
- Objective: Review form and timbre
- Vocabulary: review
- Materials: clay, instruments
- Assessment: Unit 5 listening quiz and sound story.

### PE 1: Kicking a rolling ball and dribbling with feet
- Objective: Kick a slowly rolling ball
- Objective: Dribble with feet
- Vocabulary: trap, dribble, soft touches
- Materials: soccer ball, cones
- Assessment: Rolling kick check.

### Health 1: Growing and changing
- Objective: Describe how people grow and change
- Vocabulary: grow, change, baby, adult
- Materials: baby photos, measuring tape
- Assessment: Life timeline.
