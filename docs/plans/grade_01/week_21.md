## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *ar words* (90 min): Read car, star, farm, park, shark; write the bossy-r pattern.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Compare with blocks* (60 min): Build 42 and 38; compare tens first.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Read about seasons* (30 min): Read 'Sunshine Makes the Seasons' (Branley) or a similar library book and list one daylight fact per season.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Chores then* (30 min): Look at photos of washing clothes on a washboard and churning butter; try shaking cream into butter in a jar.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Public art* (30 min): Look at photos of public sculptures (Statue of Liberty, local park sculptures) and discuss.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Kick cues* (30 min): Learn plant foot beside ball, kick with inside of foot.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Body outline* (20 min): Trace your body on butcher paper.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *or words* (90 min): Read for, corn, horn, storm, fork; sort ar/or picture cards.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Symbols* (60 min): Learn >, <, =; write comparisons for 10 pairs.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Lamp-and-globe model* (30 min): Walk a tilted globe around a lamp and notice that your area gets more light in summer and less in winter.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Chores now* (30 min): List appliances that do those jobs today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Neighborhood walk* (30 min): Find public art in your community and sketch one piece.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Target kick* (30 min): Kick into a cone goal from 10 feet.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Add organs* (20 min): Draw and label heart, lungs, brain and stomach.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 103, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Opinion talk* (90 min): Read 'Green Eggs and Ham'; tell whether Sam's friend should have tried them and give a reason using 'because'.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Card game* (60 min): Two-digit 'War' with two cards each; record with symbols.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Season chart* (30 min): Make a 4-box chart showing daylight, weather and clothing for each season using monthly sunrise/sunset data.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Jobs outside* (30 min): Compare jobs of grandparents' generation with jobs today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Ensemble* (30 min): Play homemade instruments together with family on a steady beat.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Distance kick* (30 min): Kick a stationary ball for distance 3 times and mark the best kick with a cone.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Lung model* (20 min): Make a lung model with a plastic bottle and balloons; pull the bottom balloon to see the lungs fill with air.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 104, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Opinion sentences* (90 min): Write 3 opinion sentences about favorite foods using 'I think... because...'.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Order numbers* (60 min): Order 5 two-digit numbers from least to greatest.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Plan the day* (30 min): Plan when you could play outside after dinner in December vs. June and explain why the plans differ.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Read* (30 min): Read 'Ox-Cart Man' by Donald Hall (library) and list the family's work through the year.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Take turns* (30 min): Each instrument plays a 4-beat solo.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Partner pass* (30 min): Pass the ball back and forth with a partner 10 feet apart using the inside of the foot; count passes.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Brain boss* (20 min): Learn the brain sends messages; play a reaction game.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 105, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): ar/or dictation; share an opinion orally in a complete sentence.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Comparison quiz (12 items).
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Match 8 cards (long days, short days, months, activities) to the correct season.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Make a then-and-now chart showing 4 household jobs done by hand long ago and by machine today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Explain why sculptures are placed in public.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): 10 kicks at target; count goals.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Point to the heart, lungs, brain and stomach on your outline and tell each organ's job.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: ar and or words; what is an opinion?
- Objective: Read ar and or words
- Objective: Tell an opinion and a reason
- Vocabulary: opinion, reason, because
- Materials: 'Green Eggs and Ham' by Dr. Seuss (library), opinion sentence frames
- Assessment: ar/or dictation and opinion sentence with 'because'.

### Math 1: Comparing two-digit numbers
- Objective: Compare two-digit numbers using tens and ones
- Objective: Use >, =, <
- Vocabulary: greater than, less than, compare
- Materials: base-ten blocks, symbol cards
- Assessment: Comparison quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Seasons and daylight
- Objective: Relate the amount of daylight to the time of year
- Vocabulary: spring, summer, fall, winter, season
- Materials: library book on seasons (e.g., 'Sunshine Makes the Seasons' by Franklyn Branley), globe or ball, lamp
- Assessment: Season card match and chart.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Family life and work long ago
- Objective: Compare work at home and outside the home across generations
- Vocabulary: generation, chore, household, appliance
- Materials: washboard or photo of one, LOC photos of family life
- Assessment: Then-and-now chart.

### Art & Music 1: Sculptures around us; instrument ensemble
- Objective: Describe public sculptures
- Objective: Play in an ensemble
- Vocabulary: public art, ensemble
- Materials: photos of public sculptures, homemade instruments
- Assessment: Ensemble performance.

### PE 1: Kicking a stationary ball
- Objective: Kick with the inside of the foot
- Vocabulary: kick, inside of foot, plant foot
- Materials: soccer ball, cones for goal
- Assessment: Kick accuracy count.

### Health 1: Inside my body: organs
- Objective: Identify heart, lungs, brain, stomach and their jobs
- Vocabulary: organ, heart, lungs, brain, stomach
- Materials: butcher paper, crayons
- Assessment: Body outline with organs.
