## Day 1 — Monday (school day 121, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Clap syllables* (90 min): Clap and count syllables in names and objects; find the vowel in each syllable.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Take away tens* (60 min): Solve 70 − 30 with ten rods.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Velcro and burrs* (30 min): Compare Velcro and a burr under a magnifier; draw the tiny hooks on both.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *First Californians* (30 min): Learn the name of the tribe(s) native to your area and locate their homeland on a map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Kente* (30 min): Look at kente cloth and learn its colors' meanings.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Jump and land* (30 min): Jump forward and land softly on two feet.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Helmet fit* (20 min): Practice the 2-V-1 helmet fit test.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 122, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Compound words* (90 min): Read sunset, rainbow, backpack, cupcake; split them and draw both parts.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Think addition* (60 min): Solve 90 − 60 by thinking 60 + ? = 90.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Invention match* (30 min): Match inventions (bike helmet, swim fins, sun hat, tweezers) to the animal or plant part they copy (turtle shell, duck feet, leaves, bird beak).
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Baskets* (30 min): Examine Calisphere photos of Pomo and Miwok baskets; describe patterns and uses.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Paper kente* (30 min): Weave strips of gold, green, red and black paper into a kente-style pattern and glue the ends.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Snake rope* (30 min): Jump over a wiggling 'snake' rope on the ground.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Egg helmet* (20 min): Drop an egg with and without a foam 'helmet' to see protection.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 123, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Seed to plant* (90 min): Read 'From Seed to Plant'; start a bean in a wet paper towel bag and draw it.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *On the chart* (60 min): Jump up columns on the 120 chart to subtract tens.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Define a problem* (30 min): Choose a human problem: keeping your head safe, staying cool in the sun, or picking up tiny things; draw the problem.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Acorns to food* (30 min): Learn how acorns were gathered and ground into flour; grind sunflower seeds with stones to model it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Drumming* (30 min): Play djembe-style call-and-response rhythms on a bucket.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Long rope* (30 min): Jump a long rope turned by two family members.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Bike ride* (20 min): Ride with helmet in a safe area.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 124, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *How things connect* (90 min): Explain how a seed connects to a flower using the book's pictures; order 4 life-cycle cards.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Money tens* (60 min): Use dimes as tens: 80¢ − 50¢.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Brainstorm* (30 min): List animal or plant parts that solve a similar problem in nature for your chosen problem.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Living today* (30 min): Read about California Indian communities today and their traditions.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Song* (30 min): Sing 'Funga Alafia' with motions.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Rhyme* (30 min): Jump to 'Teddy Bear, Teddy Bear' rhyme.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Playground rules* (20 min): Make a list of playground safety rules and practice at a park.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 125, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Syllable count of 12 printed words; retell the plant life cycle in order.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): 10 subtracting-tens problems.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain one biomimicry example in two sentences: the nature part and the invention.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain two contributions of American Indians to California culture.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Syllables and compound words; plant life cycle text
- Objective: Count syllables in printed words
- Objective: Read compound words
- Vocabulary: syllable, compound word, life cycle
- Materials: 'From Seed to Plant' by Gail Gibbons (library), bean seeds and a zip bag with wet paper towel
- Assessment: Syllable sort and life-cycle sequencing.

### Math 1: Subtracting multiples of 10
- Objective: Subtract multiples of 10 from multiples of 10
- Objective: Relate to addition
- Vocabulary: subtract, difference, multiple of 10
- Materials: ten rods, 120 chart
- Assessment: Subtracting tens quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Biomimicry: people copy nature
- Objective: Identify human inventions inspired by plant and animal parts
- Objective: Define a problem to solve
- Vocabulary: biomimicry, mimic, invent
- Materials: Velcro strip, burrs or seed heads (if available), magnifier, invention picture cards
- Assessment: Biomimicry explanation and problem statement.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: California's first peoples
- Objective: Describe how American Indians lived in California and contributions they made
- Vocabulary: American Indian, tribe, basket, acorn
- Materials: Calisphere photos of Native Californian baskets, local tribe information (e.g., Ohlone, Tongva, Chumash, Miwok)
- Assessment: Oral explanation with evidence.

### Art & Music 1: Art and music of West Africa
- Objective: Explore kente cloth and drumming
- Vocabulary: kente, djembe, call and response
- Materials: paper strips in kente colors, drums or buckets
- Assessment: Kente weaving and call-and-response.

### PE 1: Jumping and landing; long rope
- Objective: Jump and land softly
- Objective: Jump a long rope
- Vocabulary: jump, land, long rope
- Materials: long rope, chalk
- Assessment: Long rope count.

### Health 1: Bike and playground safety
- Objective: Wear a helmet correctly
- Objective: Follow playground rules
- Vocabulary: helmet, fit, playground
- Materials: bike helmet, bike or scooter
- Assessment: Helmet fit check.
