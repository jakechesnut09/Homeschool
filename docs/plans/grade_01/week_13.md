## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Magic e with a* (90 min): Add e to cap, tap, can, mad to make cape, tape, cane, made; read each pair aloud.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Big doubles* (60 min): Learn 6+6 through 10+10 with egg cartons and ten-frames.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Lighthouses* (30 min): Read about lighthouses and how ships use them.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Veterans Day* (30 min): Learn what a veteran is and why we honor them on November 11; look at Library of Congress photos of veterans.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Look at art* (30 min): Look at a photograph of a woven basket and a quilt; describe textures.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Find your heart* (30 min): Place a hand on your chest to find your heart; listen with a paper-tube stethoscope.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Sugar in drinks* (20 min): Count sugar cubes in soda vs. milk vs. water.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 62, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Long or short?* (90 min): Listen to 12 spoken words and hold up a short or long card for the vowel sound.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Near doubles* (60 min): Solve 7 + 8 as 7 + 7 + 1; play a near-doubles card game.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Flashlight code* (30 min): Invent a flashlight blink code and send messages.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Thank-you card* (30 min): Write a thank-you card to a veteran (family member or local VA).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Texture weaving* (30 min): Weave paper strips into a simple mat.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Before and after* (30 min): Feel heartbeat at rest, then after 1 minute of jumping jacks; compare.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Breakfast power* (20 min): Discuss why breakfast fuels the brain; plan a healthy breakfast.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 63, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Fiction or nonfiction* (90 min): Compare a storybook with a nonfiction animal book; list 3 differences on a T-chart.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Three addends* (60 min): Solve 'Sam saw 4 birds, 6 squirrels, and 3 rabbits'; choose which two to add first.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Color filters* (30 min): Use colored cellophane for different signals.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Thanksgiving history* (30 min): Learn about the 1621 harvest feast of the Wampanoag and the Pilgrims from a children's nonfiction book.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Play melody* (30 min): Play 'Hot Cross Buns' on a glockenspiel or piano app (E-D-C).
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Heart-pumping game* (30 min): Play freeze tag for 10 minutes.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Snack decisions* (20 min): Use 'stop, think, choose' to pick between snack pairs.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 64, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Ask about facts* (90 min): Read a National Geographic Kids 'Frogs' (or similar) book and ask and answer 3 questions about frog facts.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Change the order* (60 min): Show 5 + 2 + 5 = 5 + 5 + 2 and explain why it helps.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Test outdoors* (30 min): Test your flashlight code at dusk from across the yard; record which messages a partner decoded correctly.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Sarah Hale* (30 min): Read 'Thank You, Sarah' and learn how Sarah Josepha Hale helped make Thanksgiving a national holiday.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Listening* (30 min): Listen to 'Flight of the Bumblebee' and tell if the melody is high or low.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Heart chart* (30 min): Draw a heart and list activities that make it pump.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Make a snack* (20 min): Make an ants-on-a-log or fruit-and-yogurt snack.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 65, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Read 10 a_e words and sort 6 books into fiction and nonfiction with a reason.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Doubles to 20 quiz and three-addend word problems.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Unit 3 check* (30 min): Complete a light assessment (sort materials, draw a shadow, explain seeing in the dark) and explain how your signal device works.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Long a with silent e; what is nonfiction?
- Objective: Distinguish long and short a
- Objective: Tell how storybooks differ from information books
- Vocabulary: silent e, long vowel, fiction, nonfiction
- Materials: a storybook and an animal information book from the library, letter tiles plus an 'e' tile
- Assessment: a_e word reading and fiction/nonfiction sort with reasons.

### Math 1: Doubles to 20 and three-addend sums
- Objective: Use doubles to 20
- Objective: Add three numbers using properties
- Vocabulary: associative, group, sum
- Materials: dominoes, dice, counters
- Assessment: Doubles quiz and three-addend problems.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Light signals and communicating
- Objective: Design a light device to communicate over distance
- Vocabulary: signal, code, lighthouse
- Materials: flashlight, colored cellophane, mirror
- Assessment: Unit 3 assessment.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Veterans Day and Thanksgiving
- Objective: Explain why we celebrate Veterans Day and Thanksgiving
- Vocabulary: veteran, holiday, harvest, gratitude
- Materials: Library of Congress photos of veterans, 'Thank You, Sarah' by Laurie Halse Anderson (library)
- Assessment: Holiday poster with correct explanations.

### Art & Music 1: Texture review; melody game
- Objective: Describe texture in art
- Objective: Perform simple melodies
- Vocabulary: texture, melody
- Materials: art from unit, glockenspiel or app
- Assessment: Unit 3 performance and weaving.

### PE 1: My heart
- Objective: Locate the heart and feel it beat faster with exercise
- Vocabulary: heart, heartbeat, pulse
- Materials: stopwatch, toilet paper tube as stethoscope
- Assessment: Heart explanation.

### Health 1: Water, breakfast and healthy snacks
- Objective: Choose healthy snacks and drinks
- Objective: Explain why breakfast matters
- Vocabulary: snack, sugar, water, breakfast
- Materials: snack items, sugar cubes, water bottle
- Assessment: Snack decision explanation.
