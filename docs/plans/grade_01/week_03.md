## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Short o* (90 min): Build and read hot, pot, dot, mop, top with tiles; change one letter to make a new word (hot→hop→hip).
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Take from* (60 min): Act out '8 cookies, eat 3' with counters and write 8 − 3 = 5.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Foil boats* (30 min): Shape two 15 cm squares of foil into two different boat designs (flat raft and tall-sided tub) and sketch each.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *What is fair?* (30 min): Discuss whether 'fair' always means 'the same'; sort scenario cards into fair and unfair.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Tool safety* (30 min): Practice safe scissors handling (walk with closed blades, cut away from body) and dot-dot-not-a-lot glue.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Levels freeze* (30 min): Move to music and freeze at the level called (high, medium, low).
- [ ] **Health 1** — *I-messages* (20 min): Learn 'I feel ___ when ___. I need ___.' and practice with 3 example problems.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Short u* (90 min): Read bug, cup, sun, run, hut; play 'I spy' with short u sounds around the house.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Hide and find* (60 min): Hide some of 7 counters under a cup; tell how many are hidden and write the subtraction.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Test* (30 min): Add pennies one at a time to each boat until it sinks; record counts in a table.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Game day* (30 min): Play a backyard game and practice taking turns, following rules and shaking hands at the end.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Shape animals* (30 min): Cut circles, squares, triangles and rectangles and glue them into an animal.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Scarf toss* (30 min): Toss a scarf high and catch it at a low level.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Listening* (20 min): Practice good listening (eyes, ears, quiet body) while a family member shares.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 13, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Characters & setting* (90 min): Read 'Owl Moon' and name the characters and setting; draw the snowy woods and label two details.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Count back* (60 min): Subtract 1, 2, or 3 by counting back on a number line.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Compare* (30 min): Compare data: which shape held more pennies and why?
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Winning and losing* (30 min): Role-play good and poor ways to win and lose; make a T-chart of sportsmanlike words.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Beat moves* (30 min): Do 4 different beat movements (pat, clap, tap, stomp) to a march, changing every 8 beats.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Directions* (30 min): Walk forward, backward and sideways safely in a game of 'Simon Says'.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Trusted adults hand* (20 min): Trace your hand and write a trusted adult on each finger.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 14, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Small moment story* (90 min): Draw a picture of something you did this summer, then tell the story aloud and write one sentence about it.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Take apart* (60 min): Solve '9 kids, 4 are girls, how many boys?' with drawings.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Improve* (30 min): Redesign the weaker boat by changing one feature (wider base or taller sides) and retest it with pennies.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Team pledge* (30 min): Write a 3-line good-sport pledge and sign it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Echo beats* (30 min): Echo 4-beat clapping patterns a family member claps.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Combination* (30 min): Gallop forward at a medium level, crab-walk backward at a low level.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Ask for help* (20 min): Role-play asking a trusted adult for help when lost, hurt or sad.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 15, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Mixed short-vowel word read (a, i, o, u) and spelling test of 8 CVC words; reread the small-moment sentence to a family member.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Mixed add/subtract story problems; explain one strategy aloud.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Draw the engineering cycle (ask, imagine, plan, create, improve) with your boat as the example.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Tell how a good sport acts in a game you played this week.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Present the shape animal and tell what shapes you used.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Perform a 4-move sequence with two levels and two directions.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Use an I-message to solve a pretend problem.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Short o and short u; personal narrative start
- Objective: Read short o and u words
- Objective: Tell a story from personal experience in order
- Vocabulary: character, setting, beginning, end
- Materials: letter tiles, drawing paper, 'Owl Moon' by Jane Yolen (library)
- Assessment: Spelling of 8 CVC words and oral retell of a small-moment story with a beginning and end.

### Math 1: Subtracting as taking from and taking apart
- Objective: Represent subtraction with objects and equations
- Objective: Count back to subtract
- Vocabulary: subtract, minus, difference
- Materials: counters, paper cups, number line
- Assessment: Mixed operation story problems and verbal explanation.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Test, compare, improve
- Objective: Test two designs and compare data
- Objective: Improve a design
- Vocabulary: test, data, improve, fair test
- Materials: paper, tape, pennies, foil
- Assessment: Data table and engineering cycle drawing.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Fair play and good sportsmanship
- Objective: Describe fair play and good sportsmanship
- Vocabulary: fair play, sportsmanship, take turns, cooperate
- Materials: simple games (tag, board game), T-chart
- Assessment: Sportsmanship T-chart and pledge.

### Art & Music 1: Safe tools and shape animals; beat and movement
- Objective: Use scissors and glue safely
- Objective: Move to the beat
- Vocabulary: tools, safety, tempo, march
- Materials: safety scissors, glue stick, shape cut-outs, GoNoodle or march recording
- Assessment: Shape animal and safe tool use observation.

### PE 1: Levels and directions
- Objective: Move at high, medium and low levels
- Objective: Move forward, backward, sideways
- Vocabulary: level, high, low, forward, backward, sideways
- Materials: music, scarves
- Assessment: Levels/directions sequence.

### Health 1: Using I-messages and asking for help
- Objective: Express needs with I-messages
- Objective: Identify trusted adults
- Vocabulary: I-message, trusted adult, listen
- Materials: sentence frame card: 'I feel ___ when ___. I need ___.', hand outline paper
- Assessment: I-message demonstration and trusted adults hand.
