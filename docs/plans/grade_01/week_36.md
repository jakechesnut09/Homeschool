## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Phonics screener* (90 min): Complete an end-of-year phonics and sight-word screener covering all patterns.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Cumulative test* (60 min): End-of-year assessment part 2: adding within 100, measurement, time, coins, data and shapes.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Notebook review* (30 min): Page through your science notebook and flag your 3 favorite investigations.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Plan a market* (30 min): Vote as a family (direct democracy) on what goods to make and sell at a pretend market.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Compare work* (30 min): Compare a September and a May drawing; note growth.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Field day 1* (30 min): Run the pillowcase sack race and the locomotor relay, cheering for others.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review game* (20 min): Play a health Jeopardy game with family.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Fluency & comprehension* (90 min): Read a cold grade 1 passage; retell and answer questions.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Math journal* (60 min): Reflect on favorite learning and make a page.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Poster* (30 min): Make a poster for your showcase project: problem, design sketch, test data and improvement.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Make goods* (30 min): Make 6 bookmarks or crafts, decide on fair prices under $1, and make price tags.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Music growth* (30 min): Compare first rhythm with latest composition.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Field day 2* (30 min): Compete in the beanbag toss and a family kickball game.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Health journal* (20 min): Make a 'My Healthy Me' book with one page each on feelings, hygiene, food, activity and safety.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Writing on demand* (90 min): Write a letter to a future first grader with capitalized names and the date.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Math scavenger hunt* (60 min): Hunt for shapes, numbers, and measurements outdoors.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Science fair* (30 min): Present the project and one favorite investigation to family, answering their questions.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Market day* (30 min): Hold a family market using coins; practice buying and selling.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Sketchbook* (30 min): Start a summer sketchbook with 3 prompts.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Field day 3* (30 min): Obstacle course and tug-of-war.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Teach someone* (20 min): Teach a family member a health skill.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Real-life words* (90 min): Find words around the house that are cozy, bright, or noisy and use them in sentences.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Teach someone* (60 min): Teach a family member your favorite math game (e.g., Make Ten Go Fish) and explain the math in it.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Reflect* (30 min): Write or dictate 3 sentences about what you learned about sound, light, sky or living things.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Year review* (30 min): Review maps, symbols, then-and-now booklet and culture projects.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Playlist* (30 min): Make a playlist of favorite pieces from the year.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Awards* (30 min): Make awards for effort and encouragement.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Healthy party* (20 min): Plan and prepare a healthy snack party.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Celebration* (90 min): Share a portfolio of favorite writing from the year with family; choose summer reading books.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Celebration* (60 min): Math fair: show projects and journals to family.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Year-end check* (30 min): Complete a year-end science review of key ideas from each unit.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Year-end check* (30 min): Complete a year-end review: label continents and oceans, match symbols, sort then-and-now pictures, and sort goods and services.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Celebrate* (30 min): Share your growth comparisons and summer sketchbook plan with family, then sing a favorite song together.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Reflect* (30 min): Share favorite activity and feelings.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Celebrate* (20 min): Present your book at the party.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Year-end review and celebration of learning
- Objective: Show end-of-year phonics, fluency and writing growth
- Objective: Use new vocabulary in conversation
- Vocabulary: portfolio, growth, celebrate
- Materials: writing portfolio from the year, end-of-year phonics screener
- Assessment: End-of-year screener, fluency check, and on-demand writing sample compared with week 1.

### Math 1: Year-end celebration and assessment
- Objective: Show mastery of grade 1 standards
- Vocabulary: portfolio, celebrate
- Materials: math journal
- Assessment: End-of-year assessment.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Science fair and year review
- Objective: Communicate results and reflect on a year of investigations
- Vocabulary: present, evidence, reflect
- Materials: science notebook, showcase project, poster board
- Assessment: Science fair presentation and year-end review.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Market day and year review
- Objective: Apply economics and citizenship learning
- Objective: Review the year
- Vocabulary: market, review
- Materials: homemade goods, coins, maps and booklets from the year
- Assessment: Year-end review and market participation.

### Art & Music 1: Year reflection and summer art plan
- Objective: Reflect on growth as an artist and musician
- Vocabulary: reflect, growth
- Materials: early and late artworks, sketchbook
- Assessment: Year-end reflection.

### PE 1: Field day celebration
- Objective: Participate in field day with sportsmanship
- Vocabulary: celebrate, field day
- Materials: field day equipment, water
- Assessment: Field day participation.

### Health 1: Health review and celebration
- Objective: Review the year's health learning
- Vocabulary: review, celebrate
- Materials: health journal
- Assessment: Year-end review.
