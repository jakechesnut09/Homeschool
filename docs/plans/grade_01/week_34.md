## Day 1 — Monday (school day 166, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Sentence types* (90 min): Write a statement, question, command and exclamation about your topic.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Halves* (60 min): Fold paper shapes in half; check if parts are equal.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Dog breeds* (30 min): Compare photos of a Chihuahua and a Great Dane; list what makes both dogs and what is different.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Grocery trip* (30 min): Visit a grocery store and list workers you see (stocker, cashier, butcher).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Museum tour* (30 min): Take a virtual museum tour; discuss what a curator and docent do.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Locomotor review* (30 min): Circuit of hop, gallop, slide, skip.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Clean-up* (20 min): Do a family neighborhood or park clean-up.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 167, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Type captions* (90 min): With help, type 4 fact captions on a computer and print them.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Fourths* (60 min): Fold into fourths; label 'fourth' and 'quarter'.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Leaf collection* (30 min): Collect 10 leaves from one tree and measure each length with paper clips.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Transport* (30 min): Learn how goods travel by truck, train, ship and plane.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Plan your show* (30 min): Draw a floor plan showing where each of your 6 artworks will hang at home and why.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Throw and catch* (30 min): Throw overhand and catch with a partner from 10 feet, saying the cues aloud.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Healthy messages* (20 min): Make a healthy-habits card for a neighbor.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 168, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Poster* (90 min): Glue captions and drawings on a poster with a title.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Equal or not* (60 min): Sort pictures into equal and not-equal shares.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Leaf graph* (30 min): Graph leaf lengths into short, medium and long groups.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Advertising* (30 min): Look at cereal box ads and discuss how stores and companies market goods.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Concert manners* (30 min): Practice audience and performer etiquette (bow, clap, listen).
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Kick and dribble* (30 min): Kick at target; dribble around cones.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Share* (20 min): Share a healthy snack with friends.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 169, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Present* (90 min): Present the poster using complete sentences; answer two audience questions.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Share food* (60 min): Cut a tortilla or sandwich into halves and fourths; compare piece sizes.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Discuss* (30 min): Discuss why leaves on one tree and puppies in one litter are not exactly the same.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Make an ad* (30 min): Design an ad poster for your homemade bread.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Dress rehearsal* (30 min): Full rehearsal of spring concert.

- [ ] **Health 1** — *Kind words* (20 min): Encourage a friend who is learning something new.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Research poster and presentation
- Objective: Publish research with drawings and digital tools
- Objective: Speak in complete sentences
- Vocabulary: poster, present, audience
- Materials: poster board, tablet or computer for typing captions
- Assessment: Presentation checklist and 4 sentence types.

### Math 1: Equal shares: halves and fourths
- Objective: Partition circles and rectangles into halves and fourths
- Objective: Understand more shares means smaller shares
- Vocabulary: half, fourth, quarter, equal share
- Materials: paper circles and rectangles, play-dough or a tortilla
- Assessment: Unit 10 assessment.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Variation within a kind of animal or plant
- Objective: Describe how individuals of the same kind vary
- Vocabulary: variation, species, individual
- Materials: dog breed photos, leaves from one tree, grid paper
- Assessment: Unit 8 assessment and leaf graph.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Transporting and marketing goods
- Objective: Describe the work of moving and selling goods
- Vocabulary: transport, truck driver, store clerk, advertise
- Materials: grocery store visit, paper for an ad
- Assessment: Job explanation.

### Art & Music 1: Museums and audiences
- Objective: Identify roles of people in museums
- Objective: Practice concert etiquette
- Vocabulary: museum, curator, docent, audience
- Materials: virtual museum tour (National Gallery or Met)
- Assessment: Docent practice.

### PE 1: Skill review: locomotor and manipulative
- Objective: Review all skills
- Vocabulary: review
- Materials: balls, cones
- Assessment: Skill checklist.

### Health 1: Healthy community
- Objective: Encourage others to be healthy
- Vocabulary: community, encourage, clean
- Materials: trash bags, gloves
- Assessment: Community action reflection.
