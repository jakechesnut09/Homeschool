## Day 1 — Monday (school day 36, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Digraph game* (90 min): Play digraph bingo with sh, ch, th, wh, ck, ng words; read each word you cover.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Strategy sort* (60 min): Sort fact cards by strategy: count on, doubles, make 10, think addition.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Mystery Science* (30 min): Watch a free Mystery Science sound lesson and answer the mystery question.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Walking tour* (30 min): Walk your neighborhood and list landmarks (library, park, store, fire station).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Tints* (30 min): Add white to blue in steps to make a tint ladder.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Circuit* (30 min): Do a 4-station circuit: jog, hop, gallop, slide for 1 minute each.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Egg teeth* (20 min): Place a hard-boiled egg in cola overnight; brush the stain to see how brushing removes stains.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 37, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Cookies* (90 min): Read 'Cookies' (Frog and Toad Together); discuss what willpower means and tell about a time you showed it.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Number rack* (60 min): Use the virtual number rack app to show facts within 10 in one push.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Instrument design* (30 min): Design and build a new instrument from household materials.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Street map* (30 min): Find your home on a printed street map or online map of your town.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Shades* (30 min): Add black to red in steps to make a shade ladder.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Animal moves* (30 min): Move like animals using locomotor skills (bunny hop, horse gallop, crab slide).
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Brush right* (20 min): Practice brushing in circles for 2 minutes on all surfaces with a timer.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 38, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Plan a story* (90 min): Plan a story about doing something with a friend: draw first, next, last pictures in a booklet.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Roll and solve* (60 min): Roll two dice, add, then subtract the smaller from the larger; record 10 rounds.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Compare instruments* (30 min): Compare your instrument with another; which is louder? higher?
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Draw the community* (30 min): Draw a community map with your home, 4 landmarks, a key and a compass rose.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Chrome Music Lab* (30 min): Make rhythms on Chrome Music Lab Rhythm.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Locomotor tag* (30 min): Play tag where the tagger calls the movement.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Floss* (20 min): Practice flossing with help; use a play-dough 'teeth' model.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 39, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Write the story* (90 min): Write a sentence under each picture using first, next, then, last, and end with how you felt.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Fact practice* (60 min): Play Khan Academy 'add and subtract within 10' practice.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Concert* (30 min): Play your instrument and explain what vibrates.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Routes* (30 min): Draw a route from home to the library and describe it using north, south, east, west.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Compose* (30 min): Write an 8-beat rhythm with ta, ti-ti and rest; perform on body percussion.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Partner feedback* (30 min): Watch a family member gallop and give one cue as feedback.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Tooth-healthy foods* (20 min): Sort foods into tooth-friendly and sticky/sugary.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 40, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Unit 2 check* (90 min): Read the booklet aloud with expression; complete a 20-word digraph reading check.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Unit 2 check* (60 min): Timed fluency check within 10 (goal: 30 facts in 2 minutes) and written unit test.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Unit 2 check* (30 min): Complete a sound assessment: draw what vibrates in 3 instruments and answer short questions about loud/soft and high/low sounds.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Give a family member directions to a landmark using your map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Perform your composition for family and explain your color ladders.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Unit 2 check* (30 min): Skill check of hop, gallop, jog and slide using cue checklist.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Fill in a 1-week brushing chart and explain why we visit the dentist.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Digraph review and friendship narrative
- Objective: Apply all digraphs in reading and spelling
- Objective: Write a narrative with two events and a closing
- Vocabulary: sequence, first, next, last
- Materials: Frog and Toad books, drawing paper stapled into a 4-page booklet
- Assessment: Friendship booklet scored on sequence words, capitals/periods; digraph reading check.

### Math 1: Fluency within 10
- Objective: Demonstrate fluency for addition and subtraction within 10
- Vocabulary: fluency, strategy
- Materials: flash cards, dice, number rack app (Math Learning Center)
- Assessment: Unit 2 fluency check and test.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Sound unit wrap-up
- Objective: Explain the relationship between sound and vibration
- Vocabulary: review, vibration
- Materials: instruments made during unit, science notebook
- Assessment: Unit 2 assessment.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: My neighborhood and community map
- Objective: Construct a map of the local community
- Vocabulary: neighborhood, community, landmark, route
- Materials: large paper, local street map (printed), colored pencils
- Assessment: Community map with key and route directions.

### Art & Music 1: Tints, shades and color in art history; rhythm composition
- Objective: Make tints and shades
- Objective: Compose a rhythm
- Vocabulary: tint, shade, compose
- Materials: white and black paint, Chrome Music Lab Rhythm
- Assessment: Rhythm composition and color ladders.

### PE 1: Locomotor skills circuit
- Objective: Combine locomotor skills
- Vocabulary: circuit, combine
- Materials: 4 station signs, cones
- Assessment: Unit 2 skill checklist.

### Health 1: Caring for my teeth
- Objective: Brush and floss correctly
- Objective: Explain why teeth need care
- Vocabulary: plaque, cavity, floss, dentist
- Materials: toothbrush, floss, hard-boiled egg and soda (egg experiment)
- Assessment: Brushing chart.
