## Day 1 — Monday (school day 126, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Split words* (90 min): Split two-syllable words between consonants (rab-bit, nap-kin, kit-ten, muf-fin) and read them.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Math stations* (60 min): Rotate through block-building, number line, and 120-chart stations.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Two sketches* (30 min): Sketch two designs for your problem inspired by an animal or plant part (e.g., a shell-shaped egg protector).
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Journeys* (30 min): Read a picture book about an immigrant child (e.g., 'The Name Jar' by Yangsook Choi, library).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Baskets* (30 min): Look at photos of Pomo baskets and discuss why they are valued.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Rope sizing* (30 min): Size the rope; practice turning and stepping over.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Smoke alarms* (20 min): Listen to the smoke alarm test and learn what to do.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 127, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Second text* (90 min): Read 'The Tiny Seed' by Eric Carle and a ReadWorks plant passage.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Khan practice* (60 min): Complete 'add within 100' and 'subtract tens' practice.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Choose and plan* (30 min): Choose one design and list materials you need; explain why its shape will work.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Angel Island* (30 min): Look at Calisphere photos of Angel Island Immigration Station and discuss what newcomers felt.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Weave* (30 min): Weave yarn on a paper-plate loom.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Jump and turn* (30 min): Turn, stop, jump (one at a time).
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Stop, drop, roll* (20 min): Practice stop, drop (cover face) and roll, and crawling low under 'smoke' (a held-up blanket).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 128, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Compare* (90 min): Make a chart of what both texts say about seeds and what only one says.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Game* (60 min): Race to 100 – roll a die, add tens or ones, record your total.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Build* (30 min): Build the prototype from household materials.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Contributions* (30 min): List foods, words, holidays and music that immigrants brought to California.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Listening* (30 min): Listen to a recording of a California Native song (PBS or Smithsonian) and describe the beat.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Continuous* (30 min): Try continuous jumps with a single bounce per turn; count your best streak.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Escape plan* (20 min): Draw a home floor plan with two exits from each room and a meeting place.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 129, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Shared research* (90 min): Measure your bean sprout and add a dated sentence to a class-style observation journal.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Error hunt* (60 min): Find and fix mistakes in 5 worked problems.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Label* (30 min): Label the prototype's parts and write which nature part each copies.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Map journeys* (30 min): Trace immigrant routes on a world map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Compare* (30 min): Compare art and music from 4 cultures studied.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Feelings* (30 min): Talk about feeling frustrated and proud while learning.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Fire drill* (20 min): Hold a family fire drill at the sound of the alarm and time how fast everyone reaches the meeting place.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Two-syllable words; comparing two plant texts
- Objective: Decode two-syllable words by breaking into syllables
- Objective: Compare two texts on the same topic
- Vocabulary: closed syllable, similar, different
- Materials: ReadWorks or Starfall plant passage, 'The Tiny Seed' by Eric Carle (library), bean bag from last week
- Assessment: Two-syllable word read and text comparison chart.

### Math 1: Mixed practice within 100
- Objective: Choose strategies to add and subtract within 100
- Vocabulary: strategy, check
- Materials: base-ten blocks, Khan Academy practice
- Assessment: Unit 7 assessment.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Design a nature-inspired solution
- Objective: Sketch and build a solution that mimics a plant or animal part
- Vocabulary: design, prototype, shape, function
- Materials: cardboard, tape, foil, sponges, paper plates, egg carton
- Assessment: Design sketches and labeled prototype.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Immigrants in California
- Objective: Explain how immigrants help define California and American culture
- Vocabulary: immigrant, journey, Angel Island, culture
- Materials: Calisphere photos of Angel Island, world map
- Assessment: Contribution explanation.

### Art & Music 1: Art and music of California Native peoples
- Objective: Explore Native Californian basketry and songs
- Vocabulary: basket, weave, tradition
- Materials: photos of Pomo baskets (Calisphere), yarn and paper bowls
- Assessment: Unit 6 cultural comparison.

### PE 1: Self-turned jump rope
- Objective: Jump a self-turned rope
- Vocabulary: turn, rhythm, single bounce
- Materials: jump rope sized to child
- Assessment: Jump rope count.

### Health 1: Fire safety
- Objective: Practice stop, drop and roll
- Objective: Make a home escape plan
- Vocabulary: smoke alarm, escape plan, stop drop and roll
- Materials: paper for floor plan, smoke alarm
- Assessment: Escape plan and drill.
