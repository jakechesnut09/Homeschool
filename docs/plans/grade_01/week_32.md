## Day 1 — Monday (school day 156, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Plan* (90 min): Plan an animal adventure story (inspired by Peter Rabbit) with a character, setting, problem and solution.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Shape sort* (60 min): Sort cut-out shapes by number of sides and corners.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Match the families* (30 min): Match 10 baby animal photos to their parents and name one trait that gave it away.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Bunny Money* (30 min): Read 'Bunny Money' by Rosemary Wells and track how Ruby's money is spent on choices for Grandma's gift.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Review art* (30 min): Lay out all art from the year in order and sort it by medium (drawing, painting, collage, clay, print).
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Tag games* (30 min): Play freeze tag and shadow tag.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Healthy lungs* (20 min): Revisit the balloon lung model and discuss what smoke does.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 157, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Draft beginning* (90 min): Write the beginning: who, where, and the problem.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Attributes* (60 min): Explain why a big red triangle and small blue triangle are both triangles.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Puppy and dog Venn* (30 min): Compare a puppy and its parent in a Venn diagram: ears, fur color, size, tail.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Choices* (30 min): Given 50 cents and 3 items priced 20, 30 and 45 cents, decide what to buy and explain what you gave up.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Select* (30 min): Choose 6 favorites and tell why for each.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Hopscotch* (30 min): Draw a hopscotch board and play with family, hopping on one foot in single squares.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Secondhand smoke* (20 min): Learn that breathing others' smoke harms health.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 158, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Draft middle/end* (90 min): Write 2 events using next and then, and a solution with how the character felt.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Build* (60 min): Build shapes with straws and playdough.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Not exactly alike* (30 min): Look at a photo of a litter of kittens and list differences between the kittens and their mother.

- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Song choices* (30 min): Choose 3 favorite songs to perform in a spring concert.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Kickball* (30 min): Play mini kickball with 3 bases, kicking a rolled ball and running the bases.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Say no* (20 min): Practice saying 'No thanks' and walking away.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Writing an imaginative narrative
- Objective: Write a narrative with two or more sequenced events, details and an ending
- Objective: Use temporal words
- Vocabulary: narrative, event, temporal word
- Materials: story planner (characters, setting, problem, events, solution)
- Assessment: Narrative writing rubric and diphthong assessment.

### Math 1: Defining attributes of 2-D shapes
- Objective: Identify defining vs. non-defining attributes
- Objective: Build and draw shapes
- Vocabulary: side, vertex, attribute, triangle, hexagon
- Materials: geoboard (or app), straws and playdough
- Assessment: Attribute quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Young animals are like their parents
- Objective: Compare traits of young animals and their parents
- Vocabulary: trait, similar, different, litter
- Materials: baby/adult animal photo pairs, Venn diagram paper, family photos
- Assessment: Evidence sentence and Venn diagram.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Saving, spending and making choices
- Objective: Explain that people make choices when they spend money
- Objective: Set a simple savings goal
- Vocabulary: save, spend, choice, goal, bank
- Materials: three clear jars labeled Save, Spend, Share, coins, 'Bunny Money' by Rosemary Wells (library)
- Assessment: Savings-goal drawing and oral explanation of a spending choice.

### Art & Music 1: Choosing work for a portfolio
- Objective: Select favorite artworks and explain choices
- Vocabulary: portfolio, select, prefer
- Materials: artwork from the year, folder
- Assessment: Portfolio selection with reasons.

### PE 1: Playground and traditional games
- Objective: Play traditional games safely
- Vocabulary: traditional game, safe
- Materials: chalk, ball
- Assessment: Unit 8 game leadership.

### Health 1: Tobacco smoke and vaping
- Objective: Explain why smoke and vapes are harmful
- Objective: Say no
- Vocabulary: tobacco, smoke, vape, lungs
- Materials: balloon lung model, refusal skill cards
- Assessment: Unit 7 assessment.
