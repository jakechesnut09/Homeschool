## Day 1 — Monday (school day 111, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Commas in a series* (90 min): Write lists of three things ('I like red, blue, and green.') using commas.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Add tens* (60 min): Solve 30 + 20, 40 + 50 with ten rods.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Parts and jobs* (30 min): Match photos of claws, beaks, shells, fins and ears to the job each does (grab, eat, protect, swim, hear).
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Prepare questions* (30 min): Write 6 questions about school, games, jobs and transportation when the interviewee was young.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Papel picado* (30 min): Look at photos of papel picado and learn when it is used.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Balloon tap* (30 min): Keep a balloon in the air using open hands, then only one hand, then elbows; count taps.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Body care* (20 min): Add healthy habits around your body outline.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 112, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Commas in dates* (90 min): Write today's date, your birthday, and a holiday date with commas.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Two-digit + tens* (60 min): Solve 34 + 20 with blocks and on the chart.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Bird beak lab* (30 min): Use tweezers, a clothespin, a spoon and chopsticks as 'beaks' to pick up rice, gummy worms and marbles; record which beak works best for each food.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Interview* (30 min): Interview a grandparent or older neighbor (in person or by video call) and record answers.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Make it* (30 min): Fold and cut tissue paper banners.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Make a paddle* (30 min): Tape a paper plate to a paint stirrer or ruler to make a paddle and decorate it.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Protecting senses* (20 min): Discuss protecting eyes (sunglasses) and ears (not too loud).

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 113, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Read two books* (90 min): Read two library books (e.g., 'Mercy Watson' and 'Elephant and Piggie'); choose your favorite.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Two-digit + ones* (60 min): Solve 42 + 5 without making a new ten.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Protection parts* (30 min): Compare photos of a turtle shell, porcupine quills and an armadillo; tell how each part protects the animal.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Compare* (30 min): Compare their answers with your life today in a Venn diagram.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Mariachi* (30 min): Listen to mariachi music; name the instruments (violin, trumpet, guitarrón).
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Paddle tap* (30 min): Tap the balloon up with the paddle; count taps.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Growth foods* (20 min): Connect protein and dairy to growing.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 114, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Plan* (90 min): Fill in an OREO organizer: your opinion, two reasons, an example, and a closing.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Make a new ten* (60 min): Solve 38 + 6 by making 40 + 4.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Moving parts* (30 min): Compare fins, wings, flippers and legs; act out how each animal moves and say what part helps.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Share* (30 min): Make a 'Then and Now' booklet with one fact per page.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Sing* (30 min): Learn 'De Colores' or 'La Cucaracha'.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Partner volley* (30 min): Hit the balloon back and forth.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Share* (20 min): Present your body outline to family, naming senses, organs and the habits that keep them healthy.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 115, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Comma check (5 sentences); read your organizer to a listener.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Mixed addition problems with drawings of tens and ones.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Draw an animal, label 3 external parts and write what each does.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Unit 4 check* (30 min): Assessment: sort pictures of then and now and explain changes in schools, transportation and family life.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Explain why people make papel picado.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Count consecutive balloon taps with the paddle and record your best of 3 tries.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Unit 5 check* (20 min): Assessment: label 5 sense organs and 4 inner organs and describe one way bodies grow.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Planning an opinion piece; commas in a series
- Objective: Plan an opinion piece with a reason and closing
- Objective: Use commas in a series and in dates
- Vocabulary: comma, series, date, closing
- Materials: opinion graphic organizer (OREO: Opinion, Reason, Example, Opinion)
- Assessment: Comma practice sentences and completed opinion organizer.

### Math 1: Adding tens and adding a one-digit number
- Objective: Add multiples of 10
- Objective: Add a one-digit number to a two-digit number
- Vocabulary: tens, ones, sum
- Materials: base-ten blocks, hundred chart
- Assessment: Addition within 100 quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Animal external parts and their jobs
- Objective: Identify animal parts and how they help animals survive
- Vocabulary: structure, function, survive, beak, claw
- Materials: animal photos (Smithsonian Learning Lab), tweezers, clothespin, spoon, chopsticks, rice, gummy worms, water in a cup, marbles
- Assessment: Labeled animal drawing with functions.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Oral history interview
- Objective: Gather information from an older family member's memories
- Vocabulary: oral history, interview, memory, source
- Materials: interview question sheet, recording device
- Assessment: Unit 4 assessment and oral-history booklet.

### Art & Music 1: Art of Mexico; music of Mexico
- Objective: Explore papel picado and mariachi
- Vocabulary: papel picado, mariachi, culture
- Materials: tissue paper, scissors, mariachi recording
- Assessment: Papel picado and song.

### PE 1: Striking with hands and paddles
- Objective: Strike a balloon or ball upward
- Vocabulary: strike, paddle, flat surface
- Materials: balloons, paper-plate paddles (plate taped to a craft stick)
- Assessment: Consecutive taps count.

### Health 1: Taking care of my body
- Objective: Connect healthy habits to growth
- Vocabulary: healthy, grow
- Materials: body outline, healthy habits list
- Assessment: Unit 5 assessment.
