## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *oi and oy* (90 min): Read coin, soil, boil, boy, toy, joy; write a sentence with two of them.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Make a clock* (60 min): Make a paper-plate clock with hour and minute hands.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Calling for help* (30 min): Listen to recordings (PBS or library) of baby birds chirping and kittens mewing; tell what each baby is asking for.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Needs and wants* (30 min): Sort pictures into needs (food, shelter) and wants (toys).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *O'Keeffe* (30 min): Look at an O'Keeffe flower painting and notice its size.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Towel toss* (30 min): Toss a ball with a towel held by two people.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *What medicine does* (20 min): Learn medicines can help when sick but only trusted adults give them.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 147, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Little Bear* (90 min): Read 'What Will Little Bear Wear?'; describe how Little Bear solves his problem.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *O'clock* (60 min): Show and write times on the hour (3:00).
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Follow the leader* (30 min): Read 'Make Way for Ducklings' and explain why the ducklings stay in a line behind their mother.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Goods and services* (30 min): Sort pictures into goods (bread, shoes) and services (haircut, doctor visit).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Close-up* (30 min): Observe a flower with a magnifier and draw it large to fill the page.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Balloon partners* (30 min): Keep a balloon up with a partner, alternating taps; count your team record.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Look-alikes* (20 min): Compare pictures of candy and pills that look alike.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 148, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Self-correct* (90 min): Practice 'Does that make sense?' rereading with a passage that has tricky words.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Half past* (60 min): Show half-past times and explain where the hour hand is.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Learning by copying* (30 min): Read about bear cubs and young chimpanzees learning to find food by watching parents.

- [ ] **Health 1** — *Labels* (20 min): Look at a medicine label with an adult.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Diphthongs oi/oy; Little Bear and context clues
- Objective: Read oi and oy words
- Objective: Use context to self-correct and find word meaning
- Vocabulary: context clue, self-correct, reread
- Materials: 'Little Bear' by Else Holmelund Minarik (library)
- Assessment: Running record with accuracy and self-corrections.

### Math 1: Telling time to the hour and half hour
- Objective: Tell and write time to the hour and half hour on analog and digital clocks
- Vocabulary: hour hand, minute hand, o'clock, half past
- Materials: paper-plate clock, household clocks
- Assessment: Clock reading quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): How young animals help themselves survive
- Objective: Identify offspring behaviors that signal needs or keep them safe
- Vocabulary: signal, cry, follow, imitate
- Materials: 'Make Way for Ducklings' by Robert McCloskey (library), Smithsonian Learning Lab animal images
- Assessment: Written offspring-behavior explanation.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Needs, wants, goods and services
- Objective: Distinguish goods and services, needs and wants
- Vocabulary: goods, services, needs, wants
- Materials: magazine pictures, sorting mats
- Assessment: Sort quiz.

### Art & Music 1: Georgia O'Keeffe flowers; Vivaldi's Spring
- Objective: Draw a close-up flower
- Objective: Hear nature in music
- Vocabulary: close-up, nature, season
- Materials: fresh flower, magnifier, Vivaldi 'Spring' recording
- Assessment: Close-up flower drawing.

### PE 1: Partner cooperation
- Objective: Work with a partner to achieve a goal
- Vocabulary: partner, cooperate, encourage
- Materials: towel, ball, balloons
- Assessment: Partner cooperation check.

### Health 1: Medicines: help or harm
- Objective: Explain safe medicine use
- Vocabulary: medicine, dose, label, trusted adult
- Materials: empty medicine bottles, candy for look-alike comparison
- Assessment: Medicine safety explanation.
