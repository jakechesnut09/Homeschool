## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Shared research: an informative 'How a Plant Grows' book
- Objective: Gather information from sources and observations
- Objective: Write an informative book with facts and pictures
- Vocabulary: research, observe, label
- Materials: bean journal, plant books, paper for a 4-page book
- Assessment: Informative book rubric and syllable/ending assessment.

### Math 1: Measuring with nonstandard units
- Objective: Measure lengths by laying units end to end
- Objective: Understand no gaps or overlaps
- Vocabulary: unit, measure, gap, overlap
- Materials: paper clips, connecting cubes, shoe
- Assessment: Measurement recording sheet.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Nature as inventor wrap-up
- Objective: Explain how plants and animals use external parts to survive and inspire inventions
- Vocabulary: survive, need, structure
- Materials: science notebook, camera or drawing paper
- Assessment: Unit 6 assessment and field guide.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Celebrations around the world
- Objective: Compare holidays and ceremonies of varied cultures
- Vocabulary: celebration, ceremony, festival
- Materials: library books on Lunar New Year, Diwali, Hanukkah, Día de los Muertos, Kwanzaa
- Assessment: Celebration presentation.

### Art & Music 1: Van Gogh's Starry Night; fast and slow
- Objective: Use brushstroke lines
- Objective: Identify tempo
- Vocabulary: brushstroke, swirl, tempo, fast, slow
- Materials: image of 'The Starry Night', oil pastels, 'In the Hall of the Mountain King' recording
- Assessment: Night sky drawing and tempo response.

### PE 1: Dance creation and jump rope show
- Objective: Combine dance and jump rope
- Vocabulary: perform, sequence
- Materials: music, jump rope
- Assessment: Unit 7 show.

### Health 1: Safe and unsafe touch; trusted adults
- Objective: Distinguish safe and unsafe touch and situations
- Vocabulary: safe touch, unsafe touch, trusted adult, no, go, tell
- Materials: trusted adults hand from Unit 1
- Assessment: No-Go-Tell role-play.
