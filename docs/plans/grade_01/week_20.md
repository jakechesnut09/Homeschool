## Day 1 — Monday (school day 96, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Mother Goose fluency* (90 min): Practice 'Jack and Jill' and 'Hickory Dickory Dock' with a partner, reading with rhythm.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Teens* (60 min): Build 11–19 and say 'one ten and ___ ones'.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Record times* (30 min): Record today's sunrise and sunset times from a weather website and check whether it is dark at dinnertime.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Photo sort* (30 min): Sort Library of Congress photos of transportation (stagecoach, steam train, Model T, airplane) from oldest to newest.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Plan a sculpture* (30 min): Sketch a robot or creature from recycled parts.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Hot potato* (30 min): Pass quickly around a circle with family.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Senses intro* (20 min): Name the five senses and matching body parts.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 97, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Fluency routine* (90 min): Do three timed repeated readings of a vowel-team decodable passage; graph words correct per minute.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Expanded form* (60 min): Write 47 = 40 + 7; play a match game with cards.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Count the hours* (30 min): Move the hand on a paper clock from sunrise to sunset hour by hour to count daylight hours.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Timeline* (30 min): Create a transportation timeline with drawings.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Build* (30 min): Assemble the sculpture with tape and glue.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Monster ball* (30 min): Throw balls to push a big ball across a line.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Smell test* (20 min): Guess spices by smell with eyes closed.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 98, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Rehearse* (90 min): Choose 2 memorized poems; rehearse with gestures and volume.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Mystery number* (60 min): Solve riddles: 'I have 6 tens and 3 ones. What am I?'
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Summer comparison* (30 min): Look up sunrise and sunset for June 21 in your city and count those daylight hours.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Then and now* (30 min): Compare a trip to San Francisco by stagecoach vs. car (time, comfort).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Make an instrument* (30 min): Build a drum, shaker or rubber-band guitar.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Catch count* (30 min): Partner catch with step back after each success.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Touch box* (20 min): Reach into a box and identify 6 hidden objects (sponge, spoon, pinecone) by touch, describing each texture.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 99, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Poetry café* (90 min): Perform poems for family and record a video; listeners give one compliment.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Write numbers* (60 min): Write numbers from words and pictures of blocks.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Bar graph* (30 min): Make a 2-bar graph comparing winter and summer daylight hours on grid paper.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Build a vehicle* (30 min): Build a wagon or train from recycled materials.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Describe timbre* (30 min): Describe the sound: rattly, booming, twangy.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Ball relay* (30 min): Run a throw-and-run relay: throw overhand at a cone, run to get the ball, and hand it to the next player.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Taste test* (20 min): Taste sweet, salty, sour foods.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 100, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Unit 5 check* (90 min): Vowel-team assessment (25 words) and fluency check on a cold passage.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Place value quiz with teen and two-digit numbers.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain which season has more daylight and how your graph shows it.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain how one invention changed how people travel.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Present your sculpture and play your instrument.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Unit 5 check* (30 min): Skill check of toss, throw and catch.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Label a body diagram with sense organs.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Poetry performance and vowel-team review
- Objective: Read poems fluently with expression
- Objective: Apply all vowel teams
- Vocabulary: expression, fluency, rate
- Materials: poetry notebook, timer, recording device (phone)
- Assessment: Vowel-team assessment and words-correct-per-minute fluency baseline.

### Math 1: Teen numbers and two-digit numbers in expanded form
- Objective: Show 11–19 as a ten and some ones
- Objective: Write numbers as tens + ones
- Vocabulary: expanded form, teen
- Materials: base-ten blocks, number cards
- Assessment: Place value quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Measuring daylight in winter
- Objective: Collect sunrise and sunset times
- Objective: Count hours of daylight
- Vocabulary: daylight, sunrise, sunset, hour
- Materials: printed sunrise/sunset times (weather service or newspaper), paper clock, grid paper
- Assessment: Daylight bar graph with explanation.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Transportation of earlier days
- Objective: Describe and compare transportation long ago and today
- Vocabulary: transportation, wagon, steam train, horse and buggy
- Materials: LOC photos of wagons, trains, early cars, timeline strip
- Assessment: Transportation timeline.

### Art & Music 1: Found-object sculpture; make an instrument
- Objective: Build a sculpture from recycled materials
- Objective: Make an instrument and describe its timbre
- Vocabulary: recycle, assemblage, timbre
- Materials: boxes, tubes, bottle caps, rubber bands, beans, containers
- Assessment: Sculpture and instrument presentation.

### PE 1: Throw and catch games
- Objective: Apply throwing and catching in games
- Vocabulary: teamwork, share
- Materials: balls, cones
- Assessment: Unit 5 skill check.

### Health 1: My five senses
- Objective: Identify the five senses and body parts
- Vocabulary: sight, hearing, smell, taste, touch
- Materials: blindfold, spices, textured objects
- Assessment: Sense organ diagram.
