## Day 1 — Monday (school day 81, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *ai words* (90 min): Read rain, tail, paint, wait, snail; learn 'ai in the middle'.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Plan a survey* (60 min): Choose a question with 3 answers (favorite pet: dog, cat, fish).
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Journal check* (30 min): Lay out two weeks of moon drawings and describe how the lit part is growing (waxing) or shrinking (waning).
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *George Washington* (30 min): Read a picture biography and list 2 facts; find him on the quarter and dollar bill.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Pattern quilt* (30 min): Make a paper-quilt square with printed patterns.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Toss cues* (30 min): Learn cues: swing back, step with the opposite foot, release, follow through.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Heart and lungs* (20 min): Feel heartbeat and breathing before and after 1 minute of jumping.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 82, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *ay words* (90 min): Read play, day, stay, gray, tray; learn 'ay at the end' and sort ai/ay words.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Collect* (60 min): Survey at least 10 friends or family members by phone or in person; record tallies.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Lamp-and-ball model* (30 min): In a dark room, hold a foam ball at arm's length and turn slowly around a lamp; watch the lit part change like moon phases.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Abraham Lincoln* (30 min): Read a picture biography and learn why he was called 'Honest Abe'; find him on the penny.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Body percussion pattern* (30 min): Create an AB body percussion pattern (clap-clap-pat).
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Target toss* (30 min): Toss beanbags into a basket from 6 feet.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Benefits* (20 min): List how activity helps heart, muscles, bones and mood.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 83, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Rain* (90 min): Read Stevenson's 'Rain' and 'My Shadow'; circle words that help you see or hear something.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Bar graph* (60 min): Turn tallies into a bar graph on grid paper with labels.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *NASA Space Place* (30 min): Explore a NASA Space Place moon activity and add one new fact about the moon to your journal.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Compare* (30 min): Compare Washington and Lincoln on a Venn diagram.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Pattern combination* (30 min): Perform your body pattern while showing your quilt square.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Distance challenge* (30 min): Move back after 3 successful tosses.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *60 minutes* (20 min): Learn kids need 60 minutes of activity daily; brainstorm fun ways.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 84, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Memorize* (90 min): Begin memorizing 'Rain'; copy it into a poetry notebook and illustrate it.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Interpret* (60 min): Write 3 statements and 2 how-many-more questions about your graph.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Predict* (30 min): Using the pattern, draw what you think the moon will look like in 3 nights; check it when the day comes.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Landmarks* (30 min): Look at photos of the Washington Monument and Lincoln Memorial and match them to each president.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Art outside school* (30 min): Tell where you make art outside lessons (cards, chalk drawings).
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Partner toss* (30 min): Toss underhand to a partner's ready hands from 8 feet, taking a step back after every 3 good tosses.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Activity log* (20 min): Start a week-long activity log, drawing each active thing you do and how many minutes it lasted.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 85, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Recite 'Rain' with expression; ai/ay word read and spelling.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Unit 5 check* (60 min): Present your survey graph to family, then complete a data assessment reading a 3-category graph.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain in 2–3 sentences that the moon's pattern repeats about every month, pointing to your journal as evidence.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Unit 3 check* (30 min): Assessment: symbols, documents, holidays and heroes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Unit 4 share* (30 min): Hold a mini-show of prints and patterns.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Make 10 underhand tosses at the basket from 8 feet and record how many land in.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain 3 benefits of activity.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Vowel teams ai and ay; nursery rhymes and Stevenson
- Objective: Read ai and ay words
- Objective: Identify words that appeal to the senses in poems
- Vocabulary: rhyme, poem, line, vowel team
- Materials: 'A Child's Garden of Verses' by Robert Louis Stevenson (Gutenberg), poetry notebook
- Assessment: Recitation and ai/ay spelling.

### Math 1: Survey project and bar graphs
- Objective: Collect data with a survey
- Objective: Interpret data to answer comparison questions
- Vocabulary: survey, bar graph, most, least
- Materials: clipboard, grid paper
- Assessment: Survey graph project and data quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Moon patterns over a month
- Objective: Explain that the moon's shape changes in a repeating pattern
- Objective: Predict the next shape
- Vocabulary: waxing, waning, cycle, reflect
- Materials: moon journal, lamp without shade, ball on a stick (foam ball and pencil)
- Assessment: Prediction accuracy and written pattern explanation.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Presidents' Day: Washington and Lincoln
- Objective: Explain the achievements of George Washington and Abraham Lincoln
- Vocabulary: president, leader, honest, union
- Materials: coins (quarter and penny), portraits from the Library of Congress
- Assessment: Unit 3 assessment.

### Art & Music 1: Pattern review; performance
- Objective: Combine visual and musical patterns
- Vocabulary: pattern, perform
- Materials: prints, instruments
- Assessment: Unit 4 portfolio.

### PE 1: Underhand toss
- Objective: Toss underhand to a target
- Vocabulary: underhand, target, follow-through
- Materials: beanbags, laundry basket, hula hoop
- Assessment: Toss accuracy count.

### Health 1: Why move every day?
- Objective: Explain benefits of physical activity
- Vocabulary: exercise, heart, lungs, energy
- Materials: timer, jump rope
- Assessment: Benefits explanation.
