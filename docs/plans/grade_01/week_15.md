## Day 1 — Monday (school day 71, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *u_e and e_e* (90 min): Read cube, tune, mule, these, Pete; mixed silent-e word sort by vowel.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Problem types* (60 min): Solve one of each: add to, take from, put together, compare within 20.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Build a sundial* (30 min): Push a pencil through the center of a paper plate, anchor it with clay in a sunny spot, and point the plate's '12' mark north.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Look closely* (30 min): Examine an image of the Declaration of Independence (National Archives) with a magnifier; find John Hancock's signature.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Found-object prints* (30 min): Stamp patterns with corks, bottle caps and sponges.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Warm-up* (30 min): Do a 3-minute warm-up of jogging in place and arm circles.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Clean hands, clean food* (20 min): Wash hands and produce before cooking.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 72, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Main topic* (90 min): Read a ReadWorks grade 1 animal passage; name the main topic and two key details.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Math talk* (60 min): Solve 'How many ways can you make 15?' and explain to a family member.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Mark the hours* (30 min): Every hour from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., mark the pencil's shadow on the plate and write the clock time beside it.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *July 4th* (30 min): Learn that the Declaration said America would be free and that we celebrate on Independence Day.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Potato stamps* (30 min): With adult help, carve simple potato stamps and print a repeating pattern wrapping paper.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Stretch* (30 min): Learn 5 stretches (butterfly, toe touch, cat-cow, arm cross, side reach).
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Cold and hot* (20 min): Learn that some foods must stay cold in the fridge.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 73, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Picture vs. words* (90 min): Look at a diagram and caption in an animal book; tell one fact you learned from the picture and one from the words.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Fact practice* (60 min): Khan Academy 'add and subtract within 20' practice set.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Predict and test* (30 min): Before checking, predict where tomorrow's 10 a.m. shadow will fall; place a coin there and see if the shadow lands on it.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *We the People* (30 min): Look at the first words of the Constitution and discuss that it is the nation's rulebook.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Verse and chorus* (30 min): Listen to 'Jingle Bells' or a seasonal song and raise hands when the chorus repeats.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Kids yoga* (30 min): Do a GoNoodle or kids yoga video.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Cook together* (20 min): Make a simple MyPlate meal (wrap or salad) with an adult.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 74, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Fact cards* (90 min): Choose an animal for a mini report; write 3 facts on index cards from a library book.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Create problems* (60 min): Write and illustrate two story problems for someone else to solve.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Sundial vs. clock* (30 min): Read the time from your sundial and from a clock at 3 different times; record both in a table.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Class constitution* (30 min): Write a 'family constitution' with 3 rules everyone signs.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Pattern songs* (30 min): Sing a cumulative song like 'The Twelve Days' or 'There Was an Old Lady' and notice the pattern.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Sit and reach* (30 min): Measure sit-and-reach with a ruler.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Kitchen safety* (20 min): Learn knife, stove and hot-pan safety rules.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 75, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Silent-e spelling test (10 words); main topic + 2 details organizer.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Unit 4 check* (60 min): Unit test: facts and story problems within 20.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain to a family member why the shadow moves (the sun's position changes) using your plate as evidence.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain what each document is and name one person connected to it (Jefferson, Washington).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Display prints and identify verse/chorus.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Lead a warm-up and stretch for family.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain 3 kitchen safety rules.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Long u and e with silent e; pictures vs. words
- Objective: Read u_e and e_e words
- Objective: Tell what info comes from pictures vs. words
- Vocabulary: main topic, key detail, caption, diagram
- Materials: 'Gail Gibbons – Frogs' or another illustrated animal book, index cards
- Assessment: Silent-e spelling test and main-topic organizer.

### Math 1: Problem solving within 20
- Objective: Solve all problem types within 20
- Objective: Explain strategies
- Vocabulary: strategy, explain, model
- Materials: counters, number line, Khan Academy practice
- Assessment: Unit 4 assessment.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Building a shadow-stick sundial
- Objective: Use shadows to track the sun's movement
- Objective: Predict shadow positions
- Vocabulary: sundial, shadow, predict
- Materials: paper plate, pencil and modeling clay, marker, clock
- Assessment: Sundial with hourly marks and a correct oral explanation.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Essential documents: Declaration of Independence and Constitution
- Objective: Identify key documents and the people connected to them
- Vocabulary: document, independence, constitution, founder
- Materials: National Archives DocsTeach images of the Declaration and Constitution, magnifying glass
- Assessment: Document explanation and family constitution.

### Art & Music 1: Stamp printmaking; patterns in music
- Objective: Make prints with found objects
- Objective: Find repeated parts in music
- Vocabulary: print, stamp, repeat, verse, chorus
- Materials: potatoes or sponges, cork, paint, paper
- Assessment: Print pattern and verse/chorus identification.

### PE 1: Stretching and flexibility
- Objective: Perform stretches
- Objective: Explain why we warm up
- Vocabulary: stretch, flexible, warm-up, cool-down
- Materials: mat
- Assessment: Stretch routine leadership.

### Health 1: Food safety in the kitchen
- Objective: Practice safe food handling
- Vocabulary: food safety, clean, cook, refrigerate
- Materials: kitchen tools, recipe card
- Assessment: Kitchen safety explanation.
