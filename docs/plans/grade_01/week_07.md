## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *-ck words* (90 min): Read duck, back, sick, rock, truck; learn that ck comes right after a short vowel.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Think addition* (60 min): Solve 9 − 4 by asking '4 + ? = 9' with cubes.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *History of sound signals* (30 min): Learn how drums, bells and whistles sent messages long ago.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Directions in the house* (30 min): Label walls N, S, E, W using a compass; follow directions like 'take 3 steps north'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Warm vs. cool* (30 min): Sort crayons into warm and cool colors; discuss what feelings they give.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Slide* (30 min): Slide sideways leading with one side, then the other, between two cones.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Pepper experiment* (20 min): Sprinkle pepper on water and touch with a soapy finger to see germs 'run away'.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *-ng words* (90 min): Read sing, ring, long, hung, bang; write a silly sentence using three -ng words.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Fact families* (60 min): Write the four facts for 3, 5, 8 using a triangle card.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Cup phone* (30 min): Build a cup-and-string telephone and test talking across a room.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Map key* (30 min): Study a park map's key and find 5 symbols on the map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Mood painting* (30 min): Paint a sunny day in warm colors and a rainy day in cool colors.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Slide tag* (30 min): Play slide tag along a chalk line: everyone may only slide sideways, and the tagger switches each round.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Six steps* (20 min): Learn handwashing steps; sing 'Happy Birthday' twice while scrubbing.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 33, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *A List* (90 min): Read 'A List' from Frog and Toad Together; make your own to-do list for tomorrow with 4 items.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Missing number* (60 min): Solve 8 − ? = 5 and ? − 2 = 6 with a number line.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Test materials* (30 min): Compare string, yarn and wire for the cup phone; record which works best.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Compass rose* (30 min): Draw a compass rose and add it to your room map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Rest* (30 min): Learn the quarter rest (silent beat); clap rhythms with rests.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Step-hop* (30 min): Practice step-hop slowly, then faster as a skip.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *When to wash* (20 min): Make a poster of times to wash (before eating, after bathroom, after coughing).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 34, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Proper nouns* (90 min): Write the names of 5 family members, your street and your town, capitalizing each proper noun.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Fact family game* (60 min): Cover one corner of a triangle card and name the hidden number.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Signal code* (30 min): Create a 3-beat drum code for messages and test it outside.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Treasure hunt* (30 min): Follow a family-made map with symbols and directions to find a hidden treasure.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Question and answer* (30 min): Clap a 4-beat rhythm 'question' and make up an 'answer' rhythm.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Locomotor music* (30 min): Change locomotor skill each time the music stops.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Glow test* (20 min): Rub on lotion with glitter, wash, and check how much is left to test your scrubbing.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 35, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Read a decodable passage with digraphs; circle common nouns and box proper nouns in 5 sentences.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Fact family worksheet (5 families) and missing-number equations.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain how the vibrations travel in your cup phone.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Make a map of your yard or street with a key of 4 symbols and a compass rose.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Explain how color changed the mood of your two paintings.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Demonstrate slide and step-hop; encourage a family member.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Demonstrate all handwashing steps.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: ck, ng, and proper nouns; Frog and Toad Together
- Objective: Read words with -ck and -ng
- Objective: Capitalize proper nouns
- Vocabulary: proper noun, common noun, ending
- Materials: 'Frog and Toad Together' (library), lined paper
- Assessment: Noun sort worksheet and digraph passage read with 90% accuracy.

### Math 1: Relating addition and subtraction; fact families
- Objective: Use addition to solve subtraction
- Objective: Write fact families
- Vocabulary: fact family, related facts, unknown
- Materials: triangle fact-family cards, counters
- Assessment: Fact family and missing-number check.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Communicating with sound over distance
- Objective: Design a device that uses sound to communicate
- Vocabulary: communicate, signal, distance
- Materials: paper cups, string, yarn, wire, drum
- Assessment: Cup-phone material comparison data.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Map symbols, keys and cardinal directions
- Objective: Use map keys and cardinal directions
- Vocabulary: map key, legend, north, south, east, west, compass rose
- Materials: compass, sticky notes labeled N, S, E, W, park map
- Assessment: Yard or street map with key and compass rose.

### Art & Music 1: Warm and cool colors; rhythm patterns
- Objective: Use warm and cool colors to show mood
- Objective: Read and create rhythm patterns
- Vocabulary: warm colors, cool colors, mood, rest
- Materials: crayons or watercolors, rhythm cards
- Assessment: Mood paintings and question-answer rhythm.

### PE 1: Sliding and skipping preview
- Objective: Slide sideways
- Objective: Try the step-hop of the skip
- Vocabulary: slide, sideways, skip, step-hop
- Materials: cones, music
- Assessment: Slide demonstration.

### Health 1: Handwashing
- Objective: Wash hands correctly for 20 seconds
- Vocabulary: soap, scrub, 20 seconds
- Materials: soap, pepper and water bowl, timer
- Assessment: Handwashing demonstration.
