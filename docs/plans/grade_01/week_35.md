## Day 1 — Monday (school day 171, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Owl at Home* (90 min): Read 'Tear-Water Tea'; tell how Owl feels and why.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Fact fluency* (60 min): Final fluency check within 10 and practice within 20.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Choose a problem* (30 min): Choose a challenge: send a message across the yard using light or sound, or solve a human problem by copying an animal part.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Home work* (30 min): List all the work family members do at home (cooking, cleaning, caring for children) and how it helps.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Set up* (30 min): Hang the matted, labeled artworks at child eye level following your floor plan.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Plan events* (30 min): Choose 5 field-day events and draw a poster for each.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review habits* (20 min): Review trackers from the year.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 172, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Mouse Soup* (90 min): Read the story frame; tell how Mouse tricks the weasel.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Place value review* (60 min): Build 5 two-digit numbers with blocks, find 10 more and 10 less for each, and compare pairs with >, <, =.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Sketch and build* (30 min): Sketch two ideas, choose one and build a first prototype.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Community helpers* (30 min): Interview a community helper (librarian, firefighter, mail carrier).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Invitations* (30 min): Make invitations with pattern borders for family.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Sack race* (30 min): Practice pillowcase sack race.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Choose goals* (20 min): Choose a summer health goal in eating, activity and hygiene.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 173, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Poppleton* (90 min): Read a Poppleton story independently; record one funny part.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Story problems* (60 min): Solve 6 mixed story problems (add to, take from, compare, three addends) and write an equation for each.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Test* (30 min): Test the prototype 3 times and record what happened in a simple data table.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Chore chart* (30 min): Make a family chore chart and take on a job for the week.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Art show* (30 min): Host the art show; act as docent.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Egg and spoon* (30 min): Practice an egg-and-spoon race with a plastic egg, walking a 15-foot course.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Plan* (20 min): Write or draw 2 steps for each goal (e.g., keep a water bottle in the fridge to drink more water).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 174, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Word categories* (90 min): Sort words from the books into categories (animals, feelings, foods) and define 3 by category and attribute ('An owl is a bird that...').
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Game day* (60 min): Play favorite math games from the year.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Improve* (30 min): Change one feature based on your data and test again.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Thank-you* (30 min): Write a thank-you card to a community or home worker.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Concert* (30 min): Perform 3 songs with instruments.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Relay* (30 min): Practice a hop-gallop-slide relay with a baton handoff.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Share* (20 min): Share your goals with family and ask one person to be your goal buddy.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 175, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Compare two Lobel characters; read a chapter aloud for fluency.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Cumulative review part 1: facts within 20, equal sign, and place value (20 items).
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Get feedback from a family member using 'one thing that works, one thing to try'.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain why work at home is important even though it is unpaid.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Reflect* (30 min): Tell what you were proud of and what you'd do differently.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Make a field day schedule with event times and invite family.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain one goal, its steps and how you will track it over the summer.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Author study: Arnold Lobel and Cynthia Rylant
- Objective: Compare books by one author
- Objective: Read grade-level text with understanding
- Vocabulary: author, favorite, category
- Materials: 'Owl at Home' and 'Mouse Soup' by Arnold Lobel; 'Poppleton' by Cynthia Rylant (library)
- Assessment: Author comparison and fluency read.

### Math 1: Review: operations and place value
- Objective: Review addition/subtraction within 20 and 100
- Vocabulary: review, fluency
- Materials: flash cards, base-ten blocks
- Assessment: Cumulative review part 1.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Engineering showcase project
- Objective: Apply science ideas from the year to design a solution
- Vocabulary: showcase, solution, prototype
- Materials: cups, string, flashlight, cardboard, tape, foil
- Assessment: Prototype and test data.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Work at home and in the community
- Objective: Recognize contributions of those who work in the home and community
- Vocabulary: volunteer, contribution, community helper
- Materials: family chore chart, thank-you card supplies
- Assessment: Chore chart and interview notes.

### Art & Music 1: Art show and spring concert
- Objective: Present art and perform music for an audience
- Vocabulary: exhibit, concert, perform
- Materials: art display, instruments
- Assessment: Art show and concert performance.

### PE 1: Field day preparation
- Objective: Plan field day events
- Vocabulary: event, relay
- Materials: cones, sacks or pillowcases, spoons and eggs
- Assessment: Field day plan.

### Health 1: My health goals
- Objective: Set personal health goals
- Vocabulary: goal, plan
- Materials: goal sheet
- Assessment: Goal sheet.
