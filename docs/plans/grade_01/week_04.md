## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Short e* (90 min): Read bed, red, pet, ten, web; segment each word into sounds and spell it with tiles.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *More or fewer* (60 min): Build two cube towers (9 and 6) and tell which has more and how many more.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Feel your voice* (30 min): Hum with fingers on your throat; hum loud and soft and describe the vibrations.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *What is voting?* (30 min): Read 'Duck for President' by Doreen Cronin (library) and talk about why the animals voted.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Mondrian look* (30 min): Look at Mondrian's 'Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow' and find straight lines and rectangles.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Obstacle words* (30 min): Build an obstacle course: crawl under a table, step over a broomstick, through a hoop, around a chair.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Read* (20 min): Read 'Last Stop on Market Street' and talk about how CJ learns to see beauty in others.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 17, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Vowel sort* (90 min): Sort 20 word cards into five short-vowel columns, read each column aloud.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Compare stories* (60 min): Solve 'Ana has 7 stickers, Ben has 4. How many more does Ana have?' with cube towers.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Ruler twang* (30 min): Hang a ruler off a table and twang it at different lengths; record what you see and hear.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Family vote* (30 min): Hold a direct vote on Friday's family movie or snack with paper ballots; count the tallies.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Mondrian design* (30 min): Use black tape or marker lines and color rectangles red, blue and yellow.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Position game* (30 min): Place a beanbag or ball where called (behind, in front, next to you).
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Same and different* (20 min): List 3 ways you are the same as and 3 ways different from a friend.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 18, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Read Henry and Mudge* (90 min): Read the first chapter of 'Henry and Mudge' together; answer questions about how Henry feels and why.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Number games* (60 min): Play 'War' with cards 0–10 and say who has more and by how much.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Rubber band guitar* (30 min): Stretch rubber bands over a tissue box; pluck and observe the vibrations.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Majority rules* (30 min): Count votes and explain that the choice with the most votes wins; discuss how the others feel.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Make a shaker* (30 min): Make a rice-bottle shaker and play the beat to 'This Old Man'.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Partner mirror* (30 min): Mirror a partner's movement on the left and right.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Kindness challenge* (20 min): Do one kind act each day and record it.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 19, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Write it* (90 min): Write a 2–3 sentence story about a pet or animal you know, using first and then; check each sentence for a capital and period.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Count to 20 and back* (60 min): Count forward and backward from 20; fill in missing numbers on a number path.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Sound walk* (30 min): List 10 sounds around the house and what is vibrating to make each one.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Make a rule by vote* (30 min): Propose a new household rule and let everyone vote on it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Beat performance* (30 min): Perform a song with shaker on the beat while singing.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Course redesign* (30 min): Design and run your own obstacle course using 6 position words.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Include others* (20 min): Role-play inviting someone new to play.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 20, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Unit 1 check* (90 min): Phonics screener of 25 CVC words and 10 sight words; read the narrative aloud and add one detail.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Unit 1 check* (60 min): Unit test: counting, writing numerals, add/subtract and compare stories within 10.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Draw and label how a sound is made, using the word vibrate.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain direct democracy in one sentence and give the family-vote example.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Unit 1 share* (30 min): Hold a mini-gallery of unit art and a beat performance; tell which line or shape you like best.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Unit 1 check* (30 min): Run the course while calling out each position word.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Make a 'kindness recipe' card with 4 ingredients.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Short e and all short vowels; narrative sentences
- Objective: Distinguish all five short vowel sounds
- Objective: Write a 2–3 sentence personal narrative
- Vocabulary: short vowel, segment, detail
- Materials: sound boxes, 'Henry and Mudge' by Cynthia Rylant (library), lined paper
- Assessment: Unit 1 phonics screener (CVC + sight words) and a scored 2–3 sentence narrative.

### Math 1: Comparing numbers and 'how many more'
- Objective: Compare quantities within 20
- Objective: Solve compare problems
- Vocabulary: more, fewer, compare
- Materials: connecting cubes, dot cards
- Assessment: Unit 1 assessment.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Vibrations make sound
- Objective: Show that vibrating materials make sound
- Vocabulary: vibrate, sound, pitch
- Materials: rubber bands, empty tissue box, ruler, drum (pot and spoon)
- Assessment: Labeled drawing explaining vibration and sound.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Direct democracy: everyone votes
- Objective: Explain direct democracy and vote on a family decision
- Vocabulary: vote, majority, democracy, ballot
- Materials: paper ballots, box for a ballot box, tally chart
- Assessment: Explanation of direct democracy with an example.

### Art & Music 1: Line and shape design; beat review
- Objective: Combine lines and shapes in a design
- Objective: Show the beat on instruments
- Vocabulary: design, pattern, instrument
- Materials: paper and markers, homemade shakers (rice in a bottle)
- Assessment: Unit 1 portfolio check and beat performance.

### PE 1: Spatial words with objects
- Objective: Use over, under, through, around with objects
- Vocabulary: over, under, through, around, next to
- Materials: chairs, broomstick, hula hoop, blanket
- Assessment: Unit 1 performance check.

### Health 1: Kindness, respect and differences
- Objective: Show caring and respect
- Objective: Appreciate differences
- Vocabulary: kindness, respect, different, include
- Materials: 'Last Stop on Market Street' by Matt de la Peña (library)
- Assessment: Kindness recipe card.
