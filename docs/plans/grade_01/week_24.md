## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Draft* (90 min): Write the opinion piece from the organizer: opinion sentence, two reasons with 'because', closing.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Open number line* (60 min): Solve 47 + 30 and 56 + 8 with jumps on an open number line.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Plant dissection* (30 min): Take apart a weed or flower and tape roots, stem, leaves and flower onto paper with labels.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Family tree* (30 min): Make a simple family tree with photos or drawings.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Great Wave* (30 min): Look at Hokusai's 'The Great Wave' and describe lines and colors.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Bounce and catch* (30 min): Bounce and catch a ball with two hands.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Crossing streets* (20 min): Practice stop, look left-right-left, listen, and hold an adult's hand.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 117, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Revise* (90 min): Listen to a family member's question and add one more detail or reason.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Quick draws* (60 min): Use sticks and dots to solve 25 + 7 and 63 + 20.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Celery lab* (30 min): Put a celery stalk in blue-colored water; check after 4 hours and next morning to see how the stem carries water.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Where are we from?* (30 min): Find places your family came from on a world map and connect them with yarn to your home.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Wave painting* (30 min): Paint your own wave with blue tints and shades.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *One-hand dribble* (30 min): Dribble with fingertip pushes, stationary.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Traffic signs* (20 min): Learn walk/don't walk signals and stop signs.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 118, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Edit* (90 min): Use a checklist to fix capitals, end marks, commas and spelling of r-controlled words.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Story problems* (60 min): Solve 'A farm has 46 chickens and 8 ducks' problems.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Root pull* (30 min): Pull up a weed and a grass clump; compare roots and explain how roots hold plants and take in water.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Traditions* (30 min): Share a family tradition (food, holiday, song) and draw it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Koto* (30 min): Listen to koto music and describe its sound.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Walk and dribble* (30 min): Dribble while walking along a straight chalk line, then a curved one.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Car safety* (20 min): Practice buckling into a booster seat correctly.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 119, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Publish* (90 min): Copy neatly and illustrate; read it aloud as a 'book review'.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Explain* (60 min): Write one sentence explaining how you added 59 + 4.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Plant protection* (30 min): Look closely (safely) at rose thorns or cactus spines and discuss how they protect the plant from animals.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Community diversity* (30 min): Visit the library or a farmers' market and note languages, foods and cultures you see.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Song* (30 min): Learn 'Sakura' and describe the melody.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Dribble tag* (30 min): Play dribble tag: everyone dribbles while trying to tag others without losing their ball.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Neighborhood walk* (20 min): Walk with an adult and use crosswalks.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 120, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Unit 6 check* (90 min): R-controlled vowel assessment (24 words) and opinion piece rubric score.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Strategy quiz with written explanations.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Plant-parts quiz: label a diagram and match each part to its job.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain one way your community is diverse and one value people share.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Explain why museums keep 'The Great Wave' safe.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Unit 6 check* (30 min): Skill check of kick, strike and dribble.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Demonstrate safe crossing steps.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Writing, revising and sharing an opinion piece
- Objective: Draft an opinion piece
- Objective: Revise with feedback
- Vocabulary: draft, revise, edit
- Materials: opinion organizer, editing checklist (capitals, spaces, punctuation, spelling)
- Assessment: Opinion writing rubric and r-controlled vowel assessment.

### Math 1: Strategies for adding within 100
- Objective: Use place value and properties to add
- Objective: Explain reasoning in writing
- Vocabulary: strategy, regroup, explain
- Materials: base-ten blocks, open number line
- Assessment: Strategy quiz with explanations.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Plant external parts and their jobs
- Objective: Identify plant parts and how they help plants survive
- Vocabulary: root, stem, leaf, flower, thorn
- Materials: celery stalk, food coloring and cups, garden weed, rose stem or cactus photo
- Assessment: Plant-parts diagram quiz.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: My family's story and our diverse community
- Objective: Describe your family's background and traditions
- Objective: Recognize diversity in the community
- Vocabulary: ancestor, tradition, diversity, heritage
- Materials: family photos, world map, yarn
- Assessment: Family tree and oral explanation.

### Art & Music 1: Art of Japan; music of Japan
- Objective: Explore Japanese prints and folk songs
- Vocabulary: woodblock print, wave, koto
- Materials: image of Hokusai's 'The Great Wave', blue paints
- Assessment: Wave painting and listening response.

### PE 1: Hand dribbling
- Objective: Dribble a ball with one hand
- Vocabulary: dribble, fingertips, push
- Materials: playground ball or basketball
- Assessment: Unit 6 skill check.

### Health 1: Pedestrian and car safety
- Objective: Practice crossing streets and car safety
- Vocabulary: pedestrian, crosswalk, seat belt, booster seat
- Materials: chalk crosswalk, booster seat
- Assessment: Crossing demonstration.
