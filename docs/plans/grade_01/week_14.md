## Day 1 — Monday (school day 66, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *i_e words* (90 min): Read bike, kite, time, ride, five; write them in sentences.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Take to ten* (60 min): Solve 13 − 4 as 13 − 3 − 1 on a ten-frame.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Sun safety and landmarks* (30 min): Learn never to look directly at the sun; stand in the same spot and sketch where the sun is above a tree, roof or pole at 9 a.m.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *The flag* (30 min): Count the stars and stripes and learn what they stand for; make a paper flag.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Pattern hunt* (30 min): Find patterns in clothing, floors and wrapping paper at home; draw three.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Muscles move bones* (30 min): Feel your arm muscle when you bend your elbow.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Cereal boxes* (20 min): Look at cereal boxes for cartoons and colors meant to attract kids.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 67, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *o_e words* (90 min): Read home, rope, bone, nose, stone; play a 'change the vowel' game (hop→hope).
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Think addition* (60 min): Solve 15 − 7 by thinking 7 + ? = 15.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Three sky sketches* (30 min): Sketch the sun's position from the same spot at morning, noon and late afternoon, labeling east and west with a compass.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Bald eagle* (30 min): Learn why the bald eagle was chosen as the national bird; view it on the Great Seal and a quarter.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Pattern borders* (30 min): Make an ABAB and ABC pattern border around a paper.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Animal strength* (30 min): Do bear crawls, crab walks and frog jumps.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Ad detective* (20 min): Watch a kid's food commercial and discuss its tricks.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 68, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Text feature hunt* (90 min): Use sticky notes to find a table of contents, heading, bold word, and glossary in a nonfiction animal book.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Number line jumps* (60 min): Show 16 − 9 with jumps on a number line.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Chalk shadow tracing* (30 min): Stand on an X in the driveway and have someone trace your shadow at 9 a.m., noon and 3 p.m.; compare length and direction.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Statue of Liberty* (30 min): Look at historic Library of Congress photos of the Statue of Liberty being built; discuss what it welcomes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Folk song* (30 min): Sing 'She'll Be Coming 'Round the Mountain' with motions.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Modified push-ups* (30 min): Practice knee push-ups and curl-ups.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Family food* (20 min): Interview a family member about favorite family foods and traditions.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 69, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Adjectives* (90 min): Describe an animal photo with 5 adjectives (color, size, feel); write 'The ___ frog is ___.'
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Story problems* (60 min): Solve 5 subtraction stories within 20 with a model.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Pattern talk* (30 min): Use your sketches and tracings to describe the pattern (rises in the east, highest at midday, sets in the west) in two sentences.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Liberty Bell* (30 min): Find the crack in a Liberty Bell photo and learn its story.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Song story* (30 min): Learn where the song came from and why people sang while working.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Hang* (30 min): Hang from monkey bars for as long as possible.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Design a healthy ad* (20 min): Make an ad for a fruit or vegetable.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 70, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Use the table of contents to find two facts; read an i_e/o_e word list.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Subtraction strategies quiz; explain a strategy for 14 − 6.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Predict and draw where the sun and your shadow will be at noon tomorrow, then check your prediction outside.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Match 6 symbols to their names and meanings.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Explain your pattern border and lead a folk song.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Record push-ups, curl-ups and hang time.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain one influence on your food choices.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Long i and long o with silent e; text features
- Objective: Read i_e and o_e words
- Objective: Use headings, table of contents and glossary
- Vocabulary: table of contents, heading, glossary, adjective
- Materials: nonfiction animal book with text features, sticky notes
- Assessment: Text-feature scavenger hunt and long i/o word list.

### Math 1: Subtraction within 20
- Objective: Subtract by making ten or thinking addition
- Vocabulary: decompose, count back, related fact
- Materials: double ten-frame, number line 0–20
- Assessment: Subtraction strategy quiz with explanation.

### Science 1 (NGSS): The sun's path across the sky
- Objective: Observe and describe the sun's daily pattern
- Objective: Use the pattern to make predictions
- Vocabulary: sunrise, sunset, pattern, east, west
- Materials: compass (or phone compass), chalk, sidewalk or driveway
- Assessment: Prediction drawing checked against a real observation.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: American symbols
- Objective: Identify the flag, bald eagle, Statue of Liberty, Liberty Bell and their meanings
- Vocabulary: symbol, landmark, liberty, eagle
- Materials: Smithsonian/LOC images of symbols, construction paper
- Assessment: Symbol matching quiz.

### Art & Music 1: Patterns all around; folk songs
- Objective: Find and make visual patterns
- Objective: Sing American folk songs
- Vocabulary: pattern, repeat, folk song
- Materials: patterned fabric, markers, lyrics to 'She'll Be Coming 'Round the Mountain'
- Assessment: Pattern border.

### PE 1: Strong muscles and bones
- Objective: Do strength activities
- Objective: Explain that muscles move bones
- Vocabulary: muscle, bone, strength
- Materials: monkey bars or sturdy bar, mat
- Assessment: Strength baseline record.

### Health 1: Influences on food choices
- Objective: Identify how family, friends and ads influence choices
- Vocabulary: advertisement, influence, choice
- Materials: cereal boxes, magazine ads
- Assessment: Healthy food ad.
