## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *l-blends* (90 min): Blend and read clap, flag, glad, plum, slip, black; tap each sound.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Balance* (60 min): Put 4 + 2 cubes on one side and 6 on the other of a hanger balance; discuss 'the same as'.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Light sources* (30 min): Sort pictures into light sources (sun, lamp, fire) and non-sources (mirror, moon).
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Nested places* (30 min): Make nesting circles: home → city → California → United States → North America → Earth.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Matisse look* (30 min): Look at Matisse's 'The Snail' and find warm and cool colors.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Body part balances* (30 min): Balance on 2 feet, 1 foot, 2 hands and 1 foot, then 5 body parts.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Why sleep?* (20 min): Learn that 6-year-olds need about 9–12 hours of sleep and why.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Read The Little Red Hen* (90 min): Read the folktale aloud; act it out with stuffed animals, repeating 'Not I!'
- [ ] **Math 1** — *True or false* (60 min): Decide if 6 = 6, 7 = 8 − 1, 5 + 2 = 2 + 5, 4 + 1 = 5 + 2 are true; prove with cubes.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Peep box* (30 min): Look into a closed shoebox with a toy inside; then open a flap to let light in and compare.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *California map* (30 min): Find your city, Sacramento (the capital) and the Pacific Ocean on a California map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Cut paper* (30 min): Make a Matisse-style cut-paper composition with warm and cool colors.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Statue game* (30 min): Freeze in a balance when music stops.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Sleep log* (20 min): Start a sleep log of bedtime and wake time for a week.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 43, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Retell* (90 min): Retell the story using a 5-finger retell (characters, setting, problem, events, ending).
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Both sides* (60 min): Solve 3 + 4 = ? + 5 with cubes.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Dark room* (30 min): Sit in a dark closet and record what you can and cannot see; add light gradually.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *U.S. map* (30 min): Find California on a U.S. map; name the states that border it (Oregon, Nevada, Arizona).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Rhythm game* (30 min): Play rhythm bingo with ta, ti-ti and rest cards.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Partner balance* (30 min): Create a balance with a partner touching hands.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Bedtime routine* (20 min): Make a picture chart for a calm bedtime routine.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 44, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Lesson* (90 min): Discuss the lesson (people who help get to share); write one sentence telling the lesson.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Write your own* (60 min): Write 3 true and 2 false equations for a family member to sort.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Evidence talk* (30 min): Explain why we need light to see, using your peep-box observations.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Globe find* (30 min): Find the United States and California on a globe; compare the globe to a flat map.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Rhythm check* (30 min): Read and clap 4 rhythm cards accurately.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Hold it* (30 min): Hold three balances for 5 seconds each.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Read* (20 min): Read a bedtime-themed picture book and discuss rest.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 45, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Spelling of 8 l-blend words; oral retell scored with retell checklist.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): True/false equations quiz (10 items).
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Draw what you saw in the box with and without light.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Label your city, California and the U.S. on blank maps.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Unit 2 share* (30 min): Show the Matisse collage and use color words to describe it.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Show balances on 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 body parts.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Review sleep log and explain how you feel with good sleep.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: L-blends; The Little Red Hen
- Objective: Blend initial l-blends
- Objective: Retell a folktale and state its lesson
- Vocabulary: blend, retell, lesson, folktale
- Materials: 'The Little Red Hen' (any public-domain version), blend cards
- Assessment: Retell checklist and l-blend spelling.

### Math 1: The equal sign and true/false equations
- Objective: Explain that = means 'the same as'
- Objective: Decide whether equations are true or false
- Vocabulary: equal sign, true, false, balance
- Materials: pan balance or coat-hanger balance, cubes
- Assessment: True/false equation quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): We see when there is light
- Objective: Explain that objects in darkness can only be seen when illuminated
- Vocabulary: light, dark, illuminate, source
- Materials: shoebox with a peephole and a lid flap, flashlight, small toy
- Assessment: Peep-box drawings with explanation.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: California and the United States on maps
- Objective: Locate your community, California and the U.S. on maps and a globe
- Vocabulary: state, country, border, capital
- Materials: globe, U.S. map, California map
- Assessment: Labeled blank maps.

### Art & Music 1: Color project: Matisse-style cut paper; rhythm review
- Objective: Create a cut-paper artwork using color choices
- Objective: Perform rhythms accurately
- Vocabulary: cut-out, collage, rhythm
- Materials: colored paper, image of Matisse's 'The Snail'
- Assessment: Unit 2 art critique and rhythm check.

### PE 1: Balancing on body parts
- Objective: Balance on 1–5 body parts
- Vocabulary: balance, base of support, still
- Materials: mat or rug
- Assessment: Balance demonstration.

### Health 1: Sleep and rest
- Objective: Explain why sleep matters
- Objective: Create a bedtime routine
- Vocabulary: sleep, rest, routine, energy
- Materials: paper for routine chart
- Assessment: Sleep log and routine chart.
