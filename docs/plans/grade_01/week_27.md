## Day 1 — Monday (school day 131, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *-s and -es* (90 min): Add -s or -es to nouns and verbs (plants, boxes, grows); read in sentences.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Line up* (60 min): Order 3 pencils/crayons from shortest to longest.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Test design A* (30 min): Run 3 trials with your first design (e.g., drop the protected egg from 30 cm, 60 cm, 90 cm) and record results.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Anansi* (30 min): Read an Anansi the Spider tale (West Africa/Caribbean) and discuss the lesson.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Observe outside* (30 min): Sketch the view from a window: sky, horizon, ground.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Beat moves* (30 min): Clap and step side-to-side to the beat of a song, then march and clap together.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *What is an emergency?* (20 min): Sort situations into emergency and not.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 132, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *-ed sounds* (90 min): Sort -ed words by sound /t/, /d/, /ed/ (jumped, played, planted).
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Direct compare* (60 min): Compare household objects by lining up ends.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Test design B* (30 min): Build a quick version of your second sketch and run the same 3 trials.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Lon Po Po* (30 min): Read 'Lon Po Po' (China) and compare it with Little Red Riding Hood.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Landscape* (30 min): Paint the landscape with watercolors.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Folk dance* (30 min): Learn the Hokey Pokey and Chicken Dance.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Learn address* (20 min): Memorize home address and a parent's phone number.

- [ ] **Math 1** — *Indirect compare* (60 min): Use a string to compare the length of a door and a table that can't be moved.

- [ ] **PE 1** — *GoNoodle* (30 min): Follow a GoNoodle dance video, then do it again with bigger, cleaner moves.

- [ ] **Math 1** — *Length hunt* (60 min): Find 3 things longer and 3 shorter than your arm.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Inflectional endings -s, -ed, -ing; prefixes un- and re-
- Objective: Read words with -s, -ed, -ing
- Objective: Use un- and re- as meaning clues
- Vocabulary: ending, root word, prefix
- Materials: word-building cards, sprouting bean
- Assessment: Inflectional-ending and prefix quiz.

### Math 1: Comparing and ordering lengths
- Objective: Order three objects by length
- Objective: Compare indirectly using a third object
- Vocabulary: longer, shorter, length, compare
- Materials: string, pencils, household objects
- Assessment: Comparing lengths performance task.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Test, compare and improve designs
- Objective: Test two designs and compare results
- Objective: Improve a design using data
- Vocabulary: test, compare, strength, weakness
- Materials: prototypes, raw eggs or water balloons, ruler and timer
- Assessment: Test data table and improvement reflection.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Folklore from many cultures
- Objective: Compare beliefs, customs and traditions found in folktales
- Vocabulary: folktale, culture, custom, compare
- Materials: 'Lon Po Po' by Ed Young, 'Anansi' tales (public domain), 'The Mitten' by Jan Brett
- Assessment: Folktale comparison chart.

### Art & Music 1: Landscapes; loud and soft
- Objective: Paint a landscape with foreground and background
- Objective: Identify dynamics
- Vocabulary: landscape, horizon, forte, piano (soft)
- Materials: watercolors, Haydn 'Surprise Symphony'
- Assessment: Landscape painting and dynamics.

### PE 1: Rhythm and dance
- Objective: Move to a beat in a dance sequence
- Vocabulary: rhythm, beat, sequence
- Materials: music, GoNoodle
- Assessment: Dance performance.

### Health 1: Emergencies and 911
- Objective: Call 911 and give name and address
- Vocabulary: emergency, 911, address
- Materials: toy phone, address card
- Assessment: 911 role-play.
