## Day 1 — Monday (school day 151, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *oo two ways* (90 min): Sort moon, food, spoon vs. book, look, foot; read both lists.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Coin features* (60 min): Study real coins with a magnifier; sort and name them.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Pattern chart* (30 min): Make a chart of 6 animals with columns for 'what parents do' and 'what babies do'.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Barter* (30 min): Trade toys or snacks with a family member; talk about problems with trading.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Plan* (30 min): Plan a 4-panel nature story (storm, sun, birds, night).
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Parachute games* (30 min): Play parachute (bedsheet) popcorn.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Poison symbols* (20 min): Learn skull-and-crossbones and Mr. Yuk symbols.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 152, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *au and aw* (90 min): Read saw, paw, draw, haul, launch; illustrate three words.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Values* (60 min): Learn 1¢, 5¢, 10¢, 25¢; make a coin chart.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Find patterns* (30 min): Circle behaviors that appear for many animals (feeding, protecting, calling) and state one pattern.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Money history* (30 min): Learn why money makes trading easier; look at historical coins (Smithsonian).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Illustrate* (30 min): Draw the 4 panels with crayons, using warm or cool colors to match each part's mood.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Capture the flag* (30 min): Play simplified capture the flag with two teams and a sock 'flag' in each yard half.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Safety hunt* (20 min): With an adult, find products under the sink with warning labels.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 153, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Two duck stories* (90 min): Read 'Make Way for Ducklings' and 'The Ugly Duckling'; compare the ducks' journeys on a chart.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Count dimes and pennies* (60 min): Count dimes then pennies (connects to tens and ones).
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Mini-book draft* (30 min): Write a 4-page mini-book about one animal family (e.g., robins) with a fact on each page.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Play store* (30 min): Run a pretend store with price tags under $1 and pay with coins.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Add sound* (30 min): Add instruments and dynamics/tempo for each panel.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Good sport* (30 min): Practice ending every game with a high five and 'good game,' and name one thing the other player did well.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Safe storage* (20 min): Help put dangerous products up high or locked.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 154, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Determiners* (90 min): Choose a/an/the and this/that/these/those to complete 8 sentences about the stories.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Play store* (60 min): Price toys under 50¢ and pay with coins.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Share* (30 min): Read your mini-book aloud and answer two questions from a listener.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Prices* (30 min): Compare prices of 3 snacks at a store or online flyer and decide which you could buy with 50 cents.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Revise* (30 min): Perform for family and revise based on one suggestion.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Feelings* (30 min): Talk about feelings when you win and lose.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Poison Control* (20 min): Learn the Poison Control number (1-800-222-1222).

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Other vowel sounds (oo, au, aw); comparing classic tales
- Objective: Read oo (moon/book), au, aw words
- Objective: Compare characters' adventures in two stories
- Vocabulary: article, this/that, adventure
- Materials: 'Make Way for Ducklings' by Robert McCloskey (library), 'The Ugly Duckling' by Hans Christian Andersen (public-domain picture-book version)
- Assessment: Comparison chart and central-message sentence.

### Math 1: Coins and their values
- Objective: Identify penny, nickel, dime, quarter and their values
- Objective: Count simple coin collections
- Vocabulary: penny, nickel, dime, quarter, cent
- Materials: real coins, play store items
- Assessment: Unit 9 assessment.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Patterns in parent and offspring behavior
- Objective: Find patterns across many kinds of animals
- Vocabulary: pattern, survive, compare
- Materials: chart paper, library animal books, stapled mini-book
- Assessment: Unit 7 assessment and mini-book.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Exchange and money
- Objective: Explain trading and using money to buy goods and services
- Vocabulary: barter, trade, money, price, exchange
- Materials: coins, play store items, price tags
- Assessment: Oral explanation and store role-play.

### Art & Music 1: Nature story in art and music
- Objective: Create a picture-and-sound story
- Objective: Revise using feedback
- Vocabulary: revise, feedback, expressive
- Materials: paper, instruments
- Assessment: Unit 7 sound-story performance.

### PE 1: Group games and sportsmanship
- Objective: Show good sportsmanship
- Vocabulary: sportsmanship, winning, losing
- Materials: parachute or bedsheet, balls
- Assessment: Sportsmanship self-rating.

### Health 1: Harmful household products
- Objective: Identify poison symbols and dangerous products
- Vocabulary: poison, Mr. Yuk, warning label, harmful
- Materials: empty cleaning bottles, poison symbol stickers
- Assessment: Product sort.
