## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *ee and ea* (90 min): Read tree, feet, sleep, eat, sea, leaf; sort and read in phrases.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Count by ones* (60 min): Count aloud from 47 to 120 and point on the 120 chart.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Stargazing count* (30 min): On a clear night, look at the sky for 5 minutes, count visible stars in one window pane's view, and record the time.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Photo detective* (30 min): Study a Library of Congress photo of a one-room schoolhouse: what do you see, think, wonder?
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Form hunt* (30 min): Compare a circle drawing with a ball; find 5 forms at home.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Scarf catch* (30 min): Toss and catch a scarf with two hands.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Screen time count* (20 min): Count screen minutes for a day.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Pronouns* (90 min): Replace nouns with he, she, it, they in 6 sentences; then use his, her, their.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Patterns* (60 min): Color columns and rows on the 120 chart; describe what changes and what stays the same.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Why not in daytime?* (30 min): Shine a flashlight on the wall in a dark room, then in a bright sunny room; explain how sunlight hides the stars.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Slate lesson* (30 min): Do today's spelling on a slate like children long ago; discuss what it would be like with no paper.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Paper sculpture* (30 min): Fold, curl and tape paper strips into a standing sculpture.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Beach ball catch* (30 min): Catch a beach ball tossed by a partner.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Active swaps* (20 min): Make a list of active swaps for screen time.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 88, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Bed in Summer* (90 min): Read Stevenson's 'Bed in Summer'; talk about how the child feels and which words show it.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Missing numbers* (60 min): Fill in missing numbers on chart pieces (puzzle cut-outs).
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Constellation viewer* (30 min): Poke the Big Dipper pattern into the bottom of a paper cup and shine a flashlight through it onto the ceiling.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Compare* (30 min): Make a then-and-now chart (desks, lunch, subjects, getting to school).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Instrument families* (30 min): Watch a PBS or Classics for Kids video on string, brass, woodwind and percussion families.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Bounce catch* (30 min): Catch a ball after one bounce.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Outdoor play* (20 min): Play outside for 30 minutes with a family member.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 89, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Shades of meaning* (90 min): Line up look, peek, glance, stare (and big, huge, giant) from weakest to strongest and act them out.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Count large collections* (60 min): Count 50–120 dry pasta pieces by grouping into tens.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Find the Big Dipper* (30 min): Use your cup map to search for the Big Dipper in the northern sky; draw what you find.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Read* (30 min): Read a picture book about school long ago (e.g., 'Going to School in 1876' or similar from the library).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Sorting* (30 min): Sort instrument pictures into families.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Catch challenge* (30 min): Count how many catches in a row you and a partner can make from 6 feet; try to beat your record 3 times.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Family challenge* (20 min): Do a family walk after dinner.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 90, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): ee/ea dictation; pronoun worksheet; reread 'Bed in Summer' fluently.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Write numbers 1–120 from a starting point; fill-in chart pieces.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Answer: 'Are stars still there during the day? How do you know?' using your flashlight evidence.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Draw yourself at school then and now with 3 labeled differences.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Present paper sculpture and name 4 instrument families.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Catch at least 5 of 10 gentle tosses with hands only (no trapping against the body).
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Compare active and screen minutes.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Vowel teams ee, ea, and pronouns; poems about feelings
- Objective: Read ee and ea words
- Objective: Use personal and possessive pronouns
- Vocabulary: pronoun, feeling word, shades of meaning
- Materials: Stevenson poems, pronoun cards (he, she, it, they, his, her, their)
- Assessment: Pronoun substitution sentences and ee/ea dictation.

### Math 1: Counting to 120
- Objective: Count, read and write numbers to 120
- Objective: Find patterns on the 120 chart
- Vocabulary: 120 chart, row, column, pattern
- Materials: 120 chart, dry-erase marker
- Assessment: 120 chart puzzle and counting check.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Stars at night
- Objective: Observe that many stars are seen at night but not during the day
- Objective: Recognize a constellation
- Vocabulary: star, constellation, night sky, Big Dipper
- Materials: flashlight, black construction paper and a pushpin, paper cup
- Assessment: Star count record and evidence-based answer.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Schools long ago and today
- Objective: Compare schools in the past and present using primary sources
- Vocabulary: past, present, one-room schoolhouse, slate
- Materials: Calisphere/LOC photos of one-room schoolhouses, small chalkboard or slate
- Assessment: Then-and-now school drawing.

### Art & Music 1: Form vs. shape; instrument families
- Objective: Tell the difference between 2-D shape and 3-D form
- Objective: Identify instrument families
- Vocabulary: form, 3-D, sculpture, strings, brass, woodwinds, percussion
- Materials: paper strips, tape, Classics for Kids instrument pages
- Assessment: Instrument family sort.

### PE 1: Catching
- Objective: Catch with hands, watching the ball
- Vocabulary: catch, ready hands, watch
- Materials: scarf, beach ball, playground ball
- Assessment: Catching count.

### Health 1: Screen time and active play
- Objective: Balance screen time and active play
- Vocabulary: screen time, balance, active
- Materials: activity log, timer
- Assessment: Activity vs. screen comparison.
