## Day 1 — Monday (school day 46, ~290 min)

- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Continents song* (30 min): Learn a continents song and point to each continent on the globe.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: R-blends; Aesop's fables
- Objective: Read words with r-blends
- Objective: Identify the moral of a fable
- Vocabulary: fable, moral, past tense
- Materials: Aesop's Fables (Gutenberg), r-blend picture cards
- Assessment: Fable–moral matching and r-blend passage accuracy.

### Math 1: Unknowns in all positions
- Objective: Solve add-to and take-from problems with unknown change or start
- Objective: Use a symbol for the unknown
- Vocabulary: unknown, start, change, result
- Materials: counters, story mats
- Assessment: Unknown-position story problems.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Transparent, translucent, opaque
- Objective: Test what happens when materials are placed in a light beam
- Vocabulary: transparent, translucent, opaque
- Materials: flashlight, plastic wrap, wax paper, cardboard, fabric, foil
- Assessment: Material test chart.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Continents and oceans
- Objective: Locate the seven continents and four major oceans
- Vocabulary: continent, ocean, globe, equator
- Materials: globe, world map, continent puzzle (paper cut-outs)
- Assessment: Continents and oceans labeling quiz.

### Art & Music 1: Texture rubbings; high and low sounds
- Objective: Make texture rubbings
- Objective: Identify high and low pitches
- Vocabulary: texture, rubbing, high, low, pitch
- Materials: crayons with paper removed, leaves, coins, sandpaper, xylophone or piano app
- Assessment: Texture rubbings collection.

### PE 1: Beam walking
- Objective: Walk forward and sideways on a narrow elevated surface
- Vocabulary: beam, arms out, focus
- Materials: 2x4 board on the ground or a curb, beanbag
- Assessment: Beam walk check.

### Health 1: Healthy habits champion
- Objective: Encourage others to practice healthy habits
- Objective: Care for the environment
- Vocabulary: habit, encourage, recycle
- Materials: poster board, recycling bin
- Assessment: Unit 2 assessment.
