## Day 1 — Monday (school day 161, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Choose a question* (90 min): Pick a topic (e.g., sea turtles) and write 2 questions you want to answer.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *3-D hunt* (60 min): Find cubes, cylinders, cones, spheres and rectangular prisms at home.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Seedling vs. adult* (30 min): Compare bean seedlings with an adult bean plant photo; list matching leaf shapes and differences in size.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Farm work* (30 min): Learn how wheat grows on a California farm and is harvested.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Mat art* (30 min): Mount art on colored paper mats.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Strength check* (30 min): Record push-ups, curl-ups, hang time.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Who helps?* (20 min): Learn about doctors, nurses, dentists and pharmacists.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 162, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Read sources* (90 min): Read two library books on the topic; jot answers on sticky notes.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Faces* (60 min): Trace faces of 3-D objects to find their 2-D shapes.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Measure leaves* (30 min): Measure the longest leaf on 5 seedlings in centimeters and record the results.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Make bread* (30 min): Bake simple bread or muffins with family; list jobs it takes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Labels* (30 min): Write labels with title, medium and artist name.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Endurance check* (30 min): Walk-jog for 10 minutes; record laps.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Checkup* (20 min): Role-play a doctor checkup with a toy kit.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 163, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Interview* (90 min): Interview a family member or librarian with 3 questions; listen and ask a follow-up question.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Compose 2-D* (60 min): Make a hexagon from triangles and trapezoids with pattern blocks.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Same kind of flower* (30 min): Compare 3 flowers from the same plant type (e.g., roses or dandelions); note color and petal differences.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Factory* (30 min): Watch a PBS clip on how crayons or apples are processed.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Rehearse* (30 min): Rehearse songs and fix tricky parts with feedback.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Flexibility check* (30 min): Measure sit-and-reach with a ruler after a warm-up and record it.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Interview* (20 min): Interview a health helper (family doctor, nurse) about their job.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 164, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Organize* (90 min): Sort notes by question; cross out notes that don't answer the question.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Compose 3-D* (60 min): Build a castle from boxes and cans; name each shape.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Plant journal* (30 min): Draw and label two seedlings side by side showing how they are alike and different.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Specialized jobs* (30 min): Make a flow chart of workers from farm to store.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Add instruments* (30 min): Add a shaker on the beat and a drum on a ta/ti-ti rhythm to one concert song.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Compare* (30 min): Compare this week's push-ups, hang, sit-and-reach and jog laps with your week 14 records.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Map* (20 min): Find clinics, pharmacies and hospitals on a community map.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 165, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Tell answers to your questions using sources; name where each fact came from.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Fill a hexagon outline three different ways with pattern blocks and record each way; name 5 solid shapes.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Write an evidence statement about plants being like, but not exactly like, their parents.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain why people specialize in one job, using the bread workers as an example.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Practice explaining one artwork to a visitor.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Set a summer fitness goal (e.g., hang 20 seconds) and write 2 ways to practice.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Match 6 health helpers to their jobs and tell which one you would visit for a toothache.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Shared research: asking and answering questions
- Objective: Generate a research question
- Objective: Gather information from books and an expert
- Vocabulary: research, question, source, expert
- Materials: library books on a chosen topic (e.g., ocean animals), interview question sheet
- Assessment: Note sheet answering 2 questions with sources named.

### Math 1: 3-D shapes and composing shapes
- Objective: Name 3-D shapes
- Objective: Compose 2-D and 3-D shapes
- Vocabulary: cube, cone, cylinder, sphere, compose
- Materials: household boxes and cans, pattern blocks (or app)
- Assessment: Shape composition task.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Young plants are like their parents
- Objective: Compare young plants with parent plants
- Objective: Measure variation
- Vocabulary: seedling, variation, measure
- Materials: bean or sunflower seedlings, adult plant of the same kind (or photo), ruler
- Assessment: Plant variation evidence statement.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: From farm to table: producing goods
- Objective: Describe specialized work to make goods
- Vocabulary: producer, factory, farm, manufacture
- Materials: 'From Wheat to Bread' (library) or similar, bread ingredients
- Assessment: Farm-to-table flow chart.

### Art & Music 1: Preparing art for display; concert rehearsal
- Objective: Prepare work for exhibition
- Objective: Rehearse music
- Vocabulary: mat, label, rehearse
- Materials: construction paper for mats, labels, instruments
- Assessment: Labels and rehearsal progress.

### PE 1: Fitness check-up
- Objective: Compare fitness to start of year
- Vocabulary: progress, goal
- Materials: fitness journal, stopwatch
- Assessment: Fitness comparison.

### Health 1: Health helpers
- Objective: Identify community health helpers
- Vocabulary: doctor, nurse, dentist, pharmacist
- Materials: toy doctor kit, community map
- Assessment: Helper matching.
