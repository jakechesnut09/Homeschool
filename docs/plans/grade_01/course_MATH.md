# Math 1 (Grade 1)

First-grade mathematics develops fluency with addition and subtraction within 10 and strategies within 20, solving story problems of every type, and understanding the equal sign. Students learn place value (tens and ones) to 120, add and subtract within 100, measure length with units, tell time to the half hour, identify coins, organize data, and reason about shapes and equal shares.

*Framework:* California Common Core State Standards for Mathematics; 2023 CA Mathematics Framework  
*Time:* 60 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Numbers to 20 and Meanings of Addition & Subtraction (weeks 1–4)
Counting tells how many; adding joins and subtracting separates.

- Week 1: Counting and writing numbers to 20
- Week 2: Adding as putting together and adding to
- Week 3: Subtracting as taking from and taking apart
- Week 4: Comparing numbers and 'how many more'

### Unit 2: Addition and Subtraction Facts within 10 (weeks 5–8)
Facts within 10 can be found with patterns and related to one another.

- Week 5: Make 10 and partners of numbers
- Week 6: Doubles and doubles plus one
- Week 7: Relating addition and subtraction; fact families
- Week 8: Fluency within 10

### Unit 3: Story Problems and the Equal Sign (weeks 9–11)
The equal sign means 'the same as'; story problems can have an unknown in any spot.

- Week 9: The equal sign and true/false equations
- Week 10: Unknowns in all positions
- Week 11: Comparing and three-addend problems

### Unit 4: Adding and Subtracting within 20 (weeks 12–15)
Making ten and doubles make bigger facts easy.

- Week 12: Making ten to add within 20
- Week 13: Doubles to 20 and three-addend sums
- Week 14: Subtraction within 20
- Week 15: Problem solving within 20

### Unit 5: Data: Sorting, Graphing and Asking Questions (weeks 16–17)
Data organized into categories helps us answer questions.

- Week 16: Sorting and organizing data
- Week 17: Survey project and bar graphs

### Unit 6: Place Value: Tens and Ones to 120 (weeks 18–22)
Ten ones make one ten; a digit's place shows its value.

- Week 18: Counting to 120
- Week 19: Tens and ones
- Week 20: Teen numbers and two-digit numbers in expanded form
- Week 21: Comparing two-digit numbers
- Week 22: 10 more and 10 less

### Unit 7: Adding and Subtracting within 100 (weeks 23–26)
We add tens to tens and ones to ones, and sometimes make a new ten.

- Week 23: Adding tens and adding a one-digit number
- Week 24: Strategies for adding within 100
- Week 25: Subtracting multiples of 10
- Week 26: Mixed practice within 100

### Unit 8: Measuring Length (weeks 27–29)
Length is measured by repeating equal units end to end with no gaps or overlaps.

- Week 27: Comparing and ordering lengths
- Week 28: Measuring with nonstandard units
- Week 29: Measurement and data project

### Unit 9: Time and Money (weeks 30–31)
Clocks show the hour and half hour; coins have different values.

- Week 30: Telling time to the hour and half hour
- Week 31: Coins and their values

### Unit 10: Geometry: Shapes and Equal Shares (weeks 32–34)
Shapes are defined by attributes and can be combined or split into equal parts.

- Week 32: Defining attributes of 2-D shapes
- Week 33: 3-D shapes and composing shapes
- Week 34: Equal shares: halves and fourths

### Unit 11: Year Review and Fluency Celebration (weeks 35–36)
Everything we learned connects: number sense, operations, measurement, and shapes.

- Week 35: Review: operations and place value
- Week 36: Year-end celebration and assessment

## Standards

- **1.G.A.1** Distinguish between defining attributes and non-defining attributes of shapes; build and draw shapes with defining attributes.
- **1.G.A.2** Compose two-dimensional or three-dimensional shapes to create a composite shape, and compose new shapes from the composite shape.
- **1.G.A.3** Partition circles and rectangles into two and four equal shares; describe shares using halves, fourths, quarters.
- **1.MD.A.1** Order three objects by length; compare the lengths of two objects indirectly by using a third object.
- **1.MD.A.2** Express the length of an object as a whole number of length units by laying multiple copies of a shorter object end to end.
- **1.MD.B.3** Tell and write time in hours and half-hours using analog and digital clocks.
- **1.MD.B.3a** Recognize and identify coins, their names, and their value (CA addition).
- **1.MD.C.4** Organize, represent, and interpret data with up to three categories; ask and answer questions about the data.
- **1.NBT.A.1** Count to 120, starting at any number less than 120; read and write numerals and represent a number of objects with a written numeral.
- **1.NBT.B.2** Understand that the two digits of a two-digit number represent amounts of tens and ones (10 as a bundle of ten ones; 11–19; decade numbers).
- **1.NBT.B.3** Compare two two-digit numbers based on meanings of the tens and ones digits, recording with >, =, <.
- **1.NBT.C.4** Add within 100, including a two-digit and a one-digit number and a two-digit number and a multiple of 10, using models and strategies.
- **1.NBT.C.5** Given a two-digit number, mentally find 10 more or 10 less without counting.
- **1.NBT.C.6** Subtract multiples of 10 in the range 10–90 from multiples of 10, using models and strategies.
- **1.OA.A.1** Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems (adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, comparing, unknowns in all positions).
- **1.OA.A.2** Solve word problems that call for addition of three whole numbers whose sum is 20 or less.
- **1.OA.B.3** Apply properties of operations as strategies to add and subtract (commutative, associative).
- **1.OA.B.4** Understand subtraction as an unknown-addend problem.
- **1.OA.C.5** Relate counting to addition and subtraction (counting on 2 to add 2).
- **1.OA.C.6** Add and subtract within 20, demonstrating fluency within 10, using strategies such as making ten, decomposing, and doubles.
- **1.OA.D.7** Understand the meaning of the equal sign and determine whether equations are true or false.
- **1.OA.D.8** Determine the unknown whole number in an addition or subtraction equation relating three whole numbers.
