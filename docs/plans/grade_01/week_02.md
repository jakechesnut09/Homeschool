## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Sound boxes* (90 min): Push a counter into an Elkonin box for each sound in pig, sit, lip, win; then write the letters in the boxes.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Put together* (60 min): Act out 'I have 4 red apples and 3 green apples' with counters and write 4 + 3 = 7.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Problems around us* (30 min): Read about engineers (e.g., 'Rosie Revere, Engineer' by Andrea Beaty, library) and list 3 problems at home that a tool could fix.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Golden Rule* (30 min): Read 'The Golden Rule' by Ilene Cooper and say the rule in your own words.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Kandinsky look* (30 min): Look at Kandinsky's 'Squares with Concentric Circles' and count the circles and squares; describe colors.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Chalk paths* (30 min): Draw straight, curved and zigzag chalk paths and walk, skip and jog along each.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Belly breathing* (20 min): Practice 'smell the flower, blow out the candle' breathing with a pinwheel.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Sight word cards* (90 min): Make flash cards for the, a, is, I, see, my; play 'Go Fish' with them and read sentences like 'I see a pig.'
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Add to* (60 min): Solve 'add to' stories (5 birds, 2 more come) with drawings; write the equation.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Define the problem* (30 min): Problem: a toy needs to cross a 20 cm 'river' between two books; draw the problem.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Role-play* (30 min): Act out 4 situations (someone cuts in line, a friend is left out) and show a Golden Rule choice.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Kandinsky circles* (30 min): Paint or crayon your own concentric circle squares on a 4-square grid.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Follow the leader* (30 min): Follow a family member's pathway around the yard, then lead.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Calm-down jar* (20 min): Make a glitter calm-down jar and watch the glitter settle while breathing.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Read-aloud questions* (90 min): Read 'The Snowy Day' and ask/answer who, what, where questions about Peter's day; draw a favorite part.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Count on to add* (60 min): Add by counting on from the bigger number (6 + 2: 'six... seven, eight').
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Sketch* (30 min): Sketch two bridge designs and label the materials.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Different opinions* (30 min): Survey family on a favorite food; practice saying 'I see it differently because...' politely.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Rhyme beat* (30 min): Chant 'Hickory Dickory Dock' while tapping the beat on a drum or pot.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Letter paths* (30 min): Walk the pathway of your name's letters.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Mindful moment* (20 min): Do a GoNoodle Flow mindfulness video and tell how your body feels after.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 9, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Handwriting i, t, l* (90 min): Practice lowercase straight-line letters i, t, l and write tip, lit, it, sit.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Shake and spill* (60 min): Shake 6 two-color counters, spill, and record all the addition combinations for 6.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Build* (30 min): Build your favorite design from paper and craft sticks.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Kindness log* (30 min): Do and record 3 kind acts this week, drawing each in a kindness log.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Beat in songs* (30 min): Play a beat-passing game with a ball to 'Bingo'.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Obstacle path* (30 min): Run an obstacle course with all three pathways.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Calm corner* (20 min): Set up a calm corner at home with 3 tools (jar, pillow, feelings chart).

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 10, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Read a short decodable text from Starfall (short a and i), then sort 12 picture cards by middle sound a or i.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Solve 6 picture story problems and write an equation for each.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Test whether the toy can cross; tell what worked and what you would change.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Draw a comic strip showing someone following the Golden Rule and explain it.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Compare your circles art with Kandinsky's and tell one way they are alike.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Name and demonstrate each pathway on command.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Role-play a frustrating moment and use a calm-down strategy.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Short i and high-frequency words
- Objective: Read short-i CVC words
- Objective: Recognize first sight words (the, a, is, I, see, my)
- Vocabulary: sight word, sound, blend
- Materials: index cards for sight words, Elkonin (sound) boxes, 'The Snowy Day' by Ezra Jack Keats
- Assessment: Picture sort accuracy and fluent reading of 6 sight words.

### Math 1: Adding as putting together and adding to
- Objective: Represent addition with objects, drawings and equations
- Objective: Use + and =
- Vocabulary: add, plus, sum, equals
- Materials: counters, two-color counters, drawing paper
- Assessment: Story-problem quiz (6 problems) with equations.

### Science 1 (NGSS): What engineers do: defining problems and designing
- Objective: Define a simple problem
- Objective: Sketch and build a solution
- Vocabulary: engineer, problem, design, sketch
- Materials: paper, tape, craft sticks, toy figurine
- Assessment: Design sketch and oral explanation of the problem and solution.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: The Golden Rule and respecting others
- Objective: State the Golden Rule in your own words
- Objective: Show respect for others' rights and opinions
- Vocabulary: Golden Rule, respect, opinion, kindness
- Materials: 'The Golden Rule' by Ilene Cooper (library), role-play cards
- Assessment: Golden Rule comic strip with explanation.

### Art & Music 1: Shapes in art; beat with rhymes
- Objective: Identify geometric and organic shapes
- Objective: Keep the beat while chanting
- Vocabulary: geometric, organic, shape, chant
- Materials: construction paper, scissors, glue, image of Kandinsky's 'Squares with Concentric Circles'
- Assessment: Circle-square artwork and chant with steady beat.

### PE 1: Pathways: straight, curved, zigzag
- Objective: Travel in different pathways
- Vocabulary: pathway, straight, curved, zigzag
- Materials: sidewalk chalk, rope
- Assessment: Pathway demonstration.

### Health 1: Calm-down strategies
- Objective: Practice 3 healthy ways to calm down
- Vocabulary: calm, breathe, strategy
- Materials: pinwheel, glitter jar materials, GoNoodle Flow videos
- Assessment: Calm-down role-play.
