## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *sh words* (90 min): Learn that s+h says /sh/; read ship, shop, fish, dish, shut and underline the digraph.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Ten-frame partners* (60 min): Fill a ten-frame with two colors in every way and record 1+9, 2+8 ... 9+1.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Dancing rice* (30 min): Stretch plastic wrap over a bowl, sprinkle rice, bang a pan nearby and watch the rice jump.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Representatives* (30 min): Learn that people elect a small group (city council, mayor) to make rules for many people.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Mouse Paint* (30 min): Read 'Mouse Paint' and name the primary colors.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Jog form* (30 min): Jog with bent arms swinging front to back; jog 3 laps around the yard.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Conflict steps* (20 min): Learn stop, talk, listen, solve; act it out with stuffed animals.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *ch words* (90 min): Read chip, chat, much, chop, lunch; sort picture cards into sh and ch piles.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Go Fish for 10* (60 min): Play Go Fish asking for the card that makes 10 with yours.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Spoon and water* (30 min): Strike a metal spoon and touch it to water; observe ripples.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Our leaders* (30 min): Look up your city's mayor and city council members and find one rule (law) they made.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Mix it* (30 min): Mix red+yellow, yellow+blue and blue+red on a plate and paint the new colors.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Gallop* (30 min): Gallop like a horse with one foot leading, then switch leads.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Compromise* (20 min): Practice taking turns or sharing to solve a toy conflict.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Spring* (90 min): Read 'Spring' from Frog and Toad Are Friends; list two words that describe Frog and two that describe Toad.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Partners of 7 and 8* (60 min): Break 7 and 8 into parts with counters; record number bonds.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Your ears* (30 min): Watch a short PBS clip about how ears hear; model an eardrum with plastic wrap.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Mock election* (30 min): Make campaign posters for two stuffed-animal candidates and have family elect a 'family representative'.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Long or short* (30 min): Sing a song and decide if sounds are long (ta-a) or short (ti-ti); use arm movements.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Gallop relay* (30 min): Gallop to a cone and back; switch leads at the cone.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Teasing vs. bullying* (20 min): Learn that bullying is mean on purpose and repeated; sort scenario cards.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 24, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Picture clues* (90 min): Reread 'Spring' and use the illustrations to tell how Toad feels when he wakes up; write one sentence with a period.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Missing part* (60 min): Solve 6 + ? = 10 and ? + 3 = 8 using a ten-frame.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Plan an investigation* (30 min): Plan how to test whether louder sounds make rice jump higher; carry it out.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Compare* (30 min): Make a T-chart comparing direct democracy (everyone votes on rules) and representative democracy (leaders make rules).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Sound pictures* (30 min): Draw long lines for long sounds and dots for short sounds while listening to a song.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Music moves* (30 min): Jog and gallop to music, changing on the beat.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Be an upstander* (20 min): Role-play telling a bully 'Stop!' and getting a trusted adult.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 25, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Dictation of 6 sh/ch words and one sentence; oral description of Frog vs. Toad.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Make-10 fluency check (1 minute) and number bond worksheet.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain your evidence that sound makes materials vibrate.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Unit 1 check* (30 min): Assessment: sort examples into direct or representative democracy and explain the Golden Rule.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Explain your color mixing chart and perform a long-short sound pattern.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Show a gallop with both lead feet and say the cues.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Unit 1 check* (20 min): Unit assessment: name feelings, show a calm-down tool, solve a conflict scenario.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Digraphs sh and ch; Frog and Toad
- Objective: Read words with sh and ch
- Objective: Describe characters using details
- Vocabulary: digraph, character trait, illustration
- Materials: 'Frog and Toad Are Friends' by Arnold Lobel (library), digraph cards
- Assessment: Dictation accuracy for sh/ch and an oral character description using two details.

### Math 1: Make 10 and partners of numbers
- Objective: Find all pairs that make 10
- Objective: Decompose numbers within 10
- Vocabulary: make ten, partners, decompose
- Materials: ten-frame, two-color counters, playing cards
- Assessment: Make-10 fluency check and number bonds.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Sound makes things vibrate
- Objective: Show that sound can make materials vibrate
- Vocabulary: energy, travel, eardrum
- Materials: plastic wrap, bowl, rice grains, tuning fork or spoon, pan
- Assessment: Investigation plan and evidence statement.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Representative democracy: choosing leaders
- Objective: Explain representative democracy with community examples
- Vocabulary: representative, elect, mayor, city council, citizen
- Materials: local city council website or newspaper, paper for a campaign poster
- Assessment: Unit 1 assessment.

### Art & Music 1: Primary colors; long and short sounds
- Objective: Name and mix primary colors
- Objective: Hear long and short sounds
- Vocabulary: primary color, mix, long, short
- Materials: red, yellow, blue tempera paint, paper plates, 'Mouse Paint' by Ellen Stoll Walsh (library)
- Assessment: Color mixing chart.

### PE 1: Jogging and the gallop
- Objective: Jog with arms bent
- Objective: Gallop with the same foot leading
- Vocabulary: jog, gallop, lead foot
- Materials: cones, music
- Assessment: Gallop and jog cue check.

### Health 1: Solving conflicts and stopping bullying
- Objective: Use steps to solve a conflict
- Objective: Recognize bullying and respond
- Vocabulary: conflict, compromise, bullying, bystander
- Materials: conflict-resolution steps poster (stop, talk, listen, solve)
- Assessment: Unit 1 assessment.
