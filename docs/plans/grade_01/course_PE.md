# PE 1 (Grade 1)

PE 1 builds fundamental movement skills through play: moving safely in space, locomotor skills (hop, gallop, jog, slide, skip), balancing and rolling, throwing, catching, kicking, striking, dribbling, jumping rope and dancing. Children learn how exercise affects the heart, muscles and bones, track simple fitness measures, and practice cooperation and sportsmanship, finishing with a family field day.

*Framework:* California Physical Education Model Content Standards; 2009 CA Physical Education Framework  
*Time:* 30 min/day

## Units

### Unit 1: Safe Movement: Space, Pathways and Levels (weeks 1–4)
Moving safely means knowing your space and your body.

- Week 1: Personal space and general space
- Week 2: Pathways: straight, curved, zigzag
- Week 3: Levels and directions
- Week 4: Spatial words with objects

### Unit 2: Locomotor Skills (weeks 5–8)
Hopping, galloping, jogging and sliding each have a special pattern.

- Week 5: Jogging and the gallop
- Week 6: Hopping
- Week 7: Sliding and skipping preview
- Week 8: Locomotor skills circuit

### Unit 3: Balance, Rolling and Tumbling (weeks 9–12)
A strong center and wide base help us balance and roll safely.

- Week 9: Balancing on body parts
- Week 10: Beam walking
- Week 11: Rolling safely
- Week 12: Balance and roll routine

### Unit 4: Fitness and My Body (weeks 13–16)
Exercise makes my heart, muscles and bones strong.

- Week 13: My heart
- Week 14: Strong muscles and bones
- Week 15: Stretching and flexibility
- Week 16: Fuel, water and endurance

### Unit 5: Throwing and Catching (weeks 17–20)
Step with the opposite foot to throw; watch the ball into your hands to catch.

- Week 17: Underhand toss
- Week 18: Catching
- Week 19: Overhand throw
- Week 20: Throw and catch games

### Unit 6: Kicking, Striking and Dribbling (weeks 21–24)
Controlled force and focus help us send a ball where we want.

- Week 21: Kicking a stationary ball
- Week 22: Kicking a rolling ball and dribbling with feet
- Week 23: Striking with hands and paddles
- Week 24: Hand dribbling

### Unit 7: Jump Rope, Rhythm and Dance (weeks 25–28)
Moving to a beat builds coordination and joy.

- Week 25: Jumping and landing; long rope
- Week 26: Self-turned jump rope
- Week 27: Rhythm and dance
- Week 28: Dance creation and jump rope show

### Unit 8: Cooperative Games and Teamwork (weeks 29–32)
Games are more fun when we follow rules, take turns and encourage others.

- Week 29: Taking turns and following rules
- Week 30: Partner cooperation
- Week 31: Group games and sportsmanship
- Week 32: Playground and traditional games

### Unit 9: Fitness Check and Field Day (weeks 33–36)
We celebrate how much stronger and more skillful we have become.

- Week 33: Fitness check-up
- Week 34: Skill review: locomotor and manipulative
- Week 35: Field day preparation
- Week 36: Field day celebration

## Standards

- **PE-1.1.1** Balance on one, two, three, four, and five body parts.
- **PE-1.1.10** Strike a balloon or ball upward and forward with the hand or a short-handled paddle.
- **PE-1.1.11** Dribble a ball continuously with one hand while stationary and moving.
- **PE-1.1.12** Perform simple rhythmic movement and dance sequences to music.
- **PE-1.1.2** Balance while walking forward and sideways on a narrow, elevated surface.
- **PE-1.1.3** Demonstrate the relationship of under, over, behind, next to, through, right, left, up, down, forward, backward, and in front of using the body and an object.
- **PE-1.1.4** Create a routine that includes two types of body rolls (log roll, egg roll, rocking-chair roll) and a stationary balance position.
- **PE-1.1.5** Hop, gallop, jog, and slide using a mature motor pattern.
- **PE-1.1.6** Jump continuously with a self-turned rope and jump a long rope turned by others.
- **PE-1.1.7** Toss and throw a ball underhand and overhand toward a target, stepping with the opposite foot.
- **PE-1.1.8** Catch a gently tossed or bounced ball using the hands.
- **PE-1.1.9** Kick a stationary and a slowly rolling ball using the inside of the foot.
- **PE-1.2.1** Explain the difference between pathways (straight, curved, zigzag), levels (high, medium, low), and personal vs. general space.
- **PE-1.2.2** Describe the critical elements (cues) of the hop, gallop, jog, and slide.
- **PE-1.2.3** Explain the critical elements of throwing, catching, and kicking (e.g., step with the opposite foot, watch the ball).
- **PE-1.3.1** Participate in physical activities that are enjoyable and challenging.
- **PE-1.3.2** Participate in moderate to vigorous physical activity for increasing periods of time.
- **PE-1.3.3** Demonstrate muscular strength and endurance activities (e.g., modified push-ups, curl-ups, hanging).
- **PE-1.3.4** Demonstrate flexibility exercises for major muscle groups.
- **PE-1.3.5** Walk and jog for increasing distances to build aerobic capacity.
- **PE-1.4.1** Recognize that physical activity makes the heart beat faster and identify the location of the heart.
- **PE-1.4.2** Explain that the body needs water, healthy food, and rest to be active.
- **PE-1.4.3** Identify that muscles move bones and that physical activity makes muscles and bones stronger.
- **PE-1.4.4** Explain why warming up and stretching prepare the body for activity.
- **PE-1.5.1** Work cooperatively with a partner or small group and share equipment.
- **PE-1.5.2** Follow rules and safety procedures and take turns during physical activity.
- **PE-1.5.3** Give positive encouragement to others and accept feedback.
- **PE-1.5.4** Identify feelings that result from participation in physical activity.
