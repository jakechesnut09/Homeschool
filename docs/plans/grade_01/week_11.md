## Day 1 — Monday (school day 51, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *s-blends* (90 min): Read stop, snap, spin, skip, swim, smell; tap sounds and spell 5 words.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Compare bigger unknown* (60 min): Solve 'Leo has 4 shells. Mia has 3 more than Leo.' using cube trains.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Shadow puppets* (30 min): Make hand-shadow animals on a wall with a flashlight and explain that your hand blocks the light.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Homes around the world* (30 min): Compare photos of an igloo, stilt house, adobe and apartment; tell how each fits its environment.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Eric Carle* (30 min): Read 'The Very Hungry Caterpillar' and look at the textured paper in the illustrations.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Log roll* (30 min): Roll sideways with arms overhead like a log.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *MyPlate* (20 min): Look at MyPlate and name the 5 food groups and their colors.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 52, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *End blends* (90 min): Read nest, hand, jump, milk, tent; underline the final blend in each.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Compare smaller unknown* (60 min): Solve 'Mia has 9 rocks; Leo has 2 fewer.'
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Size change* (30 min): Move a toy closer and farther from the light; measure shadow size.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Clothing and weather* (30 min): Sort clothing cards for snowy, rainy, hot and desert places.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Textured paper* (30 min): Paint papers and add texture with forks, sponges and combs.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Egg roll* (30 min): Tuck knees and roll sideways like an egg.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Sort* (20 min): Cut food pictures from grocery ads and sort them onto a paper-plate MyPlate.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 53, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Who is talking?* (90 min): Read Three Billy Goats Gruff; use quotation marks as a clue to who speaks and color each speaker's words a different color.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Three addends warm-up* (60 min): Roll 3 dice and add, looking for a make-10 or double first.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Outdoor shadows* (30 min): Trace your shadow with chalk at 9 a.m. and again at noon.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *California places* (30 min): Compare life at the beach, in the Sierra Nevada mountains and in the desert (food, clothing, fun).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Melody direction* (30 min): Move hands up, down or same while singing 'Jack and Jill'.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Rocking chair* (30 min): Rock on a rounded back with chin tucked.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Kitchen hunt* (20 min): Find one food from each group in the kitchen.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 54, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Noun-verb match* (90 min): Practice 'The goat trips' vs. 'The goats trip'; complete 6 sentences choosing the right verb form.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Problem solving* (60 min): Mixed story problems; draw a model for each before writing the equation.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Shadow puppet theater* (30 min): Make cardboard shadow puppets on sticks and perform a short shadow show behind a bedsheet lit by a lamp.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Getting around* (30 min): Compare transportation in snowy places (snowmobile), islands (boats) and cities (trains).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Draw a melody* (30 min): Draw lines showing melody direction for 'Twinkle, Twinkle'.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Practice* (30 min): Practice each roll 5 times safely.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Lunch check* (20 min): Check today's lunch for food groups.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 55, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Perform the story with stick puppets, changing voices for narrator and each character; blend word check.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Unit 3 check* (60 min): Unit test on story problems and equal sign.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain why a shadow forms when an opaque object blocks light.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Unit 2 check* (30 min): Assessment: locate places on maps and explain how one environment affects people's lives.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Share your textured papers and draw-a-melody chart.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Demonstrate two rolls with control.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Sort 15 food cards into groups.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: S-blends and end blends; Three Billy Goats Gruff
- Objective: Read s-blends and final blends (st, nd, mp)
- Objective: Identify who is telling the story
- Vocabulary: narrator, dialogue, verb
- Materials: 'The Three Billy Goats Gruff' (public-domain version), craft sticks for puppets
- Assessment: Puppet show voices for narrator/characters and singular/plural verb sentences.

### Math 1: Comparing and three-addend problems
- Objective: Solve compare problems with bigger/smaller unknown
- Objective: Add three numbers
- Vocabulary: compare, fewer, three addends
- Materials: cubes, dice
- Assessment: Unit 3 assessment.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Shadows
- Objective: Explain how shadows form
- Objective: Change shadow size and shape
- Vocabulary: shadow, block, beam
- Materials: flashlight, toys, chalk
- Assessment: Shadow investigation record.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: How place shapes the way people live
- Objective: Describe how weather and environment affect food, clothing, shelter, transportation, recreation
- Vocabulary: climate, environment, shelter, transportation, recreation
- Materials: photos of homes around the world (igloo, stilt house, adobe), Library of Congress or Smithsonian images
- Assessment: Unit 2 assessment.

### Art & Music 1: Eric Carle collage; melodies go up and down
- Objective: Create a textured paper collage
- Objective: Show melodic direction
- Vocabulary: collage, texture, melody, up, down
- Materials: tissue paper, paint, forks and sponges for texture, 'The Very Hungry Caterpillar' by Eric Carle
- Assessment: Melody direction chart.

### PE 1: Rolling safely
- Objective: Perform log roll, egg roll and rocking chair roll
- Vocabulary: log roll, egg roll, rocking chair
- Materials: mat or soft grass
- Assessment: Roll demonstration.

### Health 1: MyPlate and food groups
- Objective: Name the five food groups
- Vocabulary: MyPlate, fruits, vegetables, grains, protein, dairy
- Materials: MyPlate printout, grocery ads
- Assessment: Food group sort.
