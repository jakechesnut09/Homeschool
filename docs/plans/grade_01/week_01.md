## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Sentence detectives* (90 min): Read aloud 'Hey Diddle Diddle' and point to each word; circle the capital letter and period in three printed sentences.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Count collections* (60 min): Count bags of 8–20 beans, organizing them on ten-frames, and write the numeral on a sticky note.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Five senses walk* (30 min): Take a backyard or park walk and record one thing you see, hear, smell and touch in a science notebook.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *No-rules game* (30 min): Play a board game with no rules for two minutes, then talk about what went wrong and why rules matter.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Line walk* (30 min): Walk and trace straight, curvy, zigzag, wavy, dotted and spiral lines in the air and on the floor with tape; draw each on paper.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Space bubble* (30 min): Warm up with arm circles, then stand in a hula hoop 'bubble' and explore reaching high, low and wide without leaving it.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Feelings book* (20 min): Read 'The Way I Feel' by Janan Cain and name the feeling on each page.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Short a sound* (90 min): Say /a/ words (cat, map, bag), isolate the middle sound, then build cat, mat, sat, hat with letter tiles and read each one.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Write numerals* (60 min): Practice writing 0–20 correctly; fix reversals of 2, 3, 5, 7, 9 with tracing and a 'start at the top' rule.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Magnifier lab* (30 min): Observe a leaf, rock and shell with a magnifying glass; draw and label details you couldn't see before.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Rules around us* (30 min): Walk the neighborhood and photograph or draw 5 signs that show rules (stop sign, crosswalk, park rules); tell what each keeps safe.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Line picture* (30 min): Read 'Harold and the Purple Crayon' (Crockett Johnson, library) and draw a one-line adventure using 4 kinds of lines.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *General space* (30 min): Walk, then jog, around cones in the yard without touching cones or people; freeze on 'Stop'.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Mirror faces* (20 min): Make happy, sad, angry, scared, surprised and proud faces in a mirror; draw two of them.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Handwriting a, c, d* (90 min): Practice lowercase a, c, d (circle letters) on lined paper; write the CVC words bad, cad, dad.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Count on* (60 min): Start at a number on the 0–20 number line (e.g., 7) and count on 3; play 'Start at ___, count on ___'.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Question jar* (30 min): Write or dictate 5 'I wonder' questions about nature and sort them into 'can test' and 'look up'.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Home rules chart* (30 min): Work with family to write 5 homeschool rules on chart paper; draw a picture for each.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Find the beat* (30 min): Pat a steady beat on knees to 'Stars and Stripes Forever'; march around the room on the beat.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Stop and go* (30 min): Play red light/green light focusing on safe stops.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Body clues* (20 min): Talk about where you feel feelings in your body (tight fists, butterflies in tummy).

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Talking rules* (90 min): Read 'Chrysanthemum' (Kevin Henkes, library) and practice listening rules: take turns, look at the speaker, ask one question about Chrysanthemum's name.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Quick images* (60 min): Look at a dot or ten-frame card for 3 seconds and tell how many; explain how you saw it.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Mystery bag* (30 min): Identify 4 hidden objects by touch only and describe the clues you used.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Rights and responsibilities* (30 min): Read 'What If Everybody Did That?' (Ellen Javernick, library) and match 3 rights with 3 responsibilities.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Beat vs. no beat* (30 min): Listen to a ticking clock, a march and rain sounds; decide which have a steady beat.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Safety rules* (30 min): Make 3 PE safety rules (stay in your space, listen for stop, take care of equipment) and practice them in a tag game.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Feelings check-in* (20 min): Start a daily feelings chart: point to how you feel each morning and tell why.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 5, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Read 10 short-a CVC words aloud and write a dictated sentence 'The cat sat.' with capital and period; tally correct sounds.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Count 3 collections, write numerals 0–20 from dictation, and count on from 5 numbers.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain to a family member how scientists observe and ask questions, using your notebook drawings.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain one home rule and one community rule and what could happen without each.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Show your line picture and name its lines; lead family in a beat-keeping game.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Show personal vs. general space on command and explain why a warm-up comes first.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Match 6 feeling words to face pictures and tell about a time you felt one.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Print concepts, sentences, and short a
- Objective: Identify the parts of a sentence (capital, spaces, end mark)
- Objective: Blend and read CVC words with short a
- Vocabulary: sentence, capital letter, period, vowel
- Materials: letter tiles or magnetic letters, primary-lined paper, Mother Goose rhymes (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Oral CVC reading check (8/10 correct) and dictated sentence with correct capital and period.

### Math 1: Counting and writing numbers to 20
- Objective: Count objects to 20 and write numerals
- Objective: Count on from any number
- Vocabulary: count, numeral, count on
- Materials: counters (beans or buttons), ten-frame mats, number line 0–20
- Assessment: Counting interview (collections up to 20) and numeral dictation.

### Science 1 (NGSS): What scientists do: observing and asking questions
- Objective: Use senses and tools to observe
- Objective: Ask testable questions
- Vocabulary: observe, question, senses, magnifier
- Materials: magnifying glass, science notebook, nature items (leaves, rocks, shells)
- Assessment: Science notebook observation entries with labeled drawings.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Why do we have rules?
- Objective: Explain why rules are needed at home, school and in the community
- Vocabulary: rule, responsibility, safe, fair
- Materials: chart paper, picture cards of community signs (stop sign, crosswalk, library 'quiet')
- Assessment: Oral explanation of two rules and their purpose.

### Art & Music 1: Kinds of lines; finding the beat
- Objective: Draw 6 kinds of lines
- Objective: Keep a steady beat
- Vocabulary: line, zigzag, curvy, steady beat
- Materials: crayons, large paper, recording of 'Stars and Stripes Forever' (Sousa)
- Assessment: Line picture labeled with 4 line types and steady-beat check.

### PE 1: Personal space and general space
- Objective: Move safely in personal and general space
- Vocabulary: personal space, general space, warm-up
- Materials: hula hoop, cones or plastic cups
- Assessment: Teacher observation of safe stopping and spacing.

### Health 1: Naming feelings
- Objective: Name at least 6 feelings
- Objective: Recognize feelings in faces and bodies
- Vocabulary: feeling, emotion, happy, sad, angry, scared
- Materials: feelings face cards, mirror, 'The Way I Feel' by Janan Cain (library)
- Assessment: Feeling-word matching and personal example.
