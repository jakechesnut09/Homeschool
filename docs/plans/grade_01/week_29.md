## Day 1 — Monday (school day 141, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *ou and ow* (90 min): Read out, house, cloud, cow, town, owl; sort words by spelling.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Plan* (60 min): Choose 3 categories of objects (short, medium, long) to measure.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Read Are You My Mother?* (30 min): Read the story and list what the baby bird needs from its mother.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Benefits* (30 min): List benefits of a diverse community (new foods, languages, ideas).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Lion and elephant* (30 min): Listen to 'Royal March of the Lion' and 'The Elephant'; describe dynamics and tempo.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Duck duck goose* (30 min): Play with family and practice turns.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Sun paper* (20 min): Place objects on construction paper in the sun to see fading and discuss sun protection.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 142, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Peter Rabbit, part 1* (90 min): Read the first half; describe Mr. McGregor's garden using the illustrations.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Measure* (60 min): Measure 10 toys with cubes and record.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Penguin parents* (30 min): Watch a PBS clip of emperor penguins and list 3 ways parents keep chicks warm and fed.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Challenges* (30 min): Discuss challenges (language barriers, misunderstandings) and how kindness helps.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Swan and aquarium* (30 min): Listen to 'The Swan' and 'Aquarium'; describe the mood.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Simon says* (30 min): Play Simon Says with locomotor and balance commands, then lead a round.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Sunscreen* (20 min): Practice applying sunscreen and wearing a hat.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 143, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Prepositions* (90 min): Tell where Peter went using under, in, through, behind; act them out with a toy.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Graph* (60 min): Graph how many toys fall into each category.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Feeding young* (30 min): Compare how birds (bring worms), mammals (milk) and some fish (guard eggs) care for young on a chart.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Paper quilt* (30 min): Make a paper quilt square showing your family culture and connect it with others' squares (family members).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Paint to music* (30 min): Paint an animal while listening to its movement.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Four square intro* (30 min): Learn four-square rules with chalk squares and play a slow-bounce version.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Water rules* (20 min): Learn swim with an adult, life jackets and no running by pools.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 144, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Peter Rabbit, part 2* (90 min): Finish the story; name Peter's problem and how it was solved.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Questions* (60 min): Answer how-many-more questions about the graph.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Protecting young* (30 min): Read about kangaroo pouches and crocodiles carrying babies in their mouths; draw one.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Shared values* (30 min): Write a sentence about values everyone shares (fairness, family, freedom).
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Favorite* (30 min): Choose your favorite movement and explain why.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Make rules* (30 min): Invent a backyard game with 3 rules and one safety rule; write or draw them.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Safety book* (20 min): Make a safety mini-book with one rule per page.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 145, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): ou/ow dictation; story map of characters, setting, problem, solution.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Diphthongs ou/ow; The Tale of Peter Rabbit
- Objective: Read ou and ow (cow) words
- Objective: Describe characters, setting, events
- Vocabulary: diphthong, preposition, problem, solution
- Materials: 'The Tale of Peter Rabbit' by Beatrix Potter (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Story map and ou/ow dictation.

### Math 1: Measurement and data project
- Objective: Measure, record and graph lengths
- Vocabulary: measurement, data, graph
- Materials: paper clips, grid paper
- Assessment: Unit 8 assessment.

### Science 1 (NGSS): How animal parents care for their young
- Objective: Identify parent behaviors that help offspring survive
- Vocabulary: offspring, parent, protect, feed
- Materials: 'Are You My Mother?' by P.D. Eastman (library), PBS LearningMedia Wild Kratts or nature clips, behavior picture cards
- Assessment: Parent-behavior card sort.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: One community, many cultures
- Objective: Explain benefits and challenges of diversity and shared values
- Vocabulary: unity, community, benefit, challenge
- Materials: community quilt materials (paper squares)
- Assessment: Unit 5 assessment.

### Art & Music 1: Carnival of the Animals
- Objective: Describe how music shows animal characters
- Objective: Create animal art
- Vocabulary: character, expressive, composer
- Materials: Saint-Saëns 'Carnival of the Animals' recording, paints
- Assessment: Listening responses with reasons.

### PE 1: Taking turns and following rules
- Objective: Follow game rules and take turns
- Vocabulary: rules, turn, fair
- Materials: cones, ball
- Assessment: Rule-following observation.

### Health 1: Water and sun safety
- Objective: Follow water and sun safety rules
- Vocabulary: sunscreen, life jacket, shade
- Materials: sunscreen, sun-sensitive paper (or construction paper)
- Assessment: Unit 6 assessment.
