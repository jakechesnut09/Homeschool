## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *oa and ow* (90 min): Read boat, coat, road, snow, grow, slow; build words with vowel-team cards.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Bundle ten* (60 min): Bundle craft sticks into tens and count bundles and loose sticks.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Data review* (30 min): Spread out sun sketches, sundial and moon journal; list three patterns you found.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Old town photo* (30 min): Look at Calisphere photos of a California main street around 1900; list what is the same and different today.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Clay safety* (30 min): Learn clay tools and safe handling; roll a ball.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Throw cues* (30 min): Learn: side to target, arm back, step opposite, throw.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Set goal* (20 min): Choose a goal like 'play outside 30 minutes every day.'

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 92, ~290 min)

- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Mystery Science sky lesson* (30 min): Watch a free Mystery Science sky/spaceship-Earth lesson and answer its mystery question in a sentence.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Jobs then* (30 min): Learn about the general store, blacksmith and milkman.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Pinch pot* (30 min): Make a pinch pot and add texture.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Wall target* (30 min): Throw at a chalk target on a wall.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Track* (20 min): Color one box on your goal chart each day you meet the goal and note the activity you chose.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Vowel teams oa, ow, and long-i (ie, igh); writing a poem
- Objective: Read oa, ow, ie, igh words
- Objective: Write a sensory poem
- Vocabulary: sense, stanza, rhythm
- Materials: poetry notebook, an object to observe (shell, leaf, orange)
- Assessment: Senses poem and vowel-team (oa/ow/ie/igh) word read.

### Math 1: Tens and ones
- Objective: Understand 10 as a bundle of ten ones
- Objective: Represent two-digit numbers as tens and ones
- Vocabulary: tens, ones, digit, place value
- Materials: craft sticks and rubber bands, base-ten blocks (or paper strips)
- Assessment: Place value mat quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Sky patterns wrap-up
- Objective: Use observations to describe predictable patterns of the sun, moon and stars
- Vocabulary: predict, pattern, observe
- Materials: science notebook, moon journal, stapled booklet
- Assessment: Unit 4 assessment and Sky Patterns book.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: Communities long ago
- Objective: Examine how communities looked and worked in the past
- Vocabulary: community, general store, blacksmith, change
- Materials: Calisphere photos of your area or a California town in the 1900s
- Assessment: Oral comparison with evidence from photos.

### Art & Music 1: Clay pinch pots; Peter and the Wolf
- Objective: Make a pinch pot safely
- Objective: Match instruments to characters
- Vocabulary: pinch pot, clay, timbre, character
- Materials: air-dry clay, recording of Prokofiev's 'Peter and the Wolf'
- Assessment: Character-instrument matching.

### PE 1: Overhand throw
- Objective: Throw overhand stepping with the opposite foot
- Vocabulary: overhand, side to target, opposite foot
- Materials: tennis balls or yarn balls, wall target
- Assessment: Overhand cue checklist.

### Health 1: Activity goal
- Objective: Set and track an activity goal
- Vocabulary: goal, track
- Materials: goal chart
- Assessment: Unit 4 goal reflection.
