## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *th words* (90 min): Read thin, this, that, path, bath; feel your tongue for voiced and unvoiced th.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Doubles* (60 min): Use a mirror with counters to see doubles (3 + 3, 4 + 4, 5 + 5); write facts.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Water jars* (30 min): Tap jars with different water levels; order them from high to low pitch.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Bird's-eye view* (30 min): Look down at a toy from above and from the side; draw both views.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Color wheel* (30 min): Paint a 6-part color wheel on a paper plate.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Hop cues* (30 min): Hop on right foot 5 times, then left foot, landing softly.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Glitter germs* (20 min): Put lotion and glitter on hands, shake hands with family and see how 'germs' spread.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *wh words* (90 min): Read when, what, which, whip, whiz; write three question words on cards and ask a family member a question with each.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Doubles in life* (60 min): Find doubles in real life (egg carton 6 + 6, legs of a spider 4 + 4) and draw them.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Band thickness* (30 min): Pluck thick and thin rubber bands; record pitch.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Room model* (30 min): Build a 3-D model of your bedroom with blocks and small boxes.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Same subject* (30 min): Compare two paintings of sunflowers (Van Gogh and another artist); talk about color choices.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Hopscotch* (30 min): Play hopscotch, tossing a beanbag into squares.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Sneeze spray* (20 min): Use a spray bottle to show how far a sneeze travels; practice the elbow sneeze.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 28, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *The Story* (90 min): Read 'The Story' (Frog and Toad); retell what Toad tries in order to think of a story.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Doubles plus one* (60 min): Solve 4 + 5 by thinking 4 + 4 + 1; practice with dominoes.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Loud vs. soft* (30 min): Pluck gently and strongly; compare vibration size.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Photo vs. model* (30 min): Photograph your room and compare: what does the model show that the photo doesn't (and vice versa)?
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Ta and ti-ti* (30 min): Learn quarter notes (ta) and pairs of eighth notes (ti-ti); clap 4-beat rhythm cards.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Hop patterns* (30 min): Hop in a pattern: right-right-left-left.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Germ hideouts* (20 min): Find places germs collect (doorknobs, phones) and wipe them with an adult.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 29, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Nouns* (90 min): Learn that nouns name people, places, and things; find 6 nouns in 'The Lost Button' and sort into person/place/thing.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Turn-around facts* (60 min): Show 2 + 7 and 7 + 2 with cubes and explain why the sum is the same.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Make a song* (30 min): Play a short tune on your water-jar xylophone.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *First map* (30 min): Draw a bird's-eye map of your room with simple symbols.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Write rhythms* (30 min): Write your own 4-beat rhythm using ta and ti-ti.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Hop and go* (30 min): Hop across lily-pad spots drawn in chalk.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Read* (20 min): Read a KidsHealth article on germs together.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 30, ~290 min)

- [ ] **ELA 1** — *Review & check* (90 min): Fill a Venn diagram comparing Frog and Toad; read th/wh word list for accuracy.
- [ ] **Math 1** — *Review & check* (60 min): Doubles and near-doubles quiz; roll-two-dice game recording sums.
- [ ] **Science 1 (NGSS)** — *Review & check* (30 min): Pitch and volume sort quiz with picture cards.
- [ ] **A Child's Place in Time and Space** — *Review & check* (30 min): Explain one thing a model shows better and one thing a picture shows better.
- [ ] **Art & Music 1** — *Share* (30 min): Play your rhythm on a drum and show your color wheel.
- [ ] **PE 1** — *Review & check* (30 min): Hop 10 times on each foot in a row.
- [ ] **Health 1** — *Review & check* (20 min): Explain 3 ways to stop germs (wash hands, sneeze into elbow, keep hands away from face) and show the elbow sneeze.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### ELA 1: Digraphs th and wh; comparing characters
- Objective: Read words with th and wh
- Objective: Compare two characters' experiences
- Vocabulary: compare, contrast, noun
- Materials: Frog and Toad Are Friends, Venn diagram paper
- Assessment: Venn diagram with at least 2 likenesses and 2 differences; th/wh word list read.

### Math 1: Doubles and doubles plus one
- Objective: Know doubles facts to 10
- Objective: Use doubles to solve near doubles
- Vocabulary: double, near double
- Materials: mirror, dominoes, dice
- Assessment: Doubles/near-doubles quiz.

### Science 1 (NGSS): Loud and soft, high and low
- Objective: Investigate volume and pitch
- Vocabulary: loud, soft, high pitch, low pitch
- Materials: glass jars and water, rubber bands of different thickness
- Assessment: Pitch/volume sort.

### A Child's Place in Time and Space: What is a map? Models vs. pictures
- Objective: Compare a 3-D model and a picture of the same place
- Vocabulary: map, model, bird's-eye view, symbol
- Materials: building blocks or small boxes, camera, paper
- Assessment: Room map and model-vs-picture comparison.

### Art & Music 1: Secondary colors and the color wheel; quarter and eighth notes
- Objective: Make a color wheel
- Objective: Read ta and ti-ti
- Vocabulary: secondary color, color wheel, quarter note, eighth notes
- Materials: paint, paper plate, rhythm cards
- Assessment: Color wheel and rhythm reading.

### PE 1: Hopping
- Objective: Hop on one foot with balance
- Vocabulary: hop, balance, non-hopping leg
- Materials: chalk hopscotch, beanbag
- Assessment: Hop count each foot.

### Health 1: What are germs?
- Objective: Explain how germs spread
- Vocabulary: germ, spread, sneeze, virus
- Materials: glitter, lotion, spray bottle with water
- Assessment: Oral explanation of germ prevention.
