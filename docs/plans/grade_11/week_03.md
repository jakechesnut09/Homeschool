## Day 1 — Monday (school day 11, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Context* (60 min): Read a short article on the Salem trials of 1692 and one on the 1950s House Un-American Activities Committee; list parallels.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *2×2 systems review* (60 min): Solve systems by graphing, substitution, and elimination; classify one, none, or infinite solutions.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Third law* (60 min): Build a balloon rocket on a string; identify force pairs.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Dred Scott to war* (60 min): Read excerpts of the Dred Scott decision and explain how it intensified sectional conflict.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Lectura* (50 min): Read a Spanish-language biography of Sonia Sotomayor and summarize her achievements.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Leading centers* (50 min): Explore walking led by head, chest, belly, and knees; create a character from each.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Dynamic warm-up* (45 min): Learn and perform a 10-minute dynamic warm-up (leg swings, walking lunges, high knees, A-skips).
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Lab safety* (55 min): Learn shop safety rules, PPE, and tool handling; pass a safety quiz.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 12, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Act I* (60 min): Read Act I aloud, taking several roles; track each character's motives (Abigail, Parris, Putnam, Proctor) in a chart.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *3×3 systems* (60 min): Solve three-variable systems by elimination; model a ticket-sales problem with three prices.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Friction lab* (60 min): Drag a shoe across different surfaces with a luggage scale; calculate coefficient of friction.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Amendments* (60 min): Analyze the 13th, 14th, and 15th Amendments and their promises.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Duda y certeza* (50 min): Practice subjunctive vs. indicative after expressions of doubt and certainty with 20 sentences.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Animal work* (50 min): Observe an animal (pet or video) and transform its movement into a human character.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Cool-down* (45 min): Learn a static stretching cool-down for major muscle groups after a 20-minute jog.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Marshmallow challenge* (55 min): Complete the spaghetti-marshmallow tower challenge with family members and reflect on teamwork.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: The Crucible, Acts I–II
- Objective: Analyze characterization and conflict in drama
- Objective: Connect allegory to McCarthyism
- Vocabulary: allegory, hysteria, McCarthyism, dramatic irony, protagonist
- Materials: Arthur Miller, The Crucible (library copy), short video/article on the Red Scare
- Assessment: Reading quiz and characterization paragraph.

### Algebra II: Systems of equations and inequalities
- Objective: Solve linear systems in two and three variables
- Objective: Model constraints with inequalities
- Vocabulary: system, elimination, substitution, feasible region, ordered triple
- Materials: Desmos, graph paper
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Physics of the Universe: Newton's third law and friction
- Objective: Identify action–reaction pairs
- Objective: Quantify friction
- Vocabulary: action-reaction, normal force, friction, coefficient of friction
- Materials: balloon, straw, string, spring scale or luggage scale, shoe
- Assessment: Newton's laws quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Civil War, Reconstruction, and the Reconstruction Amendments
- Objective: Analyze effects of Civil War and Reconstruction
- Vocabulary: Reconstruction, 13th/14th/15th Amendments, Black Codes, sharecropping, Dred Scott
- Materials: Reconstruction Amendments text, photos of Freedmen's Bureau schools (LOC)
- Assessment: Unit 1 test.

### Spanish IV: Héroes y personajes públicos
- Objective: Describe public figures
- Objective: Use the subjunctive with impersonal expressions and doubt
- Vocabulary: el liderazgo, el héroe, la valentía, luchar por, dudar que
- Materials: biographies of Sonia Sotomayor, Frida Kahlo, César Chávez (Spanish-language articles)
- Assessment: Presentation and quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Character through improvisation
- Objective: Develop characters through physical choices
- Vocabulary: leading center, gesture, character walk, given circumstances
- Materials: costume pieces (hats, scarves)
- Assessment: Character profile and performance.

### Lifetime Fitness: Warm-up, cool-down, and safety
- Objective: Design dynamic warm-ups and static cool-downs
- Objective: Practice safe exercise habits
- Vocabulary: dynamic warm-up, static stretch, hydration, heat illness
- Materials: cones, water bottle
- Assessment: Routine demonstration and safety quiz.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Teamwork and documentation
- Objective: Document a design cycle
- Objective: Practice collaboration
- Vocabulary: Gantt chart, role, safety, PPE
- Materials: safety glasses, craft materials, marshmallows and spaghetti
- Assessment: Safety quiz and Unit 1 quiz.
