## Day 1 — Monday (school day 16, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Act III* (60 min): Read the courtroom act; trace how logic breaks down and cite the line where Hale turns.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Vertex form* (60 min): Graph y = a(x−h)²+k using transformations; identify vertex, axis, and max/min.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Momentum* (60 min): Calculate momentum for everyday objects; compare a truck and a baseball.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Industrial growth* (60 min): Map railroads and resources; explain geographic advantages that fueled U.S. industry.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Fuentes* (50 min): Read an article, analyze a chart, and listen to an audio clip on heritage language maintenance.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Story spine* (50 min): Learn the story spine ('Once upon a time... until finally') and improvise five stories.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Target zones* (45 min): Calculate maximum heart rate and target zones using the Karvonen formula.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Line work* (55 min): Practice horizontal, vertical, and angled lines and construction lines.

- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Circles & arcs* (55 min): Sketch circles and arcs using boxes and diagonals.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: The Crucible, Acts III–IV and literary argument
- Objective: Analyze theme development
- Objective: Write a literary argument essay
- Vocabulary: integrity, tragic hero, theme, claim, counterclaim
- Materials: The Crucible, film clip of the 1996 adaptation (library/streaming)
- Assessment: Literary argument essay (rubric) and Unit 1 test.

### Algebra II: Quadratic functions in three forms
- Objective: Graph quadratics in standard, vertex, and factored form
- Objective: Convert between forms
- Vocabulary: vertex form, standard form, factored form, axis of symmetry, completing the square
- Materials: Desmos, algebra tiles or drawn area models
- Assessment: Quiz on quadratic forms.

### Physics of the Universe: Momentum and impulse
- Objective: Show momentum is conserved
- Objective: Relate impulse to force and time
- Vocabulary: momentum, impulse, conservation, elastic collision, inelastic collision
- Materials: two toy cars, clay, ruler, marbles, PhET Collision Lab
- Assessment: Momentum quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: The rise of industry and big business
- Objective: Explain growth of industry and trusts
- Objective: Compare views of industrial leaders
- Vocabulary: trust, monopoly, vertical integration, horizontal integration, Social Darwinism, Social Gospel
- Materials: Carnegie, 'The Gospel of Wealth', political cartoons of trusts (LOC)
- Assessment: Cartoon analysis.

### Spanish IV: Ensayo: la identidad
- Objective: Write a formal persuasive essay
- Objective: Integrate sources
- Vocabulary: sin embargo, además, por lo tanto, en conclusión, según
- Materials: three short sources on identity (article, chart, audio)
- Assessment: Persuasive essay and Unit 1 test.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Long-form improvisation and storytelling
- Objective: Create structured improvised stories
- Vocabulary: Harold, story spine, callback, beat
- Materials: story spine template
- Assessment: Long-form performance.

### Lifetime Fitness: Heart rate and intensity
- Objective: Calculate target heart rate zones
- Objective: Monitor intensity
- Vocabulary: target heart rate, maximum heart rate, heart rate reserve, RPE
- Materials: stopwatch or heart-rate app, jump rope
- Assessment: Quiz and heart-rate graph.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Freehand sketching fundamentals
- Objective: Sketch lines, circles, and arcs
- Objective: Use proportion
- Vocabulary: sketch, construction line, object line, proportion
- Materials: grid paper, pencils, eraser
- Assessment: Sketch portfolio.
