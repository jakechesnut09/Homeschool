## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Poetry of a new nation: Whitman and Dickinson
- Objective: Analyze form and style in poetry
- Objective: Write a poetry comparison
- Vocabulary: free verse, catalog, slant rhyme, dash, enjambment, common meter
- Materials: Whitman, 'Song of Myself' sections 1, 6, 52 and 'I Hear America Singing', Dickinson, 'Because I could not stop for Death', 'Hope is the thing with feathers', 'Tell all the truth but tell it slant'
- Assessment: Poetry comparison essay, recitation, Unit 3 test.

### Algebra II: Graphing polynomial functions
- Objective: Sketch polynomial graphs from zeros and end behavior
- Objective: Interpret key features
- Vocabulary: end behavior, turning point, relative maximum, multiplicity
- Materials: Desmos, graph paper
- Assessment: Graphing quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Thermal energy and heat transfer
- Objective: Investigate thermal energy transfer
- Objective: Explain equilibrium
- Vocabulary: thermal energy, temperature, specific heat, thermal equilibrium, conduction, convection, radiation
- Materials: insulated cups, thermometer, hot and cold water, food coloring
- Assessment: Calorimetry lab report.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: The 1920s: prosperity and culture
- Objective: Analyze 1920s economic and cultural change
- Vocabulary: consumerism, installment buying, flapper, Harlem Renaissance, mass media
- Materials: 1920s advertisements, Langston Hughes poem, Coolidge speeches
- Assessment: Paragraph.

### Spanish IV: Ética y tecnología: la inteligencia artificial
- Objective: Discuss ethical issues in technology
- Objective: Form and use the imperfect subjunctive
- Vocabulary: la inteligencia artificial, la ética, reemplazar, el riesgo, el beneficio
- Materials: Spanish-language news article on AI (e.g., BBC Mundo), imperfect subjunctive chart
- Assessment: Imperfect subjunctive quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Monologue performance and critique
- Objective: Perform a monologue for an audience
- Objective: Give constructive critique
- Vocabulary: slate, audition, critique, criteria
- Materials: performance space
- Assessment: Monologue performance rubric.

### Lifetime Fitness: Designing a strength program
- Objective: Design a progressive 4-week strength program
- Objective: Estimate loads safely
- Vocabulary: full-body routine, split routine, reps in reserve, periodization
- Materials: program template, dumbbells, jugs, or bands
- Assessment: Strength program design.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Introduction to CAD
- Objective: Navigate CAD software
- Objective: Create simple 3D models
- Vocabulary: CAD, sketch, extrude, constraint, parametric
- Materials: computer with Onshape for Education (free) and Tinkercad
- Assessment: CAD part check.
