## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: The Harlem Renaissance
- Objective: Analyze voice and form in Harlem Renaissance poetry and fiction
- Objective: Connect art to the Great Migration
- Vocabulary: Harlem Renaissance, Great Migration, blues form, double consciousness
- Materials: Langston Hughes, 'The Negro Speaks of Rivers', 'Harlem', 'I, Too', Claude McKay, 'If We Must Die', Countee Cullen, 'Incident', Zora Neale Hurston, 'How It Feels to Be Colored Me'
- Assessment: Presentation rubric and poetry quiz.

### Algebra II: Properties of logarithms
- Objective: Prove and apply log properties
- Objective: Expand and condense log expressions
- Vocabulary: product property, quotient property, power property
- Materials: practice set
- Assessment: Properties quiz with proof.

### Physics of the Universe: Wave-particle duality
- Objective: Evaluate wave vs. particle models of light
- Vocabulary: photon, photoelectric effect, diffraction, wave-particle duality
- Materials: laser pointer, hair strand, CD, PhET Photoelectric Effect
- Assessment: CER and quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Postwar society and the suburban boom
- Objective: Analyze postwar economic boom and social change
- Objective: Explain California's postwar growth
- Vocabulary: GI Bill, baby boom, suburbanization, Levittown, interstate highway, California Master Plan
- Materials: GI Bill text, Levittown advertisements and restrictive covenants, 1960 California Master Plan for Higher Education summary
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish IV: Unidad 5: presentación profesional
- Objective: Research and present on a career in a Spanish-speaking country
- Objective: Use formal register
- Vocabulary: el campo laboral, la demanda, el requisito, la formación
- Materials: slides, career information in Spanish (e.g., Spanish government employment site)
- Assessment: Presentation rubric and Unit 5 test.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Writing a play critique
- Objective: Write a formal theatre critique using criteria
- Vocabulary: critique, review, production values, criteria, interpretation
- Materials: a full filmed or live production (local theatre, community college, or streaming)
- Assessment: Critique rubric.

### Lifetime Fitness: Cycling and active transportation
- Objective: Ride safely and plan active commutes
- Vocabulary: ABC quick check, cadence, bike lane, helmet fit
- Materials: bicycle and helmet, route map
- Assessment: Active commute plan.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Testing and iteration
- Objective: Write and run a test plan
- Objective: Iterate based on data
- Vocabulary: test plan, independent variable, failure mode, iteration
- Materials: phone-stand prototype, weights (coins, books), protractor
- Assessment: Test report and Unit 6 test.
