## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Hemingway, Faulkner, and the Modern short story
- Objective: Analyze style (iceberg theory, stream of consciousness)
- Objective: Write an imitation and analysis
- Vocabulary: iceberg theory, minimalism, stream of consciousness, nonlinear plot, Southern Gothic
- Materials: Hemingway, 'Hills Like White Elephants' (library anthology), Faulkner, 'A Rose for Emily' (library anthology)
- Assessment: Style imitation and Unit 6 test.

### Algebra II: Exponential and logarithmic equations and modeling
- Objective: Solve exponential and log equations
- Objective: Use logs in real models
- Vocabulary: logarithmic scale, pH, decibel, Richter
- Materials: Desmos, cooling cup of hot water and thermometer
- Assessment: Cooling lab and Unit 6 test.

### Physics of the Universe: Digital information and wave technology
- Objective: Evaluate digital transmission
- Objective: Communicate how devices use waves
- Vocabulary: analog, digital, bit, fiber optic, signal
- Materials: flashlight for Morse code, water and laser (total internal reflection)
- Assessment: Technology report and Unit 5 test.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Cuba, Vietnam, and the limits of containment
- Objective: Analyze Cold War crises in the 1960s
- Objective: Explain the Vietnam War and protest
- Vocabulary: Bay of Pigs, Cuban Missile Crisis, Gulf of Tonkin Resolution, Tet Offensive, domino theory
- Materials: JFK's Cuban Missile Crisis address, Gulf of Tonkin Resolution (DocsTeach), Vietnam War protest photos
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### Spanish IV: El medio ambiente
- Objective: Discuss environmental problems and solutions
- Objective: Use the subjunctive with expressions of will and necessity
- Vocabulary: el cambio climático, la sequía, la deforestación, reciclar, la huella de carbono, es urgente que
- Materials: BBC Mundo or UN en español article on the Amazon, household recycling bins
- Assessment: Recommendation letter.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Theatre spaces and set design
- Objective: Explain stage types
- Objective: Design a set for a scene
- Vocabulary: proscenium, thrust, arena, black box, ground plan, rendering
- Materials: graph paper, shoebox, cardboard, glue (model box)
- Assessment: Model box presentation.

### Lifetime Fitness: Aquatics or outdoor challenge
- Objective: Demonstrate water safety or complete an outdoor challenge
- Vocabulary: water safety, freestyle, treading water, rip current
- Materials: community pool access (or alternative trail challenge)
- Assessment: Quiz and log.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Human factors and ergonomics
- Objective: Apply anthropometric data to design
- Objective: Evaluate ergonomics
- Vocabulary: ergonomics, anthropometry, percentile, human factors, accessibility
- Materials: tape measure, anthropometric data table (online), household tools
- Assessment: Ergonomic redesign.
