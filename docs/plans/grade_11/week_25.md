## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Research: notes, outline, and drafting
- Objective: Integrate sources and avoid plagiarism
- Objective: Draft a sustained research paper
- Vocabulary: paraphrase, signal phrase, in-text citation, plagiarism
- Materials: research notes, outline template
- Assessment: Rough draft checkpoint.

### Algebra II: The Pythagorean identity and other trig values
- Objective: Prove the Pythagorean identity
- Objective: Find trig values from one value and quadrant
- Vocabulary: identity, reciprocal function, secant, cosecant, cotangent
- Materials: unit circle
- Assessment: Identity quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Plate tectonics and crustal ages
- Objective: Evaluate evidence for plate tectonics
- Vocabulary: plate boundary, seafloor spreading, subduction, transform fault, magnetic striping
- Materials: seafloor age map, puzzle pieces from map
- Assessment: Plate tectonics CER.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Nonviolent direct action
- Objective: Analyze strategies and leaders of the movement
- Vocabulary: boycott, sit-in, Freedom Rides, SCLC, SNCC, nonviolence
- Materials: Montgomery Bus Boycott documents, King, 'Letter from Birmingham Jail' and 'I Have a Dream', Malcolm X speech excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish IV: La salud pública
- Objective: Discuss health and wellness issues
- Objective: Use por and para accurately
- Vocabulary: la salud mental, la nutrición, la vacuna, el sistema de salud, prevenir
- Materials: OPS/PAHO Spanish-language health infographic, por/para chart
- Assessment: PSA and quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Costume and makeup design
- Objective: Design costumes that reveal character
- Vocabulary: costume plot, rendering, silhouette, period, stage makeup
- Materials: colored pencils, thrift-store or closet pieces, basic makeup
- Assessment: Costume portfolio.

### Lifetime Fitness: Group fitness: circuits and boot camp
- Objective: Design and lead a safe group workout
- Objective: Use cueing and modifications
- Vocabulary: circuit, station, cueing, modification, progression
- Materials: cones, jump rope, bands, mats
- Assessment: Leadership rubric.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Design communication and pitching
- Objective: Communicate design ideas visually and orally
- Vocabulary: rendering, storyboard, elevator pitch, presentation
- Materials: Onshape rendering or screenshots, slide software
- Assessment: Pitch rubric and quiz.
