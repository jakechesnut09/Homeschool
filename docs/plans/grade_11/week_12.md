## Day 1 — Monday (school day 56, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Context & preface* (60 min): Read Garrison's preface and Douglass' Ch. 1; note how prefaces were used to authenticate narratives.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Open-box lab* (60 min): Cut squares from cardstock corners to make boxes; model V(x) = x(L−2x)(W−2x) and find max volume.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Efficiency* (60 min): Calculate efficiency of common devices (light bulbs, car engines).
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Red Scare* (60 min): Study the Palmer Raids and Sacco and Vanzetti case.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Fuentes* (50 min): Read an article, interpret a graph, and listen to a podcast segment on teens and smartphones; take notes in Spanish.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Origins* (50 min): Read about the Festival of Dionysus and the structure of a Greek theatre.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Workout A+* (45 min): Complete Workout A with a progression (added rep or load) and log it.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Revolve* (55 min): Model a bottle or knob using a revolve feature.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 57, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Ch. 2–4* (60 min): Read and record examples of slavery's effect on enslavers as well as the enslaved.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Pascal's Triangle* (60 min): Build Pascal's triangle and connect rows to (a+b)^n coefficients.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Design* (60 min): Design a solar oven with constraints (size, materials).
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Nativism* (60 min): Analyze the Immigration Act of 1924 quota tables and the KKK revival.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Esquema* (50 min): Outline an essay answering ¿Deben prohibirse los teléfonos celulares en las escuelas? with a thesis and counterargument.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Antigone* (50 min): Read key scenes of Antigone and discuss conflict between law and conscience.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Retest* (45 min): Retest 90° push-ups, curl-ups, and plank hold; compare with week 1.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Fillets & chamfers* (55 min): Add fillets and chamfers and explain why designers soften edges.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass, Ch. 1–6
- Objective: Analyze how Douglass develops central ideas
- Objective: Evaluate rhetorical purpose of a slave narrative
- Vocabulary: abolitionist, slave narrative, dehumanization, literacy
- Materials: Douglass, Narrative of the Life (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Reading quiz and close-reading response.

### Algebra II: Polynomial modeling and the Binomial Theorem
- Objective: Model with polynomial functions
- Objective: Expand binomials
- Vocabulary: Pascal's triangle, binomial coefficient, volume model
- Materials: cardstock, scissors, ruler
- Assessment: Box lab and Unit 3 test.

### Physics of the Universe: Engineering: energy conversion devices
- Objective: Design a device converting one form of energy to another
- Vocabulary: efficiency, energy conversion, input energy, output energy
- Materials: cardboard, foil, black paper, plastic wrap (solar oven), thermometer
- Assessment: Solar oven design report.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: The 1920s: conflict
- Objective: Analyze conflicts over civil liberties and culture
- Vocabulary: Red Scare, Palmer Raids, quota, Prohibition, Scopes trial
- Materials: Immigration Act of 1924, Scopes trial transcript excerpt
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Spanish IV: Unidad 3: ensayo argumentativo
- Objective: Write an argumentative essay integrating three sources
- Objective: Cite sources in Spanish
- Vocabulary: la tesis, la evidencia, el contraargumento, citar, refutar
- Materials: three sources on technology and youth (article, graph, podcast clip)
- Assessment: Argumentative essay and Unit 3 test.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Greek theatre
- Objective: Explain the origins of Western theatre
- Objective: Perform Greek chorus work
- Vocabulary: tragedy, chorus, catharsis, amphitheater, hubris, mask
- Materials: Sophocles, Antigone (Gutenberg), paper plates for masks
- Assessment: Quiz and chorus performance.

### Lifetime Fitness: Strength reassessment
- Objective: Reassess muscular fitness and evaluate the program
- Vocabulary: reassessment, progressive overload, plateau
- Materials: fitness journal, mat
- Assessment: Retest results and quiz.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): CAD features
- Objective: Use revolve, fillet, chamfer, shell, and pattern features
- Vocabulary: revolve, fillet, chamfer, shell, pattern, feature tree
- Materials: Onshape
- Assessment: Feature model.
