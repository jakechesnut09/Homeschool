## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Language and identity
- Objective: Analyze how authors use language to explore identity
- Objective: Write an analytical essay
- Vocabulary: code-switching, identity, vignette, bilingualism
- Materials: Amy Tan, 'Mother Tongue', Sandra Cisneros, The House on Mango Street (selected vignettes), Richard Rodriguez, 'Aria' excerpt, Gloria Anzaldúa, 'How to Tame a Wild Tongue' excerpt
- Assessment: Analytical comparison essay.

### Algebra II: Normal distributions
- Objective: Use mean and standard deviation to fit a normal model
- Objective: Estimate population percentages
- Vocabulary: normal distribution, standard deviation, z-score, empirical rule
- Materials: data on heights or SAT scores, calculator/Desmos normal distribution tool
- Assessment: Normal distribution quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Composition of matter in the universe
- Objective: Explain evidence from the composition of matter
- Objective: Describe dark matter and dark energy evidence
- Vocabulary: primordial nucleosynthesis, hydrogen-helium ratio, dark matter, dark energy, rotation curve
- Materials: NASA element-abundance chart, string, washers (rotation-curve model)
- Assessment: Quiz with CER items.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: America in the world after the Cold War
- Objective: Analyze U.S. Middle East policy and post-Cold War conflicts
- Objective: Explain responses to 9/11
- Vocabulary: Gulf War, globalization, 9/11, War on Terror, Patriot Act
- Materials: George H. W. Bush address on the Gulf War, George W. Bush address to Congress, Sept. 20, 2001, 9/11 Memorial & Museum oral histories
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish IV: Historia hispana de California
- Objective: Trace Spanish-speaking communities in California history
- Vocabulary: el rancho, la misión, el bracero, la frontera, el movimiento chicano
- Materials: Calisphere photographs, Spanish-language articles on California history
- Assessment: Timeline presentation.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Blocking and staging
- Objective: Block a one-act play
- Objective: Use composition and picturization
- Vocabulary: composition, picturization, stage areas, blocking notation, focus
- Materials: taped floor plan, prompt book, pencil
- Assessment: Blocking documented in prompt book.

### Lifetime Fitness: Fitness consumerism
- Objective: Evaluate fitness products, apps, and claims
- Objective: Use community fitness resources
- Vocabulary: supplement, fad, evidence-based, gym membership, community recreation
- Materials: ads for fitness products, local parks & recreation website
- Assessment: Consumer guide.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Capstone: prototype build I
- Objective: Plan fabrication and build the main structure safely
- Vocabulary: fabrication plan, tolerance, fit, jig
- Materials: materials per BOM, hand tools, safety glasses
- Assessment: Build log check.
