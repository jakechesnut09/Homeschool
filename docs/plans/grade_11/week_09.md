## Day 1 — Monday (school day 41, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Emerson* (60 min): Read excerpts of 'Self-Reliance'; select five aphorisms and explain each in plain language.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Long division* (60 min): Divide polynomials by binomials using long division, connecting to numerical long division.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Work* (60 min): Calculate work done lifting and pushing objects.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Panama Canal* (60 min): Study the canal's construction and the U.S. role in Panama's independence.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Vocabulario* (50 min): Learn 25 technology terms and survey family members on screen time.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Selection* (50 min): Read 10 age-appropriate monologues and choose a contemporary or classical piece.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Hip hinge* (45 min): Learn the hip hinge with a dowel and perform Romanian deadlifts with light weight.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Data collection* (55 min): Measure the length of 50 'identical' items and record in a spreadsheet.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 42, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Nature* (60 min): Read the 'transparent eyeball' passage from 'Nature'; take a 20-minute solo nature walk and write a journal entry in imitation.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Synthetic division* (60 min): Use synthetic division for divisors of the form x−a; compare speed.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Kinetic & potential energy* (60 min): Calculate KE and PE for a roller coaster; relate to particle motion and fields.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Diplomacy* (60 min): Compare the three presidents' diplomacy using primary source quotes.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Futuro* (50 min): Learn future tense and predict technology in 2050.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Read the play* (50 min): Read the full play the monologue comes from and write the given circumstances.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Lunges* (45 min): Learn forward, reverse, and lateral lunges.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Central tendency* (55 min): Calculate mean, median, mode, and range by hand and with spreadsheet functions.

- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Standard deviation* (55 min): Calculate standard deviation and explain what it reveals about manufacturing consistency.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Transcendentalism: Emerson and Thoreau
- Objective: Determine central ideas of Transcendentalist essays
- Objective: Connect ideas to modern life
- Vocabulary: Transcendentalism, self-reliance, nonconformity, civil disobedience, Oversoul
- Materials: Emerson, 'Self-Reliance' and 'Nature' excerpts, Thoreau, Walden ('Where I Lived...') and 'Civil Disobedience'
- Assessment: Seminar participation rubric and short argument.

### Algebra II: Dividing polynomials and the Remainder Theorem
- Objective: Divide polynomials using long and synthetic division
- Objective: Apply the Remainder and Factor Theorems
- Vocabulary: synthetic division, remainder theorem, factor theorem, quotient
- Materials: practice set
- Assessment: Division quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Work, energy, and power
- Objective: Define work, kinetic and potential energy
- Objective: Calculate power
- Vocabulary: work, kinetic energy, potential energy, joule, power, watt
- Materials: stairs, stopwatch, bathroom scale
- Assessment: Stair power lab and quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Panama, Latin America, and diplomacy
- Objective: Compare Big Stick, Dollar, and Moral Diplomacy
- Vocabulary: Big Stick, Roosevelt Corollary, Dollar Diplomacy, Moral Diplomacy
- Materials: Roosevelt Corollary text, Panama Canal photos
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### Spanish IV: La tecnología en la vida diaria
- Objective: Discuss technology use
- Objective: Use the future tense
- Vocabulary: la red social, descargar, la pantalla, el algoritmo, la privacidad
- Materials: Spanish-language infographic on social media use
- Assessment: Writing.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Choosing and analyzing a monologue
- Objective: Select an appropriate monologue
- Objective: Score the text
- Vocabulary: monologue, subtext, operative word, scoring
- Materials: monologue collections (library), Drama Notebook lists
- Assessment: Monologue progress check.

### Lifetime Fitness: Lower body strength
- Objective: Perform lower body exercises safely
- Vocabulary: hinge, lunge, glutes, hamstrings, quadriceps
- Materials: dumbbells or filled water jugs, resistance bands
- Assessment: Video form check.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Statistics for quality
- Objective: Calculate descriptive statistics for measurement data
- Objective: Interpret variation
- Vocabulary: mean, median, mode, range, standard deviation, normal distribution
- Materials: calipers, 50 identical items (pencils, nails, crackers), spreadsheet
- Assessment: Quality-control report.
