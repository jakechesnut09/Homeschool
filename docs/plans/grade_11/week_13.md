## Day 1 — Monday (school day 61, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Ch. 7–9* (60 min): Read; identify how learning to read changes Douglass' self-understanding.

- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Review: mechanical energy* (60 min): Mixed problems on work, KE, PE, and power.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Douglass Ch. 7–11 and Harriet Jacobs
- Objective: Analyze turning points in narrative
- Objective: Compare male and female slave narratives
- Vocabulary: chiasmus, turning point, emancipation, perspective
- Materials: Douglass, Narrative Ch. 7–11, Harriet Jacobs, Incidents in the Life of a Slave Girl excerpts (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Discussion rubric and comparative exit response.

### Algebra II: Rational expressions
- Objective: Simplify, multiply, divide, add, and subtract rational expressions
- Vocabulary: rational expression, excluded value, LCD, complex fraction
- Materials: practice set
- Assessment: Rational expressions quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Energy unit synthesis
- Objective: Synthesize energy concepts
- Vocabulary: review
- Materials: review packet
- Assessment: Unit 3 test.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Causes of the Great Depression
- Objective: Explain causes of the Depression
- Vocabulary: stock market crash, buying on margin, Federal Reserve, bank run
- Materials: stock market data graphs, Hoover speeches
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish IV: El arte visual: Velázquez, Goya y Picasso
- Objective: Describe and interpret artworks
- Objective: Use the passive voice with ser and se
- Vocabulary: el cuadro, el retrato, el pincel, la pintura al óleo, fue pintado por
- Materials: Museo del Prado online collection (Las Meninas, El 3 de mayo), Museo Reina Sofía online (Guernica)
- Assessment: Comparison paragraph and quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Medieval, commedia dell'arte, and world traditions
- Objective: Compare theatrical traditions across cultures
- Vocabulary: commedia dell'arte, lazzi, stock character, Noh, kabuki, mystery play
- Materials: commedia character images, Noh and kabuki video clips
- Assessment: Comparison writing.

### Lifetime Fitness: Flexibility and mobility
- Objective: Distinguish flexibility from mobility
- Objective: Use stretching methods safely
- Vocabulary: static stretching, dynamic stretching, PNF, range of motion, foam rolling
- Materials: mat, towel or strap, foam roller (optional)
- Assessment: Mobility routine.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Modeling real parts
- Objective: Model existing objects from measurements
- Vocabulary: design intent, reference geometry, sketch plane
- Materials: calipers, household objects, Onshape
- Assessment: Modeled part.
