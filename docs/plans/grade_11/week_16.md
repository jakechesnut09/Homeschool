## Day 1 — Monday (school day 76, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Realism intro* (60 min): Notes on Realism vs. Romanticism; read 'The Jumping Frog' (a California story) and identify the frame narrative.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Nth roots* (60 min): Evaluate nth roots and explain why 8^(1/3) = 2 using exponent properties.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Faraday's lab* (60 min): Use PhET Faraday's Lab to explore induced current.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Rise of dictators* (60 min): Map aggression by Japan, Italy, and Germany 1931–1939 and explain the policy of appeasement at Munich.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Patrimonio* (50 min): Explore UNESCO pages on the Alhambra, Machu Picchu, and Chichén Itzá and record five facts each.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Choose scene* (50 min): Read three two-person scenes, choose one with a partner, and read the full play it comes from.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Grip & groundstrokes* (45 min): Learn continental grip and practice forehand and backhand against a wall (50 each).
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Why reverse engineer* (55 min): Learn legitimate uses of reverse engineering and safety for disassembly.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Mark Twain and regional humor
- Objective: Analyze satire and dialect
- Objective: Distinguish stated vs. intended meaning
- Vocabulary: Realism, regionalism, local color, dialect, satire, vernacular
- Materials: Twain, 'The Notorious Jumping Frog of Calaveras County', Twain, Adventures of Huckleberry Finn Ch. 1, 16, 31 (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Quiz and irony analysis paragraph.

### Algebra II: Rational exponents and radicals
- Objective: Convert between radical and rational exponent form
- Objective: Simplify radical expressions
- Vocabulary: nth root, index, rational exponent, radicand
- Materials: practice set
- Assessment: Radicals quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Electromagnetic induction and generators
- Objective: Show changing magnetic fields produce current
- Vocabulary: induction, generator, motor, Faraday's law, alternating current
- Materials: magnet, coil of wire, LED, PhET Faraday's Electromagnetic Lab
- Assessment: Induction CER.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Road to World War II
- Objective: Explain the events leading to U.S. entry into WWII
- Objective: Analyze FDR's wartime foreign policy
- Vocabulary: isolationism, appeasement, Neutrality Acts, Lend-Lease, Four Freedoms
- Materials: FDR, Four Freedoms speech (1941), FDR, 'Day of Infamy' draft with handwritten edits (DocsTeach), Charles Lindbergh America First speech excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz with sourcing item.

### Spanish IV: La arquitectura y el patrimonio
- Objective: Describe architectural heritage
- Objective: Use the pluperfect
- Vocabulary: el patrimonio, la catedral, la mezquita, las ruinas, había construido
- Materials: UNESCO World Heritage Site pages (Alhambra, Machu Picchu, Chichén Itzá)
- Assessment: Virtual tour and quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Scene study: partner work
- Objective: Analyze and rehearse a contemporary two-person scene
- Objective: Listen and react truthfully
- Vocabulary: scene partner, Meisner repetition, subtext, stakes
- Materials: two-person scene from a contemporary play (library collection)
- Assessment: Off-book run and journal.

### Lifetime Fitness: Pickleball fundamentals
- Objective: Perform forehand, backhand, serve, and dink
- Objective: Learn rules and scoring
- Vocabulary: dink, kitchen, volley, third-shot drop, underhand serve
- Materials: pickleball paddles and ball (or a public court), wall
- Assessment: Skill check and rules quiz.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Reverse engineering: observation and disassembly
- Objective: Analyze a product's function and structure
- Vocabulary: reverse engineering, functional analysis, subsystem, fastener
- Materials: small screwdriver set, safety glasses, inexpensive product (stapler, flashlight, retractable pen)
- Assessment: Notebook check.
