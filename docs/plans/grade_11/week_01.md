## Day 1 — Monday (school day 1, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Course launch* (60 min): Set up a reading journal and take a 20-minute diagnostic writing sample responding to 'What makes someone an American?'
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Parent function gallery* (60 min): Graph linear, quadratic, absolute value, square root, cube root, and cubic parents in Desmos; record domain, range, and intercepts.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Anchor phenomenon* (60 min): Watch crash-test footage and list questions about forces in collisions; start a driving question board.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Enlightenment roots* (60 min): Read excerpts from Locke and Montesquieu and chart ideas that appear in the Declaration.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Presentación personal* (50 min): Write and record a 2-minute self-introduction including heritage, interests, and goals.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *What is theatre?* (50 min): Discuss what makes theatre different from film; set up a performance journal with goals for the year.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Course launch & PACER prep* (45 min): Learn the five fitness components; measure resting heart rate and complete a 1-mile walk/run for time.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Engineering careers* (55 min): Research five engineering disciplines (mechanical, civil, electrical, biomedical, industrial) and record salary, education, and a California employer for each.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 2, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Creation story* (60 min): Read 'The World on the Turtle's Back' and annotate values it reveals; write a 150-word objective summary.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Translations* (60 min): Use sliders for f(x)+k and f(x−h); write rules for 10 transformed graphs from pictures.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Position-time graphs* (60 min): Roll a toy car along a taped track, time each meter, and graph position vs. time.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Declaration* (60 min): Analyze the Declaration's preamble and list of grievances using a SOAPS organizer.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Repaso del pretérito* (50 min): Review preterite regular and irregular forms with a 30-verb drill and a story chain.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Focus games* (50 min): Play mirror, Zip-Zap-Zop (with family), and 'counting to 20' focus games; journal what improved concentration.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Muscular endurance* (45 min): Complete curl-up and 90° push-up tests using Fitnessgram cadence; record results.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Engineering notebook* (55 min): Set up a bound engineering notebook with table of contents, dated entries, and signing conventions.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 3, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Of Plymouth Plantation* (60 min): Read Bradford's chapters on the Mayflower voyage and first winter; chart evidence of 'providence' language.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Stretches & reflections* (60 min): Investigate a·f(x) and f(bx); match 12 equation cards to graphs.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Velocity & acceleration* (60 min): Roll a ball down a ramp; use slow-motion video to measure velocity changes and calculate acceleration.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Constitution* (60 min): Map the structure of the Constitution and identify checks and balances in Articles I–III.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Pretérito vs. imperfecto* (50 min): Contrast the tenses in English and Spanish; rewrite a childhood memory using both correctly.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Trust & physical awareness* (50 min): Practice trust walks, neutral stance, and space-walking with tempo changes 1–10.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Flexibility* (45 min): Complete back-saver sit-and-reach and trunk lift; practice correct technique first.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Design that changed the world* (55 min): Read about an innovation (e.g., the Golden Gate Bridge, the iPhone) and summarize the problem it solved.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 4, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Comparing worldviews* (60 min): Build a two-column comparison of the two texts' views of nature and community; vocabulary: build 10 cards from roots in the readings.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Origins: Native American and Puritan narratives
- Objective: Compare an Iroquois creation story with Bradford's account
- Objective: Summarize objectively and identify worldview
- Vocabulary: worldview, Puritan, plain style, providence
- Materials: The Iroquois Constitution excerpt / 'The World on the Turtle's Back', Bradford, Of Plymouth Plantation (Gutenberg), reading journal
- Assessment: Timed comparative paragraph scored on evidence and summary; vocabulary quiz.

### Algebra II: Parent functions and transformations
- Objective: Identify parent functions
- Objective: Apply vertical/horizontal shifts, stretches, and reflections
- Vocabulary: parent function, translation, reflection, vertical stretch, domain, range
- Materials: Desmos, graph paper
- Assessment: Transformations quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Describing motion
- Objective: Represent motion with position-time and velocity-time graphs
- Objective: Calculate velocity and acceleration
- Vocabulary: displacement, velocity, acceleration, vector, scalar
- Materials: toy car, meter stick, phone stopwatch/slow-motion video, masking tape
- Assessment: Motion graphs quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Enlightenment and the founding documents
- Objective: Explain Enlightenment roots of American government
- Objective: Analyze the Declaration and Constitution
- Vocabulary: natural rights, social contract, separation of powers, federalism
- Materials: Declaration of Independence and Constitution (archives.gov), Locke, Second Treatise excerpt
- Assessment: Constitution analysis paragraph.

### Spanish IV: ¿Quién soy? Repaso del presente y los tiempos pasados
- Objective: Describe oneself and one's background
- Objective: Review preterite vs. imperfect
- Vocabulary: la herencia, el orgullo, la identidad, los antepasados, el apodo
- Materials: SpanishDict conjugation tables, notebook
- Assessment: Narrative and grammar quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Building the ensemble
- Objective: Develop trust and focus
- Objective: Reflect in a performance journal
- Vocabulary: ensemble, focus, trust, neutral stance
- Materials: open space, performance journal
- Assessment: Tableau performance and journal.

### Lifetime Fitness: Baseline fitness testing
- Objective: Complete a health-related fitness assessment
- Objective: Record baseline data
- Vocabulary: Healthy Fitness Zone, aerobic capacity, muscular endurance, body composition
- Materials: measuring tape, stopwatch, sit-and-reach box (or yardstick and box), cones
- Assessment: Baseline fitness profile.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): What engineers do
- Objective: Describe engineering disciplines and careers
- Objective: Set up an engineering notebook
- Vocabulary: engineering, technology, engineering notebook, discipline
- Materials: bound grid notebook, pen
- Assessment: Notebook check and career reflection.
