## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Civil rights rhetoric
- Objective: Analyze rhetoric and reasoning in seminal civil rights texts
- Objective: Evaluate effectiveness of structure
- Vocabulary: nonviolent direct action, just/unjust law, rebuttal, kairos
- Materials: Martin Luther King Jr., 'Letter from Birmingham Jail', Malcolm X, 'The Ballot or the Bullet' excerpt, John F. Kennedy, Inaugural Address
- Assessment: Timed rhetorical analysis.

### Algebra II: Arithmetic and geometric sequences
- Objective: Write sequences recursively and explicitly
- Objective: Model with sequences
- Vocabulary: sequence, common difference, common ratio, recursive, explicit
- Materials: stacking cups or blocks
- Assessment: Sequences quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Stars and the elements
- Objective: Explain how stars produce elements
- Vocabulary: nucleosynthesis, main sequence, supernova, H-R diagram, red giant
- Materials: star data table, spectroscope (CD and box)
- Assessment: Presentation.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Nixon, Watergate, and the 1970s
- Objective: Explain the Watergate crisis
- Objective: Analyze 1970s economic and environmental policy
- Vocabulary: Watergate, impeachment, executive privilege, stagflation, EPA, détente
- Materials: Nixon resignation speech, U.S. v. Nixon excerpt, Earth Day 1970 photos
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish IV: El realismo mágico: García Márquez
- Objective: Analyze magical realism
- Objective: Narrate in the past with all past tenses
- Vocabulary: el realismo mágico, lo cotidiano, lo sobrenatural, la hipérbole
- Materials: Gabriel García Márquez, 'Un señor muy viejo con unas alas enormes' (library anthology)
- Assessment: Creative story and quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Devised theatre
- Objective: Collaboratively create a devised piece from source material
- Vocabulary: devising, verbatim theatre, Viewpoints, composition, found text
- Materials: interview recordings, newspaper archives or letters
- Assessment: Devised showing.

### Lifetime Fitness: Officiating and sport leadership
- Objective: Officiate a game
- Objective: Organize a community recreation event
- Vocabulary: official, hand signals, fair play, risk management, bracket
- Materials: whistle, rulebook (free online), bracket template
- Assessment: Event plan and officiating reflection.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Capstone: research
- Objective: Research existing solutions and relevant science
- Vocabulary: prior art, benchmarking, specification
- Materials: library databases, Google Patents
- Assessment: Research summary.
