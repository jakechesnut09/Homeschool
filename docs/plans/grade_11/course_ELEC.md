# Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE) (Grade 11)

A Career Technical Education course in the Engineering and Architecture sector (Engineering Design pathway). Students learn the engineering design process, technical sketching and dimensioning, precision measurement and statistics, and parametric 3D CAD modeling with free tools (Onshape for Education, Tinkercad). They reverse engineer household products, study materials and manufacturing, build and test prototypes, and finish with a semester-long capstone that solves a real problem for a real user, documented in an engineering notebook and digital portfolio.

*Framework:* California Career Technical Education Model Curriculum Standards – Engineering and Architecture Sector, Engineering Design Pathway (C) and CTE Anchor Standards  
*Time:* 55 min/day
*UC/CSU a–g:* g · *Credits:* 10.0

## Units

### Unit 1: The Engineering Design Process and Careers (weeks 1–3)
Engineers solve problems through an iterative, documented design process.

- Week 1: What engineers do
- Week 2: The design process
- Week 3: Teamwork and documentation

### Unit 2: Technical Sketching and Drawing (weeks 4–7)
Sketches and drawings are the universal language engineers use to communicate ideas precisely.

- Week 4: Freehand sketching fundamentals
- Week 5: Pictorial sketching: isometric and oblique
- Week 6: Multiview (orthographic) projection
- Week 7: Dimensioning technical drawings

### Unit 3: Measurement and Statistics (weeks 8–10)
Precise measurement and statistical analysis ensure parts fit and products are consistent.

- Week 8: Precision measurement
- Week 9: Statistics for quality
- Week 10: Applied geometry and unit review

### Unit 4: Computer-Aided Design (CAD) (weeks 11–15)
Parametric CAD lets engineers model, modify, and document designs quickly.

- Week 11: Introduction to CAD
- Week 12: CAD features
- Week 13: Modeling real parts
- Week 14: Assemblies
- Week 15: Working drawings

### Unit 5: Reverse Engineering (weeks 16–18)
Taking products apart reveals the design decisions behind them.

- Week 16: Reverse engineering: observation and disassembly
- Week 17: Reverse engineering: analysis and redesign
- Week 18: Semester review and exam

### Unit 6: Materials, Manufacturing, and Prototyping (weeks 19–22)
Material choice and manufacturing process determine a product's cost, performance, and sustainability.

- Week 19: Materials and their properties
- Week 20: Manufacturing processes
- Week 21: Prototyping
- Week 22: Testing and iteration

### Unit 7: User-Centered Design and Ergonomics (weeks 23–25)
Good design starts with the people who will use it.

- Week 23: Human factors and ergonomics
- Week 24: User research and empathy
- Week 25: Design communication and pitching

### Unit 8: Ethics, Sustainability, and Intellectual Property (weeks 26–27)
Engineers have responsibilities to the public, the environment, and other inventors.

- Week 26: Engineering ethics and safety
- Week 27: Sustainability and intellectual property

### Unit 9: Capstone Design Project (weeks 28–36)
A complete design cycle—from problem to tested prototype—shows mastery of engineering design.

- Week 28: Capstone: problem identification
- Week 29: Capstone: research
- Week 30: Capstone: ideation and selection
- Week 31: Capstone: CAD and drawings
- Week 32: Capstone: prototype build I
- Week 33: Capstone: prototype build II
- Week 34: Capstone: testing and iteration
- Week 35: Capstone: technical report and portfolio
- Week 36: Capstone presentation and course reflection

## Standards

- **CTE-ANCHOR-10.0** Technical Knowledge and Skills: apply essential technical knowledge and skills common to the sector, following procedures when carrying out experiments or tasks.
- **CTE-ANCHOR-11.0** Demonstration and Application: demonstrate and apply knowledge and skills in classroom, laboratory, and workplace settings.
- **CTE-ANCHOR-2.0** Communications: acquire and accurately use sector terminology and communicate in oral, written, and multimedia formats.
- **CTE-ANCHOR-5.0** Problem Solving and Critical Thinking: conduct short and sustained projects using critical and creative thinking, logical reasoning, and analysis.
- **CTE-ANCHOR-6.0** Health and Safety: demonstrate health and safety procedures, regulations, and personal health practices in the workplace.
- **CTE-ANCHOR-8.0** Ethics and Legal Responsibilities: practice professional, ethical, and legal behavior, including intellectual property.
- **CTE-ANCHOR-9.0** Leadership and Teamwork: work with peers to promote divergent and creative perspectives, effective leadership, and group dynamics.
- **CTE-EA-C1.0** Understand the history, impact, and careers of engineering design.
- **CTE-EA-C2.1** Use the engineering design process to identify problems, generate and evaluate solutions, and document work in an engineering notebook.
- **CTE-EA-C2.2** Define design problems with criteria and constraints and evaluate solutions with decision matrices.
- **CTE-EA-C3.1** Produce freehand technical sketches using isometric, oblique, and multiview (orthographic) projection.
- **CTE-EA-C3.2** Apply dimensioning standards and line conventions (ASME Y14.5 basics) to technical drawings.
- **CTE-EA-C4.1** Use precision measuring tools (rulers, calipers) and apply units, accuracy, precision, and tolerances.
- **CTE-EA-C4.2** Apply statistical analysis (mean, median, standard deviation) to measurement and quality data.
- **CTE-EA-C5.1** Create parametric 3D solid models of parts using computer-aided design (CAD) software.
- **CTE-EA-C5.2** Create CAD assemblies with constraints/mates and generate working drawings from 3D models.
- **CTE-EA-C6.1** Reverse engineer a product to analyze its function, structure, materials, and manufacturing processes.
- **CTE-EA-C7.1** Select appropriate materials based on mechanical properties, cost, and sustainability.
- **CTE-EA-C7.2** Describe manufacturing processes (additive, subtractive, forming) and build and test prototypes safely.
- **CTE-EA-C8.1** Apply human factors, ergonomics, and user-centered design to product development.
- **CTE-EA-C9.1** Communicate design solutions through technical reports, presentations, and portfolios.
