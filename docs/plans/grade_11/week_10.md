## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Dark Romanticism: Hawthorne and Melville
- Objective: Analyze allegory and symbol
- Objective: Compare Romantic and Dark Romantic views of human nature
- Vocabulary: allegory, symbol, ambiguity, Dark Romanticism
- Materials: Hawthorne, 'Young Goodman Brown' and 'The Minister's Black Veil', Melville, 'Bartleby, the Scrivener'
- Assessment: Timed comparative essay.

### Algebra II: Factoring and zeros of polynomials
- Objective: Factor higher-degree polynomials
- Objective: Find all zeros including complex
- Vocabulary: sum of cubes, rational root theorem, multiplicity, Fundamental Theorem of Algebra
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Zeros quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Conservation of energy
- Objective: Model energy transformations in closed systems
- Vocabulary: conservation of energy, system, mechanical energy, energy dissipation
- Materials: PhET Energy Skate Park, marble, foam pipe insulation track
- Assessment: Spreadsheet model and CER.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: World War I
- Objective: Explain U.S. entry into WWI
- Objective: Analyze home front effects
- Vocabulary: neutrality, Zimmermann Telegram, Espionage Act, Great Migration, Fourteen Points
- Materials: Zimmermann Telegram (DocsTeach), WWI propaganda posters, Schenck v. U.S. excerpt
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish IV: Inventos y ciencia hispana
- Objective: Learn about Hispanic scientists
- Objective: Use the conditional
- Vocabulary: el invento, el científico, investigar, descubrir, la vacuna
- Materials: articles on Severo Ochoa, Ellen Ochoa, Guillermo González Camarena
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Monologue rehearsal
- Objective: Refine monologue performance
- Vocabulary: blocking, focal point, emotional recall, moment before
- Materials: mirror, recording app
- Assessment: Rehearsal journal.

### Lifetime Fitness: Upper body and core strength
- Objective: Perform upper body and core exercises
- Vocabulary: deltoids, latissimus dorsi, core stability, anti-rotation
- Materials: dumbbells or jugs, resistance bands, mat
- Assessment: Workout log.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Applied geometry and unit review
- Objective: Calculate area, volume, mass, and density for parts
- Vocabulary: volume, surface area, density, mass, unit conversion
- Materials: kitchen scale, water and measuring cup, small parts
- Assessment: Density lab and Unit 3 test.
