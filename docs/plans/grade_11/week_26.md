## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Research: revision and presentation
- Objective: Revise for argument and style
- Objective: Present findings orally
- Vocabulary: revision, works cited, abstract, hanging indent
- Materials: word processor, slide software
- Assessment: Final research paper and presentation rubrics.

### Algebra II: Graphing sine and cosine
- Objective: Graph sinusoids with amplitude, period, midline, and phase shift
- Vocabulary: amplitude, period, midline, phase shift, frequency
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Sinusoid quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Natural hazards and human activity
- Objective: Explain how natural hazards influence human activity
- Vocabulary: earthquake, tsunami, liquefaction, magnitude, hazard mitigation
- Materials: USGS earthquake map, tray of sand and water (liquefaction)
- Assessment: Shake table test and Unit 6 test.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Civil rights legislation and voting rights
- Objective: Analyze the Civil Rights Act, Voting Rights Act, and 24th Amendment
- Vocabulary: Civil Rights Act of 1964, Voting Rights Act of 1965, 24th Amendment, Freedom Summer, Great Society
- Materials: Civil Rights Act of 1964 (DocsTeach), LBJ, 'We Shall Overcome' speech, Selma march photos
- Assessment: Short argument.

### Spanish IV: Unidad 6: debate formal
- Objective: Participate in a formal debate
- Objective: Use complex sentences across time frames
- Vocabulary: estar a favor de, estar en contra de, refutar, en mi opinión, no cabe duda de que
- Materials: debate rubric, sources from Unit 6
- Assessment: Debate rubric and Unit 6 test.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Production design concept
- Objective: Develop a unified design concept
- Vocabulary: production concept, design meeting, collaboration, unity
- Materials: design portfolio materials
- Assessment: Design portfolio and quiz.

### Lifetime Fitness: Self-defense basics
- Objective: Practice awareness, de-escalation, and basic self-defense techniques safely
- Vocabulary: situational awareness, de-escalation, fighting stance, palm strike, escape
- Materials: mat, pillow or kick pad
- Assessment: Technique demonstration and quiz.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Engineering ethics and safety
- Objective: Analyze engineering failures and ethical codes
- Vocabulary: code of ethics, public safety, whistleblower, failure analysis, factor of safety
- Materials: NSPE Code of Ethics (online), case study readings
- Assessment: Ethics case analysis.
