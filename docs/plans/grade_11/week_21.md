## Day 1 — Monday (school day 101, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Ch. 4–5* (60 min): Read; analyze Gatsby's invented biography and the reunion scene's use of weather.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Defining logs* (60 min): Convert between exponential and logarithmic form; evaluate log₂32 mentally.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *EM spectrum* (60 min): Build a scale model of the spectrum.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Korean War* (60 min): Map the phases of the Korean War and analyze Truman's firing of MacArthur.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Lectura* (50 min): Read an article on Spain's long workday and the myth of the siesta; answer 10 comprehension questions.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Teatro Campesino* (50 min): Learn about Luis Valdez and El Teatro Campesino's actos performed for striking farmworkers in Delano.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Map reading* (45 min): Learn map symbols and contour lines.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Prototype types* (55 min): Compare low- and high-fidelity prototypes using examples from IDEO and product teardown videos; review craft-knife and hot-glue safety.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 102, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Ch. 6–7* (60 min): Read; trace the confrontation at the Plaza and how money divides 'old' and 'new' wealth.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Common & natural logs* (60 min): Evaluate log and ln with a calculator; use the change-of-base formula.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *UV lab* (60 min): Test sunscreen and fabrics with UV beads.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Red Scare* (60 min): Read excerpts from McCarthy's Wheeling speech and Margaret Chase Smith's 'Declaration of Conscience'.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Si hipotético* (50 min): Learn si + imperfect subjunctive → conditional (Si tuviera más tiempo, viajaría) and complete 15 transformations; compare with English 'if I were'.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Acto* (50 min): Read and rehearse Las Dos Caras del Patroncito with masks and placards.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Compass bearings* (45 min): Learn to take and follow bearings.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Cardboard mock-up* (55 min): Build a full-size cardboard mock-up of a phone or tablet stand and test viewing angle with your device.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: The Great Gatsby, Ch. 4–9
- Objective: Analyze theme development
- Objective: Evaluate interpretations of the novel
- Vocabulary: American Dream, tragedy, nostalgia, class
- Materials: The Great Gatsby, film adaptation clip (1974 or 2013)
- Assessment: Literary argument essay on Gatsby.

### Algebra II: Logarithms and their graphs
- Objective: Define logarithms as inverses of exponentials
- Objective: Graph logarithmic functions
- Vocabulary: logarithm, common log, natural log, change of base
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Logarithm quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: The electromagnetic spectrum
- Objective: Describe EM waves
- Objective: Evaluate claims about EM radiation effects
- Vocabulary: electromagnetic spectrum, ionizing radiation, ultraviolet, infrared
- Materials: UV beads, sunscreen, plastic wrap
- Assessment: Claim evaluation report.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Hot wars and the Red Scare
- Objective: Analyze the Korean War and McCarthyism
- Objective: Explain nuclear deterrence
- Vocabulary: Korean War, McCarthyism, HUAC, arms race, brinkmanship, SEATO
- Materials: Margaret Chase Smith, 'Declaration of Conscience', Duck and Cover (1951) film, Hollywood Ten statements
- Assessment: Comparison essay.

### Spanish IV: El ocio y el bienestar
- Objective: Discuss leisure and work-life balance
- Objective: Use hypothetical si clauses
- Vocabulary: el ocio, la siesta, el equilibrio, el estrés, la jornada laboral
- Materials: Spanish-language article on the siesta and Spain's work schedule, survey form
- Assessment: Cultural comparison and quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Diverse American voices: Hansberry, Valdez, and Wilson
- Objective: Investigate cultural perspectives in American theatre
- Vocabulary: acto, El Teatro Campesino, Chicano theatre, Century Cycle, cultural context
- Materials: Lorraine Hansberry, A Raisin in the Sun excerpt, Luis Valdez, Las Dos Caras del Patroncito (acto), August Wilson, Fences excerpt (library)
- Assessment: Research talk.

### Lifetime Fitness: Orienteering and navigation
- Objective: Use a map and compass
- Vocabulary: orienteering, bearing, contour line, pace count, control point
- Materials: compass, printed park map, flags or markers
- Assessment: Navigation test.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Prototyping
- Objective: Build low- and high-fidelity prototypes
- Objective: Use tools safely
- Vocabulary: prototype, fidelity, mock-up, rapid prototyping
- Materials: cardboard, hot glue, foam board, craft knife, cutting mat, 3D printer at a library makerspace (if available)
- Assessment: Prototype comparison entry.
