## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: The Great Gatsby, Ch. 1–3
- Objective: Analyze narrator and setting
- Objective: Trace symbols of the American Dream
- Vocabulary: Jazz Age, narrator, symbol, conspicuous consumption
- Materials: F. Scott Fitzgerald, The Great Gatsby (Gutenberg/public domain)
- Assessment: Reading quiz and setting paragraph.

### Algebra II: Compound interest and e
- Objective: Model compound and continuous growth
- Objective: Transform exponential expressions
- Vocabulary: compound interest, continuous compounding, e, effective rate
- Materials: calculator, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Interest quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Sound and wave interactions
- Objective: Investigate reflection, refraction, interference, and resonance
- Vocabulary: interference, resonance, Doppler effect, standing wave
- Materials: glass bottles, water, tuning app, phone
- Assessment: Noise-canceling explanation.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Origins of the Cold War
- Objective: Explain containment and the origins of the Cold War
- Objective: Analyze postwar international institutions
- Vocabulary: containment, Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan, NATO, United Nations, iron curtain
- Materials: Churchill, 'Iron Curtain' speech, Truman Doctrine speech, Universal Declaration of Human Rights
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish IV: El mundo del trabajo
- Objective: Discuss careers and job searches
- Objective: Write a formal cover letter
- Vocabulary: el currículum, la entrevista, el sueldo, solicitar, la experiencia laboral
- Materials: sample Spanish CV and job ads
- Assessment: CV and cover letter.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Our Town and theatrical convention
- Objective: Analyze non-realistic staging
- Objective: Perform with minimal set
- Vocabulary: Stage Manager, presentational, representational, minimalism, pantomime
- Materials: Thornton Wilder, Our Town (library)
- Assessment: Performance and comparison.

### Lifetime Fitness: Hiking and trail safety
- Objective: Plan a safe day hike
- Objective: Apply Leave No Trace
- Vocabulary: Ten Essentials, Leave No Trace, elevation gain, trail etiquette
- Materials: daypack, water, layers, trail map app, California State Parks website
- Assessment: Trip report.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Manufacturing processes
- Objective: Describe additive, subtractive, and forming processes
- Vocabulary: additive manufacturing, subtractive manufacturing, injection molding, CNC, casting
- Materials: videos of manufacturing processes, library makerspace info
- Assessment: Manufacturing quiz.
