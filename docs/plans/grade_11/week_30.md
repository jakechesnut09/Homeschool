## Day 1 — Monday (school day 146, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Models* (60 min): Read two model personal essays (e.g., Amy Tan's 'Mother Tongue' as a model of voice); note structure and reflection.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Sigma notation* (60 min): Write and evaluate sums in sigma notation.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Parallax* (60 min): Measure parallax with thumb and objects; relate to stellar distances.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *New Right* (60 min): Read Reagan's First Inaugural ('government is the problem') and explain the rise of modern conservatism.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Lorca* (50 min): Read Act I of La casa de Bernarda Alba aloud by roles and discuss oppression and silence.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Casting & rehearsal* (50 min): Cast your ten-minute play with family/friends and rehearse it as a staged reading.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Macronutrients* (45 min): Learn roles of carbohydrate, protein, and fat; read five food labels; complete a 30-minute workout.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Brainstorm* (55 min): Generate at least 20 ideas using brainstorming and SCAMPER.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Personal narrative and the college essay
- Objective: Write a reflective personal narrative
- Objective: Use narrative techniques
- Vocabulary: reflection, anecdote, pacing, voice, personal insight
- Materials: UC Personal Insight Questions (universityofcalifornia.edu), model essays
- Assessment: Personal narrative rubric.

### Algebra II: Series and sigma notation
- Objective: Use sigma notation
- Objective: Derive and apply finite geometric series formula
- Vocabulary: series, sigma notation, partial sum, annuity
- Materials: spreadsheet
- Assessment: Unit 8 test.

### Physics of the Universe: Observing the night sky
- Objective: Use observations to understand the universe
- Vocabulary: constellation, light-year, parallax, magnitude
- Materials: star chart app, binoculars
- Assessment: Unit 7 test.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: The Reagan era and the end of the Cold War
- Objective: Analyze conservative resurgence
- Objective: Explain the end of the Cold War
- Vocabulary: New Right, Reaganomics, deregulation, glasnost, perestroika, nuclear freeze
- Materials: Reagan, 'Tear Down This Wall' speech, Reagan First Inaugural excerpt, Nuclear freeze rally photos
- Assessment: Quiz and debate rubric.

### Spanish IV: Teatro y cine
- Objective: Interpret drama and film
- Objective: Use reported speech
- Vocabulary: el guion, la escena, el personaje, dijo que, el estilo indirecto
- Materials: Federico García Lorca, La casa de Bernarda Alba Act I (library), a Spanish-language film (e.g., 'Coco' in Spanish or 'El laberinto del fauno' clips)
- Assessment: Film review and presentation.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: New work festival
- Objective: Present original work in a staged reading
- Objective: Lead a talkback
- Vocabulary: staged reading, talkback, Critical Response Process
- Materials: scripts in binders, music stands or chairs
- Assessment: Festival rubric and reflection.

### Lifetime Fitness: Nutrition for performance
- Objective: Plan meals to fuel activity
- Objective: Understand macronutrients and hydration
- Vocabulary: macronutrient, carbohydrate, protein, hydration, pre-workout meal, recovery
- Materials: food labels, MyPlate guidelines, food log app
- Assessment: Food-log analysis and quiz.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Capstone: ideation and selection
- Objective: Generate and select design concepts
- Vocabulary: ideation, SCAMPER, morphological chart, concept selection
- Materials: sketch paper, decision matrix template
- Assessment: Concept selection.
