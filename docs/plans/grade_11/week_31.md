## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: War and memory: The Things They Carried
- Objective: Analyze structure and truth in fiction
- Objective: Interpret metafiction
- Vocabulary: metafiction, story-truth, happening-truth, Vietnam War
- Materials: Tim O'Brien, The Things They Carried (library) – title story, 'On the Rainy River', 'How to Tell a True War Story'
- Assessment: Analytical response.

### Algebra II: Data collection: surveys, experiments, and observational studies
- Objective: Distinguish study types
- Objective: Explain role of randomization
- Vocabulary: population, sample, bias, random assignment, observational study, experiment
- Materials: random number generator, news articles citing studies
- Assessment: Study design quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Evidence for the Big Bang
- Objective: Construct an explanation of the Big Bang
- Vocabulary: redshift, Hubble's law, cosmic microwave background, expansion
- Materials: balloon and marker
- Assessment: Big Bang CER.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Immigration and a changing population
- Objective: Explain how immigration policy transformed American society
- Objective: Analyze U.S.–Mexico relations
- Vocabulary: Immigration Act of 1965, IRCA (1986), NAFTA, Sunbelt, demographic shift
- Materials: Immigration and Nationality Act of 1965 signing remarks, U.S. Census data tables, NAFTA overview
- Assessment: Oral history and explanation.

### Spanish IV: Unidad 7: ensayo literario
- Objective: Write a comparative literary essay in Spanish
- Vocabulary: el tema, el símbolo, la tesis, comparar
- Materials: unit texts
- Assessment: Literary essay and Unit 7 test.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: The director's role and play selection
- Objective: Explain the director's responsibilities
- Objective: Select and analyze a one-act play
- Vocabulary: director, prompt book, concept, royalties, casting
- Materials: public-domain one-acts (e.g., Glaspell's Trifles, Chekhov's The Proposal, Wilde excerpts), binder for prompt book
- Assessment: Prompt book check.

### Lifetime Fitness: Sleep, recovery, and stress
- Objective: Explain recovery and the role of sleep
- Objective: Use activity to manage stress
- Vocabulary: recovery, overtraining, sleep hygiene, cortisol, active recovery
- Materials: sleep log, mat
- Assessment: Recovery plan.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Capstone: CAD and drawings
- Objective: Model the final design in CAD
- Vocabulary: assembly, working drawing, bill of materials
- Materials: Onshape
- Assessment: Design review.
