## Day 1 — Monday (school day 26, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Common Sense & The Crisis* (60 min): Read excerpts and label appeals and parallel structure; note how Paine addresses a mass audience.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Defining i* (60 min): Explore why x²+1 = 0 has no real solutions; define i and simplify powers of i.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Falling objects* (60 min): Drop objects of different mass; measure g from timed drops.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *New immigrants* (60 min): Compare Ellis Island and Angel Island experiences using Angel Island wall poetry.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Celebraciones* (50 min): Watch short videos on Día de Muertos and Las Fallas and take notes.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Laban efforts* (50 min): Explore the eight Laban efforts (punch, float, glide, slash, etc.) through movement improvisation.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *HIIT science* (45 min): Learn aerobic vs. anaerobic energy systems; complete a 4-minute Tabata of squat jumps.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Glass box* (55 min): Place a block inside a clear box, trace each face, and unfold it to see how standard views align.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 27, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Henry's speech* (60 min): Read and deliver the final paragraph aloud; chart rhetorical questions, allusions, and their effect.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Operations* (60 min): Add, subtract, and multiply complex numbers; plot sums on the complex plane.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Inverse square law* (60 min): Model inverse square law by shining a flashlight on graph paper at increasing distances.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Tenement life* (60 min): Analyze Jacob Riis photographs and text.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Cláusulas adjetivales* (50 min): Practice subjunctive in adjective clauses (Busco una fiesta que tenga...).
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Mime* (50 min): Practice mime illusions (wall, rope pull, weight) and create a 1-minute mime scene.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Shuttle runs* (45 min): Complete 6×30-second shuttle runs with 90-second recovery.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Three views* (55 min): Sketch front, top, and right-side views of 6 block models, choosing the most descriptive front view.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Paine, Henry, and the Declaration
- Objective: Analyze rhetorical structure of founding documents
- Objective: Evaluate reasoning in seminal U.S. texts
- Vocabulary: self-evident, grievance, syllogism, rhetorical question, unalienable
- Materials: Paine, The Crisis No. 1 and Common Sense excerpt, Patrick Henry, Speech to the Virginia Convention, Declaration of Independence (archives.gov)
- Assessment: Timed rhetorical analysis paragraph.

### Algebra II: Complex numbers
- Objective: Define i and complex numbers
- Objective: Perform operations and solve quadratics with complex solutions
- Vocabulary: imaginary unit, complex number, complex conjugate, complex plane
- Materials: graph paper for the complex plane
- Assessment: Complex numbers quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Newton's law of universal gravitation
- Objective: Use mathematical representations of gravitation
- Vocabulary: gravitational force, inverse square law, gravitational constant, weight
- Materials: PhET Gravity Force Lab, flashlight and graph paper (inverse square)
- Assessment: Gravitation quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Immigration and urbanization
- Objective: Explain the new immigration and city life
- Objective: Analyze political machines and nativism
- Vocabulary: new immigrants, Americanization, tenement, political machine, nativism, Chinese Exclusion Act
- Materials: Jacob Riis, How the Other Half Lives photos, Angel Island poems (Calisphere), Thomas Nast cartoons
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Spanish IV: Tradiciones y celebraciones
- Objective: Describe celebrations
- Objective: Use the subjunctive in adjective clauses
- Vocabulary: la quinceañera, el Día de Muertos, la Semana Santa, la ofrenda, celebrar
- Materials: videos on Día de Muertos and Las Fallas, paper, marigolds (optional)
- Assessment: Presentation rubric.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Movement and physical theatre
- Objective: Use Laban efforts and physical storytelling
- Vocabulary: Laban efforts, weight, time, flow, mime, physical theatre
- Materials: open space, music playlist
- Assessment: Physical theatre piece.

### Lifetime Fitness: Interval and HIIT training
- Objective: Compare continuous and interval training
- Vocabulary: HIIT, Tabata, work-to-rest ratio, anaerobic
- Materials: timer app, cones
- Assessment: HIIT design.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Multiview (orthographic) projection
- Objective: Create multiview drawings with correct alignment and line types
- Vocabulary: orthographic projection, front view, top view, right-side view, hidden line, center line
- Materials: grid paper, clear plastic box or shoebox (glass-box model), blocks
- Assessment: Multiview quiz.
