## Day 1 — Monday (school day 86, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Timeline review* (60 min): Build a literary-period timeline (Puritan to Realism) with two texts, traits, and a key quotation per period.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Review: functions & quadratics* (60 min): Mixed review on transformations, systems, quadratics, and complex numbers.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Review: forces* (60 min): Review Newton's laws, momentum, and gravitation problems.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Timeline review* (60 min): Build an illustrated timeline from 1877 to 1945 with 25 events and cause-effect arrows.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Repaso de gramática* (50 min): Complete a mixed grammar review on subjunctive, perfect tenses, and passive voice.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Final rehearsal* (50 min): Run the full show once at performance pace, including the curtain call.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Aerobic retest* (45 min): Retest the 1-mile run/walk and record resting heart rate.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Review: sketching* (55 min): Mixed practice on isometric and multiview sketching.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 87, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Practice passages* (60 min): Complete a practice set of unseen prose and poetry passages with multiple choice and short response.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Review: polynomials & rationals* (60 min): Mixed review on polynomial zeros, graphs, and rational functions.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Review: energy* (60 min): Review energy conservation and thermal transfer.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Document skills* (60 min): Practice sourcing, contextualizing, and corroborating with a 5-document set on women's suffrage.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Lectura y audio* (50 min): Practice an authentic reading and a listening passage with multiple-choice questions.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Showcase* (50 min): Perform the winter showcase for invited family and friends and record it.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Muscular retest* (45 min): Retest push-ups, curl-ups, and trunk lift.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Review: measurement* (55 min): Practice measurement and statistics problems.
