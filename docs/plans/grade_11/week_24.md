## Day 1 — Monday (school day 116, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Choose a topic* (60 min): Brainstorm topics linking an American author to historical context; narrow to an arguable research question.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Radians* (60 min): Wrap string around a paper plate to construct one radian; convert between degrees and radians.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Earth layers* (60 min): Model Earth's layers with a hard-boiled egg; compare to seismic data.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Jim Crow* (60 min): Analyze Plessy v. Ferguson and Harlan's dissent alongside Jim Crow photographs.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Datos* (50 min): Interpret graphs of California reservoir levels and Mexico City water supply with Spanish questions.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Light qualities* (50 min): Experiment with intensity, color, and angle using flashlights and cellophane on a face.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Rhythm* (45 min): Count music in eights at different tempos and learn grapevine, step-touch, box step, and V-step.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Empathy* (55 min): Learn the design thinking empathize stage and watch a user-research example video.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Research: question, sources, and evaluation
- Objective: Develop a research question
- Objective: Evaluate source credibility
- Vocabulary: research question, primary source, secondary source, credibility, annotated bibliography, database
- Materials: public library databases (e.g., JSTOR free articles, Gale via library card), Purdue OWL MLA guide
- Assessment: Annotated bibliography rubric.

### Algebra II: Radian measure and the unit circle
- Objective: Measure angles in radians
- Objective: Use the unit circle to evaluate trig functions
- Vocabulary: radian, unit circle, reference angle, coterminal, arc length
- Materials: paper plate, string, protractor
- Assessment: Unit circle quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Earth's interior and convection
- Objective: Model Earth's interior and mantle convection
- Vocabulary: crust, mantle, core, convection, seismic wave
- Materials: pot of water, food coloring, oatmeal flakes, hard-boiled egg
- Assessment: Convection model rubric.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Roots of the civil rights movement
- Objective: Trace legal strategy to end segregation
- Objective: Analyze landmark cases
- Vocabulary: Jim Crow, Plessy v. Ferguson, NAACP Legal Defense Fund, Brown v. Board of Education, Mendez v. Westminster
- Materials: Plessy and Brown opinions (excerpts), Mendez v. Westminster (1947) summary, Executive Order 9981
- Assessment: Paragraph.

### Spanish IV: Agua y sequía: California y América Latina
- Objective: Compare water challenges
- Objective: Interpret data in Spanish
- Vocabulary: el acuífero, el embalse, el riego, escasear, la conservación
- Materials: charts of reservoir levels, article on water crises in Mexico City or Chile
- Assessment: Recorded cultural comparison.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Lighting and sound design
- Objective: Use light and sound to create mood
- Vocabulary: intensity, color temperature, cue, sound effect, underscore
- Materials: flashlights, colored cellophane, desk lamps, free sound libraries
- Assessment: Cue run.

### Lifetime Fitness: Dance fitness
- Objective: Count music and perform rhythmic patterns
- Objective: Create cardio dance choreography
- Vocabulary: tempo, eight-count, choreography, cardio dance
- Materials: music playlist, free dance-fitness videos
- Assessment: Choreography performance.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): User research and empathy
- Objective: Conduct user interviews
- Objective: Define needs with personas
- Vocabulary: empathy, user interview, persona, needs statement, journey map
- Materials: interview guide, sticky notes
- Assessment: Persona and needs statements.
