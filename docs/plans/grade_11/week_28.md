## Day 1 — Monday (school day 136, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Act III* (60 min): Read Act III; trace Walter's transformation and what 'come into his manhood' means.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Ferris wheel* (60 min): Write a sinusoidal model for a rider's height on a Ferris wheel from given dimensions.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Isotopes* (60 min): Review isotopes and nuclear notation.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Bakke* (60 min): Read excerpts from Justice Powell's opinion in Regents of the University of California v. Bakke and chart what the Court allowed and prohibited.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Martí* (50 min): Read Versos sencillos I and 'Cultivo una rosa blanca'; identify rhyme and meter; listen to 'Guantanamera'.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Idea* (50 min): Brainstorm 10 premises from personal, family, or community stories; pick one with a clear conflict and a single setting.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Throws* (45 min): Practice backhand and forehand disc throws for accuracy at 10 and 20 yards.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Problem hunting* (55 min): List 15 problems observed at home or in the community and select three candidates.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 137, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Compare productions* (60 min): Watch the 1961 film's final scene and compare it to the script's stage directions.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Daylight data* (60 min): Collect monthly daylight hours for your California city and fit a sinusoidal model.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Decay* (60 min): Model alpha, beta, and gamma decay.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Prop 209* (60 min): Read the 1996 Proposition 209 ballot arguments for and against and summarize each side's strongest evidence.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Darío* (50 min): Read 'Sonatina' and identify modernista imagery and musicality.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Outline* (50 min): Outline the play in 6–8 beats with the turning point placed around minute 7.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Cutting* (45 min): Learn cuts and creating space; practice give-and-go with a partner.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *User interviews* (55 min): Interview at least two potential users for each of the top candidates.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: A Raisin in the Sun, Act III and performance
- Objective: Analyze resolution and theme
- Objective: Evaluate interpretations of drama
- Vocabulary: climax, dignity, interpretation, staging
- Materials: A Raisin in the Sun, 1961 film clip
- Assessment: Theme essay and play test.

### Algebra II: Modeling periodic phenomena
- Objective: Model real periodic data with sinusoids
- Vocabulary: periodic, sinusoidal regression, tide, daylight hours
- Materials: NOAA tide data or daylight-hours table for your city, Desmos
- Assessment: Daylight model report and Unit 7 test.

### Physics of the Universe: Nuclear processes
- Objective: Model fission, fusion, and radioactive decay
- Vocabulary: isotope, half-life, fission, fusion, alpha decay, beta decay
- Materials: pennies or M&Ms for half-life, PhET Nuclear Fission
- Assessment: Half-life lab and quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Civil rights DBQ and affirmative action
- Objective: Evaluate later civil rights issues
- Objective: Write a DBQ
- Vocabulary: affirmative action, Regents v. Bakke, Proposition 209, busing
- Materials: Regents of the University of California v. Bakke excerpt, Prop 209 ballot text
- Assessment: DBQ and Unit 8 test.

### Spanish IV: Poesía: Martí, Darío y Mistral
- Objective: Read and interpret poetry
- Objective: Recite a poem
- Vocabulary: el verso, la estrofa, la rima, la metáfora, el modernismo
- Materials: José Martí, Versos sencillos (I and XXXIX), Rubén Darío, 'Sonatina', Gabriela Mistral, 'Los que no danzan' (library)
- Assessment: Recitation and explication.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Writing a ten-minute play
- Objective: Write and revise a complete ten-minute play
- Vocabulary: ten-minute play, table read, revision, dramaturg
- Materials: play-format template, recording app
- Assessment: Ten-minute play second draft.

### Lifetime Fitness: Ultimate and invasion-game strategy
- Objective: Apply offensive and defensive strategies in invasion games
- Vocabulary: spirit of the game, stack, person-to-person defense, zone defense, give-and-go
- Materials: flying disc, cones
- Assessment: Strategy analysis.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Capstone: problem identification
- Objective: Identify a real problem and user
- Objective: Write a design brief
- Vocabulary: problem statement, design brief, stakeholder, scope
- Materials: engineering notebook, interview guide
- Assessment: Design brief and Gantt chart.
