## Day 1 — Monday (school day 6, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Bradstreet* (60 min): Read two Bradstreet poems aloud; annotate the extended metaphors and identify the conflict between earthly and heavenly love.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Piecewise graphs* (60 min): Graph piecewise functions by hand with open/closed endpoints; evaluate at boundary values.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Inertia* (60 min): Perform coin-card-cup and tablecloth inertia demos; explain using Newton's first law.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Great Awakenings* (60 min): Read about the First and Second Great Awakenings and their ties to abolition and reform.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Video* (50 min): Watch a TED en Español talk on bilingualism and take notes on three main ideas.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Yes, and* (50 min): Practice 'yes, and' vs. 'yes, but' dialogues and identify blocking offers.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *SMART goals* (45 min): Write two SMART goals based on baseline data; do a 20-minute circuit (jump rope, squats, planks).
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Design process model* (55 min): Learn the steps (define, research, ideate, select, prototype, test, iterate, communicate) and diagram them in the notebook.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 7, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Edwards' sermon* (60 min): Read the first half of 'Sinners'; highlight imagery of spiders, fire, and flood and label each as an appeal.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Step functions* (60 min): Model USPS postage or parking-garage prices with a step function; graph and interpret.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Free-body diagrams* (60 min): Draw free-body diagrams for 8 scenarios including a car braking and a book on a table.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Religious liberty* (60 min): Analyze Jefferson's Danbury letter and the Virginia Statute for Religious Freedom.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Subjuntivo presente* (50 min): Review present subjunctive formation and use with emotion (me alegra que, es triste que).
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *CROW* (50 min): Improvise 2-minute scenes establishing character, relationship, objective, and where within the first 30 seconds.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *FITT* (45 min): Learn frequency, intensity, time, type; design a FITT plan for one goal and walk/jog 25 minutes.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Design brief* (55 min): Write a design brief for a paper bridge spanning 20 cm with limited materials.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 8, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Rhetorical appeals* (60 min): Finish 'Sinners'; complete a SOAPSTone chart and explain why fear was an effective strategy for his audience.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Absolute value equations* (60 min): Solve |2x−5| = 9 type equations and check for extraneous answers.

- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Conversación* (50 min): Hold a 10-minute conversation with a partner/tutor on the advantages of speaking two languages.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Status* (50 min): Explore high- and low-status physical and vocal choices in short scenes.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Training principles* (45 min): Learn overload, progression, specificity, and reversibility; complete an interval walk/jog.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Brainstorm* (55 min): Sketch five bridge ideas in 15 minutes and select one with a decision matrix.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Puritan poetry and sermon
- Objective: Analyze figurative language and tone in Bradstreet and Edwards
- Objective: Identify rhetorical appeals
- Vocabulary: conceit, imagery, ethos, pathos, logos, sermon
- Materials: Bradstreet, 'To My Dear and Loving Husband', 'Upon the Burning of Our House', Edwards, 'Sinners in the Hands of an Angry God'
- Assessment: SOAPSTone chart and discussion rubric (evidence, building on ideas).

### Algebra II: Piecewise and absolute value functions
- Objective: Graph piecewise and step functions
- Objective: Solve absolute value equations and inequalities
- Vocabulary: piecewise function, step function, greatest integer, absolute value inequality
- Materials: Desmos, postal rate or tax bracket tables
- Assessment: Quiz on piecewise and absolute value.

### Physics of the Universe: Newton's first and second laws
- Objective: Analyze data relating net force, mass, and acceleration
- Objective: Draw free-body diagrams
- Vocabulary: inertia, net force, newton, free-body diagram, mass
- Materials: toy car, rubber bands, washers as added mass, PhET Forces and Motion: Basics
- Assessment: CER lab report.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Religion, rights, and democratization
- Objective: Explain religious liberty principles
- Objective: Trace expansion of democracy before 1860
- Vocabulary: Establishment Clause, Free Exercise Clause, Great Awakening, Jacksonian democracy
- Materials: Jefferson's letter to the Danbury Baptists, Virginia Statute for Religious Freedom
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish IV: Lengua e identidad: el bilingüismo
- Objective: Discuss bilingualism and identity
- Objective: Use the present subjunctive with expressions of emotion and opinion
- Vocabulary: el bilingüismo, el spanglish, la lengua materna, sentirse orgulloso, pertenecer
- Materials: Gloria Anzaldúa or TED en Español talk on bilingualism (video), subjunctive chart
- Assessment: Opinion paragraph rubric.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Improvisation fundamentals
- Objective: Apply 'yes, and' and CROW (character, relationship, objective, where)
- Vocabulary: improvisation, yes, and, offer, block, CROW
- Materials: timer, suggestion cards
- Assessment: Improvisation rubric.

### Lifetime Fitness: SMART goals and the FITT principle
- Objective: Write SMART fitness goals
- Objective: Apply FITT to plan workouts
- Vocabulary: SMART goal, FITT, overload, progression, specificity
- Materials: fitness journal, jump rope
- Assessment: Goal sheet and FITT plan.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): The design process
- Objective: Apply the design process steps
- Objective: Define criteria and constraints
- Vocabulary: design brief, criteria, constraints, iteration, decision matrix
- Materials: paper, tape, scissors, pennies
- Assessment: Paper bridge challenge.
