# Spanish IV (Grade 11)

Spanish IV moves students toward Intermediate Mid–High proficiency through thematic units modeled on the AP Spanish Language and Culture themes: personal and public identities, families and communities, science and technology, beauty and aesthetics, contemporary life, global challenges, and Hispanic literature. Students read and listen to authentic sources daily, hold sustained conversations, write formal essays and emails, and give presentations, while mastering advanced grammar (imperfect subjunctive, si clauses, perfect tenses, passive voice). Spanish is the language of instruction.

*Framework:* 2019 California World Languages Standards (Intermediate Mid target); 2020 California World Languages Framework  
*Time:* 50 min/day
*UC/CSU a–g:* e · *Credits:* 10.0

## Units

### Unit 1: Identidades personales y públicas (weeks 1–4)
Our identities are shaped by language, heritage, and the communities we belong to.

- Week 1: ¿Quién soy? Repaso del presente y los tiempos pasados
- Week 2: Lengua e identidad: el bilingüismo
- Week 3: Héroes y personajes públicos
- Week 4: Ensayo: la identidad

### Unit 2: Familias y comunidades (weeks 5–8)
Family structures and community traditions differ across the Spanish-speaking world but serve similar human needs.

- Week 5: La familia en el mundo hispano
- Week 6: Tradiciones y celebraciones
- Week 7: Comunidad y ciudadanía
- Week 8: Unidad 2: conversación y evaluación

### Unit 3: Ciencia y tecnología (weeks 9–12)
Technology changes how we live and communicate, raising new ethical questions.

- Week 9: La tecnología en la vida diaria
- Week 10: Inventos y ciencia hispana
- Week 11: Ética y tecnología: la inteligencia artificial
- Week 12: Unidad 3: ensayo argumentativo

### Unit 4: Belleza y estética (weeks 13–18)
Art, architecture, and music express a culture's values and history.

- Week 13: El arte visual: Velázquez, Goya y Picasso
- Week 14: Arte latinoamericano: los muralistas y Frida Kahlo
- Week 15: Música y baile
- Week 16: La arquitectura y el patrimonio
- Week 17: Ideales de belleza y la moda
- Week 18: Repaso del semestre

### Unit 5: La vida contemporánea: educación y trabajo (weeks 19–22)
Education, work, and leisure reflect each society's priorities and opportunities.

- Week 19: El sistema educativo
- Week 20: El mundo del trabajo
- Week 21: El ocio y el bienestar
- Week 22: Unidad 5: presentación profesional

### Unit 6: Desafíos mundiales: el medio ambiente y la salud (weeks 23–26)
Environmental and health challenges require global cooperation and local action.

- Week 23: El medio ambiente
- Week 24: Agua y sequía: California y América Latina
- Week 25: La salud pública
- Week 26: Unidad 6: debate formal

### Unit 7: Literatura hispana (weeks 27–31)
Literature lets us enter the perspectives of writers across the Spanish-speaking world.

- Week 27: El cuento hispanoamericano: Quiroga y Borges
- Week 28: Poesía: Martí, Darío y Mistral
- Week 29: El realismo mágico: García Márquez
- Week 30: Teatro y cine
- Week 31: Unidad 7: ensayo literario

### Unit 8: Comunidades hispanohablantes en EE. UU. y California (weeks 32–33)
Spanish-speaking communities have shaped California's history, culture, and future.

- Week 32: Historia hispana de California
- Week 33: Comunidades hoy: entrevista y comunidad

### Unit 9: Proyecto final y examen (weeks 34–36)
Proficiency is shown by communicating with confidence across all modes.

- Week 34: Proyecto final: documental o podcast
- Week 35: Proyecto final: presentación
- Week 36: Examen final

## Standards

- **WL-COMM1-IM** Interpretive communication (Intermediate Mid): understand main ideas and many details in authentic texts, audio, and video on familiar and some unfamiliar topics.
- **WL-COMM2-IM** Interpersonal communication (Intermediate Mid): sustain spontaneous conversations and written exchanges, asking and answering questions on a range of topics.
- **WL-COMM3-IM** Presentational communication (Intermediate Mid): present information, narrate, and describe in connected sentences and paragraphs across time frames.
- **WL-COMM4-IM** Settings for communication: use the language in real-world settings beyond the classroom, including digital communities.
- **WL-COMM5-IM** Receptivity: recognize and use culturally appropriate gestures, register (tú/usted), and strategies to negotiate meaning.
- **WL-COMM6-IM** Language comparisons: compare linguistic structures (e.g., subjunctive, aspect) of Spanish and English to deepen understanding of both.
- **WL-CONN1-IM** Connections: build, reinforce, and expand knowledge of other disciplines (history, science, art, literature) through Spanish.
- **WL-CULT1-IM** Cultures: investigate, explain, and reflect on the relationship between practices, products, and perspectives of Spanish-speaking cultures.
- **WL-CULT2-IM** Cultural comparisons: compare products, practices, and perspectives of Spanish-speaking cultures with one's own.
