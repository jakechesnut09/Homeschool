## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Argument essay: the American promise
- Objective: Write an evidence-based argument synthesizing multiple texts
- Vocabulary: synthesis, warrant, concession, rebuttal
- Materials: Unit 4 texts, essay planning template
- Assessment: Argument essay and Unit 4 test.

### Algebra II: Rational equations and applications
- Objective: Solve rational equations and identify extraneous solutions
- Objective: Model work and rate problems
- Vocabulary: extraneous solution, work rate, cross-multiplication
- Materials: practice set
- Assessment: Unit 4 test.

### Physics of the Universe: Magnetism and electromagnetism
- Objective: Show electric current produces a magnetic field
- Vocabulary: magnetic field, electromagnet, solenoid, Oersted
- Materials: iron nail, insulated wire, D battery, paper clips, compass
- Assessment: Electromagnet lab report.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: The New Deal
- Objective: Evaluate the New Deal
- Vocabulary: New Deal, alphabet agencies, Social Security, Wagner Act, court-packing
- Materials: FDR's First Inaugural and fireside chats, WPA posters
- Assessment: DBQ.

### Spanish IV: Música y baile
- Objective: Describe musical traditions and their origins
- Objective: Use the subjunctive in adverbial clauses
- Vocabulary: el ritmo, la letra, el flamenco, la cumbia, el son, para que, antes de que, con tal de que
- Materials: Spotify/YouTube playlist: flamenco, tango, cumbia, son cubano, salsa, song lyrics
- Assessment: Recorded presentation.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Shakespeare scene work
- Objective: Perform a Shakespeare scene with a partner
- Objective: Compare filmed interpretations
- Vocabulary: blocking, comic timing, aside, soliloquy
- Materials: Folger text of A Midsummer Night's Dream, two filmed versions (library/streaming)
- Assessment: Scene performance and history test.

### Lifetime Fitness: Mind-body fitness: Pilates and balance
- Objective: Build core control and balance
- Vocabulary: Pilates, balance, proprioception, neutral spine
- Materials: mat, pillow (balance)
- Assessment: Quiz and flexibility log.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Working drawings
- Objective: Generate working drawings from CAD models
- Vocabulary: working drawing, title block, section view, exploded view, bill of materials
- Materials: Onshape drawings tool
- Assessment: CAD practical.
