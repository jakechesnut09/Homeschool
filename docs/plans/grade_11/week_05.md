## Day 1 — Monday (school day 21, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Autobiography* (60 min): Read Franklin's 'project of arriving at moral perfection'; list his 13 virtues and mark his tone toward himself.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Factoring review* (60 min): Factor trinomials with a ≠ 1, difference of squares, and GCF; solve by zero-product property.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Define the problem* (60 min): List criteria and constraints for an egg-in-cart crash device (limited materials, mass limit).
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Working conditions* (60 min): Read The Jungle excerpt and Lewis Hine child labor photos; record working conditions.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Vocabulario* (50 min): Learn 25 terms for family relationships and roles; build a family tree with descriptions.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Breath* (50 min): Practice diaphragmatic breathing lying down with a book on the abdomen; sustain an 's' sound for time.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Run form* (45 min): Learn running form (posture, arm swing, cadence ~170 steps/min) with drills.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Isometric axes* (55 min): Learn the 30° isometric axes and sketch three stacked-block shapes on isometric grid paper.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 22, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Aphorisms* (60 min): Read 20 Poor Richard sayings; rewrite five in modern English and explain the reasoning each assumes.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Square roots & completing the square* (60 min): Solve equations by taking square roots and completing the square.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Design* (60 min): Sketch three designs and choose one using a decision matrix.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Unions & strikes* (60 min): Compare the Knights of Labor and AFL; study the Pullman and Homestead strikes.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Pretérito perfecto* (50 min): Learn present perfect (he vivido, ha cambiado) and describe how your family has changed.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Resonance* (50 min): Explore chest, mask, and head resonance with humming and sirens.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Base run* (45 min): Complete a 30-minute easy run or brisk walk at conversational pace.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Isometric practice* (55 min): Sketch 8 objects (stapler, phone stand, LEGO builds) isometrically, starting from a bounding box.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 23, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Satire* (60 min): Read 'Remarks Concerning the Savages of North America'; identify verbal irony and its target.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Quadratic formula* (60 min): Derive the quadratic formula by completing the square on ax²+bx+c = 0; solve 8 equations.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Build & test* (60 min): Build a prototype and test it in a ramp crash; record results.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Farmers' grievances* (60 min): Analyze the Omaha Platform's demands.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Datos* (50 min): Analyze a chart of household sizes in Spain, Mexico, and the U.S.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Articulation* (50 min): Drill 10 tongue twisters at increasing speeds and record before-and-after clips.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Cross-training* (45 min): Cycle, swim, or row 30 minutes at moderate intensity.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Isometric circles* (55 min): Use the four-center method to sketch isometric ellipses, then sketch a mug and a cylinder with a hole.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Franklin and the Enlightenment self
- Objective: Analyze Franklin's voice and purpose
- Objective: Evaluate satire and self-presentation
- Vocabulary: aphorism, Enlightenment, satire, rationalism
- Materials: Franklin, Autobiography excerpt (thirteen virtues) and Poor Richard's aphorisms (Gutenberg)
- Assessment: Quiz and reflective explanatory piece.

### Algebra II: Solving quadratic equations
- Objective: Solve by factoring, square roots, completing the square, and the quadratic formula
- Objective: Use the discriminant
- Vocabulary: quadratic formula, discriminant, zero product property, root
- Materials: index cards, Desmos
- Assessment: Method-choice assessment.

### Physics of the Universe: Engineering challenge: egg crash protection
- Objective: Design and refine a collision-protection device
- Vocabulary: criteria, constraint, prototype, iteration, crumple zone
- Materials: egg, toy car or cart, straws, paper, tape, rubber bands
- Assessment: Design report and Unit 1 test.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Labor and the Populists
- Objective: Explain labor conditions and responses
- Objective: Evaluate the Populist movement
- Vocabulary: union, strike, collective bargaining, Populist, free silver
- Materials: Sinclair, The Jungle excerpt, Omaha Platform (1892), Bryan, 'Cross of Gold' excerpt
- Assessment: Paragraph.

### Spanish IV: La familia en el mundo hispano
- Objective: Compare family structures
- Objective: Use the present perfect
- Vocabulary: la familia extendida, el compadrazgo, convivir, criar, el hogar
- Materials: INE (Spain) or Census family data chart, Spanish-language article on extended families
- Assessment: Comparison writing.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Voice: breath, resonance, and articulation
- Objective: Develop breath support and articulation
- Vocabulary: diaphragm, resonance, articulation, projection, tongue twister
- Materials: yoga mat, mirror, recording app
- Assessment: Vocal recording comparison.

### Lifetime Fitness: Building an aerobic base
- Objective: Apply progression to aerobic training
- Vocabulary: aerobic base, long slow distance, pace, cadence
- Materials: running shoes, route map app
- Assessment: Training log review.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Pictorial sketching: isometric and oblique
- Objective: Create isometric and oblique sketches of real objects
- Vocabulary: isometric, oblique, receding axis, pictorial, ellipse
- Materials: isometric grid paper, wooden blocks or LEGO, coffee mug
- Assessment: Pictorial sketch quiz.
