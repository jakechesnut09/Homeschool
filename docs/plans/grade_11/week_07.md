## Day 1 — Monday (school day 31, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Wheatley* (60 min): Read Wheatley's poem and Occom letter; analyze how she uses her audience's own beliefs to argue for freedom.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Projectile lab* (60 min): Toss a ball, video it, record height vs. time points, and fit a quadratic regression in Desmos.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Ellipses* (60 min): Draw ellipses with string and pins; calculate eccentricity.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Muckrakers* (60 min): Read Tarbell or Steffens excerpts and identify targets.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Lectura* (50 min): Read a Spanish-language page from a local nonprofit and summarize its mission.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Pitch & inflection* (50 min): Read a neutral sentence ten ways to convey different subtexts.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Pacing* (45 min): Plan a pacing strategy for a 5K based on training data.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Dimensioning rules* (55 min): Learn line types and ASME basics (no double dimensions, dimension features in their most descriptive view).

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 32, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Abigail Adams* (60 min): Read 'Remember the Ladies'; identify tone shifts and the argument beneath the playful diction.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Interpreting the model* (60 min): Use the regression to find max height and landing time; compare to h = −16t² + v₀t + h₀.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Kepler's laws* (60 min): Analyze planet data to verify Kepler's third law (T² ∝ a³).
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Federal reform* (60 min): Chart reforms of Roosevelt, Taft, and Wilson (Pure Food and Drug Act, Federal Reserve, 16th–17th Amendments).
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Mandatos* (50 min): Review formal and informal commands and write a volunteer instruction guide.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Dialect awareness* (50 min): Listen to the International Dialects of English Archive samples and discuss respectful use of dialect.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Easy run* (45 min): Do a 20-minute shake-out run plus strides.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Practice* (55 min): Fully dimension multiview drawings of three block parts.

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Rhetorical analysis essay: voices excluded and included
- Objective: Write a full rhetorical analysis essay
- Objective: Analyze Wheatley and Adams as counter-voices
- Vocabulary: rhetorical situation, exigence, audience, diction
- Materials: Phillis Wheatley, 'On Being Brought from Africa to America' and letter to Samson Occom, Abigail Adams, 'Remember the Ladies' letter
- Assessment: Rhetorical analysis essay and Unit 2 test.

### Algebra II: Quadratic modeling and linear-quadratic systems
- Objective: Model projectile and area problems
- Objective: Solve linear-quadratic systems
- Vocabulary: projectile motion, regression, intersection
- Materials: tennis ball, stopwatch, phone video, Desmos
- Assessment: Projectile lab report and Unit 2 test.

### Physics of the Universe: Orbits and Kepler's laws
- Objective: Predict orbital motion with Kepler's laws
- Vocabulary: orbit, ellipse, eccentricity, period, Kepler's laws
- Materials: string, two pushpins, cardboard, PhET Gravity and Orbits
- Assessment: Kepler data analysis.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: The Progressive Era
- Objective: Analyze Progressive reforms
- Vocabulary: muckraker, initiative, referendum, recall, trust-busting, Hiram Johnson
- Materials: Ida Tarbell excerpt, Meat Inspection Act, California 1911 reforms
- Assessment: Quiz.

### Spanish IV: Comunidad y ciudadanía
- Objective: Discuss volunteerism and community
- Objective: Give formal commands
- Vocabulary: el voluntariado, la ciudadanía, colaborar, la ONG, donar
- Materials: Spanish-language website of a local community organization
- Assessment: Formal email and quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Voice and body together: dialects and choral speaking
- Objective: Integrate voice and movement
- Objective: Explore choral speaking
- Vocabulary: dialect, choral speaking, pace, pitch, inflection
- Materials: recording app, choral text (e.g., Whitman excerpt or Greek chorus ode)
- Assessment: Choral performance.

### Lifetime Fitness: Cardio assessment: 5K challenge
- Objective: Complete a timed endurance event
- Objective: Evaluate progress
- Vocabulary: pacing strategy, negative split, taper
- Materials: measured 5K route
- Assessment: 5K time and reflection.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Dimensioning technical drawings
- Objective: Apply ASME dimensioning rules
- Objective: Specify tolerances
- Vocabulary: dimension line, extension line, leader, tolerance, datum, chain vs. baseline dimensioning
- Materials: ruler, grid paper, sample part (e.g., a LEGO brick or bracket)
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.
