## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Romantic fiction: Irving and Poe
- Objective: Identify characteristics of Romanticism and Gothic fiction
- Objective: Analyze how structure creates suspense
- Vocabulary: Romanticism, Gothic, unreliable narrator, atmosphere, foreshadowing
- Materials: Irving, 'The Devil and Tom Walker', Poe, 'The Fall of the House of Usher', 'The Tell-Tale Heart'
- Assessment: Quiz and analytical paragraph.

### Algebra II: Polynomial operations
- Objective: Add, subtract, multiply polynomials
- Objective: Recognize closure
- Vocabulary: polynomial, degree, leading coefficient, closure, identity
- Materials: algebra tiles or area-model templates
- Assessment: Operations quiz with proof.

### Physics of the Universe: Coulomb's law and the two forces at a distance
- Objective: Compare gravitational and electrostatic forces
- Vocabulary: charge, Coulomb's law, electric field, induction, conduction
- Materials: balloons, wool, tape, aluminum foil, PhET Coulomb's Law
- Assessment: Electroscope and Unit 2 test.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Imperialism and the Spanish-American War
- Objective: Explain U.S. expansion overseas
- Vocabulary: imperialism, yellow journalism, annexation, Open Door Policy
- Materials: Hearst/Pulitzer headlines, Anti-Imperialist League platform, McKinley's rationale
- Assessment: Argument.

### Spanish IV: Unidad 2: conversación y evaluación
- Objective: Sustain a simulated conversation
- Objective: Write a cultural comparison
- Vocabulary: comparar, a diferencia de, al igual que
- Materials: recorded conversation prompts
- Assessment: Unit 2 test.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Stanislavski and given circumstances
- Objective: Define objectives, obstacles, and tactics
- Objective: Analyze given circumstances
- Vocabulary: objective, obstacle, tactic, given circumstances, magic if, super-objective
- Materials: Stanislavski overview reading, script excerpts
- Assessment: Scene analysis.

### Lifetime Fitness: Resistance training fundamentals
- Objective: Explain muscle physiology and training principles
- Objective: Perform bodyweight exercises safely
- Vocabulary: muscular strength, muscular endurance, hypertrophy, repetition, set, concentric, eccentric
- Materials: exercise mat, resistance bands
- Assessment: Form checklist.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Precision measurement
- Objective: Measure with rulers and calipers
- Objective: Distinguish accuracy and precision
- Vocabulary: accuracy, precision, caliper, resolution, significant figures
- Materials: ruler, digital or dial caliper (low-cost), coins, bolts, LEGO bricks
- Assessment: Measurement practical.
