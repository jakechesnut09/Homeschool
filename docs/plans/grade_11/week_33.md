## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Contemporary poetry and Native American voices
- Objective: Analyze contemporary poetic forms
- Objective: Present a poetry interpretation
- Vocabulary: spoken word, persona, prose poem, sovereignty
- Materials: Joy Harjo, 'Remember' and 'Perhaps the World Ends Here', Rita Dove, 'Testimonial', Li-Young Lee, 'Persimmons', Natalie Diaz, 'The Facts of Art' (Poetry Foundation)
- Assessment: Performance and explication rubric.

### Algebra II: Simulation and margin of error
- Objective: Use simulation to estimate a margin of error
- Objective: Decide if a model is consistent with data
- Vocabulary: simulation, sampling distribution, margin of error, confidence
- Materials: coins, dice, spreadsheet random functions
- Assessment: Simulation quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Earth's formation and early history
- Objective: Construct an account of Earth's formation using ancient materials
- Vocabulary: accretion, radiometric dating, zircon, meteorite, differentiation
- Materials: two colors of candy or beans in a bag (radiometric dating model), Moon crater images
- Assessment: Timeline and Unit 8 test.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Contemporary domestic issues
- Objective: Analyze persistent social and economic issues
- Objective: Evaluate the effects of technology since 1945
- Vocabulary: digital revolution, Silicon Valley, Great Recession, income inequality, homelessness
- Materials: Pew Research Center data on women in the labor force, BLS data, California Environmental Quality Act summary
- Assessment: Unit 9 test.

### Spanish IV: Comunidades hoy: entrevista y comunidad
- Objective: Use Spanish in the local community
- Objective: Conduct an interview
- Vocabulary: la entrevista, la comunidad, el negocio, las raíces
- Materials: recording app, local Spanish-language media (Univision, La Opinión)
- Assessment: Interview profile.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Rehearsal process
- Objective: Lead rehearsals
- Objective: Give effective notes
- Vocabulary: rehearsal schedule, notes, run-through, off-book deadline
- Materials: rehearsal schedule, prompt book
- Assessment: Rehearsal report.

### Lifetime Fitness: Designing a personal fitness plan
- Objective: Design a comprehensive personal fitness plan
- Vocabulary: personal fitness plan, periodization, adherence
- Materials: plan template, fitness journal
- Assessment: Fitness plan draft.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Capstone: prototype build II
- Objective: Complete and assemble a functional prototype
- Vocabulary: assembly, fit check, fastener, integration
- Materials: fabricated parts, fasteners and adhesives, calipers
- Assessment: Assembled prototype and as-built update.
