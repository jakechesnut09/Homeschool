## Day 1 — Monday (school day 176, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Review I* (60 min): Review literary periods and key rhetorical terms with a self-made study guide and flashcards.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Review: Units 1–5* (60 min): Mixed review on quadratics, polynomials, rationals, and radicals.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Review: forces & energy* (60 min): Rework missed problems from Units 1–3 tests and complete a mixed problem set on momentum and energy conservation.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Review I* (60 min): Create a decade-by-decade chart of 1900–2000 with political, economic, social, and foreign policy columns.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Repaso de gramática* (50 min): Review all grammar from the year with a mixed practice set.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Video review* (50 min): Watch the production video and log strengths and growth areas.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Five-year plan* (45 min): Write a 5-year fitness plan covering college or work life, with yearly goals for each fitness component.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Rehearsal* (55 min): Build a 10-minute slide presentation and rehearse it twice with timing.

## Day 2 — Tuesday (school day 177, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Review II* (60 min): Practice an unseen poem and a nonfiction passage with timed responses.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Review: Units 6–9* (60 min): Mixed review on exponentials, logs, trig, sequences, and statistics.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Review: E&M & waves* (60 min): Complete a concept map linking fields, induction, and EM waves, then solve 10 wave/circuit problems.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Review II* (60 min): Practice document analysis and a timed thesis using a continuity-and-change prompt.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Repaso oral* (50 min): Practice simulated conversations and a cultural comparison.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Critique* (50 min): Write a 600-word self-critique of the production using theatre criteria.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Barriers* (45 min): List likely barriers after high school (schedule, cost, location) and a specific strategy and community resource for each.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Panel presentation* (55 min): Present to a panel of family and community members (ideally including an engineer) and demonstrate the prototype.

## Day 3 — Wednesday (school day 178, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Final exam I* (60 min): Reading comprehension: unseen literary and informational passages with multiple-choice and short answers.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Final exam part 1* (60 min): Non-calculator section.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Review: Earth & space* (60 min): Make a one-page study sheet on plate tectonics, stellar life cycles, and Big Bang evidence.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Final exam part 1* (60 min): Multiple-choice and stimulus-based questions covering all units.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Examen parte 1* (50 min): Final exam: reading and listening interpretive section.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Artistic statement* (50 min): Write an artistic statement about what theatre means to you.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Lead a workout* (45 min): Plan and lead a 40-minute family workout combining cardio, strength, and flexibility.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Q&A follow-up* (55 min): Write responses to the panel's questions and list next steps if the product were developed further.

## Day 4 — Thursday (school day 179, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Final exam II* (60 min): Timed 60-minute argument essay drawing on at least three texts from the year.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Final exam part 2* (60 min): Calculator section with modeling tasks.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Final exam part 1* (60 min): Multiple-choice and short-answer covering all nine units.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Final exam part 2* (60 min): Timed DBQ on continuity and change in the federal government's role in civil rights.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Examen parte 2* (50 min): Final exam: persuasive essay with sources and a formal email reply.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Portfolio* (50 min): Assemble a digital portfolio with headshot, résumé, monologue video, design work, and play.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Presentation* (45 min): Present your five-year plan and year-long fitness data in a 5-minute talk.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Career pathway plan* (55 min): Research community college and university engineering programs in California and write a pathway plan.

## Day 5 — Friday (school day 180, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Wrap-up* (60 min): Review exam results, set summer reading goals, and choose three books for senior year.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Reflection* (60 min): Review exam errors and write a reflection on growth and readiness for Precalculus.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Final exam part 2* (60 min): Data-analysis section: interpret two unfamiliar data sets and write CER explanations.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Reflection* (60 min): Write a reflection on how the course changed your understanding of America.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Examen parte 3* (50 min): Final exam: simulated conversation and 2-minute cultural comparison.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Final exam* (50 min): Take a written final on theatre terms, history, and production roles.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Reflection* (45 min): Write a 400-word reflection on how your attitudes toward fitness changed this year.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Final exam* (55 min): Take the written final covering the design process, drawing, measurement, CAD, materials, and ethics.
