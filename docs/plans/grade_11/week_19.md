## Day 1 — Monday (school day 91, ~440 min)

- [ ] **English 11 – American Literature** — *Modernism intro* (60 min): Notes on WWI, urbanization, and Modernism; read Imagist poems by Pound and Williams and write two Imagist poems of your own.
- [ ] **Algebra II** — *Linear vs. exponential* (60 min): Compare constant difference vs. constant ratio in tables.
- [ ] **Physics of the Universe** — *Wave types* (60 min): Make transverse and longitudinal waves with a Slinky.
- [ ] **U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century** — *Two-front strategy* (60 min): Map Midway, Normandy, Battle of the Bulge, Iwo Jima, and Okinawa; explain 'Europe first' and island hopping.
- [ ] **Spanish IV** — *Sistemas* (50 min): Compare the educational systems of Spain, Mexico, and the U.S. in a chart.
- [ ] **Theatre & Performing Arts** — *Realism* (50 min): Read about Ibsen, Chekhov, and the rise of realism and the fourth wall; read a scene from A Doll's House.
- [ ] **Lifetime Fitness** — *Groundstrokes* (45 min): Learn forehand and backhand groundstrokes and rally against a wall.
- [ ] **Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE)** — *Material families* (55 min): Classify common household items by material family (metals, polymers, ceramics, composites).

## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Modernism and the poetry of disillusionment
- Objective: Identify features of Modernism
- Objective: Analyze fragmented structure and imagery
- Vocabulary: Modernism, Imagism, fragmentation, dramatic monologue, Lost Generation
- Materials: T.S. Eliot, 'The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock', Ezra Pound, 'In a Station of the Metro', William Carlos Williams, 'The Red Wheelbarrow', Robert Frost, 'Mending Wall' and 'The Road Not Taken'
- Assessment: Quiz and poetry explication.

### Algebra II: Exponential growth and decay
- Objective: Graph and interpret exponential functions
- Objective: Model growth and decay
- Vocabulary: exponential growth, decay factor, asymptote, growth rate, half-life
- Materials: coins or M&Ms (decay simulation), Desmos
- Assessment: Decay lab and quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Wave properties
- Objective: Relate frequency, wavelength, and speed
- Vocabulary: wave, wavelength, frequency, amplitude, transverse, longitudinal
- Materials: Slinky, rope, PhET Wave on a String
- Assessment: Wave quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Fighting the war, the Holocaust, and the atomic bomb
- Objective: Explain Allied strategy and major battles
- Objective: Evaluate the decision to use atomic bombs
- Vocabulary: D-Day, island hopping, Manhattan Project, Holocaust, 442nd Regimental Combat Team, Navajo Code Talkers
- Materials: Eisenhower's D-Day order and 'in case of failure' note (DocsTeach), Truman's statement on Hiroshima, Veterans History Project interviews (LOC)
- Assessment: Unit 6 test.

### Spanish IV: El sistema educativo
- Objective: Compare educational systems
- Objective: Use si clauses (present and future)
- Vocabulary: la carrera, la matrícula, el bachillerato, la beca, la universidad pública
- Materials: article on education in Spain and Mexico, UC/CSU Spanish-language info page
- Assessment: Paragraph.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Realism and Trifles
- Objective: Identify conventions of realism
- Objective: Analyze and perform Trifles
- Vocabulary: realism, fourth wall, subtext, naturalistic acting
- Materials: Susan Glaspell, Trifles (public domain), Chekhov or Ibsen excerpt
- Assessment: Scene performance and paragraph.

### Lifetime Fitness: Tennis and racquet tournament
- Objective: Perform tennis groundstrokes and serve
- Objective: Compete with sportsmanship
- Vocabulary: groundstroke, topspin, deuce, let, etiquette
- Materials: tennis racket and balls (public court)
- Assessment: Tournament evaluation.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Materials and their properties
- Objective: Compare material properties
- Objective: Select materials
- Vocabulary: tensile strength, hardness, ductility, elasticity, composite, polymer
- Materials: samples of wood, aluminum foil, plastic, cardboard, weights
- Assessment: Materials lab report.
