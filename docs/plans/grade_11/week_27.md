## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: A Raisin in the Sun, Acts I–II
- Objective: Analyze character and conflict in drama
- Objective: Connect the play to housing discrimination history
- Vocabulary: deferred dream, redlining, foil, matriarch
- Materials: Lorraine Hansberry, A Raisin in the Sun (library), Hughes, 'Harlem'
- Assessment: Quiz and foil chart.

### Algebra II: Other trig graphs
- Objective: Graph tangent and reciprocal trig functions
- Vocabulary: asymptote, tangent graph, reciprocal graph
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Trig graphs quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: The Sun
- Objective: Model the Sun's energy and life span
- Vocabulary: nuclear fusion, core, photosphere, solar wind, sunspot
- Materials: pinhole projector materials, NASA SDO images
- Assessment: Sun quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: The movement expands
- Objective: Analyze civil rights movements of other groups
- Vocabulary: Chicano Movement, United Farm Workers, American Indian Movement, Equal Rights Amendment, Title IX
- Materials: Delano grape strike documents (Calisphere), Alcatraz occupation proclamation, NOW Statement of Purpose
- Assessment: Comparison chart.

### Spanish IV: El cuento hispanoamericano: Quiroga y Borges
- Objective: Analyze short stories
- Objective: Discuss narrative technique in Spanish
- Vocabulary: el narrador, el desenlace, el ambiente, el realismo mágico, lo fantástico
- Materials: Horacio Quiroga, 'El almohadón de plumas' (public domain), Jorge Luis Borges, 'Los dos reyes y los dos laberintos' (library)
- Assessment: Literary analysis paragraph.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Elements of playwriting
- Objective: Use dramatic structure in original writing
- Objective: Write in standard play format
- Vocabulary: inciting incident, rising action, climax, resolution, stage directions
- Materials: play-format template, Trifles
- Assessment: Scene draft.

### Lifetime Fitness: Volleyball: skills and strategy
- Objective: Perform forearm pass, set, and serve
- Objective: Apply rotation and team strategy
- Vocabulary: forearm pass, set, overhand serve, rotation, free ball
- Materials: volleyball, net or rope between two posts
- Assessment: Skills test and diagram.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Sustainability and intellectual property
- Objective: Evaluate product life cycles
- Objective: Explain patents and IP
- Vocabulary: life cycle assessment, cradle to cradle, patent, trademark, copyright, prior art
- Materials: Google Patents search, product packaging
- Assessment: Unit 8 quiz.
