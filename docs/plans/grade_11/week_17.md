## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Realist and Naturalist short fiction
- Objective: Analyze how authors develop and relate story elements
- Objective: Analyze irony and point of view
- Vocabulary: Naturalism, situational irony, stream of consciousness, determinism
- Materials: Kate Chopin, 'The Story of an Hour', Ambrose Bierce, 'An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge', Charlotte Perkins Gilman, 'The Yellow Wallpaper', Jack London, 'To Build a Fire'
- Assessment: Explanatory essay on structure.

### Algebra II: Radical functions, equations, and inverses
- Objective: Graph square and cube root functions
- Objective: Solve radical equations
- Objective: Find inverse functions
- Vocabulary: inverse function, one-to-one, composition, extraneous root
- Materials: Desmos, pendulum (string and washer)
- Assessment: Radical functions quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Materials science
- Objective: Explain why molecular structure matters in designed materials
- Vocabulary: conductor, insulator, semiconductor, polymer, alloy
- Materials: materials samples: copper, plastic, rubber, glass
- Assessment: Infographic rubric.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: The home front and Japanese American incarceration
- Objective: Analyze constitutional issues on the home front
- Objective: Describe wartime mobilization and California's industrial growth
- Vocabulary: Executive Order 9066, Korematsu v. United States, rationing, Rosie the Riveter, Executive Order 8802, Bracero Program
- Materials: Executive Order 9066 (DocsTeach), Dorothea Lange and Ansel Adams photos of Manzanar (Calisphere/LOC), Korematsu v. U.S. majority and Murphy dissent excerpts
- Assessment: Civil liberties response.

### Spanish IV: Ideales de belleza y la moda
- Objective: Discuss how beauty standards are defined by culture
- Objective: Use the future perfect and conditional perfect
- Vocabulary: los cánones de belleza, la moda, la autoestima, la publicidad, habría cambiado
- Materials: Spanish-language magazine ads (online), article on beauty standards and social media
- Assessment: Blog post and quiz.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Showcase rehearsal
- Objective: Refine scenes and monologues for an audience
- Objective: Plan front-of-house elements
- Vocabulary: run-through, notes, cue, house, program
- Materials: costume pieces, simple props, printer for programs
- Assessment: Dress rehearsal notes.

### Lifetime Fitness: Badminton and racquet strategy
- Objective: Perform clears, drops, and smashes
- Objective: Apply offensive and defensive strategy
- Vocabulary: clear, drop shot, smash, base position, rally scoring
- Materials: badminton rackets and shuttles (or a community center)
- Assessment: Skill test and comparison.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Reverse engineering: analysis and redesign
- Objective: Model parts and propose improvements
- Vocabulary: material analysis, manufacturing process, improvement, Design for Assembly
- Materials: calipers, Onshape, disassembled product
- Assessment: Reverse engineering report.
