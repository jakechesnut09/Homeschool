## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Oratory of the Civil War era
- Objective: Analyze structure and rhetoric of speeches
- Objective: Evaluate speakers' reasoning
- Vocabulary: anaphora, antithesis, parallelism, allusion, jeremiad
- Materials: Douglass, 'What to the Slave Is the Fourth of July?', Sojourner Truth, 'Ain't I a Woman?' (both versions), Lincoln, Gettysburg Address and Second Inaugural
- Assessment: Speech delivery rubric and timed rhetorical analysis.

### Algebra II: Rational functions and graphs
- Objective: Graph rational functions with asymptotes and holes
- Objective: Rewrite rational functions
- Vocabulary: vertical asymptote, horizontal asymptote, hole, inverse variation
- Materials: Desmos
- Assessment: Rational graphs quiz.

### Physics of the Universe: Electric circuits
- Objective: Build and analyze circuits
- Objective: Apply Ohm's law
- Vocabulary: current, voltage, resistance, Ohm's law, series, parallel
- Materials: AA batteries, holders, small bulbs or LEDs, wires, PhET Circuit Construction Kit
- Assessment: Circuits quiz.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: Human toll: Dust Bowl and migration to California
- Objective: Analyze the human toll of the Depression
- Vocabulary: Dust Bowl, Okies, Hooverville, Mexican repatriation
- Materials: Dorothea Lange photos (Calisphere/LOC), Woody Guthrie songs, Steinbeck excerpts
- Assessment: Essay.

### Spanish IV: Arte latinoamericano: los muralistas y Frida Kahlo
- Objective: Analyze murals as social commentary
- Objective: Use relative pronouns
- Vocabulary: el mural, el muralismo, la identidad mestiza, que, quien, lo que, el cual
- Materials: images of Diego Rivera murals (Detroit Industry, San Francisco Pan American Unity), Frida Kahlo, Las dos Fridas
- Assessment: Mural design and explanation.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Shakespeare: language and verse
- Objective: Speak Shakespearean verse
- Objective: Analyze text for performance
- Vocabulary: iambic pentameter, verse, prose, scansion, Globe Theatre
- Materials: Folger Shakespeare text of A Midsummer Night's Dream
- Assessment: Shakespeare speech.

### Lifetime Fitness: Yoga foundations
- Objective: Perform foundational yoga postures with alignment
- Objective: Use breathing to manage stress
- Vocabulary: asana, vinyasa, alignment, diaphragmatic breathing, sun salutation
- Materials: mat, free yoga video (e.g., Yoga with Adriene)
- Assessment: Pose sequence demonstration.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Assemblies
- Objective: Create assemblies with mates
- Objective: Check for interference
- Vocabulary: assembly, mate, degrees of freedom, interference
- Materials: Onshape
- Assessment: Assembly check.
