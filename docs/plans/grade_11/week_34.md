## Objectives, materials & assessment

### English 11 – American Literature: Argument and media: the American Dream today
- Objective: Synthesize multiple sources in different media
- Objective: Write a synthesis argument
- Vocabulary: synthesis, bias, data, op-ed
- Materials: Pew Research Center short reports on economic mobility, two op-eds on the American Dream, a TED talk or podcast segment
- Assessment: Synthesis essay and grammar test.

### Algebra II: Comparing treatments and statistics project
- Objective: Use randomization tests to compare treatments
- Objective: Conduct a statistical study
- Vocabulary: randomization test, statistical significance, treatment group, control
- Materials: materials for chosen experiment (e.g., paper airplanes, plant seeds)
- Assessment: Statistics project and Unit 9 test.

### Physics of the Universe: Energy resources and trade-offs
- Objective: Evaluate competing energy solutions with cost-benefit analysis
- Objective: Specify criteria for a global challenge
- Vocabulary: renewable, nonrenewable, levelized cost, capacity factor, carbon intensity
- Materials: California Energy Commission electricity-mix data, home electric bill
- Assessment: Energy plan recommendation.

### U.S. History & Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century: History research project: question and sources
- Objective: Develop a historical research question
- Objective: Locate primary and secondary sources
- Vocabulary: historiography, thesis, primary source, annotated bibliography
- Materials: National History Day theme page, Calisphere, LOC, DocsTeach
- Assessment: Bibliography rubric.

### Spanish IV: Proyecto final: documental o podcast
- Objective: Plan a culminating presentational project
- Vocabulary: el guion, la edición, el podcast, el documental
- Materials: recording app or video editor
- Assessment: Project checkpoint.

### Theatre & Performing Arts: Technical rehearsal
- Objective: Integrate design elements
- Objective: Run a cue-to-cue
- Vocabulary: tech rehearsal, cue-to-cue, stage manager, call time
- Materials: set pieces, costumes, lights, sound from Unit 7 designs
- Assessment: Tech run.

### Lifetime Fitness: Implementing the plan
- Objective: Carry out and monitor a personal fitness plan
- Vocabulary: adherence, self-monitoring
- Materials: fitness journal
- Assessment: Plan implementation log.

### Introduction to Engineering Design (CTE): Capstone: testing and iteration
- Objective: Test against specifications
- Objective: Gather user feedback and iterate
- Vocabulary: test plan, specification, user testing, iteration
- Materials: prototype, measurement tools, spreadsheet
- Assessment: Test report.
