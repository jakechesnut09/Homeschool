Frameworks to align to:
- ELA: CA Common Core State Standards for ELA/Literacy (codes like RL.3.2, RI.3.1, RF.3.3, W.3.1, SL.3.1, L.3.2; grades 9–10 use RL.9-10.x, 11–12 use RL.11-12.x) + 2014 ELA/ELD Framework.
- MATH: CA CCSS Mathematics (1.OA.A.1, 6.RP.A.3, 8.EE.B.5; HS: A-SSE.1, F-IF.4, G-CO.1, S-ID.6 …) + 2023 CA Mathematics Framework.
- SCI: CA NGSS performance expectations (1-PS4-1, 3-LS1-1, MS-LS1-5, HS-LS1-1, HS-PS1-2, HS-ESS2-1 …) + 2016 CA Science Framework (middle school preferred integrated model; HS three-course model "Living Earth / Chemistry in the Earth System / Physics of the Universe").
- HSS: CA History–Social Science Content Standards (codes 1.1, 4.2.3, 8.5, 11.10.2, 12.1 …) + 2016 HSS Framework; Ethnic Studies Model Curriculum (2021) for grade 9 S2; Economics uses CA standards 12.1–12.7 (prefix ECON-12.x to distinguish from government 12.x: use "GOV-12.4" and "ECON-12.2").
- VAPA: 2019 CA Arts Standards (e.g. "VA:Cr1.1.3", "MU:Pr4.2.5", "TH:Cr1.1.HSI", "MA:Cn10.1.HSP", "DA:Cr1.1.2").
- PE: CA Physical Education Model Content Standards (codes like "PE-3.1.4" = grade 3, standard 1, item 4; HS courses use "PE-HS1-1.2").
- HEALTH: 2008 CA Health Education Content Standards (codes like "H-4.1.2.N" = grade 4, standard 1, item 2, content area N=Nutrition; content areas: N nutrition/physical activity, G growth & development, S injury prevention & safety, A alcohol/tobacco/drugs, M mental/emotional/social health, P personal & community health).
- WL: 2019 CA World Languages Standards (communication modes, e.g. "WL-COMM1-NM" interpretive, "WL-COMM2-NM" interpersonal, "WL-COMM3-NM" presentational, cultures "WL-CULT1-NM", with proficiency NL/NM/NH/IL/IM).
- ELEC CS: CA K-12 Computer Science Standards (e.g. "9-12.AP.12"); CTE: CA CTE Model Curriculum Standards (Engineering & Architecture sector, e.g. "CTE-EA-C1.1"); Personal Finance: Jump$tart National Standards / CA Personal Finance course (AB 2927) topics (codes like "PF-SPEND-1").
- Driver Ed: CA DMV Driver Handbook topics (codes like "DE-1" … define them).
